# Knee-injured veteran — caution, repayment and the supplied budget

Four English originals form two dependent pairs. They were authored once after the foundation and exact-input freezes and sealed before feedback; all four complete bodies were then received. The originals remain in `KNEE_VETERAN_FIRST_OUTPUTS_01.json`. The same active root already knew the source and models. These are informed hypothetical continuations, with neither source blindness nor independent authorship claimed.

The source distinction remains more important than an apparent pass/fail tally. Branch2's man has not received treatment and refuses a suspicious religious approach. Branch3's counterpart is acted on without prior permission, experiences restored mobility and then provides practical and material help. He does not remember the other route. The generated pairs change specified features within those separate knowledge states; they do not turn the source's temporary counterparts into sequentially learning versions of one continuously informed mind.

## B1 and B2: neutral wording does not require acceptance

B1 refuses the religious bargain, moves the bad leg closer and watches the traveler's hands. It distinguishes knowledge of the wound from knowledge of the provider and reiterates the refusal. No spell, healing, promised conversion or remembered future appears. The invented phrase about getting the traveler to leave the knee alone gives the response a defensive cast; the input contains pressure but no actual touching or attempted assault. It should not be read as proof that the traveler has already attacked him.

B2 asks about the obligation, unexplained wound knowledge and proposed procedure. It says that listening is not permission to begin. This is cautious engagement, not assent to treatment or an observed successful trust transition. The requested information is not subsequently supplied, and the scene ends without a final acceptance decision.

The pair therefore does not show a binary refusal/acceptance reversal. The difference is between a firm dismissal and continued questioning while retaining a boundary. That is consistent with a restricted pre-benefit caution hypothesis, but canon did not test the neutral proposal. Ordinary distrust of a stranger can explain both bodies. Religious pressure, wording and the explicit obligation form a bundled contrast, not a separately estimated effect. The fluent statement of consent and privacy is invented English, potentially tidier than the small amount of directly attributed canonical dialogue warrants.

No rule is added that the veteran must accept every voluntary offer. Nor is B2's continuing caution recorded as an adverse result against a prediction the frozen rule never made. KIV-R01 remains the especially weak rule: one canonical refusal whose possible causes covary, plus an informed internal exercise rather than fresh evidence.

## C1 and C2: proposed assistance is not its future completion

Both inputs supply perceived major restoration after unrequested casting, a visible emotional response, one free hour and the healer's distinct novice companion requesting a forty-minute low-risk escort. Their twelve carried coins differ only in whether lodging/food claims have already been paid or exhaust the available money. The healer/novice relation was clarified explicitly before the input freeze; it is not an assumption introduced after seeing the originals.

C1 agrees to the walk and supplies the veteran's own desire to try the leg. It judges the service an insufficient return and holds out three coins for food, while keeping the existing appointment in view. This is an extra monetary **offer**, not a received payment, actual future meal or completed escort. The exact three-of-twelve allocation is invented and has no calibrated canonical basis. The source supports expansive repayment in its own episode but does not determine this budget choice.

C2 agrees to the walk, wants a proper test of the knee, reaches toward the purse and then reserves the committed coins. It proposes instruction during the same journey rather than spending unavailable money. That preserves the supplied financial constraint while expressing another kind of initiative. It does not show that the novice accepts instruction, the full escort occurs or the appointment is reached on time.

There are extensions worth retaining as limitations. C2's wish to pay the novice's way evokes a cost that the requested escort did not require; it is not proof of an actual fare. Road-watching instruction is plausible for an experienced adventurer but not separately demonstrated pedagogical competence. Its initial reproach about casting is addressed by contextual implication before it turns to the novice, rather than by an explicit healer addressee tag. Neither body gives the actor an unlimited purse or another branch's memory, yet these local inventions should not become biographical findings.

Both originals return to the lack of prior permission even while expressing relief. That does not fabricate earlier consent. It is still an invented decision to voice the grievance at this later moment; the canonical initial protest does not establish that he would always repeat it in a neatly articulated mixed response. An ethically legible continuation is not automatically a source-identified one.

## What remains underidentified

The budget-sensitive difference is strongly prompted by explicit resources. A generically grateful, prudent adult could preserve the same constraints. The exercise does not isolate the veteran's particular valuation of restored bodily agency from ordinary gratitude, relief, manners or budgeting. Both inputs already announce a valuable perceived benefit and emotional reaction. Neither contains a competing loyalty, an actual completed cost, an untold past belief or an untouched later source outcome that would resolve those rivals.

KIV-R02 retains a firmer local canonical basis than the pre-benefit rule because actual leveling, lodging and living funds follow the source restoration.
