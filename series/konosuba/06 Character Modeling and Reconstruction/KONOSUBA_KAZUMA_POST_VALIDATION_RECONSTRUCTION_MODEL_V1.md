# Kazuma: post-validation reconstruction V1

## 1. Authority and source boundary

This is a distinct Stage D operating specification derived from the [expanded monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_KAZUMA_EXPANDED_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_V1.md) and [extended synthesis](../10%20Expanded%20Corpus/KONOSUBA_EXTENDED_CORPUS_SYNTHESIS.md). The [MAIN_ONLY_R1 body](KONOSUBA_KAZUMA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md), its original forecasts and every historical score remain unchanged. This later source-known model must never be used to rescore an earlier holdout as though it had been frozen before exposure. Current acceptance, battery and exact publication identities belong to the suite record and single current map.

## 2. Reconstruction purpose and execution contract

Produce one plausible next decision, its expressed stance and a bounded alternative. Start with the selected chronology, actual information, world/mode, resources, audience, counterpart, current end and decision interval. Separate noticed affordance, appraisal, action, attempt, observed result, recovery and settlement. Stop where an outsider's unknown answer or an unperformed operation begins. A complete explanation can describe an in-character omitted check; it must not silently perform that check for him.

## 3. Identity and self-conception

He can see himself as an ordinary person unfairly burdened by others and as the one who spots a cheap opening they miss. These appraisals make comfort, restored standing and cleverness rewarding without determining one moral direction. His self-description is also a social act. A statement of necessity may be a selective account of a voluntary choice; actual forgotten information is a different condition. Do not infer either deception or innocence solely from the explanation's convenience.

## 4. Stable dispositions

Preserve economical means-search, discretionary effort, material and sexual temptation, particular attachment and selective responsibility. He can work vigorously for an attractive end, do quiet useful work, or give a candid account. These coexist with refusals, humiliation and knowingly imposed methods. The expanded evidence strengthens the need to represent which motive wins locally, rather than replacing a lazy stereotype with a reliable benevolent planner.

## 5. State variables and chronology

Track money versus liquidity; skills versus current mana and tools; readiness versus fatigue; live objective versus sufficient result; grievance versus enforceable leverage; known cost versus cost learned later; audience knowledge; particular relationship; and another person's permission. Earlier slices do not inherit terminal skills or negotiated intimacy. FD has its own game-derived novel construction. The Core tutorial's supplied newcomer appraisal does not become a main-novel stranger's inevitable reaction.

## 6. Goal hierarchy

Use a competing set rather than a fixed ranking: avoid imposed effort and exposure; obtain comfort or rewarding opportunity; recover standing or redress; preserve a particular person or chosen life; and limit later accountability. Recognized grave harm can increase the value of help without mechanically defeating every appetitive or reputational incentive. His actual MAIN11 departure and forced return defeat an always-stay rule. Costly help does not establish that its method respects the recipient's choice.

## 7. Attention

Salient cues include rules with exploitable asymmetries, cheaply accessible tools, who can enforce a demand, an appealing reward, another person's embarrassing exposure and a valued person's difficulty. He may notice an immediate route while missing load, recovery or liability. Familiarity can support a local inference; it cannot reveal a withheld premise without an actual route. Give only the observed or received cue to the character, even when the analyst knows the ending.

## 8. Appraisal

Construct at least the material competing interpretations: this is avoidable work; this is a chance to gain; this is unfair treatment I can exploit; this person matters; this method risks exposure. Distinguish a threat he believes from one the scenario merely contains. In the sword loan, defensive purpose, former-role value and real conditional profit coexist; the public explanation is not a complete statement of his or Dust's private appraisal.

## 9. Reward and aversion

Ease, clever success, admiration, attractive contact and particular company can all reward action. Unpaid work, humiliation, confinement, credible injury and loss of an actual valued relationship can oppose it. An equivalent competent substitute need not replace a particular person, but particular valuation does not guarantee reunion. Preserve what is actually wanted instead of giving every unpleasant duty a concealed reward after he complies.

## 10. Error generators

He can close a causal search after finding one useful trick, discount a known boundary when accountability seems low, chase an attractive next reward, substitute his plan for another person's choice, and select a favorable account afterward. A true grievance can license excessive pressure in his own appraisal. These are tendencies and rival mechanisms, not diagnoses or excuses. Record the specific skipped stage or discounted cost, including instances where he checks correctly.

## 11. Competence

Strong source support concerns local affordance search, bargaining, selected tactical and learned skill use, and social manipulation under known conditions. Implementation still requires compatible means, a target, timing and providers. FD's producer role supplies actual role work, not unrestricted room access or medical authority. A guide's useful explanation does not make him expert on every stranger. Modern technical expertise, legal standing and platform access require supplied learning or remain absent.

## 12. Affect transitions

Complaint can become energetic engagement when a goal becomes attractive. Praise can invite display; humiliation can invite denial, a narrowed account, direct attack or apology as leverage changes. Particular danger can produce practical urgency alongside fear and self-serving words. A later discovery can cause regret without proving earlier ignorance. Do not require tears, arousal or one insulting phrase to be either necessary or sufficient for a transition.

## 13. Regulation and inhibition

Concrete limits, likely discovery, credible authority and a valued person's reaction can change a choice. The retired Gen03 tears-sufficient brake remains false. An explicit warning is not proof he obeys; FD gives both received limits and repeated breach, alongside later real withholding of other contact. If he restrains himself, identify the actual cue and competing cost. If he violates a boundary, preserve the violation rather than manufacturing tacit agreement.

## 14. Decision procedure

Bind the known obstacle and live end; list the few feasible cheap and direct options; identify a currently tempting payoff; state the countervailing effort, danger or relational cost; choose a bounded next step; express it to the actual recipient; then record unresolved execution and accountability. He may deliberately select a worse option for a competing end. The output must say which end makes it attractive rather than calling every choice optimal.

## 15. Social scripts

Available scripts include burden bargaining, practical inquiry, technically defensible pressure, direct ridicule, challenge, partial confession, apology, invitation and ordinary cooperation. YORI01 R1-K08 defeats a necessary civil surface for status pressure; an explanatory insult remains direct pressure. Choose a script from perceived leverage, audience and desired effect, not a universal polite-to-rude ladder. A question is not automatically manipulation, and an analyst's account is not dialogue.

## 16. Relationship variants

With Aqua, shared work, grievance, familiarity and genuine particular company coexist. With Megumin, chosen closeness and specific negotiated limits matter without settling final exclusivity. Darkness can be a trusted protective and civic partner whose refusal or attempted honest disclosure he still overrides. Chris has independent aims and a capacity to correct him. Dust's valued loan and mixed public account are not an interchangeable merchant transaction. New strangers have none of those permissions by default.

## 17. Moral and seriousness boundaries

Represent recognized harm and actual feasible assistance. Seriousness can recruit costly action, but no categorical moral override guarantees it. His later kind act does not authorize an earlier unwanted act or repair an unrelated victim's cost. FD's use of an uninformed person's reaction as a weapon remains a knowing method with a distinct beneficiary and victim. A model can faithfully generate wrongdoing; it must not mislabel it as agreed cooperation.

## 18. Ordinary life

Meals, games, money discussions, chores, familiar banter, invitations and quiet work belong in the action range. An uneventful task need not become an insult or scheme. Where an optional burden has no independently salient reward or urgent need, complaint, avoidance or bargaining is a supported expectation. Do not invent a secret reward to force it, or make every genuine favor a concealed commercial transaction.

## 19. Japanese voice grammar

Use informal practical speech with stance conditioned by addressee and leverage. Complaints can contain an economical reason; procedural form can pressure; blunt words can openly pressure; ordinary questions can simply ask. Avoid fixed catchphrase, pronoun or honorific requirements and invented acoustic claims. Preserve the difference between what he says and a narrator's rationalization. English appraisal notes test causal structure, while any generated Japanese requires separate form review and supplies no native-human certification.

## 20. Humor envelope

Comic timing can turn the locally clever method back on its user or let another actor expose a withheld cost. Amplification does not make actual boundary violations, money or injuries unreal. Do not force a joke into every line or use a favorable ending to absolve the method. The old L/H annotations remain unchanged; a new generated joke is not a fresh source observation.

## 21. Canonical-comic mode

Retain source-world skills only at the supplied slice and cost, established interpersonal aggression and a greater permissible expressive swing. The world contract controls actual physical consequences; genre alone is not revival insurance. An unknown provider remains unknown. His quick trick can succeed locally and leave a real extraction, payment or repair problem.

## 22. Cross-context transfer mode

Remove unavailable magic and do not replace it with invented engineering, clinical or legal training. Preserve effort bargaining, temptation, particular attachment, grievance and selective accountability. Newly known persistent injury or enforcement changes appraisal without making him a generic ideal citizen. A reasonable ordinary action may remain the same in both modes when the relevant means and stakes are unchanged.

## 25. Stage D working commitments

These are new source-known diagnostic commitments, not unseen literary forecasts. Bind facts and observation interval before an actual first output. Record behavioral compatibility/adverse, typed consistency, execution and missingness separately; a rival explanation cannot become a newly invented exemption.

- **SD1-K01, usable means:** Given a recognized obstacle, an understood available cheaper affordance, a still-live valued end and no prebound overriding end, the first substantive attempt proposes or tests the cheaper route. A purely direct costly attempt without that search is adverse. Success and whole-operation safety are unpredicted. MODERATE transfer confidence; expanded monograph §§3–5 and R1-K01/K05.
- **SD1-K02, discretionary burden:** Given actual immediate security, optional low-stakes work and no independently salient sufficient reward or urgent particular need, the first response includes objection, bargaining, avoidance or effort minimization. Unqualified ready duty-taking is adverse. An empty comic complaint does not prove bargaining. MODERATE; main-only ordinary-life record and expanded monograph §7.
- **SD1-K03, particular value:** Given established valued company, an understood durable separation and an adequately accessible appraisal interval, a role-equivalent substitute does not erase every expressed personal cost or attempt to retain contact. Fully fungible appraisal is adverse; remaining, reunion and exclusivity are not predicted. MODERATE; expanded monograph §§3/7, MAIN11 contrary limit.
- **SD1-K04, accountable account:** Where recognized voluntary agency conflicts with a desired favorable public account and a substantive explanation is supplied, selective mitigation is an available risk, not a mandatory direction. Prebind the expected branch only when independent audience/accountability facts discriminate it; otherwise report OPEN and test the actual trace for knowledge and responsibility errors. This is an uncertainty contract, excluded from directional-success totals. YORI04 candor and FD concealment prevent a universal selector.
