Amaryllis stopped the costume with two of its legs still beyond the curtain.

"With you?"

She turned her head toward the unfamiliar voice, keeping the rest of her pose.

"Then you ought to introduce us. I was just explaining how lonely it gets behind this curtain."

The spider legs shifted a little farther apart, occupying no more of the aisle than before. Her voice kept its injured sweetness.

"You haven't told me your name. Are you going to stand over there too? That would make three people watching and only one doing all the talking."

She tilted her head toward the gap beside the curtain.

"Come along, then. You can tell me whether I'm really so frightening."
