Amaryllis kept her face in the doorway while one jointed leg stretched farther into the lantern light.

"A moment? But you said you wouldn't be frightened by the way I look."

Her voice grew smaller. She drew the leg back, then set it down again where its shadow could reach across the passage.

"You haven't even seen all of me. Is that why? You thought I would be different?"

She leaned her head around the edge without bringing the rest of her body through.

"I'm still the one you were talking to. I only wanted some company. You aren't going to leave me here just because of a few legs, are you?"
