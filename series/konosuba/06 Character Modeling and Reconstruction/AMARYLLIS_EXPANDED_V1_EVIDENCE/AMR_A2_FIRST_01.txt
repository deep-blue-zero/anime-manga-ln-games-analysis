"You might be frightened?"

Amaryllis repeated it softly, as though trying out the words. One spider leg came into the lantern light, then another; her head remained beside the doorway.

"Then perhaps you haven't decided yet. You should have a proper look before you decide."

She paused with the rest of her body still hidden. The speaking traveler's request hung between them, and she turned her face toward the lantern instead of answering it.

"Would it be better if I came out slowly? Or if you came over here? It's very lonely, talking from opposite sides like this."

She placed one foot a little farther forward, with exaggerated care.
