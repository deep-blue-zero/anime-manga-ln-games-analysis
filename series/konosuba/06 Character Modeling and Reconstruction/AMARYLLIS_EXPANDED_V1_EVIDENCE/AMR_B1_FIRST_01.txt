The spider costume stopped halfway out from the curtain.

"Vanir?"

Amaryllis lifted her head, and the pleading note disappeared from her voice.

"Oh, these are your guests. I beg your pardon. You have caught me making rather an elaborate introduction."

She drew the costume back enough to keep its legs out of the open aisle, then inclined her head toward the two visitors.

"Please excuse the interruption. I was beginning to think our rehearsal had become unusually absorbing."

Her attention returned to the voice beside the curtain.

"Will you be staying with us? It would be a pity to waste such an attentive audience. But perhaps you would prefer to introduce us first."
