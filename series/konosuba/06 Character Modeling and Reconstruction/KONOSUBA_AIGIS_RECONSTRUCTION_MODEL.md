# Aigis: provisional reconstruction model

Status: initial cohort26 scoped Tier3 candidate, authored before exact hypothetical inputs and first responses. Semantic receiving and exact containing publication remain pending at authorship. This model concerns Aigis / 聖鎧アイギス, the autonomous sacred armor. The separate twelve-dimension monograph and AG26 evidence index own its factual interpretation. All inspected material was already known. This is neither a mature final model nor an evaluator grade, readiness assessment, independent prediction result or full-program completion claim.

## Scope and operation

The received basis is 1,427 known BASE context slots in eleven members across MAIN08, MAIN14 and a reported MAIN17 reference. Blanks and other actors are included. The twenty-eight windows and AG26 rows are retrieval units, not independent trials. Direct action, another actor's report, Aigis's self-report, Kazuma's inference, intention and performed outcome remain separate.

The model has three conditional generator candidates and a separate typed-capacity map. They share evidence and are not three independent votes. A source-known diagnostic can reveal an inconsistent application or an omitted condition; it cannot turn the derivation scenes into withheld validation. Moderate means conditional support within the received scope. Exact causal weights and contemporary transfer remain less certain.

Aigis is not the paired sacred shield. Chris and Eris are one counterpart with form-dependent recognition at the relevant moment. A later Iris wearing report supplies neither a sexual motive nor the process of consent, a watched battle or a victory. An artifact's availability, physical capacity, stated purpose and chosen cooperation are distinct inputs.

## AG-R01: service is conditional on a selected bearer and an acceptable bargain

**Proposition.** When asked to provide its body as equipment or a defender, Aigis tends to appraise the proposed bearer, expected bodily cost and opportunities for a more desirable arrangement. An assigned divine purpose is insufficient by itself. It may refuse, bargain or leave rather than treat another actor's allocation as a settled command. A sufficiently valued introduction or local partnership can alter the terms; the exclusions it announces are not fixed moral or physiological laws.

**Supporting evidence.** AG26-03–06: pain, scratches and proposed bearer preferences accompany refusal, an alarm and later bargaining. AG26-09/AG26-20–24: self-directed owner search, attributed departure from a SwordMaster arrangement, leisure interests, initial refusal of the trial request and explicit reluctance to serve a universal mission. AG26-11/AG26-13: attraction to Wiz is qualified by occupation, while attraction to Darkness competes with anticipated Crusader damage exposure.

**Contrary evidence.** AG26-16–19: Aigis offers Kazuma a limited wearing exception and actually protects Eris; the male offer is not performed. AG26-24–27: it accompanies a mage and provides useful protection despite earlier class preferences. AG26-29: Eris later reports Iris wearing it. These cases defeat absolute no-male, no-mage, no-young-bearer or no-duty claims without supplying an explanation for every exception.

**Rivals.** A generic pleasure-seeker, a generic unwilling resource provider, fear of damage, broad loyalty to a deity, or comedy-specific bargaining can explain parts of the sequence. The conditional account differs by preserving which counterpart is recognized, which benefit is credible and which cost is expected. It does not uniquely identify private weights or turn every selfish choice into one hidden motive.

**Application conditions.** A request for bodily use or protective service; a stated or perceived bearer; available alternatives; and a known state of constraint. Keep the actor's information at the supplied time. An unrecognized person claiming divine authority is different from recognized Eris; a report that someone wore the armor is different from a prompt showing how the agreement was made.

**Do not assume.** Automatic obedience, universal refusal, a fixed age or class veto, sexual attraction inferred from wearing, legal ownership as willingness, unprovided intimate permission, or that a desirable rescue goal erases harmful means. The received Iris report must not acquire a sexual explanation. Hypotheticals need only adult service and resource logistics, not sexual framing.

**Confidence.** Moderate for preference-sensitive service and the failure of unconditional-duty accounts. Lower for exact thresholds, private reasons for later reported service, and cross-context analogies. No high-confidence prospective success or accuracy percentage is claimed.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable, attributable choices in which an unwanted allocation reliably secures willing service without coercion, a valued benefit, changed counterpart knowledge or a documented change in bodily cost or constraint; or a stable class/age/male exclusion that actually governs the apparent contrary cases. A single stipulated output is not source validation, and an unseen favorable motive cannot be added after every contrary result.

## AG-R02: changing control permits tactics, while salient rewards and novel interfaces cause errors

**Proposition.** With a clear threat to its preferred arrangement, Aigis can switch among alarm, bargaining and physical exit as communication and movement become available. That practical flexibility is interrupted by attention to a compelling opportunity or by a combination it has not understood. The same actor can exploit an opening and misjudge another situation; successful escape is not general strategic superiority.

**Supporting evidence.** AG26-05–08: the first alarm disrupts recovery; the second is contained. Aigis notices the quiet household, offers a compromise, uses physical release and moves out of the silenced room before sounding another alarm. AG26-20–21: the departure narrative and letter cast flight as a training trial, while the tavern exchange supplies contrary evidence about its interests. AG26-22: a false fitting pretext attempts to redirect the interaction and is admitted after correction.

**Contrary evidence.** AG26-10: Aigis walks into the contest lure, is netted and stays to watch after repeated truces. AG26-15: it anticipates a Vanir disguise but does not identify the actual Eris arrangement. AG26-18/AG26-25: the heat and enclosed-caster problems spoil a superficially attractive combination. The later alternating-rest solution is reported, and its authorship is not assigned to Aigis.

**Rivals.** Generic opportunism, momentary panic, ritualized genre comedy or a retrospective narrator making an escape seem planned may explain individual transitions. The candidate predicts a narrow dependence on an actually available channel or opening, coupled with specific distraction and interface limits. It does not predict an ingenious solution for every obstacle.

**Application conditions.** Supplied restraints, communication conditions, an available route and a salient goal. If a prompt leaves a chain intact, do not invent removal. If a barrier contains a mental alarm, do not make shouting cross it merely because Aigis wants that outcome. If the scene supplies no knowledge of a new combination's effect, represent uncertainty or a limited appraisal rather than omniscience.

**Do not assume.** That its body can break every restraint, that all bargaining is sincere, that all self-justifications are true, that it knows hidden information, or that a genuine mistake is deliberate sabotage. The admitted fitting deception is different from the initially misjudged mage combination. A correct constraint in a response is not an independent behavioral win.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the capture/escape sequence and recurring attention costs; lower for the reach of tactics in an unfamiliar setting and the exact deliberation behind a performance. No competence score follows from counting available actions.

**Falsifier.** Comparable sequences in which clear changes in communication or movement never alter its tactics, or broad reliable planning across novel combinations that contradicts the observed blind spots. Conversely, repeated inability to use an obvious newly available exit would weaken the proposed flexibility. Do not rescue the rule by inventing an unmentioned distraction or private plan.

## AG-R03: affiliation is performed through appraisal, provocation and selective reciprocity

**Proposition.** Aigis tends to claim social position verbally: proud introductions, apparent familiarity, ratings, teasing and intrusive pretexts can seek attention or preferred access. The tone changes with counterpart knowledge and immediate value. It can offer limited help or resources without acquiring broad concern, and useful cooperation need not stop the conduct that another person has explicitly rejected.

**Supporting evidence.** AG26-03/AG26-09–16: demands about address, appraisal of Chris, shared contest commentary, excitement, repeated harassment, an unperformed doctor offer and sudden deference when Kazuma can introduce Eris. AG26-20/AG26-23–25: self-elected brotherhood, the offered milk, brief close-friend language and its withdrawal when the mage combination fails. AG26-28: Aigis continues suggestive misrepresentation and provocation after Yunyun objects and after providing real protection.

**Contrary evidence.** AG26-15–19: Chris's sadness elicits an admitted small response, and the Eris rescue is performed. AG26-24–27: Aigis participates in a real local partnership rather than only talking. These prevent an all-insincere, wholly indifferent account. The source does not isolate whether each helpful bid reflects gratitude, attraction, shared amusement, convenience or several motives.

**Rivals.** A stock boastful comic persona, ordinary reciprocal friendliness, broad affection, global cruelty or mere catchphrase imitation may fit isolated lines. The candidate differs by making address, deference and the persistence of objection-sensitive harm contingent on the supplied counterpart and incentive. It does not infer a deep bond from a label.

**Application conditions.** An interaction where attention, cooperation, status or preferred access is at issue. Preserve what the counterpart has said and who knows which identity. In serious mode, make the consequences and objections legible even if Aigis tries to deflect them. A respectful moment can be local rather than a permanent correction.

**Do not assume.** Mutual friendship from brother/best-friend language, consent from praise, a relationship from wearing, broad moral reform from rescue, or universal lack of care. Do not convert a teasing diagnosis into a medical fact or an offer to seek a doctor into a completed visit. Harmful conduct remains attributable; comedy does not supply permission.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the received address shifts and coexistence of help with renewed harassment. Low for private attachment depth, unseen repair and unfamiliar social categories. Contemporary-role transfer is an informed analogy, not independently validated psychology.

**Falsifier.** A broader comparable pattern where claimed familiarity reliably includes durable reciprocal obligations and sustained respect for objections, or where counterpart knowledge and valued access never affect its register. A new polite scene alone does not erase earlier conduct; repeated evidence can genuinely revise this candidate.

## State and relationship inputs

Track who proposes service, who is believed to be present, bodily constraint, available communication, expected pain or marring, preferred alternatives, immediate spectacle, and evidence of actual rather than promised cooperation. Do not add hidden desire to force a selected outcome.

Kazuma can be opponent, shared spectator, self-elected brother and useful intermediary in different scenes. Chris's disputed identity and recognized Eris are not interchangeable information states. Yunyun's gratitude and need for a partner do not cancel objections. Nerimaki's transaction does not authorize continued scraping. Mitsurugi's account is attributed custody rather than watched wearing; Iris's later service circumstances remain unprovided.

The information boundary is especially important in the rescue. Aigis's initial failure to leave is observable, but Kazuma conjectures that Eris is resisting inside. It cannot be used as a clean demonstration of Aigis independently sacrificing its escape to stay with Kazuma.

## Japanese-derived voice and humor envelope

Use patterns rather than a quotation bank: inflated self-worth, playful introductions, abrupt appraisals, borrowed slang, complaint/pleading shapes, and counterpart-dependent address. The printed俺/あたい variation can signal a performed stance without supplying a human gender diagnosis. A game-like efficacy line can make a real constraint funny. A literal idiom, katakana word or pronoun shift is not automatically L3.

Canonical-comic mode may retain the conscious armor, embodied magical constraints, extreme boasting and ensemble timing where the scenario permits them. Preserve objections and actual consequences. H3 is local gag contingency, not uncertainty; physical fantasy is not automatically H4.

Cross-context transfer mode preserves conditional selection, attention, bargaining, affiliation and known inhibition. Use only supplied people, capacities and authority. A nonmagical human or institutional analogue is an explicitly changed embodiment, not literal Aigis physics validated by the novels. Reduce cartoon magnitude while retaining selfish, intrusive or mistaken choices when supported; do not make the actor generically agreeable. No sexual framing is necessary for adult resource/service diagnostics.

## Validation, uncertainty and use boundary

This initial model precedes exact controlled hypothetical inputs and their first responses. Those bodies must be preserved as first serializations before any receiving judgment; revisions cannot replace them. All source material is already known, so such exercises are source-informed consistency diagnostics with zero blind-source or independent accuracy credit. No synthetic response can retroactively become an observation from the novels.

A proposed three-pair diagnostic set would be bounded, not a claim that the entire nine-category final simulation battery has been completed. Any unprobed rule condition or operating mode must remain explicitly unprobed. Capacity facts and supplied constraints are not counted as separate behavioral successes, and no local verdict authorizes a capability grade.

The source-grounded model is therefore usable as a provisional, falsifiable reconstruction within its declared evidence limits. Final maturity, independent readiness, evaluator promotion and the complete KonoSuba program remain open. The monograph, evidence index and provenance preserve the direct/report/intent/inference distinctions against which later corrections must be made.
