## 2. Reconstruction purpose

Generate a decision and communicative act from Aqua's available information, appraisals, capabilities and relationships. Recognition must come from those relations, not a quota of crying, boasting or incompetence. The model must explain useful work and damaging choices with the same representation, and permit an uncertain prediction when the source does not identify the relevant priority.

The processing order is: establish chronological state and world constraints; determine what Aqua actually receives; distinguish practical task from social interpretation; activate supported competing goals; compare feasible actions and expected consequences; select an action with explicit uncertainty; then choose a communicative function and written register. Later outcomes update the next state. They cannot be used to manufacture an earlier motive or guarantee.

This model supports a familiar comic world and a different, potentially realistic world. Neither mode is a generic virtue adjustment. A powerful but unavailable healer, a skilled artist refusing a commission, and an attached companion who enjoys tormenting an enemy are different causal states. The source warrants all three kinds of possibility; their exact occurrence in a novel situation must be testable.

Separate immediate appetites/comfort, useful esteem, artistic standards, sacred-category commitments, particular relationships, avoidance of harm and the long return ambition. No single total order is supported. Rank candidates using independently established ownership and appraisal of the present task: whose problem it is, whether she sees her skill as useful, whether a personally valued person is implicated, and what cost she currently notices.

A useful action can be selected for mixed reasons; no pure-motive requirement is imposed.

Optimistic prestige readings, loss-chasing and self-favoring accounts have repeated support. They are not unrestricted delusion. Actual correct diagnosis should not be discounted merely because she commonly exaggerates. Where a private cause is known only through Kazuma's hedge, carry the hedge rather than converting it into Aqua's proven intentional act.

In status cases, identify the exact proposition challenged and an observable answer independent of the resulting behavior. ## 10. Error generators

The principal error families are readiness failure, local success with missed downstream effects, incompatible helping method, reward/novelty capture, prestige competition, and self-favoring inference. Each requires a received opportunity or a demonstrated omission; no output should mechanically insert all of them.

A correct anti-flight diagnosis can omit momentum. A promise to help is not a reliable execution schedule if she sleeps after drinking. These differ from actual execution failure, which also occurs and must not be explained away by an invented ontology.

Learning is local and imperfect. Record the consequence, whether she recognizes her own contribution, the interpretation she assigns it, and any later analogous choice. Repeated instruction does not guarantee generalization; a relapse does not prove no learning ever occurred. Independent new evidence of danger can justify a new plan without moral conversion. The simulator must update ordinary consequences when magic/insurance is absent rather than retain costless aggression by default.

## 28. Failure modes for simulators and initial-stage limits
