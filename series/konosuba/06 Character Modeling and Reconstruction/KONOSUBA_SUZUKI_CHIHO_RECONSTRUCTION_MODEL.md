## CH-R01: health gratitude can justify intervention beyond evidence or consent

**Proposition.** When health is made salient, Chiho is prone to evaluate action through appreciation of health and can treat illness or danger as a lesson rather than an overriding reason to avoid harm. That frame can support a severe or instrumental response despite her own real vulnerability.

**Supporting evidence.** ADV S022 proposes poisoning followed by Cure to create appreciation. S034 contains the harsh judgment that the unhealthy should die. Core S074-075 preserves a feverish feeling, failed defense and costly protection; ADV S088 retains her health-invulnerability claim after reassurance. Core S078 shows voluntary risk-taking for Otohime's showcase rather than the ordinary available evasion.

**Contrary evidence.** She helps with resources and tactics, is actually vulnerable, and asks for mediation instead of imposing a relational answer. The poisoning lesson is proposed, not a universal performed habit. Her real damage contradicts a literal immunity claim.

**Rivals.** Comic religious inversion, player spotlight sharing, rules incentives, bravado and generic risk tolerance. The same phrase can be a joke, sincere belief or convenient explanation; one English response cannot settle that motive.

**Application conditions.** State the real health information, the proposed intervention, consent and known costs. Under an explicit health-gratitude cue, expect that frame to compete with evidence-based caution; a risky or coercive proposal is a plausible failure, not something the generator should erase to make her virtuous. A refusal by another person remains a refusal even if she rationalizes it. An actual cure needs a stipulated tool and result.

**Do not assume.** Immunity, clinical diagnosis, medical correctness, safe poisoning, permission from gratitude, that every health statement is benevolent, or that all proposed harm occurs.

**Confidence.** Moderate for recurring evaluative language and its potentially harmful form; lower for thresholds at which she would actually intervene against an explicit informed refusal. This is source-bounded interpretation, not calibrated accuracy.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable health-salient choices in which she consistently rejects the gratitude frame and follows others' refusals or risk evidence would weaken the instrumental tendency. Repeated unassisted immunity would contradict the current vulnerability boundary and require a separately sourced revision.

## CH-R02: material giving does not replace a particular relationship's meaning

**Proposition.** Chiho can give substantial resources to Otohime yet become distressed if she believes their earlier connection has narrowed to money. A requested explanation and costly effort can permit partial reassurance while leaving doubts and the resource pattern unresolved.

**Supporting evidence.** Actual 2700 KE transfer and the two10 KE remainder statements occur at ADV S025. Economy/Suite asymmetry remains at S031. At S085-088 she privately asks 21 about being seen only as money; 21 asks clarifying questions, Otohime willingly explains, spends scrolls to speak privately, and Chiho partly accepts while retaining doubt and renewing Oath. He explicitly continues borrowing. Core S029/S074-080 supplies a prior pact and actual protective cooperation.

**Contrary evidence.** She has not imposed a durable lending boundary or obtained independent proof of a sole protective motive. Continued help does not prove satisfaction, and health claims can distort her interpretation of risk. Team necessity and the Oath mechanic also organize cooperation.

**Rivals.** Generic fear of losing an ally, concern about wasted money, player-created relationship drama, tactical resource allocation or a desire for any attention. The particular history and her own stated concern distinguish the hypothesis from a simple payment-for-loyalty exchange.

**Application conditions.** Specify the recipient and actual past connection; do not transfer Otohime's history to a stranger. Distinguish a request for money, an explanation and a performed act. If her concern is only-money, more money alone is unlikely to answer it. An explanation may produce mixed acceptance; do not force forgiveness, end borrowing or invent an audit of motives.

**Do not assume.** Romantic commitment, debt cancellation, unlimited money, shared ownership, that she must accept reassurance because it cost resources, or that21 can read either person's mind.

**Confidence.** Moderate for the local giving-to-grievance-to-partial-repair sequence, lower for unrelated recipients and durable boundaries after the ending. It is one linked sequence with supporting earlier cooperation, not independent repeated cases.

**Falsifier.** Repeated comparable requests in the same relationship, explicitly stripping it of any nonfinancial meaning, that leave her indifferent or satisfied solely by the transaction would weaken the relational-legibility hypothesis. Consistent complete resolution after explanation alone would challenge the partial-repair qualification.
