# KONOSUBA V04–V06 Checkpoint — Model Generation 0.2

## 1. Checkpoint function

This document closes the first prospective-validation tranche. Model Generation 0.1 was frozen after V03, and its 20 predictions were written before V04 exposure. V04, V05, and V06 were then read sequentially with the frozen prediction text left unchanged.

This checkpoint therefore has stronger epistemic status than a retrospective V01–V06 summary. It records:

1. what the frozen model actually predicted;
2. where V04–V06 confirmed it;
3. which predictions remained untested;
4. which predictions require revision;
5. new mechanisms discovered only after the freeze;
6. the Generation 0.2 model that will govern V07–V09 prospective testing.

No V07 or later main-series evidence is used here.

## 2. Validation result at a glance

Of the 20 frozen Gen 0.1 predictions:

- **16 CONFIRMED** across the V04–V06 tranche;
- **2 PARTIAL / revision-bearing** (`MG01-D02`, `MG01-J01`);
- **2 NOT_TESTED** under a sufficiently clean matched trigger (`MG01-A03`, `MG01-M04`);
- **0 cleanly FALSIFIED as complete mechanisms**.

This count is not a model score in isolation. Several confirmations are more diagnostic than others, and the untested/partial predictions must remain visible. The strongest prospective successes are those where the model predicted not merely a behavior but a mechanism:

- Kazuma's indirect exploit preference plus post-solution consequence blind spot;
- Aqua's competence/deployment asymmetry and loyalty-by-action;
- Megumin's identity challenge response plus social-strategic cognition;
- Darkness's protector/contribution/belonging hierarchy;
- asymmetric routing as the party's success architecture;
- costly action as a better attachment signal than polite speech.

The most important revisions are:

- **routing is more fundamental than Kazuma personally**;
- **seriousness realigns voice toward the active role rather than always reducing theatricality**;
- **Megumin's Explosion identity has a higher-order belonging/contribution override**;
- **Kazuma has at least two distinct foresight failures: post-transition consequence blindness and status/recognition overextension**;
- **Kazuma's repeated desire for a "normal party" is not supported as a stable terminal preference once actual separation becomes possible**.

## 4. Model Generation 0.2 — Satou Kazuma

### 4.1 Core generative structure
Kazuma is best modeled as an **adaptive low-resource optimizer whose competence is sharply context dependent**.

He is strongest when:

- rules can be inferred;
- direct power is unfavorable;
- many weak tools can be combined;
- the environment can be manipulated;
- opponent incentives or expectations can be exploited;
- resources can be recycled;
- the problem is novel or poorly structured.

He is less exceptional in straightforward high-stat contests or conventional mass warfare.

### 4.2 Goal/reward hierarchy
High-salience variables now include:

- safety and comfort;
- financial security;
- sexual reward/opportunity;
- status repair / recognition;
- clever mastery;
- concrete loyalty to "his people";
- entrusted responsibility;
- preservation of valued companions' identities;
- membership in the current party/home ecology.

The last variable is stronger than Gen 0.1 recognized. Actual separation is aversive even when Kazuma complains that he wants a better party.

### 4.3 Distinct error generators
Do not merge these:

1. **post-transition consequence blind spot:** elegant exploit works; immediate new physical/system state is under-modeled;
2. **local-dominance/status escalation:** success/recognition makes an apparently weak target or easy achievement look worth overpursuing;
3. **grievance retaliation:** low-cost leverage after humiliation is hard to ignore;
4. **power/status intoxication:** asymmetric authority plus low accountability can sharply degrade restraint;
5. **sexual salience:** attractive/sexual opportunity distorts risk and fairness weighting;
6. **self-serving causal compression:** public/internal account selectively emphasizes respectable motives.

### 4.4 Seriousness/loyalty
Concrete relational harm and entrusted responsibility repeatedly override comfort. Kazuma can still complain and calculate while doing the loyal thing.

### 4.5 Relationship model
Kazuma's intimacy often consists of possessing unusually accurate predictive models of companions and being willing to use those models both for care and torment.

The party itself has become a valued end state.

### 4.6 Confidence
**HIGH** for exploit competence, mixed motives, grievance/status sensitivity, concrete loyalty, and party attachment.

**MODERATE-HIGH** for post-transition blind spot and status-overextension as distinct error mechanisms.

**MODERATE** for how far power/status intoxication generalizes outside comic low-accountability conditions.

## 5. Model Generation 0.2 — Aqua

### 5.1 Core generative structure
Aqua combines **extreme domain power** with variable goal discipline, status sensitivity, and weak downstream stakeholder modeling.

### 5.2 Context law
Her competence becomes more reliably visible when external structure constrains deployment:

- large-scale support warfare;
- explicit purification/healing tasks;
- artifact sealing;
- priestly ritual.

Under such conditions she can be one of the most valuable actors in the setting.

### 5.3 Failure engines
Current distinct mechanisms:

1. status/recognition assertion;
2. performance/hedonic salience replacing the assigned instrumental goal;
3. sacred-category action outrunning tactical consensus;
4. high-output success with under-modeled externalities;
5. possibly tedious-duty shortcutting — **OPEN**, not yet prospectively replicated.

### 5.4 Updating
Global weak-updating remains rejected. V06 adds evidence that Aqua can learn local risk classes and make reasonable context-sensitive decisions.

### 5.5 Attachment
Acute distress in Kazuma can activate straightforward care. Ordinary insult intensity remains a poor attachment measure.

### 5.6 Confidence
**HIGH** for domain competence, recognition sensitivity, sacred-category behavior, action-based loyalty.

**MODERATE-HIGH** for externality/goal-substitution mechanisms.

**MODERATE/OPEN** for tedious-duty maximal shortcut as a general rule.

## 6. Model Generation 0.2 — Megumin

### 6.1 Core generative structure
Explosion is a protected identity/aesthetic commitment supported by high social and tactical cognition outside the protected domain.

### 6.2 Identity hierarchy
Gen 0.2 must distinguish two trigger classes:

- **external practical criticism / rivalry / status challenge -> defend or intensify Explosion identity**;
- **internalized fear of burdening valued companions -> possible self-sacrificial abandonment of Explosion identity**.

Acceptance of the identity by a valued counterpart can reverse the second pathway.

### 6.3 Regulation
V06 demonstrates that Megumin can suppress anger/retaliation when a valued companion's social position would be harmed. Her temper is therefore high-intensity but not globally uncontrollable.

### 6.4 Context law
Explosion's practical value rises dramatically when environment/institution supplies target separation and timing.

### 6.5 Social cognition
Framing, embarrassment leverage, selective truth, competition, and relationship-role manipulation remain strong.

### 6.6 Confidence
**HIGH** for identity commitment, competitive response, social cognition, and group support.

**MODERATE-HIGH** for belonging/contribution as a higher-order override.

Direct valued-companion friendly-fire inhibition remains **OPEN** prospectively despite strong V03 evidence.

## 7. Model Generation 0.2 — Darkness / Dustiness Ford Lalatina

### 7.1 Core generative structure
Darkness is simultaneously:

- a highly competent noble actor;
- a high-end defensive Crusader;
- a chosen ordinary-party peer;
- a person with strong masochistic/powerlessness rewards;
- a protector whose duty/fairness system repeatedly outranks self-protection.

None of these should be collapsed into another.

### 7.2 Protector hierarchy
Protection now includes:

- bodily interposition;
- absorbing area threat;
- safeguarding places/people;
- taking responsibility;
- defending a companion's dignity/fair treatment even against royal hierarchy.

### 7.3 Masochism boundary
Pain remains potentially rewarding. Meaning, relational context, shame type, control, and duty determine valence.

The pain-only coercion hypothesis is retained at lower confidence until cleanly tested.

### 7.4 Noble/peer architecture
The central conflict is not "real Darkness versus fake Lalatina." Both roles are real capacities.

The protected variable is **equal peer membership**: she does not want status to eject her from the party's ordinary treatment.

### 7.5 Confidence
**HIGH** for protector duty, defensive competence, meaningful-contribution need, and belonging/status sensitivity.

**MODERATE** for pain-only coercion generalization.

## 8. Model Generation 0.2 — Ensemble

### 8.1 Routing-source theorem
Revise Gen 0.1:

> **Asymmetric specialists require routing/structure, but Kazuma is only one possible source of it.**

External military institutions, domain experts, or highly structured environments can substitute.

Kazuma's unique value is flexible endogenous routing when no stable structure already exists.

### 8.2 Party membership as terminal value
The party is not merely a combat configuration. The current relationships and household routine have become valued states in themselves.

### 8.3 Affection without idealization
This remains strongly confirmed. More attachment has not produced conventional politeness.

## 9. Model Generation 0.2 — Japanese voice

### 9.1 Revised seriousness rule
Reject a universal monotonic movement toward plainness.

Use:

`serious trigger -> highest-priority role/identity selected -> relationship and social context applied -> register shifts toward that role`

Examples:

- Darkness becomes formally corrective/protective before Iris;
- Yunyun becomes more theatrically Crimson-Demon-like when duty requires courage;
- Kazuma becomes more thief-theatrical after deciding to commit to the heist;
- Aqua becomes plainer/gentler when caring for genuinely distressed Kazuma.

### 9.2 Humor distribution
L0–L2 remain dominant. L3 should continue to be tracked but not assumed central.
