# KonoSuba: extended-corpus synthesis V1

## 1. Expansion changes who can explain an event

The expanded corpus makes a single narrator's account a less sufficient account of the social world. It gives Dust a continuing life, Megumin an earlier set of choices, Vanir and Wiz a workplace, and other participants practical aims that do not begin when Kazuma notices them. The central quartet remains distinctive, but its apparent dependence on one organizer, one corrective voice or one seriousness switch becomes harder to sustain. The wider evidence supports distributed capacity, selective responsibility and particular attachment. It also preserves exploitation, failed restraint and unresolved repair. Competence and care do not make those adverse acts disappear.

This conclusion belongs to a retrospective synthesis. The [coverage/admission matrix](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/KONOSUBA_SOURCE_COVERAGE_AND_ADMISSION_MATRIX.md) and [component register](KONOSUBA_EXPANDED_COMPONENT_COVERAGE.json) govern what each source can contribute. The accounting includes nineteen supplemental supplied editions, seventeen main-edition excluded-component programs with the original principal readings retained, and five mixed artbook, fanbook and TRPG programs. Their declared publication gates are recorded separately. The forty-one object identities are neither forty-one independent experiments nor a count of equally authoritative biographies. This essay adds no new primary reading, image inspection or independent observation.

The main-only derivation and unchanged-R1 holdout results remain independently recoverable. Dust06/07, MASK and Yorimichi01 were encountered as designated validation sources before the later whole-edition programs. Reading the Dust books in their declared natural sequence afterward could clarify a history, but could not restore first exposure or make the earlier narrator's unknowns into prior knowledge. Publication order, in-world chronology and analyst encounter order therefore remain three different records. ## 2. Dust's chosen present survives the disclosure of his past

Across Dust01–07, the recurring problem is selective deployment. Dust can identify a practical opening, organize people and use exceptional means, yet pursue an immediate reward, conceal information or override a recipient. The early contrast between spear capacity and ordinary conduct does not establish that the ordinary conduct was a disguise. His desired present life, companions and freedom have value in their own right. The later disclosure of ライン・シェイカー and the old partner contract explains constraints and particular attachments without retroactively making every previous failure deliberate. Dust03 gives useful recruitment, inference and reassurance alongside direct rescue obstruction and forced participation. Dust04 adds spy detection and a protective injury without cancelling loss chasing, false accusation or a unilateral leisure wager. Dust05 makes the acknowledged error in an earlier protective separation consequential for the actual partner. Dust06 adds checking and coordination while retaining late accounting, false recruitment and comfortable assumptions. These are conditional changes, not steps on one irreversible moral ladder. [Dust02](Dust/KONOSUBA_DUST_V02_DEEP_READING.md), §13; [Dust03](Dust/KONOSUBA_DUST_V03_DEEP_READING.md), §13; [Dust04](Dust/KONOSUBA_DUST_V04_DEEP_READING.md), §13; [Dust06](Dust/KONOSUBA_DUST_V06_DEEP_READING.md), §12.

By Dust07, audience is an operative variable. A disclosure to the party, an acknowledgment to an enemy and concealment from the wider town carry different costs. The sword loan involves a valued possession and real defensive purpose, a performed profit explanation and an actual conditional resale benefit. The MAIN16 and Dust07 accounts of the initial loan are views of one event, not independent repetitions; later use and continued nonreturn are different states. A model needs both the value of helping and the value of controlling how that help is recognized. [Dust07](Dust/KONOSUBA_DUST_V07_DEEP_READING.md), §§4–6/13; [C49 evidence index](../08%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/KONOSUBA_COHORT49_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).

The unnumbered continuation prevents Dust07's historical final-volume framing from becoming corpus exhaustion. It does not become an invented numbered volume eight or another clean R1 test. Present affection is not an unrestricted claim on Small, Leen, the child or former associates. ## 9. The revised causal account needs several independent questions

The expanded corpus repeatedly defeats a shortcut from a desirable outcome to a desirable process. The same resource can be obtained, used successfully, lost in recovery and left unpaid. The same person can understand a warning, dislike an action, remain quiet for another priority and later object. The same provider can agree to one bounded service and refuse another. The same skill can work under an incomplete target model. The same protective attachment can motivate rescue and override the protected person's choice.

| Question | Material distinction | Consequence for reconstruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is known now? | Actor information, narrator disclosure and analyst later knowledge differ. | Never grant a future explanation to an earlier decision. |
| What is valued? | Comfort, status, specialty, chosen belonging and particular care can coexist. | Represent rival goals instead of selecting one virtuous true self. |
| What means are available? | Equipment, range, mana, institutional access and provider permission differ. | Separate ability from access and actual deployment. |
| What was received or agreed? | A warning, a task, an unwanted act and a specific assent have different owners. | Silence and successful coordination do not create consent. |
| What actually happened? | Plan, attempt, execution, recovery and settlement are different stages. | Score a prediction against its eligible stage, not the eventual favorable ending. |
| Is this another observation? | Reprints, alternate views and shared events retain dependency. | Do not turn more routes into independent confirmations. |

This structure is descriptive support, not a newly validated simulator. It preserves the old YORI01 R1-K08 and DUST07 R1-E06 failures, MASK E08 dissent, Gen05 J01 failure, ambiguous branches and all other frozen outcomes. A later explanation of a failed forecast cannot become a successful forecast. The [expanded Kazuma monograph](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_KAZUMA_EXPANDED_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_V1.md) and [expanded ensemble synthesis](../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/KONOSUBA_EXPANDED_PARTY_ENSEMBLE_SYNTHESIS_V1.md) provide attributable topical consequences.

## 12. What this synthesis changes, and what would test it

The material revision is a more conditional account of action: selective deployment of real means under rival goals, partial knowledge, other people's agency and phase-specific costs. Particular attachment matters, yet does not guarantee safety, autonomy or restraint. Distributed competence matters, yet does not guarantee reliable handoffs. Chosen ordinary life matters, yet does not make wrongdoing harmless. Later clarity about a past identity matters, yet does not replace the earlier uncertainty. These conclusions integrate the broader corpus while leaving genuinely unresolved motives and missing outcomes open.

The six StageD successors must convert these arguments into distinct four-principal, party and transfer instructions with traceable revisions and preserved main-only originals. The final battery must include mundane inconvenience, resource conflict, social novelty, public embarrassment, moral pressure, fear/danger, competence-domain shift, relationship conflict and cross-universe novelty. Inputs, actual first responses and later feedback must be frozen and saved separately. Same-lineage, source-known scenarios are diagnostic; they cannot supply pristine holdouts, independent accuracy or human Japanese-performance evidence.
