# Blessing: retained author codes and H3 protocol qualification

This is a postfeedback author qualification of my initial selected L/H register, not a new source reading, independent coding pass or reassigned set of outcomes. I agree with root's definitional objection. Some initial H3 rationales conflate the protocol class with source-plane restrictions, uncertain extrapolation, unobserved motives or unperformed events. Those are important cautions, but they do not by themselves establish H3.

The governing method defines H3 as **gag-contingent behavior**: “The scene may contain weak evidence, but the behavior is too dependent on a local punchline to generalize without recurrence.” H1 can describe substantially literal action despite comedy. H2 concerns a real disposition with amplified intensity. H4 concerns nonliteral comic license. Source authority, continuity admission, event access, dependent reproduction, linguistic portability and H-class treatment are separate questions. A source-plane label is not an H class.

## Specific author correction

ARTB-CODE-001 explicitly says H3 applies to “generalizing the camera economy beyond this album fiction.” That is an incorrect use of the protocol definition. Beyond-album generalization requires an authority/continuity qualification; it does not automatically make the actual frame utterances gag-contingent. CODE002 similarly retains a real evasive stance while leaving photographer identity/access unestablished. The unestablished access is not sufficient H3 evidence about the evasion.

The same distinction matters elsewhere. Unknown sexual motive (006), a proposed rather than performed adoption (008), unobserved visits (011/018), unverified sales outside the frame (015), or dependent re-presentation rather than a fresh encounter (017/029) do not alone meet H3. A nickname's Japanese word formation (016) is a language-mechanism issue; it is not automatically a weak behavioral disposition. Unresolved photographic access (024/030) is not automatically H3 either.

Creator commentary and promotional staging need their own authority labels. Artist appraisal, design comparison, production-memory uncertainty, recollection and hypothetical failure in 034–045 cannot be turned into main-character H3 behavior merely because they are comic or paratextual. Imagined/illustrator scenes 032/033 and the drawing-song procedure046 similarly require representation/event-status distinctions; an existing H4 label does not supply the missing H3 warrant. Nor does every static illustration automatically qualify for H4. That latter issue is outside this narrow H3 audit and remains unrescored.

This does not establish that H3 is impossible in every affected case. The album's local camera device, a one-off nickname performance, countdown confinement or movie-title role-play might support a specific gag-contingency argument. Such a conclusion needs an identified behavior and an explanation of its dependence on the local punchline. Neither source exclusion nor an H3 label should decide that question in advance. I supply no new formal replacement classes here and do not turn this qualification into a zero-H3 result.
