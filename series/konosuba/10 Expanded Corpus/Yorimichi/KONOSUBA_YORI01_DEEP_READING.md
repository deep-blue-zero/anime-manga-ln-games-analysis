## 2. Architecture, chronology and the central change

The collection repeatedly makes a useful action coexist with a bad account of why it occurred, whom it benefited or what it permits next. Aqua really mediates a farewell and heals an almost lost patient. Kazuma really carries Megumin, rescues Aqua, crafts a useful arrow and coordinates a contingent attack. Darkness really investigates and protects. Megumin really produces destructive effects and resists a humiliating convenience. None of those facts converts all their claims into truth, every relationship into consent, or a first successful phase into a finished operation.

The anthology form makes that distinction unusually clear. It can place the reader beside the same household in different earlier states without making those states successive chapters of one uninterrupted campaign. The Wiz story explicitly follows obtaining the mansion (U020P0014). Chris's story follows Beldia and debt; Kazuma says their only prior meeting was the skill lesson and still prays to an unmet Eris (U018P0004/0122/0392). The Vanir story follows his defeat and the debt's clearance (U022P0004). The visiting quartet thanks Kazuma for Hans (U024P0430–0436). These anchors constrain interpretation more than table-of-contents order or the reader's knowledge of later novels.

Knowledge also differs inside a scene. The noble client recognizes Darkness while Kazuma thinks she is mishandling etiquette. Aqua can hear a spirit whom Kazuma cannot. The deputy knows why she values Darkness's connection, while Kazuma interprets her attention through his own status. The public believes Megumin's current victim performance partly because Kazuma has actually acquired a damaging reputation. The reader can recognize Chris's divine irony without Kazuma doing so. A reconstruction that gives everyone the reader's later knowledge destroys several causal mechanisms the stories actually enact.

The central longitudinal addition is therefore a set of **bounded coexistences**, not a new purified character type. Service and harm, care and appetite, competence and incomplete preparation, apology and unresolved cost, sincere belief and indirect causation remain together. Ordinary craft, meals, cold, wages, borrowing and social address are not filler between real adventures. They specify which options are available and why a person takes one rather than another.

## 7. Humor mechanics and the H axis

The [91 selected whole premises](KONOSUBA_YORI01_HUMOR_REGISTER.md) contain **90H1 and one H2**. They are purposive interpretation records, with some overlapping intervals, not a census or a behavioral frequency. No category quota is imposed. H1 retains literal world-compatible acts even when fantasy or deception enables the effect. The spirit, curse, spell and head rotation are supplied world facts or source claims, not automatic evidence that an actor's behavior is nonliteral.

Several distinct mechanisms recur. A self-authorizing label is contradicted by the speaker's next act: honest informant, lawful penitent, useful healer, generous judge. A social audience is changed to make a different account effective, as with Megumin's public theft accusation. A technically successful act changes meaning when its recipient or remaining dependency is considered. A reasonable general rule fails at a particular scale or with this particular party. A genuine warm scene is interrupted by a boundary the warmth did not abolish.
