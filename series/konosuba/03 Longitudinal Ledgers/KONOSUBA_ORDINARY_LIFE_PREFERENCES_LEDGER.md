# KONOSUBA Ordinary-Life and Preferences Ledger — Through V07

## Purpose

Captures low-stakes behavior needed for later simulation. A single mention remains weak evidence unless reinforced.

## Kazuma

### Routine / leisure
- Pre-isekai: school avoidance, online gaming, limited-edition game purchase motivates leaving home.
- Wants fantasy adventure initially, but not constant lethal risk.
- Envisions long-term life as safe commerce plus occasional easy quests.

### Work
- Can perform construction/manual labor competently enough to sustain himself.
- Dislikes a life consisting only of construction despite former hikikomori identity.

### Money / possessions
- Strong preference for security/housing once he gains money.
- Later large reward immediately evokes reduced adventuring rather than luxury spending.

### Food/drink
- Eats cheap smoked-lizard style food during poverty.
- Uses beginner magic to make water/fire for coffee, suggesting coffee preference/value.

### Social
- Avoids asking intimidating men or attractive young women for directions; comfortable enough with older woman.
- Collects information by talking with other adventurers and values social information networks.

## Aqua

### Routine / work
- Adapts readily to construction labor, bathing/eating/sleeping stable routine.
- Can become absorbed in ordinary work enough to forget Demon King mission.
- Later works at food/croquette sales and resents being scolded.

### Money
- Requests individual reward allocation when confident of own performance.
- Spends anticipated cabbage income before realizing it.
- Accumulates tavern debt.
- Becomes flattering/pleading toward Kazuma when he has money.

### Leisure / art
- Deeply values party trick `花鳥風月`.
- Refuses repeated paid performance because she regards repetition-for-money as artistically degrading/third-rate.

### Food/drink/sociality
- Freely shares/steals food from Kazuma's plate.
- Epilogue shows enthusiastic drinking/party participation.

## Megumin

### Material needs
- Recruitment occurs while hungry and socially rejected.
- Will accept food, bath, and basic expenses rather than salary to secure party membership.

### Routine / hobby / identity maintenance
- Daily Explosion practice is non-negotiable enough to require regular target search.
- Needs someone to carry her home after casting; Kazuma fills role.
- Better/larger/harder targets increase attraction.

### Social
- Can use public social misunderstanding strategically.
- Sensitive to praise/insult about Explosion and Crimson Demon identity.

## Darkness

### Equipment
- Values new armor sincerely and is upset when Beldia damages it.

### Adventure preference
- Selects or favors strong/dangerous opponents rather than safe quests.
- Wants genuine rather than staged inability/punishment.

### Social life
- Has acquaintances among Axel adventurers who tease her.
- Post-battle reveals desire to drink socially with men she thought dead.
- Genuine emotional exposure produces unwanted embarrassment distinct from fetishized shame.

## Ensemble ordinary-life baseline

V01 establishes poverty and shared inconvenience as formative conditions:
- stable sleeping;
- cheap meals;
- construction/side jobs;
- tavern/guild as social hub;
- reward allocation disputes;
- dependence on one another for transport, healing, money, and access to quests.

This low-status daily life is not filler: it creates the party's familiarity and gives later simulations a mundane baseline.


# V02 Update

## Kazuma
- Wants a warm, stable winter home strongly enough that the mansion materially changes his attachment to Axel.
- Takes guild piecework/side work to service debt rather than simply refusing labor.
- Socially drinks with Dust/Keith but reports little taste appreciation; intoxication/social bonding is more salient than beverage flavor.
- Can delay premium alcohol/crab leisure for a stronger erotic reward.
- Uses explicit bath-privacy precautions; wants fantasy sexual experience separated from accidental real encounter.
- Grave cleaning becomes a daily routine.
- Enjoys games and low-cost domestic entertainment; bonus material remains quarantined, but main-text board-game scenes already support household play.

## Aqua
- Strong preference for fireplace warmth and idling during winter.
- Likes alcohol intensely and saves premium bottles for deliberate enjoyment.
- Demonstrates sophisticated crab/alcohol serving knowledge.
- Casual high-skill visual art with water suggests creation is intrinsically easy/pleasurable.
- Wants snow sprite as pet/refrigeration device after naming it; attachment can override immediate bounty value.
- Avoids repetitive cemetery labor through shortcut rather than through simple refusal.

## Megumin
- Dislikes being unable to contribute in dungeon contexts.
- Shared household games are part of leisure baseline.
- Premium crab is sufficiently rewarding to delay Explosion temporarily, but she immediately imagines casting afterward.
- Under fear, seeks physical/social proximity and asks Kazuma not to leave.
- Competition within Explosion is a major non-monetary reward.

## Darkness
- Family is wealthy/high-status enough to send luxury crab and premium alcohol.
- Performs armor/equipment care at home.
- Participates in games and domestic shared meals.
- Privacy/reputation concerns remain meaningful even when an incident also intersects with masochistic/sexual reward.
- Attachment to Axel has an undisclosed duty/family dimension, not only generic adventurer preference.

## Ensemble household baseline through V02
The party now shares a stable residence. Relevant simulation defaults include:
- fireplace/resource disputes;
- communal meals and luxury-food events;
- casual games;
- work/debt discussions;
- bedrooms/bath privacy;
- grave/house maintenance;
- routine teasing that does not imply relationship rupture.

The shift from stable poverty to a large house should be tracked as a state change rather than treated as timeless personality context.

## Ordinary-life synthesis through V03
Simulation should now treat the mansion/party life as a meaningful home ecology rather than a neutral staging area. Kazuma seeks warmth/security, Aqua claims comfort territory and entertainment, Megumin imports caregiving/competition into domestic life, and Darkness values the ability to live as a peer rather than as a noble object of deference.

## V04 ordinary-life update

### Kazuma
- **Comfort/home:** kotatsu and mansion wealth rapidly produce domestic retirement behavior. On return from travel, the mansion's relaxed atmosphere is explicitly experienced as especially comfortable.
- **Work/risk:** Vanir's product deal is attractive primarily because it can remove the need for ordinary labor and dangerous questing.
- **Travel:** hot-spring trip appeal includes sexual novelty/mixed-bath opportunity rather than abstract sightseeing alone.
- **Possessions:** cares about new sword identity enough that Megumin's `ちゅんちゅん丸` engraving genuinely bothers him.
- **Money/ethics:** refuses a reward for a crisis his own party substantially caused; opportunism has a reputation/fairness boundary.
- **Domestic sociality:** enjoys watching Aqua/Megumin board-game conflict while drinking tea made by Darkness; low-intensity group presence is rewarding in itself.

### Aqua
- **Comfort:** shares wealth-induced laziness and preference for warm house.
- **Food/drink:** continues strong alcohol/quality-drink salience; Kazuma can manipulate her through anticipated reward.
- **Religion/community:** Axis followers and hot-spring infrastructure are not merely abstract doctrine; she treats their refuge as a personal responsibility worth severe pain.
- **Recognition:** being dismissed by her own followers is emotionally painful.
- **Travel:** enjoys hot springs and social attention, but the trip becomes duty once contamination is recognized.

### Megumin
- **Leisure:** board games are a confident low-stakes activity; challenges Aqua over sofa territory.
- **Travel/stimulation:** more willing than Kazuma/Aqua to break domestic stagnation and push for outings.
- **Naming:** repeatedly asserts whimsical naming authority over Kazuma's sword (`ちゅんちゅん丸`) and baby dragon (`じゃりっぱ`).
- **Social routine:** comfortable enough with party to conduct aggressive pranks but reacts strongly when Kazuma's usual retaliation pattern changes.

### Darkness
- **Domestic competence:** can prepare tea properly despite other practical awkwardness.
- **Family gifts:** actively buys souvenirs for father/servants and worries about their reception; sacrifice of gifts to Hans is a real cost.
- **Status preference:** prefers companions and allies to continue ordinary treatment rather than defer to Dustiness rank.
- **Tourism:** can enthusiastically consume fantasy/tourist expectations alongside Kazuma.

### Mansion as emergent home
V04 is the strongest low-stakes evidence yet that the Axel mansion is a shared home rather than a temporary base. The closing tableau—tea, sofa, board game, arguments, immediate visitor interruption—functions as the group's baseline domestic ecosystem.


# V05 Ordinary-Life / Preference Update

## Kazuma
- Daily Explosion accompaniment/evaluation has become a stable routine despite repeated complaints.
- Strong preference for future financial autonomy, mansion comfort and a shared low-risk life with the party.
- Thinks in terms of emergency liquidity/security once major income is expected.
- Gaming identity remains positive and is used to narrate old-world competence/status.
- Childhood Valentine's story shows long-standing comparative-status sensitivity and willingness to engineer impressions.
- Increasingly enjoys/values Megumin specifically enough to preserve her eccentric identity over practical optimization.
- On return home, immediately rejects further travel and seeks housebound comfort.

## Aqua
- Continues to be drawn to novelty objects and entertainment; successfully brings Game Girl home.
- Uses access to the game device as a bargaining chip for household chores.
- Shares Kazuma's comfort-seeking enough that his laziness can make her feel licensed to relax.
- Repeated casual entertainment/art performances remain an intrinsic competence/reward domain.

## Megumin
- Distinctive robe is identity-significant battle clothing; buys replacements/copies when available.
- Family history includes material poverty/food insecurity, important for interpreting money/food behavior.
- Values Komekko and Yunyun strongly beneath abrasive presentation.
- Explicitly likes the current life with Kazuma/Aqua/Darkness and imagines continuing it.
- Daily Explosion is both personal practice and shared ritual with Kazuma.
- Desire to be useful to companions becomes strong enough to influence long-term skill-plan decisions.

## Darkness
- Continues to prefer ordinary adventurer-peer treatment to aristocratic deference.
- Readily acts as social restraint when Kazuma/Aqua become antisocial at home.
- Family/noble obligations re-enter ordinary life through the Dustiness servant and royal invitation.

## Yunyun
- Wants friendship/belonging strongly, but can become uncomfortable when attention/recognition takes the highly theatrical Crimson Demon form.
- Plans to return to Axel despite improved village status, suggesting Axel social ties remain important.

## Household baseline after V05
The mansion is now explicitly treated as a desired end-state rather than merely a base:
- Kazuma wants to stay home permanently once wealthy;
- Aqua and Kazuma compete/cooperate over games and chores;
- Megumin defends Kazuma in the post-trip conversation;
- Darkness acts as a practical/social brake;
- new outside obligations are experienced as intrusions into a valued domestic equilibrium.


# V06 Update

## Kazuma
- Adapts to luxury extremely quickly: late rising, servants, soft bed, personalized butler naming, expectation of service.
- Likes competitive games enough to refuse court etiquette that would require throwing matches for Iris; winning/fair contest is part of how he creates intimacy.
- Wealth has changed his relationship to adventure: comfort/home security are increasingly terminal goals rather than temporary recovery states.
- Nevertheless, actual separation from the party reduces the appeal of pure comfort.
- Likes status/deference more than his anti-authority self-image might imply when he personally occupies the high-status role.

## Aqua
- High-end alcohol remains a major reward and possession object; she is willing to appropriate the party's reward into a bottle framed as reflecting her particular contribution.
- Performance/art is intrinsically rewarding enough to displace shop/customer objectives.
- Domestic conflict can switch rapidly into generosity when the activity becomes a shared celebration.

## Megumin
- Daily Explosion remains ordinary routine and date-like relational activity with Kazuma.
- Enjoys recognition and heroic performance; public praise can push her into avoidable boast commitments.
- Forms friendship with Iris through street outing, rough ordinary behavior, and later letter writing.

## Darkness
- Cleaning and simple cooking are competent when she chooses conservative tasks; domestic incompetence should not be assumed globally.
- Values noble family/household enough to worry intensely about party embarrassment, while also valuing ordinary treatment after status disclosure.
- Tea-making / practical care reappears immediately after returning home.

## Iris
- Strong latent preference for ordinary youth experiences: school, festivals, fair games, street food, unsupervised shopping, peer conflict, travel/adventure.
- Luxury is not the scarce resource; **ordinary autonomy and equal-age sociality are**.
- Rapidly learns and enjoys underhanded game tactics once permitted to compete normally.

## Shared home state
The return to Axel ends in immediate restoration of the party's ordinary ecology: drinks, snacks, arguments, teasing, role disputes, and a collective celebration. V06 strengthens the idea that the house/party routine is itself a valued home state, not merely a staging area for quests.

# V07 Update

## Kazuma
- Public reward/status quickly increases generosity, expensive consumption and self-conception (`俺の時代`).
- The mansion's value is relational rather than purely material: when Darkness/Megumin are absent and Aqua is occupied, the same luxurious house feels conspicuously empty and lonely.
- Passive-income/merchant retirement remains genuinely desired, but V07 proves it can be sacrificed to prevent a core companion's permanent removal.
- Cooking is now represented as a plausible fallback livelihood after IP liquidation.

## Aqua
- The supposed dragon egg becomes a major caretaking object; Aqua invests money, attention and mana into the imagined future `ゼル帝`.
- Strongly owned relationship goals can make her ignore ordinary reward: she refuses tips while performing outside the Dustiness estate because the purpose is to bring Darkness out.
- Routine cemetery duty remains vulnerable to delay/forgetting, but V07 does not supply a new shortcut experiment.

## Megumin
- Darkness's absence is experienced as a missing part of normal household/party life, not simply a combat vacancy.
- Friendship terminology with Yunyun is valuable enough to be used as practical motivational currency.
- Daily/identity Explosion remains central, but V07 shows it can be routed into community/relationship defense.

## Darkness
- Explicitly describes life with the party as enjoyable enough to throw everything away to protect her companions.
- Equal-peer membership is an ordinary-life need with unusually high emotional salience.
- She still expects ordinary teasing rather than noble deference after return; the party immediately provides it.

## Shared home state after V07
`お帰り` / `ただいま` makes the home metaphor explicit. The mansion/party ecology survives a credible permanent-separation event and resumes insults, sexual accusations, money arguments and ordinary banter almost immediately. Home is therefore not synonymous with calm; it is **stable reciprocal membership in the existing high-friction ecology**.

# V08 Update

## Kazuma
- Japanese festival/cultural-event nostalgia becomes a real motivational variable; he explicitly contrasts current involvement with school events he largely skipped.
- Likes event design, cooking, fireworks, alcohol, attractive attention and public recognition when participation is self-authored.
- Comfort/retirement preference remains real, but does not imply aversion to all sustained effort.
- Romantic success is strongly desired; rejection/social-moral ambiguity is strongly anxiety-producing.

## Aqua
- Annual worship is now an explicit ordinary-life aspiration.
- Celebration, alcohol, follower attention and Emperor Zel caretaking are high-salience everyday rewards.
- Will spend substantial personal money on church/festival identity projects, then expect household subsidy for comfort.

## Megumin
- Wants festival/date experiences with Kazuma and deliberately schedules private time.
- Romantic desire coexists with stronger immediate pulls from Explosion/adventurer role.
- Ends V08 by inviting Kazuma to her room for an important private conversation.

## Darkness
- Explicitly says the mansion is deeply comfortable and compares the emotional state to the family feeling she imagines she might have had with her deceased mother.
- Can perform acting-lord work but wants ordinary adventuring life back once the obligation ends.
- Cute clothing/stuffed-animal preferences are acknowledged alongside knight/adventurer identity.

## Chris / Eris
- Values ordinary casual friendship that divine hierarchy usually prevents.
- The Chris flower and its persistence meaning are personally important.
- Low-demand private gratitude produces stronger comfort than compulsory public spectacle.

## Household state after V08
The mansion remains a high-friction home: romantic ambiguity increases, but ordinary food, money, festival, work, teasing and caretaking routines continue rather than being replaced by formal courtship structures.
