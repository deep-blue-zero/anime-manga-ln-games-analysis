# KONOSUBA Ordinary-Life and Preferences Ledger — Through V16

## Purpose

Captures low-stakes behavior needed for later simulation. A single mention remains weak evidence unless reinforced.

## Kazuma

### Routine / leisure
- Pre-isekai: school avoidance, online gaming, limited-edition game purchase motivates leaving home.
- Wants fantasy adventure initially, but not constant lethal risk.
- Envisions long-term life as safe commerce plus occasional easy quests.

### Work
- Can perform construction/manual labor competently enough to sustain himself.
- Dislikes a life consisting only of construction despite former hikikomori identity.

### Money / possessions
- Strong preference for security/housing once he gains money.
- Later large reward immediately evokes reduced adventuring rather than luxury spending.

### Food/drink
- Eats cheap smoked-lizard style food during poverty.
- Uses beginner magic to make water/fire for coffee, suggesting coffee preference/value.

### Social
- Avoids asking intimidating men or attractive young women for directions; comfortable enough with older woman.
- Collects information by talking with other adventurers and values social information networks.

## Aqua

### Routine / work
- Adapts readily to construction labor, bathing/eating/sleeping stable routine.
- Can become absorbed in ordinary work enough to forget Demon King mission.
- Later works at food/croquette sales and resents being scolded.

### Money
- Requests individual reward allocation when confident of own performance.
- Spends anticipated cabbage income before realizing it.
- Accumulates tavern debt.
- Becomes flattering/pleading toward Kazuma when he has money.

### Leisure / art
- Deeply values party trick `花鳥風月`.
- Refuses repeated paid performance because she regards repetition-for-money as artistically degrading/third-rate.

### Food/drink/sociality
- Freely shares/steals food from Kazuma's plate.
- Epilogue shows enthusiastic drinking/party participation.

## Megumin

### Material needs
- Recruitment occurs while hungry and socially rejected.
- Will accept food, bath, and basic expenses rather than salary to secure party membership.

### Routine / hobby / identity maintenance
- Daily Explosion practice is non-negotiable enough to require regular target search.
- Needs someone to carry her home after casting; Kazuma fills role.
- Better/larger/harder targets increase attraction.

### Social
- Can use public social misunderstanding strategically.
- Sensitive to praise/insult about Explosion and Crimson Demon identity.

## Darkness

### Equipment
- Values new armor sincerely and is upset when Beldia damages it.

### Adventure preference
- Selects or favors strong/dangerous opponents rather than safe quests.
- Wants genuine rather than staged inability/punishment.

### Social life
- Has acquaintances among Axel adventurers who tease her.
- Post-battle reveals desire to drink socially with men she thought dead.
- Genuine emotional exposure produces unwanted embarrassment distinct from fetishized shame.

## Ensemble ordinary-life baseline

V01 establishes poverty and shared inconvenience as formative conditions:
- stable sleeping;
- cheap meals;
- construction/side jobs;
- tavern/guild as social hub;
- reward allocation disputes;
- dependence on one another for transport, healing, money, and access to quests.

This low-status daily life is not filler: it creates the party's familiarity and gives later simulations a mundane baseline.

# V10 Update

**Evidence boundary:** Japanese V10 main narrative only. Pr/C1/C2/C3/C4/C5/Ep correspond to `text/part0009.html`–`text/part0015.html`, using all-body P numbers. Observed preferences, self-reports, temporary incentives and unverified predictions are distinguished. V10 is the first volume of the open V10–V12 tranche; no future state is assumed.

## Kazuma

- **Household rhythm and comfort:** he wakes close to noon when Darkness has bound him; the promise of three days' food and care makes the imposed stay temporarily attractive. He keeps a chamber pot because leaving a warm winter bed is unpleasant, explicitly a convenience preference rather than inability to use a toilet. During travel he tentatively attributes sleeplessness to the unfamiliar pillow, and he cools drinking water with Freeze. These are concrete comfort practices rather than a universal refusal to act. (C1 P0053–0078, P0113–0115; C2 P0449–0457.)
- **Practical skills and curiosity:** cooking, escape and newly learned lip-reading are identified among his small skills. Lip-reading was acquired with idle curiosity about gossip, then becomes useful for private conversations and whispered rewards. Its utility should not be retroactively substituted for the original motive. (C1 P0034; C2 P0051–0056; C3 P0476–0481.)
- **Cooking and food:** he takes the camp cooking role partly to recover brotherly standing, prepares fried rice, gyoza and egg soup with Iris, and is caught out by the living cabbage. His stated aim is to offer something unfamiliar to a princess and simple enough for him to make. He recognizes rice toppings as ordinary food and crayfish as distinct from prestigious lobster, initially evades the latter, then enjoys the finished dish. (C2 P0264–0294, P0299–0317, P0332–0359, P0380–0392, P0426–0444.)
- **Fugu and limits of enjoyment:** he says he had never eaten fugu in Japan, takes confidence from Aqua's healing ability, enjoys the meal intensely and describes his own limited food vocabulary. After the near-poisoning he says he would prefer no more fugu. This is a within-volume change following an adverse experience, not an immutable dislike from the outset. (C1 P0240–0245, P0257–0279.)
- **Tourism and games:** he wants sightseeing and freedom from supervising companions, enjoys exploring an unfamiliar city, joins a card tournament after recognizing a familiar game structure, spends for strong cards and enjoys an oppressive winning combo. His pleasure in gaming and winning is directly shown; the claim that it was a sporting “good match” conflicts with the opponent's actual distress. (C3 P0038–0048, P0081–0104, P0174–0226, especially P0201–0216.)
- **Sexual leisure and attention:** during his solo excursion he searches for a local equivalent of the sexual-service business. He notices bodies and tests flirtatious opportunities, but actual Megumin's direct jealousy produces fluster rather than smooth confidence.
