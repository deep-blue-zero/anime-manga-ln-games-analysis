# KONOSUBA Character State Ledger — Through V16

## Purpose

Cumulative longitudinal state for the mandatory reconstruction subjects. This ledger records what is currently supported, what remains provisional, and what later volumes must test. It is not a trait list; each state is tied to behavior-generating mechanisms.

## Satou Kazuma

### Starting state
- Sixteen-year-old school-avoiding hikikomori; online/game-centered routine.
- Socially inhibited, especially around attractive young women, but not globally incapable of normal interaction.
- Strong gamer-schema vocabulary for interpreting unknown systems.
- Initially excited by isekai adventure and cheat-power fantasy.

### V01 stable-looking dispositions
- Safety/comfort seeking, but also wants stimulation and meaningful achievement.
- Strong resentment response to humiliation and condescension.
- Opportunistic, willing to ignore prestige/honor conventions when they structurally disadvantage him.
- Sexually attentive and vulnerable to sexual/social embarrassment.
- Capable of spontaneous protective action before deliberation.
- Context-sensitive moral judgment: does not accept “monster = enemy” when conduct contradicts category.

### V01 competence state
- Low-to-normal raw stats except extreme luck and somewhat high intelligence.
- Generic Adventurer class enables broad skill access.
- Strong practical improvisation: weak skills are combined creatively.
- Strong battlefield systems reasoning: observes emergent behavior, infers rules, coordinates teammates.
- Updates hypotheses quickly after failure or new evidence.

### V01 liabilities
- Gamer/genre expectation can anchor initial assumptions.
- Lust and attractiveness bias attention.
- Retaliatory impulses can produce unnecessary escalation.
- Public embarrassment creates exploitable leverage.
- Self-serving rationalization can make dirty tactics feel axiomatically fair.

### Seriousness overrides observed
- Perceived immediate danger to schoolgirl → intervenes instantly.
- Ordinary adventurers killed → comic/game framing weakens sharply.
- Darkness wounded → expresses concern despite knowing she likes pain.
- Wiz shown benevolent → intervenes against Aqua's categorical attack.

### End-of-V01 motivational state
- Explicitly prefers safe commerce + occasional easy adventuring.
- Property-damage debt and party consequences force renewed Demon King commitment.
- Heroic mission is therefore externally/reactively maintained, not yet an internal calling.

### Confidence
MODERATE. Strong V01 internal replication, but no cross-volume validation yet.

## Aqua

### Starting state
- Divine psychopomp/goddess with strong status self-conception.
- Initially treats Kazuma from a position of institutional/status superiority and uses his humiliation for amusement.
- Becomes involuntarily transferred with him after his retaliatory loophole choice.

### V01 capacity state
- Very high physical/magical stats except intelligence and luck.
- Immediate advanced-class access; chooses Archpriest.
- Proven healing, curse removal, undead purification, sacred purification, resurrection, enormous water generation.

- Strong artistic/party-trick aptitude; values it intrinsically.

### V01 stable-looking dispositions
- Needs recognition of goddess/Archpriest status.
- Highly reactive to ridicule of divine competence.
- Enjoys immediate social/hedonic rewards and can lose sight of distant objectives.
- Can mock others aggressively while being highly sensitive to reciprocal humiliation.
- Enjoys ordinary work/social life more than her nominal return mission predicts.

### V01 liabilities / error generators
- Status threat often triggers escalation.
- Genuine power encourages overconfidence without adequate collateral planning.
- Weak multi-step consequence forecasting.
- Poor financial forecasting: treats expected gains as available resources.
- Weak updating while pride is activated.
- Rigid hostility toward undead categories before context is assessed.

### Countervailing evidence
- Gives sensible equipment advice early.
- Works effectively at manual labor.
- Performs priestly obligations when role/duty is salient.
- Removes Darkness's curse casually and efficiently.
- Resurrects freshly killed adventurers.
- Can express gratitude and defend her current party/life against Kyouya's condescension.

### End-of-V01 state
- Integrated enough into Axel/party life to describe it as enjoyable.
- Still wants recognition and material reward; large flood creates party debt.
- Party role is simultaneously greatest support asset and greatest collateral-risk source.

### Confidence
MODERATE for status/collateral pattern; HIGH that “useless” is not a valid total competence description.
