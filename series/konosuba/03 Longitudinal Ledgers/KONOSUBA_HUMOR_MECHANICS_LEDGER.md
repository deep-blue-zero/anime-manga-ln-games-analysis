### H3 — gag-contingent / weak generalization

### H4 — nonliteral comic license
V01 has relatively little that should be treated as wholly nonliteral because the fantasy rules support many extreme events. The strongest H4 candidates are cartoon-like physical/emotional magnitudes rather than the underlying motives themselves.

## Seriousness-interruption function

V01 repeatedly lets a gag pattern continue into high stakes and then reveals its boundary:
- Darkness enjoys danger yet refuses to retreat while protecting others.
- Kazuma is cynical until actual people are cut down.
- Megumin performs grandiosity until guilt over Darkness's curse.
- Aqua's comic unreliability coexists with genuine resurrection of the dead.

This “joke stops / mechanism remains” boundary is a primary reconstruction target.

## Emerging V01 humor thesis

Most humor is not random absurdity. It is generated by **stable but incompatible reward functions** colliding with a world whose mechanics are literal enough for bad decisions to create real downstream costs.

# V05 Humor-Mechanics Update

## 1. Core volume pattern: competence and absurd objective functions
V05 broadens the series' comedy from "party members have distorted priorities" to an entire culture where technical competence and ridiculous aesthetic priorities coexist. Crimson Demon adults are elite spellcasters while treating theatrical names, dramatic entrances, tourist traps, and contrived role identities as ordinary social practice.

**Mechanism:** expectation that ridiculous self-presentation predicts incompetence -> repeated inversion through genuine excellence.

## 2. Tranquility Girl — social-script predation
- **Setup:** helpless lonely-girl appearance activates empathy/protection.
- **Mechanism:** audience/Kazuma know category danger but immediate social script resists killing.
- **Reveal:** exact plea is repeated to a new victim, then predator complains in ordinary language.
- **Humor:** dramatic irony + social-script exploitation + sudden de-anthropomorphizing evidence.
- **Evidence class:** H1 for Kazuma decision updating; H2/H3 for exaggerated presentation.

## 3. Female-orc reversal
- **Setup:** Kazuma imports standard fantasy assumptions about orcs.
- **Reversal:** local sex/gender ecology makes him the prey.
- **Function:** exposes schema anchoring and creates a genuine fear-state relationship test with Aqua/Yunyun.
- **Evidence:** do not transfer the cartoon sexual-threat mechanics literally; preserve Kazuma's intense fear and Aqua's serious comforting response.

## 4. Crimson Demon declarations
The declarations are not merely boke behavior. They are a **cultural performance genre** shared by highly competent people. Jokes arise from:
- massive mismatch between register and mundane activity;
- outsiders imitating it;
- Yunyun refusing it despite belonging to the culture;
- Darkness failing it through name/status embarrassment;
- serious battle making the performance socially meaningful.

## 5. Tourism / lost technology
Sacred sword, wishing spring, ancient facility, weapon-as-clothesline and related sites combine:
- fantasy reverence;
- scam tourism;
- Earth technology misrecognition;
- reader dramatic irony.
Mostly L0-L2 rather than L3.

## 6. Kazuma's romantic/status failure loops
V05 uses attraction as an attention distortion and grievance source. The portable behavioral evidence is that Kazuma becomes more status-sensitive, impulsive, and self-justifying when intimacy/attraction is salient. Do not transfer specific sexual-comedy setups literally into cross-context simulation.

## 7. Megumin peer bragging
Megumin weaponizes technically true but implication-heavy relationship facts against classmates. Humor comes from selective truth + audience inference + competitive status, not from social naivete.

## 8. Final Chapter reversal
**Expected optimization:** Megumin finally learns practical advanced magic after five volumes of complaints.

**Reversal:** Kazuma, the person who most complained, secretly invests all saved points in stronger Explosion.

The joke succeeds because the relationship model has changed. It simultaneously resolves character growth:
- Megumin proves she can choose group utility over identity;
- Kazuma proves he values her identity over the utility he demanded.

**Evidence class:** H1 for underlying choices/relationship; H2 for extreme game-build framing.

## 9. Sylvia body/gender-category reveal
The source uses a period-specific sexual-category panic joke around Sylvia's chimera body. For current analytical use:
- treat the portable evidence as Kazuma's rigid attraction schema colliding with unexpected information;
- treat the exact gag framing as culturally/period-sensitive H3 rather than a general moral or identity claim;
- do not reproduce derogatory framing in reconstruction outputs.

## 10. V05 L-level distribution
Major mechanisms remain dominated by:
- **L0:** situation/reversal, role routing, status, character motive;
- **L1:** timing/wording emphasis;
- **L2:** cultural performance, social register, fantasy/Japanese gamer conventions;
- **L3:** present but not dominant.

This strongly supports MG01-J02 through a second prospective volume.


# V06 Update

## High-value V06 mechanisms

1. **Technically true heroic reframing (H1/H2, L1-L2):** Kazuma's adventure stories remain rooted in real events while motive/status emphasis is strategically altered.
2. **Role inversion (H2/H3):** the low-level "weak" Kazuma becomes a terrifying masked castle invader because beginner skills are sequenced adversarially.
3. **Competence misallocation (H1/H2):** Aqua performs astonishing court/shop art while forgetting the instrumental commercial objective.
4. **Status collision (H1/H2):** Darkness's impeccable noble role coexists with friends who know and publicly deploy `Lalatina` as a genuine aversion.
5. **Masked-identity dramatic irony (H1/H2):** Megumin admires the mysterious masked thief without recognizing Kazuma; Kazuma later wants credit for his own alter ego.
6. **Body-swap power fantasy/reversal (H2/H3/H4):** royal deference rapidly degrades Kazuma's restraint; the swap ends at maximum expected payoff and returns him into the consequences of Iris/Megumin's street behavior.
7. **Institutional reputational reversal (H1/H2):** Kazuma is publicly treated as useless for dying to kobolds while his thief alter ego is admired for defeating the same elites who dismiss him.
8. **Return-to-baseline domestic bathos (H1):** after national crisis, royal separation, artifact theft, and future-marriage symbolism, the party immediately resumes alcohol, snack, teasing, and age-status arguments at home.

## L0-L3 assessment
The principal humor remains situation, role, causal reversal, status, rhythm, and pragmatic register. Japanese-specific language adds precision—especially address and formal/informal shifts—but L3 wordplay is still a minority mechanism. MG01-J02 is confirmed across V04-V06.

## Comic-license boundary
The body-swap bath escalation should not be transferred literally into grounded scenarios. The useful reconstruction evidence is the **direction** of Kazuma's response to asymmetric power, sexual opportunity, and delayed accountability. Literal timing/intensity is H3-amplified.

# V07 Update

## Major V07 humor mechanisms

### HM-V07-01 — Heroic action immediately punctured by stable vice
Kazuma pays twenty billion, crashes a wedding and rescues Darkness; minutes later he hides behind Darkness from Megumin's Explosion. The joke preserves attachment and cowardice simultaneously rather than allowing a heroic scene to replace his normal model. **H2/H3 boundary.**

### HM-V07-02 — Sacred competence destroys melodrama
Darkness/father reach a near-death emotional farewell; Aqua instantly removes the demonic curse. The comedy is a causal reversal produced by genuine extreme competence, not incompetence. **H1 + structural L0.**

### HM-V07-03 — Masochistic relief contaminates rescue language
Kazuma's coercive-sounding `身体で払ってもらう` is intended as adventurer labor/debt rhetoric; Darkness's masochistic/sexual interpretation produces an immediate bodily reaction. Underlying relief, attraction and masochistic disposition are real; magnitude is comic. **H2.**

### HM-V07-04 — The "bad wizard" is the hero
Megumin adopts the villainous bride-stealing role in a genuinely prosocial rescue. Role inversion creates humor and grandeur simultaneously. **H1/H2, L0-L2.**

### HM-V07-05 — Friendship scarcity as tactical currency
Megumin can recruit Yunyun into criminal danger by calling her `我が親友`, then immediately resume abrasive denial/asymmetry. The joke depends on established relationship reward structure. **H1/H2, L1-L2.**

### HM-V07-06 — Legal status becomes a new teasing weapon
Interrupted wedding plus filed marriage paperwork yields the possibility that Darkness is technically divorced; Kazuma creates `バツネス`. This is a smaller L3-ish lexical joke embedded in a larger L0-L2 relationship scene.

### HM-V07-07 — Aqua's dragon egg
The joke works because Aqua's prestige/rarity/caretaker incentives are psychologically coherent enough to sustain an obviously false interpretation. **H2** rather than pure nonliteral gag.

## Humor evidence conclusion through V07
Major V07 comedy remains dominated by causal reversal, role collision, status, pragmatic timing and relationship scripts. Japanese-form-dependent L3 material exists but is not the principal engine. This strengthens MG02-J02.
