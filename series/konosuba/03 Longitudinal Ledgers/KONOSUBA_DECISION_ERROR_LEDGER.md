## Emerging causal families

### Aqua — status-driven capability misdeployment
`status/competence challenge → prove self → deploy real extreme ability → under-model collateral → mixed success/disaster`

### Megumin — identity-rational impracticality
`Explosion/identity opportunity → utility objections recognized → identity value dominates → cost knowingly accepted → self-justifying theater if socially challenged`

### Darkness — reward-driven self-handicapping under protector override
`personal danger → masochistic reward` but `other person endangered → protector action first → danger transferred to self → erotic reinterpretation second`

### Kazuma — adaptive opportunism plus ego/hedonic distortions
`observe system → infer exploit → use low-prestige efficient tactic`; failures cluster more around attraction, resentment, humiliation, and imagined genre scripts than around inability to reason.

## V02 causal-family revisions

### Aqua
Keep **status-driven capability misdeployment**, but add two distinct pathways:
- `repetitive obligation -> avoid labor -> powerful shortcut -> weak system model -> externality`
- `undeniable causation + social guilt -> rapid admission -> remediation`

### Megumin
Split Explosion behavior into at least:
- identity/aesthetic commitment;
- audience/competitive display;
- fear/panic fallback;
- tactically routed output.

### Darkness
Preserve reward-driven self-handicapping but treat **protector/place duty** as an independent higher-order motive. Humiliation reward must be content-sensitive.

### Kazuma
Preserve adaptive opportunism, but add:
- concrete party/trust loyalty;
- sexual reward with meaningful real-world boundaries;
- fast exploit tunnel vision that can omit immediate physical/collateral properties.

## V03 error-model synthesis

Best modeled as an **exploit-seeking, mixed-motive pragmatist**. His failures often come from self-serving framing, status retaliation, sexual/social bias, or exploit tunnel vision—not inability to reason.

### Aqua
At least three independent failure routes are now required:
1. status/self-aggrandizement;
2. high-powered shortcut with incomplete externality modeling;
3. affective sacred/evil antipathy causing premature action.

### Megumin
The dominant “error” is a consciously protected value commitment. Outside that commitment, V03 supplies strong evidence of flexible strategic reasoning.

### Darkness
Her combat distortion is knowingly purchased in exchange for a preferred experience. Under coercion, the same pain reward can become a defense rather than a liability.
