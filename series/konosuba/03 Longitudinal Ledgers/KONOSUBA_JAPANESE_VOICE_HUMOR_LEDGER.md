# KONOSUBA Japanese Voice and Humor Ledger — Through V14

## Method caution

These are V01 distributions, not permanent labels. Forms become model evidence through recurrence, context, and contrast.

## Kazuma

### V01 patterns
- first-person narrative centered on `俺`;
- can use ordinary politeness strategically with strangers/clerks (`すいません`, thanks, requests);
- speech toward party rapidly shifts to plain/direct forms;
- internal narration freely uses devaluing reference such as `こいつ`;
- `駄女神` condenses his Aqua appraisal into a recurring insult category;
- outward speech in crisis is often short/direct while internal narration performs longer causal analysis;
- uses game/RPG metalanguage as cognitive framing, not merely decorative references.

### Humor/voice implication
Kazuma's comedic voice often comes from the gap between **long internal rationalization** and **short socially abrasive output**.

## Aqua

### V01 patterns
- initial divine/institutional presentation can collapse immediately into colloquial mockery;
- invokes `女神`, `アクア様`, and related status language when challenged;
- fear produces repeated calls, crying, pleading, and emotionally compressed syntax;
- anger/status challenge produces direct imperative and self-assertion;
- after genuine competence she often solicits praise/recognition;
- mockery is socially aggressive but does not prevent rapid help-seeking from Kazuma.

### Humor/voice implication
The key Japanese effect is frequently **status-register incongruity**: divine self-framing attached to petty, needy, or highly ordinary speech.

## Megumin

### V01 patterns
- stylized introduction/Explosion language uses grand self-mythologizing forms such as `我が名はめぐみん`;
- practical/hungry/negotiating speech can collapse to ordinary `私` and normal politeness/plain speech;
- can deliberately intensify ambiguous sexual/social wording to pressure Kazuma;
- Beldia encounter shows rapid fabrication of heroic register after being caught doing something embarrassing;
- Explosion incantation is both spell performance and identity performance.

### Humor/voice implication
Megumin's voice is **register-switching**, not permanently theatrical. The contrast between grand `我` performance and ordinary practical speech is itself comic evidence.

## Darkness

### V01 patterns
- firm, knightly/direct declaratives in ordinary and protective contexts;
- protector speech becomes concise and duty-framed (`守る`, Crusader obligation);
- sexual/masochistic excitement changes semantic content dramatically while preserving enough formal seriousness to heighten incongruity;
- post-Beldia eulogy reveals softer, sincere register;
- genuine emotional exposure produces a different embarrassment register from desired sexual humiliation.

## Provisional language-level conclusion

V01 suggests that KONOSUBA's largest jokes are usually **structurally portable**, but Japanese carries substantial extra information about:
- status aggression;
- theatrical self-positioning;
- mock/real politeness;
- emotional collapse;
- dyadic familiarity;
- timing through minimal responses and register shifts.

The mature simulation model should generate psychology first, then relationship/communicative goal, then Japanese-derived register/stance—not catchphrases first.


# V02 Update

## Kazuma

- Cemetery accountability ends in `「……………………はい。本当にごめんなさい」`, a notable contrast with her usual argumentative rhythm.

## Megumin
- Destroyer anxiety produces stammering and loss of the polished chuunibyou cadence.
- Kazuma's challenge to Explosion pride restores full declarative/theatrical voice. Voice performance is therefore confidence- and identity-state dependent.
- Haunted-house fear similarly reduces role-language and increases direct dependency language.

## Darkness
- V02 adds a vulnerable relational register distinct from masochistic performance. Her question `「……ワガママで頑固な仲間は嫌いか？」` seeks reassurance rather than stimulation.
- Bath aftermath and Destroyer epilogue show that shame/sexualization cannot be inferred from content alone; pragmatic relationship meaning determines the register.

## Translation sensitivity
Most central V02 humor remains L0-L1. High-value L2 material clusters around:
- Aqua/Eris seniority and status address;
- mock/strategic politeness;
- collapse into apology or pleading;
- Megumin's switch between theatrical and frightened speech;
- Darkness's unusually plain relational reassurance request.

No major V02 main-narrative conclusion depends on an L3 wordplay mechanism.

# V05 Japanese Voice / Humor Update

## Kazuma
- **Register portability:** readily adopts Crimson Demon grandiose introduction style when it gains social approval, demonstrating deliberate style-switch capacity rather than fixed bluntness.
- **Narrative framing:** game/NEET experiences can be rendered in heroic-sounding Japanese without literal fabrication; social meaning is altered through selection and register.
- **Serious fear:** after the orc incident, speech loses performative confidence and becomes direct help-seeking; this fits the prior seriousness-compression pattern.
- **Final Chapter:** when Megumin says she will abandon Explosion, Kazuma drops sexual/romantic overinterpretation and responds in direct practical language, another core-cast J01 support case.

## Aqua
- **Goddess assertion:** `本物の神様` / goddess-role claims remain easy to activate in status/performance contexts even when literally true.
- **Serious care:** Kazuma's acute fear suppresses the normal teasing register; Aqua uses comparatively plain reassurance/comfort.
- **Cultural imitation:** adopts Crimson Demon introduction format quickly, showing performative register is available as a social/status tool.

## Megumin
- **Cultural grounding:** `我が名は...` is now established as a Crimson Demon social genre, not uniquely private speech pathology.
- **Identity challenge:** Explosion criticism produces sharper, more aggressive language and social leverage.
- **Private gratitude:** direct thanks to Kazuma and concern about being a burden use markedly less theatrical language.
- **Deliberate parody:** adopts an unusually generic schoolgirl-friendship style toward Yunyun specifically to embarrass her. This is intentional mimicry, not baseline voice.
- **Final identity restoration:** after Kazuma preserves Explosion, the return to `我が名はめぐみん` carries state-transition meaning: she has resumed the identity she was prepared to surrender.

## Darkness
- **Noble/name pressure:** attempting Crimson Demon theatrical self-introduction through Dustiness/Lalatina identity produces embarrassment and breakdown rather than fluent performance.
- **Protector mode:** danger-facing speech remains more direct and duty-centered than eroticized comic baseline.

## Yunyun — J01 counterexample
V05 requires a major nuance to the frozen J01 directional hypothesis.

Yunyun ordinarily avoids Crimson Demon theatrical declarations because they embarrass her. Under serious duty, she deliberately performs a full heroic introduction while serving as bait, and the act becomes the social signal through which the village recognizes/accepts her.

Therefore:

> **Seriousness does not universally reduce theatricality. It realigns register toward the identity/role the speaker treats as necessary in that moment.**

For Kazuma/Aqua/Megumin/Darkness, this often still means less theatrical, more direct speech. For Yunyun, serious communal duty temporarily makes the culturally theatrical register *more* appropriate and therefore more likely.

MG01-J01 should be adjudicated **PARTIAL / REVISE** after V05 rather than treated as cleanly confirmed.

## Translation-sensitivity notes
- Crimson Demon declaration humor is mostly L2: translation can convey the propositions, but the social fact that this is a shared cultural register matters.
- Tourist/technology jokes are mostly L0-L2.
- Kazuma's heroic framing of gaming experience is L1/L2 because pragmatic implication matters more than literal falsehood.
- V05 does not overturn the prediction that L3-only wordplay is a minority of high-value comedy.


# V06 Update

## Model-level revision
V06 confirms the V05 counterexample to the original J01 directional rule. Replace:

> serious state -> less theatrical speech

with:

> **serious state -> active role/identity becomes more authoritative -> register shifts toward the language appropriate to that role.**

The resulting speech may be plainer, more formal, or more theatrical depending on speaker and role.

## Kazuma
- Royal guest: capable of strategic politeness when useful.
- Iris private play: rapidly shifts to ordinary blunt older-brother/peer-like informality.
- Public self-presentation: lengthy, technically defensible heroic framing.
- Castle heist: thief-role theatricality **increases** after he commits to the mission; seriousness and performance are compatible.
- Private separation anxiety: internal language loses much of the public heroic frame and directly names exclusion/unease.

## Aqua
- Court: can perform correct bowing/formality when motivated, showing sociolinguistic capacity is not the limiting variable.
- Performance state: expansive confident entertainer register.
- Kazuma acute distress: comparatively gentle, practical speech.
- Domestic alcohol conflict: rapidly returns to ordinary demanding/blaming register.

## Megumin
- Royal dinner restraint: quiet/direct explanation that she is holding back because Darkness would suffer.
- Battle victory: highly theatrical Crimson Demon self-declaration is role-congruent serious performance, not a failure to become serious.
- Iris relation: competitive/older-sister role language coexists with genuine friendship.

## Darkness
- Host/noble: polished `ダスティネス・フォード・ララティーナ` formal presentation.
- Serious protector of Kazuma before Iris: calm formal corrective speech continues while physically injured.
- Ordinary party peer: abrasive familiar speech resumes immediately outside role constraints.
- `ララティーナ` remains a high-salience unwanted address precisely because it collapses private/noble identity boundaries in uncontrolled contexts.

## Iris
- Address progression is a major relational locator: formal `カズマ様` / guest relation -> `お兄様` older-brother attachment -> private epilogue `お兄ちゃん`.
- Royal public register and private age-level speech are sharply differentiated.
- Nondeferential interaction increases her ordinary competitive and mischievous speech rather than merely reducing politeness.

## Translation sensitivity
V06's most important linguistic effects are L1-L2: honorific/address systems, court hierarchy, technically truthful self-presentation, role speech, and pragmatic shifts. L3 remains important locally but is not the main carrier of characterization or comedy.

## JV-V09-01 — The Explosion appraisal is a shared relationship register

- **Source:** part0009.xhtml P0009–0025.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Kazuma and Megumin, during their familiar post-casting evaluation and carry home.
- **Anchor/basic gloss:** 「精進します！」 — “I'll keep improving.”
- **Form and pragmatics:** Megumin's earnest, deferential acceptance follows Kazuma's elaborate expert evaluation. Both inhabit an evaluative routine, then move immediately into ordinary complaint and teasing about the continuing transport burden.
- **Channel/class:** dialogue, with Kazuma's narrative framing; **L1–L2** for register contrast, **L0** for the underlying inconvenience.
- **Model implication:** shared stylized language can be genuine affiliation as well as performance. It does not make her practical claim about needing to be carried innocent of self-interest. **Confidence: HIGH** for the interaction; no claim that the entire technical appraisal is independently measured.

## JV-V09-02 — The same written object becomes “babysitting”

- **Source:** part0010.xhtml P0061, P0081–0083, P0116–0118.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Megumin first gives Kazuma a protective charm, then leaves Darkness to occupy him.
- **Anchor/basic gloss:** 「カズマのお守り」 — “looking after/babysitting Kazuma,” in the second occurrence.
- **Form and pragmatics:** the earlier お守り means an amulet. At P0116, the preserved ruby explicitly reads 守 as も, selecting おもり in the babysitting expression. The written resemblance lets protective care pivot into teasingly treating the recipient as someone who needs supervision; Kazuma immediately objects to the assigned direction of care.
- **Channel/class:** dialogue; a local **L3** written reading contrast, embedded in portable relationship comedy.
- **Model implication:** affection and patronizing teasing coexist in the same domestic sequence. This is a local effect, not evidence that form-dependent wordplay dominates the volume. **Confidence: HIGH** for the ruby/form distinction; **MODERATE-HIGH** for its deliberate callback function.

## JV-V09-03 — Preference repair and reluctant deference reveal distinct states

- **Source:** part0010.xhtml P0184–0191, P0398–0410.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Darkness to Kazuma, first seeking the particular restraint she wants, later returning to ask for practical help after their quarrel.
- **Anchors/basic glosses:** 「それがいい」 / 「それでいい」 — “that is what I want” repaired into “that will do”; 「カズマ……さん……」 — a hesitant respectful address.
- **Form and pragmatics:** the immediate が→で repair attempts to disguise specific desire as mere acceptance, but the subsequent insistence exposes the preference. The later さん and polite request mark constrained dependence on the very person she has just challenged.
- **Channel/class:** dialogue; **L2** for stance and interpersonal repositioning, with **L1** repair timing.
- **Model implication:** neither “formal noble” nor “eager masochist” suffices as a fixed voice. Request, embarrassment, anger, and dependence select different outputs. **Confidence: HIGH**; the comic situation's exact intensity should not be generalized.

## JV-V09-04 — A plain vulnerable request succeeds where inducements fail

- **Source:** part0011.xhtml P0314–0350, P0353–0372, P0478–0486.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Megumin asks Kazuma to accompany her; earlier appeals use Iris, promised private time, and recognition, while the later request acknowledges her need and concern for Chomusuke.
- **Anchors/basic glosses:** 「……ダメですか？」 — “Is that too much to ask?”; 「しょうがねえなあー！」 — roughly “All right, I can't leave it at that.”
- **Form and pragmatics:** bowing, an apology for the request, and the tentative final question expose vulnerability rather than insisting that Kazuma should find the reward attractive. His outward complaint remains while his decision changes.
- **Channel/class:** dialogue plus contemporaneous internal narration; **L1–L2**.
- **Model implication:** receptive care does not require polite or heroic outward self-presentation. Do not reduce the result to a universal “ask gently” rule; their history and his recognition of her uncertainty are material conditions. **Confidence: HIGH** for this contrast.

## JV-V09-05 — A truth device tests wording, not whole relationships

- **Source:** part0011.xhtml P0451–0476.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Aqua questions a constrained Megumin, then addresses Kazuma while checking whether the lie-detecting device works.
- **Anchor/basic gloss:** 「この魔道具は正常よ」 — “This device is working properly.”
- **Form and pragmatics:** Aqua first exploits the device to expose the inadequacy of Megumin's simple rejection of the Axis followers. She then supplies exaggerated praise of Kazuma, allows the device to reject it, and reframes the insult as a practical calibration.
- **Channel/class:** dialogue and narrated device response; **L0–L1**, with pragmatic stance relevant at **L2**.
- **Model implication:** Aqua can originate an effective social/inferential maneuver and occupy the corrective role. A rejected bundle of praise does not establish that every positive attitude within it is absent. **Confidence: HIGH** for the exchange; deliberately limited inference about individual motives.

## JV-V09-06 — Imported authority language can serve petty retaliation

- **Source:** part0012.xhtml P0512–0544.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Kazuma addresses hostile fortress knights while identifying Darkness's rank; Megumin and Aqua join after the hierarchy reverses.
- **Anchor/basic gloss:** 「この御方をどなたと心得る！」 — “Do you know who this person is?”

- **Model implication:** formal competence and noble-role language are usable for grievance and bargaining, not only morally elevated duty. Darkness's embarrassment remains separate from the efficacy of the maneuver. **Confidence: HIGH**.

## JV-V09-07 — Divine self-presentation can escalate rather than dignify

- **Source:** part0013.xhtml P0256–0300.
- **Speaker/addressee and state:** Aqua and Wolbach in their first direct confrontation, with the party attempting to hold the enemy in place.
- **Anchor/basic gloss:** 「謝って！」 — “Apologize!”
- **Form and pragmatics:** Aqua repeatedly substitutes status demands, classification disputes, and follower rivalry for the intended delay.
