# KONOSUBA Relationship State Ledger — Through V16

## Purpose

Tracks dyadic and ensemble relationship mechanisms that materially condition behavior. V01 already shows that the party's intimacy is expressed more through practical dependence, tolerated aggression, protection, and shared routines than through idealizing affection.

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua

### Origin
Kazuma is humiliated by Aqua's mockery after death; he retaliates by selecting her as his isekai “advantage.” Their relationship begins through **status reversal and forced interdependence**.

### V01 default interaction
- reciprocal insults and status attacks;
- frequent physical/verbal coercion within comic register;
- fast recovery after argument;
- practical reliance despite contempt language;
- each learns the other's vulnerabilities quickly.

### Known leverage
**Kazuma over Aqua:** challenge divine competence/status; exploit desire for praise; restrict resources; physically stop categorical attacks.\
**Aqua over Kazuma:** public embarrassment; needling hikikomori/social insecurity; access to healing/support; financial consequences.

### Trust evidence
- Kazuma calls on Aqua as decisive anti-undead and water asset despite insulting her.
- Aqua repeatedly runs toward/calls for Kazuma under fear.
- Aqua defends current life with Kazuma's party when Kyouya frames it as mistreatment.
- Kazuma assumes responsibility for routing her powers even after repeated collateral problems.

### Boundary/open question
Ritual aggression is intense. Later volumes must distinguish what the pair can say/do without relational threat from what would constitute genuine betrayal or injury.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin

### Origin
Kazuma initially rejects her as operationally impractical. Megumin uses social pressure to force inclusion.

### V01 development
- Kazuma carries Megumin whenever Explosion incapacitates her.
- Daily Explosion practice becomes shared routine.
- Kazuma develops genuine aesthetic appreciation and tactical understanding of her spell.
- Megumin accepts Kazuma's timing commands in combat.
- When she is cursed indirectly by her own behavior, Kazuma accepts shared responsibility rather than abandoning her.

### Interaction style
- teasing and sexual embarrassment tolerated;
- Kazuma calls her “頭のおかしい” type terms, but also praises successful Explosion;
- Megumin can retaliate physically/verbal-socially;
- serious guilt suppresses the joke quickly.

### Current hypothesis
Acceptance of Megumin's defining impracticality is a major bonding mechanism. Kazuma does not try to reform Explosion out of her once she is admitted; he instead builds tactics around it.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness

### Origin
Kazuma immediately recognizes her high-spec/low-function pattern and tries to avoid recruiting another liability. Darkness is attracted to his perceived cruelty.

### V01 development
- Kazuma understands her defensive value during cabbage and Beldia fights.
- He is alarmed when she actually bleeds, showing differentiation between consensual comic masochism and real injury risk.
- Darkness accepts Kazuma's battlefield coordination and requests an opening to strike.
- Darkness directly protects Kazuma when Beldia targets him.

### Interaction style
Kazuma's blunt insult style strongly stimulates Darkness's masochism, but this does not mean all embarrassment works on her: her overheard sincere eulogy produces unwanted shame.

## Aqua ↔ Megumin

### Reinforcement risk
They can validate each other's impractical values. Aqua admires Megumin's romantic inefficiency; later accompanies repeated castle bombing because she resents Beldia.

### Functional complement
Aqua can solve status ailments/curses that Megumin's reckless behavior creates. Megumin supplies destructive force Aqua lacks in conventional attack magic.

## Aqua ↔ Darkness

### Protection
Darkness physically/suspiciously protects Aqua when Kyouya tries to remove her from the party.

### Divergent reward
Aqua fears threats that Darkness actively wants, producing frequent comic contrast rather than direct rivalry.

## Megumin ↔ Darkness

### Early connection
Both oppose Kyouya's attempt to reframe/remove Aqua and show immediate party-allegiance reactions.

### High-value event
Darkness intercepts Beldia's death curse meant for Megumin. Megumin subsequently shows genuine guilt and attempts to take responsibility.

## Party ensemble

### Emerging attachment markers
- visible anxiety when Kazuma considers other parties;
- carrying incapacitated members;
- transferring full lake-purification reward to Aqua after her ordeal;
- stepping between companions and lethal threats;
- defending current party life against external “rescuer” framing;
- collective tactical follow-through under Kazuma's coordination;
- rapid return to banter after crisis.

### Emerging comic system
The party often becomes worse because members trigger one another:
- Aqua + Megumin → retaliatory castle bombing;
- Kazuma insult → Aqua status overperformance;
- Kazuma verbal cruelty → Darkness erotic escalation;
- Megumin target fixation → Kazuma becomes transport/tactical support.

### Current ensemble thesis
The group is not held together by harmony. It is held together by **mutual utility, tolerated dysfunction, shared history, protection, and growing preference for these specific people despite continual complaint**.


# V02 Relationship Update

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- Resurrection makes dependence existentially explicit: Aqua retrieves Kazuma from death; Kazuma nevertheless deflects affection with mock rejection.
- Dungeon exploration shows high two-person functional complementarity: his stealth/system thinking plus her undead/night-vision specialization.
- Aqua's status boasting is often regulated by Kazuma withdrawing attention/support rather than by abstract argument.
- Kazuma's treasure-sharing attempt after Keele suggests he notices and responds to Aqua's subdued serious mood.
- Cemetery causation produces unusually direct Aqua accountability rather than prolonged dyadic combat.

**Current dyadic rule:** ritualized contempt coexists with extremely high practical trust; emotional gratitude is often converted into insult, teasing, or task-solving rather than spoken warmly.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- Shared haunted-house fear creates reciprocal dependency rather than Kazuma-as-caretaker only.
- Kazuma physically inhibits an indoor panic Explosion.
- Before the Destroyer, he accurately uses Megumin's Explosion pride to regulate performance anxiety.
- Mana-transfer sequence shows increasing willingness to coordinate intimate/awkward physical procedures when operational stakes are clear, while both still protest the sexual framing.

**Current dyadic rule:** Kazuma increasingly treats Megumin's fixation as a control surface to route rather than a defect to cure.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- Bath misunderstanding introduces sexual/romantic ambiguity but is poor evidence for knowingly coercive baseline behavior because Kazuma believes he is dreaming.
- Afterward both accept face-saving ambiguity; Kazuma does not publicly expose Darkness's private response.
- Darkness explicitly seeks reassurance that her duty-driven stubbornness does not make Kazuma dislike her.
- Kazuma distinguishes her civic duty from Aqua-style selfishness and validates it despite predicting poor tactical utility.

**Current dyadic rule:** insults can be erotically reinforcing, but both maintain reputation/privacy needs and can seek/offer serious relational validation underneath the comic script.

## Aqua ↔ Wiz
- Strong antagonistic sacred-category script, with Aqua repeatedly treating interaction as goddess-versus-lich contest.
- Wiz's benevolence and Kazuma's intervention can inhibit destruction, but Aqua remains tempted to purify her secretly.
- Aqua's divine mana is physically harmful to Wiz, making the relationship literally incompatible at a resource-transfer level.
- Despite hostility, Aqua feels guilt when her attack leaves Wiz weak and volunteers to handle the mansion exorcism.

## Party ensemble / cohabitation
- Mansion life creates stable shared domestic routines rather than transient adventurer association.
- Luxury meal scenes, games, fireplace disputes, chores, and grave care provide low-stakes relational evidence.
- Destroyer battle shows mature role routing: Kazuma coordinates, Aqua supplies barrier/healing/mana, Megumin supplies output, Darkness anchors commitment to defend.
- The one-day swap strongly supports that Kazuma's presence changes *how* the women's extreme traits combine.

### Updated ensemble thesis
The party is developing into a **high-friction household and operational system**. Affection is still rarely idealized; belonging is evidenced through rescue, cohabitation, secrets, routines, practical dependence, and willingness to remain in one another's crises.

## Aqua ↔ Wiz / Vanir
- Aqua's goddess hierarchy creates persistent friction with Wiz despite acknowledging/helping her in other contexts.
- Vanir's demon identity triggers immediate sacred hostility; their antagonism is structural, not merely personal insult.

## Party-level state through V03

- **STRENGTHEN:** conflict/insult tolerance is itself evidence of relationship stability; decreasing verbal aggression is not required for increasing attachment.
- **NEW:** outside institutions repeatedly misread or simplify party dynamics, while insiders possess highly specific vulnerability/reward knowledge that improves coordination and cruelty simultaneously.

# V05 Relationship Update

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
**Major state change: mutual romantic interest becomes textually supported, without formal couple status.**

Evidence cluster:
- daily Explosion evaluation/carry-home routine now functions as a stable shared ritual;
- quiet travel discussion confirms both value the current party life and imagine a comfortable future together with the group;
- Megumin explicitly thanks Kazuma for accepting/carrying/supporting her despite Explosion's burden;
- trust is calibrated rather than naive: she expects opportunism and can withdraw/repair trust after manipulation;
- Megumin uses relationship implication/status against peers and later expresses direct affection, then partially softens it when Kazuma reacts strongly;
- Megumin is willing to abandon Explosion to stop burdening Kazuma/party;
- Kazuma refuses that self-sacrifice by preserving Explosion in her build, choosing her identity over generic party optimization;
- post-return, they unusually give one another credit for the Sylvia victory.

**Model implication:** attraction is now a legitimate causal variable for jealousy, embarrassment, attention and prioritization. Do not simulate them as an established couple yet. Their ordinary interaction still includes insults, manipulation, retaliation and uncertainty.

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- **STRENGTHEN — sibling-like antagonism with serious safe-base override:** after the orc attack Aqua suppresses teasing and comforts Kazuma; he clings to her without status defense.
- **STRENGTHEN — synchronized domestic avoidance:** epilogue has Kazuma and Aqua immediately coordinate to expel a visitor they assume brings trouble.
- **PRESERVE — sacred/status friction:** Kazuma still discounts or needles Aqua in ordinary state; serious care does not produce generalized gentleness.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- **STRENGTHEN — protector reciprocity:** Darkness offers herself as hostage in Kazuma's place; Kazuma repeatedly relies on her as durable delay/shield.
- **STRENGTHEN — grievance/intimacy coexistence:** even while captured, Kazuma uses leverage to demand apology for ordinary-party mistreatment, showing safe retaliation survives serious attachment.
- **NEXT-TRANCHE PRESSURE:** royal invitation and Dustiness anxiety make V06 a direct test of whether Kazuma respects or exploits Darkness's status boundaries under institutional pressure.

## Megumin ↔ Yunyun
- **STRENGTHEN — rivalry as affiliation:** Megumin attacks Yunyun's Explosion critique, humiliates her socially, and keeps score aggressively while also worrying about her, crying at her village acceptance, and recognizing her importance.
- **REVISE — Yunyun belonging:** Yunyun now receives explicit village admiration after performing the cultural heroic role she usually rejects; acceptance is pleasurable in substance but embarrassing in form.
- **Model implication:** hostility cues between these two cannot be mapped directly to relational distance. Competition is one of the relationship's principal maintenance mechanisms.

## Megumin ↔ family
- Strong concern for Komekko motivates return even while Megumin masks broader worry.
- Poverty/history and Komekko's behavior give Megumin's family attachment material stakes.
- Mother actively treats Kazuma as a desirable match after learning his wealth; this external pressure is not equivalent to Megumin's own consent or relationship state.

## Aqua ↔ party under serious fear
V05 supplies unusually clean evidence that Aqua can become gentle/supportive when a companion is genuinely frightened. Treat this as a seriousness-conditioned script rather than a new permanent tone.

## Darkness ↔ party / noble identity
V05 continues the split between peer-adventurer belonging and noble-role exposure. Darkness is comfortable using her body/risk tolerance to protect companions but much less comfortable with public self-display tied to Dustiness identity. The royal invitation at the epilogue directly threatens to pull the party into her aristocratic role world.

## Ensemble relationship state through V05
The party increasingly functions as **reciprocal cleanup infrastructure**:
- Kazuma repeatedly constrains/routs others' liabilities;
- Megumin explicitly says she will clean up Kazuma's mistake because he usually cleans up theirs;
- Darkness repeatedly takes danger onto herself;
- Aqua supplies rescue/healing/comfort despite insults.

This supports a mature formulation of affection without idealization:

> They do not become nicer as attachment deepens; they become more willing to absorb one another's costs, preserve one another's identities, and return after conflict.
