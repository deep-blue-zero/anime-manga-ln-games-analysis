# KONOSUBA Relationship State Ledger — Through V07

## Purpose

Tracks dyadic and ensemble relationship mechanisms that materially condition behavior. V01 already shows that the party's intimacy is expressed more through practical dependence, tolerated aggression, protection, and shared routines than through idealizing affection.

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua

### Origin
Kazuma is humiliated by Aqua's mockery after death; he retaliates by selecting her as his isekai “advantage.” Their relationship begins through **status reversal and forced interdependence**.

### V01 default interaction
- reciprocal insults and status attacks;
- frequent physical/verbal coercion within comic register;
- fast recovery after argument;
- practical reliance despite contempt language;
- each learns the other's vulnerabilities quickly.

### Known leverage
**Kazuma over Aqua:** challenge divine competence/status; exploit desire for praise; restrict resources; physically stop categorical attacks.\
**Aqua over Kazuma:** public embarrassment; needling hikikomori/social insecurity; access to healing/support; financial consequences.

### Trust evidence
- Kazuma calls on Aqua as decisive anti-undead and water asset despite insulting her.
- Aqua repeatedly runs toward/calls for Kazuma under fear.
- Aqua defends current life with Kazuma's party when Kyouya frames it as mistreatment.
- Kazuma assumes responsibility for routing her powers even after repeated collateral problems.

### Boundary/open question
Ritual aggression is intense. Later volumes must distinguish what the pair can say/do without relational threat from what would constitute genuine betrayal or injury.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin

### Origin
Kazuma initially rejects her as operationally impractical. Megumin uses social pressure to force inclusion.

### V01 development
- Kazuma carries Megumin whenever Explosion incapacitates her.
- Daily Explosion practice becomes shared routine.
- Kazuma develops genuine aesthetic appreciation and tactical understanding of her spell.
- Megumin accepts Kazuma's timing commands in combat.
- When she is cursed indirectly by her own behavior, Kazuma accepts shared responsibility rather than abandoning her.

### Interaction style
- teasing and sexual embarrassment tolerated;
- Kazuma calls her “頭のおかしい” type terms, but also praises successful Explosion;
- Megumin can retaliate physically/verbal-socially;
- serious guilt suppresses the joke quickly.

### Current hypothesis
Acceptance of Megumin's defining impracticality is a major bonding mechanism. Kazuma does not try to reform Explosion out of her once she is admitted; he instead builds tactics around it.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness

### Origin
Kazuma immediately recognizes her high-spec/low-function pattern and tries to avoid recruiting another liability. Darkness is attracted to his perceived cruelty.

### V01 development
- Kazuma understands her defensive value during cabbage and Beldia fights.
- He is alarmed when she actually bleeds, showing differentiation between consensual comic masochism and real injury risk.
- Darkness accepts Kazuma's battlefield coordination and requests an opening to strike.
- Darkness directly protects Kazuma when Beldia targets him.

### Interaction style
Kazuma's blunt insult style strongly stimulates Darkness's masochism, but this does not mean all embarrassment works on her: her overheard sincere eulogy produces unwanted shame.

## Aqua ↔ Megumin

### Reinforcement risk
They can validate each other's impractical values. Aqua admires Megumin's romantic inefficiency; later accompanies repeated castle bombing because she resents Beldia.

### Functional complement
Aqua can solve status ailments/curses that Megumin's reckless behavior creates. Megumin supplies destructive force Aqua lacks in conventional attack magic.

## Aqua ↔ Darkness

### Protection
Darkness physically/suspiciously protects Aqua when Kyouya tries to remove her from the party.

### Divergent reward
Aqua fears threats that Darkness actively wants, producing frequent comic contrast rather than direct rivalry.

## Megumin ↔ Darkness

### Early connection
Both oppose Kyouya's attempt to reframe/remove Aqua and show immediate party-allegiance reactions.

### High-value event
Darkness intercepts Beldia's death curse meant for Megumin. Megumin subsequently shows genuine guilt and attempts to take responsibility.

## Party ensemble

### Emerging attachment markers
- visible anxiety when Kazuma considers other parties;
- carrying incapacitated members;
- transferring full lake-purification reward to Aqua after her ordeal;
- stepping between companions and lethal threats;
- defending current party life against external “rescuer” framing;
- collective tactical follow-through under Kazuma's coordination;
- rapid return to banter after crisis.

### Emerging comic system
The party often becomes worse because members trigger one another:
- Aqua + Megumin → retaliatory castle bombing;
- Kazuma insult → Aqua status overperformance;
- Kazuma verbal cruelty → Darkness erotic escalation;
- Megumin target fixation → Kazuma becomes transport/tactical support.

### Current ensemble thesis
The group is not held together by harmony. It is held together by **mutual utility, tolerated dysfunction, shared history, protection, and growing preference for these specific people despite continual complaint**.


# V02 Relationship Update

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- Resurrection makes dependence existentially explicit: Aqua retrieves Kazuma from death; Kazuma nevertheless deflects affection with mock rejection.
- Dungeon exploration shows high two-person functional complementarity: his stealth/system thinking plus her undead/night-vision specialization.
- Aqua's status boasting is often regulated by Kazuma withdrawing attention/support rather than by abstract argument.
- Kazuma's treasure-sharing attempt after Keele suggests he notices and responds to Aqua's subdued serious mood.
- Cemetery causation produces unusually direct Aqua accountability rather than prolonged dyadic combat.

**Current dyadic rule:** ritualized contempt coexists with extremely high practical trust; emotional gratitude is often converted into insult, teasing, or task-solving rather than spoken warmly.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- Shared haunted-house fear creates reciprocal dependency rather than Kazuma-as-caretaker only.
- Kazuma physically inhibits an indoor panic Explosion.
- Before the Destroyer, he accurately uses Megumin's Explosion pride to regulate performance anxiety.
- Mana-transfer sequence shows increasing willingness to coordinate intimate/awkward physical procedures when operational stakes are clear, while both still protest the sexual framing.

**Current dyadic rule:** Kazuma increasingly treats Megumin's fixation as a control surface to route rather than a defect to cure.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- Bath misunderstanding introduces sexual/romantic ambiguity but is poor evidence for knowingly coercive baseline behavior because Kazuma believes he is dreaming.
- Afterward both accept face-saving ambiguity; Kazuma does not publicly expose Darkness's private response.
- Darkness explicitly seeks reassurance that her duty-driven stubbornness does not make Kazuma dislike her.
- Kazuma distinguishes her civic duty from Aqua-style selfishness and validates it despite predicting poor tactical utility.

**Current dyadic rule:** insults can be erotically reinforcing, but both maintain reputation/privacy needs and can seek/offer serious relational validation underneath the comic script.

## Aqua ↔ Wiz
- Strong antagonistic sacred-category script, with Aqua repeatedly treating interaction as goddess-versus-lich contest.
- Wiz's benevolence and Kazuma's intervention can inhibit destruction, but Aqua remains tempted to purify her secretly.
- Aqua's divine mana is physically harmful to Wiz, making the relationship literally incompatible at a resource-transfer level.
- Despite hostility, Aqua feels guilt when her attack leaves Wiz weak and volunteers to handle the mansion exorcism.

## Party ensemble / cohabitation
- Mansion life creates stable shared domestic routines rather than transient adventurer association.
- Luxury meal scenes, games, fireplace disputes, chores, and grave care provide low-stakes relational evidence.
- Destroyer battle shows mature role routing: Kazuma coordinates, Aqua supplies barrier/healing/mana, Megumin supplies output, Darkness anchors commitment to defend.
- The one-day swap strongly supports that Kazuma's presence changes *how* the women's extreme traits combine.

### Updated ensemble thesis
The party is developing into a **high-friction household and operational system**. Affection is still rarely idealized; belonging is evidenced through rescue, cohabitation, secrets, routines, practical dependence, and willingness to remain in one another's crises.

# V03 Relationship Update

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- Aqua's repeated jailbreak attempts create a new relational category: **operational unreliability with attachment persistence**. Kazuma's fear decreases because she keeps returning, even while her plans fail.
- Both continue to cast the other as the main problem; the epilogue proves this mutual attribution persists privately, not only as public banter.
- Kazuma still protects Aqua's practical position (e.g. defending Wiz's role against simplistic blame), while Aqua's loyalty appears through action rather than respectful speech.
- **Current model:** high-trust/dependence, low deference, high permitted aggression, rapid blame exchange, robust return/repair behavior.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- Chapter 2 shows shared opportunistic framing and ease in collaborating on social manipulation.
- The bath scene adds mutual embarrassment/sexual-category evidence but does not establish a romantic relationship.
- Kazuma's previous ability to activate Megumin's competitive Explosion identity remains relevant; V03 adds that he can trust her outside the protected Explosion domain to act strategically.
- **Current model:** strong coordination, compatible mischief, ritualized embarrassment, growing bodily/age awareness; romance remains OPEN.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- Absence triggers sustained worry despite Kazuma's explicit denial of romance.
- Darkness's noble revelation creates a high-value acceptance event: the party insists she remains “Darkness,” reducing her visible anxiety.
- Kazuma deliberately exploits intimate knowledge of her erotic reward system to win the duel.
- Darkness's father and Balter both perceive unusual closeness; Vanir's supernatural reading confirms sexual/relational thoughts exist on both sides, but his teasing does not establish commitment.
- Kazuma explicitly instructs Darkness not to make unilateral self-sacrificial bargains again because everyone worried.
- During the Vanir crisis, Kazuma accepts responsibility for ordering Explosion on her; Darkness entrusts the decision to the party despite lethal risk.
- **Current model:** high trust, high irritation tolerance, strong vulnerability knowledge, robust protector/coordination bond, sexual/romantic possibility OPEN, no stable romance claim.

## Megumin ↔ Yunyun
- Rivalry is now clearly multi-functional: competition, teasing, status hierarchy, structured contact, and a channel for affection.
- Megumin can exploit Yunyun's embarrassment aggressively while privately monitoring her loneliness and intending to thank her.
- Yunyun aids the effort to clear Kazuma despite remaining outside the core party.
- **Current model:** adversarial affiliation. Do not collapse into either “enemies” or secretly gentle friendship.

## Darkness ↔ party
- Noble identity disclosure tests whether status changes peer treatment; explicit acceptance as “Darkness” visibly matters.
- Party worry during her absence and Kazuma's later boundary-setting establish that self-sacrifice affects others even if Darkness eroticizes risk.
- Her belonging need is now developmentally grounded in childhood loneliness and joy at first gaining adventurer companions.

## Aqua ↔ Wiz / Vanir
- Aqua's goddess hierarchy creates persistent friction with Wiz despite acknowledging/helping her in other contexts.
- Vanir's demon identity triggers immediate sacred hostility; their antagonism is structural, not merely personal insult.

## Party-level state through V03
- **STRENGTHEN:** affection is better predicted by costly behavior than gentler speech.
- **STRENGTHEN:** conflict/insult tolerance is itself evidence of relationship stability; decreasing verbal aggression is not required for increasing attachment.
- **NEW:** outside institutions repeatedly misread or simplify party dynamics, while insiders possess highly specific vulnerability/reward knowledge that improves coordination and cruelty simultaneously.

## V04 relationship update

### Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- Wealth produces a shared domestic-laziness equilibrium rather than reducing their conflict style.
- Kazuma uses Aqua's reward salience to get her out on the Lizard Runner quest; later uses her Blessing as a precise mechanical multiplier in the Hans source fight.
- Kazuma initially suppresses Hans information to preserve vacation because the Axis cult is abstractly low-value to him. Aqua's concrete distress over her followers changes his willingness to engage. This is strong evidence that **relationship-conditioned stakes matter more than shared ideology**.
- Aqua resurrects Kazuma without hesitation and later accepts extraordinary pain to protect her followers while Kazuma/Wiz try to support her. Their speech remains hostile, but practical interdependence deepens.
- Kazuma's disbelief in Aqua's goddess identity remains a painful relationship asymmetry: he knows her enormous power but still treats the literal identity claim as implausible.

### Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- Megumin's `ちゅんちゅん丸` naming is an invasive prank/intimacy act; Kazuma's threat of later Steal/payback remains inside a recoverable conflict script.
- After Kazuma's death, Megumin's voice/behavior softens, showing that prank aggression does not predict indifference to serious harm.
- Megumin can detect that Kazuma's unnaturally gentle response is socially more threatening than ordinary retaliation; she has a detailed model of his normal grievance behavior.
- During Hans, Kazuma can simply tell her that finishing the boss is her job; she accepts the identity-congruent role and executes. Their coordination increasingly depends on **mutual knowledge of pride/reward structures**, not obedience.

### Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- Tourism/mixed-bath material preserves sexualized teasing and punishment, but the major V04 development is noble identity.
- Kazuma uses Dustiness authority instrumentally when institutions block action, even though Darkness dislikes it.
- When Darkness worries that noble status changes how people treat her, Kazuma reassures her by continuing to frame her as the party's `ダクネス` / reliable Crusader rather than `Lalatina` the noble. Ordinary disrespect functions as belonging reassurance.
- Kazuma sacrifices Darkness's family souvenirs to bait Hans but explicitly promises to help replace them, recognizing the item's relational value rather than dismissing it.
- Darkness physically covers the party from Hans fragments after Kazuma assigns the role; this is high-trust lethal-risk coordination.

### Aqua ↔ Megumin
- Megumin volunteers to accompany Aqua because she predicts Aqua will cause trouble; this is caretaking expressed as anticipation of dysfunction rather than gentleness.
- Both continue mutual insult, yet Megumin is unusually sympathetic after Aqua's hot-spring disaster and later distress.

### Aqua ↔ Darkness
- Darkness is the first party member to yield when Aqua cries about poisoned springs and helps convert Aqua's concern into group action.
- Darkness's protector tendency and Aqua's sacred-duty tendency align in the Hans climax rather than competing.

### Megumin ↔ Darkness
- Their shared private assessment of Kazuma in the bath shows they can discuss him positively when they believe he is absent.
- Both preserve ordinary treatment after Darkness's noble identity becomes operationally relevant.

### Party ↔ Wiz
- Wiz is increasingly treated as a genuine ally rather than an undead exception: travel companion, combat specialist, and moral agent.
- Aqua's sacred power remains physiologically dangerous to Wiz, so affection does not remove structural incompatibility.
- The final Hans plan requires Kazuma to entrust mana and timing to Wiz while Aqua handles purification, a particularly strong cross-category cooperation case.

### Party ↔ place/home
- The Axel mansion now functions as a relationship container. V04 ends with all four immediately resuming tea, sofa, board-game, and argument routines, and Kazuma explicitly experiences the atmosphere as comforting.


# V05 Relationship Update

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
**Major state change: mutual romantic interest becomes textually supported, without formal couple status.**

Evidence cluster:
- daily Explosion evaluation/carry-home routine now functions as a stable shared ritual;
- quiet travel discussion confirms both value the current party life and imagine a comfortable future together with the group;
- Megumin explicitly thanks Kazuma for accepting/carrying/supporting her despite Explosion's burden;
- trust is calibrated rather than naive: she expects opportunism and can withdraw/repair trust after manipulation;
- Megumin uses relationship implication/status against peers and later expresses direct affection, then partially softens it when Kazuma reacts strongly;
- Megumin is willing to abandon Explosion to stop burdening Kazuma/party;
- Kazuma refuses that self-sacrifice by preserving Explosion in her build, choosing her identity over generic party optimization;
- post-return, they unusually give one another credit for the Sylvia victory.

**Model implication:** attraction is now a legitimate causal variable for jealousy, embarrassment, attention and prioritization. Do not simulate them as an established couple yet. Their ordinary interaction still includes insults, manipulation, retaliation and uncertainty.

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- **STRENGTHEN — sibling-like antagonism with serious safe-base override:** after the orc attack Aqua suppresses teasing and comforts Kazuma; he clings to her without status defense.
- **STRENGTHEN — synchronized domestic avoidance:** epilogue has Kazuma and Aqua immediately coordinate to expel a visitor they assume brings trouble.
- **PRESERVE — sacred/status friction:** Kazuma still discounts or needles Aqua in ordinary state; serious care does not produce generalized gentleness.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- **STRENGTHEN — protector reciprocity:** Darkness offers herself as hostage in Kazuma's place; Kazuma repeatedly relies on her as durable delay/shield.
- **STRENGTHEN — grievance/intimacy coexistence:** even while captured, Kazuma uses leverage to demand apology for ordinary-party mistreatment, showing safe retaliation survives serious attachment.
- **NEXT-TRANCHE PRESSURE:** royal invitation and Dustiness anxiety make V06 a direct test of whether Kazuma respects or exploits Darkness's status boundaries under institutional pressure.

## Megumin ↔ Yunyun
- **STRENGTHEN — rivalry as affiliation:** Megumin attacks Yunyun's Explosion critique, humiliates her socially, and keeps score aggressively while also worrying about her, crying at her village acceptance, and recognizing her importance.
- **REVISE — Yunyun belonging:** Yunyun now receives explicit village admiration after performing the cultural heroic role she usually rejects; acceptance is pleasurable in substance but embarrassing in form.
- **Model implication:** hostility cues between these two cannot be mapped directly to relational distance. Competition is one of the relationship's principal maintenance mechanisms.

## Megumin ↔ family
- Strong concern for Komekko motivates return even while Megumin masks broader worry.
- Poverty/history and Komekko's behavior give Megumin's family attachment material stakes.
- Mother actively treats Kazuma as a desirable match after learning his wealth; this external pressure is not equivalent to Megumin's own consent or relationship state.

## Aqua ↔ party under serious fear
V05 supplies unusually clean evidence that Aqua can become gentle/supportive when a companion is genuinely frightened. Treat this as a seriousness-conditioned script rather than a new permanent tone.

## Darkness ↔ party / noble identity
V05 continues the split between peer-adventurer belonging and noble-role exposure. Darkness is comfortable using her body/risk tolerance to protect companions but much less comfortable with public self-display tied to Dustiness identity. The royal invitation at the epilogue directly threatens to pull the party into her aristocratic role world.

## Ensemble relationship state through V05
The party increasingly functions as **reciprocal cleanup infrastructure**:
- Kazuma repeatedly constrains/routs others' liabilities;
- Megumin explicitly says she will clean up Kazuma's mistake because he usually cleans up theirs;
- Darkness repeatedly takes danger onto herself;
- Aqua supplies rescue/healing/comfort despite insults.

This supports a mature formulation of affection without idealization:

> They do not become nicer as attachment deepens; they become more willing to absorb one another's costs, preserve one another's identities, and return after conflict.


# V06 Update

## Kazuma ↔ Iris
- New high-density relationship state. Iris values Kazuma specifically because he refuses normal court deference, plays to win, teaches her dubious ordinary-life behavior, and gives access to a noisy peer-like world.
- Kazuma initially values castle luxury/status and the novelty of being called older brother, but the relationship becomes independently causal: he continues the castle heist after an easy retreat because Iris's safety and their temporary sibling-like bond matter.
- Iris's lived V06 relation is best modeled as younger-sister/older-brother substitute plus admiration. Her epilogue privately connects the stolen ring to the kingdom's hero-marriage tradition, but this is symbolic/future-oriented and should not overwrite present age-level behavior.
- Address progression is analytically important: formal royal distance → `お兄様` in sustained relationship → private `お兄ちゃん` in the epilogue.

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- V06 strongly deepens mutual protective and manipulative knowledge.
- Darkness risks royal punishment and physical injury to defend Kazuma from unfair contempt; Kazuma later tries to protect her/Chris from aristocratic fallout by keeping Chris's thief identity away from noble channels.
- Kazuma explicitly sabotages Darkness's suitor market from mixed territorial irritation despite denying a desire to date her. This is attachment/possessiveness evidence, not proof of committed romance.
- Darkness knows Kazuma's sexual escalation boundary well enough to call his bluff during the castle retrieval.
- Their ordinary aggression persists through very high trust and costly defense.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- V05 mutual romantic interest remains active but unsettled.
- V06 adds jealousy/territorial friction around Iris's younger-sister position and continued Explosion-trip intimacy.
- Megumin defends Kazuma's reputation emotionally but can restrain retaliation when Darkness would pay the social cost.
- She does not recognize masked-thief Kazuma and admires the persona as a heroic oddball; this provides a useful split between her model of Kazuma and attraction to a decontextualized competence display.

## Kazuma ↔ Aqua
- V06 again resembles sibling-like antagonism rather than romance.
- Aqua is capable of unusually simple care when Kazuma is genuinely distressed; Kazuma explicitly experiences her as reliable in that state.
- They still immediately return to alcohol/food/property conflict. Care does not soften baseline speech.
- Mitsurugi independently reads them as close despite witnessing immediate disagreement, reinforcing familiarity rather than harmony as the relational signal.

## Kazuma ↔ Chris / Eris
- Chris and Kazuma form a highly effective temporary infiltration dyad: teasing/blame transfer coexist with operational trust.
- Kazuma lets Chris escape at Alderp's estate partly because she is Darkness's friend and because exposure would create noble consequences.
- Eris entrusts artifact recovery without public reward; Kazuma ultimately accepts the responsibility when the concrete risk becomes clear.

## Megumin ↔ Iris
- Rapid competitive-affiliative bond: Megumin escorts Iris, teaches informal/rough interaction, later writes to her, and frames the farewell conflict partly as a "younger-sister slot" contest.
- The structure resembles her Yunyun style: rivalry and care need not be opposites.

## Darkness ↔ Iris
- Darkness combines noble obedience with an older-sister/fairness function strong enough to override deference when Iris mistreats Kazuma.
- Iris trusts Darkness enough that the slap ultimately becomes corrective rather than relationship-ending.
- Darkness's willingness to cross rank for moral instruction is a high-weight relationship boundary.

## Party membership
- Claire's proposed party reorganization is the first direct separation stress test.
- Kazuma's reaction strongly suggests that "replace these problem companions" is grievance language rather than a stable end-state preference.
- The party's relational value is now visible not only in costly rescue but in **resistance to efficient separation**.

# V07 Update

## Kazuma ↔ Darkness
- **Highest-density dyadic update so far.** V07 makes permanent separation credible, then shows both sides behaving against that outcome while refusing simple relational labels.
- Darkness values Kazuma's continued nondeferential treatment after noble-status revelation; equal-peer interaction is part of the attachment itself.
- Kazuma's bedroom intrusion produces real mutual sexual tension, but future relational consequences still constrain behavior; sexual salience is strong without being globally decisive.
- Kazuma researches Alderp, keeps generating sellable inventions, experiences the empty mansion as lonely, fails to emotionally substitute Dust as frontliner, and liquidates his future passive-income/IP base to stop the marriage.
- Darkness's rescue motive toward Kazuma remains strong: she voluntarily enters the Hydra to recover his corpse and later tries to protect him/the party from her financial burden by leaving.
- **Core mismatch:** Darkness interprets self-removal as protection; Kazuma interprets permanent removal as an unacceptable loss to be solved.
- Post-rescue `お帰り` / `ただいま` is the clearest home/belonging marker yet.
- The immediate return to sexual accusations, retaliation and `バツネス` teasing strongly preserves **affection without idealization**.

## Aqua ↔ Darkness
- Aqua repeatedly attempts to retrieve Darkness through spectacular public performances and then uses her legitimate priestly role to sabotage the wedding ceremony from inside.
- She does not require polished emotional language to behave as though Darkness's departure is unacceptable.

## Megumin ↔ Darkness
- Megumin independently rejects the resignation, threatens noble property, recruits Yunyun, and enters the wedding with charged Explosion as deterrence.
- Her embarrassed `仲間でしょうが` formulation continues the pattern that attachment is easier for her to enact than to state plainly.

## Kazuma ↔ Megumin
- Megumin explicitly pressures Kazuma toward the version of himself she values: the person who complains, says `しょうがない`, then finds a solution. This is both relational expectation and behavioral modeling.
- V05 mutual-interest evidence remains active, but V07 also adds jealousy/friction around the Kazuma-Darkness bedroom incident. Do not convert this into settled couple exclusivity.

## Megumin ↔ Yunyun
- Megumin knows that `親友` is powerful enough to mobilize Yunyun into dangerous aid and uses it deliberately.
- Yunyun's joy is genuine; Megumin immediately returns to abrasive asymmetry afterward. Rival/friend attachment remains stable under teasing.

## Party ↔ Axel community
- The party's social network is now relationship-relevant infrastructure. Adventurers accept Kazuma's Hydra coordination, refuse Alderp's monetary recruitment, and then obstruct his guards.
- Darkness's conclusion that she likes the people of the town is reciprocally grounded rather than sentimental projection.

## Chris/Eris ↔ Kazuma
- Kazuma's long-running interactions with Chris provide enough behavioral data to identify her as Eris when combined with goddess-side address habits and appearance.
- He uses a low-cost verbal trap rather than confrontation. Their `助手君` / `お頭` partnership now has a divine identity layer that must be tracked prospectively from V08 onward.

## Darkness ↔ Dustiness father
- Father repeatedly prioritizes Darkness's chosen life over noble-marriage convenience and ultimately asks whether her current life is enjoyable enough to abandon everything for it.
- Darkness answers that it is enjoyable enough to throw everything away to protect her companions. Family duty and party identity are therefore not simple adversaries; the father validates the party path.
