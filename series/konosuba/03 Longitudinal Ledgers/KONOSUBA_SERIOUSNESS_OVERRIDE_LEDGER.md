# V05 Seriousness-Override Update

## Satou Kazuma
- **Acute fear override:** female-orc attack strips away status defense; he openly seeks help and accepts Aqua's comfort.
- **Concrete harm/responsibility override:** once his facility decision visibly contributes to village destruction, rationalization weakens and he joins repair/combat.
- **Relationship identity override:** practical/safety preference for advanced magic is superseded by desire to preserve Megumin's chosen identity.
- **Counter-pattern:** romantic/grievance frustration around Sylvia can temporarily make him *less* cautious and more direct, demonstrating seriousness is not always morally improving.

## Aqua
- **Companion-trauma override:** Kazuma's genuine distress suppresses mockery and activates nurturing behavior.
- **Sacred cue override:** demon/sub-demon classification rapidly activates anti-evil action.
- **Boundary:** novelty capture (Game Girl) can coexist with a serious mission, so "serious Aqua" is not a fully stable mode.

## Megumin
- **Family/relationship concern override:** concern for Komekko/Yunyun defeats initial dismissiveness about returning home.
- **Reputation override:** willingly exposes Explosion-only identity to village ridicule to repair Kazuma's mistake and protect the village.
- **Identity override:** accumulated guilt about being a burden becomes strong enough that she is prepared to give up Explosion specialization itself.
- **Restoration condition:** trusted companion acceptance of her chosen identity reverses the self-sacrificial abandonment and produces visible relief/joy.

## Darkness
- **Companion-capture override:** offers herself as substitute hostage for Kazuma.
- **Protector mode:** delays Sylvia and accepts exposed roles without needing an erotic incentive to explain the action.
- **Status vulnerability:** noble/name self-exposure produces genuine anxiety/embarrassment distinct from desired pain.

## Yunyun
- **Belonging/duty override:** serious communal need suppresses ordinary embarrassment enough for her to perform the full Crimson Demon heroic register and act as bait.
- **Voice implication:** seriousness can increase theatricality where role enactment is itself the seriousness behavior.


# V06 Update

## SO-V06-01 — Megumin protects Darkness's social stakes
- **Trigger:** Kazuma is insulted at the royal dinner.
- **Default comic impulse:** retaliate/escalate.
- **Override:** explicit awareness that a scene would hurt Darkness.
- **Behavior:** suppresses retaliation.
- **Model effect:** temper is relationship-conditioned, not uncontrollable.

## SO-V06-02 — Darkness protects Kazuma against royal contempt
- **Trigger:** Iris calls Kazuma weak/liar and refuses retraction.
- **Override hierarchy:** fairness/protector duty > royal deference > self-protection.
- **Behavior:** slaps Iris, takes Claire's blade, calmly explains why Kazuma owes no proof.
- **Model effect:** seriousness can activate noble guardian role and extend protection to dignity/social harm.

## SO-V06-03 — Kazuma rejects the easy heist exit
- **Trigger:** castle infiltration compromised; Chris recommends retreat.
- **Competing baseline:** comfort, safety, wealthy retirement.
- **Override:** memories of Iris + entrusted artifact danger.
- **Behavior:** explicitly decides to start trying "now" and continues.
- **Model effect:** strongest K02 evidence through V06.

## SO-V06-04 — Aqua comforts rather than teases
- **Trigger:** Kazuma is genuinely socially devastated after body-swap misconduct.
- **Default script:** insult/retaliation.
- **Behavior:** tells him to stay quiet and offers food.
- **Model effect:** acute distress is a reliable cue for care-state activation.

## SO-V06-05 — Party-separation threat
- **Trigger:** Claire proposes Aqua/Megumin/Darkness join Mitsurugi and Kazuma retire.
- **Default rhetoric:** Kazuma often claims he wants a normal party.
- **Behavior/affect:** exclusion anxiety, defense of his unique follow-up role.
- **Model effect:** grievance fantasy is not equivalent to desired separation.

## SO-V06-06 — Iris constrains her own farewell request
- **Trigger:** imminent separation from Kazuma after an unusually free week.
- **Potential desire:** ask him to remain.
- **Behavior:** requests only a future rematch/game.
- **Model effect:** royal self-regulation remains active even under strong attachment; private epilogue carries more of the unspoken future wish.

# V07 Update

## SO-V07-01 — Darkness self-sacrificial noble/protector mode
- **Trigger:** family/public debt and father's apparent terminal illness.
- **Comic-default suppression:** ordinary masochistic boasting becomes secondary to controlled planning, resignation and responsibility language.
- **Authoritative role:** Dustiness heir / protector / burden absorber.
- **Important failure:** seriousness improves commitment but not procedural judgment; she hides information and tries to erase herself from the party.

## SO-V07-02 — Darkness belonging vulnerability
- **Trigger:** uncertainty whether party will accept her back.
- **Voice:** unusually deferential `もう一度、私を仲間にしてもらえますか`.
- **Resolution:** `お帰り` / `ただいま` restores ordinary peer register almost immediately.

## SO-V07-03 — Kazuma rescue commitment
- **Trigger:** Darkness marriage becomes concretely solvable after Vanir supplies information/liquidity.
- **Override:** comfort/wealth preservation loses to relational duty; all IP/reward capital is sold.
- **Residual personality:** complaint, denial, sexual framing and cowardly reflexes remain. Seriousness changes priority, not personality deletion.

## SO-V07-04 — Aqua relationship-owned performance
- **Trigger:** Darkness is inaccessible inside noble estate.
- **Behavior:** repeated spectacular public magic designed to lure her out; refuses tips.
- **Model effect:** strong relationship goal can discipline Aqua's performance salience rather than being displaced by it.

## SO-V07-05 — Aqua sacred override
- **Trigger:** Dustiness father under demonic curse.
- **Behavior:** immediate correct sacred spell; no extended uncertainty.
- **Result:** actual cure destroys the solemn deathbed framing.

## SO-V07-06 — Megumin "bad wizard" rescue role
- **Trigger:** companion forcibly leaving through marriage.
- **Behavior:** heightened theatrical entrance, charged Explosion deterrence, explicit `仲間` claim.
- **Model effect:** confirms seriousness -> authoritative role -> role-consistent register, including increased theatricality.

# V08 Update

## Kazuma
- Megumin confession briefly suppresses defensive joking enough for an attempted reciprocal answer.
- Eris crowd danger activates direct protective action and willingness to remain behind.
- Chris/Eris secret-duty request overrides immediate retirement preference.
- Once seriousness passes, status/profit/sexual reward re-enter quickly; override is state-bound rather than moral transformation.

## Aqua
- Learned overconfidence risk is salient during insect clearing, producing quiet support behavior.
- A different serious identity threat - Eris/Axis status competition - later activates defensive escalation rather than caution.
- This is strong evidence that seriousness does not have one direction; it activates the currently authoritative motive/role.

## Megumin
- Romantic seriousness produces direct liking language.
- Masked-thief danger activates hero-support/Explosion behavior.
- Fireworks defense activates adventurer identity strongly enough to override planned romantic pacing.

## Darkness
- Private gratitude activates formal noble/relational speech and controlled vulnerability.
- Acting-lord role activates governance/fairness competence.
- Public sexualized identity exposure activates anger, not masochistic compliance.
- Fairness toward Megumin inhibits further private escalation with Kazuma.

## Chris / Eris
- Hidden-work discussion activates divine-duty seriousness.
- Chris-mode friendship restores casual teasing immediately afterward.
- Child gratitude produces private emotional release without public goddess performance.

## General V08 rule
V08 strongly confirms: `serious trigger -> authoritative role/identity -> relationship/audience -> register/behavior`. The output may be formal, plain, theatrical, aggressive, or quietly supportive depending on the role.
