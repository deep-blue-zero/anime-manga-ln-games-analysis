## Ensemble competence

V01's strongest team state is complementary:
- Kazuma supplies coordination/weak-tool exploitation;
- Aqua supplies sacred/support/water power;
- Megumin supplies area destruction;
- Darkness supplies defense/time.

The party is strongest when external structure constrains each member to the domain where they are exceptional and prevents their preferred failure mode from controlling the whole encounter.
