## 2. Volume-level thesis

V08 is superficially an "event/festival" volume, but analytically it is a dense test of **reward salience, incentive design, identity performance, relationship ambiguity, and the difference between learning a failure class and acquiring global self-control**.

The strongest volume-level result is Aqua. V08 gives a prospective matched contrast inside one book:

1. before the insect operation she explicitly remembers that getting carried away causes disasters and spends the battle quietly healing and buffing;
2. once the Axis side begins winning the festival and she receives sustained worship, recognition becomes the active reward and she reverts to escalating demands, zero-sum competition with Eris, and finally a pyramid-style scheme whose moral defect she rationalizes through a legal loophole.

So V08 does **not** say "Aqua learned" or "Aqua did not learn." It says:

> **Aqua can learn a recognizable risk pattern and regulate behavior when that lesson is salient, while a different high-reward state can still re-weight the objective function enough to bypass that learned caution.**

Kazuma is tested at a different scale. His K01 competence is no longer confined to combat or infiltration. He converts religious rivalry into festival funding and merchant profit, then later converts a beauty contest into both an Eris-side prestige intervention and a behavioral trap for Aigis. His distinctive capability is increasingly best described as **incentive and state architecture** rather than merely clever tactics.

The relationship layer also becomes irreducibly plural. Megumin directly says she likes Kazuma; Kazuma attempts to answer but Explosion interrupts the reply. Darkness later gives a highly serious account of the mansion/party as the happiest year of her life, moves toward physical intimacy, then stops after Kazuma invokes Megumin and after recognizing that she chose a moment when Megumin was detained. Chris/Eris becomes a third kind of intimacy: secret-sharing, admiration, teasing, work partnership, and a deliberately nondeferential friendship with a goddess. None of these relations should be collapsed into generic "harem progress."

Finally, V08 gives the best within-volume support yet for the revised Japanese-voice rule: serious stakes align speech with the **authoritative role**, and different roles push speech in different directions rather than toward one universal sober register.

## 3. Narrative architecture

### 3.1 Prologue - festival as displaced Japanese adolescence

The prologue frames the festival through Kazuma's surprise that someone who barely participated in school cultural festivals in Japan is now helping run a major event in another world. This motif returns repeatedly. His advisor enthusiasm is not only profit seeking. The festival becomes an opportunity to enact a social-seasonal experience he largely missed in his old life.

The prologue also establishes that even Axis followers can become unusually restrained when the event matters to them. This is an early warning against treating factional behavior as fixed incompetence rather than state-dependent priority weighting.

### 3.2 Chapter 1 - Chris/Eris identity and relationship-mode disclosure

Kazuma formally deduces that Chris and Eris are the same person by combining small recurring cues rather than receiving a plot dump: Chris alone uses `Aqua-san`, Eris/Chris share the cheek-scratching habit, and prior remarks about a demanding senior retrospectively match Aqua-induced resurrection cleanup.

Once exposed, Eris explicitly asks Kazuma not to treat the Chris form with stiff divine deference. She wants the familiar `お頭` / `助手君` relationship and ordinary casual speech to continue. This matters because it establishes that "Chris" is not simply a disguise hiding the real Eris. It is a **chosen social operating mode** through which Eris can have friendship, adventure, and ordinary reciprocity.

Her earthly work has at least two motives:

- recover divine artifacts whose original holders have died;
- answer Darkness's prayer for companions by becoming her friend.

This retroactively strengthens the interpretation of Chris as a relational act of divine care rather than a mere operational alias.

### 3.3 Chapter 1 - Megumin's confession and interrupted reciprocity

Megumin thanks Kazuma for rescuing Darkness and says directly:

> `やっぱり私は、何だかんだと文句を言いながらも最後には皆を助けてくれる、そんなあなたが好きみたいです`

The evidentiary point is not only the word `like`. Kazuma asks whether he should take it as a real confession. Megumin knowingly leaves the interpretation in his hands rather than retracting it as a joke. Kazuma then tries to answer affirmatively enough that the sequence would have advanced the relationship, but Megumin fires Explosion before hearing him.

Kazuma's immediate post-Explosion denial is therefore poor evidence that he lacks interest. The internal sequence before the interruption shows fear of rejection, deliberate preparation to reciprocate, and excitement at the possibility of becoming romantically successful. The denial afterward is a status-defense reaction to a failed vulnerable moment.

### 3.4 Chapters 1-2 - joint festival as incentive engineering

Aqua wants to replace the annual Eris festival with an Aqua festival because she resents that Eris is celebrated while she is not. Kazuma does not accept the replacement proposal. Instead, he identifies a system with several exploitable incentives:

- Axis followers want recognition and a festival;
- Eris followers will respond competitively rather than yield prestige;
- merchants profit from a larger event;
- both churches can be made to contribute funding;
- Kazuma can be paid as advisor.

The resulting plan is not "talk both sides into cooperation." It is nearly the opposite: **design bounded competition so that rivalry produces a larger shared public good**.

That is high-value K01 evidence because it generalizes his systems reasoning into political-economic organization. The same cognition that turns a monster into a sequence of state transitions can turn two hostile religious communities into a self-funding event system.

Kazuma later tells Chris a selectively flattering explanation centered on helping frightened townspeople forget the Demon King's threat, while omitting the advisor fee and the explicit merchant-side profit logic. The altruistic component is not false; the narration and social presentation are **causally selective**.

### 3.5 Chapter 3 - the insect operation as a controlled competence/regulation test

The pre-festival insect-clearing operation is one of V08's highest-value scenes because roles are externally structured by the guild.

Aqua explicitly says:

> `賢い私は学習したの。調子に乗るとロクな事にはならないんだって`

She then behaves accordingly. She does not chase spectacle. She quietly heals injured adventurers and distributes support magic while others attract and kill targets. Kazuma is startled precisely because this is a clean departure from the expected overdeployment pattern.

This is direct prospective support for the **learning/anticipation branch of MG02-A03**. Aqua can remember a generalized causal lesson and modify behavior before disaster occurs.

Darkness simultaneously demonstrates D03. Multiple monster-attraction potions make her an exceptional anchor: she takes the most attacks and is genuinely useful. Her later ant episode supplies a crucial D02 boundary. Ants inside armor begin as aversive itching/panic; after sustained exposure she reappraises the experience into erotic reward. The same physical stimulus changes valence through meaning and attention.

Megumin supplies the inverse routing lesson. Kazuma explicitly tells her that Explosion is unsuitable in the forest because the environment cannot safely absorb it. She casts overhead anyway, knocks down allies, kills nearby insects, and provokes a much larger wave. This is not evidence against M03; the M03 trigger - safe target separation - is absent. It is unusually good **boundary evidence** showing why separation matters.

### 3.6 Chapters 3-4 - advisor competence, recognition escalation, and festival nostalgia

Kazuma becomes increasingly invested in festival planning. He proposes Japanese-derived features, negotiates with merchants and Darkness, solves implementation problems, and enjoys being treated as an expert advisor. His stated motives remain mixed:

- profit;
- sexualized/cosplay spectacle;
- nostalgia for Japanese festival culture;
- desire for public success;
- genuine town-wide enjoyment;
- pride in being the person who knows how to make the event work.

The merchant council's praise after sales roughly double is especially important for K03. Recognition does not create his engagement from nothing, but it **increases pursuit intensity**. He announces that he will get serious, continues expanding the project, accepts more social reward, drinks with admirers, and eventually becomes the widely recognized "black mastermind" behind the rivalry architecture.

V08 therefore supplies cleaner K03 support than V07, though the resulting overextension is social/institutional rather than lethal.

### 3.7 Chapter 4 - romance plans repeatedly lose to active identity/role

Megumin arranges what is plainly intended as a romantic festival/fireworks progression: Yunyun accompanies them early, but Megumin quietly tells Kazuma that they can return together after the fireworks.

Then the fireworks reveal themselves as an adventurer defense operation against seasonal monsters. Megumin immediately prioritizes participation and later attempts to fire Explosion inside town badly enough that police detain her.

This should not be simplified into "Megumin cares more about Explosion than Kazuma." Her confession remains active; she is disappointed the planned interaction fails and later seeks to repair it. The better model is:

> **romantic desire and Explosion/adventurer identity coexist, but an immediately activated role can dominate behavior even when it sabotages a desired relational outcome.**

### 3.8 Chapter 4 - Darkness's gratitude and the meaning of home

Darkness visits Kazuma privately and gives her most explicit account yet of what the party/home ecology means to her. She says that the year since meeting him has been the happiest and most eventful of her life and describes the mansion in explicitly domestic terms: `お前達と暮らすこの屋敷はとても居心地が良い`.

Her register then shifts from ordinary Darkness speech into a formal noble/relational expression of gratitude, ending with `心から、感謝します`.

This is strong J01 evidence because seriousness does not merely make her "less horny." It activates a **grateful noblewoman / emotionally exposed companion** role with its own lexical and politeness profile.

The scene approaches physical intimacy. Kazuma is attracted and would continue, but blurts Megumin's name. Darkness then explicitly recognizes that choosing a time when Megumin is detained would be unfair, limits the interaction to the previously promised Hydra reward kiss, and defers the V07 rescue gratitude to another time.

This supplies important relationship evidence about Darkness: desire is real, but so are fairness, embarrassment, and awareness of the emerging triangular structure.

### 3.9 Chapters 4-5 - Aigis, adaptive heist planning, and desire as an exploitable system

The first Aigis theft fails because the supposedly inert armor is sentient and loudly calls for help. Kazuma and Chris adapt on the second attempt with communications suppression and anti-magic countermeasures. When Aigis still proves immune to magic/skills and able to move independently, Kazuma eventually stops treating it as an object to steal and starts treating **Aigis's preferences** as the system to exploit.

The Miss Goddess Eris contest is therefore a multi-objective K01 solution:

- restore Eris-side festival prestige;
- gather attractive candidates likely to draw Aigis;
- capture Aigis when it appears;
- if necessary, use Eris herself as the terminal preference-match that makes compliance voluntary.

Aigis appears almost immediately, proving that the behavioral model is correctly targeted.

### 3.10 Chapter 5 - public Darkness is not a scalar humiliation machine

Darkness's participation in the contest supplies an excellent D02 contrast. She is nervous about public presentation as Lalatina, receives escalating sexualized heckling, and eventually reacts with genuine anger, attacking the crowd when the teasing becomes intolerable.

This should be read beside the insect scene, where an initially unpleasant bodily stimulus is eventually eroticized. The two scenes support the same general law:

> **For Darkness, valence depends on the power/meaning structure of the experience, not simply its objective unpleasantness or embarrassment level.**

Public identity exposure, coerced sexual spectacle, equal-peer teasing, combat pain, helplessness, and chosen erotic danger are psychologically different classes.

### 3.11 Chapter 5 - Eris recognition, duty, and the cost of being conscientious

Chris admits that she does not need public praise for the hidden work she performs, but Kazuma's knowledge makes her feel that some of the effort is rewarded: `キミのおかげでちょっとだけ報われるよ`.

Later Eris appears in her true form. The crowd immediately recognizes her after she gives the simple self-identification `名はエリスと申します`. The worship response validates what Aqua has wanted all volume, but Eris reacts differently: she is embarrassed, overwhelmed, and keeps accepting handshakes because she has difficulty refusing sincere believers.

This reveals a new Eris boundary. Her conscientiousness is a virtue but can become **over-compliance** under direct human need. Kazuma and Aigis have to create the exit structure for her.

The epilogue then gives Eris a much quieter reward. Two children explain that they want to thank the goddess for unseen work that keeps people safe. They later give Chris a flower without realizing who she is. Chris says her chosen name comes from that flower, whose language is `諦めない心`. The scene joins divine duty, ordinary friendship identity, and private recognition without requiring public deference.

### 3.12 Epilogue 2 - no clean moral convergence

After the festival, Kazuma gives his advisor earnings to Aqua because she had spent her Hydra money and savings on the church/festival; this is another example of abrasive language coexisting with material support.

Aqua immediately imagines an easy future in which Kazuma finances her life and she receives annual worship. Darkness, finally free of acting-lord work, wants to return to quests. Megumin sits beside Kazuma and ends the volume by inviting him to her room for an important private conversation.

The volume therefore does not close by stabilizing everyone into a better person. It closes by preserving the same ecology while making the underlying relationships and reward systems more legible.

## 4. Satou Kazuma - V08 reconstruction update

### 4.1 MG02-K01 is confirmed at institutional scale

V08 contains at least three independent K01 demonstrations:

1. religious rivalry becomes a self-funding joint-festival mechanism;
2. failed Aigis theft produces revised countermeasures rather than repetition;
3. Aigis's sexuality/preferences become the exploitable rule, leading to the beauty-contest capture and Eris compliance solution.

The important generalization is that Kazuma does not merely find loopholes in explicit game-like rules. He can model **people and institutions as incentive systems**.

### 4.2 MG02-K02 receives strong Chris/Eris evidence

Kazuma initially wants no part of another divine-artifact problem. Chris/Eris asks seriously; he agrees. During the contest crisis he stays behind to draw the angry crowd while Eris escapes inside Aigis, despite having no public reward attached to the risk.

The motive remains mixed - he also finds the festival fight exciting - but that does not reduce the entrusted-duty evidence. The scene specifically follows an established secret responsibility shared only with Eris/Chris.

### 4.3 MG02-K03 finally gets a cleaner noncombat replication

Advisor praise and visible commercial success increase Kazuma's engagement. He moves from "help make the festival happen" toward increasingly expansive programming, public recognition, profit seeking, sexual spectacle, and celebratory excess.

The consequence is not another death. It is institutional/social overextension: he becomes exposed as the person who deliberately designed the rivalry and is arrested after escalating the final contest into a riot.

Thus K03 should be **STRENGTHENED with a broader consequence class**: recognition-driven escalation can produce physical danger, reputational trouble, or scope creep depending on the domain.

### 4.4 K04 is not cleanly tested

V08 contains romantic rivalry and temporary absence, but no credible party transfer/departure/replacement event comparable to V06-V07. Do not count romance ambiguity as a K04 confirmation.

### 4.5 Kazuma is increasingly capable of sustained work when the work is self-authored

The volume complicates "Kazuma is lazy." He wakes late and fantasizes about permanent retirement, yet spends many days in meetings, logistics, event design, monster clearing and implementation because the festival lets him combine autonomy, recognized expertise, nostalgia, profit, creativity and social reward.

The better ordinary-life rule is:

> **Kazuma resists imposed low-reward labor far more than effort itself. Self-authored projects with immediate feedback can elicit sustained high effort.**

## 5. Aqua - V08 reconstruction update

### 5.1 MG02-A01 is confirmed by the guild insect operation

Aqua is placed in a clear support role and becomes an unusually reliable high-throughput healer/buffer. The guild supplies target structure and keeps her from having to invent the objective. Her raw class/domain competence again becomes obvious when the environment constrains deployment errors.

### 5.2 MG02-A03: learning is real and selective

The insect operation is the cleanest forward-looking evidence yet that Aqua can learn. She explicitly states the causal lesson before the battle and behaves differently because of it. This rejects any residual model in which her previous disasters generate no accessible learning at all.

But the later festival supplies the necessary boundary. The lesson does not become a global trait called "prudence." Once sustained worship and religious competition become salient, she begins treating praise itself as the goal. Her second formulation is revealing:

> `賢い私は学習したわ。ここにいると、皆が崇めてくれるって！`

She has learned **another** local causal fact - staying in the Axis church produces worship - and optimizes that reward.

This is not contradictory learning. It is a competition between learned models under different reward weightings.

### 5.3 MG02-A02 is confirmed very strongly

Aqua starts with an instrumental objective: create a festival for herself / give Emperor Zel a chance to see her admired. Once the Axis booths succeed, praise and outcompeting Eris become terminal rewards. She refuses to leave the church because she is being worshipped and pushes for future Aqua-only control.

The pyramid-style scheme is the end point of that salience capture. She knows the scheme's structure from Kazuma's old explanation and uses the absence of explicit law as a defense. When confronted, she does not deny causation. She argues that Eris's surprise appearance forced escalation, that she deserves her own celebration, and that the scheme is "not yet illegal."

This is almost exactly the **face-saving/status-defense branch of MG02-A03**. Because a legal/status-preserving defense remains available, she does not reach the V02 cemetery-style plain apology/remediation state.

### 5.4 A04 remains untested

V08 contains hard work and festival preparation, but no clean repetitive low-status maintenance task paired with an attractive maximal one-step substitute. Do not stretch the pyramid scheme into A04; its trigger is recognition competition, not tedious maintenance.

### 5.5 Aqua is capable of large voluntary expenditure for identity/community projects

She spends Hydra earnings and savings on festival/church goals, then uses Kazuma's later money on rebuilding and future festival infrastructure. This is financially imprudent, but not equivalent to pure personal consumption. Religious-group identity and celebration are real terminal goods for her.

## 6. Megumin - V08 reconstruction update

### 6.1 Romantic interest is now direct textual evidence

V08 moves the Kazuma/Megumin relationship beyond inference. Megumin explicitly says she likes the kind of person Kazuma is when he complains but eventually helps everyone. The object of attraction is therefore not generic coolness; it is a **behavioral pattern she has observed longitudinally**.

Kazuma's interrupted attempt to reciprocate and the epilogue invitation to her room make mutual romantic interest a high-confidence active variable. It still does not establish formal couple status.

### 6.2 M01 gets partial, not exact, support

During the insect operation Kazuma tells Megumin that Explosion has no useful role in the forest. Megumin refuses the exclusion and casts anyway. This is identity-relevant, but the frozen M01 trigger specifies an outsider/rival challenge to Explosion status or relational guilt that might invert specialization. Neither branch is cleanly instantiated.

Outcome: **PARTIAL SUPPORT**, not a full matched confirmation.

### 6.3 M03 is not tested; its boundary is

The forest scene lacks safe target separation and Explosion causes widespread knockdown plus a larger insect wave. That is strongly consistent with why M03 was written, but the prediction is conditional. Because its trigger is absent, V08 should record **NOT_TESTED with inverse-condition boundary support**, not a confirmation.

### 6.4 M02 and M04 remain untested

No scene cleanly asks Megumin to suppress retaliation because it would materially harm a companion, and no best-use Explosion choice requires directly endangering a valued companion. Her helping the masked thieves does not qualify.

### 6.5 Hero worship becomes prosocial action

Megumin's admiration for the masked thieves is not passive fandom. Believing they are in trouble, she creates a massive Explosion diversion to aid their escape without knowing they are Kazuma and Chris. This is a useful example of a theatrical identity narrative mobilizing real prosocial risk.

## 7. Darkness / Dustiness Ford Lalatina - V08 reconstruction update

### 7.1 D03 is confirmed again under externally structured defense

The insect operation is almost an ideal Darkness task. She takes extra attraction potions, stands where attacks will converge, absorbs more hits than ordinary frontliners and materially protects the formation. Her offensive accuracy is irrelevant.

This further supports the context rule: **the more a task resembles aggro control, endurance, interposition or resistance, the less useful the franchise label "ineffective Crusader" becomes.**

### 7.2 D02 receives unusually strong meaning-sensitive evidence

V08 places multiple unpleasant experiences side by side:

- ants in armor: initially genuine distress, later erotic reappraisal;
- public Lalatina identification: acute embarrassment;
- escalating sexualized stage demands: anger and physical retaliation rather than erotic compliance;
- intimate private gratitude: vulnerability with voluntary approach and control.

The clean result is not "Darkness likes some humiliation." It is that **agency, privacy, role, audience, power relation and symbolic meaning** determine whether an experience is rewarding or aversive.

### 7.3 D01 extends into governance/accountability

As acting lord, Darkness becomes the institutional brake on both Kazuma and Aqua. She handles permits, public-order problems, arrests, the pyramid-scheme aftermath and restitution to victims. This is protector/fairness duty expressed through civic administration rather than bodily interposition.

### 7.4 D04 is not newly tested

Her father recovers and the acting-lord burden ends. There is no new genuine family-versus-party incompatibility. V08 instead shows the **post-crisis integration state**: she can perform noble responsibility temporarily and then return to quest/peer identity without abandoning either.

### 7.5 Home is explicitly family-coded

Darkness's private gratitude goes beyond "I like adventuring." She links the mansion's comfort to an imagined experience of living with the mother she never knew. This makes the party/home variable emotionally deeper than a preferred peer group: it partly supplies a missing family-like domestic experience while retaining equal-peer treatment.

## 8. Chris / Eris - secondary reconstruction expansion

V08 materially upgrades Chris/Eris as a secondary reconstruction candidate.

### 8.1 The two modes are continuous, not fake/real

Eris can move between divine formality and Chris's thief/friend register, but both modes contain the same identifiable habits, duty orientation, embarrassment and responsiveness. She herself asks Kazuma to preserve Chris-mode familiarity.

Model implication:

`role + embodiment + audience -> register/persona weighting`, not `true personality vs disguise`.

### 8.2 Duty and low self-promotion coexist with a real need to be seen

Eris says she does not perform hidden anti-Demon-King work for praise, yet being known by Kazuma makes the work feel rewarded. The child-flower epilogue makes this even clearer: she is moved by gratitude precisely because it arrives without status demand or public spectacle.

This is different from Aqua's recognition hunger. Eris does not seek the stage, but **recognition still has emotional value**.

### 8.3 Conscientiousness creates an over-compliance failure mode

When worshippers ask for handshakes, Eris keeps obliging even as the crowd becomes dangerous. Kazuma explicitly notices that she has difficulty refusing. This is a new potential decision-error class:

`legitimate need/request -> duty to respond -> insufficient refusal threshold -> overload / need for external exit structure`.

### 8.4 Chris's name is an identity statement

The epilogue reveals that Chris takes her name from a flower associated with `諦めない心`. This is not proof of a total self-concept, but it coheres with the repeated pattern of lonely, uncredited persistence in artifact recovery and human aid.

## 9. Relationship reconstruction

### 9.1 Kazuma <-> Megumin

Current state after V08:

- mutual attraction: **HIGH confidence**;
- explicit Megumin romantic interest: direct textual evidence;
- Kazuma intent to reciprocate: direct internal evidence, interrupted before completion;
- formal couple status: **not established**;
- practical relationship: still ordinary party membership, teasing, dependence and Explosion conflict.

The important causal feature is that Megumin is attracted to a repeated **care pattern** in Kazuma, while Kazuma is simultaneously attracted to her appearance, intimacy, exclusivity possibility, shared history and increasing willingness to approach him directly.

### 9.2 Kazuma <-> Darkness

V08 confirms real two-way sexual/romantic possibility but with more explicit moral friction than a pure harem reading suggests. Darkness chooses a private moment, nearly advances, then acknowledges that exploiting Megumin's detention was unfair. Kazuma wants the interaction but is aware that proceeding immediately after the Megumin festival scene would make his own conduct difficult to justify.

This is a genuine relationship branch, not merely Darkness's masochistic gag system.

### 9.3 Kazuma <-> Chris/Eris

This relationship is structurally different from both romantic branches:

- shared divine/artifact secrets;
- joint criminal/heroic operations;
- mutual teasing;
- Kazuma's explicit admiration;
- Eris's gratitude for being seen;
- deliberately preserved nondeference.

Kazuma can flirt because Chris-mode friendship makes an otherwise unreachable goddess socially approachable. His attraction is real, but the strongest current evidence is **trusted secret-partner friendship with admiration**.

### 9.4 Aqua <-> Eris/Chris

V08 makes the senior/junior goddess relation visible as asymmetrical labor. Aqua assumes Eris will absorb burdens; Eris admits that requests from her senior are difficult to refuse. Aqua's jealousy is partly status competition and partly familiarity so strong that she treats Eris's support as available infrastructure.

This relation deserves longitudinal tracking because it may explain both Eris workload and Aqua's expectations.

## 10. Decision/error analysis

- revises Aigis plan after failure;
- recognizes preference-based solution when force/stealth fail;

- sexual reward can distort implementation priorities;
- selective self-presentation to Chris hides advisor/profit motives;

**Strengths**

**Strengths**

- can plan romantic/social encounters;
- acts on admiration/loyalty even when identity of beneficiary is unknown;

**Errors / distortions**
- exclusion of Explosion from a tactical plan can trigger unauthorized casting;
- active Explosion/adventurer role can override desired romantic timing;

### Darkness

**Strengths**

**Strengths**

## 13. Japanese voice and register

## 14. Kazuma narrator audit

### 14.1 Festival altruism is selectively complete

Kazuma tells Chris that the joint festival is about giving frightened townspeople relief from Demon-King anxiety. That motive is plausible and becomes genuine as he invests in the event. But he explicitly omits advisor compensation and earlier profit/rivalry engineering.

This is classic current-model Kazuma narration/social presentation: **often factually true components arranged into a more respectable causal story**.

### 14.2 Romance "routes" reveal self-awareness and self-serving dramatization

Kazuma recognizes the ethical/social problem of advancing with Darkness immediately after Megumin's near-date and explicit confession. At the same time, his internal "route" framing turns real people into dating-game structure because that schema helps him interpret an unfamiliar romantic situation.

The schema is not delusion. It is a **cognitive compression tool** that can oversimplify other people's agency.

### 14.3 Chris/Eris admiration is unusually explicit and therefore high-confidence

Kazuma calls Chris/Eris the only person he can straightforwardly respect, emphasizing her hidden labor, responsibility and shared secrets. Because this appraisal persists alongside sexual joking and because his later behavior protects her without public reward, it deserves high evidentiary weight.

### 14.4 "Lazy" self-description needs context correction

V08 contains many days of real labor. Kazuma still genuinely prefers comfort, but his willingness to work rises sharply when autonomy, expertise, reward feedback and social ownership are high. Narrator labels like NEET/lazy remain real history and preference signals, not complete behavioral laws.
