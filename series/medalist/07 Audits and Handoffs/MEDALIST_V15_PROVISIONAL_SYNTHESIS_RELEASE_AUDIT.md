# Provisional synthesis release audit at the V15 boundary

This audit closes the synthesis phase that the [readiness and role-gap audit](MEDALIST_V15_SYNTHESIS_READINESS_AND_ROLE_GAP_AUDIT.md) opened. It lists what was released, says how each artifact was produced and checked, records the contradictions found between them and against the ledgers, and states what the release does not certify. It is a control document.

The release is a checkpoint on an ongoing series. It is not a finished-work reconstruction and it is not a frozen release in the architecture's sense. Every artifact is `PROVISIONAL_THROUGH_VERIFIED_BOUNDARY` and reopens when a claim it depends on changes.

## How the artifacts were produced

The integrated synthesis, the readiness audit and this audit were written by the integrator. The other seven were drafted by delegated synthesis agents, each given one assignment and a common brief. The brief confined them to the frozen readings, the two fanbook audits and the seven ledgers; forbade any source file, image or web lookup; fixed the boundary and the current claim versions; listed the corrections the corpus had already made; and required dense citation, counterevidence, a claims table and stated revision dependencies. No agent saw a page of the manga. Their evidence is this project's recorded descriptions, and each study says so.

The architecture prefers fresh synthesis against frozen inputs with a local integrator owning receipt and convergence. That is the route taken.

## Review performed

1. **Identifier check, mechanical, all eight.** Every observation, claim-version, state, transition, performance, institution, chronology, form, motif, revision, readiness, open-question and freeze identifier cited in each artifact was checked by script against the readings and ledgers. No unknown identifier remains. The check establishes that an identifier exists. It does not establish that the sentence beside it describes the row correctly.
2. **Hygiene scan, all eight.** No local path, no personal address, no reference to private working directories. Japanese appears only as single terms that the readings themselves discuss. No statement about any event after Score 61. No external rule document is cited; the skating study states that correspondence with the real sport was not checked.
3. **Corrections scan, all eight.** Each was searched for the superseded views listed in the readiness audit: none treats the Volume 13 declaration as a durable answer, none adopts the hypothesis about the heroine's formation, none merges the trainer's account with the heroine's own, none supplies a cause for the Volume 13 collapse or a motive for the champion-coach's departure, none calls the coach one of three brothers.
4. **Targeted reading by the integrator.** Read in full: the integrated synthesis; in the coach's monograph the thesis, the history table and the section on what his childhood explains; in the heroine's monograph the section on the two wishes; the opening third of the supporting study; outlines and authors' reports for the remainder. The seven drafts total about 313 KB and were not all read line by line by the integrator. That limit is a debt of this release and is stated in the readiness row.
5. **Authors' own uncertainty reports.** Each drafter returned a list of conclusions held with reservation. They are carried in the artifacts' claims tables. The ones that bear on other artifacts are entered below.

## What this release does not certify

- That any conclusion will survive the final's result. Every study ends on an open state.
- That the seven delegated drafts are free of local misdescription. The identifier check is mechanical and the integrator's reading was targeted.
- Any correspondence between the story's rules and the real sport.
- Any page-layout effect that depends on facing-page geometry, which is verified for almost no spread.
- Any operational or simulation use of the monographs.
- Global character registration, which belongs to the repository's curation agent and is not written here.
