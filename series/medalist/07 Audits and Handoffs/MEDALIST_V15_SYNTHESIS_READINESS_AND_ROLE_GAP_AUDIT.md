## Conditions on every promoted artifact

1. It declares `MD-FR-FB02` as its boundary, `ONGOING` and `PROVISIONAL_THROUGH_VERIFIED_BOUNDARY`.
2. It cites observation, ledger and claim identifiers that exist at the input commit.
3. It names the source layer of anything that is not a story page, and builds no trait on a creator statement alone.
4. It gives counterevidence or a rival reading for each major conclusion, and says what later evidence would decide.
5. It reproduces no dialogue and carries no private path.
6. It lists the claim versions it depends on. A later change to one of them reopens the artifact; a completion note is not a substitute for revalidation.

## Corrections the syntheses must carry

- The judgement at Volume 13 that Tsukasa's declaration was a durable answer to Inori's guilt was withdrawn at Volume 15 (MD-RV-0259, MD-RV-0261).
- MD-CL-0028 is a hypothesis about Inori's formation that is not adopted; only its mechanism is established.
- Kanayumi's account of what Inori believes and Inori's own statement differ and are both recorded (MD-KN-0145, 0146, 0147).
- Tsukasa is the second of four brothers (erratum at FB01).
- The cause of Inori's collapse at the All-Japan Junior is not stated by the work.
- Yodaka's motive for leaving Hikaru is unknown beyond his stated reason.
