# Coach and pupil: a directional study of Tsukasa Akeuraji and Inori Yuitsuka

This study models pair `MD-PAIR-0001`, Tsukasa Akeuraji and Inori Yuitsuka, in two directions and through time. It does not retell either life; two monographs do that. Its subject is what each gives, withholds, reads and misreads in the other, and what each knows at each point.

The pair's visible conduct was steady from the first volume: reasons are given, wishes are honoured, risks are not punished. Beneath it ran a history of knowledge that was unequal from Volume 4 and was registered as the pair's central fact at Volume 9 (`MD-CL-0027@1`). The first attempt to close it, in Volume 13, succeeded as speech and failed as release, and neither party saw the failure. This project did not see it either, and Volume 15 corrected both (`MD-RV-0259`, `MD-RV-0261`). At the boundary the matter is closed in form and open in effect.

The frozen state is `MD-FR-FB02`. The story stands in the warm-up before the free skate of the Junior Grand Prix Final, and the result is not known. Everything here is provisional. Evidence is cited from the [relationship ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_RELATIONSHIP_RIVALRY_AND_MENTORSHIP_LEDGER.md), the [chronology ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_CHRONOLOGY_INFORMATION_AND_RETROSPECTION_LEDGER.md), the [claims ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md) and the readings. All dialogue is paraphrased.

## Two directions across time

The ledger carries ninety-four transitions for Tsukasa Akeuraji and Inori Yuitsuka. One is entered as joint (`MD-RL-0049`); the rest run one way. For each phase the two directions are stated separately, then the balance of power and the options.

### V01–V03: a method of choosing, and promises

**Tsukasa→Inori.** He sets out alternatives and tells her not to guess what he wants (`MD-EV-V01-0025`; `MD-RL-0006`). He also promises victory outright, which hides an uncertainty he admits elsewhere (`MD-CL-0007@1`). By her first competition the promise has become a commitment about his own conduct, and after her first defeat he apologizes for the coaching side (`MD-RL-0021`, `MD-RL-0047`).

**Inori→Tsukasa.** She asks for help toward a goal she names herself (`MD-RL-0004`). She adds an unplanned element on his permission and tells him at once (`MD-RL-0022`). Before a meet she apologizes for making him help someone like her (`MD-RL-0043`). Guilt toward him precedes any belief about his career.

**Power and options.** He holds the knowledge and the plan; her mother, Nozomi, holds consent. Her instruments are her stated wish and her disclosure, and here she uses both. He overrules her on her body, with reasons: rest ordered against her protest (`MD-EV-V03-0032`), then a cap on training volume (`MD-CL-0007@4`).

### V04–V08: the first withholding, and stakes that fuse

**Inori→Tsukasa.** She gives him a true reason for hurrying and withholds another: she fears he will be blamed for her failures, and wants to be strong enough to be able to choose him (`MD-EV-V04-0012`; `MD-RL-0065`). The ledger holds tact and concealment both live. At the national novice championship she asks him to believe in her once more and vows to make him a gold medalist's coach (`MD-EV-V08-0042`). His standing has become her stake.

**Tsukasa→Inori.** He plans as if the urgency were hers alone (`MD-KN-0036`, `MD-KN-0082`). He declines the promise of recovery she offers at the lake (`MD-RL-0145`). He stakes his whole work on her skate and declares its result (`MD-EV-V05-0041`; `MD-CL-0007@5`). At her appeal he sets aside a completed judgement for the safer layout (`MD-RL-0175`).

**Power and options.** Practical power has moved toward her: her appeal changes his plan, and he then resolves never again to make her ask to be believed in (`MD-EV-V09-0017`), which forecloses refusal (`MD-CL-0007@9`). He could have held the layout. She could have told him what the vow was for.

### V09: the asymmetry

**Inori→Tsukasa.** Her interior states it during her free skate: by becoming his pupil she ended his dream, and she means to make that worth it (`MD-EV-V09-0031`; `MD-KN-0083`). Both departures from the plan trace to him by her own account: a jump re-learned in secret because he was hurt on it (`MD-EV-V09-0023`), and an ending changed alone (`MD-EV-V09-0030`). After fourth place she offers him composure and keeps her grief for her sister Mika (`MD-RL-0209`).

**Tsukasa→Inori.** He overhears the secret and lets her keep it. He answers the unilateral change with thanks and says nothing of it (`MD-RL-0205`, `MD-RL-0208`). He tells her she is not only what is evaluated (`MD-EV-V09-0041`). He is not shown to know what she believes (`MD-KN-0084`). Then he leaves to study for her junior career.

**Power and options.** In mid-program he has no instrument but her trust. Afterwards he has every instrument and uses none: no reproach, and no question about why. The evaluation stays divided between respect and abdication (`MD-CL-0007@9`).

### V10–V12: words that do not arrive, a creed said to others

**Inori→Tsukasa.** On his return she presents results, hears analysis as correction and avoids his eyes (`MD-RL-0226`). Inori tells Hikaru Kamisaki that she is on the ice thanks to the one who believes in her victory and that gold proves she ought to be there, and tells Okazaki Iruka that her dream is not for herself alone (`MD-EV-V10-0016`, `MD-EV-V10-0040`; `MD-KN-0098`). To Iruka at night she says she fears what her slowness costs him (`MD-EV-V11-0034`; `MD-KN-0107`). In November she believes that but for her he would still be at his old rink (`MD-EV-V12-0030`; `MD-KN-0123`).

**Tsukasa→Inori.** He sees the surface within a day, will not put the failure on her, and guesses at shame over a jump (`MD-RL-0227`; `MD-KN-0099`). He comes to know her empty assent and her discounting of his praise (`MD-KN-0109`). He does not hear the creed. He has staked his pride as a coach on her gold and told only Hitomi Takamine, and has not told Inori the arena was his rink (`MD-EV-V12-0031`; `MD-KN-0122`).

**Power and options.** Each now withholds from the other. Yet on whether to move to a richer club they answer alike in separate rooms: it is hers to decide once convinced (`MD-EV-V11-0046`). `MD-CL-0027@3` sets this congruence against the silence. The silence is real and is not the whole of how they understand each other.

### V13–V15

The next section treats these. In direction: in Volume 13 she deceives him, rages, then hears him and rewrites her vow (`MD-RL-0310`, `MD-RL-0313`); he draws a line, fails to be heard, listens, and tells her his history (`MD-RL-0311`, `MD-RL-0312`). In Volume 14 he explains his own face before she can build on it (`MD-RL-0323`), and nothing returns to what she believed (`MD-KN-0140`). In Volume 15 he vows that a third failure of his responsibility is unthinkable (`MD-RL-0339`), learns from Kanayumi Miho and then from Inori, and promises a change in himself (`MD-RL-0360`); Inori claims the weight as hers and is glad of his promise (`MD-RL-0359`, `MD-RL-0361`).

By then Kanayumi, a third adult, has said aloud that his standing rises with Inori's results and that Inori knows it (`MD-EV-V15-0021`). The dependence runs both ways. He had four options that evening: to hide his face better, to tell her the burden is unnecessary, to forbid it, or to change his own conduct. He proposes the first, prepares the second, and chooses the fourth.

## The knowledge history

### What Inori believed, and from what

The belief has an older and a newer form, and they should not be merged.

The older form is that Inori Yuitsuka ended Tsukasa Akeuraji's skating by becoming his pupil. Her interior gives it in Volume 9 with its source: she pieced it together from what people around her said about a man who had staked everything and stopped on the day he took her on (`MD-EV-V09-0031`). The Volume 9 reading observes that this is not a fantasy, since in the first volume he was auditioning for ice shows and gave that up to coach. It is fed in Volume 12 by the discovery of his rink, and in Volume 13 by strangers' remarks that he competed briefly and is young enough to dance still (`MD-EV-V13-0041`). He is not shown to know of those remarks (`MD-KN-0133`).

The newer form concerns the present: that being cared for costs the carer, that her slowness takes from him, that protection is shameful (`MD-EV-V11-0034`–`0036`; `MD-CL-0029@1`). In Volume 15 she dates it herself to middle school.

A third element is a reading and not a belief. During his absence she supposed her loss had sent him away (`MD-KN-0092`). Volume 10 narrowed this: she had been told the purpose at a handover, and the supposition stands only as her interpretation (`MD-KN-0094`).

### What Tsukasa had said, and to whom

Before Volume 13 Tsukasa had disclaimed sacrifice four times and never to Inori. To Kago Kōichi: his work cannot be seen and her performance is all of it (`MD-EV-V05-0015`). To Yodaka Jun: coaching is his way of using what he has (`MD-EV-V06-0037`). To Hikaru Kamisaki, twice: it was never a sacrifice, he did it for his own happiness, and he will not make Inori pay one (`MD-EV-V08-0037`, `MD-EV-V09-0046`). Tsukasa and Inori held incompatible accounts of one event and neither had heard the other's (`MD-KN-0085`).

### Three attempts

| Attempt | What Tsukasa said to Inori | Form | Uptake |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V09, a playground (`MD-EV-V09-0041`) | She is not only what is evaluated; she may aim at first without anyone's expectation. | A statement about worth, made without knowing her belief. It removes the ground of a debt and does not name it. | Heard as words; she later quotes it (`MD-CL-0027@2`). The belief is unchanged in V12. |
| V13, a platform (`MD-EV-V13-0038`/`0039`) | Her results cannot disappoint him; his troubled face was not about her result; she owes no one a medal; he did not give up skating to coach. | A prepared list, opened by an objection to missed school. | Not taken in. By his own analysis nothing after his first sentence arrived. |
| V13, a bench (`MD-EV-V13-0042`–`0045`) | His history of hiding, believing a verdict and punishing himself; then that coaching her is his dream and stands in for nothing. | A story about himself, with the declaration last. | Taken in (`MD-KN-0131`). She restates it and vows to make him the coach of the strongest and happiest skater. She does not say what she had believed. |

The platform sentence about not having given up skating is the direct answer to the older belief. It is the one that did not arrive.

### What the project concluded at Volume 13

`MD-CL-0027@5` judged the silence broken from Tsukasa's side and heard, and the asymmetry resolved in substance and incomplete in form. `MD-CL-0029@3` treated the bench as the first answer to Inori's guilt from the person it concerned, and the Volume 13 reading called it durable. One caveat was recorded: her new vow was still a vow to give him something. Volume 14 was read as a month of the settlement holding (`MD-CL-0027@6`, `MD-CL-0029@4`), with the note that a clean skate cannot test it.

### What Volume 15 showed

Three statements are made in one evening, and they are not the same statement.

**Inori's, to the press.** She has made many people suffer and been a burden; she must repay it with the best result; she will give everything so that the self who was allowed to skate need not be ashamed (`MD-EV-V15-0016`). This is the Volume 10 creed, a month after the bench.

**Kanayumi Miho's, to Tsukasa.** Inori feels she skates at the sacrifice of his life. She reads adults' faces. He says he is happy whatever the result, and he suffers when she does not win, blames himself and pares himself further. Winning is her one means of lessening what he pays, and not winning is a punishment for having chosen skating (`MD-EV-V15-0017`; `MD-KN-0145`). Kanayumi has grounds: a shared room abroad and a year of watching. It is still an adult's inference, and the phrase about his life is Kanayumi's, as is the image of Inori among broken crystal that illustrates it (`MD-VF-0137`).

**Inori's, to Tsukasa.** It is narrower and goes further (`MD-EV-V15-0048`; `MD-KN-0146`). She dates it to middle school. She names her mother Nozomi, who cannot come home for work, and Tsukasa, who agonizes and labours, and the kind people who work harder when she fails. Gold would repay everyone. Then she adds what no adult had guessed. To be told she need not strain is what she hates; it is like having her strength drained. She was glad in December because she thought he had at last entrusted her with a part (the readings record her word as 役割). She will not let go of skating whoever suffers. She wants to make him happy as he wants to make her happy. She will not be treated as weak. She wants to carry everything and still land lightly.

She does not say that she ended his career. The older form of the belief is neither voiced nor withdrawn (`MD-KN-0147`).

On Tsukasa's side the volume names a matching error, and he names it himself. He had taken the matter for closed in December because he had said the words. To say that he told her and she would not let go passes the responsibility to her; he rejects his own thought that she minds too much as cowardly (`MD-EV-V15-0024`). He had treated his declaration as a pledge.

So the bench was misread from both ends. She heard it accurately and took it as a commission. He spoke it sincerely and took it as a settlement (`MD-RT-0104`).

### The correction, twice over

As a finding about the relationship, this explains the bench better than the earlier account did. Inori, who experiences reassurance as a draining of strength, cannot be released by a reassurance. A declaration that his dream is to coach her gave her something to do for him. The vow she made on the spot said so.

As a revision of this project's judgement, it withdraws two things. `MD-CL-0029@5` withdraws the judgement that the declaration was the first durable answer to her guilt, and restates care-as-debt, on her own account, as a role she has chosen and will not surrender. `MD-CL-0027@7` restates the Volume 13 closure as partial in a way neither party saw. The caveat recorded at the time was the accurate part. The earlier readings stand unedited; `MD-RD-0074` corrects `MD-RD-0066`.

The error has a describable cause. The project counted accurate restatement as uptake. That had been the right test since Volume 10, when his words did not arrive at all. The bench passed it. What it did not test was the use to which a correctly heard sentence would be put. The same caution applies to the stairwell, where she again restates him correctly.

A rival reading should be kept. The bench may have released her for a time, with the creed returning under the pressure of a final; Volume 14's captions carry no language of owing. Against this stand her own words about December and Kanayumi's report of what Inori let slip in Bangkok, months before the bench. The page favours the commission and does not exclude a real, shorter relief.

### What the stairwell did and did not settle

It settled these things. She told him, in her own terms, at his question. He learned why words could not lift it: what she reads in him is true. He did not answer with a fifth disclaimer. He promised conduct: to audition for the ice show again some day, to stay her coach, and to be not unhappy if she does not win (`MD-EV-V15-0050`; `MD-KN-0153`). He said his own problem is his to solve. He gave her a test in place of a prohibition: to take up only suffering she can call her own life. She agreed after a silence, understood that he is not leaving, and held him to the ice show as a promise.

It did not settle these. She did not say the weight is changed, and withdrew nothing (`MD-CS-0333`). He does not know whether it reached her; the page prints his doubt twice. The older belief is unaddressed. What she told her mother Nozomi an hour earlier, that she would not mind vanishing into a gift, she has not told him (`MD-KN-0148`). He told Kago his childhood in full and told Inori one sentence of it (`MD-KN-0149`). And his promise is a sentence about how he will be after a result. No result has occurred.

## Recurring mechanisms

### Correction is not taken in; what comes sideways is

The Volume 13 reading states the rule and three cases. In Volume 10 Tsukasa Akeuraji's analysis of Inori Yuitsuka's Lutz was heard as one more correction; what restored the jump was a contest in which he had to learn beside her (`MD-EV-V10-0018`, `MD-EV-V10-0029`/`0030`). In Volume 11 rules explained since February had not gone in (`MD-KN-0106`), and her first step toward watching her own body came from a quota of attempts (`MD-EV-V11-0010`). In Volume 13 a list failed and a story succeeded. Another case fits: his praise falls flat where Kanayumi Miho's identical judgement lands (`MD-EV-V11-0009`).

The counter-cases bound it. In Volumes 1 to 8 direct instruction arrived routinely, and she asked for it in his own form (`MD-RL-0149`); she accepted a refusal at the boards on a scoring reason (`MD-RL-0045`). In the stairwell he asks a plain question and gets a plain answer (`MD-CL-0004@13`). And her own account of the Lutz is that a belief that her skating was worse than everyone's blocked it (`MD-EV-V10-0029`), which is a misjudgement about herself and not a fact about his manner. The mechanism is therefore conditional. It describes speech from him that she can hear as a verdict on her, during a period in which she was concealing. It is not established as a trait of how she learns.

### Inori conceals and acts alone; what Tsukasa does on finding out

| Case | What Inori did | What Tsukasa did on learning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V09, the Lutz | Re-learned in secret the jump he was hurt on (`MD-RL-0204`). | Overheard; let her keep the secret; admitted the jump on a tally. |
| V09, the ending | Changed the layout in mid-program (`MD-RL-0207`). | Thanks; silence about the change; a verdict of complete defeat on himself (`MD-EV-V09-0044`). |
| V10 | Presented results, avoided shared video, reverted to drills (`MD-RL-0226`). | Diagnosis without blame; a device; help sought from senior coaches (`MD-CS-0210`). |
| V13 | Skipped school by degrees, against the pair's founding rule (`MD-EV-V13-0034`). | Read it as autonomy, was hurt, let weeks pass; then said he could not approve, and listened (`MD-RL-0311`). |
| V15 | Told the press and not him; said she had nothing more to discuss (`MD-EV-V15-0047`). | Told by Kanayumi; asked her directly; set aside his prepared answer. |

Three regularities hold. He never punishes the concealment. He takes its cost as his own failure. And until Volume 15 he does not ask her why: his responses manage the situation around her, with a tally, a game, a generous reading. Kanayumi names this to his face as acting that has been seen through (`MD-EV-V15-0021`).

The concealment is not general. She discloses a great deal to him throughout: a resolution on a train, the end of a cold feeling, a childhood grief, her reason for choosing the quadruple (`MD-RL-0048`, `MD-RL-0068`, `MD-RL-0113`, `MD-RL-0152`). What she withholds is specific. It is whatever would show him what she thinks she costs him. Faces are also misread in the other direction: she once hid her delight so that he would go on demonstrating, and he took it for disappointment (`MD-KN-0064`).

### Tsukasa's face as Inori's burden, and as the ground of her trust

Inori reads Tsukasa's face at the boards in Volume 3 (`MD-EV-V03-0022`) and sees him unguarded by a lake after his injury (`MD-EV-V07-0014`). In Volume 10 Gori Seiji guesses that she moves by adults' faces and heeds Tsukasa because his face cannot lie (`MD-EV-V10-0028`). In Volume 13 Gori teaches that an adult's face is a reward or a punishment (`MD-EV-V13-0037`), and on the platform Inori asks Tsukasa why he only looks troubled (`MD-KN-0132`). In Volume 15 Tsukasa vows to win in a page of dark close-ups with Inori drawn small behind him, looking (`MD-VF-0136`).

`MD-CL-0028@5` carries the result. The mechanism is established: she reads his face first and assigns its cause to herself. The premise first attached to it, that her capacities were formed under deprivation, is a hypothesis this project has not adopted, and this study does not use it.

Volume 15 changes the remedy and the valuation. The Volume 13 lesson was to govern his face. Kanayumi bounds it: that suits a younger child, and to a pupil who knows the facts it is a lie that will fail (`MD-RT-0105`). What must change is what the face truthfully shows, which means his conduct after a loss. And the same readable face is why a child who probes adults came to trust him; Kanayumi calls it his greatest strength (`MD-EV-V15-0023`). Volume 14 had shown that side already. Inori names bodily habits of his that he did not know (`MD-EV-V14-0027`), and takes his joy as gladness for her (`MD-EV-V14-0033`). The limit is the ledger's: the bounding is Kanayumi's judgement alone.

### The criterion that Tsukasa does not overrule Inori's stated wish

`MD-CL-0007` has twelve versions, and they describe Tsukasa's path. Absolute promises give way to permission to fail, refusals with reasons, and candour about limits (`@1`–`@4`). He changes a layout at her appeal against his own judgement (`@8`), thanks her for an override that failed (`@9`), and defines his part as staying beside her wish while licensing others to caution her (`@10`). In Volume 13 he draws a line himself, at missed school and unregulated volume (`@11`). In Volume 15 he restates the criterion as a principle (`@12`).

One clarification is needed. The ledger calls Volume 13 the criterion's first exception. He had overruled her on her body in Volumes 3 and 4. Across all three the pattern is the same, and it is the more exact statement of the criterion: he will forbid a harm to her body, and he will not overrule a purpose. `@12` draws that distinction in terms.

Volume 15 gives the criterion its ground. He had prepared to tell her that drawing others in is no fault. He does not, because guidance along the safe road for a child's own good is a rightness that serves the guardian, and it was done to him (`MD-EV-V15-0049`). The counterevidence is in the same scene. He has just carried her up twenty floors over her objection. And he names what he declines to press down: a heat he recognizes from Yodaka Jun, which is one with great suffering. The evaluation stays favourable with his own reservation attached, and no result can settle it.

### Reciprocity

Each wants to repay the other, and each has been told that nothing is owed.

Inori's wish is explicit from Volume 8 and is rewritten twice. Tsukasa's sense of receiving from her is as old (`MD-RL-0038`). At the lake, when she tells him half asleep that he must have been born to skate, he weeps and vows to her sleeping to make her a skater the world calls born to it (`MD-EV-V07-0016`; `MD-RL-0146`, `MD-RL-0147`). She does not know the weight of what she said.

Inori was told by Tsukasa that she owes no one a medal, and by Okazaki Iruka and Hitomi Takamine that being cared for is no ground for guilt (`MD-EV-V13-0038`; `MD-CL-0026@4`). Tsukasa was told as much years earlier, by his teacher Takamine Shō, who held that supporters are participants and not sacrifices, and by Meiko, who taught him to share a problem (`MD-EV-V15-0026`, `MD-EV-V15-0032`). His thought on being helped was that he must repay the favour with results at all costs, which the Volume 15 reading identifies as the sentence Inori gave the press. Neither was changed by being told.

That symmetry is the strongest internal reason to doubt that words alone will alter her. It is also why his answer takes the form it does. He thanks her for having tried to make him happy, and changes what there is to repay.

## Physical and formal evidence

**Carrying.** Tsukasa Akeuraji carries Inori Yuitsuka three times. After a fall in Volume 3 he carries her off (`MD-EV-V03-0029`). At the lake in Volume 7, days after cracking his ribs on her harness, he carries her on his back to prove he is sturdy, and she falls asleep there (`MD-EV-V07-0015`/`0016`). In Volume 15 he carries her up a stairwell over her protest, and when he asks how long she has thought she makes people suffer, she asks to be put down (`MD-EV-V15-0047`). She answers standing, some steps apart. The visual ledger notes that carrying is made literal before it is discussed (`MD-VF-0144`). Her own verb in the speech that follows is to carry. An hour before, he had asked himself what coach loads a skater with weight in a sport where the lighter body jumps better. She claims the weight and the light landing both.

**Hands and cords.** The pair's habitual contact is a tether before risk: the drawstrings of his jacket, which she ties in a bow, grips in both fists, and keeps while he is away (`MD-EV-V05-0041`; `MD-RL-0225`; `MD-MOT-0001`). The harness of Volume 7 is the one contact that injures the holder, and she accepts it only when told he has chosen the risk knowingly (`MD-RL-0148`).

**Who watches whom.** He thanks her after her worst result for letting him watch from nearest (`MD-EV-V09-0036`). She watches him more than he knows. Volume 15 draws her behind him as he sets the stake, so that the reader sees the fact Kanayumi Miho will state twenty pages later.

**The program and the film.** In her short program she takes her first jump as a boy's first victory, the one that earns the right to be taught, and in captions casts Tsukasa as the ghost who makes the boy strong. The drawing agrees (`MD-VF-0129`). The film then removes its helper, who says he leaves out of love, and she follows it only as far as claiming the last jump as her own (`MD-EV-V14-0029`). The Volume 14 reading cautioned that the ending is a film's, and Volume 15 bears it out. Nothing has him withdraw; he promises to stay her coach and to have a life of his own (`MD-MOT-0016`). And she rejects the ending in the other direction. She would have the gifted double stay and the ordinary self vanish, for the sake of everyone who helped (`MD-EV-V15-0046`). She says this to her mother Nozomi and then calls it only a film. The ghost that was Tsukasa in Volume 14 is, in this speech, her own gift. How her wish to carry and her wish to vanish relate is open (`MD-CL-0002@15`).

## Other coaches, other pupils

The series sets at least eight coaching arrangements beside that of Tsukasa Akeuraji and Inori Yuitsuka (`MD-CL-0024@9`, `MD-CL-0025@10`, `MD-CL-0026@9`). Each isolates something in the two of them.

**Yodaka and Hikaru (`MD-PAIR-0011`).** Yodaka Jun is a coach who hid his role, bound Hikaru Kamisaki by a contract and a rule under which one practice fall is final, and taught that victory needs sacrifice (`MD-RL-0180`, `MD-RL-0285`). On the night Hikaru won in her own manner Yodaka debriefed her, said he was not angry, and ended his coaching, on the stated ground that his road ends with a skater letting her coaches go (`MD-EV-V13-0028`–`0030`). His motive beyond that statement is unknown (`MD-CL-0016@14`). Three things follow for Tsukasa and Inori. Inori and Hikaru each departed from a plan and neither coach reproached it; Tsukasa answered with thanks and stayed, Yodaka with a debrief and left (`MD-CL-0024@7`). Inori and Hikaru have each had to infer what her coach is to himself. ## Creator-layer context

Everything here is from Official Fanbook 02 as audited: the author's conception as reported, expressly revisable, and in two cases raw working notes. None of it is story evidence, and no trait is built on it.

Of Tsukasa Akeuraji's early line that Inori Yuitsuka must not read his wishes, the author is reported as concluding that to push up what the child has decided is real coaching (`MD-EV-FB02-0030`). One statement bears on the knowledge history and cuts against his own disclaimer. ## Resolved in form, open in effect

**Resolved in form.** Tsukasa Akeuraji and Inori Yuitsuka have each said to the other what had been withheld (`MD-CL-0027@7`). Inori has stated her position as a claim and not a confession. Tsukasa has identified his own part: while denying sacrifice he had put his faith in his own (`MD-EV-V15-0045`). He has replaced reassurance with a commitment about his conduct. Both errors about the bench are named on the page.

**Open in effect.** Everything listed above as unsettled by the stairwell, and two further things: whether she hides less, and whether the heat he recognized in her proves a strength or a cost. These are the V15 reading's open questions MD-OQ-V15-02, 03 and 04.

**What conduct after a result would show.** These are tests, not forecasts.

After a gold medal, the informative conduct is how each describes it. If she speaks of repayment made and a place secured, the role stands as she stated it. If she speaks of it without the vocabulary of owing, that is first evidence of a change, and one win cannot show whether it lasts. On his side, the vow of that afternoon framed gold as a responsibility twice failed. Whether he treats the medal as that debt discharged, or as hers, distinguishes the man of the afternoon from the man of the stairwell.

After any other colour, his side is tested first. In Volume 9 he called fourth place his complete defeat; in Volume 11 he wept that fourth was not good enough (`MD-EV-V11-0045`). A repetition would falsify the promise in the only medium she trusts. On her side the tests are whether she conceals, whether she trains against his line of Volume 13, and whether she applies the test he gave her or tells him that she cannot.

After either, two things would count for more than the result: whether he asks for help and in time auditions, and whether she tells him the thing she called only a film.
