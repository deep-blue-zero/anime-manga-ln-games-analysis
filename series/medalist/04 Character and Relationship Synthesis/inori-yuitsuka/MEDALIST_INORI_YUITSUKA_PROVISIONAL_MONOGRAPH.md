# Inori Yuitsuka — a permission that has to be earned again

This is a provisional monograph on Inori Yuitsuka, key `MD-CHAR-0004`, written to the combined freeze `MD-FR-FB02`. Its evidence is the fifteen frozen readings, the two fanbook audits and the seven ledgers, chiefly the [character ledger](../../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_CHARACTER_DEVELOPMENT_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md) and the [claims ledger](../../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md). It paraphrases throughout. It is an argument about a character in an unfinished work, at a point where she is in mid-air.

## Thesis

Inori Yuitsuka began as a child who was not allowed to skate. At thirteen, second in the short program of a world junior final by a third of a point, she still describes her place on the ice as something granted that results must justify. That is the constant. It appears as the narration over a memory of her sister Mika's competition (`MD-EV-V02-0001`), as a small child's dream about who may sit in a kiss-and-cry (`MD-EV-V06-0019`), as thanks for having been made someone others expect things of (`MD-EV-V09-0040`), as a creed that gold proves she ought to be there (`MD-EV-V10-0040`), as a fear of being thought not in earnest (`MD-EV-V13-0008`), as a first jump that asks to be granted the right (`MD-EV-V14-0023`), and to the press as a debt to be repaid so that the self who was allowed to skate need not be ashamed (`MD-EV-V15-0016`). Seven volumes, seven settings, in defeat and in a clean skate. `MD-CL-0001@15` calls it conditional tenure.

What developed is everything built on that ground. Her technique went from single jumps to two quadruple Salchows and a level-four step sequence. Her agency went from a plea to a claim (`MD-CL-0002@15`). Her handling of pressure became a trained skill with a name (`MD-CL-0015@13`). Her candour, withheld from Tsukasa Akeuraji on one point since Volume 4, became complete in form in Volume 15 (`MD-CL-0027@7`). The measure of what she lacks or owes has taken five forms: a late start, a gap in talent, Hikaru Kamisaki as a rival, a proof by gold, and at last the labour of the people who help her.

The study's central claim is therefore double. Inori is not a child moving from insecurity toward confidence; the work has shown her competent, composed and generous for years while the premise stood. And the premise has not been removed by any answer she has been given. The corpus once thought it had been, at Volume 13, and withdrew that view on the evidence of Volume 15 (`MD-RV-0259`, `MD-RV-0261`). What Volume 15 adds is that she now holds the premise as a role she has chosen and will not surrender (`MD-CL-0029@6`).

What is open is whether that choice is agency or the old compulsion in a composed voice (`MD-CL-0015@14`); how her wish to carry everything relates to her wish, said the same evening, to vanish into her gift; whether Tsukasa's lesson about guilt was taken in; and how she meets a real error inside a program, which no admitted page shows after December (`MD-CL-0019@12`). None of these is decided here.

The strongest counterweight to the thesis is her delight. She learns by pleasure as often as by lack (`MD-EV-V04-0038`, `MD-EV-V09-0028`, `MD-EV-V10-0030`), says in Volume 15 that she is glad she can skate (`MD-EV-V15-0046`), and makes most of her darkest statements on nights after defeats or on the eve of a final. A reader could hold that the premise is a mood of evaluative occasions and not a standing self-understanding. The Volume 14 instance, spoken inside a calm and successful skate, is the best evidence against that reading; it is one instance.

### V01: a plan to deserve permission

The first thing shown of Inori is concealment in the service of a plan. At eleven she has taught herself from an annotated manual, hidden it, and means to be ready before she asks (`MD-EV-V01-0004`, `MD-CS-0002`). A bonus chapter, at that layer, shows the practice beginning at seven with Sekoma, a rink clerk, who took worms for ice time and paid her entry himself (`MD-EV-V01-0035`). At school she describes herself as unable; the work gives no diagnosis and shows ability varying by context (`MD-CS-0002`).

The plea follows an adult's affirmation that precedes any assessment of skill (`MD-EV-V01-0007`). Only then does she say why skating matters to her (`MD-EV-V01-0008`). Within the volume she chooses an early test, asks for help toward the world's best after meeting Hikaru Kamisaki, and refuses to guess what Tsukasa Akeuraji wants when choosing a training path (`MD-EV-V01-0010`, `MD-EV-V01-0018`, `MD-EV-V01-0025`). `MD-CL-0002@1` reads this as voiced agency expanding, with compliance toward an admired adult as a live alternative. Two things set here persist. The permission of her mother, Nozomi, runs only through middle school (`MD-EV-V01-0014`), and that term has never been revisited on the page. And concealment came first; the candour had to be invited.

### V02–V04: the deficit named, and bidden farewell

Her first competition supplies the pattern the later ones vary. Inori overhears Nozomi ask that the jumps be made easy, blames herself, reverses the thought within pages, and tells Nozomi that she wants to win and lose as an athlete and wants to be watched (`MD-EV-V02-0005`, `MD-EV-V02-0006`). She falls on the first jump, gets up smiling, and adds an element Tsukasa had deferred (`MD-EV-V02-0011`). That night, in a bonus chapter, she names what drives her as a cold dissatisfaction and says she fears that joy will dissolve it (`MD-EV-V02-0030`).

Volume 3 gives her fullest early self-account: a place for failure was always prepared for her before she tried, and she chooses to be the self Tsukasa believed in (`MD-EV-V03-0012`, `MD-CS-0033`). It also gives her first defeat and its cost. She had trained two days on shin pain she took for soreness, and protested rest because she was behind (`MD-EV-V03-0032`). On the train home she accepts that lost time cannot be recovered, refuses it as a reason to stop, and renounces haste (`MD-EV-V03-0040`, `MD-CL-0019@1`). Inori and Tsukasa choose a year of building. Then, in anger at an insult to Tsukasa, she sets herself a deadline and a borrowed target (`MD-EV-V03-0055`, `MD-CS-0045`).

Volume 4 holds the resolution and adds the first thing Inori keeps from Tsukasa. Her stated reason for hurry is that she has changed. Her operative reason, told to Ryoka Miketa as a secret, is fear that Tsukasa will be blamed for her failures, and a wish to be strong enough to be able to choose him (`MD-EV-V04-0012`, `MD-KN-0036`). On the last evening of camp she says that the cold feeling let her work, that its role ends when she is no longer the girl who started late, and that she owes it goodbye and thanks (`MD-EV-V04-0038`). `MD-CL-0015@3` recorded this as a phase with a term, by her account, in a season in which nothing went wrong. Later volumes show the form outliving that content.

### V05–V06: a title, and the measure moves to a person

At the regional qualifier Inori commits herself aloud before Kojō Riina, an Olympian, and cameras, corrects herself at once when rebuked, and announces that she skates for gold (`MD-EV-V05-0010`, `MD-EV-V05-0031`). `MD-CL-0002@5` identifies the form: under strong feeling, before people above her, she binds herself in words. Before her skate she says she understands the difference in talent and in time, and goes on (`MD-EV-V05-0041`). She wins with the event's first clean program, counting her own last spin (`MD-EV-V06-0015`).

The kiss-and-cry yields the earliest disclosed form of the premise: a recurring childhood dream of sitting there, and a prayer that it would stop because waking was sad (`MD-EV-V06-0019`). Victory does not rest her. A week later she sees Hikaru's triple Axel, judges the gap real, falls four times, and skates the rest of the session without stopping (`MD-EV-V06-0045`–`0047`). `MD-CL-0015@5` promotes the conduct to a standing way of moving toward a gap and declines to say what it rests on.

### V07–V09: both modes in one skate, and the belief disclosed

Volume 7 separates two states on the page. After Tsukasa is hurt Inori loses a jump to a guarding she cannot command (`MD-EV-V07-0011`). She classes saying she is frightened as selfish, then says it aloud with its two sources, Mika's fracture and Tsukasa's (`MD-EV-V07-0019`, `MD-EV-V07-0025`). With fifteen minutes left she is frantic and pleading; redirected to sensation, she lands unaided (`MD-EV-V07-0026`–`0028`). She had chosen the quadruple over restoring the Lutz by asking for both branches to be set out, as Tsukasa once taught her, and later says she chose it for the attention, because one who is not beaten by attention can become the best (`MD-EV-V07-0024`, `MD-EV-V07-0039`).

At the national event Inori reopens an instruction not to watch Hikaru and gives reasons; after Hikaru's score she asks Tsukasa to believe in her again and vows to make him a gold medallist's coach (`MD-EV-V08-0027`, `MD-EV-V08-0042`). `MD-CL-0002@8` counts this as the disagreement held against Tsukasa's reasons that earlier versions had named as the test.

Inori's free skate is the densest single body of evidence about her. She lands two quadruple Salchows, falls on a Lutz she had relearned in secret because Tsukasa was hurt on it, and is on the music at once (`MD-EV-V09-0023`, `MD-EV-V09-0024`). Her step sequence is called at level four, the first by a novice in eleven years by the page's statement (`MD-EV-V09-0039`). Then she changes the layout alone and attempts a triple Axel no adult had approved (`MD-EV-V09-0030`). The reasoning is given in her own captions: she believes that by becoming Tsukasa's pupil she ended his dream, and means to prove otherwise (`MD-EV-V09-0031`, `MD-KN-0083`). The approach is frantic and uses the vocabulary of punishment; it is checked at take-off by Tsukasa's remembered sentence and her own farewell to the cold feeling (`MD-EV-V09-0032`, `MD-EV-V09-0033`, `MD-RT-0058`). The jump is landed without a fall and downgraded. She is fourth, 0.52 behind the bronze by the printed scores.

`MD-CL-0015@8` draws the conclusion that holds through the boundary: the attentive mode governs execution, including recovery; the compelled mode governs one decision. Afterwards the two divide by audience. To Tsukasa she offers composure and thanks (`MD-EV-V09-0040`). To Mika she says that anything but first was unbearable (`MD-EV-V09-0043`). Alone she swears never to lose again and to prove her strength by gold (`MD-EV-V09-0048`). Tsukasa's answer in the playground, that a person is not made only of what others want, is heard: she quotes it later and declines it (`MD-EV-V09-0041`, `MD-EV-V10-0016`).

### V10–V12: a rule of self-reliance, shame at being spared, a claim on risk

In the junior class Inori is thirteen, growing, in pain, and short of her autumn jumps. She makes a rule not to telephone the absent Tsukasa, so as to do well without him (`MD-EV-V10-0005`). When the months do not deliver growth, the rule becomes concealment: results presented, problems solved alone, old drills resumed without a word (`MD-EV-V10-0021`, `MD-CL-0002@10`). She cannot answer Hikaru's greeting and cries from chagrin (`MD-EV-V10-0012`). To Hikaru she states the creed in full: she has never aimed at gold on grounds, cannot forgive a self that fails to take it, is on the ice thanks to the one who believes in her victory, and must go on proving with gold that she ought to be there (`MD-EV-V10-0040`). The lost Lutz returns inside a game, as a puzzle of small knacks, and she feels little at landing it because she ought to be able to (`MD-EV-V10-0030`). A side chapter set the morning after her own defeat shows the other face: she tells Sonidori Rio not to belittle the gold everyone aimed at (`MD-EV-V10-0050`).

Volume 11 gives her own account of her motives, which the corpus prefers to the adults' theories (`MD-RD-0056`). Rest feels to her like not working (`MD-EV-V11-0008`). Tsukasa's praise no longer registers; the identical judgement from Kanayumi Miho, the trainer, does (`MD-EV-V11-0009`). She accepts a quota of attempts and learns to stop after a failure and look for its cause (`MD-EV-V11-0010`). In the leader's chair she catches in herself a wish that others would fail, and refuses it (`MD-EV-V11-0025`). That night she tells Okazaki Iruka that what she hates in slow results is what they may cost Tsukasa, that a life with hardship removed is shameful, that she takes from Tsukasa without knowing, and that it may be unfair to fight only where she cannot stand alone (`MD-EV-V11-0034`–`0036`, `MD-CL-0029@1`). Next day she separates a defeat whose causes she can see from a defeat at full strength, which she still cannot bear (`MD-EV-V11-0040`). Invited toward a richer club, she says she will decide a turning in her life only when convinced (`MD-EV-V11-0046`).

Volume 12 reports a series win she is not shown skating (`MD-EV-V12-0024`) and brings her to her first junior nationals in the arena that was Tsukasa's home rink. Overhearing his old acquaintances, Inori thinks that but for her he would still be pursuing his dream there (`MD-EV-V12-0030`, `MD-KN-0123`). After Iruka is carried off in the warm-up she steadies herself, tells Tsukasa she can skate, and on the ice claims the sport's risk as her own: every road holds injury, and she wants hers here (`MD-EV-V12-0041`). `MD-CL-0029@2` marks it as the first statement of hers that answers her shame at being spared. It is one sentence at a moment of crisis.

### V13: what she cannot bear

Inori's short program fails on every element that depends on precision (`MD-EV-V13-0004`–`0006`, `MD-PERF-0107`). The work states no cause, and this study supplies none. The page offers a mechanism for the first jump only.

What follows is the most exact statement of her fear. Failure and ridicule do not frighten her; she fails at too much for that. What she cannot bear is to be thought not in earnest, and without the result she has no means of proving it (`MD-EV-V13-0008`). She performs cheer for her family and runs from Hikaru. She is shown strangers with banners and told that support does not wait on results; it does not prevent what follows (`MD-EV-V13-0011`, `MD-IN-0134`). Watching Hikaru's free skate she weeps with longing for the effort itself (`MD-EV-V13-0022`).

Then she tries to pay. She feigns illness to train in school hours, breaking by degrees the rule she and Tsukasa agreed at the start as a condition of aiming at the Olympics (`MD-EV-V13-0034`). A blade cracks under her. On a platform she says that practice is the only thing she can do, that she is an expected skater now and had to win, that she would sooner ruin her legs than lose, and asks Tsukasa why he only looks troubled (`MD-EV-V13-0040`). Tsukasa names a new cold feeling, a wish to punish herself (`MD-MOT-0015`). Nothing after his first clause is taken in (`MD-EV-V13-0039`). She lets the train leave.

By a broken ride Tsukasa tells her his own history and that coaching her is his dream and no substitute. Inori repeats it accurately, infers that her happiness as his pupil fulfils it, and vows to make him the coach of the strongest and happiest skater (`MD-EV-V13-0044`, `MD-EV-V13-0045`). She also discloses a mechanism: she speaks ill of her failing self inwardly because it lets her feel like someone else, and it has begun to hurt. Tsukasa makes the power to get up a second aim of the final; she finds it very hard and accepts it (`MD-EV-V13-0046`, `MD-CL-0019@10`). This scene was real. It was also misread by both of them, as Volume 15 shows.

### V14: the film read as her own story

A month later the attentive mode operates under the conditions in which it failed. Inori runs a routine, places each passage by a scene of her program's film, gives herself single instructions, and when the hall claps as it did before her fall and the fall returns on the approach to the same jump, attends to the jump (`MD-EV-V14-0023`, `MD-EV-V14-0024`, `MD-SK-0085`). The step sequence is back at level four (`MD-EV-V14-0028`).

She decides what the program means. Its hero, admitted to a school, is like her on the day her mother said she might skate; its helper is Tsukasa, and her strength is not hers alone; its ending she will complete by her own strength (`MD-EV-V14-0022`, `MD-EV-V14-0028`, `MD-EV-V14-0029`). She ends over her own reflection (`MD-EV-V14-0030`). No caption concerns debt or punishment. `MD-CL-0029@4` recorded, correctly, that a clean skate could not show whether the debt was discharged, renamed or only quiet. Nothing went wrong, so the aim set in December, recovery after a real error, was not tested (`MD-CL-0019@11`).

### V15: three statements

Inori goes into the free skate second by 0.33. Soon afterwards she tells the press that she has made many people suffer and been a burden, that she must repay it with the best result, and that she will give everything so that the self who was allowed to skate need not be ashamed (`MD-EV-V15-0016`, `MD-CS-0317`). The statement reaches Tsukasa through Kanayumi, with Kanayumi's own account attached.

That evening, lying in Nozomi's lap, Inori says she does not find her film's ending happy; had the hero stayed a genius he could have made everyone happy; in his place she would not mind being taken over and vanishing. Nozomi holds her and refuses it. Inori says it is only a film, that she is glad she can skate, and that she will win. When Tsukasa appears she sits bolt upright (`MD-EV-V15-0046`, `MD-CS-0330`).

In a stairwell Inori first says she has nothing to discuss. Asked how long she has thought that her skating makes others suffer, she asks to be put down from Tsukasa's back and answers standing (`MD-EV-V15-0047`, `MD-EV-V15-0048`, `MD-CS-0331`). The content is set out in the dedicated section below. Tsukasa abandons his prepared message, declines to call her wish a mistake, promises something about his own life, and offers a test in place of a prohibition: to bear only what she can call her own life (`MD-EV-V15-0050`). Inori agrees after a silence, is overjoyed that she will one day see him skate, and withdraws nothing (`MD-EV-V15-0051`, `MD-CS-0333`). The page prints Tsukasa's doubt twice. Next day Karasuba Daria, a team-mate, pins her hair, the warm-up begins, and she jumps (`MD-EV-V15-0052`).

## Self-concept

Inori Yuitsuka describes herself by reference to an earlier self she disliked: the no-good child, the one who could not skate. The figure appears at eleven (`MD-EV-V03-0012`), is thanked and dismissed at twelve (`MD-EV-V04-0038`), and returns at thirteen as what failure sends her back to (`MD-EV-V15-0048`). By her account the present self exists by permission and by other people's labour. That is self-report, repeated across years, and the study treats it as her standing self-understanding with moderate-to-high confidence.

It is not self-deprecation in the ordinary sense, and the difference is diagnostic. She is glad to be called a genius (`MD-EV-V09-0040`). She takes being recruited as evidence that she has become strong (`MD-EV-V11-0046`). She resolves on the best step sequence in the world and gets its level (`MD-EV-V14-0028`). She told Sonidori Rio, who had belittled his own gold, that his self-deprecation was a defence she knew (`MD-CS-0222`). What she doubts is not her ability. It is her entitlement.

One element is disclosed and not explained: the inward disparagement that makes her feel like someone else (`MD-EV-V13-0045`). The work sets a film's last line about being able to love oneself beside it and does not say that anything in her has changed (`MD-CL-0019@11`).

## Motives, fears and ambivalence

Inori Yuitsuka's stated aims are stable and her own: the world's best, a gold medal, Hikaru Kamisaki, the Olympic stage seven years on (`MD-EV-V01-0018`, `MD-EV-V14-0010`). Her stated fears are specific. She fears falling because she remembers two fractures (`MD-EV-V07-0025`). She fears being thought not in earnest (`MD-EV-V13-0008`). She fears hurting people without knowing it (`MD-EV-V15-0013`). She does not fear failure or laughter, by her own word.

The ambivalence lies between two accounts of why she skates. In one she is drawn: by what lies beyond a difficulty, by steps she loves, by Hikaru, whom she calls a light of hope (`MD-EV-V04-0038`, `MD-EV-V09-0028`, `MD-EV-V13-0025`). In the other she pays: for Tsukasa's choice, for her mother's absence, for many kind people's work. She does not experience these as opposed. `MD-CL-0015@14` puts it that she plans, calmly, to pay.

## Attention and how she learns

The work's best-supported distinction about Inori Yuitsuka is between two modes (`MD-CL-0015@6` onward). In the compelled mode a closing window governs; she cannot perceive, hurries, and pleads. In the attentive mode she inventories sensation and can act. Both have appeared within ten pages in pursuit of one jump. Since Volume 11 she distinguishes them herself.

She learns fastest from what arrives sideways. Technique is extracted from Tsukasa Akeuraji's example (`MD-EV-V03-0027`), from staring at Sonidori Rio (`MD-MOT-0005`), from a contest, a quota, a story. Instruction that arrives as correction is often not taken in: a rule about junior layouts told repeatedly and not absorbed (`MD-EV-V11-0024`), the empty assent Tsukasa learns to recognize (`MD-EV-V11-0032`), a platform speech of which only the first clause lands (`MD-EV-V13-0039`). Tsukasa's own account in Volume 10, that her speed came from a concentration that pares her down and narrows her view, is his inference (`MD-EV-V10-0044`).

The established mechanism belongs here. She reads adults' faces first and assigns their cause to herself (`MD-CL-0028@5`). She said so of Tsukasa's troubled look (`MD-EV-V13-0040`); a comic scene has her read disappointment where he meant self-reproach (`MD-EV-V14-0004`); Kanayumi Miho, the trainer, states it as fact (`MD-EV-V15-0017`). Kanayumi also identifies Tsukasa's readable face as the reason a child who probes adults could trust him (`MD-EV-V15-0023`). The hypothesis that her capacities were formed under deprivation is not adopted. Her own dating of the feeling to middle school tells against extending it backward.

## Decision patterns

Four patterns in Inori Yuitsuka's conduct have enough independent instances to state.

She commits aloud under strong feeling before people above her: to Rio, to Riina, to Iruka, to Hikaru, to the press (`MD-CL-0002@6`, `MD-EV-V10-0040`, `MD-EV-V15-0016`). Each commitment is provoked.

She deliberates well when given branches. The quadruple was chosen against her first leaning by a method she asked for (`MD-EV-V07-0024`). The offer of a new club was deferred until she is convinced (`MD-EV-V11-0046`).

She conceals for another's sake and takes the cost on herself: her reason for hurry (V04), her delight (V07), the Lutz (V09), her difficulties (V10), her homesickness (V11), her school-day training (V13), and on the last evening the claim that nothing is wrong (`MD-EV-V15-0047`). `MD-CL-0002@7` calls shielding Tsukasa Akeuraji a standing feature of how she is with him. The same volume in which she states a principle against harming people in ignorance is the one in which she has kept Tsukasa ignorant for a year; the page does not draw the connection, and within the day she tells him.

She acts alone at the decisive point when the compelled mode decides. The layout change and the triple Axel are the clear case (`MD-EV-V09-0030`). It has one instance in competition. The Volume 13 truancy is its off-ice form. Whether Volume 15 changes this is untested.

## Ordinary behaviour

The corpus is thin on Inori Yuitsuka's life away from evaluation, and `MD-CL-0015@1` already flagged the gap. What is shown is consistent. She loses and forgets things: skates on a train, a timetable, what the world ranking is, a lesson (`MD-EV-V03-0006`, `MD-EV-V11-0051`, `MD-EV-V14-0002`, `MD-EV-V14-0004`). Each time her first response is self-reproach, sometimes comic. Worms are a private resource from the age of seven: currency for ice, a way to settle nerves, the creature with which she could skate (`MD-EV-V02-0036`, `MD-EV-V04-0037`). She gives away the sweets she packed for herself (`MD-EV-V11-0013`). She eats with her team before a competition and asks for a treat after a loss while performing cheer (`MD-EV-V13-0002`, `MD-EV-V13-0007`). School is shown as a place of difficulty in Volume 1 and afterwards mainly as the rule she breaks in Volume 13. She makes friends easily and across rivalry: Ploy Sepaban, a Thai skater, and Okazaki Iruka, the winner, whom she invites shopping after losing to her (`MD-EV-V11-0039`, `MD-CS-0243`).

No claim about her temperament outside sport is made beyond these.

## What skating is to Inori

It is the one place where Inori Yuitsuka is not the child for whom failure was arranged in advance. A memory of herself at seven carries the thought that the skates make her strong (`MD-EV-V03-0025`), and at thirteen she says that she wants to live on the ice and cannot let go of it whoever suffers (`MD-EV-V15-0048`). It is also a debt register. Both are in the same sentence of the stairwell: she needs the gold in order to stay. Skating is therefore home and tenancy at once. The study does not rank them.

## Authority and agency

Inori Yuitsuka's relation to authority is not obedience. She has disagreed with Tsukasa Akeuraji on reasons and prevailed (`MD-EV-V08-0027`), overridden him in a program (`MD-EV-V09-0030`), deceived him (`MD-EV-V13-0034`), and refused his train. She seeks from Okazaki Iruka, a rival, the unkind information adults omit (`MD-EV-V11-0014`). She also credits Tsukasa with the habit of deciding only when convinced. `MD-CL-0002@15` records the endpoint: a claim to the right to bear a cost, each sentence of which refuses the position of someone to be relieved.

The rival mechanism has been live since `MD-CL-0002@1`: that her initiative is rewarded compliance with an admired adult. Volumes 8, 9 and 13 leave it little room. What remains is narrower. Her largest independent acts have been taken on Tsukasa's behalf.

## Family and resources

Nozomi Yuitsuka, Inori's mother, first refused, by her own account out of fear of others' judgements, and changed after watching her daughter fall and stand (`MD-EV-V02-0010`, `MD-CL-0014@1`). Since then the household's care is constant and quiet: costumes, a promise of support when the skates are lost, nights beside Inori since the growing pains began, a telephone's position checked when she vanishes (`MD-EV-V03-0006`, `MD-EV-V11-0052`, `MD-EV-V13-0041`). Nozomi returned to full-time work in Inori's first junior spring (`MD-EV-V10-0006`). Inori's father cooks at home and joins her abroad (`MD-EV-V02-0025`, `MD-EV-V11-0040`). No one at home makes results a subject.

`MD-CL-0014@9` states the limit this reaches: the household that never presses her is the one in which Inori has concluded that her failures make her mother work. Her resources otherwise are a small club, a coach, Tsukasa, whose income is low by a bonus chapter (`MD-EV-V01-0036`), Sekoma's private subsidy, and from Volume 9 a federation designation. She is aware of being supported and counts it as cost.

## How Inori is with different people

**Tsukasa Akeuraji, her coach.** Inori Yuitsuka addresses him by his given name with the teacher's title from the first lesson (`MD-EV-V01-0009`), holds the cords of his jacket before she skates, borrows his manner when alone (`MD-EV-V05-0004`), and has made his standing her stake since Volume 8. She manages what he sees. She wanted his face to share her anger and found worry (`MD-EV-V13-0040`). At the boundary each has disclosed present burdens and commitments (`MD-RL-0359`, `MD-RL-0360`, `MD-RL-0361`). Inori has not explicitly voiced the older belief that she ended his skating career, and accurate hearing does not establish that her burden has lifted (`MD-KN-0147`, `MD-KN-0153`).

**Nozomi Yuitsuka, her mother.** The person to whom Inori first said what she wanted (`MD-RL-0024`), for whom she performs contentment after a loss, and to whom alone she says the thing about vanishing, then takes it back (`MD-RL-0358`).

**Mika Yuitsuka, her sister.** Inori began skating for Mika, will not hear her called bad at jumps (`MD-RL-0132`, `MD-EV-V06-0048`), and keeps her grief for her (`MD-EV-V09-0043`). Mika's fracture is one of Inori's two sources of fear and one of three stopped skaters she thinks of on the bench (`MD-EV-V13-0041`).

**Hikaru Kamisaki.** Friend before rival; then the measure. Inori named her a special rival for the whole road (`MD-EV-V08-0027`), checks an unworthy wish against what Hikaru would never feel (`MD-RL-0266`), cannot face her after failing, and calls her a light of hope (`MD-RL-0301`). Hikaru, for her part, withdrew her reading of Inori as the likeness of Hikaru's own coach, Yodaka Jun, privately after seeing that the laughter was for the adults (`MD-RL-0300`; see its channel erratum). Hikaru’s strongest-self rival judgment and Inori’s light-of-hope thought remain separate interiors (`MD-EV-V13-0025`), alongside their actually spoken promise in the thicket.

**Okazaki Iruka.** Fear, then defiance, then confidence. Inori took a fall to shield Iruka (`MD-EV-V10-0020`), confided her shame to her and took her hand (`MD-RL-0258`), was told by Iruka that she has a complex about being treasured, and now means to skate for Iruka's share and wears her hair after Iruka's (`MD-EV-V14-0006`, `MD-EV-V15-0053`, the last a strip).

**Karasuba Daria.** Inori took Daria's counsel at face value, sensed anger she could not name, and when offered an apology asked what she had done and gave thanks for the anger (`MD-RL-0330`, `MD-RL-0341`).

**Ryoka Miketa.** Inori's first friend in skating; the one Inori sought out after beating her, asking only whether they could skate together (`MD-RL-0026`); the first person told her secret (`MD-EV-V04-0012`).

With peers Inori is open, quick to repair and unresentful. With adults who care for her she is bright and guarded. The contrast is one of the firmer findings of the corpus and is noticed inside the story by Hikaru (`MD-EV-V13-0008`).

## Written Japanese

The readings record wording, not sound. Inori Yuitsuka's Volume 4 wish turns on 選べる, to be able to choose; her Volume 9 answer to Ahiru Miku, a skater who quit for others' sake, is the plain past of the same verb. Her word for what gold supplies is 証明, proof, which passed to her from Yodaka Jun by way of Tsukasa Akeuraji. Her gratitude in Volume 9 is for being 期待される. The key word of Volume 13 is 本気. In the pool her words for herself are ずるい and 恥ずかしい, and Okazaki Iruka's word for what Inori has a complex about is 大事にされる. In Volume 14 her two captions turn on 力: not hers alone, then her own. To the press she uses 負担 for what she has been, 報いる for what she must do, and 許してもらえた of the self permitted to skate. To Tsukasa she says 役目 of the role she thought she had been entrusted with (`MD-EV-V15-0048`; original occurrence 0185). The term 冷たい気持ち is first hers in Volume 2’s Short Program 3 (`MD-EV-V02-0030`, bonus fiction); she bids it farewell on Volume 4’s story pages (`MD-EV-V04-0038`), and Tsukasa reuses it in Volume 13. She mishears a loan-word for resilience and later acts it out; she does not know Karasuba Daria's word for presumptuous and asks. An editorial aside notes that she has little dialect (`MD-EV-V01-0034`).

Original dialogue supports an interlocutor contrast, with exceptions. She uses 私/わたし and 司先生 with polite requests to Tsukasa, but the gold exchange also gives the plain contracted question 先生も信じてる？ (`MD-EV-V06-0003`). Her V15 mother-facing reassurance has plain endings よ and さ; after Tsukasa arrives she replies 大丈夫です (`MD-EV-V15-0046`, occurrences 0175–0179). Formal grammar can carry an uncompromising refusal, as with Iruka and in the entrusted-role speech. These are located written-language findings, not a frequency census or response rule; the [speech/interlocutor audit](../../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/MEDALIST_JAPANESE_SPEECH_AND_INTERLOCUTOR_AUDIT_THROUGH_V15.md) owns the diagnostic examples and exceptions.

## How the manga frames Inori

**The cold feeling (`MD-MOT-0015`).** A verbal motif Inori Yuitsuka and Tsukasa Akeuraji share for effort turned against the self. Inori named it and bade it farewell; Tsukasa asked whether her strength was built on it; he used it of her self-punishment and of his own.

**Falling and getting up (`MD-MOT-0006`).** Her first competition jump is a fall answered by a smile. By Volume 9 a fall restores feeling to her legs and staying upright is not success. By Volume 13 getting up is an aim set beside the medal; by Volume 15 its obstacle is named as guilt. In Volume 14 recovery is drawn from a memory and not from an error.

**Seeing and being seen (`MD-MOT-0005`).** Inori asked to be watched at eleven. She chose a quadruple for the attention. At thirteen being seen is what punishes: Hikaru as the one person she cannot bear to be seen by, an adult's face as reward or penalty. Volume 15 draws her small behind Tsukasa, looking at him as he vows to win (`MD-VF-0136`).

**The double and the water (`MD-MOT-0016`).** A lake imagined as ice steadies her from Volume 7. In Volume 14 the film's ghost wears the hero's face, Tsukasa is drawn as the ghost, and Inori ends over a reflection that is only hers. In Volume 15 she says she would let the double take her over. The work does not endorse it.

One image in Volume 15 is an adult's inference drawn as a picture of her and should be read as such: a page of Inori kneeling among broken crystal under Kanayumi Miho's sentence about punishment (`MD-VF-0137`). The stairwell's own image, a girl in a team jacket holding pale lumps and ringed by pointing hands, accompanies Inori's statement and repeats a ring drawn earlier round Tsukasa as a boy (`MD-VF-0144`).

## Competence versus confidence; limits

Inori Yuitsuka's competence is documented by calls and outside appraisal: level-four step sequences at three events, the highest printed component total among those who had then skated in the final’s short program; Baek Horang’s component breakdown is not printed, and Baek Horang's coach, a rival coach, who reads a placing from Inori's levels and spins (`MD-CL-0008@13`, `MD-CL-0008@14`, `MD-SK-0089`). Her confidence in execution is real and trained (`MD-SK-0088`). Neither prevented November, and the foundation is not a guarantee (`MD-EV-V13-0005`).

Her demonstrated limits are these. Under a closing deadline her acceptance of limits has depended on someone else saying it (`MD-CL-0019@5`). She has not been shown recovering inside a program after an error since the aim was set. She trains through pain and has to be rationed (`MD-EV-V03-0032`, `MD-EV-V11-0010`). She cannot see her own habits of feeling and can name Tsukasa Akeuraji's (`MD-EV-V14-0027`). She does not take in what arrives as reassurance, and now says why.

## The two wishes, and whose account it is

### What Kanayumi says and what Inori says

The phrase about the sacrifice of Tsukasa's life is Kanayumi's inference. It has grounds and may be right. It is not in Inori's words. Punishment, likewise, is Kanayumi's word here; it was Inori's own vocabulary once, in the approach to a jump in Volume 9 (`MD-EV-V09-0032`), and Tsukasa's name for her conduct in Volume 13. In Volume 15 Inori does not use it, and when Tsukasa says that neither of them needs punishing, her uptake is a word of assent (`MD-EV-V15-0051`).

Inori's own account adds what no adult anticipated. Being told that she need not strain is what she hates; it is like having her strength drained. She was glad in December because she took Tsukasa's history as a part entrusted to her. She wants to make him happy as he wants to make her happy. She will not be treated as weak. And it leaves something out. The oldest form of the belief, that she ended Tsukasa's own skating, held since Volume 9 and restated in November (`MD-KN-0123`), is neither voiced nor withdrawn. What she tells Tsukasa concerns his present labour. Whether she still holds the older form is not shown.

### Two wishes left undistinguished

To Tsukasa, standing, Inori says she wants to hold all of it, to carry and carry, and still be the strongest skater, who lands lightly. To her mother, Nozomi, earlier the same evening and lying down, she says she would not mind being taken over by the gifted double and vanishing, if that made everyone happy; then that it is only a film.

The two share a premise. The people who help her are owed their return, and the return is the result. They differ in what becomes of her. In the first there is a bearer, and the bearer is the point: `MD-CL-0002@15` reads every sentence as a refusal to be relieved. In the second the bearer is removed and the gift remains. `MD-CL-0029@5` calls the second a darker form of the same thought. Inori does not relate them, and the work does not relate them for her (`MD-OQ-V15-03`).

Three readings are compatible with the page.

1. They are one wish at two levels of guard. With Tsukasa she states the version that keeps her dignity; with her mother, at rest, the version in which the cost of being herself is highest. On this reading the role is partly a defence against the wish to disappear.
2. The wish to carry is the considered position and the remark about vanishing is a thought about a film, tried aloud and dropped. She follows it at once by saying she is glad she can skate. A month earlier she captioned the same ending as her own and finished over her own reflection (`MD-EV-V14-0029`, `MD-EV-V14-0030`).
3. They are in tension and she has not noticed. The first asserts a self; the second gives one up. Tsukasa's test, to bear only what she can call her own life, and his warning that winning for his life would mean living his life, are aimed at exactly this difference, though Tsukasa has not been told of the second wish (`MD-KN-0148`).

The evidence does not select among them. Against the first reading, the stairwell statement was not prepared: Inori had just denied having anything to discuss, and it came when she asked to stand. Against the second, Nozomi was alarmed, and the page leaves the alarm unanswered. Against the third, nothing shows the two colliding. The film's ending is not applied by the work to Inori and Tsukasa; in Volume 15 Tsukasa promises to stay and to have a life of his own.

What would discriminate: Inori's conduct after a result of either colour; whether she ever declines a burden by Tsukasa's test; whether she tells Tsukasa what she told her mother; how she treats the ordinary self when the gift fails her inside a program.

The following is fanbook material: the author's conception as reported by an editorial writer in October 2024 and September 2025, expressly revisable, or reproduced working drafts. None of it is story fact, and no trait above rests on it.

## Why no operational reconstruction model is offered

Three reasons, each sufficient.

The boundary falls inside a competition. Inori Yuitsuka's state is defined by a segment not yet skated; any rule about her conduct under pressure would be fitted to a test whose result is unknown.

The change of Volume 15 is untested. She agreed to a test for burdens and withdrew nothing. No page shows her after the stairwell except a warm-up. A model would have to choose between the December vow, the creed and the new rule as the governing state, and the evidence does not choose (`MD-RD-0072`).

The series is ongoing, and this project has already had to withdraw one confident reading of her on the next volume's evidence. The readiness ledger records a bounded provisional monograph and no model (`MD-RD-0072`, `MD-RD-0077`). No reconstruction rule is proposed here.

The endpoint is `MD-FR-FB02`: Japanese manga Volumes 1–15, Scores 1–61, with Official Fanbooks 01 and 02 as audited supplements.
