# Character reconstruction contract

This defines future character work; it is not a profile of Inori, Tsukasa or any other character. It is subordinate to [the global reconstruction specification](../../../characters/RECONSTRUCTION_CAPABILITY_SPEC.md). The [character/readiness ledger](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/MEDALIST_CHARACTER_DEVELOPMENT_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md) has no subjects or assessments at bootstrap.

The V01 closure now records separate evidence-based assessments in that ledger: bounded protagonist/supporting-role arguments, insufficient operational-model evidence and unresolved/excluded fanbooks for this manga-only scope. No character package, reconstruction rule or global capability is promoted by the reading. The contract below is unchanged.

## Separate promotion decisions

Use project-local `MD-CHAR-####` keys upon first admitted evidence. Resolve any existing global entity/subject through its registry before proposing enrollment; leave global IDs null until curated. Manga and adaptation subjects retain separate continuity. A normal developmental change does not create a new global character.

| Artifact decision | Required evidence and analytical responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Dedicated character evidence index | Recurring observations across at least two materially different situations are hard to retrieve through ensemble ledgers; stable source/observation links can solve that gap. |
| Scoped character monograph | A sustained developmental/psychological argument across a closed tranche, covering behavior and self-report, consequential relationships, competence/limits, Japanese speech where meaningful, manga presentation, counterexamples and unresolved questions. A bounded supporting-character study may be narrower and must say so. |
| Operational reconstruction model | Time-indexed, knowledge- and relationship-conditioned rules that exclude plausible responses, expose alternatives and support real tests. A monograph's existence does not establish this readiness. |
| Simulation/domain use | Required context/relationship/state coverage plus rule validation and abstention conditions; readiness for one domain is not universal capability or global grading. |

Counts are necessary retrieval indicators, not sufficient thresholds. Multiple quotations of one scene are one context. For a durable disposition normally require corroboration in multiple independent situations and explicit counterexample search; a single scene may support only a narrow, situational rule. A reviewer must assess whether the proposed scope exceeds the actual evidence. No numeric personality score or A–E capability grade is assigned here.

## Monograph/model content

Distinguish stable dispositions from immediate state, vulnerability/coping from an isolated reaction, internal goals from imposed objectives, memories/self-account from witnessed past, competence from confidence, and demonstrated limitations from self-deprecation. Connect changes to identifiable events without assuming every difference is growth.

A substantial monograph explains self-concept, motivations/fears/ambivalence, attention and appraisal, decision patterns, learning, ordinary behavior, interpersonal expectations, skating's significance, authority/agency, family/resources, speech/presentation, formal framing and limits. Answer each with evidence or explicit uncertainty; do not force absent domains into speculative prose. Monographs, models and evidence indexes name their subjects and every other character under the method's [entity naming and referential clarity](MEDALIST_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md#entity-naming-and-referential-clarity) rule.

The operational model uses:

`identity + continuity + source-time + knowledge + relationship + available options + bodily/resource constraints + stakes → appraisal → competing goals → bounded response range`.

For each `MD-RULE-####` record applicability, trigger, required knowledge, relationship condition, proposed mechanism, supporting observation IDs, counterexamples, alternative mechanisms, negative constraints, confidence/basis and a discriminating test. Distinguish `DISPOSITION_CHANGE / KNOWLEDGE_CHANGE / RELATIONSHIP_CHANGE / CONTEXT_CHANGE / REVEALED_NOT_NEW / UNRESOLVED`.

Coaching models additionally separate a coach's technical diagnosis, pedagogical strategy, perception of athlete needs, decision and athlete response. Athlete models separate training capacity, performance under pressure, self-efficacy and judged results. Do not equate competition rank with character worth, assume coach authority is benevolent/abusive, or infer an injury/diagnosis without suitable evidence.

## Directional relationships and speech

Model A→B and B→A independently using the relationship ledger. State what each knows, wants, expects and misunderstands; trust, dependence, rivalry, admiration, cooperation and refusal may coexist. Trace available alternatives and power asymmetry, keeping structural conditions in the institutions ledger. Less-focalized characters receive narrower motive claims rather than an invented interior.

Written Japanese constraints include interlocutor-specific address, honorifics, self-reference, register, withholding and repair where inspected. Source text, gloss and uncertainty belong to original observation rows. Manga evidence does not establish actor voice. The model must not supply later knowledge, future maturity, contemporary therapeutic vocabulary or stereotyped motivational speeches without a source basis.

## Readiness and validation

The local ledger separately assesses monograph and model domains as `UNASSESSED / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / BOUNDED_PROVISIONAL / DOMAIN_READY`. Each assessment names source endpoint, context, independent evidence, counterevidence, omissions, test status and reviewer/date. The absence of a row means unassessed, not ready.

Every proposed monograph/model scope declares the manga boundary and admitted supplemental set. Review relevant fanbook dispositions as `NOT_DUE / CLOSED_INTEGRATED / EXPLICITLY_EXCLUDED / UNRESOLVED`; NOT_DUE needs a verified later admission position. Complete due fanbook audits before claiming a combined boundary synthesis, or explicitly limit a provisional study to manga-only evidence with omissions and consequences for readiness. Unverified coverage is unresolved, not proof that a supplement is irrelevant.

Editorial profiles and relationship captions do not replace observed behavior; ratings retain their evaluator/date/axes. Creator commentary supports attributed intent, not automatic interiority or character knowledge. Verified bonus fiction may add a distinct context, while reprints add no independent situation. A retrospective disclosure changes the current model through a named claim revision and knowledge boundary; it does not grant an earlier character later information or rewrite the earlier prospective rule/test.

Use retrospective source reproduction, contrasting interlocutors/stakes, negative constraints and ordinary-life tests. Prospective tests must be frozen before later eligible evidence; specify an actual opportunity to test, an adjudication window and falsifier. No opportunity means `UNTESTED`. Model familiarity can contaminate holdouts; label remembered-source fitting honestly.

Generated scenarios and a model's self-consistency are not manga evidence. Cross-series transfer is a separate extrapolation decision with changed premises, knowledge/resources supplied, evidence distance, plausible range and abstention. Readiness is bounded by domain and state; an owner/authorized global process controls formal capability assessments and discovery.

## Revision and acceptance

Keep the same topical home and rule IDs across sessions. Evidence-driven revisions go through the claims ledger and current model; preserve rejected rules and test history. A failure may expose wrong knowledge, overgeneralization or an unmodeled relationship condition. Do not rescue every rule through unfalsifiable exceptions.

A monograph must make an explanatory argument, and a model must constrain behavior. Review these independently after source/ledger reconciliation. Future packages may use `04 Character and Relationship Synthesis/<local-subject>/` for monograph, model and evidence index only when independently justified; there are no placeholder character packages at bootstrap. Specialized global curation remains outside the authoring scope.
