# Analytical method

This method decides how to observe and judge the Japanese manga by Tsurumaikada. The [synthesis architecture](MEDALIST_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) allocates accumulated results; the [reading specification](MEDALIST_SOURCE_UNIT_READING_SPEC.md) defines transaction closure. This is an `INITIAL`, `active_provisional` foundation awaiting owner review. It establishes no narrative conclusions and authorizes no manga reading.

## Witness hierarchy and scope

Japanese collected and serialized manga constitute the primary continuity. Use one authenticated Japanese tankōbon edition as the default sequential witness; magazine chapters, official digital serialization, revised editions, bonuses and colophons retain separate witness identities. A collected revision governs a reading of that edition without erasing what its earlier magazine witness established. Compare both when a material difference exists. Record whether a statement describes continuity, an edition-specific rendering, or publication history.

The [source map](MEDALIST_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE_MAP.md) separates source inventory, structural verification, admission, narrative inspection, sequential closure and public publication boundaries. Anime S1/S2 belong to adaptation continuity; creator/publisher commentary to supplemental evidence; localization to translation witnesses; reception to secondary interpretation. None fills an absent Japanese volume or silently supplies a character's manga history. The present V15/Score 61 lead does not authorize skipping to V13 or reading V01–V15 retrospectively.

## Fanbook and other supplemental source authority

Classify an official fanbook by component rather than assigning the whole book one literary rank. The COTE guidebook precedent supplies the four core distinctions DATA, RATING, EDITORIAL and FICTION; Medalist additionally distinguishes attributed creator commentary and technical reference. These are source layers, orthogonal to the observation/inference/confidence labels below. Classifications here are a future contract, not a claim that either available fanbook contains every class.

| Source layer | Permitted use and limit |
| --- | --- |
| `FB-DATA` | Explicit documentary facts such as dated biographical fields, chronology or recorded results; attribute them to the fanbook and retain scope/date. A documentary record does not automatically settle a disputed depicted event. |
| `FB-RATING` | Measurements, evaluations or rankings if present; retain evaluator, date, axes and observation conditions. A score or result is not total competence, personality or human worth. |
| `FB-EDITORIAL` | Retrospective profiles, relationship captions, summaries and official framing; compare with richer manga evidence rather than importing the caption as a motive or timeless trait. |
| `FB-FICTION` | Verified creator-authorized bonus manga or other new fiction, with continuity and disclosure position established. It is literary evidence in its own witness, not merely editorial commentary; gag, crossover or unresolved-continuity material remains labeled accordingly. |
| `FB-CREATOR` | Attributed creator interviews or production commentary; evidence for the stated intention/process. Intent, depicted effect and a character's knowledge remain separate. |
| `FB-TECHNICAL` | Attributed skating explanations or reference material; distinguish the book's explanation from in-story statements and independently verified external knowledge. Real-world applicability still requires the technical/date/category checks below. |

Inventory component form as well: original/reprinted manga, profile, interview, illustration, table, explanation or promotion. Reprints cross-reference the prior witness and do not supply another independent context. Illustrations may support a bounded visual claim, not an assumed mainline event; promotional/later-territory components have explicit exclusions. Unknown attribution or continuity stays unresolved.

Encounter supplements at their verified analytical position under the architecture's checkpoint rule. Preserve story chronology, publication chronology, analytical disclosure order and character knowledge separately. A newly disclosed old fact is not automatically new character development. A fanbook adds to or revises current interpretation through an attributed later observation; it never silently replaces an earlier manga freeze or grants a character the reader's later knowledge.

## Evidence grammar

Every consequential observation identifies the exact witness, local locator, speaker/observer, represented time, available context and inspection limitations. Use the following labels consistently:

| Class | Evidential force |
| --- | --- |
| `OBSERVATION` | A feature actually inspected on the source page; includes a depicted action or attributed utterance. |
| `SELF_REPORT` | What a character says, thinks or remembers; sincerity, accuracy and persistence remain separate questions. |
| `INFERENCE` | A supported explanatory claim with its premises, alternatives and possible falsifier. |
| `HYPOTHESIS` | A live account awaiting discriminating evidence. |
| `EXTERNAL_REFERENCE` | Verified knowledge about real-world skating, institutions or publication; not manga narrative evidence. |
| `EVALUATION` | An ethical/aesthetic judgment with criterion and descriptive premises stated. |
| `UNRESOLVED` | Evidence gaps or surviving alternatives prevent adjudication. |

Separate event, motive, narrative framing, creator intent and reception. The source depicting someone's belief does not establish its truth. Use qualitative confidence `HIGH / MODERATE / LOW / UNRESOLVED` with a reason for the particular proposition; transcription/identification confidence and interpretive confidence may differ. Confidence is not an invented probability or a substitute for a locator.

## Prospective transaction

The default atomic unit is one complete Japanese tankōbon volume; chapters and page sequences are internal units. Freeze entering state from preceding closed manga evidence before opening the candidate. V01 enters with no manga findings, character states or predictions. Record prior exposure, including incidental later-volume publisher snippets returned during reconnaissance; evidence-bounded prospectivity is not a claim that the analyst is blind or lacks model familiarity.

Read admitted mainline pages in verified spine/chapter order, including silent pages and sequences that resist OCR. Track actual coverage and excluded bonus/paratext components. Within-volume impressions may change; record diagnostic immediate impressions separately from the final interpretation through that volume. Freeze exit state before opening the next unit. Later revelations update retrospective records and current claims, never the earlier prospective freeze or original prediction.

A magazine-only Score can be one transaction after its exact placement and complete installment are verified. Website splits such as ①/② are delivery segments, not automatically independent chapters. An unusually long chapter may be segmented for processing but retains one exit freeze unless an owner-reviewed architecture amendment justifies a different analytical unit. Do not create a tankōbon freeze from a mixture of unverified editions.

## Character development and psychology

For Inori Yuitsuka, Tsukasa Akeuraji and consequential characters encountered in admitted evidence, distinguish expressed self-concept, observed capability, externally attributed ability and inferred self-efficacy. Track aspirations, internal/external goals, fear, shame, pride, confidence, help-seeking, coping, setbacks, learning and relationships to skating only when evidenced.

A transient reaction is not a durable disposition. Test persistence across materially different situations; identify what changed, what merely became visible and what reflects a new context or new knowledge. Preserve contradictory behavior and differences between thought, speech and action. Avoid diagnostic labels and invented latent motives. A claimed developmental mechanism needs a precipitating event, a plausible link and competing explanations.

Written Japanese evidence belongs beside the observation that needs it: address/honorifics, self-reference, politeness, hedging, intensity, evasion, terminology and register shifts. Retain only short diagnostic wording, an attributed gloss, translation alternatives and exact balloon locator. Distinguish thought, narrated text and spoken dialogue. Lettering, punctuation and a drawn expression cannot establish acoustic timbre, pitch or actor delivery.

## Coaching, mentorship, authority and care

Capture athlete→coach and coach→athlete separately: expectations, trust, dependence, disclosure, instruction, challenge, reassurance, refusal and repair. Record age/experience/institutional asymmetry only where established, available alternatives, costs of refusal, who initiates goals and who can revise them. Ask how the coach learns and how technical objectives interact with confidence, agency and welfare.

Evaluate demanding coaching by evidenced conduct and consequences; success does not itself validate a method, and demanding instruction does not itself prove abuse. Distinguish an adult's intention, understanding of the athlete, actual intervention and effect. A young athlete's assent does not settle whether sacrifice was voluntary; assess viable choices and pressure. Ethical claims require an explicit care/agency/responsibility criterion and counterevidence.

## Figure skating as a technical system

Keep four channels explicit: `IN_UNIVERSE_STATEMENT`, `DEPICTED_PERFORMANCE`, `VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_KNOWLEDGE` and `ANALYTICAL_INFERENCE`. Capture jump identity and attempted rotations only to demonstrated resolution; takeoff/edge, air position, landing, spin/step features, choreography, program composition, training constraints, conditioning, fatigue, injury/recovery, strategy and competition results may warrant distinct observations.

A panel can suggest a trajectory or position without establishing full rotation, edge correctness, execution quality or a judge's call. Prefer an attributed in-story diagnosis when that is all the source provides; do not promote it to independent technical measurement. Record attempted element, depicted outcome, character commentary, official in-story call and analyst interpretation separately. Separate attempt, successful execution, repeatability in training and success under competition pressure.

When scoring matters, record event/category, program segment, stated rules, date/season if known, attempted/called element, base value, grade/execution adjustment, components, deductions and result only where supplied. Preserve unknown fields. Do not reconstruct an official total from incomplete panels. Arithmetic checks may verify stated numbers; they do not authenticate the governing rules or judge an unseen performance.

External comparisons require an identified official ISU/JSF or competition document, edition/effective season, section, age category and match to the in-story context. Store that reference and applicability assessment in the skating ledger when actually used. A contemporary rule set cannot silently govern an undated or fictional competition. External biomechanics or injury claims need appropriate primary specialist evidence; neither a drawing nor an inferred emotion supplies medical facts.

## Competition, institutions and artistic expression

Rivalry can coexist with admiration, friendship, envy, cooperation, respect and resource asymmetry. Track directional aims, recognition and status, what each participant knows/misunderstands, and behavior under comparison. Losing/winning is a competitive event, not an automatic psychological or ethical verdict.

Record resources and constraints: fees, facilities, ice time, equipment, coaching, travel, family obligations, selection rules and credentialing. Distinguish an evidenced barrier from a broader claim about Japanese skating. Attribute institutional rules and separate nominal rules from implementation. Depicted inequality does not by itself establish the work's political endorsement.

For each consequential performance, keep technical execution, expressive choices, judged evaluation, audience reaction and the performer's own aim distinguishable. Ask whether the work connects or tensions these dimensions; do not presume technique and artistry are opposed. The sports ledger owns performance facts, the visual ledger owns how pages render them, and a linked claim carries interpretation of their relationship.

## Manga form and locators

Inspect panel scale/order, reading rhythm, page turns, spatial continuity, speed/rotation marks, implied motion, negative space, viewpoint, faces, posture, gesture, internal/external juxtaposition and repeated composition. Describe the formal feature first, then the interpretive effect and an alternative. Check page boundaries and facing-page/spread layout before claiming reveal timing; a viewer's single-image display can alter that experience.

Follow the source map's locator contract: immutable witness key + volume + verified Score + ordered spine/image coordinate + panel/balloon if needed. Digital coordinates never imply printed pagination. Evidence observation records live in frozen source-unit readings; repeated visual features link those observation IDs through the visual ledger. Copyrighted pages, page archives, OCR dumps and bulk dialogue remain outside Git.

## Contradiction and completion

Before harmonizing an apparent contradiction, test time, perspective, incomplete knowledge, deception, remembered versus shown events, edition revision, localization and adaptation differences, paratext scope and genuine inconsistency. Register competing accounts when no test settles them. Material changes use `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN` in the claims ledger.

A reading completes only when full admitted coverage, claims/counterevidence, affected ledgers, readiness implications, locators, prediction adjudication and frozen exit state are consistent and the repository transaction closes. [The reading specification](MEDALIST_SOURCE_UNIT_READING_SPEC.md) supplies the reusable contract. Missing primary access holds the affected reading unavailable; summaries and later witnesses cannot repair it. The sequential lock remains CLOSED until explicit owner approval and all applicable initiation/admission requirements.
