# Synthesis and corpus architecture

This architecture defines what the prospective manga readings build. The [method](MEDALIST_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs how evidence is judged. Generation `MEDALIST_BOOTSTRAP_V1` begins at lifecycle **INITIAL** with **active_provisional** authority, pending owner review, zero narrative findings and no predecessor architecture superseded. `canonical_home` identifies the sole intended home; it does not claim owner approval or promotion to canonical status.

Higher-level authority is the live Git epoch/scope, initiation policy, authority/supersession policy, reasoning/capability and long-series policies, handoff/continuation contracts, source boundaries and repository checklist. [The current entrypoint](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) links those controls and is the only first-read project surface.

## Source model and topology

The [source map](MEDALIST_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE_MAP.md) owns exact source identity, availability, admission and public publication reconnaissance. Japanese collected/serialized manga are primary continuity with edition-specific witnesses; numbered tankōbon volumes are the default sequence. Magazine-only chapters, delivery splits, collected revisions, bonuses and paratext are classified independently. Anime S1/S2, translation, creator/publisher supplements and reception are distinct evidence lanes. No complete manga/serialization ceiling is inferred from the visible Drive subset.

Existing directories have actual responsibilities: `00 Frameworks and Methods/` holds five governing specifications; `03 Longitudinal Ledgers/` holds seven justified cumulative instruments; `07 Audits and Handoffs/` holds the bootstrap receipt. Future `02 Sequential Readings/`, `04 Character and Relationship Synthesis/` and `05 Specialist Synthesis/` arise only with real admitted/artifact-ready work. Checkpoints/audits remain in the audits area; a future release has an explicit scope manifest rather than a competing entrypoint. No empty directories or placeholder character/specialist files are created.

Git contains interpretive Markdown, contracts and sanitized provenance. Drive contains primary/derived evidence; temporary processing/transport artifacts remain in the working plane. Source authentication, narrative inspection, publication, semantic acceptance and integration are different states.

## Accumulation and single-home rule

`source identity → frozen source observations → scoped claims and domain states → justified specialists → reconciled synthesis`.

The source-unit reading is the canonical home of the inspected page/panel observation `MD-EV-…` and its context. A ledger links that observation and owns only its distinctive cumulative responsibility. The claims ledger owns claim identity/version, confidence, alternatives and revision. Domain ledgers own state histories and queryable projections citing claim versions. Source identity remains in the source map. A specialist makes a scoped argument from those records rather than becoming another factual ledger.

Evidence local to one unit with no recurring or downstream value remains in that reading. Japanese wording/gloss belongs beside the observation that needs it, linked from character/relationship/form rows; no separate language ledger is warranted at day one. Character development and readiness are separate record types in one home because readiness is derived from that character's state evidence and gaps. Ethical claims link conduct/options in the relationship/institutional homes with explicit evaluative criteria in the claims ledger; there is no generic ethics ledger.

## Ledger record and history contract

Each ledger supplies its own schema, identifiers, row trigger, evidence requirements and history behavior. Prefixes `MD-CS / RL / SK / IN / TM / KN / VF / CL / PR / RV` never migrate to another responsibility. IDs remain stable; numbers express allocation order, not assumed story chronology. Typed links connect cast, competitions, performances, propositions, motifs and external rules.

Append-only event/revision records retain original formulation, exact observation refs, first known-after source unit and confidence/basis. Targeted current-view updates preserve the old event/version chain. Source-time validity and the unit in which the analyst learned it are independent. Errata append a correction with a reason and impacted links. Do not repurpose an existing ID, delete failed expectations or replace history by a convenient current summary.

The frozen exit state stores the claim and domain-row versions actually in force, with a compact state snapshot. Later ledger edits do not alter what `MD-FR-VNN` knew. Historical prospective understanding, later retrospective explanation and current mature synthesis are three explicit views.

## Sequential contribution and closure

[The reading specification](MEDALIST_SOURCE_UNIT_READING_SPEC.md) defines the full section contract. Each authorized volume contributes source/provenance and complete coverage; entering state and developments; narrative/form/character/relationship/sport evidence; material claims/counterevidence; affected ledger/readiness updates; predictions/adjudications if warranted; a frozen exit state; source-map/entrypoint advancement; verification and atomic persistence.

One volume is a transaction, not merely one prose file. Internal processing chunks do not grant later evidence. V01 begins with no narrative findings. A partial volume never advances the completed boundary. For magazine-only material, verify exact chapter identity/placement and complete delivery first; the method permits a whole Score transaction without inventing a collected-volume boundary.

The continuation policy defaults future requests to `single_operation`; a bounded continuous run needs owner authorization and individually closed transactions. Next-candidate metadata does not authorize a reading. The bootstrap leaves `SEQUENTIAL_ANALYSIS_LOCK = CLOSED` and owner review as the next permitted operation.

## Character, relationship and specialist promotion

The [reconstruction contract](MEDALIST_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) separately gates an evidence index, monograph, operational model and domain use. Context density, ordinary behavior, state transitions, speech/form evidence where relevant, contradictions and testability matter more than mention counts. Local readiness does not enroll a global character or grant simulation capability. No character package exists at bootstrap.

Specialists are **conditional responsibilities**, not a promised document list:

| Candidate responsibility | Promotion evidence and questions | Required upstream state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Athlete–coach relationships | Consequential instruction, response, trust/agency and repair across differing situations; both directions and asymmetry assessed | Character + relationship + institution; verified technical context where the argument depends on it |
| Competitive psychology | Evidence separates aspiration/self-efficacy, pressure, coping and actual performance; includes contrary cases and alternatives | Character + sport + chronology/knowledge + peer/authority context |
| Rivalries and peer networks | Multiple independent interactions and changing recognition/comparison/cooperation; no competitor reduced to antagonist | Directional relationships + character states + competition contexts |
| Technical development/performance | Verifiable progression and limits, program/strategy evidence, attributed calls and rule applicability | Sport ledger + primary locators; external references only if making real-world claims |
| Institutions/resources/family support | Recurring constraints/decisions with options, costs, implementation and counterexamples | Institution + relationships + character/knowledge state |
| Manga visual storytelling | Diagnostic sequences and recurring/contrasting composition support a formal argument; layout/locators verified | Visual + source observations; sport context when motion is material |
| Artistic expression/identity | Multiple performances distinguish athlete aim, depicted expressivity, judging and audiences without presuming opposition | Sport + visual + character + claims |
| Adaptation and AV performance | Admitted episode evidence, exact mapping, actually inspected required channels and tested divergent framing | Separate AV matrix/crosswalk + closed relevant manga territory |

A specialist begins from a meaningful closed tranche whose exact boundary and exclusions are explicit. It must pose an independent question, make an argument, trace consequential claims, consider competing readings, and identify unresolved evidence. A single diagnostic case may support a bounded study, not a full-domain thesis. If support is thin, retain the responsibility as anticipated.

## Synthesis dependencies and convergence

`closed sequential tranche → coverage/ledger reconciliation → architecture/role-gap audit → locator/claim stabilization → character/relationship and justified specialists → cross-specialist contradiction review → bounded integrated synthesis → validation/release audit`.

Technical/rule and structural conditions precede claims that evaluate coaching strategy or resource effects. Character/knowledge/relationship states inform psychology and mentorship; neither can infer unseen performance. Sport and visual specialists feed artistry/identity; they retain distinct evidence channels. Adaptation comparison follows verified manga/episode mapping and AV completion. A behavioral model is optional and independently validated; simulation readiness is not a prerequisite for literary synthesis.

Before integration, inspect claim dependencies, competing mechanisms and cases omitted by a polished specialist. A central premise changed by new source/AV evidence reopens its affected specialist and dependents, followed by bounded revalidation. Do not merely append a reassuring completion note. Cross-series comparison follows a stabilized scoped Medalist argument and names continuity, categories and comparison limits; an imported ethical framework does not dictate initial findings.

For fresh synthesis, dispatch a pinned Git commit with full method/architecture, source/knowledge boundary, completed readings, relevant ledgers/claim versions, locators, omissions, intended artifact IDs/paths and acceptance criteria. Hash delivered outputs where bytes exist, provide full prose, name producer/receiver and mandatory channel debt. The receiver compares base/candidate/current, preserves unaffected material and records semantic acceptance separately from Git validation. The global handoff contract governs this transfer.

## Planned manga-to-anime lane

S1 and S2 are separate episode/source inventories within adaptation continuity; do not equate anime episode “score” labels with manga Score IDs. Future crosswalk rows map manga witness/Score/page sequences to season/episode/time intervals with `EXACT / PARTIAL / REORDERED / ADDED / OMITTED / UNRESOLVED` relationships. A many-to-many map accommodates changed order and performance boundaries.

Compare dialogue/address, additions/omissions, character framing, acting, choreography/animation, spatial continuity, music/sound, pacing and emphasis only after relevant sources are admitted. Promotional clips identify targets; they cannot certify complete episodes. A screenshot-only or subtitle-only package is a labeled partial derivative.

Use the [episode-bundle specification](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md) for synchronized provenance, timestamps, clean images/index, Japanese dialogue, cross-modal links, aligned audio access, QA and missing layers. Record actual image/audio/continuous-AV inspection capability. For motion questions use `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED / VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION / VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION` with material reason, affected claim, source interval and inspection result. Samples cannot establish movement between endpoints; unresolved required AV debt blocks that claim's completion. No AV ledger is created before a justified admitted lane.

## Contradiction, amendment and lifecycle

Test chronology, knowledge, unreliable account, edition revision, translation, adaptation, paratext scope and genuine inconsistency before resolving conflict. The claims ledger carries `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN` and affected homes. A disputed witness/locator triggers a bounded source audit; a surviving interpretive conflict remains OPEN.

Review after V01, the first owner-selected meaningful tranche, a recurring uncaptured dimension, new source/edition class, major retrospectivity, sequential completion and before specialist/convergence/release. Add/split/merge infrastructure only if independent recurrence, evidence accumulation and retrieval cannot be adequately served by current homes. A recurring Japanese linguistic corpus may justify its own ledger later; no symmetry-driven expansion is required.

Record material amendments in this architecture's revision history and entrypoint with affected IDs/dependencies and any needed owner review. Backfill only a material gap unresolved by existing readings/retained primary sources; label it retrospective and preserve freezes. Move from INITIAL to EVOLVING as evidence warrants, STABILIZED after responsibilities are sufficiently known, and FROZEN only for an explicitly accepted scoped release. A new session/model does not start another corpus.
