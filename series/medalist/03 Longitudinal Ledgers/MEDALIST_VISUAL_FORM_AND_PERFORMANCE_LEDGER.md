# Visual form and performance representation

This ledger owns recurring formal features and their interpretive history. Athletic execution/calls belong in the skating ledger; inferred emotion belongs in character/claim records. It links the original page observation rather than owning another description of its facts. **Zero visual feature/motif rows existed at bootstrap; V01 records appear below.**

## Schema and IDs

Use `MD-VF-####` for a formal evidence record and `MD-MOT-####` only when a recurring motif/compositional pattern has independent retrieval value. Fields: feature/motif, source observation IDs, continuity/component, page/spread/sequence context, panel-order certainty, viewpoint, observable formal construction, proposed effect, alternatives, linked performance/character/relationship rows, claim/version, confidence/basis, recurrence/counterexamples and prior/revision.

Relevant mechanisms include panel size/density and rhythm, page-turn suspense, viewpoint/close-up, spatial continuity, rotation/speed/balance marks, posture/gesture/faces, internal-versus-external juxtaposition, negative space, repeated composition and technical-versus-expressive presentation. Record how static sequential art invites a movement reading without claiming it measures that movement.

The motif projection contains active occurrences, source span, supporting/counterexamples, current bounded interpretation and unresolved recurrence/meaning. A single image may warrant a local close reading without a motif ID.

## Trigger and evidence standard

Add a row when a feature recurs, affects consequential interpretation, connects performance representations, or needs retrieval for a visual specialist. A striking illustration alone does not establish a recurring formal strategy. Specify what is actually drawn before describing its psychological, ethical or artistic effect.

Locators must recover original witness, Score, spine/image occurrence and panel; page-turn/spread claims additionally require actual page-layout verification. Note viewer order, cropping, resolution and ambiguity. An OCR-only reading cannot certify visual evidence. No raw page/crop/contact sheet is committed.

For athletic questions, link `MD-PERF-…` and state whether the formal depiction supports an execution observation or merely an implied motion. Audience/judge reaction and athlete aim remain separately attributed. An expressive drawing does not by itself prove the character's artistic intention.

## History and revision

Source observations remain frozen; feature/motif identity and earliest occurrence are not reassigned. Add later occurrences/counterexamples with their knowledge boundary, then update the current interpretation through `MD-RV-…` when material. A retrospective compositional parallel can be recognized later without pretending it was recognized after the earlier volume.

A future visual specialist makes an argument from diagnostic sequences, controls/contrasting cases and technical-state context. It must not duplicate the sport ledger or treat anime movement as evidence for manga panels.
