# Relationships, rivalry and mentorship

This ledger owns A→B interpersonal state and its transitions. Shared labels and competitive proximity do not establish reciprocity. The [method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/MEDALIST_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs observation. **Zero relationship rows exist at bootstrap.**

## Schema and identifiers

Assign `MD-PAIR-####` to a verified pair and `MD-RL-####` to each directional transition. Fields: pair, from/to `MD-CHAR-…` keys, continuity, valid story-time/range and certainty, revealed-after unit, context, expectations/knowledge reference, dimensions, initiation/response, demonstrated options, state formulation, evidence IDs, claim/version, confidence/alternatives, previous transition and revision.

Dimensions may coexist: trust, technical reliance, psychological safety, admiration, rivalry, friendship, cooperation, envy, recognition/status, dependence, obligation, misunderstanding, boundary/conflict and repair. Record only evidenced dimensions; an unobserved reverse direction is unknown, not zero.

Coaching records distinguish instruction/support, authority exercised, goals negotiated/imposed, athlete assent/refusal, coach learning, and observed effect. Power/resource conditions link `MD-IN-…` rather than being independently restated here. Competitive outcome references `MD-PERF-…`/`MD-COMP-…`; it does not supply relational meaning by itself.

The current directional projection contains pair + direction, source endpoint, active transition IDs, bounded relationship account and unresolved divergences. A→B and B→A always occupy separate records even when a mutual summary eventually becomes justified.

## Trigger and evidence standard

Create a row for a consequential change/revelation in expectation, trust, reliance, rivalry, recognition, cooperation, refusal or repair. An encounter with no enduring analytical consequence remains local. Link actual action/utterance observations and knowledge records. Distinguish believed understanding from mutual understanding, adult intention from athlete effect, stated friendship from behavior and apology from changed conduct.

A demanding coaching episode warrants examination, not an automatic verdict; successful results cannot resolve a welfare/agency concern. A relationship claim requires explicit supporting evidence, material alternatives/counterevidence and qualitative confidence. Never infer an absent party's feelings from the focal character's report.

## Mutability and revision

Transition IDs, source boundary, directional participants and historical formulations remain fixed. The current projection may change only with an appended transition or claims-ledger revision. A corrected identity receives an explicit alias/correction chain, not ID reassignment. Preserve rupture, unresolved conflict and rejected explanations; later reconciliation cannot rewrite the earlier directional state.

Link affected character-state/readiness rows and frozen reading IDs. A future dyadic specialist must retrieve both directions and their divergent knowledge/authority contexts; it receives no independent authority over raw events.
