# Skating, training and competition

This ledger owns sport-specific accumulated interpretation and structured competition context. Source observations remain in frozen readings. A score or drawn position never automatically establishes confidence, artistry, welfare or technical correctness. **All tables are empty at bootstrap.**

## Three linked record types

`MD-COMP-####` identifies a depicted competition context: name as represented, continuity, location/date/season certainty, age/category, segment, eligibility/rules as stated, source observations and unresolved applicability. Do not invent a real-world homologous event.

`MD-PERF-####` identifies one program/attempt within that context: athlete key, segment/order, stated planned element versus attempt, depicted execution, in-story technical call, supplied scoring components/deductions/total/rank, expressive aims, observer/judge attribution, evidence and uncertainty. Numerical values stay null when unsupplied. A score arithmetic check is separate from authenticating the rules.

`MD-SK-####` records a material training/skill/conditioning transition: athlete/coach, story time and known-after unit, element/skill, prior demonstrated limit, intervention, new demonstrated scope, fatigue/injury/recovery as represented, consistency/risk, performance refs, evidence channel, claim/version, confidence, alternatives and revision. An attributed diagnosis is labeled as such; neither successful attempt nor technical ambition proves repeatable mastery.

## Four-channel contract

Each technical row declares `IN_UNIVERSE_STATEMENT / DEPICTED_PERFORMANCE / VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_KNOWLEDGE / ANALYTICAL_INFERENCE` for each relevant assertion. Preserve distinct statement, depiction, call and evaluation fields. A single still sequence may not show takeoff edge, full rotations or landing; identify resolution/continuity limits instead of judging unseen execution.

External references `MD-EXT-####` carry issuing body, official document URL/identifier, title, edition/effective season, category, section, access date, exact question, in-story context match, known mismatches and applicability `MATCHED / PARTIAL / UNRESOLVED / NOT_APPLICABLE`. Verify ISU/JSF/competition rules at use time. No rules are imported during bootstrap. A present-day rule cannot silently govern an undated scene.

Relevant recurring domains include jumps/spins/steps, program construction, strategy, difficulty versus consistency, physical conditioning/recovery, and judged versus audience/performance aims. Physical/medical causal claims require their own adequate external source and stay distinguishable from narrative representation.

## Trigger, links and history

Add a record when a new technical limit, intervention, performance, call/result, rule discrepancy or strategic tradeoff materially affects later retrieval. Routine repetitions with no state implication can remain in their reading. Link character `MD-CS-…`, relationship `MD-RL-…`, institution `MD-IN-…` and visual `MD-VF-…` rows; those homes own their respective interpretations.

Historical performance/context identity and observed numbers remain fixed unless a documented source/identification erratum appends a correction. Current competence/technical explanations may revise via new `MD-SK-…` and `MD-RV-…` records; retain previous formulations and external-rule applicability history. Every interpretation needs observation IDs, confidence/basis and viable counterevidence. External knowledge alone does not authenticate a depiction.
