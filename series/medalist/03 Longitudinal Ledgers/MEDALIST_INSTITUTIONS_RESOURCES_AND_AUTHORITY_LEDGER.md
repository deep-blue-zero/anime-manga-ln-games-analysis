# Institutions, resources and authority

This home accumulates consequential opportunities, constraints and authority decisions. It distinguishes narrative representation from external social claims and ethical evaluation. Pair trust belongs in the relationship ledger; rules/scoring applicability belongs in the skating ledger. **No institution, resource or decision is asserted at bootstrap.**

## Schema

Use `MD-ORG-####` for an institution/setting actually encountered and `MD-IN-####` for a constraint, rule or decision event. Required fields: entity/actor/affected people, continuity, story time/known-after unit, domain, explicit rule or resource condition, how learned, nominal rule versus implemented behavior, available alternatives/costs, decision-maker, expressed rationale, actual intervention/effect, evidence IDs, related context/relationship IDs, claim/version, confidence, alternatives, prior row and revision.

Domains can include training finances, ice/facility access, equipment, coaching/travel, family time/obligations, selection, credentialing and incentives. Quantities/costs remain unknown unless supplied; narrator/character estimates remain attributed. Do not generalize a depicted household or club into Japanese skating as a whole.

The current projection names the constraint/institution, bounded effective range, active evidence/state rows and unresolved implementation questions. It does not own original event facts or repeat the relational response as another independent account.

## Row trigger and claim requirements

Add a row when a material constraint changes, a previously unknown barrier becomes visible, or an adult/organization's decision changes meaningful options. A one-time incidental prop need not become infrastructure. Record who can choose/refuse, which alternatives are practically available and whether a claimed sacrifice is self-chosen, requested or imposed.

Separate intent, understanding, action and consequence. Ethical evaluation links a `MD-CL-…` claim with stated criterion (care, responsibility, welfare, agency or another justified lens) and counterreading. Success cannot itself absolve a decision; depicted inequality does not itself establish a political thesis. Broader factual comparisons require labeled, verified external references rather than assumed social knowledge.

## Mutability and revision

Historical event/source identity and original stated conditions remain intact. Current projections may update only through a new event/revision. Preserve uncertain dates, competing accounts and rejected explanations; a later resource disclosure may be `REVEALED_NOT_NEW` rather than a new condition.

Link `MD-COMP-…`/`MD-EXT-…` for sport rules, `MD-RL-…` for mentorship/family response, `MD-CS-…` for development and `MD-KN-…` for consequential knowledge. Every interpretive row requires a frozen source observation, confidence/basis and alternatives; a revised structural interpretation points to its `MD-RV-…` record and affected specialist.
