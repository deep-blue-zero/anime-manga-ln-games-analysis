# Chronology, information and retrospection

This home prevents timeline and knowledge collapse. It owns temporal ordering, consequential propositions and later links to earlier events. Psychology, pair state and original observation remain in their own homes. **No chronology or knowledge rows exist at bootstrap.**

## Three schemas

An event index `MD-TM-####` names the represented event, story-time/range and certainty, disclosure order/known-after unit, event kind (`PRESENT / FLASHBACK / MEMORY_REPORT / RETROSPECTIVE_REVELATION / UNRESOLVED`), participants, observation refs, competing ordering, chronology confidence and interpretation claim/version. This is the canonical ordering index, not a second transcript of the event.

A proposition `MD-PROP-####` identifies a consequential question; `MD-KN-####` binds one participant's state toward it at a story/source boundary. Record `KNOWN / BELIEVED / SUSPECTED / MISTAKEN / WITHHELD / UNKNOWN / UNRESOLVED`, basis, disclosure/omission/deception attribution, higher-order belief only when consequential, action relevance, evidence and confidence. Represented truth and each person's belief are distinct; analyst/reader knowledge is not imported into a character.

Retrospection `MD-RT-####` links the new observation/known-after unit to prior event/claim/freeze, specifies disclosed history versus changed interpretation, old and new formulations, confidence, alternatives, affected ledgers and revision ID. A later recollection can change explanatory relevance without authenticating every remembered detail.

## Trigger and links

Add timeline anchors only when needed for development, competition sequence, source placement or causality. Add knowledge rows when who knows/believes/withholds a fact materially explains a decision or misunderstanding. Do not inventory every spoken proposition. An event's chronological certainty may be high while its motive is uncertain.

Source-unit observations/locators supply the evidence. Link character, relationship, sport and institution rows that depend on time/knowledge; those homes do not restate the proposition as separate authority. Japanese ambiguous phrasing remains beside the original observation; the knowledge row links it rather than resolves it from translation alone.

## History and revision

Stable event/proposition/participant IDs, first disclosure and original knowledge states are retained. Current ordering or represented-truth projections may revise through an appended event, `MD-RT-…` and claims-ledger revision. Preserve uncertain ordering rather than inventing dates/ages.

No retrospective row alters `MD-FR-VNN` or an earlier prediction. Historical “known after VNN” and current mature explanation must be separately recoverable. Correct a misidentified source/event through an explicit erratum and affected-link review; never silently harmonize testimony with shown history.
