# Zenless Zone Zero — claim history and form register

## Claims and questions

**Row unit:** one bounded formulation/version of an assertion or prospective question. Fields: `ZZZ-Cnnn`, text, evidence class, attributed voice, exact source/lock/state scope, complete reasoning's reading heading, all necessary native locators, support, counterevidence, strongest credible rival, discriminating condition, uncertainty/extrapolation limits, dependencies, transition, predecessor, revision reason, `validated_through`, `last_retested` and pending source impact.

Use `PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN`. Changes append the old and new formulations; no retrospective editing of earlier knowledge. Classify contradictions by source revision, identity/join error, perspective/unreliability, chronology, variant/unlock/branch, nested frame, localization/performance or genuine unresolved inconsistency. A translation difference need not be a world-state contradiction. Predictions are recorded before exposure and adjudicated after evidence, preserving failed predictions and the original information boundary.

Retest only advances `validated_through` after actual affected evidence review; new build metadata alone cannot do it. Major narrative intake reviews all affected OPEN/conflicting claims. Keep unresolved alternatives visible through synthesis; no model or public summary is allowed to silently simplify them.

Current literary claim/question rows: **none**. Source acquisition gaps live in the source register, not as invented narrative hypotheses.

## Form, language and ordinary expression

**Row unit:** one inspected instance or a justified pattern in source wording, address, speech register, narration, visual staging, music or performed voice. Fields: form ID, native locator/witness, speaker/thinker/addressee and identity status, context/state, channel actually inspected, CHS anchor and limited working translation where necessary, localization variants, observation versus interpretation, recurrence/dedup basis, counterinstance, complete reading argument and synthesis duty.

Update when an instance affects a claim or creates a recurring formal question. Keep independent cases and their contexts; do not replace them with a personality adjective. An isolated detail can remain `LOCAL_ONLY` in its reading, then be promoted by linking its full evidence when recurrence earns cumulative retrieval. Ordinary expression and minor interaction are not discarded for low plot prominence. Source-language register is distinct from vocal delivery; no voice or runtime perception has been inspected here.

Current form/language/performance rows: **none**. Claim-driven channel gaps route to the Source AV queue. This register never imports bulk dialogue, scripts or media.

## Acceptance and history

Each unit's transaction lists new/retested claims, state/identity effects, form records, dependencies made stale, unresolved channel checks and explicit reviewed/no-supported-change outcomes. The full source-facing argument remains available; this retrieval apparatus cannot satisfy a reading by itself. Frozen records receive errata or superseding versions. Every transition preserves source identity, state and earlier reasoning.
