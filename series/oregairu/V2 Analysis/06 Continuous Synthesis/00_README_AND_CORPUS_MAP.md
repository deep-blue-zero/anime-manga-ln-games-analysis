## Keep source categories distinct

| Material | Use in this corpus |
|---|---|
| Mainline Japanese novels | Governing fictional evidence, with each supplementary story placed in its actual chronology. |
| Qualified Watari-authored supplements | Christmas drama/prose and individually audited anthology stories retain their declared authority, textual/audio limits and chronological bands. Official packaging alone does not settle admission. |
| Alternate continuities | Ketsu and other alternate routes remain separate from mainline characterization; new analysis requires its own validated material and scope. |
| Adaptations | Adaptation-specific staging or dialogue cannot silently become a Japanese-novel fact. |
| Guest-author fiction | Official anthology contributions by other writers remain separately classified derivative/reception evidence. |
| Interviews, production history and paratext | Can contextualize the work; retrospective statements do not automatically override published fiction. |
| Literary studies and reconstruction specifications | The first argue from evidence; the second compile bounded behavioral inference. Neither supplies missing primary-source content. |

Shin is excluded. Pending leads for 結3, 刻 and JR Central's campaign stories remain acquisition/classification questions in the registered map and source record. Their absence does not block completion of the existing admitted corpus. No copyrighted source payloads belong in the public analytical release.
