# OREGAIRU V2 — Evidence Locator and First-Pass Revision Ledger
## Post-adversarial publication bridge from mature claims to Japanese primary evidence

## 0. Purpose

This document is the **publication bridge** between the frozen specialist syntheses and the continuous full-series synthesis that follows.

It answers a question that none of the earlier infrastructure documents answers by itself:

> **Given everything learned in the Japanese-primary reread, the Phase-4 longitudinal ledgers, the twelve specialist syntheses, and the Phase-6 hostile source audit, what formulation of each major claim is currently authorized—and how can that formulation be traced back to the Japanese source while preserving what changed from V1?**

The required traceability target is:

> **current mature claim → Phase-5 specialist home → Phase-6 disposition → Phase-4 ledger/checkpoint → sequential volume analysis → locator → Japanese source**

The historical route is attached in parallel:

> **V1 claim → Phase-4 V1→V2 transition → Phase-5 formulation → Phase-6 stress result → current wording**

This is deliberately **not** a second copy of the 1,780-row primary-source locator infrastructure, and it is not a replacement for the 86-claim V1→V2 ledger. Those remain canonical for their own semantic responsibilities. Document 13 curates the claims most likely to enter the final literary synthesis and records their current publication-safe forms.

---

## 1. Authority and non-duplication rules

### 1.1 Evidence precedence

For any claim routed here, authority remains:

1. **Japanese primary witness at the cited locus**;
2. canonical sequential V2 deep reading and its source-locator ledger;
3. relevant prospective checkpoint when historical boundary matters;
4. relevant Phase-4 longitudinal ledger;
5. **Phase-6 adversarial disposition**, which controls whether the Phase-5 wording survives;
6. this Document-13 publication bridge;
7. Phase-5 specialist synthesis as frozen provenance;
8. V1 material as historical provenance.

The final full-series synthesis may compress this infrastructure into readable prose, but it may not silently reverse the hierarchy.

### 1.2 What this document does not supersede

This artifact does **not** supersede:

- `OREGAIRU_V2_PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md` — complete retrieval graph and locator grammar;
- `OREGAIRU_V2_V1_TO_V2_REVISION_LEDGER.md` — formal Phase-4 routing of all 86 historical V1 claim IDs;
- `OREGAIRU_V2_PHASE6_ADVERSARIAL_JAPANESE_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` — controlling hostile-review dispositions for Phase-5 claims;
- Documents 01–12 — frozen specialist provenance.

It **does** control which wording from those layers is safe to inherit into Document 14.

### 1.3 Exact-quotation rule

Japanese phrases reproduced below are short anchors for deterministic retrieval, not substitutes for the source. For publication-grade quotation:

1. follow the source route named in the row;
2. open the sequential deep reading;
3. recover the cited locator;
4. reopen the Japanese EPUB/page image/audio at that locus;
5. collate punctuation or edition variance locally if material.

### 1.4 Confidence vocabulary

- **VERY HIGH** — direct Japanese wording plus longitudinal/counterevidence review strongly support the claim at its stated scope.
- **HIGH** — claim is supported by multiple independent source acts; residual ambiguity does not materially threaten the formulation.
- **MEDIUM-HIGH** — strong analytical synthesis with explicit source support, but wording is not a textual doctrine or depends on a comparative model.
- **MEDIUM** — useful interpretation that should remain visibly inferential.
- **OPEN** — current evidence does not responsibly close the question.

The mature architecture survives, but several of its cleanest formulations do not survive verbatim. The governing correction is:

> **Oregairu becomes more answerable without becoming transparent, non-strategic, procedurally pure, independent of social pressure, or free of coercive episodes.**

The final synthesis must therefore distinguish four levels that Phase 5 sometimes compressed:

1. **textual statement** — what a speaker actually says;
2. **speaker model** — the interpretation a character currently offers;
3. **recurrent pattern** — what multiple scenes support longitudinally;
4. **V2 analytical synthesis** — the project's best explanation of that pattern.

No sentence gains series-wide sovereignty merely because it arrives late or sounds definitive.

# 6. OPEN questions that must remain visible in the final synthesis

These are not gaps to conceal. They are current limits of the corpus.

1. **Aesthetic reward of coercive competence.** The novels criticize Hachiman's jurisdictional excess, but they also make his strategic competence narratively pleasurable and effective. The exact balance remains open.
2. **Haruno's causal influence on Yukino's self-model.** Yukino owns many choices; how deeply Haruno's framing shapes those choices cannot be purified away.
3. **Yui's residual romantic hope after V14.** Continued feeling is real; the degree to which hope, friendship, habit, rivalry, and ordinary affection each govern later proximity remains mixed.
4. **Haruno's total future.** Succession pressure is real; “over-authored future” remains a hypothesis rather than a complete state description.
5. **Yukino's eventual vocation.** Interest in her father's work is real; career settlement is not.
6. **Retrospective ethics of the bridge.** Mutual selection does not retroauthorize coercive staging, but the novels do not provide a simple formula for how much coercion can coexist with a later relation experienced as mutually chosen.
7. **Indirectness as aesthetic preference.** The series may value mediated/indirect communication more strongly than a purely procedural vocabulary captures.
8. **Structural intertexts.** Document 11's hierarchy remains incompletely source-audited.
9. **Exact internal order of the four Watari anthology stories.** Broad post-V14 placement is usable; precise order remains open.
10. **Supplementary warmth and selection bias.** Ordinary-life bonus material may normalize the selected relation more than a less celebratory sample would.

# 9. Publication rules for `14_OREGAIRU_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md`

The final continuous synthesis should follow these rules.

## 9.1 Distinguish fact from synthesis

When using project language such as:

- answerable involvement;
- chosen interdependence;
- consequence-bearing authorship;
- transformed inheritance;
- reputation price system;
- relationships surviving consequence;

write it as **V2 analytical formulation**, not as though it were Watari's own terminology.

## 9.2 Preserve local coercion and asymmetry

Do not let the terminal argument erase:

- Hachiman's manipulation of decision environments;
- Yui's explicit method criticism;
- Yukino's unequal social/family constraints;
- Haruno's interpretive pressure;
- the upstream power involved in supposedly free final choices;
- the fact that formal consent can occur after substantial agenda-setting.

## 9.3 Do not certify the ending

The final synthesis may state that:

- Hachiman/Yukino is the selected romantic pair;
- the pair mutually claims future involvement;
- Yui receives a real answer and real loss;
- the triad continues in changed form;
- ordinary-life material shows near-term durability.

It may **not** state that these facts prove the final relation perfectly healthy, independent, uniquely genuine, destined, or permanently stable.

## 9.4 Preserve the prospective/retrospective split

Later evidence can recontextualize earlier scenes but cannot erase what an earlier volume had not yet earned. In particular:

- V9 `本物` cannot be written as a coded confession whose final object was already settled;
- V11 `全部ほしい` cannot be rewritten with V14's later semantic content;
- V12 `共依存` must retain its immediate diagnostic force even though later volumes limit its sovereignty;
- V13's declared closure regime must remain genuinely threatening at that boundary;
- V14.5 cannot retroactively make the V14 ending inevitable.

## 9.5 Keep rejected claims out

The NON-GOV list in §5 is binding unless new primary evidence is admitted later under a new revision cycle.

The outgoing publication baseline is:

> **Oregairu begins with characters repeatedly trying to protect themselves and one another by converting desire into roles, inference, usefulness, institutional pretext, self-sacrifice, protective ambiguity, and theories about what relationships are. Across the series, those devices do not disappear. What changes is that more of the desire, uncertainty, objection, and consequence behind them becomes attributable to particular people whose answers can alter the relation. Hachiman remains strategic; Yukino remains proud and controlled; Yui remains socially adaptive; institutions remain coercive; performance remains unavoidable; reliance remains real. The ending is therefore not purification. It is a historically produced romantic choice and continuing social relation in which more claims can be answered without pretending that answerability has eliminated power, pain, indirection, or the possibility of being wrong.**

That formulation is **audited V2 synthesis**, not a quotation or authorial theorem.
