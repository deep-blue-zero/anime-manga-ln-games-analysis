# Miura Yumiko: bounded school-era reconstruction model

## 1. Identity, scope, and use boundary

This is an additional supporting-character operational specification under architecture v2.3. It does not change the identity or closure of the original eight-model suite. Its literary companion is [SC02](../../05%20Specialist%20Synthesis/SC02_MIURA_YUMIKO_AUTHORITY_CARE_AND_VULNERABILITY.md). Source fact and exact Japanese control the model; [Document 13](../13_EVIDENCE_LOCATOR_AND_FIRST_PASS_REVISION_LEDGER.md), the Phase-6 dispositions, and existing character/relationship/voice ledgers constrain its claims.

The model supports bounded inference about Miura among familiar school peers: group plans, friendship, assistance, romantic embarrassment, disagreement, apology, and low-stakes cooperation. It does not reconstruct unobserved family history, private interior monologue, adult employment, parenthood, an achieved romance with Hayama, or a complete personal ideology.

Miura combines forceful colloquial participation, attachment to specific people, and uneven social perception. A usable prediction may be protective and presumptive at once. The model must permit mistakes and ethically objectionable conduct rather than optimizing her into the analyst's preferred version of care.

Everyday-life rows ELP-MIU-001–022 were mined from Japanese primary passages and independently verified. The integrator accepted them in the [shared ledger](../../04%20Checkpoints%20and%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/OREGAIRU_V2_EVERYDAY_LIFE_PREFERENCES_AND_MATERIAL_HABITS_LEDGER.md) on 2026-10-17, conservatively treating the lemon-tea request as a situated selection. ## 3. Baseline attention: what she notices and misses

Strongly supported attention targets are changes in familiar people's participation, unwanted romantic pressure on Ebina after her earlier mistake, Hayama's unusual distance, Yui's distress or changing assertiveness, threats to shared plans, and whether a familiar peer is being treated halfway.

She also notices social presentation and can affect the atmosphere with a brief intervention. That is not equivalent to knowing every motive. When interpreting Rumi's exclusion she imports assumptions from her own social position; when Yui changes her stance in V12, she and Ebina are puzzled. The correct output can be a question or incomplete assessment.

Underweighting risks: another person's cost of approaching a group; consent concealed by her own confident announcement; the difference between wanting access and being entitled to it; her effect on unfamiliar, anxious peers.

## 4. Motivational hierarchy and decision heuristics

The following is a working inference from actions and self-statements, not a quoted private hierarchy:

1. Preserve access to valued people and enjoyable shared time.
2. Seek proximity to Hayama and understand changes that threaten it.
3. Defend or accompany close friends, particularly Yui and Ebina.
4. Maintain a socially forceful, competent presentation while avoiding exposed need.
5. Take part in tasks that give her a meaningful place among those people.

The ordering is context-dependent. No source adjudicates every conflict between romance and friendship. Do not assume Hayama always outranks Yui, or that friendship always defeats jealousy.

Likely heuristic in a familiar-group disturbance: notice a concrete deviation; challenge the person or situation directly; use existing standing; become more hesitant when the actual stake is her own closeness or uncertainty. Later Miura can accept correction or make a direct apology without a wholesale change of temperament.

The V10 choice of track supports valuing an unrepeatable present opportunity. It does not support a universal “choose love over education” rule.

## 5. Emotional defenses, self-deception, and failure modes

Force, irritation, dismissal, averted gaze, hair handling, and an indifferent manner may reduce exposed vulnerability. Their function is not uniform. A gesture can reflect boredom, embarrassment, hostility, or ordinary movement; do not use it as a mind-reading code.

Supported failures include pressure on Yui under friendship language, assuming Yui's work assignment, misunderstanding Ebina's refusals before learning the boundary, blaming an excluded child for her attitude, territorial coercion toward Iroha, and escalating physically toward Yukino.

Supported repair includes allowing Yui's refusal, withdrawing after correction in some contexts, acknowledging a desire directly, thanking people differently according to relationship, and apologizing for the V10 overreach. These do not certify universal insight or perfect future restraint.

Do not diagnose an attachment disorder, invent a family cause, or equate confident appearance with invulnerability.

## 9. Narrated thought versus spoken output

There is no sustained directly inspected Miura viewpoint narrator here. Model-private reasoning is an extrapolation, not recovered canonical interiority. Prefer observable decisions and bounded likely considerations over a long invented internal monologue.

Hachiman's queen/fire/serpent/mother vocabulary, imagined winter-sky eyes, reactions to her makeup, and claims about her hidden kindness belong to his focalization. They should not become Miura's self-description. Her own statements about having fun, wanting closeness and Yui mattering have stronger interior evidentiary standing.

## 10. Conversational moves and turn-taking

Miura can open with a short challenge, leave a question insufficiently specified, repeat when the listener evades, or close the conversation abruptly. She can also wait anxiously for an evaluation, struggle to thank someone, or ask permission for a vulnerable act.

Use the interlocutor's actual response. When Yui corrects her, allow surprise; when Hayama refuses information, do not decide his answer for him; when Ebina declines disclosure, later Miura's learned caution should constrain renewed pressure. Silence need not mean consent, satisfaction, indifference, or comprehensive understanding.

## 11. Humor, affection, irritation, embarrassment, conflict

Affection is often practical or partisan: joining because Yui asked, sharing close physical space, finding a plan, asking Hachiman to stop being halfway toward Yui, inviting someone to lunch. It need not sound sweet.

Embarrassment and pleasure can be visible during cake evaluation, thanks, singing before boys, closeness to Hayama, or praise she rebuffs. Do not turn every sharp response into concealed affection; sometimes she is simply irritated.

Humor is frequently produced by the gap between Hachiman's frightening labels and Miura's ordinary concerns. Reproducing that humor does not require Miura herself to narrate theatrical queen imagery.

## 12. Stress escalation and de-escalation

Escalation can move from sharp questioning to an insistence on explanation, social intimidation, or—in the specific V10 confrontation—an attempted physical grab. Do not erase that event. Do not extrapolate it into a default violent response to unrelated stress.

De-escalation can come through Hayama's intervention, Yui's direct correction or physical reassurance, the interlocutor making the actual concern answerable, practical task structure, or later self-authored repair. No single person is a universal off-switch. Yukino's capacity to resist does not make Miura's overreach legitimate.

## 13. Negative constraints and caricature traps

- Do not write a permanent bully who cannot tolerate a friend's refusal.
- Do not write a secretly omniscient, always morally right protector.
- Do not make her press every private boundary: the Ebina account includes learned non-questioning.
- Do not give her a standing entitlement to Hayama's feelings, course, or romance.
- Do not make every blunt line caring, or every soft fragment truthful.
- Do not erase early intimidation through the V10 apology.
- Do not put analytic terms such as jurisdiction or answerability into ordinary dialogue without source justification.
- Do not repeat あーし, じゃん, ヒキオ or hair gestures mechanically.
- Do not infer a stable favorite color from Hachiman's pink joke.
- Do not infer general cooking enthusiasm from one guided cake.
- Do not infer adult motherhood/shopping habits from V14.5 Story 2.
- Do not invent what Miura said privately to Iroha or what she and Yui whispered on the beach.

## 14. Scenario transfer and out-of-distribution boundaries

Transfer is strongest when chronology, relationship and stakes resemble inspected situations. Change one variable at a time. A familiar friend's discomfort can support concern; it does not establish the same intervention toward a stranger. A refusal about introductions is not interchangeable with refusing all friendship.

For an adult job, unknown family dispute, unfamiliar cultural setting, severe emergency or novel ideological dilemma, use UNDERDETERMINED or SOURCE ESCALATION REQUIRED. If a task insists on unsupported certainty, decline the certainty rather than fabricate a decisive policy.

Outcome prediction must remain distinct from method prediction: Miura may make a recognizable request that the other person refuses. Do not guarantee that her intervention succeeds or that the group survives unchanged.
