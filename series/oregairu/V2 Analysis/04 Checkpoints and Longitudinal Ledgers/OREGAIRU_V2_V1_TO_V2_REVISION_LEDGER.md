# OREGAIRU V2 — V1 → V2 Revision Ledger
## Formal claim transitions at Phase-4 closure

## 0. Purpose and authority

This document closes Phase 4 by answering a narrower question than the specialist syntheses that follow:

> **What happened to the first-pass interpretation after the Japanese novels were reread sequentially and the major longitudinal domains were rebuilt from source-grounded evidence?**

The historical V1 synthesis is **not** treated as obsolete or embarrassing. It supplied unusually productive questions, many of which survived. But its claims no longer govern merely because they appeared in the earlier full-series synthesis. Current authority comes from the Japanese-primary V2 chain:

`Japanese source → V2 sequential deep reading → checkpoint → Phase-4 ledger → this revision route → later specialist synthesis`.

The historical source is `OREGAIRU_V2_V1_CLAIM_AUDIT.md`. It remains provenance and a record of what V1 actually believed. This ledger does not rewrite that artifact; it routes it.

### Transition vocabulary

- **PRESERVE** — formulation survives substantially intact; later synthesis may refine wording without changing the claim's structure.
- **STRENGTHEN** — V2 supplies broader, cleaner, or more direct support than V1 had.
- **REVISE** — core insight survives but the governing formulation, scope, mechanism, or implied causal theory changes materially.
- **DOWNGRADE** — claim remains useful as a local hypothesis, counter-gaze, or partial model but loses authority or generality.
- **REJECT** — formulation fails under V2 evidence and should not be used as current authority.
- **OPEN** — evidence is insufficient, outside the active mainline boundary, or intentionally unresolved; later specialist/adversarial work must not pretend closure.

Where V1 used dispositions such as `RETAIN-STRONG`, `RETAIN-NARROW`, `SOURCE-BACKFILL`, `OVERCLAIM-RISK`, `REOPEN`, or `SUPPLEMENTARY-ONLY`, those were *pre-reread hypotheses*. This ledger records the post-reread state.

# 1. Executive revision thesis

V1's most important achievement survives:

> **Oregairu repeatedly studies people converting desire, care, fear, competence, and interpretation into forms that reduce the need to own what they want.**

V2 does not overturn that insight. It makes it less totalizing.

The main movement from V1 to V2 is therefore not "wrong → right" but:

`master explanation → mechanism taxonomy`

`binary authenticity → historically singular, answerable, revisable relation`

`independence → authorship with chosen reliance`

`self-sacrifice → one instrument inside a broader jurisdiction problem`

`Yui as preserver → Yui as active social-political agent whose preservation work is one capability among several`

`Haruno diagnosis → strong adversarial model without narrator sovereignty`

`romantic ending as proof → romantic choice as a real but non-certifying answer`

`voice as nuance → voice as primary literary evidence`

The strongest V2 gain is procedural. The series increasingly asks not simply whether an act is caring, sincere, effective, or costly, but:

- who owns the problem;
- who may define it;
- whose desire is being named;
- whose refusal has force;
- which kind of help has actually been authorized;
- who bears the cost;
- whether the social role can be revised;
- whether the speaker distinguishes what they know from what they infer;
- whether a relationship can survive answers that change its form.

That framework explains why V2 retains so much of V1 while rejecting several of its cleanest slogans.

## 3.1 Hachiman epistemic / narratorial claims

### H-01 — Cynicism functions as a closed epistemology

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger`

V2 confirms that negative explanations receive privileged admissibility precisely where favorable interpretations would expose Hachiman to hope or desire. The revision is not that cynicism is false, but that its evidentiary asymmetry is defensive and domain-specific.

### H-02 — Romantic “denseness” is frequently active false-negative control

**Transition:** **REVISE**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Japanese Voice Ledger`

V2 separates possibility detection, probability assignment, and behavioral permission. Hachiman often notices romantic evidence but refuses to act as though it licenses certainty; some cases are defensive suppression, others are legitimate uncertainty.

### H-03 — He projects ideals onto Yukino and experiences correction as betrayal

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Character State Ledger`

The reread strengthens the idealization model: early Yukino becomes partly a projection of Hachiman’s preferred solitary self. Later correction wounds that model without establishing Yukino as deceptive.

### H-04 — “People cannot understand one another” is insight and evasion

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Japanese Voice Ledger`

V2 sharpens the distinction between epistemic humility and avoidance. Hayama’s privacy boundary shows that not-knowing can be proper; the Service Club crises show that not-knowing cannot justify replacing answerable inquiry with private verdicts.

### H-05 — Hachiman’s self-sacrifice is often a form of control

**Transition:** **REVISE**  
**Current V2 home:** `Request / Intervention Ethics Ledger; Dependency / Autonomy Ledger`

Preserve the important subset, reject the master formula. The broader failure is private assignment of problem meaning, roles, options, and costs; self-sacrifice is one recurrent instrument among several.

### H-06 — He excludes his own wellbeing from moral accounting

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Request / Intervention Ethics Ledger; Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger`

The cultural-festival pattern survives strongly. V2 adds that self-cost is not ethically private because other people bear relational, reputational, and emotional externalities even when Hachiman can tolerate the direct harm.

### H-07 — He weaponizes social intelligence

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Social Role / Performance Ledger`

V2 repeatedly confirms high competence in reading hierarchy, incentives, shame, face, and pretext. The mature distinction is that predictive accuracy does not confer jurisdiction over other people’s choices.

### H-08 — His methods can consume other people’s integrity

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Request / Intervention Ethics Ledger`

The wording is severe but directionally sound. V2 generalizes the point as involuntary cost and role allocation: helpers can preserve an outcome while making other people carry deception, complicity, reputation, or denied speech.

### H-09 — He mistakes voluntary action for exclusive ownership of consequences

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Request / Intervention Ethics Ledger; Dependency / Autonomy Ledger`

V2 strongly confirms that voluntary self-cost does not eliminate third-party standing. The recipient, observers, collaborators, and affected institutions may still have legitimate claims about method and consequence.

### H-10 — His desire for total understanding contains a controlling impulse

**Transition:** **REVISE**  
**Current V2 home:** `Authenticity / Honmono Ledger; Japanese Voice Ledger`

The early wish for certainty can become jurisdictional, but V2 refuses to define Hachiman’s mature desire as total understanding. The late linguistic movement is toward asking, bounded uncertainty, and continuing effort to understand.

### H-11 — His growth is better measured by requests than by sociability

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Request / Intervention Ethics Ledger`

This becomes one of V2’s strongest longitudinal metrics. Growth is not loner-to-extrovert; it is movement from unilateral solution toward request, reliance, first-person desire, toleration of refusal, and answerable involvement.

### H-12 — Personal desire becomes a sufficient reason only very late

**Transition:** **STRENGTHEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; Relationship State Ledger`

V14 decisively strengthens this. Hachiman strips away client request, rescue, incapacity, club duty, promise, and usefulness until continued involvement is justified because he wants it.

### H-13 — Hachiman’s humor often functions as emotional conversion

**Transition:** **REVISE**  
**Current V2 home:** `Japanese Voice Ledger`

The mechanism survives but must remain local. Humor, references, and abstraction can camouflage threatening affect, but they also perform genuine comedy, cognition, genre play, reader alignment, and coordination.

### H-14 — The novels risk flattering the punitive outsider fantasy

**Transition:** **OPEN**  
**Current V2 home:** `Hachiman Epistemic/Narratorial Ledger; future Hachiman specialist synthesis`

V2 supplies substantial counterpressure: Hachiman’s social competence is real, but the narration also exposes his jurisdictional errors and self-serving theories. Whether the series still grants the outsider fantasy excessive rhetorical pleasure remains a valid Phase-6/formal question.

# 4. Formal resolution of the seven Phase-1 adversarial tests

## Test A — Is “self-sacrifice as control” the best master concept?

**Resolution: REVISE.**

The test fails at the level of a master explanation and succeeds at the level of a recurrent mechanism. V2 replaces the slogan with a taxonomy of private problem-definition, unilateral role assignment, cost allocation, neededness, substitution, strategic self-cost, and ordinary protective risk. The most general ethical vulnerability is **jurisdiction without adequate answerability**. Self-sacrifice remains especially important in the cultural-festival and Kyoto sequences, but later analysis must name what the sacrifice actually does.

## Test B — Is “Yukino self-removal” a coherent late arc or retrospective compression?

**Resolution: REVISE NARROWER / STRENGTHEN AS LATE PATTERN.**

The late pattern is real. The retrospective character essence is not. V2 finds too much pursuit, competition, boundary-setting, pride, explicit will, and self-authored action to describe Yukino generally as someone who loves by removing herself. V13 is precisely powerful because self-removal is a historically produced solution, not a timeless essence.

## Test C — Does `本物` converge on “accountable continuation”?

**Resolution: REVISE.**

The procedural practice survives strongly; the lexical-definition claim does not. `本物` is never cleanly converted into one final dictionary meaning. V2 instead tracks a progression from false-positive fear and projection critique through an answerable request toward a late practice of self-authored, mutually answerable, historically singular, revisable continuation. V14.5's anti-`神聖視` correction makes certification itself suspect.

## Test D — Does Hachiman/Yukino reach the deepest level of his self-conception?

**Resolution: REVISE.**

As a statement about Hachiman's eventual chosen future and the kind of claim he finally makes, this remains powerful. As an objective hierarchy that makes every other relationship categorically shallower, it overreaches. V2 preserves Hachiman/Yui as genuine ordinary intimacy and rejects using the final route to rewrite earlier ambiguity as secretly settled.

## Test E — Is Yui primarily a preserver?

**Resolution: REVISE / STRENGTHEN ACTIVE-AGENT MODEL.**

Preservation is one of Yui's recurrent strategies, not her governing essence. She is an atmosphere reader, infrastructure builder, agenda setter, risk manager, requester, rival, friend, and eventually a person who accepts an unfavorable answer without surrendering all relational claims. Her late development is increased explicitness, not the discovery of agency from nothing.

## Test F — How authoritative is Haruno's diagnosis?

**Resolution: DOWNGRADE AUTHORITY; PRESERVE MECHANISM VALUE.**

Haruno remains one of the series' best interpreters. V2 also establishes that she is an invested participant whose language can overreach, whose preferred cure is not sovereign, and whose own need for a non-counterfeit answer matters. `共依存` survives as a bounded mechanism model, not an authorial theorem about feeling or remedy.

## Test G — Does first-person form structurally over-credit Hachiman?

**Resolution: STRENGTHEN AS FORMAL RISK; OPEN FOR PHASE-6 QUANTIFICATION.**

The concern survives and is sharpened by V2's interludes, female relational labor, and the distinction between who creates an opening and who receives narratorial climax credit. The Japanese Voice Ledger already treats interludes and formally unattributed passages as redistribution of voice authority. A fuller formal judgment belongs in the Hachiman, Yui, and narrative-style specialist documents and should be actively stress-tested in Phase 6.

# 5. Source-method revisions

## 5.1 OCR-first reading → REJECT as current authority model

V1's OCR-first workflow was practical but insufficiently controlled. V2 now gives the Japanese commercial EPUB or verified Japanese prose witness priority, uses fixed-page scan/PDF witnesses where available for locator and visual verification, and treats OCR as navigation unless independently checked. Anthology 02 is the clearest qualified case: its OCR-derived EPUB has source-admission value but exact quotations remain scan-verification dependent.

## 5.2 Uneven locator quality → STRENGTHEN as a diagnosed infrastructure problem, then materially repair

V1 correctly identified locator inconsistency. V2 responds with standardized per-volume evidence tables, checkpoint routes, ledger crosswalks, and `OREGAIRU_V2_PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATOR_LEDGER.md`, which indexes 1,780 already-frozen primary-source locator rows rather than duplicating them.

## 5.3 Japanese voice underdeveloped → STRENGTHEN and substantially resolve

V1 correctly predicted a major gain. The Japanese Voice Ledger now establishes speaker-specific register, pronoun/address discipline, modality, hedging, ellipsis, requests, benefactive grammar, lexical families, and voice redistribution as core literary evidence. The principal correction is that linguistic development does **not** mean stylistic convergence.

## 5.4 “Self-sacrifice = control” overgeneralization → REVISE

Resolved through the Request / Intervention Ethics and Dependency / Autonomy Ledgers. The general category is unilateral jurisdiction or substituted authorship; self-sacrifice is one recurring mechanism.

## 5.5 Yukino self-removal projected backward → REJECT as character essence

Preserve the late V13 strategy and its historical causes. Do not let that late solution rewrite early Yukino's corrective idealism, competition, pride, competence, pursuit, and boundary-setting.

## 5.6 Yui underweighted as strategist → STRENGTHEN

The V2 character, relationship, role, and voice ledgers all expand Yui's active agency. This is one of the clearest improvements over V1.

## 5.7 Supporting women perform emotional/social labor around Hachiman → STRENGTHEN, with formal-credit question OPEN

Yui, Yukino, Komachi, Hiratsuka, Miura, Iroha, and others repeatedly create openings, stabilize channels, supply advice, or bear social costs. The remaining question is not whether this labor exists, but how the first-person form distributes recognition and narrative climax.

## 5.8 V1 compressed distinct mechanisms into elegant total theories → STRENGTHEN diagnosis

This is the single clearest meta-revision. V2 consistently replaces elegant binaries with granular distinctions: observation/inference/jurisdiction; motive/standing/method; reliance/incapacity; role/performance/falsity; desire/certification; directness/firmness; influence/substitution; romantic choice/authenticity proof.

# 12. Terminal V1 → V2 statement

The first pass was not a failed analysis awaiting replacement. It was a strong interpretive model that had already located many of Oregairu's deepest concerns: defensive interpretation, costly helping, competence, accommodation, performance, dependency, the genuine, and the difficulty of owning desire.

Its principal weakness was **elegance**.

V1 often found one beautiful formulation and let it carry several mechanisms at once:

- self-sacrifice as control;
- self-removal as care;
- preservation as Yui's mode;
- codependency as diagnosis;
- authenticity as accountable continuation.

The Japanese-primary V2 reread repeatedly found that those formulations were directionally intelligent but too compressive.

The mature revision is therefore:

> **Oregairu is less a story about stripping away false forms until genuine feeling remains than a story about learning to inhabit imperfect forms without letting those forms replace the people inside them. Its characters do not become transparent, independent, roleless, or certain. They become somewhat more capable of owning desire, distinguishing knowledge from inference, asking rather than deciding, relying without surrendering authorship, performing without treating performance as falsity, accepting answers that alter the relationship, and continuing without certifying the result as perfect.**

That is the point at which V1 hands authority to V2.
