# OREGAIRU V2 — Relationship State Ledger

## Function

This ledger consolidates the relationship-state evidence generated by the complete V2 reread through Volume 14.5. It is not a romance ranking, a list of who is closest to whom, or a retrospective proof that the final pairing was inevitable. Its purpose is to preserve the changing structure of relationships while keeping each participant's independently evidenced character state intact.

The governing rule is simple:

> **A relationship is not a scalar.**

A relationship can become more trusting and less easy at the same time. Disclosure can rise while harmony falls. Attachment can strengthen while the existing form becomes unsustainable. Reliance can become healthier even as dependence becomes more visible. A romantic answer can close one uncertainty while preserving friendship, grief, jealousy, embarrassment, or future conflict.

Accordingly, this ledger tracks relational state as a vector rather than reducing it to “closer” or “farther apart.” It also preserves asymmetry. If Yui desires Hachiman romantically before Hachiman reciprocates, the correct state is not “uncertain romance” in a symmetrical sense; it is a relation containing **one-sided romantic desire plus increasing recognition pressure**. If Yukino trusts Hachiman's competence while rejecting his method, trust and authorization must be recorded separately.

Every entry routes back to canonical volume deep readings and checkpoint freezes. Exact Japanese wording and fixed-page/EPUB locators remain governed by those source artifacts. This ledger is the canonical longitudinal routing layer for later specialist synthesis.

# 1. Relationship-state protocol

## 1.1 Governing dimensions

The V2 method requires relationship state to be tracked across at least the following dimensions:

- **trust** — confidence in the other's competence, intention, discretion, or continued presence;
- **disclosure** — how much personally consequential information is voluntarily made answerable;
- **expectation** — assumptions about what the other will do, know, notice, or continue to provide;
- **permission to intervene** — what kinds of help, interference, correction, or involvement have actually been authorized;
- **dependence / reliance** — what functions, emotional regulation, identity, or action are routed through the other person;
- **obligation** — debt, role, promise, institutional duty, family duty, or moral burden attaching the parties;
- **jealousy** — possessive or comparative affect where textually supported;
- **embarrassment** — especially where embarrassment changes what can be said or done;
- **physical proximity** — chosen nearness, touch, shared movement, or avoidance;
- **conversational ease** — silence, banter, teasing, directness, or strain;
- **conflict tolerance** — ability to disagree, refuse, accuse, or disappoint without automatic dissolution;
- **reciprocity** — whether care, request, disclosure, cost, or attention can move both ways;
- **willingness to impose** — readiness to make a request, demand, correction, or claim on the other;
- **willingness to refuse** — readiness to say no without treating refusal as relational annihilation;
- **ordinary presence** — ability to inhabit low-stakes time together rather than existing only through crisis;
- **future-oriented assumption** — whether the relationship is imagined as continuing into tomorrow, next year, university, adulthood, or an unspecified future;
- **romantic desire** — separately attributed by participant rather than assumed from pair status;
- **recognition of the other's desire** — distinct from one's own desire and from willingness to answer it.

A derived dimension used throughout this ledger is **relational jurisdiction**: who acts as though they possess the right to define, preserve, end, or redesign the relationship. This is not a feeling. It is a procedural property inferred from requests, refusals, intervention methods, and acknowledged standing.

## 1.2 Evidence rules

1. **One participant cannot silently define both sides.** Hachiman's interpretation of Yukino, Yukino's interpretation of Yui, Haruno's diagnosis of the trio, or Yui's reading of Hachiman retains its original evidence class.
2. **Asymmetry is a valid state.** Mutuality must not be invented to make a table neat.
3. **Behavior can contradict narration without erasing narration.** If Hachiman denies particularity but continues seeking, remembering, helping, or protecting a relationship, both layers remain evidence.
4. **Conflict can be relational progress.** A relation that survives a meaningful refusal may be stronger than one preserved through avoidance.
5. **Harmony can be relational regression.** The hollow post-election club demonstrates that peaceful interaction can coexist with depleted relation.
6. **Reliance is not automatically dependence, and dependence is not automatically pathology.** The relevant question is what capacity the relation enables or disables and whether participants retain authorship.
7. **Ordinary life is evidence, not proof.** Post-crisis ease can strengthen a model of durability but cannot certify permanent health.
8. **Later outcomes do not rewrite prospective ambiguity.** The final Hachiman/Yukino relation does not convert early Yukino evidence into foreknown romance, and Yui's final non-reciprocity does not invalidate earlier dyadic possibility or warmth.
9. **Institutional continuity and relational continuity are distinct.** The Service Club may remain legally open while relational life is hollow; later it may change membership while relational practice survives.
10. **The triad is a system.** A pairwise interaction can alter the third participant's available choices even when the third is absent.

## 1.3 Phase backbone

The checkpoint sequence supplies the longitudinal spine:

| Boundary | System-level relation state |
|---|---|
| Checkpoint A, V01–V03 | emergent attachment housed inside institutional pretext |
| Checkpoint B, V04–V06.5 | cost-bearing relation with growing trust, reliance, and conflict tolerance |
| Checkpoint C, V07–V09 + Christmas coda | preservation crisis repaired through answerability; ordinary continuation becomes possible |
| Checkpoint D, V10–V11 | different desires become explicit; relation remains connected after nonidentity |
| Checkpoint E, V12–V14 | romantic non-equivalence is answered; chosen interdependence replaces diagnostic/separation logic |
| Final durability checkpoint, V14.5 Stories 4–5 | chosen form proves revisable enough to survive ordinary change without becoming sacred |

The movement is not “distance -> closeness.” It is better summarized as:

> **pretextual coexistence -> particular attachment -> costly preservation -> answerability -> conflict-bearing nonidentity -> chosen non-equivalence -> revisable ordinary continuity.**

# 2. Executive longitudinal relationship thesis

The deepest relationship change in Oregairu is not that the characters finally become close. They become capable of letting closeness generate **claims, refusals, consequences, and revisions**.

Early relationships are protected by role, debt, jokes, institutional tasks, idealization, and deniable kindness. These devices are not wholly false. They create real opportunities for connection. The problem appears when the device becomes more authoritative than the people using it.

That produces the series' repeated relational failure pattern:

> attachment exists -> direct stakes are dangerous -> a role, diagnosis, debt, or private inference is used to manage the stakes -> one person decides too much -> the relationship is preserved in form while losing answerability.

The mature alternative is not maximal confession. It is a relationship in which:

- people can ask without owning the answer;
- refuse without automatically ending the relation;
- help without turning incapacity into the justification for closeness;
- state desire without converting desire into jurisdiction;
- accept that another person's desire can remain different;
- continue ordinary life after a painful answer;
- and allow a meaningful form to change without calling the change betrayal.

The final V2 relationship thesis is therefore:

> **Oregairu moves from relationships protected from consequence toward relationships capable of surviving consequence.**

This is why the ending can contain an explicit Hachiman/Yukino romantic choice, real Yui loss, continuing Yui/Yukino friendship, continuing triadic relation, and later institutional succession without requiring any of those facts to cancel the others.

# 4. Hachiman ↔ Yukino relationship ledger

## RS-HY-01 — V01–V03: adversarial intimacy and idealization risk

**Trust:** grows quickly around competence, directness, and shared tolerance for silence.

**Disclosure:** ideological and situational rather than emotionally confessional. They know meaningful things about each other's worldview but far less about personal history and desire than Hachiman's sense of similarity suggests.

**Ease:** unusually high in bounded work, insults, silence, and deliberately unreal role-play. This ease is genuine but partly protected by the assumption that conventional intimacy is impossible.

**Expectation:** Hachiman begins turning Yukino's observed directness into an essential proposition: `雪ノ下は噓を吐かない`. Trust therefore contains an idealization risk from the start.

**Reliance:** competence-based and voluntary. Neither is yet dependent in a strong sense.

**Romantic state:** Hachiman's attraction is clear. Yukino's romantic desire is not prospectively established.

**Delta:** the relation becomes particular before either party can name it as friendship. Institutional language such as `うちの部員` gives Yukino a safer way to claim Hachiman than ordinary friendship language would.

**Freeze:** **adversarial intimacy with growing competence-trust and emerging epistemic idealization.**

**Source route:** V01 §6.1; V02 §6.3; V03 §6.2; Checkpoint A §6.1.

## RS-HY-02 — V04–V06.5: idealization breaks; revision becomes intimacy

Volume 4 deepens bounded personal disclosure. Their nighttime silence becomes a shared intimacy rule: Yukino signals family pressure, Hachiman deliberately does not press.

Volume 5 then demonstrates the limit of that rule. The accident history damages trust not because Yukino is proved generally deceptive, but because Hachiman had converted limited evidence into an identity claim. His disappointment is itself evidence of emotional particularity: a casual acquaintance could not violate an expectation that strong.

Volume 6 repairs the relation through a more provisional method. Hachiman criticizes Yukino's overfunctioning. Yukino criticizes his refusal to explain himself: when he never gives excuses, he denies the other person equal interpretive room. Her proposal, `もう一度問い直す`, becomes the better relational method.

By the Volume 6 ending:

- idealized transparency is weaker;
- contradiction is more survivable;
- trust is less absolute and therefore more usable;
- conflict tolerance rises;
- neither claims complete understanding;
- ordinary ease returns.

Volume 6.5 confirms that this state can function operationally. Hachiman can worry about Yukino overworking; Yukino can accept collaborative conflict; the pair can disagree over method without treating disagreement as rupture.

**Freeze:** **revised mutual recognition: less idealized, more conflict-tolerant, more capable of provisional understanding.**

**Source route:** V04 §6.2; V05 §6.1; V06 §6.1; V06.5 §6.1; Checkpoint B §14.1.

## RS-HY-03 — V07–V09: trust is spent, telepathy fails, answerability begins

Volume 7 intensifies authorization. Yukino entrusts Hachiman with judgment. He uses that entrusted discretion in a method she strongly rejects. The injury is not simply that he hurts himself; it is that **trust in judgment becomes operational license**.

Volume 8 repeats the jurisdiction problem differently. Yukino's election decision has explicit volitional content, but Hachiman discounts it because he privately predicts a relational future he cannot bear to risk. Formal preservation succeeds; relational life becomes hollow. Their old high ease in silence no longer functions because silence now carries suppressed conflict rather than compatible distance.

Volume 9 changes the procedure. Hachiman stops claiming that he can privately derive the correct relational answer and becomes the requester. Yukino does not pretend to understand his `本物` request completely. She accepts it while preserving incomprehension and later asks him for future help.

The immediate Christmas coda strengthens this new state. Hachiman gives Yukino a particularized gift whose full symbolic reason he cannot explain; Yukino chooses to wear it. Meaning does not need to be exhaustively shared before an action can be offered and answered.

**Dimensions at Checkpoint C:**

- trust: high but no longer based on presumed transparency;
- disclosure: higher, with uncertainty explicitly marked;
- permission to intervene: future care is permitted, not unlimited;
- conflict tolerance: materially higher after V9;
- reciprocity: restored;
- ordinary presence: demonstrably possible again;
- romantic desire: materially charged but unresolved;
- jurisdiction: beginning to shift from private inference toward request/answer.

**Freeze:** **high significance with damaged telepathy and restored answerability.**

**Source route:** V07 §6.1; V08 §6.1–6.5; V09 §6.1 and §6.4; Christmas coda §7.1; Checkpoint C §16.1.

## RS-HY-04 — V10–V11: direct inquiry improves before refusal tolerance fully matures

Volume 10 contains a crucial procedural improvement: Hachiman asks Yukino about her future rather than simply deriving it. Yukino voluntarily discloses; Hachiman treats that answer as private rather than common property. The relation therefore gains both disclosure and privacy ethics.

But Hachiman's protection logic remains dangerous. He can recognize Yukino's uncertain self-location while still acting as though he knows what form her autonomy should take.

Volume 11 makes the contradiction explicit. The club's warmth is real. Hachiman notices Yukino's favorable gestures and then suppresses their standing. Yukino, meanwhile, becomes more able to correct both Yui and Hachiman when they begin deciding her feelings or future. Her `私の気持ちを勝手に決めないで` marks a jurisdictional boundary.

She then asks them to hear her request.

**Freeze:** **high mutual significance plus direct inquiry and chosen reliance, with autonomy conflict now answerable rather than merely inferred.**

**Source route:** V10 §6; V10.5 §6; V11 §6.1 and §18.3; Checkpoint D §18.1.

## RS-HY-05 — V12–V13: autonomy experiment, rivalry authorization, and contested closure

Volume 12 clarifies that Yukino's request is not “help me with the prom.” She asks Hachiman and Yui to witness the final result of a self-authored attempt. She explicitly refuses Hachiman's direct help: `あなたの力はもう借りないわ` / `お願い。私にやらせて`.

This lowers Hachiman's authorization despite high attachment.

Volume 13 tests whether he can remain involved without simply taking over. His dummy prom changes the decision environment rather than substituting for Yukino's operational project. Crucially, Yukino later **consents to rivalry**. Direct-help authorization and rivalry authorization are therefore separate states.

Yukino then identifies her own reliance: she had come to assume Hachiman would somehow make the prom work and says `あなたに依存していたの`. Hachiman simultaneously recognizes the danger of treating his desire to help as proof that the relation is not codependent.

The wager produces a proposed closure. Yukino asks him to fulfill Yui's wish and confirms that this is her own wish. The speech is genuinely self-authored, but it also attempts to allocate Hachiman and Yui's future. Hachiman supplies the triadic correction: `これは俺とお前が勝手に決めることじゃない`.

**Freeze:** **strong attachment and explicit dependence evidence inside a relation whose participants are increasingly capable of setting boundaries, but still capable of using self-authored choices to decide too much for others.**

**Source route:** V12 §6.1–6.4; V13 §6.1 and request/intervention sections; Checkpoint E §§4–6, 12.1.

## RS-HY-06 — V14: reciprocal life claim under recognized capability

Volume 14 rejects both dominant shortcuts: separation is not automatically the cure, and romance does not prove that the relationship was healthy all along.

Hachiman eventually states the reason for continued relation as his own desire: `俺は関わり続けたい`. This is a major shift in obligation structure. Continued involvement no longer derives final authority from club duty, a rescue role, an old promise, or Yukino's need.

The bridge scene remains procedurally imperfect. Hachiman engineers the encounter and initially physically stops Yukino. V2 does not erase that. The scene becomes more answerable after the hold loosens: Yukino stays, challenges his wording and valuation of her, is given room to reject or discard what he offers, and then makes her own counter-request: `あなたの人生を、私にください`.

Later, the cleaner autonomy image arrives:

`一人で立てるのに……`

`知ってる`

Hachiman offers support while explicitly recognizing capacity. Yukino knowingly takes it.

Her later `あなたが好きよ。比企谷くん` supplies conventional romantic naming without reducing the larger life claim to one word.

**Freeze:** **mutually chosen romantic/life involvement; reciprocal claims rather than savior/beneficiary roles; support increasingly legitimated by choice rather than incapacity; continuing risk of over-involvement remains.**

**Source route:** V14 §§6.1, 7, 9–10, 18.4; Checkpoint E §12.1.

## RS-HY-07 — V14.5 Stories 4–5: ordinary futurity without fusion

The true post-ending material lowers the register of the life claim into routine practice.

Hachiman and Yukino:

- compare cram schools;
- imagine meeting even if they attend different universities;
- prefer shared study without requiring identical academic paths;
- exchange one-month anniversary gestures;
- joke awkwardly about one-year and ten-year horizons.

Hachiman explicitly rejects the idea that Yukino should lower her academic target to match him. Proximity is preferred, not purchased through self-reduction.

The anniversary scene supplies future inclusion without future certification. Neither can meaningfully guarantee ten years. The relation is durable enough to imagine the future and uncertain enough to remain honest about not knowing it.

**Terminal freeze:** **chosen interdependence becomes mundane enough to plan around without becoming totalizing enough to erase difference.**

**Source route:** V14.5 §§6.1, 15; final durability checkpoint §§4, 17.6.

# 5. Hachiman ↔ Yui relationship ledger

## RS-HYUI-01 — V01–V03: particular care offered, particular meaning disputed

Yui creates intimacy early through naming, repeated approach, requests, waiting, direct contact, and explicit preference for Hachiman's involvement. Hachiman notices much of this but filters favorable interpretation through prior humiliation and the accident/debt model.

The Volume 2 rupture is the first major jurisdictional failure in the dyad: Hachiman tells Yui what her kindness “really” means and attempts to close the relationship as debt. Yui rejects that causal account.

Volume 3 turns conflict tolerance into evidence. Yui returns, states that she does not want the relation to end, and survives a direct disagreement over why it exists.

**Freeze:** **real but asymmetric trust; increasing disclosure; strongly active romantic possibility from Yui; Hachiman resistant to granting positive evidence behavioral standing.**

**Source route:** V01 §6.2; V02 §6.1; V03 §6.1; Checkpoint A §6.2.

## RS-HYUI-02 — V04–V06.5: future claims become harder to dismiss

Yui increasingly states concrete wants rather than merely creating opportunities. The fireworks invitation, future one-on-one plans, and V6 `待たないで、こっちから行くの` movement all reduce Hachiman's room for deniability.

Hachiman begins relying on Yui operationally and thanking her. This is important because the dyad is not only romantic tension; it becomes practical partnership.

At the same time, Hachiman repeatedly interrupts or abstracts moments that could force explicit interpretation.

**Freeze:** **particular care, active future pursuit, practical reciprocity, and growing romantic legibility; Hachiman continues using ambiguity as answer-avoidance.**

**Source route:** V04 §6.1; V05 §6.2; V06 §6.2; V06.5 §6.2; Checkpoint B §14.2.

## RS-HYUI-03 — V07–V09: Yui becomes a methodological boundary-setter

In Kyoto, Yui does more than reveal jealousy or desire. She sets a method boundary and recognizes Hachiman's self-implicated blind spot.

During the election and Christmas crisis, she repeatedly rejects solitary repair. She acknowledges her own complicity and insists that the difficult conversation continue rather than allowing procedural smoothing to replace relational repair.

At Checkpoint C, Yui's romantic desire is strongly legible, but the more important change is procedural: she increasingly claims standing to tell Hachiman that his way of protecting people also affects her.

The Christmas coda adds her continuity proposition: `終わらなくても、いいと思うけど……`. She can imagine reciprocity without a terminal zero balance more readily than Hachiman can.

**Freeze:** **strong care, strong one-sided romantic desire, direct boundary-setting, growing reciprocity, and increasing pressure on Hachiman to stop using indefinite non-answer as protection.**

**Source route:** V07 §6.2; V08 relationship sections; V09 §6.2 and §6.4; Christmas coda §7.2; Checkpoint C §16.2.

## RS-HYUI-04 — V10–V11: ordinary partnership coexists with impending incompatibility

The dyad remains genuinely warm. Yui offers shared aftermath when Hachiman's work fails, trusts his capacity while criticizing his suffering, and eventually asks him on the aquarium outing.

She completes the old cookie request. She states that she wants everything and has decided.

Her Volume 11 proposal is not passive sacrifice. It is a claimant's proposal for preserving the whole ordinary world she values. Hachiman rejects it because the desired conclusion decides too much in advance.

The important relation state is therefore not “Yui loses.” It is that **ordinary intimacy becomes ethically inseparable from answerable romantic consequence**.

**Freeze:** **caring, future-capable dyad with highly explicit Yui desire; Hachiman recognizes non-overlapping wishes; preservation can no longer pretend the romantic question is neutral.**

**Source route:** V10 §6; V10.5 §6; V11 §§6.1–6.3; Checkpoint D §18.2.

## RS-HYUI-05 — V12–V13: intimacy remains real while Yui rejects rescue ambiguity

Volume 12 contains some of the strongest two-person ordinary-life evidence for the pair, including the IKEA sequence and shared photograph. V2 preserves this as real dyadic warmth; it is not retroactively downgraded because Hachiman later chooses Yukino.

Yui's more important change is epistemic and ethical. She knows visible tears can trigger Hachiman's rescue behavior and says she does not want to become `可哀想な子`. She wants him to hurt her properly rather than protect her through ambiguity: `ヒーローじゃないのを知ってるから、ちゃんと傷つけて欲しかった`.

In Volume 13, she collaborates with Hachiman, continues ordinary contact, and separates certainty of feeling from certainty about relational ontology. She can know she loves without claiming she knows whether the relation is `本物`.

**Freeze:** **real dyadic intimacy plus an increasingly explicit demand for an answer rather than rescue.**

**Source route:** V12 §§6.2, Yui interludes, 18.6; V13 §§6.2, 9.4; Checkpoint E §§5, 12.2.

## RS-HYUI-06 — V14: romantic non-reciprocity becomes answerable

Hachiman gives Yui the answer the series has delayed: she does not need to wait for some future version of him. The answer causes genuine grief. That cost must not be removed by later social continuation.

Yui nevertheless helps move him toward Yukino, not because her own desire disappears but because she refuses the protective arrangement in which nobody is allowed to choose.

Her final return is relationally significant precisely because romantic ambiguity is no longer the price of continued presence.

**Freeze:** **romantic non-reciprocity explicit and painful; social intimacy and moral standing continue; Yui is allowed both loss and future presence.**

**Source route:** V14 §6.2 and final Service Club chapter; Checkpoint E §12.2.

## RS-HYUI-07 — V14.5: relation after answer, not ambiguity instead of answer

Post-ending material does not reopen the romantic choice. Yui remains active in the club, participates in ordinary plans, and retains influence. There is no evidence that Hachiman's answer has been withdrawn or that Yui's pain has vanished.

The significance is durability: the dyad remains socially inhabitable after an answer that did not satisfy Yui romantically.

**Terminal freeze:** **continued relation after answer, not continued ambiguity instead of answer.**

**Source route:** V14.5 §6.2; final durability checkpoint §17.7.

# 6. Yukino ↔ Yui relationship ledger

## RS-YY-01 — V01–V03: friendship becomes particular before the triangle stabilizes

Yui is the faster relationship-namer. She interprets Yukino's bluntness as `本音`, gives her a nickname, calls her a friend, initiates physical affection, and assumes continuation.

Yukino is initially bewildered by positive reception but reciprocates behaviorally: she watches Yui's confrontation with Miura, smiles at success, acts on her requests, later plans a birthday expression even if Yui never returns, and admits Yui's presence is helpful.

By Volume 3, the friendship has its own communication channel, plans, touch, gratitude, and reliance.

**Freeze:** **rapidly particular friendship in which Yui supplies explicit relational language and Yukino increasingly supplies reciprocal action.**

**Source route:** V01 §6.3; V02 §6.2; V03 §6.3; Checkpoint A §6.3.

## RS-YY-02 — V04–V06.5: reciprocal reliance and productive difference

Yui explicitly promises that she will not become one of the people who envies and rejects Yukino. In Volume 6 she admits possessiveness: `あたし、思ってたよりずっとゆきのんのこと好きなのかも`.

The relation then gains a major autonomy marker: Yukino directly asks Yui for help; Yui admits limitation rather than performing competence she lacks; Yukino compensates instead of withdrawing the request.

This is an unusually healthy early model of differentiated reliance.

By 6.5, Yui can criticize Yukino's plan and Yukino can remain affectionate. The dyad tolerates disagreement inside continued closeness.

**Freeze:** **particular friendship becoming practical interdependence with high conflict tolerance.**

**Source route:** V04 §6.3; V05 §6.3; V06 §6.3; V06.5 §6.3; Checkpoint B §14.3.

## RS-YY-03 — V07–V09: friendship survives rivalry and political conflict

The crucial V2 correction is that rivalry does not replace friendship.

Across Kyoto, the election, and Christmas:

- both have stakes involving Hachiman;
- both can desire different futures;
- Yui can criticize Yukino;
- Yukino can resist Yui's framing;
- political competition becomes real;
- neither must pretend harmony to preserve relation.

The Christmas coda adds embodied ease, teasing, calibrated gifts, and ordinary leisure immediately after crisis.

**Freeze:** **real rivalry inside a particular friendship whose continuation no longer requires harmony.**

**Source route:** V07–V09 relationship sections; Christmas coda §7.3; Checkpoint C §16.3.

## RS-YY-04 — V10–V11: friendship becomes capable of carrying incompatible proposals

Yui continues to include Yukino rather than trying to convert Hachiman contact into exclusion. Yukino separately prepares care for Yui. Ordinary ease persists.

At the aquarium, Yui states the future she wants. Yukino refuses to have her feelings determined by either Yui or Hachiman and then asks them to hear her own request. Yui agrees.

This is relationally more mature than harmony: both girls can make claims that threaten the old form and remain in the conversation.

**Freeze:** **real friendship, real rivalry, real care, and enough conflict tolerance that neither romantic victory nor unanimity is required for continued standing.**

**Source route:** V10–V11 relationship sections; Checkpoint D §18.3.

## RS-YY-05 — V12–V13: decision displacement is named; self-removal is refused

Yui recognizes that she has partly routed the decisive choice through Yukino: `あたしは彼女に依存したの`. V2 freezes this as **decision displacement**, not a theorem that the friendship itself is pathological.

Yukino's V13 closure request then makes the reciprocal danger visible: self-authored self-removal can still attempt to decide Yui's future.

Yui rejects that premise. Her response is future practice: lunches, sleepovers, trips, April, and years. She insists that Yukino remains part of her desired future independent of romantic outcome.

**Freeze:** **the friendship becomes explicitly non-substitutable by the romantic contest.**

**Source route:** V12 §§6.3–6.4; V13 §6.3 and closure sections; Checkpoint E §12.3.

## RS-YY-06 — V14: rivalry becomes explicit without friendship erasure

Before Hachiman resolves the romance, Yukino and Yui speak directly about the proposed ending. Yui rejects unilateral closure. Yukino states her feeling. Yui reciprocates disclosure.

After Hachiman chooses Yukino, Yui's return is not only about Hachiman. Yukino visibly welcomes her, and the final consultation is jointly inhabited.

The relation therefore survives because each girl is allowed to remain a subject rather than becoming a sacrificial function of the other's happiness.

**Freeze:** **romantic rivalry fully explicit; friendship retains independent authorship and future standing.**

**Source route:** V14 §6.3; Checkpoint E §12.3.

## RS-YY-07 — V14.5: friendship becomes ordinary again without historical erasure

Yukino and Yui shop together, coordinate Komachi's gift, exchange glances, divide attention, and present the mug jointly. Their friendship is not staged as solemn forgiveness.

This ordinary ease strengthens durability but does not prove that Yui's earlier pain disappeared.

**Terminal freeze:** **friendship survives by becoming ordinary again without pretending the conflict never occurred.**

**Source route:** V14.5 §6.3; final durability checkpoint §17.8.

# 7. Service Club triad ledger

## RS-TRI-01 — V01–V03: institutional pretext becomes relational infrastructure

Yui is not merely a mediator between an established Hachiman/Yukino dyad. She is the first active constitutor of “the three of us” through names, invitations, requests, friendship language, and emotional feedback.

The club's earliest relational function is double:

1. tasks and roles provide a socially usable pretext;
2. repeated presence gives people who cannot directly say “stay” a place to keep returning.

Yui's temporary absence in Volume 3 proves that her presence is no longer neutral. When she returns, the three bodies occupy the room again, but Hachiman already perceives asymmetry.

**Freeze:** **an emergent three-person place whose institutional form lets attachment exist before all three can safely name what it asks of them.**

## RS-TRI-02 — V04–V06.5: the triad becomes cost-bearing distributed competence

The trio increasingly functions through differentiated strengths:

- Yukino: structure, administration, formal argument;
- Yui: social translation, audience sense, atmosphere and relational reach;
- Hachiman: adversarial diagnosis, incentive analysis, loopholes.

The key improvement is that distributed competence becomes possible. The solution system does not always require Hachiman to be the sole meaningful actor or Yukino to absorb all labor.

But the triad also becomes emotionally cost-bearing. Withdrawal, humiliation, idealization, and overwork now affect all three.

**Freeze:** **institutionalized relationship whose members are becoming mutually consequential; not yet pathological, but no longer low-cost.**

## RS-TRI-03 — V07–V09: preservation is exposed as insufficient

Kyoto reveals the cost of unilateral judgment. The election reveals that formal continuity can be preserved while personal authorship is discounted. The hollow post-election club is the decisive negative control: the institution remains open, yet relational life is depleted.

Volume 9 repairs procedure rather than producing unanimity. Hachiman becomes requester. Yui keeps the conversation alive. Yukino accepts without pretending full comprehension. Different responses are allowed to remain different.

The Christmas coda then demonstrates one voluntary ordinary continuation: `また明日`, negotiated scheduling, differentiated gifts, leisure.

**Freeze:** **re-opened covenant capable of ordinary continuation; answerability is livable before the triangle is solved.**

## RS-TRI-04 — V10–V11: from reopened covenant to explicit incompatibility

The trio regains warm ordinary life, but that warmth cannot indefinitely contain nonidentical desire without consequence.

By Volume 11:

- Yui states a desired future;
- Hachiman admits the answer he seeks is his own ideal, not neutral logic;
- Yukino refuses both of them the authority to decide her feelings;
- Yukino becomes requester.

The relation survives the discovery that the members do not want identical things.

**Freeze:** **connected after nonidentity, not reconciled through unanimity.**

## RS-TRI-05 — V12–V13: the triangle becomes a jurisdiction problem

The dependency vocabulary enters the text. Each member identifies or is accused of a different mechanism:

- Yukino: reliance on Hachiman/Yui and expectation that Hachiman will solve;
- Yui: decision displacement onto Yukino;
- Hachiman: self-worth reinforced through being needed;
- Haruno: `共依存` as systemic diagnosis.

The critical relational result is not a verdict that the triad is pathological. It is the recognition that no two members can ethically decide the whole system for the third.

Hachiman explicitly states this near the end of V13. Yukino's self-removal request is therefore self-authored but not automatically sovereign over the triad.

**Freeze:** **declared closure regime whose authority remains contested.**

## RS-TRI-06 — V14: known non-equivalence replaces protected ambiguity

The mainline ending removes romantic equivalence:

- Hachiman and Yukino mutually choose one another;
- Yui's continuing love is known;
- Yui's grief is real;
- Yukino/Yui friendship is independently affirmed;
- the Service Club is recreated;
- Yui returns voluntarily;
- Yukino invites her into the conversation.

The remaining problem is explicitly framed as potentially ongoing.

This is not the old triangle restored. The old triangle depended partly on ambiguity. The final triad depends on the capacity to continue after the ambiguity is gone.

**Freeze:** **continued triadic relation after acknowledged non-equivalence.**

## RS-TRI-07 — V14.5: the institution survives by allowing form to change

The post-ending succession material makes the final anti-sanctification correction. Hachiman realizes he has begun to `神聖視` the current Service Club configuration and explicitly rejects the idea that their relationship was ever perfect.

Komachi becomes president. Iroha gains practical continuity standing. New members are allowed. Mugs and people can change. Yukino teaches Komachi tea.

Continuity becomes transmissible practice rather than identical membership.

**Terminal freeze:**

> **The Service Club's deepest continuity is not who occupies the chairs, but whether people continue creating a place where difficult requests, imperfect answers, and forms of care can be worked through.**

# 8. Hachiman ↔ Hayama relationship ledger

## RS-HHAY-01 — V02–V04: bounded cooperation before ideological rivalry hardens

Hachiman can diagnose parts of Hayama's group structure with unusual accuracy. Hayama can accept that analysis without immediate defensiveness and can invite Hachiman into bounded cooperation.

The early relation therefore contains mutual competence recognition before it becomes strongly adversarial.

## RS-HHAY-02 — V06–V09: competing social intelligences and moral outsourcing

Volume 6 makes the methodological conflict explicit. Hayama's `どうして、そんなやり方しかできないんだ` follows Hachiman using Hayama as an instrument in a plan. Hachiman simultaneously respects the decency that makes Hayama react.

Across Kyoto and the election, each becomes a mirror for the other's intervention logic. Hayama preserves groups through smoothing, expectation management, and social capital; Hachiman prefers explicit cost concentration and adversarial leverage.

They understand each other unusually well while disliking what that understanding reveals.

**Freeze at C:** **ideological rivals, mutually useful interpreters, each capable of projecting a needed self-image onto the other.**

## RS-HHAY-03 — V10–V11: privacy, expectation, and refusal

The career-choice material humanizes Hayama's role. Hachiman wants information; Hayama refuses. Hachiman's desire to know does not erase Hayama's standing to withhold.

This dyad becomes a test of Hachiman's improved but incomplete refusal tolerance.

The larger V2 correction is that Hayama's social performance is not simply fake. Expected conduct can be burdensome and still partly owned.

## RS-HHAY-04 — V12–V14.5: proxy guilt is refused; rivalry becomes ordinary

Hayama later attempts to route childhood regret into Hachiman's responsibility for Yukino. Hachiman refuses: `その後悔はお前のもんだろ。勝手に俺に託すんじゃねぇよ`.

This is a healthier boundary than Hachiman's earlier readiness to absorb costs others wanted solved.

By the post-ending state, the pair can coexist without requiring philosophical reconciliation.

**Terminal freeze:** **de-idealized rival interpreters with bounded cooperation and clearer ownership of guilt, privacy, and responsibility.**

# 9. Hachiman ↔ Iroha relationship ledger

## RS-HIRO-01 — V07.5–V08: manipulation, imposed candidacy, and mutual use

Iroha enters through an imposed institutional problem. Hachiman recognizes and uses her strategic presentation; she in turn learns his willingness to engineer outcomes.

The early relation is not innocent mentorship. Both know manipulation is occurring, though their standing and experience are asymmetric.

## RS-HIRO-02 — V09–V10: installed role becomes reciprocal working relation

As Iroha begins owning the presidency, Hachiman's relation to her changes. She is no longer merely the beneficiary or product of his election solution. She develops independent institutional judgment and her own desires.

The dyad gains teasing ease, mutual knowledge, and practical cooperation.

## RS-HIRO-03 — V10.5–V14: mixed motive and functional indirectness

Iroha is a major control case against the rule that indirectness equals falsity. Flirtation, strategic pretext, real care, institutional competence, selfish desire, and enjoyment coexist.

Hachiman remains prone to over-helping her, while Iroha becomes increasingly capable of managing the office without him. The relation therefore improves when support becomes less identity-defining.

## RS-HIRO-04 — V14.5: post-choice boundary work remains unfinished

Iroha explicitly admits that a present is an excuse: `プレゼントは言い訳ですよ`. The pretext is transparent enough that performance and sincerity cannot be cleanly separated.

She also assumes a weekend plan before receiving a fully clean yes; Hachiman characterizes the process as `なし崩し`.

There is no basis for reopening the romantic route, but there is clear evidence that post-choice life still requires boundary work.

**Terminal freeze:** **mixed-motive, high-ease relation with real trust, flirtatious access, and continuing low-stakes authorization problems.**

# 10. Yukino ↔ Haruno and Yukino ↔ family relationship ledger

## RS-YHAR-01 — early series: admiration, imitation, asymmetrical interpretive power

Yukino's relation to Haruno contains admiration and comparison before it contains any simple rivalry. Yukino can say she wanted to become like Haruno while also insisting that her own dispositions are not merely a newly fabricated copy.

Haruno holds substantial informational and social power. She can provoke Yukino, name family expectations, and influence how others interpret Yukino.

## RS-YHAR-02 — middle series: differentiation becomes necessary

As Yukino's competence becomes public, the sister relationship increasingly turns on whether achievement is self-authored or merely another iteration of Haruno's path.

Haruno's readings are often perceptive, but her authority over Yukino's meaning becomes progressively less acceptable.

## RS-YHAR-03 — V11–V13: care, diagnosis, and overreach coexist

Haruno can support Yukino's attempt to choose, can offer family-specific assistance, and can identify real dependency mechanisms. She can also ask whether Yukino has a self and present `共依存` as a totalizing explanation.

V2 therefore refuses both the villain model and the oracle model.

Their relation contains real care expressed through provocation and social pressure, but care does not grant Haruno sovereign interpretive authority.

## RS-YFAM-04 — V12–V14: direct family politics replace silent inheritance

Yukino increasingly states future desires and contests her mother directly. The prom provides an institutional arena in which family power, student authorship, precedent, and social influence become explicit.

Hachiman enters this family world, but the romance does not rescue Yukino from it. He becomes incorporated into a future social field she still has to negotiate herself.

**Terminal freeze:** **differentiation without severance; family conflict becomes part of authorship rather than a problem solved by romantic exit.**

# 11. Hachiman ↔ Komachi relationship ledger

## RS-HK-01 — V02–V05: secure attachment as a control case

The sibling relation can survive insult, worry, direct affection, embarrassment, imposition, and ordinary dependence because neither party treats each interaction as a referendum on whether the bond exists.

Hachiman can accept Komachi's gratitude and physical affection more safely than comparable attention from peers because particularity is not in doubt.

## RS-HK-02 — V07.5–V09: framing without destination control

Komachi often creates opportunities, nudges, and social frames. This is manipulation in a broad sense, but typically low-coercion because she leaves the destination available to the participants.

This makes her an important comparison for Hachiman's stronger tendency to engineer outcomes.

## RS-HK-03 — V10–V12: differentiation without relational annihilation

Komachi's school transition and future separation provide a direct autonomy counterexample. Independence does not require the sibling relation to disappear.

This becomes especially important when Hachiman begins treating distance from Yukino as synonymous with `自立`.

## RS-HK-04 — V14–V14.5: succession and changed form

Komachi becomes Service Club president without becoming a copy of Yukino, Hachiman, or Yui. Hachiman must resist deciding what the club should become for her.

He eventually recognizes her as `比企谷部長`, while the older members transmit practice without owning the future institution.

**Terminal freeze:** **secure attachment plus differentiation; one of the series' clearest demonstrations that continuity can survive changed role, distance, and institutional form.**

# 12. Hachiman ↔ Hiratsuka relationship ledger

## RS-HHIR-01 — early series: coercive entry into a beneficial institution

Hiratsuka begins by imposing the Service Club on Hachiman. The relation is therefore never a pure autonomy model. Her pedagogical authority is real and sometimes forceful.

Yet she consistently treats him as capable of change rather than as a fixed cynical essence.

## RS-HHIR-02 — V05–V09: mentorship becomes question-preserving

Ramen, private conversations, and the V9 correction shift the relation from teacher assigning tasks toward adult helping Hachiman frame problems he must answer himself.

Her mature contribution is not “here is the correct answer.” It is that zero harm is impossible, calculation is incomplete, and Hachiman must become aware of what his choices cost.

## RS-HHIR-03 — V10–V14: iterative recognition and differentiation

Hiratsuka continues shaping options without owning final choice. In Volume 14 she explicitly rejects `共依存` as a borrowed totalizing label and refuses to let Hachiman use theory instead of feeling.

The final farewell supplies a clean differentiation image: when she offers a hand to help him stand, he stands under his own power and turns the offered hand into a handshake of gratitude.

**Terminal freeze:** **legitimate dependence ending in one form without invalidating its history; mentorship succeeds by making final authority unnecessary.**

# 13. Secondary relationship controls

## 13.1 Saki ↔ Taishi — protection becomes more reciprocal

Saki's attempt to protect family finances initially shuts Taishi out of decisions that affect him. The Volume 2 resolution does not destroy her protective motive. It gives Taishi standing to say that her self-protection affects the family and introduces an institutional tool that reduces the material burden without replacing her agency.

**Control function:** care can remain care while becoming more answerable.

## 13.2 Sagami ↔ Meguri — recognition without absolution

Meguri recognizes Sagami's effort without pretending competence failures or earlier behavior did not occur. That accurate recognition helps Sagami continue.

**Control function:** people may need recognition that neither idealizes nor humiliates them.

## 13.3 Hachiman ↔ Sagami — sustainable strangerhood

The relationship does not heal into friendship. By 6.5 they can occupy the same institutional world without continuously reopening the cultural-festival injury.

**Control function:** successful relation does not always mean intimacy; functional distance can be a legitimate endpoint.

## 13.4 Hayama's friend group — preservation by smoothing

Hayama's group demonstrates a social system that often preserves continuity by avoiding direct conflict and distributing face-saving interpretations. This is neither purely fake nor fully answerable.

**Control function:** group stability can be real while the method of maintaining it suppresses individual authorship.

## 13.5 Yui ↔ Iroha — female social intelligence without simple alliance/opposition

Across the later series Yui can recognize Iroha's strategic behavior without reducing her to manipulator, and Iroha can understand Yui's social standing without fully sharing her motives.

**Control function:** social intelligence can produce accurate recognition without emotional sameness.

## 13.6 Komachi ↔ Iroha — succession creates a new continuity axis

By V14.5, Iroha and Komachi develop a bickering but functional senior/junior relation. Iroha remains at Soubu after the older trio leaves; Komachi formally asks for her help.

**Control function:** institutional continuity can migrate into new relationships rather than requiring the founding triad to remain physically present.

| Hachiman–Yukino | insults safe; personal contradiction less safe | explicit refusal, correction, rivalry, and romantic disagreement can be survived |

| Hachiman–Iroha | manipulation nested in imposed role | teasing, refusal, pretext, and boundary friction become ordinary rather than catastrophic |

## 14.2 Reliance and authorization

| Relationship | Problematic pattern | Stronger later pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Hachiman–Yukino | trust or need interpreted as broad license to intervene | explicit refusal, consent to rivalry, chosen support under recognized capability |
| Hachiman–Yui | Hachiman decides what Yui's kindness means | Yui defines her own desire and asks for a real answer |

| Hachiman–Komachi | opportunity engineering | succession with destination left to Komachi |
| Hachiman–Hiratsuka | teacher imposes institution | mentor shapes options while final choice returns to Hachiman |

## 14.3 Ordinary presence

Ordinary life is a major evidentiary dimension because crisis can force contact that does not generalize.

- **Hachiman/Yukino:** silence, reading, tea, later study planning and anniversaries.
- **Hachiman/Yui:** contact, outings, work, IKEA, club life after non-reciprocity.
- **Yukino/Yui:** messages, touch, study plans, gift shopping, post-ending coordination.
- **Triad:** tea, clubroom routine, leisure, Christmas, recreated Service Club, later succession.
- **Hachiman/Iroha:** office work, banter, errands, post-ending invitation.
- **Hachiman/Komachi:** domestic banter, food, exams, school transition.

The V2 rule is that ordinary presence **strengthens evidence of durability but does not certify permanence or erase conflict**.

## 14.4 Future-oriented assumption

The series' relational grammar changes when future time becomes speakable.

Early Hachiman often treats relation as a ledger that should be brought to zero. Yui repeatedly imagines continuation first. Yukino becomes increasingly able to make future requests. The Christmas coda gives Hachiman `また明日`. Volume 14 turns continued involvement into an explicit life claim. Volume 14.5 lowers that claim into universities, cram schools, meetings, anniversaries, and institutional succession.

The mature form is not guaranteed permanence. It is **permission to make future assumptions while preserving revision**.

# 15. Major V1 → V2 relationship revisions

## 15.1 REJECT — “the Service Club is Hachiman plus two girls who orbit him”

Yukino/Yui develops early, directly, and independently. Their friendship has its own messages, touch, requests, rivalry, future claims, and post-romantic durability.

## 15.2 REVISE — “Hachiman/Yukino are defined by effortless mutual understanding”

They are often unusually readable to one another, but the major arc specifically dismantles presumed telepathy. Their mature relation becomes stronger when incomplete understanding is admitted and answerability replaces inference-as-jurisdiction.

## 15.3 REJECT — “Yui mainly preserves the triangle because she is too kind to choose”

Yui sometimes preserves ambiguity for self-protective reasons, but she also sets method boundaries, claims desired futures, rejects self-erasure, asks for an answer, bears loss, and continues friendship afterward.

## 15.4 REVISE — “Yukino withdraws from the triangle because she recognizes Hachiman/Yui as the natural pair”

Early triadic asymmetry exists, but later Yukino self-removal is a much more complex act involving authorship, dependence, family/selfhood, guilt, and an attempt to allocate others' futures. The text does not ratify withdrawal as the mature solution.

## 15.5 REJECT — “romantic choice dissolves the triad”

Romantic equivalence ends. The triad does not. The final relation becomes explicitly non-equivalent and remains socially/institutionally active.

## 15.6 REVISE — “codependency explains the Hachiman/Yukino relation”

Dependency mechanisms are real. `共依存` is a useful bounded model, but not a total relationship definition or automatic separation prescription.

## 15.7 STRENGTHEN — “Yui/Yukino is one of the series' central relationships”

V2 strengthens this substantially. Their independent friendship is structurally necessary to understanding why the ending can preserve a triad after romantic non-equivalence.

## 15.8 REVISE — “Hayama and Hachiman are moral opposites”

They are rival social intelligences who repeatedly illuminate each other's blind spots. Both preserve, manipulate, and carry expectations differently; neither is the simple good or bad alternative.

## 15.9 REVISE — “the ending proves the chosen relationship is genuine”

The ending proves that Hachiman and Yukino choose reciprocal involvement. It does not certify permanent health or metaphysical `本物`. Hachiman's `疑い続けます` and later `神聖視` correction explicitly resist certification.

## 15.10 STRENGTHEN — “the Service Club is a relationship technology as well as an institution”

This survives V2 but requires refinement. The institution can support attachment, conceal conflict, become hollow, reopen answerability, and finally outlive its founding membership through transmitted practice.

# 16. Relationship contradictions that must remain open

1. Hachiman/Yukino can be mutually chosen and still require future boundary work.
2. Yukino's dependence evidence can be real without proving incapacity or invalidating chosen support.
3. Yui can accept romantic non-reciprocity while residual romantic feeling remains unresolved.
4. Yukino/Yui can be genuine friends and genuine romantic rivals simultaneously.
5. Hachiman can respect Yukino's authorship more than before and still use manipulative institutional methods.
6. Yui's atmosphere work can protect others' agency and can also postpone consequences she fears.
7. Iroha's pretexts can be sincere and can still create soft-coercion problems.
8. Hayama's public role can be externally expected and partly self-owned.
9. Haruno can diagnose a real mechanism and still overclaim the diagnosis's normative authority.
10. The final Service Club can be durable precisely because it changes, which means durability cannot be equated with preservation of current form.
11. Hachiman/Yui ordinary warmth before the ending remains real even though the romantic answer is negative.
12. Hachiman/Yukino early attraction and particularity remain real without making the final outcome prospectively inevitable.
13. A relationship can become more conflictual because it has become more answerable.
14. A relationship can become more peaceful because people have stopped saying what matters; peace is therefore not a sufficient metric of health.
15. Post-ending viability strengthens the mainline model but cannot retroactively prove that the bridge or final choice was risk-free.

# 18. Outgoing authority and next analytical route

## 18.1 Canonical relationship conclusions

The following propositions are now frozen for later synthesis unless new primary evidence requires revision:

1. Relationships in Oregairu must be modeled multidimensionally; “closer/farther” is insufficient.
2. The Service Club is a triadic system, and pairwise changes alter the third member's available choices even in absence.
3. Hachiman/Yukino develops from adversarial competence-trust through failed idealization and jurisdictional overreach into mutually chosen involvement under recognized capability.
4. Hachiman/Yui contains real ordinary intimacy and real one-sided romantic desire; the final negative romantic answer does not retroactively make the dyad trivial or false.
5. Yukino/Yui is an independently authored central friendship whose survival makes post-romantic triadic continuity structurally possible.
6. The central relational advance is not perfect understanding but answerability: claims can be made, refused, revised, and survived.
7. Conflict tolerance is often a stronger maturity signal than harmony.
8. Reliance becomes healthier when it preserves or increases authorship rather than when relation disappears.
9. Romantic choice removes equivalence but does not erase the triad's prior or continuing reality.
10. The post-ending Service Club survives through transmission and revision, not identical preservation.
11. `共依存` describes bounded reinforcement mechanisms but cannot serve as the final ontology of the central relationships.
12. The terminal V2 relation model is **revisable interdependence under ordinary change**.
