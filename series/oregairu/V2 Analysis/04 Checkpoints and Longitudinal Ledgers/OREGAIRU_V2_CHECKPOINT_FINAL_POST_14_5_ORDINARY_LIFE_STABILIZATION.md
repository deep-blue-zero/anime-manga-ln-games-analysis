# Oregairu V2 — Final Checkpoint: Post-14.5 Ordinary-Life Stabilization

## Purpose of this checkpoint

This checkpoint performs the final prospective state freeze of the mainline Oregairu V2 reread after the chronology-valid post-ending material in Volume 14.5.

It does **not** reopen the Volume-14 final choice. Checkpoint E remains the authoritative mainline endpoint for the prom/final-choice movement. Volume 14.5 is admitted only afterward, and only according to its internal chronology. Its function is therefore narrower and, in some respects, more demanding:

> **Does the mainline ending remain inhabitable when the characters are no longer protected by climactic necessity?**

The test is not whether the chosen relationship becomes perfect, whether Yui stops hurting, whether Iroha stops being provocative, whether Hachiman stops rationalizing, or whether the Service Club freezes into its final Volume-14 arrangement. Those would be stronger claims than the source supports.

Instead, ordinary-life stabilization is defined here as the capacity of the post-ending system to absorb low-stakes variance without immediately recreating the earlier crisis logic. The relevant tests are therefore:

- whether Hachiman and Yukino can assume a shared future without collapsing independent trajectories;
- whether reliance can become ordinary without returning to rescue, incapacity, or hidden jurisdiction;
- whether Yui can remain a subject inside the relational system after real romantic non-reciprocity;
- whether Yukino and Yui's friendship becomes behaviorally active rather than ceremonially preserved;
- whether Iroha's mixed motives and pretexts remain legible without being forced into “fake” versus “true” selves;
- whether Hachiman can notice when concern for Komachi becomes nostalgia-driven control;
- whether the Service Club can survive succession by transmitting practices rather than preserving identical people and roles;
- whether `本物` remains an anti-certification problem once the vocabulary itself disappears from ordinary life;
- and whether the series can imagine continuity without confusing continuity with permanence.

The governing question is therefore:

> **Can a relationship remain worth choosing after it stops being the final answer and becomes merely the next ordinary day?**

The answer is again qualified but affirmative.

# 1. Executive frozen thesis

The strongest final-checkpoint formulation is:

> **Volume 14.5 strengthens the mainline ending not by proving that Hachiman and Yukino chose the one correct relationship, but by showing that the post-choice system can function without treating that choice as sacred. In the chronology-valid post-ending stories, Hachiman and Yukino translate their reciprocal life claim into ordinary plans while explicitly preserving separate academic trajectories; Yui remains an active participant whose friendship with Yukino and institutional standing do not depend on romantic equivalence; Iroha continues to use transparent pretext, flirtation, and assumed familiarity, proving that romantic choice does not abolish mixed motive or future boundary work; and Komachi's succession forces Hachiman to confront his own attempt to `神聖視` the existing Service Club. The final correction is therefore institutional and epistemic as much as romantic: what mattered can be transmitted without reproducing the same configuration. The ending survives ordinary life because it becomes revisable practice rather than sacred form.**

Twelve final propositions are frozen.

1. **Checkpoint E is PRESERVED.** Volume 14.5 does not revise the mainline endpoint; it tests its durability.
2. **Ordinary stabilization is not proof of long-term permanence.** The post-ending window is short and explicitly future-uncertain.
3. **Hachiman-Yukino chosen interdependence survives its first ordinary-life tests.** They can prefer proximity without demanding identical institutions, abilities, or futures.
4. **Yukino's reliance remains chosen rather than incapacity-based.** She can ask for a shared environment while retaining a more ambitious academic path.
5. **Hachiman's support model improves when he protects Yukino's trajectory from being lowered for his sake.** This is a strong post-ending control against fusion.
6. **Yui's romantic loss remains real, but it no longer exhausts her subjecthood.** She participates, advises, coordinates, and helps shape succession.
7. **Yui-Yukino friendship is behaviorally active after the romantic answer.** It is not merely a declared truce.
8. **Iroha remains mixed, strategic, attractive, and boundary-testing.** Post-choice life is not purified of charged relationships.
9. **Hachiman's major post-ending self-correction is anti-sanctification.** He recognizes that he has begun treating the current Service Club as the supreme completed form and explicitly rejects that premise.
10. **The Service Club's strongest continuity model is transmission rather than preservation.** People, cups, roles, and membership may change while practices and forms of attention persist.
11. **The disappearance of `本物`, `偽物`, `依存`, and `共依存` from Volume 14.5 is not proof that those problems are solved.** It indicates that ordinary conduct can proceed without the old diagnostic vocabulary governing every interaction.
12. **The final V2 endpoint is durable but non-certified.** The post-ending state is best described as **revisable interdependence under ordinary change**.

Compactly:

> **Checkpoint E chose continuation under doubt. Volume 14.5 shows that such continuation can become ordinary without becoming sacred.**

# 2. Evidence boundary and chronology control

## 2.1 Incoming state: Checkpoint E remains immutable

Checkpoint E froze the mainline endpoint before any Volume-14.5 evidence was admitted.

Its outgoing conditions remain authoritative:

- Hachiman and Yukino have mutually chosen romantic and life involvement.
- Yukino's independent capacity is explicit: `一人で立てるのに……` / `知ってる`.
- Yui has received a real negative romantic answer and real grief.
- Yui remains in the relational system by her own choice.
- Yui-Yukino friendship has independent standing.
- `共依存` no longer operates as a total diagnosis or automatic separation cure.
- dependency mechanisms remain historically real and remain possible future risks.
- Hachiman's intervention ethics have improved but are not perfected.
- the final Service Club contains known romantic asymmetry rather than protected ambiguity.
- `本物` remains uncertified; Hachiman commits to `疑い続けます` rather than declaring final proof.

This checkpoint does not alter those propositions retroactively.

## 2.2 Positive evidence window: Stories 4 and 5 only

Volume 14.5's chronology audit establishes:

- Story 4 begins after mid-April, when Hachiman is explicitly a third-year student (`V14_5-E039`, `V14_5-E045`).
- Story 5 occurs roughly one month after the recreated Service Club begins operating (`V14_5-E054`, `V14_5-E058`).

These are the only stories that positively update the post-mainline state.

Story 4 tests:

- Hachiman's ordinary social embeddedness after romantic choice;
- Iroha's continued mixed-motive access;
- whether pretext remains compatible with recognizable desire;
- and whether Hachiman can inhabit a chosen relationship without every other attractive person becoming narratively inert.

Story 5 tests:

- Hachiman-Yukino future planning;
- chosen reliance in low-stakes academic life;
- Yui's continued institutional and friendship standing;
- Iroha's epistemic discipline and future continuity role;
- Komachi's authorship as new club president;
- and whether the Service Club can survive succession without preserving its current form.

## 2.3 Story 3 is corroborative but cannot update the post-ending state

Story 3 occurs during spring break after the joint prom but before the new term (`V14_5-E015`). It therefore sits inside the late-Volume-14 movement rather than after Checkpoint E.

Its strongest finding—that Hachiman experiences finite shared time as a reason to participate rather than withdraw—corroborates the final movement, but it cannot be used to make the bridge, Yui's rejection, or the final club retrospectively easier.

The relevant late-V14 movement is:

> Hachiman counts remaining shared time, recognizes that ordinary days end like festivals, and then answers the group's call with `ああ、今行く` (`V14_5-E037`-`V14_5-E038`).

This is important, but chronology keeps it outside the final checkpoint's positive evidence window.

## 2.4 Stories 1-2 route backward

Stories 1-2 occur immediately after Volume 9, before Volume 10 (`V14_5-E001`, `V14_5-E007`). Their function is to refine the post-Volume-9 state, not the ending.

They remain useful for adversarial control because they show that the later Hachiman/Yukino choice must not be projected backward as inevitability. Hachiman has strong Yukino salience in Story 1 and warm, future-plausible interaction with Yui in Story 2. Komachi's imagined future partner remains explicitly unknown in form (`V14_5-E014`).

Those stories therefore support the methodological rule:

> **later resolution cannot colonize earlier ambiguity.**

They do not belong in this checkpoint's post-ending state.

## 2.5 Explicit exclusion

The final checkpoint excludes:

- Shin;
- Ketsu/Yui alternate continuity;
- anthology stories not already chronology-routed into their relevant strata;
- later adaptations;
- fanbook interpretation;
- and all post-14.5 material not yet source-audited.

The checkpoint is therefore a **Volume-14.5 terminal ordinary-life freeze**, not a universal statement about every later Oregairu publication.

# 3. What “ordinary-life stabilization” means

The phrase can easily become misleading.

It does **not** mean:

- every important conflict disappears;
- Yui's grief has healed completely;
- Hachiman and Yukino are now a perfectly healthy couple;
- Iroha has accepted fixed interpersonal boundaries;
- Hachiman stops narrating defensively;
- Yukino stops wanting reassurance or shared environment;
- the Service Club has found its permanent final membership;
- or everyone knows what the future will look like.

The more defensible definition is structural:

> **A relation is stabilized when ordinary variation can occur without the system immediately reverting to the old mechanism of hidden desire, unilateral sacrifice, diagnostic certainty, or preservation-through-silence.**

Under that standard, Stories 4-5 supply several meaningful positive controls.

Hachiman can notice Iroha's attractiveness without treating attraction as a crisis requiring a new ontological decision. Yukino can express a wish to attend the same cram school without framing herself as incapable of studying alone. Hachiman can prefer future proximity while explicitly rejecting the idea that Yukino should lower her academic ambition to match him. Yui can say what Hachiman and Yukino are too cautious to say about new members without seizing the decision from Komachi. Iroha can infer why Komachi hesitates and then mark the inference as inference rather than fact. Komachi can hear multiple views, change her mind, and remain the person who decides whether the club should be listed. The older group can honor the existing club through gifts and ritual while simultaneously allowing new membership and changed practice.

The system still contains friction.

Iroha schedules a weekend plan before receiving a crisp assent (`V14_5-E052`). Hachiman still hides emotion behind mock-formal language and practical rationalization. His concern for Komachi initially becomes projection. Yukino remains awkwardly dependent on circumlocution when stating that she wants to share a cram-school environment. Yui's future emotional state is not fully described.

Stabilization therefore means **survivable imperfection**, not moral completion.

# 4. Hachiman and Yukino: the life claim becomes mundane without becoming trivial

## 4.1 Separate trajectories are treated as compatible with shared future

The strongest ordinary-life evidence comes from the cram-school conversation.

Yukino imagines a university future in which they may take separate classes and meet afterward (`V14_5-E071`). Hachiman initially responds with realism: they may not attend the same university.

The crucial next step is not that he fantasizes about sameness. It is that he explicitly protects difference.

The source states that Yukino is pursuing a national/public-university humanities path while Hachiman is oriented toward private humanities. Hachiman says that if Yukino were to lower her target to match his level, he would `普通に全力で止めてしまう`—plainly and forcefully stop her (`V14_5-E072`).

That matters because Volume 12-14 had repeatedly asked whether closeness would require diminished self-authorship.

Post-ending evidence answers:

> **No. Hachiman can desire proximity while treating Yukino's independent aspiration as a constraint that his desire must respect.**

His subsequent line is similarly important:

> `大学違っても待ち合わせはするだろうし`

He frames different universities as compatible with continued meeting (`V14_5-E073`). The narration marks this as a wish rather than knowledge about next year.

This is exactly the distinction the final movement needed:

> **future inclusion without future foreclosure.**

## 4.2 Yukino's request for a shared environment is chosen reliance

The cram-school sequence then pushes in the opposite direction.

Yukino initially assumes they will visit the next cram school together:

> `一緒のとこ、行くんだと思ってた`

(`V14_5-E076`).

After embarrassment and rationalization, she finally says:

> `一緒の方が頑張れると思うし……`

(`V14_5-E077`).

This is reliance evidence.

It should not be downgraded because the series previously worried about dependency. Nor should it be inflated into proof of renewed pathology.

The relevant distinction is capability.

Nothing in the scene suggests that Yukino cannot study independently, cannot select a school, cannot sustain academic work, or needs Hachiman to perform the task for her. Her stated preference is environmental and motivational: being together would help her work.

That makes the scene a strong ordinary-life instance of the Checkpoint-E model:

> **support can be wanted without being required by incapacity.**

## 4.3 Hachiman accepts the request while still hiding behind rationalization

Hachiman's internal response is comic and excessive. He immediately thinks that attending the same cram school now feels unavoidable, then generates practical reasons why this would be productive, culminating in `よし、言い訳終了` before stating a mock-objective conclusion (`V14_5-E077`).

This is not complete directness.

He still uses rationalization as emotional camouflage.

But the function has changed.

Earlier rationalization often replaced desire: if Hachiman could explain an action through duty, utility, or necessity, he could avoid owning the personal motive.

Here the reader can see the desire first. The practical language is an embarrassed cover for something already acknowledged.

The mature difference is therefore:

> **camouflage remains, but it no longer successfully erases the desire it is camouflaging.**

## 4.4 The one-month anniversary rejects mandatory reciprocal accounting

Yukino later brings handmade cookies and reveals that the occasion is one month since their relationship entered its current form (`V14_5-E084`-`V14_5-E085`).

Hachiman immediately worries that he prepared nothing.

Yukino responds that this is something she chose to do herself and that he was not obligated to have a corresponding gift ready (`V14_5-E086`).

That line is small but important after the series' long history of relational accounting, repayment, obligation, and fear of debt.

Yukino's gesture does not create automatic symmetric obligation.

It is:

> **self-authored care without a claim that reciprocity must be simultaneous or identical.**

This does not abolish reciprocity as a relational value. It distinguishes reciprocity from immediate ledger-balancing.

## 4.5 Ten years is not a promise

The anniversary conversation escalates awkwardly from one year to ten years. Both characters become acutely embarrassed (`V14_5-E087`).

The scene is tempting to read as a long-term contractual guarantee.

The source does not support that.

Hachiman explicitly cannot picture one year clearly, much less ten. His strongest concluding thought is not “we will definitely still be together.” It is that he cannot imagine forgetting this moment even after decades.

The evidence therefore supports:

> **memory projected into the future, not future possession projected into certainty.**

That preserves Checkpoint E's anti-certification stance.

## 4.6 Frozen Hachiman-Yukino post-ending state

The final ordinary-life state is:

> **mutually chosen romantic/life involvement translated into repeated mundane planning; independent trajectories remain legitimate; shared environment can be requested; support is not justified by incapacity; emotional directness improves but remains partially masked; and future inclusion becomes ordinary without becoming guaranteed.**

This **STRENGTHENS** Checkpoint E's chosen-interdependence model.

It does not prove permanent health.

# 5. Yui after romantic loss: continued subjecthood without restored ambiguity

## 5.1 Volume 14.5 does not give Yui a new romantic answer

Checkpoint E remains authoritative on the romantic result.

Hachiman did not choose Yui romantically. Yui cried. She returned with the loss known rather than hidden.

Stories 4-5 do not reverse this.

There is no new confession, no romantic reset, no scene in which Hachiman resumes treating Yui as an unresolved alternative, and no textual statement that the rejection was provisional.

The appropriate classification is therefore:

> **continued standing after romantic non-reciprocity.**

## 5.2 Yui remains behaviorally active in the Service Club

The final story presents Yui not as a fragile guest around the chosen couple, but as one of the people who actually make the room function.

She participates in the succession discussion. She coordinates Komachi's surprise with Yukino. She distracts Komachi while Yukino prepares the mug. She supplies the line that Hachiman and Yukino cannot bring themselves to say:

> `いいじゃん、載せようよ。新入部員じゃんじゃん入れよう`

(`V14_5-E093`).

Iroha's viewpoint is explicit about why this matters. Yui understands the care and hesitation behind Hachiman and Yukino's silence, yet says the straightforward thing anyway.

The resulting competence can be formulated as:

> **Yui can understand complexity without making complexity a reason not to speak.**

This is a mature extension of her long-standing atmosphere-reading ability.

Earlier, Yui's sensitivity could feed accommodation and postponement. Here the same sensitivity supports explicit speech.

## 5.3 Yukino-Yui friendship is active rather than ceremonial

Yukino and Yui coordinate naturally throughout the final sequence (`V14_5-E098`-`V14_5-E099`). They shop together for Komachi's gift, exchange glances, coordinate timing, divide roles, and jointly explain what the new mug means.

When Komachi is moved by the gift, Yukino and Yui exchange an ordinary warm look rather than a solemn display of post-triangle forgiveness.

The scene does not prove that Yui has no residual pain.

It proves something narrower and more useful:

> **their friendship is capable of generating ordinary cooperative action after the romantic answer.**

That is enough to strengthen Checkpoint E's claim that Yui-Yukino friendship has independent standing.

## 5.4 The cram-school line remains ambiguous

Iroha asks Yui about cram schools and Yui indicates that Hachiman's school may be relevant (`V14_5-E089`). Story 5 then explicitly resists a simple romantic interpretation (`V14_5-E090`).

The evidence is insufficient to prove:

- indefinite romantic pursuit;
- complete emotional detachment;
- or a new competitive strategy.

The correct status remains **OPEN / ambiguous**.

This matters methodologically because final synthesis should not invent a cleaner emotional endpoint for Yui than the source provides.

## 5.5 Frozen Yui post-ending state

> **Yui remains romantically non-reciprocated, socially central, institutionally active, friendship-capable, and able to speak desires or proposals that others overcomplicate. Her continued presence is evidence of subjecthood after loss, not evidence that the loss disappeared.**

Checkpoint E is **STRENGTHENED**, not revised.

# 6. Iroha: romantic choice does not purify the social world

## 6.1 Hachiman still recognizes Iroha's attractiveness

Story 4 begins with Hachiman seeing Iroha among her classmates and remembering, almost freshly, that she is highly attractive (`V14_5-E040`).

This is analytically useful because it refuses a sanitized romantic ontology.

Choosing Yukino does not make every other person visually or emotionally neutral.

That ordinary fact matters for the ending model.

A relationship durable only under the assumption that attraction to everyone else disappears would be an artificially fragile model.

Volume 14.5 instead leaves Hachiman embedded in a social world containing other charged relationships.

## 6.2 Iroha's pretext is almost openly acknowledged

Iroha leaves her classmates under student-council cover, returns to Hachiman, and later says explicitly:

> `プレゼントは言い訳ですよ`

(`V14_5-E051`).

This strongly confirms the project's earlier Iroha formulation:

> **pretext can be knowingly shared without becoming pure falsity.**

The “gift” is an excuse for continued contact. Hachiman understands that the excuse is not exhaustive of the motive.

The scene therefore continues to undermine any simple authentic-self versus performed-self binary.

## 6.3 But transparent pretext is not clean authorization

Iroha then announces the weekend plan before Hachiman supplies a crisp yes. He eventually gives a delayed acknowledgment, and the narration calls the result `なし崩し` (`V14_5-E052`).

This is precisely the sort of low-stakes imperfection a final checkpoint should preserve.

Iroha's maneuver is not Kyoto-level relational coercion. The stakes are low, the context is legible, Hachiman could plausibly refuse, and no major life decision is seized.

But it is still not a model of fully explicit negotiation.

So the final state remains:

> **ordinary relational life continues to require boundary work even after the major choice has been made.**

## 6.4 Iroha does not reopen the route

Nothing in Story 4 establishes that Hachiman wishes to undo the choice made in Volume 14.

Attraction, embarrassment, and flirtatious access are insufficient evidence for romantic reversal.

The correct classification is:

> **OPEN as a post-choice boundary-management problem; REJECT as evidence that the romantic route is reopened.**

## 6.5 Iroha's epistemic discipline is unexpectedly important

In Story 5, Iroha begins inferring what Komachi is trying to protect. She then interrupts her own inference:

> `……いや、お米ちゃんがほんとのところ、どう思ってるかは知らないけど。`
>
> `わたしがわかるのは、わたしのことだけ。だから、わたし基準に想像するしかない。`

(`V14_5-E094`).

This is one of the cleanest explicit epistemic limits anywhere in the post-ending material.

Iroha does not refuse inference. She marks its basis.

That is almost exactly the discipline Hachiman's narration has needed throughout the series:

> **model another person if necessary, but do not convert your model into unmarked access to their interiority.**

The final checkpoint therefore treats Iroha not merely as comic disruption, but as an important epistemic control.

# 7. Hachiman, Komachi, and the ethics of succession

## 7.1 The old intervention problem returns in miniature

Hachiman worries about what will happen to Komachi when the older trio graduates. He imagines her “left behind” in the Service Club and begins thinking about recruitment (`V14_5-E060`-`V14_5-E062`).

He does recognize one important limit:

> Komachi rebuilt the club; it is now her club; he will leave in less than a year; his preferences should not automatically govern its future.

Yet his concern still contains projection.

He sees possible loneliness in Komachi's relaxed attitude even while admitting that he does not actually know her inner state (`V14_5-E062`).

The old problem has returned at lower stakes:

> **care tempts Hachiman to turn his own anticipated loss into another person's problem.**

## 7.2 Nonintervention alone is not enough

Hachiman then confronts the opposite error.

Simply telling Komachi:

- “do whatever you want,” or
- “decide for yourself”

would not necessarily constitute respect for autonomy.

He eventually calls such language potentially a form of responsibility transfer (`V14_5-E082`).

This is a major refinement of the V2 autonomy model.

The alternative to control is not abandonment.

A mature participant may owe:

- relevant information;
- honest preference;
- history;
- warning;
- encouragement;
- or an account of why the choice matters.

What they must not do is convert those inputs into authorship over the answer.

## 7.3 Yui models speech without jurisdiction

Yui's contribution is especially clean.

Hachiman and Yukino hesitate to say that the club should be listed for recruitment because they fear imposing their preferred future. Yui says it plainly (`V14_5-E093`).

But she does not then decide the matter for Komachi.

This is a strong example of:

> **stating a preference without claiming jurisdiction over the recipient's decision.**

## 7.4 Iroha supplies pressure and epistemic caution

Iroha frames the timing problem sharply: if they do not address succession while the older members are still present, the younger members may become trapped by the current arrangement after it has already turned into the past (`V14_5-E092`).

That is not neutral. It is an argument for change.

Yet Iroha simultaneously limits her certainty about Komachi's motives (`V14_5-E094`).

Again, the procedure is plural rather than pure.

## 7.5 Komachi remains the final author

Komachi hears:

- Hachiman's worry;
- Yukino's broader conception of the room;
- Yui's direct recruitment preference;
- Iroha's continuity argument;
- and the historical reminder that Hachiman and Yui themselves entered the club midstream.

She eventually decides that the club can be listed, explicitly linking her decision to Iroha having taken the issue seriously (`V14_5-E102`).

The causal field is social.

The decision remains Komachi's.

This is exactly what the mature autonomy model should permit:

> **authorship does not require causal isolation from other people's reasons.**

# 8. `神聖視`: the final anti-sanctification correction

## 8.1 Hachiman identifies the post-ending danger himself

The most consequential post-ending self-correction occurs when Yukino points out that new people may enter the Service Club just as Yui and Hachiman once entered when she was president (`V14_5-E080`).

Hachiman realizes:

> `俺は俺たちという関係性に拘泥しすぎていたのかもしれない。`
>
> `いや、神聖視していたと言ってもいい。`

(`V14_5-E081`).

The vocabulary is stronger than mere nostalgia.

`拘泥` implies being caught or fixated.

`神聖視` implies sacralizing the current configuration.

He recognizes that he has begun treating the present form—including Komachi—as:

> `至上で最高で完璧に完結している`

supreme, best, perfect, and complete.

This is exactly the wrong lesson to derive from Volume 14.

## 8.2 The series explicitly denies that the relationship was ever perfect

Hachiman immediately asks:

> `俺たちの関係性が完璧だったことなどあるだろうか。`

and answers:

> `否、まったくの否である。`

He then describes the relationship as something that has always:

- distorted;
- frayed;
- sometimes broken;
- remained tenuously connected;
- continued expanding;
- and kept making mistakes (`V14_5-E082`).

This is not romanticization of damage.

It is rejection of retrospective purification.

The ending did not reveal that everything painful before it was secretly good. Nor did the final choice produce a completed, error-free object afterward.

The post-ending lesson is:

> **a relationship can become worth continuing without becoming retrospectively perfect or prospectively fixed.**

## 8.3 `神聖視` becomes the ordinary-life counterpart to `本物`

The complete Volume-14.5 lexical audit contains zero instances of:

- `本物`;
- `偽物`;
- `共依存`;
- `依存`

(`V14_5-E117`).

But the philosophical problem has not disappeared.

It has migrated.

Instead of asking whether the relation is `本物`, Hachiman now catches himself treating its **current form** as sacred.

The ordinary-life danger is therefore no longer only false certification through a word.

It is false certification through **preservation of form**.

That produces the final authenticity refinement:

> **Do not replace “this is genuine” with “this configuration must therefore remain unchanged.”**

# 9. The Service Club: continuity through transmission rather than identity

## 9.1 Formal identity was always unstable

The Service Club remains difficult to define institutionally (`V14_5-E061`). It is called a service club but does not operate like a conventional volunteer organization. Its requests are private, its relationship to the student council is unusual, and its membership boundaries have long been porous.

That ambiguity survives the ending.

The final checkpoint does not treat this as failure.

Instead, the club's future becomes a problem of **transmission under ambiguity**.

## 9.2 Iroha's mug is a boundary-expanding artifact

Komachi receives a new mug as the new president. Iroha receives a matching mug even though she points out that she is not technically a member (`V14_5-E099`-`V14_5-E102`).

Yukino's answer is essentially that the formal distinction no longer captures lived belonging.

This is important because institutional continuity is already exceeding formal category.

The club is not preserved by insisting that only official members matter.

It is preserved by acknowledging the network that actually sustains it.

## 9.3 Hachiman formally transfers standing

Hachiman addresses Komachi:

> `改めまして、比企谷部長。よろしくお願いします`

(`V14_5-E100`).

The line is deliberately modest.

It does not tell Komachi what kind of president she must be. It does not command her to reproduce Yukino's club. It does not declare the original trio's meaning.

It recognizes office and relationship:

> **you are the president now; I will relate to you as such.**

This is transfer rather than preservation.

## 9.4 Yukino transmits practice

At the end, Yukino teaches Komachi how to make tea (`V14_5-E104`-`V14_5-E106`).

Hachiman imagines that:

- cups will change in number and shape;
- the room's appearance will change;
- the original group's physical traces may disappear;
- people will leave;
- and the current tableau will be lost.

Then he sees Komachi making tea under Yukino's supervision and thinks:

> `きっとこの部屋の紅茶の香りは変わらない。`

(`V14_5-E106`).

This should not be read as metaphysical permanence.

The tea itself may change. Future members may prepare it differently. The sentence is Hachiman's poetic expectation.

Its strongest supported meaning is:

> **practice can outlive configuration because somebody teaches somebody else.**

This is the final institutional model of Oregairu V2.

## 9.5 Frozen Service Club state

> **The Service Club survives not by reproducing the same members, roles, emotional geometry, or objects, but by remaining a place where practices of attention, request, friction, and care can be inherited and transformed by people who were not part of the original arrangement.**

# 10. Dependency and autonomy after the diagnostic vocabulary disappears

## 10.1 The words disappear; the problem does not

Volume 14.5 contains no `依存` or `共依存` lexemes (`V14_5-E117`).

It would be a serious error to infer that dependency has therefore been solved.

The underlying autonomy questions remain visible in behavior:

- Can Yukino want shared environment without lowering herself for Hachiman?
- Can Hachiman care about Komachi's future without deciding it for her?
- Can Yui state what she wants for the club without controlling Komachi?
- Can Iroha influence others without treating inference as certainty?
- Can the club's current members leave without making the future a betrayal?

The absence of diagnostic vocabulary is best interpreted as a change in **operating register**.

The characters are no longer forced to explain every ordinary interaction through the language of dependency.

## 10.2 Chosen interdependence survives its best low-stakes test

The Hachiman-Yukino cram-school sequence is the strongest positive control.

Yukino prefers shared environment.

Hachiman accepts.

But he explicitly refuses the premise that she should lower her academic target to match him (`V14_5-E072`).

That means closeness and differentiation coexist in the same scene.

This strengthens the final model:

> **chosen interdependence = capability preserved + reliance permitted + difference retained + refusal still conceptually meaningful.**

## 10.3 Concern can still become controlling care

Hachiman's worry about Komachi shows that the old risk has not disappeared.

He nearly converts his own fear of departure into an assumption that Komachi will be abandoned. He admits he does not know what she really feels (`V14_5-E062`).

This is valuable counterevidence.

Post-ending growth is not immunity.

The final checkpoint therefore freezes:

> **Hachiman has better corrective procedures, not a permanently corrected personality.**

## 10.4 “Decide for yourself” is not the mature opposite of control

Hachiman's later self-correction explicitly rejects both:

- “preserve the club for us,” and
- “just decide for yourself”

as sufficient ethical responses (`V14_5-E082`).

This is a subtle but crucial final result.

Autonomy requires authorship, but authorship can be supported by honest relational input.

A refusal to provide any input can itself become abandonment or responsibility dumping.

The final autonomy model is therefore not minimal-contact liberal isolation.

It is **answerable participation**.

## 10.5 Final dependency/autonomy freeze

**PRESERVE:** dependency mechanisms remain possible risks.

**STRENGTHEN:** capability and support can coexist.

**STRENGTHEN:** shared environment can be chosen without becoming proof of incapacity.

**STRENGTHEN:** autonomy permits influence, advice, and inherited practice when final authorship remains with the participant.

**REJECT:** post-ending health requires emotional or practical self-sufficiency.

**REJECT:** absence of `共依存` vocabulary proves the mechanism has vanished.

Final formulation:

> **The series ends not with independence from others, but with increasingly explicit differentiation inside chosen involvement.**

# 11. Request and intervention ethics in ordinary life

Iroha's `プレゼントは言い訳ですよ` (`V14_5-E051`) is almost maximally transparent pretext.

- the target can plausibly refuse;
- the pretext is effectively disclosed;

The final post-ending evidence says: sometimes, imperfectly, yes.
