Core method:

> **Read every volume simultaneously as it could be understood at publication time and as it can be re-understood with full-series hindsight, without allowing the latter to erase the former.**

## Required evidence discipline

Treat these as different classes:

- textual fact;
- character assertion;
- Hachiman observation;
- Hachiman inference;
- Hachiman self-description;
- behavioral evidence;
- analytical inference;
- literary interpretation;
- ambiguity;
- retrospective recontextualization.

Hachiman is **not generically unreliable**. Reliability is domain-specific.

He is often strong at:

- social hierarchy;
- incentives;
- status;
- reputation;
- group maintenance;
- deniable pretexts;
- labor distribution;
- institutional pressure.

He is weaker when interpretation implicates:

- his own desire;
- vulnerability;
- rejection;
- being needed;
- dependence;
- romantic possibility;
- self-sacrifice;
- his perceived right to determine another person's answer.

## Relationship dimensions

Track independently:

- trust;
- disclosure;
- expectation;
- permission to intervene;
- dependence/reliance;
- obligation;
- jealousy;
- embarrassment;
- proximity;
- ease;
- conflict tolerance;
- reciprocity;
- willingness to impose/refuse;
- ordinary presence;
- future assumptions;
- romantic desire;
- recognition of another's desire.

The Service Club is a **triadic system**. Pairwise movement can affect the absent third.

## Request / intervention ethics

For every major intervention ask:

- who owns the problem?
- what was explicitly requested?
- what need is only inferred?
- what authorization was granted?
- what method was used?
- who bears the cost?
- whose agency increases or decreases?
- are values being substituted?
- is refusal meaningful?
- does immediate success produce downstream relational damage?

Current governing distinction:

> **caring about someone ≠ having reason to act ≠ having authority to decide.**

## `本物` rule

Track `本物` longitudinally. Do **not** import the ending meaning into earlier uses.

Do not presume one speaker's authenticity vocabulary is the series doctrine.

## Dependency / autonomy rule

Do not equate:

- reliance;
- trust;
- mutual aid;
- emotional dependence;
- reinforcement of dysfunction;
- controlling care;
- chosen interdependence.

Later `共依存` language must not be projected backward as a universal diagnosis.

## Supplements

Track both:

- publication chronology;
- story chronology.

Do not allow a later-published supplementary story to contaminate an earlier prospective freeze simply because it is bound under an earlier volume number.
