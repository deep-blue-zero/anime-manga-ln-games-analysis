# OREGAIRU V2 — Phase 6 Adversarial Japanese-Source Audit
## Breaking the synthesis before publication

## 0. Executive status

Phase 5 produced twelve canonical specialist syntheses. Phase 6 does **not** ask whether those documents are persuasive. It asks whether their strongest claims remain defensible after the argument is deprived of its own explanatory momentum and forced back through the Japanese source chain.

The audit therefore treats elegance as a risk factor. A formulation is especially likely to be tested when it:

- compresses many volumes into one conceptual formula;
- converts one character's statement into a series-level principle;
- turns a repeated lexical item into a stable technical term;
- interprets a successful outcome as proof of ethical procedure;
- describes an ending state more cleanly than the scene itself;
- uses retrospective knowledge to stabilize earlier ambiguity;
- assigns strong authority to an interlude or counter-gaze;
- makes ordinary Japanese grammar carry philosophical weight;
- or promotes supplementary material beyond its authorship, chronology, or source quality.

This audit is intentionally asymmetric. A claim that survives hostile review earns stronger downstream standing. A claim that fails is not rescued because it fits the rest of V2.

### Governing result

The V2 corpus survives Phase 6 substantially intact, but **not unchanged**.

The compact disposition matrix audits **95 high-consequence claim rows**. Its primary dispositions are **31 PRESERVE, 23 STRENGTHEN, 27 REVISE, 9 DOWNGRADE, 3 REJECT, and 1 OPEN**, plus one mixed **PRESERVE / OPEN** row for the Watari anthology band whose broad chronology survives while exact internal ordering remains unresolved. These counts are not quality scores; they show how much of Phase 5 survives verbatim versus survives only after scope control.

The principal architecture—Hachiman's movement away from unilateral interpretive control; Yukino's increasing authorship; Yui's active desire and post-loss subjecthood; the triad's survival under romantic non-equivalence; `本物` as a longitudinal problem rather than a purity certificate; `共依存` as mechanism rather than feeling ontology; and the importance of ordinary post-crisis continuation—remains strongly supported.

However, the audit identifies a recurring overreach in Phase 5: several documents made the mature procedural model **too clean**. The Japanese novels retain more coercion, strategic indirection, asymmetrical power, mixed consent, comic pressure, and unresolved interpretation than the phrase *answerable involvement* can imply if read as a deliberative ideal.

The most important correction is therefore:

> **Oregairu does not replace manipulation with pure consent, indirection with directness, dependence with autonomy, or ambiguity with transparent mutual recognition. It moves toward relations in which more desires and objections become attributable and consequential, while coercive, strategic, performative, and uncertain elements remain present and must continue to be judged locally.**

A second correction concerns authority:

> **No late sentence—not `本物`, `共依存`, `神聖視`, `まちがい続ける`, a Hiratsuka maxim, a Haruno diagnosis, or a Hachiman self-correction—may function as an omniscient series theorem merely because it fits the mature synthesis.**

The downstream corpus must preserve the difference between **textual statement**, **speaker model**, **recurrent pattern**, and **analytical synthesis**.

# 1. Audit authority and revision behavior

## 1.1 What this document can revise

This audit may disposition Phase-5 claims as:

- **PRESERVE** — wording and scope survive hostile review;
- **STRENGTHEN** — Japanese primary evidence supports the claim more directly or broadly than Phase 5 stated;
- **REVISE** — governing insight survives but wording, scope, causal structure, or ethical qualification must change;
- **DOWNGRADE** — claim remains plausible but no longer has enough support to govern final synthesis;
- **REJECT** — the tested formulation should not be carried into Document 13 or the full-series synthesis;
- **OPEN** — the claim cannot responsibly be closed with the present evidence.

It does **not** silently rewrite Documents 01–12. Those artifacts remain frozen provenance for the specialist layer. Where this audit conflicts with a Phase-5 formulation, Document 13 must route the old claim to the audited replacement.

## 1.2 Evidence precedence used here

For an audited claim, the preferred chain is:

> specialist proposition → Phase-4 ledger → checkpoint/deep reading → locator family → Japanese primary witness

When a mature claim is contradicted by a direct Japanese passage, the direct passage controls. When two passages pull in different directions, the audit narrows the claim rather than choosing the passage that best fits V2.

Qualified Watari-authored supplementary evidence may strengthen durability or ordinary-life claims only within its admitted chronology. Guest-author anthology material cannot govern mainline character interiority.

## 1.3 Sampling rule

Phase 6 is not a second line-by-line reread of every page. It is a **high-consequence hostile sample**. The audit prioritizes propositions whose failure would materially change the final synthesis and reopens the volumes where the conceptual burden is concentrated:

- V06 — Sagami, intervention, self-sacrifice and cost allocation;
- V08 — projection, Yukino's `これは私の意志よ`, narrator epistemic limitation;
- V09 — request procedure, speech/understanding, `俺は、本物が欲しい`;
- V11 — Yui's `全部ほしい`, triadic preservation, owned desire;
- V12 — `依存` / `共依存`, Haruno's diagnosis, Yukino's self-description;
- V13 — `紛い物` / `贋作`, separation, Hayama/Haruno countermodels;
- V14 — Yui/Yukino confrontation, dummy prom, bridge, romantic selection;
- V14.5 — succession, `神聖視`, noninterference, near-term futurity.

Earlier volumes and the .5 volumes remain routed through the frozen deep readings and ledgers unless a tested claim required reopening them.

# 2. Executive revision ledger

The most important Phase-6 transitions are these.

## 2.1 The bridge: reciprocity survives; procedural cleanliness does not

Phase 5 correctly recognized the V14 bridge as the moment Hachiman stops pretending that involvement is only duty and states a future-facing desire to remain involved with Yukino. The Japanese primary text strongly supports that content:

- `俺は関わり続けたいと、思ってる。義務じゃなくて、意志の問題だ。`
- `時間とか感情とか将来とか人生とか`
- `諸々全部やるから、お前の人生に関わらせてくれ`
- Yukino eventually answers: `あなたの人生を、私にください`
- and later: `あなたが好きよ。比企谷くん`

But the procedure leading there is not cleanly consent-preserving. Hachiman tells Yui he will destroy the available defenses and escape routes:

- `……まずはそれを全部潰す`
- `そうやって、俺もあいつも逃げられないところに引っ張り出さないと`

Yui immediately objects:

- `そういうことじゃないと思う`

The dummy-prom strategy then engineers Yukino's decision environment. Yukino herself later says:

- `あの状況で断れるわけないじゃない。あなた本当になんなの？`

and compares the method to aggressive solicitation. Hachiman admits that he exaggerated and provoked. On the bridge, he physically prevents immediate departure before his grip loosens; only after that does Yukino remain voluntarily and the exchange become reciprocal.

**Disposition:** REVISE.

**Audited formulation:**

> **The bridge establishes reciprocal future involvement only after a coercively engineered path into the conversation. The relation becomes more answerable because Yukino's eventual answer has real force and Hachiman names his own desire; it does not become procedurally pure. Later reciprocity cannot retroactively authorize the manipulation that created the encounter.**

This revision affects Documents 02, 05, 07, 10, and any final use of “answerability.”

## 2.2 Yui's `全部ほしい`: preserve the lexical recurrence, reject a fixed object

V11 gives:

- `あたしは全部ほしい。今も、これからも。`
- `あたしはもうちゃんと決めてる`

There, “everything” includes the desire to preserve the three-person world while retaining her own romantic desire. V14 deliberately reuses the phrase but changes its semantic burden. Yui now includes the painful answer itself:

- `甘いのも、苦いのも、痛いのも、苦しいのも。`
- `傷も痛みも、全部欲しい。`
- `……だから、ゆきのんの気持ち、教えて`

This is not simple repetition. The lexical continuity marks **development inside desire**.

**Disposition:** REJECT the claim that `全部ほしい` names one stable object across V11–V14; STRENGTHEN the claim that it is a longitudinal Yui marker.

**Audited formulation:**

> **`全部ほしい` is a recurring Yui phrase whose object changes: from preserving incompatible goods under ambiguity toward accepting that “everything” also includes another person's answer, pain, and changed form.**

## 2.3 `共依存`: the mechanism/feeling distinction becomes stronger

V12 initially gives Haruno's label:

- `共依存っていうのよ`

Hachiman finds the mechanism compelling, but explicitly brackets the truth of Yukino's supposed dependence:

- `雪ノ下が俺に依存しているかの真偽はさておくとしても`

Later he states the decisive limitation:

- `共依存は仕組みだ。気持ちじゃない。言い訳にはなっても、理由にはなってくれない。`

**Disposition:** STRENGTHEN.

The V2 mechanism-level reading is not merely a defensive reinterpretation. Hachiman himself distinguishes the explanatory structure from the feeling and from final motive. Haruno's prediction of “independence” remains a speaker-specific model, not a binding cure.

## 2.4 `神聖視`: strong local correction, narrower series authority

V14.5 provides unusually direct self-correction:

- `俺は俺たちという関係性に拘泥しすぎていたのかもしれない。いや、神聖視していたと言ってもいい。`
- `俺たちの関係性が完璧だったことなどあるだろうか。否、まったくの否である。`
- the relation has continued while `未だにまちがい続けながら広がる`.

This decisively blocks treating the current club configuration as perfect and closed. But the speaker remains Hachiman, reflecting on a specific succession problem.

**Disposition:** REVISE.

**Audited formulation:**

> **`神聖視` is the strongest late local correction against Hachiman freezing the Service Club's current form as perfect. Its implications align with the wider V2 pattern of revisability, but it should not be quoted as an omniscient final theorem of the entire series.**

## 2.5 `また明日`: reject automatic privileged evidentiary status

Phase 5 sometimes treated ordinary future-facing formulae as especially powerful durability evidence. V14.5 shows why this needs restraint. `また明日` can occur inside ordinary scheduling and may be nearly conventional.

**Disposition:** REJECT as a general rule; PRESERVE only as contextual corroboration.

**Audited formulation:**

> **A future-assumption marker such as `また明日` gains analytical weight only when prior context made continued encounter uncertain or costly. The formula itself is not inherently stronger than explicit declaration.**

## 2.6 Counter-gazes: complication, not formal displacement of Hachiman

Phase 5 correctly rejects Haruno, Hayama, Hiratsuka, and Komachi as omniscient interpreters. It overstates, however, when counter-gaze plurality is described as formally “decentering” Hachiman's first-person authority. The novels remain overwhelmingly organized through his first-person narration. Interludes and external diagnoses complicate that authority; they do not establish equal polyphony.

**Disposition:** DOWNGRADE / REVISE.

> **Counter-gazes constrain Hachiman's interpretive monopoly without removing the formal privilege produced by first-person narration.**

## 2.7 “Transformed inheritance”: retain as synthesis, not textual doctrine

The family/succession specialist's phrase *transformed inheritance* productively connects Yukino, Haruno, Hayama, Iroha, Komachi, and the Service Club. The Japanese text does not itself formulate a general theory under that name.

**Disposition:** REVISE.

> **Transformed inheritance is a high-level V2 synthesis with strong local support in the succession material, not a term the final synthesis should present as an explicit series doctrine.**

# 3. Cluster A — Hachiman: epistemology, self-deception, and jurisdiction

## A1. Mechanism-reading remains one of his strongest competencies — STRENGTHEN

The hostile test asked whether V2 flatters Hachiman by calling him reliable about social mechanisms while merely discounting his interpersonal conclusions. V8 supplies unusually direct evidence for the distinction. He is capable of observing his own analytical procedure:

- `勝手に観察し、勝手に分析し、勝手に結論づけている`

That sentence does not mean every conclusion is false. It means the **jurisdictional leap** from observation to conclusion is textually visible to the narrator himself.

The same pattern recurs elsewhere: he can map labor allocation, reputation, face-saving, candidate viability, and incentive structures with high accuracy, yet still be wrong about what another person must want.

The mature claim therefore survives:

> **Hachiman is often strongest at structural description and weakest when structural prediction becomes privately authorized interior judgment.**

The audit adds one qualification: “mechanism” is itself an analytical category. The novels do not divide his cognition into formal modules. Document 13 should identify the category as V2's explanatory vocabulary, not his own technical theory.

## A2. Projection as projection — STRENGTHEN

V8's Orimoto reflection is one of the cleanest Japanese anchors in the entire corpus:

- `ただ一方的に願望押しつけてたというか、勘違いしてただけで、それを本物とは呼ばない`

The point is narrower and stronger than “Hachiman was wrong about Orimoto.” The text explicitly connects unilateral wish-projection and misunderstanding with something that cannot be called `本物`.

This supports both the Hachiman reliability thesis and GEN-02.

## A3. “Selective admissibility” survives, but only as recurrent pattern — PRESERVE

The specialist formulation that Hachiman grants unfavorable interpretations easier access to belief than favorable ones remains persuasive across his romantic and social self-description. However, Phase 6 does not elevate it into a hidden psychological law. Some false negatives remain ordinary uncertainty, comic convention, or limited information.

**Audited wording:**

> **Hachiman recurrently treats interpretations that protect him from hope, entitlement, or vulnerability as safer to admit than interpretations that would require desire or reciprocity; this is a pattern, not a universal algorithm.**

## A4. V9 is a procedural self-repositioning — STRENGTHEN

The exact scene is stronger than a thematic paraphrase. Hachiman says:

- `一つ、依頼がしたい`

and consciously occupies the side of the requester rather than simply acting as a club solver. His narration explicitly foregrounds doing this through the proper method and procedure.

The key change is not that the Service Club instantly understands him. It does not. Yukino says:

- `私には、……わからないわ`

The gain lies in Hachiman giving his desire **standing as a request that can fail to be understood**.

This strongly supports “procedure changes before object.”

## A5. Direct speech is not the cure — STRENGTHEN

V9 contains the sharpest anti-slogan pairing:

- `言わなくてもわかるっていうのは、幻想だ`
- `言ったからわかるっていうのは傲慢なんだよ`

The final synthesis must preserve both halves. Oregairu rejects telepathic intimacy and rejects speech-as-guaranteed-transmission. That makes later Yui's insistence on communicative effort more precise: speech is necessary enough to attempt, not sufficient to guarantee understanding.

## A6. The bridge is not a clean endpoint of the jurisdiction arc — REVISE

The content of Hachiman's eventual request is more accountable than his earlier self-sacrificial maneuvers because he names his desire and accepts that Yukino may not want it. But the path he chooses to force the encounter still uses agenda-setting, strategic constraint, provocation, and momentary physical restraint.

This prevents a simple developmental arrow:

> unilateral manipulation → clean answerability.

The stronger model is:

> **Hachiman becomes better at making his own stake attributable while remaining capable of coercive strategy. Growth limits and exposes his old repertoire; it does not erase it.**

## A7. “Neededness” remains real but should not absorb ordinary competence — PRESERVE / DOWNGRADE SCOPE

V12 makes Hachiman's attraction to being needed textually salient. It does not follow that every act of competence or helping is covert self-worth regulation. The final synthesis should use neededness where the source chain explicitly supports it and avoid retroactive psychologization of ordinary helpfulness.

## A8. Hachiman's abstraction is not principally defense — DOWNGRADE

His systems language, jokes, accounting metaphors, literary references, and elaborate models are part of his genuine cognitive style. They can function defensively, but Phase 6 rejects any tendency to make “abstraction = avoidance” the default causal explanation.

> **Abstraction becomes ethically relevant when it replaces another person's answer or blocks first-person ownership; otherwise it remains part of Hachiman's ordinary intelligence and comic voice.**

# 4. Cluster B — Yukino: authorship, competence, family, and reliance

## B1. Competence is real — PRESERVE

Nothing in the hostile pass supports a reading in which Yukino's competence is mainly a defensive facade. The series repeatedly treats it as genuine capability. The analytical risk lies elsewhere: because she often can do something, other characters and readers may infer that she should do it alone or that competence itself proves authorship.

YK-SYN-01 survives without revision.

## B2. `これは私の意志よ` has strong first-person authority — STRENGTHEN

V8 gives:

- `姉さんは関係ないわ。あの人の言うことを真に受けたりなんかしない。これは私の意志よ`

Immediately afterward, Hachiman refuses to certify motive purity:

- `実際のところはわからない。雪ノ下姉妹の関係性に触れられるほど、俺は彼女たちを理解していない。`

This combination is exactly what the mature V2 method needs. Yukino's statement controls **what she claims and owns**; the narrator's ignorance prevents the analysis from upgrading that ownership into proof that Haruno/family influence is absent.

**Audited formulation:**

> **Yukino's first-person assertion must not be overwritten by Hachiman or Haruno, but self-authorship and causal purity are different questions.**

## B3. “Haruno is irrelevant to this choice” — REVISE

If any Phase-5 wording implies Yukino's V8 sentence proves Haruno had no causal effect, that wording fails. The source gives the opposite evidentiary caution immediately afterward. Preserve the authority of Yukino's self-attribution; keep influence open.

## B4. Freedom versus practiced authorship — PRESERVE

The distinction remains useful across the family arc. Having formal permission or theoretical options does not guarantee that a person has experience making consequential choices that others will recognize as hers. This remains an analytical model rather than a quoted series term, but it fits the source without requiring motive invention.

## B5. First-person possessives are evidence, not a magic authorship code — REVISE

The repeated late `私の意志`, `私の気持ち`, `私の依頼`, `私の願い` cluster remains significant. But Japanese first-person possession is ordinary grammar; Phase 5 occasionally made the pattern sound more diagnostic than it can be alone.

**Audited formulation:**

> **Repeated first-person possessive grammar strengthens an authorship reading when it appears at moments where others have been assigning Yukino's motives, requests, or future. It is corroborative evidence, not a freestanding score of agency.**

## B6. V12 dependence self-description is neither false nor total — DOWNGRADE totalization

Yukino says she understands herself as dependent and describes pushing burdens onto others. That self-description deserves evidence status. It occurs, however, inside a scene already framed by Haruno's diagnostic vocabulary and during Yukino's attempt to justify a separation/remedy.

Hachiman immediately disputes the interpretation. Later the novels refuse to treat `共依存` as feeling ontology.

Thus:

> **Yukino's V12 dependence claim is strong evidence that she experiences a burden-transfer/reliance problem; it is not an objective total diagnosis of her relationship or proof that all reliance is pathological.**

## B7. Capability-aware support remains the best late autonomy image — PRESERVE

The late exchange whose logic is “you can stand alone / I know” remains powerful precisely because support is offered after capability is recognized. It rejects both incapacity-as-precondition-for-care and solitude-as-proof-of-autonomy.

The audit keeps the claim but labels “terminal autonomy image” an analytical description, not a doctrinal endpoint.

## B8. Romance does not solve family or vocation — PRESERVE

No Japanese evidence justifies folding Yukino's family, educational, housing, and vocational future into the romantic resolution. Hachiman's life-claim increases the stakes of their future; it does not answer what Yukino will do with her father's work, her mother, Haruno, or institutional family obligations.

## B9. Interest in her father's work is a real axis, not yet a secured career plan — DOWNGRADE predictive weight

The father's-work material establishes independent vocational interest and future orientation. The audit rejects any stronger inference that the text has settled Yukino's eventual career.

# 5. Cluster C — Yui: atmosphere, desire, loss, and the changing meaning of “everything”

## C1. Yui is an active constitutor of the triad — PRESERVE

The hostile pass found no basis for reducing Yui to mediator or losing heroine. Her repeated acts of naming, invitation, scheduling, ordinary maintenance, request-making, objection, and emotional translation are causal to the club's continuity.

## C2. V11 `全部ほしい` is owned desire — STRENGTHEN

The source is explicit:

- `あたしは全部ほしい。今も、これからも。`
- `あたしはもうちゃんと決めてる`

This decisively defeats the claim that Yui preserves ambiguity because she is unable to choose.

## C3. `全部ほしい` is not one stable object — REJECT fixed-semantic formulation

V14 deliberately reuses the phrase while expanding it to pain, wounds, the answer, and Yukino's feelings. The final synthesis should trace the phrase longitudinally rather than translate both moments into the same English proposition.

The V11 and V14 uses are continuous in appetite but different in **what must be included in “everything.”**

## C4. Communication as effort is strongly supported — STRENGTHEN

Yui says:

- `それ、言わなきゃ絶対わかんないよ`
- `伝わらなくてもっ！ ていうか、ヒッキー、伝える努力してないだけじゃん`
- `その分、あたしがわかろうとするからいいの。ゆきのんもたぶんそうだよ`

This does not make Yui the series' communication oracle. It does establish a mature alternative to Hachiman's use of imperfect transmission as a reason not to speak.

> **The answer is not “speech reveals truth,” but “imperfect speech can create something another person is able to answer and continue trying to understand.”**

## C5. Yui's objection has independent jurisdictional force — STRENGTHEN

During the V14 planning conversation, Yui immediately rejects Hachiman's “destroy the escape routes” method. This is important because it prevents the final couple's eventual reciprocity from monopolizing normative authority. The person who loses romantically can still correctly criticize the method by which the chosen pair reaches its confrontation.

## C6. Romantic loss is real — PRESERVE

The text gives an actual Hachiman/Yukino choice and Yui's grief. Post-choice continuity cannot be used to pretend the romantic asymmetry remained unresolved.

## C7. Continuing feeling after rejection is not itself pathology — REJECT pathological inference

The novels leave room for residual hope, habit, affection, friendship commitment, and mixed motive. They do not authorize a rule that continued proximity after rejection is evidence of unhealthy dependence.

## C8. Post-loss subjecthood is strongly preserved — PRESERVE

Yui continues to make practical judgments, sustain friendship, participate in the club, and imagine a future. The final synthesis should not narratively “reimburse” her with hidden reciprocity, but it also must not administer her out of the story.

## C9. “Making atmosphere capable of carrying answers” remains useful but is a V2 synthesis — REVISE label

The phrase captures Yui's development well. Phase 6 simply marks it as analytical compression, not a concept spoken or systematically theorized by the novels.

# 6. Cluster D — Service Club triad and romantic choice

## D1. Three-person system, not merely triangle — PRESERVE

The club's history, requests, ordinary routines, and post-choice succession exceed romantic competition. This remains one of the most important structural corrections to route-centric readings.

## D2. The V9 `本物` request is first a triadic procedural event — STRENGTHEN

Hachiman makes the request to the Service Club configuration, not to Yukino privately as a disguised confession. Yui actively pushes continuation after Yukino says she does not understand. Romantic implications matter, but retrospectively reducing V9 to pair-selection would erase the scene's actual institutional and triadic form.

## D3. Yukino's feelings cannot be allocated by Hachiman and Yui — STRENGTHEN

V14 makes this problem explicit. Yui asks Yukino to tell her own feelings. Yukino's earlier impulse to solve Yui's wish by withdrawing is no longer enough. This directly supports the claim that benevolent self-removal can still overreach another person's future.

## D4. Self-authorship is necessary but not sufficient — STRENGTHEN

The audit finds this especially strong in the final movement. A person can sincerely own a proposed sacrifice or withdrawal and still be attempting to settle a shared relationship unilaterally. The ethical problem cannot be solved simply by proving “this was my choice.”

## D5. Hachiman/Yukino is the selected romantic dyad — PRESERVE

The Japanese ending is unambiguous enough to govern this state. Hachiman asks for continuing life involvement; Yukino reciprocates and explicitly says she likes him.

## D6. Romantic selection does not retroactively falsify Hachiman/Yui or Yukino/Yui — PRESERVE

The V14 Yui/Yukino material is especially important. Their friendship and rivalry remain independently authored even when they recognize incompatible romantic futures.

## D7. Final triad = continuation after answer, not restored ambiguity — PRESERVE

The club survives, but the romantic relation is no longer equally open. This remains the cleanest state description.

## D8. “Conflict tolerance is stronger than harmony” — REVISE as comparative heuristic

The novels repeatedly show false or costly harmony, but they do not establish a universal hierarchy in which visible conflict is intrinsically more mature. Some accommodation is genuinely caring and some conflict is destructive.

> **Maturity is better indicated when disagreement can alter the relationship without requiring participant deletion; neither harmony nor conflict alone is the metric.**

## D9. “Revisable interdependence under known non-equivalence” — REVISE as terminal analytic model

The formulation survives as a powerful V2 synthesis, especially after V14.5 and the Watari anthology band. It must be labeled as the analyst's abstraction rather than the series' final explicit definition of relation.

# 7. Cluster E — `本物`, performance, counterfeit, and mixed motive

## E1. `本物` is longitudinal rather than fixed — STRENGTHEN

The primary-source sequence is internally resistant to a fixed definition.

V8 rejects projection mistaken for knowledge. V9 desires complete understanding while simultaneously calling such desire `独善的`, `独裁的`, and `傲慢`, then calls the resulting confession potentially `欺瞞` or `贋作`. V13 contrasts Haruno's rejection of `紛い物` with the possibility that an imperfect origin can produce singular value. V14 resolves concrete choice without issuing an external authenticity certificate. V14.5 shifts attention to the danger of sanctifying the successful form.

A single early definition cannot responsibly contain this movement.

## E2. Projection mistaken for knowledge is the clearest negative criterion — STRENGTHEN

V8 directly names unilateral wish-projection and misunderstanding as something not to call `本物`. This remains the most source-secure negative criterion in the corpus.

## E3. V9 changes procedure before object — STRENGTHEN

The source itself proves this. Hachiman asks through request grammar, acknowledges that speaking will not guarantee understanding, and ends without a positive shared definition. Yukino's `わからない` is part of the scene's meaning, not a temporary failure to be overwritten by later romance.

## E4. Performance is not falsity — PRESERVE

Nothing in the hostile pass supports a mask/true-self ontology. Social scripts, politeness, cute registers, club roles, public personas, and pretexts can coexist with real feeling.

## E5. Motive purity is not the authenticity threshold — PRESERVE

Yui's care and possessiveness, Hachiman's care and neededness, Iroha's mixed pretexts, and Yukino's pride and fear repeatedly coexist. The text does not demand purified motive before action becomes real.

## E6. Sincerity does not certify method — PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN through the bridge

V14 is powerful counterevidence to any sincerity-based moral shortcut. Hachiman's final desire is sincere; his preparatory method remains coercive. Yukino's withdrawal can be sincere and still allocate the relationship beyond her own jurisdiction.

## E7. Pain does not certify genuineness — PRESERVE

Neither self-sacrifice nor emotional injury functions as a truth test. The novels frequently show people producing pain through genuine care, fear, and conviction.

## E8. Directness is not a truth code — STRENGTHEN

V9 explicitly denies that saying something guarantees understanding. V14 Yui asks for communicative effort rather than magical transparency. This is among the most secure Phase-6 survivors.

## E9. “Not fake” is weaker than “genuine” — REVISE lexical hierarchy

As an analytical shorthand, the distinction is useful: a relation can resist total falsification without satisfying Hachiman's maximal aspiration. But Japanese terms such as `偽物`, `紛い物`, `贋作`, `欺瞞`, `本物`, `真実`, and `信実` do not form a stable technical ladder across all speakers.

> **Use “not fake” versus “genuine” as a local interpretive distinction, never as a dictionary-like hierarchy supposedly encoded by the novels.**

## E10. “Answerability without certification” — REVISE, preserve core

This remains one of V2's most productive summaries. Phase 6 narrows its normative force:

> **Mature relations increasingly make desire, objection, and consequence answerable, but they continue to use strategy, convention, pretext, asymmetrical influence, and partial understanding. “Answerability” must not be read as requiring explicit deliberative consent at every step.**

## E11. Artificial origin is not destiny — PRESERVE

The Service Club's imposed origin, Iroha's engineered presidency, and the V13 counterfeit vocabulary all support the proposition that provenance alone does not determine current relational meaning.

## E12. Final configuration is not sacred — REVISE authority level

The V14.5 `神聖視` correction strongly supports the local claim. Final synthesis should say the novel **invites** a broader anti-sanctification inference, not that Hachiman's line certifies the entire series' ontology.

## E13. Transparent pretext can be sincere — PRESERVE with boundary condition

A pretext can become shared relational grammar when participants can recognize, refuse, or revise it. But transparency does not automatically neutralize pressure. This boundary becomes important for Iroha and Hachiman alike.

# 8. Cluster F — helping, self-sacrifice, dependency, and social power

## F1. Care supplies motive, not sovereignty — PRESERVE

The source repeatedly supports the difference between reason to act and authority to decide for somebody else. This remains central.

## F2. Reason to act can precede explicit request — PRESERVE

The series contains emergencies, indirect requests, people unable to ask, and institutional duties. An ethics that required prior explicit request for every intervention would not fit the novels.

## F3. Accurate diagnosis does not create jurisdiction — PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN

Haruno is the strongest negative control. A diagnosis may be perceptive and still lack authority to settle another person's feeling or required remedy.

## F4. Self-sacrifice as “cost-allocation mechanism” — REVISE as V2 tool

The phrase usefully reveals why Hachiman's willingness to pay with his own reputation does not authorize collateral costs imposed on other people. But “cost-allocation mechanism” is the audit's conceptual vocabulary, not a category the novel itself theorizes.

The core survives:

> **Bearing one's own cost does not confer ownership over costs assigned to others.**

## F5. Outcome and procedure are independent axes — REVISE from independence to separability

They are analytically separable, not literally independent. Successful outcomes can alter later moral evaluation; bad procedure can sometimes be constrained by emergency; failure can reveal informational defects in procedure.

> **Evaluate outcome and procedure separately before considering their interaction.**

## F6. Noninterference can be abdication — STRENGTHEN

V14.5 states this directly when Hachiman rejects `好きにしたらいい` / `自分で決めればいい` as potentially `責任転嫁の逃げの言葉` in the Komachi succession context.

This is one of the strongest corrections to simplistic autonomy language.

## F7. `共依存` as reinforcement map rather than ontology — STRENGTHEN

The exact V12 language gives unusually firm primary support. Keep the diagnosis useful for neededness, expectation, burden transfer, and decision displacement; reject its use as proof that feeling is false or separation uniquely required.

## F8. Chosen interdependence — REVISE as positive analytic model

The final relation does contain capability-aware support, reliance, refusal, and continuation. Yet “chosen interdependence” should remain V2's model, not an asserted lexical endpoint of the novels. Power and skill asymmetries do not vanish.

## F9. The opposite of control is not withdrawal — PRESERVE, but revise “answerable involvement” locally

The novels strongly reject the idea that ethical maturity means noninvolvement. Hachiman continues to act. The audit only removes the temptation to treat his late involvement as cleanly answerable by definition.

## F10. Reputation as an actor-specific “price system” — REVISE as heuristic

Document 08's metaphor remains analytically productive: reputational position changes the social cost of speech, deviance, sacrifice, and intervention. But the series does not present reputation as a literal unified price mechanism.

Use it as an explanatory heuristic, not ontology.

## F11. Low status can create tactical expendability; high status can create expectation constraint — PRESERVE

Hachiman and Hayama remain useful inverse cases. The claim should not be universalized beyond the demonstrated social context.

## F12. Agenda-setting is power — STRENGTHEN

The V14 dummy-prom sequence makes this especially clear. Hachiman may return a nominal decision to Yukino while deliberately constructing the options, timing, pressure, audience, and incentives under which she decides. Formal final choice does not exhaust power analysis.

## F13. Distributed leadership can preserve authorship — PRESERVE

Meguri and later institutional examples support the distinction between depending on others and diffusing responsibility so completely that no one owns a decision.

# 9. Cluster G — counter-gazes and interpretive authority

## G1. No counter-gaze is an oracle — PRESERVE

The novels repeatedly expose access and blind spots together. Haruno, Hayama, Hiratsuka, and Komachi see different Hachimans and Yukinos.

## G2. Haruno: high-value hypothesis generator, non-sovereign diagnostician — STRENGTHEN

The V12 `共依存` sequence gives the clearest proof. Hachiman adopts part of her model while explicitly suspending judgment about whether Yukino's dependence is true. Later he separates mechanism from feeling.

Haruno's insight survives; her right to define the relationship does not.

## G3. “Hiratsuka is the strongest adult procedural ethic” — REVISE

The phrase risks circularity because the V2 framework itself values the distinctions Hiratsuka articulates. She is more defensibly described as:

> **the most sustained explicit adult source of procedural correction in Hachiman's development.**

This preserves her importance without appointing her the final moral authority.

## G4. Hiratsuka's development is not a clean coercive-founder → autonomy-mentor arc — REVISE

Her founding intervention into the Service Club is coercive. Later she increasingly returns choice and asks Hachiman to articulate reasons, but she remains capable of pressure and strategic framing. Her maturation should not be narratively over-smoothed.

## G5. Komachi as “low-coercion” engineer — DOWNGRADE

Komachi often manipulates opportunities rather than outcomes, but sibling intimacy and comedy normalize pressure that would look stronger in another relation. Keep her as a useful ordinary-life comparison, not a low-coercion benchmark.

## G6. Counter-gaze plurality formally decenters Hachiman — DOWNGRADE

The first-person architecture remains dominant. Interludes and counterviews **constrain** the narrator's monopoly; they do not produce equal formal authority.

## G7. Yukino has highest authority on attributable first-person choices, not all causal explanation — PRESERVE / REVISE

When Yukino says “this is my will,” that claim cannot be replaced by Haruno's or Hachiman's interpretation. It does not prove she has perfect knowledge of every influence on herself.

# 10. Cluster H — family, adulthood, futurity, and inheritance

## H1. “Adulthood = consequence-bearing authorship” — REVISE from definition to synthesis

The formula usefully unifies many late decisions. The source does not explicitly define adulthood that way. Carry it forward as a V2 synthesis:

> **A major late-series pattern links maturity with owning consequential choices under incomplete information rather than merely escaping dependence.**

## H2. Haruno as over-authored future — DOWNGRADE / OPEN

Family succession pressure is real. The available text does not establish enough detail about Haruno's eventual future to make “over-authored” a settled total state, much less the strongest case in the corpus.

Document 13 should preserve the hypothesis and its evidence while lowering confidence.

## H3. Yukino as under-recognized chooser — REVISE

The phrase captures a real family pattern but can imply that others never recognize her agency. Later family interactions are more situational. Use:

> **Yukino repeatedly struggles to have consequential choices treated as hers within a family system that often anticipates, tests, or conditions them.**

## H4. Yukino's father's work — PRESERVE axis, DOWNGRADE prediction

Independent vocational interest survives. Career settlement does not.

## H5. Mrs. Yukinoshita's apparent progression — REVISE

## H6. Class/material-resource analysis — PRESERVE only where direct

Kawasaki's scholarship/cost concerns and the Yukinoshita/Hayama families' institutional resources clearly make self-authorship differently expensive. The final synthesis should resist constructing a comprehensive class theory the novels do not fully elaborate.

## H7. Transformed inheritance — REVISE as analytic endpoint

The phrase remains strong for Service Club succession, Iroha's presidency, and family-role negotiation. Label it as V2 synthesis, not explicit doctrine.

## H8. Bridge as “mutual opportunity cost” — REVISE

The future-facing content genuinely invokes time, emotion, future, and life. But the route into the exchange remains coercively engineered. The final synthesis may call the eventual claim reciprocal without describing the entire bridge event as a clean model of mutual cost-bearing.

## H9. Near-term shared planning does not settle destiny — PRESERVE

V14.5's `一緒のとこ、行くんだと思ってた` and related planning make shared near-term expectations real. They do not prove marriage, lifelong co-location, or predetermined careers.

# 11. Cluster I — Japanese voice, narrative form, comedy, and intertextuality

## I1. Linguistic form is evidence, not truth value — STRENGTHEN

The hostile pass repeatedly confirms this. Soft speech can be firm; direct speech can be manipulative; polite form can carry refusal; first-person narration can mark uncertainty; performed registers can carry real desire.

## I2. Narrated Hachiman and spoken Hachiman must remain distinct — PRESERVE

Internal abstraction is not equivalent to what he can put into interpersonal circulation. V9 and V14 demonstrate how difficult first-person speech can be despite enormous internal verbal productivity.

## I3. `と思う` can mark ownership, but is not inherently ethically superior — PRESERVE / REVISE

In `俺は関わり続けたいと、思ってる`, the phrase usefully locates the claim as his stance rather than external necessity. But ordinary Japanese modality cannot be given a stable moral score.

## I4. Yukino's `私の` cluster — REVISE as corroborative

As above, first-person possession matters when the dramatic problem is others assigning her motives. It must not be treated as a quantitative agency marker.

## I5. Benefactive grammar — PRESERVE as targeted evidence only

Who does something for whom can illuminate agency allocation, indebtedness, or assumed jurisdiction. The construction does not independently resolve the ethics of the act.

## I6. Interludes complicate authority; they do not automatically create polyphony — REVISE

Unattributed or formally ambiguous interludes require attribution discipline. They can expose perspectives unavailable to Hachiman without becoming a fully equal narrative architecture.

The V13 `紛い物` / `贋作` material remains important, but claims about which character “owns” an unattributed thought must remain bounded by formal evidence.

## I7. Comedy is analytical medium, not automatic defense — PRESERVE

The novels genuinely think through jokes, genre scripts, and references. The audit keeps the warning against turning every joke into a symptom of repression.

## I8. Structural intertext hierarchy — OPEN

Document 11's distinction between structural and decorative intertexts is methodologically sound, but Phase 6 has not reopened enough of every named source-reference pair to certify the precise hierarchy. Carry the major intertexts forward as well-supported interpretations, not closed proposition-level facts.

## I9. Mature endpoint = responsibility for who says/knows/wants — PRESERVE as analytic formulation

This remains a strong synthesis of the voice ledger, provided it is labeled as analysis rather than a linguistic rule encoded by grammar itself.

# 13. Japanese lexical and attribution audit

## 13.1 `本物` / `偽物` / `紛い物` / `贋作` / `欺瞞`

The audit preserves the lexical distinctions established in Phase 4 and Document 11. These terms overlap but are not interchangeable technical synonyms.

V9 is especially resistant to purification. Hachiman desires `本物` while describing his own desire as potentially arrogant and his own confession as possibly `欺瞞` or `贋作`. This means the word cannot responsibly be converted into a stable authorial stamp for whichever later relationship the reader prefers.

V13 further breaks an origin-purity model. A form described as `紛い物` or `贋作` can still be historically singular or irreplaceable. Haruno's insistence on “the genuine alone” exists alongside a competing Hayama-side possibility that feeling can grow from compromised form.

**Audited rule:** always preserve speaker, grammatical object, and local contrast.

## 13.2 `依存` and `共依存`

The audit strengthens the restriction:

- a character's `依存` self-description is evidence of that character's model and experienced mechanism;
- Haruno's `共依存` is a diagnostic proposal;
- Hachiman's later `共依存は仕組みだ。気持ちじゃない` is a direct internal limitation on turning that proposal into feeling ontology.

Do not translate every reliance relation into “dependency.”

## 13.3 `わからない`

`わからない` can mark ignorance, refusal, overwhelm, uncertainty, or honest epistemic limit. It is not automatically mature. It gains Phase-6 importance when a scene **allows it to remain unresolved rather than replacing it with sovereign inference**.

V9 Yukino's `私には、……わからないわ` matters because the request remains real even without immediate conceptual comprehension.

## 13.4 `ちゃんと`

`ちゃんと` is semantically broad and ordinary. It can mark adequacy, proper procedure, seriousness, or self-demand. The audit rejects treating every occurrence as evidence of the same ethical concept. Use only clustered, context-specific recurrence.

## 13.5 `全部`

Yui's repeated `全部` is precisely the kind of word that demonstrates why lexical recurrence must not be mistaken for semantic stasis. Track the object and surrounding clauses each time.

## 13.6 Formally unattributed interludes

The audit reaffirms the V2 rule: formal non-attribution remains formal non-attribution. Context can support a likely speaker, but the final synthesis must distinguish:

- explicit attribution;
- strong contextual inference;
- thematic resemblance.

This is especially important when using V12–V13 interludes to construct counter-gazes or authenticity theory.

# 17. Outgoing canonical thesis set after adversarial review

The following claims are safe to route into Document 13 as the **audited mature baseline**, with the stated authority limits.

## 17.1 Hachiman

Hachiman's social intelligence remains real. His characteristic epistemic risk is not generic unreliability but **conversion of observation and prediction into private jurisdiction**. He becomes better at admitting ignorance, making desire attributable, requesting rather than merely administering, receiving help, and allowing another person's answer to matter. He does **not** cease using manipulation, strategy, abstraction, or pretext.

## 17.2 Yukino

Yukino's competence is genuine. Her growth is not from dependence to independence but toward greater authorship over consequential choices and relations. First-person claims such as `これは私の意志よ` have high standing without proving freedom from influence. Reliance can coexist with capability; romance does not settle family or vocation.

## 17.3 Yui

Yui is an active relational agent whose atmosphere sensitivity is part of her stable competence. Her `全部ほしい` develops rather than repeats unchanged. By V14 she explicitly includes painful knowledge and another person's answer in the field of what she is willing to receive. Romantic loss is real; continued subjecthood and feeling are also real.

## 17.4 Triad and romance

Hachiman/Yukino is the selected romantic pair. That choice creates known non-equivalence without retroactively falsifying Hachiman/Yui intimacy or Yukino/Yui friendship. The final triad is continuation after answer, not restored romantic ambiguity.

## 17.5 Authenticity

`本物` is a longitudinal regulative problem, not a fixed purity definition. The clearest negative criterion is unilateral projection mistaken for knowledge. Performance, mixed motive, pain, directness, and romantic success do not independently certify or disqualify relation. “Answerability without certification” remains a V2 synthesis, now qualified to include persistent indirection and power asymmetry.

## 17.6 Helping and dependency

Care, competence, sacrifice, prediction, and promise create reasons to act but not blanket sovereignty. `共依存` has real explanatory force as a reinforcement mechanism and explicitly does not define feeling. Ethical involvement cannot be reduced either to control or to withdrawal. Agenda-setting and option construction must be included in jurisdiction analysis.

## 17.7 Social systems

Reputation, role, group hierarchy, institutional office, and face are causally real and alter the price of action. They do not exhaust personhood. “Price system” is a heuristic, not an ontology. Formal final choice can coexist with substantial upstream power exercised through option construction.

## 17.8 Counter-gazes

Haruno, Hayama, Hiratsuka, and Komachi constrain Hachiman's interpretations through different kinds of access. None has series-wide sovereignty. First-person form nevertheless continues to privilege Hachiman's perspective; counter-gazes complicate rather than abolish that formal asymmetry.

## 17.9 Adulthood and future

The late novels repeatedly associate maturity with owning consequential choices inside constraint, but “consequence-bearing authorship” and “transformed inheritance” are V2 analytical syntheses. Yukino's future remains materially open beyond romance. Near-term pair planning does not settle lifelong destination.

## 17.10 Voice and form

Japanese voice remains core evidence. No register is a truth code. Hedges, possessives, benefactives, ellipsis, politeness, directness, and interludes require local interpretation. The cast does not converge on one transparent speech style; stable idiolects become capable of carrying more attributable desire, uncertainty, objection, and commitment.

## 17.11 Supplements and ordinary life

Chronology and authorship govern supplementary authority. No standalone `V06_75` exists in the locked current corpus. Watari-authored post-V14 supplementary stories can strengthen ordinary durability without rewriting prospective mainline ambiguity. V14.5's `神聖視` is a powerful local anti-freezing correction whose wider significance should remain an inference rather than an authorial theorem.

# 20. Final Phase-6 judgment

The adversarial audit does not overturn Oregairu V2. It makes the project less tidy and therefore more faithful to the novels.

The specialist layer was broadly correct to move away from familiar binaries:

- genuine versus fake person;
- dependence versus independence;
- directness versus performance;
- altruism versus selfishness;
- winner versus loser;
- family obedience versus liberation;
- harmony versus conflict.

The hostile Japanese pass shows, however, that V2 occasionally replaced those binaries with a new clean vocabulary—*authorship*, *jurisdiction*, *answerability*, *chosen interdependence*, *transformed inheritance*—and then risked treating that vocabulary as if the characters had finally learned to behave according to it.

They have not.

Hachiman still engineers situations. Yukino still tries to solve shared problems through unilateral choice. Yui still wants incompatible goods. Haruno still diagnoses from inside her own investment. Hiratsuka still pressures as well as teaches. Iroha still uses pretext. The final couple reaches reciprocity through an encounter containing real coercive elements. The Service Club survives not because everyone now understands one another correctly, but because misunderstanding and asymmetry no longer always have to be hidden behind the same protective arrangement.

That yields the strongest audited final formulation:

> **Oregairu's mature relational achievement is not procedural purity. It is the increasing inability of any single explanation, role, diagnosis, sacrifice, or protective fiction to settle the relationship by itself. Desire becomes more attributable; objection becomes more consequential; support can coexist with capability; loss does not require deletion; and successful forms remain revisable. Yet strategy, performance, unequal leverage, and incomplete understanding persist. The series becomes more answerable without becoming transparent.**

That formulation is deliberately less utopian than Phase 5's cleanest language.

It is also better supported.

Phase 6 is therefore **closed** once this audit is archived and routed. The next architecture-defined artifact is:

**`13_EVIDENCE_LOCATOR_AND_FIRST_PASS_REVISION_LEDGER.md`**

Its task is publication control: convert the specialist claims and the adversarial dispositions into one deterministic evidence-and-revision graph before the continuous full-series synthesis is written.
