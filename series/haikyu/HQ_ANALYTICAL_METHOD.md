# Haikyu!! analytical method

## 1. Scope and responsibilities

This source-bounded method adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) to a 45-volume supplied colour-edition sequence and two separate companion publications. The [architecture](HQ_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) assigns cumulative and synthesis duties. No narrative is admitted now, and no character or plot knowledge may be imported from familiarity or an adaptation.

The method is designed to preserve individual and collective action, causal learning, relationships and visual timing where the source establishes them. It must remain capable of reading low-stakes routine, humor, language and institutional pressure in equal depth. A result, group label or character adjective cannot substitute for the sequence of perceptions, decisions and graphic presentation that supports it.

Before a future run, record prior exposure and quarantine remembered information. A fresh session is not automatically blind. Keep chapter-level disclosure frontiers within each authorized volume; a later reveal cannot become an earlier actor's motive or knowledge.

## 2. Exact witness and page coverage

The candidate main lane is `HQ-COL-Vnn`, provisionally numeric V01→V45 after order verification. Each selected file requires immutable digest, language/edition receipt and a versioned spine-to-image occurrence map. Presence, transport, identity verification, admission, inspection and closure are distinct. Changed bytes create a different witness until equivalence is demonstrated.

Treat the colour edition as its own visual source. Do not strip colour, infer monochrome composition/tonal meaning from it, or attribute colouring decisions to a creator without evidence. An eventual monochrome comparison is separately scoped, with exact corresponding pages and differences retained. A colour effect established in this edition is not automatically an original-serialization effect.

The Chronicle and Final Guidebook are separate witnesses, not V46/V47. Classify their components as editorial data, creator statement, interview, technical explanation, retrospective commentary, illustration, narrative/bonus fiction or unresolved. Creator statements can establish stated intent, not settle what a page demonstrates. Facts disclosed there cannot enter a prospective main reading unless separately admitted at that frontier.

Map all covers, inserts, narrative pages, extras, colophon and previews. Page review states are REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED and UNRESOLVED_READABILITY. Record an actual inspection range and reason for exclusions. Archive validity, readable images and complete narrative page order are different facts. OCR/search cannot certify page review, especially silent or complex pages.

## 3. Locator and retrieval contract

Use both human and deterministic coordinates: `HQ-COL-Vnn / verified chapter / iNNNN / visible printed label if any / panel ordinal / balloon ordinal`. The one-based image occurrence follows the locked witness's reading order, including covers; it is not an inferred printed page. Anchor it to the immutable digest, spine/member image identity and mapping version. Panel/balloon ordinals follow verified reading order, with uncertain alternatives recorded.

A spread is one relational evidence object with both image occurrences and pairing basis. Do not claim a page-turn reveal from a single-page display until facing-page layout is verified. Crops preserve coordinates and full-page context. For contested text, reopen the image, identify the balloon/tail/position and confirm the Japanese; OCR is never quotation-grade evidence by itself.

Load-bearing claims receive a retrieval check from the locked witness. A pending/failed route remains RETRIEVAL_UNRESOLVED and cannot support a benchmark or settled central argument. Public prose includes sanitized keys, eligible brief quotations and locators, never bulk pages, OCR dumps or restricted source IDs.

## 4. Action sequence, perception and collective causality

Segment by source-established shifts of goal, position, configuration, information or formal organization. For each movement retain entering conditions, participants as known, perception, appraisal, available options, choice/non-action, coordination and consequence. Distinguish explicit causation from coincidence, commentator explanation, retrospective inference and omitted intermediate motion.

Where the source depicts a structured activity, record its phase, positional relations, stated constraints and visible results only when supported. A score, rule or named technique must be read from the page, not filled from external sporting knowledge. A technical explanation by a participant may be mistaken or strategic; compare it with depicted action and alternative accounts.

Keep depicted simultaneity, page succession and event succession separate. Parallel panels, reaction inserts, replayed views and interior thought may reorganize one interval; more panels do not automatically mean more elapsed time. A still panel or a sequence's endpoints do not establish the exact motion between them. Identify what is shown, what is schematically implied and what remains unknown.

Collective outcomes require more than attributing success/failure to one figure. Preserve individual knowledge, coordination, timing, role allocation, resources, constraints and competing contributions. A result does not prove intention or stable ability. Test alternative causes, including changed context and opportunities, before treating an outcome as development.

## 5. Character, relationship and group state

Discover names and identities from admitted pages. Separate direct speech, visible behavior, narrated/thought text, others' reports and role/public presentation. Expressions are ambiguous evidence; a drawn face does not uniquely establish emotion or motive. Rendering shifts can be framing, not literal physiological change.

Distinguish disposition, temporary state, resources, knowledge, learned repertoire, role pressure and newly disclosed information. Track before/after evidence and rival explanation. A learned or performed behavior is not therefore false. Ordinary practice, routine conversation, rest, waiting, practical decisions and low-stakes interactions are first-class evidence where present; crisis or high-stakes action cannot alone establish baseline personality.

Relationships are directed A→B and B→A with separate expectations, knowledge, access, dependence, trust/conflict and source-earned dimensions. Record what becomes possible between subjects, and which context or institution conditions it. Repeated co-presence does not automatically prove closeness. Group membership, role and institutional rule each need evidence; a confident participant does not speak for every member or institution.

Where development is claimed, classify the cause as DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW or UNRESOLVED. Keep earlier states and ordinary counterexamples. Absence becomes negative evidence only when a real opportunity existed.

## 6. Japanese language and graphic voice

Attribute speaker, thinker, narrator and recipient using tails, placement and context. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing attribution. Distinguish spoken balloons, thought, retrospective narration, editorial captions, handwritten aside and diegetic signs/screens.

For consequential language, retain short verified Japanese wording, base/ruby distinctions, address/self-reference, ellipsis, politeness and sentence endings. Label exact quote, working transcription and paraphrase; an English gloss must expose important alternatives. Analyze speech as appraisal → objective → speech act → recipient/audience → register → expression. Require comparable contexts before declaring a stable voice or relational shift.

Sound effects are drawn text: distinguish sound-imitating and manner/state expressions, note size/placement/integration and give a labeled gloss. Bold lettering, balloon shape and graphic trembling do not establish measured acoustic delivery. No anime acting or music may supply the missing evidence.

## 7. Page form and colour

Read panel size, gutters, borders/bleeds, framing, gaze, composition, background inclusion/absence, reaction placement, silence and spread rhythm. Connect feature → plausible effect → alternative explanation. Preserve how a page organizes attention between individuals and group action instead of reducing it to event content.

Record rendering-register changes such as stylization, exaggeration or abstraction as presentation, with local convention and exceptions. A striking image is not automatically a symbol; require recurrence, variation and dramatic relation. Silent pages are inspected as fully as dialogue-heavy pages.

Colour observations specify this witness, visible region and effect. Distinguish contrast, emphasis, identification and other supported functions from speculative symbolic meaning. Verify legibility at suitable resolution and retain image uncertainty. An external assumption about original monochrome art cannot resolve a colour-edition ambiguity.

## 8. Claims, counterreadings and sensitivity

Classify claims as OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION or UNRESOLVED, with confidence and reason. Separate depiction, explanation, evaluation and endorsement. Do not prefilter morally difficult evidence or excuse it through later outcomes; describe only what supports the analytical claim.

Every volume's governing thesis has a serious rival and a disconfirmation condition. Preserve causal, relational, linguistic and visual counterexamples in full prose. Later information uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN with former/new formulations and routes. Never rewrite prospective freezes.

Conflicts may be edition differences, page-order errors, perspective, ignorance, commentary unreliability, formal stylization, chronology or genuine inconsistency. Keep them distinct and unresolved when needed. Smooth series prose is not a reason to harmonize evidence.

## 9. Future reading acceptance and full depth

An accepted volume reading contains: exact receipt/coverage; entering state and frozen expectations; a developed thesis/rival; movement-by-movement causal and page analysis; subject changes and directed relations; group/institutional evidence if earned; Japanese register and graphic form; colour and recurrence with counterexamples; claim transitions; synchronized ledger manifest; exit freeze; limitations and completion decision.

The full analysis must show how a consequential movement works, not just name its outcome. Preserve ordinary-life and language passages, uncertainty, rival causes and the relation of panels to reader knowledge. No universal word cap or summary table can replace substantive source-facing analysis.

Before semantic acceptance, promotion or integration, sample the introduction, a substantial middle section, conclusion/exit state, every major relationship and ambiguous actor passage. Check known names, purposeful roles, pronouns, concealment and speaker/thinker/recipient attribution. Classify findings as JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE, SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY, KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED, UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION, AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT or INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION; record scope/findings and repair supported defects only.

Close the full reading and six registers atomically. A scoped “no material change” records inspection, not a missing update. Unreadable pages, unresolved central locators or required missing visual coverage remain debts; a completed ZIP scan cannot close them.

## 10. Reconstruction and transfer

A later monograph and conditional reconstruction model are different responsibilities. The architecture requires source-bounded time/context/relationship states, ordinary controls, exceptions, uncertainty and independently evaluated tests. No cast model or readiness grade exists now.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before source exposure; use RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST after exposure. Synthetic output remains a derived test and cannot enter primary evidence or canonical characterization. Preserve full readings, state histories and counterarguments during [handoff](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md). Frozen readings are corrected through linked revisions/errata under [archive authority](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), never rewritten to match later knowledge.
