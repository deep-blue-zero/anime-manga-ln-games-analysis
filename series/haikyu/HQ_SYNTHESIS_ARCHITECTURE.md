# Haikyu!! synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose and INITIAL authority

This foundation is designed for a long colour-edition manga corpus in which future readings must retain individual and collective causality, relationship development, ordinary contexts, Japanese expression and graphic construction. These are analytical responsibilities anticipated from the source form and inventory, not findings about unread events.

The boundary is supplied main V01–45 plus separately cataloged Chronicle and Final Guidebook. No narrative, paratext content, cast or continuity has been admitted. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) alone routes present authority, and the [method](HQ_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs reading. Canonical status covers INITIAL rules and attributed metadata; architecture owner approval and narrative readiness are unclaimed.

Apply the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction reference](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md), and the current archive/execution/handoff controls. This architecture cannot authorize a run.

## 2. Source hierarchy and colour/paratext boundaries

Locked and actually inspected Japanese main pages govern claims about their depicted text/art in this colour edition. Source metadata establishes only reported inventory identity. A rendered page must be legible and correctly ordered before an observation can support interpretation; an import integrity result does not establish that condition.

The colour witness is not interchangeable with monochrome, magazine serialization or an adaptation. Its colouring provenance and revisions remain unknown. An eventual comparison uses separate exact identities and page crosswalks; colour observations cannot silently become claims about an uninspected original.

The Chronicle and Final Guidebook are separate source classes. Component classification distinguishes data/editorial text, stated creator intent, retrospective commentary, art, technical explanation and narrative material. Authority is claim-specific: a creator statement is evidence of a stated intention, not automatic proof of narrative effect. Companion content has a publication/disclosure boundary and requires separate admission; it cannot fill early character knowledge.

External technical or reception sources can be admitted only for a defined question and retain EXTERNAL_REFERENCE status. Derived readings, ledgers and monographs route evidence rather than create independent corroboration. Primary pages, OCR and private source receipts stay in the evidence plane.

## 3. Artifact structure and justified day-one infrastructure

The six initialized registers are mandatory because they have different recurring duties across the 45-volume planning range:

| Existing register | Owns | Does not replace |
|---|---|---|

| [Collective-action/state ledger](COLLECTIVE_ACTION_AND_STATE_LEDGER.md) | Actions, timing, information, coordination, group/institutional changes | Full causal argument |
| [Character/relationship/language ledger](CHARACTER_RELATIONSHIP_AND_LANGUAGE_LEDGER.md) | Individual states, directed ties, ordinary controls and written register | Monographs or voice models |
| [Visual-form/colour register](VISUAL_FORM_AND_COLOUR_REGISTER.md) | Page/spread design, rendering and colour-specific evidence | Full formal close reading |
| [Claim/evidence/revision ledger](CLAIM_EVIDENCE_AND_REVISION_LEDGER.md) | Retrieval, theses, rivals, changes and forecasts | Substantive argumentative prose |
| [Checkpoint/handoff register](CHECKPOINT_AND_HANDOFF_REGISTER.md) | Transactions, freezes, roles, dependencies and acceptance | Source or synthesis completion itself |

The current map, method and architecture complete the existing Markdown set. No future directory is created merely for symmetry.

Future `readings/HQ_COL_Vnn_READING.md` holds each authorized full volume reading. Future `checkpoints/HQ_CPnn.md` holds a developed tranche reconciliation. Evidence-earned character, relationship and collective-action/form specialists use distinct homes under `studies/`; a dedicated companion comparison uses `studies/paratext/`. Future `synthesis/HQ_CONTINUOUS_SYNTHESIS.md` integrates the work, and `reconstruction/<subject-id>/` separates evidence, operational model and independent tests. These are planned homes, not existing artifacts or authorized tasks.

A specialist needs an independent question and responsibility. A new file must not duplicate an existing topical home; revision belongs in that home with history. Do not create a dossier for every name or an institution ledger for an institution not established by evidence.

## 5. Sequential transactions and long-series checkpoints

The source unit is a verified volume with chapter/movement frontiers inside it. Verify main numeric order and actual chapter/page mapping before the first run; do not derive chapter numbers arithmetically. Publication/edition order, depicted time, page disclosure and analyst admission remain separate.

Before opening the next authorized unit, freeze entering claims, states, unresolved questions and any prospective forecasts. After inspection, deliver the complete reading with all six synchronized registers and scoped no-change findings where appropriate. Record actual inspection debt, not a blanket “complete.”

For the 45-volume planning range, the authorized run must set a bounded tranche cadence and restart point before admission. A provisional ceiling of five volumes between integration checkpoints is a planning proposal, not a current run authorization or an asserted story arc. Evidence density, a major state discontinuity, conflicting causal accounts or source problems can trigger an earlier checkpoint. Do not invent named arcs from familiarity.

Each checkpoint reconciles full readings, collective/individual states, visual/linguistic patterns, claims and unresolved obligations. Freeze the complete input set and state, preserving ordinary scenes and contrary evidence. A session reset resumes from that corpus and the last accepted transaction, not a synopsis. Reaching V45 inventory position alone cannot certify source completeness, longitudinal reconciliation or full-work completion.

## 6. State, continuity and epistemic boundaries

The state key is `witness/version + source frontier + chapter/disclosure position + depicted time/phase + subject/group knowledge + role/resources + directed relationship + interpretive mode`. Record unknown values explicitly. If a depicted activity has formal phase/position/score, establish it from source evidence rather than external rules.

Panel sequence can show successive events, simultaneous perspectives, reactions or replay. Store the inferred relation and its evidence. Commentator explanation, participant belief, narrator presentation and analyst causal model remain distinct. Information available to a reader is not automatically available to every participant.

Use PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY, EDITION_COMPARATIVE and UNRESOLVED modes. Later learning or skill is not retroactively present earlier. State changes distinguish disposition, knowledge, relationship, context, role, resources and REVEALED_NOT_NEW. Companion facts have their own lane and never silently change the main prospective record.

Continuity and sameness are source-supported relations. A name, uniform, role or visual resemblance alone does not settle identity. Preserve source-required anonymity and later disclosure links rather than rewriting an early reading.

## 7. Claims, conflict and preservation

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. A revision retains old/new formulations, boundary, evidence, counterevidence, rival disposition, authority destination and dependencies. Mutable current views do not erase historical rows.

Classify conflict as witness/edition difference, page-map error, timing/chronology, knowledge asymmetry, report reliability, role/audience, rendering convention, interpretive disagreement, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Keep several explanations live where needed. An uncertain action chain must not become certain simply because a later performance result looks compatible.

A material revision triggers review of action, character/relationship, language, visual claims, specialists and models separately. Preserve valid unaffected analysis. Frozen readings are corrected through linked errata/revisions or explicitly authorized replacement, never overwritten to make historical expectations look prescient.

## 8. Specialists and continuous synthesis

Character monographs integrate diachronic behavior, motives/rivals, knowledge, ordinary-life evidence, relationships, language, presentation and literary function. Adequate context and relationship coverage matter more than appearance counts. A relationship study is warranted when directional development and surrounding group/institutional constraints need independent sustained treatment.

Potential collective-action, institutional, page-form or colour specialists remain anticipated until recurring source evidence supports a consequential question. Commission them with an exact frozen boundary, full readings, evidence routes, counterexamples and a defined contribution to the synthesis. A no-independent-specialist decision requires an evidence-based rationale and an identified owner for the role.

The continuous synthesis is not a sequence of event summaries or concatenated monograph conclusions. It must connect collective and individual development, graphic presentation, ordinary practice, language and causal uncertainty across the accepted corpus. Where specialists disagree, adjudicate through source returns or preserve the unresolved alternatives. Do not replace extensive incoming arguments with smoother abbreviated prose.

## 9. Reconstruction pathway and independent evaluation

Reconstruction is intended for source-established subjects when evidence supports a bounded model. It is distinct from literary monographs and remains NOT_ASSESSED now. No subject roster or personality is populated from familiarity. Designated discovery/evaluator agents own global character/capability registries.

Separate evidence/retrieval, operational policy and independent test record. Key a model by subject, exact source/edition frontier and state, with excluded paratext/adaptations and latent exposure declared. Do not infer motor execution or acoustic delivery beyond the depicted still-image/written evidence.

Coverage describes twelve dimensions: goals/values; appraisal/threat; motive conflict; knowledge/errors; attention/reasoning; affect/regulation; state/disposition; directed relations; role/audience; action repertoire/inhibitors; written/graphic expression; development/exceptions. Record missing contexts and evidence-channel limitations, not self-awarded readiness letters.

A candidate policy specifies trigger/stakes/current state, response family, inhibitors, overrides, exceptions, support, counterevidence, rival and falsifier. It separates durable disposition from temporary condition and traces appraisal → decision → communicative objective → recipient-conditioned speech/graphic expression. Every projection records confidence and evidence distance from observed contexts; define an abstention or multiple-plausible-responses rule when the requested state, relationship, language or situation lacks support. OBSERVED evidence is not automatically CANDIDATE_POLICY or PROMOTED_POLICY. Test ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, false/incomplete information, changed recipient, role/audience shift and consequential choice where adequate evidence exists.

Freeze genuine held-out predictions before exposure. Independent evaluators assess SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED or UNRESOLVED. Exposed work is RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Use meaningful perturbations, decoys, negative controls and baselines, with opportunity denominators and limits. Separate decision fidelity from Japanese/graphic voice fidelity; no unsupported accuracy percentage.

Synthetic outputs remain labeled derived tests and never become canonical quotes, primary evidence or independent corroboration. A monograph's quality does not certify predictive readiness.

## 10. Separate gates

| Gate | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|
| H0 Foundation | Source/colour/paratext boundaries, all sixteen duties and operational infrastructure reviewed |
| H1 Run/admission | Bounded owner scope, verified source/capability and OPEN precondition; absent now |
| H2 Sequential completion | Full readings, actual page coverage and accepted atomic transactions |
| H3 Longitudinal reconciliation | Individual/collective/relationship, timing/knowledge and form histories reconciled |
| H4 Claim stabilization | Central retrieval tested, alternatives retained and consequential revisions propagated |
| H5 Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap and ordinary/context/linguistic/visual coverage assessed; inputs adequate or holds explicit |
| H6 Specialist completion | Full scoped monographs/studies accepted with source returns and limits |
| H7 Specialist convergence | Conflicts/dependencies addressed; no-specialist route justified and responsibilities assigned |
| H8 Integrated synthesis readiness | Complete full inputs, stable boundaries and adequate role coverage reviewed |
| H9 Synthesis acceptance | Sustained integrated argument retaining causal, ordinary, linguistic and formal detail |
| H10 Reconstruction evaluation | Independent source-bounded held-out assessment, separate from literary readiness |
| H11 Final validation | Actual files, routes, authority, names/references, depth and unresolved obligations checked |
| H12 Release/freeze | Defined immutable corpus, limits, current routing, history and designated integration acceptance |

Each gate is independent. Source-complete is not synthesis-ready; a partial-boundary release is not an official complete-work declaration. Unresolved interpretation can survive with explicit limits, while missing required inspection remains a hold.

## 11. Execution, transfer and extension

Use SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS for future readings/interpretive ledger updates, DEEP_SYNTHESIS for checkpoints, specialists, monographs and models/evaluation, and PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST for propagation-sensitive architecture and continuous synthesis. Stable mechanical mapping is BOUNDED_STANDARD, hashes ROUTINE_FAST. The [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md) resolves actual model settings; do not invent them.

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) favors source-capable local readings/ledgers, fresh adequately supplied synthesis stages, independent review and a designated integration owner. This bootstrap has metadata/local document capability only. Verify transport, processing, STILL_IMAGE/TEXT inspection, interpretation and persistence separately under [capability routing](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

[Handoffs](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) include base snapshot, exact complete input/output paths and digests, witness/admission/knowledge boundary, authority, owners and unresolved obligations. Return full outputs and change/evidence manifests. The integrator compares base/candidate/current, retrieves central evidence and checks that no incoming argument or counterexample was compressed away. One mutable owner per register applies even with parallel specialists.

New volumes, monochrome witnesses or companion material extend inventory only after reconciliation and do not extend a run. Add schemas for demonstrated recurring responsibilities, record migrations and backfill only affected evidence proportionately. Current ledgers preserve append-only history; accepted readings/checkpoints freeze. Archive genuine superseded reasoning with reciprocal authority metadata; do not create empty historical layers.
