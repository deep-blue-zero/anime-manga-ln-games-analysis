## Update, reconstruction and history

Append source-grounded states and directed edges after each full accepted reading, with evidence and full-analysis routes. Include mundane conversation and practice where present. A scoped no-change record states what was inspected. Later identity disclosures add history rather than overwrite earlier anonymity.

Checkpoint review tests whether evidence is dominated by dramatic outcomes, one interlocutor or quotable dialogue. Written voice needs repeated context-sensitive evidence; lettering does not establish sound. Relationships stay directional and source-specific, with no obligatory scalar scores.

Future models remain separate from monographs and evidence. A policy must have trigger/stakes/state, response family, inhibitors, exceptions, rivals, counterevidence and falsifier, plus confidence, distance from observed contexts and an abstention/multiple-response rule. Trace appraisal → decision → recipient-conditioned expression; distinguish disposition from temporary conditions. Synthetic predictions never become evidence. Independent held-out evaluation is required; exposed work is RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Global character discovery/capability registries belong to designated agents.
