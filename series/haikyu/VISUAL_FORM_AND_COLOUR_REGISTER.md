# Visual form and colour register

## Responsibility

The supplied colour edition needs a distinct visual record. This register retains evidence routes for graphic timing, collective/individual framing, rendering changes and colour effects. It does not claim equivalence with an original monochrome edition. No source page has been inspected.

## Schemas

| Record / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Page/spread form `HQ-VF-nnnn` | Exact witness/page occurrences; verified facing relation; panel reading order; framing/gaze; gutters/borders/bleeds; scale; silence/reaction placement; described feature; proposed effect; alternative explanation; full analysis |
| Rendering/lettering `HQ-VR-nnnn` | Exact region/panel; ordinary/exaggerated/abstract register as source-established; graphic convention; local baseline; anomaly; speech/sound-effect type; nonliteral versus diegetic inference; counterexample |
| Colour observation `HQ-VC-nnnn` | Colour-witness identity; exact region; visible contrast/palette/emphasis or other bounded feature; interpretation; rival; reproduction/readability limits; colouring provenance UNKNOWN unless evidenced; prohibited monochrome inference |
| Visual recurrence `HQ-VX-nnnn` | Earlier/later exact pages; repeated and changed features; selection basis; causal/formal relation proposed; counterinstances; confidence; full comparative home |
| Modality debt `HQ-VD-nnnn` | Needed page/region/spread; missing/unreadable/mapping issue; affected claims; required route; owner; completion evidence |

A page-turn claim requires verified edition layout. A crop retains coordinates and full-page context. A sound effect is a drawn convention, not measured sound; a change of rendering does not automatically change fictional physiology. Still-image evidence cannot certify exact intervening motion.

## Update and comparison rules

After each accepted reading, append consequential observations with actual inspection routes. Report scoped no-material-addition where appropriate, without treating uninspected pages as reviewed. Require feature/effect/alternative analysis in full prose; an attractive screenshot or motif tag does not settle interpretation.

A colour-to-monochrome comparison remains deferred until both exact witnesses and aligned pages are authorized/inspected. Do not normalize away colour or silently attribute it to the original creator. A new witness or image mapping triggers affected visual-claim retrieval review.

At checkpoints, test whether formal claims overfit memorable dramatic pages. Include quiet transitions, group/individual framing changes and counterinstances when available. Preserve the full close reading and prior interpretations through claim revisions.
