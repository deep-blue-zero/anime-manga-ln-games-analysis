# Three Days of Happiness — synthesis architecture

## 1. Proportionate design and authority

This is an `INITIAL` architecture for one supplied standalone novel. It adopts the semantic duties of the [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md) without copying a long-series folder tree. The current method, source register and a sectioned notebook suffice for day one. Future readings preserve full arguments; the notebook supplies cross-unit retrieval, never an abbreviated substitute for them.

No actual chapter structure, cast, thematic thesis or developmental arc is assumed. Source-unit mapping precedes a reading schedule. Architecture review and a later bounded run authorization are separate; no owner approval or narrative admission is claimed here.

## 2. Artifact responsibilities and homes

| Artifact | Governing responsibility | Creation / status |
|---|---|---|
| `TDH_SOURCE_REGISTER.md` | What exact witness exists, which units are admitted/covered, and how locators resolve | Required now; inventoried, unadmitted |
| `TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md` | Cross-unit state, claim history, form/register retrieval and readiness, in separate typed sections | Required now; no analytical observations |
| `readings/TDH_U<ordinal>_DEEP_READING.md` | Full source-facing argument for a mapped unit, with coverage receipt and ledger transaction | Future authorized reading only; no empty folder |
| `checkpoints/TDH_<boundary>_CHECKPOINT.md` | Frozen state at a genuine pause or major interpretive boundary | Only if a multi-session boundary needs durable freeze; no arbitrary unit quota |
| `studies/TDH_<subject>_<scope>_MONOGRAPH.md` | A sustained character or relationship question, integrating evidence rather than repeating chronology | Earned by an independent question and evidence across contexts |
| `studies/TDH_<question>_STUDY.md` | A formal, linguistic or thematic question not adequately answered by the integrated reading | Optional, evidence-earned, one primary question per home |
| `synthesis/TDH_NOVEL_SYNTHESIS.md` | Continuous argument about the whole admitted novel, integrating causality, perspective, form, character and counterreadings | Mandatory eventual destination after completion audit |
| `audits/TDH_<boundary>_AUDIT.md` | Coverage, unresolved claims, authority and retrieval checks; full handoff inventory when transferring work | At an actual milestone, not empty day-one apparatus |

Reading order for an analyst starts at the current entrypoint and current source/state records, then the needed full readings. Production order is source mapping → authorized units with state transactions → completion audit → reconciliation → necessary studies → integrated novel synthesis. A future public reader guide is produced last; it cannot become an alternative working entrypoint.

## 3. Responsibility matrix

| Recurring dimension | Sequential capture | Cumulative home | Final destination / trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative causality and disclosure order | Coherent event argument in each reading | Notebook State, with event and information anchors | Novel synthesis; retain rival causal accounts |
| Development, motive and self-presentation | Named actor, action, inference and counterexample | Notebook State, character rows | Subject study if independent question; otherwise novel synthesis |
| Directional relationship and knowledge asymmetry | A→B and B→A separately | Notebook State, relationship and epistemic rows | Relationship study only if distinct burden; synthesis always accounts for material changes |
| Voice, register, address and narration | Original-language anchors and context | Notebook Form/register | Form or subject study if recurring argument; otherwise direct synthesis |
| Ordinary life and non-crisis conduct | Small behavior and actual opportunities | Notebook State plus Form/register as appropriate | Character account; cannot be discarded for lack of plot impact |
| Motif, rhythm, focal distance and illustrative framing | Exact inspected textual/image evidence | Notebook Form/register | Integrated formal argument, or separate study if earned |
| Claims, competing readings, predictions and ethical judgments | Explicit reasoning and falsification | Notebook Claims | Reconciled synthesis with unresolved alternatives visible |
| Social/institutional constraints, if recurring | Source-established roles and consequences | Notebook State typed context rows initially | Promote a dedicated home only if independent retrieval exceeds this compact record |
| Source identity, edition/translation conflict and coverage | Witness and unit receipt | Source register | Scope/method statement and admission review |
| Reconstruction evidence and limits | State-qualified behavior and language | Notebook Readiness links, retaining full evidence in readings | Separate evidence dossier/model/evaluation only after gates |
| Isolated texture without cumulative consequences | Full local argument where useful | Explicitly local-only reading section | No automatic specialist artifact; retain retrievable anchor if later recurrence changes classification |

The notebook is intentionally a broad standalone-work notebook with distinct section contracts, not a narrowly named ledger overloaded with unrelated data. If a dimension earns a separate ledger, migrate full rows with an ID-preserving crosswalk and keep earlier history reachable.

## 4. State, history and contradiction

Every interpretive record has two boundaries: source disclosure/reader horizon and depicted time (known, relative or unresolved). Character knowledge includes acquisition route and may differ from narrator knowledge and analyst inference. Initial continuity is one **unmapped supplied novel witness**, not a claim that its story is temporally linear. Embedded frames or additional continuities must be identified when encountered and receive separate selectors.

States and claims are append-only transitions. A mutable current view can point to the latest valid state; it never deletes earlier formulations. Revision records retain both witnesses and rival interpretations. A changed edition gets a new source alias, and must not relocate existing citations silently. Frozen readings/checkpoints receive linked errata or a superseding generation, preserving original bytes and authority history.

## 5. Character and reconstruction pathway

Character and relationship analysis is a central responsibility even if no independent monograph is earned. A monograph needs a sustained question supported by distinct contexts, temporal transitions and counterevidence; length or protagonist status alone is insufficient. It integrates psychology, action, language, relations, form and function. It cites exact claims/locators and cannot substitute broad reading-file links for evidence.

Reconstruction is downstream and optional per subject, not an automatic deliverable. Use the [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md): preserve named identity, validity period, knowledge limits, interlocutor-conditioned behavior/register, values in conflict, everyday repertoire, unstable contexts, contradictions and negative constraints. Mark insufficient coverage rather than inventing a personality. Sparse evidence in a standalone work may justify only a narrow contextual model or no usable model at all.

The dependency is full readings and claim reconciliation → state-bounded evidence dossier → separate operational model → independent held-out assessment. A literary monograph may use the same evidence but is a distinct artifact. Before fitting a model, reserve unconsumed source units or contexts through a split recorded in the readiness section; if all evidence has already been read, arrange a genuinely independent evaluation boundary and disclose the exposure limitation rather than claim blindness. Do not train on held-out answers.

Label tests of already consumed material `RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST`, never held-out validation. Genuine held-out assessment requires material or evaluator-controlled contexts absent from model construction, with the separation and prior exposure recorded. If no such boundary is available, retain the evaluation gap and a provisional model scope; retrospective success cannot silently close it.

Evaluation covers ordinary behavior, motivational conflicts, directional relationships, information constraints, Japanese voice/register, change across time and adversarial counterexamples. Use qualitative failure analysis, not invented likelihood percentages. Models must abstain outside supported state, language, continuity and relationship scopes. Generated dialogue/scenarios remain labeled synthetic, stored outside literary evidence, and cannot feed back as observations. The designated evaluator alone grades capability in its registry; this project assigns no capability tier and edits no global character output.

## 6. Gates, dependencies and extension

**Bootstrap gate:** method/architecture/source reconciliation and required section contracts exist; current authority is unambiguous; the lock remains closed. **Admission gate:** an authorized bounded operation binds source identity, maps units/locators, resolves material access/legibility gaps, records exposure and review ownership, and obtains explicit run authorization. No successful bootstrap test implies admission.

**Unit gate:** the method's full reading, coverage receipt and all required notebook/source updates form one transaction. An unfinished ledger transaction is an unfinished reading. **Novel coverage gate:** every mapped narrative unit is accounted for; paratext and illustrations have explicit inclusion decisions; gaps and untested claims are enumerated. **Synthesis gate:** load-bearing claims have been returned to source, contradictions reconciled or retained as competing accounts, and independent counterreading completed. No “final chapter reached” shortcut is accepted. **Reconstruction gate:** distinct evidence/model/evaluation responsibilities and held-out limits satisfied; no readiness inferred from literary completion.

After novel coverage, preserve six distinct completion decisions even if their records share one compact audit document:

| Gate | Required decision and evidence | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Each covered unit has its accepted transaction; state/knowledge/directionality, claims, form/register and readiness records agree with full readings; contradictions and missing roles are enumerated | Any claim that cumulative state is settled |
| Character and specialist readiness | Role-gap review checks whose perspective, relationships, ordinary behavior and formal evidence remain underrepresented; determine which independent character, relationship or specialist questions are earned | Starting studies from an incomplete or selectively compressed evidence packet |
| Specialist convergence | Complete earned studies and resolve overlapping responsibilities and conflicting interpretations; if no separate studies are needed, record an evidence-based decision that the novel synthesis can discharge each question directly | Treating optional studies as silently skipped responsibilities |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Freeze the reconciled input inventory, expose remaining disagreements and confirm every recurring dimension has a destination and adequate source route | Drafting the continuous novel argument from summaries alone |
| Final validation | Independent review returns to decisive passages, tests counterreadings, name/reference clarity, coverage and claim-to-source retrieval; defects are repaired or scope-limited openly | Declaring the integrated account complete merely because prose exists |
| Release and freeze | Record exact source and analytical boundary, validated artifacts, authority/supersession metadata, retained limitations and next allowed operation; preserve immutable bytes and append future errata | Calling a provisional synthesis a released or frozen corpus |

The recorded outcome of one gate does not assert the next. Reconstruction assessment remains a separate downstream gate even after a literary release.

New specialist infrastructure requires a recurring dimension with independent accumulation, consequence for later interpretation, no adequate existing home and a retrieval need. Record the question, source evidence motivating the amendment, affected responsibilities and backfill plan. Lifecycle moves from `INITIAL` to `EVOLVING` only with actual reviewed use; stabilization and freezing need a real corpus milestone. Revisions update the entrypoint's routing and preserve prior authority metadata.

## 8. Execution and handoff

Metadata/locator reconciliation is `BOUNDED_STANDARD`; substantive readings and interpretive ledger updates are `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS`; character/reconstruction reconciliation is `DEEP_SYNTHESIS`. Load-bearing architecture and final integration use `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` with independent adversarial review because mistakes contaminate all downstream readings. These classes separate intellectual responsibility from execution surface. Resolve actual model/tool availability at dispatch using live repository routing; no product choice here is permanent.

A future run owner coordinates source admission, review and publication; a reader writes full units plus transactions; an independent reviewer tests source fidelity, counterreadings and compression risk. Substantial synthesis defaults to a fresh synthesis context under repository routing. It receives the complete admitted readings, current and historical notebook records, source/coverage map and unresolved questions, not a summary-only packet. Return-to-source is mandatory for decisive claims. A transfer manifest lists exact files, source boundary and pending obligations; later incorporation verifies full delivered contents and drift before authority promotion. Local checkpoints preserve work if a session ends; they never open the run lock.
