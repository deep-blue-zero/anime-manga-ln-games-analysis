# Three Days of Happiness — analytical method

## 1. Adoption, witness and reading unit

This method adapts the [generic novel method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_NOVEL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) to one supplied Japanese standalone volume. Pair it with [the architecture](TDH_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md); neither artifact permits reading while the entrypoint lock is closed. No narrator, character, plot, theme or ending has yet been established by direct inspection.

Use the Japanese text as semantic anchor once admitted. Translation is a separately identified interpretive witness, never a silent substitute. The source register must bind a content fingerprint and stable internal coordinate map first. Digital display pages are not stable across readers. The small set of reported illustrations requires a still-image lane if any visual claim is made; their number does not establish their content or narrative status.

After authorization, establish chapter and embedded-section boundaries from the admitted witness. Read in its actual order; close a chapter or a coherent causal sequence rather than a fixed word quota. If a long chapter requires several working sessions, retain one cumulative coverage manifest and mark partial work honestly. A short novel still needs full textual encounter: one EPUB is neither one paragraph of synopsis nor proof that one undifferentiated report can preserve all its argument.

## 2. Prospective knowledge discipline

Before each unit, name the exact prior admitted frontier, the reader's available knowledge and each relevant speaker's known or believed facts. Record disclosure order separately from depicted time; a retrospectively narrated earlier event does not give a character later knowledge. Tag recollection, reported speech, hypothetical or dream only when the text warrants that classification. Unknown chronology remains unknown.

Use a prospective first pass. Record later reinterpretations as dated revisions of claims, without rewriting what an earlier reader could reasonably infer. The final return through the whole novel is a separately scoped retrospective operation. Familiarity with the title, adaptations, plot summaries or remembered quotations is quarantined as prior exposure and cannot supply evidence or remove uncertainty. Neither the title nor a closing passage is allowed to predetermine the first pass.

## 3. Factual account and interpretation

Keep four voices distinct: narrator, focalizing consciousness, speaking character and analyst. An utterance establishes that someone said it; it does not automatically establish its truth, motive or the work's endorsement. Separate `OBSERVATION`, `INFERENCE`, `HYPOTHESIS` and `VALUE_JUDGMENT`. A narrator's generalization must be tested against represented action, omissions and alternative perspectives before being adopted as an explanation.

For each causal movement, identify initiating conditions, available alternatives, choice or resistance, consequence and the textual connection between them. A temporal sequence alone does not prove causation. Explain how language, framing or withheld information changes the reader's judgment. Preserve a coherent narrative account alongside interpretation so a later reader can evaluate what the argument actually explains.

Every load-bearing claim has a precise source anchor, an explicit reasoning chain, a credible rival or disconfirming condition, scope and uncertainty. Distinguish a merely possible rival from one supported by text. A claim can remain `OPEN`; avoiding false certainty is better than inventing an unifying explanation. Ethical judgment must specify whose evaluative standard is operating. Depiction, causal explanation, sympathy and endorsement are separate propositions.

## 4. Japanese language, form and viewpoint

Inspect wording where it matters: person-reference, address, omitted subjects, politeness, sentence endings, quotation framing, hesitation, repetition and recipient-conditioned register. Record who speaks to whom, under what social and informational conditions, and why a linguistic choice bears on interpretation. Do not turn an isolated pronoun into a timeless personality label. Distinguish narrator prose from dialogue and narrated interiority; ambiguous attribution stays ambiguous.

Use short original-language anchors and a clearly labeled working translation where needed to make an argument inspectable. Explain load-bearing alternatives in translation rather than silently choosing the one that favors a thesis. Preserve paragraph rhythm, scene transitions, dialogue spacing, repeated phrasing and changes in focal distance when they bear on the reading. A rendered typography claim requires inspection of the actual rendering; plain extraction does not verify it. Image references identify the inspected image and its position, and never substitute for prose evidence.

No word limit governs a reading. Use enough connected argument to substantiate causality, viewpoint, form and counterreadings; avoid repetitive quotation and plot recital. Compression is acceptable for navigation only after the complete argument has a durable home.

## 5. Character, relationships and everyday texture

Track what a named person does, says, believes, chooses and fails to do in the bounded situation. Separate inferred motivation from explicit self-report. Development, immediate stress, social performance, ignorance and habitual disposition are competing explanations, not synonyms. Record a genuine change relative to a prior state and retain the evidence for both states.

Relationship evidence is directional: A's trust, expectation or dependence toward B does not establish B's attitude toward A. Identify participants and asymmetry, the occasion, and the informational route. A narrator's account of another person has a perspective boundary. Avoid labels such as “the relationship deepens” without specifying what changed, for whom and why.

Ordinary behavior, humor, preferences, small initiatives, repair, annoyance and silence may constrain a character account as strongly as a crisis. Capture them during reading; do not mine only dramatic passages later. Also record situations in which an apparently established disposition does not appear, while distinguishing a real counterexample from an unobserved opportunity. Empty source coverage cannot support “never.”

## 6. Evidence routing and revision

Use `TDH-JP-01::<spine-href>#<stable-fragment-or-paragraph>::<chapter/section>` after binding that witness in the source register. Paragraph indices are one-based within a named XHTML file and are derived from the recorded normalization version; they do not renumber silently. Printed page numbers, if actually present, are an additional coordinate. Illustrations use the exact resource member and associated spine position. Until mapped, a locator is `UNRESOLVED`, not a guessed page.

Claim IDs `TDH-Cnnn` and state IDs `TDH-Snnn` route from synthesis to [the notebook](TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md), to a stable reading heading and then to the source locator. Distributed claims retain each necessary anchor; one “representative example” cannot certify an unexamined universal. Quote only the limited text necessary to explain the claim; complete text and media stay in the governed evidence plane.

Use `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` or `OPEN` on claim transitions. Retain original formulation, boundary and reasoning. Classify a conflict as attribution, information-state change, unreliable report, source/edition difference, interpretation, or unresolved textual inconsistency before trying to reconcile it. Do not smooth a contradiction to make the work or character easier to model.

## 7. Future reading acceptance

A finished reading contains: witness and exact coverage receipt; entering state and exposure boundary; coherent factual reconstruction; sustained interpretive argument with language/form evidence; named character and directional relationship changes; ordinary-life evidence or honest coverage limit; counterreadings; claim transitions; exiting state; and a transaction listing source, state, claim, form/register and readiness updates. Use stable headings rather than conversational references. Record each required home as updated or “reviewed, no supported change,” with reasons where absence matters.

Acceptance requires full unit coverage and return to each load-bearing anchor. Unread text, extraction damage or an uninspected illustration that bears on a claim remains an explicit gap. A reading can be provisional while a bounded gap is repaired, but the affected completion claim and downstream synthesis remain blocked. A summary, evidence table or handoff memo alone never satisfies the reading contract.

Before semantic acceptance, sample the introduction, a substantial middle passage and the exit synthesis for identifiable named subjects, clear pronouns, purposeful role labels, correct speaker/thinker/recipient attribution and preserved source concealment. Extend the sample to every major relationship where confusion is possible. Record the sampled scope and classify material findings under handoff §15.6 as `JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE`, `SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY`, `KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED`, `UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION`, `AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT` or `INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION`. Repair only evidence-supported defects; counts or automatic name substitution do not certify referential clarity.

At the end of the novel, audit all units and unresolved claims before a retrospective synthesis. Reconsider early claims using later evidence in a new layer, while retaining their original knowledge boundaries. The standalone design has no artificial multi-volume tranche requirement; pauses instead follow mapped chapters and causal transitions.

## 8. Reconstruction boundary

Future reconstruction is an intended derived use where a subject earns sufficient evidence; no subject is currently selected or ready. Capture state, interlocutor, language/register, behavior, contradictory contexts and negative constraints during the first pass. Do not manufacture a dossier from familiarity or treat a monograph as an operational model. [Architecture §5](TDH_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#5-character-and-reconstruction-pathway) defines the evidence-to-model and independent evaluation gates. Synthetic output is never literary evidence.
