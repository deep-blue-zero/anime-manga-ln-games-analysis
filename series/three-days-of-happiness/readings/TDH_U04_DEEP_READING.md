## 3. Current-unit story synopsis

**A call.** The narrator opens by announcing that from here his foolishness "accelerates" (P0004). Kusunoki tells Miyagi he is only making a phone call and steps outside so that she will not hear. She follows him anyway (P0005). It is the first call he has initiated in a long time (久しぶり). He stares at the name 「ワカナ」 on his phone while insects sing in the wood behind the apartment (P0006–P0007). He is apparently nervous. Since childhood, he reflects, he has never invited anyone or sought conversation. That cost him many possibilities and spared him as much bother, and he feels neither regret nor satisfaction (P0008). He presses the call button in the blank second after stopping himself thinking. Only as it rings does it occur to him that she might not answer. After eight rings he gives up, partly relieved (P0009–P0011).

**Who Wakana is.** Wakana is a female junior at his university. He had meant to invite her to a meal and, if things went well, to have her stay by his side until his life ran out (P0012). With the end of his life now definite, the first change he felt was a loneliness unthinkable for his former self: an urgent need to talk with someone (P0013). She was, he says, the only person at university who had shown him goodwill (P0014).

They met this spring at the secondhand bookshop of chapter 2, when she had just entered university. He glared at her to get out of his way. She took the glare to mean they had met before, and asked so. He said no. She recovered with a smile: "So we met in this bookshop, then? How lovely" (P0014–P0020). They met again at university a few days later, had lunch several times and talked at length about books and music, skipping lectures (P0021). She said he was the first person her age she had met who read more than she did. He answered that reading gave him nothing, like soup poured from a pot into a small dish that overflows so nothing stays. She replied that what one reads remains somewhere and helps without one knowing (P0022–P0024). When he called heavy reading unhealthy, something for people with nothing else to do, she asked whether he had nothing to do. "Nothing except my part-time job," he said. She smiled openly, said she would "give him more things to do," poked his shoulder, and entered her own number and address in his phone (P0025–P0027).

Had he known then that Himeno had already been through pregnancy, marriage, childbirth and divorce, and had forgotten him, he reflects, he would have made more eyes at Wakana. That is his hardened retelling of Miyagi's report. But in spring he still cherished the promise with Himeno and was resolved to stay "unsold" until twenty. So he never contacted Wakana himself, and kept her messages and calls brief, so as not to raise her hopes (P0028). "I was always a hopelessly ill-timed person" (P0029).

**The bounce.** Unwilling to leave a voicemail, he carefully drafts an email: 「突然で悪いけど、明日、どこか行かないか」 ("Sorry it's sudden, but want to go somewhere tomorrow?") (P0032). A reply comes at once, and for a moment he is comforted that someone still cares (P0033). Then he realizes it is not from Wakana but a delivery-failure notice. He takes it that she has changed her address without telling him, judging there was no need to stay in contact (P0034–P0036). It could be her oversight, he grants, and a notice might still come. But he feels sure: the deadline had long passed (P0037–P0039).

**The answer check.** Seeing him stare at the phone, Miyagi seems to grasp what has happened. She comes over and looks: 「さて、答えあわせといきましょう」, "Well then, let's check the answers" (P0040–P0041). In what he hears as a tone as detached as if he were not there, she tells him several things (P0044–P0045):
- Wakana was his last hope, the last person who might have loved him.
- Had he responded in spring, they would by now, she thinks, be compatible lovers.
- His lifespan's value would probably not have fallen so low.
- But it is "a little late."
- Wakana no longer cares about him; indeed she somewhat resents him for not returning her goodwill, and wants to show him the boyfriend she recently found.

「以後、あなたを好きになろうとしてくれる人は、〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕」: no one who would try to like him will *ever again* appear. People see through it more than one thinks, she adds, that *he* regards others only as tools to fill his loneliness (P0046). From the window of the next apartment come the voices of several university students partying. Their light looks far brighter than his. Once he would not have cared; now it stings (P0047).

At the worst moment his phone rings: Wakana returning his call. He thinks of ignoring it, but answers so as not to be called again (P0048). She asks whether he called, and whether something is wrong (「どうかしたんですか？」, P0049). Perhaps she speaks as usual, he thinks, but after what he has just been told her voice sounds reproachful to him (P0050). With forced cheer he says he called by mistake. She laughs: of course, he's not the type to call people himself. He hears mockery in it, as if she were saying that was why she gave up on him. He agrees, thanks her for calling back and hangs up (P0051–P0055). The party next door grows louder (P0056).

**Night supermarket.** Not wanting to go back in, he smokes two cigarettes. He then goes to a nearby supermarket and buys a six-pack of beer, fried chicken and cup ramen. This, the narrator says, is the first time he touches the ¥300,000 from his lifespan. He had meant to splurge but has no idea what luxury would be (P0059). Miyagi fills her basket with calorie bars and mineral water. He cannot picture her actually eating them; eating, the most primal human act, does not suit someone so lacking in human warmth (P0060). He privately muses that they look like a cohabiting couple shopping together, a foolish but happy illusion he hopes passersby share (P0061). He stresses that he has always found her very existence a nuisance. But he has long secretly envied people who go out in loungewear with a live-in girl to buy food and drink. So shopping at night with a young woman is fun, even for surveillance's sake: 「むなしい幸せ」, "an empty happiness," but he truly feels it (P0062–P0063). Miyagi pays first at the self-checkout, and they walk back together. The party next door continues (P0064).

**Envy and family.** Honestly, he envies the partygoers, a feeling he says he never had before; he used to wonder only what could be so fun. Awareness of death, he says, seems to have normalized the values he had desperately distorted: 「人並みに、人恋しかった」, "like anyone, I longed for people" (P0065–P0067). Most people might turn to family at such a time, he thinks, but family is not warm for everyone. Whatever happens, he will not contact his family in his remaining three months (P0068–P0069). Since childhood, he explains, his younger brother kept taking his parents' love. The brother surpassed Kusunoki in every way: straighter in character, taller and better-looking, never without a girlfriend from twelve to his present nineteen. He attends a better university and pitched at Kōshien in high school. While Kusunoki stalled and even lost abilities, the gap widened (P0070). Love naturally shifted to the brother. Treated by his parents as a failure (失敗作), Kusunoki did not find it unfair. Equal love would have been the unfair thing, and he would have done the same: love the child more worth loving, invest where the return is greater (P0071). The chance of going home to be wrapped in his family's unconditional love is nearly zero, lower than his chance of being welcomed into the party next door (P0072).

**Drinking.** He drinks beer with the fried chicken while the water boils, and is pleasantly drunk by the time the ramen is ready (P0073). He approaches Miyagi, who is writing in her notebook in the corner, and invites her to drink with him. Anyone would have done, he notes, so long as they drank with him (P0074). She declines without looking up: she is working. Asked what she is writing, she says it is a behavioral observation record of him. "Then let me tell you: I'm drunk." "I'd imagine so. You look it." "And not only that: right now I want to drink with you." "I know. You said so just now," she answers wearily (P0075–P0082).

**Ending condition.** It is most likely still the night of day one (§4.9). Kusunoki's call to Wakana ended with his own lie, after she reopened contact. Miyagi has testified that Wakana resents him, that no one who would try to like him will appear again, and that he regards others as tools. He invites Miyagi, who declines because she is working.

## 4. Factual and causal reconstruction

1. **Loneliness as the first felt effect of a definite end.** Kusunoki reports that the first change he felt, once the end became definite, was intense loneliness (人恋しさ, P0013). He then says, hedged (ようだ), that death-awareness "normalized" values he had "desperately distorted" (P0066–P0067). That is his own account. Its scope is the envy of revelers and the longing for company. It is not connected by the text to the ten-year-old's 「世の中に起こることが何もかも嫌なんだ」 (Ch01 P0082). It conflicts with other self-statements in the unit:
   - P0065's 「これまで、こんな気持ちになったことはなかった」 three paragraphs after P0062's long-standing secret envy of couples;
   - P0008's 「特に後悔も満足もない」, reversed within the hour by P0013 and P0028–P0029;
   - P0013's 「真っ先に感じた変化」, loneliness, competing with U03's first-felt relief (Ch03 P0032–P0035) and with the relief of the final three days' solitude (Ch03 P0069).

   His self-accounts look local rather than cumulative (B-U04-06, C-U04-07/20, D1-U04-18/67; `TDH-C020`).
2. **The Wakana history and the promise.** Wakana took the recorded initiatives: the misrecognition (P0015), the "so we met here" (P0018), the "more things to do" and the entry of her details (P0027). Who started the lunches is unstated (P0021). In spring he kept her at a distance. He never contacted her himself and kept her messages and calls brief "so as not to raise her hopes," because he "cherished the promise with Himeno" and meant to stay unsold until twenty (P0028). This is passive cutting-short, not the 「はっきりと拒否した」 of Ch01 P0094. Ch01's phrase may describe other cases (middle and high school), and Wakana declared nothing calling for refusal (C-U04-04). It is the first datable instance of conduct the narrator attributes to fidelity, as self-report. P0008's lifelong non-initiation independently accounts for his never contacting her first. So the fidelity reason is overdetermined for that part, though not for the cutting-short (`TDH-C003` v3).

   In retelling Himeno's history here, the narrator reorders Miyagi's account (妊娠、結婚、出産、離婚; in Miyagi's account Himeno gave birth before marrying). He hardens her ほとんど覚えてません ("barely remembers") into 忘れてしまっている ("has forgotten"), and narrates it all as fact (B-U04-07, C-U04-11, D1-U04-29).
3. **The call's motive, and Miyagi's "tool" charge.**
   - **The motive.** The call is driven by loneliness without a particular object: "I wanted to talk with someone" (P0013). Wakana qualifies because she alone showed him goodwill (P0014); no feeling of his for her is stated. His wishes are phrased in benefactives with himself as beneficiary: いてもらいたかった (P0012), 飲んでくれれば (P0074) (B-U04-03). He invites Miyagi with "anyone would do" (P0074) and enjoys shopping with "a young girl" (P0062). In P0028 Wakana figures as the course he would have taken had he known Himeno's state.
   - **The charge.** Miyagi says he "regards" others as tools (道具くらいにしか見ていない, P0046). His narration offers support for it: P0013, P0062, P0074.
   - **Counters (B-U04-03, C-U04-02, D1-U04-20).** P0012 names a specific person, the one who had shown him goodwill. P0021 records real rapport about books and music. P0028's 「期待させてはいけない」 shows consideration for her. P0074 is a drunk thought about a drinking partner.
   - **Verdict.** The charge is partly supported and contested (INFERENCE). His new longing for company is distinct from goodwill toward a person, which fits Ch03 P0026.
4. **The bounce and its interpretation.** The delivery failure is an observed fact (P0035). That Wakana changed her address is a strong inference. That she did so "without telling him" because she judged contact unnecessary is his inference, told in assertive form (P0036). He himself raises an oversight or a pending notice as rivals (P0037), before choosing certainty (P0038). Because the email bounced, Wakana never received the invitation. She knows only of a missed call, which he then passes off as a misdial, and nothing of his sale. Her callback shows her number still works (D1-U04-13/16/27, C-U04-03). Her prompt callback and 「どうかしたんですか？」 (P0048–P0049) are weak evidence against the claim that she no longer cares. On his own hearing, her words may have been in her usual tone (おそらくいつもと変わらない口調, P0050).
5. **Miyagi's "answer check" as testimony, at graded certainty.** Miyagi's speech moves through five levels (B-U04-01, C-U04-01, D1-U04-15):
   1. A past-tense verdict: Wakana "was" his last hope.
   2. Hedged counterfactuals, marked かもしれなかった, と思います and でしょう: lovers by now; his lifespan's value would not have fallen so far.
   3. Flat assertions about Wakana's present: she no longer cares, somewhat resents him and wants to show off a recent boyfriend.
   4. An absolute, emphasized forecast: 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕.
   5. A character diagnosis (P0044–P0046).

   Softeners such as 「……でも、ちょっと、遅かったですね」 and 「いえ、それどころか」 partly resist the narrator's 「突き放した口調」 (B-U04-17, C-U04-10).

   *Scope (INFERENCE).* The hedging matches the scope she claimed in chapter 3, knowledge of "what might have happened" (Ch03 P0108). The flat present claims and the categorical forecast appear to exceed it. Rivals:
   - Ch03 P0108 covers his life's 周辺 ("surroundings"), which could include Wakana;
   - Ch03 P0118 says her advice is based on "your future";
   - the forecast may cover only his three months;
   - 二度と may be rhetorical.

   All of it is single-source testimony with no stated route, and it carries the credibility questions of `TDH-C015`. Kusunoki makes no reply and challenges none of it, unlike his argument in U03 (C-U04-15).

   *Lifespan value (INFERENCE).* 「寿命の価値がこんなに落ちることもなかった」 is the first link between a specific missed choice and the appraisal. It concerns his lifespan's *price*, not its length, so it does not answer why his remaining life was short (F-U02-f). It implies the value once stood higher. Rival: 落ちる may mean only "be this low" (B-U04-04; `TDH-C018`).

   *Timing.* The sequence reverses chapter 3's. There Miyagi spoke before he could be hurt; here he is hurt first and told afterward (D1-U04-14). Rival: nothing shows she knew whom he would call before P0040. Her Ch03 undertaking was not breached, since Wakana is not a list item and she spoke only after the act. Her intervention follows a template: 手元を覗きこむ, then あなたのことなんて (B-U04-09, C-U04-10).
6. **Wakana's callback, filtered.** Kusunoki hears blame and mockery in Wakana's call. The two bracketed reproaches (P0050, P0053) are his imaginings, not her words. He flags them himself: 先ほどの話のせいか ("perhaps because of what I'd just been told"), 気がした, ように聞こえた. The imagined line at P0053, 「だからこそ、私はあなたを見限ったんですよ」, even addresses him as あなた, Miyagi's form of address. Wakana always says クスノキさん (B-U04-02). Wakana's actual words, 「クスノキさん、自分から人に電話かけるような人じゃありませんもんね」 (P0053), fit friendly teasing as well as dismissal. The text gives no independent access to her intent (OBSERVATION; `OPEN`). He lies that he called by mistake (P0051).
7. **The family account.** For the first time Kusunoki discloses his family, in his account only (P0068–P0072):
   - a younger brother, nineteen, excelling in every way and favored;
   - himself treated by his parents as a failure;
   - his refusal of family contact for his remaining time.

   He frames the parents' preference in market terms, 愛し甲斐 and 投資し甲斐: love and investment go where the return is greater (P0071). Yet he opens with a grievance verb, 「弟に両親の愛情を奪われ続けてきた」, "kept being robbed of my parents' love by my brother" (P0070) (C-U04-06, D1-U04-19). 「何が悪いというのか？」 may be endorsement or bitter irony (B-U04-10).

   *Relation to U02.* The text does not itself link this account to U02's unstated 「親には頼れない事情」 (Ch02 P0007), though the account is compatible with it.

   *Silences.* No brother appears in chapter 1's household scenes. Chapter 1's 「親同士の仲がよく」 described relations between the two households, not parental warmth toward him. How he knows his brother's present life while avoiding the family is not stated.

   *Pattern (INFERENCE).* His acceptance of a low valuation from his parents parallels his calling the shop's verdict "fair" (Ch02 P0171). He did, however, contest Miyagi in U03 (Ch03 P0120).
8. **First spending of the ¥300,000.** The narrator says he first touches the lifespan money at the supermarket, and cannot imagine what luxury would be (P0059). This continues U03's eroded imagination (Ch03 P0079). The statement sits awkwardly with the beer and yakitori bought on the night of the sale, 「もう金に糸目をつける必要はない」 (Ch02 P0192–P0193). Either those were paid from other cash, such as the book and CD proceeds, or the narration is inconsistent (D1-U04-48; `OPEN`, minor).
9. **Chronology.** No day break is marked. The night setting, the neighbors' party and the email's 「明日」 fit a continuation of the night of day one, after the U03 list (B-U04-15, C-U04-21, D1-U04-01; INFERENCE, moderate-strong). U03's "day one has ended" is glossed accordingly: day one's daytime had ended, and its night continues here.
10. **Mistiming.** The chapter is thick with timing words:
    - 間抜け (P0004);
    - 間の悪い (P0029);
    - 期限はとっくに過ぎていた (P0039);
    - 遅かった (P0044);
    - 最悪のタイミング (P0048).

    They continue U01's emphasized 〔傍点〕ずれて〔/傍点〕 as a self-diagnosis (B-U04-05).

    *Counterpoint (C-U04-05).* "Ill-timed" recasts a deliberate decision (固く決心, P0028) as a matter of timing. Miyagi places responsibility on his conduct (あなたの方から, P0044), and the narrator's とっくに disagrees with her ちょっと.

    *Rival.* The timing frame may be fair, since he could not have known Himeno's state in spring.

**Not established.**
- Whether ワカナ is a given name or a surname.
- Whether Wakana changed her address deliberately.
- How Miyagi knows Wakana's name and feelings, and whether her account is accurate.
- Whether Miyagi heard the callback.
- Whether he would have told Wakana he is dying (unobserved, not denied).
- Why Naruse goes unconsidered when he wants someone to talk with, and why Wakana was not on the list written hours earlier.
- Whether P0062's 「ずっと疎ましく感じている」 is narrating-present or story-present.
- The brother's name.
- What Miyagi records.

## 5. Narration, focalization and time

**Proleptic self-judgment.** 「ここから俺の間抜けぶりは加速する」 (P0004) opens the chapter from the later narrating instance and pre-evaluates the action as foolish. P0029, 「いつだって俺は、救いがたく間の悪い人間だった」, does the same for the spring.

**Counterfactual regret built on testimony.** P0028's 「…知っていたら、俺はワカナに対してもっと色目を使っていたのだろう」 is the narrator's hypothetical. It treats Miyagi's U03 report, reordered and hardened (§4.2), as fact.

**The narrator's hearing of others.** Wakana's voice and laugh reach the reader as Kusunoki now hears them, as reproach (P0050) and mockery (P0053). He marks that this hearing depends on Miyagi's words. The reading must not convert it into Wakana's attitude.

**Miyagi's speech, quoted.** Miyagi's long speech (P0044, P0046) is quoted directly. The comment on her tone, 「目の前にいる人間について語っているとは思えないほど突き放した口調」 (P0045), is Kusunoki's perception.

**Deadpan close.** The closing exchange (P0075–P0082) ends on dialogue, with no narrative comment beyond 「面倒くさそうに」.

## 6. Japanese language and form

- **Title as speech.** The chapter title 答えあわせといきましょう is spoken by Miyagi at P0041. 答えあわせ ("checking the answers") may echo the 正解 ("correct answer") of the moral lesson (Ch01 P0021, P0032) and of the narrator's 「正解だったのだろう」 (Ch02 P0124). That Miyagi's might-have-beens function as an answer key to his life is a HYPOTHESIS, low to medium (B-U04-08; `TDH-F013`).
- **Sesame-dot emphasis.** 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕 (P0046), "will *never again* appear," puts the chapter's single emphasis on a categorical future negation inside Miyagi's speech. Whether the dots render her stress or the narrator's marking is undecidable. It is the fifth emphasis so far. The earlier four differ in kind: ずれて (a shared self-diagnosis), 桁違い (a scale), 売れ残ってる (dream-Himeno's question) and 例の約束 (a label) (`TDH-F014`).
- **Market idiom in family love.** 「より愛し甲斐がある方を愛し、より投資し甲斐がある方に投資して、何が悪いというのか？」 (P0071) uses 〜甲斐 ("worth the effort / return") in parallel for love and investment. His self-label 失敗作 ("a failed product") makes him a manufactured good (`TDH-F005` recurrence).
- **Wakana's register.** Wakana speaks polite です/ます with playful warmth: 「すてきですね」 (P0020); 「今度、増やしてあげますよ。やること」 (P0027), with inversion and the benefactive あげる; a poke on the shoulder (P0027). She addresses him as クスノキさん (P0026, P0049, P0053). He speaks to her in plain form: 「いや」, 「そういうことになるんだと思う」, 「悪かったな、間違えてかけちまったんだ」 (P0016, P0019, P0051).
- **The soup metaphor.** His image of reading as soup poured from a pot into a small dish that overflows (P0023) presents himself as lacking a 受け皿 ("receiving vessel"). Wakana counters that reading "remains somewhere and helps without one knowing" (P0024). This is the first sustained intellectual exchange the novel reports from his university life, his self-devaluation set against another person's charitable view.
- **Happiness as illusion.** 幸せ appears twice, both times qualified: 「馬鹿げた──けれども幸せな──錯覚」 (P0061) and 「むなしい幸せ」 (P0063). Both attach to an illusion he knows is false. Its relation to the title's 幸福 is a HYPOTHESIS (B-U04-19).
- **人並み.** 「人並みに、人恋しかった」 (P0067) is the second appearance of 人並み. In U02 he valued himself above it (人並以上, Ch02 P0119); here he concedes ordinariness of feeling.
- **Light and sound.** Three details stage his exclusion: the neighbors' window "far brighter" than his (P0047), the noise rising (P0056), and footsteps through the wall (P0064). The staging recalls the shrine steps above the festival (Ch01 P0058).

## 7. Characters

**Kusunoki.**
- **Contact.** He initiates a call for the first time in a long while, nervously (P0006–P0011). He narrates a lifelong habit of never inviting anyone (P0008). In spring he kept Wakana at a distance for the promise (P0028).
- **Feeling.** He narrates acute loneliness (P0013) and envy of partygoers (P0065), framed by himself as normalized values (P0066–P0067).
- **After Miyagi's account.** He acts on it. His hearing of Wakana is colored by it (P0050), though whether he holds it as true is not stated. He answers Wakana's callback with a lie, in forced cheer (P0051–P0052).
- **Family.** He refuses contact. He calls his parents' preference for his brother fair while voicing grievance (P0069–P0072).
- **Spending.** He spends some of the lifespan money but cannot imagine luxury (P0059).
- **Miyagi.** He takes "empty happiness" in a pretend domesticity with her while stating that he has always found her very existence a nuisance (P0061–P0063). Drunk, he invites her to drink, with "anyone would do" (P0074). His speech to her is plain and teasing (P0078, P0080).
- **Ordinary detail.** Cigarettes outside, beer, fried chicken, cup ramen; a phone and a carefully drafted email.

**Wakana (ワカナ).**
