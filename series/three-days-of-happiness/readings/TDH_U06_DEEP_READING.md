He has declined for now the option of selling down to three days, held back by a "devil's-proof hope." He knows Miyagi's hedged distance rule. His list is untouched. Naruse is unidentified.

## 3. Current-unit story synopsis

**Morning: the list examined.** The rain continues into the next morning, giving Kusunoki an excuse not to act at once and time to think (P0004). As he looks over his list, Miyagi comes over and asks how he will spend the day. He braces for bad news, but it seems to be just a question, and she looks down at the list (P0005).

He looks at her again. He has thought since they first met, he says, that her looks are good. In fact, as far as looks go, she is exactly his type (〔傍点〕そのもの〔/傍点〕): cool eyes, melancholy brows, a tightly set mouth, a shapely head, soft-looking hair, nervous fingers, slender white thighs (P0006–P0008). That, he says, is why he has been careful in front of her since she appeared; he cannot even yawn carelessly. With a watcher so much to his taste nearby, he is ashamed of his twisted desires and pathetic wishes. Had the watcher been an ugly, fat, unclean middle-aged person, he could have thought honestly about what he wanted (P0009–P0010).

Miyagi offers a "personal opinion": is the list really what he wants from the bottom of his heart? To her it looks like "things someone other than you would do before dying" (P0011–P0013). He concedes that perhaps there is nothing he wants to do before dying, and that he is imitating someone because he cannot do nothing (P0014). She thinks there is a way more suited to him, and returns to her place (P0015–P0016). He resolves that morning to be more honest about his twisted desires and pathetic wishes, and to spend the last three months more vulgarly, more shamelessly, by instinct. With nothing to lose, there is nothing to dress up (P0017–P0019). He looks at the list again and phones an acquaintance, who answers within a few rings (P0020–P0021).

**The rainbow.** By the time he reaches the station with his umbrella the rain has stopped. He calls it a fitting emblem of his bad timing (P0024). It is his first train trip in a while. In the platform waiting room he buys a cola and drinks it in three gulps; Miyagi drinks mineral water with her eyes closed (P0026). Through the window he sees a faint rainbow. He realizes he had entirely forgotten that rainbows exist (P0027–P0028). With fresh eyes he counts only five colors. Mixing them on an imaginary palette, he finds he has skipped orange and indigo (P0029).

"Yes, you'd better look well," Miyagi says. "It may be the last rainbow you see" (P0030). He agrees, adding that this may also be the last time he uses this waiting room, drinks a cola or throws an empty can. He tosses the can into the bin: anything may be the last, but that was always so, even before he sold his lifespan (P0031–P0033). Still, her words unsettle him. How many more CDs will he hear, how many books read, how many cigarettes smoked? Dying, he thinks, means never again doing anything but going on being dead (P0034–P0037).

**Naruse, and an imperceptible companion.** He is to meet Naruse at a restaurant fifteen minutes by bus from the station (P0040). Naruse was a high-school friend. He was of average height or a little shorter, with a face a bit too deep-featured. He was quick-witted, a charming talker, and liked by others. It seems strange to Kusunoki now that they were close (P0041). They shared one view: most things leave room to be laughed off. In high school they would sit for hours in fast-food places, mocking everyday life almost irreverently (P0042). He wants to laugh everything off like that again; that is his first aim (P0043). His second is that Naruse will misread his relationship with Miyagi in a way convenient to him (P0044–P0046). He admits this is pathetic. But sadly, he says, it is the first thing since the sale that he has clearly wanted to do (P0047).

Miyagi sits next to him on the aisle side of a four-seat table, close, watching him and unbothered when their eyes meet (P0045). He starts to ask her to deflect whatever the man about to arrive might ask, but a smiling waitress interrupts, and he orders coffee. Then he asks Miyagi whether she wants anything (P0048–P0053). Miyagi looks oddly awkward. She tells him he had better not speak to her in front of people. She "meant to have" explained at the start that watchers cannot be perceived by anyone except the person they watch. She tugs the waitress's sleeve, and the waitress shows no reaction (P0054–P0059).

Every sensation she gives people, she explains, is processed as "never having happened." If she lifts a glass, it does not look to others as if it floats, vanishes or stays still. It is simply made "not to have happened." She is perceived neither as present nor as absent. The exception involves the watched person, the only one who can perceive the watcher. "Me as you perceive me" can be erased, but "you perceiving me" cannot. "In short, Mr. Kusunoki, you looked just now as if you were talking to empty space" (P0060). The waitress is looking at him as at a madman (P0061–P0062).

A few minutes later, sipping his coffee, he is thinking of leaving without seeing Naruse. He would have done so had Naruse arrived a few dozen seconds later. Naruse comes in, and Kusunoki beckons (P0063). Naruse greets him effusively, clearly unaware of Miyagi beside him. "Long time! You doing well?" "Oh, absolutely." Not something a man who will die within half a year should say, he thinks (P0064–P0067).

**The one remark.** By the time they have exchanged news, they are talking as in high school. He hardly remembers what about. Their aim is to carve things up through their own grammar, and they laugh at trifles (P0070). He decides not to mention his remaining life. He might not be believed, and he does not want to spoil the occasion. Naruse would grow careful, rein in his jokes and feel obliged to console him (P0071). Until 〔傍点〕その一言〔/傍点〕, "*that one remark*," he thinks, he was enjoying himself (P0072).

"By the way, Kusunoki," Naruse says, as if remembering, "are you still drawing?" "No," he answers at once, then chooses his words: "I stopped completely after I started university." "I thought so," Naruse laughs. "I'd have wondered what to do if you still were" (P0073–P0075). That is the end. In less than ten seconds, the goodwill toward Naruse built over three years vanishes (P0076–P0078). As Naruse keeps up the banter, Kusunoki addresses him silently. 〔傍点〕それだけは〔/傍点〕, *that one thing*, you mustn't laugh at. True, I gave it up. But that is never a reason to laugh at it. I thought you of all people understood (P0079–P0084). His smile becomes a formality. He lights a cigarette and only nods along (P0085).

Miyagi, beside him, says, "Well then, shall we check the answers?" He shakes his head slightly, and she goes on regardless (P0086–P0088). He has probably come to dislike Naruse a little, she says. But Naruse does not like him as much as he thinks either. "In the original course," two years from now they would have met much like today, quarreled over a trifle and parted on bad terms. He should leave early, before that happens: expecting anything of this man will bring nothing good (P0089).

He loses his temper at Miyagi. He says it is not because his friend was insulted, nor because he was told what he did not want to know, nor because of her sarcasm, nor because his anger at Naruse, who had "derided" his former dream, was displaced onto her (P0090). He is not sure why. Everything is suddenly unbearable at once: Naruse chattering in front of him, Miyagi's gloomy talk beside him, two young women talking in near-pure exclamations, theater people expounding behind him, a student group clapping and shouting. 〔傍点〕うるせえよ〔/傍点〕, *shut up*, he thinks. Why can't they be quieter? (P0091–P0093).

The next moment he throws the glass in his hand at the wall on Miyagi's side (P0094). It shatters loudly. The restaurant goes quiet for an instant, then is noisy again. Naruse stares wide-eyed, staff run over, and Miyagi sighs (P0095). What on earth is he doing, he asks himself (P0096). He leaves several thousand-yen notes on the table and flees (P0097).

**Evening to night.** On the bus back he sees an old batting center, gets off, and hits about three hundred balls until his hands are numb and bloody and he is drenched in sweat (P0098). He drinks a Pocari Sweat and watches men who seem to be on their way home from work swinging bats. Perhaps because of the lighting, the tints of things look oddly bluish (P0099). He does not regret parting with Naruse that way. He now doubts that he ever liked Naruse, rather than himself, through Naruse's affirming him (P0100). Naruse, who changed, and he, who could not; Naruse is probably the one in the right (P0101–P0102).

On a train full of high-school students coming back from club activities he feels suddenly old (P0103). At night he stops at a convenience store. Large moths sit motionless in the parking lot, and a male and a female university student in tracksuits and sandals are buying much what he is buying, beer and snacks (P0104). At home he eats canned yakiniku warmed with green onion and drinks beer. Thinking of how many more liters he can drink before dying makes the beer taste better (P0105).

He apologizes to Miyagi. He was confused, he says; sometimes he flares up and acts like that. "Yes, I know," she says. He sees something like wariness in her eyes, and thinks it understandable (P0106–P0107). "Are you hurt?" "No. Unfortunately." "I really am sorry." "It's all right. It didn't hit me" (P0108–P0111). He invites her to drink once she finishes her observation record. "…You mean you want to drink with me?" Not the reaction he expected. He decides honesty is best: "Sure. I'm lonely." "I see. But I'm sorry, I'm working." "Then say so from the start." "Sorry. I just wondered why you would say that." "I get lonely like anyone. The others you've watched must have longed for company before they died too, right?" "I don't remember," she says (P0112–P0119).

After the beer, a hot shower and brushing his teeth, healthy sleepiness comes, thanks to the batting (P0120). Lying down in the dark, he thinks he must revise his view. However near death one is, the world does not suddenly become kind. It is kind, probably, only to those already dead. He must have half expected it to turn kind (P0121–P0123).

**Ending condition.** It is the night of day two. Kusunoki is in bed with the light off, drowsy after the batting center.
- He knows, from Miyagi's statement and her demonstration on the waitress, that no one but the watched person can perceive a watcher.
- The reader has learned, through Naruse's question, that Kusunoki used to draw, that he stopped on entering university, and that he calls it his "former dream."
- His regard for Naruse ended, on his side, when Naruse laughed at the thought that he might still be drawing.
- After Miyagi's second "answer check" he threw a glass at the wall on her side and fled. Miyagi has testified that Naruse does not like him as much as he thinks.
- He apologized to her, and she declined his invitation to drink.
- He has concluded that the world does not grow kind to the dying.

## 4. Factual and causal reconstruction

1. **Attraction and self-censorship.** Kusunoki states plainly, for the first time, that Miyagi's looks are exactly his type. He dates the impression to their first meeting (初めて会ったときからずっと思っていた, P0007–P0008). He explains his self-consciousness since she appeared by this attraction (P0009–P0010). The attraction is OBSERVATION, his self-report. It is a delayed disclosure. Through U03–U05 it coexisted with "machine-like" (Ch03 P0077), "lacking human warmth" (Ch04 P0060) and "a nuisance all along" (Ch04 P0062) (B-U06-12, C-U06-09). That it also explains his U05 restraint before the fireworks (Ch05 P0039–P0040) is the analyst's INFERENCE (moderate-strong); he does not refer to that scene. His comparison with an "ugly, fat, unclean middle-aged" watcher (P0010) is a hypothetical foil, uncharitable in its typing.
2. **The list as imitation, and the first want.** Miyagi's opinion is that the list looks like "what someone other than you would do before dying" (P0013). He accepts it and adds that perhaps he wants nothing (P0014). She mentions "a way more suited to you" (P0015). It is left unexplained, and he does not ask. This is his third decision not to pursue something about her (Ch03 P0128, Ch05 P0080; C-U06-14).

   The phrase 「歪んだ欲望や情けない願望」 ("twisted desires and pathetic wishes") appears at P0010 and again at P0018. It ties his shame before Miyagi to the morning resolve (D1-U06-05). The resolve's first concrete want is that Naruse misread his relationship with Miyagi "in a direction convenient to me" (P0046). Most naturally that means as a girlfriend (INFERENCE). It is a performance before another person, so it turns the resolve against 取り繕う (dressing things up) into managing appearances (C-U06-05). It echoes his wish that passersby share the "couple" illusion (Ch04 P0061). It is defeated before Naruse arrives, by imperceptibility (§4.3).

   His claim that this was the first thing he "clearly wanted" since the sale (P0047) sits against earlier wants: Wakana by his side (Ch04 P0012–P0013), a drinking partner (Ch04 P0074), watching the fireworks (Ch05 P0039) (D1-U06-28). The resolve against dressing things up is broken with Naruse: the "absolutely" of P0066, the concealment of P0071, the formal smile of P0085. It is kept only with Miyagi, 「こういうときは正直に答えた方がいいんだろうな」 / 「寂しいから」 (P0114) (B-U06-17, C-U06-10). His apology to her (P0106) is the first sincere apology to her on the page.
3. **Watchers are imperceptible (`TDH-C024`).** Miyagi states that watchers cannot be perceived by anyone except the watched, and demonstrates it on the waitress's sleeve. Every sensation she gives others is processed as "never having happened." The watched person's perceiving of her cannot be erased, so he appears to talk to empty space (P0057–P0062).
   - **Status.** The demonstration, the waitress's look and Naruse's not noticing her are narrated OBSERVATION. They are the first third-party confirmation of any watcher-system claim (B-U06-05, D1-U06-01). The mechanism is Miyagi's testimony.
   - **Nullification, not invisibility.** The rule, voiced in agentless passives (処理される, させられる), is perceptual nullification: a lifted glass neither floats nor vanishes (B-U06-05).
   - **The prior explanation.** She says, hedged, that she explained it "at the start" (一応、最初にも説明したつもりだったんですけど, P0057). Whether she did is undecidable.
   - **Scope.** In U02 he saw and spoke with her as the shop's clerk before he was a watched subject. Whether the rule is bound to her watcher role is open (D1-U06-16/17).
   - **Consistency with earlier scenes (INFERENCE).** The rule is consistent with Miyagi's "please treat me as if I weren't here" (Ch03 P0062), with her paying "first, quickly, at the self-checkout" (Ch04 P0064), and with Kusunoki's wish that passersby share his "couple" illusion (Ch04 P0061), which could not have been met. He is not shown making that connection. The reader learns the rule only now.
   - **The one-way public channel.** In public she can speak freely, while he can only shake his head (P0088; C-U06-29).
4. **The drawing disclosure (`TDH-C025`).** Naruse's question, 「お前、まだ、絵は描いてるのか？」 (P0073), discloses for the first time in the narrative that Kusunoki used to draw. He says he stopped completely after entering university (P0074). The narrator calls it his 「かつての夢」, "former dream" (P0090). The disclosure is delayed: in five chapters of self-portrait the narrator never mentioned it. 絵 leaves the medium unspecified.

   The chapter's attention to color gains retrospective significance: the rainbow's missing hues and the "imaginary palette" (架空のパレット, with a possible pun on 空に架かる, P0029), and the bluish tints at the batting center (P0099). That significance is INFERENCE (moderate). What he drew, how far he pursued it and why he gave it up are not stated. Miyagi's U05 account of the forfeited life never mentioned drawing (B-U06-01).
5. **The break with Naruse.** Three years of goodwill vanish in ten seconds over Naruse's laughing 「まだ描いてたら、どうしようかと思ったよ」 (P0075–P0077).
   - **Laughter or derision.** The narration says only that Naruse laughed (笑った, P0075). "Derided" (嘲笑った) is Kusunoki's later word, presupposed inside a denial (P0090) (B-U06-04, D1-U06-18). Naruse's intent is not given.
   - **The injury.** His silent address, 「それだけは、笑っちゃいけなかったんだ」 (P0081), locates the injury in the laughter at an abandoned dream, not in the abandonment. He calls the dream only それ. He says he 諦めた ("gave it up"), where to Naruse he had said 描かなくなった ("came to stop drawing") (B-U06-01).
   - **An uncommented irony.** Their shared view was that "most" things (大抵の物事) leave room to be laughed off (P0042), while he wanted to laugh off "everything" (すべて, P0043). Now one thing must not be laughed at (D1-U06-35, C-U06-03). That he never told Naruse it was off-limits is an INFERENCE from absence.
   - **A tension with U03.** Three years of 好意 for Naruse (P0077) sit against Ch03 P0026, where he could not feel even 「ほんの僅かな好意」 for anyone he met after Himeno. His retrospective doubt (P0100) partly reconciles them (C-U06-26).
   - **Retrospect.** He doubts that he ever liked Naruse, rather than himself, through Naruse's affirmation (P0100). He sets down 「変わってしまったナルセと、変われなかった俺と」 and judges Naruse "probably" right (P0101–P0102). Whether that verdict is the experiencing self's or the narrator's is undecidable, and what changed is not specified. INFERENCE: 変われなかった ("could not change") answers, at least, his drunk U05 vow 「何かを変えてみせる」 ("I'll change something"; Ch05 P0009). Rival: it refers to his whole failure to adapt, or to the attachment to the abandoned dream.
6. **Miyagi's second "answer check."** Miyagi proposes the check (P0087). He shakes his head (P0088). She goes on regardless (P0089).
   - **Content.** Her claim about his feelings is hedged (ちょっぴり嫌いになったでしょうが). Her claims that Naruse does not like him as much as he thinks, and that "in the original course" (本来であれば) they would have quarreled and parted in two years, are flat (B-U06-11). She advises leaving 「そうならないうちに」, "before that happens" (`TDH-C026`).
   - **Understatement.** Her "a little" understates the narrator's "my goodwill vanished" (D1-U06-03).
   - **Timing.** She spoke only after the hurt, which fits her U03 undertaking not to interfere with his other list items (Ch03 P0126). Whether she kept or strained that promise is contestable (C-U06-04, D1-U06-32).
   - **Three deflations.** She has now deflated the regard of three people from his past: Himeno, Wakana and Naruse (C-U06-04).
   - **A trajectory of consent.** The answer checks run from contest (Ch03 P0120) to request (Ch05 P0026) to refusal over which she continues (P0088) (C-U06-04, D1-U06-69).
   - **Thematic parallel to the cancelled quarrel.** Kusunoki’s reproach to Naruse is silent. Kusunoki describes losing his temper toward Miyagi and thinks an insult; he throws the glass at the wall on her side before leaving (P0079–P0097). The encounter resembles the estrangement she reports but does not realize the predicted spoken quarrel with Naruse. 「そうならないうちに」 presupposes that the outcome is still possible, in tension with "can never happen" (Ch05 P0028). INFERENCE: this is the second instance, after U05's drink and music, of the counterfactual echoed in altered form. It bears on `TDH-C017`'s "the future is not fixed." Rival: leaving early would have avoided it, and the trigger differs (B-U06-36, C-U06-08).
   - **Tense.** The Naruse quarrel is past-tense under 本来であれば (P0089). Himeno's suicide, also two years ahead, was non-past (Ch03 P0101). This is weak evidence, with rivals, that Himeno's death is still "on schedule" in Miyagi's usage. Rivals: she also narrated his own cancelled future in the non-past (Ch05 P0052, P0059), and only P0089 carries 本来であれば (B-U06-10; `TDH-C015`). Her 「何もいいことはありません」 negates the いいこと of his hope formula (B-U06-37).
7. **The glass.** Kusunoki throws a glass at the wall on Miyagi's side, and it does not hit her (P0094, P0111).
   - **His account.** The narrator says his temper was directed at Miyagi (ミヤギに対して癇癪を起こした). He denies four specific motives (P0090) and offers a sudden intolerance of noise (P0091–P0093). 〔傍点〕うるせえよ〔/傍点〕 is thought, not spoken (D1-U06-19/20).
   - **Rivals.** The evidence of his headshake refusal, the direction of the throw and the apology to her alone bears on anger at Miyagi herself. The narrator affirms that anger, and it bears on the denied "unwanted knowledge" motive. Anger displaced from Naruse is raised by the narrator only to be denied. It remains a live INFERENCE from sequence (injury, then Miyagi's commentary, then the throw), but no specific evidence singles it out (C-U06-06, D-U06-07).
   - **A second account.** His spoken excuse to Miyagi, that he was "confused" and "flares up sometimes" (P0106), differs from the narrated one, noise (B-U06-04). Its closest structural parallel is Ch02 P0163's 「…のは…からではなく」.
   - **Consequence under the rules.** By day's end no consequence has followed, and no report is narrated. He is not shown weighing the risk (C-U06-07). By the relayed rules, 不適切な行動 ("inappropriate behavior") reported to headquarters would end his life (Ch03 P0067). The act caused others trouble: shattered glass, staff, customers. Rival explanations: Miyagi did not report it; it did not count as inappropriate behavior; or the rules are not as relayed (`TDH-C027`).
   - **"I know."** Miyagi's 「ええ。知ってます」 (P0107) may come from her knowledge of his life or from what she has just seen; compare Ch04 P0081, where 知ってます referred to what she had just heard. INFERENCE, undecided.
8. **Batting and blood.** Three hundred balls until his hands are numb and bloody (P0098) is the chapter's physical aftermath. The exertion produces "healthy sleepiness" (P0120).
9. **Counting what remains: dread, then relish.** The same counting of what remains produces dread in the morning: CDs, books and cigarettes (P0035–P0036). At night it produces relish: liters of beer (P0105). It reverses within one day (B-U06-20, C-U06-17, D1-U06-74). That is the clearest instance so far of the "small, certain happiness" Miyagi recommended (Ch05 P0025).
10. **The ending's revision.** The morning resolve, 「わかっているはずなのに」 (P0019), is answered by the night revision: 「どうやら認識を改める必要があるみたいだな」 (P0122), with 「わかりきっていたはずなのに」 (P0123). The night line reuses Miyagi's word 認識 and the same "ought to have known" construction (B-U06-09). It revises only his expectation that the world would turn kind as death nears. INFERENCE (moderate): U05's hope is recast as that expectation and hedged rather than cancelled. Rival (B): P0123 may concern only interpersonal kindness. The ending does not name the hope or the option of selling down, and he decides nothing (D1-U06-41).
11. **"I don't remember."** Asked whether the others she has watched grew lonely before death, Miyagi answers 「覚えていません」 (P0119). The reply neither confirms nor denies prior subjects. It sits uneasily with her U05 recall of "about five" similar declarations (Ch05 P0019) (C-U06-11). Evasion, privacy and literal forgetting are all possible.

## 5. Narration, focalization and time

**Delayed disclosure.** The narrator withheld two things until now. The drawing surfaces through another character's question (P0073), and its label, 「かつての夢」 (P0090), arrives only inside a list of denied motives. The attraction to Miyagi, dated to their first meeting, is disclosed only at P0007–P0008 (B-U06-12). This parallels U03's delayed watcher rules and this unit's delayed imperceptibility rule. In each case the narration releases, at the point of consequence, facts the experiencing self had or should have had.

**Focalization and disclosure order.** At P0051 the narration keeps the experiencing self's pre-disclosure belief that the waitress smiled at "us" (俺たち) (B-U06-06, D1-U06-15). Miyagi's 「そうですね」 (P0030) answers his look at the rainbow. It does not establish access to his inner attitudes (B-U06-23).

## 6. Japanese language and form

- **Sesame-dot emphasis, four times.** The four emphases are 〔傍点〕そのもの〔/傍点〕 (P0008), 〔傍点〕その一言〔/傍点〕 (P0072), 〔傍点〕それだけは〔/傍点〕 (P0081) and 〔傍点〕うるせえよ〔/傍点〕 (P0092), the last in rough register. That brings the running count to ten. U04–U05's two emphases sat in Miyagi's verdicts; all four here fall on his own affect and judgment (`TDH-F017`).
- **Miyagi's exposition of nullification.** Three formulations carry it: 「『なかったこと』として処理されるんです」, 「『いる』ことを知覚されないどころか、『いない』ことさえ知覚されない」, and 「『あなたに認識されている私』そのものは『なかったこと』にできても、『私を認識しているあなた』だけは、『なかったこと』にできない」 (P0060). Her bracketed abstractions give the rule a quasi-logical form (`TDH-F018`; the label rests partly on a participant whose independence on it was compromised, so it carries low weight).
- **The title's verbs.** 変わってしまった人 uses the regretful completive 〜てしまう, "has (sadly) changed." 変われなかった人 uses the potential negative, "could not change." P0101 places Naruse and Kusunoki in the two slots, and P0102 sides with Naruse, hedged (多分). The voice of the verdict is undecidable (§4.5).
- **Last things.** 「最後に見る虹」 (P0030) prompts a list of possible lasts: waiting room, cola, can. The list widens to CDs, books and cigarettes (P0035), and then to the aphorism 「死ぬというのは、死に続ける以外のことすべて、二度とできなくなるということなのだ」 (P0037). 二度と echoes Miyagi's emphasized U04 term (Ch04 P0046) (INFERENCE, low). At night the counting turns to relish (P0105; §4.9).
- **Color.** The rainbow's five colors and two missing hues on the imaginary palette (P0029), and the bluish tints (P0099), are visual attention that the drawing disclosure (P0073) recasts as that of someone who drew (INFERENCE, moderate; §4.4).
- **Registers.**
  - Kusunoki → Miyagi: 「監視員さん」 (P0048, P0106) and plain speech.
  - Miyagi: polite throughout. Her 「残念ながら」 (P0109) is unglossed, and its tone is open.
  - Kusunoki → Naruse: 「ああ、そりゃもう」 (P0066).
  - Naruse → Kusunoki: 「クスノキ」 and お前 (P0073), the first bare-name, お前-style peer address in present-day speech (B-U06-38).

## 11. Competing interpretations, claims and forecasts

**Interpretation A: the dream as the hidden center.** The drawing, withheld for five chapters and disclosed by Naruse's question, is the "special self" whose loss underlies everything. On this reading it underlies his sense of being exceptional (U01–U02), the forfeited life's "once special self" (Ch05 P0052) and his inability to "change." Support: P0073–P0084, P0090, P0101. Weakeners: the text says almost nothing yet about the dream's content, and Ch02 P0120 says his specialness 「何かに支えられていたわけではない。ただ過去の栄光を引きずっていただけだ」, "was not supported by anything; I was only dragging past glory."

**Interpretation B: the injury as self-love.** By his own later account, his goodwill toward Naruse may have been self-love through an affirming friend (P0100). The break exposes how his relations depend on being mirrored, consistent with Miyagi's U04 charge (Ch04 P0046). Rival: the injury is real and specific, laughter at a renounced dream (P0081–P0083).

**Interpretation C: imperceptibility as the frame of his isolation.** That no one else can perceive his only constant companion literalizes his isolation. In public he appears alone, or mad (P0060–P0062). HYPOTHESIS. Support is so far this one scene.

## 12. Exiting state

It is the night of day two of the three months. Kusunoki is in bed with the light off, with a "healthy" drowsiness he credits to the batting center (P0120–P0121).

**The list and the morning resolve.**
- Miyagi offered, as her "personal opinion," that his list looks like what "someone other than you" would do before dying. He conceded that perhaps there is nothing he wants to do, and that he is imitating someone. Her remark that there is "a way more suited to you" was left unexplained, and he did not ask (P0011–P0016).
- He resolved to be more honest about his "distorted desires and pathetic wishes" (P0018). That is the phrase he had just used for what he is ashamed of in front of Miyagi, whose looks, he says, have been exactly his type since they met (P0007–P0010).
- He phoned an acquaintance (unnamed at P0020; Naruse by strong inference from P0040). List item 7, meeting and talking with Naruse, has been carried out: the first item carried out, and it ended in rupture. The text links no other act to the list (the rainbow, the beer). Items 6 (the will) and 8 (Himeno) are untouched.

**Naruse.**
- Naruse is a high-school friend. Kusunoki had two aims in meeting him. One was to laugh things off together again. The other was to have Naruse misread his relationship with Miyagi "in a direction convenient to me" (most naturally, as a girlfriend: INFERENCE). He called this second wish pathetic and "the first thing I clearly wanted" since the sale. Earlier wants sit against that claim (Ch04 P0012–P0013, P0074; Ch05 P0039).
- He told Naruse nothing of the sale and said he was well. Naruse does not know of the sale, did not perceive Miyagi, and saw the throw.
- Naruse asked whether he still draws. He said he had stopped entirely after entering university. Naruse laughed (笑った): "I thought so. I'd have wondered what to do if you still were." Naruse's intent is not given; "derided" (嘲笑った) is Kusunoki's later word (P0090). By his account, three years of goodwill vanished in under ten seconds. His reproach to Naruse went entirely unspoken.
- This is the reader's first knowledge that Kusunoki drew and that he calls it "my former dream." What he drew, how far he pursued it, and why he gave it up are not stated.

**Miyagi's imperceptibility.**
- Miyagi told him that watchers cannot be perceived by anyone except the person they watch. What she does to others is processed as "never having happened," and she is perceived neither as present nor as absent. The waitress's non-reaction to her tug and Naruse's not noticing her are narrated; the mechanism is her testimony. To others, he appeared to be talking to empty space.
- She said she "meant to have" explained this at the start. Whether she did is undecidable. He saw and spoke with her at the shop before he was a watched subject (Ch02), so whether the rule is bound to her watcher role is open.
- His earlier wish that passersby would take them for a couple (Ch04 P0061) could not have been met. He is not shown making that connection.

**The answer check and the glass.**
- After the drawing remark, Miyagi proposed a second "answer check." He shook his head, and she went on. She said:
  - he has probably come to dislike Naruse "a little" (hedged, でしょう);
  - Naruse does not like him as much as he thinks;
  - "in the original course" they would have met in two years, quarreled over a trifle and parted.

  She advised him to leave early. All of this is single-source testimony. Naruse's warm greeting and their easy talk are the only counter-indications.
- He lost his temper "at Miyagi." The narrating self denies four motives, says it is at a loss to say what he was angry at, and offers the din of the restaurant instead. 「うるせえよ」 was thought, not said. He threw his glass at the wall on Miyagi's side; it did not hit her (P0111). He left several ¥1,000 notes and fled.
- He is alive at the end of the day. No report is narrated, and he is not shown weighing the risk. Whether the act was reported, or fell short of the relayed "inappropriate behavior" (Ch03 P0067), is not stated.

**The night.**
- At a batting center he hit about three hundred balls, until his hands were numb and bloody. He found he did not regret the parting. He doubted he had ever liked Naruse rather than himself, through Naruse's affirmation. He set down "Naruse, who had changed, and I, who could not," with Naruse "probably" in the right. Whether that verdict belongs to the experiencing self or the narrator is undecidable, and what changed and what Naruse is right about are not specified.
- Counting how many more liters he could drink made the beer taste better. That morning, the same counting of CDs, books and cigarettes had filled him with dread.
- He apologized to Miyagi, saying he had been confused and sometimes flares up. She said, "Yes. I know." Whether that comes from her knowledge of his life or from what she saw is not stated. He read wariness in her. She said she was not hurt, "unfortunately," and that it had not hit her.
- He invited her to drink "because I'm lonely." She asked whether he meant with her, declined because she is working, and said she had wondered why he would say that. Asked whether the others she has watched grew lonely before death, she said, "I don't remember." The reply neither confirms nor denies prior subjects. It sits uneasily with her recall of "about five" similar declarations (Ch05 P0019).
- In bed he concluded that he must revise his view. The world does not suddenly become kind because death is near; it is kind, "probably," only to those already dead. This revises an expectation he finds in himself. It does not name or revoke U05's devil's-proof hope, and he decides nothing.
