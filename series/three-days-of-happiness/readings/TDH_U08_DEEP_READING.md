# U08 — chapter 8 「不適切な行動」

**Content note.** This chapter depicts an intended sexual assault, begun with a touch and abandoned. It recounts domestic violence, a mother's rejection and abandonment of her child, and that mother's killing. A child is coerced over a debt, and there is talk of whether to go on living. This reading describes these events plainly and briefly.

## 3. Current-unit story synopsis

**Before the first train.** Kusunoki wakes a few hours before the first train and drinks an energy drink from a vending machine. His whole body aches. It is still dim; morning higurashi, crows and turtledoves are calling (P0004). Back inside, he sees Miyagi stretching where she sits. To him it looks like the most human of all the gestures of hers he has seen (P0005). Perhaps because of the humid night, she has taken off her summer cardigan and laid it on her lap, leaving her slender white shoulders bare (P0006).

**The impulse.** "Probably," the narrator thinks, he was confused. The cause might be his three months left, repeated disappointments, his sleepy head, fatigue and pain, or that he liked Miyagi's looks more than he realized (P0007). Whatever the reason, he suddenly wants to do something cruel to her. Put more directly, he wants to push her down and use her as an outlet for every feeling (P0008). It is the most inappropriate act he can think of, and certain to have his life ended. But, he reasons, it would only bring his death a few months forward, and he had written "don't resist desire" on his list (P0009). He has deliberately kept her outside that kind of desire until now. Once he looks at her that way, she seems to him the most fitting victim. She always seems to keep her guard up, which makes her occasional weakness stand out; perhaps that is why he wants to expose it and make her feel how weak she really is (P0010).

**The question.** He stands in front of her. Sensing something wrong, perhaps, she straightens as if bracing herself (P0011). He asks how much lag there is between a watcher confirming a subject's "inappropriate behavior" and ending his life (P0012–P0014). A wary look comes into her eyes, and she asks why. He says he wants to know how long it would take for him to be killed if he assaulted her here and now (P0015–P0016). She shows no surprise. She looks at him more coldly than ever, as if in contempt (P0017–P0018). The report takes an instant, she says; from there it takes less than twenty minutes, and escape is absolutely impossible (P0019). So he would have more than ten minutes to do as he likes, he returns at once. Miyagi looks away and says, in a weak voice, that no one said that (P0020).

**The touch, and the stop.** Silence. Strangely, she does not try to flee; she just stares at her knees (P0021–P0022). He reaches out (P0023). He had expected abuse or a struggle. When his hand touches her bare shoulder, she only stiffens, with what looks to him like a sad face (P0024). He imagines what he is about to do: pull her roughly down, pin her to the floor and act on his desire. She might be injured; another scar might be added to the big one on her knee; the remaining light might be taken from her already dark eyes. Afterward, perhaps, she would say coldly, "…Are you satisfied?" (P0025; imagined, not spoken). Would that satisfy him? What is he trying to do? (P0026–P0027). His excitement subsides at once. An intense emptiness wells up in its place: looking at her eyes, which seem utterly resigned, he feels her sadness pass into him (P0028–P0030). He takes his hand away and sits two seats from her, ashamed of his shortsightedness (P0031–P0032).

**After.** It must be hard work, he says, dealing with scum like him. Still looking away, she says she is glad he understands (P0033–P0034). He thinks the ¥300,000 valuation is fair after all; one step more and he would have done something irreparable (P0035). Dangerous work, he says; surely there are quite a few like him, who go mad before death and turn their anger on the watcher? She shakes her head slowly. He is, if anything, an easy case, she says; many went to more extreme lengths. He hears it as said to cover for his fault (P0036–P0039). He has wondered since they met about the large scar on her knee, but stays silent: his sudden concern would only annoy her (P0040).

**Her account.** Instead he asks why she does this job. "Simply put, because I had no choice," she says. He wants the not-simple version. She looks surprised: she had thought he had no interest in anyone but Himeno. Not so, he says; if he did not find her attractive, he would not have done what he just did. "…I see. Thanks, I'm sure," she says, dryly (P0041–P0046). He tells her she need not talk about it. It is not a past worth hiding, she says. Besides lifespan, health and time can be sold, as she has said; she sold time, about thirty years (P0047–P0050). This is what he has long wondered: what selling time means (P0051–P0053). Most watchers, she says, came to that shop as he did and sold time; in the end it was as if they had sold their safety and relationships too. Before that she was an ordinary person, like him (P0054–P0056).

He had vaguely assumed she was born cold, sarcastic and tough. Hearing this, he thinks those traits may have been acquired desperately, in order to survive (P0057). Having sold thirty years, she will be freed at about forty, she agrees, "if I survive until then," with a self-mocking smile (P0058–P0059). It also means, he reflects, that she will remain an invisible person for decades (P0060). Why go so far for money? There are a lot of questions today, she says. It is not an interesting story, she warns; he says it will still be more interesting than his reasons for selling his lifespan. She looks up at the timetable: there is still time before the first train. She begins to speak (P0061–P0066).

Her account, summarized and hedged as she hedges it, runs as follows (P0069, P0072):
- **Her parents.** Her mother sold decades of time to buy lifespan. Miyagi still does not understand why; perhaps it was to keep waiting for Miyagi's father. He had left, she was told, just before Miyagi was born, after leaving lasting scars on the mother's body. If so, Miyagi thinks, anyone would have done: her mother simply had no one else to cling to (すがる).
- **Mother and daughter.** Miyagi disliked her mother, who disliked her too and habitually said she wished she had never given birth to 〔傍点〕こんなの〔/傍点〕, "*this thing*."
- **Abandonment.** When Miyagi was about six, as she remembers it, her mother sold time and vanished from her sight as a watcher. Miyagi then lived for some years at an aunt's, treated as a nuisance there too.

He judges that she is not overcome, and that she probably dislikes how her words seem to invite sympathy (P0070). The rest is told flatly, as if about someone else (P0071):
- **Her mother's death.** When Miyagi was ten, her mother died. The cause is unclear, but that a person she was watching killed her is certain. However far one extends one's lifespan, death by injury or illness is, she was told, another matter.
- **The debt.** A man who came with the news told her that her mother had left a huge debt, since she had died before working off the time she sold. To repay at once, Miyagi could sell lifespan, time or health; otherwise they would choose and collect by force.

So she chose time, he says. Yes, she says: just over thirty years of her time covered the debt, and so she lives as a watcher. The job is dangerous and lonely, she says, but gives deep insight into the value of life and how people live. When she has repaid the debt, she thinks she will live "properly," more than anyone; seen that way, it is not such a bad job (P0073–P0074).

**Two valuations.** She speaks of it, to him, like a salvation (P0075). To him, her life can only be a tragedy (P0076). He would sell such a life off, he says: nothing guarantees she will live to repay it, her mother died, and even if she survives, the best of her life will be over. In her own words, she would then only "have just reached the starting line." Selling her lifespan would be better (P0077). She would have, she says, if her lifespan had been worth an ordinary amount (P0078). How much was it? "The same as yours," she says, as if amused (P0079–P0081). Ten thousand yen a year. The reason she is harder on him than she needs to be, she thinks, is probably that she could not forgive herself for being worth so little; she supposes she sees herself in him somewhere. She apologizes for taking it out on him (P0082).

Then, he says, wouldn't it be better to die quickly, since there is nothing to hope for (P0083)? Yes, she says, exactly. That she does not, she supposes, means she has her mother's blood: she is a hopeless fool who cannot stop trying to survive though there is no point in living, and perhaps she will even die the same way. But it cannot be settled so simply: "something good might happen someday" (P0084). He jokes that he knows a man who kept saying that and was due to die at fifty without gaining a thing (P0085). "…I know him too," Miyagi says, smiling (P0086).

**The light.** Laughing with her, he lights a cigarette. Miyagi stands, takes one of his cigarettes and puts it in her mouth. He holds up the lighter to light it for her, but it seems to have run out of fuel and will not catch (P0087). She points at the cigarette in his mouth and brings her face close; understanding, he does the same (P0088). The tips of the two trembling cigarettes touch, and the fire passes slowly to hers (P0089). Seeing her, for the first time, let the tension go from her shoulders in front of him, he resolves: he will at least be remembered as the watched person she found it easiest to be near (P0090–P0091). He looks across the tracks. The night is about to end (P0092).

**Ending condition.** It is just before dawn on day four, at the home-town station, before the first train.
- Kusunoki began an intended sexual assault on Miyagi and abandoned it. He touched her bare shoulder, then withdrew in emptiness and shame. He calls himself scum, accepts his low valuation, and is alive.
- Miyagi has testified to the lag of the misconduct rule: the report is instantaneous, death follows in under twenty minutes, and there is no escape.
- Miyagi has told him her history, as testimony:
  - her mother bought lifespan by selling time, became a watcher, and was killed by a person she watched;
  - she herself inherited her mother's debt at ten and sold about thirty years of time;
  - her own lifespan was valued, like his, at ¥10,000 a year.
- She has apologized for treating him harshly, explaining it, hedged, as self-reproach and identification with him. They have shared a joke about his forfeited life, and a light she initiated.
- He has resolved, unspoken, to be remembered as the watched person she found easiest to be near.

## 4. Factual and causal reconstruction

1. **The impulse and its stated causes.** The narration hedges its own explanation: 「多分、俺は混乱していたのだと思う」 (P0007). It offers alternative causes joined by かもしれない:
   - three months left;
   - repeated disappointments;
   - sleepiness;
   - fatigue and pain;
   - liking her looks more than he realized (自分で考えている以上に; cf. `TDH-C028`).

   The desire is stated in escalating directness: to do her harm (酷いこと); to push her down (押し倒したく); to use her as an outlet for every feeling (P0008). P0010 calls it "that kind of desire" (その手の欲望) and her the "victim" (被害者). P0025 speaks of 欲望を行使する. Together these establish an intended sexual assault: the euphemisms are OBSERVATION, and "sexual" is a strong INFERENCE from them. He also names 嗜虐心, a sadistic urge, aimed at exposing a weakness he reads in her (P0010). His reasoning calls it 「今の俺が思い付く中ではもっとも不適切な行為」. It accepts certain death as only a few months' loss, and invokes list item 3, 「欲望に逆らわない」 (P0009). INFERENCE: the list's "don't resist desire" becomes a license. This continues the dying man's license of U07 (Ch07 P0073; `TDH-C032`), in its most dangerous form. Further points:
   - **Dropped limit.** It drops the limit Miyagi set on the list, 「他人に迷惑をかけない範囲で」 (Ch03 P0126). The intended act is the very harm the watcher system is said to exist to prevent (Ch03 P0067), and he applies the system's own term to himself (自暴自棄, P0010; Ch03 P0067).
   - **"Tools."** INFERENCE: the wish to use her "as an outlet for every feeling" literalizes Miyagi's Ch04 charge that he sees others only as tools to fill his loneliness (Ch04 P0046).
   - **Continuity.** It continues his relish at having "scored a hit" on her (Ch07 P0053).
   - **Selection rationale.** It turns on 不適切 and 適切: the "most inappropriate act" (P0009), the "fitting" victim (P0010).
   - **The excuse formula.** 「混乱していたのだと思う」 repeats the formula of his apology for the glass (Ch06 P0106). The stacked causes diffuse the motive. "Liked her looks more than he realized" is knowledge the narrator holds that the experiencing self, by his own account, lacked. 疲労や痛み includes the pain he chose at the station (Ch07 P0088).
   - **The three months.** The hope attached to his remaining time restrained him from selling down in U05 (Ch05 P0070–P0073; `TDH-C017`). Here the months are discounted to "only a few months." たった数か月 (P0009) reverses his Ch05 「たった三十万円だろうと、これは俺の命だ」 (Ch05 P0014).
   - **Agency grammar** (B). The desire arrives through spontaneous 〜たくなる. Miyagi is the grammatical subject who 「俺の嗜虐心を強く刺激する」, so the cause is lodged in her. He conjectures his own coming act (行使するのだろう, P0025), and the touch is given to 「俺の手」 (P0024). The collapse has emotions as its subjects (P0028–P0030). Only 手を伸ばした, 手を離し and 恥じた are narrated as his acts. The step 「もう少し直接的にいうならば」 (P0008) repeats the move of Ch06 P0008, 「はっきりいってしまおう」.
2. **The lag question and the misconduct rule** (`TDH-C014`). He asks the lag between a watcher's confirming 不適切な行動 and death (P0014), and says plainly that he means assaulting her (P0016). Miyagi's testimony: 「連絡は、一瞬で済みます。そこから二十分もかかりませんよ。そして、逃げることは絶対に不可能です」 (P0019). This refines the relayed 「即座に…その場で」 (Ch03 P0067) with a time bound, and it is given while he is threatening her. His 「十分以上は自由にできる」 draws her averted, weak 「誰もそんなことはいってませんよ」 (P0020). It refuses his inference as unsaid, without stating the contrary or saying she would report. 「二十分もかかりませんよ」 is an upper bound, so his "more than ten minutes" does not follow from it, and 自由にできる is itself ambiguous. The euphemism breaks down across the exchange: 尽きさせられる (P0009), 尽きさせる (P0014), 殺される (P0016).

   INFERENCE: the conversation continues to the edge of dawn (P0066, P0092). The scene is not timed, but its length very probably exceeds twenty minutes, and he is alive. So one of three things holds:
   - she did not report (rivals within this: a bluff, a report withdrawn, headquarters declining);
   - the touch did not count as reportable misconduct;
   - her testimony, or the rule, is not as stated.

   The text does not say which. She does not flee (P0022, 「不思議なことに」). Why is undecidable. The text allows fear or freezing, resignation (cf. P0082, P0084), experience (P0038), duty, or confidence that he would stop.
3. **Her response, in his reading.** Through his perception are narrated:
   - the wary look in her eyes (P0015);
   - her coldness, "as if" in contempt (軽蔑するように, P0017–P0018);
   - her averted eyes and weak voice (P0020);
   - her stiffened body and sad face (P0024);
   - eyes that "seem" utterly resigned (諦めきったような目, P0030). The narration does not give her interior. Her sentences are reported directly.
4. **The stop.** As narrated, he stops through his own imagining (P0025–P0027), not through her resistance; the rule plays no narrated part. The rivals are unnarrated fear of death and exhaustion or pain (P0004, P0007); the desire "subsides" (鳴りを潜めていった) rather than being resisted. The imagined sequence (P0025) runs:
   - pulling her roughly down, pinning her to the floor, and acting on his desire (組み伏せ…欲望を行使する);
   - injury, a second scar on her knee, the loss of light from her eyes;
   - her cold "…Are you satisfied?" Two questions follow: would that satisfy him, and what is he trying to do? His excitement subsides; emptiness replaces it, and her sadness "passes into" him (伝染してきた, P0028–P0030). He withdraws two seats away and is ashamed of his 短絡さ, shortsightedness (P0031–P0032).
   - **Imagined speech.** Two utterances in 「」 are imagined, not spoken: his unvoiced taunt 「本当はこんなに弱いんだぞ」 (P0010), and her 「……満足しましたか？」 (P0025), which generates his self-question (P0026).
   - **His self-assessment.** He calls himself クズ, "scum" (P0033), and she replies dryly that she is glad he understands (P0034). He does not apologize to her in so many words, unlike after the glass (Ch06 P0106–P0110). The rival is that 「大変な仕事なんだな」 is an apology in his idiom. He accepts the ¥300,000 valuation as fair (P0035), the first time he reads the appraisal as deserved by his conduct. Earlier he accepted its figures and fairness (Ch02 P0158–P0159, P0171) and in Ch05 defied it (Ch05 P0009, P0014). The appraisal's stated criteria concern happiness, realization and contribution (Ch02 P0170), not conduct. "One step from the irreparable" (P0035) is his own verdict on the act.
   - **Other readings.** Any further moral verdict must name its standard. The text itself frames the act as 不適切 and his own word for himself is クズ.
5. **Her mitigation and his justification.** Miyagi says he is "if anything an easy case"; many went to more extreme lengths (P0038). He reads this as said to cover for him (庇うように, P0039). It is testimony about prior subjects; it also implies she has been in danger before (INFERENCE). Its scope, this incident or his whole case, is open. Then, when she remarks, surprised, that she had thought he cared only about Himeno (P0044), he replies that he would not have acted as he did if he did not find her attractive (P0045). He thereby presents the intended assault as evidence of attraction. This is his first *spoken* avowal of attraction; the Ch06 admission (Ch06 P0007–P0010) was unvoiced narration. Her reply, 「……そうですか。それはどうも」 (P0046), is dry; its tone is not glossed by the narrator.

   **Three framings of one act.** Kept separate:
   - **Narrated motive:** an outlet, cruelty and sadism (P0008–P0010).
   - **Spoken to her:**
     - 乱暴 (P0016);
     - turning one's anger on the watcher, which generalizes him into a type (P0036);
     - proof of attraction (P0045).

   With her 八つ当たり (P0082), the chapter has three idioms of displaced aggression: 捌け口 (his narration), 怒りの矛先 (his speech), 八つ当たり (her apology). The narration does not comment on the gap between his framings. He lets Miyagi's use of Himeno's name pass without objection (P0044), unlike Ch07 P0040. His denial of interest in Himeno alone echoes Ch03 P0111.

   **Prior subjects.** Her "many went to more extreme lengths" (P0038) implies she remembers prior subjects at least in kind (INFERENCE). That qualifies her 「覚えていません」 (Ch06 P0119). The rival is that remembering extreme subjects and not remembering lonely ones are compatible.
6. **The knee scar.** The large scar on her knee, 「出会ったときから気になっていた」 (P0040), connects to the large bandage on her right knee seen in Ch03 P0045 (INFERENCE: the same injury). "Since we met" loosely covers the shop meeting, where she wore a suit; the bandage was first seen at Ch03 P0045. 膝 at P0022 may be "lap" or "knees." He does not ask; this is the fourth time he declines to pursue something about her. Its origin is not given. The "lasting scars" of P0069 are on her *mother's* body, left by her father, and must not be linked to Miyagi's knee.
7. **Time-selling and watchers** (testimony; `TDH-C035`). Miyagi says:
   - she sold time, about thirty years (P0050); selling time means working it off as a watcher (働き終える, P0072);
   - most watchers came to the shop and sold time;
   - in effect they sold safety and relationships too (P0054);
   - before that she was an ordinary person (P0056).

   His reflection that she "will remain an invisible person (透明人間) for decades" (P0060) is his inference from her account. It is consistent with her mother becoming 透明 (P0069). She will be freed at about forty, "if I survive" (P0058–P0059). His figure of "about forty" presupposes that she began at about ten, which she discloses only at P0072. His own earlier estimate of her age, nineteen or twenty (Ch03 P0046), would imply about fifty if she had begun recently. His basis is unstated, and she simply agrees. This is the first explicit purchase of lifespan in the text; the shop's 売買 (Ch02 P0185) had allowed for it. By testimony, this settles the U02 question whether a transaction can be a purchase. Her 「監視員の大半は、あなたと同じようにあの店を訪れて」 (P0054) does not fit her own route: a debt and a messenger, with no shop visit narrated (P0072). The tension is unremarked.
8. **Her mother** (Miyagi's testimony, heavily hedged: よくわかりません, と思います, かもしれません, そうです, と記憶しています; P0069).
   - **Her parents.** Her father left just before her birth (hearsay). He left "lasting scars on [her mother's] body," as Miyagi puts it. Her mother cursed him yet, Miyagi thinks, wanted him back.
   - **The purchase.** Her mother sold decades of time to buy lifespan, perhaps to keep waiting for him. That is Miyagi's guess, and she disowns understanding it.
   - **Mother and child.** Mother and daughter disliked each other. The mother habitually said she wished she had never borne 〔傍点〕こんなの〔/傍点〕.
   - **Abandonment.** The mother vanished as a watcher when Miyagi was about six. Miyagi was then treated as a nuisance at her aunt's.
   - **Echoes.** Miyagi's 「その相手は誰でもよかった」 and 「他にすがるべき相手がいなかった」 echo his own 「相手は誰でもよかった」 (Ch04 P0074) and the すがる of U07 (Ch07 P0083, P0086). HYPOTHESIS, low weight: the waiting mother mirrors his fidelity to Himeno, a mirror neither character draws.
   - **Death and debt.** When Miyagi was ten, her mother died. The cause is "not clear," but that a person she watched killed her is "certain" (P0072). Extended lifespan does not protect against injury or illness, as she was told (なのだそうです). The mother died before working off her time, and the debt passed to Miyagi as the nearest person. Offered lifespan, time or health, with forced collection if unpaid, she chose to sell just over thirty years of time (P0072–P0074).
9. **New rules of the economy** (testimony only; `TDH-C035`).
   - Lifespan can be *bought* by selling time (P0069, P0072).
   - Extended lifespan does not prevent death by injury or illness (P0072; hearsay within testimony, なのだそうです).
   - Unworked time becomes a debt inherited by the nearest kin, collectable by force in any of the three commodities (P0072).
   - Watchers can be killed by those they watch (P0072; P0036–P0038).

   These extend what was said at the shop (Ch02) and the watcher rules (Ch03).
10. **Her valuation and her apology.** By her testimony, her lifespan was appraised, like his, at ¥10,000 a year (P0080–P0082), the minimum price at the shop (Ch02 P0162). She explains her harshness toward him, hedged: probably (たぶん…と思います) she could not forgive herself for being worth so little, and she supposes (でしょうね) she sees herself in him (P0082). She apologizes for 八つ当たり, taking it out on him, with 「ごめんなさい」. Her earlier apologies used 申し訳ありません and すみません. He does not answer the apology. INFERENCE: this reframes her earlier verdicts and "answer checks" (U04, U06, U07), from her side, as partly self-directed. It does not retract their content. Her identical price, for a life he calls tragic, also strains his reading of his own price as deserved (P0035; Ch02 P0170) (INFERENCE).
11. **Two valuations of her life.** She frames her future as salvation (救いのように, P0075, his reading): after repaying she will live "properly" (〝ちゃんと〟) more than anyone (P0074). He calls it a tragedy (P0076–P0077) and quotes back her own Ch05 verdict on him, 〝ようやくスタート地点に立ったというだけ〟 (Ch05 P0019), attributed aloud (「あんたの言葉を借りれば」). The silent uptake of U05–U07 turns explicit. The Ch05 exchange is replayed with roles reversed (B). He gives the verdict ("better to die quickly") with her own hedge (こういっては何ですけど, Ch07 P0083 → 「こんなことをいうのも何だが」, P0083). She concedes, then refuses the conclusion. She gives her price as 「一年につき、一万円」, echoing the shop's per-year formula (Ch02 P0162), so her Ch05 logic now applies to her. ## 5. Narration, focalization and time

**Self-explanation under hedge.** The narrator explains the impulse with stacked alternatives under 多分…と思う (P0007), then 何でもいい (P0008). The explanation is offered and declined at once. No retrospective moral commentary is added beyond 恥じた (P0032), his self-label クズ (P0033), and "one step from the irreparable" (P0035).

**Imagining as turning point.** P0025 runs his imagined sequence in conjectural form (のだろう, かもしれない, ×4). The two bare questions of P0026–P0027 are his turning point. The stop is narrated as an affective shift (P0028–P0030), not a decision argued.

**Embedded narration.** P0069 and P0072 are long blocks of Miyagi's direct speech, set off by a double blank (P0067–P0068). At 595 characters, P0069 is the longest paragraph in the text so far; the previous longest were Miyagi's speeches about him (Ch05 P0019, P0052). The manner ぽつぽつ is rendered as one block, with three levels of quotation. The narrator frames them, reading her pause (おそらく, P0070) and describing her delivery as 淡々と…まるで他人のことのように (P0071). Within her speech, her own hedges mark what she knows from hearsay (そうです), memory (と記憶しています) and guess (と思います, かもしれません).

**Readings of Miyagi.** The narration marks his readings of her:
- the "most human" gesture (ように見えた, P0005), a superlative relative to her other gestures;
- the wary look (警戒の色, P0015) and contempt (軽蔑するように, P0018);
- the sad face (悲しそうな顔, P0024) and resigned eyes (諦めきったような, P0030);
- sensing danger (感じ取ったのか, P0011);
- covering for him (庇うように, P0039);
- not overcome (なさそうだ, P0070);
- as if speaking of salvation (救いのように, P0075);
- amused (おかしそうに, P0081);
- relaxing for the first time (肩の力を抜いた, P0090).

**Vantage.** 「今の俺が思い付く中では」 (P0009) may mean the experiencing self's present or the narrating present; the text does not settle it, though the novel's usage elsewhere favours the experiencing self (Ch05 P0026). The present-tense generalizations of P0007–P0010 are likewise undecidable between free indirect discourse and the narrating present. Her 「死に方まで一緒」 is her speculation, not narrative prolepsis.

**Pacing.** The rationalization (P0007–P0010) runs to some six hundred characters, the shame to one short sentence (P0032).

**Time.** The unit runs from a few hours before the first train to the edge of dawn (P0004, P0066, P0092), the end of night three into day four. The back-story spans Miyagi's birth, age six and age ten.

## 6. Japanese language and form

- **Sesame-dot emphasis.** 〔傍点〕こんなの〔/傍点〕 (P0069), "*this thing*," is the twelfth emphasis and the unit's only one. It is the first emphasis inside an embedded quotation of a third person: Miyagi quoting her mother. こんなの refers to the child as a thing (`TDH-F022`).
- **The escalating restatement.** 酷いことをしてやりたく → もう少し直接的にいうならば…押し倒したく → 利用してやりたく (P0008) climbs by explicit self-correction. The repeated 〜てやりたい marks a malefactive "do to her."
- **The rule's vocabulary.** He quotes 〝不適切な行動〟 with とやら, "this so-called" (P0014). The narration names his own act 不適切な行為 (P0009). Speech and the title use 行動, the institutional term of Ch03; the narration uses 行為. The pun is 「もっとも不適切な行為」 / 「被害者として適切な相手」 (P0009–P0010).
- **てやる valence.** Hostile four times (してやりたく, 利用してやりたく, 暴いてやりたく, 思い知らせてやりたく; P0008–P0010); kind once (火を点けてやろう, P0087).
- **Adversative passive.** 「暴れられたり」 (P0024) imagines himself as the sufferer of her resistance.
- **Echoes and uptake** (`TDH-F021` recurrence).
  - He quotes her Ch05 phrase 〝ようやくスタート地点に立ったというだけ〟 back at her (P0077).
  - She uses すがる of her mother, 「他にすがるべき相手がいなかった」 (P0069), the verb that passed between them in U07 (Ch07 P0083, P0086).
  - Her 〝いつかいいことがあるかもしれない〟 (P0084) is set in 〝〟. Its いいこと may echo Himeno's prophecy 「いいことが起きて」 (Ch02 P0015); this is INFERENCE, and the rival is ordinary phrasing.
- **Idioms made literal.**
  - 時間を売る becomes indentured service.
  - 「私の前から姿を消した」 (P0069): the mother literally became invisible to her six-year-old.
  - 透明人間 occurs for the first time (P0060), with 透明 of the mother (P0069). U06 spoke of 知覚 and U07 of 見える. English "invisible man" would gender 透明人間; "invisible person" is used here.
  - 寿命を買う is a new transaction.
- **Pronoun distance.** In her account 母 becomes 彼女 and 「娘の私」 (P0072), then 母親 (P0084). This confirms 「まるで他人のことのように語られた」 (P0071).
- **Her apology register.** 「八つ当たりばっかりして、ごめんなさい」 (P0082) is her first ごめんなさい. Earlier she used 申し訳ありません and すみません. ごめんなさい is softer than her usual polite register.
- **Registers.**
  - His 「あんた」 persists; he never uses her name.
  - Her polite register runs from crisp testimony (P0019) through a weak voice (P0020) to the humble-keigo shield 「ご理解いただけたようで、何よりです」 (P0034). Then come her first えっと, あんまり, ばっかり, ほら and じゃないですか (P0048–P0084), and 「クスノキさん」 once, at "the same ordinary person" (P0056).
  - Self-mockery appears (自嘲めいた笑み, P0059). 「今日は、質問が多いですね」 (P0062) remarks on his change of manner.
- **The 人間 lexicon.** 「もっとも人間的」 (P0005) answers the earlier 人間味 verdicts (Ch03; Ch04). 生まれつき ×3 … 思い込んでいた (P0057) is a retraction marker, hedged by かもしれない. Her 「普通の人間でした」 (P0056) is past tense.
- **Contagion and space.** 伝染 (P0030), つられて (P0087), 火が移る (P0089). Touch moves from one-way 触れる (P0024) to reciprocal 触れあう (P0089). Position moves from 真正面 (P0011) to 二つ隣 (P0031) to faces drawn together, with Miyagi as initiator (P0088). B flags the last two items as possibly made salient by prior exposure; they rest on textual anchors.
- **Mirrored contacts.** His hand touches her bare shoulder (P0024); then two cigarette tips touch, at her initiative (P0089). INFERENCE: the chapter pairs an unconsented touch with a consented one.

**The aunt (past).** She housed Miyagi for some years and treated her as a nuisance.

**The man who brought news (past).** He informed ten-year-old Miyagi of her mother's death and the debt, and set out the three options.

## 11. Competing interpretations, claims and forecasts

**Interpretation A: the license undone from within.** The list's "don't resist desire" and the dying man's license (U07) reach their limit. As narrated, he stops through his own imagining of harm and a contagious sadness. The rule, which he had just measured and accepted, plays no narrated part; the rivals are unnarrated fear and exhaustion. The chapter tests whether his situation licenses anything, and, as narrated, finds that it does not. Support: P0009, P0025–P0032, P0035. Weakener: the stop is affective and sudden (あっという間に, P0028), not reasoned. His later framing of the act as attraction (P0045) shows the lesson incomplete.

**Interpretation B: Miyagi as his mirror.** Her account presents her as a double of him in sharable respects:
- the same price, ¥10,000 a year;
- the hope formula;
- self-contempt.

A rejecting parent is hers alone; no text shows a parent rejecting him (Ch02 P0007 says only that he could not rely on his parents).

Her apology names the identification from her side (P0082). His resolution (P0091) answers it from his. Support: P0050–P0086. Weakener: all of it is her single-source testimony, and his reading of her relief.

**Interpretation C: the reversal of watcher and watched.** The chapter opens with him measuring the time he would have to harm her. It closes with her telling him how a watcher died at a subject's hands (P0072) and speculating that she may die the same way (P0084). INFERENCE: the danger of the job, raised by him (P0036), is the chapter's turn from his threat to her vulnerability (see F-U08-c).
