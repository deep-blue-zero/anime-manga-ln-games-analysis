# U10 — chapter 10 「私の、たった一人の幼馴染へ」

**Content note.** This chapter contains a farewell letter disclosing a planned suicide, and an account of a person's wish to die unnoticed. This reading describes these matters plainly and briefly.

## 2. Entering context and exposure boundary

**Entering frontier.** U09 was accepted in commit `d186f0bb`; the branch was then merged with main. The precise entering state is U09 §12.

**State at entry.**
- **The encounter.** At the summer festival, at the shrine of the childhood prophecy, a woman Kusunoki recognizes as Himeno has said his name.
- **His plan.** He means to talk with her once more and to give her what remains of his ¥300,000.
- **Miyagi's testimony on Himeno (Ch03 P0101).** Himeno gave birth at seventeen, married at eighteen, divorced, and now lives with her family. She is "set" to die by jumping two years later, leaving a grim farewell letter. She "barely remembers" him or the promise.
- **The prolepsis.** Miyagi did not oppose his visit. The narrator has said that he later understood she had known "that feeling" long before him.

## 3. Current-unit story synopsis

**After the encounter.** Kusunoki hardly remembers what he and Himeno said at the shrine, or even what she wore. He was too excited for content to matter (P0004–P0005). It seems she was not there for the festival: she had parked at the shrine on work business, a job "dealing with people," which she will not specify (P0006). She agrees to a meal, not drinks, two days later (P0007–P0009). Happy enough to forget Miyagi for a while, he hears her say she had not expected this either. He calls it too good a story, and she agrees such things happen (P0010–P0013). At home he crosses out the Himeno item on his list (P0016).

**The rehearsal.** He asks Miyagi, by name, to stand in for Himeno the next day so he can practise; he has never properly gone out with a woman (P0017–P0024). He knows no one else can see her, and she confirms it. He does not care what others think so long as Himeno thinks well of him (P0025–P0028). She adds that she does not understand women her age. He replies that she is like any girl, apart from being a bit cuter than usual (P0029–P0032).

They buy clothes and boots on her advice, and he has his hair cut; he looks transformed (P0035–P0042). She says he looks like someone promised a happy future. He tells her that, smiling, she would look like a "library fairy," and that he means her, not Himeno. She thanks him stiffly. Both of them, she remarks, are worth next to nothing as people (P0043–P0055). At an Italian restaurant in a side street, a couple at the next table whisper about the man talking to himself (P0056).

Tipsy, he walks along the river swinging her hand. She lets herself be led, looking troubled (P0057). Seemingly used to it, she tells him to practise courting her as Himeno. He says that the best thing in his life was her appearing and the worst her disappearing, and that her answer now could swap them (P0058–P0059). Himeno might laugh it off, Miyagi supposes. Asked her own answer, she says she does not understand it, and he calls it a joke (P0060–P0065). He offers a view that character is mostly situation (P0066–P0070). At an observation deck popular with couples, he tells her how, as children, he pulled Himeno back from a gap in the spiral-stair railing. Miyagi's expression is complicated (P0071–P0073). At this point, the narrator says, she knew more than he did and had not told him the crucial thing; perhaps she wanted to let him dream to the limit (P0074–P0077).

**The dinner.** On a rainy afternoon Himeno arrives twenty-seven minutes late at the station bookshop. He had begun to fear her promise was a polite evasion (P0080–P0085). To him she is beautiful beyond measure (P0086–P0089). They banter about a "next time." She takes the umbrella from him, recalling that as children it was he who forgot his and sheltered under hers. He takes it back and holds it toward her (P0090–P0101). She asks what he was doing at the shrine; looking for her, he says; "liar," she says (P0102–P0105). He thought it was going well, he says, and does not want to know what she was really thinking (P0106–P0109).

The narrator announces the "answer check," 「さて、答えあわせといこう」. At the restaurant, he says, he commits a grave mistake. If it is to be called a mistake at all, its formation lay further back and took shape in stages. In the end it would save him, and at the same time he would learn why Miyagi had tried to stop him (P0112–P0117). At her insistence he speaks first:
- his grades and memory declined;
- he fell behind at a leading high school and went on to an ordinary university, paying his own way after arguing with his parents;
- he has not held a paintbrush since the winter he was seventeen (P0118–P0120).

She says she loved his paintings and used to take them home to look at, though rivalry kept her from saying so (P0121–P0127). She mentions dropping out of high school, expecting he had heard at a reunion. He feigns surprise, and says he never went to an elementary-school reunion, thinking she would not come (P0128–P0131). In the narrator's gist, her account is a marriage to a graduated senior, a hasty dropout, a mismatch and a divorce. It omits the childbirth he had heard of from Miyagi (P0132–P0133). She calls herself unchanged since the summer they parted, and asks whether he has changed (P0134–P0136).

Losing his composure, he says he too never changed. "Just a few days ago," he says, he sold his lifespan for ¥10,000 a year. He pours out everything in a pleading tone, and points at Miyagi as "a girl who talks coldly but is actually nice" (P0137–P0146). Himeno asks whether he knows how unreal this sounds. Yet she says she cannot think it a lie, since she can tell when he lies (P0147–P0149). His joy is hard to describe (P0150). She too has been hiding something, she says. Coughing into a handkerchief, she suggests they continue after the meal, and goes toward the restroom (P0151–P0153). 〔傍点〕ヒメノは〔/傍点〕、〔傍点〕二度と戻ってこなかった〔/傍点〕, *Himeno never came back* (P0154).

**Her letter.** Miyagi checks the restroom and reports Himeno gone (P0155–P0159). He feels drained and ill. Miyagi sits opposite and eats Himeno's pasta (P0160–P0165). Asked why Himeno vanished, Miyagi says she probably took him for mad. That was true in a sense, the narrator says, but the truth was more complex; Miyagi knew it and, he says, hid it for his sake (P0167–P0172). Outside, a waiter hands him a note from "your companion," written on a page torn from a notebook (P0173–P0175). Reading it, he learns, he says, that Miyagi had been lying all along (P0176–P0177). Did she know 〔傍点〕これ〔/傍点〕 and hide it? "Yes. I'm sorry." No need to apologize, he says; it was a good dream. The apology, the narrator says, was his to make (P0178–P0182). In his original life, he asks, Himeno's aim would have been achieved? Yes, Miyagi says: before his eyes Himeno was to carry out 〔傍点〕それ〔/傍点〕. Two unattributed lines add: to make him watch, and to settle a long grudge (P0183–P0186).

The letter is headed "To my one and only childhood friend." In gist, it says she had meant to make herself indispensable to him and then kill herself before his eyes, out of spite: 死んでやる, at "that observation deck." The reason was long resentment of his failure to answer her call for help. She now drops the plan, since he seems to have grown far stranger than she. It closes by hoping that, of all he said, at least his short remaining life is true (P0187–P0200). What a fool he is, he thinks: he lived alone to avoid exactly this, and should have trusted his own way to the end (P0202–P0204).

**The money and the rain.** On the bridge by the station he folds her letter into a paper aeroplane and sends it toward the river, where it drifts and is carried away (P0207). He hands out the envelope of money meant for her, one note at a time, to passers-by, whose reactions vary (P0208–P0209). Miyagi catches his sleeve and asks him to stop; he shakes her off, saying he is not bothering anyone (P0210–P0211). He gives away the cash in his wallet too, down to the thousand-yen notes, and must walk home (P0212–P0215). It rains. He refuses the shelter of Miyagi's blue umbrella; he feels like getting wet, he says. She closes it and walks soaked behind him: "As you see, I feel like getting wet" (P0216–P0224).

At a bus stop he dozes. Waking cold, he finds her asleep beside him, hugging her knees, and pities her (P0225–P0229). He finds an unlocked community hall and carries her there. She, a lighter sleeper than he, could hardly have failed to wake, he reasons; she pretended to sleep throughout, in his account (P0230–P0233). He makes beds of cushions and lights an old mosquito coil (P0234–P0235). As he has done since about age five, he falls asleep imagining the world he would have wanted (P0236–P0242).

**Night and morning.** Waking at midnight, he senses something that may have been a wishful dream; if real, nothing could make him happier (P0245–P0247). Someone crouches by his pillow. He knows it is Miyagi by her smell, clear like a winter morning (P0248). He keeps his eyes closed. She strokes his head gently for less than a minute and seems to murmur something the rain drowns out (P0249–P0252). How much she saves him, he thinks. But he tells himself she is here only for work, kind because he is going to die, and not out of any feeling for him. Misplaced hope would make them both unhappy. He resolves to die quietly, expecting nothing of anyone, unnoticed like a cat (P0253–P0259). In the morning Miyagi is whistling Nina Simone's "I Wish I Knew" as she tidies the cushions. "Let's go home," she says. "Yeah," he answers (P0260–P0263).

**Ending condition.** It is the morning after the dinner, in a community hall, with Miyagi.
- Himeno has left a letter. It says she had planned to die in front of him, in spite, for his not answering her call for help, and has dropped the plan because he seems to have grown stranger than she.
- Miyagi testifies that in his original course Himeno would have carried it out before him. She admits she knew and hid it; he concludes that she lied all along.
- He told Himeno of the sale. She said she believed him; her letter casts doubt on that.
- He has thrown the letter into the river and given away the money meant for her and the cash he carried.
- He carried Miyagi to shelter; she was pretending to sleep, in his inference. In the night she perhaps stroked his head.
- He has resolved to expect nothing and to die quietly.

## 4. Factual and causal reconstruction

1. **The reunion, as remembered.** He remembers almost nothing of the shrine conversation, or her clothes (P0004–P0005), so its content is unrecoverable. Her reason for being there comes as hearsay (らしい, のだそうだ, P0006): work, and a car parked at the shrine. Her job is evaded as 「人を相手にする仕事」. INFERENCE, low: this bears on the narrator's claim of hidden connection (Ch09 P0138–P0139) only as chance. Miyagi's 「こうなるとは、私も予想してませんでした」 (P0011) is of undecidable sincerity, given what she knew (P0074).
2. **The strike-through.** He crosses out list item 8 after one encounter and one appointment (P0016). That is OBSERVATION. Its reason is unstated: it might mark the item as met, or as now in hand.
3. **The rehearsal** (`TDH-C046`).
   - **The request and its cost.** He asks her by name (「ミヤギ、頼めるか？」, P0024). He accepts the social cost of being seen with no one. To him, Himeno's opinion is all that matters (P0028).
   - **Miyagi's self-deprecation.** She says she does not understand women her own age (P0029). It answers his U09 remark that she is timid about herself (Ch09 P0098); here it is 卑屈, "self-abasing" (P0030).
   - **His compliments.** These are open, and voiced: "a bit cuter than usual" (P0030); the "library fairy," and "I mean you" (P0046–P0052).
   - **Her reply.** 「人間としての価値はゼロに近い」 (P0054) restates their shared minimum price (Ch08 P0080).
   - **Hand-holding.** On the walk he holds and swings her hand while drunk (P0057). It is his first narrated touch of her since U08 (Ch08 P0024). The text gives acquiescence, not consent: 「困ったような顔で、俺に手を引かれるままになっていた」. She seems to grow used to it (慣れてきたらしい, his reading; P0058).
   - **The courtship line.** It addresses "you" (P0059). Asked her own answer, she says she does not understand (P0064). INFERENCE: the line is double-addressed. It is not a confession to Miyagi (SALIENCE_FLAG: romance readings are a trap). His 「冗談だよ。気にしないでくれ」 (P0065) echoes Himeno's Ch01 retraction, 「……冗談だよ。忘れてちょうだい」 (Ch01 P0092). The line's 「目の前から消えた」 is fulfilled, in the reader's retrospect, by Himeno's 「あなたの前から消えます」 (P0198). Her compliment that he looks "promised a happy future" (P0045) was spoken while she knew the grudge (dramatic irony; INFERENCE).
   - **The railing memory.** He tells of pulling the child Himeno back from a gap in the observation deck's spiral-stair railing (P0072). By the letter's disclosure, 「あの展望台」 is where she planned to die (P0192). That it is the same deck is a strong INFERENCE, not stated. It links the place only in the reader's retrospect; he draws no connection then.
   - **The prolepsis.** The narrator says Miyagi then knew more, had not told him the crucial thing, and perhaps (かもしれない) wanted to let him dream to the limit (P0074–P0077). This is the second withheld-content prolepsis about Miyagi after Ch09 P0042–P0046. It is resolved within this unit (P0184, P0190–P0200).
4. **The dinner.**
   - **His relief.** She is twenty-seven minutes late. He feared her promise was a polite evasion (社交辞令), and is relieved (P0081–P0085).
   - **His view of her.** He perceives her as beautiful beyond measure (P0086–P0089), and his own voice as changed (P0092).
   - **The umbrella.** She takes his umbrella from him, recalling that as children he always forgot his and sheltered under hers. He takes it back to hold it toward her (P0097–P0099).
   - **The narrator's retrospect.** He thought it was going well, and believed his goodwill was returned (P0106–P0108). He does not want to know what she was thinking (P0109). That is a retrospective refusal of knowledge, and it implies that her inner state then differed from his belief (INFERENCE).
5. **The "answer check" and the mistake** (P0112–P0117). 「さて、答えあわせといこう」 takes over Miyagi's phrase (Ch04 P0002, P0041; Ch06 P0087). The narrator says that at the restaurant he commits a grave mistake (P0113). If it is to be called a mistake, its formation, he says conditionally, lay further back, built up in stages (堅実に); he would repeat it if given the chance (P0113–P0114). That the mistake is his confession of the sale (P0137–P0146) is a strong INFERENCE, not stated. C's rivals for what was "formed in stages" are his long isolation, his reliance on Himeno, and his habit of seeking sympathy. He says it will 「俺の身を救う」 (P0115), which need not mean his life was saved. 「同時に」, at the same time, he would learn why Miyagi had tried to stop him (P0116–P0117). That resolves, from the narrating position, the motive of her U03 and U07 warnings (Ch03 P0095, P0112; Ch07 P0041). It is not the same as Ch09's withheld 「わからなくはない」 meaning, which stays open.
6. **His ten years** (P0119).
   - **Decline.** His grades and memory declined.
   - **School and money.** His parents insisted on a famous university, and he paid his own tuition and living costs. That adds to Ch02 P0007's 「親には頼れない事情」.
   - **The paintbrush.** 「十七歳の冬以降、一度も絵筆を握っていない」. The medium is now painting (絵筆), and the stopping point the winter of seventeen. That stands in tension with Ch06 P0074, where he told Naruse he stopped drawing after entering university. The rivals are loose speech to Naruse, or drawing as distinct from painting.
   - **A coincidence of timing.** The winter of seventeen is the winter Himeno did not visit after their exchange (Ch09 P0063). That is INFERENCE from timing; the text draws no connection.
7. **Himeno on his paintings** (P0121–P0125). She loved them, envied him, took them home and gazed at them, and hid her admiration out of rivalry. She is a second voice on the drawing, after Naruse (Ch06 P0073–P0075). Unlike Naruse's laugh, she says 「残念だな」. He contrasts her with "some man" (P0122).
8. **Himeno's account against Miyagi's testimony** (`TDH-C015`).
   - **What she says.** She dropped out, married a graduated senior, and later divorced (P0128, P0133). She expected he had heard at a reunion; he feigns surprise (わざとらしく, P0129). By his account he never attended an elementary-school reunion, expecting her absence (P0131). That partly answers Miyagi's reunion charge (Ch07 P0083).
   - **What she omits.** The childbirth (P0133), which Miyagi had testified to (Ch03 P0101).
   - **The order.** Her order (marriage, then dropout) differs from Miyagi's (childbirth at seventeen, dropout, marriage at eighteen). The childbirth remains Miyagi's testimony alone. Her account at P0133 is the narrator's gist in quotation marks.
   - **Her self-diagnosis.** She has not changed since the transfer, cannot bear imperfection, and grew arrogant from early cleverness (P0134). This echoes the title of Ch06, 「変わってしまった人、変われなかった人」, and Naruse (INFERENCE).
9. **The confession of the sale** (P0137–P0146). He loses composure and knows it is foolish but cannot stop (P0139–P0140). He tells her everything "in a tone to draw sympathy" (P0145), and points to Miyagi by name, calling her 「案外いい子」. That makes Himeno the first person other than Miyagi to be told of the sale (INFERENCE from the frontier); with Naruse he deliberately withheld it (Ch06 P0071). His 「つい数日前」 (P0138) understates the time since the sale, roughly two weeks. She doubts the plausibility but says she believes him on the strength of long acquaintance (P0147–P0149). Her letter contradicts this: it calls him 「おかしく」 and hopes only his short life is true (P0196, P0200). On the walk she had called his true statement a lie (「うそつき」, P0104). Her plan to become 「欠かせない存在」 (P0195) leaves all her warmth at dinner undecidable: the paintings, the self-account. It does not prove it false. A rival reconciles the two: she believed him sincere but deluded. She cannot see Miyagi (「ヒメノには見えないだろうけど」, P0146).
10. **Her disappearance** (P0151–P0159). She announces a hidden matter, coughs into a handkerchief, and goes toward the restroom. No illness should be inferred from the cough. Then comes 〔傍点〕ヒメノは〔/傍点〕、〔傍点〕二度と戻ってこなかった〔/傍点〕 (P0154).
11. **Miyagi's concealment** (`TDH-C050`).
    - **Her partial answer.** Asked why Himeno left, she says 「おそらく、頭がおかしくなったと思われたんでしょうね」 (P0169). The narrator: true in a sense, but the truth was more complex, Miyagi knew it, and she hid it 「俺のために」 (P0170–P0172).
    - **The motive.** 「俺のために」 is the narrator's attribution of her motive. INFERENCE: no rule forced the silence, since in Ch03 she saw no reason not to tell him the future (Ch03 P0116).
    - **Concealed and lied.** The text keeps 隠していた, concealed (P0172), apart from 嘘をついていた, lied (P0177), and never says what the lie was. The narrator's 「俺のために」 is unhedged.
    - **The lie.** After reading the letter he learns that 「ミヤギがずっと嘘をついていた」 (P0177). This pays off Ch02 P0146–P0147: his first-meeting intuition that 「あの女が関わっているところに、嘘はない」, which the narrator called unreliable in retrospect. She admits that she knew and hid 〔傍点〕これ〔/傍点〕: 「そうです。すみません」 (P0178–P0180). "The lie" is his conclusion; her admission is to knowing and hiding.
    - **What she lied about.** INFERENCE, strong: at least her Ch03 testimony that Himeno 「あなたのことなんてほとんど覚えてませんから」 (Ch03 P0101). Himeno's letter says she resented him 「ずっと」 (P0193), and she recalls the umbrella, his paintings and their rivalry (P0098, P0121–P0127). The promise clause of the same sentence is untested. Other candidate scopes:
      - the omission that he was the suicide's target;
      - the advice that meeting her would be "no good";
      - her silence at the deck.

      The scope of "all along" is otherwise unspecified.
    - **The original course.** She confirms (testimony, from a witness just shown concealing) that in his original course Himeno's aim would have been achieved: 「クスノキさんの目の前で、ヒメノさんは〔傍点〕それ〔/傍点〕を実行することになっていました」 (P0183–P0184). The unattributed lines P0185–P0186 add the purpose: to make him watch, and to settle a long grudge. That is his gloss on the letter. It reuses Miyagi's Ch04 verdict on Wakana: 見せつける, and 恨み (Ch04 P0044). It is a revenge suicide, not a plan to harm him. He uses Miyagi's original-course formula himself, 本来…ことになっていた (P0183; cf. Ch06 P0089).
    - **A pattern** (C). Himeno is the third neglected tie revealed as estranged, after Wakana and Naruse (Naruse as less fond than he thought, not resentful). Wakana and Himeno each mean to 見せつけ at him (Ch04 P0044; P0185). The answer-check mode is inverted: Miyagi gives a softened half-truth, and the truth arrives in Himeno's hand.
    - **Consistency.** The jump fits Ch03's 飛び降り自殺; Ch03 omitted that he was its target. Ch03's 陰惨な遺書, "two years later," the child and the family home are not corroborated in U10. Whether she remembers the childhood promise is untested. Whether "two years later" and "in front of you" belong to the same course is not reconciled in the text (INFERENCE: the original course would include a later reunion).
12. **Himeno's letter** (P0190–P0200; testimony). Its salutation is the chapter's title. It is in 私 and polite です/ます, with lines set like verse. In gist:
    - a revenge suicide planned before his eyes at 「あの展望台」, once she had made herself 「欠かせない存在」;
    - the motive, lifelong resentment of an unanswered 「助けの求め」;
    - the plan dropped because he seems 「おかしく」;
    - a closing barb hoping his short life is true.

    **Spite** (B). The letter puts her own death in the malefactive 〜てやる three times: 死んでやる (P0191), 落っこちてやる (P0192), 死んでやろう (P0195). Her death is aimed at him. This echoes the children's ten-year revenge fantasy, 「見返してやるんだ」 (Ch01 P0076). An English "I meant to die in front of you" loses the spite. The reader sees the letter only as he rereads it, in the present (「もう一度、手紙に目を通す」, P0187).

    The call for help is unidentified. 「身に覚えがないと言うかもしれません」 anticipates his not recognizing it. INFERENCE, moderate to strong: it was her letter at seventeen, which he found "unlike her" (Ch09 P0055–P0060), and to which he replied in kind. The rivals are another, unnarrated appeal, or her sense of the letter as a whole. He did reply (Ch09 P0061), so "not answering" names a failure of response, not a silence. Her letter at seventeen mentioned a possible winter visit; he has not held a brush since "the winter at seventeen" (P0119). If the inference holds, it vindicates Miyagi's Ch07 charge (Ch07 P0083), which he never examines. The letter's final line, hoping his short life is true, is a barb.
13. **His response.** He shows no anger at Himeno or Miyagi. He absolves Miyagi (「いい夢を見させてもらったよ」, P0181) and lacks the strength to admit his own fault (P0182). He reproaches himself, 「馬鹿だなあ、俺は」, and regrets leaving his solitary way (P0202–P0204). He never asks whether Himeno will still die, nor considers her plea. It is a return to his U01 stance (INFERENCE).
    - **The paper aeroplane.** He folds the letter into a paper aeroplane and sends it toward the river (P0207). The plane lands in the water and drifts away.
    - **The money.** He gives away the envelope of money meant for her, and the cash in his wallet, to strangers (P0208–P0212). That disposes of what remained of the ¥300,000 he had reassigned to her (Ch09 P0039), and of his Ch05 vow (Ch05 P0014). Other funds he may have are not mentioned.
    - **"Not bothering anyone."** 「別に人に迷惑はかけてないだろ」 (P0211) echoes Miyagi's limit, 「他人に迷惑をかけない範囲で」 (Ch03 P0126).
    - **Shaking her off.** He shakes off her hand (振り払った). That is the first physical rebuff of her narrated since U08, short of aggression.
14. **The rain and the mirrored refusal** (P0216–P0224). He refuses her umbrella, 「見ての通り、濡れたい気分なんだ」, and she closes it and returns his words, 「見ての通り、濡れたい気分なんです」. The umbrella scene with Himeno (P0097–P0099) is thus answered by Miyagi's shared exposure. That pairing is INFERENCE.
15. **Carrying her; feigned sleep** (P0228–P0234). He pities her and carries her to shelter. Miyagi crouches and strokes his head for less than a minute, and seems (ようだった) to murmur something inaudible. 透き通った, used of Himeno's voice (Ch01 P0064; Ch09 P0148), is now used of Miyagi's smell (P0248). That is INFERENCE, a lexical transfer. Whether it happened is undecided.

## 5. Narration, focalization and time

**Prolepses.** The narration repeatedly speaks from after the event:
- Miyagi knew more and withheld it (P0074–P0077);
- he does not want to know Himeno's thoughts (P0109);
- the mistake "would save" him, and he "would learn" why Miyagi tried to stop him (P0115–P0117);
- Miyagi hid the truth, for his sake, as he puts it (P0171–P0172);
- at narrating time he still cannot say whether the head-stroking happened (P0245–P0247).

The narrator had stated Miyagi's now-falsified "she has forgotten me" as fact (Ch04 P0028).

The withheld-content prolepsis about Miyagi's warnings (P0117) is resolved within the unit; 止めようとしていた refers back to Ch03 and Ch07, since in U09 and U10 she does not stop him. The Ch09 prolepsis about 「わからなくはない」 is not resolved, and the two must not be merged (`TDH-C010`). The experiencing self knows nothing until P0176.

**"さて、答えあわせといこう"** (P0112). It is the narrator's narratee management, borrowing Miyagi's institutional phrase; compare 「さて、手紙の話だ」 (Ch09 P0024).

**Embedded document.** Himeno's letter (P0190–P0200) is set off by blanks, in polite 私 style with verse-like lines. It is the second letter given verbatim, after the capsule letter (Ch09 P0031–P0035). Its salutation is the chapter title. His reaction follows at P0202–P0204, also set off.

**Narration as a single unit.** P0102–P0105 switches to the historical present with indirect speech without quotation marks (訊く, 答える, ぶつけてくる, いう). That is the same bracketless present-tense form as the Ch03 dream, so the happy walk reads like a dream (B, INFERENCE).

**Effect before cause.** The chapter tells effects before causes. The disappearance comes before the letter, and the "mistake" before its nature. Its reunion with Himeno opens almost as the reunion with Naruse did: he hardly remembers what they said (P0004–P0005; cf. Ch06 P0070).

**His readings.** These are marked as his:
- Miyagi's complicated face (P0073);
- Himeno's beauty (P0086–P0087);
- Himeno's 傷付いた少女のような目 (P0135);
- Miyagi's feigned sleep (P0232–P0233);
- the night touch, possibly a dream (P0245).

**Time.**
- **Festival night.** Himeno is met at the shrine.
- **The next day.** The rehearsal.
- **The day after.** The rainy dinner, the money given away, the walk in the rain, and the night in the community hall.
- **The following morning.**

INFERENCE: about days ten to thirteen. Back-story covers the observation deck in childhood, his schooling, and the winter of seventeen.

## 6. Japanese language and form

- **Sesame-dot emphasis.** P0154 has two spans, 〔傍点〕ヒメノは〔/傍点〕 and 〔傍点〕二度と戻ってこなかった〔/傍点〕. P0178 has 〔傍点〕これ〔/傍点〕 and P0184 has 〔傍点〕それ〔/傍点〕. Counting spans as before, these are the fourteenth to seventeenth. If the split marking at P0154 counts once, the count stands at sixteen. これ and それ are the first emphasized demonstrative placeholders since 〔傍点〕あれ〔/傍点〕 (Ch07 P0015). Ch08 P0069's emphasized こんなの is a demonstrative used of the daughter, not a placeholder for an unnamed matter. 〔傍点〕これ〔/傍点〕 and 〔傍点〕それ〔/傍点〕 point at the letter's content, the planned suicide, without naming it (`TDH-F027`).
- **The title as letter.** 「私の、たった一人の幼馴染へ」 (P0190) gives the chapter title to Himeno's salutation. TDH-F006 had flagged its warmth against the narrator's distanced 「いわゆる『幼馴染』」 (Ch01 P0042). The warm address heads a letter of hatred and revenge (INFERENCE).
- **答えあわせ.** Miyagi's phrase, taken over by the narrator (P0112), recurs from Ch04 P0002 and P0041 and Ch06 P0087 (`TDH-F021` uptake).
- **Mirrored lines.** 「見ての通り、濡れたい気分なんだ」 is returned as 「…なんです」 (P0218, P0223).
- **透き通った.** Formerly Himeno's voice (Ch01 P0064; Ch09 P0148), it is now Miyagi's smell (P0248).
- **Self-judgment idioms.** 馬鹿だなあ、俺は (P0202); 猫のように人知れず (P0258); 一年につき一万円程度の価値しかない人間 (P0044, ironic); 人間としての価値はゼロに近い (P0054, hers).
- **Registers.** He calls her ミヤギ to her face several times (P0024, P0046, P0063, P0167). Her 「すみません」 (P0180) is formal against her U08 ごめんなさい. Himeno's register is casual, with 君 and クスノキ, and a quizzing 「正直でよろしい」.
- **Song.** 「アイ・ウィッシュ・アイ・ニュー」 is given in katakana (P0260).
- **Orthography.** Himeno's letter writes 言う in kanji (P0193). The narration elsewhere uses いう, with the Ch07 teacher's speech the only other exception (D1).

## 11. Competing interpretations, claims and forecasts

**Interpretation A: the prophecy reversed.** The summer of the prophecy brings not "something very good" but a revelation. Himeno, his one hope, resented him, and had meant to die before him in spite. He responds by casting off the letter and the money, and by returning to solitude (P0202–P0204, P0258). Support: P0190–P0200. Weakener: her letter abandons the revenge (P0196–P0198). Its last line is a barb, not goodwill.

**Interpretation B: Miyagi beside Himeno** (SALIENCE_FLAG). The chapter sets Miyagi beside Himeno:
- the proxy date that goes without disaster;
- the umbrella refused and the soaking shared;
- the carrying, and the feigned sleep (his inference);
- the night touch (possibly a dream);
- 透き通った transferred.

His last resolve forbids hope of her. INFERENCE. The touch may be a dream, and her kindness is, in his self-admonition, work.

**Interpretation C: concealment as care.** Miyagi hid Himeno's plan. The narrator attributes the concealment to care, 「俺のために」 (P0172), and he concludes that she lied "all along" (P0177). The narrator calls the result a good dream (P0181) and the mistake a rescue (P0115). Her warnings (U03, U07) are now explained (P0117). Weakener: her motives are given only by the narrator, from a later standpoint. The scope of the lie is unnamed.
