## 2. Entering context and exposure boundary

**Entering frontier.** U08, accepted in commit `eb8c6c6e`. The precise entering state is U08 §12.

**State at entry.** It is just before dawn on day four, at the home-town station.
- **U08.** Kusunoki began and abandoned an intended sexual assault on Miyagi. She told him her history (testimony): she sold about thirty years of time after inheriting her mother's debt, and her lifespan was valued, like his, at ¥10,000 a year. She apologized for her harshness.
- **His resolve.** He resolved to be remembered as the watched person she found easiest to be near.
- **Open items.**
  - His capsule letter is pocketed, its content not narrated.
  - A letter-like 〔傍点〕あれ〔/傍点〕 at seventeen is withheld.
  - His farewell letter is unsent.

**Forecasts.** Open claims are `TDH-C001`–`C039`. The principal froze three forecasts before exposure (F-U09-a–c). F-U09-a is title-derived. Prior parametric exposure is declared and quarantined.

**Paratext caution.** The admitted contents page gives later chapter titles. One of them, chapter 15's 「賢者の贈り物」, coincides with the title of the O. Henry story he reads in this unit (P0111). The coincidence is recorded here once, as an observation about paratext. No inference about later events is drawn from it. **Terms.**

- **"Himeno's letter," "his reply," "the exchange."** The letter Himeno sent him in the summer he was seventeen; his reply two weeks later; the two together (P0054–P0064). Ch07 P0015's withheld 〔傍点〕あれ〔/傍点〕 is identified with his reply by strong inference (§4.4).

## 3. Current-unit story synopsis

**A thousand cranes.** For several days after the station night, Kusunoki stays in his four-and-a-half-mat room and folds paper cranes, going out only to eat (P0004). Is it a senbazuru, Miyagi asks, and what for? "For the happy remaining life of a dying man," he says (P0005–P0010). The meaningless work pleases him. Cranes of nine colours fill the room, and he asks whether any prayer is purer than an act that is meaningless and beautiful (P0011–P0014).

He often wants to talk to Miyagi but tries not to start conversations: he must not make her his emotional support, which seems unfair (P0015). She, in his reading, is softening; when their eyes meet she now properly looks away, which he counts warmer than being stared at like an object (P0016). Perhaps she opened up at the station, he thinks, or perhaps watchers are instructed to grow gentler as death nears; she is beside him only as her job, and forgetting that would invite betrayal (P0017–P0018). After five days he finds several cranes too well made to be his, and supposes some busybody folded them while he slept. He hangs the senbazuru from the ceiling (P0019–P0021).

**The capsule letter.** That night, emptying his jeans pockets, he finds his capsule letter and rereads it; he had already read it once (P0024–P0028). Addressed by his ten-year-old self to himself in ten years, it asks something the boy can ask only of him: if he is still "unsold," to go and see Himeno, because each seems unable to manage without the other (P0031–P0035).

He deliberately shows it to Miyagi. She remarks, as if impressed, that he was an honest, kind child, and asks what he will do (P0037–P0038). He will go and see Himeno, he says. He knows it is foolish to cling to a childhood friend unseen for ten years, but it is 〔傍点〕十年前の俺の頼み〔/傍点〕, *the request of me ten years ago*, and he wants to respect it. He may be hurt or disappointed more, but he must see for himself. He wants to talk with her once more, and to give her the ¥300,000 from his lifespan, less what he has spent, in return for her having given him a life. He expects Miyagi to object, he says, addressing her by name, but it is his money (P0039). She will not stop him, she says; it is not that she cannot understand the feeling (P0040). He is taken aback (P0041). He did not think about her words then (P0042). Later, the narrator says, recalling them, he would understand: far from merely understanding, she knew that feeling, long before him (P0043–P0046).

He will try her family home tomorrow morning, he says; she lives there now? Since parting from her husband she seems to have depended on it, Miyagi says (P0047–P0048). She glances up at him; he supposes she fears another unreasonable outburst at her speaking of Himeno. Out of character, he thanks her, and she answers, seemingly relieved (P0049–P0051).

**The exchange at seventeen.** He knows Himeno's later address because of a letter she sent him in the summer he was seventeen (P0054). It struck him as strangely unlike her: ordinary news of exam study and plans, in a round hand, with no sarcasm or abuse (P0055–P0060). Two weeks later he replied in the same vein, adding that he would be glad if she visited (P0061). No answer came, and she did not visit (P0062–P0063). He had meant, he thinks, to write honestly, at great effort, that he wanted to see her (P0064). Probably, the narrator infers, she was by then already pregnant by the man she would marry at eighteen and divorce the next year (P0065). Her letter at least gave him her address (P0066).

**Two on a moped.** He needs a university library computer to locate the house. Kicking his moped into life, he remembers that he must not go more than a hundred metres from Miyagi. "That's right," she says: she is sorry, but he cannot go far on his own; the bike seats two, though, doesn't it (P0069–P0071)? His second-hand Cub 110 has a tandem seat; there is no spare helmet, but no one can see her (P0072). She climbs on with "Excuse me" and rests her hands on his stomach (P0073–P0075). He rides slowly through a nostalgic morning under a towering cumulonimbus, where outlines look sharper than usual yet hollow (P0076–P0078). The university feels aloof; the students seem happy creatures of another world (P0079). He prints a map and breakfasts from a vending machine; Miyagi buys a doughnut (P0080).

**Miyagi's three wishes.** How would she spend her last few months, he asks? She cannot know until then, she says, and warns him again that people will take him for a strange man talking to himself (P0081–P0082). He does not mind; he is strange. People in the lounge do eye him suspiciously. Perhaps, he reflects, he would rather be remembered as a suspicious person than not remembered at all (P0083–P0084).

Walking out, she says she has thought seriously about his question (P0085–P0086). She would want three things. First, to go to a lake where as a child she saw a starry sky, the only beautiful scene she truly "knows." Second, to make her own grave, even just a big stone, so long as it lasts decades after her death. Third, like him, to go and see someone once important to her (P0088–P0092). Whom, she cannot tell Kusunoki. A man, then? "Something like that" (P0092–P0095). He reasons that it must be someone from before she became a watcher at ten, as he recalls it (P0096). Even at the risk of being hurt, she would probably go, she says, so she has no right to deny what he is doing. He laughs that she is timid about herself; she knows nothing of her own future, she says (P0097–P0099).

**Himeno's house.** The house is found easily (P0102). It bears the only 姫野 nameplate nearby, so it seemed she must live there. It is a shabby, forgettable place, unlike the fine Japanese-style house of her childhood (P0103–P0104). He rings three times, half expecting no one, and no one answers (P0105). He spends the afternoon in the musty city library, reading short stories by Auster, Miyazawa Kenji, O. Henry and Hemingway (P0106–P0110). As he reads 『賢者の贈り物』, Miyagi moves beside him to read the same page and edges closer; a girl with a calming smell, he thinks (P0111–P0114). Reading with someone makes the reading denser (P0115–P0116). Back at the house he waits an hour into the dusk. At Miyagi's reproachful look he picks up his cigarette ends, then gives up for the day (P0117–P0118). He cannot deny some relief that Himeno did not appear (P0119).

**The festival.** A wrong turn brings him near his family home and to a summer festival at the shrine, his first in ten years (P0120–P0122). On a whim he buys one item from every stall, "a boy's dream," and has Miyagi help him eat it; by the twelfth item both are sick of food, and she licks a candied apple with a prim face (P0123–P0126). The scene below is unchanged from that day ten years ago (P0127). As he now recalls it, he and Himeno sat on these steps, feeling they had no right to join the crowd, waiting for "something" (P0128). She prophesied that "something very good" would come in the summer ten years on. As he recalls it, she proposed that, if neither had found a partner, the two "unsold" ones be together (P0129). Now he is in that summer: she, second-hand; he, not for sale; neither with an owner. They are alone again (P0130–P0132). At the shrine showered with higurashi voices, he prays again (P0133–P0134).

Late in the evening, beside him, Miyagi's pencil moves on her notebook. He gathers the rubbish and stands (P0135). Someone climbs the steps, and at the outline his time stops (P0136–P0137). People will call it too good a story, the narrator says, but things connect in perverse ways unnoticed by those involved (P0138–P0139). Joy runs through him; memories from their first meeting at four to her vanishing at ten pass through his head; however changed, he could not fail to know her (P0140–P0142). He calls hoarsely, "Himeno" (P0143–P0144). The woman stops and looks at him with what seem hollow eyes; her face changes, he sees, to astonishment. "…Kusunoki?" she says, in the clear voice of that day (P0145–P0148).

**Ending condition.** It is a summer-festival night at the shrine near his family home, about a week after the station night.
- He has folded and hung a senbazuru. Some cranes he supposes "a busybody" folded.
- He has reread his capsule letter, which asks him to go and see Himeno if still unsold. He has resolved to see her and give her what remains of his ¥300,000.
- The exchange at seventeen has been disclosed: Himeno's letter, his reply, and her silence.
- Miyagi has told him her three wishes, including a once-important person, "something like" a man.
- No one answered at Himeno's house. At the festival a woman he recognizes as Himeno has climbed the steps and said his name.

## 4. Factual and causal reconstruction

1. **The senbazuru.** He folds a thousand cranes over five days (P0004, P0019) "for the happy remaining life of a dying man" (P0010).
   - **Prayer.** The narration calls meaningless, beautiful action the purest prayer (P0014). INFERENCE: this echoes the children praying at the shrine in Ch01 (P0061), and anticipates his praying again at the same shrine (P0134).
   - **The extra cranes.** Several cranes are "too well made" to be his (上手くでき過ぎている, P0019). He supposes some busybody (お節介焼き) folded them while he slept (P0020). INFERENCE: Miyagi. お節介 has passed between them: his accusation (Ch03 P0120) and her self-description (Ch07 P0041). She is never named here, and the attribution is his guess.
   - **The title.** でき過ぎ here anticipates the chapter title's できすぎた話 (P0138) (INFERENCE: a lexical pun). B's HYPOTHESIS, flagged by B as possibly made salient by prior exposure and of low weight, is that a hidden hand lies behind a too-neat event. The rival is a plain defence of coincidence. No agency behind the meeting is claimed here (C).
   - **The charm.** A senbazuru is conventionally a charm for recovery (analyst's cultural knowledge, not stated in the text). Here it is folded by a dying man "for my happy remaining life" and called pure prayer because it is meaningless.
2. **Restraint and suspicion toward Miyagi.**
   - **Restraint.** He restrains himself from talking to her, so as not to make her his emotional support, which he thinks unfair (P0015).
   - **Her softening.** He reads a softening in her: she now looks away when their eyes meet, which he counts warmer than being stared at like an object (P0016). Both the softening and its warmth are his reading. A rival he does not consider is wariness after the U08 assault, which the chapter never mentions. Her last narrated aversions came during and after his threat (Ch08 P0020, P0034). The station night is only 「駅での会話」. 「いつか手酷い裏切り」 inverts the formula "something good might happen someday": first his own (Ch03 P0033), quoted by Miyagi of his forfeited self (Ch05 P0059), and voiced as hers at Ch08 P0084. He resolves not to take comfort (安心) from her (P0015), yet finds her smell calming (落ち着く, P0114) and admits relief (安心) at Himeno's absence (P0119).
   - **Two explanations.** He offers two, both かもしれない: she opened up at the station, or watchers are instructed to be gentler as death approaches (P0017). The second is his speculation about a rule, not testimony. He warns himself that she is beside him only as her job, and that forgetting it invites betrayal (P0018).
   - **Relation to U08.** INFERENCE: the restraint answers his U08 resolve (Ch08 P0091) and his self-reproach (Ch08 P0033) with distance, not intimacy.
3. **The capsule letter** (`TDH-C033` → v2).
   - **Already read.** He had already read it once (一度は目を通した手紙, P0028). That resolves U07's open question whether he read it: he did, off the page.
   - **Content.** It is given verbatim; whether in full is not stated (P0031–P0035). Its 「あなたにしか、頼めないことがあります」 means "there is something I can ask only of you." He asks his future self, if still 売れ残っている, to go to see Himeno: "Himeno seems to need me, and I seem to need Himeno" (だめみたい ×2). 売れ残り is Himeno's word from the promise (Ch01 P0089, P0095).
   - **The best friend's name.** The excerpt shows no best-friend name at its end. Ch07 P0024's inference that he named Himeno is neither confirmed nor refuted, though Himeno is plainly the letter's subject.
   - **Showing Miyagi.** He shows it to Miyagi deliberately (あえて, P0037), unlike the farewell letter he hid from her (Ch07 P0014).
   - **When written.** INFERENCE: 売れ残っている implies the letter was written after the shrine proposal. "Go to see" implies he expected to be separated from her, which bears weakly on why her own capsule letter was missing. The order of burial and transfer remains open.
   - **Strain.** The boy's mutual dependence (だめみたい ×2) strains Ch03 P0025's "slight" liking at ten. The rival is that dependence is not liking.
4. **The exchange at seventeen** (`TDH-C030` → v2).
   - **Himeno's letter.** In the summer he was seventeen, Himeno wrote to him. Her letter was trivial and, to him, strangely unlike her (P0054–P0060).
   - **His reply.** Two weeks later he replied with similar content, by his summary adding that he would be glad if she visited (P0061). His self-assessment is separate: 「正直に『ヒメノと会いたい』という気持ちをそこに書いたつもりだった」, he *meant* to write honestly, at great effort, that he wanted to see her (P0064). No reply came, and she did not visit (P0062–P0063).
   - **あれ identified.** INFERENCE, strong: Ch07 P0015's 「十七歳のときの〔傍点〕あれ〔/傍点〕」 is his reply, the only letter he says he wrote at seventeen. The text never links them explicitly.
   - **Miyagi's charge.** Her question was whether he ever sent Himeno a letter 「あなたの方から」, on his own initiative (Ch07 P0083). His one letter was a reply, so her charge holds (INFERENCE). C's HYPOTHESIS, low weight, is that her precise wording fits knowledge of the exchange.
   - **The pregnancy.** The narrator's 「おそらく…宿していたのだろう」 (P0065) infers that she was pregnant by then. That inference rests on Miyagi's Ch03 testimony: childbirth at seventeen, marriage at eighteen, divorce the next year (Ch03 P0101). The narration states the marriage and divorce as fact, with prospective 〜ことになる and no hearsay marker (結婚し…離婚することになる相手). Only the timing is hedged, and とっくに ("long since") strengthens the content inside the hedge. It also assumes, beyond Miyagi's testimony, that the child was the husband's (相手の子). Her letter's talk of exam study and university sits uneasily with childbirth at seventeen. The chronology is undecidable.
   - **The address.** Himeno's letter supplies her later address (P0066). He has known it since he was seventeen. INFERENCE: going to her door now is the first time he seeks her, and he acts after his child self's request.
   - **The trivial letter.** HYPOTHESIS, reader-side: Himeno's letter has the form he considered for his own farewell letter, trivial everyday news (Ch07 P0013). The text draws no connection.
5. **His resolve and Miyagi's assent** (P0039–P0046).
   - **The resolve.** He frames it as honouring 〔傍点〕十年前の俺の頼み〔/傍点〕, emphasized (P0039). He concedes its foolishness and wants to see for himself. He means to talk with her once and give her the ¥300,000, minus a few tens of thousands, in return for her having given him a life (人生). List item 8's confession (想いを打ち明ける) is absent. The aim is restated in item 7's words, 会って話がしたい (Ch03 P0086–P0093), and becomes talk plus a gift. In 尊重してやりたい the present self grants a favour downward to the boy; 尊重 is Miyagi's word from Ch03 P0126. Repaying the 人生 she gave him with money from selling his 寿命 keeps two words for "life" apart. The money is reassigned from his Ch05 vow to make the ¥300,000 count (Ch05 P0014). In the same speech comes the first use of her name in his quoted speech, by frontier search: 「多分ミヤギはこのことに反対するだろうが」, used for "you." The rival is a quasi-third-person reading.
   - **Her answer.** Miyagi neither objects nor warns, as she did in U03 and U07 (Ch03 P0095, P0112; Ch07 P0041). She says, by litotes, that it is not that she cannot understand the feeling (わからなくはない, P0040), and he hears it as assent (肯定, P0041). The narrator's later gloss calls it 俺の気持ち (P0044). Later she says she has no "right" (権利) to deny it (P0097).
   - **The prolepsis.** The narrator then speaks from a later point: 「後に俺はその言葉を思い返して、そこでようやく本当の意味を理解する」. She did not merely understand; she knew the feeling, 「俺よりも、ずっと前に」 (P0043–P0046). Earlier narratorial look-backs disclosed their content (Ch02 P0147; Ch03 P0027), and Ch03 P0128 claimed no later understanding. This is the first prolepsis to place a later understanding beyond the current story moment while withholding its content. It is also the first forward reference into Miyagi's interior, and it corrects her quoted words (「わからなくはない」どころではなかった) (`TDH-C010` annotation). It repeats the move of Ch03 P0128, where he noticed her sadness and did not think about it. The scope of その気持ち is open: she had felt such a feeling herself, or she knew his feeling before he did. When "later" falls is also open. Her own explanation at P0097 is partial and must not be taken as the full meaning. INFERENCE, low weight: it is connected to her "once-important person" (P0088–P0097). That link is not stated. This knowledge belongs to the narrator, not to the Kusunoki of this chapter.
6. **Miyagi on Himeno now** (testimony; `TDH-C015`). 「夫と別れてからは、ずっと実家を頼っているみたいです」 (P0048, hedged みたい) restates Ch03's divorce and her return to her family home (Ch03 P0101). Her source is unstated. His reading of Miyagi's upward glance is 「抵抗があるのだろう…心配しているのだ」 (P0049): his reading, ending in a flat のだ. Her calling Himeno 「かつて大切だった人」 "like you" (P0088) passes without his objection, unlike Ch07 P0040 (C). His 「ありがとう」 is 柄にもなく, out of character (P0050). Her relief is 安堵したように (P0051), also his reading.
7. **The distance rule reconfirmed.** He recalls the hundred-metre rule unprompted (P0070), and Miyagi confirms it (「そうなんです」, P0071). It is the first mention since U05. She says that he cannot go far on his own (一人で遠くまでいくのは, P0071), and proposes riding pillion. He reasons that no one can see her, so no one will object to the missing helmet (P0072; `TDH-C024`). Her 「私を乗せるのが、どうしても嫌だというのでない限りは」 (P0073) leaves him the choice. Her hands rest on his stomach (P0075).
8. **A public scene.** In the university lounge, people look at him suspiciously as he talks to empty air (P0084). This is the first narrated public misperception since U06's waitress (Ch06 P0060–P0062). Miyagi warns him, as she did 「以前にも」 (P0082). He says he is a strange one anyway. He wants to be suspected: perhaps (かもしれない) it is better to be remembered as suspicious than not remembered at all (P0084). INFERENCE: this continues the memory theme of his forfeited course (「誰にも記憶されず」, Ch05 P0059) and his U08 resolve (Ch08 P0091). It reverses his earlier self-consciousness about others' eyes (Ch05 P0040) and about being seen talking to empty air (Ch06 P0063). His wish moves from passing as a couple (Ch04 P0061; Ch06 P0046) to being remembered at all (C). His stance toward happy strangers moves from anger and envy to estrangement (P0079) (C).
9. **Miyagi's three wishes** (testimony; `TDH-C043`).
   - **The lake.** A childhood starry sky, the only beautiful scene she "knows" (P0090). INFERENCE (B): her three wishes map onto his list items 5 (see beautiful things), 6 (a farewell letter: a trace after death) and 8 (Himeno: a once-important person).
   - **The grave.** A stone that remains decades after her death (P0092). INFERENCE: a wish to leave a lasting trace, like his wish to be remembered.
   - **The person.** A once-important person she will not name to him: "something like" a man (P0092–P0094). He reasons, with 確か and おそらく, that the person dates from before she became a watcher at ten (P0096). Her decision to go despite possible hurt matches his (P0097). Her 「自分の未来については、何も知りませんから」 (P0099) contrasts with her knowledge of his future (INFERENCE).
   - **What is textual.** The person's identity and dating are undetermined. "Before ten" is his inference. His やっぱり (P0093) presumes a man. 「まあ、そんなところです」 is a soft assent, not a plain yes. Not pressing her is his fifth desistance about her (C).
   - **Flagged note** (SALIENCE_FLAG, low weight, B and C). Two readings are possibly made salient by memory. By her visibility rules, the person could date from after ten. And in 「クスノキさんにはいえません」 the は may be contrastive.
   - **Hedges.** Her soft 「そんなところです」 occurs three times in the unit (P0088 「そんなところです」, P0094, P0113).
   - **Age at ten.** "Watcher at ten" is his recollection. In U08 she said she sold time at ten (Ch08 P0072–P0074); she did not say she began watching then.
10. **Himeno's house.** The 姫野 nameplate gives Himeno's surname in kanji for the first time, and establishes ヒメノ as a surname, open since U01 (P0103–P0104). The 姫 ("princess") of the name grounds his childhood sense that the stately house suited her. The house is shabby and characterless, unlike her childhood home, a fine Japanese-style house (P0104). He supposes he half expected her absence (薄々勘付いていた…だろう, P0105). His later relief at her non-appearance (P0119) echoes his relief at an unanswered call (「心のどこかで安堵している自分がいる」, Ch04 P0010). Any decline in her circumstances is INFERENCE from the house. After a second visit he admits relief that she did not appear (P0119).
11. **The library.** He chooses only short stories, perhaps from doubt about his stamina (P0110). The O. Henry story he reads is 「賢者の贈り物」 (P0111). Miyagi moves beside him and reads the same page, close; her smell calms him (P0113–P0114). Reading with another person is a first, and denser (P0116). Every physical approach in the chapter is Miyagi's: the pillion ride (P0075), walking beside him (P0085), moving next to him and closer (P0111–P0113), and reaching into his bag (P0125). No touch of his is narrated.
   - **Her purchases.** She buys a doughnut from a vending machine (P0080), consistent with earlier purchases (Ch04 P0064; Ch06 P0026); the nullification rule concerns sensations she gives to people (Ch06 P0060). It is her first narrated eating, further counterevidence to the earlier 人間味 verdicts (Ch03 P0077; Ch04 P0060) (C). Prompted by her reproachful look, he picks up his cigarette ends into a portable ashtray (P0117).
12. **The festival and the prophecy.** A wrong turn brings him near his family home and to the shrine festival, his first in ten years (P0120–P0122), as getting lost once brought him to the bookshop (Ch02 P0025–P0026; INFERENCE).
    - **Food.** He buys one item from every stall, "a boy's dream," and shares it with Miyagi (P0123–P0126). It belatedly enters the festival the children, as he now recalls it, felt they had no right to join (P0128). Ch01 P0058 gave the crowd's cheerfulness as the reason they did not go down. 「手伝ってくれ」 frames feeding her as her favour to him. The narration speaks of their two stomachs together: 「そもそも二人とも、胃袋はかなり小さい方なのだ」. INFERENCE: on the steps where he sat with Himeno, his companion is now Miyagi, and 二人とも pairs them as Ch01 P0061's 二人 paired him with Himeno.
    - **The scene.** The scene below is unchanged from "that day" ten years ago (P0127); P0127 repeats Ch01 P0058 almost verbatim. He recalls the prophecy and the "unsold" proposal (P0128–P0129; Ch01 P0067–P0070, P0087–P0092). P0129 is his paraphrase and must not be cited as Himeno's Ch01 words. ## 5. Narration, focalization and time

**Prolepsis from the narrating position.** P0042–P0046 is the first prolepsis to place a later understanding beyond the current story moment while withholding its content: 「後に俺はその言葉を思い返して、そこでようやく本当の意味を理解する」. Earlier look-backs disclosed their content (Ch02 P0147; Ch03 P0027). 理解する is a non-past prolepsis ("would understand"), followed by short lines set as single paragraphs (P0044–P0046). Together with the commentary at P0138–P0139 and the conjecture of P0065, it bears on `TDH-C010`. The narration knows more than the experiencing self, and withholds it.

**Embedded document.** P0031–P0035 sets the capsule letter off with blanks, in a child's register (僕, あなた, です/ます) and lines broken like verse. It is the first letter given verbatim, though whether in full is not stated; Ch07 P0010 was a summary.

**Retrospective digression.** P0054–P0066 interrupts the forward narration to explain the address, with an explicit narratorial frame (「…について話さなければならない」, P0054).

**Narratee and present.** The narrating instance shows itself in narratee management (「さて、手紙の話だ」, P0024) and in 「今はそのことを喜ぼう」 (P0066), whose vantage is undecidable. At the climax, 「今、俺はその十年後の夏にいる」 and 「今俺は再び…祈っていた」 (P0130, P0134) may fuse the narrating and story "now" (INFERENCE). The rival is a free indirect story-present.

**Readings of Miyagi.** His readings are marked:

- her being 感心したように (P0038);
- her hesitation (だろう…のだ, P0049);

- **Echo chains.**

- **Commodity metaphors.** 売れ残り, unsold (P0033, P0129–P0130); 中古品, second-hand; 非売品, not for sale; 所有者, owner (P0130–P0131). These extend the shop's commodification of lives to the promise (`TDH-F025`).

- **Registers.** His 「ありがとう」 (P0050) is marked 柄にもなく. Miyagi's polite register now includes 「失礼します」 (P0075), 「いただきます」 (P0125) and 「んー…」 (P0082). She addresses him as クスノキさん (P0092, P0097).

## 11. Competing interpretations, claims and forecasts

**Interpretation A: the past's request fulfilled by coincidence.** His ten-year-old self's letter sends him to Himeno (P0033). The house is empty (P0105), but chance, a wrong turn and a festival, brings her to the shrine of the prophecy, in its tenth summer (P0120–P0148). The narrator names the coincidence (できすぎた話) and asserts hidden connection (P0138–P0139). Support: P0127–P0141. Weakener: the narrator's "connections" are asserted, not shown.

**Interpretation B: two people going to see someone once important.** He goes to see Himeno, and Miyagi says she would go to see her once-important person (P0088, P0097). The narrator's prolepsis says she knew that feeling "long before" him (P0046); using it as support presupposes the reading that she had felt it herself. INFERENCE: the chapter pairs them as people of the same predicament, extending U08's "the same price" (Ch08 P0080). Weakener: her person is unnamed, and the prolepsis's content is withheld.

**Interpretation C: memory and trace.** Several details concern leaving a trace:
- he would rather be remembered as suspicious than not at all (P0084);
- she wants a grave that lasts decades (P0092);
- he wants to give Himeno his money as thanks (P0039);
- he wishes, from U07, to live on in her memory (Ch07 P0013).

The chapter gathers these wishes to leave a trace. HYPOTHESIS.
