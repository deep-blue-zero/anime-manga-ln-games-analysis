# Three Days of Happiness — integrated novel synthesis

## 0. How to read this synthesis

**Object.** This is the continuous argument about the whole of 三秋縋『三日間の幸福』 in witness `TDH-JP-01` (MediaWorks Bunko electronic edition), as [architecture §2](../TDH_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#2-artifact-responsibilities-and-homes) requires. It integrates the fifteen accepted readings ([U01](../readings/TDH_U01_DEEP_READING.md) … [U15](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md)), the [notebook](../TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md) and the [completion audit](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md). It does not replace them. Every load-bearing statement routes to a locator, a claim or state ID, or a reading heading, and the full argument for each local point stays in the reading cited.

**Locators.** `ChNN Pnnnn` abbreviates `TDH-JP-01::text/part00(NN+3).html#Pnnnn::ChNN` under normalization `TDH-NORM-v1`, as bound in the [source register](../TDH_SOURCE_REGISTER.md). Claim IDs (`TDH-Cnnn`), state IDs (`TDH-Snnn`) and form IDs (`TDH-Fnnn`) route to the notebook's [Claims](../TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md#claims-and-questions), [State](../TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md#state) and [Form and register](../TDH_LONGITUDINAL_NOTEBOOK.md#form-and-register) sections. Where a claim has several versions, the latest is meant unless a version is named. Where a reading's §13.2 records an adopted reviewer correction, the corrected reading is the one cited.

**Labels.** `OBSERVATION` is what the text says or shows. `INFERENCE` is reasoned from it, graded where the grade matters. `HYPOTHESIS` is a candidate that the text supports without settling. `VALUE_JUDGMENT` names whose standard is operating. *Testimony* marks a character's account, which establishes that it was said, not that it is true. Japanese anchors are kept short and given a working translation.

**Four voices.** "The narrator" is the narrating instance, the one who writes the record. "Kusunoki" without qualification is the experiencing self at the time of the scene. The two differ in knowledge, and §4 shows that the difference is datable. Miyagi, Himeno and every other figure are reached only through the narrator's prose, their quoted speech and their written documents.

**Character portraits.** This synthesis carries its own character account, because causality and counterreading cannot be argued without it. The person-level portraits are the business of the three monographs: [Kusunoki](../studies/TDH_KUSUNOKI_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md), [Miyagi](../studies/TDH_MIYAGI_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md) and [Himeno](../studies/TDH_HIMENO_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md). They were drafted in parallel and were not inputs here.

**Exposure and outside knowledge.** The author of this synthesis, like every participant in the run, is a language model that may carry training-derived familiarity with this published novel. That familiarity is quarantined and is used as evidence nowhere. Public knowledge of O. Henry's "The Gift of the Magi" is used in §9.2 only, and is flagged where used; the text's own anchor is Ch09 P0111. The afterword (`part0019`) is cited once, in §13.3, and only as labelled authorial paratext.

**Status.** Accepted after independent final validation (architecture §6, Gate 5), recorded in the [completion audit §7](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#7-gate-5--final-validation); its relative links were checked at installation.

**Role letters.** Grades attributed to B, C or D1 come from the run's reading participants under [architecture §8.1](../TDH_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#81-multi-agent-reading-configuration): B is the Japanese language, narration and form specialist; C the character, relationship and epistemic specialist; D1 the independent critical reviewer in its blind first phase.

**The retrospective layer.** The whole of this document is written with the ending known. §12 is the marked retrospective layer: it reconsiders early readings and claims with later evidence while keeping their original knowledge boundaries. Elsewhere, where an argument depends on knowing what a passage could not have known when written, it says so and gives the record's own chronology (§4.1).

## 1. The argument in brief

The novel begins with a classroom question, what a human life is worth in money (Ch01 P0004–P0005), and ends with an unverifiable prospect: that three days not yet lived will "probably" be worth more than thirty years and thirty days (Ch15 P0124–P0127). Between the two, it runs that question through a sequence of appraisals, each delivered with authority and each later undercut:

- the clever pupil's lifetime earnings (Ch01 P0009);
- the boy's private three billion (Ch01 P0028);
- the shop's floor price of ¥10,000 a year (Ch02 P0162);
- Miyagi's narrated forfeited future (Ch05 P0052–P0059) and her "answer checks" (Ch04 P0041–P0046; Ch06 P0086–P0089);
- Himeno's prophecy (Ch01 P0070), scored three times;
- the figure Miyagi gives as the true one, thirty yen (Ch12 P0166);
- the appraiser's price on thirty days of future fame (Ch14 P0165, P0193).

The appraisers are not refuted. The figure that governed the first half of the book proves, by Miyagi's admission, to have been a lie told as a gift (Ch12 P0157–P0172), and value is relocated into something the buyers, by Miyagi's testimony, discount: being treated as present by another person (Ch12 P0183; Ch13 P0016–P0018). INFERENCE, strong ([U12 §11](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation B; `TDH-C057`).

The novel does this through four means:

- **A dated record.** A first-person record whose composition is itself placed inside the fiction: begun about day thirty, finished to "yesterday" on the first morning of the last three days (`TDH-C010` v5; Ch12 P0052; Ch15 P0007, P0010).
- **Prolepses that withhold.** The narration promises meanings it does not yet supply: a betrayal (Ch11 P0154–P0158), a wisest decision (Ch13 P0090).
- **A borrowed form, the answer check.** Miyagi's institutional 「答えあわせ」 is taken over by the narrator (Ch04 P0041; Ch10 P0112; Ch11 P0141; `TDH-F013`, `F028`, `F029`).
- **A fantastic device.** Watcher invisibility literalizes the narrator's isolation and is then turned inside out, from imperceptibility to shared convention to sight (Ch06 P0060; Ch14 P0048; Ch15 P0076).

At the end, lexical chains close one after another: いる/いない, 確か, 三十, 売れ残り, 裏切り (`TDH-F037`).

The resolution is narrated from one side. Four features keep it visible as such:

- Its decisive facts rest on testimony from a self-declared liar (Ch12 P0193; Ch15 P0090–P0105).
- The two prolepses that seem to point at the ending were written, by the record's own chronology, before it (audit [§3.2](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#32-contradictions-retained), item 7).
- Each lover's final gift overrides the other's stated wish (Ch14 P0128, P0142).
- The closing ledger counts only the narrator's own lost futures (Ch15 P0125–P0126).

The happiness of the three days is narrated only for the first afternoon: 「それなのに、俺は幸せだった」, "and yet I was happy" (Ch15 P0099). The span as a whole is a hedged prospect (多分…のだろう, Ch15 P0124–P0127). The novel thus offers a strong romantic resolution and, in the same sentences, the means to read it against itself. This synthesis keeps both in view.

## 2. The frame of the price of a life

### 2.1 The lesson and the parable

The first thing the narrator remembers on hearing that lifespan can be sold is a moral-education lesson at ten (Ch01 P0004). Its structure governs the book.

- **Earnings set aside.** The teacher accepts a clever pupil's lifetime-earnings answer, two to three hundred million yen, as 「一つの正解」, "one correct answer" (Ch01 P0009, P0013). She then asks the class to discard it: 「ただ、ここでは、一度そういう考え方を捨ててほしいんです」, "but here, I'd like you to set that way of thinking aside for once" (Ch01 P0013). The pupil is named as Himeno only later, at Ch01 P0038.
- **The parable.** An unknowable 『なにか』 with unlimited money longs for a human life and offers to buy thirty of your sixty remaining years. What would it pay per year (Ch01 P0016–P0021)?
- **Something like an answer.** The narrator then fixes the outcome in advance: 「正解はない」 was said, 「しかし正解らしきものは存在した」, "but something like a correct answer existed." At twenty he would actually sell his lifespan (Ch01 P0032; `TDH-C001` v1, `TDH-C002`).

The chapter thereby discloses the sale before the reader knows a price, buyer or motive ([U01 §5](../readings/TDH_U01_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)). The suspense moves at once from *whether* to *what it means*.

Three pieces of the lesson recur at decisive points:

- **The set-aside instruction.** It returns, emphasized, as the key to the price lie (Ch12 P0154; `TDH-F031`).
- **The lifetime-earnings figure.** It is the boy's measure of others and his own estimate before the appraisal (Ch02 P0134–P0138). Later it measures Miyagi's debt, which 「遙かに上回る」, "far exceeds," it (Ch13 P0006).
- **The disposal-fee thought.** The boy privately priced a gloomy classmate's life as something that might come with a disposal fee (Ch01 P0030). Ch02 assigns the thought to the child and turns it on the adult (Ch02 P0138; `TDH-C004` v2).

A further lexical echo has not been registered in any reading. The class clown's joke that no one would pay even 三百円 to live his life (Ch01 P0031) stands one order of magnitude above the thirty yen at which, by Miyagi's testimony, Kusunoki's life was valued (Ch12 P0166). A second, incidental 三百円 is the price of vending-machine noodles (Ch03 P0077), which dilutes the echo. OBSERVATION of the figures; INFERENCE of design, weak.

### 2.2 The appraisal and the first sale

Chapter 2 makes the parable an institution ([U02 §4](../readings/TDH_U02_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-S011`).

- **The figure.** The female clerk later named Miyagi reports the floor price, 「最低買取価格の一万円」, and thirty years and three months of remaining life, about ¥300,000 in all (Ch02 P0162).
- **The criteria.** They are relayed as hearsay from a separate advisory body: happiness, realization and contribution (Ch02 P0170).
- **The disclaimer.** She calls the figure 「普遍的な価値を示すものではありません」, "not a statement of universal value" (Ch02 P0167).
- **Kusunoki's response.** He finds the fairness crushing (Ch02 P0171–P0172). He sells all but three months (Ch02 P0181).

His stated reasons are habituation to cheap selling and a wish to leave 「この店からも。この人生からも」, "this shop, and this life" (Ch02 P0181, P0184). Money is not cited for the final decision, and the three months kept are not explained (`TDH-C009`).

The narrator surrounds the scene with judgments that the experiencing self could not make:

- **The salutary frame.** He perhaps needed to be denied completely, so selling was 「正解だったのだろう」, "probably the right thing" (Ch02 P0123–P0125).
- **The intuition about the clerk.** 「あの女が関わっているところに、嘘はない」, "where that woman is involved, there are no lies," is discredited in the next breath: 「後になって振り返ってみると、俺の勘がいかに当てにならないか」 (Ch02 P0146–P0147).

Both are retrospective, and §4.1 dates both to day thirty or later; the first is also hedged. The salutary frame, "deflation as cure," is the narrator's, not the event's ([U02 §11](../readings/TDH_U02_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretations A and B).

**What the appraisal establishes.** OBSERVATION: the narration confirms that the shop exists (Ch02 P0073) and that Kusunoki felt the loss in his body (Ch02 P0187). It never confirms the valuation, the criteria or the remaining-life figure. These are the shop's claims, accepted by him on a bodily sense he later calls unreliable (`TDH-C012` v1). That status never changes. No figure in the novel is ever checked by anyone outside the institution and its staff (`TDH-C012` 1b–1d; audit [§3.1](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#31-retrospective-layer-early-claims-read-with-later-evidence)).

### 2.3 Market idiom for persons: the unsold and the sold

U01 proposed, as a HYPOTHESIS, that the novel's two subjects form one design. On this proposal, the market idiom that prices a life also prices a marriageable person, and "the man who stays 'unsold' for one person is the man who sells his years" ([U01 §11](../readings/TDH_U01_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C; `TDH-F005`).

Chapters 1–9 bear this out. INFERENCE, strong for those chapters. The marriage-market idiom (売れ残り, 中古品, 非売品) last occurs at Ch09 P0130; afterwards the market idiom prices lives, not marriageability.

- **The proposal.** Himeno's retracted proposal is phrased in that idiom: 「売れ残り同士、一緒になりませんか？」, "shall we two leftovers get together?" (Ch01 P0089).
- **His fidelity.** It is a display of remaining 〝売れ残っている〟 (Ch01 P0095).
- **The dream.** The dream-Himeno asks, emphasized, whether he is still *unsold* (Ch03 P0017; `TDH-F011`).
- **The festival.** At the festival he reprices both of them: she 中古品, "second-hand"; he 非売品, "not for sale"; neither with an 所有者, "owner" (Ch09 P0130–P0131; `TDH-F025`). 中古品 is his VALUE_JUDGMENT, made on Miyagi's testimony about Himeno's marriage. The standard it applies is the market idiom the children shared.
- **Love as investment.** His family account extends the idiom: love as investment in the better product (Ch04 P0071; [U04 §11](../readings/TDH_U04_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C).

One further reading is not drawn by the text and has weak support. In the middle of the book Kusunoki and Miyagi are both, by her testimony, priced at or near the floor (Ch08 P0080–P0082, doubtful since Ch12, `TDH-C038`; Ch12 P0166). The leftovers idiom is never applied to them: 売れ残り last occurs at Ch09 P0130, where Kusunoki calls himself 非売品, "not for sale." By the end both are revalued upward: his thirty days sell "unbelievably high" (Ch14 P0193), and her value, by her testimony, rose (Ch15 P0105). That Himeno's "two leftovers" proposal is realized with Miyagi is therefore a HYPOTHESIS, low. If the reading is kept, its better anchor is the prophecy's 私たち, answered as a joke through Miyagi's お金持ち (Ch01 P0070; Ch15 P0106–P0107; §2.6), not the floor price. No sentence links Ch01 P0089 to the ending.

### 2.4 Thirty yen: the price lie and the teacher's key

The governing figure of chapters 2–11 is overturned in Ch12, by a route the text makes inspectable ([U12 §4.7](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-C001` v3, `TDH-C057`).

1. **The hint.** The substitute, the watcher who stands in for Miyagi during her two-day absence (Ch12 P0004–P0012), laughs at his belief: 「本当に、自分の寿命が三十万だっていわれて、疑いもせずに信じちまったのか？」, "you really believed, without a doubt, that your life was worth three hundred thousand?" (Ch12 P0094).
2. **The key.** Kusunoki reasons overnight. The narration places the teacher's words beside his reasoning, emphasized: 〔傍点〕ここでは〔/傍点〕、〔傍点〕一度そういう考え方を捨ててほしいんです〔/傍点〕 (Ch12 P0154).
3. **His question.** Why had he believed that a life must sell for millions? 「どうして見ず知らずの俺に、〔傍点〕あんたが〔/傍点〕三十万も渡す気になったんだ？」, "why did *you* decide to give a stranger three hundred thousand?" (Ch12 P0155–P0157).
4. **Denial and concession.** Miyagi denies several times, then concedes: 「……三十円です」 (Ch12 P0140–P0166). The ¥300,000 was hers (Ch12 P0170).

The status of the thirty yen is testimony. The substitute only hinted, and Kusunoki's own two reports of what he said disagree: 「もう一回確認してみろっていわれただけ」, "he only told me to check again" (Ch12 P0147), against 「ミヤギが嘘をついていると聞いたとき」, "when I heard that Miyagi was lying" (Ch12 P0150). That is contradiction 2 in audit [§3.2](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#32-contradictions-retained). The thirty yen and the gift rest on Miyagi's word alone, after she has been shown concealing (`TDH-C001` v3 counterevidence). Two rival causal accounts of the lie's effect stand side by side and cannot be decided:

- **His.** Told "thirty yen" at the start, he would have sold everything, not even three days. Her lie gave him his three months (Ch12 P0173).
- **Hers.** Had she told the truth, he might not have sold at all, and would have lived longer (Ch12 P0170).

What the scene does with value is clearer than what it establishes about price. Miyagi disowns the institutional figure: 「三十円なんて、どこかの偉い人たちが勝手につけた値段でしかない」, "thirty yen is just a price some important people set as they pleased." To her he is now worth "thirty million or three billion" (Ch12 P0174). That answers his drunk vow to make the ¥300,000 「三千万や三十億よりも価値のある三十万」, "a three hundred thousand worth more than thirty million or three billion" (Ch05 P0014). The vow was spoken to her. INFERENCE, a lexical echo ([U12 §4.7](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).

The teacher's instruction to set aside the earnings view, recalled at the hinge, dissolves the appraisal's authority without disproving its figure. Value becomes relational, not appraised ([U12 §11](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation B). INFERENCE, strong.

### 2.5 The second sale: fame, eternity and thirty days

The second appraisal values what the first could not foresee (`TDH-C069`). By the appraiser's testimony, given in person, Kusunoki is now set to paint for his remaining thirty-three days 「死ぬ気で」, "with everything he has." The watcher girl would be beside him the whole time, and his name would remain in art history forever (Ch14 P0165). This fits Miyagi's hedged testimony that 社会貢献度 and 知名度, social contribution and name recognition, were 「やたらと優遇されていた」, "heavily favoured" (Ch13 P0018; `TDH-C012` 1c–1d).

Kusunoki refuses the eternity: 「俺がいない世界で俺が永遠になっても、何にも嬉しくありませんよ」, "becoming eternal in a world without me would not make me glad at all" (Ch14 P0168). He sells thirty of thirty-three days (Ch14 P0157–P0158). The narrator closes the chain begun at Ch02 P0122, where he dreamed of being a name everyone knows: 「こうして俺は、永遠になり損ねた」, "and so I failed to become eternal" (Ch14 P0196).

The price is narrated without a figure as 「目を疑うくらい高く」, "unbelievably high." It fell 「あと一歩」, "one step," short of her debt (Ch14 P0193). Ch13 P0006 had put her debt far above the lifetime-earnings figure of Ch01 P0009. So, by inference, thirty days of a man whose whole life was, by Miyagi's testimony, valued at thirty yen now fetch, by the narrator's account, more than the "ordinary person's" lifetime figure that Himeno cited. INFERENCE, moderate. It rests on the narrator's debt figure, whose source is unstated (`TDH-S071`), and on the debt's not having changed.

The substitute supplies the irony: 「三十年より価値のある三十日、か」, "thirty days worth more than thirty years, huh" (Ch14 P0194). The 三十 ledger closes over the book: 三十億, 三十万, 三十円, 三十年, 三十日, 三十三日, 三日, 三年 (`TDH-F036`).

The purpose of the sale is never stated in Ch14. That it serves her debt is a strong INFERENCE (`TDH-C061` v3). It is stated only in Ch15: 「すべてを犠牲にして守ろうとした相手」, "the one I tried to protect by sacrificing everything" (Ch15 P0098; `TDH-C061` v4).

### 2.6 Her sale and "rich"

Miyagi's return supplies the last valuation, all of it her testimony (Ch15 P0090–P0105; `TDH-C071`).

- **The substitute's relay.** He told her that Kusunoki had cut his lifespan to the limit and repaid most of her debt (Ch15 P0096).
- **Her sale.** She sold "the same as you," 「全部売っちゃいました。あと三日しか、残ってません」, "I sold it all; I have only three days left" (Ch15 P0094). The object is unexpressed; lifespan is a strong inference.
- **Her value.** Thanks to him, her life's value 「ちょっとは上がっていたみたいなんです」, "seems to have gone up a little" (Ch15 P0105). Her debt is paid, with money left over that three days could not spend (Ch15 P0105).

He answers 「お金持ちか」, "rich, then," and she 「ええ。お金持ちです」, "yes, rich" (Ch15 P0106–P0107). This answers, by lexical inference, the half of Himeno's prophecy that Ch14 had judged unfulfilled: 「俺は、最後まで、偉くも、お金持ちにもなれなかった」, "to the last I became neither great nor rich" (Ch14 P0199; Ch01 P0070; [U15 §4.14](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). The riches are hers, not his: he has ¥187 (Ch15 P0008) and remains "neither great nor rich." The prophecy's 私たち, "us," is answered as a joke, through Miyagi, with riches that three days cannot spend.

### 2.7 What the novel does with value

**The movement.** The movement is clear: from appraisal by an institution to value conferred between two people. Three points mark it:

- the teacher's discarded "one correct answer" (Ch01 P0013) returns as the key (Ch12 P0154);
- the buyers' metric, which discounts subjective happiness (Ch13 P0016–P0018), is set aside by Miyagi (Ch12 P0174);
- the last line measures three days against the futures the appraisers forecast, not against money (Ch15 P0124–P0127).

INFERENCE, strong.

**Value converted, not escaped.** The movement is not an escape from money. Every gift in the novel is a transaction:

- a lie that, by Miyagi's admission, moved ¥300,000 (Ch12 P0170);
- a second sale whose proceeds, by the narrator's account, fell just short of her debt, and by the substitute's report relayed by her repaid most of it (Ch14 P0193; Ch15 P0096);
- a third sale whose proceeds, by her testimony, paid the rest with money over (Ch15 P0105).

The scale that, by the appraiser's testimony and the narrator's hearsay of the outcome, nearly frees Miyagi is the shop's own scale, fame and contribution (Ch14 P0165, P0193). His value "rises" by the criteria he refuses, and she says hers rose by his presence (Ch15 P0105). The novel relocates the *source* of value but keeps money as its *vehicle*, and lets the institution's metric pay for the relational outcome.

This is a counterreading of moderate strength (§11.5). It does not reverse the movement. It qualifies any reading of the ending as a repudiation of pricing. That the novel endorses the shop's metric is not established; that it repudiates the metric is not established either. Miyagi's VALUE_JUDGMENT against the thirty yen (Ch12 P0174) is hers. The narrator's last valuation is hedged and comparative (Ch15 P0124–P0127).

### 3.2 Disclosure order

The novel discloses outcomes early and meanings late. In each case below the narration holds back, until the point of consequence, something the narrator knew or the experiencing self had ([U06 §5](../readings/TDH_U06_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time); [U10 §5](../readings/TDH_U10_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)).

Two effects follow. First, suspense is almost never about *what happens*. The sale, the separation, Himeno's disappearance at the dinner (Ch10 P0154, emphasized before her letter explains it) and the second sale (before its rationale) are told before their causes ([U10 §5](../readings/TDH_U10_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time), "effect before cause"; [U14 §5](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)). Second, the reader's judgment of Miyagi is managed by these withholdings. Her early verdicts are given flat (Ch04 P0046; Ch05 P0052–P0059), and their status as possibly lies is disclosed only at Ch10 P0177 and Ch12 P0193. INFERENCE, moderate: a first-time reader meets her, from Ch03 to Ch09, chiefly as the voice of the system. The narrator, who knew at writing time that she had lied about Himeno (§4.1), lets that impression stand. He flags only the unreliability of his first intuition about her (Ch02 P0147), a sad look he did not pursue (Ch03 P0128) and, from Ch10, that she knew more than she said (Ch10 P0074–P0077).

### 3.3 Causal accounts and their rivals

The novel's main causal movements, each with initiating conditions, the choice and the textual connection, and with the rivals the readings retained:

1. **The first sale** (`TDH-C009`). The conditions are poverty, collapse and a day of selling his possessions (Ch02 P0004–P0011). The stated causes are habituation and a wish to exit (Ch02 P0181, P0184). Rivals: despair produced by the verdict (Ch02 P0172); the quasi-logic that worthless years are no loss (HYPOTHESIS); face-saving (HYPOTHESIS). The narrator's "salutary" frame (Ch02 P0123–P0125) is hindsight. By Miyagi's later testimony, the figure he acted on was false (Ch12 P0166), so his choice was made inside her lie. Whether it was caused by it cannot be decided: his counterfactual and hers are opposite (§2.4).

2. **Keeping three months, not three days.** The hope that 「それでも、いつかいいことがあるかもしれない」, "even so, something good might happen someday" (Ch05 P0059, P0070–P0073), restrains him from selling down. In Miyagi's account of the forfeited life, the same hope, 「ある種の悪魔の証明に過ぎません」, "no more than a kind of devil's proof," kept him living on to a lonely death at fifty (Ch05 P0059; `TDH-C017` v2). At this stage the hope is passive. Later, decisions help create some of the outcomes he had hoped for: Miyagi credits his listening and acknowledgment of her in public (Ch12 P0183–P0188); Kusunoki keeps taking her out and speaking her name (Ch13 P0090–P0102), while Shinbashi and the town choose how to respond (Ch14 P0046–P0048). The sketchbook offers an unexpected opportunity, but Kusunoki chooses to pursue the unfamiliar feeling and draw through the night (Ch14 P0085–P0097, P0189–P0191). Whether it was "salvation or trap" (Ch03 P0034) is answered by the narrator's judgment, 「生きててよかった」, "I'm glad I lived" (Ch14 P0201). That is a self-report.

3. **The Himeno strand's collapse.** His capsule letter sends him to Himeno (Ch09 P0033–P0039). A coincidence the narrator calls 「できすぎた話」, "too good a story," and defends as hidden connection, brings her to the shrine (Ch09 P0138–P0139). His confession of the sale at dinner precipitates her flight and her letter (Ch10 P0137–P0200). By that letter, her revenge plan was dropped because he seemed "stranger" than she (Ch10 P0196). By the narrator's account the "mistake" saved him (Ch10 P0115). Miyagi's testimony supplies the counterfactual: in the original course Himeno would have died before his eyes (Ch10 P0183–P0186). The counterfactual is a liar's testimony, and Himeno's fate in the altered course is never given (`TDH-C015` v2; `TDH-C048`).

4. **The turn toward Miyagi.** On the station night, immediately after the aborted assault, his revision of her "inborn" coldness and his resolve to be remembered as the subject easiest to be near (Ch08 P0057, P0091) follow her account of her history. By her own later testimony, her regard for him changed after the same night, because he 「私の話を真面目に聞いてくれた」, "listened to my story seriously," and sympathized; from then he was "no longer just a subject" (Ch12 P0183). OBSERVATION: her testimony and his narration both place a change of regard on that night. INFERENCE, moderate: the turn toward each other as persons begins on the night of his worst act. It is not the origin of everything that follows. His attraction predates it: the narrator dates it to the first meeting (Ch06 P0007–P0008), and on day one Kusunoki savours the 「馬鹿げた──けれども幸せな──錯覚」, "foolish but happy illusion," of shopping like a cohabiting couple (Ch04 P0061–P0063). Her love, by her account, rests chiefly on what followed, his treating her in public as present, and her 「だから、好きなんです」 answers his offer to keep doing so (Ch12 P0183–P0188). The reading is graded in §11.2.

5. **The debt goal** (`TDH-C061`). It forms at Ch12 P0210 and is weighed and found impossible by honest means (Ch13 P0006–P0031). Miyagi releases him and asks for memories instead (Ch13 P0085–P0087). By strong inference he sets it aside and resolves on the "foolish" life (Ch13 P0090), yet it is carried out in Ch14 by the second sale. Two readings remain: (i) it was set aside and revived when a means appeared by chance, the recovered ability to paint, grasped 偶然, "by chance" (Ch14 P0093); (ii) it was never renounced (cf. 「こちらにも意地があった」, "I had my pride too," Ch13 P0029). Ch15 P0098 states the purpose but does not settle which (`TDH-C061` v3–v4). No decision scene is narrated; the plan exists by Ch14 P0111.

6. **The town's convention** (`TDH-C063` v2). Kusunoki's public assertion that 「ミヤギは確かにここにいる」, "Miyagi is certainly here" (Ch13 P0073), and his outings "one shouldn't do alone" (Ch13 P0101) make him known. Shinbashi's intervention (`TDH-C066`) becomes, by the narrator's account, the trigger for a shared convention, 「共通の約束事」 (Ch14 P0048). The causal link from Shinbashi to the town is asserted by the narrator (「シンバシの予想は正しかった」, "Shinbashi's prediction was right," and 「この件をきっかけに」, "with this incident as the trigger," Ch14 P0046–P0047), not shown.

7. **The painting regained** (`TDH-C067`). The occasion is the sketchbook found at night (Ch14 P0081–P0085), hers by strong inference. The narrator's explanation comes much later: he had to draw once 〔傍点〕自分のために〔/傍点〕, "*for himself*," and Miyagi's sleeping face gave him the chance (Ch14 P0190). The narrator never connects the public "foolish" conduct to the painting ([U14 §11](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation A, weakeners).

8. **Her sale.** The substitute's report is the occasion (Ch15 P0096). Her motive is not stated beyond 「話を聞き終える頃には、私はもう、決めていました」, "by the time he finished, I had decided" (Ch15 P0096), and her apology (Ch15 P0102). That she acted "to be with him" is an inference (Ch15 P0121); a rival holds that she acted for herself, to end her invisibility and the debt's life ([U15 §11](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation A, rival, low to moderate). The condition she gave at Ch08 P0078, that she would have sold her lifespan had it been worth 人並, "an ordinary amount," may be met by her hedged account of a value that 「ちょっとは上がっていた」, "rose a little" (Ch15 P0105). INFERENCE, weak to moderate: "ordinary" and "rose a little" are not the same. Her Ch08 valuation is itself doubtful (`TDH-C038`).

## 4. The record and its narrator

### 4.1 Composition chronology

The narrating instance is the most consequential fact the novel discloses late. The claim history `TDH-C010` v1–v5 records it in five steps:

1. **After the sale** (v1, U02). The narrating instance lies after the sale; its position relative to the three months is open (Ch02 P0070–P0073, P0124, P0147).
2. **Later than day ten** (1a, U09). The first prolepsis to withhold its content places the narrator beyond the story moment (Ch09 P0042–P0046; [U09 §5](../readings/TDH_U09_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)).
3. **The record names itself** (v2, U12). 「このとき俺が自分のノートを購入することを思い付かなかったら、この記録が書かれることもなかっただろう」, "if I hadn't thought to buy a notebook then, this record would never have been written" (Ch12 P0052). He writes from the moral lesson onward (Ch12 P0054). By the next evening 「記録が現在に追い付いていた」, "the record had caught up with the present" (Ch12 P0074). Strong INFERENCE: the narration presents itself as continuous with that notebook, begun about day thirty.
4. **Written while dying** (v3–v4, U13–U14). 「俺がこの記録に割ける時間も少なくなってきた」, "the time I can spare for this record is running short" (Ch13 P0004). The narrating present of Ch14 lies after the second sale, at summer's end: 「三十日を売り払ってしまった今」 (Ch14 P0173); 「〝十年後の夏〟が、いよいよ終わろうとしている」 (Ch14 P0197).
5. **Finished before the reunion** (v5, U15). On the first morning of the last three days: 「昨日までの出来事を書き終えると、万年筆を置いて」, "when I had finished writing the events up to yesterday, I put down the fountain pen" (Ch15 P0007). After sleeping and smoking, 「ノートを見直して必要な細部を付け足すと」, "having reviewed the notebook and added the necessary details," he rides out (Ch15 P0010). By strong inference this is the Ch12 notebook and pen, and Ch14's narrating present is this morning. Ch15 itself narrates past the last narrated writing, to the reunion and a prospect. How, when or whether Ch15 was written is not stated, and 「この記録」 does not occur in it ([U15 §4.2, §5](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)).

**The rival.** The printed text may be a later redaction drawing on the notebook, and 「昨日までの出来事」 need not coincide with the end of Ch14. Identity of the record with chapters 1–14 is itself an inference (B's rival, carried in `TDH-C010` v2 and v5).

The record's own account yields a writing chronology with knowledge horizons:

| Writing stage | What it covers | When, by the record | Known to the writer | Not yet known |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First drafting | The moral lesson to the present (Ch01 to the opening of Ch12) | About days 30–31, during the substitute's two days (Ch12 P0054, P0074) | The Himeno lie (Ch10 P0177); Miyagi's childhood friend (Ch11); her absence | The price lie; her love; the rule lies; the debt goal; the second sale; the reunion |
| Later sessions | At least Ch12–Ch13 | Within his remaining life, time running short (Ch13 P0004–P0005) | The price lie and the rule lies (Ch12); the foolish decision and the town (Ch13) | The second sale (until it happened); the reunion |
| Final morning | "Up to yesterday" (the second sale; Ch14), then review and "necessary details" | First morning of the last three days (Ch15 P0007, P0010) | The sale; her farewell, 「さようなら。幸せでした」 (Ch14 P0150) | The reunion; her sale |
| Ch15 | The first day of the last three | After the reunion; no writing scene | Everything narrated | The three days; any death |

### 4.2 What the narration can and cannot know

The table limits what the ending can mean for passages written before it. On the record's own account, the only narrated review of the earlier pages is the final morning's, when he reviews the notebook and adds "necessary details" (Ch15 P0010). Ch12 P0075 describes what writing did to his memories; it is not a revision of pages. The later sessions (Ch13 P0004–P0005) are not said to revise earlier pages. The one narrated review precedes the reunion. Nothing in chapters 1–14 is said to have been revised after Miyagi's return.

Four consequences follow.

- **Early chapters voice their own time's knowledge.** The verdict on Himeno's prophecy in Ch02 is framed 「今のところ」, "so far" (Ch02 P0015). The figure 「最低買取価格の一万円」, "the minimum purchase price of ten thousand yen," is reported without comment (Ch02 P0162). Both fit a first drafting before the price lie was known, or a deliberate preservation of the experiencing self's horizon ([U14 §5](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)). Ch02 P0138, 「まだまだ自分の価値の低さを甘く見ている」, "I am still underestimating how low my worth is," fits either horizon: it anticipates the ¥10,000 floor, and may or may not have been written knowing the thirty yen. The text does not fix which.
- **Prolepses about Miyagi before Ch12 fit the first drafting.** The Ch02 discrediting of his intuition (Ch02 P0146–P0147), the Ch09 prolepsis that she "knew that feeling" (Ch09 P0043–P0046) and the Ch10 prolepses about her concealment (Ch10 P0074–P0077, P0115–P0117) are all compatible with a first drafting on day thirty. Each concerns things known by then ([U12 §4.6](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).
- **The two great prolepses were written before the reunion.** The Ch11 "betrayal" and the Ch13 "wisest decision" can mean the reunion only through an unnarrated revision (audit [§3.2](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#32-contradictions-retained), item 7). §9.3–9.4 draw the consequence.
- **The prophecy verdict was written alone.** Ch14's 「生きててよかった」, "I'm glad I lived" (Ch14 P0201), was written before Miyagi came back ([U15 §4.2](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction), D1).

### 4.3 Two endings

The record's chronology gives the novel two endings. INFERENCE, strong for the structure, with B's redaction rival stated in §4.1.

**The record's ending (Ch14).** It was written on the first morning of the last three days, after Miyagi's farewell. He then believes she is watching someone else and prays that she will forget him (Ch15 P0012). The record ends with the sale, a failed eternity (Ch14 P0196) and a verdict on Himeno's prophecy: 「彼女の予言は、半分外れた」, "her prophecy was half wrong," and 「半分当たった」, "half right." 「〝とってもいいこと〟は、確かに起きた」, "'something very good' certainly happened" (Ch14 P0198–P0201). That judgment of a life is made in the expectation of dying without her.

**The narration's ending (Ch15).** It begins with the act of finishing the record (Ch15 P0007) and goes past it: absence, doubt, the crowd, the return, her account, and a prospect.

So the happiness verdict of the record does not depend on the reunion. The reunion supplements a judgment already made. Its negative clause, "neither great nor rich" (Ch14 P0199), still holds for him: the riches of Ch15 are Miyagi's, and the "rich" half is answered only as a joke, through her (Ch15 P0105–P0107; §2.6).

The narrated present opens on the threshold of the last three months and closes on the threshold of the last three days:

- **Ch02.** 「こうして俺の最後の三か月が始まる」, "so my last three months begin" (Ch02 P0195). As starts go, he says, it is close to the worst (Ch02 P0196).
- **Ch15.** 「これから三日間、どんな風に過ごしましょう？」, "how shall we spend these three days?" (Ch15 P0121), and a prospect of the greatest worth (Ch15 P0124–P0127).

OBSERVATION of the parallel; INFERENCE of design, weak to moderate.

### 4.4 The reader presupposed and "leaving nothing"

Writing is a site where the narrator's feelings both appear and fail ([U07 §11](../readings/TDH_U07_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C, HYPOTHESIS at U07). The whole novel bears this out. INFERENCE, moderate.

- **The farewell letter.** It cannot be begun without a reader (Ch07 P0004). Its one summarized draft, a declaration of lifelong love, is disowned as containing 「致命的な間違い」, "a fatal mistake," somewhere (Ch07 P0010–P0012). He considers a blank letter, or 「手紙が出したかった」, "I wanted to send a letter," truer (Ch07 P0013). Nothing is sent.
- **The capsule letter and Himeno's letter.** Both are embedded verbatim, and each redirects his life: the first sends him to Himeno (Ch09 P0031–P0035), the second ends that pursuit (Ch10 P0190–P0200).
- **The record.** Kusunoki gives a purpose for writing but leaves his audience open. Asked 「誰かに読ませるのか？」, "will you show it to someone?", he says 「さあな」, "who knows," and calls it 「デフラグみたいなもの」, "something like defragging" (Ch12 P0058–P0059). By Ch13 it presupposes a reader who 「この記録を最後まで読めば」, "reads this record to the end" (Ch13 P0090), possibly rhetorically (`TDH-C010` v3).

Against the record stands the summation 「俺は、何も残さずに死ぬ」, "I will die leaving nothing" (Ch15 P0111). That is contradiction 8 in audit [§3.2](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#32-contradictions-retained). The rival sense is "no works and no fame." The Ch12 answer does not settle who might read the record, and the summation follows his refusal of posthumous fame (Ch14 P0168) ([U15 §4.15](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). His conduct cuts against the strong sense: that morning he was finishing a record that addresses a reader. HYPOTHESIS, conditional on the identity of record and printed text: the book is the trace its narrator says he will not leave.

The record also declares what it does to memory. 「記憶を再解釈した上で文字に置き換えることによって」, "by reinterpreting memories and putting them into words," good memories became easier to savour and bad ones easier to accept (Ch12 P0075). OBSERVATION: by its own account, the narration is a reinterpreting rewrite. That bears directly on reliability (§4.6) and on the self-forgiveness of the close, 「すべてを許せた」, "I could forgive everything" (Ch15 P0118). HYPOTHESIS: the record is the instrument of the forgiveness it reports.

### 4.5 The narrator's habits and the answer check

The narrator's habits are stable across the book ([U01 §5](../readings/TDH_U01_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time); `TDH-F001`–`F037`):

- **Correction of choice into incapacity.** 「参加しなかった。正確にいうと、できなかった」, "I didn't take part; to be exact, I couldn't" (Ch01 P0023; also Ch01 P0053).
- **Motives listed and denied.** Four motives for the glass are each denied, then he admits uncertainty (Ch06 P0090–P0091).
- **Self known from outside.** His own collapse and face are known only by らしかった, "apparently" (Ch02 P0004, P0149).
- **Retrospective labels of folly.** 勘違い, "misapprehension" (Ch01 P0035; Ch05 P0007); 「ここから俺の間抜けぶりは加速する」, "from here my foolishness accelerates" (Ch04 P0004); five uses of 見当違い, "off the mark" (Ch13; `TDH-F033`); and finally 愚か, "foolish," of himself and of her (Ch13 P0090; Ch15 P0100, P0112).
- **Narratee management.** 「……話を査定の方に戻そう」, "let's go back to the appraisal" (Ch02 P0148); 「さて、手紙の話だ」, "now, about the letter" (Ch09 P0024).

**The answer check.** The most important habit is borrowed. 「さて、答えあわせといきましょう」, "well then, let's check the answers," is Miyagi's institutional phrase and the title of chapter 4 (Ch04 P0041; `TDH-F013`). It introduces verdicts that grade his life against a key he cannot see: Wakana's resentment and the absolute 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕, "will *never again* appear" (Ch04 P0046); his parting from Naruse (Ch06 P0086–P0089). The narrator then takes the phrase over:

- 「さて、答えあわせといこう」 introduces his own account of the "mistake" (Ch10 P0112).
- 〔傍点〕答えあわせといこう〔/傍点〕, emphasized, introduces his own conclusions about Himeno (Ch11 P0141). The capsule was dug up by Himeno; her letter at seventeen was 〔傍点〕ヒメノなりのＳＯＳでした〔/傍点〕, "*it was Himeno's SOS*"; she will soon die (Ch11 P0142–P0148; `TDH-F029`).

The answer-giver passes from Miyagi to the narrator, and the form lends his inferences borrowed authority (`TDH-F029`). The novel then shows that answer checks vary in reliability:

- **The SOS thesis.** It is never confirmed by Himeno. The narration endorses only that 「俺はもっと早くに気付くべきだったのだ」, "I should have noticed sooner" (Ch11 P0147; `TDH-C042` 1b).
- **The price-lie deduction.** It is borne out by Miyagi's testimony (Ch12 P0166; [U12 §9](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#9-ordinary-life-texture-and-counterexamples)).
- **The last "answer."** It is Miyagi's again: 「答えは単純ですよ。つまり、私も、あなたと同じことをしたんです」, "the answer is simple: I did the same as you" (Ch15 P0090). OBSERVATION: the 答えあわせ form begins with Miyagi (Ch04 P0041; Ch06 P0087), and the novel's last 答え is hers (Ch15 P0090). The novel's first answers are not hers: they are the clever pupil's lifetime earnings and the teacher's 正解 (Ch01 P0009, P0013, P0021, P0032). INFERENCE, weak, that the return of the last answer to Miyagi is designed.

### 4.6 Focalization and other minds

Every other person is reached through Kusunoki. His readings are typically marked as his. Of Miyagi on day one, the melancholy and the sadness are hedged with 心なしか, "faintly, it seemed," and 「機械じみている」, "machine-like," is hedged with 「とでもいうか」, "one might say." "Emotionless eyes" (感情のない目) and 「人間味がないのだ」, "she lacks human warmth," are unhedged (Ch03 P0045, P0049, P0077, P0128; [U03 §5](../readings/TDH_U03_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)). The town's reactions are known later, by hearsay, with the route unstated (らしい, ようなのだ, Ch13 P0102). That the town accepted Miyagi 「共通の約束事として」, "as a shared convention," and not as belief, is his account of other people's states of mind (Ch14 P0048). Miyagi's interior is reached only through her speech, her documents and his readings. Her notebook is never read by him (Ch12 P0049). Her sketchbook is hers only by strong inference (Ch14 P0081).

Two consequences limit any account of the relationship. First, every statement of what Miyagi felt is either her testimony or his reading. The two coincide often but not always, and his readings were sometimes contradicted by her later testimony (Ch10 P0256 against Ch12 P0183–P0188). Second, the narrator's knowledge of Miyagi is datable by §4.1, and he presents it as reinterpreted memory (Ch12 P0075). Relationship evidence stays directional (§6–§8).

## 5. Presence and perception

### 5.1 The rules of seeing, as testimony

Every rule the novel gives about who can perceive a watcher comes from testimony, mostly Miyagi's. The narration demonstrates parts of it, with the waitress, Naruse and the cats. Some of it is later declared a lie, and no one outside the system ever states a rule (`TDH-C024` v1–v3; `TDH-S035`, `S040`, `S061`, `S065`).

**What Miyagi says.**

- **Day one.** 「私のことは、いないと思っていただいて結構ですから」, "please feel free to think of me as not here" (Ch03 P0062).
- **Nullification (Ch06).** 「私が人に与える感覚は、すべて『なかったこと』として処理されるんです」, "every sensation I give people is processed as 'never having happened'" (Ch06 P0060). She is perceived neither as present nor as absent. The one exception is the watched person, whose perceiving cannot be nullified. She shows this on a waitress, and Naruse corroborates it by not noticing her (Ch06 P0054–P0064; `TDH-F018`).
- **The off-duty exception (Ch07).** Off duty, as at the shop, she is visible only to people intending to sell lifespan, time or health (Ch07 P0046–P0049).
- **Time-sellers (Ch08).** Her mother, having sold time, 「透明になって誰からも見えなくなってしまう」, "became transparent and could not be seen by anyone" (Ch08 P0069). Kusunoki infers that Miyagi will stay an invisible person for decades (Ch08 P0060, his inference).

**Further rules.**

- **Animals.** By Miyagi's testimony, with a demonstration the narration shows, dogs and cats can see watchers: 「この通り、犬や猫には見えるんですよ」, "as you see, dogs and cats can see us" (Ch11 P0103–P0105).
- **Machines.** By Miyagi's testimony, vending machines, unlike shop clerks, 「私たちを無視しませんからね」, "don't ignore us" (Ch11 P0068).
- **Watchers.** By the substitute's testimony, watchers cannot see one another (Ch12 P0033).
- **The rule lies.** Miyagi declares two rules lies: the misconduct rule and the hundred-metre rule (Ch12 P0193; `TDH-C059`). The three-day withdrawal is never retracted. The narration treats it as operative: 「ここからは、監視員の目は一切ない」, "from here on there are no watchers' eyes at all" (Ch15 P0005; `TDH-C059` v2).

**What the narration shows.** In Ch15 Miyagi, having sold all but three days, is seen by everyone (Ch15 P0076). That the invisibility of time-sellers ends with the role is an INFERENCE, moderate to strong. It rests on her Ch08 testimony, and no rule is stated (`TDH-C024` v3). It also carries out Kusunoki's Ch11 guess at her wish: 「監視員を辞めること──つまり、透明人間でなくなること」, "to quit being a watcher, that is, to stop being an invisible person" (Ch11 P0196). The text draws no link.

**Contradiction 6.** The appraiser's counterfactual has the watcher girl beside him through all thirty-three remaining days (Ch14 P0165). That conflicts with the three-day withdrawal (audit §3.2, item 6). It may be an unresolved textual inconsistency or the appraiser's simplification.

### 5.2 From imperceptibility to convention to sight

The novel turns the device of invisibility inside out in five stages. Each reverses the meaning of being seen talking to no one.

1. **Exposure** (Ch06). Asking Miyagi what she would like to drink makes him look as if 「誰もいない空間に向かって喋りかけている」, "talking to a space where no one is"; the waitress looks at him as at a madman (Ch06 P0060–P0062). The device literalizes his isolation: his only constant companion cannot be perceived by anyone else ([U06 §11](../readings/TDH_U06_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C).
2. **Welcomed suspicion** (Ch09). In a university lounge he 「積極的に怪しまれたいとさえ思っていた」, "even wanted to be actively suspected." He would rather be remembered as suspicious than not remembered at all (Ch09 P0084; `TDH-C045`). The motive is self-regarding.
3. **Her account of being "present"** (Ch12). Miyagi testifies that this public conduct made her 「どうしようもなく嬉しかった」, "helplessly happy." 「どんなときも一貫して私のことを〝いる〟ものとして扱ってくれたのは、クスノキさん、あなたが初めてだったんです」, "you were the first, Kusunoki-san, who always and consistently treated me as someone 'there'" (Ch12 P0183). His "madness" is reframed as a gift. The reframing is hers. His stated motives had been partly self-regarding, and her 「あなたにとっては、何でもないことなんでしょうけど」, "for you it's probably nothing," anticipates the asymmetry ([U12 §11](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C and counterweight).
4. **Assertion and convention** (Ch13–Ch14). He tells a disbelieving acquaintance 「ミヤギは確かにここにいる」, "Miyagi is certainly here," and asks him to respect the belief as he respects the disbelief (Ch13 P0073). He does "everything you shouldn't do alone" in public (Ch13 P0101). His stated motive has shifted: it makes her laugh (Ch13 P0064). Through Shinbashi (`TDH-C066`), by the narrator's account, the town takes up her presence as 「共通の約束事」, "a shared convention," not as belief: 「ミヤギの存在が仮説の域を出ることはなかった」, "her existence never went beyond hypothesis" (Ch14 P0046–P0048). He states his preference: 「相手にされない真実より、楽しまれる虚構の方が、ずっといい」, "an enjoyed fiction is far better than an ignored truth" (Ch14 P0104).
5. **Pretence, then sight** (Ch15). Alone on the first day, he for the first time *pretends* she is there: 「ミヤギがいるふり」 (Ch15 P0013). The act that had made her presence real to others makes her absence real to him (Ch15 P0016–P0019). Then her voice comes from behind, 「確かに彼女は、そこにいた」, "she was certainly there" (Ch15 P0070), and 「ミヤギの姿は、誰の目にもはっきりと映っていた」, "Miyagi's figure was clearly reflected in everyone's eyes" (Ch15 P0076). Two people testify that she looks as they had imagined (Ch15 P0083, P0087). That corroborates his descriptions as Shinbashi's match had (Ch14 P0030).

INFERENCE, strong: the novel's question "is she there?" moves from perception to social convention to shared sight, and is answered by the scene, not by argument ([U15 §11](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation D). **Limits.** Shared sight proves her presence and visibility. It does not prove the narrator's reliability in general, and the shop's reality is answered only by her testimony and his body (Ch15 P0096; Ch02 P0187).

### 5.3 いる/いない, 確か and 肯定

Three lexical chains carry the presence argument and close at the end (`TDH-F037`).

**いる / いない.** The chain runs:

- 「いないと思っていただいて結構」, "please think of me as not here" (Ch03 P0062);
- 〝いる〟ものとして扱ってくれた, "treated me as 'there'" (Ch12 P0183);
- 「ミヤギは確かにここにいる」, "Miyagi is certainly here" (Ch13 P0073);
- 「ミヤギは、もういないということだ」, "it means Miyagi is no longer here" (Ch15 P0006), and 「ミヤギはさ、もう、いないんだ」, "Miyagi's, well, gone now" (Ch15 P0027);
- 「確かに彼女は、そこにいた」, "she was certainly there" (Ch15 P0070);
- 「隣にこの子が、いてくれるから」, "because this girl is beside me" (Ch15 P0116).

The last makes her presence a benefactive gift to him (くれる; `TDH-F036`).

**確か.** Miyagi's forecast that his forfeited future would contain 「何一つ確かなもの」, "not one certain thing" (Ch05 P0052), is answered by five uses of 確か in Ch13, whose title is 「確かなこと」, "certain things" (`TDH-F033`). The chain continues through 「しかし、確信があった」, "but I felt certain," in the night of the painting (Ch14 P0090). It ends in Ch15: 「確信はある」, "I am certain," of his death (Ch15 P0022); 「確かめる術はなかった」, "there was no way to confirm it," of her existence (Ch15 P0024); 「確かに彼女は、そこにいた」 (Ch15 P0070). The narration ends with two certainties: his death, and her presence. INFERENCE, moderate ([U13 §11](../readings/TDH_U13_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation B).

**肯定.** This chain has not been registered in the readings. 存在を肯定, with the benefactive くれる, occurs exactly three times:

- the children on the shrine steps waited for 「自分たちの存在を肯定してくれる、すべてを納得させてくれる、『何か』」, "a 'something' that would affirm our existence and make sense of everything" (Ch01 P0060);
- Ch09 recalls the same words at the same steps (Ch09 P0128);
- in Ch15 the narrator calls the photo-shop manager 「初めてミヤギの存在を肯定してくれた人」, "the first person who affirmed Miyagi's existence" (Ch15 P0045). Ch15 calls him 店長, "manager"; the narrator identifies him with the photo-shop 店主, "owner," of Ch11 (Ch11 P0079–P0094).

Other uses of 肯定 have other senses (Ch06 P0100; Ch09 P0041; Ch11 P0125). OBSERVATION.

**The sense shifts.** At Ch01 and Ch09 the awaited something would validate the children's being. At Ch15 a person accepts that Miyagi exists at all.

**The transfer reading.** The affirmation the two children awaited is transferred at the end to Miyagi's existence. HYPOTHESIS, low to moderate.

**The rival.** A stronger text-internal route answers the awaited something differently. Himeno takes up the children's 「何か」 at once as その『何か』, the good thing that will happen in the summer ten years on (Ch01 P0070; `TDH-F002`). Ch09 chains the awaited something directly to the prophecy (Ch09 P0128–P0129). Ch14 scores the good thing as having happened, for him (Ch14 P0201). On this rival the awaited affirmation is answered as the prophecy's good thing, not transferred to Miyagi.

**Two "firsts."** They must be kept apart.

- The narrator's: he calls the photo-shop manager the first to affirm Miyagi's existence (Ch15 P0045). That "first" discounts Himeno's Ch10 statement that she could not think his story a lie (Ch10 P0149), which her letter undercuts ([U15 §4.9](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).
- Miyagi's: she testifies that Kusunoki was the first to treat her consistently as present (Ch12 P0183).

### 5.4 The fantasy reading

The novel itself raises the reading that Miyagi, and the shop, were a fantasy. On the first day alone Kusunoki asks: 「ミヤギという女の子は、本当に実在していたのだろうか？」, "did the girl called Miyagi really exist?" Were she, or even the shop, 「死期を悟った俺が追い詰められた挙句に生み出した、都合のいい幻想」, "a convenient fantasy produced by a cornered man who had sensed his death" (Ch15 P0023)? It turns on himself his Ch02 verdict on the old bookseller's tale, 「そんなに都合のいい話があるわけないじゃないか」, "there's no way such a convenient story exists" (Ch02 P0067; [U15 §4.7](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). Ch02 had raised and exhausted a series of rational explanations: heat, a head blow, the old man's senility, a scam, a risky job, a prank ([U02 §5](../readings/TDH_U02_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)).

Against the fantasy reading stand:

- the crowd's sight (Ch15 P0076–P0087);
- earlier partial affirmations, all testimony: the photo-shop owner (Ch11 P0079–P0094); Shinbashi's admission that he senses her to a degree (Ch14 P0027–P0028); the doubly hedged touch of Suzumi, one of Shinbashi's drinking friends (Ch14 P0052–P0055);
- cats (Ch11 P0103–P0105);
- the narrator's assertion that the shop existed (Ch02 P0073).

For it stand the narrator's own doubt, the absence of any independent check on the institution, and the fact that every rule was relayed by its one representative.

Grade: low. The text resists it at its decisive scene. It is kept because the novel raises it in its last chapter and does not comment on it after the reunion ([U15 §4.7](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). A rival reading of the doubt keeps it as a state, not a thesis. It measures the depth of his grief at her absence (Ch15 P0019), and the scene answers it as a fact of the story.

## 6. Kusunoki: development and its limits

The person-level portrait is the [Kusunoki monograph](../studies/TDH_KUSUNOKI_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md)'s. This section carries the account the integrated argument needs: what changes, on what evidence, and where the change stops.

### 6.1 The entering disposition

The early chapters establish four dispositions. In the first three, the narrator's label sits uneasily with the conduct shown.

- **Self-inflation, labelled later as error.** The boy thought himself exceptional. The narrator calls this 勘違い, "misapprehension" (Ch01 P0035). At twenty, before the appraisal, he still could not escape feeling special. He valued his remaining life at about six hundred million and dreamed of 「永遠」, "eternity," a name everyone would know (Ch02 P0119–P0122). The 勘違い label therefore belongs to the post-appraisal narrator (`TDH-C004` v2).
- **Contempt.** It extends from classmates, in his own assertion as narrator (「どうしようもなく冴えない親の生んだどうしようもなく冴えない子供」, "hopelessly dull children born to hopelessly dull parents," Ch01 P0035), to himself, a beaming stranger and the pigeon-feeder (Ch02 P0133, P0138, P0191). Ch06 adds the "ugly, fat, unclean middle-aged" foil and customers as noise (Ch06 P0010, P0091). The narrator revises his self-estimate earlier and more clearly than his estimate of others (`TDH-C004` v1–3a).
- **Non-initiation.** 「子供の頃からずっと、自分から誰かを遊びに誘ったり、会話を求めたりといったことをしないできた」, "ever since childhood I have never invited anyone out or sought conversation myself" (Ch04 P0008). He attributes his refusals of romantic interest to Himeno's proposal, so as to show her he stayed "unsold" (Ch01 P0094–P0095). The attribution is his. Its rivals, misanthropy, incapacity for performance and display, are not exclusive of it (`TDH-C003` v1–v3). The first datable fidelity case is his cutting-short of Wakana in spring (Ch04 P0028).
- **Poverty without poverty of taste.** He has a carefully curated used library and CDs (Ch02 P0008). He rations a cider (Ch02 P0091–P0092) and says poverty seems to have eroded his imagination (Ch03 P0079).

### 6.2 The development, stage by stage

The development is real. Its key turns are attested by conduct as well as by self-report, and each turn has a counter-instance close by ([U01](../readings/TDH_U01_DEEP_READING.md#7-characters)–[U15 §7](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#7-characters); `TDH-S009`–`S086`).

- **Days 1–2: loneliness and its first failures.**
  - His hedged account says death has "normalized" values he had "desperately distorted": 「人並みに、人恋しかった」, "like anyone, I longed for people" (Ch04 P0065–P0067; `TDH-C020`).
  - The account is internally unstable. He invites Miyagi to drink because anyone would do (Ch04 P0074). His resolve to be shamelessly honest yields, as its first want, a performance designed to be misread (Ch06 P0017–P0047).
  - His goodwill toward Naruse ends in ten seconds over a laugh at his drawing (Ch06 P0073–P0078). He throws a glass at the wall on Miyagi's side (Ch06 P0094), then apologizes sincerely (Ch06 P0106).
  - He concludes that the world is kind only to the dead (Ch06 P0123).
- **Day 3: the past closed.**
  - He reads every confidential capsule letter on a dying man's license (Ch07 P0073). He finds his name isolated in the chart he draws (Ch07 P0076).
  - He relishes having hurt Miyagi with a careless remark (Ch07 P0053).
  - He chooses a night on a station bench as self-punishment (Ch07 P0088).
- **Pre-dawn, day 4: the nadir.**
  - He conceives an intent to assault Miyagi: 「ミヤギを押し倒したくなった」, "I wanted to push Miyagi down" (Ch08 P0008). It is licensed by list item 3, "don't resist desire" (Ch08 P0009).
  - He asks the lag before the misconduct penalty and touches her bare shoulder (Ch08 P0012–P0024).
  - As narrated, he stops through imagining the harm and feeling her sadness pass into him (Ch08 P0025–P0030). The rule he had just measured plays no narrated part ([U08 §11](../readings/TDH_U08_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation A).
  - He calls himself scum (Ch08 P0033). No apology to her for that night is narrated, then or later (`TDH-S042`). At Ch10 P0182 the narrator says that the apology was rather his to make, but he lacked the strength to admit 「自分の非」, "my fault"; which fault is not named ([U12 §4.4](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). He later offers attraction as the reason for the impulse (Ch08 P0045).
  - The same night he revises his view of her coldness (Ch08 P0057) and resolves to be remembered as the subject she found easiest to be near (Ch08 P0091).
- **Days 4–13: restraint, hope and collapse.**
  - He tries not to lean on her, and suspects that her kindness is work (Ch09 P0015–P0018).
  - He resolves to honour his ten-year-old self's request and give Himeno the money (Ch09 P0039).
  - After the dinner he gives the money away to strangers (Ch10 P0208–P0215). He resolves to die quietly 「猫のように人知れず」, "unnoticed, like a cat," expecting nothing from anyone (Ch10 P0258).
- **Days 14–17 and after: a way that suits him.** He photographs vending machines as 「俺に向いたやり方」, "the way that suits me" (Ch11 P0077). That echoes Miyagi's unasked 「もっとあなたに向いたやり方」, "a way more suited to you" (Ch06 P0015; `TDH-C052`, echo lexical).
- **Days 30–32: confession and a goal.**
  - He confesses his conduct, including the near-assault, to the substitute (Ch12 P0042).
  - He writes the record (Ch12 P0052–P0075). He thanks Miyagi for the lie and asks to be grieved (Ch12 P0173, P0205).
  - He forms his first sustained goal aimed at another's welfare: to pay off her debt, which he calls 「身のほど知らず」, "presumptuous" (Ch12 P0210–P0215; `TDH-C061`).
- **Ch13–Ch14: the foolish life and the sale.**
  - He resolves to end his life 「これまでに出会ったどんな人間よりも愚かしい人間として」, "as a person more foolish than anyone I had ever met" (Ch13 P0090). He asserts her presence in public; his stated motive is now her laughter (Ch13 P0064, P0073).
  - He becomes 「この町で、一番の道化」, "the greatest clown in this town" (Ch14 P0065), and says he has made peace with the world (Ch14 P0119).
  - He paints again (Ch14 P0085–P0097). He asks her to forget him (Ch14 P0128). He sells thirty days against her plea (Ch14 P0142, P0157).
- **Ch15: alone, then not.**
  - He grieves, doubts, weeps before children and invents a story that she left him (Ch15 P0013–P0040).
  - At her return he says 「たったの三日でさえ、俺はミヤギなしでは生きられそうになかった」, "even for just three days, it seemed I couldn't live without Miyagi" (Ch15 P0103).
  - His summation forgives 「すべて」, "everything" (Ch15 P0118).

### 6.3 What changes, and the limits of the change

Four changes are attested in both states, by conduct as well as words:

1. **From eternity to presence.** The dream of a name everyone knows (Ch02 P0122) gives way to the refusal of posthumous fame (Ch14 P0168) and the renunciation of memory: 「もう、誰にも覚えていてもらえなくたっていい」, "it's fine now if no one remembers me" (Ch15 P0115).
2. **From non-initiation to initiative for another.** The man who never invited anyone (Ch04 P0008) forms a goal for Miyagi's debt (Ch12 P0210). He asserts her presence day after day (Ch13 P0101), and sells his last month for her (Ch14 P0157; Ch15 P0098).
3. **From contempt for clowning to pride in it.** He despised the clever class clown (Ch01 P0031). By strong inference that boy is the one he now says he envied (Ch14 P0119), and is proud that half the town's smiles are goodwill (Ch14 P0117). Ch04 P0064–P0067 concerns envy of the neighbors partying and how he previously reacted to groups enjoying themselves. His wish to shop with a partner (P0061–P0062) and childhood envy of a particular boy’s ability to make himself liked (Ch14 P0119) do not contradict that narrower statement. Ch14 does reinterpret his former contempt and loneliness.
4. **From "the world is kind only to the dead" to "peace with the world."** Compare Ch06 P0123 with Ch14 P0119 and Ch15 P0118.

The limits are equally attested:

- **Key turns are self-reports.** 「世界と仲直りすることに、俺は成功した」, "I succeeded in making peace with the world" (Ch14 P0119), and 「すべてを許せた」, "I could forgive everything" (Ch15 P0118), are his own statements. The object of 許す is unspecified (`TDH-C072`). The town's regard, by his own account, is half mockery, and its view of him is 「可哀想で面白い人」, "a pitiable, amusing person" (Ch14 P0049, P0117). A rival reads the "peace" as his reframing of being laughed at, not a change in the world (§11.7).
- **Consent and stated wishes.** The Ch08 indifference to consent recurs in a gentler key. He kisses her unasked and calls it 自分勝手, "selfish," licensed by the thought that no one will reproach it (Ch13 P0157–P0161). His sale overrides her explicit plea and is carried out on her day off (Ch14 P0142, P0155) (§11.2–11.3).
- **No avowal.** He names no feeling in return to her confession (Ch12 P0188–P0192). No avowal is made in Ch15 ([U15 §4](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction), absences). His feeling is shown in acts, and in the narration's 愛おしい, "dear," of her sleeping face and of her "betrayal" (Ch14 P0080; Ch15 P0100).
- **Thin strands.** His family is never contacted (Ch04 P0069). The account of them stays his (`TDH-C019`). Himeno's predicted death, which he asserted (Ch11 P0148), drops out of his concerns.

**Competing explanations.** Method §5 requires that development, stress, performance and the license of impending death be kept apart. The text gives evidence for each. The license of a dying man is invoked for the capsule (Ch07 P0073) and the impulse (Ch08 P0009). Performance governs the Naruse outing (Ch06 P0044–P0047) and the public outings (Ch13 P0101). The "normalized" values are hedged and internally contradicted (`TDH-C020`). The development the narrator claims is best supported where conduct changes against his earlier stated dispositions without an audience: the goal for her debt (Ch12 P0210), the sale (Ch14 P0157), and the grief alone (Ch15 P0013–P0019). INFERENCE, moderate to strong.

### 6.4 Ordinary life, and why it is not decoration

Ordinary life in the novel is not texture alone. It carries the plot ([U02](../readings/TDH_U02_DEEP_READING.md#9-ordinary-life-texture-and-counterexamples)–[U15 §9](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#9-ordinary-life-texture-and-counterexamples)).

- **Money as a ledger of days.** The rationing (Ch02 P0091; Ch03 P0076–P0079) and his first touch of the ¥300,000, for beer, fried chicken and cup ramen (Ch04 P0059), are kept in view. So are the money given away (Ch10 P0208–P0215), the "last war chest" (Ch11 P0026–P0028), casual labour (Ch13 P0101) and ¥187 (Ch15 P0008).
- **Vending machines.** He wanted as a child to be one. Miyagi glosses them: always there, warmth for money, a clear-cut relationship, permanence. Unlike shop clerks, she adds, they do not ignore watchers: 「私たちを無視しませんからね」, "they don't ignore us" (Ch11 P0049–P0068). They give him the rounds and the album (Ch11 P0074–P0078). The rounds give him the photo-shop owner, whom the narrator later calls the first to affirm Miyagi's existence (Ch11 P0079–P0094; Ch15 P0045).
- **The Cub.** It carries the pillion rides that come out of a rule she later calls a lie (Ch09 P0069–P0075; Ch12 P0193), and his last ride alone (Ch15 P0010).
- **Cider.** It marks the day of the first sale and the first morning of the last three days: savoured to the last drop after the referrals (Ch02 P0091–P0092), bought from a machine after the record is finished (Ch15 P0007). OBSERVATION.
- **The bedtime habit.** Since about five he has fallen asleep imagining the world he would have wanted to live in (Ch10 P0236–P0242). On the night of the sketchbook, what he paints is exactly this: 「五歳ごろから欠かさず続けていたあの習慣、寝る前にいつも頭に浮かべていた景色たち」, "the scenes from that habit I had kept up without fail since about five, the scenes I always pictured before sleep": the world he had wanted to live in, memories that never were (Ch14 P0191). INFERENCE, strong: the most private ordinary habit in the book becomes the content of the recovered art, and so of the second sale's value (`TDH-C067`).

**The painting history** is contradiction 1 in audit [§3.2](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#32-contradictions-retained). He told Naruse he stopped drawing 「大学に入ってからは」, "after entering university" (Ch06 P0074). He told Himeno he had not held a brush since the winter of seventeen (Ch10 P0119). The narration says he painted again at nineteen and lost the ability (Ch14 P0186–P0187). The classification is attribution and unreliable report. Concealment is the leading rival: he would hide the failure at nineteen from both. Loose speech, and drawing against painting, are lesser rivals ([U14 §4.14](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). It remains unresolved.

The bloom is also his chosen theory. The talent would have needed immense time, and he was fated to lose the chance in a traffic accident. The sale and Miyagi's presence shortened that time: 「俺はそう考えることにしている」, "that is what I have decided to think" (Ch14 P0174–P0176). He adopts the accident of Miyagi's forfeited-future account (Ch05 P0056) as fate without confirmation. That account maims; it does not kill. The cause of his death at about fifty is never given (audit §3.1; F-U02-f not answered).

## 7. Miyagi: a figure known through testimony and lies

The person-level portrait is the [Miyagi monograph](../studies/TDH_MIYAGI_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md)'s.

### 7.1 The status of the evidence

Miyagi is the novel's principal witness to everything outside Kusunoki: the rules, the forfeited futures, Himeno's life, her own history, and at the end the substitute's report and her own sale. She declares herself a liar: 「他にも私は、あなたにたくさん嘘をついてきたんです…嘘ばっかりなんです」, "I've told you many other lies… it's all lies" (Ch12 P0193).

The admitted lies are:

- the price (Ch12 P0166);
- "Himeno," unspecified. Kusunoki names it as "barely remembers me," omitting the promise clause of Ch03 P0101 (Ch11 P0197). She does not answer;
- the misconduct rule;
- the hundred-metre rule. Both rules, she says, were 「私の身を守るための方便」, "expedients to protect myself" (Ch12 P0193; `TDH-C059`).

Her 「たとえば」, "for example," leaves the scope open. Never retracted are:

- her biography (Ch08 P0069–P0074);
- the watcher economy (Ch08 P0050–P0060);
- the three-day withdrawal;
- the forfeited-future accounts (Ch05 P0052–P0059; Ch06 P0089);
- her valuation of herself at ¥10,000 a year. That one is doubtful, since "the same as yours" was said inside the price lie (`TDH-C038`).

The substitute corroborates two points of her biography: he bought her time, and she sold it at ten (Ch12 P0033, P0040). His testimony is itself single-source.

### 7.2 What is shown, not told

Against the unreliability of her testimony stands a consistent record of conduct. It is narrated by Kusunoki, and its interpretations are his readings.

- **Days 1–3.** She watches from a corner in 三角座り, knees drawn up, and writes a 「行動観察記録」, "behavioural observation record" (Ch03 P0059–P0063; Ch04 P0077). She buys calorie bars and mineral water (Ch04 P0060). By his inference from a scent, a conclusion he finds hard to accept and puts off, she bathes while he sleeps (Ch05 P0077–P0080). She leans out to watch the fireworks he will not look at (Ch05 P0041). She applies sunscreen "for my own mood" (Ch07 P0045–P0052). She sleeps in snatches (Ch07 P0090).
- **The station night.** She stays still under his hand; why she stayed is undecidable (`TDH-C036`). Afterwards she tells her history, gives her first warm smile in the narration, and lights her cigarette from his (Ch08 P0086–P0089). A self-mocking smile, 自嘲めいた笑み, comes earlier the same night (Ch08 P0059).
- **Days 4–13.** She folds cranes "too well made" to be his (Ch09 P0019–P0021, by inference) and proposes the pillion ride (Ch09 P0071). She reads O. Henry beside him (Ch09 P0111–P0114). She walks soaked rather than shelter alone (Ch10 P0223). She perhaps strokes his head in the night; at narrating time he still cannot say whether it happened (Ch10 P0245–P0252). In the morning she whistles as she tidies up, as she will again on the swan boat (Ch10 P0260; Ch13 P0098).
- **Days 14–17.** She enters his futon (Ch11 P0017–P0019, P0211). She sketches a cat and refuses to show more (Ch11 P0106–P0114). She guides him with closed eyes to the star lake (Ch11 P0161–P0176). She leaves a notebook by his pillow (Ch11 P0215).
- **Ch13–Ch15.** She asks him, sulking, 「……構ってくださいよ」, "pay attention to me" (Ch13 P0027). She draws their days and his sleeping face in the sketchbook, hers by strong inference (Ch14 P0081–P0084). She weeps, pleads and says goodbye (Ch14 P0138–P0150). She returns: 「ただいま、クスノキさん。……探しましたよ」, "I'm home, Kusunoki-san… I looked for you" (Ch15 P0067).

The conduct contradicts Kusunoki's early verdicts of machine-likeness (Ch03 P0077; Ch04 P0060). He revises the origin of her coldness, not its existence (Ch08 P0057), and the word 機械 is never retracted. F-U03-C3 is closed as CONFIRMED in part, as adjudicated at U08 ([U15 §11](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts)). The later counterevidence is overwhelming, but no explicit revision follows. OBSERVATION. Her notebook is never read by him, and its contents are never narrated (Ch12 P0049). What she wrote of him is his inference from the substitute's changed manner.

### 7.3 Her verdicts and their reversal

Miyagi's verdicts on Kusunoki are the novel's most emphatic statements. Three of the first six emphases fall inside her speech: 〔傍点〕例の約束〔/傍点〕 in her Himeno report (Ch03 P0101), and two verdicts on him (`TDH-F011`, `F014`, `F015`):

- 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕, no one who would try to like him will *ever again* appear (Ch04 P0046);
- he has only 〔傍点〕ようやくスタート地点に立った〔/傍点〕, *just reached the starting line* (Ch05 P0019).

Her own confession, 「だから、好きなんです。あなたのこと」, "that's why I love you" (Ch12 P0188), falsifies the first from inside. The text does not reconcile the two (`TDH-C058`). Either the verdict was among her lies or it described the original course ([U12 §4.9](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).

The verb returns, inverted. When Kusunoki argues that a better man may yet appear for her, she answers 「現れません」, "[he] won't appear," twice (Ch14 P0135–P0137; `TDH-F035`). INFERENCE, strong: the absolute "will not appear" passes from a verdict on his loveability to a refusal on her own behalf.

The forfeited future (Ch05 P0052–P0059) is the longest of her verdicts. Kusunoki accepts it 「すんなり」, "smoothly," which he himself finds 「奇妙」, "strange" (Ch05 P0060–P0061). Its status is the testimony of a self-declared liar. Kusunoki's later fate-theory rests on its crash (Ch14 P0174).

### 7.4 Horizon: testimony shaped by purpose

Her statements about her own future conflict, as contradiction 3 in audit §3.2 records:

- decades of remembering their days (Ch13 P0085);
- death at any time (Ch13 P0123);
- death before repayment (Ch12 P0163);
- thirty years alone (Ch14 P0142).

INFERENCE, low to moderate: each fits the persuasive purpose of its scene. To release him from the debt goal, she has decades of memories ahead. To justify visiting Enishi now, she could die at any time. To excuse the gift, she would die before repaying anyway. To plead for his last month, she faces thirty years alone.

Two rivals reduce the conflict:

- **The concessive rival.** 「あなたがいなくなってから何十年経とうと」 may be concessive, "however many decades pass," and not a forecast that she will live them (Ch13 P0085; `TDH-C058` 1a).
- **The single-horizon rival.** Her own account allows one uncertain horizon throughout: freedom at about forty 「それまで生き延びられたら」, "if I survive until then" (Ch08 P0059), in a job whose dangers killed her mother (Ch08 P0072). On that reading the statements vary the emphasis on one uncertain future rather than contradict one another. A further interpretation, that her outlook tracks the relationship, is equally open.

The audit's classification, testimony shaped by purpose, is kept. The conflict is retained, not reconciled.

### 7.5 The double

The novel pairs Miyagi with Kusunoki in sharable respects:

- the same price, by her testimony (Ch08 P0080);
- the same hope formula, 〝いつかいいことがあるかもしれない〟, "something good might happen someday" (Ch08 P0084);
- the same root of despair behind her gift and his giving-away (Ch12 P0170);
- a childhood friend who mirrors Himeno, and 「あなたの気持ち、とてもよくわかるんですよ」, "I understand your feelings very well" (Ch11 P0199–P0207);
- the Enishi scene, in which he sees himself and Himeno (Ch13 P0150–P0151);
- four uses of 同じ, "the same," at the end (Ch15 P0090–P0108; `TDH-F037`).

INFERENCE, strong ([U08 §11](../readings/TDH_U08_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation B; [U11 §11](../readings/TDH_U11_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation B).

The asymmetries are real. Both report rejection by a parent, in different degrees and by different routes. Hers is her testimony of her mother's words, 〔傍点〕こんなの〔/傍点〕、生まなければよかった, "*this thing*, I wish I'd never had it" (Ch08 P0069). His is his own account of being treated by his parents 「失敗作のように」, "like a failure," beside his favoured brother (Ch04 P0070–P0071; `TDH-C019`). The explicitness and degree differ. She sold time, not lifespan, until the end (Ch11 P0156). She is invisible and he is not. Most of the parallel rests on her testimony.

### 7.6 Directional relationships

The two directions must be kept apart ([method §5](../TDH_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md#5-character-relationships-and-everyday-texture)).

**Kusunoki → Miyagi.** The sequence runs:

- irritation and "machine-like" (Ch03 P0057, P0077);
- an attraction dated to the first meeting but disclosed only on day two (Ch06 P0007–P0008);
- cruelty and the aborted assault (Ch07 P0053; Ch08);
- revision of her coldness (Ch08 P0057);
- restraint, and suspicion that her kindness is work (Ch09 P0015–P0018; Ch10 P0256);
- trust at the lake (Ch11 P0182);
- thanks and the embrace (Ch12 P0161, P0198–P0202);
- the goal (Ch12 P0210); the kiss (Ch13 P0157);
- the wish to be forgotten (Ch14 P0128); the sale (Ch14 P0157);
- grief (Ch15 P0018–P0019);
- the "betrayal" found dearer than anything (Ch15 P0100).

**Miyagi → Kusunoki,** by her testimony and his narration. The sequence runs:

- duty and graded verdicts (Ch03–Ch06);
- identification and apology (Ch08 P0082);
- "no longer just a subject" after the station night (Ch12 P0183);
- love (Ch12 P0188);
- "already saved" (Ch13 P0085);
- refusal to forget, and the plea (Ch14 P0129, P0142);
- farewell (Ch14 P0150);
- her sale (Ch15 P0094); "I looked for you" (Ch15 P0067).

Her feelings at the station kiss are unnarrated: 「誰も、咎めません」, "no one will reproach it," said looking down, with its object unexpressed (Ch13 P0162–P0163; `TDH-C065`).

## 8. Himeno: the promise, the letters and the prophecy

The person-level portrait, bounded by her sparse evidence, is the [Himeno monograph](../studies/TDH_HIMENO_NOVEL_MONOGRAPH.md)'s.

### 8.1 The source of the frames

Himeno supplies the frames by which the novel measures its hero:

- the lifetime-earnings figure (Ch01 P0009);
- the prophecy of greatness, riches and a good thing in the summer ten years on, when 「ようやく『生きててよかった』って心の底から思える」, "at last we'll think from the heart, 'I'm glad I lived'" (Ch01 P0070);
- the "leftovers" proposal (Ch01 P0089).

In the shrine dialogue she is the main initiator and source of frames (`TDH-C007`), though the rival of selective memory stands. INFERENCE, strong: the ending answers Himeno's frames rather than Himeno herself. "Rich" answers the prophecy (Ch15 P0106–P0107). 「生きててよかった」 is quoted as her words come true (Ch14 P0201). That the proposal's form is realized with another partner is a HYPOTHESIS, low (§2.3). Himeno is not mentioned in Ch15.

### 8.2 The promise

The proposal is conditional, unaccepted and retracted: 「……冗談だよ。忘れてちょうだい」, "just kidding; forget it" (Ch01 P0092). 約束, "promise," is the narrator's construal (`TDH-C006`). The novel tests the construal in four ways:

- **The dream.** The dream-Himeno keeps it, still 〔傍点〕売れ残ってる〔/傍点〕, "*unsold*" (Ch03 P0017).
- **Miyagi's report.** Himeno barely remembers him, and certainly not 〔傍点〕例の約束〔/傍点〕, "*that promise*" (Ch03 P0101). That report is later shown false in its first part. Himeno recognizes him and says his name (Ch09 P0145–P0148), and her letter's plan presupposes long memory of him (Ch10 P0194) (`TDH-C015` v2).
- **The capsule letter.** His ten-year-old self asks him to go and see her if still unsold (Ch09 P0031–P0035).
- **The festival.** He repeats the Ch01 shrine scene near-verbatim, with pointed substitutions. Ch01's hedged 「祈っていたのかもしれない」, "were perhaps praying" (Ch01 P0061), becomes 「祈っていた」, "was praying" (Ch09 P0134; `TDH-F026`). His paraphrase of the proposal keeps its condition, that neither has found a partner in ten years. It drops "having become great" and Himeno's 情けないことに, drops the retraction, and turns her polite question into 「売れ残り同士、一緒になろう」, "let's get together, two leftovers" (Ch09 P0129; Ch01 P0087–P0092). U09 §4, `TDH-C044` and `TDH-S052` had described it as "without condition or retraction"; all three have since been corrected ([audit §10](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#10-corrections-after-acceptance)).

Whether Himeno ever held the promise as he did is never established. Her letter speaks of resentment and a plan, not of the proposal.

### 8.3 The letters

Two documents carry Himeno's adult voice, both mediated.

**The exchange at seventeen.** It is reported by him (Ch09 P0054–P0066; `TDH-C030` v2, `TDH-C042`). Her letter was 「彼女らしくない」, "unlike her": trivial news, a round hand, no sarcasm. His reply was similar, with "I'd be glad if you visited." He had meant to write honestly that he wanted to see her (つもりだった). No answer came.

**Her letter after the dinner.** It is headed by the chapter's title, 「私の、たった一人の幼馴染へ」, "to my one and only childhood friend" (Ch10 P0190; `TDH-F006`, `F028`). Under a warm salutation it discloses:

- a plan to make herself indispensable to him and then die before his eyes, out of spite, at "that observation deck";
- its cause: 「私の助けの求めに応じなかったくせに」, "you who never answered my call for help" (Ch10 P0194);
- its abandonment, because 「あなたは私よりもずっと、おかしくなってしまったみたいですね」, "you seem to have gone far stranger than me" (Ch10 P0196);
- a closing barb, hoping that at least his short remaining life is true (Ch10 P0200).

She had said at dinner that she could not think his story a lie (Ch10 P0149). Belief and doubt are both in play ([U10 §9](../readings/TDH_U10_DEEP_READING.md#9-ordinary-life-texture-and-counterexamples)).

Kusunoki's later answer check makes her letter at seventeen 〔傍点〕ヒメノなりのＳＯＳ〔/傍点〕, "*Himeno's SOS*," on a new premise that she "never wrote to anyone." It also concludes that she will soon die (Ch11 P0145–P0148). The narration endorses only that he should have noticed sooner (Ch11 P0147). The retelling idealizes her (神格化, "deification") and echoes his own Ch07 plan for a disguised letter, so insight and projection cannot be told apart (`TDH-C042` 1b; `TDH-C054`). Inference from her letter (Ch10 P0194) supports reading the letter at seventeen as the unanswered call, moderate to strong. The "never wrote" premise and the forecast of death are his.

### 8.4 The prophecy, scored three times

The prophecy is the novel's own frame for its present: "the summer ten years on" is the story's summer.

1. **Ch02, before the sale.** 「ヒメノの予言は、外れた」, "Himeno's prophecy missed" (Ch02 P0015). It is limited by 「少なくとも俺の方には今のところ」, "at least on my side, so far." It quotes her selectively: the good thing and being glad to have lived, but not greatness or riches ([U02 §5](../readings/TDH_U02_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)).
2. **Ch14, written alone after the sale.** It was 「半分外れた」, "half wrong": he became neither great nor rich. It was 「半分当たった」, "half right": something very good happened, and he could say from the heart that he was glad he lived (Ch14 P0198–P0201).
3. **Ch15, after the reunion.** 「お金持ち」, "rich," returns as a joke about money that three days cannot spend (Ch15 P0106–P0107). The riches are Miyagi's, not his (Ch15 P0105; he has ¥187, Ch15 P0008). The prophecy's 私たち is answered, as a joke, through Miyagi. That is lexical; the narrator draws no link.

Greatness, 偉く, is scored as unfulfilled (Ch14 P0199) and never revisited; only riches is answered at the end. His fame in the town as 「ちょっとした有名人」, "a minor celebrity," and its greatest clown (Ch14 P0049, P0065) answers greatness, if at all, ironically. HYPOTHESIS, low ([U13 §11](../readings/TDH_U13_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts); `TDH-C063`, which leaves 有名人 and 知名度 unlinked).

The prophecy was for 私たち, "us" (Ch01 P0070). The novel scores it only for him, and leaves her half of it unscored. OBSERVATION. Himeno's own summer of the prophecy brought the reunion she had meant to turn into revenge and abandoned (Ch10 P0194–P0196). What followed for her is not told.

### 8.5 Her fate, and the strand's disappearance

Himeno's fate is never narrated.

- **Miyagi's testimony.** In the original course Himeno 「飛び降り自殺することになっています」, "is set to jump to her death," in two years (Ch03 P0101). Later, Miyagi says, she would have done it before his eyes (Ch10 P0183–P0186). Both statements come from a witness shown concealing, and both describe a course that, by the same witness, no longer applies (Ch03 P0108).
- **Kusunoki's belief.** He asserts that she will soon kill herself (Ch11 P0148). That is his inference, unsupported (`TDH-C015` 2a).

After Ch11 Himeno continues to shape how Kusunoki accounts for his choices: he invokes what he understood as their promise when he starts writing (Ch12 P0054) and retrospectively gives it as his reason for putting down the brush at seventeen (Ch14 P0182–P0184). His return to drawing begins in the pleasure of drawing Miyagi (Ch14 P0189–P0191). Keep Himeno’s childhood prediction of greatness (Ch01 P0070–P0076) distinct from her conditional proposal about marrying (P0087–P0089). Himeno also enters later comparisons: her lifetime-earnings figure for Miyagi's debt (Ch13 P0006), the Enishi counterfactual (Ch13 P0150–P0151), and the prophecy (Ch14 P0197–P0201). The romance is resolved. The life of the woman whose plan of revenge suicide set its turn in motion is left suspended (§11.6).

## 9. The ending and the title allusion

### 9.1 The last day narrated

Chapter 15 narrates one day: the first of the last three ([U15 §3–§4](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-C070`–`C072`).

- **Morning.** He says he had long decided how to spend the days (Ch15 P0007). That stands against his later admission that he was 「途方に暮れていた」, "at a loss" (Ch15 P0103). He finishes the record, sleeps, buys a cider and counts ¥187 (Ch15 P0007–P0009). He rides alone to their places.
- **Prayers.** He prays first that the subject she now watches will not turn desperate and attack her, which recalls her mother's death at a subject's hands (Ch08 P0072). Then he prays that she works on and repays her debt, lives days happy enough to forget him, and finds someone she values more and who values her more (Ch15 P0012). Their premise, that she is now watching someone else, is false by her later account (Ch15 P0094).
- **Absence and doubt.** The pretence of her presence collapses into grief and doubt (Ch15 P0013–P0024). Children ask after her, and he weeps (Ch15 P0025–P0037).
- **The crowd.** A crowd gathers. He invents a story that she gave up on him; why he invents it is not stated (Ch15 P0040). The crowd condemns the absent Miyagi. Only the photo-shop manager defends her (Ch15 P0041–P0049). A runner says 「そのミヤギってやつは、本当にろくでもない女だな」, "that Miyagi must be a real no-good woman" (Ch15 P0048).
- **Her return.** After a section break and a dash, 「──そのとき、背後から声がしたのだ」, "and then a voice came from behind" (Ch15 P0053), she arrives. She takes up the runner's word, 「そうです、ろくでもないミヤギです」, "yes, I'm the no-good Miyagi" (Ch15 P0079). Everyone sees her (Ch15 P0076).
- **Her account.** It follows his incomprehension (Ch15 P0080–P0096).
- **His naming.** He names her act a betrayal and finds it dearer than anything (Ch15 P0097–P0100).
- **Each calls himself the fool.** 「馬鹿ですよね、私」, "I'm an idiot, aren't I," she says; 「馬鹿なのは、むしろ俺の方だったよ」, "if anyone's the idiot, it's me," he answers (Ch15 P0102–P0103). She apologizes; he does not.
- **The close.** It is set in short lines: dying leaving nothing; eternity and memory no longer needed, 「隣にこの子が、いてくれるから」, "because this girl is beside me"; 「すべてを許せた」, "I could forgive everything" (Ch15 P0111–P0118). Her question follows, then his hedged prospect (Ch15 P0121–P0127).

**Absences with real opportunity** ([U15 §4](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)):

- No avowal of love is made.
- Neither asks about the deaths to come.
- Buying back lifespan is never raised, although by Miyagi's Ch08 testimony lifespan can be bought with time (Ch08 P0069).
- His wish that she forget him is never discussed. Her return answers it in act only.
- The grave wish, Himeno, the farewell letter, the sketchbook, the photograph and her knee scar are not mentioned.
- Whether their three days align is not stated.

### 9.2 The gift of the Magi

The chapter title 「賢者の贈り物」 is the Japanese title of O. Henry's "The Gift of the Magi." The text's own anchor is Ch09 P0111. There Kusunoki reads the story in the library, and Miyagi moves from opposite him to read the same page beside him (Ch09 P0111–P0114).

*Public literary knowledge, flagged, not evidence from this source:* the story opens with a sum of one dollar and eighty-seven cents, sixty cents of it in pennies, counted three times. A poor couple each sell their most prized possession to buy the other a gift, and each gift is made useless by the other's sacrifice. The narrator calls such givers the wisest.

On the first morning Kusunoki finds ¥187 in his wallet, 「そのうち六十円はすべて一円玉」, "sixty of it all in one-yen coins," counted three times (Ch15 P0008). He notices 「奇妙な偶然」, "a curious coincidence," and does not name it (Ch15 P0009). INFERENCE, strong, resting on public knowledge of the story: the sum and its details adapt the story's opening ([U15 §4.3](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). No character is said to model an act on the story.

**The crossed gifts.** He sold his last days to free her (Ch15 P0098). She sold hers, lifespan by strong inference, by inference to be with him (Ch15 P0094–P0096, P0121). Her act defeats his purpose, that she be freed and live on. His act had defeated hers, stated in her Ch14 plea, that he not throw away their time (Ch14 P0142). INFERENCE, strong ([U15 §11](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation A).

The novel's version is not a mutual cancellation. Miyagi reports hearing from the substitute that Kusunoki had paid most of her debt; that news prompted her own sale. Her proceeds covered the balance and left money over (Ch15 P0094–P0096, P0102–P0105). What is cancelled is each one's intention for the other, not the gifts' use ([U15 §6](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#6-japanese-language-and-form)).

The word 贈り物 never appears in the body of Ch15. In the body of the novel it occurs only as the title of the story he reads (Ch09 P0111). In Ch15 the title supplies 賢, "wise," against the chapter's 愚か and 馬鹿, "foolish" and "idiot" (Ch15 P0100, P0102–P0103, P0112). 賢 does occur in the body, as 賢者 in the story's title (Ch09 P0111) and 賢い in the "wisest decision" (Ch13 P0090), and in passing elsewhere (Ch05 P0025; Ch11 P0039). That meets the paradox of Ch13 P0090: the most foolish life as the wisest decision (§9.4).

The novel also names its own coincidences as such. 「できすぎた話だと、人はいうだろう」, "people will call it too good a story," says the narrator at the festival (Ch09 P0138). Chapter 9 is titled 「できすぎた話」, and the O. Henry reading falls in it. At the end comes 「奇妙な偶然」 (Ch15 P0009). INFERENCE, moderate: the narration anticipates the charge of contrivance by stating it, and defends hidden connection, 「本人も気付かないところで…繋がっているもの」, "things connect where those involved do not notice" (Ch09 P0139; `TDH-F025`).

### 9.3 The "betrayal" (`TDH-C056`)

**The prolepsis.** At the close of a section in Ch11, before the section break (Ch11 P0159–P0160) and before the star lake, Miyagi's childhood-friend story and her disappearance (Ch11 P0161–P0215), the narrator says he had wholly overlooked one possibility: 「ミヤギが、俺を裏切るという可能性を」, "that Miyagi would betray me" (Ch11 P0154–P0158). It is framed by three parallel lines of praise. She is the girl 「常に隣にいて」, "always beside me," and secretly supporting him (Ch11 P0155). She shared his despair yet 「寿命ではなく時間を売ることを選んだ」, "chose to sell time, not lifespan" (Ch11 P0156). She is a 「優しい優しい女の子」, "a kind, kind girl" (Ch11 P0157).

**The candidate.** In Ch15 the narrator says the one he tried to protect 「俺の気持ちを裏切り、再びその生を投げ打ってしまった」, "betrayed my feelings and threw away her life again" (Ch15 P0098), and 「彼女の裏切りが、彼女の愚かさが、今は、何よりも愛おしかった」, "her betrayal, her foolishness, were dearer to me now than anything" (Ch15 P0100). Verb and agent match. The object differs: "me," 俺を裏切る, in Ch11 (Ch11 P0158); "my feelings," 俺の気持ちを裏切り, in Ch15 (Ch15 P0098). Two of the framing lines answer directly: "chose time, not lifespan" against her 「あなたと同じ」, "the same as you" (Ch15 P0094); "always beside me" against 「隣にこの子が、いてくれるから」 (Ch15 P0116). OBSERVATION of the match; INFERENCE, moderate to strong, that her sale is the referent (`TDH-C056` v2).

**The sense of 裏切る.** In this text the word means the defeat of an expectation or intention, not wrongdoing: 「自分の思い出に裏切られる」, "to be betrayed by one's own memories," immediately precedes the prolepsis (Ch11 P0153). Ch09 forecast a 「手酷い裏切り」, "a cruel betrayal," if he forgot that she was beside him only for work (Ch09 P0018).

**Rivals,** as graded in the readings:

- the price lie, weakened since U12 ([U12 §11](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation A);
- her love as a defeated expectation, low to moderate: her love (Ch12 P0188) defeats exactly his expectation that her kindness was only work (Ch10 P0256), inverting the "cruel betrayal" Ch09 P0018 had forecast if he forgot that she was there only for work;
- her absence at the end of Ch11, very low.

**The composition argument.** The following observation is not registered in the readings. The first drafting caught up with the present at Ch12 P0074 (§4.1). That was before the substitute's hint (Ch12 P0080–P0100), and so before the price lie (Ch12 P0166) and her confession (Ch12 P0188). Every candidate except her absence therefore requires a revision of Ch11 after first drafting, not only her sale:

- **Her sale.** The passage must have been added or revised after the reunion, which no narrated writing covers (Ch15 P0007, P0010).
- **The price lie, or her love.** Either requires a revision after about day 32. The only narrated review of the notebook, 「ノートを見直して必要な細部を付け足す」, "reviewing the notebook and adding the necessary details" (Ch15 P0010), falls after the price lie and before the reunion, and so offers a narrated occasion for such a revision. The later sessions (Ch13 P0004–P0005) are not said to revise earlier pages.
- **Her absence.** Only this candidate needs no revision. Applied strictly, a no-revision reading would therefore favour it, though the readings grade it very low. The chronology discriminates little.

Ch15 is itself narrated from after the reunion, with no writing scene (U15 §5). On the record–text identity, the narrating instance demonstrably continues past the last narrated writing. The record's narrated chronology therefore offers a narrated occasion (Ch15 P0010) only for a revision about the price lie or her love, but every candidate except her absence requires some revision. The composition evidence favours the rivals only weakly. INFERENCE, weak.

**Two levels.** At the level of the novel's design, the lexical answer is the strongest evidence: the same verb and agent, and two framing lines answered. At the level of the fictional record, identifying the betrayal with her sale requires a revision after the reunion that no writing scene narrates, or B's rival that the printed text is a later redaction (`TDH-C010` v5). Both statements hold. The narrator does not say that her act is the one foreshadowed.

**Status.** The controlling statement is the grade of U15 and the audit: moderate to strong for her sale, with the composition caveat (`TDH-C056` v2; audit [§3.1](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#31-retrospective-layer-early-claims-read-with-later-evidence)). The identification stays open at the level of the text's explicit statement. This synthesis adds only the weak observation that Ch15 P0010 supplies a narrated occasion for a revision about the rivals.

### 9.5 What "three days" names

The span of the title takes six principal senses across the body. The list is a selection: it omits, among others, his counterfactual of selling 「三日も残さずに」, "without keeping even three days" (Ch12 P0173), and the counts of Ch15 (Ch15 P0004, P0022, P0105). OBSERVATION of the selected sequence; INFERENCE, strong, of the reversal.

1. **Mundane.** Its first body occurrence is three days spent listening to CDs before selling them (Ch02 P0022; `TDH-F008`).
2. **Solitude as relief.** The withdrawal rule, relayed on day one, closes with 「最後の三日間だけは、一人になれるのだ」, "for the last three days, at least, one can be alone" (Ch03 P0069). The subject is open, generic or first person; the affect is relief.
3. **Escape from Miyagi.** He says he should have kept only three days, and asks whether she too would leave his side then. She answers: 「ええ。どうしても私の存在が気に食わないというなら、そうすることも選択肢の一つですよ」, "yes; if you really can't stand my presence, that is one option" (Ch05 P0065–P0068).
4. **The unsellable remainder.** 「最後の三日間だけは、買い取りを行わないそうだ」, "the last three days, it seems, they do not buy" (Ch14 P0158).
5. **Unbearable alone.** 「たったの三日でさえ、俺はミヤギなしでは生きられそうになかった」, "even for just three days, it seemed I couldn't live without Miyagi" (Ch15 P0103).
6. **Company.** 「これから三日間、どんな風に過ごしましょう？」, "how shall we spend these three days?", and 「多分、その三日間は、…もっともっと、価値のあるものになるのだろう」, "probably those three days will be worth far, far more" (Ch15 P0121–P0127).

Selling down to three days begins as a way to be rid of her and ends as the way both come to be together.

The happiness of the three days is narrated only for the first afternoon: 「それなのに、俺は幸せだった」, "and yet I was happy" (Ch15 P0099). The span as a whole is a hedged prospect (多分…のだろう, Ch15 P0124–P0127). Forecast F-U01-a ("three days" names a bounded span treated as precious) is confirmed only prospectively. The title is paratext and is not evidence of what the days held ([U15 §4.16](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction), D1).

### 10.2 The grammar of exchange and favour

- **Benefactive framing of selling.** The first sentence of the novel frames the sale as a favour received: 寿命を買い取ってもらえる, "one can have one's lifespan bought" (Ch01 P0004). The boy's thought frames it as a favour conferred: 売ってやってもいい, "I'd do it the favour of selling" (Ch01 P0020; `TDH-F001`). The clerk reserves information for customers who お売りいただいた, "have kindly sold" (Ch02 P0175; `TDH-F010`).
- **From conferring to receiving.** At the end the benefactive verb casts him as receiver of her presence: 「隣にこの子が、いてくれるから」 / 「笑っていてくれるから」, "because she is beside me / laughing for me" (Ch15 P0116–P0117; `TDH-F036`). INFERENCE, moderate: the grammar of favour moves from his conferring a sale on a buyer to his receiving a presence.
- **てやる.** In Ch08 the auxiliary of doing-to is hostile four times and kind once (`TDH-F023`). Ch08's 押し倒したくなった, "wanted to push her down" (Ch08 P0008), returns reversed in Ch14 as 「ミヤギは俺を押し倒した」, "Miyagi pushed me down," to weep on his chest (Ch14 P0138; `TDH-F035`).
- **The future as cancelled past.** A life's value depends on how full a life one 送るはずであった, "was to have led" (Ch02 P0058; `TDH-F010`). 「起こるかもしれなかったこと」, "what might have happened," becomes 「絶対に起こり得ないこと」, "what can never happen" (Ch05 P0064; Ch14 P0173). The last lines measure the three days against two such futures, 俺が送るはずだった, "that I was to have lived," used twice (Ch15 P0125–P0126).

### 10.3 Register and address

**Miyagi.** She opens in business-formal speech, 「今日から監視員を務めさせていただく、ミヤギです」, "I am Miyagi, who will serve as your watcher from today" (Ch03 P0050). She drifts to colloquial-polite speech, with a characteristic final から (`TDH-F012`). Her polite register to him and her クスノキさん persist to her last line (Ch15 P0121). Her 「ただいま」, "I'm home" (Ch15 P0067), is the one homecoming formula in her speech to him. Other domestic formulas come earlier: 「おはようございます、クスノキさん」 (Ch12 P0106) and her 「おやすみなさい」 (Ch12 P0135). It answers, by inference, the return she once expected her childhood friend to be awaiting (Ch11 P0203; [U15 §4.14](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).

**Kusunoki.** He answers her in plain speech with あんた (Ch03; emphasized as 〔傍点〕あんたが〔/傍点〕 at the price-lie hinge, Ch12 P0157). His first spoken 「ミヤギ」 comes in Ch09 (`TDH-S048`).

**Himeno and the children.**

- Her proposal shifts from plain to polite speech at the moment of embarrassment, and he answers in her register: 「なんですか、それは」, "what is that supposed to be?" (Ch01 P0087–P0091; `TDH-F004`).
- The children's dialogue in Ch01 runs on 私たち and 俺たち with no vocatives. The first non-dream vocatives between them are at the festival, 「ヒメノ」 and 「……クスノキ？」 (Ch09 P0143–P0148; [U09 §6](../readings/TDH_U09_DEEP_READING.md#6-japanese-language-and-form)).
- His capsule letter is in a child's 僕, あなた and です/ます (Ch09 P0031–P0035). Himeno's letter is in polite 私 style (Ch10 P0190–P0200).

**Others.** The substitute speaks rough masculine speech (Ch12). Shinbashi speaks polite 僕-speech (Ch14). Those registers locate them socially and are local.

**The narrator.** He is 俺 throughout. In speech he re-voices the capsule letter's 僕 as 〔傍点〕十年前の俺の頼み〔/傍点〕, "*the request of the me of ten years ago*" (Ch09 P0039; `TDH-F024`).

### 10.4 Repetition and mirroring

The novel builds its relations by repeating scenes and lines with roles exchanged:

- the Ch01 shrine scene repeated near-verbatim at the festival, with the hedge removed (Ch09 P0127–P0134; `TDH-F026`);
- 「濡れたい気分なんだ」 / 「濡れたい気分なんです」, "I feel like getting wet," his line answered in hers (Ch10 P0218, P0223; `TDH-F028`);
- her deceiving hypothetical, which leads him blind to the stars, answered by his, which leads her to fireflies (Ch11 P0177; Ch12 P0118–P0127; `TDH-F032`);
- 「自由に受け取る」, "take it as you like," exchanged and reversed (Ch10 P0038–P0040; Ch12 P0132–P0134);
- 「たくさん悲しんでくれ」, "grieve a lot" (Ch12 P0205), reversed into 「忘れてくれ」, "forget me" (Ch14 P0128);
- 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕 inverted into 「現れません」 (Ch04 P0046; Ch14 P0135–P0137);
- the runner's 「ろくでもない」, "no-good," adopted by Miyagi (Ch15 P0048, P0063, P0079);
- 同じ, "the same," four times at the end (Ch15 P0090–P0108).

Taken together, the mirrored exchanges are the formal correlate of the double (§7.5). INFERENCE, strong.

### 10.5 Motif and rhythm

**Motifs** (`TDH-F008`, `F020`, `F025`, `F030`, `F036`):

- **The three and thirty ledger** (§2.5).
- **Vending machines** (§6.4).

### 10.6 Paratext and images

These items are paratext and are not narrative evidence:

- **The cover.** A young woman with shoulder-length hair, a drink can and a folder stands at a railing beside a young man seen from behind. The frontispiece repeats the scene without her. Audit [§3.3](../audits/TDH_U15_AUDIT.md#33-forecast-closure) confirms by paratext inference, moderate, that she is a figure of the narrator's present (F-U01-g). ### 10.7 Quotation marks

The record uses three kinds of quotation mark, and the notebook's form rows use them as evidence (`TDH-F002`, `F005`, `F007`, `F016`, `F018`, `F032`, `F034`).

**「」, speech, and its absence.** 「」 frames spoken dialogue. Two stretches go bracketless and are marked by that as remembered or dreamlike: the Ch03 dream dialogue with Himeno (Ch03 P0008–P0022; [U03 §5](../readings/TDH_U03_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)) and the happy walk with her in Ch10, in the historical present (Ch10 P0102–P0105; [U10 §5](../readings/TDH_U10_DEEP_READING.md#5-narration-focalization-and-time)). In Ch01 his reply to the retracted proposal is set without brackets, そいつはよかった、と俺も笑った, "good for her, I laughed too," which leaves it undecidable whether the words were said or thought (Ch01 P0093).

**『』, nested speech and the parable's なにか.** 『』 marks speech inside speech and bracketed abstractions:

- the teacher's 『なにか』 (Ch01 P0016–P0021);
- Miyagi's 『なかったこと』, "what never happened," and 『私を認識しているあなた』, "you perceiving me" (Ch06 P0060; `TDH-F018`).

The parable's buyer is written in hiragana, 『なにか』. The children's awaited "something" is in kanji: 「何か」 in narration (Ch01 P0060), and Himeno's その『何か』 inside her speech (Ch01 P0070). Any identification across the split is a HYPOTHESIS (`TDH-F002`). That bears on §5.3 and on Appendix A14.

**〝〟, cited and ironized words.** 〝〟 holds a word at arm's length: cited, marked, not owned. Its occurrences run in a recognizable order. OBSERVATION of the occurrences:

- **The market idiom.** 〝売れ残っている〟 (Ch01 P0095; `TDH-F005`); 〝三十〟, 〝三十億〟, 〝三十万〟 at the appraisal (Ch02 P0149, P0152; `TDH-F007`).
- **The parable's language.** 〝人間らしい生活〟, "a human way of living," inside the teacher's speech (Ch01 P0016).
- **Miyagi's categories carried into his prose.** 〝起こるかもしれなかったこと〟 and 〝絶対に起こり得ないこと〟 (Ch03 P0108; Ch05 P0028, P0051, P0064). In Ch05 the modal かもしれなかった appears only inside 〝〟 (`TDH-F016`). Also 〝問題行動〟 and 〝不適切な行動〟, her terms for misconduct (Ch03 P0065; Ch08 P0014).
- **Her own words recalled.** The hope 〝いつかいいことがあるかもしれない〟 (Ch08 P0084). Her Ch05 verdict recalled in Ch13 in 〝〟 without its emphasis (Ch13 P0045; `TDH-F034`).
- **Presence.** 〝いる〟 in her confession (Ch12 P0183; `TDH-F032`). 〝ミヤギ〟 in Shinbashi's scene (Ch14 P0022, P0026). Finally 〝ミヤギ〟 as the sense stored in his body (Ch15 P0069).
- **The prophecy's words.** 〝十年後の夏〟 and 〝とってもいいこと〟, cited from Himeno at the verdict (Ch14 P0197, P0201).

INFERENCE, moderate: the 〝〟 marks trace the book's movement from the market idiom, through Miyagi's modal categories, to presence.

## 11. Counterreadings, graded

These readings resist the romantic resolution or qualify the argument above. Each is graded on textual support, and its evaluative standard is named where it makes an ethical claim ([method §3](../TDH_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md#3-factual-account-and-interpretation)). Depiction, causal explanation, sympathy and endorsement are kept apart.

### 11.1 The closing ledger is his alone

**Grade.** Strong as observation; moderate as interpretation.

**The observation.** The last valuation compares the three days with 「俺が送るはずだった悲惨な三十年間」, "the miserable thirty years I was to have lived," and 「俺が送るはずだった有意義な三十日間」, "the meaningful thirty days I was to have lived" (Ch15 P0125–P0126). Both are his forfeited futures. Miyagi's forfeited decades are not in the ledger ([U15 §4.16](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction), B). She has sold "all," and by the Ch14 hearsay she was some three years from freedom (Ch15 P0094; Ch14 P0193).

**What the narration does with her loss.** It acknowledges the loss as what he *ought* to grieve: 「きっと俺は、そのことを、悲しむべきなのだろう」, "surely I ought to grieve over it" (Ch15 P0097). It then sets that aside: 「それなのに、俺は幸せだった」, "and yet I was happy" (Ch15 P0099). Her motive is unstated (Ch15 P0096). Her inner state reaches the reader as testimony and as his narration of her laughter and tears (Ch15 P0104, P0108).

**The reading.** The novel's final happiness is valued from one side, by a narrator whose standard, presence over eternity and memory (Ch15 P0114–P0117), is his own.

**Against it.** Miyagi names her loss herself. She apologizes for 「あなたがせっかく取り戻してくれた人生を、自ら捨てるようなことをしてしまって」, "throwing away the life you went to the trouble of buying back" (Ch15 P0102). She presents her act as joyful, and her value as raised by him (Ch15 P0104–P0105). The text does not conceal her loss; it subordinates it. VALUE_JUDGMENT, the analyst's: by a standard of reciprocity in valuing a shared act, the closing ledger is one-sided. By the narrator's standard it is coherent.

### 11.2 The aborted assault and the turn toward each other

**Grade.** Moderate for the structural link; moderate for the reading; the ethical question unsettled.

**The observation.** By Miyagi's testimony, her regard for him changed 「駅の一件の後」, "after the incident at the station," because he listened to her seriously and sympathized with her situation; from then he was "no longer just a subject" (Ch12 P0183). His narration places his own change of regard on the same night: the revision of her coldness and the resolve to be remembered as the subject easiest to be near (Ch08 P0057, P0091). The same night contains his intended assault, stopped after he touched her bare shoulder (Ch08 P0008–P0032). OBSERVATION: her testimony and his narration both place a change of regard on the night of his worst act.

**Its limit.** INFERENCE, moderate: the turn toward each other as persons begins that night. It is not the origin of the romance as a whole. His attraction predates it (Ch06 P0007–P0008; Ch04 P0061–P0063). Her love, by her account, rests chiefly on what followed, his treating her in public as present (Ch12 P0183–P0188; §3.3, item 4).

**The recastings.** The act is recast in later scenes, not erased (audit §3.1, `TDH-C036`):

- **Her offer.** She invites him to do anything to her, 「ええ、どんなにひどいことでも」, "yes, however cruel," and he answers with an embrace (Ch12 P0195–P0199).
- **The kiss.** He kisses her unasked, licensed by 「どうせ誰にも咎められないんだったら、これくらい自分勝手なことをしてやるさ」, "if no one will reproach it anyway, I'll be this selfish" (Ch13 P0161). Her stiffening, 身を固く (Ch13 P0159), recalls 身体を強張らせた at the station (Ch08 P0024). By his narration she then responds (Ch13 P0159). Her 「誰も、咎めません」, "no one will reproach it," is said looking down, with its object unexpressed (Ch13 P0162–P0163; `TDH-C065`).
- **The reversal.** In Ch14 Miyagi pushes him down (Ch14 P0138).

No apology to her for Ch08 is narrated. He acknowledged the act to her that night without apology: he calls himself クズ, "scum" (Ch08 P0033), and refers to 「さっきみたいな行動」, "what I did just now" (Ch08 P0045). He confessed it a month later to a third party, the substitute (Ch12 P0042).

**The reading.** The romance absorbs the near-assault rather than confronting it. The repeated contacts turn an act of attempted domination into reciprocal touch, and consent is supplied only through his narration. INFERENCE, moderate.

**Against it.** The text keeps the act in view. He calls himself scum (Ch08 P0033) and confesses to a third party (Ch12 P0042). By her testimony, her change turned on his listening, not on the act. The stop is narrated as his own recoil from imagined harm (Ch08 P0025–P0030). The recastings can be read as repair rather than absolution. Miyagi’s conditional Ch12 offer precedes an embrace (P0193–P0199), not permission for the later kiss. Kusunoki narrates her surprise and subsequent response, but her wishes before his unasked initiative are not established; he calls that initiative 自分勝手, "selfish" ([U13 §11](../readings/TDH_U13_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), reading (iv)).

### 11.3 Gifts that override stated wishes

**Grade.** Strong.

The romance is enacted as a chain of unilateral gifts, each concealed or carried out against the other's stated wish:

1. **Her price lie.** She gave him ¥300,000 without his knowledge, against no wish he had stated. She calls it 「ひとりよがりな利他行為」, "self-righteous altruism," and 「歪んだ厚意の押し付け」, "a warped kindness forced on someone" (Ch12 P0170, P0172).
2. **His sale.** It was carried out on her day off with the substitute (Ch14 P0110–P0114, P0155), against her explicit plea: 「せめて、この時間だけは──一緒にいられる時間だけは、自分から捨てるようなことは、しないでください」, "at least this time, the time we can be together, please don't throw it away yourself" (Ch14 P0142). Her farewell may be acceptance; it is not consent, and its scope is unstated ([U14 §11](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation C).
3. **Her sale.** It defeated his wish that she forget him and live to find someone better (Ch14 P0128, P0134; Ch15 P0012).

The parallel between 1 and 2 is asymmetric. Her gift overrode no stated wish of his; his overrode an explicit plea. INFERENCE, strong: the novel's love is enacted as giving that does not ask.

The O. Henry frame names such giving wise (the chapter title). The body names it foolish and a betrayal (Ch15 P0098–P0103). Neither lover reproaches the other, and each calls himself or herself the fool. Which act was good for whom is a VALUE_JUDGMENT that the text distributes between title and body and does not settle.

### 11.4 The ending rests on testimony and on prolepses written before it

**Grade.** Strong as a caution; low as a thesis that the ending is false.

**What carries the ending.** Everything that makes it cohere is testimony or inference:

- what she sold;
- that the substitute told her and handled her procedures;
- that her value rose and her debt is paid (Ch15 P0090–P0105);
- why she is now visible (`TDH-C024` v3, inference);
- whether their three days align (unstated).

The witness has declared herself a liar (Ch12 P0193). The only independent confirmation at the end is sight (Ch15 P0076). The two prolepses that seem to point at the ending were written before it by the record's own chronology (§4.2, §9.3–9.4).

**Against it.** Where her testimony was tested, the record is mixed.

- **Borne out.**
  - She returned on the day she promised (Ch14 P0114; Ch15, by day count, moderate).
  - Himeno's own account includes dropout, marriage and divorce (Ch10 P0133).
  - The childhood friend was seen, and seen happy (Ch13 P0146–P0149).
  - The day off was the substitute's statement, not hers, and her return bears it out (Ch12 P0007, P0106).
- **Falsified by an independent test.** Himeno remembers him, against Miyagi's report that she barely does: she recognizes him and names him (Ch09 P0145–P0148), and her letter presupposes long memory of him (Ch10 P0194).
- **False by her own admission.** The price and the two rules (Ch12 P0166, P0193).

The three-day withdrawal is treated as operative by the narration (Ch15 P0005). The reading that the ending is fabricated has no support. The caution is that it is *told*: by a witness to a narrator, and by a narrator in a chapter for which no writing scene exists.

### 11.5 Value converted, not escaped

**Grade.** Moderate.

The relational value of the ending is paid for in the shop's currency (§2.7). By the appraiser's testimony and the narrator's account, the metric that values fame and contribution prices his thirty days high enough to nearly free her (Ch14 P0165, P0193). By the narrator's hearsay and her testimony, her freedom, and their three days, are bought with money (Ch14 P0193; Ch15 P0096, P0105). This qualifies, without reversing, the reading that the novel repudiates the pricing of a life.

### 11.6 Himeno used and dropped

**Grade.** Strong as narrative economy; the ethical charge is the analyst's VALUE_JUDGMENT.

**The charge.** Himeno supplies the frames (§8.1), and her letter is the novel's turning point (Ch10 P0190–P0200). Her own life is reported by others or summarized: a child at seventeen by Miyagi's testimony, a divorce, dependence on her family home, a plan of revenge suicide abandoned, a death forecast. It is left unresolved while the romance resolves. After Ch11 she survives in the narration only as a measure (Ch13 P0006; Ch14 P0197–P0201). By a standard that weighs the represented suffering of a secondary figure, the novel's attention is unequal.

**Against it.** The silence is consistent with the narrator's perspective boundary. He has no further access to her. Her letter drops the plan (Ch10 P0196). Miyagi's forecast belonged, by her own account, to a course that "can never happen" (Ch03 P0108). The novel does not claim to know her fate.

### 11.7 "Peace with the world" as becoming the clown

**Grade.** Moderate.

Kusunoki says he succeeded in making peace with the world (Ch14 P0119). The rival reading takes this as his reframing of being laughed at: he has become 「この町で、一番の道化」, "the greatest clown in this town" (Ch14 P0065), the role of the clever class clown (Ch01 P0031), by strong inference the boy he says he hated and envied (Ch14 P0119). The town's regard is 「可哀想で面白い人」, "a pitiable, amusing person," and 生温かい目, "indulgent eyes" (Ch14 P0049). By his own count half its smiles are mockery (Ch14 P0117) ([U14 §11](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#11-competing-interpretations-claims-and-forecasts), Interpretation D, rival). The reconciliation is with being laughed at, by his own report. Against this rival stand the crowd's conduct in Ch15, which comforts and blesses (Ch15 P0037, P0085–P0087), and the photo-shop manager's defence of the absent Miyagi (Ch15 P0044–P0046).

### 11.8 The record as an instrument of self-forgiveness

**Grade.** HYPOTHESIS, low to moderate.

The record says that rewriting made bad memories easier to accept (Ch12 P0075). Its last moral statement is 「すべてを許せた」, "I could forgive everything" (Ch15 P0118), with its object unspecified. On this reading the record's own moral accounting is the forgiver's. That accounting includes the hedged causes of the Ch08 impulse (Ch08 P0007), the folly labels and the "betrayal" made dear. The reader receives a narrative already processed toward acceptance. The rival is that the record is unusually self-exposing: it shows his contempt, his relish at hurting her and his intent of assault without softening the acts themselves.

### 11.9 The fantasy reading

**Grade.** Low (§5.4).

## 13. Limits

### 13.1 What the novel never shows

The novel never narrates these, and this synthesis does not supply them:

- **The three days.** Nothing beyond the first afternoon of the first day is narrated (Ch15 P0124–P0127 is a prospect).
- **Any death.** His, hers, or any shared death.
- **Whether their three days align** (Ch15 P0094, unstated).
- **Himeno's fate.** Her forecast suicide, her child (known only by Miyagi's testimony) and her life after the dinner.
- **The grave wish** (Ch09 P0092), after Ch13.
- **The cause of his death at about fifty** in the forfeited course. Miyagi's crash maims but does not kill (Ch05 P0056; F-U02-f, not answered).
- **The institution.** Headquarters, the advisory body, the buyers, and any check on the valuations beyond the testimony of its staff.
- **The contents of Miyagi's notebook** (Ch12 P0049), and the identity of the sketchbook with it.
- **The photograph** of Miyagi beside the moped, never developed in the narration (Ch11 P0089–P0093; F-U11-f).
- **The origin of the wound on her knee.** On day one it is bandaged and 「相当深い傷らしく」, "seems to be quite a deep wound" (Ch03 P0045). Ch08 calls it a large scar (Ch08 P0025; F-U08-e).
- **The figure of the second sale** (Ch14 P0193).
- **The record's survival,** and its identity with the printed text.
- **Others' attitudes.** Kusunoki's family after Ch04; Wakana's and Naruse's attitudes beyond Miyagi's testimony; whether the old woman remembered him (Ch11 P0149).
- **Unexplained choices.** Where Miyagi searched for him (Ch15 P0067); why he invented the break-up story (Ch15 P0040); what 「すべて」, "everything," he forgives (Ch15 P0118).

### 13.2 Limits of this synthesis

**What it rests on.** It rests on the accepted readings, the notebook and the audit, and on return-to-source checks of the decisive passages in the normalized text. It has not re-read every chapter in full. Where it cites a reading heading for an argument, that reading's full coverage receipt is the evidence of coverage.

**New observations.** The observations listed in Appendix A are new here. They have not passed the multi-agent review that the readings passed, and the final validation gate should test them.

**Labels.** The synthesis author's possible prior familiarity with the novel is quarantined (§0). No passage, figure or plot point outside the admitted witness is used.

### 13.3 The afterword, as labelled authorial paratext

*Authorial paratext (S21, `part0019`), cited only as framing, not as evidence of a narrative fact or as interpretive authority.*

In the afterword the author writes of a kind of "fool" (馬鹿) who makes his own hell. Such fools, he holds, are cured at the latest just before death; the lucky ones may find a cure earlier (part0019 P0004, P0008). He writes of the beauty of the world as seen too late by such a person. He disclaims any intention to speak, through this novel, about the value of life or the power of love (part0019 P0004–P0011).

This synthesis takes nothing from the afterword. It notes two facts about it:

- The body text stages valuation explicitly and repeatedly (Ch01 P0004–P0032; Ch02 P0162–P0172; Ch12 P0154–P0174; Ch14 P0165–P0194). The authorial disclaimer therefore does not decide what the text does.
- The afterword's vocabulary of fools coincides with the body's (Ch15 P0100, P0102–P0103, P0112). That is a fact about the paratext, not a key to the body.
