# Three Days of Happiness — Miyagi (ミヤギ)

## Opening orientation

Miyagi is the young woman who appraises Kusunoki's lifespan at the shop in chapter 2, where she is unnamed (女店員, "the female clerk"). She appears at his door the next day as his 監視員, "watcher," and gives her name (Ch03 P0050). From then until the night before his second sale (Ch14 P0148–P0152) she is beside him almost constantly, and no one else can perceive her. In the last chapter she comes back, visible to everyone, and says she has sold all but three days of her own life (Ch15 P0053–P0105).

**At a glance (each item is argued below):**

- **Roles.** Off-duty clerk at the shop (Ch02). Kusunoki's watcher (Ch03–Ch14), with a two-day and a one-day day off (Ch12 P0007; Ch14 P0114). In Ch15, by her own account, a person with three days left who is no longer a watcher.
- **Age and past.** About nineteen or twenty, on Kusunoki's estimate (Ch03 P0046). She became a watcher at about ten, paying an inherited debt by selling some thirty years of her time. That comes from her own testimony (Ch08), and the substitute watcher's account backs it in part (Ch12 P0033, P0040).
- **Defining condition.** Only the watched person can perceive her. Anything she does to anyone else is "processed as never having happened" (her testimony, demonstrated: Ch06 P0057–P0062). Animals can see her (Ch11 P0103–P0105). In Ch15 everyone sees her (P0076).
- **Voice.** She is polite to Kusunoki from first to last. She addresses him only as あなた and クスノキさん, and refers to herself as 私. Her speech shifts from shop keigo, through cool colloquial-polite speech with dry irony, to a teasing, sulking and finally weeping politeness that is never given up.
- **Arc in brief.** She delivers bad news, polices him and keeps her distance (Ch03–Ch07). After he nearly assaults her she tells her history, and their relations change (Ch08). She softens and goes along with him (Ch09–Ch11). She admits the price lie, confesses love, and says several of the rules were lies (Ch12). She objects to his sacrificing his remaining time for her and asks for memories instead (Ch13). She begs him to stay and says goodbye (Ch14). Then, by her account, she undoes his sacrifice with one of her own, and she returns (Ch15). The narrator accepts her account (Ch15 P0098–P0100), and her visibility is consistent with it (`TDH-C024` v3).
- **Evidence condition.** The text never shows her interior directly. Everything about it comes from what she does and says as Kusunoki records it, from his readings of her face and tone, and from her testimony. At Ch12 P0193 she says she has told him "a lot of lies," names some, and leaves the rest open. No statement of hers can be taken at her word alone after that, and statements made before it are thrown into doubt as well.

**Boundary.** Native chapters 1–15 of TDH-JP-01, read through the accepted readings U01–U15 and the notebook. The afterword and end matter are excluded. Miyagi does not appear in chapter 1. The novel's ending does not narrate the three days or anyone's death.

## 1. How Miyagi reaches the page

### 1.1 Kusunoki's record

The novel is Kusunoki's first-person record. By strong inference it is the notebook he starts during the substitute's two days, at about day 30 (Ch12 P0052). By the next evening the record had "caught up with the present" (記録が現在に追い付いていた, Ch12 P0074). That was before the substitute's hint about the price (P0079–P0100) and before her confession (P0136–P0193). Writing continued after Ch12. On the first morning of his last three days he brings the notebook up to the day before and revises it (Ch15 P0007, P0010). Ch15 itself is narrated from after the reunion, and the text does not say when or whether it was written (`TDH-C010` v2–v5). Identifying the printed text with that notebook is itself an inference.

This matters for Miyagi in particular. On this chronology, the early verdicts on her, "machine-like" and "lacking humanity" (機械じみている, 人間味がない, Ch03 P0077), were first written down by a man who already knew her history (Ch08) and her concealment about Himeno (Ch10), but not yet the price lie or her confession. Whether they were revised afterwards is not fixed ([U12 §4, item 6](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). The narration presents them as what he perceived at the time. It also marks its own hindsight. It flags his first-day trust in her honesty as unreliable (Ch02 P0146–P0147). It looks ahead: she understood his feeling "long before" he did (Ch09 P0043–P0046), she knew more than she said at the observation deck (Ch10 P0074–P0077), she hid things "for my sake" (Ch10 P0171–P0172), and a praise of her leads into his failure to foresee her "betrayal" (Ch11 P0154–P0158). The text does not fix which of these passages were revised later, whether after Ch12 P0074 or after the reunion.

### 1.2 Five channels of evidence

1. **Narrated action and speech.** What she does and says, as Kusunoki records it. This is OBSERVATION within his record.
2. **Her testimony.** What she says about the system's rules, about other people's futures, about her past, and about her own motives and feelings.
3. **Kusunoki's readings of her feeling.** Most are hedged: 心なしか ("somehow"), ようだった ("seemed"), かもしれない ("perhaps"). Some are flat, like "machine-like." The reviewers repeatedly restored hedges that had been hardened into fact (U07 D-U07-01; U08 D-U08-02; U13 D2-U13-05). This portrait keeps them.
4. **The narrator's retrospective glosses and prolepses about her interior** (§1.1). Each is Kusunoki's attribution. None comes from her.
5. **Third parties.** The substitute's account of her records (Ch12 P0033–P0040). Shinbashi's partial sense of her presence (Ch14 P0026–P0030). The photo-shop owner's acceptance (Ch11 P0080–P0083). The Ch15 crowd, who see her.

### 1.3 The standing problem (Ch12 P0193)

At Ch12 P0193, Miyagi says she has told him many lies besides the price and "the matter of Himeno." By way of example (たとえば) she names two: that he would be put to death if he tried to cause others trouble, and that he would die if he went more than a hundred metres from her. She calls them "expedients to protect myself" and adds 「嘘ばっかりなんです」, "it's all lies."

This statement reaches in two directions:

- **Backward.** たとえば makes the list open-ended. 「ヒメノさんのこと」 is called a lie without saying which statement about Himeno. So every earlier piece of her testimony is now uncertain, item by item ([U12 §4, item 10](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-C059`).
- **Forward.** Everything she says after P0193 comes from someone who has called herself a liar. That includes her claims about valuation, her plea, her account of her own sale and her reported feelings. None of it can rest on her word alone. Some of it later events bear out, or her conduct fits it; that is noted item by item.

The retraction itself is also testimony. Nothing in the text contradicts it. No report or punishment followed his glass-throwing (Ch06), his night trespass (Ch07), or his touching her with intent to assault (Ch08). That fits either reading: the rule was false, or she never reported him (`TDH-C014` v3).

Grades used below:

- **N:** narrated.
- **T+:** testimony borne out by a demonstration, another witness or later events.
- **T:** testimony that no other source supports and that she never retracts.
- **T−:** testimony she later retracts or admits was false.
- **T×:** testimony contradicted by events or by her own later words, and never reconciled.
- **T?:** testimony given after P0193.
- **R:** Kusunoki's reading of her feeling.
- **"T, strained" or "T, in tension":** testimony in tension with her own later knowledge or statements, but not contradicted.
- **"Doubtful":** testimony given while a lie she later confirmed was in force (`TDH-C038` v1a).
- **"Part T+":** some elements borne out, the rest uncorroborated.
- **Combined grades,** such as "T?, T+ (moderate)": the first grade is the statement's standing, the second the degree to which later events bear it out.

## 2. Identity and circumstances

**Name.** ミヤギ, in katakana, like クスノキ and ヒメノ. No kanji or other name is ever given. She introduces herself in a work formula, 〜を務めさせていただく、ミヤギです ("I am Miyagi, who will serve as …", Ch03 P0050). That is the convention for a surname (INFERENCE, moderate), but the text does not settle whether it is a surname or a given name.

Kusunoki calls her あんた from day one (Ch03 P0106). In Ch05–Ch06 he also uses an ironic 監視員さん, "watcher-san" (Ch05 P0042; Ch06 P0048, P0106). He first speaks her name at Ch09 P0039. Through Ch12 he alternates ミヤギ and あんた; the last あんた to her is at Ch12 P0177. From Ch13 only ミヤギ is recorded. In the closing narration she is この子, "this girl" (Ch15 P0116). The town calls her ミヤギさん, ミヤギちゃん (Ch14 P0053), and once 「ろくでもない女」, "a no-good woman" (Ch15 P0048). She takes that last phrase for herself (Ch15 P0063, P0079).

**Appearance**, all through Kusunoki's eyes:

- **At the shop:** a suit and thin-framed glasses; she could be anywhere from eighteen to twenty-four (Ch02 P0110, P0128).
- **On his doorstep:** a cotton blouse, a saxe-blue dungaree skirt, shoulder-length black hair curling inward, and a large bandage on her right knee over what seems, by its look, to be a deep wound (「相当深い傷らしく」, Ch03 P0045). He now puts her at nineteen or twenty (Ch03 P0046). She puts the glasses on so that he will recognize her, then cases them neatly (Ch03 P0041, P0049).
- **His inventory of what attracts him:** cool eyes, melancholy brows, tightly closed lips, soft hair, nervous fingers, slender pale thighs (Ch06 P0008).
- **His later description to Shinbashi:** not tall; pale; slight; usually cool-eyed, with an occasional reserved smile; glasses for fine print, perhaps because her eyes are a little weak (his guess); semi-long hair curling inward (Ch14 P0029).
- **Her smell:** shampoo (Ch05 P0077); "a calming smell" (Ch09 P0114); "a clear smell like a winter morning" (Ch10 P0248).
- **Belongings:** a carry case (Ch03 P0039), a bag, a blue folding umbrella (Ch10 P0216), and notebooks (§6.1, §11.1).

The knee wound is never explained. Kusunoki holds back from asking about it (Ch08 P0040), and it is never raised again.

**Position in the story.** Several of the plot's turns go through Miyagi:

- Her price lie gave him money he thought was his. On his own counterfactual, it also kept him from selling everything at once (Ch12 P0173).
- She discourages and then conceals where his Himeno plan leads (Ch03; Ch10).
- She gives him a vocabulary for his own life (§7.6).
- Her confession sets his goal of paying her debt (Ch12 P0210–P0215).
- Her drawings and her sleeping face start his painting again (Ch14 P0081–P0097, P0190).
- By her account, her sale in Ch15 undoes his last sale and gives the ending its shape.

**Time.**

- Ch02: day 0.
- Ch03: day 1.
- Ch08: before dawn on day 4.
- Ch09–Ch11: roughly days 4 to 28.
- Ch12: about day 30, with a two-day day off.
- Ch13: from about day 32.
- Ch14: under fifty days left; the second sale is on her one-day day off, about day 57.
- Ch15: the next day, the first of his last three days.

The notebook State rows `TDH-S021`–`TDH-S084` carry the depicted-time bounds.

## 3. Personal history (all testimony unless marked)

### 3.1 What is told, in depicted order

1. **Her father** left just before she was born, leaving lasting scars on her mother's body. This is hearsay within her testimony (「去ったそうです」, "he left, I'm told"; Ch08 P0069).
2. **Her mother** was always discontented. She cursed the absent father, and, as Miyagi reads her, wanted him back. She sold decades of time to buy lifespan. Miyagi can only guess why: perhaps to go on waiting for him. If so, Miyagi concludes, anyone would have done; the mother simply had no one else to cling to (すがる). Miyagi says she disliked her "miserable" mother. Her mother disliked her too, and often said 〔傍点〕こんなの〔/傍点〕、生まなければよかった ("*this thing* — I wish I'd never had it") (Ch08 P0069). It is the first sesame-dot emphasis to fall on Miyagi herself (`TDH-F022`). A later one marks her as the giver: 〔傍点〕あんたが〔/傍点〕 (Ch12 P0157, his speech).
3. **Abandonment** came when Miyagi was about six. Her mother became a watcher and so vanished from her sight. Miyagi then lived at an aunt's house for some years and was treated as a nuisance there (Ch08 P0069).
4. **Her childhood friend.** Before she became a watcher there was a boy who mattered to her. She later says he was to her what Himeno is to Kusunoki: two misfits leaning on each other in a "co-dependent world of two" (Ch11 P0203). She remembers a lake from childhood where she saw "frighteningly beautiful" stars. It is the most beautiful thing she truly 『知っている』, "knows" (Ch09 P0090; Ch11 P0163).
5. **Her mother's death and the debt.** When Miyagi was ten, her mother was killed by a person she was watching; Miyagi says the cause of death is unclear. A man came to tell her this and told her about the debt. She could pay it by selling lifespan, time or health; otherwise one of the three would be taken by force (Ch08 P0072). She chose time and sold "just over thirty years" (Ch08 P0074). The substitute's account backs this: he handled the purchase of her time, he says she was ten, and he calls what she sold "the one thing you must never sell" (Ch12 P0033, P0040).
6. **Her first day off as a watcher.** She went to see her friend, expecting to find him grieving and waiting. He had adapted to the world within a month, "in the same way" as the people who had shut the two of them out. She concluded she had been a fetter to him. She admits she had wanted him unhappy, waiting for a return that would never come, but just about breathing. She has not gone to see him since (Ch11 P0203–P0205).
7. **About ten years as a watcher**, up to the story's present (simple arithmetic from the ages above; not stated). She mentions about five earlier subjects who made speeches like his (Ch05 P0019), many who were more extreme (Ch08 P0038), and others' last pastimes (Ch11 P0038–P0047). She expects to be freed at about forty, "if I survive until then"; the age forty is Kusunoki's arithmetic, and she agrees to it (Ch08 P0058–P0059).
8. **His appraisal.** She appraises Kusunoki in Ch02. On her later testimony, she lied about his value and gave him her own money (Ch12).

What she knew from the beginning: his name (Ch02 P0143); his "synopsis" (あらすじ), which was full of Himeno (Ch03 P0110); the important might-have-beens of his life and those around him (Ch03 P0108); his true price (her Ch12 testimony); and, by his report, his appraisal file (Ch14 P0134). She says she knows nothing of her own future (Ch09 P0099).

### 3.3 How this history bears on her conduct

- **Self-reproach as harshness.** She explains her early severity: 「その程度の価値しかない自分のことが許せなかった」, "I couldn't forgive myself for being worth so little," and she saw herself in him (Ch08 P0082). That is her own reading of her motive (たぶん, "probably"). It recasts the early chapters. It does not show that the earlier coldness was put on.
- **Rules as armour.** The misconduct and distance rules were, she says, "expedients to protect myself" (Ch12 P0193). INFERENCE, moderate: they answer the danger that killed her mother (Ch08 P0072). The text does not link the two.
- **Inheritance and fatalism.** She ties her own refusal to die to "the same blood" as her mother and jokes that she may die the same way too (Ch08 P0084). Later she expects to die, like her mother, before the debt is paid (Ch12 P0163).
- **The clinging chain.** すがる runs through her words: her mother had no one else to cling to (Ch08 P0069). She tells Kusunoki not to cling to the past (Ch07 P0083). She says her friend is the last place she can cling to, because "I don't know anything else" (Ch11 P0207). The lexical recurrence is an OBSERVATION. That she sees in herself her mother's dependence, and in Kusunoki her own, is INFERENCE (low to moderate).
- **Wanting to give.** She says she may have wanted, once, to be the one who simply gives, doing for someone like herself what no one did for her (Ch12 P0172; `TDH-C057`).

## 4. Personality and psychology

**Values.**

- *Small certain happiness over a grand reversal.* She tells him to stack up 小さくても確実な幸せ, "small but certain happiness," rather than gamble on a comeback (Ch05 P0025).
- *Not wasting time.* "A waste of time"; "but it saved you time, didn't it?" (Ch03 P0112–P0114).
- *Beauty she has actually seen.* The star lake (Ch09 P0090); fireflies, "they really do glow" (Ch12 P0123).
- *Things that last.* She likes vending machines for their 不変性 and 永遠性, permanence and eternity (Ch11 P0066). She wishes for a grave that will stand for decades after her death (Ch09 P0092). She calls "please don't change" a childish wish that has never once been granted (Ch13 P0129).
- *Being treated as present.* What she valued most in him was that he treated her as 〝いる〟, "there," in every place and situation (Ch12 P0183).
- *Giving.* Ch12 P0172; Ch13 P0113.
- *Small self-respect.* She wears sunscreen for "my own mood," though no one can see her (Ch07 P0052).

**Goals.** Stated goals change over the book:

- Early, they belong to the institution: a peaceful remaining life for him (Ch03 P0118).
- In Ch08 they are hers: to pay off the debt and then live "properly" (〝ちゃんと〟), better than anyone (Ch08 P0074).
- In Ch09, three wishes (§10).
- In Ch13: memories of him (P0087); to give something (P0113); to know his wish (P0115); to see her friend once (P0123).
- In Ch14: to keep the month they have left (P0142).
- In Ch15, by her account: to spend three days with him.

**Motives.** Except where her conduct shows them, her motives come only as her own hedged self-interpretation (たぶん, かもしれません) or as Kusunoki's attribution. For the early chapters, care and duty remain undecided rivals, and the reviewers kept that open (U05 D-U05-04). He himself suspects her softening may be an instruction to be kinder as a subject's death approaches (Ch09 P0017). Her Ch12 testimony settles the question only as far as testimony can.

**Fears and vulnerabilities**, by her testimony:

- dying as her mother did (Ch08 P0084; Ch12 P0163);
- violent subjects (Ch08 P0038; Ch12 P0193);
- being left behind and found unnecessary (Ch11 P0203–P0205);
- disappointment (Ch13 P0129);
- being alone for thirty years after his death (Ch14 P0142).

Kusunoki also reads in her "a weakness she occasionally shows," because she always seems on guard (Ch08 P0010). That reading appears inside his account of a predatory impulse, and should be weighed as such.

**Contradictions that the text leaves standing:**

- On day one she tells him to treat her as absent (いないと思っていただいて結構, Ch03 P0062). Later she says the greatest gift was being treated as present (Ch12 P0183).
- She tells him no one will ever again try to like him (Ch04 P0046). Then she loves him (Ch12 P0188).
- She tells him not to cling to past relationships (Ch07 P0083). Her own last refuge is a childhood friend (Ch11 P0207).
- She advises small, certain happiness (Ch05 P0025), and in the end, by her account, sells her remaining decades for three days (Ch15 P0094).
- She begs him not to throw away the time they have (Ch14 P0142), then, by her account, does the same thing to the life he had bought back for her (Ch15 P0102).
- She says she does not remember whether earlier subjects were lonely (Ch06 P0119), then tells their stories (Ch11). That is a tension, not a strict contradiction, since the stories concern other things (§1.3 row 17).
- Her view of her own future changes: hopeful (Ch08 P0074), fatalistic (Ch12 P0163), then decades of remembering (Ch13 P0085), then thirty years alone (Ch14 P0142).

**Coping strategies:**

- dry irony and deflation (§7.3);
- generalizing from experience ("everyone says the same thing," Ch05 P0017);
- falling back on the work frame ("I'm working," Ch04 P0075; Ch06 P0115);
- controlling information: telling, withholding and lying (§5);
- running herself down before others can;
- holding the hope she herself called unprovable (Ch08 P0084).

**Self-conception.** Her self-descriptions:

- "a hopeless fool" (Ch08 P0084);
- someone who cannot understand what women her age think (Ch10 P0029);
- worth "close to zero" as a person, said of both of them while the price lie stood (Ch10 P0054);
- someone who knows "nothing else" but her friend (Ch11 P0207);
- someone whose kindness is "warped goodwill forced on others" (Ch12 P0172);
- "all lies" (Ch12 P0193);
- "only ever on the receiving end" (Ch13 P0113);
- "an idiot, aren't I" (Ch15 P0102);
- in jest, 「ろくでもないミヤギ」, "the no-good Miyagi" (Ch15 P0079).

Against these, she once gives a professional self-image: a dangerous, lonely job that teaches a lot about the value of life and how people live (Ch08 P0074). She also offers a few playful boasts: 「偉いでしょう」, "aren't I good?" (Ch11 P0111); 「そうでしょう？」, "told you" (Ch11 P0193).

## 5. Cognition and decision-making

**What she notices.**

- *His face.* She reads the failed call from his expression (Ch04 P0040). She sees through his feigned sleep (Ch11 P0010).
- *His plans.* She guesses his debt plan (Ch13 P0025: "I think I know roughly what you're thinking"). She guesses his plan to sell from the wish he states, to be forgotten (Ch14 P0130–P0131).
- *Public attention.* She glances around before warning him (Ch09 P0082).
- *His list,* line by line (Ch03 P0096–P0097; Ch06 P0011–P0013).
- *Concrete things:* stars, fireflies, the road to the lake that she recognizes from childhood (Ch11 P0163).

**How she reasons.**

- *From many cases to a type.* She has heard about five such speeches (Ch05 P0019). She uses a gambler's analogy for people who have kept losing at life (Ch05 P0019). Her rhetorical questions corner him: "What could someone who achieves nothing in thirty years change in three months?" (Ch05 P0021); "Don't you think that's too convenient?" (Ch07 P0083).
- *Graded certainty* in her forecasts. Counterfactuals are hedged, present-tense claims are flat, forecasts are absolute, and a diagnosis comes last (Ch04 P0044–P0046; [U04 §4](../readings/TDH_U04_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-C018`).
- *Rules in quasi-logical form.* She explains the system in bracketed abstractions, for example 『私を認識しているあなた』, "you, perceiving me" (Ch06 P0060; `TDH-F018`).

**How she prioritizes when obligations conflict.** As a watcher she cannot forbid him anything except by threat, and she cannot help him except with information. She manages the conflict between duty and feeling mainly through information:

- *A lie that gives.* The price (Ch02).
- *Lies that protect.* The rules (Ch03, Ch05, Ch08).
- *Concealment that spares him.* Himeno's plan: she gives him only part of the explanation, then admits it when he asks (Ch10 P0169, P0180).
- *Limited non-interference.* "At least this time" she will not interfere with the rest of his list (Ch03 P0126). She does not stop his visit to Himeno, "it's not that I can't understand" (Ch09 P0040).

Even her unsolicited verdicts are fenced with personal-opinion hedges (Ch06 P0011, P0013; Ch07 P0041, P0083).

**Under uncertainty.** She claims to know nothing of her own future (Ch09 P0099). She takes for herself the hope she had called "a kind of devil's proof" when he held it (Ch05 P0059; Ch08 P0084; `TDH-C017` v2b).

**Decision speed.** Most of her disclosures come slowly, after hesitation and pauses: Ch05 P0027; Ch08 P0066; the gap before 「……三十円です」 (Ch12 P0165); several seconds before agreeing to grant his wish (Ch14 P0126). The decision she reports in Ch15 was immediate: "by the time he finished telling me, I had already decided" (Ch15 P0096). The contrast is part of the portrait. The decision itself is testimony.

## 6. Conduct as a watcher, and behavioral patterns

### 6.1 Ordinary conduct on duty

- **Posture and place.** She sits in a corner of the room in 三角座り, knees hugged to her chest, which gives Ch03 its title (Ch03 P0059). She keeps the pose throughout: chin on knees (Ch05 P0018; Ch12 P0171), and again at the lake (Ch11 P0207). Kusunoki imitates it twice: trying to sleep that way (Ch11 P0012), and in the opposite corner while he confronts her (Ch12 P0159). She smiles at the second (Ch12 P0160).
- **Following.** She stays about five steps behind him (Ch03 P0074) and comes after him when he tries to phone in private (Ch04 P0005, ちょこちょこ, "trotting along"). She rides pillion once the hundred-metre lie applies (Ch09 P0070–P0075).
- **Writing.** She keeps a notebook she calls a 行動観察記録, a "behavioural observation record" of him (Ch04 P0077). She writes or draws at the station night, the dig and the shrine (Ch07 P0070, P0090; Ch09 P0135).
- **Sleep and bathing.** On his inference she sleeps a minute and wakes for five, all night (Ch07 P0090; Ch11 P0011). She bathes while he sleeps (Ch05 P0077–P0078, his deduction; Ch11 P0006–P0010).
- **Separate accounts.** She buys her own food (Ch04 P0060), pays at the self-checkout (Ch04 P0064) and buys her own meal ticket (Ch11 P0029). She refuses alcohol while working (Ch04 P0075; Ch06 P0115; Ch10 P0018).
- **Asking about his day.** 「今日はどんな風に過ごすんですか？」, "how will you spend today?" (Ch06 P0005; Ch07 P0028; Ch12 P0109).
- **Public discretion.** She warns him three times not to talk to her in public (Ch06 P0055; Ch09 P0082; Ch11 P0043).
- **Errands.** She checks the women's restroom for Himeno at his request (Ch10 P0156–P0158).

### 6.2 Non-intervention and enforcement

She lets him trespass at night, borrow a shovel, and read every confidential letter in the time capsule; she only writes in her notebook (Ch07 P0068–P0080; `TDH-C014` v1a). When he throws a glass at the wall on her side, she sighs (Ch06 P0095). Later she says she is not hurt, 「残念ながら」, "unfortunately," whose tone is open (Ch06 P0109).

When he asks how long a report takes, plainly with assault in mind, she answers coldly, in his reading, and states the deterrent (Ch08 P0019). She does not run (P0022). By her Ch12 testimony that deterrent was a fiction. She never reports him; whether she could have is unknown (`TDH-C014` v2–v3; `TDH-C036`).

### 6.3 Cooperation

Once their relations change, she joins in readily:

- she probably folds some of the cranes (Kusunoki infers it, Ch09 P0019–P0020) and later folds cranes openly with him (Ch11 P0048);
- she reads the same page beside him (Ch09 P0111–P0113);
- she shares the festival food (Ch09 P0125);
- she plays Himeno for his rehearsal and advises on his clothes and haircut (Ch10 P0025–P0041);
- she looks through the vending-machine photographs with him each night (Ch11 P0078);
- she guides him to the lake (Ch11 P0163–P0185);
- she goes along with every "thing one shouldn't do alone" in Ch13 (Ch13 P0101).

### 6.4 Conflict

She does not escalate. In the early sparring she:

- answers his "freedom to be hurt" argument with a conditional apology, then a warning (Ch03 P0122–P0124);
- says "I see" to his vow and goes back to her notebook (Ch05 P0012);
- continues her verdict over his head-shake (Ch06 P0088);
- promises not to bring up Himeno lightly, and does so again (Ch07 P0041, P0083).

When he shakes off her sleeve as he gives the money away, she does not insist (Ch10 P0210–P0211). When he refuses her umbrella, she closes it and walks behind him soaked, returning his line: 「見ての通り、濡れたい気分なんです」, "as you can see, I feel like getting wet" (Ch10 P0216–P0223; `TDH-F028`).

### 6.5 Stress responses

- **Threat (Ch08):** wariness, cold contempt (his reading), then a weak voice, staying put, and a sad face as she stiffens (P0015–P0024, his readings). Afterwards a shield of humble keigo: 「ご理解いただけたようで、何よりです」, roughly "I'm glad you understand," said without looking at him (P0034).
- **Confronted with a lie (Ch12):** evasion, "I don't quite know what you mean" (P0140); then flat denial, 「もちろんですよ」, "of course" (P0143); denial twice more (P0148, P0158); then concession through reasons, apology and self-blame (P0162–P0172). She offers herself for any punishment (P0195–P0197).
- **Grief (Ch14):** refusal, 「いやです」, "no"; pleading; pushing him down; the first tears he has heard from her (P0129–P0145).
- **Seeing Enishi happy (Ch13):** no expression at all (P0150). Her feelings are not narrated.

### 6.6 Mistakes and corrections

She calls some of her own conduct mistakes:

- a "slightly careless remark" (Ch03 P0122);
- her harshness (Ch08 P0082);
- hiding Himeno's plan (Ch10 P0180);
- the price lie, which she calls 「よけいなこと」, "meddling," since without it he might have kept his life (Ch12 P0170, P0172);
- throwing away what he had bought back for her (Ch15 P0102).

Her apologies change with the occasion. Formal 申し訳ありません and すみません come first (Ch03 P0122; Ch06 P0117; Ch10 P0180). Her first softer ごめんなさい comes at the station (Ch08 P0082) and returns in Ch12 P0170 and Ch15 P0102.

### 6.7 Notable exceptions

- She leaves her post to watch fireworks from the window (Ch05 P0041).
- She shares his bed for a day's sleep, then promises to go back to her corner (Ch11 P0018–P0023).
- On the last night before her absence she gets into his bed herself (Ch11 P0211).
- She shakes Shinbashi's hand and holds Suzumi's (Ch14 P0035, P0055).

## 7. Speech and communication

*Medium limit.* All of this is written register in Kusunoki's record. No voice is performed. Tags such as 面倒くさそうに ("wearily"), 皮肉っぽく ("ironically"), 弱々しい声で ("in a weak voice"), おずおず ("timidly"), 得意気に ("proudly") and 拗ねた様子で ("sulkily") are his hearing of her.

### 7.1 Register by phase

1. **Shop keigo (Ch02).** 「いらっしゃいませ」, "welcome" (P0109). She names the commodity before he can speak: 「時間ですか？　健康ですか？　寿命ですか？」, "time? health? lifespan?" (P0112). Then come お時間をいただきます, "I'll need some of your time" (P0129); いかがいたしましょう, "how would you like to proceed" (P0151); the institutional 我々 ("we") and the honorific お売りいただいた, "who have sold [to us]" (P0167, P0175). Her register is humble, procedural and impersonal (`TDH-F009`).
2. **Business-polite drifting to colloquial-polite (Ch03–Ch07).** Humble 務めさせていただく and つきましては, "accordingly" (Ch03 P0050, P0056), slide into contracted 覚えてません, "doesn't remember" (P0101), and させてもらいます (P0116). A sentence-final から recurs, half explanation, half dismissal: いないと思っていただいて結構ですから, "you may consider me not here" (P0062); 私は止めませんから, "I won't stop you" (P0126) (`TDH-F012`). Her verdicts come with personal-opinion hedges, and her ironies are cool.
3. **Loosening (Ch08).** At the station, the first えっと ("um"), あんまり, ばっかり, ほら ("you see") and じゃないですか as a confirmation tag, "isn't it?" (P0084), appear. An earlier …んじゃないですか？ at Ch05 P0039 is a line Kusunoki only imagines her saying. So does her first ごめんなさい ([U08 §6](../readings/TDH_U08_DEEP_READING.md#6-japanese-language-and-form)).
4. **Playful polite (Ch09–Ch11).** 「んー…」, "hmm" (Ch09 P0082); 「失礼します」 as she climbs onto the pillion (Ch09 P0075); 「いただきます」 (Ch09 P0125). Her でしょう？ turns from argumentative to teasing. 「クスノキさん、クスノキさん」 (Ch11 P0161). 「いいことを教えてあげましょう」, "let me tell you something good," with a benefactive that, for once, makes her the giver (Ch11 P0162; `TDH-F030`).
5. **Rude content in polite form (Ch12–Ch13).** 「うるさいですね、だまって慰められてくださいよ」, "you're noisy; just be quiet and let yourself be comforted" (Ch12 P0178). 「構ってくださいよ」, "pay attention to me," sulking (Ch13 P0027). 「馬鹿じゃないですか？」, "are you an idiot?" (Ch13 P0131). Formal set phrases carry intimate content: 見当違いだといわざるを得ません, "I must say that's off the mark," used to refuse his self-sacrifice (Ch13 P0025).
6. **Pleading (Ch14).** 「いやです」 (P0129); 「頼みますから」, "I'm begging you" (P0132); 「現れません」, "he won't appear" (P0135), then 「現れませんよ」 (P0137); 「お願いですから」, "please," three times (P0140, P0142). The farewell is 「さようなら。幸せでした」, "goodbye; I was happy" (P0150), in the past tense with no subject.
7. **Return (Ch15).** 「ただいま」, the domestic "I'm home" (P0067). 「ろくでもないミヤギです」 (P0079). Her last line turns the watcher's daily question into a shared plan: from "how will *you* spend today?" (Ch06 P0005; Ch12 P0109) to 「これから三日間、どんな風に過ごしましょう？」, "how shall *we* spend the next three days?" (Ch15 P0121).

Her main clauses to Kusunoki are polite throughout, with three exceptions:

- one plain main clause in her account of her friend, 「…知りたくなかった。」, "I didn't want to know [that he could live on alone]" (Ch11 P0205);
- the exclamation 「こういうの、好きだなあ」, "I like this kind of thing" (Ch13 P0059);
- her imitation of Himeno's voice in the rehearsal (Ch10 P0062).

Plain forms appear in other places too:

- **Sentence-final subordinate clauses that trail a polite sentence:** 〜から (Ch12 P0183, twice; Ch13 P0129), 〜のに (Ch12 P0170), 〜ので (Ch11 P0022), 〜のか (Ch15 P0090).
- **Inside polite quotation frames:** the wish at Ch11 P0010 (§7.5), and 「与えられてばっかりだな、と思いまして」, "I was thinking I only ever receive" (Ch13 P0113).
- **Verbless fragments:** 「なぜ？」 (Ch11 P0181), 「たとえば？」 (Ch13 P0157) and the tease 「恥知らずの、照れ屋さん」 (Ch13 P0063).

The plain trailing clauses cluster in her emotional disclosures (Ch11–Ch13). Politeness is never given up. Instead it changes function, from distance, to a frame for play, to a frame for pleading and grief.

### 7.2 Address and reference

- **To Kusunoki:** only あなた and クスノキさん. Never あんた, never his bare name, never 君. Intimacy shows in modifiers: 「酔っ払いのクスノキさん」, "drunken Mr. Kusunoki" (Ch10 P0058); 「恥知らずの、照れ屋さん」, "shameless, bashful you" (Ch13 P0063); 「ねえ、クスノキさん」 at moments of disclosure (Ch11 P0177; Ch12 P0193; Ch13 P0085).
- **Herself:** 私. 私たち for watchers (Ch03 P0118; Ch06 P0057; Ch11 P0068). 我々 only in the shop (Ch02 P0167).
- **Others:** さん for everyone: ヒメノさん, ワカナさん, ナルセさん, シンバシさん (Ch14 P0038). Family as 母, 父, 伯母. Her friend only as 彼, "he." The name Enishi is never in her quoted speech; Kusunoki reports it as hearsay (Ch13 P0139).
- **His address to her:** あんた from day one (Ch03 P0106); an ironic 監視員さん in Ch05–Ch06; her name from Ch09 P0039, alternating with あんた through Ch12 (last あんた to her at Ch12 P0177); only ミヤギ from Ch13 (§2). That asymmetry is never repaired: he speaks plainly, and she stays polite (`TDH-F009`, `TDH-F012`).

### 7.3 Humour and teasing

Her humour is mostly dry and comes from inside the polite form:

- **Deflation:** 「でも、時間の節約にはなったでしょう？」, "but it saved you time, didn't it?" (Ch03 P0114); 「知ってます。さっき聞きました」, "I know; you told me already" (Ch04 P0081).
- **Irony:** 「見張っていてほしいんですか？」, "do you *want* to be watched?" (Ch05 P0043); 「よほど後ろめたいことでもあるんですかね」, "something to feel guilty about?" (Ch05 P0045); 「ありませんよ。残念ながら」 (Ch06 P0109).
- **The needle about Himeno:** 「ヒメノさんに関することを除けば、でしょう？」, "except for anything to do with Himeno, right?" (Ch07 P0039); "I thought you weren't interested in anyone except Himeno" (Ch08 P0044).
- **A shared joke:** he says he knows a man who, saying just that, was due to die at fifty having gained nothing. The man is himself, in her account of his forfeited course (Ch05 P0059). She answers 「……それ、私も知ってます」, "…I know him too," and smiles (Ch08 P0085–P0086). It is her first warm smile; a self-mocking one (自嘲めいた笑み) comes earlier that night (Ch08 P0059).
- **Mock-formal handling of compliments:** 「どう受け取るかは、あなたの自由です」, "how you take it is up to you," and 「いちいちいわなくていいです」, "you needn't say it every time" (Ch10 P0038, P0040). Later he plays the same lines back to her, and she answers 「自由に受け取ります。すごく」, "I'll take it freely. Very" (Ch12 P0133).
- **Teasing:** 「…口説いてみてくださいよ、酔っ払いのクスノキさん」, "go on, chat me up [as if I were Himeno], drunken Mr. Kusunoki" (Ch10 P0058); 「恥知らずの、照れ屋さん」 with a poke to his shoulder (Ch13 P0063); hitting him with a cushion, 「勝手なこといわないでください」, "don't say whatever you like," when he tells the party she loves him (Ch14 P0059).
- **Boasting in play:** 「偉いでしょう」 (Ch11 P0111); 「そうでしょう？」 (Ch11 P0193).
- **Joining a fiction:** on her return she takes up the crowd's verdict on the absent Miyagi: 「そのミヤギって人は、ろくでもない女ですね」, "that Miyagi person is a no-good woman" (Ch15 P0063), then 「ろくでもないミヤギです」 (P0079).

### 7.4 The answer check and its spread

「さて、答えあわせといきましょう」, "well then, let's check the answers," is her formula for delivering might-have-beens. It gives chapter 4 its title (Ch04 P0002, P0041), and at Ch06 P0087 it softens to …ましょうか. Inside her verdicts fall two sesame-dot emphases: 〔傍点〕二度と現れません〔/傍点〕, "will *never again* appear" (Ch04 P0046), and 〔傍点〕ようやくスタート地点に立った〔/傍点〕, "have *only just* reached the starting line" (Ch05 P0019). It is undecidable whether the dots mark her stress or the narrator's (`TDH-F013`–`F015`). The narrator later takes the formula over for his own conclusions (Ch10 P0112; Ch11 P0141; `TDH-F029`).

### 7.5 Silence, evasion and the unguarded voice

**Her deflections:**

- she ignores his question whether watchers are obliged to snoop (Ch03 P0099);
- she gives "the simple way of putting it" first (Ch08 P0042);
- she will not name the important person (Ch09 P0092);
- she changes the subject when asked to show her drawings (Ch11 P0113);
- she answers his question about the Himeno lie with the story of her friend (Ch11 P0197–P0199);
- she evades or denies four times before conceding (Ch12 P0140, P0143, P0148, P0158).

**Pauses:** Ch05 P0027; Ch12 P0165; Ch14 P0126.

**What is withheld from the reader:** at night, after stroking his head, she murmurs something lost in the rain (Ch10 P0252). Whether the stroking happened is itself uncertain to the narrator (P0245–P0247).

**Her most unguarded recorded speech** is addressed to a man she thinks is pretending to sleep. She says that if he is feigning out of consideration for her, 「…だったらいいのにな、と思います」, "I'd like it if it were…, I think" (Ch11 P0010). A plain wish sits inside a polite frame. After her confession she says 「いなくなる人のこと、好きになっても、しかたないんですけどね」, "though there's no point loving someone who's going to disappear." The line is unbracketed, but P0190 marks it as speech. What the unbracketed setting does is HYPOTHESIS (Ch12 P0189; [U12 §6](../readings/TDH_U12_DEEP_READING.md#6-japanese-language-and-form)).

### 7.6 Her words in his narration

Kusunoki's narrating voice takes over her terms:

- 悪魔の証明, "devil's proof," becomes his 悪魔の証明的希望, "devil's-proof hope" (Ch05 P0059 → P0070);
- 自ら飛び込んで, "jumped in of your own accord" (Ch03 P0124 → Ch05 P0004);
- 馴染めず, "unable to fit in" (Ch05 P0052 → P0081);
- すがる (Ch07 P0083 → P0086);
- 向いたやり方, "the way that suits you" (Ch06 P0015 → Ch11 P0077);
- the closed might-have-been, 起こるかもしれなかったが、絶対に起こり得ないこと (Ch05 P0028 → Ch14 P0173);
- スタート地点, "the starting line" (Ch05 P0019 → Ch08 P0077; Ch13 P0045).

The uptake is an OBSERVATION (`TDH-F016`, `TDH-F021`). It shows how far her framing became the frame in which he judges his own life.

## 8. Visibility

Her condition is laid out by testimony and then tested.

**Rules (testimony):**

- On duty only the watched can perceive her, "basically" (Ch07 P0047).
- Her effects on others are nullified. She is not perceived as there, nor even as not there (Ch06 P0060).
- What cannot be nullified is the watched person's perception of her. So he appears to be talking to empty air (Ch06 P0060).
- Off duty, people intending to sell can see her (Ch07 P0049).
- Animals can see her (Ch11 P0105). Machines "do not ignore" watchers (Ch11 P0068).
- Watchers cannot see one another; this is the substitute's testimony (Ch12 P0033).

**Demonstrations:** the waitress (Ch06 P0058–P0062); Naruse (P0064); the cats (Ch11 P0103–P0104); Enishi and the boat attendant (Ch13 P0093–P0094, P0140–P0153).

**Her stance toward it changes:**

- Day one: "think of me as not here" (Ch03 P0062).
- Early on she warns him against speaking to her in public (Ch06 P0055; Ch09 P0082; Ch11 P0043).
- After her confession, the core of what she valued: he treated her as present everywhere, which was 「夢のよう」, "like a dream," to someone whose job was to be ignored (Ch12 P0183; `TDH-F032`). That reframes his public "madness" as the gift she cherished.
- When he publicly insists she is 「確かにここにいる」, "certainly here," she thanks him and leans against him (Ch13 P0073–P0078).
- In the town that half-accepts her, she shakes Shinbashi's hand with both hands, delighted in Kusunoki's narration (Ch14 P0035). She asks him to pass on her thanks (P0038) and holds Suzumi's hand (P0055). Shinbashi says he partly senses her and that Kusunoki's description matches his own imagining (testimony; Ch14 P0026–P0030; `TDH-C066`).
- Her lament in Ch14 still includes not being able to show others their joined hands (Ch14 P0142).
- In Ch15 everyone sees her (P0076). Strangers find she looks as they had imagined (P0083, P0087).

**Miyagi’s explanation and its limit.** Miyagi says that doing what Kusunoki did explains both her presence and why others could see her (Ch15 P0089–P0094). The institutional mechanism is unstated. It is an inference that, with the debt cleared and her life sold down to three days, she is no longer a watcher. Her own testimony ties invisibility to selling time, through her mother (Ch08 P0069) (`TDH-C024` v3). Kusunoki's guess at the lake, that her wish was to stop being invisible (Ch11 P0196), is carried out in effect, though the text draws no link.

For context, Kusunoki → Miyagi:

- irritation and attraction from the first meeting, disclosed only later (Ch06 P0007–P0010);
- an aborted sexual assault (Ch08 P0007–P0032; `TDH-C036`);
- a resolve to be remembered as the subject easiest to be near (Ch08 P0091);
- restraint, so as not to lean on her (Ch09 P0015);
- a resolve to expect nothing, on the premise that her kindness is only work (Ch10 P0256–P0258);
- the goal of paying her debt (Ch12 P0212);
- public assertion of her presence (Ch13);
- the second sale "to protect" her (Ch15 P0098).

He never names a feeling for her in reply to her confession (Ch12 P0184–P0192), though he says many tender things.

### 9.2 Her mother, father and aunt

She describes her mother with dislike and analysis together. She guesses at the mother's motives with hedges (きっと, "surely"; かもしれません, "perhaps"; 多分, "probably"). She sees her mother's dependence as having nothing to do with who the man was: anyone would have done (Ch08 P0069). She pauses in the telling. Kusunoki infers that she minds sounding as if she wants sympathy, and she then tells her mother's death "as if it were someone else's" (Ch08 P0070–P0071). She owns the inheritance in blood, folly and possibly in manner of death (Ch08 P0084). Her father exists for her only as hearsay. Her aunt treated her as a nuisance (Ch08 P0069). Nothing in the text shows a later relationship with any of them.

### 9.3 Her childhood friend (Enishi)

By her account they were two misfits dependent on each other (Ch11 P0203). She wished him miserable and faithful, and found him adapted (Ch11 P0205). Even so, he is the last thing she can cling to, so she would see him before dying (Ch11 P0207).

In Ch13, on her own request (P0123), she leads the search through her home town. She knows his likely haunts but not his house (P0139). When they find him she hides behind Kusunoki, then edges forward until she is standing right beside him (P0140). She insists on staying to see 「彼が今愛しているのが、どんな人なのか」, "what kind of person he loves now" (P0145). She watches him meet a woman with no expression (P0150). Afterwards she says she had meant to take advantage of being unseen, for example to hug him by force, but did not (P0153–P0155). Her feelings are not narrated. Kusunoki reads the couple as happy (P0142–P0149). The reviewers held "Miyagi reached closure" as a non-claim (U13 §13.2).

### 9.4 The substitute

Her records come to the reader partly through him. He was the one who bought her time. He has exchanged two or three written messages with her, since watchers cannot see one another. He calls her "a pitiable child" and is protective of her (Ch12 P0033–P0040). He skims her notebook and is then less hostile to Kusunoki (Ch12 P0048–P0049). He hints at the price lie (P0094–P0100).

By her Ch15 testimony he called her right after Kusunoki's sale and "did the paperwork too" (Ch15 P0096). Whether that was within his duties is not stated. How he reached her is not stated either.

### 9.5 Himeno (through Miyagi)

Miyagi and Himeno never meet visibly. Kusunoki points out the invisible watcher to Himeno (Ch10 P0146). Miyagi is the source of most of what Kusunoki believes about Himeno's present, and she keeps the worst of it from him:

- she reports Himeno's past and a scheduled suicide (Ch03 P0101);
- she says Himeno depends on her parents (Ch09 P0048);
- she speaks Himeno's probable line in the rehearsal (Ch10 P0062);
- she keeps her face "complicated" at the observation deck (Ch10 P0073);
- she gives a partial explanation after Himeno vanishes (Ch10 P0169), then admits she knew (Ch10 P0180).

Two small acts are recorded without comment. She asks whether he will say the same compliment to Himeno (Ch10 P0051). She eats Himeno's abandoned pasta: 「冷めてもおいしいですね」, "it's good even cold" (Ch10 P0163–P0164). Their function is open: practicality, dry comfort or something else.

### 9.6 Wakana and Naruse

She has no relationship with either. They are subjects of her verdicts (Ch04 P0044–P0046; Ch06 P0089). Wakana's friendly call back (Ch04 P0048–P0053) is the only independent sign of her, and Kusunoki hears it through Miyagi's words.

### 9.7 The town, the photo-shop owner, Shinbashi and his friends, the Ch15 crowd

She bows to the photo-shop owner, knowing he can't see it (Ch11 P0082). With Shinbashi's circle she is grateful, playful and physically affectionate (Ch14 P0035–P0063). In Ch15 she is witty with strangers (P0063, P0079).

### 9.8 Earlier subjects

She speaks of them as types and as small stories:

- about five who made his kind of vow (Ch05 P0019);
- many who were more extreme (Ch08 P0038);
- one who spent his last month lying in the back of a moving truck;
- others who liked ruins, railway sleepers or abandoned arcade machines (Ch11 P0036–P0040).

Her 「景色が後ろに飛んでいく、あの感じが新鮮で」, "that feeling of the scenery flying away behind you is fresh" (Ch11 P0040), implies she rode in that truck too (INFERENCE, moderate). She tells their harmless parts at bedtime, "like reading picture books to a child" (Ch11 P0047). That is in tension with her earlier 「覚えていません」 (Ch06 P0119), which concerned only whether they were lonely.

## 10. Wishes

Asked how she would spend a few months left, she first says she doesn't know. Later she brings back "a fairly serious answer" with three wishes (Ch09 P0082–P0099; `TDH-C043`):

1. **The star lake.** A lake where, as a child, she saw stars that were the most beautiful thing she has known (Ch09 P0090). It is fulfilled in Ch11. She recognizes the road ("I've been here, long ago, before I became a watcher"), guides him there, and makes him keep his eyes down and then closed. She leads him by the hand to a jetty and tells him when to look (Ch11 P0163–P0185). On the way she asks a deceiving hypothetical: what if she were leading him somewhere dangerous, like a cliff or a bridge? When he says he sees no reason she would, she answers "I see," sounding bored (Ch11 P0177–P0183; `TDH-C055`). She was deceiving him at that moment about the price. The image of a fall echoes Himeno's planned leap from the observation deck (INFERENCE, low; no link is drawn). At the lake she lies beside him. When he says he understands why she wanted to see it again, she says 「そうでしょう？」 (Ch11 P0193).
2. **A grave.** Not necessarily a bought plot: even a big stone she decides is her grave, so long as it lasts at least some decades after her death (Ch09 P0092). This wish is never taken up again (`TDH-C043` v3b). It stands, unremarked, beside Kusunoki's refusal of posthumous fame (Ch14 P0168) and his closing contentment to leave nothing (Ch15 P0111).
3. **A once-important person.** "Something like" a man, whom she would visit "even if I might be hurt." That is why she says she has no right to deny his visit to Himeno (Ch09 P0092–P0097). He is identified as her childhood friend (Ch11 P0199–P0201). She sees him in Ch13 (§9.3).

**Later wishes:**

- to know his wish, as her own (Ch13 P0115);
- to give rather than only receive (Ch13 P0113);
- memories "that will warm me any number of times" (Ch13 P0087);
- that he stay one more month (Ch14 P0142).

His wish, revealed at the fireworks, is that she forget him completely (Ch14 P0128). She refuses at once (P0129).

## 11. Preferences and ordinary life

**Food and drink.**

- *On duty:* calorie bars and mineral water bought in bulk (Ch04 P0060); mineral water drunk with eyes closed (Ch06 P0026); a doughnut nibbled (Ch09 P0080); a beef-bowl meal (Ch11 P0029).
- *Shared with him:* festival food (Ch09 P0125–P0126; she licks the twelfth item, a candied apple, with a prim face); grilled char and soft-serve (Ch12 P0115).
- *Himeno's cold pasta* (Ch10 P0163–P0164).
- *Small appetite:* the narrator says both have small stomachs (Ch09 P0126).
- *Alcohol:* refused while working (Ch04 P0075; Ch06 P0115). 「お酒は飲めませんよ」 (Ch10 P0018) can mean "can't" or "mustn't." Whether she drinks at Shinbashi's party is not stated (Ch14 P0056).
- *Smoking:* once, a cigarette taken from him at dawn (Ch08 P0087–P0089). Otherwise she looks reproachfully at his cigarette ends (Ch09 P0117).

**Habits and comforts.**

- the corner and the hugged knees (§6.1);
- brief, broken sleep (Ch07 P0090);
- bathing while he sleeps (Ch11 P0006);
- sunscreen (Ch07 P0045);
- whistling: Nina Simone's 「アイ・ウィッシュ・アイ・ニュー」 as she tidies cushions (Ch10 P0260), and 「スタンド・バイ・ミー」 on the swan boat (Ch13 P0098); the text does not develop either song;
- reading over his shoulder (Ch09 P0111–P0113);
- folding cranes and throwing them over his head (Ch11 P0048).

**Likes she states:**

- vending machines, for their constancy and their clear-cut exchange, and because they don't ignore watchers (Ch11 P0066–P0068);
- his Auggie Wren-style project of photographing meaningless things: 「そういうの、私も好きですよ」, "I like that kind of thing too" (Ch11 P0070);
- his left shoulder getting wet under a small umbrella, which looks comic to others but means something warm: 「こういうの、好きだなあ」 (Ch13 P0059–P0061);
- the swan boat, which makes her laugh throughout (Ch13 P0095–P0096);
- stars and fireflies (Ch09 P0090; Ch12 P0123–P0126).

**Dislikes and aversions:** being asked about her drawings (Ch11 P0113); talking in public on the job (§6.1); his self-sacrifice (Ch13 P0025; Ch14 P0132). Cats do not particularly like her (Ch11 P0105).

**Stable versus situational.** The duty habits (corner, notebook, separate meals, no alcohol) change once she is no longer on duty: she shares his bed, eats with him and shops with him. The aesthetic preferences recur (permanence, the "actually seen," the comic-but-warm) and look stable. The bulk calorie bars are a duty measure, not shown to be a taste.

### 11.1 Her drawing

- **The first sign (Ch11).** In a park she sketches a cat in pencil in her notebook. He says it isn't very good. She answers proudly that this is why she practises, and dodges his request to see more (Ch11 P0106–P0114; `TDH-C053`). He wonders whether her "observation record" was really her hobby all along (P0107). She had earlier been heard running a pencil over the notebook beside him at the shrine (Ch09 P0135).
- **The sketchbook (Ch14).** A sketchbook, introduced as 「例のスケッチブック」, "*the* sketchbook" (Ch14 P0081), though no sketchbook has been mentioned before, holds drawings of their places. By the list these run from day 2 to Ch13: the station waiting room, the restaurant where they met Naruse, the time-capsule school, the secret base, the room of cranes, the library, the festival, the riverbank, the observation deck, the community hall, the motorbike, the sweet shop, vending machines, the phone booth, the star lake, the bookshop, the swan boat, the Ferris wheel. Then: 「そして、俺の寝顔」, "and my sleeping face" (Ch14 P0083–P0084).
- **What is not established.** That the sketchbook is hers is strong inference. Whether it is the notebook of Ch11–Ch12, which Kusunoki handed to the substitute, or another book is open. When the drawings were made, at the time or from memory, is unknown. So is the quality of her work ([U14 §4](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction); `TDH-C067`).
- **What it did for him.** "In return," he draws her sleeping face on a new page. It is his first finished drawing in years, and that night he recovers his technique (Ch14 P0085–P0097). The narrator credits her: drawing "*for myself*," for pure pleasure, was what he needed, and 「その機会をくれたのが、ミヤギだったというわけだ」, "and it was Miyagi, then, who gave me the chance" (Ch14 P0190). INFERENCE (moderate): her drawing leads, through his, to the second sale's high price. The narrator's own chosen theory credits her presence with the flowering of his talent (Ch14 P0175–P0176). The price rests on the appraisal's painting future (Ch14 P0172, P0193; hearsay). The sale's purpose is stated only in Ch15 (P0098; `TDH-C061` v3–v4). The text's accounts of when he stopped painting conflict (Ch06 P0074; Ch10 P0119–P0121; Ch14 P0182–P0188; `TDH-C067`), but the conflict does not affect her part.
- **What she draws.** She drew what she watched: his days and his sleep. Her watching and her drawing turn out, at least in part, to be one activity (INFERENCE). Her reaction to his drawing of her is never narrated.

## 12. Romantic and intimate psychology (only as evidenced)

- **Attraction and attachment**, by her testimony: he stopped being "just a subject" after the station night. His talking to her and treating her as present delighted her. 「だから、好きなんです。あなたのこと」, "That's why I love you" (Ch12 P0183–P0188). She frames the love as hopeless from the start: "there's no point loving someone who's going to disappear" (P0189).
- **Her romantic history** is the childhood friend: co-dependent, possessive in wish, never resumed (§9.3). She also states, as a prediction, that no other man will appear for her (Ch14 P0135–P0137).
- **Physical intimacy.**
  - *Hand-holding.* At the drunken riverside rehearsal she lets him lead her by the hand, looking troubled, then seems to grow used to it (らしい; Ch10 P0057–P0058). Her later testimony counts hand-holding by the river among the "dream-like" things (Ch12 P0183). That is a case where his reading of her face and her own account diverge.
  - *Embraces:* after the confession (Ch12 P0199); in public around the town (Ch14 P0049); through the night at Ch14 P0144; at the door at Ch14 P0149. In Ch15 she embraces him first (P0064).
  - *Kisses.* At the station after seeing Enishi, he kisses her unasked. By his narration she first stiffens, then responds (Ch13 P0157–P0159). The reviewers held any consent claim beyond his narration of her response as a non-claim (U13 §13.2). At Shinbashi's party she blushes and is kissed in front of everyone (Ch14 P0063).
  - *The bed.* From back to back (Ch11 P0019), via the last night when she gets in herself (Ch11 P0211), to "the boundary had gone" and sleeping face to face (Ch14 P0079–P0080).
  - *Her touch:* perhaps stroking his head (Ch10 P0250); a poke to the shoulder (Ch13 P0063); pushing him down at the fireworks (Ch14 P0138), which inverts his Ch08 impulse 押し倒したくなった, "wanted to push her down" (`TDH-F035`).
- **Boundaries.**
  - *Under threat in Ch08* she neither flees nor fights. She stiffens, with a face he reads as sad and resigned (Ch08 P0022–P0030). Why she stayed is unknown; rivals include fear, resignation, a bluff, and duty (`TDH-S042`).
  - *Her offer in Ch12*, to do anything to her, "however cruel" (Ch12 P0195–P0197), is a self-punishing offer. He turns it into an embrace, and she calls that 「ひどいこと」, "a cruel thing," because now she cannot forget him (P0203–P0204).
- **Jealousy.** Two items, both open. She asks whether he will say the same thing to Himeno (Ch10 P0051). She deflects his "and what would you say?" with "I don't understand" (Ch10 P0064). Possessiveness toward her friend is her own testimony (Ch11 P0205).
- **Intimate preference** beyond this is not evidenced.

## 13. Capabilities and limitations

- **Professional knowledge.** She knows the shop's procedures and the watcher system as she presents it. She knows the "synopsis" of his life and others' might-have-beens (Ch03 P0108–P0110). She knows something of valuation criteria, testimony against her Ch02 disclaimer (Ch13 P0016–P0018).
- **Practical skills.** She remembers a childhood road well enough to navigate a mountain route at night (Ch11 P0163–P0171). She gives restrained fashion advice: cleanliness is enough (Ch10 P0036). She draws, in pencil, by her own account as a learner (Ch11 P0111). She folds cranes well, by inference (Ch09 P0019–P0020). She whistles. She endures broken sleep and long watching.
- **Supernatural condition.** She can handle objects and people without anyone perceiving it (Ch06 P0058–P0060). She is perceptible to the watched, and off duty to would-be sellers. Animals see her. On the substitute's testimony, she cannot see other watchers.
- **Limits on her use of power.** She cannot forbid anything. After Ch12 her deterrents are, by her own account, fictions. Whether she could report anyone at all is unknown. Her real lever is information, and the text shows her both using it and withholding it.
- **Capacity versus willingness.**
  - She could have told him the true price, and did not.
  - She could have stopped his Himeno visit by telling him the plan, and chose not to (Ch09 P0040; Ch10 P0074–P0077).
  - She could perhaps have reported the Ch08 approach, and did not.
  - She could have let him give away all the money, and tried to stop him (Ch10 P0210).
  - In Ch15, with most of her debt paid by his sale (Ch14 P0193), she could have worked off the remainder: about three more years, by hearsay. By her account she sold all but three days instead.

## 14. Longitudinal development

### 14.1 What actually changes

- **Register and manner.** The change from procedural keigo to cool polite speech, to loosened, playful, sulking and pleading polite speech, is shown across contexts and is not only a later revelation (§7.1).
- **Distance.** She moves from the corner and five steps behind to the pillion, the shared page, the bed and the embrace. Kusunoki's narration, with a hedge, dates her softening to after the station night (Ch09 P0016–P0017). Her own testimony puts the change in her view of him at the same point (Ch12 P0183). The two sources agree, but both are interested.
- **Stance toward visibility,** from "think of me as not here" to joy at being "there" (§8).
- **Outlook on her future,** from the hope of a proper life after repayment (Ch08 P0074), to dying before repayment (Ch12 P0163), to decades of memory (Ch13 P0085), to thirty years alone (Ch14 P0142), to selling it all, by her account (Ch15 P0094). The changes track her relationship with him. They are not one development.
- **Role in the giving.** At first the one who withholds and corrects. Then, secretly, a giver (the ¥300,000, by her testimony). Then the one who feels she only receives (Ch13 P0113). Finally the one whose act gives the ending its value (Ch15).

### 14.2 What is revealed rather than developed

- Her history (Ch08).
- Her motive for early harshness (Ch08 P0082).
- The price lie and gift (Ch12).
- The rule lies (Ch12).
- Her concealment of Himeno's plan (Ch10).
- Her drawing (Ch11, Ch14).

These recast earlier scenes without showing that she changed. Kusunoki's revision of his own view, from "inborn" coldness to "traits acquired to survive" (Ch08 P0057), is a change in him, not in her.

### 14.3 What stays constant

- Politeness toward him.
- Self-deprecation.
- Watchfulness: she reads his intentions throughout.
- The posture.
- A habit of mirroring his words back to him: the soaking (Ch10), the freedom-to-take-it lines (Ch12), the promise to grieve (Ch12 P0186 / P0206).
- A willingness to be hurt for a person who matters: "even if I might be hurt" (Ch09 P0097), which she carries out at the Enishi station and, by her account, in Ch15.

## 15. The last movement: confession, plea, farewell, return

**Confession (Ch12 P0138–P0207).**

- She evades or denies four times (P0140, P0143, P0148, P0158). She first says she doesn't quite know what he means (P0140), then holds to the lie: "of course," "your value is that much" (P0143).
- She guesses the substitute's role (P0146), denies again (P0148, P0158), and smiles when he sits in her posture in the other corner (P0160).
- She gives her reasons:
  - a shared root of loneliness and despair behind both their gifts (P0170);
  - perhaps a wish to be the giver (P0172);
  - she apologizes that without the lie he might not have sold at all (P0170).
- She names the real value, thirty yen, in a small voice (P0166). Then she tells him that to her he is worth "thirty million, three billion" (P0174), echoing his drunk Ch05 vow (Ch05 P0014). She rejects his "don't console me" with mock rudeness (P0178–P0180).
- Then the change of view, the joy of being present, and the avowal (P0183–P0189), followed by the disclosure of the rule lies (P0193).
- The exchange ends in mirrored promises: he will do this for her "until I die," and she will grieve him "until I die" (P0186, P0205–P0206; `TDH-F032`).

**Objection and requests (Ch13).** She says she understands he is trying to pay her debt. She is glad, but asks him not to spend his remaining time that way, and calls the idea off the mark (P0025). She asks to be paid attention (P0027). She says she is already saved and will live on remembering (P0085), and asks instead for memories (P0087). This request is what the narrator credits with his decision to live foolishly (P0090). That "wisest decision" prolepsis is never explicitly resolved (`TDH-C064`).

**Plea (Ch14 P0108–P0145).**

- At the fireworks he thanks her for everything. She corrects him playfully: "and 'please keep it up,' surely? We still have more than a month" (P0124).
- She promises to do anything she can (P0127), and he asks her to forget him (P0128).
- She refuses at once, then realizes what he means to do the next day (P0130–P0131) and asks him not to do anything foolish (P0132).
- To his argument that a better man may appear, she says twice that none will (P0135, P0137). She pushes him down, cries for the first time in his hearing, and pleads.
- She lists what she already endures (P0142):
  - his coming death;
  - not seeing him on her days off;
  - not showing others their joined hands;
  - thirty years alone afterwards.

  So at least this time together, she asks, he should not throw away himself.
- He answers only by stroking her hair (P0143). They sleep embracing; her tears do not stop (P0144–P0145).

**Farewell (Ch14 P0148–P0152).** At midnight she leaves. They embrace again at the door. She smiles "lonelily" (his reading), says 「さようなら。幸せでした」, bows, and walks away in the moonlight. Her finality goes against her own "only one day" (P0114). INFERENCE, moderate: she expects that if he sells down to three days the watcher will be withdrawn, so she will not come back as his watcher. That rule is known only from her own testimony, and she never retracted it. Rivals:

- 幸せでした sums up the night, with no finality meant;
- she expects reassignment;
- she cannot bear to come back;
- another rule applies;
- the three-day rule is itself a lie (`TDH-C059` v1a; [U14 §4](../readings/TDH_U14_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)).

His reply and her reaction to his sale are not narrated.

**His sale and her debt (Ch14 P0193).** The narration says his thirty days sold for an extraordinarily high price, which fell "just short" (あと一歩至らなかった) of clearing her debt. That the proceeds went to her debt is strong inference. By hearsay (らしい), she would be free after about three more years of work. This is the situation her Ch15 decision answers.

**Return (Ch15 P0053–P0108).** As strangers condemn the absent "no-good" Miyagi, her voice agrees from behind him: "yes, when he's such a good person." She laughs, repeats that Miyagi is a no-good woman, and hugs him. Then: 「ただいま、クスノキさん。……探しましたよ」, "I'm home, Kusunoki. … I was looking for you" (P0053–P0067). Everyone sees her. She jokes with the crowd (P0079).

**What she did, by her own account (Ch15 P0089–P0108).** All T?:

- She "did the same thing" as he did (P0090).
- She sold "everything" and has "only three days left" (P0094). What she sold is unexpressed. That it was lifespan is strong inference from his question about how much she sold, her 「あなたと同じ」, "the same as you," and the word 寿命 being used only of his sale in that scene ([U15 §4](../readings/TDH_U15_DEEP_READING.md#4-factual-and-causal-reconstruction)). Her three days fit the shop's refusal, reported for his sale, to buy the last three days (Ch14 P0158) (INFERENCE, moderate).
- The substitute told her right after Kusunoki's sale that Kusunoki had paid most of her debt. She had decided by the time he finished speaking, and he handled the paperwork (P0096).
- Thanks to Kusunoki her own life's value "seems" (みたい) to have risen, so the debt is fully paid and there is "more money than we could spend in three days" (P0105).
- She praises him, "you bought back most of my life in thirty days," and apologizes for throwing away the life he had bought back: 「馬鹿ですよね、私」 (P0102).
- In mock grandeur they agree that she is now 「お金持ち」, "rich" (P0106–P0107). By lexical inference that answers Himeno's old prophecy of wealth (Ch01 P0070–P0072; `TDH-C071`).
- She asks how they will spend the three days (P0121).

What she does not say: her motive; when she sold; whether her three days line up with his; whether the substitute's help was proper. Buying back lifespan with time, which her own Ch08 testimony allowed, is never raised. The narrator calls her act 「彼女の裏切り」, "her betrayal," and 「彼女の愚かさ」, "her foolishness," both "dearer than anything" (P0100). That ties back, moderately to strongly, to the Ch11 prolepsis of her "betraying" him (`TDH-C056` v2). The three days themselves, and any death, are not narrated (`TDH-C072`).

## 16. Operational behavioral profile (conditional, literary)

*This is a compact reading aid derived from the portrait. It does not claim that any reconstruction model exists or has been tested. It assigns no capability grade. **Speech defaults for this profile:** polite main clauses to Kusunoki under all pressures, with plain trailing clauses (〜から, 〜のに, 〜ので) in emotional disclosure; あなた / クスノキさん; さん for others; sentence-final から, ね and でしょう; dry irony early, teasing later; formal set phrases carrying intimate content; apologies graded from 申し訳ありません to ごめんなさい.

**Abstention zones.** The profile should not be used for:

- her behaviour during the three days or at death (unnarrated);
- her life off duty, on days off, or with other watchers;
- her conduct toward anyone at length other than Kusunoki;
- her inner response to Enishi;
- the contents of her notebook or sketchbook;
- sexual or intimate preference beyond what §12 records;
- her real valuation or the true rules of the system;
- whether any of her uncorroborated testimony is true;
- situations needing a register toward a superior or an institution (only Ch02 shop keigo is evidenced);
- her speech when angry at him (the text never clearly shows her angry; her closest moments are sulking and pleading).

## 17. Interpretive uncertainty

1. **Testimony standing.** Almost every load-bearing fact about her past and the system rests on her word. P0193 leaves open which statements were lies (`TDH-C059`). Her own valuation (Ch08), the three-day rule, her history, her "already saved" and her Ch15 account cannot be checked against independent evidence.
2. **Early motive.** Care and duty both fit the early chapters. Her Ch12 testimony dates personal feeling from the station night, which leaves the first days to duty and self-reproach. Kusunoki's rival, that she was instructed to soften (Ch09 P0017), is never refuted except by her testimony.
3. **The price lie's motive.** She gives three, all hedged: shared despair, wanting to give, and "anyone would have done" (Ch12 P0170–P0183). Whether the gift was aimed at him or at anyone is undecidable, and by her account it was first anyone.
4. **"No one will ever like you again."** Her later love contradicts it, and the text never reconciles the two. Rivals: it was a lie; it described the original course; it was self-exclusion (`TDH-C058`).
5. **"I don't remember"** (Ch06 P0119), against her later stories. Rivals: evasion, a lie, or forgetting only that particular point.
6. **Why she stayed in Ch08**, and whether she could have reported him. Unknown.
7. **The Ch09 prolepsis.** That she knew the feeling "long before" him is given content only by inference: the childhood friend (Ch11) or the shared root (Ch12 P0170) (`TDH-C010` v1a–1c).
8. **The head-stroking** (Ch10 P0250). The narrator himself is unsure whether it was a dream.
9. **The notebook and the sketchbook.** Identity, contents, when the drawings were made, and whether she left the notebook behind on purpose are all open (`TDH-C056` v1a; `TDH-C067`).
10. **The betrayal prolepsis** (Ch11 P0154–P0158). Her Ch15 sale is the moderate-to-strong referent. The live rivals are the price lie (weakened), her love as a defeated expectation (low to moderate), and her absence (very weak). If the referent is her sale, the Ch11 passage was written or revised after the reunion, which no narrated writing covers (`TDH-C056` v2).
11. **Her farewell's finality.** Explained by the three-day rule (moderate). Rivals are listed in §15.
12. **Ch15:** what she sold (lifespan, strong inference), her motive, when she sold, whether she is still a watcher, and why she is visible. None is stated.
13. **The "wisest decision"** (Ch13 P0090). That her foolishness answers his is a HYPOTHESIS among candidates, with no sentence linking them (`TDH-C064` v2).
14. **Composition.** Ch01–Ch11 and the opening of Ch12 were first drafted on about days 30–31 (Ch12 P0074), before the price-lie hint and her confession. Later parts were written afterwards, and the whole was revised on the first morning of the last three days (Ch15 P0010). Early "machine-like" verdicts are staged then-perceptions. Which passages carry later revision is unknown, for example Ch11 P0154–P0158 if the "betrayal" is her sale (`TDH-C010`).
15. **Unknowns with no opportunity in the text:** her knee wound; her schooling after ten; whether ミヤギ is a surname; her full name; where she lives off duty; who her other subjects were by name.
16. **Gaps where the text had an opportunity:** the grave wish never comes back; buying back lifespan is never raised in Ch15; her reaction to his completed sale is not narrated; her response to his drawing of her is not narrated.
