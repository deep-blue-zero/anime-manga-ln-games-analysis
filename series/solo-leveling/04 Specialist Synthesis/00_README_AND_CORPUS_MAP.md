# 00 — README and Corpus Map
## Scope, source hierarchy, navigation, and executive orientation

## 1. What this corpus is

This is the definitive project-level analytical corpus for *Solo Leveling* across the complete supplied Chugong prose and the official English manhwa through Volume 15. It is designed as a multi-document reference rather than a single compressed essay. Each document has a defined analytical home, while Document 13 preserves the chronological evidence trail needed to check later full-series conclusions against what was knowable at each stage.

The corpus covers:

- the complete Korean main story;
- all **21 numbered `외전`** side stories;
- the two separately designated **`후일담`** postscripts;
- official English manhwa Volumes **1–15**;
- adaptation structure, visual grammar, recomposition, and manhwa-original material where the supplied Chugong prose has no counterpart.

The Korean omnibus explicitly marks the main-story endpoint with `[나 혼자만 레벨업 完]`, later closes the twenty-one side stories with `[나 혼자만 레벨업, 외전 完]`, and then introduces a separate `나 혼자만 레벨업 후일담` block containing `베르의 기억` and `다시 만날 때까지`. The two postscripts are therefore **not** treated as Side Stories 22–23.

**Complete-series spoilers apply throughout Documents 00–12.** Document 13 preserves volume-local interpretive horizons, but its later-correction fields also contain full-series spoilers.

## 2. Source hierarchy and hard evidentiary boundaries

The corpus follows a fixed hierarchy.

### 1. Original Korean novel — governing prose source

Chugong's Korean novel governs:

- exact Korean wording and terminology;
- Jinwoo's continuous interiority;
- motive and ethical hesitation;
- humor, embarrassment, and mundane cognition;
- family and economic reasoning;
- institutional and political explanation;
- explicit metaphysical exposition;
- the structure and content of the `외전` and `후일담`.

The supplied omnibus is textually complete but **bibliographically flattened**. Its thirty-three HTML bodies are digital packaging units, not reliable literary chapters or original Korean print-volume divisions. Early headings survive through `전직 퀘스트`; later prose is therefore referenced by verified `index_split_XXX` regions plus scene anchors. This corpus does not invent fourteen-volume Korean prose boundaries that the source file does not preserve.

### 2. Official English manhwa — governing visual and adaptation source

The official English manhwa governs:

- panel and page composition;
- body language and visual hierarchy;
- collected-volume sequence;
- page-level iconography;
- public reaction and gaze;
- adaptation pacing;
- omission, expansion, relocation, transformation, and recomposition;
- material that is demonstrably manhwa-original relative to the supplied prose.

All fifteen CBZs passed the Phase 1 machine audit: **4,479 JPEG image files representing 4,635 logical pages**, including 156 logical pages encoded as spread images.

### 3. Original Korean-language manhwa — unavailable

The project does **not** currently possess the original Korean-language manhwa edition. Therefore:

> **Korean novel wording is never presented as proof of the original wording of a manhwa speech balloon.**

A comparison between Chugong's Korean prose and an English comic line is an **adaptation comparison**, not automatically a translation audit.

### Material outside current scope

The anime is not part of the governing corpus. It may later receive a separate adaptation document, but no audiovisual-anime claim is silently imported into this synthesis.

## 3. Executive thesis

*Solo Leveling* is not fundamentally a story in which weakness is morally defective and strength repairs the person. Sung Jinwoo already possesses moral agency before he possesses effective agency. His foundational wound is that good judgment, care, and responsibility can become causally useless when the person exercising them is weak, dependent, disposable, and unable to protect those he values. The System answers that wound by making effort effective, but it also tries to reserve the purpose of Jinwoo's growth to an external author. His full arc is therefore better described as:

> **constrained agency → effective agency → leverage → autonomy → sovereignty → chosen responsibility → self-authorship → inheritance without erasure → life beyond usefulness → generational autonomy**

Jinwoo's central desire is **non-subordination more than domination**. He wants to become difficult to own, exploit, discard, or overwrite. Yet the achievement of sovereignty creates the series' principal political and ethical problem: once almost nobody can constrain him, his private judgment can become decisive for weaker people, shadows, institutions, nations, history, and his own child.

The mature formulation used throughout this corpus is:

> **Sung Jinwoo is a sword that recognizes constitutions, cooperates with them, and often protects the space in which they operate—but never concedes final authority over his exceptional judgment. His moral achievement is that the sword acquires restraint, attachment, discrimination, and a self-authored protective code. His political limitation is that personal virtue cannot reproduce procedure, distributed authority, independent review, or succession.**

The final power fantasy is therefore not simply “become strong enough to rule everyone.” It is:

> **become strong enough that nobody can own your future, then learn not to own everyone else's.**

## 4. Adaptation thesis

The novel and manhwa are not competing containers for an identical work.

The novel keeps Jinwoo psychologically continuous through money, family logistics, humor, caution, embarrassment, institutional reasoning, resentment, ethical hesitation, and ordinary cognition even as his scale becomes mythic.

The manhwa increasingly converts cognition into **visual punctuation**: eyes, silhouettes, kneeling and standing, vertical scale, shadow mass, reaction shots, body transformation, spatial dominance, and concentrated psychological tableaux. It also recomposes chronology and emphasis. Important examples include the Kasaka and Taeshik power imagery, Esil's expansion, compressed or delayed Japanese political causation, forward-loading in the Architect confrontation, the stronger firefighter/family vocation surrounding Japan, and Volume 13's restructuring of closure around life beyond usefulness.

The compact adaptation thesis is:

> **Novel Jinwoo is psychologically continuous; manhwa Jinwoo is psychologically punctuated and visually mythologized.**

The manhwa then reverses its own escalation after the reset. Gardens, running, courtship, work, family, and fatherhood deliberately reduce visual scale, asking whether a body drawn as myth can return to ordinary human space.

## 7. Evidence labels

The corpus uses the following analytical labels:

- **TF — Textual Fact:** explicit fact in Chugong's Korean prose.
- **VF — Visual Fact:** directly visible manhwa evidence.
- **AF — Adaptation Fact:** demonstrable difference or structural relationship between prose and manhwa.
- **KC — Korean-Language Claim:** interpretation dependent on Korean wording or terminology.
- **SI — Strong Inference:** highly supported inference not directly stated.
- **TI — Thematic Interpretation:** interpretive synthesis of recurring evidence.
- **VJ — Value Judgment:** normative ethical or political evaluation by this analysis.
- **UA — Unresolved Ambiguity:** source does not settle the issue.

These labels are not a hierarchy from “true” to “false.” They identify **what kind of claim is being made**.

## 8. Cross-reference and locator conventions

Internal references use document number and filename where useful: e.g. **Document 06** or `06_SHADOW_ARMY_PERSONHOOD_MONARCHY_AND_DEPENDENT_SOCIETY.md`.

Manhwa locators use **Volume + logical page**, e.g. `V15 p061`. The Phase 1 audit distinguishes logical pages from individual spread-image files.

Korean prose locators use:

- surviving heading where available;
- `index_split_XXX` scene region where later heading structure disappears;
- scene anchor or distinctive wording when needed.

The `index_split` number is a retrieval locator, **not a literary chapter or print-volume claim**.

The intended traceability chain is:

> **full-series claim → specialist document → Document 13 evidence entry → manhwa page / Korean scene zone → primary source**

## 9. Naming and romanization policy

Character names generally follow the stable English romanizations used across the project and official English manhwa, while Korean terms are preserved in Hangul when linguistic form matters.

Examples include **Sung Jinwoo, Yoo Jinho, Cha Hae-In, Go Gunhee, Woo Jinchul, Thomas Andre, Ashborn, Igris, Beru, Bellion**, and **Tusk/Fang** where source naming variation itself is analytically useful.

Honorific or relational Korean is not replaced when the relationship encoded by the term matters. In particular, `형` / `형님` is discussed directly rather than being reduced without comment to “Boss.”

No Korean comic wording is reconstructed from the Korean novel.

## 10. Short glossary

**`나 혼자만 레벨업`** — approximately **“Only I level up” / “I alone level up.”** The title foregrounds metaphysical exception more strongly than a preference for solitude.

**`인류 최약병기`** — **“humanity's weakest weapon.”** More than “weakest hunter”: Jinwoo begins as failed utility, later becomes incomparable utility, and finally must live beyond utility.

**`무력한 자의 용기`** — **“The Courage of the Powerless.”** Moral agency can precede effective force; the phrase does not imply that deprivation is morally necessary.

**`기브 앤 테이크`** — Korean phonetic form of **“give and take.”** Jinwoo's early reciprocal moral grammar: return care with care and harm with harm, later expanded beyond reciprocity toward chosen guardianship.

**`형님`** — deferential older-brother address used by a male speaker; can contain hierarchy, respect, and kinship simultaneously. “Boss” communicates only part of the relation.

**`군주`** — monarch, lord, sovereign, ruler. The term has genuine political semantics and is central to the distinction between possessing final command and possessing legitimate authority.

**Player** — the System-designated developmental role through which Jinwoo alone can level. It creates effective agency while concealing the intended destination of his development.

**System** — the Architect's growth apparatus for preparing Jinwoo as a vessel for Ashborn. It is both enabling and coercive: it reopens growth while reserving the purpose of growth to an external author.

**Shadow Monarch** — the sovereign title/domain associated with command over the shadow army. Jinwoo ultimately receives Ashborn's inheritance without being erased into Ashborn.

**Rulers** — cosmic actors opposed to the Monarchs who intervene in the human world through vessels and the Cup of Reincarnation. Their strategic role does not by itself prove moral legitimacy.

**Monarchs** — cosmic sovereign beings associated with destruction, armies, domains, and vesselhood. `군주` is therefore more than ornamental fantasy rank.

**Cup of Reincarnation** — the Rulers' time-reversal artifact. Jinwoo uses the opportunity it provides to replace the catastrophic hunter-era history with a timeline in which he bears the hidden war himself.

## 11. Manhwa-only Suho material

A hard source boundary governs all later Suho analysis:

> **Official English manhwa Volume 15 p061–p267, Parts 5–6, is manhwa-original relative to the supplied Chugong prose.**

Part 4 reaches the supplied prose-derived terminal sequence by p060. The adolescent-Suho trials, renewed progression interface, judgment/retreat pedagogy, `Beautiful World`, and final-door sequence belong to the adaptation continuation.

They may be analyzed as:

- visual/adaptation evidence;
- the adaptation team's theory of inheritance;
- a development of questions already latent in the prose.

They may **not** be cited as Chugong-prose evidence.

## 12. Known limits and unresolved archival questions

The corpus deliberately preserves several limits rather than inventing certainty:

1. **Original Korean-language manhwa wording is unavailable.**
2. **Exact paragraph-level Korean locators after the surviving heading structure require raw-EPUB remount for publication-grade quotation.**
3. **Some exact late `외전`-to-manhwa Part allocation remains source-uncertain even though the broader correspondence and the V15 p061 hard boundary are secure.**
4. **The `Jin-i` label required by the initial architecture's shadow matrix could not be verified in the current primary-source map or completed synthesis. Document 12 therefore preserves it as a UA rather than inventing an identity.**
5. Successful outcomes are never treated as automatic proof of ethical or political legitimacy; normative conclusions remain analytical judgments rather than facts stated by the narrative.

---

## 13. Analytical precedence

> **This corpus supersedes earlier project-level Solo Leveling summaries and thematic syntheses. Earlier volume analyses remain part of the evidentiary history, but where a mature full-series conclusion conflicts with an earlier provisional interpretation, the final multi-document corpus takes precedence unless the earlier claim is explicitly preserved as an unresolved counter-reading.**

Within this corpus, primary sources remain superior to synthesis. If a later source audit shows that an analytical claim conflicts with the Korean novel or the verified manhwa page sequence, the claim should be corrected rather than defended for consistency.

The final corpus should therefore be used in this order of authority:

> **primary source → evidence ledger / correspondence audit → specialist synthesis → compact comparative proposition**

That order preserves the project's central methodological commitment: full-series hindsight should deepen interpretation without being allowed to rewrite the evidence that made the interpretation possible.
