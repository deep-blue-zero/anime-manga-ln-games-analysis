## XXIX. Young Miyagi experiences one pair of shoes as abandonment; adult Miyagi experiences it as temporary absence

This may be one of the clearest ways to express her entire university arc.

Middle-school Miyagi:

> My friend left.
> There is one pair of shoes.
> The room has too much empty space.
> I hate having people over because the emptiness afterward becomes worse.

Adult Miyagi:

> Sendai isn't here.
> She is working.
> **She'll be back tonight.**

The physical circumstance is nearly identical:

> Miyagi is alone in the home.

The psychological meaning has changed.

Why?

Because absence has been inserted into a credible pattern of return.

This is exactly the transformation Volume 8 began establishing.

Volume 9 confirms it externally.
