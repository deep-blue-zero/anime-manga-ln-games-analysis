# XXI. The platypus ending is a miniature social-integration parable

Sendai piles Miyagi's cat chopstick rests on the platypus and tells them:

> **「仲良くね」**
> Get along.

The cats say:

> 「こんばんにゃー」

The platypus answers politely.

Then secretly says:

> 「にゃあ」 Shuukura - LN08 Release SS - カモ…

This is absurdly cute.

It also neatly stages the university arc.

Old objects and new objects do not have to eliminate one another.

Different attachments can inhabit the same room.

The solution is not:

> determine which one is most important and remove the others.

It is:

> **make a larger relational world.**

Volume 8 is increasingly about whether Miyagi and Sendai can do that with actual people.
