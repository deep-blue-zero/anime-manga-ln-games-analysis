# SHUUKURA V07 SS — 「宇都宮舞香の夏休み」

The Kakuyomu two-part **「宇都宮舞香の夏休み」** is particularly useful here.

Maika is preparing to return to her family home for summer.

Miyagi refuses to return to hers.

Maika presses a little on Ami's behalf, but Miyagi remains firm. She would rather stay where she is. She even starts giving Maika practical household reminders—locks, garbage—the same sort of things Maika's mother has just told her. Shuukura - LN07 Release SS - 宇都…

That is quietly striking.

Miyagi is acting like someone who has acquired a **household consciousness**.

And the place she intends to remain is not the apartment associated with her father's absence.

It is the home she shares with Sendai.

The story no longer has to announce:

> this new apartment has become home.

Miyagi's holiday behavior says it.

# XXXIII. Maika and Ami now know the impossible fact

When Maika returns home and meets Ami, the old trio's comic rhythm resumes immediately.

Then they discuss Miyagi.

Ami knows:

> Miyagi and Sendai live together.

This is explicitly no longer a secret because Miyagi herself told her. Shuukura - LN07 Release SS - 宇都…

Ami still finds it almost impossible to reconcile with the social model she remembers from school.

That is a lovely endpoint to the outside-observer arc we've been following since Volume 1.

Originally:

> Miyagi and Sendai having any meaningful connection seemed implausible.

Then:

> perhaps they secretly know each other.

Then:

> perhaps Miyagi has a secret romance.

Then:

> perhaps Miyagi and Sendai are secretly friends.

Now:

> **they openly live together, and that still sounds unbelievable.**

The secret has narrowed.

It is no longer:

> Are Miyagi and Sendai connected?

It is:

> **What, exactly, does their connection mean?**

# XXXIV. Maika has become an authorized witness rather than a detective

Maika tells Ami:

> 「志緒理、仙台さんとすごく仲良くて、二人を見たら驚くよ」

Shiori and Sendai are extremely close; seeing them together would surprise you. Shuukura - LN07 Release SS - 宇都…

That sentence marks a major evolution in Maika's narrative function.

She spent the high-school arc outside the relationship, carefully resisting conclusions.

Now she has entered their home.

She has interacted with Sendai.

She has seen how they behave.

She can testify that the relationship is real.

She still does not know its full nature.

But she is no longer reconstructing it from suspicious fragments.

She has become a **social witness to the household**.

That means Miyagi and Sendai's relationship is slowly entering the ordinary world.

Not through confession.

Through exposure.

---

# XXXV. Miyagi not returning home is more important than the SS makes it sound

Ami and Maika understandably want the old trio together again.

Miyagi chooses not to go.

This shouldn't be read as her abandoning her friends.

The SS makes clear that their friendship persists.

What has changed is the location around which Miyagi organizes **home**.

Maika goes home to her family.

Ami waits there.

Miyagi stays with Sendai.

That is a subtle adult transition.

Childhood and high-school friendships remain important.

But holiday return no longer automatically outranks the household Miyagi is building in the present.

Her life has acquired another center of gravity.
