# SHUUKURA V08 Internal SS — 「日曜の午後、私たちが会うまでに」

# IV. The internal bonus 「日曜の午後、私たちが会うまでに」 makes that development explicit

Haneda's afterword says exactly what the bonus is designed to do: show Miyagi's conflict over Sendai's invitation to the café and Sendai's behavior before Miyagi arrives.

The parallel POV is excellent.

### Miyagi

She knows seeing working Sendai may make everything worse.

If she sees one unknown Sendai, she'll want to see:

-   tutoring Sendai;

-   university Sendai;

-   every Sendai unavailable to her.


She understands:

> **「すべての仙台さんを知ることは不可能」**

Knowing every Sendai is impossible.

And she recognizes that seeking complete knowledge can simply intensify her desire to possess.

That is unusually good self-diagnosis.

She even reaches something close to:

> There are things people do not need to know.

This resembles the epistemic restraint Maika has modeled from the beginning.

But Miyagi ultimately goes anyway.

Why?

She manufactures the usual excuses:

-   staying home is boring;

-   Sendai told her she could come;

-   going with Maika is normal;

-   this doesn't mean she wants to see Sendai.


The excuses are comically transparent.

But the behavior matters.

Miyagi crosses the boundary voluntarily.

---

# V. Sendai, meanwhile, is waiting

The Sendai half makes the scene even better.

Sendai insists Miyagi probably won't come.

Then repeatedly looks toward the entrance.

Noto notices.

This is a wonderful reversal of the old relationship.

High-school Sendai arrived because Miyagi summoned her.

Now Sendai is at work hoping:

> **Miyagi will choose to come to her.**

And Miyagi does.

When the door opens, Sendai briefly thinks she is imagining her.

Then Miyagi says:

> Sendai-san told me to come with Maika, so I came.

Naturally, Miyagi still needs the invitation to function as cause.

Sendai answers:

> **「来てくれて嬉しい。待ってた」**

I'm happy you came. I was waiting.

Miyagi accuses her of lying.

Sendai isn't lying.

This is one of Volume 8's quiet transformations:

> **Miyagi is learning to go out into Sendai's world; Sendai is learning to wait there for Miyagi.**

Neither requires possession.

Both require trust that the other person may cross the distance voluntarily.
