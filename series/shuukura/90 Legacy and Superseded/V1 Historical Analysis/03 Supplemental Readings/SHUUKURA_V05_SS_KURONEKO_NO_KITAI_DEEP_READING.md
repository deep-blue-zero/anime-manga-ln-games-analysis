# SHUUKURA V05 SS — 「黒猫の期待」

**「黒猫の期待」** comes from a completely different emotional register. The black cat was chosen by Sendai and brought into Miyagi's old room. It also dislikes the dark box, because confinement reminds it of the bag in which it was originally transported. But when Miyagi packs it beside the crocodile, the darkness is suddenly **not lonely**. The cat becomes excited about the move partly because Miyagi has told it that moving means seeing Sendai. Shuukura - LN05 Release SS - 黒猫…

That produces an almost perfect miniature of the domestic theme:

> **The same cramped box feels different when someone else is inside it.**

Place matters.

Company changes what the place means.

And the two objects are literally packed together:

-   the object from Miyagi's pre-Sendai domestic life;

-   the object Sendai chose for Miyagi.


The old home and the new relationship are not discarded separately.

They are transported in the same box.

Then the main novel adds a third plush object: the **platypus tissue cover**, purchased for the shared apartment using their shared money.

That gives us a neat material progression:

**crocodile:** Miyagi's old private world.

**black cat:** Sendai's gift entering Miyagi's private world.

**platypus:** something created directly by their shared household.

# 「ワニの憂鬱」 and 「黒猫の期待」 are comic, but they sharpen the transition surprisingly well

The two commemorative SS should not be treated as literal omniscient testimony; their plush narrators are deliberately whimsical.

But as paratext they are unusually well placed.

The crocodile experiences moving as **displacement**. Sendai has changed Shiori's future, displaced the old room, introduced a rival black cat, and seemingly stolen attention. Yet the crocodile also declares that wherever Shiori goes is where it belongs. Shuukura - LN05 Release SS - ワニ…

That is almost the old attachment world learning that:

> continuity does not require unchanged geography.

The black cat has the opposite orientation. It is frightened of confinement, but once it discovers the crocodile beside it, the box stops feeling lonely. It then looks forward to finishing the move because moving means seeing Sendai. Shuukura - LN05 Release SS - 黒猫…

So the two SS quietly state the Volume 5 domestic thesis before the protagonists can:

> **A place can change completely and remain tolerable if somebody familiar is there with you.**

For Miyagi and Sendai, “home” is being transferred from architecture to relationship.
