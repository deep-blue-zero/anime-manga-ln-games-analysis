# SHUUKURA V06 BOOK☆WALKER SS — 「私たちのサイダーとオレンジジュースの行方」

**「私たちのサイダーとオレンジジュースの行方」** looks trivial on the surface.

They need drinks.

That's basically it.

Which is why it is analytically excellent.

Miyagi notices they are out of cider and orange juice but does not want to admit she wants something else, because she anticipates Sendai proposing a late shopping trip.

Later, Sendai is away at university and learns Miyagi is shopping.

She rushes home and asks Miyagi to wait a few minutes at the supermarket.

She literally runs because she wants to join her.

That alone represents an enormous distance from Volume 1.

Nobody is buying the other's time.

Sendai is spending effort merely to catch a grocery errand.

Then comes the best line of the SS:

> 「でも、ゴールは遠いほうがいい。」

The goal is better when it is farther away.

Sendai deliberately extends their route through the store by adding ingredients for stew.

The practical objective is the checkout.

The emotional objective is:

> **make the walk together last longer.**

This is almost the inverse of the original title premise.

Once, their time together was precisely measured:

a specific day, a specific payment, an implicit ending.

Now Sendai wants inefficient shopping because efficiency would make shared time end sooner.

The old relationship asked:

> How much time does ¥5,000 purchase?

The new relationship asks:

> **How slowly can we reach the register?**

That is a lovely piece of miniature writing.

# The grocery trip is also the closest thing yet to effortless dating

Sendai still technically manufactures a reason.

They need groceries.

They need drinks.

They might make stew.

But the excuse has become almost transparently irrelevant.

The relationship is moving through stages:

> necessary reason
> → dubious reason
> → obviously invented reason
> → reason neither person really believes
> → no reason.

The cat outing belongs around the third stage.

The supermarket SS is around the fourth.

Chapter 11's:

> 「したかったから。それだけ」

briefly reaches the fifth.

The formal progression of *Shuukura* may ultimately be less about “will they confess?” than:

> **How much intimacy can survive once every excuse has been removed?**
