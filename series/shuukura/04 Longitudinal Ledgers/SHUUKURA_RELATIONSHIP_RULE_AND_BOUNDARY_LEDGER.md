# Directional relationships, rules and boundaries

Schema initialized; **zero relationship findings, rules or commitments admitted**. Questions about attachment, dependence, autonomy, exclusivity, jealousy, agency and power remain questions.

## Responsibility and boundaries

This ledger owns `A → B` state independently of `B → A`, plus shared-rule records only where shared acceptance is evidenced. A rule that one person believes exists may differ from a proposed, agreed, performed or enforced rule. The disclosure ledger owns who knows or believes it; the claims ledger owns interpretation of its significance; source readings own detailed interactions. A relationship label never substitutes for this directional evidence.

For each relevant observed interaction record initiative, response, refusal or accommodation, pressure and alternatives actually available, concealment or disclosure, action and consequence, and repair. Distinguish payment, practical obligations, requests, permissions, desires and consent to the particular act. Neither payment nor acquiescence alone proves desire or a general permission. Record ambiguity and contradictory behavior without resolving it through a preselected moral or diagnostic label.
