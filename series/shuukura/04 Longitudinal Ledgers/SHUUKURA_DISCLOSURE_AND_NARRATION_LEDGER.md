## Responsibility and boundaries

This ledger owns propositions and who can know, believe, suspect, deny, conceal or misunderstand them at a specified point. Keep subject knowledge distinct from narrator access and analyst knowledge. A private narrator's account is evidence of that account, not automatic objective truth. The character ledger references information states, the relationship ledger references disclosure effects, and the claims ledger owns disputed interpretations.

Identifiers: `PROP-<witness>-<unit>-NNN` for a bounded proposition and `EPI-<witness>-<unit>-NNN` for disclosure/belief events. Do not renumber on revision. Native passage order, narrated-event time and disclosure order are separate axes. Retrospective disclosures append events and links to earlier states without rewriting earlier prospective freezes.
