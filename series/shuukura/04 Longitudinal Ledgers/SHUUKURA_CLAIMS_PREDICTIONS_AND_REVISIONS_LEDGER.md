## Responsibility and stable identities

This is the single canonical home for adjudication and revision history. Source observations remain in their unit readings, cumulative state in the three other ledgers, and developed arguments in their accepted specialist/integrated homes. Use `CLM-<unit>-NNN.vN`, `PRED-<unit>-NNN` and `V1X-NNN`, where the inventory's unit ID already identifies its witness lane and the record additionally binds its edition revision. Preserve prior formulations, dated source boundary, evidence set, and decision rationale. A current projection points to the latest version without deleting history.

Distinguish `SOURCE_FACT`, `INTERPRETATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `UNCERTAINTY`, and `NORMATIVE_EVALUATION`. A narrator's explicit statement is a source fact about a statement; its objective truth may be a hypothesis. Confidence is a reasoned qualitative assessment and does not replace evidence.

## Claim revision schema

| Claim ID / version / prior version | Type / witness / boundary | Exact formulation and scope | Evidence observation IDs / locators | Strongest counterevidence and competing explanation | Confidence with reason / missing evidence | Disposition and rationale | Current authoritative argument location / unresolved obligations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Standard revision vocabulary:

| State | Decision meaning |
| --- | --- |
| PRESERVE | The bounded earlier claim survives the new evidence without material change |
| STRENGTHEN | Additional independent support or resolved alternatives justify greater confidence or a supported extension |
| REVISE | Meaning, mechanism, scope or temporal applicability materially changes |
| DOWNGRADE | Support weakens or alternatives strengthen; retain only the warranted confidence or scope |
| REJECT | The bounded claim is contradicted or untenable; preserve its record and rejection evidence |
| OPEN | Evidence is insufficient or competing explanations remain unresolved |

These are evidence dispositions, not a forced linear progression. Novelty earns no credit. An uncertainty can remain OPEN at final release.
