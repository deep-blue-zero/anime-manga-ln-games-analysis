Standard revision vocabulary:

| State | Decision meaning |
| --- | --- |
| PRESERVE | The bounded earlier claim survives the new evidence without material change |
| STRENGTHEN | Additional independent support or resolved alternatives justify greater confidence or a supported extension |
| REVISE | Meaning, mechanism, scope or temporal applicability materially changes |
| DOWNGRADE | Support weakens or alternatives strengthen; retain only the warranted confidence or scope |
| REJECT | The bounded claim is contradicted or untenable; preserve its record and rejection evidence |
| OPEN | Evidence is insufficient or competing explanations remain unresolved |
