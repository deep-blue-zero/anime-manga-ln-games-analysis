# 約束の日と、可愛いと、私たち: a promised outing and the audience for praise

Sendai Hazuki has chosen Miyagi Shiori’s clothes, applied her makeup and braided her hair for an outing with Utsunomiya Maika. Sendai is delighted with the result and troubled that the day will not belong to the pair alone. Maika's enthusiastic response then makes that preparation public. Miyagi can recognize Sendai's good taste, compliment Maika and smile toward her while privately resisting repeated praise and keeping her own judgment of Sendai's beauty unspoken. The shared day begins without making those different responses identical. (O002–003/O007–011.)

## Preparation fulfills one offer while exposing another wish

Sendai opens with the words she spoke yesterday: she wanted to do Miyagi's hair and makeup. Today she describes the result in the common space. Clothes selection, makeup and a braid are already performed, and Miyagi appears especially cute to her. This is Sendai's attraction and satisfaction, not an objective beauty measurement. Her delight in going out with that Miyagi has an explicit qualification: they will leave and travel as two but meet Utsunomiya and become three. The anticipated loss concerns a whole day alone together, rather than an actual cancellation of the outing. (O002.)

The practical promise is specific. Sendai is going to help choose **Utsunomiya's clothes**; Miyagi has asked her to join the three-person trip for that purpose. That promise must not be confused with the already-completed styling of Miyagi. Sendai thinks Miyagi therefore has to go out with her. This is her account of the arrangement's force, not proof that Miyagi has no physical agency or that every detail of the day was mutually chosen. Preparation, an obligation to a friend, private preference and participation are related without being interchangeable. (O003.)

Miyagi says yes to leaving but does not move, instead gripping Sendai's skirt. Asked what she wants, she says nothing and lowers her gaze. Sendai kisses her; Miyagi replies that this was not what she meant and pushes Sendai's abdomen. The first kiss has no named bodily target in this sentence. The skirt grip creates ambiguity about Miyagi's immediate purpose, but the source does not turn it into affirmative permission for that contact. Sendai's answer does not resolve Miyagi's objection. (O003.)

Sendai privately calls the kiss necessary so she can keep her promise to Utsunomiya with a smile. She recognizes Utsunomiya as Miyagi's important friend and also her own friend. That social obligation gives her an articulated reason to manage her expression; it does not establish a right to take a kiss from Miyagi. The reader hears this rationale without Miyagi being shown hearing or accepting it. The dark-heart language names Sendai's feeling in a figurative register, not a diagnosis. (O003.)

## A skirt's material and an unfinished departure

Sendai briefly wants to discuss fabrics and choose a skirt made of material Miyagi likes, but lateness constrains her. She leaves the common space and Miyagi follows. ## Publicly credited work meets a private limit

Miyagi privately says that praise is difficult, especially from close people. Her short silence gives Sendai an opening to join. Maika recognizes the clothes as Sendai's selection, asks about the makeup and praises the braid. Sendai confirms that she did all three and explains that she had wanted to try braiding at that particular place. Their dialogue publicly identifies who performed the appearance work. It does not disclose everything Miyagi privately felt while receiving it. (O009.)

Sendai says she is satisfied with having made Miyagi so cute; Maika says she too is satisfied to see cute Shiori. Sendai is glad to hear this and proposes that she might coordinate a whole outfit again. **The future proposal belongs to Sendai**, not Maika, and remains a possibility without Miyagi's assent or another styling occasion. Maika's present pleasure must not be converted into her authorizing repeated access to Miyagi's body or appearance. (O009.)

Miyagi herself credits Sendai's taste. She thinks she looks much better with Sendai's selections than with her own. Valuing a result and resisting its indefinite repetition are compatible. She then changes the conversation by saying they should stop standing there talking and go inside.
