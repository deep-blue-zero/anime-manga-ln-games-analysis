# 私たちの一日: learning the familiar person again

Miyagi Shiori knows a Sendai Hazuki who remains composed even while behaving casually in Miyagi's old room. An early Golden Week morning gives her an appearance that does not fit that expectation. The small domestic encounters contain both welcome discovery and friction: a breakfast offer fills an awkward silence; a cleaning turn permits observation but does not make observation welcome; Sendai values the time while reporting actual pain.
