## A remembered choice without an available reason

Miyagi’s spoken response reduces the earlier act to lips touching and disputes calling it a kiss. Sendai challenges the plausibility of that description, and Miyagi replies that she was not behaving normally. These are responses Sendai actually receives; the episode does not consist of absolute silence. They also fall short of a full account of why Miyagi chose the site. The private わざわざ at p17 matters: Miyagi deliberately touched there and remembers doing so. The subsequent explanatory limit does not make the act accidental or forgotten.
