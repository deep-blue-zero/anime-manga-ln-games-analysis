# Film synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority and source model

Generation CPK-G1 has one root and one [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). This architecture pairs with [the method](CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md); no prior architecture is superseded. Its canonical scope is procedural, not narrative. The intended mature corpus must explain how character choices, directed relationships, chronology/disclosure, Japanese expression, cinematic construction and musical/sonic form interact across one film, with recoverable source support and live competing readings.

Public metadata is sufficient for this INITIAL design: one original Japanese animated musical feature with temporally significant sound and picture. It does not establish film inspection, a final scene map or themes. F01 is the intended single film unit; source edition/encode is not yet bound. The [inventory](CPK_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md) separates the film, Japanese text/lyrics, English translation, synchronized derivatives, production paratext, cultural comparisons and nonauthoritative commentary. A new encode or release is a distinct witness, not a new film unit or an automatically interchangeable source.

The owner requests native continuous-AV primary analysis followed by independent bundle-mediated audit. The [mirror reconciliation route](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md#1-when-the-protocol-applies) permits this arrangement. The method family explicitly assigns continuous motion, editing, voice, music development and audiovisual synchronization to verified AV inspection, and exact Japanese wording to a verified text witness. The bundle lane retains its own capability limits. Neither route inherits higher authority from a provider name.

## 5. Synthesis, specialists and reconstruction

Mandatory analytical responsibilities are character/causality, directed relationships, cinematic construction, Japanese-sensitive language, musical/sonic/voice form, and counterreading. After source and claim reconciliation, the role-gap review names the substantive artifact sections that discharge each. One sustained integrated synthesis may carry them all for a feature. An independent character monograph, dyadic study, musical-dramaturgy study, visual-form study or chronology/ending study becomes necessary only when an independently recurring, load-bearing question cannot be adequately argued inside that synthesis. Theme/cultural/production specialists are anticipated possibilities, not findings.

Character interpretation distinguishes changed conduct from newly disclosed state, self-report from inferred motive, ordinary from crisis contexts and the conditioning effect of particular interlocutors. Relationship synthesis needs evidence in each direction, temporal development, power/knowledge differences and contrary cases. Formal/music arguments connect observed structure to dramatic consequences while testing convention, mix/encode artifacts and rival interpretations. There is no universal prose cap; full causal/inferential reasoning, important evidence and alternatives must survive transfer and revision.

Behavioral reconstruction is a **deferred derived use**, not an initialized model or a condition for film analysis completion. If later authorized and adequately evidenced, adapt the [generic reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md). Per subject/state create distinct evidence map, operational model and independent validation record under `reconstruction/`; a literary monograph is not the model. Bind source horizon, knowledge, relationship context, ordinary/stress evidence, speech baselines, negative constraints, alternatives and uncertainty. Policies must specify triggers/stakes/state → response family, inhibitors, exceptions and falsifiers rather than adjectives. Freeze predictions before unseen tests; after whole-film exposure use RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST unless genuine pre-exposure withholding was arranged. Single-film evidence may be insufficient. Synthetic dialogue/simulations never become narrative evidence. Global discovery/capability grades remain with designated agents.

## 6. Contradiction, claims and reconciliation

Before harmonizing a conflict, check source/encode identity, clock drift, frame sampling, text corrections, Japanese versus English, speaker/recipient, presentation versus event order, knowledge, self-report/reliability, continuity and paratext status. Distinguish factual contradiction, unsupported assertion, interpretive disagreement, incomplete coverage and evidence inaccessible through the inspecting lane. More than one explanation may remain live.

The claim ledger preserves original formulation and source locators, each transition and reason, current formulation, counterevidence, rival accounts, dependencies and authority home. Use `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`. Rejection requires evidence or a demonstrated failure of support at the claimed scope; inability to inspect is not disproof. A supported alternative can coexist with a preserved interpretation. Significant changes trigger review of dependent character, relationship, formal, musical and synthesis claims.

The reconciliation profile owns the mirror route: primary AV author → blind bundle verifier → integrator, with capability-qualified targeted AV return where needed. The verifier owns its audit artifacts, not the primary reading or mutable project ledgers. The integrator accepts full outputs, checks base drift and writes accepted results through to canonical homes. Preserve the original candidate and blind log byte-for-byte; later corrections use appended audit/revision records or separately identified successors.

## 7. Production order and completion gates

Production order is source/capability/owner authorization and OPEN run → full-film observations, reading and synchronized state → primary freeze → independent bundle observations and freeze → claim audit → bounded return/independent verification → claim/state reconciliation → source-completion and architecture/role-gap review → earned specialist arguments or identified integrated sections → convergence → integrated synthesis → semantic/repository validation → scoped release/freeze. A separately authorized audit is verification of the same F01 witness, not an unrecorded admission of another edition. Resolve a changed witness before proceeding.

| Gate | Acceptance condition |
|---|---|
| INITIAL bootstrap | Source-bounded governing pair and meaningful day-one schemas; CLOSED, no run |
| Admission/execution | Recoverable owner F01 range, exact witness, verified capabilities, current method/infrastructure and consistent OPEN/active scope under continuation policy |
| Primary candidate completion | All authorized intervals/modalities accounted for; full prose, state/claims, anchors and reinspection; no hidden required-channel gaps |
| Independent audit | Blind log frozen before interpretations, or honest contamination disclosure; claim audit and actual coverage complete for declared scope |
| Longitudinal reconciliation | Scene/knowledge/subject/directed-state, music/form, claim dependencies and clocks agree or carry explicit disputes |
| Specialist readiness | Source completion and role-gap findings; required evidence stabilized; named owner/inputs for every remaining duty |
| Specialist convergence | Rival character/form/music accounts compared; exact integrated carrying sections identified when no standalone studies are needed |
| Integrated-synthesis readiness | Required inputs accepted; remaining OPEN matters honestly bounded without depending on unresolved load-bearing observations |
| Validation/audit | Source fidelity, locators, Japanese, counterevidence, attribution/name/reference and actual channel coverage checked; exact-head repository audit separately required |
| Release/freeze | Accepted scope, versions and immutable artifacts enumerated; integration by CI agent/owner; missing required evidence blocks corresponding completion |

Source completion does not itself authorize synthesis or further admission. A bounded candidate can be frozen as a partial experimental result with unresolved requirements; it must not be labeled complete. Optional enrichment can remain open. A required channel cannot be silently relabeled optional to finish. Audit failure, disputed load-bearing temporal observations or incomplete text blocks the corresponding claim/release, not necessarily unrelated bounded work.

At the end of a later run, record truthful completion and no next candidate under the continuation policy. Do not create fake closure records now. Byte-sealed run stops or terminal closures, if later appropriate, follow initiation §3.8 and specialized owner/control routes; project prose cannot amend global seals.

## 8. Lifecycle, archive and extension

Lifecycle is INITIAL. Review after the first substantial mapped movement, after primary coverage, after audit, before specialist/integrated synthesis and before release. Role-gap findings use SATISFIED, PARTIALLY_SATISFIED, MISSING, MISROUTED, TOO_THIN or NOT_APPLICABLE, with carrying home, evidence, consequence and repair owner. Add a ledger/study only for an earned independent accumulation/retrieval responsibility; backfill only material gaps using retained readings or authorized source reinspection. Record material amendments in architecture version history and current routing.

Entrypoint, procedural method/architecture and current pointers are mutable through targeted, attributable revisions. Observation/state histories and claim adjudications are append-preserving. Witness locks, primary candidate and associated pre-audit evidence snapshot, blind observations, predictions and accepted release artifacts freeze with exact bytes/hashes in the reconciliation/release record. Superseded thinking remains recoverable with the [archive authority quartet](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md).
