# What the two readings established, missed and left unverified

This evaluation follows the pre-disposition structural comparison, the complete registered-claim audit, 35 additional consequential-assertion checks, and the symmetric self-audit. It is a comparison of two preserved analytical executions, not an accepted replacement monograph or a controlled model ranking.

Sol's strongest supported thesis is that personal growth does not exhaust the value of the particular beloved: Iroha can acknowledge a sufficient life without Kaguya and still want a continuing life with her, including Yachiyo's older history. Gemini's strongest supported emphasis is the work of extending that attachment into ordinary embodied participation, without erasing the older virtual self. Those readings converge on care, unfinished work, retained history and refusal of a convenient ending. They differ over what the film chiefly asks: whether growth can license continued particular desire, or whether love reaches fulfillment through tactile everydayness. Neither difference requires an error verdict. Gemini's stronger universal rejection of disembodied consolation is less well supported than a situated wish for warmth and pancakes.

## Material omissions and their consequences

**Maternal ambivalence and volitional refusal are interpretation-changing.** Sol's independent O0022/O0026/O0035 retain the financial condition, childhood assertion demands, and Iroha's choice not to repeat “you cannot understand.” Gemini's retroactive account of uncomplicated maternal warmth and an internally projected condition cannot absorb these merely as extra detail. Repair changes the account of freedom: Iroha already makes an ethical distinction before the late phone call.

**Care precedes the tournament and modifies its choices.** Sol independently records Kaguya recognizing overload, obtaining leave and nursing. Moving this after the loft makes the tournament participants less developed than they are and conceals why later restraint matters.

**Musical completion is a process, not an already-solved loop.** Sol separates file inheritance, one-chorus acceptance, later recognition, completion after departure, transmission and historical reuse. The father-to-Yachiyo origin link is not shown. A loop can still make earlier care retrospectively moving without supplying a causally originless melody.

**Knowing the older beloved involves consent and pressure.** FUSHI resists and warns; Iroha presses. Gemini's unsolicited benevolent disclosure suppresses an ethical counterexample. Sol's interpretation must not use “particular love” to waive that concern.

**The credits preserve pain.** Their Japanese lyric text is present in the admitted witness. This is a consequential omission from an argument about overcoming tragic closure, although lyrics alone do not dictate the score's affect or character beliefs.

**Gemini's confirmed details improve Sol without replacing it.** Bracelet adjacency, shared kanji, web-language specificity and final-stage rendering add formal/behavioral density. Sol's broader static survey did not guarantee noticing them. These are acknowledged as supplementation or correction, with question/exposure provenance retained.

Harmless differences include scene count, different emphasis on an ordinary meal, and selecting different examples where neither claim is exhaustive. These should not inflate an error list.

## Why differences cannot be reduced to a model ranking

The two runs have different roles, source interfaces, sampling, auditory access, context and prompts. Sol additionally had a blind question phase and later adversarial source returns. Its post-exposure discoveries cannot count toward independent superiority. Gemini's full reported auditory route gives it access that this execution lacked; it does not automatically validate its observations. The same witness removes edition mismatch as the best explanation for the checked factual conflicts. Timestamp errors, compressed scene memory, subtitle/speech distinctions and inferential overreach are documented explanations, not diagnoses of hidden model processes.

The packet itself is not perfectly neutral: it presupposes some target facts and gives the count in Q22. The pre-question independent freeze remains the strongest experimental baseline. The second freeze preserves claim-blind answers honestly, including resistance to misleading intervals, but does not erase prompt-selection or answer-cue effects. No source-derived external interpretation was admitted before either freeze.

## What should survive reconciliation

Preserve ordinary material care, the difference between becoming more able to live and wanting this person, the older identity's historical weight, public/virtual participation alongside its limits, sustained collaborative body work, and a provisional ending. Add Gemini's confirmed visual and naming particulars with correct attribution. Repair source contradictions at observation/knowledge/claim homes before building a new synthesis. Defer the exact sonic ring, voice handoff, acoustic drops, trajectories and timing to the bounded AV queue. The integrated account must have a new identity and explicit acceptance decisions; neither frozen original should be rewritten or promoted by this comparison.
