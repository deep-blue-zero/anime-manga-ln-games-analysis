# Independent structures compared before claim adjudication

This record was composed after reading the complete Gemini primary monograph, source inventory, observation/music/scene ledger, character/relationship ledger, and reconciliation record, and before assigning any claim-level dispositions. The two Sol pre-exposure products are frozen; the comparison cannot retrospectively change what was discovered independently. Exact Gemini originals are preserved under comparison/gemini-baseline with verified Git blob identities and SHA-256 in GEMINI_BASELINE_RECEIPT.json.

## Movement and causal organization

Sol discovers seven movements: capacity/hospitality through 23:31; shared practice through 54:11; contest through 75:14; chosen life and parting through 108:31; refusal of closure through 115:44; recognition of the older beloved through 127:15; practical continuation through the tail. Gemini discovers six: origin through 20:05; ascent through 50:12; contest/domesticity through 80:05; concert/parting through 110:05; refusal/revelation through 127:14; scientific continuation and coda. Neither number is inherently superior. Sol separates refusal from the older life's recognition because discovering survival does not by itself settle whether a shared future is wanted. Gemini groups those events into a single return from false closure, giving embodiment a stronger directional culmination.

Both identify departure, refusal of a tidy ending, older identity, and long practical continuation as load-bearing. But some divergence is factual rather than segmentational: Gemini places the tale discussion at 20–24 minutes, fever after the contest, brother recognition after victory, and a post-loss song before the battle actually ends according to Sol's frozen source returns. These are source-check targets, not evidence that six movements are aesthetically inferior. Repairing event order may change the developmental story attributed to those boundaries.

## Chronology and knowledge

Both preserve younger Kaguya versus older Yachiyo as different experiential horizons, and notice that recognition later recontextualizes early fandom/intimacy. Both separate the full audience's retrospective knowledge from what Iroha can know at an earlier scene. Gemini develops a more explicit closed-loop/open-future philosophical model. Sol treats the sung/composed inheritance as circulating through time but declines to give the melody a demonstrated originless bootstrap or to infer that father/child first heard it from Yachiyo. Gemini's attribution of the body/slug/DOGE route and exact point of Iroha's recognition differ from Sol's observations and need explicit checking.

A particular caution: the existence of a consistent later history does not by itself show that Yachiyo knows every future event or that causal necessity explains every nonintervention. Both readings need to distinguish remembered history, intended concealment, practical power, and theoretical impossibility. The final continuation can remain narratively open without proving a metaphysical escape from determinism.

## Characters and ordinary behavior

Both read Iroha as competent, overworked and constrained by familial expectations, rather than merely cold. Both take ordinary labor, meals, rest and music seriously. Both distinguish a changed sustainable adult work habit from a mere spectacular rescue. Sol emphasizes the tension between protection and agency: Iroha's boundary-setting, Kaguya's unauthorized spending, the mother's actual conditions, and a brother whose affectionate account of the mother remains his account. Gemini offers a more unified grief-armour-to-generative-devotion arc and reads maternal harshness as essentially protective.

These accounts overlap but are not identical. A mother can care, impose conditions, and later permit a new course without either total vindication or total rejection. A playful cohabitant can be loving while breaching financial consent. The stronger integrative account should retain those incompatible pressures rather than deciding every difficult action was secretly benevolent.

## Directed relationships and plurality

Both have separately directed evidence for central dyads. Both see caregiving reverse and develop, and both refuse to erase the older self when the younger-looking body appears. Gemini's detailed examination of the final lift's weight, glow and shadows supplies more precise visual support than Sol independently recorded; Sol's blind answers subsequently verified this contrast after the questions prompted it. It must not be counted as an independent Sol discovery.

Gemini's account stresses the progression from dependent child to reciprocal partner and then plural co-presence. Sol stresses why reciprocity does not render either partner replaceable and why the earlier care cannot be equated with a debt account. Both recognize friends and the male trio as ongoing support, though Gemini's battle membership/role sequence needs checking. Sol treats disclosure through FUSHI as an ethical tension under pressure; Gemini celebrates its autonomous truth-telling. This is a consequential disagreement about direction, consent and intervention, not merely different praise for a mascot.

## Japanese expression and attribution

There is independent convergence on the pragmatic expense-sharing response, the intimate farewell, the force of the refusal to end, and the adult rest/work maxim. Gemini develops greater detail around mixed historical/web registers and the family-name kanji pattern. Sol's independent record gives more weight to Kaguya's negative volitional formulation: not wanting to say Iroha could not understand, rather than being unable to say it. Sol also preserves the mother's unheard request as a hard information limit.

Gemini alleges a precise closing narrator handoff that Japanese SDH does not mark. Sol cannot hear it and therefore neither confirms nor refutes it; a source-qualified listener is needed. The two runs must not resolve actual voice identity by majority vote or by a subtitle tag alone. Conversely, exact quotations and cue numbers remain checkable even when hearing is unavailable; claims of verbatim text cannot borrow authority from a general AV capability statement.

## Cinematic form

Both identify the embedded streaming end-screen as part of the film and connect its interruption to a refusal of prescribed closure. Both recognize scale contrasts between domestic space and virtual spectacle. Gemini makes a much more elaborate argument through title withholding, opening/closing spatial bookends, AR sightlines, micro-inserts, color changes, physical shadows and supposed acoustic removal. Some of this detail was outside Sol's independent selection; some was checked during B2; some still requires continuous evidence. All three provenance classes must remain separate.

Sol's caution about static images is a genuine capability limit, not evidence that cinematic form is unimportant. Gemini's finer perceptual route can potentially establish many such claims, but its stated roughly 1 fps visual sampling does not independently establish subsecond insert contents. Deterministic cut detection can locate a boundary without identifying its semantic content. Descriptions of editing, tint, ontology and universal viewer reaction require different kinds of support.

## Musical and sonic structure

Both find music causally important beyond fame: soothing, collaboration, inherited incompletion, transmission across separation, survival, and continuation. Sol retains 19 musical/sonic events largely through verified lyrics, SDH reports and dramatic context, while explicitly hearing nothing. Gemini groups 14 events and develops exact arrangements, instrumentation, voice textures, unison/a-cappella recurrence, acoustic drops and Vocaloid history. These are unequal evidence routes and unequal interpretive granularity, not a valid model ranking.

The proposed song locations, melody origin, graduation versus balcony completion, and availability of credit lyrics create factual checkpoints. External Vocaloid history is distinct from on-film evidence even where culturally plausible. Gemini's explicit disclaimers about unavailable credit lyrics must themselves be tested against Track 4, not accepted as a conservative omission without checking.

## Governing arguments

Gemini: authentic love/maturity reject mythic loss and disembodied consolation in favor of shared finite tactile everyday life, preserving the historical Yachiyo. Sol: becoming able to live without the beloved does not exhaust wanting this particular beloved and a shared unfinished future; work and reciprocal care make continuation possible without guaranteeing its permanence. These are compatible in part. Touch and food are concrete reasons a future is wanted, while the continued virtual companion and the explicit value of Tsukuyomi limit a generalized anti-digital conclusion.

Sol places more weight on the distinction between personal growth and consuming another person's value as an instrument of growth. Gemini places more weight on embodiment as the positive alternative to both tragic resignation and virtual sufficiency. An integrator should preserve the meaningful difference: the film may value embodiment without requiring that all authentic love be bodily, or calling all earlier virtual intimacy inadequate.

## Alternatives and unresolved questions

Both seriously consider technological wish fulfillment and the risk that money or obsession substitutes for grief work. Gemini adds a strong closed-loop determinism rival and distinguishes helping a living partner from resurrecting the father. Sol develops emancipation and grief-continuity rivals, plus ethical pressure, ongoing uncertainty and the costs of exceptional access. Those are opportunities for mutual strengthening, not reasons to declare a winner.

Shared unresolved territory includes exact body/identity partition, long-term life after the staged reunion, completeness of sensory embodiment, and unverifiable future harmony. Gemini labels several world-rule issues open but sometimes embeds unverified premises in the open question itself (for example, an originless melody or a completed ban). An open question should not shelter a faulty premise.
