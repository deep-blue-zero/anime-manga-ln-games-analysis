# Character and directed-relationship state

## Responsibility and controls

This home preserves who acted/knew/said what, at which presentation and story-time boundary, toward whom and under which conditions. It indexes full arguments and [descriptive observations](CPK_OBSERVATION_LEDGER.md); it does not replace the reading, certify a character model or admit publicity biographies.

Established subjects: **0**. State/knowledge events: **0**. Directed-edge events: **0**. Japanese expression samples: **0**. Ordinary-context samples: **0**. Reconstruction is **NOT_READY / NOT_ATTEMPTED**. No subject name or initial personality is seeded from metadata, remembered plot or the traditional tale.

## Identity and temporal state

After inspection, allocate source-established subject keys and stable run-scoped record IDs. Preserve aliases and concealment at the relevant disclosure horizon; an unknown identity remains unknown. Do not conflate roles, avatars/personae or similarly named subjects without film evidence. Identity links can themselves be INF/INT claims with alternatives.

State key: `F01 witness + run + presentation/disclosure position + event-time constraints + subject/alias horizon + context/interlocutor + knowledge proposition + interpretive mode`. Modes distinguish reconstruction of scene-level knowledge from retrospective full-film interpretation. They do not imply genuine prospective exposure. Apparent contradiction may reflect altered audience knowledge rather than character development.

Keep observed action, spoken self-report, reported belief, visible state, inferred motive and interpretation separate through method evidence classes, attributed source roles and claim references. A confident actor does not establish world rules. A dramatic peak cannot alone establish ordinary disposition.

## Subject/state and knowledge event schema

Required fields: `record_id | subject key and source-established name/alias | run/scene/obs refs | witness/clock locator | presentation frontier | event-time constraint/confidence | context/stakes/resources | interlocutor/audience | observed behavior or attributed statement | proposition/belief | holder and acquisition evidence | audience versus subject knowledge | stated/inferred goal and rivals | entering/exit state | change kind | evidence classes | claim refs | counterexample | uncertainty | full reading anchor`.

Change kind distinguishes disposition, temporary condition, knowledge, relationship, context/resource, revealed-not-new and unresolved. These are row descriptions, not a new corpus evidence taxonomy. Preserve ordinary/low-stakes samples and missed diagnostic opportunities; absence of evidence is not evidence of a negative policy.

## Directed-edge event schema

Required fields: `edge_event_id | from_subject → to_subject | run/scene/obs refs | witness/clock locator | disclosure/event-time horizon | observed interaction/communication | stated tie or expectation and speaker | inferred affect/obligation and claim refs | trust/attention/power/knowledge asymmetry | entering/exit relation | context-dependent conditions | reverse-edge reference or UNESTABLISHED | counterevidence/alternative | uncertainty | reading anchor`.

Capture A→B and B→A independently. A declaration, a reaction, a shared performance and an analyst's interpretation of intimacy are different evidence. Reciprocity requires both directions; a single label such as friendship or romance cannot substitute for the conditional relationship argument. Changes caused by musical action link the exact musical-event record.

## Japanese expression and ordinary behavior schema

Required fields: `sample_id | subject/speaker and recipient | run/scene/obs refs | witness/clock and cue/onscreen anchor | Japanese TXT witness/confidence | bounded wording or paraphrase with reason | speech/thought/song/sign distinction | register/address/grammar | communicative act | context/stakes/audience | literal gloss and material translation alternative | AO delivery ref if actually inspected | behavior/state/edge claim refs | baseline or contrast role | exceptions/limits`.

Published analysis uses only brief justified quotations; raw subtitle/lyric dumps stay outside Git. English phrasing cannot silently become a Japanese voice sample. A lyric allocation needs an adequate film-matched Japanese witness; perceived delivery does not determine wording or inner emotion. A normal conversation can be more diagnostic of baseline than a climactic song; preserve both when available.

## History, derived uses and closeout

Append material state/edge/expression events; preserve prior state and knowledge frontiers. A current-state summary may update by pointer with a dated reason and claim transition, never by erasing contrary evidence. Frozen source-run state remains recoverable in the primary snapshot. Accepted audit findings update this home only through the integrator and claim history.

Before character/relationship synthesis, review coverage across actual contexts, interlocutors, ordinary/stress behavior, Japanese expression and counterexamples. Missing evidence remains a gap. A later reconstruction model requires separate authorization, evidence/model/test artifacts and independent assessment as architecture §5 specifies. Synthetic scenes cannot supply missing observations. Global character/reconstruction registries are not this author's write surface.
