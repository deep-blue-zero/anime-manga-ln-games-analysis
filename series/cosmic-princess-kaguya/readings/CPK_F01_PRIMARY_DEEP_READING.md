## §3. Character Architecture and Directed-Relationship Analysis

### §3.1 Sakayori Iroha (`酒寄彩葉` / `いろP`): Bereavement Armour, Economic Self-Sufficiency, and the Arc from `東大文一` Martyrdom to `休むも仕事！`

A superficial plot summary might mistake Sakayori Iroha (`SUBJ-IROHA`) for a standard stoic anime honors student softened by a whimsical magical girl. Close multimodal inspection (`FRM-01`–`03`, `FRM-05`, `FRM-12`, `FRM-18`–`19`) reveals a far sharper psychological portrait (`CPK-R1-C0002`). In `2030`, seventeen-year-old second-year student (`2年3組12番`, `FRM-12`) Iroha is neither cold nor unimaginative; she is **grief-armoured**. Between January `2020`—when seven-year-old Iroha co-recorded `タイトル未定 ( 彩葉と共作 ).wav` with her composer father Tomohisa (`酒寄朝久`, `FRM-07`)—and her middle-school move to Tokyo (`FRM-02`), two losses reshaped her behavioral economy: Tomohisa died, and older brother Asahi (`酒寄朝日`) broke away from home to chase streaming (`S0016`). Left behind with mother Momiji (`酒寄紅葉`), adolescent Iroha heard Momiji's anxious Kansai warning (`気張って無理し… すぐに泣いて帰ってくる思ってます`, `FRM-02`) not as protective worry, but as a challenge she had to defeat through total self-sufficiency.

Consequently, every choice in Iroha's `2030` baseline (`K0002`–`K0003`) obeys an iron policy of **preemptive invulnerability**:

1. **Academic & Career Prestige as Emotional Shield**: On her `進路希望調査票` (`FRM-12`), Iroha selects Japan's single most competitive humanities credential—**`東京大学 文科一類`** (`第一志望`) and **`一橋大学 法学部`** (`第二志望`) aiming for **`法科大学院（弁護士）`**—citing objective bar-passage metrics rather than any personal desire to practice law. Her summer schedule (`FRM-05`, `O0013`) leaves literally zero unallocated daylight hours between `バイト` shifts and six-subject Common Test drills.
2. **Musical Quarantine**: She keeps Tomohisa's `music > tomohisa` folder backed up on her PC (`FRM-07`), yet refuses to touch the school piano (`O0004`) because composition is bound up with bereavement and perceived impracticality.
3. **Compartmentalized Virtual Solace**: Her sole private indulgence is enshrining Yachiyo's standee on a bookshelf `神棚` (`FRM-01`) and slipping into Tsukuyomi anonymously as `いろP`.

Kaguya disrupts this armour not by lecturing Iroha, but by creating situations where Iroha's competence is enlisted for **play, care, and shared risk** (`S0004`–`S0018`). Yet the decisive proof that Iroha has genuinely transformed arrives **after** Kaguya vanishes (`S0028`–`S0033`). Had `F01` ended at `01:51:30` (`めでたし めでたし`), Iroha would have enacted the melancholy *bildungsroman* compromise: treasuring a lost magical summer while dutifully marching off to `東大文一` law school. By shattering the parody Netflix post-play box (`O0028`), reclaiming her career sheet (`もうちょい迷います！`), confessing her vulnerability to Momiji (`S0029`), and pivoting into neural-haptic bionics (`FRM-18`–`19`), Iroha redirects her formidable discipline from **defensive self-punishment** into **generative devotion**. Crucially, `S0033` (`02:09:01`, `J0015`) includes an ordinary low-stakes workplace exit specifically to falsify any suspicion that adult Iroha merely traded law-school overwork for mad-scientist obsession: at twenty-seven, she shuts down her lab terminal on schedule and calls out **`「お先に失礼しまーす！ 休むも仕事！」`** (*"Resting is part of the job too!"*)—internalizing the exact bodily care Kaguya taught her in `S0013` and `S0018`.

### §3.2 Bidirectional Dyads Across Time: `Iroha ↔ Kaguya` (`E0001`–`E0004`) and `Iroha ↔ Yachiyo` (`E0005`–`E0008`)

Modeling `A → B` and `B → A` separately exposes how carefully `F01` constructs reciprocity without collapsing distinct temporal horizons (`CPK-R1-C0005`, `C0007`, `C0014`):

- **`Iroha → Kaguya` (`E0001` → `E0003`) vs `Kaguya → Iroha` (`E0002` → `E0004`)**:
  In `MOV-I`, `Iroha → Kaguya` is protective, financially sacrificial (`23,532円` `ふじゅペイ`, `FRM-03`), and verbally guarded, whereas `Kaguya → Iroha` is uninhibited, tactile, and secretly shadowed by knowledge of lunar retrieval (`K0007`). - **`Iroha → Yachiyo` (`E0005`) vs `Yachiyo → Iroha` (`E0006`) and `Yachiyo ↔ Kaguya` (`E0007`–`E0008`)**:
  Across `S0001`–`S0030`, no relationship in `F01` carries a steeper epistemic asymmetry than `Iroha ↔ Yachiyo`. ## §4. Chronology, Epistemic Horizons, and the 8,000-Year Bootstrap Loop

### §4.1 Scene-Bound Concealment vs Prospective Clues (`S0004` – `S0030`)

A defining structural achievement of `F01` is that its third-act twist—**`月見ヤチヨ` (`SUBJ-YACHIYO`) is `かぐや` (`SUBJ-KAGUYA`) after an 8,000-year crash into Jōmon antiquity (`S0031`)**—never cheats its own earlier presentation (`CPK-R1-C0009`). Throughout `S0001`–`S0030`, the film maintains scrupulous `SCENE_BOUND` plausibility while planting five verifiable prospective anomalies whose full causal weight snaps into focus retrospectively:

1. **The `S0004` `Remember` Lullaby (`00:07:28`–`00:07:53`, `P2-CLIP-01`, `O0006`)**: Infant Kaguya stops crying immediately when Iroha glances at her `神棚` Yachiyo standee and sings `Remember`. At `SCENE_BOUND`, Iroha jokes `ヤチヨパワー、すげぇ…`; retrospectively, the melody resonates with Kaguya's lunar soul—and Yachiyo wrote `Remember` *from* the melody Iroha and Kaguya co-forged.
2. **Yachiyo's Classical Diction (`00:33:59`, `O0011`, `J0005`)**: When Kaguya shouts her Yachiyo Cup challenge, Yachiyo murmurs **`いと かわゆし`** in genuine classical Heian grammar. At `SCENE_BOUND`, this sounds like whimsical moon-princess roleplay by an AI/VTuber diva; retrospectively, Yachiyo literally lived through the Heian court alongside Fujiwara no Atsutada (`S0031`).
3. **The Post-Logout Real-Name Whisper (`00:34:45`–`00:35:15`, `P2-CLIP-02`, `O0012`, `J0006`)**: Pressing charms (`忘れ物`) into `いろP`'s hands over solo piano `Remember` (`M0006`), Yachiyo waits until `いろP` logs out (`00:35:01`) before whispering **`「いつも来てくれてありがとね…… 彩葉。」`** At `SCENE_BOUND`, an attentive viewer must wonder how Tsukuyomi's host knows `いろP`'s real given name `彩葉` (perhaps admin registration records?); `S0031` reveals it was an 8,000-year beloved whispering the name she could not yet speak aloud to seventeen-year-old Iroha's face.
4. **The `2020/01` `.wav` Anomaly (`01:17:23`, `FRM-07`, `O0019`, `M0008`)**: Discovering that Tomohisa's decade-old `タイトル未定 ( 彩葉と共作 ).wav` carries `Remember`'s exact melody creates an acute intra-diegetic mystery in `S0019` (`K0016`) thirty-nine minutes *before* `S0031` explains how the tune circulated between Yachiyo and the Sakayori household.
5. **Yachiyo's Serene Restraint During the `2030/09/12` Crisis (`S0022`–`S0026`)**: Yachiyo repels the early `月人` scouts in `S0023` (`K0018`) yet does not prevent Kaguya's `2030/09/12` ascension in `S0026`—because Yachiyo *remembers* ascending on `2030/09/12`, awakening to `Reply` on the Moon, and crashing `8,000` years into the past to become Yachiyo in the first place.

### §4.2 Causal Mechanics of the Loop and `2030`→`2040` Escape Velocity

Reconstructed across `S0001`–`S0036`, `F01`'s macro-chronology combines a **closed Novikov-type causal bootstrap loop (`~6000 BCE` ↔ `Sept 2030`)** with an **open linear forward trajectory (`Sept 2030` → `2040`)** (`CPK-R1-C0010`, `C0014`):

1. **`~6000 BCE` (Jōmon Coastline) → `2020s CE`**: After Kaguya's stolen bamboo-shoot lunar time-craft collides with a deep-space meteor shower (`01:54:55`, `S0030`), she crash-lands ~8,000 years in Earth's past stripped of her physical body (`魂だけの自分`), while stowaway `犬DOGE` fuses with a white sea-bunny into `FUSHI` (`O0032`). Anchored to the salvaged bamboo-shoot lunar core (`FRM-14`), disembodied Kaguya witnesses eight millennia of Japanese history (`FRM-15`–`17`), adopts the persona **Runami Yachiyo**, constructs virtual **Tsukuyomi** atop the wired bamboo core (`FRM-14`), and releases **`Remember`**—carrying the melody she survived on (`この曲で、生き残れた`, `02:06:26`).
2. **`2020/01` → `Summer 2030`**: Seven-year-old Iroha, already immersed in music with father Tomohisa (`酒寄朝久`) and Tsukuyomi's early cultural sphere, co-records the melody as `タイトル未定 ( 彩葉と共作 ).wav` (`FRM-07`; exact first origin of the tune remains an intentional ontological bootstrap paradox, `CPK-R1-U0003`). In late spring `2030`, pre-loop fugitive **Kaguya** flees the Moon into utility pole `743A` (`S0003`), hears Iroha sing `Remember` (`S0004`), spends an incandescent summer with Iroha (`S0006`–`S0022`), co-completes **`Reply`** from the `.wav` (`S0019`–`S0025`), and has her conscious memories wiped by `天の羽衣` on **`2030/09/12`** while **`犬DOGE` stows away un-erased at `01:47:56`** (`O0025`).

## §5. Cinematic Construction, Dual-World Design, and Meta-Platform Form

### §5.1 Macro-Framing: Pancake-Island Bookends (`S0001` ↔ `S0036`) and the 130-Minute Withheld Title Drop (`AM-01`, `AM-06`)

Director Yamashita Shingo structures `F01`'s visual container around two bold formal decisions (`CPK-R1-C0001`). First, the film opens at `00:00:00.000` (`O0001`) without distributor bumpers or a title card on a surreal floating two-stack buttered pancake island inside Tsukuyomi's starry expanse, cutting hard (`scdet` `71.008` at `00:01:20.956`, `AM-01`) into Iroha's mundane alarm—and returns to that **exact pancake island** in the post-credits stinger (`02:19:54`–`02:20:12`, `O0040`), where what initially looked like mysterious cosmic iconography is comically unmasked as Yachiyo's 8,000-year craving for Iroha's household pancakes (`蛇足ぅ〜！！`). Second, `F01` withholds its main title card **`超かぐや姫！ COSMIC PRINCESS KAGUYA!`** for **130 minutes and 26 seconds**, unveiling it only at `02:10:26.360`–`02:10:33.367` (`O0038`, `AM-06`) immediately after physical Kaguya, adult Iroha, and virtual Yachiyo leap onto their `2040` comeback stage (`FRM-20`) and Yachiyo concludes, `ひとまず、このお話はここでおしまいね`. Formally, this 130-minute delay enacts the thesis of `S0021`'s anthem **`Ex-Otogibanashi`** ("Former Fairy Tale"): everything up to `2040` was merely the prequel dismantling *Taketori Monogatari*'s tragic script, whereas the true **"*Chō* Kaguya-hime!"** begins at `02:10:26` when embodied life commences.

### §5.2 Ontological Linework and Sightlines: Nagae Akihiro's Weighted Tokyo vs Hechima's Self-Luminous Tsukuyomi and AR-Only `月人` (`FRM-08`, `FRM-27`–`28`)

Rather than treating virtual reality as a generic neon backdrop, `F01` splits character design and compositing physics along ontological lines (`FRM-27`–`28`, `CPK-R1-C0015`):

- **Real-World Tokyo (`現実キャラクターデザイン：永江彰浩`)**: Rendered with matte surface textures, subtle pencil-grain line weight, warm/fluorescent domestic lighting, cluttered environmental specificity (`9` energy-drink cans and `Vintage` grammar books on Iroha's shelf in `FRM-01`; `ふじゅペイ` shopping baskets in `FRM-03`; handwritten fridge magnets in `FRM-10`), and consistent gravitational weight. Characters sweat, slouch, blink off smart-contact lenses (`01:24:08`, `P2-CLIP-03`), and cast dark contact shadows onto floors and walls.
- **Virtual Tsukuyomi (`ツクヨミキャラクターデザイン：へちま`)**: Rendered with saturated cyan-magenta-gold internal bloom, elastic silhouette deformation, zero-gravity aerial camera trajectories (`SEG-06` `KASSEN` tracking shots, `O0016`), and self-luminous rim lighting in which avatars radiate light rather than absorbing room shadow (`O0009`, `O0020`).
- **The AR Sightline Rule for `月人` (`S0022`, `FRM-08`, `O0022`)**: When the lunar retrieval countdown (`2030/09/12`) strikes at `01:24:42` (`P2-CLIP-03`), Yamashita stages a brilliant point-of-view split in the Tokyo subway: unaugmented commuters ride the escalator oblivious beside a `SMART SIGHT` advertisement (`FRM-08`), whereas shots framed *through* a rider's AR glasses reveal towering white-suited `月人` with red palace-lantern heads marching among humans. Lunar law is thus established visually as an information-layer entity—imperceptible to naked earthly sight, overwhelming inside Tsukuyomi's digital sky (`S0025`), and powerless against tactile human stubbornness once Kaguya acquires a real biological-synthetic chassis in `2040`.

Far from a throwaway fourth-wall gag, this five-second hijack aligns **two forms of false closure**: (a) classic Japanese *mono-no-aware* coming-of-age resignation ("treasure the vanished magical girl and go study for Todai") and (b) streaming-platform algorithmic consumption ("cry politely and auto-play the next idol live in 10 seconds"). ## §8. Governing Thematic Synthesis, Rival Readings, and Full-Film Exit State

### §8.1 Governing Argument (`CPK-R1-C0020`): Tactile Everydayness Against Mythic Loss and Disembodied Consolation

Synthesizing its narrative causality (`§2`), bidirectional relationships (`§3`), loop chronology (`§4`), dual-world form (`§5`), musical ring (`§6`), and sociolinguistic detail (`§7`), **超かぐや姫！ (*Cosmic Princess Kaguya!*)** advances a unified, deeply humane governing argument (`CPK-R1-C0020`):

> **Authentic love and human maturity require rejecting both classical myth's aestheticization of irreversible loss (*Taketori Monogatari*'s `天の羽衣` and `めでたし めでたし`) and digital modernity's temptation of frictionless, disembodied para-sociality (`Tsukuyomi` alone), choosing instead the patient, collaborative labor of building shared, finite, tactile everydayness (`触覚`, `生活費折半`, `休むも仕事！`, `しあわせパンケーキ`)—without erasing the historical self (`Yachiyo`) that endured the wait.**

Every layer of the film reinforces this refusal of noble resignation. Classic Japanese literary sensibility from *Taketori Monogatari* through modern *bildungsroman* anime frequently equates growing up with gracefully accepting separation: the celestial maiden ascends, the youth treasures the memory, and life resumes on schedule (`東大文一`). `F01` stages that exact ending at `01:51:30` (`S0028`)—and exposes it as an algorithmic trap (`FRM-13`) that mistakes self-suppression for adulthood. Conversely, `S0031`–`S0032` refuses pure digital utopianism: even as ruler of gleaming virtual Tsukuyomi, 8,000-year disembodied Yachiyo is profoundly lonely because virtual light cannot transmit skin warmth (`触れたら、あったかいかな`, `O0034`) or taste buttered pancakes (`O0040`). Only when Iroha invests ten years of real scientific labor (`FRM-19`) supported by healed family and peer networks (`Momiji`, `Asahi`, `Rai`, `Noi`, `Roka`, `Mami`) does the film earn its title at `02:10:26`.

### §8.2 Testing Strong Rival Readings and Disconfirmation Conditions

Per [CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md](../CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) §5, this governing synthesis is tested directly against the **two strongest competing interpretations** of `F01`:

1. **Rival Reading 1 — *Venture-Funded Techno-Denial of Bereavement***:
   - *Formulation*: Rather than healthy devotion, `F01`'s third act (`S0028`–`S0034`) indulges an escapist fantasy in which a bereaved teenager refuses to process grief—first over her dead father Tomohisa, then over departed Kaguya—and uses elite STEM brilliance plus wealthy older-brother venture capital (`S0033`) to literally manufacture a synthetic replacement body, turning mourning into an engineering problem solvable by money.
   - *Supporting source evidence*: Iroha explicitly reverses her acceptance of loss at `01:51:38`, spends a full decade focused on bionics (`2030`–`2040`), relies on Asahi/Rai/Noi's investment check (`S0033`), and tests multiple chassis (`YC型`, `O0036`).
   - *Why the text resists Rival 1 (and what would disconfirm `C0020`)*: First, `F01` explicitly contrasts Iroha's relationship to **human mortality** with **reversible cosmic separation**: Iroha never attempts to resurrect deceased father Tomohisa (`SUBJ-TOMOHISA`), instead honoring his mortality by completing `タイトル未定 ( 彩葉と共作 ).wav` as `Reply` (`S0019`–`S0025`). Second, Kaguya is *not dead* in `S0032`—she is alive and conscious right in front of Iroha as bodiless Yachiyo asking if touch is warm (`O0034`); building a prosthetic/haptic vessel for a living disembodied partner is assistive embodiment, not necromancy. Third, `S0029` (reconciling with Momiji) and `S0033` (`お先に失礼しまーす！ 休むも仕事！`, `02:09:01`) explicitly demonstrate that adult Iroha is psychologically healthier, better rested, and socially more connected than `2030` Todai-law Iroha ever was. (`C0020` would be disconfirmed if `2040` showed Iroha isolated, manic, or erasing Yachiyo's autonomous existence.)

2. **Rival Reading 2 — *Closed Bootstrap Determinism Negating Human Agency***:
   - *Formulation*: Because `Remember` (`S0004`), `2020/01` `.wav` (`S0019`), `犬DOGE`'s stowaway (`S0026`), `Reply` (`S0030`), and Yachiyo's 8,000-year vigil (`S0031`) form an unbreakable causal circle (`C0010`), Iroha's dramatic shattering of the Netflix UI (`S0028`) was always pre-determined by past history (`Tsukuyomi` already existed in `S0001`!), rendering the film's celebration of anti-fatalist choice illusory.
   - *Supporting source evidence*: In `S0001`–`S0010`, Yachiyo and `Remember` already exist in `2030`, proving that `S0028`–`S0030`'s balcony song had *already* caused the `6000 BCE` crash before the movie began.
   - *Why the text resists Rival 2*: `F01` cleanly separates **two chronological regimes** (`§4.2`). Up to `01:55:00` (`S0030`), the loop is indeed self-consistently closed—kept intact precisely by Yachiyo's agonizing voluntary choice not to interfere prematurely (`E0006`). **From `S0032` (`02:06:55`) to `2040` (`S0034`), however, no bootstrap constraint exists**: nothing in past history required Iroha to spend `2030`–`2040` inventing haptic bionics (`FRM-19`) or bringing embodied Kaguya onto a `2040` stage beside Yachiyo (`FRM-20`). The closed loop preserves survival (`この曲で、生き残れた`); free human choice after `2030` builds embodiment (`本当のハッピーエンド`).
