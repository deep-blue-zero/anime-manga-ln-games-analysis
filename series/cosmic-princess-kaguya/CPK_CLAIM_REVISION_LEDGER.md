## Initial claim schema

Required fields: `claim_id | originating run | exact original formulation | full argument home/anchor | witness and knowledge boundary | supporting observation/state/music IDs | bounded source/clock locators and anchors | evidence classes and inspection route | inference level | effective sampling for temporal claims | confidence/reason | counterevidence | strongest rival explanation(s) | disconfirmation condition | support qualifier | unresolved REQUIRED/OPTIONAL evidence | downstream dependencies | current formulation/home | acceptance provenance`.

Support qualifiers follow the generic method: strongly supported, supported but contestable, provisional, open, contradicted. They describe evidential standing, not transition history or artifact authority. The original formulation and its locators remain recoverable even after rejection. A text quotation, self-report and inferred intention must not merge into one unqualified fact.

## Revision and audit schema

Append one attributable event per disposition: `revision_id | claim_id | queue_id if any | originating candidate freeze ref | earlier formulation/locator | transition | proposed/current formulation | reviewer run | inspected interval and source/clock map | evidence/observation IDs | REPORTED/ANCHORED/disputed status where relevant | discrepancy kind | supporting argument and counterevidence | alternative explanation | confidence/reason | primary-author response | integrator acceptance/deferment | affected homes and propagation result | unresolved obligation/next owner | audit artifact anchor`.

| Transition | Meaning at this claim's declared scope |
|---|---|
| PRESERVE | Existing formulation survives the checks actually performed; disclose untested channels |
| STRENGTHEN | Additional support justifies a stronger evidential standing or bounded formulation |
| REVISE | Change the formulation because source evidence, attribution or reasoning warrants it |
| DOWNGRADE | Narrow scope/confidence or return to provisional standing because support is weaker than claimed |
| REJECT | Evidence contradicts the claim or the asserted evidential basis demonstrably fails; record which |
| OPEN | Material uncertainty or dispute remains; do not manufacture closure |

Audit discrepancy kinds are **factual contradiction, unsupported assertion, interpretive disagreement, incomplete coverage, evidence inaccessible through the bundle**. They describe the reason for a disposition, not new archive transitions. Record NONE for a checked claim with no discrepancy. Multiple kinds may apply. Inaccessible evidence normally prompts OPEN or a bounded verification request; it does not alone justify REJECT. A defensible interpretive disagreement can coexist with PRESERVE and a recorded alternative.

Proposed verifier transitions are separate from accepted current formulations. Only the designated integrator changes current pointers after source-grounded receipt/acceptance. Record all six transition totals, including PRESERVE and OPEN, and the denominator of actually audited claims in the reconciliation record. Never evaluate by counting disagreements alone.

## Contradictions, counterevidence and propagation

Every material disputed premise has a record with exact opposing source routes, possible clock/witness/translation/attribution/knowledge causes, affected claims, missing observation, next owner and acceptance condition. The reconciliation profile bounds return and independent verification; unresolved load-bearing observations stay provisional. An attractive integrated thesis is not evidence against an inconvenient observation.

Maintain a dependency link from accepted claim to each subject/relationship, musical/formal argument, study, model or synthesis that uses it. A revision records whether each dependent home was updated, remains supported independently, or is blocked pending review. Frozen candidates/readings are corrected by errata or current claim revisions rather than changed bytes. Preserve important prior reasoning and rivals, not only the newest label.
