## 2. Formal frame, mythic contestation, and platform refusal

*Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* announces its governing formal problem—**who gets to declare a story finished, and at whose expense**—through the architecture of its opening, false ending, delayed title card, and post-credits coda (`CPK-INT-C0001`, `CPK-INT-C0008`).

### 2.1 Classical *setsuwa* opening and `130`-minute title withholding

The file witness begins with two standard distributor presentation cards (`00:00:01–00:00:11`: Netflix logo `AVFRM-01A` at `00:00:04.505` and `NETFLIXオリジナルアニメーション` `AVFRM-01B` at `00:00:09.009`), neither of which displays the film's title. At `00:00:13.013` (`AVFRM-01C`), the diegetic image opens in silence on a patterned kimono hem drifting across a cosmic disc-island where two giant pancakes rest under butter and syrup beside miniature torii, pagoda roofs, bamboo groves, and lunar rabbits (`CPK-INT-O0001`). Over this miniature cosmos, `月見(るなみ)ヤチヨ` speaks `[JA#0001]` (`00:00:26.026`, furigana explicitly glossing `月見` as `るなみ`, joining Latin/lunar *Luna* to Japanese `月見` rather than ordinary `つきみ`, `CPK-INT-J0001`) after reciting the classical Heian *setsuwa* opening of *Taketori Monogatari* (`今は昔 竹取の翁といふものありけり`, `00:00:16–00:00:23`, `CPK-R1-J0001`) and redefining the feature as what happens *after* a canonical closure: `これは ハッピーエンドの その先の物語` (`00:00:14–00:00:15`). At `00:01:20.956`, an abrupt hard cut (`scdet = 71.0`, `AM-01`) snaps from mythic floating islands to a ringing smartphone alarm in high-school sophomore `酒寄彩葉`'s cramped Tokyo apartment (`00:01:21`).

The film withholds its actual main title card (**`超かぐや姫！ / COSMIC PRINCESS KAGUYA!`**) for **`130` minutes and `26` seconds**, dropping it onto black in complete musical and vocal silence (`FC = -82.06 dBFS`, `FL/FR = -79.23/-79.13 dBFS`) at **`02:10:26.360–02:10:33.367`** (`AVFRM-10B`, `CPK-INT-O0022`)—only after Kaguya has been seized by the Moon (`01:48:20`), crashed `8,000` years into Earth's Jōmon past (`01:54:55`), endured millennia inside virtual space as `ヤチヨ` (`01:57:04–02:02:45`), and awakened ten years later (`10年後`) in an embodied terrestrial chassis (`02:08:34–02:10:25`). What looks in isolation like stylistic bravura performs a precise structural claim: the protagonist pair do not yet inhabit *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* while trapped inside *Taketori*'s inherited script of luminous visitation and obligatory amnesia; the title is earned only once human stubbornness, collaborative song, and ten years of engineering rewrite the myth's jurisdiction.

### 2.2 Contesting *Taketori* from tablet reading to bracelet hijack

Within Act I, the inherited *Taketori* script is explicitly read and rejected onscreen. At `00:13:38–00:14:21` (`S0005`, `CPK-INT-O0003`), young Kaguya scrolls through *Taketori Monogatari* on an electronic **tablet** (not a printed picture book) and slams the ending shut in contemporary *gyaru* speech (`[JA#0271–0272]`: `バッドエンドじゃん！ / 超バッドエンドじゃん！`, `CPK-R1-J0004`). What outrages her is specifically the classical tale's presentation of memory-erasing lunar repatriation—sliding the *ama no hagoromo* (`天の羽衣`) over the princess's shoulders so human attachment evaporates—as celestial benevolence.

Yet the film refuses to treat mythic fatalism as an easily discarded superstition. At `01:24:15.54` (`S0021`), during an unguarded domestic rooftop conversation after the summer beach trip, Kaguya jokes in colloquial ease about staying with Iroha forever (`[JA#1791]`: `結婚しよっかな～`), followed at `01:24:20` by the pragmatic domestic rider `[JA#1793]` `生活費折半してくれるなら…` (`CPK-R1-J0008`). Between those two lines—at **`01:24:16.250`** (`PE12` / `CPK-INT-O0009`)—an unannounced close-up cuts to Kaguya's silver lunar wrist-bangle flashing white, eleven seconds before the `01:24:27` hijack projects the lunar summons **`2030/09/12`** (`FRM-08`). While the exact statutory rule governing lunar recall remains unstated (`CPK-R1-U0004`), the editing binds Kaguya's spoken wish for reciprocal earthly domesticity directly to institutional seizure.

### 2.3 The `~6.0`-second streaming post-play shatter (`01:51:33.3–01:51:39.4`)

After the `2030/09/12` graduation concert fails to stop Kaguya's departure (`01:48:20`) and Iroha attempts to return to compliant exam study (`01:49:10–01:51:32`), Yachiyo's classical voiceover attempts to seal the film in *Taketori* resignation (`[JA#2320]`: `めでたし めでたし`). Across **`~6.006` seconds (`144` frames, `01:51:33.353–01:51:39.359`)**, the film fuses two seemingly opposite engines of closure: thousand-year literary fatalism (`めでたし めでたし`, treating female self-denial and grief as elegiac beauty) and twenty-first-century algorithmic autoplay (treating narrative heartbreak as disposable content to be cleared in ten seconds for the next stream). Across all four structural hinges, form is the argument: happiness is neither mythic fate nor platform consumption, but an improvised, slightly unruly continuation seized past the scheduled end.

## 3. Domestic economics, capacity, and negotiated care

A central strength of the reconciled corpus (`CON-02`, `CON-03`, `CPK-INT-C0002`, `CPK-INT-C0003`) is refusing two opposite caricatures of `酒寄彩葉`'s Tokyo life: neither framing her as an abandoned victim surrounded by callous adults, nor flattening her exhaustion into painless teen competence.

### 3.1 Iroha's double shift, maternal pressure, and Roka/Mami's everyday scaffolding

From `00:01:21` onward, second-year high-school student Iroha (`FRM-12`: `2年 3組 12番 酒寄 彩葉`) sustains an extraordinary regimen of academic discipline, wage labor, and household management (`FRM-01` exam manuals `即読英単語`, `やっておきたい英語長文`, `Vintage`; `FRM-05` six-subject `東大文一` Common Test and `バイト` wall schedule; honor-roll standing). Crucially, dialogue at `00:56:58–00:57:18` (`[JA#1092–1098]`, `CPK-INT-K0002`) clarifies the institutional terms of her solitude: Iroha **chose** to move out after conflicts with her mother `酒寄紅葉` following her father `酒寄朝久`'s death, and Momiji permitted independent living on the explicit condition that Iroha fund her own tuition and living expenses (`自分で学費も生活費も稼ぐって言うから 一人暮らし許したのよ`).

The directed tie between mother and daughter (`E0009`–`E0010`, `CPK-INT-E0001`–`E0002`) is thus asymmetric and tensely loving rather than villainous. In `FRM-02` (`00:03:54`), Momiji texts in blunt Kansai-ben (`気張って無理し… すぐに泣いて帰ってくる思ってます`), mixing maternal worry that Iroha is overstraining herself with a sharp prediction of failure that makes asking for help feel like surrender. When Kaguya later presses Iroha on why she abandoned piano and songwriting (`01:35:45–01:36:35`), Iroha confesses in `[JA#2029]` (`01:36:14`, `CPK-INT-J0004`): **`だから 本当のこと 言いたくなかったの`** (*"That's why I didn't want to say what I really wanted"*). Rather than proving that Momiji issued an explicit prohibition banning music (`SA01` / `P002` repair), the negative-volitional `言いたくなかった` + explanatory `の` exposes grief-conditioned **self-silencing**: watching Momiji shoulder widowed reality, Iroha locked her father's co-composed `.wav` away (`FRM-07`), sold or stored her keyboard Habits, and steered toward `東京大学 文科一類` / `法科大学院（弁護士）` (`FRM-12`). When Iroha finally phones Momiji after shattering the false ending (`01:52:16–01:52:55`, `S0029`), the film mutes Iroha's actual plea into silent wind and lip movement (`[JA#2333]`, `CPK-INT-U0009`), then lets Track 1 return in warm, unhesitating Kansai-ben (`[JA#2334]`: **`ええよ やってみ`**, *"Sure. Give it a shot"*, `CPK-R1-J0012`). Once spoken aloud rather than pre-emptively swallowed, the request is met with permission.

Nor is Iroha socially isolated prior to Kaguya's arrival (`CON-02`). Classmates **`綾紬芦花` (`Roka`)** and **`諌山真実` (`Mami`)** (`CPK-INT-E0003`–`E0004`) consistently monitor her sleep deprivation, feed and tease her at school (`[JA#0039–0044]`, `[JA#0735–0740]`), collaborate in costume/stream logistics and summer excursions (`[JA#1020–1035]`, `[JA#1442–1470]`), and cushion her grief after the `2030/09/12` separation (`[JA#1950–2005]`). Kaguya does not rescue Iroha from friendless isolation; she breaches Iroha's *interior* fortress against unproductive desire.

### 3.2 Kaguya's spending breaches, mutual caregiving, and debt restitution

Found inside a glowing roadside utility pole (`00:05:18`, reworking *Taketori*'s shining bamboo stalk into urban Tokyo infrastructure), Kaguya initially destabilizes Iroha's fragile budget (`CPK-INT-C0003`). Her impulsive infant/child demands drain Iroha's `ふじゅペイ` balance (`FRM-03`: `23,532円` = `20,532円` cash + `3,000 pt`), and her unilateral move into a spacious luxury apartment (`[JA#0765–0799]`) forces the pair into high-stakes `ツクヨミ` streaming to survive. Any honest reading must retain the real material stress those breaches inflict on a self-supporting minor (`CON-03`).

At the same time, the film tracks a genuine **inversion of domestic caregiving** well before Act III (`E0001`–`E0002`, `CPK-INT-E0005`). In `S0013` (`00:40:12–00:42:50`), seeing exhausted Iroha mechanically flavor pancakes with savory `めんつゆ` (`FRM-10`: `めんつゆ…きんし！！`), Kaguya bans the shortcut and masters fluffy `しあわせパンケーキ` (`かたくりこ` + `れもんじる`). Crucially in causal sequence (`SA03` / `P010` precision anchor), **before** the `KASSEN` battle begins (`00:48:30–00:51:40`, `S0014A`, preceding `KASSEN` at `00:52:40`), Iroha collapses with a work-and-study fever; Kaguya stays up nursing her overnight and cooks gentle `たまご粥` (`[JA#0902–0921]`), proving her care is neither belated guilt after combat loss nor parasitic whimsy. And before her involuntary lunar extraction on `2030/09/12`, Kaguya wires an exact electronic restitution transfer (`FRM-11`, `2030/09/12 22:49`), shifting from playful all-hiragana fridge notes (`FRM-10`) into formal apologetic keigo (`使っちゃった分、かえす！ ご迷惑かけました！`, `CPK-R1-J0010`) so Iroha is left materially unburdened—even though money is precisely what cannot heal Iroha's bereavement.

## 4. Virtual affordances, competitive metrics, and multimodal staging

Rather than staging a simplistic binary between `"alienating virtual escapism"` and `"authentic offline flesh"`, *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* treats virtual space (`ツクヨミ`) as a **real, enabling socio-technical medium** whose affordances emancipate voice and community precisely up to the threshold of touch (`CPK-INT-C0006`, `CPK-INT-C0007`, `CON-07`).

### 4.1 `KASSEN` combat defeat versus `Yachiyo Cup` affective victory

Tsukuyomi's tournament design (`S0014`–`S0015`) dramatizes two rival economies of attention. In `KASSEN`—explicitly structured as a **`3対3` territorial battle** (`[JA#0825]`, where `いろP` and Kaguya recruit `犬DOGE` as their third unit member against `Black onyX`'s Akira, Rai, and Noi)—raw combat optimization wins: `Black onyX` takes the match `2–1` (`[JA#0988]`). Yet the overarching `Yachiyo Cup` weights post-match audience conversion alongside combat points. Refusing defeatism, Kaguya pivots into an exuberant `ハッピーシンセサイザ` (`EasyPop`) encore (`00:55:21–00:56:16`, `M0007`), converting spectators through infectious delight rather than dominance and winning the `Yachiyo Cup` overall (`00:56:20`). Tsukuyomi rewards joy and participatory warmth even when sheer competitive firepower loses.

### 4.2 Selective AR perception and the `-107.45 dBFS` real-room farewell hush

In Act III, multimodal staging sharpens the ontological boundary between virtual projection and embodied room space:

1. **AR-selective lunar incursion (`01:25:10–01:25:34`, `AV-04`)**: On the Tokyo subway (`FRM-08`, `AVFRM-04A` at `01:25:16.843`), golden lunar vessels and `2030/09/12` countdown glyphs fill the sky **only** for viewers wearing Tsukuyomi smart-contact AR lenses; unaugmented commuters read phones and stare ahead unreacting. Celestial fate operates simultaneously as an ancient lunar statute and an encrypted network layer invisible to ordinary civic space.
2. **Acoustic rupture at farewell (`01:47:36.20–01:47:42.25`, `AV-06`)**: At the height of the `2030/09/12` graduation live, as virtual armies clash overhead, the soundtrack executes an extraordinary subtraction. Between `[JA#2266]` (`01:47:34.25–01:47:36.20`, Kaguya leaning her forehead against Iroha's back in their darkened apartment to whisper `彩葉… 大好き`) and `[JA#2267]` (`01:47:42.29`), native `5.1` PCM drops to **`-107.45 dBFS` across all six channels for `4.1` strict seconds (`~6.05` s perceptual hush)** (`AM-04`, `CPK-INT-O0012`). Stripping away festival synth, crowd roar, and battle effects exposes what Tsukuyomi's spectacle cannot preserve: the quiet physical pressure of Kaguya's weight against Iroha's shoulders seconds before the `天の羽衣` turns Kaguya's amber-gold irises cyan-blue (`01:47:54`) and `犬DOGE` leaps onto her skirt (`01:47:56`) as both vanish moonward.

### 4.3 Split survival (`犬DOGE` vs soul-only Kaguya) and three-figure stage co-presence (`FRM-20`)

Reconciled `AV-03` and `S0031` observations resolve how the film bridges `8,000` years without collapsing Kaguya and Yachiyo into a single undifferentiated avatar (`CON-04`, `CON-08`):

- **Physical craft versus disembodied consciousness (`01:54:55–01:58:20`)**: When Kaguya steals a bamboo-shoot time pod on the Moon holding `犬DOGE` in her arms (`AVFRM-03A/B/C`) and collides with a meteor shower (`01:54:55`), **only `犬DOGE` survives intact inside the physical bamboo pod** (`[JA#2371]`: `乗ってたの タケノコに 犬DOGEだけ！`), whereas Kaguya's lunar body is destroyed (`[JA#2414–2417]`: `肉体は滅びて 魂だけになっちゃった`), leaving her disembodied consciousness sheltered inside the tiny `ウミウシ` (`sea-bunny`) companion (`FRM-14` `CPU/ENERGY/RAM` core) across `8,000` years from Jōmon coastal shrines to early-2000s internet forums (`FRM-16` `2003/10/10` `★月に帰りたくなるときもあるよ同盟`; `FRM-17` `2005/07/28` `yachi8000`) until she builds `ツクヨミ` as **`月見ヤチヨ`** solely to meet Iroha again.

## 5. Musical and sonic architecture across time

Music in *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* is not decorative accompaniment; it is the primary causal vessel through which memory survives bereavement, amnesia, and millennia (`CPK-INT-C0005`, `CPK-INT-M0001`–`M0010`). Native `5.1` PCM channel inspection (`AM-02`, `AM-03`, `AM-06`) and Track 4 `[SDH]` cue verification (`AV-02`, `AV-07`, `AV-08`, `AV-09`) establish **six distinct compositional and acoustic stages** of the shared `Remember` / `Reply` musical spine—while strictly honoring `CPK-R1-U0003` (`OPEN`) by refusing to invent an unstated causal arrow for whether Yachiyo or Iroha's father `酒寄朝久` originated the melody first:

## 6. Diachronic Japanese registers, pragmatics, and family naming symmetry

Across `[JA#0001–2716]` and onscreen typography, *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* uses shifts in Japanese register—not exposition—to index millennial exhaustion, regional kinship, adolescent self-censorship, and ontological plurality (`CPK-INT-C0007`, [CPK_CHARACTER_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md](../CPK_CHARACTER_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER.md) §§4, 7.4):

- **Yachiyo / Kaguya's four-stratum diachronic voice**: One consciousness speaks four historically sedimented registers: (1) vibrant contemporary *gyaru* youth speech as `2030` Kaguya (`超バッドエンドじゃん！`, `結婚しよっかな～`, all-hiragana fridge notes `めんつゆ…きんし！！` in `FRM-10`, shifting deliberately into adult keigo `ご迷惑かけました！` in `FRM-11`); (2) raw, unvarnished `2005` `2channel`-style net slang on `yachi8000`'s BBS (`FRM-17`: `自分で調べろカス… 弥生時代以前の飯くえねえ`, betraying `8,000` years of culinary and existential weariness); (3) serene classical Heian past-hearsay narration and archaic affect as public Tsukuyomi host `月見(るなみ)ヤチヨ` (`今は昔…ありけり`, `いと かわゆし`, `めでたし めでたし`); and (4) affectionate peer teasing once relieved of solitary stewardship on the `2040` lift (`[JA#2587]`: `（ヤチヨ）ヤッチョは先週ぶり～`, `CPK-INT-J0007`).
- **Sakayori family code and naming symmetry**: Both Momiji (`FRM-02`, `[JA#2334]` `ええよ やってみ`) and Iroha's private vulnerability are inflected by Kansai familial warmth beneath Tokyo standard-dialect self-control, while end-credit cast verification (`FRM-23`: **`酒寄朝久 — 鈴村健一`**) completes a striking generational kanji pair (`CPK-R1-J0018`): **`朝久` (`Tomohisa`) → `朝日` (`Asahi`)** sharing `朝` (*morning*) alongside **`紅葉` (`Momiji`) → `彩葉` (`Iroha`)** sharing `葉` (*leaf/foliage*), subtly inscribing family continuity across bereavement.

Under [CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md](../CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) §6, the uncorroborated speaker identity of `[JA#2595–2597]` remains strictly **`OPEN` (`CPK-INT-U0010`)**, yet the lexical insertion of **`ひとまず`** (*"for now / provisionally"*) combined with colloquial quotative `ってことで` transforms `01:51:32`'s suffocating `めでたし めでたし` into an open-ended pause rather than a terminal seal.

## 7. Governing integrated thesis — growth, particular love, and tactile continuation

Synthesizing its formal, economic, relational, linguistic, and musical evidence (`CPK-INT-C0009`), *Cosmic Princess Kaguya!* argues that **because Kaguya's chaotic love reawakens Iroha's buried capacity to want, create, and ask for help, Iroha refuses *both* literary resignation (`めでたし めでたし`) and algorithmic disposability (`次のエピソードまであと10秒`)—not merely to achieve abstract creative self-actualization, but to answer one irreplaceable person across `8,000` years and ten years of material engineering so they can touch, eat pancakes, and sing together past the scheduled ending.**

Neither half of this thesis works without the other (`CON-06`):
- **Without Iroha's agency and relational growth**, the finale would collapse into unearned techno-magic: Iroha can build the `2040` tactile chassis only because Kaguya first broke her grief-frozen self-denial (`言いたくなかったの`), prompting Iroha to smash the false ending, ask Momiji (`ええよ やってみ`), finish `Reply`, and adopt sustainable adult collaboration (`休むも仕事！`) backed by Asahi, Rai, Noi, Roka, and Mami.
- **Without particular, tactile, stubborn attachment to Kaguya herself**, the film after `01:53:04` would be redundant (`[JA#2716]` `蛇足`): at `02:05:21–02:05:45` (`[JA#2521–2526]`), Yachiyo explicitly offers the mature-"closure" moral—that `8,000` years of watching humanity and reuniting in virtual space is already `"plenty of a happy ending"` (`十分ハッピーエンドだよ`)—and Iroha weeping on her knees rejects it outright (**`かぐやと いたい`**), matched immediately by Yachiyo's own confession that she wants physical warmth and pancakes (`[JA#2535–2537]`: `触れたら あったかいかな`). Emancipation supplies the capacity; particular love supplies the destination.

## 8. Live rival interpretations and ethical counterweights

Per [CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md](../CPK_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) §5 and [CPK_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md](../CPK_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md) §§3, 5 (`CPK-INT-C0011`), four serious counterreadings remain live interpretive lenses grounded in real film tensions rather than dismissed strawmen:

1. **Creative emancipation as the primary dramatic core**: One can read the `2040` android reunion as an exuberant fairy-tale/sci-fi `蛇足` (`[JA#2716]`) atop a fundamentally realistic coming-of-age story whose decisive dramatic turn occurs at `01:51:39–01:53:05`, when Iroha tears back her `東大文一` survey, speaks honestly to her mother, and finishes her father's unfinished song. *Why not sole authority*: `30` full minutes of runtime (`01:51:40–02:20:12`)—including the `8,000`-year shrine sequence, ten-year haptics lab, and `111`-cue credit roll—are devoted to refusing symbolic closure alone.
2. **Venture-backed platform wish fulfillment**: Far from escaping platform capitalism after shattering the `01:51:33` post-play UI, Iroha achieves Kaguya's embodiment through private venture investment from elite streamers (`帝アキラ`, `雷`, `乃依`, `02:07:53–02:08:33`) and culminates on Tsukuyomi's grandest commercial concert stage (`02:09:48`). The film critiques *passive algorithmic closure* while remaining optimistic about creator-led, capital-backed virtual infrastructure.
3. **Refusal of mourning and unwaived privacy tension**: Read against *Taketori*'s Buddhist impermanence (`もののあはれ`), Iroha's ten-year refusal to accept Kaguya's bodily death and `FUSHI`'s unconsented disclosure of Yachiyo's `8,000`-year memories (`[JA#2485–2491]`, `CON-10`) expose how fiercely possessive human rescue can be. The `原画` credit roll (`ray` + `メルト`) honorably preserves this cost by keeping `8,000` years of pain un-erased (`あの痛みは 忘れたって消えやしなかった`).
4. **Experienced open agency inside remembered history (`SA07` / `CON-09`)**: Although the `8,000`-year loop (`2030` → Jōmon → `2030`) forms a causal circle in story time, neither character experiences deterministic safety: Yachiyo hides her identity (`00:35:06`) out of dread of losing Kaguya's brief happiness, and Iroha acts without foreknowledge until `01:57:04`—and then breaks out of the loop entirely into uncharted linear history (`2030` → `2040`).
