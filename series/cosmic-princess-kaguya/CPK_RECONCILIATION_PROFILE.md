# Independent audit and mirror reconciliation

## 1. Route and ownership

Use the [corpus reconciliation protocol's mirror route](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md):

**CONTINUOUS_AV primary author → BUNDLE_MEDIATED independent verifier → designated integrator**, with verified continuous-AV return/verification for material temporal disputes. This profile adapts roles and film scope, not the corpus evidence requirements. The verifier owns only its independent log/audit, the primary author owns the original candidate until freeze, and the integrator owns accepted write-through to cumulative homes and integrated synthesis. No reviewer silently rewrites the primary reading or ledgers.

Owner-requested operational assignments: Gemini 4 Carbon primary, a separate OpenAI auditor, a later capability-qualified integrator. No model has been invoked or verified by this bootstrap. Resolve actual model/version/surface/effort at execution and record uncertainty; do not infer capabilities from the model label. A single provider may later fill several roles only with the required fresh-context and disclosure controls. Argon remains a separately authorized possible control, not an automatic next stage.

## 2. Stable candidate and transfer record

After the primary run's truthful scope closeout, retain the **complete** original reading, descriptive observations, state/claim snapshot, original locators, source/derivative identities, coverage and execution profile at an immutable commit and exact hashes. Preserve its full arguments, alternatives, limitations and unresolved obligations. A bounded partial result can be frozen but cannot be labeled complete.

The future reconciliation record carries the handoff contract fields: transfer ID, owner instruction/source scope, repository/branch and full base commit, target paths/IDs, delivered file bytes/hashes, policy/method versions, witness and clock maps, actual inspection state, output scope, required/optional debts, receiving roles, preservation constraints and receiver receipt. Hashes belong in the record, not in stale entrypoint prose. If bytes are unavailable, mark integrity unverified and block replacement. No candidate, freeze or receipt exists now.

Freeze the primary candidate as noncurrent experimental evidence. Keep `draft_noncurrent`, empty supersession arrays and `do_not_use_as_current_authority: true` unless a separate evidence-supported acceptance decision establishes another appropriate state **before** freeze. The eventual integrated synthesis is a distinct accepted artifact. Do not change the frozen candidate's front matter later merely to make it current or superseded; use an external current pointer/wrapper. Original assertions and their IDs remain inspectable even if all are later revised.

## 3. Blind packet and exposure control

The integrator prepares two physically distinct input sets. A fresh verifier context receives only the first until its observation log is frozen. File separation is necessary even if all objects reside in one repository; tool/retrieval access must follow the allowlist.

| First input set — allowed before freeze | Withheld until after freeze |
|---|---|
| Applicable live governance and this method/profile | Primary reading, its thesis/synopsis and any substantive interpretation |
| Sanitized F01 witness identity, hashes and clock/calibration metadata | Populated primary observation, character/relationship and claim ledgers |
| Film bundle, Japanese/English track provenance, extraction/QA and known technical gaps | Primary scene labels encoding interpretation, specialist notes, completed audits |
| Task scope, independent coverage criteria, neutral observation questions | Adjudication packet: claim formulations, disconfirmation criteria, reading sections and expected answers |
| Technical source/shot/cue/frame indexes without interpretive annotations | External plot summaries/reviews, publicity biographies and source-derived model descriptions |

Do not give the verifier an indiscriminate repository snapshot or “read every linked file” instruction. Prepare a sanitized input manifest from the full technical inventory, excluding primary run interpretations. Track exact paths/hashes and actual exposed materials. Strip unintended analytical annotations from index derivatives; preserve originals separately. Necessary title/speaker identifiers may remain; title knowledge and remembered source familiarity are disclosed, not hidden.

The independent pass should inspect the full available film bundle before primary-derived neutral questions where practical; this reduces question-selection anchoring. Then answer the neutral queue with surrounding context. The queue asks observations, never agreement: fields are `queue_id | neutral question | channel | film interval/clock | nearby cue/frame | VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION or VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION | REQUIRED/OPTIONAL`. Questions may be grouped by scene/music passage, but do not disclose the target claim's expected answer. If an interval is unknown, label it unknown and resolve navigation before pretending it is bounded.

The withheld adjudication packet uses the same queue IDs and carries original claim ID/formulation, disconfirmation condition and exact reading anchors. Separating the question from the answer does not eliminate selection bias; log the queue origin and order. A verifier that saw primary prose/claims before freeze records `blind_order_achieved: false`, what it saw and when. Continue truthful bounded review if authorized; do not call it independent blind observation.

## 4. Verifier's independent pass and freeze

First accept the technical transfer: same film/encode or verified cross-edition mapping, complete input hashes, bundle classification, calibrated clock and adequate source access. Verify image and any auditory inspection separately. Original Japanese/lyrics require an adequate text witness. Record source/runtime/sampling limits and exact interval/channel coverage. A missing audio route is UNAVAILABLE, not silent audio completion.

Inspect independently, log descriptive observations before interpretation, then answer the neutral queue. The future `audits/CPK_F01_BLIND_OBSERVATIONS.md` contains execution/clock profile, coverage by channel/interval, observation IDs and bounded anchors, unsupported/inaccessible channels and initial independent questions/interpretations distinctly labeled. Freeze its exact bytes/hash in the reconciliation record **before** opening primary claims. If later appendices are needed, write a separate audit record rather than changing the frozen log. Post-exposure observations must be labeled as such and cannot be backdated into the blind pass.

The verifier is not required to hallucinate continuous visual trajectories. Use frames for static/endpoints, verified audio for auditory observations, tools for measurements, and Japanese text for wording. Missing sampled motion cannot falsify a continuous report. Escalate when a material question needs unavailable continuity or precision.

## 5. Claim audit after exposure

Only after freeze, open the complete primary reading and adjudication packet. Record the exposure event and verify both freeze manifests. The verifier's claim-audit artifact assesses consequential factual, causal, character/relationship, formal, music/voice and linguistic claims individually. Review every load-bearing primary claim plus source/locator validity and an explicitly declared representative scope of minor claims. Mark unaudited remainder rather than infer full coverage from a selected sample. New bundle findings have their own IDs and proposals; they are not invented transitions on absent primary claims.

For every reviewed claim record the claim/queue ID, original formulation, inspected interval and source/clock, independent versus post-exposure evidence IDs, evidence route/status, proposed transition, discrepancy kind, confidence/reason, counterevidence, alternative and proposed bounded wording where needed. Use exactly `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`. The claim ledger defines the five discrepancy kinds and write-through schema. Report PRESERVE and untestable OPEN decisions as well as revisions/rejections.

Factual accuracy and defensible interpretation are separate judgments. A second supported reading is not necessarily a contradiction; a correct conclusion with invented supporting details still has an evidence defect. A bundle-inaccessible channel is a verification debt, not a license to declare either model correct. No premium/newer model receives automatic adjudicative priority.

## 6. Grounded contest and bounded escalation

Apply the corpus asymmetry rule with roles exchanged. A source-channel observation deserves serious consideration, not categorical truth. ANCHORED load-bearing AV evidence requires calibrated anchors, adequate temporal resolution, focused reinspection and the corroboration required by the method. REPORTED observations remain distinguished; interpretations have no perceptual privilege.

The verifier can raise a **contradiction** from evidence it can inspect: witness/locator/clock failure, internal inconsistency, primary-language conflict or frame evidence at the relevant time, or an assertion outside lane permissions. A **verification request** is different: missing anchoring/corroboration, unknown/inadequate sampling, uncertain speaker/voice attribution, suspected shot conflation, conflicting timing metadata or independent AV disagreement. Record the concrete grounds, source interval, affected claims and consequence. Mere preference for the existing interpretation supports neither.

Return a material disputed item to the primary AV reviewer **once**, with a neutral question and specific grounds. Reinspection must record actual route/resolution and outcome. If still unsettled, route **once** to independent verification: another blind verified CONTINUOUS_AV observer, higher-resolution targeted inspection, deterministic measurement or human viewing. Use the smallest faithful interval with sufficient context, preserving film-clock mapping and current transport constraints.

Do not loop until consensus. Continued disagreement or unavailable verification leaves the observation and dependent claims provisional with `OPEN`. Do not accept or reject a load-bearing disputed observation by provider prestige. Material unresolved REQUIRED items block corresponding completeness/release claims; optional questions may remain open in an otherwise adequate scoped result.

## 7. Integration and summary

The integrator performs full-file receiver acceptance and a three-way base/candidate/current comparison. Stage acceptance is not endorsement. Preserve adequate incoming prose, locators, alternatives and substantive disagreements. Apply accepted transitions through the claim ledger, update affected state/form/music homes, resolve Japanese divergence through the text route and write an integrated argument only after the architecture's role-gap and convergence gates. Do not change frozen primary/blind bytes.

The future reconciliation summary records: route; source/AV and bundle hashes; clock maps, offsets/drift, calibration anchors/precision; effective visual sampling; actual channel/interval coverage; blind order/exposures; candidate/blind/audit freeze identities; claimed/audited/unaudited denominators; all six transition counts; new findings proposed/accepted/deferred; contested claims; return/verification routes; provisional observations; open disputes and REQUIRED debts; accepted/rejected/deferred outputs; write-through paths; actual publication/integration status. Do not fabricate zero-valued “results” before execution. A technical pass is not semantic certification.

## 8. Evaluation contract and future control

Assess factual/source fidelity, consequential evidence support and locator validity; character/relationship specificity and knowledge boundaries; narrative/thematic argument and rivals; cinematic/temporal sensitivity; music/voice/sound and Japanese interpretation; uncertainty/counterevidence; actual coverage, hallucinations and false precision; and findings enabled by direct AV inspection that materially change the reading. Keep factual correctness separate from interpretation quality. Word count, elegance and number of claims are not quality measures. Record accepted findings and warranted abstention alongside errors.

This planned relay compares **different evidence routes and roles**. It is not by itself a controlled ranking of Carbon against OpenAI: modality, temporal resolution, text availability, context, exposure and author-versus-verifier tasks can explain differences. Track those factors explicitly. Inaccessible claims and unresolved disagreements are not adjudicated ground truth.

If an Argon control is later authorized, predeclare a distinct run/candidate/evidence envelope and input/exposure manifest before execution. Hold source hashes, scope, instructions, allowed context and evaluation criteria constant where possible; record every modality/sampling/configuration difference and actual model identity. Freeze each independent result before cross-exposure. Prefer a fresh evaluator with anonymized candidate labels where practical; distinguish blinded artifact labels from unavoidable film recognition. A prior exposed session cannot become blind by deleting its prose. No control run, paid invocation or comparative score is authorized now.
