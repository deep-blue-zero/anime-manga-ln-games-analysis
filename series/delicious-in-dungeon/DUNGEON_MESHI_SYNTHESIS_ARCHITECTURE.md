## Standing source-reading configuration

For every substantive volume, an accountable principal who is also the analytical integrator inspects the full admitted unit and owns its canonical reading and ledger transaction. An independent specialist reconstructs the page-level representation of ecology, preparation, route and group cost, with independent observations and counterexamples. On dense volumes add a distinct specialist for directed party decisions, access to information, ordinary behavior and Japanese register. A separate independent critical reviewer directly inspects the diagnostic pages, preferably before reading the interpretation, and challenges causal steps, visual attribution, actor identity, knowledge horizons, exceptions, and lost character evidence. There is no standing reduced unit category.

The independent contexts begin with the assignment and authorized source frontier. Each derives source observations before seeing another interpretation and records actual exposure. Prevent spoilers across asynchronous or parallel assignments and freeze forecasts before exposure to resolution in every context where credit is claimed. The principal preserves the roles, boundaries, inspected coverage, evidence, rivals, corrections, remaining obligations and disposition of each material contribution in the unit checkpoint. No separate agent ledgers are needed. The principal reconciles before one synchronized state update; reservations remain traceable in the claim ledger. If independent contexts or actual diagnostic-page access are unavailable, record the capability blocker and leave acceptance and high-water advance pending.
