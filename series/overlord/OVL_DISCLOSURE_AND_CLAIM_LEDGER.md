# Disclosure and claim ledger

This ledger starts at **NONE**. Its stateful material is the difference between what an actor could think at a given moment and what a future reader can establish. Stable subject, change and evidence IDs link it to [command and world ledger](OVL_COMMAND_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md). Full argument and exact source context remain with the reading or the responsible specialist.

| Record | Preserve for evidence and revision retrieval |
| --- | --- |
| Disclosure and knowledge | Holder and proposition; acquisition source including witnessed, inferred, overheard, rehearsed or asserted; event bounds and when the reader first sees this account; disclosure/receipt path; certainty and audience; next change, public/private evidence IDs |
| Material interpretation | Named actors, claim scope, provenance, locator and full argument anchor; competing causal account and disconfirmation condition; adverse evidence and current status; relation to world/command entries |
| Prospective test | Stable expectation ID; entering or within-unit freeze and actual exposure; who frames what result to expect and why; diagnostic outcome and true resolution horizon; separate adjudications of outcome and reasoning; failed, open and contaminated captures retained |
| Revision | Dated transition, previous claim version and boundary, new evidence including rival or corrected source; current formulation and scope; affected readings/portraits and what remains open |

A lord's plan, a subordinate's report about it and a local witness's experience are different evidence objects. Do not upgrade one when another happens to align. Preserve predecessor arguments, affected counterfactuals and evidence retrieval when the current interpretation changes.
