# Synthesis architecture

This is the INITIAL architecture for Overlord, governed by [method](OVL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md), the [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), the [archive authority policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), and the [continuation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_SEQUENTIAL_EXECUTION_SCOPE_AND_CONTINUATION_POLICY.md). [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the sole first-read surface. This generation has no predecessor or admitted analytical claims. [source register](OVL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) scopes candidate units, conflicts, and exclusions one witness at a time.

## Cumulative architecture: orders, effect and disclosure

The sixteen available books justify two analytical stores. [command and world ledger](OVL_COMMAND_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md) allows an institutional or character analyst to find a change by affected actor, practice or jurisdiction. [disclosure and claim ledger](OVL_DISCLOSURE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) allows a reviewer to find why the project believed a cause, who knew about it, and what later evidence changed. Use shared stable IDs to connect these views rather than copying parallel summaries. Retain exact Japanese anchors and full arguments in the source-facing reading or responsible mature specialist; index a cross-volume case only if it matters to a major claim.

| Responsibility | Capture from sequential units | Canonical retrieval and destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game-derived categories and local systems | Named claimant; expected vs displayed effect; weak or adverse test and actual conditions | [command and world ledger](OVL_COMMAND_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md) rule entries link to [disclosure and claim ledger](OVL_DISCLOSURE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) revisions; anticipated mechanics/epistemology study of when use licenses belief |
| Commands, control and institutions | Issuer and recipient's actual words, agency and possible refusal, implementation point, effect on affected outsiders and limits of authority | [command and world ledger](OVL_COMMAND_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md) by actor/institution; anticipated study of delegated action, governance and perspectives at different scales |
| Beliefs, causation and formal disclosure | Who observes, recounts or infers; event/retrieval order; where focalization changes claim confidence; negative evidence and disputed dates | [disclosure and claim ledger](OVL_DISCLOSURE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) and source-facing arguments; anticipated chronology/reliability work where independent and decision-relevant |
| Character, relationship and ethical evidence | Distinctive language, ordinary preference, evidence of self-conception, goals, fear, coercion, chosen commitments, directed relationships and actual exceptions | [command and world ledger](OVL_COMMAND_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md) person/dyad and qualification view; `characters/OVL_<SUBJECT>_MONOGRAPH.md`; separately argued relationship/ensemble studies when evidence warrants them |
| Diagnostic maps, plates or motifs | Actual framing and exposure under the authorized witness, locator and effect | Original reading; cross-reference a recurrent case only if it bears on a controlling interpretation |

The books can expose the same event through conflicting motives or vocabulary. First distinguish word meaning, scope of an alleged rule, information distribution, unreliability, competing causal explanations and physically inconsistent depictions. Check witness and encoding → original language attribution → decision boundary → actual effects and counterexamples. Route edition disputes and testimony provenance through [source register](OVL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) and the reading. A manga, anime or web version can only be a separate study under new permission. Preserve dated material transitions `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` and `OPEN` in [disclosure and claim ledger](OVL_DISCLOSURE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) with full previous and current argument locators, counterevidence and downstream repair flags.

## Standing source-reading topology

The proposed configuration requires an accountable principal analyst who inspects the whole admitted unit and is also the analytical integrator; separate independent specialists for testable world mechanics and confounders; for institutional change, command transmission and affected perspectives; and for Japanese focalization, role-specific speech and individual characterization; and a distinct independent critical reviewer. The specialist contexts form their own observations from assigned original-language evidence before receiving the principal's interpretations. The critical reviewer directly inspects decisive and contradictory source evidence, checking systems claims with weak or negative tests, game-world assumptions mistaken for proven conditions, mismatched names or aliases, where authority and responsibility are credited, counterreadings about affected people and whether ordinary behavior and falsifiers survive integration, before acceptance. The reviewer receives the interpretation after inspecting decisive evidence where practical. No present unit type qualifies for the reduced configuration; record any later authorized change under [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). Verify these roles, evidence access and separation before first start; a runtime without adequate independent contexts is a required-capability stop. An owner-authorized deviation must state its exact scope and is recorded as a deviation, without ordinary acceptance or high-water advance unless explicitly authorized.

The unit checkpoint is the contribution and reconciliation record. Keep participant identity/role and separation, assignment, actual exposure and inspected coverage, material locator-backed observations, rivals and corrections, unresolved evidence obligations, and explicit adoption, qualification, rejection or open disposition for each material finding. Keep the finding and its reasoning recoverable; no generic participation summary, duplicated canonical artifact, or permanent per-agent ledger. Freeze prospective captures before resolution, separately by context where needed. Only the integrator updates shared readings, cumulative state and entrypoint after reconciliation. Record observed execution facts without inferring model settings. Keep source material and private transcripts in the evidence plane.

## Convergence and acceptance

Audit after the first complete volume, on the first evidenced institutional or rules shift, and after any completed multi-volume arc within the authorized tranche. Reconcile claim and world-state keys, distribute gaps by responsible evidence owner, and audit the viewpoints most likely to disappear from an authority-centered account. At the scope stop, stabilize rules and limits before comparing outcomes across institutions. The world-mechanics study supplies established constraints to a delegated-power argument; command/outcome and viewpoint findings in turn may show that a purported world rule was only a local inference. Convergence checks place focused character and relationship work against evidence of behavior, cognition and constraints, independent of reputation. Require specialist evidence where rule adjudication and delegated action are materially consequential; keep a standalone disclosure, ethical or ensemble study only where its question needs independent multi-volume retrieval. Resolve or bound disputed causal premises before integrated synthesis can account for the accumulated evidence. If a central premise changes during evidence completion, repair both the initiating specialist argument and its downstream dependencies before acceptance.

The principal closes each source transaction only with full assigned primary coverage, source-grounded independent contribution, independent critical examination, reconciled material disagreements, preserved evidence and counterexamples, consistent cumulative history and truthful exposure and authority boundaries. This is the sequential completion gate; the admitted, committed run boundary must also reconcile with its declared stop. Confirm longitudinal state and still-open evidence before a role-gap and specialist readiness review; require the qualified character work and the material specialist roles before authorizing a full synthesis. Review fidelity, locator retrieval, counterreadings, referential clarity and handoff acceptance separately from the decision to freeze or release. Any separately commissioned reconstruction model needs evidence-bounded validation of its own; a monograph or qualified portrait supplies no capability grade.

Use [initiation §5.5.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard) for qualification and complete standalone portraits. Assess every person with differentiated evidence, not merely leads; record stable identity, boundary, outcome, rationale, largest gaps, reassessment trigger and a path once a monograph actually exists. A qualified person creates a production obligation. An untested dimension remains insufficient evidence; an apparent absence needs an opportunity test. Include supported ordinary preferences, speech and address, decision patterns, relationships, changes, exceptions and limits. Document separate self-containment and source fidelity results before acceptance; no current portrait silently replaces a frozen earlier one.

Apply the [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md): fresh framework design is eligible, with local integration and witness-binding owner, local principal plus independent source-aware sequential specialists, then delivery of a frozen bounded snapshot to fresh retrospective specialist and synthesis contexts. Before transfer, name the convergence owner and freeze witness, exposure, claim and revision IDs, full argument links and unresolved checks. Specialists produce disjoint target artifacts; local acceptance addresses character, translation and original art debts and reconciles against both dispatched and current material. Authority promotion and Git integration remain separately assigned. No audiovisual completion is required for the collected prose.

Under the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md), use `ROUTINE_FAST` for deterministic bookkeeping, `BOUNDED_STANDARD` for metadata and locator checks, `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS` for unit interpretation, `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` for sustained specialist/character work and `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` for foundation, high-impact disputes and final integration review. A scoped transfer under the [analytical handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) includes the frozen admitted/exposure horizon, retrievable evidence and argument records, open obligations, and named output scope and receiver; reconcile against live state, retest material claims and names/references (§15) before any promotion.

Keep the entrypoint and cumulative views mutable; dated claim changes preserve the older version, counters and new evidence. Complete prospective captures, accepted historical checkpoints and any authorized release remain intact; later repairs use linked errata, claim revision or an attributable successor. Revisit INITIAL design at the first meaningful source boundary, at the planned title-specific checks above and before integrated synthesis. Add cumulative structure only when independent recurrent evidence or a retrieval gap warrants it; revise the architecture and entrypoint together and backfill only a material gap existing records cannot responsibly repair.
