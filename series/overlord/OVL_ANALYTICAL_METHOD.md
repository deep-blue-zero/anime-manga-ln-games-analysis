## Determine the mechanism and the observer separately

Read each authorized Japanese volume in its disclosure order. Break action into significant moves and relays rather than importing a remembered map or an encyclopedia's fixed history. When someone uses a game-derived category, retain whose category it is, the conditions under which that person would expect it to apply, what is actually done, the observed effect and an alternative mechanism or limiting case. An asserted name, inventory description or thought experiment can inform a test without proving this world's rule. Separate expected effects, technical capacity, choice to act, measured range and local or durable consequences. Preserve failed trials and cases the prose does not test.

Reconstruct material commands from named issuer and literal wording through recipients' interpretations, independent discretion and means, then what is enacted and what each party sees or reports. A formal order, a desired result, an agent's own purpose and the outcome can diverge. Test command, opportunism, accident, competing action, deference and rehearsed performance against scenes from different social positions. Include people who absorb costs or are affected by decisions, especially when a switch of focalizer makes elite and local knowledge diverge. An observer who interprets competence or coercion one way does not speak on behalf of the narrative. Extend a local institutional change only when the prose supports its geographic or social scope.

Distinguish event time, text disclosure, acquisition of knowledge by each actor, Japanese publication position and project admission. Split identity, in-story alias and designation from the continuity inference used to link them; the same group or title name in remembered material proves nothing about the person presently acting. Mark viewpoint scene by scene, separating narrator presentation, interiority, report, observation, inference and unresolved contradictions. Use alternate focalizers, counterexamples and ordinary acts of work, cooperation, leisure, preference or fear to challenge a single sweeping account of loyalty or personality. Evaluate formal loyalty, practical power, capacity and consent independently and in each direction.
