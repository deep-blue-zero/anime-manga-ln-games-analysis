# Re:Creators — Synthesis Architecture

## INITIAL structure and canonical ownership

Re:Creators has one self-contained anime sequence. A franchise hierarchy or a season-by-season master duplication would add no justified responsibility at bootstrap. The current [source register](RC_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) identifies one candidate encode family. All other versions and supplemental works require independent identity and admission decisions.

The day-one infrastructure is the source register, two combined ledgers and the execution/reconciliation plan. These files contain real governance, record contracts and explicit empty narrative state. No placeholder episode reading, character portrait or specialist synthesis is created.

| Responsibility | Sequential contribution | Canonical cumulative home | Eventual analytical output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source identity and language/clock limits | Verify the admitted unit and evidence routes | Source register | Source-bounded scope statement in every synthesis |
| Episode events, causality and current-unit synopsis | Complete readable episode account with locators | Frozen episode reading when accepted | Integrated series narrative and causal synthesis |
| Direct observation and source-language evidence | Stable evidence records | Evidence and claim ledger | Traceable support for all specialist works |
| Character state, cognition, behavior and development | Evidence-linked state deltas | Character and world ledger | Self-contained qualified character monographs |
| Directed relationships and creator/creation responsibility | Interaction, asymmetry and consequential change | Character and world ledger | Relationship studies when distinct evidence warrants them |
| Narrative levels, knowledge and world constraints | Separate disclosure, event and character-knowledge changes | Character and world ledger | Narrative ontology and causality study if justified |
| Authorship, autonomy, responsibility and audience claims | Test claims and adverse cases | Evidence and claim ledger | Thematic studies with competing positions preserved |
| Audiovisual form, performed voice and motifs | Calibrated interval evidence and independent review | Evidence and claim ledger | Integrated AV synthesis and specialist formal studies |
| Forecasts and interpretive revisions | Freeze, adjudicate and retain history | Evidence and claim ledger | Account of supported conclusions and irreducible uncertainty |
| Independent findings, objections and dispositions | Source-aware review and adjudication | Unit acceptance checkpoint, linked to both ledgers | Audit and handoff evidence |
| Character qualification and fidelity | Bounded evidence assessment | Character and world ledger qualification section | Production obligations and independent usability/fidelity results |

The reading owns its full argument and local synopsis. A ledger owns a defined cumulative record and links back to that argument. A specialist synthesis owns a larger integrated interpretation, with a declared evidence boundary. It may propose corrections but cannot silently replace the underlying evidence or rewrite a frozen prospective reading.

## Planned outputs and their creation triggers

A future authorized reading may create `02 Sequential Readings/RC_E##_DEEP_READING.md` and `checkpoints/RC_E##_TRANSACTION.md`. The placeholder notation is a naming rule, not an existing path. Create each file only for an actually authorized unit. Register its exact path and accepted boundary in the corpus map.

Create `03 Character Analyses/` only when a qualified monograph is written. Use `RC_<STABLE_SUBJECT_TOKEN>_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH_<BOUNDARY>.md`, with a stable source-supported subject token and explicit admitted episode boundary. Record the actual path in the qualification register. A subject/continuity or incarnation at one boundary has exactly one canonical current portrait, with `artifact_type: character_monograph` and complete authority metadata. Successors preserve the prior portrait and its boundary through the correction/supersession route.

Create `04 Specialist Syntheses/` for actual relationship, thematic, narrative-level or audiovisual work that has sufficient differentiated evidence. A later integrated series synthesis belongs under `05 Integrated Synthesis/` when its source-facing dependencies are stabilized. Add actual files to the map as they become available. No fixed character roster or predetermined thematic document set is authorized by this architecture.

## Sequential transaction and cumulative update

Before each unit, freeze the entering state, admissible horizon and testable prospective hypotheses. Analysts independently inspect only their assigned admissible source scope, then reconcile through the [plan](RC_AV_EXECUTION_AND_RECONCILIATION_PLAN.md). A complete unit transaction couples the reading, evidence and claim additions, character/world changes, forecast adjudication, specialist findings, critical objections, dispositions and checkpoint.

Use stable IDs and targeted updates. The same observation should not be copied into multiple competing records. A character state refers to evidence and claim IDs. A claim names the supporting observations and opposing evidence. The checkpoint lists affected IDs and exact accepted artifacts. Commit the high-water mark only when the entire transaction is accepted, required channels complete, output receipts verified and repository controls satisfied. An unfinished component leaves the prior accepted boundary intact.

For disputed interpretations, preserve the best supported account and its serious alternatives. New independent findings may expand the reading even when they do not refute a principal claim. Record both rejected corrections and their evidence-backed rejection reasons. Compression review asks whether important causal links, unusual details, independent disagreements and uncertainty remain reconstructable from the public analytical record.

## Character qualification and production obligations

The qualification register in [the character and world ledger](RC_CHARACTER_AND_WORLD_LEDGER.md) is the canonical home. Assess at meaningful episode/tranche boundaries, before character synthesis and after revisions that materially change a portrait's basis. Assess all differentiated characters, including secondary, adult and antagonistic figures. Do not preselect only leads or require an arbitrary appearance count.

Each assessment records a stable character identity, boundary, outcome, evidence-based rationale, largest limitations, next reassessment trigger and eventual actual monograph path. Use `QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` or `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION`. A qualification decision is a documented evidence judgment, not a discovery-registry entry or reconstruction capability grade.

Every qualified character creates a monograph production obligation. A portrait must independently explain identity and circumstances, history with event/disclosure order, psychology and values, cognition, behavioral patterns, linguistic and performed voice, directed relationships, ordinary habits/preferences, evidenced intimate life where relevant, abilities and limits, development, a conditional operational profile where supported, uncertainty and traceability. Mark genuinely unknown or inapplicable areas with the reason instead of inventing completeness.

The monograph must work for a reader who has not followed the ledgers. It should convey the character's particular life, conduct and relationships with enough context to understand its examples. Embedded references are support, not a substitute for the portrait. Independent review records **self-contained usability** and **source fidelity** as separate findings under [initiation §5.5.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md). A reconstruction-readiness assessment is a separate specialized operation. Do not call the character layer complete while qualified obligations remain unfulfilled.

## Synthesis phases and readiness

1. Complete the authorized sequential source boundary with independent unit review.
2. Audit role and evidence coverage, resolve material gaps and perform authorized targeted completion.
3. Stabilize observations and claims while preserving adverse evidence, revision history and open disagreements.
4. Produce required character monographs and evidence-justified specialist studies from frozen inputs in fresh contexts.
5. Reconcile dependencies, terminology, competing explanations and cross-document inconsistencies.
6. Write the full-series synthesis from the stabilized source-facing and specialist corpus.
7. Audit synthesis fidelity, independent usability, source boundaries, required AV completion and publication readiness.

A fresh synthesis context receives the full frozen inputs necessary for its responsibility, their hashes, source horizon, active claims, disagreements and evidence-access routes. A compressed handoff summary alone is insufficient. Prefer the [long-series hybrid protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md)'s fresh substantial synthesis and local evidence completion/integration arrangement while selecting actual stages by verified capability and authority.

## Maturity, corrections and authority

The architecture begins at INITIAL and may extend at meaningful admitted boundaries when evidence demonstrates a new responsibility. Extend deliberately instead of prepopulating speculative artifact classes. Backfill existing readings only when the new responsibility exposes a material evidence gap and the source/correction scope permits it.

The corpus map, source register, ledgers, qualification records and architecture remain mutable with preserved identities and transition histories. Accepted episode readings, prospective freezes, observation freezes and acceptance checkpoints become frozen. Amend them only through the [archive and supersession policy](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md), preserving the former state and reason. A later revelation updates current cumulative understanding without contaminating the earlier admitted horizon. New editions retain their own clocks and source IDs.

The continuing branch is a publication workspace. `main` supplies repository analytical authority after authorized integration. A passing structural check, an independent draft or a completed specialist transfer does not by itself promote authority. Global series routing, character discovery and reconstruction indexes retain their separate owners.
