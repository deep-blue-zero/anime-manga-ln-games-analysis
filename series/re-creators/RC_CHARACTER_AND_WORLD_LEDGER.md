# Re:Creators — Character and World Ledger

## Current state

Admitted narrative boundary is **NONE**. There are no character, relationship, world, knowledge or monograph-qualification records. This file initializes their canonical responsibilities and record contracts without asserting narrative facts.

Evidence and interpretation identities live in the [evidence and claim ledger](RC_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md). This ledger stores their cumulative state consequences. The full local argument remains in the accepted episode reading. Future records must distinguish direct observation, a character's assertion, supported inference, extrapolation and unknown information.

## Stable identities and update rule

Assign `RC-CHAR-####` to a source-supported character identity and `RC-REL-####` to a directed relationship. A stable internal ID may precede a publicly disclosed name. Record names/aliases by the horizon at which they are known, and preserve unresolved identity without leaking later knowledge. Establishing an identity later does not retroactively license that name in a frozen earlier reading.

State changes use `RC-STATE-####`; knowledge/disclosure events use `RC-KNOW-####`; narrative-level/world constraints use `RC-WORLD-####`. Never recycle an ID. Every record names its applicable episode boundary, supporting evidence/claim IDs, previous state or superseded record, current formulation, contrary evidence, uncertainty and accepted change reason. Retain history and update only affected records.

## Character state contract

A character record identifies the person, current circumstances and disclosed background. It separates in-universe event order from disclosure order, and separates a person's knowledge from audience knowledge. Preserve motives and values with their inferential basis, problem-solving and attention patterns, recurring behavior and exceptions, performed/linguistic voice, ordinary habits and preferences, abilities and constraints, and meaningful developmental changes. Identify evidence that would distinguish competing motive explanations.

Record intimate or sexual information only where evidenced. A lack of evidence is unknown, not a negative trait or absence of intimacy. A behavioral operational profile is conditional on circumstances and observed limits, never a deterministic simulation claim. Source evidence and confidence may differ among dimensions of the same portrait.

For a material state transition preserve the before state, triggering action/disclosure, mechanism of change, after state, evidence and plausible competing interpretation. Later information can revise current understanding while leaving the historically warranted interpretation recoverable.

## Directed relationship contract

Each relationship record fixes subject → counterpart, boundary and type of supported relation. Capture subject knowledge/belief about the counterpart, trust or mistrust, obligations, dependence, care, fear, resentment, power, cooperation or conflict only as evidenced. Include the encounter or action that changes the relation and its practical consequences. A reciprocal relation requires its own direction or explicitly differentiated reciprocal fields.

Creator/creation relations need particular care. Record which authority, responsibility or dependency a participant claims, what action demonstrates its actual reach, what the other participant recognizes or resists, and which interpretation remains open. Do not collapse an authored origin, social relationship and effective control into one fact.

## Narrative levels, world constraints and knowledge

A world record distinguishes a source-supported level or domain, the participants' descriptions of it, demonstrated operating constraints and apparent exceptions. Changes in conditions require a before/after account. For a proposed cross-level cause, link the initiating event to the result and identify missing steps or alternatives. Keep claims about audience belief and fictional autonomy tied to the source's actual evidence.

Knowledge records name the fact or belief, who learns/asserts/misunderstands it, the acquisition event, audience disclosure point, reliable versus disputed status and affected choices. Private character knowledge, public claims and viewer inference remain separate. An event's fictional date does not determine when a reading may use it.

## Character qualification register

No character has yet been assessed. At meaningful admitted boundaries, register each materially differentiated candidate, including secondary, adult and antagonistic characters. Use the complete obligation in the [architecture](RC_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md), not a fixed protagonist list.

Each future assessment records:

- Stable character identity and known name at the assessment horizon.
- Admitted source boundary and assessment date.
- Outcome `QUALIFIED_PENDING`, `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` or `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION`.
- Evidence-based reason, dimension coverage, greatest gaps and the next reassessment trigger.
- Eventual actual monograph path, once a real portrait exists.
- Separate independent findings for self-contained usability and source fidelity, with objections and dispositions.
- Any later revision that invalidates the qualification or fidelity result.

A qualified character is an outstanding production obligation until an accepted independent portrait meets the current boundary's requirements. Ledger entries and cross-links alone never discharge it. Discovery registry entries and reconstruction capability assessments have different owners and do not substitute for this review.

## Acceptance and retention

An accepted unit checkpoint identifies all additions and changes here, their evidence/claim IDs, independent objections and dispositions. Preserve materially distinct interpretations and source gaps in linked claim records. Before accepting state deltas, the reviewer verifies that the alleged change occurred, that the actor had the attributed knowledge, that the causal mechanism is supported and that significant contrary behavior was retained. The ledger stays mutable but never erases a former state without a recoverable transition.
