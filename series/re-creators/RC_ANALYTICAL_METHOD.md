# Re:Creators — Analytical Method

## Governing method family and scope

The [generic anime method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_ANIME_EPISODE_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) supplies the governing analytical responsibilities. The [continuous audiovisual method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CONTINUOUS_AUDIOVISUAL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) supplies the AV companion. The series-specific [execution and reconciliation plan](RC_AV_EXECUTION_AND_RECONCILIATION_PLAN.md) implements the [reconciliation protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) and preserves the [medium/lane policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md). Together these documents form the declared method family.

All procedures below are future requirements. No character identity, event, theme, source assertion or interpretation has been admitted. The task's interests in creators, creations, authorship and narrative levels are questions to investigate, not findings or a predetermined thesis.

## Episode order and opening synopsis

Each future reading identifies its source ID, edition, episode label, admitted horizon, inspection lanes and limitations. Establish the entering character/world state and freeze prospective expectations before exposing the analysts to the current episode. Keep that freeze historically intact even if the episode immediately disproves it.

After those short identity and entering-state sections, begin the human-facing reading with a substantial **current-episode story synopsis**, before extended diagnostic or thematic analysis. Finalize it only after all applicable prospective captures are frozen. Narrate the episode's actual sequence in readable paragraphs. Identify who acts, what the characters are trying to accomplish, the circumstances and obstacles, consequential choices and encounters, causal turns, disclosures and the ending situation. The synopsis should let a returning reader recover what happens without reconstructing it from claim IDs or a motif list. Provide scene/interval locators for material events. Distinguish shown events from a character's account, a hypothetical scenario, apparent events or an unresolved identity. Describe the episode's outcome only through its exit boundary.

The synopsis does not replace detailed source-facing analysis. Retain the observations, alternatives and causal steps needed to evaluate each material conclusion. Preserve ordinary behavior, short interactions and apparent inconsistencies when they qualify an attractive main thesis. A later episode's explanation belongs in a later revision record, never in the original episode synopsis as though it was already known.

## Source-facing passes

The principal makes a complete source-facing structural pass through the authorized episode in its declared inspection lane. When the execution plan's per-unit evidence justification activates the AV-primary mirror, this includes an uninterrupted continuous-AV pass followed by focused reinspection of decisive scenes. Otherwise the bundle-mediated pass uses targeted AV inspection for localized questions. Before interpretation, record an interval-based event and performance log distinguishing directly observed image/sound, dialogue testimony and provisional inference. Track scene transitions, staging, changes of attention, action and response, temporal discontinuities, music entrances/exits and meaningful silence to the extent the inspected evidence supports them. A frame contact sheet cannot certify uninterrupted observation.

Reinspect load-bearing intervals, verify exact dialogue against primary-language evidence, and cross-check anchors against the canonical bundle clock. The independent specialists inspect their assigned evidence before receiving the principal's interpretation where feasible. Their observation logs and source limitations survive integration. The principal remains responsible for the entire episode's causal and character account even when specialists examine concentrated questions.

## Causality, agency and narrative constraints

For each consequential action, reconstruct the actor's available information, options, reasons, external constraints and actual consequences. Separate what the viewer knows from what each participant could know. Trace how a decision changes another participant's choices. Distinguish opportunity from capacity, intention from success and a rule asserted by a character from a constraint demonstrated by events.

Treat creators and creations as individually evidenced participants. Examine the direction and practical limits of authority, dependence, responsibility and autonomy. Do not infer an automatic psychological identity between a creator and a creation, or assume authorship gives unlimited control. When narrative levels or worlds are distinguished by the source, record each level's local rules, who asserts the boundary, what crosses it and the observation that supports or challenges its operation. An apparent inconsistency may concern incomplete knowledge, a changed condition, deception, a production convention or a genuine unresolved contradiction.

Questions about audience belief, fictional existence and responsibility require a causal account of what the episode establishes. A persuasive speech is evidence of its speaker's rhetoric and commitments. Assess the work's treatment through consequences, competing voices, framing and contrary cases before attributing a philosophical position to the work. Preserve meaningful uncertainty about the relation between a character's theory and the observed world.

## Character and directed relationship analysis

Character analysis begins with particular conduct in context. Record recurring tendencies alongside exceptions, pressure conditions and changes. Infer a motive only after separating expressed reasons, observable incentives, plausible alternatives and evidence that could discriminate among them. Avoid diagnoses, personality shorthand and retrospective inevitability as substitutes for reasoning.

Trace the direction of relationships. A's trust in B and B's trust in A are separate claims. Track knowledge, obligations, dependency, affection, resentment, fear, cooperation, conflict, negotiated roles and changes in how each participant addresses the other when the source supports them. Preserve how an interaction changes a later choice. Shared participation in a scene does not by itself establish relationship development.

Longitudinal records must preserve identity and circumstances, disclosed history, psychology and values, cognition and knowledge, behavior, linguistic and performed voice, ordinary preferences, abilities and limits, relationship asymmetry, development and unresolved evidence. Intimacy or sexuality is included only where evidenced. Quiet or peripheral characters remain eligible for substantial portraits when their evidence is differentiated enough. Future independent monographs must synthesize these responsibilities in readable prose rather than reproducing ledger rows.

Use the character's established name in analytical prose when the admitted source supports it. Keep deliberately hidden or disputed identities hidden at their historical horizon. Generic role descriptions are useful when identity is actually unknown or the role itself matters. Reintroduce the name when the subject changes and repair ambiguous pronouns, especially in creator/creation comparisons. Perform the handoff contract's bounded name-and-reference check on the opening, interior, conclusion, claim-bearing passages and transfers before acceptance.

## Japanese, dialogue and performed voice

Japanese is the intended primary language, but the present audio track's language remains unverified. Confirm it before attributing Japanese wording or performance to this witness. The Chinese subtitle tracks are translation witnesses. Their wording cannot alone establish a Japanese lexical choice, form of address, grammatical subject, omitted pronoun, politeness level or ambiguity.

Preserve small necessary source-language quotations with exact locators, speaker attribution and translation status. Separate literal wording, idiomatic interpretation, subtitle rendering and analytic gloss. If primary-language text is unavailable, a qualified listener must verify any proposed transcription directly against replayable audio and record uncertainty. A listening transcription remains auditory or human observation, never TXT primary-language text. Exact-wording claims requiring TXT stay unresolved until a primary-language text layer is verified. Automated transcription is a draft witness with an error profile, not self-authenticating dialogue.

Inspect register shifts, naming and address, hesitation, interruptions, repetition, timing, silence, breath, pace, intensity and interactional response. Do not convert acoustic features directly into emotion without contextual evidence and alternatives. Voice-actor identity or character identity cannot be established through uncorroborated voice recognition.

## Image, motion, editing and sound

Study framing, relative scale and position, camera movement, gaze, gesture, blocking, animation timing, transitions, shot duration, sound placement and music in their local sequence. Consider how duration and rhythm change the force of a line or action. Distinguish represented space from a diagram inferred from selected shots, and recurring composition from a motif with demonstrated narrative function.

A static frame supports a visible configuration at its own timestamp. A dialogue transcript supports text, subject to verification. Continuous video supports temporal and performed relations only within the actual sampled capability. Audio-only replay can verify timing or acoustics but not the image/sound relation by itself. Do not infer continuity, off-screen action, fine gesture onset or synchronization from sparse samples.

For any important formal claim, record the relevant interval, what was directly perceived, how it changes the interpretation and at least one plausible alternative such as production convention, economical staging or ordinary continuity. A repeated motif needs occurrences and contrasts, not visual resemblance alone. Openings, endings, previews and recaps are identifiable components with their own spoiler and analytical status. Do not skip or admit them automatically.

## Evidence, alternatives and output obligations

Separate observation, translated text, source-language interpretation, character assertion, inference and speculation in prose and ledger records. Every major episode thesis tests at least two plausible alternatives where the evidence permits. State what would favor each and the strongest adverse case. If the evidence cannot discriminate, preserve the alternatives as unresolved. Do not manufacture equally plausible rivals or average incompatible findings into vague consensus.

The episode body must include causal reconstruction, significant character and relationship changes, knowledge/disclosure changes, narrative constraint tests, audiovisual and dialogue evidence, counterexamples and a bounded provisional synthesis. Use the [ledgers](RC_EVIDENCE_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md) to retain exact evidence and claim histories. Link downstream state changes to their evidence. Conclude with the episode's actual exit state, adjudicated incoming forecasts, newly frozen prospective questions, remaining uncertainties and required reinspection.

Acceptance requires independent source-aware specialist findings and independent critical examination of decisive evidence, explicit reconciliation dispositions, required-channel completion and an atomic reading/ledger/checkpoint transaction. No number of objections or agreement among model providers establishes quality. Meaningful analytical improvement is demonstrated by corrected evidence, recovered distinctions, better tested explanations and surviving source-supported findings.
