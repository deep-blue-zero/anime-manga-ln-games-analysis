# Re:Creators — AV Execution and Reconciliation Plan

## Project-specific lane decision

The [lane policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md) retains bundle-mediated anime as the default. The [reconciliation protocol §1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) recognizes the reverse relay for AV-primary sources, including AV-first character studies. This project adopts that mirror route for declared AV-primary responsibilities in performed character, temporal form and sound-image analysis. The owner's intended continuous-AV principal motivates the assignment. It does not create a corpus-wide rule or verify a runtime's capabilities.

Before each authorized unit, declare its evidence needs and inspection plan. Whole-episode continuous AV is justified when the required question depends on the episode's temporal form or dispersed performed patterns that cannot be localized adequately beforehand. Record that reason and the required complete-episode inspection responsibility. Use targeted intervals when the evidential question can be localized. Do not invoke full-episode escalation merely because a video file or a favored model is available. If the unit does not justify AV-primary whole-episode inspection, use the governing bundle-mediated lane with targeted AV verification and record the actual role/capability assignment. No route may omit the complete episode's governing anime, causal, language, character and evidence responsibilities.

For a unit that meets the mirror conditions, the relay is **continuous-AV principal → independent bundle/text/source verification → accountable integration**, with independent critical review. The author performs the entire justified continuous pass and focused reinspection. The bundle remains the canonical citation coordinate system and preserves exact dialogue, frame and audio verification. A provisional AV draft cannot reach acceptance before its required bundle/text checks. The mirror changes stage order and evidential emphasis, not source authority, admission or claim standards.

## Preparation and evidence packets

A run record declares authorized unit range, entering and terminal boundaries, actual roles, known prior exposure, required versus optional channels and the evidence route for each material responsibility. Record VIDEO_TARGETED_ESCALATION or VIDEO_FULL_EPISODE_ESCALATION with its evidence justification where applicable. Freeze the entering state and forecasts. Keep later episodes, reference wikis and retrospective plot material outside the analysts' admissible scope. Record prior familiarity or accidental exposure promptly. Reassign a prospective responsibility when contamination makes it unreliable, and preserve the affected finding's limitation.

Provide independent reviewers a neutral observation packet first where feasible. It includes exact source/derivative IDs, hashes, durations, stream selection, relevant intervals, neutral questions and known technical limitations. Exclude the principal's interpretation and desired answer. Freeze the source-facing observation log before sharing the adjudication packet with the claim queue and principal's argument. Record `blind_inspection: true` only for actual withheld-interpretation inspection; otherwise record false and the exposure. A neutral packet can identify a diagnostic interval without disguising an already supplied conclusion.

Every substantial responsibility receives the source context it needs. Specialists and critical reviewers inspect decisive evidence directly, including surrounding context necessary to test an objection. A summary or selected screenshot cannot stand in for unavailable performance or source-language evidence.

## Clock and capability calibration

Record exact parent/derivative hashes, complete duration, component cuts, video/audio streams, time bases and start offsets. Use at least three shared beginning/middle/end anchors for a full-episode crosswalk. Prefer exact cue boundaries and corroborated AV events, then waveform or frame/PTS checks when needed. Register offsets, drift, residual error, excluded regions and interval-specific uncertainty. A consistent offset does not establish absence of drift.

The default navigation tolerance is 0.5 seconds. Claims about shorter timing require proportionately finer calibration and a verified effective sampling rate. Record actual native video perception and effective visual sample interval; use UNKNOWN when undisclosed. Unknown or coarse sampling cannot establish a brief gesture, exact onset or disputed sound-image synchrony. Route fast motion and fine synchronization to higher-resolution targeted video, frame/PTS analysis, audio waveform measurement or competent human inspection. Preserve what each route actually supports.

A load-bearing observation becomes ANCHORED only when every supporting observation has a calibrated interval and cue/frame anchor, has been re-viewed, lies within effective resolution and has required corroboration. Otherwise it remains REPORTED. Native source frame-rate metadata is not the model's effective sampling rate.

The private bundle must satisfy the [semantic specification](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md), including source metadata, clean timestamped frames and indexes, primary-language text, supplemental translation status, dialogue/frame mapping, complete audio and extraction QA. A contact sheet, subtitle file, clip or audio derivative remains a partial derivative. Verify current transport limits before sending any object. Register permitted conversions with hashes and exact retained coverage. This foundation conducts no uploads, conversions or model benchmarks.

## Adjudication and bounded reinspection

After the observation freeze, compare each queued claim with the independent evidence. Record PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT or OPEN, including observation IDs, calibration status, reasoning, proposed bounded formulation and affected artifacts. Retain useful new findings separately before deciding their integration. Preserve every material independent distinction, including objections that do not change the final thesis.

An anchored contrary observation warrants a source-based response. Reinspect the decisive interval, examine alternative explanations and either accept the correction or give an evidence-backed rebuttal. Disagreement alone never requires replacing a supported reading. Use one bounded return round. If a material observation remains unsettled, permit one independent verification through a fresh blind AV context, higher-resolution route, deterministic measurement or competent human viewing. Continued disagreement remains OPEN with all evidence routes recorded. Do not keep polling models until they agree.

Required unresolved evidence or material disputes block the dependent completion claim. Optional unresolved details remain explicitly provisional and cannot carry a settled conclusion. Scope-limited acceptance must identify what was and was not established rather than silently reclassifying a required channel as optional.

## Acceptance record and handoff

The future unit checkpoint preserves the actual role/capability/exposure record, observation freeze, independent findings, critical objections, principal replies, reinspection results, every transition and new finding, open dependencies, required-channel completion, changed ledger IDs and exact accepted artifact receipts. Full private inputs/outputs remain retrievable within the authorized environment. The public analytical record must independently preserve all meaningful findings, evidence distinctions, rival interpretations and final dispositions; a short agreement summary is insufficient.

The critical reviewer checks source accuracy, causal completeness, mistaken state changes, chronology leakage, absent significant behavior, Japanese/performance overreach, unsupported philosophical attribution and loss of analytical detail during integration. Before semantic acceptance, apply the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md), including its bounded named-subject/reference review. Record substantive improvement and surviving uncertainty, not an objection-count score.

Accept a reading only with its coupled ledger updates, completed required review and consistent checkpoint. Freeze accepted reading/prospective records, then update the mutable corpus map through the authorized transaction. Fresh-context synthesis receives complete relevant frozen inputs and source routes, not only condensed briefing prose. Later substantive syntheses and monographs require their own independent review; an earlier episode review does not certify them.
