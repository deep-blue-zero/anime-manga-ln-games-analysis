## 2. Central model: repertoire expands while difference remains

**Decision: PRESERVE and STRENGTHEN as a longitudinal working model; REJECT conversion into one ideal camper.**

The best new test is Ema, because she enters with uncertain interest and a separate art/library/work trajectory. V16 does not simply place a junior beside experts: she checks a cold destination against a coworker's experience, helps revise shelter, handles transport while the seniors are sleepy, and finds interest in railway material through drawing and visual analogy. V17 adds actual camp experience, cooking pleasure, dawn drawing, warm equipped lodging, and a cost-qualified request to repeat. Those are genuine additions to her experienced repertoire. They are not merely the reader's prediction that she will enjoy camping. [E16-04–E16-10; E17-03–E17-14; CS43–44, CS55–57.]

The strongest rival is social assimilation: Ema enjoys it because she is being absorbed into the seniors' preferred identity. That reading fits the shared trip superficially, but loses several specific distinctions. She remains the person who draws while Nadeshiko walks and Ena sleeps or works on paracord. Her tent-pitching competence comes from work, her chair omission needs another person's equipment, and her repeat request names expense. Nothing shows club enrollment, cessation of art, or universal camping expertise. At s123 her private question about whether camping fits her is an active inquiry; the later request supplies one answer about this experience, not a permanent total identity.

The same model survives in established subjects with less dramatic novelty. Nadeshiko's hospitality includes not knowing how to adapt a regional food, receiving others' suggestions, and learning Aoi's curry variation. Ena combines initiating, teaching, lending and making with chosen sleep. Rin initiates shared riding, accepts food and technique, reconsiders a license, and still has explicit positive solo preference in the earlier spring material. Ayano is simultaneously eager for roads, capable of food provision, occasionally reluctant to walk, and willing to negotiate a gentler route. Repertoire expansion does not mean every capacity improves or every desire is fulfilled.

This is an IN organized from multiple DE clusters, not an unrestricted law of the manga. A future refusal, withdrawal, boredom, or durable loss would have to be recorded on its own terms. The model must not become unfalsifiable by calling any outcome “expansion.” The blocked viewpoints and final ranch gate in V18 are real non-accomplishments, even when the day also contains pleasure.

These routes should not be collapsed into a single “new generation joins” story. Ema's messages with Mei in V17 sustain their own peer relation while they travel with different people; Mei is not thereby co-present on the Gunma trip. [E17-01; RS49.]

The seniors depend on her awake attention during transit; her destination concern contributes to changing accommodation; she urges the actual taxi attempt; her workplace tent practice is useful; she reads the sign that corrects Nadeshiko's conjecture; her drawing constitutes a legitimate parallel camp activity. These episodes strengthen adaptive social practice. They do **not** demonstrate a new club rule, a changed administrative procedure, or transfer of institutional leadership. An ad hoc mixed-age holiday is a social test of the hypothesis, not automatically a formal institutional test. [E16-05–06; E17-03–04, E17-09, E17-11; RS35–36, RS50, RS54.]

Mei's evidence remains bounded to the earlier accepted state plus new remote contact. The specific V12 firewood handoff remains unestablished. Campfires, recipes, borrowed tents, Ema's work, or grandfather's mesh do not complete that object-specific line. ## 4. Knowledge circulation: plural directions, uneven receipt

**Decision: STRENGTHEN distribution; REVISE any implication of automatic reliability, equal access, or frictionless transfer.**

Age and club seniority do not consistently determine the direction of useful information. Ema's cold warning relay, taxi encouragement, work-trained pitch, and sign reading affect senior action. Nadeshiko contributes earlier railway experience, food practice and hospitality, but also learns recipes and snow-brightness information. Ena relays Rin's views, supplies her own outdoor explanation, and receives Ema's practical care. Aoi's attributed recipe reaches a trip she does not attend. Ayano's classmate affects the route without being a motorcyclist or co-traveler. Grandfather supplies specific routes, ingredients and advice while missing a crucial communication step. [E16-05–09; E17-03–04, E17-09, E17-12; E18-04–05, E18-08, E18-16.]

Distribution is not an assertion that every contribution is correct. Nadeshiko's fire-water-marker guess is wrong; Ema reads the sign. At V17 s146 Ema points to snow as an additional cause and **Ena** provides the reflectivity explanation. Crediting the entire exchange to Ema would erase an actual division of observation and explanation. The change in Nadeshiko's knowledge does not make her generally ignorant about weather, any more than her food skill makes her omniscient about local dishes. [E17-09, E17-12; CS56, CS58–60; RS55.]

V18 makes timing and receipt especially visible. The mesh is physically received at s077 but its function is still unknown; s113 supplies the practical explanation/use. The closure warning is known to grandfather, omitted during the visit, emailed late, and missed by Rin until the barrier. Information existing somewhere in a network is not the same as information available in a rider's decision at the relevant time. A late apology is another event, not proof the earlier warning worked. [E18-08, E18-12, E18-16–17; CM91, CM94, CM97; CR38 correction overlay.]

Private memory also does not become shared knowledge. Grandfather's recollection of Saki's ban on showing the photographs is reader-accessible; the pair receive a secrecy request, not demonstrably the exact prior prohibition. His later thought that difficulty becomes a travel memory is not the riders' endorsement or narrator judgment. Ayano's report about hidden persimmon in the lunch supplies Rin with information she did not detect by taste. [E18-07, E18-16–17; RS64, RS67.]

The bonus scenes offer a deliberately adverse case for a benign “everyone teaches everyone” account. Aoi fabricates a frightening institutional explanation, later admits it, and Toba supplies a plain answer. In another gag Aoi invents an etymology and Chiaki assists; Ena recognizes incorrect history and Rin assents, but the observers' awareness is not proof that Nadeshiko has heard a correction. Familiarity permits playful misinformation as well as useful teaching. This is not evidence that all Aoi instruction is false; it is evidence against treating every confident utterance as reliable transmitted knowledge. [E18-19–20; CS71–72; RS69–70; JV56–58.]

## 5. Competence is domain-, task-, and occasion-specific

**Decision: STRENGTHEN situated competence; REJECT stable expert/novice rank as a sufficient model.**

Nadeshiko researches an elaborate route in V16 but misses the usable once-daily bus. The remedy includes dropping the original rush, not merely executing her first plan more energetically. In V17 she mistakes which deadline requires arrival at camp; she later performs the taxi call, while Ema's encouragement and a nearby available vehicle help make it work. She can make a good meal and a wrong marker conjecture in the same trip. Her V16 spring bicycle error likewise coexists with stamina, scenic pleasure, and a later recognition that she needs more map checks. [E16-03, E16-12; E17-03, E17-09; CS41, CS47, CS59.]

Ema pitches quickly because she has practiced at work, yet forgets the chair she tried. Ena's loan is materially necessary to this comfortable arrangement. A practiced assembly task does not certify complete packing competence; one omission does not invalidate the practiced skill. Her interpretive curiosity at a railway museum does not require existing train expertise, and liking an activity does not establish command of its history. [E16-08; E17-04; CM45, CM65–66.]

Rin's research pleasure and practical planning remain actual, but road access defeats her information state. Her grandfather's knowledge is useful and incomplete in execution. At s157 Rin's sunny-day rockfall reassurance ends in an expedient proposal to proceed. That speech is a character's qualified rationalization under time and terrain pressure, not established geological knowledge. Ayano questions it. Treating every hedge as good uncertainty management would make the competence claim immune to failure. Here uncertainty is expressed, but adequate safety knowledge is not demonstrated. [E18-01, E18-16, E18-18; CS67; CM97–98, CM101; JV55.]

Ayano cannot be reduced to the reckless opposite of a reliable Rin. She suggests easier151 after the pleasant moderate ride, later renews the152 mission, declines one distant walk but undertakes the Shimoguri walk, and proposes the optional pond. Rin herself accepts the extra time because she is interested. Different preferences, fatigue, vehicles, information and momentary goals explain the variation better than a fixed planner/improviser binary. The route discussions support character difference without proving deterministic traits. [E18-10–11, E18-14–15, E18-18; CS68; RS59, RS68.]

No new cycling performance licenses an Aoi upgrade. The earlier domain limit survives without a new test. Nor may grandfather's mechanical history make him a universal route authority, or a nonmotorcyclist classmate's road interest imply a motorcycle qualification. Each competence statement needs its own observed task, source of knowledge, result, and limit.

## 6. Supported autonomy and recoverability under a harder test

**Decision: PRESERVE supported autonomy; STRENGTHEN its mechanisms; NARROW recoverability to contingent, partial recovery.**

Support does allow more ambitious self-directed activity. Ema's cold and gear concern is accommodated by a bungalow rather than treated as a failure to be a proper camper. Family members facilitate the first stage of transport; Ema then takes active responsibility in transfers and taxi use. Ena's chair loan and Ema's later bedding care solve different needs without reducing one person to permanent provider. Rin and Ayano accept food, equipment, family advice, facilities and remote route knowledge while retaining choices. [E16-05–06; E17-04, E17-11; E18-05–08; RS35–36, RS51, RS62.]

The adverse cases restrict the earlier word “recoverability.” V17's taxi is not a guaranteed social skill outcome. There is a nearby available car, an expense, an actual call, and local workers who help reception. Without those conditions the original schedule may fail. V18's closed Inabu viewpoint does not reopen because an alternative view exists. The first suggested road workaround fails because both vehicles fall under the depicted restriction; a second route is closed; the pair actually backtrack about60km. By s158 the newly desired destination is again behind a gate. [E17-03; E18-05, E18-16, E18-18; CM64, CM89, CM98.]

The defensible claim is therefore that support and adaptation can preserve a workable activity or permit a revised goal. They do not guarantee access, restore time, eliminate cost, or complete the original objective. Rin's qualified satisfaction with the detour meal at s147 is a local positive evaluation, not an assertion that every obstacle was necessary or retrospectively justified. A trip may contain successful lunch, actual laundry, a costly backtrack and an unreached pond simultaneously.

Care without takeover also remains qualified. The earlier accepted paternalism counterevidence is not erased. Within this tranche the album disclosure occurs despite Saki's private prohibition; it is a favor to Rin at another person's privacy expense. The absent mother has not given present consent. Grandfather's omission and late apology also complicate an idealized reliable-support model. His generosity and fallibility are both evidence. The manga's low-conflict handling cannot by itself settle the ethics of the disclosure or establish that everyone approves.

“Safe alternatives” is consequently an analytical question, not a blanket certification of the final route. Bungalow choice and actual public services are concrete alternatives; rough-road reassurance remains unverified, and the narrative stops before the intended pond. The framework should describe who can stop, pay, borrow, revise or ask, while retaining cases where an option is unavailable.

## 7. Difficulty, refusal, and social non-escalation

**Decision: PRESERVE a strong low-shame regulation pattern; keep difficulty-without-ordeal a qualified secondary inference.**

The seniors can shorten a route, choose a paid shortcut, accept borrowed gear, and stay in an equipped bungalow without an identity-level accusation. Ena's explanation that she prefers a mat makes her chair offer intelligible as preference and availability rather than mandatory sacrifice. Nadeshiko can ask for night-toilet company and also choose an independent daytime walk during the same stay. Ayano can decline one walk or propose151 without being expelled from the shared journey. Rin can postpone a bath or voice time concern about the pond. These are differentiated refusals and accommodations, not interchangeable evidence of unconditional agreement. [E16-03, E16-06; E17-04, E17-10–11; E18-02, E18-10–11, E18-18.]

The pattern does not mean that there is no anxiety, annoyance, pressure, or risk. Nadeshiko is alarmed by the deadline mistake. Ema politely declines a chair before accepting the reiterated offer. Ayano presses the license idea; Rin's answer remains consideration. Grandfather apologizes after an actual information failure. Quiet presentation must not be rewritten as universal explicit consent, perfect equality or absence of consequences.

The new rival to “difficulty without ordeal” is the characters' actual appetite for interesting difficulty. Ayano seeks winding and rougher roads; Rin accepts an inconvenient detour partly because they came this far; grandfather privately frames trouble as memorable. The record therefore supports neither compulsory endurance nor an anti-difficulty philosophy. It supports heterogeneous valuation of effort, rest and curiosity. Acceptance of challenge can coexist with practical accommodation, and a private rationalization cannot serve as a universal moral of the work.

Low-conflict comedy may also expose a problem without resolving it. The final closed gate is funny and obstructive. False history is funny and false. The reader is not obliged to convert either into a frictionless social or practical success. **Non-escalation is a mode of handling friction, not proof that friction never matters.**

## 8. Solitude, companionship, and the scale of a shared activity

**Decision: PRESERVE chosen solitude and configuration flexibility; no new unrestricted modular-travel promotion.**

The earlier spring special gives direct positive solitude evidence: Rin enjoys her lakeside/forest attention before Nadeshiko arrives and imagines a quiet solo-use campsite. She also accepts the shared camp and contributes to dessert. Solitude is not represented only as loneliness waiting for a cure. Because the special is chronologically earlier, it should be filed as another dated expression of a compatible preference, not a post-Golden-Week return or reversal. [E16-11, E16-13–14; CS46; RS38–39.]

V17 supports a more local coexistence within company. Nadeshiko walks, Ema draws, and Ena rests or makes things. A selfie reconnects them, but not every moment is jointly performed. Nadeshiko's chosen walk does not prove a new solo overnight, and her nighttime fear does not invalidate daytime autonomy. Ema's artwork is actual separately from the dream walks. [E17-08, E17-10–11, E17-13; RS54.]

V18 demonstrates chosen shared riding rather than the cure of Rin's supposed isolation. Adjacent separate tents, negotiated rest, different bodily strain and different tastes remain visible. Her private inference that Ayano looks happiest riding is an affectionate observation, not unrestricted access to Ayano's whole interior. Their actual two-person journey has prior direct foundation in V10–V11; it is not a first-ever Rin–Ayano connection generated by this volume.

“Modular group travel” must retain a narrower meaning than configuration flexibility. The strongest candidate here is the V16 spring bicycle/scooter arrival with differentiated experiences before the shared camp. It supports meaningful separate experience and flexible assembly. It does not automatically establish another full case of the earlier intentionally differentiated parallel travel architecture. The V17 afternoon activity split and V18 separate approaches likewise should not be counted merely because people are apart. **Preserve the established narrow model; do not inflate recurrence with every separation.**

## 9. Work, money, objects, and material access

**Decision: STRENGTHEN materially situated leisure; keep each constraint and provision actor-specific.**

V16 gives Rin an actual employer context: the bookshop's holiday closure, explained through Takeda's plans, frees time. That does not retrospectively make the V05 liquor-store schedule hers. Ema's workplace practice supplies a transferable tent skill; its transfer is specific rather than proof that employment generates general camping mastery. These are two distinct links between work and leisure. [E16-01–02; E17-04; current E05-04/ER-003.]

The Gunma trip depends on transport schedules, fare choices, hired transport, borrowed equipment and shelter. Ema's V16 thought about asking for money is not yet an actual loan; V17 supplies the later maternal borrowing report and acknowledges high cost. The final request for nearer future camping is a resource-qualified intention, not proof that cost has disappeared or that the next trip occurred. [E16-05–06; E17-03, E17-09, E17-14; CM39–41, CM64, CM71, CM81.]

V18 keeps desire distinct from acquisition. Rin admires a compact tent priced around60,000yen without buying it. Saki's motorcycle stored at grandfather's house is offered conditionally on a future license, not transferred. Ayano's savings plan and wish for a larger bike are future-oriented; Rin's reply is license consideration. There is no witnessed cash payment to infer where only a stated campsite fee is shown. Equipment, labor, gifts, prices and aspirations should never be flattened into a general wealth profile. [E18-02, E18-07, E18-13; CM83, CM90; RS64, RS66; CR38.]

Material practice also creates form. Ayano's independently pitched tent lets both campers rest; the second site's distant facilities remain a real preference cost; rice consumption prevents the planned omurice finish and prompts a different egg finish. The gifted mesh is initially opaque and later useful. Laundry consumes time but can overlap lunch. These are observed task consequences rather than decorative inventories. [E18-11–12, E18-17; CM93–95, CM99.]

The rival claim that access is effortlessly universal fails. Desired equipment is unaffordable now, distant repetition is expensive, local service availability is contingent, and roads are closed. The opposite claim that cost excludes these people from all leisure also fails: the depicted solutions are borrowing, altered distance, shared resources and different choices. The record demonstrates shaped access, not a complete account of household finances or a general class thesis.

## 10. Family and weak ties without a composite caregiver

**Decision: STRENGTHEN distinct provision and information routes; preserve “without destiny” and add consent/receipt limits.**

Admitted V05/V08 complements sharpen the baseline without becoming new tranche recurrence. V05 mother relays a frozen-road warning and changed plan; grandfather actually transports Rin and the scooter. Nadeshiko mediates her father's eel funding; grandmother hosts; Ayano brings snacks and recognizes Rin from photographs. V08 father assigns a carrier task and takes USB work; grandfather diagnoses the charging issue; mother offers a qualified gift explanation; Rin performs the key test. No single “supportive family” should be credited with every hand, purchase, diagnosis or motive. [E05-05–07; E08-05–08; RS41–48.]

V18 builds on the V08 distinction between learning that mother rode and actually seeing the photographs. Mother suggests asking grandfather about the remembered viewpoint but does not know its exact location herself. Grandfather supplies history, route advice, food and possible future machine access; Rin chooses to visit with a souvenir and obtains Ayano's agreement. The source does not portray family as authors of the whole trip. [E18-01, E18-05, E18-07–08; RS58, RS62, RS64.]

The album brings previously deferred material into Rin's actual experience. It does not make riding a hereditary destiny, establish current maternal riding skill, or show permission to use the motorcycle. The photo prohibition and late warning also prevent generosity from becoming a complete reliability or consent model. The grandfather's own pleasure, regret and rationalization are his, not Rin's automatically matching interior.

Weak ties remain practical infrastructure: coworker's cold report, town-hall/campsite concern, an available taxi, an unnamed classmate's road knowledge, and a neighbor's temporarily sheltered dog. They differ in intimacy, obligation and knowledge. The dog is not grandfather's new permanent pet; Rin's desired touch is not achieved, and an8× video or later photographs are substitutes with their own limits. The dog emerging only after departure is reader knowledge, not a secret accomplishment for Rin. [E16-05; E17-03; E18-04, E18-06, E18-10, E18-17; RS63.]

## 11. Place, mobility, season, and geographical evidence layers

**Decision: STRENGTHEN place as a causal, bodily and formal system. No external geography promotion.**

The Gunma route cannot be described accurately by a list of attractions. A once-daily bus changes the V16 itinerary; gradients and lighter loads affect the walk; a museum gives Ema an unanticipated visual domain; deadlines and taxi availability affect camp arrival; ropeway plus a brief **walk** lead to the summit. The three-minute description is not the ropeway ride's duration. ## 14. Visual form, focalization, comedy, and environmental attention

**Decision: STRENGTHEN ordinary-life narrative sufficiency; preserve the difference between formal effectiveness and normative flourishing.**

The chief actions still turn on ordinary procedures. Reading a timetable, waiting, choosing a meal, pitching, getting tired, asking for help, borrowing a chair, finding a toilet companion, doing laundry, or discovering a barrier can organize a substantial sequence. The work does not need an exceptional catastrophe or identity conversion to make those actions causally and aesthetically sufficient. That is a claim about narrative organization. It does not yet prove that this way of life is universally available, morally superior, or a comprehensive theory of a good life.

V16's illustrated itinerary builds expectation, then the timetable correction reorganizes it. The reduced walk and museum are not merely consolation named in dialogue: they receive their own attention, questions, comedy and drawing.
