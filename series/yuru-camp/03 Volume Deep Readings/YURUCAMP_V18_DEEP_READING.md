## 3. Pass B — character baselines and bounded deltas

### Rin: enacted companionship, incomplete upgrading and fallible judgment

The strongest event delta is modest but decisive: the proposed ride with Ayano is now actually happening. Rin approaches alone, camps beside her friend, visits family with her and shares the next day's riding. This expands an existing repertoire of solitude, remote connection and chosen company; it does not establish that solo camping was deficient. Her own tent, equipment, fatigue, curiosity and decisions remain identifiable throughout the dyad. The small reunion and later separated tents allow friendship without requiring continuous fused activity. (`E18-02`, `E18-04`.)

Her planning preference has both effective and contrary expressions. She anticipates laundry, wants recommendations before departure because shops may be closed, considers distances, remembers family help, chooses a site, adjusts meal timing and makes washing productive during lunch. Yet she misses an email, is not prepared for the tunnel's legal restriction, remains attached to152 when Ayano offers151, and agrees to the pond deviation despite time pressure. The final rocky-road reassurance exposes the gap between wanting a manageable risk and knowing it is manageable. This does not cancel her experience. It narrows any model in which “careful planner” would predict uniformly conservative choices. (`E18-01`, `E18-11`, `E18-16`–`E18-18`.)

The license discussion also resists an irreversible growth marker. Sitting on Saki's bike feels good, but Rin initially prefers a moped for now. Scooter strain then makes a larger machine attractive; future northern rides reopen the question; her final answer is to consider it. Material difficulty, curiosity, family access and Ayano's invitation all contribute, without yielding an enacted decision. Likewise, desiring a compact tent does not create the money to buy one. Her dog enthusiasm adds specific interior and expressive evidence rather than a generic sentimental side. ## 7. Visual rhythm, environmental attention and gag scope

The volume repeatedly gives procedures time. Setting up camp can occur while a friend reclines; meal planning can delay shopping until almost closing; a laundry sequence grants washing and drying their own panels. Environmental views and maps do not float outside this ordinary time. They measure the relation between a desired route and the work of reaching, feeding and maintaining oneself along it. The title くうねるはしる is enacted through the alternation of riding, eating and lying down, without requiring a thesis that all camping has one purpose.

The frankfurter sequence uses detail and repetition: hot juice interrupts a bite, close chewing repeats, then a large reaction releases the built-up sensation.
