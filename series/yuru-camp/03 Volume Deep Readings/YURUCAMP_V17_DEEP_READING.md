# 1. Orientation after reconstruction

The principal V17 change is Ema's passage from a prepared first trip to a completed experience she asks to repeat. It is not a membership story. She camps with borrowed equipment, briefly enjoys outdoor dinner, tests her own drawing practice in a new time and place, accepts help after forgetting a chair, contributes practical judgments, protects a sleeping senior from the cold and finally asks to go camping together again. The last invitation also preserves a limit: distant trips cost too much to repeat frequently, so the seniors suggest somewhere nearby.

The trip is successful without every plan succeeding. Nadeshiko has researched an unusual registration system but misunderstands where the deadline must be met. Their taxi recovery depends on Ema suggesting a call, Nadeshiko making it, a nearby car being available, and adults communicating concern. The intended pizza restaurant is closed on arrival. A borrowed chair is still at home. Later comfort and enjoyment do not erase these errors or make them necessary stages of growth.

Nor does a shared trip require everyone to use free time alike. At Nozori Nadeshiko goes for a quiet walk, Ema draws, and Ena sleeps. The sequence is particularly useful because Ema is testing whether camping suits her: her answer can develop through a practice she already likes. She does not have to imitate an experienced camper's preferred activity. This strengthens CP15's repertoire model with a newcomer case while leaving the durability of her interest beyond this trip open.

# 2. Pass A — ordered reconstruction

## 2.1 Ch93: a food specialist confronts an unfamiliar local dish

The trio travel from Yokokawa through Takasaki to Gunma-Sōja, then take a bus onward. Nadeshiko and Ena greet arrival with old-man-style sound play; Ema observes their oddness and later tells Mei the seniors are funny. Her live messages report their previous walk, museum and Clubman visit, with photos of the seniors, café and dog. These are images of already experienced V16 stops, not additional visits occurring on the bus. Mei sends her prior Karisaka gratin photo as a hungry-friend tease and attributes the Outclub's promising food to Nadeshiko. Calling her food担当 is casual peer description, not evidence of an official office (s005–009; E17-01).

Nadeshiko's actual worried face interrupts Ema's confidence about dinner. She wants to serve yaki-manju but has never eaten it. The narrator explains its basic steamed-bun/miso-sauce form. A home flashback supplies her father's report that a Gunma acquaintance loves it yet worries outsiders may expect too much from a simple local food. That report is qualified by variation among shops. Modern adaptations sound attractive, but Nadeshiko dislikes beginning by changing a dish whose original she has not tried. Her 「焼きまんじゅうは私にとってまだ想像上の食べ物なんだよ…」 is uncertainty inside a practiced food interest, rather than general culinary incompetence (s010–013; E17-01).

Ema proposes making basic and adapted versions. Nadeshiko sees the safety but feels it compromises the challenge; she wants one arrangement that preserves both. Ena calls the worry excessive. At the supermarket, Ena suggests yakitori from a truck because they have scarcely eaten since breakfast. Ema enjoys it. The imagined train-seat kamameshi meal explains why their earlier seating did not allow it; it does not retroactively complete that meal. Looking at the skewers gives Nadeshiko an idea, and she moves the party into shopping (s014–017; E17-01/02).

The basket reveals a concrete menu before its result: Mizusawa udon, rice, pork, vegetables, takuan, tenkasu, souvenir yaki-manju, powdered potato, meatballs and starch. Ena suggests powdered drinks to reduce carrying bulk. Nadeshiko relaxes after solving her creative problem; Ema privately notices the change. On the bus at 15:45 the seniors assume they can register around 16:00 before a 17:00 limit. Ema anticipates trying borrowed kit. Nadeshiko explains provisional telephone reservation, forms by mail/FAX, and same-day townhall paperwork. She thinks the awkward process may explain available spaces. On arrival she proposes a bath in the adjoining hot spring after registration (s018–024; E17-02).

## 2.2 Ch94: registration error, recovery and first-camp material limits

The clerk corrects their premise: townhall paperwork/payment is followed by campsite check-in, also required by 17:00. The next bus leaves 16:21, reaches its terminus 16:35, and still leaves a steep 1.5-kilometer walk estimated at 28 minutes. Ema first proposes running, but Nadeshiko explains the uphill terrain. Their alarm is comic, including a narrator's sleeping-rough flag; no sleeping rough actually occurs. Ena proposes an emergency ten-minute bath while they wait, with a transition into the next chapter (s025–028/031; E17-03).

The bath explanation compresses changing, washing and concentrated soaking into an absurdly efficient Outclub technique. Aoi and Chiaki's mastery illustration is explanatory comedy, not their presence in Gunma. After the real short bath Ema would like longer. Nadeshiko considers a taxi; Ena doubts availability in a rural Golden Week setting. Ema urges 「ダメ元で電話してみましょう」. Nadeshiko calls, learns a car can come in ten minutes, and explicitly credits Ema's suggestion. A nearby office and waiting car make it lucky. She says she should notify the worried townhall worker; that callback is not shown. The separately confirmed relay is townhall to campsite staff at037. Agency is distributed across suggestion, action, service and contingency (s031–035; E17-03).

They arrive in time. The steep final approach makes the abandoned walking option look unrealistic with luggage. Good-view pitches are already taken, but there is space. Staff have heard from townhall about the walkers from Yamanashi and are relieved they arrived. Check-in is completed; rules prohibit ground fires and allow free wood without overuse. Nadeshiko recalls another campsite changing free wood to a fee after excessive use. This is an actual resource-use discussion, not the identity-specific V12 firewood handoff (s036–038; E17-03).

After the Abt-shirt gag, they divide tasks. Ema quickly assembles the tent because she has done it several times at work. A first camper can have a task-specific skill before the first overnight. The borrowed Shinobi tent is light and expensive, with a 59,000-yen caption; its thin fabric/poles require care and its small size imposes limits. Nadeshiko reports Rin wanted one and might buy it after earning money, not that Rin has bought it. Ema then discovers she left the chair at home after a test setup. Skill and packing failure coexist. Ena offers her own chair, explains that she enjoys reclining on a mat and pillow, and insists Ema use it for her first camp. Ema sits comfortably. An unseen hair arrangement leaves her with rabbit-like ears when Nadeshiko brings food (s039–046; E17-04).

Cold Mizusawa udon produces repeated chewing expressions, then descriptions of texture and sesame richness. Nadeshiko acknowledges the water-disposal inconvenience of cold noodles at camp while choosing a local dish anyway. For the next dish she asks both companions to help. Powdered potato, hot water and starch become cakes; cakes, short negi and meatballs are fried, buns browned separately, then stacked on skewers with the manju sauce and toasted over the fire. It is やきとり風焼きまんじゅう. Rather than changing the bun beyond recognition, she makes adjacent foods share its flavor. The method answers her opening dilemma materially (s047–052; E17-05).

Ema experiences the bun as fluffy/breadlike, likes the mixed textures, and privately enjoys a little salt as it toasts. Her 「ちょっと楽しい」 at the end of the meal is a small actual judgment about this outdoor dinner, recalled alongside middle-school outdoor-school experience. It is not yet the final wish to camp again. Nadeshiko announces one more substantial udon dish; Ena and Ema have already eaten well (s053–054; E17-05).

## 2.3 Ch95: appetite changes a judgment, then a dream changes the evidentiary frame

Two process pages show packaged rice, pork, carrot, cut udon, seasonings, takuan and tenkasu becoming ぴっぴ飯. The narrator calls the use of Mizusawa rather than Sanuki udon a betrayal, since the dish is identified with Sakaide in Kagawa. This is culinary wordplay and geographic food information. Ema recalls unfavorable television framing of the dish and worries about politely eating something Nadeshiko has worked to make. That negative expectation belongs to her remembered media, not an authoritative narrator's verdict (s057–060; E17-06).

Actual chewing revises her expectation: textures, ginger, crispy additions and modest heat work. She asks 「先輩 おかわりしてもいいですか？」. Nadeshiko happily agrees and explains her family's adaptation: firmer/longer noodles, a higher udon-to-rice balance, thicker crisp pork and a little doubanjiang. Her father makes it occasionally. Ema realizes she need not have worried; an imaginary prophet-dog joke praises faith in Nadeshiko. The evidence is local pleasure and a second helping, not a general decision never to doubt a senior (s061–063; E17-06).

Condiment sachets produce another practical conversation. Ena notices the convenience; Nadeshiko explains the weight, waste and hot-weather storage problems of full bottles on a foot journey, compared with a car and cooler. The imagined noodle party is illustrative, not a new gathering. After tea, Ema declines more snack because she is full. Fire and quiet become the scene's center. Nadeshiko likes nighttime fire/smell; Ena reports Rin sometimes finds lighting one bothersome. The preferences coexist. No absent Rin conversion is enacted. Conversation about Nozori's altitude, cold nights and snow fades as Ema grows sleepy (s064–069; E17-06/07).

The next sequence appears to show Ema waking at 04:11, leaving her tent, thinking of drawing, visiting a platform and overhearing two men discuss career/talent and returning home. She later meets a husky-associated worker who explains the spelling gummma as another world; the face becomes frightening. At 080 she wakes in her sleeping bag and explicitly calls it a strange dream. The whole apparent awakening and walk at 070–079 must therefore be classified as dream content. The men's biographies, an asserted fire lesson, the view-platform visit and supernatural geography are not established actual events. The subjectivity and delayed reveal are real formal facts, but the dream cannot be used to populate other characters' knowledge or a real travel itinerary (E17-07).

## 2.4 Ch96: actual art, fuller bathing, Haruna and the cost of movement

The following chapter supplies independent evidence of what the dream cannot: Ema actually sits drawing on her tablet beside the fire before dawn. She has drawn outdoors at parks/riversides in daylight, but this hour and atmosphere are new. She likes it. Nadeshiko greets her. This establishes actual art without depending on the dream's claim about learning to light a fire; the page shows a fire already burning and does not require naming its lighter. At 07:00 they pack, rest at 08:00 and check out around 08:30. Ema looks uneasily at the sign, relieved it is not dream-gummma; Ena asks what is wrong (s083–087; E17-08).

A taxi takes them to Ikaho, where they finally take an unhurried bath. Ema mentions yesterday's ten-minute limit and Nadeshiko acknowledges it apologetically. The text explains local bath waters and the lack of a Nozori hot spring on site; a neighboring bath would require a long drive. Volcano discussion and imagined lakeside-hot-spring development remain conversation/illustration, not newly discovered facilities. They leave because the Haruna bus imposes a connection. Pleasure does not remove the timetable (s088–090; E17-08).

Ropeway views, tiny distant Fuji, Haruna Fuji's regional name, a three-minute walk to the local summit/shrine after the earlier ropeway ascent turn Nadeshiko's proposed Fuji climb into a joke. This is an actual local sightseeing ascent, not climbing the high Mount Fuji. The lake-side pause and discussion of similar slopes lead to an invented Kai/Suruga territorial analogy. Geographic attention and silliness coexist. The bus returns them toward Takasaki for the next rail leg (s091–097; E17-09).

Before the remote site they shop for warming oden, selecting Ena's mochi pouch and Ema's hanpen. They finally buy station bentos and use forward-facing seats with tables. Ema chooses kamameshi, the seniors different boxes; all share rather than requiring everyone to make the same order. They change at Naganohara-Kusatsuguchi to the Nozori bus. Souvenir/alcohol footnotes are narrator material, not purchases or drinking by the minors (s098–103; E17-09).

Ema privately acknowledges substantial transport expense and a small loan from her mother. Reaching the landscape she has repeatedly seen at work makes it seem worthwhile; pizza remains part of the attraction. Then Ena asks about red roadside triangles. Nadeshiko invents a campsite-marker explanation; Ema reads 防火水そう and corrects it, while Nadeshiko humorously defends her story. The meal planner and route researcher can still be wrong about an ordinary sign. Ema can contribute by looking, without becoming a general geographical expert (s104–106; E17-09).

# 4. Character reconstruction increments

## 4.1 Ema: from a possible guest to someone who can ask for repetition

The important sequence is not invitation→automatic conversion. Ema enters with expectations about food, borrowed equipment and a desired landscape. She experiences hunger, timetable stress, forgotten kit and a closed restaurant. She also judges specific pleasures: a first outdoor dinner is a little fun, drawing before dawn is enjoyable, the real lake has a depth unlike its picture, warm bedding works, and actual pizza tastes unlike familiar versions. Only after these experiences does she ask to go again. Her own financial qualification belongs to the same request, not an unrelated afterthought.

Her capacities are temporally specific. Job experience explains tent setup before she has overnight experience. She can suggest checking for a taxi without knowing a car is available. She can read a sign and point out snow’s contribution to night brightness without mastering route research or camping; Ena supplies the explicit reflectivity explanation. Her forgotten chair is important contrary evidence to treating an efficient setup as general first-trip preparedness. She accepts Ena's loan, later supplies a diffuser and covers sleeping Ena. This is participation through several existing abilities and responsive small acts; it is not a sudden expert identity.

Her voice retains a junior/senior distinction. Remote messages to Mei are plain and playful; speech to the seniors uses polite suggestions and requests. Private thoughts can be more blunt, doubtful or speculative. That distribution matters more than turning her into an invariant “quiet girl.” She initiates questions, proposes action and makes the final request. There is no evidence that she has joined the Outclub, promised frequent distant travel, bought equipment, camped alone or abandoned drawing/library interests.

## 4.2 Nadeshiko: creative competence without infallibility

Nadeshiko's unfamiliar-food dilemma reveals an internally discriminating enthusiasm. She wants to offer regional food, worries about adaptation before basic experience, tests a structural answer and explains it through cooking. The resulting meal does not require her companions to share every prior assumption; Ema can arrive doubtful and like it after tasting.
