# Method Amendment 001 — Geography Evidence Layers

Phase-0 source inspection confirmed the presence of two Japanese *Rurubu Yuru Camp△* travel-guide volumes. Because these sources combine real-world travel information, franchise imagery, maps/routes, tourism recommendations, and place-specific practical material, all future place/geography analysis must distinguish three evidence classes:

1. **`MANGA_INTERNAL`** — what the Japanese manga itself depicts, narrates, labels, or formally emphasizes.
2. **`REAL_WORLD_GUIDE_CONTEXT`** — geographic, travel, camping, transport, food, or local-context information supplied by the Rurubu guides or later verified real-world sources.
3. **`FRANCHISE_TOURISM_FRAMING`** — ways the official/franchise-adjacent travel material packages places for fans, pilgrimage, tourism, or adaptation-linked consumption.

No statement from layer 2 or 3 may be silently converted into a claim about layer 1.

For example:

> A campground's real-world scenic importance does not by itself prove that the manga uses it symbolically.

The correct analytical sequence is:

> manga observation → optional real-world/contextual verification → comparison → only then interpretation.

This amendment strengthens the governing method's existing anti-overreading rule and does not otherwise alter its evidence ladder.
