## 5. Current longitudinal freeze through Volume 15

The current checkpoint preserves **repertoire expansion under preserved difference** as the central longitudinal model.

The strongest current refinements are:

### Adaptive inter-cohort continuity

Preferred over a broad `legacy` thesis.

The V12–V15 sequence is:

`future-junior possibility → visible next cohort → actual membership → institution changed by newcomer practice`

Mei joins the Outclub but does not simply inherit an unchanged institution. Her cycling interest changes what the group does. Ema demonstrates the equally important alternative: meaningful participation in the same social/material ecosystem without institutional absorption.

### Distributed, multidirectional knowledge circulation

This supersedes `informal pedagogy` as the broad umbrella term. Knowledge can move through:

- family teaching and provision;
- experienced acquaintances and weak ties;
- teachers;
- retail work;
- observation and imitation;
- peer co-learning;
- trial and revision;
- senior-to-junior explanation;
- and newcomer-to-institution influence.

### Modular group travel

Now a mature but bounded recurrent pattern after Izu, Oigawa, and the V15 cycling/support-car trip.

The term applies only when a single shared trip is deliberately structured through differentiated movement/experience modules with coordination and reconvergence. Ordinary parallel activity does not qualify.

### Supported autonomy and recoverability architecture

`SUPPORTED_AUTONOMY` remains strong.

A strong subclaim is now `RECOVERABILITY_ARCHITECTURE`:

> support can increase the difficulty a participant can responsibly choose when it lowers the cost of rerouting, stopping, or being recovered without predetermining that decision.

The V15 support car is the clearest example so far.

### Difficulty without endurance moralization

A new strong secondary inference. V15 permits strenuous cycling, fatigue, slowing, food stops, bathing, rerouting, and eventual pickup without treating suffering or unaided completion as the proof that the trip was authentic.

### Aoi

Current cycling model: **competent beginner with relative seniority and co-learning capacity**.

Reject `AOI_BICYCLE_EXPERT` and avoid treating school/club seniority as technical mastery.

### Mei

Current model: active newcomer with her own practice preferences and institution-shaping agency. She is not a blank recipient of an older cohort's culture.

### Ema

Current state: non-member participant in the wider social/material ecosystem who develops self-authored curiosity about trying camping. Through V15 she has not joined the Outclub and has not yet been shown camping.

The broader inter-cohort question has been answered without this object-level payoff. No depicted V13–V15 scene shows the V12 firewood being handed to or used by a junior.

Current state: **OPEN, downgraded in importance**.

### Broad `legacy`

Not the preferred current term. It overstates stability and one-way inheritance relative to the observed adaptive process.

### Campsite stewardship

Locally strong evidence exists, especially in V13, but it is not yet a sufficiently recurrent thematic claim.

### Ordinary flourishing

Still `HOLD`. The manga strongly validates ordinary-life narrative sufficiency and varied forms of leisure, but that is not yet identical to a fully articulated normative philosophy of ordinary flourishing.

### Romance / yuri

Unsupported through V15 under the project's evidentiary standard. Relational intensity, familiarity, and aesthetically resonant framing remain insufficient without distinct romantic evidence.
