Confidence concerns the stated bounded proposition, and is separate from verification.

## Relationship model controls

An arrow records this episode’s initiative, help or information flow; it does not imply equal reverse behavior. A bidirectional sign requires both directions in the cited scene(s), not presumed symmetry. Distinguish close friendship, family responsibility, club coordination, weak-tie hospitality and newly formed contact. Romance is unsupported at this boundary, not ruled out for all possible future evidence. Silent comfort is evidenced where the scene supports it; private motives are not transcribed from faces.
