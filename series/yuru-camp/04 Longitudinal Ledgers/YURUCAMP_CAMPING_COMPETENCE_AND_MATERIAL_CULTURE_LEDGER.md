Confidence concerns the stated bounded proposition, and is separate from verification.

## Use constraints

This is an account of depicted knowledge, errors and material relations, not outdoor safety advice. Real-world technical assertions require separate current authoritative verification. Keep transport, cooking, camping, repairs, money, administrative work and interpersonal judgment in separate competence domains. Receiving help, using a pickup, admitting uncertainty and retreating can be competent responses; they do not certify the original plan as sound.
