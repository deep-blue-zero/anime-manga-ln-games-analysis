# Yuru Camp Character State Ledger

V01–V15 records were consolidated retrospectively under [architecture §6.0](../00%20Frameworks/YURUCAMP_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md). V16, V17, V18 and V19 records extend the same ledger from separately recorded complete Japanese-primary prospective readings. Separately dated V05/V08 bounded primary complements were admitted on 2026-10-23; their later admission does not rewrite the original backfill or an earlier prospective state. Stable row IDs follow admission order, not narrative time; this ledger is not a character model. The [reconstruction specification](../00%20Frameworks/YURUCAMP_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md) governs any later modeling. The [current-state map](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) remains the sole first-read route. The V16 main narrative, earlier-spring special and bonus comedy retain their distinct temporal and narrative modes. V17 actual events, mediated reports, private thoughts, dreams, retrospective memories, Heya comedy and anniversary metafiction are likewise kept distinct. V18's remembered planning, present journey, private thoughts, reports, conditional wishes and bonus/metafictional material retain separate knowledge and mode boundaries; its main story ends before the pond, Suwa and the promised reunion. V19 separately establishes the third camp, Suwa shore and actual household reunion, while the pond remains unvisited and rebuilding/revisiting the old tower remains reported/future. Its fatigue projections, uncertain initial dream seam, actual awakening, Heya strips and alternate-world under-jacket are not pooled into main-world events.

Each E-record in the [locator/revision ledger](YURUCAMP_PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATOR_AND_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md) supplies the exact source object, volume/chapter/raw image locator, canonical reading/checkpoint, observation scope, alternatives, and verification status. Raw numbers in V01–V15 rows are archive image names, **not printed pages**. V16–V19 use EPUB reading-spine ordinals and exact image members through the current source manifest; these are not old CBZ raw positions or printed pagination. A row's support boundary is the earliest boundary established by its cited evidence, not necessarily the character's first-ever occurrence. V01–V15 rows were consolidated on 2026-10-17; V16, V17 and V18 sequential rows were added on 2026-10-23 from the respective complete primary-source readings. V19 sequential rows were added on 2026-10-24 UTC after its complete primary reading and separate integration pass. The V05/V08 complements carry their separate 2026-10-23 retrospective admission date and do not upgrade any older ANALYSIS_EXTRACTED row. Later audit knowledge is never attributed to an earlier reader.

`PRIMARY_VERIFIED` certifies only the stated underlying observations, not a universal behavioral rule, readiness grade, or independent held-out validation. `ANALYSIS_EXTRACTED` means the canonical analysis was checked but its source observation was not independently reinspected for that record. A mixed row remains `ANALYSIS_EXTRACTED`; verified components remain individually retrievable. DE = direct observation; RP = recurrent pattern; IN = inference; TH = theme; CA = counterevidence. Confidence concerns the stated bounded proposition, and is separate from verification.
