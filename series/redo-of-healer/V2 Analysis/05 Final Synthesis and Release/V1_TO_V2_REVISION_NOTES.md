## 1. Revision model

V2 is a Japanese-primary re-entry and analytical-resolution revision of the completed V1 corpus. It preserves the eleven-document architecture while allowing Japanese evidence to strengthen, complicate, downgrade, or overturn V1 formulations. The governing rule was **expand resolution, not volume**: additions had to supply evidence, counterevidence, mechanism, developmental chronology, another character's subjectivity, epistemic precision, or comparative utility.

V1 remains separately preserved as historical provenance. V2 does not silently replace it.

## 3. Keyaru / Keyarga

V1's integration thesis survives but is more precisely grounded. Volume 4 establishes explicit internal conflict before the later self-metaphors; Volume 8 calls Keyarga an idealized `仮面 / mask`; Volume 9 calls him `鎧 / armor`; Volume 10 behaviorally tests whether that armor can loosen without restoring helplessness. V2 therefore treats Keyarga as a deliberately constructed defensive identity/persona and strong inference, not as a medical diagnosis of separate persons.

The positive chronology is also corrected. Apple farming is biography/livelihood in Volume 1; Volume 4 explicitly develops apples, sweets, feeding, and making people happy as constructive aspiration; Volume 9 imagines cooking and a restaurant after revenge; Volume 10 supplies the causal autobiography linking orchard, parental loss, Hero aspiration, Jioral objectification, revenge, and renewed self-questioning. The nourishing self precedes the heroic and revenge selves.

## 4. Personhood and relationships

Freia is no longer forced into the binary “Flare with amnesia” versus “wholly unrelated replacement.” V2 separately tracks body, retained skills, dispositions, autobiographical memory, social identity, accumulated history, self-ratification, and later agency. Vanargand strengthens evidence for deeper dispositional continuity without erasing the coercive violence of the founding rewrite.

Ellen's origin is anchored to the explicit Volume-4 `洗脳` sequence. Her later knowledge of Norn, selective reconstruction of Norn's strategic capacities, refusal of complete restoration, and expressed preference for Ellen become philosophically important **without retroactively creating consent**.

Setsuna, Kureha, Eve, Ragna, Lapis, and Guren receive independent agency/history treatment. Later loyalty, attachment, or flourishing never converts coercive founding conditions into freely chosen origins.

## 5. Bullet and antagonistic mirrors

Bullet becomes Keyaru's deepest psychological mirror because he combines unusually accurate understanding of others with refusal to recognize their jurisdiction over themselves. His Volume-10 intervention is not called redemption merely because its outcome may be necessary; beneficial result and legitimate method remain distinct. Blade, Norn, Prohm, Hakuou, Hiseki, and Faran are separated into different failure modes instead of collapsed into generic villainy.

## 6. Politics and institutions

V2 replaces compressed political summary with institutional comparison. Jioral is analyzed as an objectification state; Branica demonstrates that interspecies coexistence can become ordinary practice; Eve has genuine revolutionary/moral legitimacy but a thin trained governing class; Panakeia is highly competent yet founder-dependent authoritarian peacebuilding.

Competence, legitimacy, durability, succession, public theater, species membership, material state capacity, and truth management are separated. Panakeia's conference maneuver is described as **in-world legal discontinuity/lawfare backed by personalized deterrence**, not presented as a real-world international-law conclusion. Aviation is traced from personal invention through battlefield advantage, deterrence, mass-production logic, alliance export, reverse-engineering risk, and expected diffusion.

## 7. Healing, Heroes, Demon Kings, and higher rules

`概念の【回復】` is confirmed as explicit canonical terminology. Healing is reconstructed as an increasingly abstract state-selection system: bodily restoration → pain/memory access → skill copying → bodily redesign → identity alteration → degradation/wrong restoration → conceptual restoration → individual temporal regression → world-scale `回帰の【回復】`.

Hero succession becomes a finite-slot model in which states can manipulate vacancies but do not directly appoint successors; Kureha's Sword-Hero trial demonstrates independent systemic qualification.

Demon King corruption is no longer described teleologically as definitively “designed to corrupt.” Black-power pressure and systemic entanglement are textual; ultimate purpose remains open. Chronos's board/piece language establishes higher intervention rules but does not prove literal human-like external players, simulation operators, authors, or readers.

## 8. Ethics

V2 explicitly separates causal explanation, culpability, justification, proportionality, autonomy, consequences, and institutional effects. Later attachment is not retroactive consent. Beneficial consequences do not automatically legitimate coercive methods. Volume 10's Ragna/Lapis crisis provides the clearest mechanical evidence that bodily action and moral authorship can diverge.

The strongest skeptical reading remains active: Keyaru may partly expand the protected category of “mine” rather than acquire a universal autonomy ethic. V2 nevertheless finds that explanation incomplete because accumulated-person irreplaceability, coercion-sensitive culpability, relinquished revenge, respect for Eve's mercy, compelled-loved-one rescue, and second-redo consultation introduce changes in kind as well as degree.

## 9. Narration, genre, and motifs

Japanese voice claims remain deliberately restrained. Keyaru is more cognitively than dialectally distinctive: `observe → classify → enumerate/model constraints → infer → plan → justify → act`. Large register changes occur most clearly through tactical role performance rather than a stable Keyaru/Keyarga dialect split.

The genre trajectory is reformulated as a `ざまぁ` engine that persists after the original revenge list and migrates into statecraft. `やり直し` becomes the master formal motif: revenge opportunity → danger to accumulated personhood → ethically burdened historical revision. Apples, cooking, feeding, and home form the strongest positive counterimage to domination.

## 10. Important corrections/downgrades

- Hero-on-Hero killing by itself does **not** explain the Volume-10 catastrophe; historical counterexamples and Bullet's history defeat the simple rule.
- Seven Keyaru Hero kills and seven Hero-marked Demon King candidates are textual correlation. Distinctive causal relevance is SI; exact mechanism remains SP/open.
- Literal external “players” remain speculative; higher rules and opposing pieces are textual facts.
- Ellen's death is reported/overwhelmingly implied but not corpse-verified; Hiseki's death is likewise high-confidence inferred without corpse verification.
- The second-reset landing point is not shown. Memory retention beyond Guren remains probabilistic/uncertain.
- The second redo is better described as **consultative/partial co-authorship or shared claims** than equal democratic authorship; operative power remains unequal.
- Hakuou is neither innocent puppet nor uncomplicated autonomous tyrant: the office exerts real corruptive pressure while leaving meaningful culpability and resistance.
- Freia/Ellen later agency and Setsuna/Ragna later attachment do not cleanse coercive origins.

## 12. Frozen endpoint

> **Redo of Healer begins as a fantasy of reversing victimhood through absolute control, develops into an inquiry into whether revenge can create a livable self, and by Volume 10 becomes a story about whether people shaped by violence can use the power to rewrite history not merely to reproduce the relationships trauma created, but to prevent some of the wounds that made those relationships necessary.**

> **Keyaru begins as a person whose body and future are authored by others; Keyarga responds by treating authorship as domination; integrated Keyaru gradually learns that the people he loves possess histories and claims that cannot be reduced to his ownership. The second redo matters because he begins, imperfectly, to share claims over the future rather than merely imposing a better version of it.**

This release is frozen through Japanese Volume 10. Future primary-source additions or corrections should create a later version rather than silently modifying V2.
