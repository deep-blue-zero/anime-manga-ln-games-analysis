# 『回復術士のやり直し』Full-Series Analytical Corpus
## Japanese light novels, Volumes 1–10

## Purpose

This document set is the definitive synthesis of the first ten Japanese light novels of **『回復術士のやり直し』 / *Redo of Healer***. It is designed as a reusable source for future literary, ethical, political, linguistic, and cross-character comparison.

The corpus does not treat the series as only a revenge plot, an erotic shock work, or a conventional overpowered-protagonist fantasy. It follows how the governing problem changes across the ten volumes:

> **child who wants to nourish**  
> **→ bereaved child who seeks heroic power**  
> **→ Healing Hero reduced to an object**  
> **→ Keyarga, who seeks autonomy through domination**  
> **→ avenger who builds a household**  
> **→ ruler who tries to build peace**  
> **→ Keyaru, who rediscovers nourishment as the life he wanted**  
> **→ second redo undertaken not principally for revenge, but to prevent the wounds that produced the people he came to love**

The first redo is authored by one traumatized man determined never to be powerless again. By the end of Volume 10, the next redo becomes a partially collaborative project: Setsuna, Freia, Kureha, Eve, and Guren acquire claims over what history should become.

That transformation is the organizing insight of the corpus.

# Spoiler and content note

Every document contains complete spoilers through Volume 10.

The source material includes sexual violence, slavery, coercive sexual punishment, grooming, torture, bodily transformation, identity erasure, genocide, mass killing, and child-coded or dependent characters placed in sexualized contexts. These subjects are discussed clinically and only to the extent required for analysis. Explicit scenes are not reconstructed.

The corpus follows four moral rules:

1. **Explanation is not exculpation.** Keyaru's victimization explains the psychological route toward domination; it does not justify domination.
2. **Attachment is not retroactive consent.** Later love or loyalty does not make a coercive origin morally clean.
3. **Real grievance and proportionate punishment are separate questions.** Many targets are guilty; Keyaru's responses can still be excessive, eroticized, or identity-destroying.
4. **Beneficial outcome does not automatically legitimate method.** This is especially important for Keyaru, Ellen, and Bullet.

# Epistemic labels

The analysis distinguishes four levels of claim.

### Textual fact
Directly established by narration, dialogue, event, or explicit system explanation.

### Strong inference
Not stated in one definitive sentence, but supported by several independent textual signals.

### Speculation
Plausible but unresolved. Volume 10's higher cosmology requires particular restraint here.

### Value judgment
An ethical, political, or literary evaluation made by the analysis.

Examples:

- **Fact:** Ellen learns she was Norn and chooses to remain Ellen.
- **Strong inference:** Keyarga functions as a trauma-built armor identity.
- **Speculation:** a literal external player controls the world as a game board.
- **Value judgment:** Freia's later personhood does not morally justify the act that created her.

# Master thesis

*Redo of Healer* begins as a fantasy of reversing victimhood through absolute control. It then becomes a study of whether revenge can create a livable self, whether possession can develop into recognition, and whether a protagonist formed by coercion can build peace without reproducing coercion as the basic structure of his world.

The series does not offer a clean redemption arc. Keyaru remains possessive, authoritarian, sexually coercive toward targets, comfortable with manipulation, and willing to concentrate power in himself and trusted intimates. Yet he also changes in ways the text increasingly makes impossible to dismiss:

- he values specific people as accumulated histories rather than replaceable bodies;
- he learns to trust companions acting outside his control;
- he recognizes coercion as relevant to culpability;
- he can relinquish revenge voluntarily;
- he admires Eve's refusal of retaliatory persecution;
- he admits fear and loneliness;
- he recovers the pre-hero dream of apples, cooking, and nourishment;
- he finally shares authorship of the next timeline.

The mature Keyaru is therefore best understood as:

> **a still-dangerous post-traumatic healer-king whose deepest positive desire is not conquest but a protected ordinary life, and whose greatest late development is beginning to accept that the people he loves may have claims over their futures that exceed his ownership of them.**

# Naming conventions

## Keyaru / Keyarga

- **Keyaru / ケヤル**: the original name and the name reclaimed after revenge.
- **Keyarga / ケヤルガ**: the altered body, name, and revenge identity constructed after escape.

The documents use **Keyarga** when discussing the revenge-dominant persona and **Keyaru** when discussing the original or integrated post-revenge self. The distinction is psychological and philosophical rather than a complete split personality or separate dialect.

## Flare / Freia

- **Flare / フレア**: Jioral princess, Magic Hero, Keyaru's first principal revenge target.
- **Freia / フレイア**: the identity created through Keyaru's destruction/reconstruction of Flare's memory and selfhood.

## Norn / Ellen

- **Norn / ノルン**: Jioral strategist and younger princess.
- **Ellen / エレン**: the identity created after Keyaru rewrites Norn.

## Demon King

“Demon King” is an office and metaphysical succession role, not a fixed species or individual identity.

## Panakeia

The successor kingdom created after Jioral is politically abolished in Volume 9. Its name derives from the healing goddess and signals Keyaru's public identity as Healing Hero.

# Short glossary

### 【癒】の勇者 / Healing Hero
Keyaru's hero class. Its apparently supportive function conceals invasive access to pain, memory, skills, body structure, and eventually time/state restoration.

### 【改良】 / Improvement
Keyaru's use of healing to optimize or alter bodies.

### 【改悪】 / Degradation
Healing inverted into destructive alteration.

### 神装武具 / Divine Armament
World-linked weapons associated with heroes and shaped by the wielder's nature or desire.

### 賢者の石 / Philosopher's Stone
The Demon King's heart and a central medium for world-scale regression/reset.

### 翡翠眼 / Jade Eye
Keyaru's acquired eye ability, central to memory recovery and analysis.

### カラドリウス / Caladrius
Black-Wing divine bird associated with disease, purification, judgment, and catastrophic power.

### グレン / Guren
Divine beast born from the egg Keyaru receives; purifier, trickster, mirror of Keyaru, and later successor to Chronos's time-related role.

### クロノス / Chronos
Time Divine Beast whose Volume 10 warning reveals that Keyaru's hero killings have destabilized a larger system.

### 黒い神 / black god
The corrupting power behind the Demon King vessel-system and black forces, though Volume 10 suggests it may exist within a still larger architecture.

# Reading recommendation

Read the documents in numerical order for the first pass. Later comparative use can begin directly with the character, ethics, politics, or metaphysics file most relevant to the question.

The governing endpoint is Volume 10:

> **The first redo is revenge-authored. The second begins only after Keyaru learns that power cannot make the people he loves interchangeable, and that a future worth rewriting must include their claims over what history should become.**
