# 2. What Phase 2 changed

Phase 2 did not overturn the V1 master thesis. It made it more precise.

The most important source-driven revisions are:

- **Keyarga’s defensive function is longitudinally explicit:** V4 self-conflict → V8 mask → V9 armor → V10 behavioral integration.
- **Nourishment predates revenge:** apple farming and food-as-happiness are present long before the Volume-10 orchard autobiography.
- **Freia/Flare continuity is dimensional:** autobiographical history is erased, while knowledge, magic, embodied continuity, deep preference, and later Freia history coexist.
- **Ellen’s origin is unambiguously coercive conditioning:** her later informed choice of Ellen matters without retroactively legitimating that origin.
- **Kureha earns the Sword Hero office through an independent qualification process:** Keyaru enables access rather than installing the status.
- **Personhood increasingly depends on lived history and agency rather than body alone:** V8 reset logic and V10 Ragna/Lapis compulsion make this explicit.
- **Panakeia’s statecraft is more concrete and more coercive than the V1 compression showed:** legal discontinuity, conference lawfare, deterrence, religion, technological proliferation, material settlement, tax policy, and delegated administration coexist.
- **Eve’s sovereignty has institutional limits:** thin administration, factions, enforcement failures, consultation, and local disobedience.
- **The metaphysical system is more integrated:** explicit conceptual healing, slot-based Hero succession, intent-responsive Divine Armaments, black conversion, divine-beast rule constraints, individual time regression, and world reset all belong to the same restoration/return problem.
- **Guren reverses Keyaru’s power thesis:** greater metaphysical power can produce less personal freedom.
- **Keyaru’s moral development is real but incomplete:** the strongest V2 conclusion is neither redemption nor “nothing changed.”
- **The second redo ends Keyaru’s exclusive authorship without producing equal collective control.**

# 5. Phase-3 opening thesis to test, not assume

The first rewrite should test the following formulation against the evidence rather than simply inherit it:

> **Keyarga is a deliberately constructed defensive identity that solves the original Keyaru’s helplessness by reversing the logic of objectification. Across Volumes 4–10, the series gradually exposes that armor as both necessary and deforming. The mature Keyaru does not return to the boy he was; he integrates the armor’s competence while recovering capacities for nourishment, vulnerability, irreplaceable attachment, and limited recognition of other people’s authorship.**

The strongest counterreading must remain active:

> **What appears to be moral integration may partly be an expansion of the circle Keyaru regards as “mine,” rather than a universal recognition of autonomy.**

A satisfactory V2 Document 02 should explain why both observations are true, where each stops explaining the text, and what Volume 10 actually changes.
