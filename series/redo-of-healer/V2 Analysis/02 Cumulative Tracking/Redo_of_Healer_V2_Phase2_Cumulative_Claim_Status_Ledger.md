# I. Status legend

- **PRESERVE** — V1 formulation remains sound with no material conceptual change.
- **STRENGTHEN** — V1 claim survives and now has materially stronger Japanese-primary support.
- **REVISE** — core insight survives but wording/mechanism/chronology must change.
- **DOWNGRADE** — V1 or earlier-project certainty exceeds what the source establishes.
- **OPEN** — question remains unresolved at the Volume-10 endpoint.

The ledger is not a substitute for the evidence tranches. It is a routing layer:

> **claim → present status → source packet → Phase-3 document home**

# IX. Claims that remain deliberately unresolved

These should appear in Document 09 and should **not** be “solved” by Phase-3 prose confidence.

1. Exact second-reset landing point.
2. Exact distribution of retained memories after reset.
3. Exact causal mechanism linking Keyaru’s Hero kills to anomalous Hero-marked Demon King candidates.
4. Identity/ontology of the level operating outside ordinary causal visibility.
5. Whether the black god is the highest antagonist or one actor/piece within a larger architecture.
6. Exact universal mechanism selecting replacement Heroes.
7. What becomes of Freia/Flare if Keyaru reaches pre-abuse Flare before the first-timeline trajectory develops.
8. What becomes of Ellen/Norn if Norn is reached before reconstruction.
9. Whether preserving memory while preventing trauma preserves identity or creates another kind of discontinuity.
10. Whether Keyaru can honor the companions’ requests without reproducing paternalistic historical control.
11. Whether Panakeia can survive without Keyaru/Ellen’s exceptional competence.
12. Whether a durable human-demon peace can become institutional rather than dependent on exceptional rulers and deterrence.
