# Chisa -- Evidence and Falsification Matrix

## Use and epistemic vocabulary

This is the claim authority within the local packet, not a replacement for the primary evidence. Read the continuous interpretation in the [deep dive](WUWA_CHISA_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md), and use this file to test it. Evidence bundles in the second half of this document resolve the shorthand used throughout the dossier.

**Source fact** means the specified source depicts or states something; self-report, testimony, clinical report, and narrator description remain distinguishable within that class. **Stable observation** identifies a repeated pattern in the represented material. **Strong inference** gives a supported explanation without pretending that the source explicitly formulates it. **Candidate thesis** is a wider interpretation intended to be challenged. **Open question** is not a positive finding. Confidence applies to the bounded proposition, not to every extension of it.

A later review should record PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, QUALIFY, SCOPE_LIMIT, REVISE, REJECT, or OPEN. Do not count additional examples as strengthening when they reuse the same dialogue, recording, or summary. No numerical composite confidence score is used.

## Claim register

### CHISA-C01 -- Being trusted does not guarantee being understood

**Class:** source self-report plus strong organizing inference. **Confidence:** high within her account; not universal biography.

`FavorWord_150805_Content` explicitly distinguishes reliable performance from being known. It supports the hypothesis that Chisa can make herself useful without expecting equivalent personal recognition. The stronger assertion that everyone before Rover valued her only instrumentally is rejected: family, Sumika, and supportive Academy memories provide counterevidence. Test later cases in which she receives care after a failure, a refusal, or an unproductive day. A warm response to excellent work alone does not settle that test. **Evidence:** CHISA-E48, CHISA-E09, CHISA-E20, CHISA-E30.

### CHISA-C02 -- Numerical and constructive aptitude precedes the crisis

**Class:** self-report, corroborated by ordinary uses. **Confidence:** high, bounded.

She places her estimation ability in childhood and independently names mathematics, tailoring, and handcraft as interests. Extraordinary Forte development is not the entire origin of her intelligence or identity. Her approximate estimates do not prove error-free quantitative perception of every unknown object. The falsification target is an explicit earlier history contradicting that account, or later demonstrations that narrow which tasks the aptitude covers. **Evidence:** CHISA-E06, CHISA-E07.

**Class:** source narrative. **Confidence:** high.

**Class:** bounded behavioral observation. **Confidence:** high for the episode, moderate for generalization.
