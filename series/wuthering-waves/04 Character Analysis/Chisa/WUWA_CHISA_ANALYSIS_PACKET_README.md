## Start here

This is the sole entrypoint for a twelve-document local reconstruction packet for **Chisa / Kuchiba Chisa**. Official name aliases are Chinese/Japanese **千咲** and Korean **치사**. It follows the source-facing reading, specialist-profile, evidence/falsification, operational-model, and AV-retrieval practices developed in the Lynae, Aemeath, and Denia packets, while giving Chisa her own analytical architecture.

The packet was originally prepared as a local working draft without repository or Drive mutation. The owner has now adopted it as **current active_provisional Git analytical authority within its declared text/audio scope**. The original rebuild did not overwrite the earlier smoke-test work, and this adoption does not claim new source acquisition, AV review, or Drive mutation.

The main reading is titled **A Future She Can Inhabit**. Its thesis is:

> Chisa turns extraordinary structural perception into an ethic of refusing to overlook particular lives. Her danger is to make herself the reliable instrument of that ethic while minimizing her own need for recognition and care. The opening of her future requires neither the abandonment of precision nor a transformation into an exuberant personality. It requires learning that she may be a participant in the life she helps preserve: someone who eats, studies, makes things, has friends, needs treatment, chooses when to leave, and can expect to be welcomed back.

The thesis is an interpretation, not an official character description. Its main unresolved test is whether belonging becomes secure when usefulness, success, or compliance are removed. Later scenes give evidence of receiving care and keeping her own future; they do not certify perfect recovery or effortless refusal.
