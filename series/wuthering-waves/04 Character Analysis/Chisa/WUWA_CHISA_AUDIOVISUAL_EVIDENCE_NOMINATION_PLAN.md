# Chisa -- Audiovisual Evidence Nomination Plan

## Responsibility and present state

This plan states what AV evidence would materially test the pre-AV reconstruction and why. The [human-retrieval crosswalk](WUWA_CHISA_AV_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_CROSSWALK.md) owns public titles, search terms, source locators, and acquisition routing. Neither document is a record of completed viewing.

No video, image, or raw voice recording was directly reviewed in this rebuild. A dialogue description of a movement, a virtual asset reference, a waveform statistic, or a public video title is not a direct visual observation. The plan uses the owner-adopted local AV amendments without asserting their current Git merge state.

The priority is not to illustrate every paragraph. It is to expose the textual model to evidence that could preserve it, narrow it, contradict it, or change its causal explanation. A particularly valuable tranche would combine acute care and loss with an ordinary later conversation. Otherwise the audiovisual version of Chisa may become disproportionately crisis-bound simply because dramatic clips are easier to find.

## 1. Authority and source class

| Dimension | Question for the collected segment | Chisa-specific safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| Diegetic authority | Is this an event, memory, dreamlike presentation, conditional game state, or nonliteral promotion? | Distinguish the tempting welcome from remembered support; distinguish actual reunion from reported reunion. |
| Representational authority | What does this witness directly show of design, movement, expression, sound, or spatial relation? | An official outfit or combat display can show a depiction without proving ordinary conduct. |
| Interpretive warrant | How well does the specific segment support the proposed claim, and what alternatives remain? | Quietness cannot automatically mean emotional distance; a close shot cannot establish exclusive romance. |
| Production framing | Which associations does the published work foreground through selection, editing, music, or composition? | Observable framing is not proof of a private, unitary authorial intention. |

These are separate judgments, not scores that can be averaged into one authority ranking. A PV can be valuable for framing and weak for literal chronology. A fan-recorded intact quest can preserve strong diegetic evidence despite being hosted by an unofficial channel; its capture alterations still need examination.

Collect four practical classes when warranted: narrative AV; matched multilingual promotional AV; unmatched official promotional AV; and official static or design material. Availability in all four languages is not a requirement for admission. Neither the absence of a counterpart nor the volume of a regional channel's uploads establishes a character difference.

## 2. What the AV pass should try to falsify

**Reserve as distance.** Does direct staging actually depict Chisa as detached, or as attentive with low overt display? Compare initial assessment, failed rescue, practiced hosting, and later peer participation. A repeated mannerism matters only after checking whether it is a reused game animation. [CHISA-E11, CHISA-E16, CHISA-E26, CHISA-E36]

**Precision as mastery.** Does the scene show effort, disorientation, exhaustion, uncertainty, or reliance on others that a transcript compresses? The breakthrough should not become a solo triumph because a trailer isolates Chisa's decisive shot. [CHISA-E13, CHISA-E21, CHISA-E23, CHISA-E39]

**Care as self-removal.** Does she remain physically and conversationally present in later group situations, or does she primarily facilitate others? Invitations and responses should be inspected together. The result may qualify the degree of change rather than reverse the entire model. [CHISA-E27, CHISA-E38, CHISA-E45, CHISA-E46]

**Ordinary pleasure as merely therapeutic.** Do food, play, making, reading, and companionship have their own expressive weight, or are they repeatedly subordinated to crisis recovery? The textual answer is already substantive; AV can complicate its emphasis without erasing the words. [CHISA-E36, CHISA-E38, CHISA-E46, CHISA-E50]

**A complete cure.** Does the later presentation explicitly establish a changed perceptual condition, or merely omit an earlier representation? Absence of a special effect is weak evidence of cure unless the narrative makes the change legible. [CHISA-E03, CHISA-E04, CHISA-E23]

**An exclusive rescuer bond.** How do the scene's framing and response time distribute attention among Rover, parents, Lynae, Sigrika, the cafe guests, and Chisa herself? Promotional pairing is a comparison surface, not replacement evidence for the story's relationships. [CHISA-E27, CHISA-E30, CHISA-E37, CHISA-E38, CHISA-E46]

## 4. Promotional and static nominations

**CHISA-PV01 -- Character showcase.** Nominate the official character-focused showcase as a production-framing witness. Test which relation among school life, repetition, cutting, isolation, care, and future it foregrounds. Do not assume every transition depicts a literal chronology. The exact searchable title and counterpart-discovery status are in the crosswalk.

**CHISA-PV02 -- Combat showcase.** Use it for the presented motion vocabulary, relationship to weapons, transition between ordinary and combat representation, and integration of sound and editing. It cannot establish a universal attitude toward violence or unrestricted power. An animation may express characterization and still obey gameplay legibility constraints.

**CHISA-PV03 -- Version trailer and contextual short.** Use them to examine the patch's positioning of Chisa and Honami, including what the trailer selects or withholds. Any reused quest footage should be linked back to the full narrative scene; repeated frames are not independent corroboration.

**CHISA-PV04 -- Alternate outfit presentation.** The source includes a separately described outfit surface. Its promotional showcase and official static images may illuminate contrast with the retained Academy uniform. The existence of a costume or a marketed seasonal setting does not prove she attended a particular diegetic outing.

**CHISA-ART01 -- Default and alternate official design witnesses.** Obtain unaltered official art with source, date, and edition. Record what is actually visible. Do not infer fabric, color symbolism, age, or habitual posture from asset paths alone.

**CHISA-ART02 -- Group promotional imagery.** Include images with Lynae or other established peers only where they answer a framing question. Compare the association with the textual relationship independently. Do not use a group arrangement as proof of an unseen conversation, romantic status, or relative closeness.

## 5. Matched-language comparison without forced symmetry

Use a prospective family ID for counterpart discovery, then assign actual witness IDs only once evidence is acquired. Each member records audio language separately from subtitle language, channel, exact title, upload date, duration, and source URL. Chinese remains the primary semantic reference within the frozen textual corpus; this does not justify assuming every PV script is identical to a Chinese game line.

Adjudicate visual/edit equivalence and semantic equivalence independently. A shared title is only a lead. A shortened regional edit, changed music mix, altered subtitle translation, or different line order can limit comparison. Keep meaningful unmatched material rather than discarding it to obtain a symmetrical set.

For Chisa, useful possible comparison questions include diagnostic versus reassuring delivery, protective discouragement versus accepted partnership, unformatted gratitude, playful participation, and the transition into command or combat mode. No particular answer is presupposed. A prior impression about another character's Japanese persona must not become the default hypothesis here.

## 6. Collection requirements

Retain enough lead-in and aftermath to identify the action, interlocutors, and response. For a choice branch, record which option was selected; do not splice alternatives as one continuous exchange. Preserve the capture's language, version, speed, skipped dialogue, overlays, commentary, and any known cuts. A clean gameplay capture is preferable when the claim concerns staging, but imperfect footage can remain useful if its limitation is explicit.

Keep an original acquired witness and record derived clips, contact sheets, subtitles, or audio extracts as children. Exact time spans refer to that particular witness, not to a generic chapter. A changed encode may preserve the event while altering loudness, frame timing, or image fidelity. Do not downmix, normalize, speed-adjust, or remove context without documenting the derivative.

The Drive evidence manifest should identify acquired witnesses, hashes where bytes are retained, provenance, source mappings, and comparison groups. Git-side analytical documents should reference those stable witness identities rather than become a second media store. An empty placeholder manifest is not created by this packet because no AV was acquired.

## 7. Direct-review and completion gate

For each reviewed target, record the specific observation, exact span or frames, source class, interpretive inference, alternatives, and effect on the claim. Allowed dispositions include preserve, strengthen, qualify, scope-limit, revise, reject, and open. Strong emotion in the analyst is not itself a claim disposition.

The hierarchy here is a bounded nomination plan, not an exhaustive catalog of every public Chisa depiction. Its unresolved public mappings and unmatched counterpart checks are visible in the crosswalk. `audiovisually_hardened` and `integrated_reconstruction_completed` remain unclaimed until the materially selected witnesses are directly reviewed and their results reconciled with the textual model.

Human perceived-voice annotation remains optional terminal enrichment. A listener's impression can nominate a test or challenge a conclusion, but it must retain its language, context, confidence, and alternative explanations. No claim that this layer is uniquely subjective is needed: visual interpretation also requires disciplined inference. The boundary is the kind of evidence and review performed, not an imagined split between judgment-free images and subjective sound.
