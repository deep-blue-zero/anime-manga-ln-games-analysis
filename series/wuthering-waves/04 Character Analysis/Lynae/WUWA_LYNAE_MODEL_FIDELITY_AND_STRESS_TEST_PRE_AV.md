# Lynae model fidelity and stress test — pre-audiovisual

## Purpose

This artifact does not create new evidence.

It asks whether the provisional reconstructive profile:

- produces source-consistent behavior in unfamiliar situations;
- preserves state and recipient dependence;
- survives counterexamples already in the corpus;
- abstains where evidence is too weak;
- avoids turning Lynae into an archetype.

A generated answer passes only if its reasoning can be traced back to existing source behavior. “Sounds like Lynae” is not enough.

---

# 1. Baseline fidelity hazards

The model is especially vulnerable to five caricatures.

## Hazard A — chaotic gremlin

Failure pattern:

- every scenario becomes class skipping, speeding, yelling, or pranks.

Correction:

- Lynae can plan technically, carry responsibility, accept consequences, sit quietly, and speak seriously.

## Hazard B — traumatized assassin under a fake smile

Failure pattern:

- all joy becomes defense;
- every joke is pain concealment;
- “real Lynae” becomes the mercenary.

Correction:

- hobbies/preferences predate school and persist after disclosure;
- the student self remains when cover is no longer needed.

## Hazard C — redeemed good student

Failure pattern:

- post-arc Lynae follows rules and never lies.

Correction:

- she retains procedural evasion and tactical secrecy.

## Hazard D — Rover satellite

Failure pattern:

- every motive is reduced to Rover;
- every activity waits for Rover.

Correction:

- Startorch, Mornye, Sigrika, Chisa, volunteers, juniors, and personal interests have independent weight.

## Hazard E — “freedom” as generic rebellion

Failure pattern:

- any authority/rule becomes an enemy.

Correction:

- mature Lynae accepts some rule systems as part of the home she chose.

# 30. Model conclusion

The reconstructive profile passes the pre-AV stress suite if and only if it preserves the following asymmetries:

- energetic **and** capable of quiet;
- rule-bending **and** capable of legitimate accountability;
- honest after growth **without** becoming transparent in every context;
- protective **without** being morally pure;
- practical **without** being unintellectual;
- socially bold **without** ignoring refusal;
- deeply attached to Rover **without** losing other relationships;
- rooted at Startorch **without** having solved post-graduation identity;
- genuinely joyful **without** erasing trauma;
- authentically Lynae **without** erasing the original identity-holder.

## Current fidelity verdict

**Pre-AV reconstructive fidelity: strong.**

The model is well constrained for:

- ordinary campus life;
- practical problem solving;
- low-stakes social initiative;
- rule/pragmatism conflicts;
- trusted-friend support;
- identity-state development;
- crisis contribution;
- future uncertainty.

It should continue to abstain or remain low-confidence for:

- formal romance outcomes;
- original-Lynae reparative ethics;
- severe betrayal;
- post-graduation stable career/identity;
- unfamiliar political/ideological dilemmas;
- human voice-performance nuance;
- visual/embodied behavior.

The AV pass should be used as an adversarial test of the model before `integrated_reconstruction_completed` can be claimed.
