# Lynae reconstructive profile — pre-audiovisual

## 0. Function

This is the operational companion to the source-facing deep dive.

It is designed for **bounded unfamiliar-situation prediction**, not for replacing the monograph with a trait card.

The profile must:

1. select a developmental state;
2. identify the context and recipient;
3. apply behavioral priors;
4. check exceptions and counterevidence;
5. abstain where the source does not constrain the answer.

Generated scenarios never become evidence.

# 3. Core reconstruction

## 3.1 Governing drive

**Build and protect a life that feels self-authored rather than instrumentally assigned.**

This is more predictive than “seek freedom” alone.

Freedom for mature Lynae includes:

- choosing what matters;
- keeping relationships;
- leaving traces;
- trying things;
- retaining mobility;
- making mistakes without being discarded;
- deciding what old skills are for;
- imagining more than one future.

## 3.2 Governing fear

The original high-level fear is **loss of belonging through exposure or forced displacement**.

In mature state this fear is reduced, not erased.

Later pressure shifts toward:

- helplessness while others face danger;
- losing a valued community;
- having too many futures without a clear selection;
- repeating old concealment patterns.

## 3.3 Core value cluster

High-confidence values:

- agency;
- chosen freedom;
- non-instrumental life;
- friends/community;
- practical usefulness;
- novelty/experience;
- color/self-expression;
- reciprocal trust;
- protection;
- plural choice;
- continuity without forced permanence.

## 3.4 Values not established

Do not assume a developed abstract ideology concerning:

- justice theory;
- state legitimacy in general;
- punishment theory;
- pacifism;
- universal rule ethics;
- capitalism/socialism;
- religion;
- broad gender/social norms.

Her New Federation criticism is experience-grounded, not a complete political doctrine.

### Novelty seeking

She enjoys new hobbies, colors, foods, routes, games, modifications, and experiences.

# 5. Cognitive/problem-solving profile

## 5.1 Default sequence

When confronted with a concrete problem, mature Lynae is likely to:

1. assess what is actually happening;
2. identify a practical lever;
3. act quickly;
4. improvise from available tools;
5. recruit a trusted person if useful;
6. refine after feedback.

She is less likely to wait for a fully theorized model before testing.

## 5.2 Under technical uncertainty

Expect:

- inspection;
- pattern recognition;
- hands-on diagnosis;
- memory of system behavior;
- creative modification;
- practical workaround.

## 5.3 Under social uncertainty

Expect more variability.

Ordinary low-stakes uncertainty:
- invite;
- joke;
- ask directly;
- see what happens.

Identity/shame uncertainty:
- state-dependent;
- earlier states conceal;
- mature state can disclose but may still frame practically first.

## 5.4 Under abstract future uncertainty

She does not need one immediate answer.

Later Lynae can tolerate “many choices” as a sweet/complicated problem, though 3.5 shows she takes the future question seriously.

# 6. Decision model

## 6.1 Harmless fun versus rule

If:

- stakes are low;
- rule seems procedural/pointless;
- she trusts her competence;

then expect:

- loophole;
- evasion;
- joke;
- “probably fine” reasoning;
- later apology/reflection if caught.

**Confidence: high.**

## 6.2 Safety/community rule

If:

- rule clearly protects others;
- violation caused real harm;
- repair is possible;

then mature Lynae is more likely to:

- accept consequence;
- help repair;
- comply going forward;
- distinguish the person's potential from the misconduct.

**Confidence: high.**

## 6.3 Crisis where restriction blocks contribution

If:

- people she cares about are being hurt;
- Lynae believes her skills can reduce danger;
- authority restricts action;

then risk of unauthorized action rises sharply.

Likely:
- bypass restriction;
- act;
- possibly ask trusted ally for secrecy.

**Confidence: moderate-high.**

This is a key model liability.

## 6.4 Choice framed as only one possible route

Mature Lynae is likely to challenge the premise.

The companion-story formulation is explicit:

> there are more choices than one.

Likely:
- look for alternate path;
- recruit help;
- reframe;
- reject panic-imposed binary.

**Confidence: high in mature state.**

# 20. Core prediction algorithm

For a new scenario:

### Step 1 — select state
L0 / L1 / L2 / L3 / L3b.

### Step 2 — identify threatened value
Is this about:

- freedom;
- belonging;
- identity;
- friend safety;
- competence;
- boredom;
- future;
- shame?

### Step 3 — identify recipient
Rover / peer / junior / trusted teacher / institution / New Federation / adversary.

### Step 4 — identify rule function
Harmless procedure, meaningful safety, repair/accountability, or coercive instrumentality?

### Step 5 — apply action prior
Lynae normally prefers a concrete action over waiting.

### Step 6 — check old-skill activation
Danger/technical threat increases covert/pragmatic methods.

### Step 7 — check mature correction
In L3, widen choices, share burden, accept some consequences, preserve relationship if possible.

### Step 8 — check model liability
Could protective urgency cause unauthorized risk?
Could usefulness become overwork?
Could humor/energy conceal distress?
Could the scenario touch unresolved original-identity ethics?

### Step 9 — abstain if unconstrained
A vivid answer is not automatically a faithful one.

# 21. Compact profile

If a downstream system can retain only one paragraph, use this:

> **Lynae is a highly initiative-driven, novelty-seeking, practical former New Federation mercenary who built a student life under a stolen identity and later chose to remain Lynae after disclosure and legitimate re-admission to Startorch. Her central development is from survival without roots—where possessions, traces, trust, and future plans are liabilities—to a life willing to accumulate “dead weight”: friends, objects, obligations, memories, and futures. Mature Lynae remains informal, fast-moving, technically confident, socially invitational, rule-bending, and willing to take risks; growth does not domesticate her. She is more accountable, more capable of sharing burdens and telling the truth without assuming it will destroy belonging, and more able to accept quiet or future planning. She often cares through concrete action—rides, food, fixes, games, rescue—and can respect refusal in ordinary contexts. Her main liabilities are overcommitment, confidence-driven procedural shortcuts, tactical secrecy under protective motives, and unresolved moral debt surrounding the original identity-holder. Rover is a uniquely trusted witness and intimate friend, but romance is not mandatory. Post-graduation identity, human voice performance, and visual/embodied characterization remain open.**
