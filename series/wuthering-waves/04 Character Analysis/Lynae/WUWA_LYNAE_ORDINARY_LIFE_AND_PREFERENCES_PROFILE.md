# Lynae ordinary life and preferences profile

## Purpose

The WUWA reconstruction protocol asks a deliberately unfashionable question:

> **What does this person do when nothing important is happening?**

For Lynae this is not peripheral characterization. Ordinary life is the object her main story is about acquiring.

Her mercenary past trained her to minimize roots, possessions, traces, and long-term commitments. Startorch lets non-instrumental habits accumulate. The ordinary-life corpus therefore provides direct evidence of development rather than “slice-of-life bonus material.”

Each section distinguishes:

- **source fact** — directly demonstrated;
- **analytical implication** — what the evidence reasonably suggests;
- **extrapolation limit** — what it does not establish.

---

# 1. Food

## 1.1 General preference: bold, colorful, situationally fun

### Source fact

`FavorWord_150909_Content` says Lynae dislikes bland food—not necessarily because of taste alone, but because it evokes bad memories and makes the world seem grayer.

Chinese:

`不管是口味还是生活，都要缤纷多彩些才好啊。`

The source explicitly connects variety/color in flavor to variety/color in life.

`FavorWord_150908_Content` says even instant noodles taste great when eaten secretly during class because the illicit timing makes the experience exciting.

Later ordinary scenes show:

- attraction to a colorful Dragon Honey Yogurt Bowl;
- recognition that she may already have eaten too much;
- comic attempt at self-restraint;
- enthusiasm for event snacks and unusual foods;
- interest in New Federation specialties after not having them for a long time;
- repeated convenience-store meal plans with Rover.

### Analytical implication

Lynae's food preference is partly sensory and partly experiential. Context can make ordinary food more appealing if it turns eating into play.

This aligns with a broader preference for novelty, color, and self-authored experience.

### Extrapolation limit

Do not infer that she likes every strongly flavored food, every sweet, or every “weird” dish. The source gives a broad aversion to blandness and several positive examples, not an exhaustive palate.

## 1.2 Appetite and restraint

### Source fact

In `Side_Coop_StingerLN_2_*`, Lynae wants the colorful yogurt bowl but notices she has already eaten a lot and tries to resist.

### Analytical implication

She is capable of recognizing bodily limits and does not simply follow every impulse.

### Extrapolation limit

One snack scene does not establish disciplined nutrition or chronic overeating.

# 2. Motorcycles, vehicles, and speed

## 2.1 Source facts

Motorcycling is one of Lynae's most persistent preferences.

She:

- ranks full-throttle riding under Lahai-Roi's sky among her favorite hobbies (`FavorWord_150906_Content`);
- modifies turbo, suspension, and brakes;
- accumulates speeding incidents;
- invents an Overflow propulsion modification that leaves a rainbow trail;
- uses rides to tour, de-stress, socialize, travel, and respond to emergencies;
- imagines future mechanic/courier work;
- spends and saves money around bike parts/upgrades;
- repeatedly promises future rides to Rover.

### Analytical implication

The bike combines several values:

- agency;
- movement;
- technical mastery;
- aesthetics/customization;
- sensory pleasure;
- social ritual;
- escape-route competence.

It is therefore one of the best ordinary objects for reconstructing her.

## 2.2 Risk calibration

### Source fact

Her modifications are not only “go faster.” Suspension and brake upgrades appear alongside turbocharging. She can diagnose mechanical issues and invent propulsion changes.

### Analytical implication

She is not best modeled as technically oblivious recklessness. Her risk appetite is mediated by confidence in competence.

### Extrapolation limit

Technical competence does not make the behavior objectively safe. The Academy repeatedly treats her driving/modification practices as disciplinary problems.

# 4. Games

## 4.1 FPS games

`FavorWord_150906_Content` lists shooting games among hobbies.

This preference is interesting because it may recycle familiar combat skill into nonlethal play, but the source does not itself state that causal interpretation.

## 4.2 Rhythm/arcade games

The 3.2 “Stellar Orbit Beats” material gives unusually detailed ordinary behavior.

Lynae:

- wants “one more game” after finishing;
- notices Mornye's suppressed interest;
- pushes her to stop overanalyzing;
- emphasizes that the activity is not class or work;
- says fixed standards are not the only thing that makes play interesting;
- permits both play-to-win and play-for-fun orientations;
- likes co-op;
- says games are best with friends;
- adapts difficulty for a newcomer;
- accepts Rover declining and leaves the invitation open.

### Analytical implication

This is strong evidence for a **plural-standard play ethic**. Lynae can enjoy mastery without requiring every activity to become performance optimization.

It also shows social pressure with an escape hatch: she pushes, but does not treat refusal as betrayal.

### Extrapolation limit

Do not infer that she is equally gracious about refusal in all emotionally important contexts.

# 5. Music, dance, film, and popular culture

## 5.1 Film

In the 3.2 invitation material she is excited that Rover remembers the kind of film she likes.

After a film whose heroine's spectacular journey fails to reach its expected destination, Lynae calls the ending tragic but says the journey was not in vain because the heroine still had her defining moment.

### Analytical implication

The scene coheres with Lynae's present-value orientation: a meaningful journey need not be invalidated by an imperfect ending.

### Extrapolation limit

Film interpretation is not a confession. Do not automatically treat every comment about a fictional character as autobiographical self-description.

## 5.2 Music

Lynae praises a song's unique sound, beat, and howling wind and describes it as making the world feel as though it is opening.

She then connects the beat to a current dance trend and admits she tried the dance and received many likes.

### Analytical implication

Her aesthetic interests are participatory. She does not only consume; she remixes experience into movement/social play.

### Extrapolation limit

No singing/music-performance profile is established.

## 5.3 Dance/social media

The “many likes” line suggests willingness to post and participate in trends, followed by embarrassment when noticed.

This is a small but useful example of a person who seeks visibility in chosen forms while remaining capable of self-consciousness.

# 7. Study and academic life

## 7.1 Attendance

Lynae is a chronic class-skipper.

The archive and ecology material repeatedly acknowledge:

- truancy;
- near-minimum attendance;
- demerits;
- professors allowing some attendance flexibility when she already knows material and will sit finals;
- a recurring need to repair credit deficits.

### Analytical implication

She does not treat seat-time itself as sacred.

## 7.2 Capability

The evidence supports:

- confidence about passing re-entry exams;
- broad practical knowledge;
- technical competence;
- ability to understand/complete courses she has taken before;
- hands-on engineering/hacking/security literacy.

### Extrapolation limit

Do not convert this into “academic genius.” The source gives uneven interests and a clear practical-versus-theory preference.

## 7.3 Practical versus theoretical learning

In `Side_Coop_StingerLN_1_*`, Lynae jokes that tools should solve the problem and leaves theory/principles to others. She tells people to get on a bike rather than overthink.

### Analytical implication

Her default cognition is applied and feedback-driven.

### Counterweight

The main plot shows sophisticated inference, technical troubleshooting, and planning. “Practical” does not mean intellectually shallow.

Only the behavioral pattern is high-confidence. The deeper motive remains open.

# 14. Social initiative

## Stable observations

Lynae frequently:

- notices someone;
- calls them over;
- proposes a next step;
- offers to guide;
- organizes an activity;
- invites;
- checks injury/status;
- sends the first message;
- converts uncertainty into “let's go.”

This is one of the highest-confidence behavioral priors in the corpus.

## Recipient sensitivity

She can also:

- accept “not now”;
- avoid digging into a past someone does not want to disclose;
- adapt game speed;
- say no reply is needed when Rover is busy;
- wait.

Therefore “forceful extrovert” is less accurate than:

> **high-initiative social actor with growing respect for recipient pace.**

# 23. What “ordinary” means for Lynae

Lynae's ordinary life remains unusually competent and unusually colorful.

“Ordinary student” does not mean the absence of weapons training, covert knowledge, engineering ability, or crisis usefulness.

It means those capacities no longer have to consume the entire definition of the person.

Ordinary Lynae gets to have:

- a bad snack decision;
- a credit deficit;
- a friend who beats a high score;
- a half-finished mod;
- a movie she wants to discuss;
- a market shopping list;
- a class she would rather skip;
- a teacher she teases;
- a lunch she accidentally abandons because someone needs help;
- a quiet minute where nothing has to be solved.

That is the strongest mundane reconstruction supported by the current source.
