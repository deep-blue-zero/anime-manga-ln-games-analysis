# Changli — inference at a time device and within a dream

Two previously underread actions test the attractive but overbroad description of Changli as someone who knows the answer before anyone else asks. They belong to **different source contexts**: the Mt. Firmament main-quest action `flow#/3147/10` and an optional roguelike/dream-mode interaction `flow#/4682/6`. They are comparable as examples of how she *states and limits a hypothesis*, not as one continuous canonical conversation or proof that the latter scene's dream speaker is the embodied Changli of every main-story moment [CHL-E37–E38; CHL-C33–C34].

## A device whose maker she does not yet understand

At Truthseeker's Pass, Rover identifies the place, and Changli recognizes a Chronosorter like one they previously encountered. She describes the cliff entrance as blocked, identifies three locks to break with ranged attacks, and pauses before explaining what troubles her (`flow#/3147/10/0–6`; `Chengxiaoshan_main_1_1_165_1–8`). The playable objective and the intellectual question are different. Her mechanical directions are local, while her question concerns the Court of Savantae's knowledge.

In ZH, she says mortals cannot *quickly* understand and use the principle of time order (`_165_8`), then says she has not seen a civilization whose technology controls time (`_165_9`). EN's “Mortals aren't supposed to be able to comprehend it” loses the qualifier about speed and sounds like a stronger impossibility claim. JA says it exceeds their understanding; KO retains difficulty in quickly understanding and using it. The shared core is a mismatch between this working apparatus and her known human technology, not a proof that no human could ever learn from Jué.

She offers one live possibility: perhaps the Sentinel taught the knowledge. She immediately asks *why* it would do so, then directs attention to Court objects inside the pass for clues (`_165_10–11`). This is a hypothesis followed by an evidence-gathering plan, not a revelation of Jué's motives or an archival finding. A later verified record of the Court's independent discovery, a direct Jué account, or another source of the technology would revise this local conjecture. The two quest-node keys `125000121_40` and `121800001_40` both reference the same flow state; their presence is not evidence that both were played or that a specific player completed the action [CHL-E37; CHL-C33].

The ten-item action contains nine accepted, source-voiced Changli turns and one Rover turn. The brief “what is it?” response after her pause is a player option, not a line she says. Each of the nine accepted semantic voice lines has four distinct, PCM-validated FLAC renders (36 objects total) in the private complete-voice index, with exact `en_vo_`/`ja_vo_`/`ko_vo_`/`zh_vo_` external-source paths. This proves object-level four-language availability and decode integrity, **not** that a human listened to the acting or watched the indicated gestures. The joins have no populated numeric Wwise event/media IDs; source key and virtual path are the available deterministic handles.

## A dream analogy is not a cosmology announcement

The second action is reachable by an interaction option on quest node `139000039_50`, which points to `剧情_1_4_肉鸽主线剧情_8_7` and actor entity `139800061` under technical talker 999. It is event/roguelike dream material, not a routine main-quest encounter. The source proves this presentation and the words attributed to Changli; it does **not** by itself settle whether this appearance has the same embodied continuity as main-story Changli. Preserve `event_mode` and `diegetic_instantiation=unknown_or_dream_presentation` when transporting its insight into a character model [CHL-E38].

She asks what a dream is, then describes recognizing her own dream through observation (`flow#/4682/6/0–1`; `Event_AYKXBRZM_112_1`, `_5`). The opening offers Rover three alternative answers: repetition of the past, a not-yet-arrived future, or another present (`_112_2–4`). Those alternatives are *Rover's selectable interpretations*, not three theories Changli asserts together. EN's later generalization about keen observation revealing “the deepest truths” is broader than ZH's report that observation let her know the truth of *this dream* (`_112_5`). One can model her observational habit without endorsing the English sentence as a universal law.

Her next move is an analogy: an amnesiac Rover in her “reality” might feel as if in a dream and might infer more from its traces (`_112_6–7`). The quotation marks around reality and the source's conditional wording invite metaphysical questions; they do not answer them. Rover can ask whether Solaris is merely their dream (`_112_8`). Changli's answer is a methodological brake: ZH calls it her own conjecture and says not to take it so seriously; EN says she could be mistaken, JA calls it a hypothesis and KO a guess (`_112_9`). A character continuation that announces “Solaris is literally Rover's dream; Changli knows this” reverses the direct disclaimer [CHL-C34].

She grounds the analogy in her limited experience: arriving in dream-Hongzhen, knowing the location while not knowing how or why she had come, then *suspecting* Rover's awakening in the Gorges of Spirits felt similar (`_112_10–12`). The subsequent Rover acknowledgment `_112_13` is an option text, not independent verification of her analogy in every route. She advises noticing small clues and hopes this dream will have helped (`_112_14–15`). That hope is personal and useful without converting an event dream into a confirmed origin story.

All ten accepted Changli turns in this action are source-voiced. Each has four distinct, PCM-validated FLAC objects (40 total) with key-matching external-source WEM basenames. Four text witnesses and four decoded render objects are available for each selected semantic line; human four-dub performance analysis remains open. The three opening options and two later options make a *choice-bearing* action. ## What can travel between the two scenes

The recurring, source-bounded trait is not “she is always right”; it is that she can move from an anomaly to a possible explanation, state where observation ends, and ask for further clues. The main quest demonstrates practical field inference under uncertainty. The optional dream encounter demonstrates an analogy she herself labels speculative. Other evidence also complicates this pattern: at `3133/4` she had hoped her feared Fractsidus/Jué inference was wrong, and the later Chronosorter records give one destroyed exception rather than a safe human procedure. A model may borrow the **questioning method** from both actions if it keeps era and medium attached. It may not fuse them into direct proof of cosmic ontology, Jué's intent, Rover's autobiographical past, or Changli's uninterrupted physical participation in the dream [CHL-E30–E31, E35–E38; CHL-C27, C31–C34].

Future human review should listen to `_165_8–11` and `_112_5–9` in all four dubs with the chosen options recorded, and inspect the event's runtime presentation before assigning a specific embodiment or acted certainty. The existing machine-level PCM proof supports retrieval, not those interpretations.
