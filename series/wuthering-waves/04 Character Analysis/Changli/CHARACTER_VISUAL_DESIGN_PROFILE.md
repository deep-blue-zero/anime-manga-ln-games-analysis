## Individualized summary

Changli combines an elegant strategist's composure with visible combustion. Coral-red hair fading into long white lengths, gold eyes, a clean white dress, black stockings, a black outer coat with quilted crimson lining, and flame-bright sleeve and weapon accents create a design that looks controlled at its center while burning at its edges.

## Evidence boundary

This first-pass profile is grounded in three decoded official-client UI textures: the large head icon, formation pile art, and activity portrait.
