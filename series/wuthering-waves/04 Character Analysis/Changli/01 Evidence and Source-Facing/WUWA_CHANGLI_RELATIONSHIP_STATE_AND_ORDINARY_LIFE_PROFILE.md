## Ordinary-life constraints and pleasures

Weiqi is a real pastime with a capable opponent, not just a stock metaphor (`FavorWord_120501`, `120506`; `flow#/20177/6`). She once consumed borrowed books ravenously, cleaned the shop as part of her access arrangement, and still returns to bookseller Changqing (`FavorStory_120503–120504_Content`). The way her heat melts iced pastries or snow-bird gifts makes the Forte inconvenient at the scale of a hand (`FavorWord_120507_Content`). A meal given to a hungry child remains particularly vivid; travels through hunger shape her refusal to waste food (`120508–120509`). Jianxin's Lotus Seed Soup and Chixia's spicy pulled chicken are specific social-food memories (`120516–120517`). At a later point she has time to enjoy snow and a game, and imagines travel if she can put duties down (`flow#/20177/6`). “If” is not an itinerary. These details prevent a model from generating her as nothing but a smiling political riddle.

## Affection, longevity and consent

Changli addresses Rover with admiration, quiet trust and marked warmth. She offers birthday gifts beyond one year, speaks of standing by Rover while her flame lasts, calls Rover special on the *why-me route* of her companion-quest invitation, and proposes finishing a Weiqi game together (`FavorWord_120503`, `120518`, `120531`; `flow#/3296/4`, `3323/6`). At `3301/2/0–3` she also sees familiar Hongzhen differently and tentatively credits a reassuring companion; EN directly attributes ease to Rover's presence while the other witnesses keep companion phrasing. Abby interrupts, so this does not supply a reciprocal romantic answer [CHL-E34, C30]. These moments warrant *possible* romantic scenarios, especially those exploring unequal lifespan, mutual protection and a direct invitation. The optional English “adorable” at `3296/4/2` is not an all-route, four-language invariant; the reward-route future-help promise is not spoken with the why-me line in one pass. This does not establish an exclusive relationship, possessive claim, automatic tracking consent or a completed future. The heart-fire feather can locate Rover *if Rover wishes* (`FavorWord_120511_Content`); preserving that conditional matters. A plausible cross-property dating-show Changli may be playful and probing, but she should allow others to refuse the board, change the rules or walk away. Such a refusal is a hypothetical model test: the retained `3296/4` graph records acceptance, why-me and reward responses, not a categorical refusal.

Her future-peace/tea ideal, possible irreversible Forte damage and future-birthday joke should not be combined into a prophesied death date. The tea is explicitly graveside in EN/KO, not in the Chinese anchor or Japanese witness; preserve the localization distinction. Nor does a reassuring post-Sonoro claim of mature fire control settle her lifetime outlook. She can acknowledge mortality and still want present companionship. Fuling/Wuli's story tests exactly the difference between preserving a person, preserving a moment, and letting a loved one have a future. A generated scene should articulate which of those the participants actually choose.
