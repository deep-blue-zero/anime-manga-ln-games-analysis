# Changli — Chronosorter evidence, the exceptional experiment and a dangerous extrapolation

Changli's Mt. Firmament investigation supplies a better test of her strategic intelligence than a generic claim that she “sees every move.” In three source actions she moves from an observed physical clue to old technical records, separates an apparatus's limited success from a human solution, and eventually argues with Jinhsi about what the proposed solution could cost. This is an account of her *reasoning under source limits*, not proof she designed every later decision or possessed direct experimental access. The direct dialogue is `flow#/3175/5`, `3176/5`, `3177/5`; the subsequent risk confrontation is `3209/3/26–47` [CHL-E12, E30–E31; CHL-C27–C28].

## A clue is not a medical verdict

At the Flare Crest, Changli identifies blood and scales as Jinhsi's (`3175/5/6`, `Chengxiaoshan_main_1_1_176_9`) and says she is *likely* close to Overclocking (`/7`, `_12`). The four witnesses preserve some sign of inference: ZH `更有可能`, EN “likely,” JA speaks of a sign, and KO `가능성이 커요`. The source gives no bedside examination or exact remaining endurance. Her urgency at `/8` is grounded in the clue and history, not clairvoyance. A scene model can let her be alarmed without converting probable danger into a confirmed collapse [CHL-E30; CHL-C27].

The next file says Jué lost a capacity to restore normal time while retaining stasis and reversal (`3176/5/0–4`, especially `Chengxiaoshan_main_1_1_179_2`). Changli attributes a citywide consequence if Jué dies to the *records' theory* (`/4`, `_5`); she says “nothing we can do” only within that documentary frame (`/7`, `_9`). She then names the Mianloong Chamber as temporary relief, not a cure, and a Chronosorter as an artificial Resonance Object with a narrow restoration range (`/8–12`, `_10–17`). There is a first-person analytical move here: she refuses to turn a working small machine into a citywide remedy. The archive verdict is provisional because a later file supplies a counterexample; the writing must not retrospectively present either document as a full clinical or engineering trial on Jinhsi [CHL-E30; CHL-C27].

## One exceptional machine, many destroyed machines

At `3177/5`, Changli says the devices operate through resonance with Jué but fail outside the Sentinel's range or Mt. Firmament's anomalous time flow and have limited capacity (`/1–5`, `Chengxiaoshan_main_1_1_205_3–9`). The researchers proposed that colliding resonant frequencies *might* produce a Second Awakening (`/6–7`, `_10–11`). The following testimony matters as a denominator: countless devices broke under the collision; **one** briefly restored time, then broke too (`/9–10`, `_16`, `_19`). She calls the latter enough to show increased resonance power was possible (`/11`, `_32`). ZH qualifies that conclusion with `至少` (“at least”); EN's “This proved” is more categorical in tone, but neither text shows a durable, safely repeatable intervention in a living person. The result is a clue to possibility and mechanism, not an observed success rate for Jinhsi [CHL-E31; CHL-C27].

The researcher floated Overclocking as a possible prerequisite (`/12–14`, `_20–22`). Rover can voice that it sounds bad, and Changli agrees that exhausting oneself leads to destruction (`/15`, `_23–24`). EN's “No, it's not” answers Rover's negative appraisal; ZH `没错`, JA and KO affirm the danger rather than contradict it. A model that tells Jinhsi merely to overexert herself until she awakens reverses this explicit warning. Nor can one damaged machine establish that a human Resonator must undergo an identical process or will be saved by it [CHL-C28].

Changli notices another feature of the one exception. The device required a human operator, while Jué appeared to act under another person's instruction; as it neared failure, a “peculiar visitor” seems to have ordered Jué, after which some energy apparently passed to the device (`/17–20`, `_26–27`, `_35`). ZH `似乎` and KO's corresponding tentative language preserve uncertainty about command and transfer. EN's “thanks to that order” sounds like a stronger causal link. The archived sequence may justify investigating Rover's ability to authorize a similar transfer, but it does not prove the visitor's exact identity solely from these lines, a reproducible procedure, or a guarantee that Rover will rescue Jinhsi. The option text after `/20` poses possible interpretations—Overclocking, near-breakdown, energy transfer—before Changli asks Rover for an idea (`/20`, `_28–31`); do not flatten those player prompts into three independent findings she certified [CHL-E31; CHL-C27].

## Why her objection is not an about-face

Later Jinhsi says she will fulfill Jué's prophecy if necessary (`3209/3/29–31`). Changli asks whether she has considered the consequences, says collision could precipitate Overclocking and compares a human catastrophe with the destroyed machines (`/32–36`). Jinhsi answers that she learned risk-taking from her teacher (`/37–38`); Changli distinguishes risk she herself took from a doctrine of dying quickly and says a living Magistrate can protect Jinzhou longer (`/39–41`). Then she explicitly says she will *not* stop Jinhsi and that she brought Rover as a possible helper (`/43–47`). That is an unresolved tension, not hypocrisy: Changli discovers a technical possibility, opposes a lethal way of obtaining it, and still seeks an option beyond refusal. Jinhsi authors her own choice; Changli's evidence and warning shape the decision space without giving her a veto [CHL-E12, E31; CHL-C03, C28].

For reconstruction, distinguish a reported experiment, Changli's inference, a present bodily clue, and a later character's intention. Let her state what she knows, what was only hypothesized, and why a unique destroyed machine does not make a friend's survival predictable. A future primary source documenting controlled repeated human successes or Changli's direct knowledge of a safe protocol would materially change the risk reading. Human-heard delivery and a runtime traversal may refine the urgency or route, but neither is claimed in this pre-AV packet.
