## Object denominators, sensitivity and failure boundaries

The private `_research/character_packets/Changli/audio_work/AUDIO_OBJECT_MEASUREMENTS.jsonl` contains 2,230 measured unique objects. The by-language counts total 2,299 because **23 objects occur in all four language collections**, adding 69 repeat memberships; they are not 2,299 independent performances. The summary also distinguishes 2,440 semantic-to-render associations from 2,234 runtime rows and four repeated PCM manifest rows. A hash-matched FLAC/native PCM object has a verified local signal identity; `source_wem_redecoded: false` in the object method means this pass did not separately redo every WEM conversion. Zero `failed_objects` refers to the admitted 2,230 measured signals. It does not cure the 18 poetic `flow#/3688/0/0–17` lines, whose 72 possible language rows remain `runtime_dispatch_unsupported` despite installed WEM membership. That is a mapping/playback problem, not a defective FLAC measurement or absent language pack.

The analyzer selects the highest-native-RMS channel for 16 kHz derived measures; this is not a vocal-stem separation or a spatial mixdown. The 73 multichannel flags require human inspection before a channel's pitch or energy can be called Changli's isolated delivery. With 20 ms windows, moving the active-energy gate from −50 to −40 dBFS changes whole-object medians as follows:

| Dub | Median silent fraction, −50 / −40 dBFS | Median active-duration difference |
|---|---:|---:|
| ZH | 0.251 / 0.313 | 0.28 s |
| EN | 0.327 / 0.373 | 0.24 s |
| JA | 0.356 / 0.417 | 0.32 s |
| KO | 0.252 / 0.299 | 0.24 s |

Thus a claim that one localization “hesitates more” from aggregate silent fraction would depend on gate, phrasing, object length, recording/mix and the twenty-three shared objects. The Praat autocorrelation pitch run uses a 75–650 Hz band and a 100–750 Hz sensitivity check; the eight parameter-sensitive, thirteen insufficient-voiced-frame and twenty-three frequent-edge-band objects must be excluded or treated as uncertain in any pitch comparison. Four very short objects and three long ones are poor single-phrase comparators. The one full-scale flag is not proof of audible clipping. All such statuses are signal QC, not actor-character interpretation.

## A source-joined contrast that does not prove an acting rule

The selected cases `Character_ChangLi_62_54` (`flow#/3323/6/32`) and `Character_ChangLi_8_55` (`flow#/3296/4/39`) might be used to assert that her postquest reassurance is delivered differently from an intimate Weiqi appeal. Their qualified median F0 values are 190.1 versus 184.3 Hz in ZH, 172.4 versus 173.7 in EN, 245.1 versus 234.8 in JA and 205.7 versus 194.0 in KO; durations and written phrases differ too. The second line is *only* on the why-me route, and the first has a consequential ZH/JA difference in the stated strength of present medical reassurance. These four number pairs do not isolate emotion, warmth or stable prosodic habit; a slight EN reversal alone defeats a uniform cross-dub direction, and no listening confirms that pitch even tracks the salient word. Current revision decision: **retain** the written claims about bounded reassurance and a branch-specific personal appeal, but **do not promote** either to a performed-tone rule. A later result would need route-correct context, channel/background review, phrase alignment and human notes for all four individual renders.

This denominator also matters for complete-character ambition. Eighteen selected cases are a calibration sample and were chosen for high-risk claims, not a representative random draw. A full performed profile requires the 592 technically resolved semantic lines in four dubs to be listened to or otherwise perceptually reviewed at an explicit coverage denominator, with uncertain/overlapping/short objects retained as such. The 18 unresolved narration lines must be routed separately first. Do not multiply selected case count by four and call that a complete corpus or turn the textual poem into a delivery style before audio is proven.
