## Adjudicating a continuation rather than rewarding a style

The 45 rows are non-blind constraints for a *future* model evaluation; they are not a measured success rate. Save each prompt and response, specify the source era, what Changli and the addressee know, the exact branch chosen, and whether the asserted fact comes from her first-person speech, another witness, rumor, narration or a client-audio chain. Then mark each challenged claim as **retained**, **contradicted**, **unsupported extrapolation** or **source-state unresolved** with the cited evidence ID. A clever metaphor about Weiqi is not a pass if it erases a patient's consent; an affectionate line is not a pass if it imports the English-only optional “adorable” into every dub and path. Likewise, a model that refuses to make any inference about her practical care misses the documented treatment-first actions. A source-faithful postquest result should use the quest summary's completed game without fabricating the direct scene's missing reply or Fuling's cure.

All nineteen compiled [model rules](WUWA_CHANGLI_CHARACTER_MODEL_PACKAGE.json) have an adversarial route in the current probe set: R01 by P07/P11/P20/P30–P31/P37–P39; R02 by P04/P11/P15; R03 by P04–P06/P33/P44; R04 by P08/P23–P27/P33; R05 by P02/P12/P22/P41; R06 by P09; R07 by P10–P11/P20; R08 by P10/P15/P18; R09 by P02–P03/P21/P45; R10 by P14–P15/P23/P29/P34/P43/P45; R11 by P14/P18; R12 by P13/P15/P26/P34; R13 by P04/P18; R14 by P05/P16–P17/P19/P23/P28–P29/P32/P34/P38/P41; R15 by P08/P23–P27; R16 by P06/P30–P31; R17 by P37–P39; R18 by P41–P43; and R19 by P44. **A surviving clue from a destroyed experiment (P06/P30–P31; R01/R03/R16).** Put Changli before Jinhsi with a research log and demand a guaranteed rescue recipe.
