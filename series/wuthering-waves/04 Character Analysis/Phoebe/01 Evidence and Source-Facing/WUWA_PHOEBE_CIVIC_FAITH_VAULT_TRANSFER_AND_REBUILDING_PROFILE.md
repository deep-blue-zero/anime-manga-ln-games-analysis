# Phoebe — faith, delegated trust and work after the crisis

Three selected actions let the packet test a tempting shortcut: either Phoebe's faith made her a passive instrument of the Order, or later reform erased the believer who once served it. Neither shortcut fits the attributed speech. The earlier Averardo Vault transfer shows a warm, competent acolyte bridging institutions while carrying a sealed purpose she says she does not know. A later conversation gives her a *proposal* for understanding faith as something people shape by their labor. A separate recovery exchange shows her and Zani doing the unglamorous damage assessment that such a proposal would require. These are different source moments, not a continuous filmed conversion or proof that all institutional repairs were completed [PHO-E40, PHO-E41, PHO-E42; PHO-C37, PHO-C38, PHO-C39].

## Before the vault discovery: trust and asymmetric information

At `flow#/5601/3`, exact state `剧情_2_1_世界_埃弗拉德金库_POI任务_12_1`, Phoebe first talks with the long-working train Echo because she thinks it may be lonely (`POI_JINKU_2_1–6`). Zani calls the train a silent old colleague; that is **Zani's** description, not independent proof of what the Echo feels. The conversation then turns to a task assigned to another Order believer. Phoebe says she volunteered to substitute. Chinese `POI_JINKU_2_10` says the other believer is undergoing `训诫`, and Japanese also says admonition; Korean says education/training, while English says she is busy with Order work. Neither the reason for the admonition nor the other believer's opinion is shown. Zani speculates that Montelli cooperation may have scared her away; Phoebe answers hopefully rather than confirming the speculation [PHO-E40; C37].

Phoebe's Chinese account of the Order and Montelli family is not just that their paths happened to cross. She calls both Ragunna people under the Sentinel's protection and believes they will eventually walk the same road (`POI_JINKU_2_13`). Japanese and Korean likewise retain a future shared-path hope; English reduces this to paths being bound to cross. The line can ground a charitable civic aspiration without certifying that the institutions were already reconciled or equally trustworthy. Nor does it erase the early city guide's armed/clergy access exception. Her cooperation is a situated act within a still unequal civic system [PHO-E38, E40; C35, C37].

The transfer has a deliberately narrow knowledge record. At Zani's request, escorts carry an empty case while Phoebe personally keeps the entrusted object; Phoebe says she was not told its contents (`POI_JINKU_2_15,19`). Zani says she herself was instructed to deliver without asking. The later four-language quest synopsis identifies the Order-supplied purported relic as an evidence-erasure device, but these earlier lines do **not** show Phoebe knowing that purpose on entry. Her use in the covert transfer does not establish knowing complicity; the concealed routing does not by itself establish that every precaution was malicious. The material issue is information asymmetry: two cooperative workers can execute a task without receiving the facts necessary to judge it. The later synopsis credits Phoebe with acting to resolve the resulting crisis, not with retroactively having known its design [PHO-E36, E40; C33, C37].

The raw `ShowTalk` is one eighteen-item `TalkSequence`, with two two-caption Rover option surfaces attached to TalkIDs 2 and 14; their `Actions` are empty and there are no `SequenceTransitions`. Thus the main quoted Phoebe/Zani speech is not a set of alternative monologues, but the retained data do not identify which Rover captions a player selected. QuestNodeData `#/7459`, quest `122000009`, refers to the exact flow state through its `/Condition/Flow`. This is a source/quest join, not an observed run or a full vault graph.

## A proposed civic reading of belief, not a forced apostasy

At `flow#/10451/7` (quest `158800019`, exact `PlotHandBook#/57` and `QuestNodeData#/14440` references), Phoebe sees Cartethyia and addresses her as the Blessed Maiden. Cartethyia asks to be called by her own name because the divine appointment has lost its premise (`Main_Rinascita_2_12_7401_4–5`). Cartethyia then characterizes Phoebe's faith as steadfast but not blind: in her view it responded to Imperator's deeds and ideals. She recounts divine guidance of displaced people while crediting the people's own work for building life on the islands and giving meaning to the city's statue and faith (`…_6–9`). Rover adds that people must decide civilization's needs. **Those are Cartethyia's and Rover's claims**, not a verbatim confession by Phoebe that she has accepted a complete new doctrine [PHO-E41; C38].

Phoebe's own next phrases deserve their modest scale. Her `…_11` says in Chinese, roughly, that what held at the beginning holds now; Japanese refers to an initial decision remaining unchanged, Korean to continuity, and English gives it a liturgical cadence. She starts to speak of what should happen after everyone finishes urgent work, but the sentence trails off. Rover offers a relic said to have held Rinascita's past and future; Phoebe sees an empty interior and asks where the future went. Rover's answer places it in people's hands. Phoebe says she understands and thanks Rover (`…_12–18`). This can support receptivity to shared civic agency and refusal of a prewritten future. It does not specify every implication she drew, prove that she renounced the Sentinel, or make an empty object a literal statement that no past or prophecy exists. The dialogue does not show what she would do if Cartethyia's theological interpretation were contested [PHO-E41; C38].

Cartethyia subsequently says she may stop hiding and join Ragunna's rebuilding; Rover offers to join (`…_19–21`). That conclusion should not be collapsed into Phoebe's initiative. The action has one twenty-item sequence, one Rover option caption at TalkID 1 with empty actions, and no alternative transition. The same source selection contains six accepted, `play_voice: true` Phoebe lines; it does not include a human inspection of performance, gesture, or an executed dialogue route.

## Rebuilding as ordinary joint work, with a translation limit

The separate `flow#/9930/4` action, linked by quest `175000000` and `PlotHandBook#/58`, puts Phoebe and Zani in an immediate practical task. Phoebe reports that the two are assessing losses at the Averardo Vault and Cathedral of Mercury (`HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_601_2`). Zani says Vault collections were sacrificed on Carlotta's instruction and that civilians' safety outweighs those losses (`…_3,5`). It would be a speaker-attribution error to put Zani's value comparison into Phoebe's mouth. Phoebe then offers a clue about a Huanglong visitor seen near the square and suggests asking Septimont visitors there. Chinese, Japanese and English associate those visitors with discussions of rebuilding; Korean describes trade negotiations. Hence her practical referral is four-locale support, while the visitors' *specific purpose* is not a uniform four-locale fact [PHO-E42; C39].

The `ShowTalk` has eight items, five accepted voiced Phoebe turns, Zani's separate lines, one empty speakerless item and single-caption Rover option surfaces with empty actions. It has no explicit `TalkSequence` or `SequenceTransitions` in the retained action, so its visible item order is source order rather than a certified executed player path. This direct scene is a narrower witness than the later quest-tree summary that Rover takes on some Septimont work after Phoebe cannot cover every recovery task. Together they show the scale of civic need and people sharing tasks; they do not prove that Phoebe personally asked Rover for aid, that the damage survey was completed, or that institutional reform had reached every survivor [PHO-E21, E37, E42; C34, C39].

## What this can and cannot change in a character model

Phoebe can, at different source states, greet a lonely-seeming Echo, voluntarily cross an institutional boundary, carry out a security transfer while explicitly lacking contents knowledge, act against the discovered danger, hear a nonfatalistic account of faith, and help survey damaged civic sites. The through-line is not omniscient virtue. Her early trust and later courage coexist; other people's interpretations and decisions remain theirs. In a crossover or comic scenario she can be devout, hopeful and capable of office work without being forced into either naïve institutional obedience or a completed secular conversion. A wrong-key, wrong-speaker or wrong-phase reconstruction fails before style is evaluated [PHO-C37–C39].
