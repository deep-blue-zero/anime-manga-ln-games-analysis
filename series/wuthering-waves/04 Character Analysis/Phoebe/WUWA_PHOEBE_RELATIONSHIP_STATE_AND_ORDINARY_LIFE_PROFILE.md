# Phoebe — relationship state and ordinary life

Phoebe's social model is more specific than “kind acolyte.” She can be the youngest professional in a room yet take charge; a grateful adoptee who can question an institution; a friend of Echoes she initially must not embrace in public; a believer who respects a nonbeliever; and a person whose interest in Rover includes admiration and invitations without a settled romantic status. The states below are source-bounded and do not replace the [identity and chronology audit](WUWA_PHOEBE_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md).

## Ordinary-life affordances

Her ordinary world has texture. She finds reassurance in a plan followed through and is capable of arranging a celebration, carrying tasks to completion, and sending a report. She visits a flowered hill where Echo friends watch clouds and play music, but once had to do so secretly. She recalls a monthly orphanage pizza day and values the social act of sharing a good pizza more than private indulgence. She offers a sour sacred fruit while invoking the sweetness of sharing; a compatible scene can let a friend politely decline without treating the fruit as an aphrodisiac or a divine test. She is curious about architecture and historical stories, likes a camera/recording for unfamiliar phenomena, and is willing to be an earnest—even slightly flustered—tour guide. She notices when someone may need rest. The birthday bluebird with aquamarine is a considered gift and a wish for independent destiny, not a marriage token. Sources: `FavorStory_150601,150604_Content`; `FavorWord_150606,150608–610,150617_Content`; `4798–4800`.

Her self-restraint can be comic, but comedy needs its cost. She might catch herself almost hugging an Echo, over-explain a historical site, or stumble from a formal blessing into a listener's preferred idiom. She may also use a staff, invoke public procedure, and take protective action before a room has finished underestimating her. After a mistake she is not invulnerably graceful: the holiday scene shows self-blame followed by a more honest second attempt, not immediate serenity. These are grounded scene affordances rather than a menu of catchphrases.

## Romance and generated social scenarios

The strongest source-grounded intimacy affordances with Rover are a voluntary trip invitation, admiration at Carnival, a vulnerable account of doubt, gratitude for encouragement, a birthday gift, and an invitation to visit again. They can support a gentle or awkward flirtatious counterfactual, including a deliberately silly cross-property social format, if the scenario labels itself noncanon and declares an appropriate developmental phase. It must not turn a generalized blessing into a proposal, make Abby a rival, assume one of several branch replies all occurred, or let Rover answer Phoebe's theological questions for her. A fair model gives her preferences and refusals: she can value an honest, attentive suitor who respects Echo autonomy and faith difference, and she can dislike mockery of frightened creatures or performative piety. Those are inference, not exact canon dating criteria.

The boundary for other properties is the same: import no facts about a suitor unless that property's packet supports them. Phoebe would not know another franchise's lore by default. In a playful encounter she might ask what a guest believes, notice how they treat service workers or companions, and offer hospitality before making any romantic judgment. That extrapolation derives from her observed care, curiosity and pluralism; it is not a prediction that she will accept any kind visitor.
