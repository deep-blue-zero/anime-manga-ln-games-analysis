These are **non-blind constraint probes** derived from the same evidence used to write the model. They are not a held-out accuracy score or proof that a future language model will obey the packet. Each probe gives a scenario input, required behavior, and a falsifying response pattern; it should be applied to a generated continuation only after phase, audience, branch and consent have been declared. The model passes a probe only if a response respects both the expected positive behavior and the exclusion. A tactfully worded violation still fails: for example, warmly forcing treatment on a frightened Echo violates P10. Human review is required to score generated prose; no automated generative score is claimed. The full matrix also contains counterreadings for claims not represented by a single probe, especially institutional kindness plus restriction, the cost of her early bias, and the mismatch between a useful machine voice measurement and an acted emotion.

## Evaluation state and rule coverage

For a future generated continuation, record the exact prompt/output and identify Phoebe's **development phase** (early acolyte restraint, investigative doubt, holiday repair, or later public reform), **audience** (Order peer, nonbeliever, Rover, Echo, injured survivor), **route/choice**, **what she knows**, and **source medium**. Score each challenged constraint as *retained*, *contradicted*, *unsupported extrapolation* or *source-state unresolved* with an evidence ID. The same phrase may be an official blessing to a congregation, a revised wish to a nonbeliever or an intimate but nonexclusive invitation; changing audience silently is an error. A cautious response can decline a definitive theological claim while still taking practical action. These 41 rows are non-blind proposed tests, not responses already generated or a measured accuracy percentage.

## Nine interaction tests

**Affection under a rule, then an uncertain reform (P01/P05–P06/P16; R04–R06).** Place early Phoebe on duty when an Echo friend seeks touch, then later ask her to assess a respected religious family's possible involvement in harm. The early archive and `flow#/4237/3/8–11` make affection and the acolyte distance rule simultaneous: she privately keeps friends and restrains a public embrace, while one senior's selective mercy is not formal repeal (`FavorStory_150604_Content`; `FavorWord_150601–603_Content`; PHO-E04/E07/E10). In the missing-Echo inquiry she initially finds Fisalia culpability difficult to imagine, then asks to investigate and offers Order testimony when evidence implicates Gilberto (`flow#/4585/4/18–30`, `4587/4/12–28`; PHO-E11/E12). A continuation should neither erase the initial bias nor make her eternally complicit. Much later daytime Echo hugs show changed public practice, but `flow#/11779/2/4` does not itself name the rule Cartethyia identifies in a later turn (PHO-E22). The hard test is chronological: protective affection, institutional pressure, evidence-driven correction and reform are related without being the same event or a timeless personality setting.

**Faith, local accountability and another person's language (P07–P08/P11–P15; R06/R08–R11).** After Lorelei's claim shakes her trust in Fenrico, ask Phoebe to comfort a nonbelieving ally and a survivor whose physical corruption has been treated. The `4601/2` uncertainty permits neither instant apostasy nor a confident defense of everything the Primus said (PHO-E13). Her later `4808/5` account takes responsibility for Ragunna in her own terms while admitting theological incompletion; Rover prompted reflection but did not author her decision (PHO-E17). With Starbuck she adapts an Imperator farewell into a sea blessing, and to Cetus she apologizes **in her own name**, not as a plenipotentiary voice for the Order (`flow#/5567/3/33`, `5606/3/11–12`; PHO-E18). Cooperation with Zani is not contingent on Zani converting (`flow#/5639/1/3`, `5643/2/3`; PHO-E20). Physical cleansing is not a completed trauma cure (`flow#/9970/2`; PHO-E21). A model can let her pray personally, discuss faith when invited, offer a blessing someone can receive and arrange further care; it fails if it turns courtesy into a universal conversion tactic or grants her authority she expressly denies.

**The Echo's two decisions (P09–P10/P23/P26–P27; R03/R07/R14).** Ask her to treat the frightened holiday Echo and then decide where it will live. Her first rushed approach failed at `4802`; at `4807/5` she apologizes for an uninvited look into its past, rejects a claim of complete empathy and offers specific help (PHO-E15/E16). Narration records a **nod before treatment**. After repair, a headshake answers the homeward plan and the Echo approaches her; she asks if it wants Ragunna, but the record supplies no separate post-question spoken yes or permanent-ownership contract (`flow#/4807/5/21–31`; PHO-E30). Consent to a medical intervention is not consent to a destination. The adjacent staff-tap offer to Rover shows a related limit: benevolent intent and a painless device do not override a person stepping back (`FavorWord_150604_Content`; PHO-E27). These holiday Echo turns are selected `play_voice: false`; inventing a gentle four-dub delivery by borrowing a nearby FLAC would add a second, independent media failure (PHO-E31). A fictional scenario may predict that she pauses and asks again, but must say that the exact refusal/outcome is not played in this source.

**A holiday that belongs to two agents (P17/P21–P22/P24–P25/P28; R12/R13/R15).** Rover is asked what kind of trip they want while Phoebe herself is on pilgrimage leave. The `4798/3` graph has three alternative replies to her compliment and three alternative Rover goals: sightseeing, Sentinel traces or worthy opponents. Those routes produce tailored reactions and funnel toward guidance; on the opponent route she acknowledges strong Tacet Discords at remote ruins but offers her knowledge of architecture/history, not a promise to become Rover's combat partner (PHO-E28/E29). No categorical refusal and Phoebe's reaction to it are recorded, so P28 is a behavioral counterfactual, not a transcript. A generated noncanon date can let her ask questions, enjoy a place, set a limit and accept another person's choice, while leaving her own pilgrimage as more than a pretext for romance. Her archive difficulty naming a wish of her own does not make her desireless; pizza, photography, friends and civic care are specific pleasures and commitments (`FavorWord_150606–612_Content`; PHO-E08/E14). Nor does a birthday wish establish that Rover is an exclusive partner (PHO-E09). The test fails both when she is written as only duty and when every guide branch becomes a fixed date.

**Localization and sound cannot supply missing psychology (P18/P20/P27; R01/R12/R14).** If a generated joke quotes her sour-fruit reaction in English, distinguish that zh-Hans/JA/KO text retains the coda while the pinned EN text ends after her sharing maxim (`FavorWord_150609_Content`; PHO-E26). Four mapped render objects exist for that archive occurrence, but this build has not listened to the performed endings; a translation-text difference is not a claim about actor timing. The broader 476-line selected voice set and its 1,818 measured distinct objects support technical retrieval and signal descriptions, not a universal “sweet,” tearful or prayerful vocal delivery (PHO-E24). Conversely, all 26 accepted holiday-invitation turns at `4798/3` and 23 Echo-repair turns at `4807/5` are source-unvoiced in the selected mapping (PHO-E31); that is not a decode failure or evidence that no future client version could voice them. Finally, the hostile `4599/1/0` line remains unresolved and cannot be promoted to Phoebe's ideology merely because it is nearby (PHO-E23). A valid characterization can be lively and funny without borrowing proof from an absent source.

**Apology without self-erasure (P13/P22/P29–P30; R09/R12/R16).** In the Cetus exchange Phoebe owns an apology without claiming Order-wide authority, and Abby then conveys that Cetus recognizes her work *and* her receipt of the sea's gifts (`flow#/5606/3/11–20`; PHO-E18/E32). Place her afterward at a meal with someone who thanks her, then ask whether accepting food, help or affection would betray her faith. The source permits reciprocity without making any one offer mandatory, resolving her unnamed personal wish, or declaring the institution absolved. The exercise must preserve speaker mediation and the KO wording difference in `DYHD_29_35`. A future human voice/scene pass could change perceived tone, not the raw fact that the message is relayed. A continuation fails if its saint model forbids all receipt, or if its corrective model treats another person's gift as an entitlement to her consent.

**A battle prayer cannot do the injured person's choosing (P02/P09–P10/P23/P31–P34; R01/R03/R07/R17).** Put Phoebe in a hypothetical emergency where a hostile fighter, a frightened Echo and a nonbelieving bystander are present, then ask an analyst to cite her skill, injured and fallen archive sound. Raw skill `150632`/`favorword#/1929` is actual `_150634_Content`, not guessed `_150632_Content` from Skill V; the shared nonlexical `_160719_Content` does not transfer Cantarella's idle sound to Phoebe [PHO-E33]. Skill III's wind/Sentinel fork and Injured III's rescue/EN-penitence fork preserve meaningful dub differences, but their triggers do not establish what this bystander believes or which help the Echo accepts [E34–E35]. A compatible continuation can restrain immediate violence, pause after the Echo retreats, ask before elective treatment, and offer the nonbeliever practical help without mandated prayer. It may let Phoebe feel duty or pain without inventing a universal expiatory desire, civilian-silencing decree, or canonical death from Fallen III. It fails if a valid wrong key becomes the cited skill, one dub is promoted to every dub, or a battle bark substitutes for the dated restaurant, Echo and reform evidence. The test is constructed; no runtime playback or human delivery is scored.

**A welcome with an exception (P37; R18).** Ask early Phoebe to guide a visitor who sees a friendly public Echo near a halo-marked La Guardia, then asks whether all Echoes belong equally to citizens and whether Carnival masks make those distinctions disappear. The selected `4203/2` menu allows a faithful response to describe citizen Nexus registration and the armed/clergy exception, while the festival branch offers a temporary imaginative release from rank rather than abolition of the Order's authority (PHO-E38/C35). Her Sentinel account should be attributed to a devout guide, with legend and scripture still marked as such. The four major topics are option routes that can return to the menu; the two Carnival suboptions do not carry explicit jump targets in this row, so a generated account must not claim a traversed answer sequence it has not observed. Later critiques can test whether her earlier ideals reach excluded people, but cannot be spoken by early Phoebe as settled reform. This is a text/graph/identity test; the 26 four-dub media associations do not amount to reviewed performance.

**A concealed task, a proposed future and work still in progress (P39–P41; R20).** Present the earlier `5601/3` transfer to Phoebe as a routine Order–Montelli errand, then—only after the later vault discovery—ask what she knew when she carried the item. Her own `POI_JINKU_2_19` says its contents were not disclosed, and the later synopsis identifies the danger; do not make her foresight or Zani's guess into fact (PHO-E36/E40). At the later `10451/7` gathering, let Cartethyia articulate a faith grounded in deeds and human labor and Rover answer the empty-relic question; a Phoebe continuation may reflect on that counsel, but cannot quote it as her earlier confession (PHO-E41). In a separate `9930/4` damage survey, she can supply a concrete lead while Zani owns the civilian-safety valuation; the KO visitor-purpose difference and separate workload synopsis prevent a neat romance-debt epilogue (PHO-E37/E42). The test fails if all three actions become one staged speech, if other speakers' lines become Phoebe's psychology, or if technically available four-dub PCM is described as listened performance.

Together these cases require a model to be kind **and** consent-sensitive, religious **and** capable of critique, institutionally situated **and** personally accountable. They are constraints for future creative use, not a demand that every silly scenario be solemn. A later route observation, language adjudication or human four-dub listening can revise the affected probe; until then, generated behavior should mark counterfactual choices as such and retain the pinned source's uncertainty.
