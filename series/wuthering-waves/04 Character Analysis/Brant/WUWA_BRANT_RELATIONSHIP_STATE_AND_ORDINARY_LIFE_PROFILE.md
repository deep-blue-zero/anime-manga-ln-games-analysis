## Ordinary-life anchors

Brant is not only a cape, blade and naval metaphor. The favor stories give stage upkeep, ticket-giving, sea wind, shared beach meals, children's candy, crew-specific brewing, trying a familiar restaurant's pizza, a sold house, an unfinished childhood piano lesson and present hurdy-gurdy study. The short archive admission of being out of money (`FavorWord_120607_Content`) and later event-prize pressure make logistics a real constraint. His belongings—the masks, tern box, ammonoid and telescope gift—matter because they relate performance, memory, navigation and invitation without being a fixed romance code.

## Hypothetical intimacy / romance affordance

A plausible *hypothetical* slow-building bond would involve a partner who can choose their own course, witness unmasked work without demanding a confession, respect the crew as individual people, share ordinary rest and meet an adventure's price honestly. A choice to follow him should remain revocable; he can invite but not overwrite agency. Textual support: his freedom ethic (`FavorStory_120602_Content`; `flow#/5357/3`), choice-aware captain offer (`5238/2`), private/home scenes (`FavorStory_120603`, `_120605`), and beach/birthday invitations. This is an affordance for writing constrained scenarios, **not** a canonical romance with Rover or anyone else. Counter-scenarios should test what he does when a suitor idolizes only his stage face, demands exclusive possession of the troupe, hides material risk from companions, or mistakes a theatrical flirtation for consent. The evidence does not warrant a numerical desirability score, guaranteed response, or a fixed preference for any crossover character.
