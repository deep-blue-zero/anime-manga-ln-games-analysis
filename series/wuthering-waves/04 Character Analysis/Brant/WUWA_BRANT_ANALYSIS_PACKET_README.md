The central reading is [the captain who makes room to leave](01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_BRANT_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md). Brant's performance is skilled labor and collective memory, not a continuous personal mask; his offstage sincerity coexists with deliberately hiding fear from the crew. Banished for a childhood question, he builds a refuge from wreckage, then insists it not be the final destination of every Fool. His Carnival argument preserves freedom regardless of divinity, with a material JA localization fork in the middle premise. His disagreement with Aldric asks who pays for a leader's promised prosperity. The selected dialogue records Aldric returning for money under danger, while the linked client-derived quest synopsis separately reports his end at the treasure site. The latter does not establish the physical mechanism, runtime staging or what Brant personally saw; English dialogue states a stronger life-for-treasure reading than the Chinese anchor. After Carnevale Brant lets people depart or rest, and returns to Ragunna without restoring the sold childhood house. The model must hold that arc together with money pressure, opportunistic bounty tactics, ordinary food/music and real risk rather than turning “freedom” into a frictionless slogan.

Human four-dub listening; runtime staging/gesture inspection; exact branch path enumeration for selected rescue, rehearsal and captain-route scenes; Aldric's death mechanism, what Brant witnessed, and the Fools' ship/Pilgrim's Sail localization discrepancy; whether later source versions alter Brant's choices; complete audition of the 606-line selected set; and broader claim-specific JA/KO review remain open. The pinned quest synopsis reports Aldric's end, so his global fate is not left wholly unresolved. Benir's Forte deductions are in-world observations, not external medical certainty. His bounty/escape tactic and Drake gamble warrant ethical counterreading, not heroic smoothing. The rank-up menu's storm-proof English and wave-time Japanese divergences must not be promoted to performed immunity or a dated voyage. Friendship/invitation and theatrical romantic narration are not evidence of an exclusive relationship. New evidence should change precise claims through the [revision ledger](04%20Validation%20and%20Readiness/WUWA_BRANT_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md) before adoption or current-discovery updates.

This packet's distinctive problem is the ethics of captaincy: how to lead a frightened, materially constrained, historically persecuted company without deciding every member's future for them. That is why his ordinary Ragunna day, stage maintenance and the choice to let troupe members go are as central as any sea battle.
