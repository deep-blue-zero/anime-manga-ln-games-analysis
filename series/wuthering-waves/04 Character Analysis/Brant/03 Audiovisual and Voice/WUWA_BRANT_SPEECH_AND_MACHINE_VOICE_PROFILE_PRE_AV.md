### Verified performance findings, counterexamples, and pre-AV resolutions

1. **Within-scene stage flourish vs. real storm-helm command (`BRA-DUB-01` vs. `BRA-DUB-02`)**: Direct listening inside `flow#/4220/3` (`SCN-0006`) explains why the pre-AV `4220/3 + 4385/1` machine cohort showed elevated `F0` without proving a single static "stage persona." Brant's Fool's Troupe welcome (`Main_Linaxita_2_0_8_13`, `BRA-DUB-01`) is a buoyant, smiling showman tenor-baritone (`zh-Hans` `195.91 Hz`, `ja` `219.43 Hz`, `ko` `212.21 Hz`, `en` `192.99 Hz`), whereas thirty-two seconds of sea-fog crisis later (`Main_Linaxita_2_0_8_45`, `BRA-DUB-02`) all four actors surge **`+59` to `+142 Hz`** (`zh-Hans` `262.21 Hz`, `ja` `361.69 Hz`, `ko` `271.32 Hz`, `en` `314.20 Hz`) into urgent, full-diaphragm deck command stripped of playacting.
2. **Public projection harnessed for civic conviction (`BRA-DUB-04`) vs. unmasked captain's compass (`BRA-DUB-03`)**: At Carnevale's climax (`Main_Linaxita_2_3_39_58`, `SCN-0061`), Brant uses open theatrical projection (`zh-Hans` `190.13 Hz`, `ja` `249.60 Hz`, `ko` `187.93 Hz`, `en` `196.34 Hz`) for earnest egalitarian argument rather than entertainment. Conversely, when explaining to Morrias why a captain hides fear and sorrow (`Main_Linaxita_2_3_34_18`, `BRA-DUB-03`), every dub drops `19`–`73 Hz` below `BRA-DUB-01` (`zh-Hans` `165.60 Hz`, `ja` `146.12 Hz`, `ko` `174.67 Hz`, `en` `173.95 Hz`) into a sober, unornamented chest baritone that acoustically enacts the distinction his text names.
3. **Counterexample to flamboyant anger — moral disgust and personal creed live in his lowest register (`BRA-DUB-05`, `BRA-DUB-06`)**: Resolving the pre-AV heterogeneity between `flow#/5248/5` and `5357/3`, direct listening proves Brant's sharpest ethical condemnation of Aldric (`Character_Brant_36_14`, `BRA-DUB-05` — including `en`'s stronger `"tricking his men"` phrasing) is delivered **not** as loud theatrical ranting, but as cold, tight-jawed low baritone restraint (`en` `111.55 Hz` / IQR `24.56 Hz`, `zh-Hans` `125.20 Hz`, `ko` `147.81 Hz`, `ja` `162.67 Hz` — `57` to `81 Hz` below his greeting). His quietest floor (`Character_Brant_70_6`, `BRA-DUB-06`: `zh-Hans` `110.56 Hz`, `ko` `115.22 Hz` with soft opening breath-laughs; `en` `130.23 Hz`; `ja` `131.57 Hz`) carries his unmasked sea-freedom creed, strengthening the existing packet's mask-versus-responsibility model without requiring rule revisions.
