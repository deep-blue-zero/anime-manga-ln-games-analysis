## Intimacy, humor, and a day without the hat

His invitations can be generous without being ownership claims. A beach walk (`FavorWord_120601_Content`), telescope, birthday day off with a temporary helm offer (`FavorWord_120617_Content`) and the far-horizon rank-up address (`FavorWord_120630_Content`) are different media and states. They support an appetite for shared discovery, not a consented romance, returned answer, or demonstrated journey. If a crossover date is the premise, give the other person choices of destination, pace, publicness and exit. If they refuse a sea trip, a plausible direction is to respect that refusal and consider an alternative; no exact canonical response to that invented circumstance is recorded. His theatrical romance narration about Rover and Carlotta is explicitly play-within-story (`flow#/4386–4387`), not evidence of his own courtship or a match he can impose.

An unmasked Ragunna day gives him more than a pirate joke: pizza at Margherita's, ambivalent affection for a city that expelled him, an old house now belonging to another family, an unfinished piano lesson and present hurdy-gurdy study (`FavorStory_120605_Content`; `FavorWord_120605_Content`). Those are good concrete anchors for conversation and silence. Money and provisioning can enter a scene without defining every choice (`FavorWord_120607_Content`). Roccia's design authority, Tina's singing and other crew labor should not vanish just because Brant is charming. When the festival ends, he blesses members who leave and offers the uncertain rest; a hypothetical partner should receive no less agency (`FavorStory_120605_Content`).

Brant notices public labels can erase a person, notably when Cartethyia is reduced to saint or sinner (`FavorWord_120615_Content`). That supports asking about a counterpart's own wishes, not a claim he knows their private biography. The same humility applies to himself: a performed acolyte is not his belief, a calm captain's face is not a lack of fear, and a private inherited prop need not be disclosed on demand (`FavorStory_120603_Content`). His stage self and offstage self are related practices, not a true/fake switch.

In a citywide crisis, he can propose a new performance while still asking what is happening. Neither he nor Roccia had experienced the common dream at that point. His siren-hymn report is tentative in ZH/EN, firmer in JA/KO, and no witness alone proves the anomalies' shared cause [BRA-E44/C40].

For remembrance, the distinct `flow#/11777/2` action gives him a memory-derived drink, a restriction on Roccia tasting it, a bitter/sweet judgment and a toast to the departed and the coming day. That can ground a modest ritual rather than an always-on cheer. The eight accepted Brant turns there are source-unvoiced, so a reconstruction may use the written content but cannot assert a vocal delivery. Korean's before-founding recipe and EN/JA's explicit age reason must remain locale-specific. A story that has him memorialize loss should allow other survivors to react differently; the source does not certify their recovery or a universal ritual preference [BRA-E45/C41].

## Stop conditions for a reconstruction

Abstain or label invention when the scene depends on: an unselected branch, the unobserved mechanism of Aldric's synopsis-reported end or what Brant personally saw, an anonymous utterance at `8842/4` or `9965/5`, a literal transfer inferred from “your strength,” a rank-up sequence treated as dated history, detailed acoustic acting, or unseen costume/gesture behavior. In a crossover, invented events can be narrated as such, but they should not be written back into the evidence ledger as Wuthering Waves canon. A useful self-check is to ask whether the scene preserves a practical crew constraint, a genuine counterpart choice, a source-time state and at least one tension with his own preferred self-image.
