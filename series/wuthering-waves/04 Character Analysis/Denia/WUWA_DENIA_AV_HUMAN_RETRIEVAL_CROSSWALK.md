# Denia audiovisual human-retrieval crosswalk

## Responsibility

This document translates source-facing AV nominations into terms a person can use to locate footage or official media.

It answers:

> **Where should a human look for the nominated Denia audiovisual evidence?**

It does not answer what the footage shows. Search terms, uploader titles, chapter labels, and timestamps become evidence only after a specific witness is acquired, provenance-recorded, and directly reviewed.

# 1. Confidence vocabulary

- **high** — the internal source family and public story unit align directly enough for reliable retrieval;
- **moderate** — the broad public chapter/patch is supported, but exact subunit or upload position requires verification;
- **low** — only dialogue or scene anchors are reliable; do not assign a public quest title as fact;
- **unresolved** — no trustworthy public mapping has yet been established.
