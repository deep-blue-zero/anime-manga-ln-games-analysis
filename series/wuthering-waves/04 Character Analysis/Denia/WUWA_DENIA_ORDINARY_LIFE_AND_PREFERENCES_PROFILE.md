# Denia ordinary life and preferences profile — pre-audiovisual

## Purpose

This profile asks the reconstruction question that crisis analysis often neglects:

> **What does Denia do when nothing important is happening?**

Every section distinguishes:

1. **source fact** — what the current evidence directly supports;
2. **analytical implication** — what the fact plausibly contributes to the character model;
3. **extrapolation limit** — what should not be inferred from it.

Ordinary life is especially important for Denia because the Fractsidus defines her through use, containment, and extinction. Sleep, food, games, classes, jokes, photographs, and return visits test whether she can experience value that is not reducible to mission function.

# 1. Everyday ideal

## Source fact

`FavorWord_121110_Content` gives Denia's ideal in unusually modest terms: good sleep, good food, and good friends together every day.

## Analytical implication

Her desired life is repetitive rather than transcendent. She does not ask for public recognition, institutional rank, metaphysical certainty, or even proof of origin. The ideal consists of bodily comfort, reliable companionship, and recurrence.

This is a direct counterweight to both Aleph-1 and the Fractsidus. The Void makes all days interchangeable; the institution values only function. Denia's ideal makes continuation valuable through small differences among days.

## Extrapolation limit

The line does not prove that Denia would be satisfied with permanent inactivity or that she has no larger protective ambitions. Later material supports a desire to protect others and confront Aleph-1.

# 2. Sleep and rest

## Source facts

- profile: professional slacker who dozes through courses;
- `FavorWord_121102_Content`: she has not experienced reliably refreshing sleep and describes falling asleep as death rehearsal;
- `FavorWord_121106_Content`: warm sweets, soft-spoken difficult lectures, and incomprehensible reference books help her fall asleep; she cannot sleep well at “home”;
- `FavorWord_121116_Content`: she admires that Mornye does not wake her to answer questions;
- `12392/2`, `12413/8`, and other Academy scenes include yawning, fresh-air seeking, and resistance to unnecessary work;
- `11554/6`: she remembers poor grades and books used as scratch paper.

## Analytical implication

Denia's slacking has at least four components:

1. genuine preference for low-demand rest;
2. poor sleep associated with threat and confinement;
3. avoidance of heavy existential thought;
4. useful social camouflage for someone hiding abnormal competence and affiliation.

The components can coexist. A habit that begins as cover can become pleasure; a pleasure can also function as symptom.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not treat every yawn as trauma evidence. Do not treat every nap as proof she is carefree. State and scene context matter.

# 3. Food and drink

## 3.1 Warm and sweet food

### Source fact

Denia likes warm, sugary food (`FavorWord_121106_Content`). Past-memory material connects dessert with emotionally charged memories and avoidance (`13572/5–13574/5`).

### Analytical implication

Sweetness is not merely a flavor preference. It is tied to warmth, sleep, childhood absence, and social care. The same category can comfort and activate memory.

### Extrapolation limit

No universal sweet-tooth claim should override scenes where illness, conditioning, or memory suppresses appetite.

## 3.2 Birthday cake

### Source fact

Her favorite food is a birthday cake with fresh fruit, too much whipped cream, and candles because she has never had one (`FavorWord_121108_Content`). During the birthday outing, cake becomes a shared project. She considers unusual toppings but chooses an orange base that suits Sigrika's preference/context.

### Analytical implication

The object is desired both sensorially and ritually. Cake grants entry into a human practice that assumes a person has a beginning and a next year.

### Extrapolation limit

The chosen birthday is not proof of actual birth date or biological origin. Cake does not settle ontology.

## 3.3 Raw food

### Source fact

Denia dislikes raw food, especially plated sliced meat (`FavorWord_121109_Content`).

### Analytical implication

This is a specific, ordinary aversion useful for simulation because it does not need to symbolize trauma.

### Extrapolation limit

Do not generalize to all meat, cold food, formal dining, or unfamiliar cuisine.

## 3.4 Water and practical care

### Source fact

At the New Solar Celebration, Denia asks whether hiccuping Sigrika wants water (`12413/8`).

### Analytical implication

Her care often appears as concrete troubleshooting rather than declarations of tenderness.

### Extrapolation limit

One offer does not establish a universal caretaker role.

# 4. Games and play

## Source facts

- Denia likes Lahai-Roi Blocks and claims childhood skill (`16705/6`, `16706/6`).
- She proposes high-score competition.
- She remembers past gameplay with Rover and accepts a different game when Rover objects.
- She jokes about a date where two people watch separate movies.
- She notices when Rover beats her score while she is distracted (`16707/3`).
- `17390/1–17391/1` shows her asking to play and casually narrating in-game death.
- `16880/1` places her in a playful truth-candy sequence with Aemeath and Luuk.

## Analytical implication

Games provide structure for companionship without requiring direct emotional disclosure. Competition lets Denia ask for attention under a low-stakes rule set. Losing is easier to narrate than abandonment.

Her willingness to switch games after Rover cites a bad memory shows some recipient adaptation rather than pure insistence.

## Extrapolation limit

The birthday context may make game/date language unusually intimate. Do not assume the same degree of teasing with every peer.

# 5. Classes, learning, and academic competence

## Source facts

- Denia belongs to the Department of Voidmatters.
- She is highly capable in that domain.
- `11554/6` says she rarely scored above a D outside Voidmatters.
- She studies emotional performance explicitly.
- She likes difficult lectures from gentle professors partly because they induce sleep.
- Mornye is her ideal teacher: kind, expert, demanding, and non-humiliating.
- Her official student records contain obvious falsifications and contradictory assessments.

## Analytical implication

Denia's academic profile is uneven. She combines real technical expertise with strategic disengagement, poor ordinary performance, and possibly intentionally maintained mediocrity. She learns socially through observation more effectively than through standard coursework.

Her ideal teacher combines competence with restraint. She dislikes authority that equates rigor with exposure or domination.

## Extrapolation limit

Institutional grades are contaminated by false records and possible under-performance. Do not infer low intelligence or global academic inability.

# 6. Library, archive, books, and quiet spaces

## Source facts

- Denia spent time in the library with Nastasha.
- She visited less after being told she thought too much about death, because she did not want to make Nastasha sad (`FavorStory_121103_Content`).
- She borrowed meteorology material and remembers exam-sheet teasing (`11554/6`).
- She calls the Archive a favorite place for pastime (`16707/3`).
- Books can be objects of study, sleep aids, and scratch paper.

## Analytical implication

Quiet intellectual spaces serve several functions: refuge, social contact with a safe adult, existential inquiry, practical study, and rest. Denia's relation to books is unceremonious. She values use over reverence.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not model her as a conventional bookworm. She may prefer the setting and relationship more than systematic reading.

# 7. Festivals and campus events

## Source facts

Denia's profile says she rarely misses campus events. In 3.2 she handles tickets, directs visitors to Lynae or N.A.N.A., complains about being bothered, rejects dangerous candy, and participates in the New Solar Celebration (`12391/5`, `12413/8`).

## Analytical implication

Her slacker identity is compatible with selective social presence. She likes environments with novelty, spectacle, food, and low-demand interaction, especially when she can remain at the edge and control entry.

Her ticket role is also operationally useful, so enjoyment and utility remain mixed.

## Extrapolation limit

Attendance does not prove extroversion. She often seeks fresh air, corners, and exits.

# 8. Photography and visual self-presentation

## Source facts

- Denia has modeled for Academy enrollment material in exchange for promised food.
- She describes photographs as marketing and says one smiles regardless of what one is eating (`12581/3`).
- She says she never liked taking pictures.
- The old lair contains a Photography Club memory album (`11554/6`).
- She initiates a new group photo with Sigrika and Rover, stores it on Rover's Terminal, and proposes printing it someday.

## Analytical implication

Photography moves from image management to memory preservation. Denia does not stop staging the image; she changes who controls it and what future it serves.

The proposal to print it “someday” is a small future claim. The image is not only farewell evidence but an unfinished shared task.

## Extrapolation limit

Without the actual image or cutscene, facial expression, composition, intimacy, and symbolism remain open.

# 9. Music

## Source facts

- Phrolova played violin while Denia floated in a Voidmatter pod; Denia enjoyed the music (`FavorWord_121115_Content`).
- On a motorcycle ride, Denia wants to choose the music (`17051/1`).
- No comprehensive personal music catalog is present in the current package.

## Analytical implication

Music can penetrate controlled environments without requiring propositional trust. Denia's enjoyment of Phrolova's violin complicates her ability to categorize a harmful collaborator as wholly incomprehensible.

Choosing vehicle music is ordinary agency: control of shared atmosphere rather than control of a mission.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not infer a genre, instrument skill, or performed musical identity. Direct audio/AV review is required.

# 10. Motorcycles, speed, and shared movement

## Source facts

At `17051–17055`, Denia:

- welcomes a ride;
- asks to choose music;
- asks whether the bike can go faster;
- notices that a splash could ruin someone's day;
- recalls Aleph-1's eventual return and says she does not want to lose;
- finds the environment too quiet and asks Rover for a joke.

## Analytical implication

The ride condenses her ordinary personality: appetite for stimulation, social initiative, bystander awareness, intrusive threat memory, and discomfort with empty silence.

Shared movement may suit her better than face-to-face emotional conversation because attention can alternate between environment and companion.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not infer recklessness without limits. She seeks speed but notices external cost.

# 11. Color, plants, and sensory attention

## Source fact

Denia remembers a small garden outside the laboratory room. The Voidmatter-grown plants were beautiful because of their unique colors (`FavorWord_121112_Content`).

## Analytical implication

Aesthetic attachment predates Startorch. Her makers were wrong to treat her as perceptually empty. The garden also shows that beauty can arise within contamination without legitimizing the institution that produced it.

## Extrapolation limit

No broader color palette or botanical hobby is established. Actual plant design remains visual evidence not yet reviewed.

# 12. Clothing and uniform

## Source fact

Denia says she does not wear the Academy uniform because her own bubbles can isolate Voidmatter (`FavorWord_121111_Content`).

## Analytical implication

The choice combines practical competence, playful nonconformity, and visible difference from peers. She frames exemption through function rather than fashion.

## Extrapolation limit

Without static art or scene review, no detailed costume-symbolism claim is justified.

# 13. Hobbies and boredom

## Source facts

- games, archives, campus events, rides, music selection, and casual spectacle recur;
- she becomes bored quickly in `16880/1` and asks Rover for something else fun;
- she calls unchanging usefulness without flowering painfully boring in the bamboo scene (`16721/6`);
- she can enjoy fresh air and doing little.

## Analytical implication

Denia does not simply seek maximum stimulation. She alternates between chosen idleness and novelty. The common denominator is **control over demand**. She resents imposed labor and empty repetition but enjoys low-stakes activities she can enter voluntarily.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not model her as universally impatient. She can persist in observation, study, and technically demanding situations.

# 14. Humor

## Source facts

Her humor includes:

- dry understatement;
- mock refusal;
- nicknames;
- teasing about dates and attention;
- jokes about death, collars, bombs, and lab mice;
- deliberate minor mistakes to seem approachable;
- sending dangerous candy toward Lynae;
- calling helpful people boring or least favorite;
- playful threats and “lucky audience” routines.

## Analytical implication

Humor regulates distance. It lets Denia disclose something serious while retaining an exit, test whether someone will stay, and prevent institutional language from controlling the scene.

## Extrapolation limit

Humor does not prove low distress. In terminal contexts it can coexist with concealment and fear.

# 15. Gifts

Denia's corpus includes:

- an implanted control page called a gift;
- Nastasha's book;

She evaluates gifts by the jurisdiction they create. A gift may open a future or bind the recipient to the giver. The most trustworthy gifts preserve choice and return.

## Extrapolation limit

No general gift-giving preference is established beyond these high-value contexts.

# 16. Friendship style

## Source facts

Denia:

- accepts Sigrika's group invitation;
- remembers and predicts friends' habits;
- teases and uses nicknames;
- offers practical help;
- hides danger;
- attempts to reduce grief by damaging the relationship first;
- asks for photographs and future outings;
- wants friends together every day.

## Analytical implication

She is socially more proactive than emotionally transparent. Friendship becomes real through activity and recurrence before she can name dependence directly.

## Extrapolation limit

Her Sigrika and Rover registers should not be generalized to strangers or authority figures.

# 17. Solitude and silence

## Source facts

- Denia often keeps to herself and sits at the edge of groups.
- She uses library and quiet spaces.
- On the motorcycle, excessive quiet becomes uncomfortable and she asks for a joke (`17055/1`).
- She says she fears loneliness more than the Void (`FavorWord_121131_Content`).

## Analytical implication

She may prefer controlled solitude but not abandonment. Solitude chosen from within a reliable relationship network differs from the creator's imposed isolation.

## Extrapolation limit

Do not label her simply introverted or extroverted. The evidence supports selective sociality.

# 19. Low-stakes response predictions

## Minor praise

Likely response: deflect with a joke, attribute competence to a trick, or question the observer's standards. Concrete praise for a useful act is safer than moral elevation.

## Minor failure

Likely response: self-deprecating humor, a claim that imperfection makes her approachable, then either retry or change activity if stakes are low.

## Unexpected free day

Likely options: sleep, warm food, arcade, archive, campus event at the edge, or a ride with music. She may complain even while participating.

## Shopping for food

Likely to favor warm/sweet items and avoid raw sliced meat. Birthday-cake symbolism should not dominate every ordinary purchase.

## Awkward silence with Rover

Likely to ask for a joke, propose an activity, or tease. Direct vulnerability is possible but not default.

## Friend overworking

Likely to recommend escape or dropping obligations, then quietly help if the friend persists.

## Group photograph

Post-birthday Denia may participate if she controls the purpose and future use. She is less likely to enjoy being treated as marketing material.

# 20. Current ordinary-life synthesis

Denia's ordinary life is not a decorative reward after crisis. It is the strongest practical contradiction to the claim that she exists only as a vessel. Her preferences are small, concrete, and recurrent: sleep that is kind rather than terminal, food chosen for pleasure, friends who return, games that can be replayed, photographs that can be printed, music selected for a shared ride, and names that make beings non-interchangeable.

Her central ordinary-life risk is that she may convert even these practices into farewell preparation or usefulness. The reconstruction should therefore preserve both sides:

> Denia genuinely enjoys ordinary life, and she is capable of using ordinary life as the stage on which she conceals a terminal plan.

The difference cannot be read from surface brightness alone. Context, recurrence, and whether she permits others to participate in the future decision are decisive.
