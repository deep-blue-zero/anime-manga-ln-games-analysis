# Denia audiovisual evidence nomination plan

## Purpose

This plan identifies the visual and audiovisual witnesses most likely to preserve, strengthen, qualify, scope-limit, contradict, or reopen the pre-AV Denia reconstruction.

It is not an acquisition manifest and does not claim that any video or image has been downloaded or directly reviewed. Search terms, public quest names, and expected scene descriptions are retrieval aids. The deterministic source identity remains the flow row/action, text key, or official media identity.

The plan follows a two-stage workflow:

1. **hierarchy and nomination** — decide what evidence is worth finding and what analytical question it owns;
2. **targeted acquisition and direct review** — locate stable witnesses, record provenance, inspect them, and revise claims.

# 1. Audiovisual authority dimensions

Each witness should be rated separately on four dimensions.

| Dimension | Governing question |
|---|---|
| diegetic authority | Does the depicted event or behavior occur within the story world? |
| representational authority | Is this an official depiction of Denia's appearance, movement, voice, combat language, or presentation? |
| interpretive authority | How strongly can the witness support a claim about her behavior, state, relationships, or psychology? |
| production-framing authority | How strongly does it reveal what Kuro chooses to foreground or associate with Denia for an audience? |

A promotional PV can have very high representational and production-framing authority while having uncertain diegetic authority. A quest cutscene can have high diegetic and interpretive authority. Static art can be strong for design and association but weak for habitual behavior.

# 2. Collection strata

## A. Core diegetic AV

Story cutscenes, in-engine dialogue, scripted traversal, and ordinary interactions. These are the primary witnesses for expression, gesture, blocking, interpersonal distance, environmental use, state transition, and embodied fatigue.

## B. Matched multilingual promotional AV

The same official edit in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English where available. These are preferred for comparative localization and performance because visual timing and dramatic framing are partly controlled.

## C. Unmatched official promotional AV

Language- or channel-exclusive PVs, shorts, trailers, and showcases. These remain valuable for production framing and language-specific presentation, but cannot independently prove cross-dub difference.

## D. Official static and representational media

Splash art, profile art, promotional illustrations, version key art, and group imagery. These support design, motif, association, costume, and audience-framing analysis rather than ordinary behavior.

# 5. Matched multilingual collection rule

For any official PV, trailer, showcase, or story upload available in multiple dubs:

1. assign one `matched_multilingual_group_id`;
2. preserve each language witness separately;
3. record whether the visual edit is exact, near-exact, or materially different;
4. compare localization wording before attributing differences to acting;
5. retain unmatched language-exclusive media as a separate official witness rather than discarding it.

Suggested group format:

`WUWA_DENIA_<MEDIA_SLUG>_<NNN>`

Example member suffixes: `_ZH`, `_JA`, `_KO`, `_EN`.

Promotional asymmetry may be recorded as a distribution fact. The absence of a matching upload in another language is not evidence that Denia herself differs by language.

# 6. Pre-AV hypotheses to attack

The review should not merely illustrate the dossier. It should attempt to falsify these claims:

1. practiced human performance becomes visibly more spontaneous in later relationships;
2. student Denia is a lived mode, not only cover;
3. Sigrika receives Denia's lightest social register and most paternalistic protection;
4. Rover receives the deepest philosophical testing and terminal disclosure;
5. creator/Aleph scenes remove most of her social cushioning;
6. “home” is visually split between confinement and chosen social belonging;
7. birthday and photography shift from fabricated/public performance to future-binding memory;
8. Denia's fear is embodied even when her dialogue remains controlled;
9. her post-return movement vocabulary suggests pleasure and agency without proving full psychological recovery;
10. official promotion foregrounds imitation and humanity while potentially compressing accountability and deception.

# 7. Observation protocol

For each acquired witness, record:

- exact source and timecode;
- characters present;
- state and relationship context;
- directly observable expression, gaze, gesture, posture, movement, proximity, blocking, framing, lighting, editing, music, and environment;
- what is inferred rather than seen;
- which textual claim is preserved, strengthened, qualified, scope-limited, contradicted, rejected, or left open;
- alternative visual reading;
- production-framing observations where relevant;
- localization/performance notes only when the compared language material is actually reviewed.

Do not use screenshot timing or subtitle existence as a substitute for direct observation.

# 9. Completion boundary

This plan supports a provisional `audiovisual_hierarchy_completed` state because source classes, claim-driven targets, deterministic locators, and human-facing retrieval routes have been identified.

It does **not** support:

- `audiovisually_hardened`;
- `integrated_reconstruction_completed`;
- any claim about Denia's facial acting, body language, camera treatment, or performed dub differences.

Those states require acquired witnesses, a manifest, direct review, recorded observations, and explicit claim revision.
