This matrix separates source facts from interpretation and records what could weaken each proposed model. It is not a substitute for the character deep dive. It is the claim-control surface for later audiovisual review, source-version updates, relationship rereads, and optional human performance work.

Classification vocabulary:

- **source fact** — directly asserted or demonstrated within the declared source boundary;
- **stable observation** — repeated pattern with low interpretive distance;
- **strong inference** — explanatory claim supported by several source surfaces;
- **candidate thesis** — integrative reading that remains materially vulnerable to later evidence;
- **open hypothesis** — plausible but not yet sufficiently tested.
