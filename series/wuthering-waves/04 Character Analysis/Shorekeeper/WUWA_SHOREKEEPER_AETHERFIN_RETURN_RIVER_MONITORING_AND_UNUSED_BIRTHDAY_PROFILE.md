## Restoration without a reset

The fish return twenty years after Rover left, and Shorekeeper credits Rover and KU-Key with their presence across the Black Shores (T1–6). She first says things *seem gradually* to resemble the past in Chinese `POI_HFZSLDZLHS_14_9`; JA and KO likewise retain the in-progress sense. EN's “Everything's back to the way it used to be” is more categorical. The next common path undercuts a full reset anyway: she recalls Rover's river maxim, wonders if the beauty is a dream, and asks whether she could remain inside the Necrostar's event horizon (T7–14). Her statement that she cannot have an independent physical form or experience human dreams while integrated with Tethys is her current self-description, not independent proof that this scene is unreal or that every later embodied appearance is impossible. ZH/KO specifically say she has *never experienced* what people call dreams; EN compresses this into lacking the ability to dream. Treat this as a bounded self-report and metaphysical doubt, not an established new black-hole entrapment [SHK-E31, SHK-C29].

She apologizes for strange thoughts since the incident. Rover's two T14 replies produce either recognition that Rover has used similar reassurance before or her acknowledgment that emotion still takes time to understand. Those are alternative local answers. After reconvergence she thanks Rover for saving the fish and remembers former Black Shores members through them (T17–18). The return represents living ecology, memory and work by Rover/KU-Key, not a resurrection of specific absent members or the restoration of a vanished past. A model that turns her uncertainty into “she now knows she is human” overstates the same source that expressly depicts her still learning.

## Attention, a portal, and the right to object

Only on the optional portal topic does she apologize for appearing to monitor Rover, say she found it difficult not to attend, and report that she acted instinctively when KU-Key could not open the portal in time (T20–23). Chinese `POI_HFZSLDZLHS_14_31` denies *intentional* surveillance; Japanese says she is not monitoring, while EN/KO also stress intent. These are her explanations, not an independent audit of all access. She then asks whether she did something unnecessary or troubling. The retained Rover options both reassure her, followed by relief at T24; the graph contains no categorical objection route at that moment. Thus neither “Rover complained and she violated a recorded refusal” nor “Rover can never object to her watching” follows. A hypothetical crossover partner may ask for boundaries; the packet should say that response is not observed here. Her final promise to watch over/support Rover at T30 is similarly a source vow, not blanket consent to tracking [SHK-E31, SHK-C30].

## Photos and a party the record does not fully show

Only if Rover selects the picture topic does she say Black Shores members secretly photographed scenes while preparing a birthday celebration, and that Hoda and others preserved the images after Rover became too busy for them to be used (T25–27). Chinese `POI_HFZSLDZLHS_14_39`, JA and KO say the *photos* went unused; EN asserts that “the party was never held.” The latter is a stronger localization witness, not a four-language established fact. The source does not show the planned event, actual attendance, consent to the candid photos, or whether a different celebration occurred. It does show that other members took initiative and kept a memory of Rover, preventing a model from reducing the Black Shores' care to Shorekeeper alone [SHK-E31, SHK-C31].

On exit she connects the pictures, fish and Rover's return with a feeling she tentatively calls happiness (T29). That is more than a binary declaration that she “has become human”; it is an inquiry into a particular changed world. T30's promise of support may be read alongside her discomfort about monitoring and her continued work for the Black Shores. It should not erase either.
