## Nine source-defined story cohorts

The [separate cohort audit](../04%20Validation%20and%20Readiness/WUWA_SHOREKEEPER_SOURCE_DEFINED_AUDIO_COHORT_AUDIT.md) selects 208 accepted story occurrences from nine exact actions that distinguish core-post constraint, change in the garden self, Tethys deception, a desire to know, reunion apology, deep-shore cost, postrepair work and two later aid decisions. They make 832 four-dub associations and 832 distinct integrity-valid measured PCM objects in this *selected* set, with no alternate object or cross-language reuse in those cells. This is 38.3% of the 543 selected semantic lines and omits the archive. Full-corpus associations, runtime rows and objects still have different denominators. The local report binds exact semantic/render IDs, source locators and PCM/FLAC hashes; it reproduced byte-identically and rejected absent/overlapping source selectors without creating output.

One action-dependent signal pattern is worth retaining only as a future listening question. With exact semantic pairs and the −45 dBFS gate, EN-minus-ZH median active-frame level is −0.10 dB in `3446/3`, +0.04 in `3756/2`, +4.28 in the confession at `3804/3`, −0.80 in the reunion at `4020/4`, +3.73 in the deep shore at `4198/2`, +4.10 after repair at `4081/3`, and −0.49 in the later tunnel decision at `10100/2`. EN is higher than ZH on 16/17, 31/31 and 17/18 individual semantic pairs in the three positive-offset actions respectively. The return toward zero in another later state alone defeats a simple chronological loudness-as-personhood trend. Recording/mix and localized wording remain uncontrolled; different active-duration gates alter medians, and no human has adjudicated a single phrase. Qualified pitch coverage is 196/208 ZH, 204/208 EN, 202/208 JA and 199/208 KO. Neither those medians nor a localized level offset can diagnose tenderness, love or a mechanical vocal state.

The source consequences remain textual: `flow#/3446/3/2` says she cannot leave her post *then*, while `4081/3/12` preserves continuing work after a changed core and does not prove unlimited departure. The retained `4198/2` action includes a shared no-more-harm promise at item 24, her proposed sole cost at 26 and her question about love at 30. They have separate line/render IDs and none supplies Rover's reciprocal answer or proves Shorekeeper's welfare was protected. The EN `3804/3/11` rationale is more explicitly best-for-both than the Chinese admission; waveform amplitude cannot reconcile that localization difference. Keep state, language, addressee, exact line and listening status attached to every later performance claim.
