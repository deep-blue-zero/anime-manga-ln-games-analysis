### Verified performance findings, counterexamples, and pre-AV resolutions

1. **Non-robotic baseline and progression from halting wonder to rhythmic emotional certainty (`SHK-DUB-01` vs. `SHK-DUB-02`)**: Direct listening refutes any stereotype of Shorekeeper as coldly mechanical. In her Chapter I deep-shore crisis question (`Heihaian_main_1_3_5619_35`, `SHK-DUB-01`), all four dubs use glass-still legato mezzo phrasing suspended around wondering internal pauses (`zh-Hans` `248.42 Hz`, `-28.32 dBFS` before `"爱吗？"`; `ja` `208.01 Hz` `"愛……なの？"`; `ko` `189.61 Hz` polite `"-걸까요?"`; `en` `198.78 Hz` `"Could this feeling be... love?"`). Notice also why the pre-AV `flow#/4198/2` `en`-minus-`zh` level offset (`+3.73 dB` cohort median; `+7.47 dB` on `SHK-DUB-01`) cannot be read as greater English emotional assertiveness: in `SHK-DUB-01`, `zh-Hans` is mastered quieter during whispered wonder (`-28.32 dBFS`), whereas in Ascension V (`FavorWord_150531_Content`, `SHK-DUB-02`), all four dubs converge in active level (`-23.28` to `-22.01 dBFS`) while pacing her heartbeat mimetics (`"噗通，噗通"` / `"トクン、トクン"` / `"쿵, 쿵"` / `"Thump, thump..."`) into serene, unwavering conviction (`"我很确定，这就是爱"` / `"It must be love"`).
2. **Clinical diagnostic precision resolving into personal tenderness (`SHK-DUB-03` vs. `SHK-DUB-04`)**: In the Chapter II Prologue Black Shores checkup (`Main_Linaxita_2_0_1_6`, `SHK-DUB-03`, `SCN-0001`), every dub (`zh-Hans` `241.12 Hz`, `ja` `191.71 Hz`, `ko` `188.51 Hz`, `en` `213.96 Hz`) balances analytical clarity about Abby's hunger and sleepiness with attentive guardian warmth. By the post-Rinascita `3.1` frequency check (`Main_LahaiRoi_3_1_2_14`, `SHK-DUB-04`), each actress begins with measured clinical observation and then drops across a deliberate mid-line pause into hushed personal devotion (`zh-Hans` `"但我还是……很担心"` at `236.30 Hz`; `ja` dropping to `183.40 Hz` on `"……心配"`; `ko` `194.59 Hz` `"그래도 걱정이 돼서요"`; `en` dropping `-25.50 Hz` to `188.46 Hz`, `-25.85 dBFS` on `"but... enough to concern me"`), corroborating the existing packet's continuity model without requiring rule changes.
