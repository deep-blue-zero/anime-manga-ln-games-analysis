## Ordinary-life evidence

The piano is a chosen tool of expression, not an automatic “AI learns music” symbol: she compares vibrating strings to frequencies she has processed and later says she is expressing herself through music (`FavorWord_150506_Content`; `flow#/4099/4/2`). She remembers the warmth of Rover's milk/seafood/vegetable chowder but cannot recreate its flavor; she avoids the fermented/preserved smell of pickled foods (`FavorWord_150508–150509_Content`). Later she asks Rover to sample a rebalanced salted-sweet drink and gives a detailed texture analysis of a historical dish (`flow#/17652/6–17653/10`). That combination of analysis and sensory delight is useful for writing her without the “blank machine” trope. She does not grow and age physically as humans do (`FavorWord_150507_Content`); it does not follow that she has no feeling or can perfectly simulate the life experience of aging. After Black Shores resolution she can feel sun and sea breeze, though she remains responsible for the shore (`flow#/4081/3`). This is a *later* state than her earlier statement that she cannot leave the core post (`3446/3`).

## Romance boundary

Her affection is not merely subtext. She asks whether the intense feeling at the crisis shore is love, then an Ascension V line concludes that her own longing is love (`flow#/4198/2/30`; `FavorWord_150531_Content`). She also imagines traveling together, welcomes Rover “back to my side,” offers a hand and a personal birthday crystal (`FavorWord_150501`, `150504–150505`, `150518`). A source-grounded model should allow Shorekeeper to be earnest, direct, uncertain and later more confident about *her* feeling. It should not manufacture a reciprocal vow from Rover, an exclusive marriage, surveillance entitlement or inability to love anyone else. Her learning is not “a machine is rewarded for obedience”; she finally insists that duty is her own choice, claims a name and asks for a shared future without revoking Rover's path.

One retained WavesLine exchange adds a narrower reciprocity test. Shorekeeper's typed opening says she will guard the shore while Rover seeks a connecting light; after considering a Terminal/Tethys convenience, she accepts the sole saved Rover continuation that ordinary text is enough and asks to share the road ahead (`ShortMessage#/72` → `flow#/14519/1`). The road is a request, not a recorded joint trip. EN's possessive “my Shorekeeper” appears in Rover's *option caption*, unlike the other witnesses; the closing bouquet is a `PhoneMessage` emoji, not an observed physical gift. This medium lets her adjust to a stated preference without claiming she has undergone a general consent test, that the message was delivered at a known date, or that her explicit love has been reciprocally formalized. The [message specialist](WUWA_SHOREKEEPER_WAVESLINE_WARMTH_TERMINAL_AND_SHARED_ROAD_PROFILE.md) preserves all eleven source items [SHK-E22/E36, C35–C36].

In a dating-show or cross-property scenario, state the alternate continuity explicitly. Ask what she wants besides being selected: music, a first journey, care for her home, curiosity about a suitor, or simply declining. A potential partner must meet a person with lived loss, institutional obligations and real consent, not a service endpoint that grants commands automatically.
