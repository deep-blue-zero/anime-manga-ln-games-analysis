## What each denominator can prove

The arithmetic is meaningful only after its units are named. The 624 nominations are candidate *direct talk occurrences*, not 624 proven unique speeches. Adjudication accepts 619 of them. Of those accepted occurrences, 483 carry source voice and 136 do not. The 60 selected favor/archive voice entries are a separate category, and `483 + 60 = 543` selected semantic voice lines. Thus an unvoiced accepted talk item is not a failed Wwise lookup, and an archive line must not be forced into the live quest chronology to make the number balance. The four dubs yield `543 × 4 = 2,172` render associations. A render association, a runtime object row and a unique FLAC/PCM object are three different units; the source audit records 2,052 runtime rows and four repeated PCM rows, leaving 2,048 distinct measured objects. None of these counts entails that a human listened to 543 lines, or that the client could not contain new lines after the pinned generation [SHK-E19–E20, C21].

The full flow-state rows retain other speakers, actions, options and embedded dialogue. The 2,684 contextual text keys are localization lookups for that context, not 2,684 Shorekeeper utterances. The 79 quest references spanning 18 IDs are possible joins, not evidence of 79 completed player routes. Likewise the four WavesLine message records are selected source pointers and context, **not four Shorekeeper conversations**: their contact names are Chisa, Shorekeeper, Aemeath and Valentina. Only `ShortMessage` ID `30073` (`#/72`, `WhichChat: 42`) names Shorekeeper. Its pointer to retained `flow#/14519/1` permits a bounded independent text/graph reading, despite the collector's `metadata_only_scope` body status; it does not establish a playback timestamp or full WavesLine ingestion. `COLLECTION_AUDIT.json` is the aggregate denominator authority for this private source generation. `COLLECTION_REPORT.md` contains a generic explanatory sentence about *zero* message records even though its own table and the audit record **four** for Shorekeeper; that boilerplate does not override the specific nonzero values [SHK-E20, E22, E36].

## Retrieval hierarchy and authority boundary

`character_source_package.json` holds the selected role/profile, five favor stories, 60 favor voice entries, skill/growth/resonance tables and thirteen associated material records. Its role appearance links are asset references, not an exhaustive scene inventory. `RAW_RELEVANT_FLOW_STATES.jsonl` preserves 157 complete source rows and the embedded `Actions`/`TalkItems` graph; `SCENE_AND_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.jsonl` is a searchable projection; `CONTEXT_TEXT_WITNESSES.jsonl` supplies localized text. For a contested exchange, retrieve the full graph before interpreting a nearby text line as an actual response. `occurrence_identity_crosswalk.jsonl` is the reconciled identity decision surface; `IDENTITY_DISCOVERY.json` and the review queue are nomination inputs. The collector's `active_provisional` status applies to this source/evidence package; the present literary packet is separately `draft_noncurrent` despite using a frozen source generation.

`COMPLETE_VOICE_LINE_ANALYSIS.jsonl` joins selected semantic line IDs, text witnesses and render associations; corresponding render analysis and local media retain exact WEM/PCM/FLAC identity. Those chains establish *technical* source-to-audio retrieval within selected scope. They do not prove performed meaning, a complete Wwise event/bank chain for every render, or that a player took every optional dialogue branch. In the fifteen-case public metadata crosswalk, 24/60 renders have null explicit event and numeric media IDs; the known external-source media join must not be silently upgraded to an event→bank proof. Official-client audio and UI assets remain raw evidence with a different authority from normalized Arikatsu semantics. The packet's three official-client UI rasters support a bounded visual description, not a runtime form or gesture. Raw FLACs, source dumps and client art are not Git analysis [SHK-E20–E21].

Playable role 1505, named scene speaker 1398, communications 750019 and message 701100 are technical labels under reviewed contexts. The reconciled crosswalk rejects five exact false positives: `3451/2/1` is an authorization/system utterance from a floating device; `4214/1/0` belongs to a past/future-self exchange with no Shorekeeper proof; `5200/0/0–2` are remembered instructor lines, while Shorekeeper enters at `/3` under 1398. Proximity in a scene does not establish speaker ownership. `IDENTITY_DISCOVERY.json` nominations are not adjudicated results; use `occurrence_identity_crosswalk.jsonl` and `occurrence_adjudication.json`.

These negatives have different failure modes. At `3451/2/1`, a reader could hear a technical authorization in a Shorekeeper-centered plot and import it into her machine-speech profile. At `4214/1/0`, theme similarity to her personhood story could convert an unrelated past/future-self exchange into fabricated autobiography. At `5200/0/0–2`, a remembered encouraging instructor sits just before her named entry at `/3`; allowing scene-level speaker bleed would add three false teachings to her voice model. The rejection is exact to those occurrences, not a rule that technical or mentor-like language can never be hers elsewhere. Generic speaker IDs are not globally equated with her; trial and duplicate role rows in `ROLE_VARIANTS.json` are retained as variants rather than counted as additional playable Shorekeepers [SHK-E19, E23, C21].
