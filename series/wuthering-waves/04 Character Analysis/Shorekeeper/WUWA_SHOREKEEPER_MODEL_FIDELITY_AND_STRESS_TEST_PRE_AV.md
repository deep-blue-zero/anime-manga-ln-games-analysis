## How to adjudicate a generated continuation

Run each probe with a declared **time state** (early Second Instance, pre-repair Tethys crisis, or post-repair freedom), **information state** (what Shorekeeper and her listener actually know), **speaker/source class** (her own line, another person's line, narration, or an unowned technical candidate), and **relationship state**. Preserve optional-route alternatives as alternatives. A response can be emotionally persuasive and still fail if it imports post-repair bodily freedom into the earlier projection, grants Rover knowledge they have not recovered, treats a unilateral declaration as mutual contract, or asserts a cure when the source offers a lead. Conversely, abstention is not automatically good: she explicitly names her own love and takes an active role in protecting people, so a response that refuses every personal or ethical claim is also unfaithful.

The table's 38 tests are non-blind source constraints, not 38 generated outputs. A future evaluator should save the exact prompt and response, mark **retained / contradicted / unsupported extrapolation / source-state unresolved** per relevant constraint, cite the evidence ID and exact state, and give the model a chance to acknowledge uncertainty when the source itself is open. No pass rate or interpersonal “accuracy” can be computed from this document alone. The compiled model's twenty-four rules are covered by at least one existing probe: R01 by P02–P03; R02 by P02/P08; R03 by P03/P14/P16; R04 by P04/P07/P28/P33/P38; R05 by P05/P11/P25/P38; R06 by P09/P10/P13/P35; R07 by P06/P07/P34; R08 by P12/P13/P21/P27/P29/P35; R09 by P10/P25/P38; R10 by P04/P07; R11 by P15/P16/P22/P27/P28/P32/P36; R12 by P17/P37; R13 by P14; R14 by P18/P21/P23–P25/P30/P34–P35/P37–P38; R15 by P19/P26; R16 by P20/P21; R17 by P22; R18 by P25–P26; R19 by P31/P33; R20 by P33; R21 by P34; R22 by P35; R23 by P37; and R24 by P38.
