# Qiuyuan — the sword, the sheath, and the unfinished judgment

> Working draft for the selected 3.6.0 corpus. The [matrix](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) gives falsifiable claim IDs and exact retrieval routes; the [source audit](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md) gives denominators and identity boundaries. Nothing here establishes a completed audiovisual, perceptual-listening, or later-version arc.

## The central problem

Qiuyuan is often easy to reduce to a single striking pose: a blind swordsman who calls himself a weapon, carries blood debts, and has no secure place to rest. The selected material is more resistant. The same man who expects to kill Scar and Geshu Lin once investigated a capital accusation against Geshu, found it false, and urged him to keep his sword sheathed. The same man who says revenge is unfinished cooks for patients according to their needs, evacuates civilians, protects a city outside his own country, and carries a condemned adversary's lantern to someone who can use it. These are not successive masks, one of which must be the “real” Qiuyuan. They are pressures within a continuous ethical life. [QIU-C02](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C07](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

The most useful thesis is that Qiuyuan repeatedly tries to make a blade answerable to something outside its edge: first a master, then a healer, then a public institution, then particular people and a cause. Each affiliation offers purpose and exposes him to a different failure. He can select whom to serve and still describe himself as a weapon; he can be right about the injustice of one accusation yet later believe he ought to have anticipated an entirely different calamity. The source does not settle that latter belief as objective truth. His tragedy is partly a failure of retrospective judgment: the fact that a decision preceded a disaster becomes, in his own account, a debt only he can pay. [QIU-C03](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C05](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## Formed by a double inheritance

The Sword Specter is a particularly poor candidate for the role of uncomplicated mentor. The archive places him inside the fire that scarred Qiuyuan's earlier life and also inside the rescue and extended apprenticeship that followed. The five-year training and final test matter because skill arrives bound to an unresolved moral debt. A training narrative normally tempts the reader to equate mastery with reconciliation. Here the given text does not warrant that equation. Nor does it display every moment of the master's off-page fate. The model can retain the double inheritance without staging an invented final killing or turning the Sword Specter into a secretly benign father. [QIU-E01](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

Doctor Zhang's hut is the first counterform to this training. Qiuyuan reaches it wounded and near an end to his capacity for self-maintenance; the doctor treats a body and a damaged Forte rather than flattering a warrior identity. Medicine can temporarily reactivate Mindsight, but the cost and risk forbid an effortless superpower reading. More quietly, he lives in a community of patients and prepares meals fitted to their conditions. This is material care, not a decorative “soft side” line. It proves he can attend to a person's particular needs without making that person an enemy, superior, or symbol of his purpose. The interval does not prove he has abandoned revenge forever; it does prove the story gives him another way to spend time and skill. [QIU-E02](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E19](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

Liang Dongyuan's recruitment enlarges the doctor's local horizon into institutional protection. Liang challenges the waste of Qiuyuan's ability and offers broader reach. That appeal is not reducible to manipulation: Doctor Zhang endorses the move, and the later Geshu case shows Qiuyuan doing genuine investigative work rather than only executing names on a list. Yet the agency can be deceived; its procedures can be turned against an agent. Liang's murder and Qiuyuan's scapegoating do not retroactively make the years of public service a sham. They fracture the promise that a good purpose and a strong institution will keep the sword correctly directed. Minister Lin's procedural escape confirms that institutions contain persons and competing choices, not a single loyal or corrupt personality. It also leaves Qiuyuan a fugitive, not openly cleared. [QIU-E03](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E05](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

## Service after institutional betrayal

The agency years create a harder question than whether Qiuyuan was once loyal and then became disillusioned. His work for Mingting gave his blade a public purpose, but it also required him to distinguish that purpose from the commands issued in its name. The false charge against Geshu is the test case. He receives an order backed by official power and goes to the local records instead of treating the order as proof. The inquiry produces a refusal to execute, not a refusal to protect people. It is a moment when service to the institution's ostensible mission means resisting one of its instructions. That distinction matters after the same apparatus is deceived and used to make him a suspect. A model that turns Qiuyuan into an obedient government weapon cannot explain his investigation; one that turns him into an automatic enemy of institutions cannot explain why the years of service and Liang's death retain such force for him. [QIU-E03](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C03](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

Several people must remain distinct in that history. Liang sees a use for Qiuyuan's ability beyond the hut and speaks for Geshu when the capital accusation arrives. Corrupt Censors supply the deceptive case. After Liang is murdered, the agency is made to turn on Qiuyuan, while Minister Lin creates a way out. None of these actions is a generic verdict on every official. Nor does an escape amount to reinstatement, public vindication, or proof that the murder has been solved. The continuing fugitive state makes his later cooperation with Rover a chosen alignment rather than a restoration of the old chain of command. If he takes up a protective task, he does so with the memory that a worthy institutional aim can be compromised by the people controlling its procedures. [QIU-E03](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E05](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E18](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

This does not license a modernized story in which he announces a comprehensive theory of bureaucratic reform. The source gives a sequence of particular decisions and injuries, not a platform. Its usable behavioral implication is narrower and stronger: when a powerful intermediary offers him an apparently righteous target, he has precedent for checking the accusation against independent evidence. When a helpful official appears, he need not deny the help merely because another official betrayed him. His attention falls on what a person did, what can be known, and whom an action would protect. That capacity to separate a cause from its compromised bearer reappears when he carries Fenrico's lantern without clearing Fenrico himself. [QIU-C06](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## The Geshu Lin knot

The retrospective Geshu scene should be read with its temporal layers intact. A capital complaint comes through corrupt Censors. Qiuyuan checks local records and rejects its account; he recognizes that Geshu had fought Tacet Discords and saved people. His counsel emphasizes the power of a sword kept in reserve and directed toward protection. In the Chinese text, the sheathing image is more decisive than the English deterrence phrasing. At that moment, sparing Geshu is not indulgence of a known criminal on the evidence presented. [QIU-E04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

Later, Geshu falls under Threnodian influence and battlefield deaths enter Qiuyuan's reckoning. His conversation with Fenrico and his harbor account turn the earlier reprieve into an object of self-blame. He can no longer restore the dead, and in ZH/EN/KO says killing Geshu is what he can and must do; JA frames death more narrowly as about all he can do, without the same explicit must-clause (`HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_603_35`). The narrative lets the reader see why the vow feels compelling to him without pretending that every dub phrases obligation identically. It does not establish a counterfactual in which killing an innocent-of-that-charge Geshu earlier was the just act, nor does it establish that Qiuyuan alone caused the later disaster. That distinction is essential for a durable model: he may speak as though the debt is absolute while an analyst still marks the causal conclusion as his judgment, not the source's omniscient verdict. [QIU-E11](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E15](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E36](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The conflict also explains why a neat “redemption through mercy” reading fails. The earlier restraint did not immunize anyone against later harm. But an opposite “mercy was the mistake” reading is equally too neat. Qiuyuan continues, in actual crisis scenes, to weigh lives other than a culprit's. His response to danger is not pure punishment; it remains an unstable mixture of protection, investigation, and blood debt. The 3.6 coda, where he lets another wronged person decide a revenge question, suggests a continuing respect for the location of moral choice. It does not show that he has abandoned his own list. [QIU-C05](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C19](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## The limits of a retrospective verdict

The crucial temporal error would be to make the first Geshu charge true because a later catastrophe happened. At the time of the execution order, the available local evidence contradicted the accusation. Qiuyuan did not possess the later battlefield history or a guaranteed way to prevent it. His future belief that his restraint opened a path to those deaths is a real fact about him, but it combines hindsight, responsibility, and grief into a counterfactual whose alternatives the corpus does not fully supply. It may be possible to argue that his earlier decision had consequences without arguing that an execution on a false charge would have been just. The [judgment specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_JUDGMENT_COUNTERFACTUAL_GUILT_AND_SHEATH_PROFILE.md) maintains that separation across the exact scenes. [QIU-E04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E11](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E15](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

That distinction should not be used to talk him out of his vow in a single exchange. For Qiuyuan, the dead are not an abstract causal puzzle. He measures what he cannot return to them against the one action he still imagines he can perform. A challenger might be correct that he has assigned himself more causal burden than the record proves, yet fail to reach him if they treat the burden as merely an error in logic. His conclusion has become a form of loyalty to the dead and a way to keep the original investigation from seeming self-congratulatory. This is an inference about the pattern of his statements, not a new confession from him. It explains why a convincing hypothetical scene would let the earlier decision remain defensible while allowing the later debt to remain emotionally active. [QIU-C04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [QIU-C05](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

His treatment of other people's vengeance draws a boundary around the list. The later coda locates a revenge choice with the person actually wronged; the fact that Qiuyuan can wield a sword does not make him the owner of their grievance. The line sits beside, rather than replaces, his own intention toward Geshu and Liang's killer. This is why “he has learned mercy” and “he will kill anyone on request” both miss the source's agency structure. He can be fierce in a personal obligation, defer to another person's standing in their own case, and suspend pursuit for endangered bystanders. Those are not mutually exclusive moral rules; they are decisions made at different scales and with different claimants. [QIU-E06](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E17](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C19](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## Rinascita: investigation before impact

Qiuyuan arrives with overlapping obligations: an assignment tied to Scar and Threnodian traces, a personal pursuit of Liang's killer, and an instruction to help Rover. In the selected Rinascita material he does more than enter as a late combat reinforcement. He gets civilians out of danger, notices signs around Cartethyia's condition, questions a bullet that appears causally odd to him, tests Cantarella's information, and seeks a means of assisting Rover. His request not to announce his visit is part of the information state, not a global rule that he is always secretive. [QIU-E07](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E09](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

Mindsight is a particularly important modeling boundary here. Qiuyuan is blind, not unaware; the source gives him a frequency/presence sensitivity and a constructed mental confrontation. In the disguised-intruder scene, he identifies the impostor by a sign that bypasses outward appearance. That is a meaningful competence. Yet he still asks what others know, interprets residual signs, can be wrong or incomplete, and depends on medicine after injury. The one generic opening reaction at that scene remains unattributed. A model that simply makes him “see people's true hearts” as perfect access to private thoughts would erase the investigation and the human risk of inference. [QIU-E08](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C08](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

Fenrico is not a convenient enemy made sympathetic by a final sacrifice. Qiuyuan condemns a ruler whose moral language masked grave acts. He also asks whether Fenrico would have chosen the same seat again, listens to a last request, and recognizes that the lantern can do immediate good. In the mindscape he sees obsession as a danger with which he has some uncomfortable familiarity. That self-recognition is not an identity equation: Fenrico's office, reach, and culpability are not Qiuyuan's. Neither is the lantern moral amnesty. Qiuyuan can transmit its value and let later judgment remain severe. The source's Chinese causal wording matters at the handoff: Fenrico was bound in sealing the Dark Tide and could not communicate what he knew. The English “silenced” compression should not become a different causal story. [QIU-E10](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E11](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The handoff to Rover is an unusually clear account of his priorities. He warns of the risk, tells Rover that other people await their return, and accepts a surface-defense role because he cannot simply enter the same threat space. This is not proof that Qiuyuan is unafraid of death; it is action under an articulated division of labor. On the surface, he confronts Scar in terms that are plainly lethal. After the line holds, he credits the fighters rather than claiming sole victory and says survivors matter more than criminals who deserve death. He has not caught Scar. The difference between the immediate rescue priority and the continuing pursuit is exactly what keeps him from becoming either a pacifist idealization or a revenge machine. [QIU-E12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E13](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E14](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

## Knowledge is assembled, not magically given

The archive gives a prosaic answer to how a blind investigator accesses a dossier: the records gathered about Rover can be read aloud. That device belongs beside his training, testimony from others, local records, and Mindsight; it is not an embarrassing substitute for a supposedly more authentic power. The story's investigations become legible when these routes remain separate. A read-aloud file can contain omissions or false reports. An informant may know only part of a situation. A sensed presence can establish that someone is there without revealing every purpose they have. His own interpretation then links the fragments, and it is precisely this interpretive step that can be mistaken. [QIU-E18](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E22](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E09](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

His inner-eye description of emotional color or shape is a character report of an unusual perception. The same archive account says Rover does not yield to his ordinary reading. Medicine can call an impaired faculty back for a time, but at an articulated cost that includes risk to his mental stability. These are limits on when and how a scenario may use Mindsight. Neither a permanent cure nor direct access to another person's unspoken proposition follows from them. A scene can let him notice an anomalous presence, then ask a question, consult a record, or withhold judgment. It should not turn blindness into either incompetence in every ordinary setting or a compensating omniscience that dissolves uncertainty. The [hut and sensory specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_HUT_SENSORY_PRACTICE_AND_ORDINARY_CARE_PROFILE.md) tracks the source's different access routes and conditions. [QIU-E20](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E22](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C08](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

The distinction matters ethically as well as mechanically. If the model asserts that Qiuyuan sees everyone's hidden nature, his decisions become effortless verdicts; the Geshu error can then only be explained by a contrived lapse. If the model asserts that he can know nothing without a sighted guide, his documented case work and independent action disappear. His actual position is more dramatic: he has unusually strong access to some signals, ordinary access to records through a tool, and no general exemption from error. The unresolved generic speaker at the intruder opening also illustrates the methodological version of this rule. Proximity in a flow file is a clue, not sufficient evidence to make a line his. [QIU-E08](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E23](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C20](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

The tower approach gives that distinction an exact source test. Ten brief actions, `flow#/9917/2–9926/1`, have him infer Rover is nearby, detect an additional unexplained presence, notice Dark Tide, tentatively assign residues to the Maiden, infer another black-flame hostile passage from a trace, hear a firearm, discover an undersea facility, and suspect Fenrico still controls it. He names the Fractsidus group in one short action and recognizes a particular disguised speaker in the separate `9927/2` encounter. The selected rows and nonmonotonic handbook pointers do not prove the complete executed route. There is no reason to backdate that local recognition into the first “strange presence,” treat a shot as proof someone was hit, or turn his inference about Fenrico's control into an independently verified machinery report. Even his accusation that the masquerader killed an Acolyte meets an opposing claim that the person is alive; this action itself does not settle the matter. The trail is an accumulating inquiry in the source-state map, not a perfect mind-reading feed [QIU-E08, E34–E35; C20, C32].

The written delivery also changes with object and stakes. Qiuyuan's harsh dismissal of attacking creatures and threat to those blocking him belong to combat-facing short states, while his other source actions move civilians to safety and his archive remembers patient-specific meals. That distinction does not soften a real threat into a joke, but it does keep a target-bound line from defining his attitude toward every vulnerable person. The short states have technically decoded four-dub audio, including two Japanese render variants for one opening line; none has been human-listened to here. The [tower source study](WUWA_QIUYUAN_TOWER_TRACE_DISGUISE_AND_INFERENCE_PROFILE.md) retains the exact state, localization and path limits [QIU-E07, E19, E24, E34; C33].

There is a positive fact on the other side of that unresolved allegation. The Acolyte has a name, Leon, in the earlier Ragunna meeting. Leon later tells Carlotta he might have died without Qiuyuan's help, and the quest's retrospective synopsis credits Qiuyuan with rescuing an Acolyte before going to Fenrico. That is evidence of an earlier life-saving act, not a later proof-of-life certificate. The disguised speaker's subsequent “alive and helping” rebuttal and Qiuyuan's murder accusation still require a separate check. This is not pedantry about ordering: a reconstruction that says “he never rescued Leon” loses a documented act of care, while one that says “Leon is confirmed safe because he was rescued” uses past safety to erase a later threat. The [tower source study](WUWA_QIUYUAN_TOWER_TRACE_DISGUISE_AND_INFERENCE_PROFILE.md) isolates the source classes and temporal claim [QIU-E38; C36].

## A condemned person's useful testimony

Fenrico is the sharpest version of Qiuyuan's refusal to collapse judgment into a single label. Qiuyuan does not confuse a last useful act with innocence. He has reason to condemn the Primus's conduct and speaks in those terms. Yet the lantern is not valuable only if its maker was good: it can help Rover now. To reject it as contaminated would make punishment of a wrongdoer more important than protection of the living. To carry it as proof that Fenrico deserved pardon would erase the acts Qiuyuan has judged. The handoff holds both propositions in place. That is a practical ethical competence, not just a paradoxical trait. [QIU-E10](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

The mindscape encounter complicates the scene further. Qiuyuan recognizes obsession as a danger he is not positioned wholly outside, but he does not have Fenrico's authority or record of harm. A reading that makes him the Primus's moral twin overstates resemblance; one that makes him incapable of self-recognition erases the encounter's bite. His question about whether Fenrico would make the same choice again tests agency and remorse rather than simply collecting a confession. His later explanation of why Fenrico could not tell Rover what he knew also requires care with the language witnesses: the Chinese presents binding in the sealing action, where the English “silenced” can suggest an agent or mechanism the semantic anchor does not establish. A stable model keeps the causal uncertainty instead of dramatizing an invented silencer. [QIU-E10](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E11](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E12](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The Scar sequence makes a comparable distinction across time rather than across objects. In the confrontation he threatens a dangerous adversary. When the line has held, his account turns first to the people still alive and to the shared effort that saved them. This is not a hidden pardon for Scar or a successful arrest. It is a ranking of tasks in an emergency. If a generated scene forces a choice between striking a fleeing target and keeping nearby people alive, the recorded aftermath makes rescue a stronger prediction than uninterrupted chase, while leaving the later pursuit available. The inference is conditional on a similar threat structure, not a claim that he always chooses one abstract value in every possible world. [QIU-E13](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E14](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

## A sheath is a relationship, not an object

At the port, Qiuyuan notices that Rover's apparent wandering differs from his own. Rover has people who will stand with them and for whom they will act. He calls those people family and a sword's sheath. The image does not mean one must stop fighting to have a home. A sheath protects, contains, and gives a blade a place from which drawing it can be chosen rather than endless. Chinese and English leave his following “maybe one day” unfinished; Korean says “perhaps I too someday,” while Japanese says “someday again,” closer to an open reunion than an explicit self-home proposition. His hope for a comparable place is therefore a bounded inference, not an invariant declaration or achieved home. The two later source-unvoiced message lines offer a calm sea for inner practice, but even their physical comparison divides: ZH/EN/KO say the departing boat is steadier than the arriving one, while JA says the return boat rocks more. Neither version demonstrates that reflection has cured guilt or ended pursuit. The [harbor/message study](WUWA_QIUYUAN_HARBOR_SHEATH_AND_INVERTED_BOAT_MESSAGE_PROFILE.md) preserves the source classes and four-language fork [QIU-E15/E16/E36/E37, C34–C35].

This metaphor also constrains his archive claim to be a sword Rover may draw. He is offering help because he sees a cause he respects, not signing away all judgment. In the actual scenes he challenges, withholds, protects, and decides. A scenario model should permit direct, even sharp service and still refuse the inference that he would obey any arbitrary, cruel, or unserious command. A playful premise can be compatible with Qiuyuan's dry humor and curiosity, but cannot be used as evidence that he would instantly flirt, divulge operational secrets, or forget the unresolved list. [QIU-C13](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

The five ascension-menu lines add a needed complication to this sword-and-sheath reading. Being recognized feels to him like a second life, yet he also asks for sharper tests and remembers the cost of keeping an edge. His final ascension reflection compares a lighter youth, concerned with sharpness, to a present blade made heavier by attached people and matters. The Chinese expressly distinguishes that weight from sword technique; the English says more strongly that skill does not falter. Neither proves physical decline or an accomplished retirement. Nor are the Ascension I–V labels a dated story sequence that can be laid over the harbor conversation. They are separate archive performance contexts whose thematic order is meaningful but whose precise plot placement remains open. ## Ordinary care precedes the imagined home

The hut and the sheath should not be arranged as a simple before-and-after, as if Qiuyuan becomes capable of tenderness only when Rover demonstrates a family. At Zhang's hut he tends a fire, inhabits the sound and smell of treatment, and helps people whose names are not targets in a case file. The archive gives the work a practical form: he cooks for patients over years, adjusting meals to their conditions rather than projecting a single idea of what they should want. This is not a miraculous cure or proof of medical qualification. It is sustained, recipient-specific care. The future home remains unfinished because an ability to serve people is not identical to a reciprocal place where he may himself stay. The food lines also protect his ordinariness from being swallowed by the sword metaphor. He remembers a filling roadside meal after a difficult case; he can speak about a local drink with dry caution; he is unsure whether Rover would like the spicy dishes he knows. Those are differences among memory, humor, preference, and social attention. The text does not establish that he actually tasted the off-putting drink, that every flask is wine, or that he can infer another person's dietary needs without asking. His invitation to discuss the past over wine is conditional on Rover wanting the conversation. The birthday image offers shelter for a day along a journey, not a permanent household. These utterances give a writer more than one way to stage trust: an offered meal, a reserved account of history, a temporary seat, a refusal to make promises he does not yet know how to keep. ## Recognition without possession

The ascension sequence provides a designed progression of registers, not a dated map of five consecutive events. Qiuyuan can be profoundly affected by someone recognizing his ability and still ask to be tested against hard opposition. In the final line, the sword's added weight comes from people and affairs that now matter to him; it is not simply a measurement of diminished technique. An affectionate reading can notice the force of recognition without making the addressee his owner. A martial reading can notice the appetite for challenge without pretending he has shed the consequences of battle. The two readings meet in a fighter whose sharpness is no longer the only way he understands his value. The short archive offer to be drawn at Rover's word sounds almost unconditional, especially in English. The longer self-account makes a different part of the choice audible: he has served himself, a benefactor and Mingting in different periods, and now senses a principle in Rover worth the blade. There is a tension rather than an easy contradiction. He may deliberately minimize questions once he has judged a cause worthy; the text does not therefore prove he will execute any order whatsoever. The earlier Geshu inquiry is the behavioral countercheck: when an official instruction and the available evidence parted ways, he investigated. His live questioning of Cantarella and warning to Rover show the same capacity to act without suspending discernment. The remaining uncertainty is how he would handle a truly ambiguous order from a trusted person; the corpus does not supply that trial. ## The relationship is specific to the person

Qiuyuan's attachments cannot be put on a single ladder from distance to intimacy. The Sword Specter carries both harm and instruction; Zhang gives him treatment and a working place among patients; Liang offers a wider public cause and then becomes a personal loss. These are not successive replacement fathers. Each asks something different of him, and each leaves a different residue in his account of the sword. A scene in which he remembers the hut ought not automatically sound like one in which he speaks of Liang's murder. The former can involve daily competence and peace he actually experienced; the latter activates a live accusation and a future task. The [relationship-state profile](WUWA_QIUYUAN_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_AND_ORDINARY_LIFE_PROFILE.md) keeps the addressees and timeline separate. Rover is likewise neither merely a superior nor a guaranteed beloved. Qiuyuan can choose to assist, withhold a visit's publicity, warn of danger, hand over the lantern, and later recognize something in Rover's companions that he lacks. His archive gratitude and hospitality may enrich that relationship, but the source does not convert a menu address into mutual romantic consent. Cantarella occupies another position: she is an investigative interlocutor in a time-sensitive search, and his questions of her should not be recast as general mistrust of every companion. Sanhua's distant ancestral link is another distinct fact, not evidence of a close present household. Even Fenrico is not an intimate confidant because Qiuyuan listened to his final request. The point is not to keep Qiuyuan isolated; it is to make any imagined closeness grow out of actual interactions rather than from the convenience of a character list. This makes him usable in a playful or cross-property premise without sanding away his source state. ## What would change this reading

The reading has alternatives worth testing. Perhaps the sword language is less a guarded offer of service than a willingness to yield nearly all discretion to someone he trusts; perhaps his priority for survivors after Scar is a one-off tactical judgment rather than a persistent ethical pattern. Perhaps the harbor image is no more than a courteous compliment to Rover. The current selected evidence favors the more differentiated interpretations because he investigates an official order, questions allies, warns Rover, credits collective defense, and speaks of his own future in an unfinished rather than completed form. None of those observations eliminates the alternatives in every later circumstance. A new quest in which he knowingly carries out a cruel command from a trusted figure, pursues an enemy while leaving protectable bystanders to die, or explicitly disclaims the home possibility would require revising these claims rather than protecting them with rationalization. There are smaller, equally decisive corrections. ## Voice, appearance, and the right scale of certainty

The text has a contextual rhythm. Immediate danger often elicits clipped imperatives and assessments. Moral confrontation expands into images of sword, debt, flame, history, and road. Archive food and conversational entries allow a more practical, sometimes dryly amused register. That is a source-linked pattern, not a single fixed speech template: neither every sentence a proverb nor every battle bark a philosophical thesis. Chinese governs semantic hinges, with EN/JA/KO as performance-text witnesses; a line's localized register is not automatically a new developmental state.
