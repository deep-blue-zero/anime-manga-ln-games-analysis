# Qiuyuan — a hut is more than a pause between swords

Qiuyuan's future “sheath” image is often the most memorable expression of his wish for a home. The pinned source has already shown him inhabiting a quieter arrangement for years: Doctor Zhang's treatment hut, its patients, stoves, medicines and meals. That interval does not cancel his later pursuit, but it prevents a model from treating care, rest or accessible everyday competence as things he can only learn *after* vengeance. This specialist follows those ordinary practices and their limits. The [central reading](WUWA_QIUYUAN_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) covers the arc; the [judgment specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_JUDGMENT_COUNTERFACTUAL_GUILT_AND_SHEATH_PROFILE.md) keeps the Geshu and revenge claims separate. The [matrix](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) routes the evidence below.

## The wounded arrival does not yield a reliable kill count

In `FavorStory_141102_Content`, Qiuyuan wakes bound in bandages and hears a stern doctor whose voice is not murderous. He thinks he ought to have died; Zhang refuses to decide his life for him. The narrative connects regret, hatred, an earlier confrontation and a moment when Qiuyuan allows weapons to strike him. It does not make the ensuing peace a frictionless conversion. He accepts shelter, puts away the sword, tends the fire and asks to remain as a helper; he also accepts a medicine recipe that could assist if he fights again [QIU-E02, C02].

One localization hinge must be explicit before the scene is used as a numerical backstory. The doctor's opening rebuke in Chinese says Qiuyuan *fought* dozens bare-handed. Japanese and Korean likewise say he faced or fought dozens. English instead says he *killed* dozens. The surrounding Chinese/JA/KO wording does not independently establish that all those people died. English is a real official witness, but the Chinese-anchored packet cannot promote its kill count to a settled source fact. A scene may acknowledge that Qiuyuan has violent history; it cannot cite this passage alone for “dozens killed bare-handed” [QIU-E27, C24]. This distinction does not exonerate him from all earlier killing. It refuses only an unsupported specific count and action.

## A different sensory world is not sensory absence

The hut passage describes the loss of the weapon-filled inner bamboo grove he once “saw,” then medicine smell, the repeated crackle of stove fire, and patients' pain, tears, thanks and laughter. It says he enjoys a peace he had not had for years and recognizes that he is not separate from the ordinary people passing through. These are textual observations about a time-indexed lived environment, not claims that blindness automatically grants perfect hearing or that later battle states feel the same. The quiet is active: he tends simmering medicine and imagines the patients and doctor in the place where he lives [QIU-E02, E28].

Zhang's comparison of medicine with a sword—its use and dose can heal or harm—also limits a sentimental reading. The hut is not a place where all weapons become bad and every remedy good. The doctor says Qiuyuan's Forte is damaged and his prepared medicine may call his inner-eye faculty back if he raises a blade. EN adds an open-ended timing phrase; ZH/JA/KO do not specify the effect's duration. The later archive makes special medicine necessary for full-force use and warns of mental disturbance, so no permanent cure is evidenced. Qiuyuan initially insists that he will not raise a blade; later source states show he does. Preserve the earlier refusal as a real position at that point, not as an eternal oath retroactively broken in bad faith. Likewise, the doctor's prediction that he will die amid blades is a character's prognosis or warning, not a canonical guarantee of his final fate [QIU-E02, C02, C08].

## Reading, sensing and asking are distinct access routes

`FavorWord_141101_Content` answers a practical question that fantasy shorthand often obscures. Qiuyuan says Mingting gathered records about Rover, and when asked how he reads them, his answer is a read-aloud function. The source does not need to erase his blindness or invoke Mindsight to explain access to a dossier. An investigation may combine institutional files, speech technology, informants, ordinary inference and his unusual senses, each with its own failure modes [QIU-E18, E22, C08].

`FavorWord_141103_Content` describes emotions as colored figures in his inner perception but says Rover resists his usual reading. `FavorWord_141111_Content` distinguishes a carried medicine from wine and states its cost: it can draw out his impaired inner faculty, with a risk of mental disturbance. The bottle is not a casual drinking prop, and a measured condition for using the faculty is not omniscient thought-reading. If a crossover character silently hides a plan, Qiuyuan may notice a presence or emotional sign under source-supported conditions, inspect a record or ask a question. The model must not invent direct access to the content of the thought or a permanent pharmaceutical cure [QIU-E20, E22, C08].

## Meals are care, memory and selective company

The archive's `FavorWord_141106_Content` says he cooked for Zhang's patients over years and adjusted food to differences in their conditions. His knowledge of some spicy Chongzhou dishes is followed by uncertainty about whether Rover would like them. This gives a concrete, recipient-sensitive action: ask what a person needs and likes before preparing a meal. It does not make Qiuyuan a universal physician, an innkeeper, or a mind-reader of dietary preference. The hut story establishes duration and context for the skill; the archive voice supplies the direct first-person recollection [QIU-E19, C16].

`FavorWord_141108_Content` remembers a meal after an exhausting Yuezhou case and a hard return to Mingting: rice, meat and wine at a roadside place. Its force is satiety and respite, not lavish taste or a claim that he always drinks heavily. `FavorWord_141109_Content` describes a green-gray local drink as unappealing and warns Rover away from its taste; the line does not unambiguously show him swallowing it. `FavorWord_141112_Content` offers a conversation about his past over wine if Rover truly wants it. These three archive objects are different scenes and functions: meal memory, humorous warning and conditional social invitation. No evidence turns every cup into medicine, every flask into alcohol, or the invitation into an exclusive romance [QIU-E20, C16–C17, C25].

The birthday line `FavorWord_141118_Content` calls worldly lodging temporary and asks to share shelter on this particular day. It is a modest offer of company, not a declaration that he has permanently entered Rover's circle. At the later harbor he recognizes that Rover already has people who form a home and sheath; he only begins to imagine his own future. The archive's wine invitation and birthday shelter can ground slow disclosure in a hypothetical story, but cannot do the relational work on behalf of an unwritten scene [QIU-E15, E21, C14, C17].

## Rest is real even before the debt is paid

`FavorWord_141110_Content` says he cannot afford to dwell on a stable ideal while he may not survive to morning and still believes he owes revenge and service. That present-tense uncertainty should not erase the actual hut years or the earlier roadside moment of relief. His stance is not “never rested” but “rest has not settled what I must still do.” This is a useful friction for character modeling: he can make a sick person food, joke dryly about a bad drink, use a read-aloud tool, take a temporary seat and still decline to promise a domestic future. A partner cannot heal him simply by giving him lodging; equally, the unresolved list does not make ordinary care performative or unavailable [QIU-E02, E19–E21, C02, C14].

For a generated scene, declare whether it is the Q1 hut interval, later fugitive service, Rinascita crisis, or an explicitly hypothetical post-departure future. Distinguish the actual addressee, immediate risk, what Qiuyuan knows, and whether the object in his hand is a medicine flask, a meal's wine or something else. If he offers care, preserve the recipient's needs rather than prescribing a made-up treatment. If he senses a clue, preserve the independent possibility that he is mistaken.
