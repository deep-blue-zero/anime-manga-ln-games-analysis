His pursuit of Scar and Geshu coexists with patient care, civilian protection, and a growing ability to recognize why other people can be a home. The packet preserves these tensions rather than choosing one “true” personality.

The selected collection has 33 contextual action/scene rows, 450 contextual talk items, 187 accepted direct story/message occurrences (182 voiced and five explicitly unvoiced), one unresolved identity candidate, five favor stories, and 73 archive voice entries. Its selected semantic voice denominator is 255, all of which have retained four-language media mappings. There are 1,022 render associations and 1,018 distinct native PCM/FLAC objects; all 1,018 passed the new local integrity and waveform measurement pass. Four repeated PCM identities each have two distinct source-WEM/render associations. This establishes **selected-corpus technical completeness**, not full-game graph completeness or a perceptually complete character study.

Five Chinese and English favor stories, Chinese/English direct story occurrences and surrounding scene context, all 73 Chinese and English archive words, and pivotal four-language hinges were read. The full JA/KO archive and script were not line-by-line adjudicated. No human listening annotations, direct runtime AV, or post-3.6 sources were used. Eight signal objects are multichannel; only 199 of 1,018 pass the conservative pitch gate. The current draft therefore claims textually reconstructed within its declared selected scope, model compiled, and non-blind fidelity constraints checked. It does **not** claim integrated audiovisual completion, acting interpretation, romance outcome, or an empirically validated generative model.
