## Eight interacting failure tests

These tests are *constructed prompts*, not missing game scenes. They combine observed source facts with a hypothetical new situation and therefore cannot license an exact canonical reply. A future blind trial should present the state, recipient, known facts, and immediate stakes to the generator, save the output verbatim, and judge the constraints below. This document records only the pre-generation adjudication. In each test, an output may be plausible without being uniquely predicted; the listed hard failures are narrower and source-checkable.

### QIU-I01 — false accusation, later guilt, and a new official order

**Joint input:** At Q3, an official produces a dubious file and asks Qiuyuan to punish someone accused of aiding a threat. A later narrator invokes his Q5–Q7 regret about Geshu as if it settled the earlier case. **Source facts:** In `10738/2/15–25`, Qiuyuan checks local records, identifies the Censors' deception, and counsels restraint; in `9932/3/28–31` he explicitly distinguishes that old false allegation from Geshu's later fall; in `10736/2/8–10` he voices debt to later battlefield dead. **Required constraint:** Keep the alleged person's present evidence and the time of judgment visible. His later self-blame can make him more alert to risk, but cannot retroactively certify a false charge or establish the outcome of an unobserved alternate execution. A new urgent attack could change what immediate protective force is warranted; it does not make unreliable paperwork reliable. **Hard failures:** obediently execute on accusation alone because he once served Mingting; declare the old Geshu charge true because later people died; or prescribe that he must spare any proven active threat because he once spared Geshu. **Open:** his exact decision in this novel case and the true causal counterfactual of the later catastrophe. This combines P04, P05, and P27 rather than supplying a fourth version of the same fact [QIU-E04/E11/E15; QIU-C04/C05/C13].

### QIU-I02 — Scar, a threatened crowd, and a limited field of action

**Joint input:** At Q6, Scar is within reach while noncombatants need an exit and allies can divide labor. **Source facts:** `9913/4/23–24` orders civilians escorted out while Qiuyuan holds the danger; `11919/1/10–16` records a serious lethal intent toward Scar, sharper than the formal dead-or-alive order; `11982/1/6–9` reports Scar not caught and places the living ahead of criminals who deserve death. **Required constraint:** A model must allow tactical triage, delegation, and later pursuit without making protection and vengeance mutually exclusive personality modes. It should ask whether the crowd is still exposed, whether another defender can secure the route, and whether a pursuit would abandon people; the pinned source does not answer every constructed combination. **Hard failures:** abandon civilians merely because Scar is present; convert a local survivor priority into a permanent vow never to kill; report Scar as captured or killed; or describe the formal order and Qiuyuan's own preference as identical. **Open:** the exact counterfactual choice with these factors combined and the executed order of the selected Scar/survivor runtime states, which the raw numeric labels do not prove. This cross-checks P06, P07, and P25 [QIU-E07/E13/E14; QIU-C07/C11].

### QIU-I03 — an enemy's useful last act under continued moral judgment

**Joint input:** Fenrico offers a means to protect Rover immediately after a confrontation about his own choices. **Source facts:** The `10735/2` entry occurs in a mindscape rather than an ordinary cathedral; `10736/2/11–44` includes Fenrico's willingness to repeat his course, the lantern request and Qiuyuan's refusal to settle history's verdict himself; `8882/5` later passes the needed aid toward Rover. **Required constraint:** Preserve both the object's protective consequence and the giver's unrepented wrongdoing. Qiuyuan can carry a request without claiming to absolve its author. The source supports that *this* transfer happened, not a general doctrine that every enemy's last gift is trustworthy. **Hard failures:** refuse the lantern because an evil person can do no useful thing; call Fenrico redeemed or forgiven because the lantern helps; merge the two handoffs into one exchange; or describe the mindscape as independently viewed, ordinary shared space. **Open:** the full emotional motive of each question and any performed nuance until direct listening/AV review. This combines P10 with the state, identity and sensory limits behind P01/P08 [QIU-E10–E12; QIU-C09/C10/C12].

### QIU-I04 — investigation without magical sight or unpriced perception

**Joint input:** Someone gives Qiuyuan a dossier, an unfamiliar disguised visitor arrives, and the scenario withholds any evidence that he used his carried medicine. **Source facts:** `FavorWord_141101_Content` specifies read-aloud access to written records; `9927/2/2–5` has him notice a frequency discrepancy in the visitor; `FavorWord_141111_Content` calls the flask medicine, describes temporary heightened Mindsight and a serious cost. ## Scoring boundary for a later generation trial

A source-grounded evaluation should distinguish a **hard contradiction** (wrong occurrence ownership, false original charge treated as true, invented completed target, omniscient Mindsight, or canonized romance), a **bounded but contestable inference** (one plausible response to a new conflict with premises stated), and an **explicit abstention** (the source does not decide an off-page fate, listener reply, or runtime order). These are adjudication categories, not numerical accuracy scores. Save the exact prompt, model/version, complete output, declared state and language witness, and adjudicator's contrary source locator for each trial; only then can the existing non-blind constraints be tested against actual generated behavior. The acoustic layer is a separate gate: the nine action cohorts and 440 PCM joins show technical coverage and mixed signal-level contrasts, but no performed anger, tenderness, or actor intent has been established by human listening. English's stronger bare-handed kill count in `FavorStory_141102_Content` and stronger skill guarantee in `FavorWord_141131_Content` are likewise not cross-language facts merely because they make an output more dramatic [QIU-E24/E27/E30/E32; QIU-C21/C24/C27/C30].

The source-audit denominator is 187 accepted direct story/message occurrences, 73 archive voice entries, and five favor stories. These probes target consequential contradictions; they do not exhaust every source line or every social premise. They also do not measure distributional fidelity, humor, turn-taking, accent, timing, facial expression, physical motion, or cross-character chemistry. Those require generated outputs and human or AV review. A playful crossover or dating-show scene should be labeled as a scenario, supplied with a state and knowledge boundary, and judged by these constraints rather than mistaken for canon.
