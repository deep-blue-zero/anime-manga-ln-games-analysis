# Qiuyuan — source-defined four-dub audio cohorts, pre-AV

This audit asks whether measured sound supports a simple performance rule such as “his revenge voice is louder than his protective voice” or a single acoustic trajectory from restraint to chosen home. It does **not** claim to have listened to the recordings. The nine cohorts were selected by exact pinned flow-state/action address because the [literary reading](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_QIUYUAN_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) and [claim matrix](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) already make those contexts materially different. They are not clusters learned from the audio and are not nine chronological scenes of equal weight.

## What the measurements do and do not say

The paired -45 dBFS-gated active-frame energy contrast (target dub minus Chinese, median of exact semantic pairs) changes with the selected action. For EN versus ZH it is **−1.11 dB** in evacuation, **+1.34 dB** in original restraint, **+0.65 dB** in the Scar confrontation, **−1.83 dB** in postbattle survivor priority, and **+0.53 dB** in the harbor/home action. The signs do not yield a stable “vengeance louder, rescue softer” rule. For JA versus ZH, all nine cohort medians are higher, from **+1.76 to +3.59 dB**; that persistent cross-dub offset is at least as compatible with recording, mastering, translation and segmentation differences as with a character-level delivery choice. KO versus ZH is lower in eight contexts and +0.31 dB in the two-line coda. None of these numbers assigns motive, tenderness, anger or narrative development to an actor.

The conservative pitch gate qualifies just **61/440** selected PCM objects. In the Fenrico-judgment and lantern cohorts, zero Chinese, English and Korean objects qualify; their few qualifying estimates are Japanese. The original-restraint cohort contains the complete selected set's eight multichannel objects (two per dub), which the analyzer flags as not speaker-isolated. Even where a median pitch exists, the surviving-language subsets differ too much to support a four-dub pitch/persona comparison. The private artifact retains -50/-45/-40 dBFS silence-gate summaries and overlapping QC flags so a future analyst can test threshold sensitivity, not hide it behind one headline median.

The *text* still supports distinct actions: protecting civilians, challenging Fenrico, threatening Scar, prioritizing survivors, and imagining a possible home. Measurement does not erase those source facts; it only declines to prove that the performance implements them by a universal volume or pitch code. The archive's patient-specific cooking and chosen-service lines have not been folded into this scene comparison. A larger machine sample alone would still not substitute for claim-conditioned four-dub listening with exact render IDs and scene context.

## Negative controls and open work

The [source census](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md) separates **five accepted but `PlayVoice=false` occurrences** from the 255 selected voiced lines: `10859/1/0`, `11244/1/0`, `14530/1/0–1`, and `16882/1/10`. The two `14530/1` post-journey message turns were checked in the raw scene ledger and are explicitly unvoiced; they may inform written state, never heard delivery. The unattributed `9927/2/0` generic reaction is an identity uncertainty, not a Qiuyuan voice-training example. These cases are not decode failures and are not silently dropped from the analytical accounting.

The next pass is human listening across all four dubs for exact claim-bearing occurrences, recording audible observations, alternate production explanations and confidence. Runtime scene/video review remains separate. Until then, QIU-C07/C11/C15/C21 are grounded in text, action and technical joins at their respective levels—not in observed actor intent or a completed performed-voice analysis.
