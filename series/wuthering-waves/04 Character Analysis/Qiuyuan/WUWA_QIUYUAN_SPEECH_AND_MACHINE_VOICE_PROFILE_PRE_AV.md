# Qiuyuan — textual speech and measured voice, pre-AV

This profile separates **what the lines say**, **what the signal measurement found**, and **what remains unheard**. Chinese is the semantic anchor; English, Japanese, and Korean are official text/voice witnesses in the selected collection. The machine-readable [fourteen-case join](AUDIO_MATCHED_SEMANTIC_CASES.json) gives semantic IDs, exact text keys/source locators, per-render IDs, WEM and PCM/FLAC hashes, durations, energy, pitch qualification, and QC flags without redistributing media. The [source audit](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md) defines the complete selected denominator.

## Textual speech modes

Qiuyuan's immediate protective speech can be short and directive: in 9913/4 he tells others to take civilians away and holds the position; in 9916/3 he sends them toward safety. The Scar confrontation at 11919/1 is equally direct but threatening rather than protective. A scenario model should let brevity serve several functions instead of assigning every short sentence the same cold emotion. The English and Chinese lines are both witnesses, but the situational contrast is established by action and addressee, not punctuation alone. [QIU-E07](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E13](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

In longer moral conversation he uses blades, blood debts, historical judgment, flame, road, and sheath imagery. The Geshu retrospective at 10738/2 uses the kept-sheathed blade to urge proportion. The Fenrico exchange at 10735/2–10736/2 turns from accusation to his own unresolved responsibility and the useful lantern. The harbor conversation at 9932/3 makes the sheath an interpersonal image. These motifs are not interchangeable decorative “samurai” sayings: their meanings change with the recipient and the state of the case. [QIU-E04](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E10](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-E15](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The archive permits a third mode: practical and dryly companionable. FavorWord_141106_Content explains meals fitted to patients, _141108_Content recalls a modest meal after a hard assignment, _141109_Content warns against an unpleasant drink, and _141112_Content invites a conversation about his past over wine. His technology joke in _141101_Content answers how a blind investigator reads files: read-aloud software. These are not combat barks and should not be generated as though every pause hides an aphorism. The archive also addresses Rover through a voice-menu context; it is not automatically synchronized with any one live story scene.

The [42-record combat-trigger profile](WUWA_QIUYUAN_COMBAT_TRIGGER_SENSORY_AND_LIMIT_LANGUAGE_PROFILE.md) adds a distinct, highly compressed surface: auditory skill/sensor calls, a lethal liberation line, an injury line warning that self-risk has limits, and three different defeat-trigger images. It also exposes a source-key trap: thirty raw favor-word `Id` values differ from the numeric suffix of their actual `Content` text key. The [exact row/key crosswalk](FAVOR_WORD_ID_TEXT_KEY_CROSSWALK.json) joins all 73 archive rows to four dub render records by pinned source locator and actual `Content`; no missing audio is inferred from those Id/key differences. Combat labels specify possible playback conditions, not dated plot outcomes. In particular, a fallen bark does not prove Qiuyuan's death, a hearing bark does not prove mind reading, and an EN paraphrase of allusive ZH does not define his universal register [QIU-E33](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C31](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests).

## Material multilingual hinges

At HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_4051_16, Chinese favors the sword kept sheathed as the important image. English expresses deterrence more overtly. Both support restraint; a model should not make the exact English maxim the entire Chinese philosophy. At Main_Rinascita_2_12_904_6, the English rendering makes Fenrico sound silenced by the Dark Tide, while the Chinese explains a sealing obligation that prevents him communicating. The source-governing causal account is the latter. In the prose `FavorStory_141102_Content`, Zhang's Chinese, Japanese and Korean rebuke says Qiuyuan fought or faced dozens bare-handed, whereas English says he killed dozens. That English kill count is not established by the Chinese anchor or other two witnesses; this is a **textual story difference**, not a four-dub performance finding. At HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_603_37–39, the home/sheath metaphor is relational; its conditional future must not be rewritten as a completed homecoming. `FavorWord_141128_Content` addresses transformative recognition more directly in JA/KO than in English's impersonal “chance” formulation. `FavorWord_141131_Content` shares an increased-weight image, but ZH denies a *swordsmanship cause* where EN more strongly says skill does not falter; KO explicitly mentions people/things to protect. These are textual distinctions, not acting observations or dated biography. The [matrix](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) and [recognition specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_RECOGNITION_BLADE_WEIGHT_AND_CHOSEN_SERVICE_PROFILE.md) record their limits. A complete line-by-line JA/KO semantic review has not been performed, so a claim about a uniquely Japanese or Korean psychological emphasis beyond these inspected hinges would be premature.

The tower clue lines sharpen this register account without supplying a heard delivery. `HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_3_1` uses Chinese `从气息来看` to mark an inference from presence; JA is expectation-like while EN renders Rover as more categorically present. The Maiden residue at `3003_1` is tentative. For the wind attribution at `3004_1`, ZH is more direct, EN says “must,” JA asks a question, and KO keeps an appearance/likelihood marker. At `4002_2` Fenrico's apparent control is again a working inference. That distribution is why a single English “Qiuyuan knows” paraphrase loses useful confidence distinctions. The tower's two harsh monster-addressed actions also do not make a wounded bystander the target of that register. The 21 accepted tower lines have 85 measured-valid four-dub render objects because the opening key has two distinct Japanese runtime variants; neither variant has been listened to here. The [tower study](WUWA_QIUYUAN_TOWER_TRACE_DISGUISE_AND_INFERENCE_PROFILE.md) and [AV crosswalk](WUWA_QIUYUAN_AV_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_CROSSWALK.md) keep the cue, text key and render separate [QIU-E34–E35; C32–C33].

The harbor warrants an additional four-witness warning: `_603_35` states the lethal obligation explicitly in ZH/EN/KO, while JA gives the shorter sense of what he *can* do; `_603_37–39` places the home/sheath first with Rover, then leaves Qiuyuan's own future elliptical in ZH/EN, explicitly “I too” in KO, and “again” in JA. These are inspected text differences, not proof that one dub's actor sounded more determined or lonely. The separate two-line message `phone_JL_15_1–2` is source-unvoiced: ZH/EN/KO make the departing boat steadier, JA says it rocks more, and all four mention a calm sea. No audible “peaceful departure” can be derived from those items. The [harbor/message specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_HARBOR_SHEATH_AND_INVERTED_BOAT_MESSAGE_PROFILE.md) retains their raw locators and uncertainty [QIU-E36–E37; C34–C35].

## Measurement and retrieval method

The installed-client voice collection has 255 selected direct semantic voiced lines: 182 story and 73 archive. Four languages produced 1,022 render associations. Four repeated PCM identities with distinct source-WEM hashes reduce these to 1,018 unique local FLAC/native PCM objects. The generic local adapter ran the Sigrika waveform analyzer (version sigrika-waveform-analysis-0.2.0, SHA-256 73c2904c38d06440a86a572d05fa3fe200e23055e68f0aeaec3be75313d3c4a2) on every unique object. The adapter checked FLAC bytes and native interleaved s16le payload hashes against the collection, selected a highest-RMS native channel for analysis rather than blindly downmixing, resampled to 16 kHz, and measured 20 ms gate activity, energy, Praat-autocorrelation pitch with a sensitivity pass, harmonicity, and spectral proxies. Object-level results and full local paths stay outside Git in the private Qiuyuan audio-work directory. There were zero measurement/hash failures. This pass did **not** independently re-decode every source WEM; it uses and verifies the existing decoded collection's retained identities.

The language counts differ slightly because variant associations and identical PCM are separate identities. The total duration is a **sum of selected object lengths**, not unique story time or a speaking-rate comparison. Translation length, padding, take selection, and segmentation can all change it. Conservative QC flags mark 796 objects with frequent pitch edge-band estimates, 264 as pitch-parameter-sensitive, 29 with too few voiced frames, eight as multichannel not isolated, two as long enough to recheck against subtitle extent, and one very short. Flags overlap. Only 199 of 1,018 objects clear the stated principal pitch gate. The Japanese subset of qualified objects is not directly comparable to the Korean subset as an actor baseline; selection bias is severe. No emotional pitch finding is claimed.

## Claim-conditioned machine checks

The exact Scar-threat case `Main_Rinascita_2_12_44_12` and survivor-priority case `Main_Rinascita_2_12_73_8` are a useful negative test for an easy but unsupported performance stereotype. Their -45 dBFS-gated median active-frame levels are, respectively, **-24.38 / -24.59 dBFS (EN), -21.29 / -20.89 (JA), -27.60 / -25.83 (KO), and -25.19 / -24.28 (ZH)**. Even the direction of the small EN difference differs from the other three, and recording level is not character anger or compassion. The paired low-energy fractions are **.12/.09, .12/.18, .34/.28, and .07/.30** in the same language order; those directions also disagree. Text and action continue to support QIU-C07 and QIU-C11, but the selected signal proxies do not justify a universal “louder threat, softer rescue” delivery rule. The threat remains a threat, not an observed killing.

The old restraint counsel `HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_4051_16` lasts 7.70/11.66/7.69/6.58 seconds (EN/JA/KO/ZH), while the later harbor/home case `HRT_Rinascita_Interludes_603_38` lasts 7.34/12.87/11.28/11.11 seconds. Different wording and recording segmentation bar a lexical-speed or emotional-change inference from those durations. QIU-C04/C05/C14 remain source-text readings; a performed continuity or rupture is **OPEN** pending listening and runtime context. The 8/56 pitch-qualified denominator further prevents a four-dub pitch narrative from these selected cases. Measurements are reproducible at their exact PCM hashes in the private object-level results; no new auditory observation is claimed.

## Source-defined story-action crosscheck

The [nine-cohort audit](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_DEFINED_AUDIO_COHORT_AUDIT.md) broadens the two-case check without treating sound as an emotion classifier. Exact actions for evacuation, original Geshu restraint, both Fenrico exchanges, Scar, surface defense, postbattle survivors, harbor/home and the later coda contain **110 disjoint semantic story lines**, **440 render associations** and **440 distinct hash-valid PCM objects**. The machine artifact is private and SHA-pinned in the report; it retains source locator, semantic ID, text key, per-language render ID and object hash for each member. Only **61/440** objects qualify for the conservative pitch gate, and eight in the original-restraint action are multichannel, not speaker-isolated.

Paired EN-minus-ZH active-level medians switch sign: evacuation −1.11 dB, original restraint +1.34, Scar +0.65, postbattle survivors −1.83 and harbor/home +0.53. Across all nine actions, JA–ZH medians are consistently positive (+1.76 to +3.59 dB), a pattern that cannot be assigned to Qiuyuan's moral state without production controls. The contrasts do not prove a stable “louder revenge, softer care” performance rule or a performed progression from sheath counsel to home. In the pinned source, five accepted story/message occurrences are explicitly unvoiced; the two post-journey message turns at `14530/1` cannot be used as heard reflection. This crosscheck strengthens the *retrieval and uncertainty boundary*, not a claim of human listening or enacted psychological change [QIU-E32](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [QIU-C30](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests).

## Current inference boundary

Text supports a contextual speech design: concise under immediate pressure; reflective imagery when arguing about responsibility; practical care and dry humor in ordinary/archive contexts; and separate attack, skill, injury, fallen and traversal trigger language. Audio supports complete selected retrieval, hashed object identity, and signal measurements with severe pitch-QC limits. It does not yet support labels like tender, husky, contemptuous, flirtatious, exhausted, or actor-intended anger. A generation prompt may request such a performance as a creative choice, but must not describe it as an observed canon recording.
