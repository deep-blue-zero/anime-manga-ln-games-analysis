## State is a partial order

The archive's sequence runs from the fire/Sword Specter apprenticeship to Doctor Zhang's hut, Liang Dongyuan's recruitment, the Geshu false case and institutional collapse, then five fugitive years and the Rinascita mission. The Geshu scene is a retrospective, even though its flow-state row is numerically later than several Rinascita scenes. In Rinascita, investigation with Cantarella and a search for a helpful object precede the Fenrico lantern's acquisition and transfer. The Scar confrontation and defended surface line do not finish the pursuit. At the port he departs; a later 3.6 coda provides a separate ethical observation. Optional trial, candy-event, gondola, archive, and phone lines have only the prerequisites their sources establish, not a universal chronological slot. [QIU-E01–E17](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The most important state variable is not “kind versus cruel.” It is the object and immediacy of an obligation: who currently needs protection, what case facts he knows, and which blood debt he believes active. State Q3's sparing of Geshu cannot be replaced by Q7's later indictment; state Q5's suspicion of the disguised visitor cannot be backdated into full knowledge at the earlier tower approach. A hypothetical scene must declare its state or preserve uncertainty about it.

The port action makes this relational boundary unusually precise. Qiuyuan gives the earlier false Geshu charge and later Threnodian harm as separate episodes before naming *Rover's* companions as a home and sheath. His own prospective “one day” is elliptical in ZH/EN, explicit as “I too” in KO, and “again” in JA; JA also casts Rover's sheath more as an eventual state. The backfilled two-line phone message is not dated by a quest prerequisite or voiced in the selected source. Its calm sea cannot settle Qiuyuan's own home, love, mental peace, or continued pursuit; the departure boat is *steadier* in ZH/EN/KO but *rocks more* in JA. A hypothetical intimate bond must be earned in its own scene, not imported from either localization [QIU-E36–E37; C34–C35].

The tower trace at `flow#/9917/2–9926/1` supplies a concrete test of this state rule: an initially unexplained presence, tentative Maiden attributions and a firearm sound precede the later local recognition of a disguised Fractsidus interlocutor at `9927/2`. Knowing that person *there* does not mean Qiuyuan knew the earlier trace's owner, every prior act or the Acolyte's fate. His accusation of murder and the adversary's denial remain conflicting testimony in that action [QIU-E08, E34–E35; C32].

## Ordinary anchors

Patient-specific cooking is the strongest ordinary-life evidence. In the Chinese archive entry FavorWord_141106_Content he distinguishes differing conditions and suitable light meals, then mentions Chongzhou's spicier food with uncertainty about Rover's taste. A faithful low-stakes scene may have him ask what a person can eat and adjust a meal; it should not turn him into an all-purpose physician, a chef who always cooks, or someone who already knows a new acquaintance's preferences. The hut in FavorStory_141102_Content supports this practiced care as behavior over time, not just one boast.

The meal memory in FavorWord_141108_Content is specific: hungry and dusty after work in Yuezhou, he returns to Mingting and remembers rice, meat, and wine at a roadside place. The value is not luxury. It is relief, satiety, and a respite between assignments. That memory gives a scenario a credible sensory anchor, but not evidence that he is an indiscriminate drinker. FavorWord_141109_Content adds a comic warning about a strange green-gray Mingting drink; the line does not prove he personally swallowed it. FavorWord_141111_Content identifies the flask he carries as medicine, not wine. FavorWord_141112_Content separately offers talk of his past over a pot of wine. It is a social affordance to candor with a particular listener, not a guaranteed confession or courtship. [QIU-E20](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

He reads institutional material through smart read-aloud technology in FavorWord_141101_Content. This small practical adaptation matters for a blind investigator: competence should not be explained by pretending blindness disappears. It can involve tools, memory, people, and specialized sensing. The profile/medical sources likewise limit his impaired Forte and medicinal enhancement. Conversely, his fondness for arch or cutting remarks in some voice-menu and scene lines does not make him socially inaccessible. A source-faithful ordinary scene can be dry, practical, and only selectively forthcoming.

Birthday wishes, ally recruitment lines, and optional rides are address-bound source surfaces. A romance scenario may be written as an explicitly hypothetical crossover, but the selected source supports no mutually acknowledged romantic relationship, exclusive claim, or eroticized obedience. The best affordance is restrained companionship: he can converse, feed, escort, protect, tease, and slowly choose what history to disclose. The strongest barrier is his stated uncertainty about life after revenge and his ongoing pursuit. Those can produce dramatic tension without inventing a canonical rejection of intimacy.
