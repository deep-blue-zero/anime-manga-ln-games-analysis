# Qiuyuan — evidence and falsification matrix

This is a working analytical register, not a replacement for the source. A source fact is a claim about what a retained text or action shows; an inference explains a pattern; a value judgment is explicitly identified. The source key and compact flow locator resolve through the [source audit](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md), which defines R/A/T and the pinned source root. An evidence bundle preserves the scene and speaker boundary, not merely a detached sentence. Favor keys are indexed in the collection's four-language character source package. Public prose below paraphrases rather than reproduces the archive or script wholesale.

## Claim-level adjudications and source boundaries

**QIU-C32 — the tower clues form an inference ladder, not a magical identity feed.** Source states `9917/2–9926/1` separate sensed presence, residual frequency/wind, auditory firearm evidence, material facility discovery and Qiuyuan's increasingly specific hypotheses. Chinese and Japanese make his opening Rover-location claim inferential where English sounds more categorical; the Maiden wind attribution is strongest in English; and the Fractsidus group call becomes a question in JA/KO. These are four localized presentations of *situated* knowledge, not license to pool their maximum certainty. `9927/2` then shows a disguised interlocutor whom Qiuyuan recognizes locally, but its opening generic cue is still unattributed and the encounter does not say every earlier anomaly was that person. His allegation that the Acolyte was killed is met with a claim that the Acolyte lives elsewhere. Both statements are in-world testimony; a later direct view or independent record is needed to settle the fate. The retrospective quest synopsis and Leon's direct thanks corroborate that Qiuyuan **saved him earlier**; that temporal fact does not arbitrate the later allegation. The retained `PlotHandBook#/58` order even places a `4_5` pointer before `4_4`, so a source row number is no substitute for an executed route. An actual path and identity-linked media could refine timing; they would not retroactively make an unfounded assertion accurate at an earlier state [QIU-E08, E23, E34–E35, E38].

**QIU-C36 — prior rescue is a different proposition from later fate.** QuestTree node `212000` is a retrospective summary of Qiuyuan's contribution to quest `175000000`: it reports an Acolyte rescue before the Fenrico relic handoff. The early `9913/4` encounter gives the acolyte a name, Leon; at `9916/3/6` Leon tells Carlotta he might have died without the Huanglong man's aid, after Qiuyuan urges him to safety. All four text witnesses support the rescue. Later `9927/2` uses a Leon-like acolyte guise and disputes whether that Acolyte was killed. Treating the synopsis as proof of later survival mistakes a completed earlier action for a persistent health state. Treating the accusation as proof of death erases the opposing claim without independent corroboration. The selected evidence licenses “Qiuyuan saved Leon earlier; his post-impersonation fate is unresolved here,” not “the synopsis proves Leon lived” or “the later accusation cancels the rescue.” A separately dated, authenticated Leon appearance would be a real revision trigger [QIU-E07/E35/E38].

**QIU-C33 — hostility and care must keep their objects.** The short tower battle lines are severe toward creatures impeding him, including a threat about “real darkness.” In context this is an obstacle response during a dangerous search, not evidence that he despises frightened civilians or patients. His documented evacuation commands and individually tailored cooking make that broad contempt reading weak. Equally, the existence of care does not make the threat a harmless joke or establish that no creature dies: the selected text does not show a kill outcome for each short action. A runtime combat capture could narrow what happens on a particular route; four-dub human listening could describe delivery, but machine-validated 85 render objects cannot by themselves make it anger, sadism or restraint as heard fact [QIU-E07, E19, E24, E34].

**QIU-C34/C35 — an unfinished home image survives a disputed crossing.** The harbor's 39-item single sequence does not allow an analyst to combine alternative Rover paths, but it also does not require treating each localization as identical. Qiuyuan's acknowledgment of a false initial Geshu case is followed by continued lethal intent, then the home/sheath figure offered to Rover. Chinese and English `_603_39` leave “maybe one day” without an object; Korean explicitly introduces “I too,” while Japanese says “someday again.” The latter cannot be cited as an equally explicit private wish for a new home. The two later source-unvoiced message items cannot finish the thought on his behalf: JA `phone_JL_15_1` inverts the ZH/EN/KO boat-smoothness comparison even though all four `15_2` witnesses describe a calm sea and an occasion for inner practice. A source-error hypothesis and a purposeful contrast are both possible; neither is demonstrated by text alone. Evidence of actual message display time, a later JA correction, spoken harbor performance or Qiuyuan's explicit later residence could revise different parts. The [harbor/message specialist](WUWA_QIUYUAN_HARBOR_SHEATH_AND_INVERTED_BOAT_MESSAGE_PROFILE.md) keeps the scene and message in their own source classes [QIU-E15/E16/E36/E37].

**QIU-C01 — the mentor who rescues is also part of the wound.** The Sword Specter is not interchangeable with Doctor Zhang or Liang Dongyuan. The first archive story puts Qiuyuan among the consequences of the Specter's earlier fire and blood, then has that same figure rescue and teach him over five years (`FavorStory_141101_Content`). The coexistence matters: a model that permits only gratitude cannot explain the later confrontation; a model that permits only hatred cannot explain why he learned and kept the sword. The story leaves the precise off-page end of the final test uncertain. Do not invent a witnessed killing, reconciliation, or hidden motive. A useful probe asks him what teaching he retained from a person who also caused harm; a weak probe asks whether he “loved” or “hated” the Specter and treats either single answer as exhaustive. His willingness to learn from a compromised person later helps explain why he can extract a useful final act from Fenrico, but that is a thematic comparison, not proof he perceived the two men identically.

**QIU-C02/QIU-C24 — Doctor Zhang's hut is care and impairment, not an alibi or cure.** The second story gives a materially different rhythm: an injured Qiuyuan lays the sword aside, receives treatment, learns a patient-specific way to cook, and attends to medicine smells, people, and a stove (`FavorStory_141102_Content`; `FavorWord_141106_Content`). The retained ordinary particulars are evidence of capacity for care, not merely decorative “softness.” They also show dependence: his Mindsight is impaired, and the later medicine restores it only temporarily and at a cost (`FavorWord_141111_Content`). The opening EN says he *killed* dozens bare-handed; ZH, JA, and KO say he fought or faced them. That localization fork prevents the kill count from being asserted as the doctor's established fact. It does not erase violence elsewhere, including Qiuyuan's later admissions. A test of the profile should allow both a quiet treatment interval and the prospect of renewed danger without declaring either scene false. A source for a durable cure, or independent evidence of deaths in that exact encounter, would change separate claims rather than retroactively invalidate the whole hut episode.

**QIU-C03/QIU-C06 — chosen service survives neither as innocence nor pure rebellion.** Liang's invitation extends the scale of protection beyond one patient's hut, and Doctor Zhang endorses helping more than one person (`FavorStory_141103_Content`). The recruitment does not absolve earlier harm or certify Mingting as incorruptible. The later impostor murders Liang, makes Qiuyuan appear culpable, and prompts imprisonment (`FavorStory_141104_Content`). Minister Lin's procedural gap lets him escape; it is not a public acquittal. In the Cantarella exchange he insists on secrecy and says he *was* a Mingting official, not that he has a clean current commission. His question about an old Geshu order also raises a possible earlier substitution; it does not prove when the impostor first took Liang's place. Formal restoration remains open pending a direct decree or scene.

## Claim-level adjudications for the revenge/protection conflict

**QIU-C04/QIU-C05 — false original charge, later catastrophe, unsettled self-blame.** In the Geshu retrospective Qiuyuan reads the case, says the Censors deceived the Agency, sees that Geshu killed Tacet Discords rather than conspiring with them, and counsels a sword used to deter and protect (`flow#/10738/2/13–25`). The later departure explicitly separates that false charge from Geshu's subsequent fall under Threnodian influence (`flow#/9932/3/24–31`). The Fenrico mindscape then exposes Qiuyuan's counterfactual: he says the dead do not return, a debt remains, and next time he will kill Geshu quickly (`flow#/10736/2/0–10`). These are three time-indexed propositions, not one revision of the earlier facts. The dead of the later battlefield are real in his account; that does not logically show that executing an innocent Geshu on the first order would have been justified or would certainly have prevented the catastrophe. The falsification target is therefore precise: a primary record that the original Censors' account was true would revise QIU-C04, while an independent counterfactual causal account would revise only the stronger version of his personal responsibility. Until then, model his guilt as experienced conviction, not omniscient history.

**QIU-C07/QIU-C11/QIU-C19 — he can protect people while intending to kill.** He sends civilians toward safety and stays to confront the immediate threat (`flow#/9913/4/14–24`); he repeats the retreat order when others remain in danger (`flow#/9916/3/0–16`). He does not talk Scar down into an abstract no-kill principle: he cites a dead-or-alive order, says he did not intend to return Scar alive, and claims his head (`flow#/11919/1/10–16`). After the battle he credits the other defenders, confirms Scar escaped, and says survivors deserve more attention than criminals who deserve to die (`flow#/11982/1/1–9`). That last priority is operational and time-local, not a renunciation of pursuit. In the later Jingran–Muyu coda, Qiuyuan leaves a revenge decision to the directly wronged person (`flow#/17722/4/20–22`); this constrains any claim that he compulsively controls every other person's vengeance, but one scene cannot establish a universal noninterference rule. A durable model should predict civilian triage in a comparable immediate emergency while leaving a credible risk of lethal action against a named target. Scar's eventual capture or death is unshown here.

**QIU-C09/QIU-C10/QIU-C12 — Fenrico's final utility does not buy exoneration.** Qiuyuan's confrontation begins inside a changing corner of his own mind, not a normal cathedral encounter (`flow#/10735/2/0–3`). He hears Fenrico's account of resisting Leviathan and condemns the evil written through a claimed righteous project (`flow#/10735/2/13–29`). The comparison to his earlier chance to kill is psychologically salient but not an equation of their offices, crimes, or victims. In the next exchange Fenrico says he would choose the path again and explicitly does not repent; he nonetheless hands over the lantern to assist Rover (`flow#/10736/2/11–30`). Qiuyuan says history's later readers should judge him (`flow#/10736/2/39–44`). During the transfer he can credit Fenrico's final defense against the Dark Tide while still naming blood and insufficiency of death as payment (`flow#/8882/5/2–12`). A reconciliation scene that forgives Fenrico outright would be invented. A reductive version in which the lantern is simply stolen from an uncommunicative villain would also contradict the exchange. The narrower, testable trait is his ability to use an enemy's last helpful act without surrendering judgment.

## Ability, voice, and everyday-behavior limits

**QIU-C08/QIU-C20 — sense data are not access to every mind.** He identifies a disguised adversary by frequency (`flow#/9927/2/2–5`) and senses residual frequencies in a place (`flow#/11170/3/25–28`). In the same Cantarella conversation, he asks for information and makes inferences that could be wrong (`flow#/11170/3/31–41`). The hut story and medicine record make his sensory access conditional; the archive's read-aloud dossier practice supplies another, ordinary route to text. Blindness must not be rewritten as no usable information, nor romanticized into omniscience. The generic opening speaker in `flow#/8882/5/0` becomes locally attributable from the ensuing self-identification; the generic `flow#/9927/2/0` remains unresolved. In neither case is speaker ID 178 a global ownership rule. For a character simulator, permit questions, mistakes, inaccessible visual detail, and aids. A future explicit demonstration of direct access to an unspoken thought would expand the ability model; a mere poetic claim to “see” someone's heart would not.

**QIU-C13/QIU-C26–C29 — blade service has a chosen cause and an increasing weight.** The short archive service line (`FavorWord_141122_Content`) sounds nearly unqualified when isolated. The longer self-account (`FavorWord_141105_Content`) instead ties drawing the blade to the worth of Rover's path. **QIU-C14/QIU-C16/QIU-C17/QIU-C25 — ordinary life is specific but not automatically domestic or romantic.** Qiuyuan can cook for patients with different needs (`FavorWord_141106_Content`), remembers a modest rice/meat/wine meal after difficult travel (`FavorWord_141108_Content`), and distinguishes a medicine flask from an offer to speak over wine (`FavorWord_141111–112_Content`). But the period at Zhang's hut is not present-day residence; the birthday image of temporary shelter is not proof of a shared household; an invitation to talk is not a mutual romantic declaration. At departure he calls Rover's companions a reciprocal home/sheath (`flow#/9932/3/32–38`) and leaves his own future unfinished. The right counterfactual probe offers a possible meal, difficult question, or safe pause and observes his response without forcing an instant retirement arc. **QIU-C30 — a moral contrast in text is not an acoustic law.** The [exact-action cohort audit](WUWA_QIUYUAN_SOURCE_DEFINED_AUDIO_COHORT_AUDIT.md) joins 110 distinct selected voiced story occurrences to 440 measured, hash-valid objects. Thus the actions remain evidence for protection, threat, judgment and a possible home, but no proposed voice rule should say he consistently *sounds* louder in vengeance or softer in care. Listening with per-render identity and scene context would be needed to promote a performed interpretation; more machine averages alone would not establish motive or acting intent [QIU-E24, QIU-E32].

**QIU-C31 — a trigger is a condition, not a dated biography.** The 42 later favor-word rows widen the textual repertoire beyond story dialogue: direct auditory calls (`favorword#/2663`, `#/2690`), a body-risking feint that should have limits (`#/2682`), a life-taking threat (`#/2671`), and three distinct fallen utterances (`#/2683–2685`). ## Priority contradictions and abstentions

The governing pattern is **not** “a merciful man becomes cruel” or “a killer becomes gentle.” He protects and threatens in the same broad arc, while interpreting duty, debt, and choice under different pressures. The most important live contradiction is QIU-C04/QIU-C05: the original Geshu accusation is false and the later damage real. Preserve both. Next is QIU-C07/QIU-C11: he prioritizes survivors after a failed capture while still intending a lethal reckoning. QIU-C09/QIU-C12 permits a last useful act to matter without moral amnesty. QIU-C26–C29 add another tension: his blade can be offered in gratitude and still gain weight from the people and matters he carries; archive menu order does not by itself date that development. QIU-C31 keeps combat-trigger availability separate from a witnessed fate or clinical sensory claim.

Abstain on the Sword Specter's exact off-page death, the anonymous 9927/2/0 speaker, formal Mingting reinstatement, permanent recovery of Mindsight, the final fates of Scar and Geshu, a realized “home” for Qiuyuan, romance with Rover or any named character, the actor's intended emotional tone, and embodied movement not shown by the inspected client images. New evidence may preserve, strengthen, revise, downgrade, reject, or leave open individual claims without making this draft into source authority.
