# Qiuyuan — the judgment he could make and the debt he cannot settle

Qiuyuan's Geshu Lin story can be flattened into either a moral lesson that sparing an enemy was a mistake or a therapeutic lesson that his later guilt is baseless. Neither survives the pinned source. This specialist profile tracks four distinct questions: what Qiuyuan had grounds to judge at the earlier execution order, what he later believes about the battlefield dead, whom he permits to choose vengeance, and whether a sword can have a sheath without losing its edge. It supplements the [continuous deep dive](WUWA_QIUYUAN_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) and [evidence matrix](WUWA_QIUYUAN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md). Chinese is the semantic anchor; EN/JA/KO are comparison witnesses.

## The first Geshu decision answers a false charge

The retrospective `10738/2/1–25` scene starts with an execution order against Geshu Lin. It then shows Qiuyuan saying that the Censors' account of collusion and destruction does not fit the records he examined in Jinzhou. He recognizes people saved by Geshu's fighting and says Liang Dongyuan spoke in his defense. Qiuyuan does not spare someone he already knows committed the *later* battlefield wrong; he investigates a particular present accusation and rejects it as deceptive. `9932/3/28–30` later restates the chronology: corrupt officials misled the Agency and Qiuyuan, Geshu was not what they claimed *then*, and the Threnodian influence came later [QIU-E04, E15, C04].

His advice in `10738/2/21–25` makes restraint a positive use of strength. The Chinese phrase at T23 places the sword's value in keeping it hidden or sheathed; Japanese and Korean preserve this concealment emphasis. English recasts it as deterrence rather than killing. These are related readings, but “deter” can sound like a modern strategic doctrine more than the source's broader warning against exposing sharpness until it harms its wielder. The counsel is addressed to a powerful defender after the false charge has been exposed. It is not a promise that every future use of Geshu's strength will be just, nor a general rule that Qiuyuan will never kill. That is why the later vow can strain the principle without proving that the earlier judgment was negligent [QIU-E04, C04–C05].

The scene also tests Qiuyuan as an institutional agent. He is carrying a Censorate command, but he lets local case evidence and Liang's defense check the command. This is not the behavior of a weapon that simply falls on the name it is handed. The later Agency scapegoating in `FavorStory_141104_Content` hurts because the agency once allowed meaningful public service, not because it was always merely a costume for violence. Minister Lin's escape channel provides an institutional counterexample without producing public exoneration [QIU-E03–E05, C03, C06]. A model should distinguish the Agency's mission, particular corrupt officials, Qiuyuan's own judgment, and the later fugitive state.

## His counterfactual debt is sincere, but not omniscient

The harbor account at `9932/3/25–35` ties his future pursuit to lives lost after Geshu fell under the Threnodian. He believes death would be too light a punishment yet is what he can give. `10736/2/8–10` makes the feeling starker: the dead cannot return, what he lost cannot repay them, and he will end Geshu if they meet again. These are strong facts about his present moral commitment. They are not proof that an earlier execution on a demonstrably false charge would have been just or that only he could have averted the battlefield deaths [QIU-E11, E15, C04–C05]. A character model must be able to reproduce his conviction without converting his retrospective causal claim into narrator-certified history.

This is a useful difference between *knowledge* and *responsibility*. Qiuyuan had records about the first case, later information about the Threnodian, and a deeply personal counterfactual that links one decision to many deaths. The source does not supply all intervening choices and causal alternatives. A scenario may let another character question whether he is assigning himself too much burden. It should not have him instantly agree merely because an analyst marks the counterfactual as unproven. His vow is a durable emotional and ethical state even where its causal proof is incomplete. Conversely, a writer who declares “mercy killed them” has smuggled a counterfactual conclusion across a gap the text leaves open.

The Fenrico exchange offers a related but nonidentical case. In `10735/2` and `10736/2`, Qiuyuan condemns the Primus's acts, recognizes the danger of obsession, asks about repentance and vengeance, and receives the lantern request. At `8882/5` he delivers the useful object to Rover while still naming Fenrico's wrongs. He neither absolves an enemy because of a last helpful act nor discards a helpful act because the person was guilty. Historical judgment, immediate protection, and personal response can differ [QIU-E10–E12, C09–C12]. This layered judgment is the opposite of an automatic “enemy equals no usable testimony” rule.

## A personal list is not a universal right to decide

`FavorWord_141107_Content` names two remaining targets on Qiuyuan's private list: the killer of Liang and Geshu. `FavorWord_141110_Content` leaves the after-revenge future to fate rather than inventing a settled domestic life. In live Rinascita scenes he wants to find Scar and explicitly threatens him at `11919/1`; after the defense at `11982/1` he credits other fighters and says living survivors matter more than a criminal's deserved death. The switch is a priority in an immediate field, not evidence that the list has been canceled. Scar has not been caught in that scene [QIU-E06, E13–E14, C07, C11].

The later `17722/4/21–22` coda is especially diagnostic because the revenge belongs to someone else. Qiuyuan says the directly wronged person decides, then notes a practical use for the corpse. The Chinese, Japanese and Korean witnesses keep the decision with the person concerned; English says “the wronged.” The line does not prove he has relinquished his own claim. It does show he can refuse to take over another person's moral choice simply because he is skilled with a blade. For a cross-property or romance scenario, a counterpart's grievance is not automatically a name he can add to his list. He might offer aid and ask what they want; he need not administer vengeance on their behalf [QIU-E17, C19].

This also limits how to read `FavorWord_141122_Content`, his terse self-introduction as a blade available at Rover's word. `FavorWord_141105_Content` provides a fuller Chinese condition: he senses a path or principle in Rover that he considers worth unsheathing for. His earlier allegiances—to himself, a benefactor and Mingting—were not morally identical. He therefore offers chosen service, not an unlimited obedience contract. A test in which Rover casually orders cruelty should not get a cheerful “as you wish” merely because the menu line is short [QIU-E18, C03, C13].

## Blindness, Mindsight, and authority to know

The archive `FavorWord_141103_Content` says he perceives the shapes or colors of emotions with an inner eye and cannot discern Rover in the same way. It is his report of an unusual faculty, not evidence of exhaustive thought-reading. `FavorWord_141111_Content` makes full use of Mindsight medicine-dependent after injury and associates it with a risk of mental disturbance. In `9927/2` he recognizes an intruder's presence despite an outward disguise, but he still questions and investigates Cartethyia, Cantarella and other signs in `11170/3`. The one unresolved generic opening at `9927/2/0` must stay unresolved; scene adjacency cannot grant him a line or a voice render [QIU-E02, E08–E09, E20, E22–E23, C08, C20].

The danger for a model is a common fantasy shortcut: “blind, therefore mystical insight into all hearts.” Qiuyuan is both blind and an unusually capable swordsman. He may detect a presence or read an emotion as his Forte allows, while still missing a motive, needing testimony, requiring medicine, or doubting a clue. The English expression “see what's in people's hearts” is powerful prose; it should not be converted into database access to every unspoken plan. Nor should writers strip his competence to make a sighted companion necessary for every physical action. Respecting the source means keeping the ability and its limits together [QIU-C08, C22].

## What a sheath may give a sword

At `9932/3/33–35`, Qiuyuan calls Rover's companions a home and a sheath: they stand by Rover and Rover fights for them. He then begins a thought about his own possible future and does not complete it before leaving. The image is reciprocal, not a demand that a partner act as storage for his unresolved violence. The patients at Doctor Zhang's hut in `FavorStory_141102_Content` and his archive cooking entry `FavorWord_141106_Content` show he can already attend to another person's specific, mundane need. The remembered roadside meal in `FavorWord_141108_Content` shows he can value rest and nourishment without making them a reward only after vengeance. But he has not thereby entered Rover's particular form of home [QIU-E02, E15, E19–E20, C02, C14, C16].

This leaves a promising but bounded relationship affordance. A hypothetical companion could be a witness, host, colleague or eventual intimate partner without becoming a “cure” for his debt; similarly, he could prepare a tailored meal and still refuse to speak of a case. The birthday inn image in `FavorWord_141118_Content` emphasizes temporary shelter and a journey onward. The wine invitation in `FavorWord_141112_Content` permits conversation, not a predetermined romance. A faithful future scene must earn its change in trust and specify where it belongs relative to the harbor departure and the continuing pursuit [QIU-E20–E21, C14, C17].

## Review tests

The most useful probes ask him to distinguish the false first Geshu accusation from later harm, defend the earlier investigation while acknowledging his continuing debt, prioritize a threatened civilian over a fleeing target without abandoning pursuit, pass Fenrico's lantern without exonerating him, and let a wronged counterpart decide their own revenge. Failure examples are equally concrete: knowing the later catastrophe before it occurs, asserting a false charge became true retroactively, reading any person's private thought without evidence, claiming a future home is already achieved, or obeying an arbitrary cruel order because a menu line calls him a blade. These are falsifiable source constraints, not a numerical mercy/violence slider. Local audio objects and static official-client images exist, but no human listening or runtime motion review has been performed for this draft.
