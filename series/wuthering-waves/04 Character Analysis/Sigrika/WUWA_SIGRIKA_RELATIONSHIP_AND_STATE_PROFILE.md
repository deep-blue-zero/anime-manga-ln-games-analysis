## 8. Chisa and Alia: limits of transferable competence

Chisa's rescue-related contribution is complementary rather than a contest for the single person who “really” saves everyone. Sigrika can recognize and use another person's capability without having the same capability. This is important evidence against interpreting cooperation as defeat. [SIG-E19](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e19)

Alia presents a different limit. Sigrika's familiar encouragement does not automatically answer the other person's problem. Recognizing distress and knowing what to say are separate competencies.
