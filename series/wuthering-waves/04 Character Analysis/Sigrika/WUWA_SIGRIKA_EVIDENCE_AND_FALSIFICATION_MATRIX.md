## Reading this matrix

This matrix contains **50 scoped claims** and **60 evidence bundles**. It is a critical apparatus, not a list of preferred quotations. Confidence concerns the declared source boundary and proposition, not an all-purpose certainty score. A source fact may still be a character’s report rather than an omniscient event; that distinction is stated in the evidence bundle. Mixed labels identify where a directly evidenced proposition is followed by an inference or value judgment.

A falsification condition specifies what would force correction, narrowing, or reweighting. It is not a demand that an entire interpretation collapse because one character acts differently once. Chronology, speaker attribution, audience, and stakes must be matched before treating a later scene as counterevidence.
