## 2. Four authority dimensions

**Diegetic authority** concerns what happens or is said within the represented world. A verified in-game recording may strongly witness a scene, but a nightmare inside that scene is not thereby truthful testimony about real family members. **Representational authority** concerns how an official source depicts a body, prop, space, or action. **Interpretive authority** concerns the support an observation provides for a particular claim. **Production-framing authority** concerns official arrangement, emphasis, design, and presentation. [SIG-E14](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e14) [SIG-E21](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e21) [SIG-E24](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e24)

These dimensions do not form one ladder. An official promotional illustration may be authoritative about how a character is marketed while not establishing a diegetic event. A direct scene may provide strong evidence of movement while weakly determining inner motive. A subtitle may establish displayed text without proving the full spoken wording, actor intention, or facial meaning.

## 4. Acquisition unit and stopping rule

Acquire the smallest **coherent exchange or action unit** that can answer a target's question, including necessary setup and response. A one-line excerpt may omit the option that conditions a prank, the recipient reaction that qualifies encouragement, or the reveal that identifies a counterpart. Conversely, collecting an entire playthrough is not necessary when a bounded, verified segment preserves those dependencies.

For each acquired witness record the actual file hash, provenance, game/source boundary where known, language, edition/edit, audio track, subtitle track, source duration, acquisition date, and local-to-source mapping. Mark unverified fields rather than filling them from filenames. Preserve a human-readable route back to the exact dialogue key and the accepted or excluded speaker occurrence.

Stop a collection tranche when it can adjudicate its declared claim questions and major counterexamples, not when it has accumulated a round number of minutes. A few views of only triumphant moments would not test the model; refusal, uncertainty, ordinary preference, and failed encouragement are deliberately included.

## 7. Clock, retiming, and channel controls

A future processing run must preserve the source presentation-time origin and distinguish it from a derivative clip's zero point. A timestamp in an extracted clip is not automatically the same time in the original recording. For audio, exact sample indices and sample rate should govern sample-bounded windows; for video, use actual frame timestamps rather than an assumed constant-frame-rate arithmetic sequence.

Where the toolkit selects a frame at or after a requested time, retain the actual selected timestamp and any offset. A target time outside available frames must not produce an invented witness. A retimed clip needs an explicitly verified mapping before observations are projected back to source time. Record all speed changes, offsets, and discontinuities rather than calling the transform a simple trim.

Preserve native channel count and known layout. Two channels are not sufficient evidence for an intended stereo stage, and the counterpart's reported spatial-layout warnings must survive any proposed downmix. Mixed scene audio is not an isolated vocal stem merely because its filename names Sigrika. Subtitle windows can help retrieval but are not authoritative diarization or exact phonetic timing.

## 8. Review record and claim revision

For each target distinguish: the exact observed action or sound; the source and time locator; the proposed interpretation; plausible counterreadings; and the claim disposition. Useful dispositions are **preserved, strengthened, narrowed, corrected, superseded, unresolved**. A revised claim should retain its previous identity and record why its scope changed.

A future reviewer might find that a line read as unqualified resolve contains hesitation, play, or a recipient-directed softening. That could narrow a delivery-dependent inference without disproving the textual assertion. A prop or gesture could clarify the distribution of technical contributions without establishing actor intent. The revision should change the affected claim, not announce that AV has either validated or invalidated the whole person.
