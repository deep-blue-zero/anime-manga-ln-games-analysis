## Audio-only retrieval bridge

A narrative AV target may also nominate a semantic voice line, but an isolated audio object cannot replace the scene's image, branch, or conversational context. For audio, join the exact `source_locator` or `text_key` to `COMPLETE_VOICE_LINE_ANALYSIS.jsonl`; then choose the required language and runtime association from `COMPLETE_VOICE_RENDER_ANALYSIS.jsonl`; then use PCM identity to resolve `DRIVE_AUDIO_OBJECT_CROSSWALK.jsonl`.

The crosswalk identifies **both** the Drive shard path and exact FLAC member. Follow those fields, not a guessed Sigrika-prefixed filename: reused objects can be stored in another immutable shard. Verify acquired FLAC bytes against the recorded SHA-256, and preserve the distinction between file hash and decoded canonical PCM identity.

The eight deterministic QC nominations in [AUDIO_RETRIEVAL_QC_COHORT.json](AUDIO_RETRIEVAL_QC_COHORT.json) already contain exact semantic/render/object/shard/member joins. They select long or low-RMS objects for technical checking, not emotional representativeness. Their audio has now been acquired, decoded, hash-verified, and measured; perceptual listening remains unperformed.

## Human review handoff fields

When a reviewer obtains a witness, append its real source URL or Drive route, file hash, language, edition, subtitle/audio track, source-time range, derivative-time mapping, actual frames or samples inspected, and a brief observation. Then record how the observation changes the specified claim, including a counterreading where material.

Do not mark a target acquired merely because search returned its title; do not mark it viewed because a toolkit produced a contact sheet; and do not mark a performance heard because a waveform was measured. Acquisition, processing, perception, interpretation, and integration are five separate events.
