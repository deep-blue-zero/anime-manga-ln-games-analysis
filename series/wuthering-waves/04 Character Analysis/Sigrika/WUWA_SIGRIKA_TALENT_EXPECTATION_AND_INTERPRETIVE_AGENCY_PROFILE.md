## 1. The specialist question

**Inference.** Sigrika's central problem is not whether she possesses a gift. It is what a gift obliges its bearer to guarantee. A particular ability becomes a reputation; reputation becomes a promise that other people need not be disappointed; and any uncertain result then threatens the whole promise. This document separates the links in that chain. The monograph explains their longitudinal development; this profile owns the conceptual distinctions needed to avoid compressing that development into either obedience or rebellion. The evidence supports real aptitude, real effort, real attachment, and real limits at the same time. None needs to be exposed as fraudulent to explain her distress. A model that makes her talent imaginary cannot explain her specialist contributions. A model that makes her talent unlimited cannot explain why instruction, collaborators, and failure matter. A model that treats every expectation as persecution cannot explain why she continues to treasure the people whose hopes have become burdensome. ## 2. Four predicates that must not be merged

**Capacity** asks what she can do under particular conditions. **Qualification** asks what an institution has certified. **Reputation** concerns what others expect. **Obligation** concerns what she believes she must undertake or ensure. The corpus repeatedly separates these predicates.

Her rune aptitude does not supply universal technical understanding. Her institutional examination history does not nullify that aptitude. Others can discuss her as a promising future candidate without the source establishing that she already occupies the anticipated office. She can feel answerable for a successful outcome even when her actual capacity makes certainty impossible. [SIG-E02](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e02) [SIG-E06](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e06) [SIG-E07](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e07)

The Exoswarm recollection is diagnostic because success does not eliminate the distinction. Preparation, improvisation, and Soliskin's assistance contribute to the result, while the final rune's failure remains psychologically consequential. She does not merely compare the actual result with defeat; she measures it against a standard in which her contribution ought to have been reliably sufficient. **Inference:** this helps explain why praise alone may intensify rather than discharge the pressure. It adds evidence that people are relying on the very reliability she doubts. [SIG-E03](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e03) [SIG-C03](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-c03)

This is not evidence that praise is intrinsically harmful, that her achievement was worthless, or that the people who praised her knew all her doubts. Those stronger propositions would require different evidence.

## 3. An expectation loop, not a diagnosis

A source-grounded explanatory sequence is:

1. An attachment or valued role gives an outcome personal importance.
2. Sigrika converts that importance into a requirement to avoid disappointing the relevant people.
3. A knowledge or performance limit threatens the requirement.
4. She answers with more preparation, self-instruction, or reassurance to others.
5. The visible effort and reassurance can conceal the limit and sustain others' confidence.
6. Confidence returns as a renewed expectation that she must meet.

This is an **interpretive model**, not an experimentally estimated causal mechanism or a clinical diagnosis. The archive, school adjustment, diaries, and later phone call support different parts of it. The model is strongest where she explicitly connects effort, expectation, and concealment; it should not explain every meal, joke, or missed event. [SIG-E01](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e01) [SIG-E03](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e03) [SIG-E04](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e04) [SIG-E18](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e18) [SIG-E33](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e33)

Its most important counterevidence is the availability of care. Teachers and friends are not uniformly demanding more. Some advise rest, offer instruction, or try to protect her. She can also enjoy an activity without reporting this entire loop. The model therefore describes a recurrent vulnerability activated by particular stakes, not a continuous hidden state beneath every cheerful sentence. ## 5. Counterpart recognition is not total self-identification

A wish to escape pressure may be genuine while the counterpart's proposed conclusion about surrender remains rejectable. Sigrika's decisive response need not consist of proving that she never wanted escape. It can acknowledge the wish while refusing the inference that the wish exhausts her. ## 10. What would weaken this interpretation?

The profile would require substantial revision if later direct evidence showed that the diary and refusal changes were entirely staged to reassure observers; that she understood her gift as universally sufficient despite the recorded limitations; or that she explicitly rejected attachment and responsibility rather than renegotiating their demands. Evidence that she still overextends under high stakes would narrow the durability of the change without automatically falsifying it. The architecture preserves separate evidence bundles so that these corrections need not trigger an indiscriminate rewrite of the whole person.
