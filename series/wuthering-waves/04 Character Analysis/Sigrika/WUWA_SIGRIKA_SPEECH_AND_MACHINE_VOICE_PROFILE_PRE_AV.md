# Speech and measured machine-voice evidence — pre-video V0.2

> **Current authority — owner adoption, 2026-09-23.** This document is current active-provisional authority for its declared analytical or planning scope and inspected text/audio evidence. Audiovisual and human-listening gaps limit only the corresponding claims. Chinese remains primary; source fact, inference, unresolved hypothesis, and value judgment remain distinct. [Packet entrypoint](WUWA_SIGRIKA_ANALYSIS_PACKET_README.md).

## 1. Evidence layers and current scope

V0.2 analyzes the actual Drive recordings, not only supplied metadata. All 2,652 direct Sigrika objects and 157 separately attributed counterpart objects were fetched through their nineteen exact archive/member routes, decoded, and checked against FLAC and native PCM payload hashes. The [method and results](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_METHOD_AND_RESULTS.md) owns the algorithm, parameters, quality gates, and aggregate corpus results. [SIG-AF-01](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-01) [SIG-AF-02](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-02)

Three layers remain separate: **textual register and semantics**, **measured acoustic properties**, and **perceptual interpretation of performance**. The first two were reviewed here. No human or other direct perceptual listening annotation was performed. Claims about perceived tenderness, embarrassment in the voice, laughter, sincerity, acting intention, or the best dub are not manufactured from pitch, energy, or text. Video is deferred to the owner’s later local 1080p-or-larger pass.

`machine_voice_profiled` is claimed for the declared feasible corpus under the method’s explicit signal-reliability restrictions. This does not claim true linguistic articulation rate, isolated clean stems, audiovisual synchronization, either human-performance state, or a completed integrated audiovisual monograph. The source’s lexical content identifies situations; the recordings constrain how those situations are represented in the available audio layer. [SIG-AF-12](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-12)

## 2. Register is recipient-conditioned

Sigrika's speech changes with what she is trying to do and whom she is addressing. In an explanatory role, rune vocabulary and procedural description make her useful to an interlocutor. With Rover she can combine the address rendered “Senior” in English with invitations, questions, apologies, and requests for support. In peer exchanges, familiar names and shared activities reduce the distance of the interaction. These are lexical and pragmatic observations, not measurements of vocal warmth. [SIG-E12](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e12) [SIG-E28](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e28) [SIG-E30](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e30) [SIG-E41](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e41)

Private diaries permit self-address that is not identical to conversation. Self-naming or self-instruction can help organize a duty or a fear, but the model must not put that pattern into every spoken sentence. The mother receives reassurance in earlier communication and more difficulty in later communication. Identical speakers can therefore use different disclosure strategies without either strategy being a wholly false persona. [SIG-E18](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e18) [SIG-E33](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e33) [SIG-E34](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e34) [SIG-C37](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-c37) [SIG-C38](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-c38) [SIG-C39](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-c39)

The confrontation introduces explicit boundary-setting: other speakers may not define or distort her thoughts and effort. Later ordinary behavior provides the scope test. She does not become permanently confrontational, abandon respectful address, or refuse every task. Speech development is better represented as an additional capacity to set a boundary than as replacement of one total voice with another. [SIG-E26](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e26) [SIG-E33](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e33)

## 3. Questions can reveal perception without proving full knowledge

At Denia's birthday, Sigrika lists the unusual behavior she has observed and asks whether something is being concealed. That sequence is evidence of social noticing. Her willingness not to demand every answer does not erase the noticing. The request for next year's birthday then identifies the particular answer that matters most. [SIG-E47](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e47)

Do not classify this solely by the surface presence of a question mark. Its force depends on prior shared behavior, an unusual present sequence, and incomplete knowledge. The same interrogative form in a camera problem or a technical explanation may do a very different job. An acoustic clustering procedure that ignores these distinctions would need subsequent contextual interpretation rather than an automatic label of anxiety or intimacy. [SIG-E12](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e12) [SIG-E37](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e37) [SIG-E47](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e47)

## 4. Material localization checks

### L01 — Responsibility and assistance, `12423/4/20`

Text key: `Main_LahaiRoi_YXBLDHJ_101_22`. Chinese combines “you can rely only on yourself” with not facing the problems alone. English shifts to a fight that is hers to finish but not fought alone. Japanese phrases self-resolution as best when possible, while Korean remains closer to the Chinese construction. These are not verbally identical theories of autonomy. The shared scene supports assistance without a supplied correct answer; the stronger or softer wording belongs to its own witness. The speaker here is Rover, not a universal self-description uttered by Sigrika. [SIG-E22](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e22)

### L02 — Holding the gold, `12966/3/34`

Text key: `Main_LahaiRoi_YXBLDHJ_128_35`. Chinese ties the gold's not falling to deciding to act. English emphasizes continuing to move forward and holding it. Both belong to a passage that acknowledges warm yet heavy expectations. The reconstruction preserves the difference and uses later actions to test scope, rather than selecting whichever translation best supports a preferred maxim. [SIG-E25](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e25)

### L03 — “This time,” `12967/1/4`

Text key: `Main_LahaiRoi_YXBLDHJ_131_5`. Chinese **这一次**, Japanese **今度**, and Korean **이번만큼은** locally qualify the act. English expands the temporal frame to “from now on.” A Chinese-primary model must not silently convert the English expansion into a permanent rule of doing only what she wants. It can predict a present assertion of choice and a developing capacity for later refusal. [SIG-E26](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e26) [SIG-C11](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-c11)

### L04 — Practicing refusal, `16901/4/1`

Text key: `XGLK_shengtai_1_2`. Chinese reports trying to turn down what she cannot manage; the Japanese wording also presents refusal, English emphasizes trying, and Korean has a more prospective conditional formulation. The existence and temporal status of practice should therefore be qualified by witness. The Chinese-primary inference of behavioral change is stronger than a purely future intention, but it does not prove universal success. [SIG-E33](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e33)

### L05 — A fulfilling life is not merely productive, `16826/2/2`

Text key: `Side_YGJJ_5_4`. Chinese **充实** carries a sense of being full or fulfilling; English uses “productive.” In context, she discusses participation and her present life. Using the English alone to argue that her recovery consists entirely in renewed output would impose a narrower evaluative frame than the primary wording warrants. [SIG-E48](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e48)

### L06 — Denia's unusual behavior, `12586/7/15–21`

Sigrika recognizes a pattern, asks whether something is hidden, and offers not to press while asking about next year. Across the inspected witnesses, the perception and future-oriented request are preserved. This supports a bounded relationship finding, not access to the full concealed plan. The English expression “playing dumb” and its counterparts must be read as a conversational position, not a diagnosis of her intelligence. [SIG-E47](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e47)

## 6. Aftermath: low level is not equivalent to low pitch

At `12423/4`, Sigrika’s text moves through failure, fear, questions about choice, and a request for time to think. The seventeen-line cohort has median pitch percentiles of approximately **75/73/58/70**, but active-frame level percentiles of approximately **11/11/20/16**, in ZH/EN/JA/KO order. That describes a particular combination of measurable properties, not an emotion detector. The emotional situation is established by the dialogue and context. [SIG-AF-03](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-03) [SIG-E22](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e22) [SIG-E23](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e23)

The explicit apology at `12423/4/2` has median F0 around 428/490/484/505 Hz, while the later question about possibly making things worse at `12423/4/24` is around 330/305/314/308 Hz. All eight of those particular estimates pass the pitch gate. Even inside this one action, a single fixed delivery template would erase variation. The request for time to think is not licensed to sound like every earlier apology merely because both occur during distress.

The nearby “I’m so scared” line is a useful quality counterexample: three of its four recordings trigger pitch-reliability exclusions. It is still present and usable for other bounded measurements. The most narratively obvious line is not automatically the best numerical pitch exemplar. This is why source relevance and signal reliability are separate gates. Same-semantic cases 01–04 retain the exact inputs in [AUDIO_MATCHED_SEMANTIC_CASES.json](AUDIO_MATCHED_SEMANTIC_CASES.json).

## 8. Denia: concern without a compulsory low-pitch intimacy register

The birthday text supports noticing and a request for continuing shared life; it does not establish complete knowledge of the hidden plan. The measured recordings do not add omniscience, romance, or sincerity certification. Cohort pitch-percentile medians lie around the middle of each language’s baseline rather than forming one uniformly low register. Active-frame level is also nonuniform: roughly the 50th/61st/27th/43rd percentiles. [SIG-AF-05](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-05) [SIG-E47](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e47)

For the final next-year request at `12586/7/22`, pitch percentiles are approximately 77/27/55/53. English’s final “Promise?” also is not semantically identical in force to all of the other localized endings. A lower English median pitch in this selected line cannot be encoded as the universal sign of Sigrika’s most authentic affection. A higher Chinese value cannot be described as shallower concern. Those would be unsupported performance judgments, not the measured result.

The useful reconstruction change is narrower: recipient-sensitive textual behavior must not be rendered by a single predetermined pitch/volume transformation. Exact word-level intonation, perceived warmth, and the relation between a gap and a facial gesture remain questions for actual listening and the deferred video stage.

## 9. The gold speech: assertion does not have one acoustic formula

At `12966/3`, the line about gold being bright, warm, and heavy and the later even-if-I-cannot line show median pitch changes of approximately **298→401 Hz (ZH), 249→250 (EN), 285→375 (JA), and 247→305 (KO)**. These are two whole-object comparisons within each witness, not universal descriptions of the entire speech. The language-specific change is explicit. [SIG-AF-06](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-06) [SIG-E25](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e25)

The text’s argument about agency remains recognizable across those witnesses, but its wording and recorded prosody do not have to be identical. English also recasts some gold imagery. A source-grounded model should preserve the ability to speak assertively without requiring the same upward pitch movement in every localization. It should not mistake one dub’s numerical signature for the character’s universal psychology.

The earlier “Enough” object at `12966/3/28` presents another trap. Its single-word/short-phrase caption maps to a 6.38–7.37 second object, with 61–83% low-energy time under the central gate. That duration cannot honestly be reported as a seven-second spoken word or an exactly measured psychological hesitation. The object may include nonlexical or event-timing material; no word alignment or perceptual classification resolves that here. [SIG-AF-07](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-07)

## 10. Family speech and the difference between archive and event

The archive phone idle `FavorWord_141221_Content` combines reassurances and imagined response intervals in the written source. Across four languages the objects last approximately 28.57–28.66 seconds. Their many internal low-energy gaps are compatible with a shared timing constraint, but without the animation/event witness that remains a **production hypothesis**, not a verified description of gesture or turn-taking. [SIG-AF-07](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-07)

`FavorWord_141204_Content` is a later retrospective account of a more candid conversation. Its narrated content supports a different relation to parental expectation, but this is not the recording of the later in-scene phone action `16901/4`. The archive and scene may discuss related developments while remaining distinct sources. Their different length, menu function, interlocutor, and wording prevent a controlled old-self/new-self vocal experiment.

More importantly, the final “this time” action `12967/1`, the later call action `16901/4`, and the later fulfilling-life action `16826/2` are marked unvoiced in the accepted line-level mapping. Their respective six, eighteen, and nineteen direct occurrences have zero resolved audio associations. No recordings were omitted from the promised 666 voiced semantic lines; these actions are outside that denominator. The correct future test is the exact attributable video/event witness, not substitution of nearby or archive audio. [SIG-AF-08](WUWA_SIGRIKA_AUDIO_REVISION_LEDGER.md#sig-af-08)

## 11. Mundane speech remains mundane evidence

The woods invitation, the sweets from home, enthusiasm for Fleet Snowfluff, and birdwatching exchanges are retained as twenty-case selections alongside crisis material. The purpose is not to reinterpret every lively contour as compensation for pressure. The ordinary-life profile already establishes those interests; the new audio resources make it possible to retrieve their actual representations without treating emotional crisis as the whole character. [SIG-E30](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e30) [SIG-E35](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e35) [SIG-E36](WUWA_SIGRIKA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#sig-e36)

No one numerical threshold distinguishes authentic pleasure from public performance. The invitation’s syntax, available recipient, relationship history, and activity matter before an expressive interpretation is proposed. The spectrum and harmonicity values are also not evidence that an interest is more sincere in one language. Their proper role here is reproducible characterization of recordings and diagnostic sample selection.
