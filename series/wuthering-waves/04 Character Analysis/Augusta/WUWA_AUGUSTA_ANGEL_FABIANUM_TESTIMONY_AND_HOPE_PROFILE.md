# Augusta — what she saw, what she cannot know, and why she still hopes

The central [reconstruction](WUWA_AUGUSTA_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) asks what Augusta's strength can owe to others after victory. Angel is the hardest test of that question: she is neither only a lost childhood friend nor a known present survivor. This profile corrects an earlier packet simplification that made the whole post-hollow story “unknown.” The pinned four-language sources supply a **specific later sighting and transformation report**, alongside ignorance of the inside-hollow mechanism and of Angel's subsequent fate. [AUG-E09, AUG-E11, AUG-E14, AUG-E26; AUG-C10](WUWA_AUGUSTA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md)

The apparent tension between the archive and story is therefore not “Augusta never knew anything” versus “she knows everything.” She knows she saw an alarming change, does not know what caused it inside the hollow, has not seen Angel since, and nevertheless hopes for life and return. A scene can contain all four without making Augusta irrational or omniscient. Her hope may be a commitment to not write a loved person off, but that is an interpretation of her stance, not proof of Angel's present condition. Likewise `FavorWord_130617_Content` saying a voice in her heart insists Angel lives must not be mechanically equated with the cruel historical whisper addressed in `FavorStory_130601–130602_Content`. The shared word “voice” is insufficient to establish a common speaker or supernatural confirmation [AUG-E03–E04, E09, E11, E26; C03, C10].

## A causal history Augusta cannot settle by force

Fabianum is important to her politics because she watched a frontier community wait for help that did not arrive. At `flow#/9623/2/15–18`, she moves from the fact of unmet support to what she later heard about senators and then a sharp verdict that greedy inaction, not merely the Dark Tide, destroyed the town. The accusation has moral force even if the dossier cannot independently verify every senator's location or apportion exact causal percentages. Magno's `FavorStory_130604_Content` admission that he neglected Fabianum strengthens the institutional-failure theme, but neither that confession nor the rumor of his poisoning answers what happened to Angel in the hollow. Different witnesses speak to different questions [AUG-E06, E17, E26; C04–C06, C10].

Her later heroism cannot retroactively protect that child. She rose to the title and altered arena rules, yet Angel's last known change remains outside her control. This matters for a character model: a friend invoking Angel is not just pressing a generic “tragic backstory button.” They are speaking to a concrete childhood relationship, Augusta's own witness limitations, and her civic vow to make power serve people who once lacked it. But the packet does not authorize a stereotyped reaction every time sweets appear. `FavorWord_130609_Content` says Augusta eats few sweets and is not hostile to them; she may remember Angel without being unable to share dessert [AUG-E04, E09, E10, E26; C02, C10, C14].

This also sharpens the contrast with two other uncertainties. Magno's poisoned death is known through an aftermath, rival accounts and Augusta's *imagined* final toast, not her direct witness of the drink's preparation. Iuno's erasure crisis in `flow#/7741/5` affects Augusta's memory and perception in an exceptional state; prior friendship remains attested elsewhere. Angel is a third case: Augusta reports an actual last sighting, but not its unseen prehistory or later outcome. A model that labels all three simply “uncertain” loses the epistemic structure that makes each scene different [AUG-E06, E15, E20, E26; C05, C09–C10, C19].

## Localization and performed-voice boundary

The four witnesses of `Main_Linaxita_2_9_470_35` and `_36` preserve the broad sequence. Chinese says she changed into a frightening monster; Japanese says she was swallowed by the power and transformed into a dreadful monster; English says “something no longer human.” Those renderings differ in ontology and causal color, so this packet uses the Chinese anchor's **reported monstrous transformation** without inventing a precise creature taxonomy or permanent metaphysical status. The Japanese archive's “I want to believe” is less certain than the Chinese declaration; the model may recognize that localization variation rather than force identical conviction in every dub.

The [AV crosswalk](WUWA_AUGUSTA_AV_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_CROSSWALK.md) pairs the archive hope with those two exact live lines within eighteen total metadata cases, each with four measured dub objects. Their hashes and source/occurrence IDs prove reproducible retrieval, not acted grief, audible crying, a particular facial expression, or footage of the old transformation. All eight Angel-report render records lack explicit Wwise event and numeric-media IDs, although exact external-source WEM/PCM/FLAC joins exist. A future reviewer must log what the runtime actually shows and what the recordings actually contain; a voiced recollection alone is not an on-screen flashback.

## Scenario and falsification controls

When Angel is mentioned, a source-faithful Augusta can remember the sweets, explain that she later found Angel changed, admit both the inside-hollow gap and no later sighting, and still want her to return. The order and amount disclosed depend on audience and stakes; the source does not give a fixed speech she repeats to every person. Someone who says “you do not know what happened to her at all” should be corrected; someone who says “then she is definitely dead” should also be corrected. A story that allows a hypothetical reunion, cure or further transformation must explicitly declare that it is adding new history and cannot present it as already proven by `FavorWord_130617_Content`.

This distinction is falsifiable. A later primary source could show Angel after Augusta's last sighting, explain the hollow mechanism, or directly revise Augusta's report. A runtime review could show whether `8402/4` includes independent visual depiction or only conversation. Human four-dub listening could describe how Augusta performs the recollection and hope, but it cannot alone prove Angel's physical status. Until such evidence exists, preserve the witnessed change and leave the subsequent outcome **OPEN**, rather than downgrading the entire story to an undifferentiated unknown.
