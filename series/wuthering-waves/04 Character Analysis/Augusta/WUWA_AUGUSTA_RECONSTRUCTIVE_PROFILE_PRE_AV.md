## Choose state, audience and stakes before register

Young Augusta fears failure under Cato and has not yet won either the arena or the Ephor's office. The rising challenger confronts exploitative death-match rules and her own temptation to equate victory with consuming the weak. The new Ephor inherits Magno's crown, Fabianum's grievance and procedural Senate resistance. The mainline hunt puts her beside Iuno and Rover under dangerous prophecy, while the Iuno-memory disturbance changes what Augusta can honestly recall. After the shared victory she can give others credit and still carry civic duty. A hypothetical gray-haired future in Ascension V is *not* an observed later state. Declaring which of these is active is more important than selecting a generic “royal warrior” tone [AUG-E03–E07, E13–E21, E29].

## Conflicts that a scene must keep intact

**Courage versus entitlement.** Augusta rises through fear and a dismissed Forte. The whisper offers an easy interpretation of strength as a right to use weaker fighters; her arena intervention refuses it without repudiating contest. Ascension I's statement that a sword depends on its wielder's will becomes meaningful only when paired with the surrendered-blade sword and nonlethal reform. Her intention cannot by itself certify that every later use of force is just. A scene should name the rules of engagement, who can walk away, and what happens to a loser [AUG-E02–E04, E10, E27, C01–C03, C24].

**Public title versus affected people.** Ascension II locates her attachment in homeland and Septimont rather than Senate walls or arena height. The Fabianum memory makes this more than a populist slogan: Magno could have a heroic career and still fail a town at the edge. Augusta can wield an office and challenge its insulation. Her later Julia-directed Silva sanction is severe and must remain open to procedural scrutiny; it is not validated merely because she is a reformer. In a different crisis, a ruler's declaration is followed by transport to a temporary camp and a check for stragglers. She is not anti-institutional; she must use law, logistics and coalition work to make promised protection durable. If a crossover suitor or political ally demands she abandon Septimont as proof of affection, the demand conflicts with the present source state even though she imagines a future beyond office [AUG-E06, E08, E17, E27, E40, E42, C04, C06, C25, C36, C38].

**Power versus corrupt rules.** The ascension IV localization fork matters at the point of action. The Chinese anchor, English and Japanese describe power becoming dangerous *under* corrupt rules; Korean describes its potential as a weapon *against* them. A model citing one language should label it and resist importing its target into every dub. The common second movement acknowledges that rewriting rules and keeping the city balanced require further learning. A scene may let Augusta confront an abusive senator directly; it must still ask how the next rule is administered and who it might harm. Neither the solar image in III nor a decisive victory gives her automatic omniscience [AUG-E06, E15, E19, E28, C04, C26, C27].

**Shared heroism versus succession.** After victory Augusta gives credit to others and resists reducing either Rover or herself to one totalizing title. She remains Ephor. Ascension V imagines quiet town, smith work or returning to the arena as an older fighter; English stresses the last more than the other witnesses. None is selected history, and her unaging-blood flourish is not literal immortality. An invented older-Augusta scene can explore who holds the city together when she steps back, but must declare itself speculative and keep arena desire, civic responsibility and bodily age distinct [AUG-E07, E19, E21, E29, C08, C15, C28].

## Social and crossover use

For a dating show, friendship contest or other crossover premise, declare what the other property supplies rather than assigning Augusta unobserved shared history. A suitor's combat skill may earn attention, but humiliating the vulnerable is a negative control, not an attractive display. Someone who can argue honestly about a public burden, respect Iuno's place in Augusta's life, and enjoy an ordinary meal or rooftop pause without demanding permanent performance of “Hero of Heroes” could plausibly reach a different register. This is a scenario affordance, not a romance prediction or a line the actor has performed [AUG-E04, E08–E13, E19–E21, C08, C11, C14].

If a hypothetical older Augusta chooses smithing or arena return, label that choice as *invented for the scenario* and do not cite the archive as having fixed it.
