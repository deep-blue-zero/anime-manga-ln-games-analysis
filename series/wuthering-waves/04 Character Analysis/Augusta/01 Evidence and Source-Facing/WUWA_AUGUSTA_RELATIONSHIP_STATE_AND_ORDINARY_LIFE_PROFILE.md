## Ordinary-life and design affordances

- **Food:** She is not picky, enjoys sharing food and offering to treat friends, but Iuno considers her choices dubious. She mostly passed childhood sweets to Angel; that does not prove a current aversion to all sweets (`FavorWord_130608–130609_Content`). A dinner scene can be generous and slightly fallible rather than a feast of heroic symbols.
- **Places and rest:** Palace roof, wind, Griffrexes and a view of Septimont give her a concrete post-duty practice (`FavorWord_130606_Content`). An empty arena after hours can be a training place, a memory of exclusion or a quiet meeting ground (`FavorStory_130605_Content`, `flow#/11364/2`). The last scene is source-unvoiced.
- **Objects:** Her assembled greatsword carries defeated blades and an earlier decision to preserve lives; after reform it remains part of her identity, not just a stat weapon (`FavorWord_130611_Content`). The solar crown and white/gold drapery are supported by three official-client UI rasters; exact runtime gait, rear construction and damage states are open ([visual profile](../03%20Audiovisual%20and%20Voice/CHARACTER_VISUAL_DESIGN_PROFILE.md)).
- **Interaction style:** She can issue ceremonial challenges and ask someone frankly to fight, but also apologize for a missed appointment, inquire about their goal, share credit and admit uncertainty (`flow#/8363/3`, `8422/2`). In an everyday scene, do not have her speak exclusively in titles and proclamations.
- **Fears and goals:** She does not hate defeat because she has never known it; she remembers failure. She can fear civic catastrophe, worry about a friend and wonder who she is beyond a prophecy. Those concerns do not render her indecisive in battle (E03 E07 E16 E20).

## Hypothetical romance and crossover boundary

Augusta's source permits strong friendship, chosen proximity, trust, gift-giving and an invitation to walk or duel, but none establishes a present exclusive romance with Rover. A crossover suitor is not entitled to the same closeness by resembling a strong warrior. A worthy duel may earn attention; honesty about purpose, refusal to exploit weaker people, respect for Iuno/Angel/Rover as existing bonds, and ability to shoulder obligations without making Augusta the entire spectacle are more important fidelity tests. A noncombatant suitor could still be interesting if courage and civic responsibility are shown in another mode—her own story rejects ability rankings as a complete measure of worth. An approach that flatters her as an infallible solitary sun or treats “win her” as a conquest objective would misread her post-battle sharing of heroism. Such scenario affordances are inferences, not consent or a predicted partner choice.

Before drafting, specify office duty, public crisis, private access, relationship knowledge and memory state. If a question depends on an unobserved gesture or a particular dub's softening, abstain until the [AV plan](../03%20Audiovisual%20and%20Voice/WUWA_AUGUSTA_AV_AND_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_PLAN.md) is executed.
