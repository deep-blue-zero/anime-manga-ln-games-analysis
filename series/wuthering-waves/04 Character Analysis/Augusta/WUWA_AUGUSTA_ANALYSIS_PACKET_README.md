After a shared victory she still asks what life can be beyond a singular hero title. The packet distinguishes Magno's rumored poisoning, Augusta's **reported sight of Angel's transformation but unknown later outcome**, and the exceptional Iuno-memory disturbance instead of treating all three as the same kind of uncertainty.
