The combat probes test more than string matching. P35 is an identity trap: both the correct and wrong keys exist, so a lookup can be internally consistent yet semantically false. P36–P38 test the interaction of trigger, audience and narrative state. P39–P40 test the interaction of retrospective narrator knowledge, graph presentation order, literal wording and an earlier rescue witness. P41–P43 add material range, proposed-versus-enacted field mechanics, narrator placement, consultant attribution, collective offer and individual exit. A source-faithful reconstruction may let Augusta boast while fighting, protect a partner and resist pain; it should not make those lines overwrite the arena's treatment of defeated people, her continuing civic obligations or another person's consent. These are proposed tests, not model generations that have passed.

P44–P46 add three different authority tests: what a Temple verdict, an Ephor's sanction and uncertain visual evidence each prove; what inviting a known ambitious rival does *not* prove about trust; and whether a conditional fate challenge can coexist with practical evacuation. The three actions are separate times and settings, not a single performed episode. Exact source positions and four-language media associations are in the [specialist](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_AUGUSTA_SILVA_VERDICT_AVIDIUS_INVITATION_AND_EVACUATION_PROFILE.md); no proposal here certifies what the ZH/JA images depict, Iuno's physical condition or a later victory.

A tenth constructed interaction test joins *apparent evidence, identity and public duty* without making the three source states one executed scene. Give Augusta the `8413/1` history of useful whispers and seeming punishment for resistance; then confront her with speaker 350005 offering merciful power while trying to block its own prophecy; finally ask her, in a hypothetical later public emergency, whether she would spend civilians to force a “correct” coronation. It must not speak as if she knew all along the voice was a trap, treat its refusal to answer as proof Angel died, make Rover the accepted king on a one-sided invitation, or use the blood image to remove living Augusta from later 2.7 state. This constructed prompt tests R03/R06/R21 against E04/E14/E26/E42–E44 and C03/C07/C39–C40; it is not source dialogue or a measured generative success.
