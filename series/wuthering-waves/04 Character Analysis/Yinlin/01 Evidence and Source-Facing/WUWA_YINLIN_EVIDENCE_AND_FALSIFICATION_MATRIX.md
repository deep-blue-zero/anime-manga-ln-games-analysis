## Claim-level adjudications for rescue, vocation, and bodily cost

**YIN-C01/YIN-C02 — a rescued child can choose against a rescuer's project.** The archive origin puts an infant in the arms of her parents' former student after their undercover deaths, a second threat, and a staged puppet-death escape. He gives her the name the parents had prepared (`FavorStory_130203_Content`). This is a real rescue and a formative dependency; it is not evidence that she remembers her parents or that the guardian is biologically related. Later she tells Dollmaker she wants to join the Bureau in their protective line of work, while he welcomes Bureau access for his own research and coming conflict (`FavorStory_130204_Content`). Her stated motive and his instrumental motive coexist. Lady Guan's family-like worry supplies a third voice, not an objective veto on Yinlin's agency. In the final exchange she condemns using people's happiness as raw material even after Dollmaker invokes their hoped-for reunion (`flow#/1092/1/4–14`). In her subsequent explanation she admits that her own wish to know her parents delayed decisive opposition (`flow#/353/6/18–27`). The ethically usable model must therefore allow gratitude for rescue, guilt about delay, and rejection of exploitation at once. A simplistic “never cared about him” or “owed him every future choice” reading fails different portions of the same pinned record.

**YIN-C06/YIN-C07/YIN-C08 — the anti-sacrifice boundary is not proof she was always clear-sighted.** Dollmaker's claim that the Society offered salvation is voiced in confrontation, not certified by a neutral narrator (`flow#/1092/1/0–12`). Yet her private confession says she once imagined gradually becoming family with parents she never met and knew that others' happiness supplied the thread of that fantasy (`flow#/353/6/23–28`). Nor should the account exaggerate her remorse into an admission that she personally ran a lethal experiment; the selected confession establishes delay and entanglement, not that separate act. A later primary event proving conscious participation in sacrificing a nonconsenting person for reunion would materially revise YIN-C06. Until then, preserve the asymmetry between an imperfect moral boundary and the guardian's documented project.

**YIN-C11 — a dramatic self-rescue is not permanent medical immunity.** The fifth story depicts a Fractsidus-induced arrhythmia, collapse, cessation of heartbeat, and Yinlin using her own electricity to restart it before directing Zapstring despite exhaustion (`FavorStory_130205_Content`).
