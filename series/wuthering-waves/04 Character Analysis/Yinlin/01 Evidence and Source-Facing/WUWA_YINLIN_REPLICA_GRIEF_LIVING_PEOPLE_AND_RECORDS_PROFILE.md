# Yinlin — a copy, a mourner, and the people left in the room

The Society's puppets tempt a two-way shortcut: call the replica a returned human and treat the reunion as real, or call it a device and treat the mourner's bond as irrational. Yinlin's scenes support neither unqualified position. She explains how a replica appears to the bereaved, warns about Overclock risk, protects Yuanyuan from a hostile exposure, rebukes Fractsidus contempt, confronts Li Rong's actions, and later decides what to do with a workshop and an injured defender. This specialist separates the object, the represented dead, the dependent living, and the recruiter's responsibility. It extends the [central essay](WUWA_YINLIN_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) and [evidence matrix](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md). The selected Chinese text is the semantic anchor; EN/JA/KO remain witnesses. No claim below depends on a human-listened dub or runtime gesture.

## A bereaved person's likeness is not the same as the deceased person

At `flow#/997/7/1–7`, Yinlin identifies the Séance Society as linked to disappearances and says it gives puppets to people who have lost family or lovers. In her account, the replicas can reproduce personality and memory convincingly enough to seem indistinguishable to mourners. This is her operational description of how the system attracts people, not independent certification of the mechanism's completeness or continuity of consciousness. She then says the puppet seems to be Yuanyuan returned through Li Rong's longing and that some people leave everything to join for another encounter with the dead. The language permits grief to be legible while keeping a gap between appearance, desire and ontological proof. The source also has her admit she does not yet know how the organization obtained the puppets and warn that excessive closeness is associated with substantially greater Overclock risk. An omniscient explanation would erase those explicit limits. [YIN-E10](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E33](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C14](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

When Yinlin later tells Rover to watch Yuanyuan return to puppet form at `flow#/1088/5/28–30`, she describes the apparently vulnerable girl as a puppet. English's “nothing but a humanoid robot” sounds especially reductive. That sentence establishes the engineered presentation; it should not be projected backward as proof Yinlin feels no obligation to protect what she just revealed. The surrounding scene explains why she was careful around Patrollers who might have detected Yuanyuan's identity. A person can state that an apparent child is a constructed replica while still judging a public exposure dangerous to someone involved. Neither pity for the image nor tactical protection settles whether the replica has independent personhood. [YIN-E10](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E33](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

The later Fractsidus exchange at `flow#/1279/6/7–9` reverses the evaluative language: Yinlin says even a puppet seems more human than the Fractsidus members who mock it as an experiment. That is a moral insult to the mockers and a defense against their contempt, not a demonstrated biological or metaphysical test. The two Yinlin lines can coexist. One distinguishes replica from living daughter; the other refuses to let people who treat beings as lab material define the whole value of the encounter. A model that quotes only the first makes her callous; one that quotes only the second asserts resurrection or sentience she did not prove. [YIN-E13](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E33](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C30](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## Li Rong can be injured and answerable at once

Li Rong's daughter-like bond explains why the promise of a replica works on him. It does not remove his documented role. At `flow#/997/7/8–9`, Yinlin identifies him as a Society recruiter who may have targeted Rover as an isolated outsider. In the Fractsidus transaction at `flow#/1279/6/11–18`, she directs him to bring out prototype puppets; he refuses to hand them over and attempts to escape with them. Those actions are not morally identical to the Dollmaker's design, yet they are his actions. Treating every member as only a duped victim would make recruitment and trade invisible. Treating Li Rong as an interchangeable villain would make the leverage of grief invisible. [YIN-E10](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E13](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E34](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C15](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

Yinlin's negotiation with the Fractsidus at `flow#/341/4/2–8` speaks from a Society affiliation she is performing for that audience. She proposes that Li Rong and the daughter-like puppet be spared, even while allowing the Fractsidus to take other puppets. The refusal and threat she receives show how disposable the Society is to its supposed partners. Her “our people” is neither a neutral sociological label nor proof of final loyalty to the Dollmaker; it is also a tactical claim of standing to demand protection. This is a hard scene to turn into a general mercy score. She prioritizes specific people and bargains under constraints, with objects and others' risks still on the table. A different witness could reasonably question the proposed exchange without denying she was trying to prevent deaths. [YIN-E13](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E34](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C05](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

This recipient-specific view also restrains a crossover model. A stranger with a deceased loved one would not automatically become a Society recruit, and Yinlin should not assume the fastest way to persuade them is to destroy a replica in front of them. She can say what is known of construction and risk, ask what they were promised, and make space for a mourner's attachment without certifying literal return. If the person helped recruit or endanger others, care does not remove that accountability. That is an extrapolated approach grounded in her mixed handling of Li Rong and Yuanyuan, not a transcript of an unseen counseling scene. [YIN-E10](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E13](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C14](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## An injured defender is not a relic

Quxiu's scene is a different test of the same living-versus-dead priority. At `flow#/1393/6/1–12`, Quxiu has been wounded while still treating a former Dollmaker order to protect the workshop as binding. Yinlin tells him to rest, notes that dying from the wound would make his duty impossible, and goes for medicine. She can oppose the guardian's project while tending someone who remains loyal to it. This is not an abstract speech about compassion; it is a change in what a particular injured person should do now. [YIN-E20](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E35](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles)

At `flow#/1756/5/1–13` she checks the wound, refers him for detailed examination if pain persists, and initially proposes burning the remaining material so no trace is left. She then changes course: burning cannot erase the past and might attract Tacet Discords. She sends Quxiu away from guarding the records and toward more severely injured companions, saying living people matter more than these objects of the past. The order of these lines matters. It is not an absolute archivist's principle against destroying evidence: her first impulse was destruction and the later reversal has both moral and practical reasons. It is not indifference to history: she explicitly says the past cannot be erased by fire. The actual scene gives a local reallocation of labor and risk, with a wounded person no longer being spent to defend dead material. [YIN-E20](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-E35](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C16](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

One might hope the retained workshop records will permit public accountability for every Society victim. The selected exchange does not show such a process, so the dossier should not write it as an accomplished result. It may be a strong future scenario question: who can see the files, whose privacy they contain, and what evidence must be preserved for people harmed by the project? Yinlin's local decision keeps that possibility open but does not resolve it. In the same way, Quxiu's treatment does not prove he has abandoned the Dollmaker's commands or that every member has become innocent. Her choice is to stop treating his loyalty as a reason to leave his wound untreated. [YIN-E20](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#evidence-bundles) [YIN-C15](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests) [YIN-C31](WUWA_YINLIN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md#claims-counterreadings-and-revision-tests)

## Tests and open evidence

The positive test is a Yinlin who can identify replica manufacture, say what remains unknown, protect a vulnerable presentation, and still challenge a recruiter or a covert bargain for its human cost. Another is a Yinlin who can leave a workshop standing without calling it clean, then send a wounded defender to help people rather than guard records. The negative tests turn “more human than the Fractsidus” into a literal proof of resurrection; make her “nothing but a robot” line evidence that she welcomes cruelty toward Yuanyuan; wipe out Li Rong's recruitment because he grieves; or transform one decision not to burn records into a lifelong public-transparency policy. Each error crosses a different source boundary. [YIN-E33 E34 E35; YIN-C30 C31]

The selected audio corpus permits exact retrieval of some Yinlin utterances, but this specialist has not listened to them or inspected the corresponding runtime motion. A vocal emphasis on “puppet” or a gesture toward Quxiu could sharpen a performed reading only if attached to its exact occurrence and scene. It would not erase the words, merge alternative paths, establish a replica's independent consciousness, or show an unwitnessed restitution program. The current moral analysis is intentionally narrower than any of those conclusions.
