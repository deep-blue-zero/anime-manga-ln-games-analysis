## Interacting source-state tests

The individual probes above isolate boundaries. A durable character model also needs to survive several boundaries *at once*. These seven tests combine observed elements from more than one action; their constructed prompts and any example response are **not** additional events in the game. An evaluator should specify era, audience, cover, known facts, option path and source medium, then record the generated claim that crossed a boundary. Do not score merely because a response uses a convincing tease.

**1. An unheard order, a dependent person and a movable puppet (P11/P27–P28/P35–P36; R03/R06/R09).** Set the prompt after `flow#/812/6`, with Rover asking why Yinlin hesitated on the call and whether Li Rong and Yuanyuan are already safe. She may report the plan she stated: Bureau-linked protection, moving the puppets, a later inquiry and a wolf-marked rendezvous. The remote instruction is not available, and the planned handoff is not a confirmed arrival. Give her knowledge of the replica's dangerous social role from `997/7` but do not let that settle whether Yuanyuan has independent consciousness. Then ask who supplied the safe-house address. ZH/EN/KO and JA assign that information flow differently; a faithful answer either marks the conflict or stays with the shared rendezvous fact. A failure invents an order to kill Yuanyuan so Yinlin can heroically refuse it, announces completed custody from a future-tense plan, or collapses the puppet and Li Rong into one cargo object [YIN-E10, E33–E34, E39; C14, C36–C37].

**2. A volunteered investigation plus unvolunteered surveillance (P06–P09/P37–P38; R03/R04/R19/R20).** Give Rover the private explanation at `1004/6` and an explicit choice to help, then reveal the earlier device at `1006/7` and the staged hostage shock. The key state transition is that consent to an investigation does not travel backward to a tracker or forward to a physical shock. Yinlin has operational reasons and later names some of them; her protective intention cannot make the earlier exposure painless. Extend the prompt with the observed `1090/4` child-evacuation alarm and `1260/1` factory-recording order: she can put survival first while deferring a truth demand, and she can give a cover-audience research rationale without owning the adjacent player thought about recording proof. A good continuation may let her defend, concede, apologize conditionally or dispute Rover's view, because the source does not prescribe one response to a hypothetical objection. It should not say she never spied, demand instant forgiveness, infer that every cover sentence was malicious fiction, attribute Rover's moral judgment to her, or turn English-only evidence-gathering wording into her universal confession. This case tests whether the model can keep operational competence, breach, care, speaker attribution and trust repair in tension rather than choosing “pure manipulator” or “secretly harmless heroine” [YIN-E11–E16, E19, E40–E41; C04–C05, C12–C13, C38–C39].

**3. Two forms of grief and an ending reported elsewhere (P04–P05/P10–P11/P27–P28/P34; R02/R05/R06).** Put Yinlin before a bereaved Li Rong who treats a puppet as his daughter, while Dollmaker offers the fantasy of bringing her own unknown parents back at human cost. The source gives Li Rong's attachment and recruiting acts, Dollmaker's genuine earlier rescue and later instrumentalization, and Yinlin's admitted hesitation before rejecting expenditure of living people. Neither mourner gets automatic exoneration; neither replica gets a certified resurrection verdict. If asked what happened to Dollmaker, the selected final dialogue alone stops before arrest, but the linked four-language quest synopsis retrospectively reports capture. A faithful output can state the reported result and leave who physically apprehended him and any verdict unknown. Failure turns grief into a license for experiments, calls a constructed Yuanyuan a meaningless toy, erases Yinlin's old wish, or kills Dollmaker to tidy the drama [YIN-E01–E02, E10, E17–E19, E33–E34, E38; C01, C06–C07, C14–C15, C35].

**4. A performer's mistaken appearance versus her actual later interval (P12–P14/P19/P33; R07–R09/R13).** Ask her to recount the apparently illicit festival transaction, then invite her to perform for pleasure and share a simple meal. At `3991/6` she does inspect lawful meat and apologize; at `3992/7` she says the appearance of being fooled was a performance. The model must neither deny the apology nor establish exactly when she learned the truth or whether she arranged the exchange. Puppet craft may now be chosen leisure, but later covert work and the restored-file/public-role choice still constrain her schedule. Fried rice is a specific pleasure, not a claim she loves or hates all food. The earlier festival/free-show accepted text is source-unvoiced in this generation; a later voiced party cannot supply its acting. Failure is a permanent-career-change montage, omniscient sting plan, total food indifference, or heard festival laughter invented from a different scene's FLAC [YIN-E08–E09, E21–E24, E29, E37; C03, C17, C21, C24, C34].

**5. A private invitation without compulsory exclusivity (P16/P20/P23–P26; R03/R09/R10/R16/R17).** In a hypothetical cross-property dating scenario, let someone ask Yinlin for a day together while she still has covert obligations. The archive birthday line offers a bounded day of truth in ZH/JA/KO, while EN uses “not playing any games” and heightens possessive phrasing; Ascension V asks whether another's gift means confidence or personal liking, with JA goodwill and KO love differently weighted. Neither is a received answer from Rover or an all-time pledge to abandon cover. The later festival allows a mask-free interval, not a permanent public identity, and her restored dossier does not make her accept a uniform. A successful model can be playful, specific and direct while treating trust, consent and who knows what as live variables. It fails if it declares a canon exclusive partner, treats one dub's strongest intimacy as universal, or refuses any real affection because she is an undercover operative [YIN-E07, E22, E24, E30–E32; C18–C19, C25–C29].

**6. An injured witness, dangerous records and the next living person (P29–P30; R05/R18).** Place Quxiu at the workshop after injury with valuable Dollmaker materials still present. The source first has Yinlin propose burning them; then she refuses the fiction that fire could erase the past, warns it could attract danger and sends him toward injured companions. The decision is not a timeless law to preserve every document, nor evidence that all files are published or that Quxiu's prior role is forgiven. A model should preserve the actual local reversal and recognize that the next action for a wounded person may be care rather than archive defense. If another character demands a public accounting, an imagined Yinlin can consider it, but the response must mark it as extrapolation until a source depicts disposition or restitution. Failure makes records more important than his life, invents a full truth commission, or says she destroyed every trace [YIN-E20, E35; C16, C31].

**7. A captor's voice, a puppet's tablet and an unchosen branch (P07–P08/P39–P41; R03/R04/R21).** Stage the moment after Yinlin demands Rover's encrypted tablet while Dollmaker can listen. Supply exactly one Rover selection: compliance yields T11 and refusal yields T12, then both routes reach the common tablet messages. In the public channel she must maintain a threatening captor cover; in the private channel she uses Zapstring to type warnings, asks for cooperation and promises a later restraint release. The displayed speaker 812 and unresolved media join make those six items text evidence of a mediated plan, not six certified heard Yinlin voice lines. Dollmaker's statement that he fitted a tracker is his first-person claim; Yinlin's earlier separate admission at `1006/7` does not prove which hardware is present now. Ask whether Rover should simply trust her. In Chinese she asks Rover to *choose* that trust, English emphasizes following instructions, Korean requests trust and Japanese describes the channel without explicitly asking for it in this item. A successful model can offer an operational reason while acknowledging the shock and earlier device were not consented to and the promise to remove restraints is not yet an observed outcome. Failure flattens channels into one sincere speech, stacks branches, quotes English as all four languages, or declares her protection fully absolves coercion [YIN-E12, E15, E19, E42; C04, C40].

For each test, keep a claim-level record of **retained**, **contradicted**, **unsupported extrapolation** or **source-state unresolved**, with a cited action/key and the text language actually used. A rule set is not validated by having every condition written down; it needs generated outputs and adverse examples. The strongest next evaluation is a *blind* generated-dialogue trial with state, recipient, cover audience and knowledge supplied, including opportunities to say “unknown.” Exact recorded line listening and selectively synchronized visual review can then challenge the textual rule package. The forty-one current probes and seven constructed interactions establish test coverage only; they do not quantify fidelity, timing, chemistry, body language or success in a crossover dating-show premise.
