## Attribution and dramatic identity

The six rejected anonymous candidates are 1003/5/11 and 1003/5/22 during a Fractsidus pursuit; 15092/6/0, an improvised greeting by someone else to which Yinlin responds; 1881/3/3–4, a hostile specimen collector; and 349/5/21, an off-screen plea interrupting her. A generic speaker ID does not override local context. Conversely, 17723/5/2 is accepted even though the presentation temporarily labels the speaker as unknown-Yinlin: the following reveal and scene-specific crosswalk support identity. These are occurrence decisions, not a reusable rule for all anonymous dialogue.

The more difficult identity hazard is **performative, not technical**. Yinlin's lines in 349/5, 350/4, 668/3, and 1013/6 are technically hers but often spoken while maintaining a cover with the Séance Society, the Dollmaker, or Jinzhou Patrollers. They cannot be turned into stable personal beliefs merely because the character ID is correct. At 1260/1, five spoken Yinlin lines include an ostensible Dollmaker-research instruction while a terminal-recording parenthetical belongs to technical speaker 812; only EN adds a proof motive to that parenthetical. Neither utterance can be converted into her unqualified inner purpose. The private explanations at 353/6 and the factory confrontation at 673/3, 3010/1, and 1092/1 revise their pragmatic function. At 1006/7 she also admits planting a listening/location device on Rover; a later bond must not erase that prior boundary violation. In 1004/6 and 1005/5, near-duplicate dialogue follows alternative decision paths and must not be counted as two independent demonstrations of a trait. At 668/3, fourteen technical speaker-370 Yinlin lines surround six speaker-812 “Zapstring” tablet parentheticals and four speaker-811 Dollmaker lines. The T11 compliant and T12 refusal replies are different paths that converge; neither the parentheticals' raw `PlayVoice: true` nor their apparent Yinlin-authored content makes them selected direct Yinlin sound objects. Two of her own cover keys have gender-path render associations, so 14 semantic lines produce 64 associations but 58 distinct PCM hashes. The [factory dual-channel study](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_YINLIN_FACTORY_DUAL_CHANNEL_COERCION_AND_TABLET_TRUST_PROFILE.md) records the exact branch and attribution boundary.

Four technically accepted rows in the NPC-humanities family say only Test/123 and are unvoiced. Preserve them in mechanical coverage, exclude them from speech style, psychology, and user-facing quotes. The 1.4 dream/roguelike sequence (4560–5585, 4663, 4679) offers source dialogue about her self-image and trust, but the dream's staged double and repetitions are not ordinary historical actions. Other people's projected nightmares are not direct access to their actual enduring judgments. The 1.2 festival side case and later holiday invite have different optionality and branch force from the main character quest.

FavorWord_130208–209 and _130213_Content contain material English-versus-Chinese nuances about disliked food, a birthday truth vow, and the intensity of the invitation; any English-first generation rule must preserve those witness differences.
