# Aemeath audiovisual evidence hierarchy and nomination plan

## Purpose

This plan answers:

> **What Aemeath audiovisual evidence should be collected, and which existing claim could it preserve, strengthen, qualify, scope-limit, revise, reject, or leave open?**

It does not claim that any witness has been acquired or reviewed. Human search terms and public names are delegated to `WUWA_AEMEATH_AV_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_CROSSWALK.md`. Exact acquired-object provenance belongs in a later Drive `AV_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.jsonl` and must not be pre-created empty.

# 1. Four authority dimensions

Every nominated witness should be scored qualitatively and independently.

## 1.1 Diegetic authority

How strongly does the witness establish that an expression, action, spatial relation, event, or state occurs in story continuity?

Highest-value Aemeath examples:

- 3.1 main-story cutscenes and scripted in-engine scenes;
- 3.3 return/Exostrider/apology scenes;
- 3.4 institutional intervention;
- 3.5 graduation/road-trip material;
- post-return character ecology at the cabin and in ordinary movement.

## 1.2 Representational authority

How strongly does the witness establish official choices about Aemeath's appearance, embodiment, movement, combat, voice, costume, mech relation, or form distinction?

Character showcases, model animation, official PVs, splash art, and key art can be high even when not diegetically literal.

## 1.3 Interpretive authority

How strongly can the witness support a claim about Aemeath's psychology, relationship state, development, ordinary texture, or ethical choice?

Context-rich narrative sequences generally outrank isolated promotional poses for habitual behavior.

## 1.4 Production-framing authority

How strongly does the artifact reveal what Kuro chooses to foreground or associate with Aemeath for an audience?

This is the primary authority dimension for:

- `Aemeath — GRADUATION SHOW`;
- combat showcase;
- version trailers;
- language/channel-exclusive PVs;
- official promotional illustrations;
- recurring pairings with Rover, Exostrider, Startorch peers, or associated shell.

## 1.5 Non-equivalence rule

High production-framing or representational authority does not prove that a depicted event literally happened or that a pose represents ordinary behavior.

# 2. Source strata

## Stratum A — core diegetic AV

Primary analytical responsibility:

- facial action and gaze;
- gesture and posture;
- blocking and interpersonal distance;
- touch and object interaction;
- visibility/materialization effects;
- reaction order;
- embodied state transitions;
- story-grounded delivery;
- timing of concealment, anger, farewell, apology, and return.

Priority: **highest**.

## Stratum B — matched multilingual promotional AV

Seek the same official PV/trailer/showcase in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English where materially equivalent editions exist.

Primary responsibility:

- controlled localization/performance comparison;
- same-edit state/persona comparison;
- production framing held approximately constant;
- separation of wording from acting.

A partial two- or three-language set remains valid. Do not fabricate missing members.

## Stratum C — unmatched official promotional AV

Include language/channel-exclusive material. Its responsibility is language-specific performance and production framing, not independent proof of cross-dub difference.

## Stratum D — official static/representational media

Collect splash art, profile art, key art, official illustrations, and campaign graphics for:

- form/embodiment design;
- Core/heart/Exostrider motifs;
- color and star imagery;
- costume/prop continuity;
- relationship pairing;
- audience-facing identity.

# 8. Pre-AV hypotheses to attack

## AV-H01 — brightness has multiple embodied functions

Text predicts genuine play, reassurance, deflection, and agency assertion. AV must test whether facial action, gaze, posture, and timing distinguish them.

## AV-H02 — child/student/ghost/returned states remain visibly continuous without being identical

Test posture, movement range, apparent weight, visibility effects, costume, framing, and interaction affordances.

## AV-H03 — truth conflict turns care into command

Test whether Aemeath physically controls distance, blocks movement, avoids gaze, reaches toward Rover, or is visually contained by Sigillum.

## AV-H04 — departure retains fear and desire beneath resolve

Test reaction order, pauses, touch, camera alignment, and child/adult intercut rather than assuming the text's determination is affective simplicity.

## AV-H05 — 3.3 return is plural rescue, not hero restoration

Test how the explorers, Exostrider, engineers, Rover, and Aemeath are given visual authorship.

## AV-H06 — the associated shell is deliberately indeterminate

Test whether animation supports mechanical routine, residual habit, transmitted response, or controlled ambiguity.

## AV-H07 — home becomes a practice of return

Test cabin object use, spatial comfort, silence, and reciprocal care after return.

## AV-H08 — promotional Aemeath foregrounds selected facets rather than replacing story complexity

Compare PV framing against the textual reconstruction and record omissions as production-framing evidence, not contradiction by default.

## AV-H09 — matched dubs may encode different persona/state distances

Use identical or near-identical PV/scene groups. Separate localized wording from performance before drawing conclusions.

# 9. Observation protocol

For each acquired witness, record:

- `av_target_id` and `witness_id`;
- exact source/public identity;
- game version and time span;
- language;
- edit and semantic equivalence where grouped;
- state/persona;
- participants;
- low-inference observations before interpretation.

Low-inference fields should include where visible:

- body orientation;
- gaze target and change;
- interpersonal distance;
- initiation/withdrawal;
- touch/object interaction;
- facial action without emotion label;
- locomotion and stillness;
- reaction order;
- camera distance/angle/movement;
- cut timing;
- lighting/color;
- materialization/visual effects;
- music/silence onset;
- spoken-line timing.

Only then record interpretation and claim transition.

# 10. Bias controls

- Do not select only tragic scenes; include ordinary and comic controls.
- Do not select only Rover scenes; include peers, Exostrider, fans, institution, dead/explorers, and shell.
- Do not use promotional attractiveness as habitual behavior evidence.
- Do not treat one language channel's greater upload volume as character difference.
- Do not compare unrelated scenes as if visual/dramatic context were controlled.
- Do not let the current thesis determine which counterexamples are downloaded.
- Preserve full parent witnesses when extracting analytical clips.
