# Aemeath audiovisual human-retrieval crosswalk

## Responsibility

This crosswalk translates exact source-facing Aemeath AV nominations into human-facing story names, dialogue anchors, and search terms. It answers:

> **How can a human collector locate the audiovisual witness nominated by the analysis?**

It does not prove that a search result is canonical evidence. Public titles, upload names, and suggested queries are retrieval aids. Any located media must still be verified, assigned a `witness_id`, and recorded in the Drive AV evidence manifest before direct analytical use.

No witness has yet been acquired. Every row therefore has `acquisition_status: retrieval_mapped` or an explicit unresolved state.

## Mapping-confidence vocabulary

- **high** — source family/version and public story identity align clearly.
- **moderate** — chapter/version is clear, but public subunit/objective or standalone clip identity is uncertain.
- **low** — dialogue/location suggests a likely public family but requires manual verification.
- **unresolved** — no reliable public-facing title has yet been established.
