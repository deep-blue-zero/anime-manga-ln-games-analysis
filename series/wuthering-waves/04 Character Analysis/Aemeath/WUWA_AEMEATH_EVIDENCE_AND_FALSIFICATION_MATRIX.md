## Matrix use rule

A later source or audiovisual witness should not be logged merely as “another example.” It should record whether it:

- `PRESERVE`s an invariant;
- `STRENGTHEN`s the evidentiary basis;
- `QUALIFY`s the causal explanation;
- `SCOPE_LIMIT`s the state, situation, or relationship range;
- supplies a counterexample;
- reveals a localization/performance-specific effect;
- `REVISE`s or `REJECT`s the claim;
- or leaves it `OPEN`.

## High-risk claims requiring AV attack

The following claims are especially vulnerable to transcript-only overreach:

1. **AEM-C09 / AEM-C41 — smile and humor functions.** Facial action, pause, gaze, and editing could distinguish genuine delight, protective deflection, bitterness, and performative reassurance.
2. **AEM-C19 / AEM-C23 — shared burden and apology.** Staging may reveal whether post-return correction is tentative, assured, reciprocal, or still dominated by Aemeath's initiative.
3. **AEM-C28 — fan/song social personhood.** Performance and audience framing may complicate the relationship between private creator and public persona.
4. **AEM-C29–C33 — Exostrider, returned embodiment, and shell.** Visual design, motion, framing, and reaction order are indispensable.
5. **AEM-C40 — speech baseline.** Text supports register, but acting/persona claims require matched listening.
6. **AEM-C44 — Rover relationship.** Physical distance, touch, gaze, scene composition, and localization may affect the interpretation without settling a categorical romance label.

## Claims that must not be silently universalized

- Childhood courage does not automatically describe adult risk calibration.
- Historical student habits do not automatically survive ghosthood or return.
- Ghost-state technical capability does not prove effortless emotional coping.
- One dub's vocal register does not define language-independent personality.
- Promotional framing does not prove diegetic habitual behavior.
- Associated-shell behavior does not become direct Aemeath evidence without a future identity resolution.
- Aemeath's promise after 3.3 does not guarantee she will never again overcommit under unique-agency pressure.
