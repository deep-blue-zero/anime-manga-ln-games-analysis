## 4. Why R02 does not exonerate every secret

The information-security explanation is source-explicit: certain memories are unavailable to the enemy, and the apparent death must be believed. That is stronger evidence of the operation's mechanism than an inferred universal inability to accept care. It therefore changes how the same conduct is explained.

It does not prove the plan was the only conceivable secure plan. It does not erase the distress of uninformed companions. It does not authorize an analyst to excuse an unrelated delay or lie by invoking an enemy-memory rule absent from that situation. The correction is narrower and more useful: preserve the actual constraints, then evaluate the decision under them.

The earlier warning about self-instrumentalization remains applicable because Cartethyia is still willing to make herself the means. But choosing a coordinated, risky strategy and requesting annihilation under hopelessness are not the same decision. The new model keeps both the shared vulnerability and the different causal structures.

## 5. Why ordinary life changes the model rather than merely lengthening it

The family conversation introduces a form of care that does not demand reciprocal recognition. The irises make return compatible with errantry. The medical consultation demonstrates that willpower can be corrected by expertise. Asking for gentleness permits an unheroic need that is not immediately converted into service. Ordinary skill, food, and drink scenes allow mistakes, learning, taste, and humor to remain themselves.

These additions constrain prediction. A model of Cartethyia should not demand reunion with her parents, reject every anchor as confinement, treat every physician as an obstacle, or make every free afternoon another act of sacrifice. It should also not infer from these capacities that she has permanently resolved secrecy, risk, grief, or public myth. The new ordinary-life profile supplies counterweights, not a cure certificate.

## 6. Cross-character methodology, not transferred psychology

The Lynae, Aemeath, and Denia packets provide the architecture: source-facing reading, explicit state and relationship modeling, ordinary-life reconstruction, falsification, bounded speech/acoustics, a downstream model, and AV acquisition routing. Their individual theses are not templates to impose on Cartethyia.

In particular, Aemeath's separate shell cannot become a reason to split Cartethyia/Fleurdelys into two lore identities. Denia's identity problem cannot become proof that Cartethyia's entire childhood was false. A recurring sacrifice pattern in another character cannot decide why the Galbrena plan is secret. Shared analytical language should expose differences in causal structure rather than make several characters versions of the same therapeutic arc.
