# Cartethyia: Audiovisual Evidence Nomination Plan

## 1. Purpose and present state

This plan specifies what audiovisual evidence could preserve, qualify, or reject the rebuilt textual model. It is not a request to collect every image or video of Cartethyia. A selection must justify its analytical responsibility, and a powerful-looking scene is not automatically more informative than a meal, a hesitation, or an accepted refusal.

Cartethyia differs from the newly built Lynae/Denia/Aemeath packets in one practical respect: **ten bounded audiovisual clips already exist in Drive**. The V0.3 anchor manifest was read, and the corresponding ten filenames and sizes were observed in the Drive clips folder. This pass did not download, play, or newly hash their video bytes. Their structured moving-image status remains `OPEN_unannotated`. The new task is partly to reuse and review existing evidence, not to pretend acquisition begins at zero.

No human comparative listening pass was performed. The owner-reported JP/EN form contrast is preserved in the speech profile as a hypothesis for an optional terminal addendum. It does not guide the textual verdict as if it were an independent observation by this analyst.

## 2. Authority is claim-dependent

| Dimension | Application to this dossier | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Diegetic | Does a bounded scene establish an action in the relevant fictional state? | Every official montage is a literal event |
| Representational | What official form, costume, movement, or performed line is shown? | Official design implies habitual personality |
| Interpretive | What particular claim can the complete context support? | A glamorous pose establishes confidence in every situation |
| Production framing | What traits, relationships, images, or contrasts does the released artifact foreground? | Released framing proves an individual creator's private intention |

The dimensions are not four scores to average. A reliable third-party client recording may be excellent evidence of a particular quest event while being a poor source for subtle audio measurement after compression. An official PV may be excellent evidence of released framing while leaving literal chronology unclear. A script describing a hand or gaze is already textual evidence; it is not equivalent to watching the gesture unfold.

## 3. Collection strata

**Core diegetic AV** receives first priority for the character model: complete bounded cutscenes, in-engine dialogue, and ordinary exchanges with known source identities. Preserve enough context to understand initiation, refusal, response, and consequences. The complete runtime sequence may include less polished dialogue animation and should not be replaced by cinematic highlights alone.

**Matched multilingual promotional AV** is useful for separating wording, performance, and editing. Prefer equivalent semantic beats and comparable visual contexts, but do not require all four languages before any useful review can occur. Different edit lengths must be recorded rather than forced into identical timestamps.

**Unmatched official promotion** remains admissible for representation and audience-facing framing. Additional Japanese or other language material should not be discarded to create symmetry. Its existence does not independently prove a difference in the underlying character.

**Static official art** can support design, props, iconography, and pairing. It cannot establish motion, timing, ordinary consent, or an action that occurred in continuity. One canonical evidence object can be referenced by several analytical targets without being copied into several character folders.

## 6. Multilingual comparison design

The preferred comparison is within each language first: ordinary speech, effortful public composure, vulnerable disclosure, and operational command. Only then compare the size and kind of the contrast across languages. A deep Japanese historical command should not be compared with a cheerful English archive greeting as if form were the only variable.

Each matched group requires an identity independent of language, separate member witness IDs, visual/edit-equivalence adjudication, semantic/script-equivalence adjudication, and aligned spans. Record spoken language separately from subtitle language. A platform may offer dubbed versions, translated subtitles, or different uploads; those are not interchangeable evidence types.

The four-language public listing of the located showcase is a useful retrieval lead. Its displayed durations differ between the Chinese entry and the other entries. That is enough to reject an assumption of identical timelines, but not enough to diagnose why the lengths differ. No actor intention, dub authenticity, or performance ranking follows from metadata.

## 7. Required observations and claim transitions

For each directly reviewed target, record the witness identity, full source span, actual reviewed subspan, technical quality, audio availability, language, character/form attribution, and the observation method. Then separate a low-inference description from a performance interpretation and a character-level inference. A look away is not automatically shame; a pause is not automatically deceit; a cut is not automatically a character's perception.

When a finding changes the reading, cite the affected C-code and record `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `QUALIFY`, `SCOPE_LIMIT`, `REVISE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`. Explain why. Corroboration need not inflate confidence if it merely repeats the same underlying event through another projection. Contrary audiovisual evidence should not be explained away simply because the textual model is attractive.

## 8. Quality, provenance, and duplication controls

Retain original source URL or client-capture provenance, acquisition date, original filename, exact content hash, duration, and any transformations. A derivative must point to its parent and retain the original time offset. Hashes establish byte identity, not interpretive truth. Do not upscale a small frame and then claim newly visible original detail. Do not treat a compressed mixed soundtrack as an isolated vocal stem.

The existing 640 by 358 audio-bearing clips are useful bounded witnesses but may be inadequate for subtle facial or costume detail. Their predecessor silent versions and old receipt hashes are different artifacts. Older I/J timing ranges were corrected in V0.3; the crosswalk uses the corrected spans. Existing I and J overlap and should not count as independent examples.

## 9. Completion and stopping rule

The hierarchy and human-retrieval plan are provisionally prepared. Exact mappings remain open for several ecology and ordinary-life scenes, and acquisition/review is not complete. `audiovisually_hardened` and `integrated_reconstruction_completed` remain false. The comprehensive machine-voice gate also remains unmet; a useful basic census is not a substitute for that gate.

A future AV tranche can close when its declared material targets have been directly reviewed or explicitly dispositioned, major alternative readings have been tested, and affected claims and the model have been revised. It need not collect every promotional artifact or require four-dub parity. A material unresolved target must remain visible rather than disappearing into a global complete flag.

Full media and deterministic acquisition manifests belong in the evidence plane. Analytical nomination, this retrieval bridge, compact observations, and claim revisions belong in the analytical plane after separate promotion. This local packet creates no new media manifest, performs no Drive mutation, and does not grant itself registry or publication authority.
