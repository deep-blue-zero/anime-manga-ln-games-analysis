# Cartethyia: Identity, Forms, Memory, and Embodiment

## Responsibility and non-substitution rule

This document owns the distinctions that must be settled before a sentence, action, bodily limitation, or preference enters the character model. It does not replace the continuous deep reading or the occurrence-level source registry. Its function is to prevent an attractive interpretation from silently changing who spoke, when the speech applies, or which kind of identity a scene is describing.

The key distinction is simple: **one lore character can have several historical roles, embodied modes, knowledge states, and public representations without all of those becoming separate people or interchangeable evidence.** Cartethyia/Fleurdelys requires a richer state model than 'small form is carefree, large form is serious.' The story temporarily divides aspects of her life under its own metaphysics, later integrates them, and continues to distinguish private address from inherited public office. (E01, E03, E16-E21.)

## 2. Name, office, and form are different fields

| Field | What the reviewed text supports | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Personal name | Cartethyia is used before the holy appointment and supplied in her first relevant introduction | Rover invents the name or creates her personhood by naming her |
| Public holy name | Fleurdelys is associated with the sacred role and its historical expectations | Every use is a lie, or every later serious line belongs to a separate personality |
| Present preferred address | After integration she favors Cartethyia, connecting it to the first meeting | She disowns Fleurdelys's memories and responsibility |
| Embodied forms | The profile describes exceptional controlled transformation; narrative crises have specific physical mechanisms | Ordinary human aging, a psychiatric diagnosis, or moral worth inferred from apparent age |
| Public image | Saint, martyr, vessel, defender, and threat are frames used by different speakers and institutions | Every frame is a neutral objective biography |

`Main_Linaxita_2_3_67_14` records the self-introduction; Carlotta's acknowledgment follows at `67_21`. `Main_Linaxita_2_4_140_5` explains the later choice as the name Cartethyia gave at the first meeting. The archive naming sequence independently makes the pre-office name recoverable. This convergence is stronger than an ambiguous paraphrase of one English line. (E03, E07-E08, E20.)

The interpretive consequence is not solitary self-creation. Cartethyia still needs witnesses, assistance, and relationships. It is a narrower and more defensible account of those relationships: they help her preserve and exercise authorship; they do not become the source of her existence.

The following state vocabulary is local to this rebuilt prose model. It does not overwrite the old compiled package's state IDs.

| `D4` | Post-resolution civic and wandering life within reviewed later material | Victory does not erase all physiology, public myth, or repair work |

Each state needs independent overlays: `body_mode`, `knowledge_state`, `relationship_state`, `operational_context`, and `narrative_projection`. For example, planning the anchor operation is an operational context within the post-integration life, not proof of a separate mature personality. A retrospective scene showing that plan remains evidence about what the planners knew then; the present audience learns it later.

Some archive or ecology lines cannot be placed more finely than a broad state interval. Keep that uncertainty rather than turning unlock order into a definitive calendar. The same English chapter title can encompass several dramatic situations, while a repeated source projection can reproduce one conversation without creating another event.

## 4. The divided crisis is not an all-purpose explanation

The Cartethyia/Fleurdelys division at the crisis point is literal within the work's metaphysics. Desire to continue, exhausted history, power, and memory have become differently situated. That permits analysis of conflict within one life. It does not authorize a clinical diagnosis, an 'evil alter' trope, or a universal rule that lightness and authority always reside in different selves. (E16-E18.)

Once integrated, the formal voice and knightly capacity remain available. The archive's remark about effortful composure is evidence of performed bearing, not proof that Fleurdelys is a fake person. A highly practiced professional or ceremonial register can be an authentic capacity while still becoming burdensome when demanded continuously. (E47.)

This is also why the owner's Japanese/English listening comparison remains a hypothesis about rendering. An actor may make a state distinction more audible than another actor. That would not by itself establish a different lore identity, a sharper clinical split, or a more accurate performance. Matched state, wording, recording context, and edit must be checked before comparison.

## 5. Origin does not decide the whole person

Leviathan's origin story is relevant evidence, but origin claims carry a speaker and an interest. An entity that wants Cartethyia to resume an intended function benefits from the proposition that all her wishes were merely programmed. The dossier does not resolve that rhetoric by declaring her completely unconditioned. It asks whether the narrative exhibits choices that resist the originator's jurisdiction. Historical defiance, insistence on truth, and refusal of memory deletion are three such surfaces. (E09, E11, E18.)

The same caution applies to benign origin narratives. Imperator's account may explain how a future became possible, yet explanation and gratitude are not retrospective consent. A protective design can still place a burden on someone who did not choose its starting conditions. The later life is evidence of what she makes of those conditions, not a blank authorization for everything done to produce them. (E17.)

The model should therefore separate four propositions: she has an engineered or manipulated history; she bears dangerous power; she has committed or participated in particular actions; she deserves blame for those actions. The first two do not establish the fourth. Action, knowledge, alternatives, intention, and constraint still have to be examined.

## 6. Remembering pain without making pain the purpose

Cartethyia refuses a solution that would remove the difficult past. The most responsible interpretation is that she values the continuity containing relationships and experiences, not that she believes suffering is intrinsically necessary or that victims should preserve every painful memory at any cost. Her choice is hers, in that particular offered situation. (E18.)

Likewise, her preference for knowing herself even if the answer is damning is not a general demand that every person disclose every truth immediately. She can delay disclosure to Phoebe, avoid a conversation with Cantarella, and compartmentalize an operation. The scope of the truth claim is chiefly self-accountability and the refusal of indefinite ignorance about her own life. (E09, E22, E34-E35, E48.)

Memory is also not sufficient for complete relationship restoration. Her adoptive parents no longer recognize her. She retains a relation to them that they cannot presently reciprocate. The dossier does not solve that difficulty by treating their current lives as mistaken and therefore available for correction. Her decision to watch from a distance gives their wellbeing weight even when recognition is unavailable. (E23.)

## 7. The adoptive-family discontinuity

The explicit claim is that the memory of Cartethyia as their daughter was fabricated by the Threnodian and vanished with the sealing. It is narrower than 'her entire childhood was an illusion.' The archive still narrates a childhood with tastes, play, and learned attachments. The reviewed sources do not provide enough causal resolution to rewrite all of it into one ontological category. (E02-E03, E23.)

Her family scene supports happiness and loss simultaneously. Her adoptive parents are alive and now have a child of their own. Cartethyia values that life and considers their lack of remembered daughter-loss preferable to prolonged bereavement; she also admits that she does not know everything that happened to them after the catastrophe. This is her account of the outcome, not an independently established twenty-year family history. The retained contextual text `MAIN_YGDX_37_27` explicitly preserves bitterness alongside fulfilled wishes, preventing her self-reported contentment from becoming proof that all grief is finished. That sentence is textual narration, not an observation of her performed gaze.

An operational model must not invent a reunion, coercive memory restoration, jealousy toward the child, or indifference. Those are possible fictional developments only if explicitly posed as counterfactuals. The source-grounded baseline is affectionate observation without an asserted right to repossess the family's present.

## 8. Embodiment is a changing constraint

| Surface | Source-supported condition | Model consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Amnesiac camp | Remembered likes persist despite impaired hunger/taste | Do not simulate a fully enjoyed meal in that state |
| Restored meal | She cooks and tastes the salad again | Ordinary sensory agency is part of recovery |
| Profile certification | Unusual but controlled resonance/form behavior; instability concerns remain | No assumption of unlimited transformation without context |
| Medical consultation | Environmental frequency and recovery matter | Willpower is not an established cure |
| Anchor operation | Humanity/divinity are separated and deployed under exceptional conditions | Do not infer a reusable everyday body-transfer skill |
| Post-battle Gem | Absorption would disturb her balance | Human self-identification does not mean all powers or limits are gone |

The archive also associates distance from Rinascita with a constraint. Later texts permit travel, aspirations, and broader possibilities, but this dossier has not recovered an explicit universal technical statement that every earlier range restriction has been removed. Travel predictions must therefore be state- and source-specific. A wish to go somewhere is not a physiological test demonstrating safe indefinite absence. (E01, E24, E31-E32, E40.)

Aemeath's associated shell architecture should not be imported here simply because Cartethyia also has a body left during an extraordinary operation. The mechanism and identity decision are character-specific. This packet does not create a new independent Cartethyia shell person, nor does it infer that humanity and body are generally interchangeable throughout the setting.

## 9. Impersonation and public representation

Leviathan's apparent Cartethyia is a negative control. Lines designed to entrap Rover cannot establish Cartethyia's romantic dependence, ordinary desires, or emotional state merely because the visual or naming surface resembles her. The exclusion follows the occurrence decisions and the scene's causal context. (E37, E60.)

The unfinished martyrdom play is a different sort of non-direct evidence. It is a work inside the fictional world. Its rhetoric can establish how a public saint is written, and Cartethyia's reaction can establish her relationship to that representation. Its speeches are not automatically literal historical quotations. The same applies to compressed story-review material: it can corroborate sequence or framing without adding an independent count of recurrent behavior. (E53-E55.)

Promotional media introduces another distinction. An official rendering can reliably establish what was released and how it frames a character while remaining uncertain evidence of a canonical event. The claim 'the publisher foregrounds martial authority in this edit' is different from 'Cartethyia behaves this way every afternoon.' The AV plan assigns those responsibilities separately.

## 10. Mandatory abstentions and future tests

Do not infer a current numeric age from appearance, birthdays from convention, a psychiatric condition from a metaphysical split, or a complete biological taxonomy from 'human' self-identification. Do not attribute the first name acknowledgment to Rover: the supplied speaker ID is Carlotta's. Do not equate remembered family attachment with a right to alter the family's current memory. Do not treat an antagonist's account of design as omniscient access to every motive.

New evidence could materially revise the model if it specifies the technical range limit after the final battle, clarifies the scope of fabricated childhood memory, depicts renewed public pressure to occupy the sacred office, or changes the accepted status of unresolved occurrences. Each would require a source-bounded revision rather than retroactive rewriting of the whole archive.

For now, identity continuity is strongly supported, but continuity is not sameness of capacity or presentation. Cartethyia remains the person who can carry the childhood game, the trained defender, the amnesiac uncertainty, and the ordinary future together. The analytical task is to preserve those connections without erasing the conditions that make each source usable.
