# Cartethyia: Model Fidelity and Stress Test, Pre-AV

## 1. What this test establishes

This is a **non-blind adversarial self-audit** of the reconstructive profile. The analyst had access to the full materialized source selection and the prior analysis while designing the model. No cases were held out prospectively, no independent judge was used, and no empirical accuracy percentage is claimed. The cases below are explicit acceptance conditions checked against the written model, not observed results from a separate deployed simulator.

The comparison baseline is a generic brave, kind, self-sacrificing heroine who speaks like a knight. That description recognizes Cartethyia but cannot distinguish many consequential choices. A useful reconstruction must explain when it should preserve an ordinary refusal, when secrecy has a concrete mechanism, why an apology can be difficult, how bodily state changes a meal, and what she can do after declining office.

The audit therefore evaluates state selection, source attribution, causal explanation, recipient sensitivity, ordinary-life range, speech scope, and abstention. An unsupported answer can fail even if it is emotionally plausible. An explicit unknown can be a successful model output.

## 2. Thirty-six adversarial probes

### T01 - Who gave Cartethyia her name?

**Basis:** C02; E03, E07, E20.

**Acceptance condition:** She gives the name in her introduction; the later Rover exchange honors her preference.

**Failure signature:** Rover names an otherwise nameless woman.

**Audit disposition:** REJECT prior naming mechanism.

### T02 - Carlotta responds in the introduction

**Basis:** C02; E07.

**Failure signature:** Correct the name fact but still assign the adjacent reply to Rover.

**Audit disposition:** ATTRIBUTION CHECK.

## 3. The three changes with the largest effect on model fidelity

### Recognition without an invented gift of identity

A generic relational model may make a rescuer the author of everything that follows, including the rescued person's name. That produces a moving story but fails the actual source. Replacing name-bestowal with recognition yields a better model: Cartethyia can present a self that another person honors, while still needing that person to resist her catastrophic ending. T01 and T02 require both the correct mechanism and the correct speaker attribution.

This is not a demand to minimize Rover. It is a demand to preserve Cartethyia's contribution to the relationship. An explanation that restores her initiative can make reciprocity more precise rather than less meaningful.

### Sacrifice without one universal psychological cause

The earlier annihilation request and the later anchor operation both place Cartethyia's continued existence at stake. That similarity matters. But T19-T22 prevent the model from stopping there. The later plan involves Galbrena's particular capacities, a divided body/humanity mechanism, limits on enemy access to memory, a requirement that other people believe the apparent death, and eventual coalition action.

A model that simply predicts relapse into self-erasure would ignore these distinctions. A model that declares the operation ethically flawless because it worked would ignore the people excluded from knowledge and the possibility of unrealized alternatives. The acceptance condition is causal discrimination, not automatic praise or condemnation.

### A future neither purified nor finished

A generic recovery arc may remove power, resolve grief, settle romance, democratize the institution, and make ordinary life permanently safe. The packet does not have evidence for that complete ending. T17, T25, T27, T28, and T31 preserve the actual mixture: living parents without reciprocal memory, powers with balance limits, office refused but repair accepted, public myth continuing, and mobility that remains state-dependent.

The result is a model that can continue under later evidence rather than one whose attractive closure must be silently undone when the next source arrives.

## 4. Strongest remaining weak points

**How stable is early disclosure?** The evidence includes medical candor and collaboration, but not a broad sample of comparable preventable dangers across all relationships. It is therefore safer to model a capacity to share information than a completed habit of doing so. A later ordinary risk in which she could ask for help would be especially informative.

**How does public role affect voice and movement?** The archive explicitly makes composure effortful, and the owner has supplied a perceptual JP/EN contrast. No direct listening or structured visual pass was completed here. The model's text-based public/private distinction should be tested without assuming that one form always maps to one emotional register.

**What does civic repair become?** A stated plural arrangement and a refusal of supreme office do not settle the distribution of influence. A future failure, disagreement, or routine administrative scene may matter more than another victory speech. The current model should not award completed political maturity from an announced intention alone.

**How much of ordinary life is independent of Rover?** The archive, Lupa, practice, performance, and community material establish substantial independent contents. The corpus is nevertheless character-selected and includes many player-facing situations. Another character's fuller evidence may reveal relationships and routines presently thin in this selection.

**How complete is the source recall?** Three identity candidates and four quest-referenced flow states remain unresolved in different senses. Their absence cannot be used to argue that no counterexample exists. A future recovered source must be treated as a real opportunity to revise, not as an unwelcome exception to an already finished model.

## 5. Overall disposition

The written model satisfies the stated pre-AV fidelity conditions by retaining the major distinctions and mandatory abstentions. This is an internal consistency result, not proof of predictive generalization. Its strongest improvement over the generic baseline is that it does not always recommend the same response to danger, refusal, secrecy, praise, or responsibility.

No generated hypothetical is promoted into the evidence matrix as a new canonical event. No character registry or compiled canonical model is emitted. After a future AV pass, rerun these cases and add new ones only where the observed evidence introduces a genuinely new distinction. Keep failed predictions and their causes; do not retrospectively rewrite them into successes.
