Character-ecology interactions and later event dialogue may have no ordinary quest title in this selection. Their absence is not repaired by inventing a title.

## 6. Acquisition and review handoff

When a candidate is found, record the actual source URL and provider, official or third-party status, original title, publication date when known, spoken and subtitle languages, complete duration, acquisition date, filename, exact hash, and source offset.
