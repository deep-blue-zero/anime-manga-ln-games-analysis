# Cartethyia: Evidence and Falsification Matrix

## 1. How this apparatus works

This is the packet's claim and evidence router, not a replacement for the continuous reading. `C01-C50` are local analytical claims; `E01-E60` are evidence bundles. An evidence bundle is a reversible route to material already read, not a new source or an assertion that every included sentence supports every associated claim.

**Epistemic kind** and **revision transition** are separate. A source fact can correct a prior inference. A strong inference can be preserved without becoming a source fact. Confidence concerns the stated scope, not the whole character. `OPEN` means the required observation has not occurred; it is not a low numeric score masquerading as a result. The separate prior-generation ledger owns comparisons with V0.2.

The primary textual witness is Chinese. The official Japanese, Korean, and English witnesses were compared selectively where wording affects an interpretation. Speaker identity, recipient, fictional chronology, player knowledge, and conditional branch all remain relevant. The basis of a claim may be a self-report, another person's judgment, an in-world text, or the staging described by a script; these do not silently become omniscient statements.

Falsification means changing a claim when adverse evidence is material, not requiring every new source to contradict it. A recurrent tendency can survive a scoped exception; an exception that changes the causal explanation can require revision even if the observed action remains the same. The two clearest examples here are the name-choice correction and the explicit information-security reason for the later operation.

## 2. Claims

### C01 - One lore entity, several noninterchangeable states

**Epistemic kind:** Source fact / modeling constraint. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E01, E16-E20, E60.

Cartethyia and Fleurdelys belong to one source-resolved lore identity. Childhood, historical office, amnesiac crisis, integrated life, and later operations still require separate state and knowledge labels.

**Counterevidence / limit:** The divided crisis is real within the fiction; saying one entity must not flatten its opposed wishes or make every form contemporaneously available.

**Falsification or escalation target:** A later explicit identity severance, independently continuous duplicate, or incompatible memory account would require revising the entity model.

### C02 - Cartethyia supplies her own name

**Epistemic kind:** Source fact; correction of a prior mechanism. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E03, E07, E20.

The name precedes the holy Fleurdelys name in the archive. She introduces herself as Cartethyia; Carlotta acknowledges it. Later she asks Rover to continue using that name.

**Counterevidence / limit:** Rover remains a crucial recognizing and rescuing witness. Rejecting name-bestowal does not reject relationally supported identity.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Recheck the original introduction and the four-language name-choice line whenever a summary says Rover named her.

### C03 - The knight is not merely an imposed adult mask

**Epistemic kind:** Longitudinal inference. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E02-E04, E21, E47.

Knight play, adventurous delight, and protective aspiration precede institutional office. Errantry later reclaims rather than invents this disposition.

**Counterevidence / limit:** An earlier aspiration can be intensified or disciplined by an institution. Childhood continuity does not prove that every later obligation was freely chosen.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Find pre-office conduct opposed to protection, or later statements that explicitly reject rather than reclaim the knight ideal.

### C04 - Ordinary pleasure is an end, not just recuperation

**Epistemic kind:** Strong inference. **Confidence:** High within represented scenes. **Evidence:** E02, E06, E23, E28-E31, E44-E49.

Food, dance, music, travel, learning, and company are pursued for their own particular pleasures. They specify the life protection makes possible.

**Counterevidence / limit:** Some leisure still rehearses competence or carries memory. Do not convert intrinsic pleasure into a claim that an activity has only one function.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Seek a later refusal of harmless pleasure solely because it lacks usefulness; compare ordinary scenes without Rover or immediate danger.

### C05 - Institutional formation can produce genuine care

**Epistemic kind:** Longitudinal inference. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E03-E04, E11, E39-E41.

Discipline and public role-shaping coexist with real attention to vulnerable people. The falsity of divine authorization does not make her compassion counterfeit.

**Counterevidence / limit:** The same training encourages self-instrumentalization and public composure. Genuine care does not legitimate the institution that exploited it.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Separate the recipients of service from institutional narrators; examine occasions when role loyalty defeats concrete care.

### C06 - Truth-seeking is strongest when her own continuity is at stake

**Epistemic kind:** Bounded behavioral inference. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E09, E18, E22, E34-E35, E48.

She prefers knowing her history, even if culpable, to an unknowable self. This is not a universal rule of immediate disclosure to everyone.

**Counterevidence / limit:** She hesitates with Cantarella, worries about Phoebe, and participates in deliberate operational concealment.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Test a self-implicating truth she could avoid without immediate danger; distinguish shame, protection, and information security.

### C07 - Origin alone does not establish moral culpability

**Epistemic kind:** Interpretive and ethical distinction. **Confidence:** High as a reading constraint. **Evidence:** E11-E19.

The sources distinguish what she was made to do, what she did, what she believes herself guilty of, and how other people judge her.

**Counterevidence / limit:** She can owe repair without having freely authored every originating harm. This distinction does not make all responsibility disappear.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Look for a freely chosen harmful act previously attributed only to origin, or recipient testimony that contests her account.

### C08 - Integration preserves difficult continuity

**Epistemic kind:** Source fact / strong inference. **Confidence:** High. **Evidence:** E16-E20.

**Falsification or escalation target:** Later deliberate memory editing, repudiation of the former self, or a source correction to recovered memories would be material.

## 4. Cross-source adjudications with unusually high leverage

**Name versus recognition.** The earlier formulation gave Rover too much causal ownership by describing recognition as name-bestowal. The archive sequence and self-introduction settle the narrower fact. The relational thesis survives because accepting a self-presented name and refusing the person's annihilation are already consequential acts. There is no need to embellish them with an invented naming scene. C02 therefore changes the mechanism rather than deleting the relationship.

**Secrecy versus avoidance.** The delayed Cantarella call supports a shame/avoidance reading. The anchor plan supplies an enemy-memory constraint and a requirement that apparent death be believed. Combining the two under one undifferentiated trait would lose precisely the contextual discrimination the reconstruction is meant to achieve. C16 and C26 belong together as an adverse pair: when evidence identifies the mechanism, psychology must not replace it; when no operational necessity is shown, a tactical explanation must not excuse avoidance automatically.

**Origin versus responsible continuity.** Hostile or institutional claims about a vessel can identify an intended function without explaining the entire lived person. Conversely, a sympathetic origin story cannot immunize later choices. C07-C09 require attention to actual alternatives, causal knowledge, and recipients rather than either inherited guilt or blanket innocence.

**Language versus performance.** The personal request for gentleness is not identical with the English general maxim; office aptitude is not identical with impatience; a temporal comparison in the post-integration exchange is inconsistent across witnesses. Those differences must be handled before any listener impression is attributed to vocal acting. C40 remains open, while C41 can already report textual differences.

## 5. Independence and non-duplication

The scene ledger preserves technical and contextual projections, not a set of independent experiments. The gentleness sequence at 7835/4 and its duplicate at 12042/7 must not be counted twice as proof of recurrence. The later wandering families at 14569/5 and 15099/5 likewise require projection awareness. Alternate drink responses cannot be concatenated into one conversation. Summary retellings and public plays can illuminate framing, but do not create additional instances of the conduct they retell.

The same discipline applies to media. Existing AV anchors I and J overlap in the source playthrough. Audio objects can recur under different runtime associations and, in some cases, across language memberships. A claim may have several useful routes while still resting on one underlying event.

## 6. Revision procedure

For later evidence, record the affected C-code, the new exact source/witness route, modality and state, and one of `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `QUALIFY`, `SCOPE_LIMIT`, `REVISE`, `REJECT`, or `OPEN`. Add the reason and retain the prior wording. These are analytical transitions, not authority promotions. No result in this document updates the project-wide character registry or changes a current Git pointer.

An AV scene can strengthen an embodied observation without strengthening a universal trait. A listener can qualify a Japanese performance tendency without changing the Chinese-primary semantic claim. A newly recovered source can change chronology while preserving motivation. The most useful revision is often the one that identifies exactly which level changed.
