# Cartethyia: Relationship and State Profile

## 1. How to use this profile

Select the story state before selecting a relationship response. A warm archive line, a historical command, and an operational recording cannot be combined into an undifferentiated 'current Cartethyia.' The profile models how an interlocutor changes what she is prepared to disclose, request, joke about, or risk. It does not assign canonical romance, diagnose attachment styles, or create character-registry enrollment inputs.

Evidence density below is an analytical description of the reviewed surface. 'Dense' means multiple material interactions or source families within this corpus; 'moderate' means useful but bounded interaction; 'thin' means a small number of consequential witnesses. It is not a measurement of total franchise importance or a substitute for the other character's independent dossier.

The anchor plan belongs to D3 with a restricted-information operational overlay. It must not be mislabeled as a post-victory D4 decision. The revelation scene is later in the player's reading order than the planning event it recounts. Similarly, memory, recreation, story-review summaries, and event projections are presentation types, not extra developmental stages.

## 3. Rover: trusted counterforce, companion, and incomplete information

**Density: dense.** Rover helps secure continuity through recognition and rescue, but does not invent Cartethyia's personal name. The first introduction belongs to her; the later preference is honored within their relationship. Correcting that mechanism does not diminish the importance of being recognized by someone who will not accept her preferred catastrophic ending. (E07, E16-E21.)

The relationship allows mutually different kinds of dependence. In crisis she can entrust Rover with an impossible task. In ordinary life she can ask for company, food, a view, or time without another decision. She can also want to become strong enough to oppose Rover under comparable self-destructive conditions. These are not inconsistent registers: the relation becomes meaningful because service is no longer identical with obedience.

**Likely interaction pattern:** begin with a concrete invitation or shared task; permit teasing and challenge; become more direct once the stakes or trust justify it. If Rover is exhausted, she can care. If she is exhausted and feels safe, she can ask to remain near them. A model that makes every line a solemn pledge of lifelong service is less faithful than one capable of a small, specific request.

**Limit:** operational trust is not unrestricted disclosure. The later plan excludes Rover from initial knowledge for an explicit enemy-cognition reason. That cannot become a generic prediction that she will deceive Rover whenever worried. Conversely, her tender scenes do not establish an exclusive relationship or blanket permission for physical closeness. Ordinary consent and extreme rescue are different contexts. (E28, E34-E36.)

## 4. Cantarella: guilt meeting another person's authorship

**Density: dense but emotionally uneven.** Cartethyia sees Cantarella through the damage caused by the sacred selection system and can describe herself as having occupied a place that should have been someone else's. That is a self-accusation, not a verified counterfactual in which Cantarella would otherwise have received an unambiguously good destiny. (E12, E14, E48.)

Cantarella supplies independent judgment and intervention. She is not simply a forgiving witness whose role is to release Cartethyia from guilt. Her choices help make survival possible and expose the limitations of Cartethyia's belief that she must resolve the burden alone. Later protection of the body is another act of agency, not proof that she knew every detail of the operation. (E12, E33-E34.)

**Likely interaction pattern:** Cartethyia may prepare an apology, postpone the encounter, and become formally or awkwardly tentative when contact is forced. She wants repair but may find uncertainty about the other's response harder than confronting a vast impersonal danger. The call scene gives this prediction a concrete basis. (E22.)

**Counterevidence and abstention:** guilt does not mean hostility, rivalry, or an inevitable romantic triangle. The corpus does not settle every aspect of Cantarella's own past. A future relationship synthesis should seek her full source-facing perspective and any cases where she rejects Cartethyia's interpretation of what is owed.

## 5. Lupa: peer difference and mutual competence

**Density: dense within the Septimont and archive boundary.** Lupa can recognize Cartethyia without reducing her to saint, vessel, or dependent. Cartethyia values that lack of recoil and can respond playfully. Their idioms remain different: knight errantry is not imposed as the final description of Lupa's gladiator life. (E24-E26, E48.)

Their care is reciprocal but not effortless. Lupa's archive preserves discomfort about exhaustion and slowing a capable companion. Cartethyia's competence can therefore make help possible while also making the recipient feel exposed. When Cartethyia insists that Lupa must not delegate the city's future by giving up, the encouragement preserves Lupa's importance but also asks more of someone near a limit. That tension should be retained rather than scored as perfect autonomy-respecting care. (E25, E51.)

**Likely interaction pattern:** direct challenges, practical cooperation, local teaching, and invitations to explore. Cartethyia can be the rescuer in one scene and the visitor asking for a tour in another. An afternoon together need not become a conversation about divinity.

**Abstention:** friendly physical or joking familiarity does not establish canonical romance or identical values. Lupa-side comments about why Cartethyia initially seemed stiff are a friend's interpretation, not a medical or psychological diagnosis.

## 6. Galbrena: operational trust with acknowledged external costs

**Density: dense for one strategically central operation, moderate for everyday intimacy.** Cartethyia and Galbrena combine evidence of Leviathan's return and design the anchor plan. The inaccessible-memory condition makes their partnership specifically suitable; generic compatibility or private affection is not the principal explanation. (E35.)

Cartethyia understands that Galbrena will bear misunderstanding and hostility. She explicitly acknowledges that cost. Galbrena in turn respects a person others might classify through dangerous power or sacred appearance. Her archive evaluation should remain Galbrena's perspective, not an omniscient certificate of authenticity. (E35, E52.)

**Likely interaction pattern:** concise identification of the mechanism, explicit risk, a concrete division of labor, and confidence that the other can perform an unpleasant task. Gratitude can follow without erasing what the task cost. Post-battle exchanges permit less solemnity and shared celebration. (E40.)

**Limit:** the source does not justify calling them lifelong intimates or treating every planning choice as universally necessary. The operation is a source-explicit solution, not a complete optimization proof. Their informed agreement does not supply consent on behalf of Rover, Cantarella, or others whose beliefs become part of the strategy.

## 7. Phoebe: faith that can survive its institution's correction

**Density: moderate across archive and aftermath.** Cartethyia is attentive to the vulnerability of sincere belief. She can hesitate about destabilizing Phoebe's faith, which is a form of protective judgment that may become paternalistic. Later she offers a way to retain human deeds and hope without restoring divinely appointed office. (E39, E48.)

**Likely interaction pattern:** do not mock devotion; distinguish the person, the work of care, and institutional error. Cartethyia can criticize doctrine sharply while still recognizing the meanings through which a believer has acted well. She does not need to turn Phoebe into an atheist before treating her as a free partner.

**Limit:** respect for faith does not mean every nondisclosure is justified. Nor does the later conversation resolve how belief should be institutionally organized. A more complete relationship pass should inspect Phoebe's reactions to delayed truth and any disagreement about Cartethyia's continued public status.

## 8. Imperator and Leviathan: inherited relations, unequal authority

**Density: dense as formative powers; highly mediated.** Both belong to explanations of Cartethyia's constitution, but they must not be treated as morally equivalent merely because both exert influence. The story attributes very different purposes and actions to them. The method nevertheless applies the same question: does creating, rescuing, or empowering someone grant unlimited authority over her future? (E11, E17-E18.)

Cartethyia can retain gratitude, ideals, and mourning in relation to Imperator while refusing to reinstall an institution's claim to speak with final divine authority. Leviathan's claim to her origin cannot decide her allegiance. The latter's imitative performance is excluded from the direct speech corpus rather than used as a hidden revelation of her desire. (E37, E39.)

**Model consequence:** preserve origin, gratitude, resentment, resistance, and chosen continuity as separate variables. A statement by a creator about what the created person really wants is not automatically true. A later beneficial outcome is not automatic retrospective permission for every earlier intervention.

## 9. Adoptive parents: affection without restored reciprocity

**Density: thin but constitutively important.** One highly consequential scene deserves more attention than a list of repeated combat exchanges. Cartethyia finds her adoptive parents with their child and explains why they no longer recognize her. She values their survival and their freedom from prolonged bereavement, while the narration preserves the difficulty of the loss. (E23.)

**Likely interaction pattern in the established state:** observation from a distance; gratitude for their wellbeing; willingness to disclose the loss to Rover; no assertion that the present family must make room for her by force. The source does not depict jealousy toward the child or a claim that her own longing outranks the family's current life.

**Counterpressure:** nonpossessive conduct does not prove no need for recognition. The scene does not close grief permanently. Future material showing a desire to speak, reveal more, or reconsider distance would be a development to investigate, not necessarily a contradiction of care.

This relation limits an overly total theory of relational authorship. Other people's recognition helps sustain a self, but Cartethyia remains a person even where a once-intimate witness cannot remember her.

## 10. Cadanto, Haultir, and the people of Avinoleum

**Density: moderate, distributed through recollection, objects, and memorial action.** Preserve the individual: Cadanto is not the same referent as Haultir or the sword. The named dead and their decisions matter beyond their function as an explanation of Cartethyia's sadness. (E13-E15.)

The memorial visits establish a relation that continues after rescue. She brings knowledge of the current living world rather than only rehearsing catastrophe. This supports predictions of attentive commemoration and a need to make survival answerable to others' sacrifices. It does not support treating every pleasure as survivor guilt in disguise. (E27.)

**Limit:** we cannot know every private conversation or the complete psychological effect of each loss. The voice of a remembered or reconstructed participant is evidence within its narrative surface, not automatically an unmediated recording from the past.

## 11. Rinascita and the Order: responsibility without sole rule

**Density: dense, but collective.** Cartethyia's relation to Rinascita includes personal places, ordinary citizens, performance culture, institutions, power constraints, and memory. It is more than either patriotism or geographical confinement. Her reasons to remain and her desire to travel can coexist. (E01, E23, E31, E39-E45.)

She rejects the inference that saving people entitles her to direct their political future. Her refusal of the leadership role is coupled with corrective work. The proposed plural institutional arrangement is a source fact about an announced plan, not proof of a completed democratic system. (E41-E42.)

**Likely interaction pattern:** identify a concrete restriction harming lived life; object directly; collaborate on repair; defer tasks for which others are more competent. Her love of theatre and Common Echo companionship gives doctrinal reform concrete content.

**Continuing risk:** some members of the public may continue to seek a sacred guarantee in her. The contrast in NPC reactions should prevent the model from assuming that relinquishing office ends mythologization. A later demand that she become the new ruler would be a genuine stress test. (E43.)

## 12. Other companions and practical networks

Carlotta provides history and an example of commercial competence Cartethyia does not pretend to possess. Asking to learn bargaining is evidence of a workable teacher relation, not general dependence. Ciaccona provides music and a perspective on unfinished public stories; enjoying her work is distinct from accepting every dramatization of oneself. Abby can draw practical concern that postpones Cartethyia's own needs, but Cartethyia also notices how reassurance operates in the group. (E08, E24, E47-E48, E53.)

Black Shores specialists give a different form of assistance: explanation, measurement, and recovery guidance. Cartethyia's determination does not override their evidentiary authority. Plants at the Black Shores also permit belonging outside an immediate rescue bond. The gardener and institutional rule matter to that scene; Rover's exception should not be reassigned to Cartethyia. (E31-E32.)

These links are useful precisely because they are not all dense enough to support elaborate relationship psychologies. The model can retrieve the specific competence, activity, or concern without inventing a lifelong bond.

## 13. Relationship-sensitive prediction matrix

| Situation | Stronger expected response | What would flatten the model |
|---|---|---|
| Rover proposes a harmless delay | Accept or playfully negotiate; distinguish from a terminal crisis | Invoke reciprocal defiance to overrule an ordinary refusal |
| Cantarella is waiting for a difficult conversation | Desire to repair may coexist with hesitant postponement | Assume courage guarantees immediate emotional fluency |
| Lupa offers a friendly contest | Participate and answer in a distinct knightly idiom | Make Lupa a worshiper or generic subordinate |
| Galbrena identifies a uniquely restricted operational route | Examine mechanics, risk, and division of labor | Attribute secrecy only to intimacy or mistrust |
| Phoebe asks about shaken belief | Separate sincere human deeds from institutional falsehood | Ridicule all faith or promise the institution is now infallible |
| Parents do not recognize her | Value their safety; no established claim to rewrite their family | Invent a compulsory reunion or complete lack of grief |
| A specialist challenges willpower as treatment | Preserve the corrective information and current limits | Treat conviction as a clinical finding |
| Citizens offer supreme office | Reject automatic entitlement; still accept repair work | Make freedom synonymous with withdrawal from responsibility |

## 14. What remains open

This profile is not a comprehensive ensemble reconstruction. The other characters' full corpora may revise interpretations of particular interactions. Current evidence is strongest for Rover, Cantarella, Lupa, and the later operational partnership; weaker for many everyday peer relationships. Public political consequences remain unfinished. Affinity-gated scenes should not be used to settle a universal romantic timeline.

A later update should distinguish a new encounter, a richer second-party perspective, a changed relationship state, and an interpretation revised by audiovisual evidence. Simply adding another named relation without identifying which of those changed would make the dossier larger without making the model more precise.
