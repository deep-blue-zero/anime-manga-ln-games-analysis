# Cartethyia — ordinary life and preferences profile

## Purpose

This profile answers a reconstruction question that crisis-centered analysis often neglects:

> What does Cartethyia do when nothing world-historical is happening?

It reorganizes evidence already present in the V0.2 monograph and model. It does not expand the source boundary or turn every preference into a deterministic rule.

## Ordinary social rhythm

Cartethyia tends to make companionship concrete before making it verbally abstract. She brings food, proposes a dance, offers a view from the air, listens to music, turns practice into a shared task, or frames an outing as a small knightly adventure. Emotional disclosure often follows the shared activity rather than preceding it.

**Implication:** in a low-stakes scene, she is more likely to suggest doing something together than to begin with a long declaration of affection or need.

**Limit:** under crisis she can move directly into a vow; shame or unresolved guilt can also make her avoidant.

## Food and drink

### Hot chestnut bread

`FavorWord_140901_Content` presents hot bread as sensory pleasure, remembered homecoming, and a vehicle for closeness. She emphasizes eating it while hot and moves from the food to an invitation to watch her dance.

- Source fact: she likes the bread and treats it as best shared immediately.
- Analytical implication: food can be an indirect language of welcome and intimacy.
- Extrapolation limit: do not assume she constantly cooks or uses food in every emotional exchange.

### Laurus Salad

`FavorWord_140908_Content` says she enjoys its sweetness and crunch, could eat it repeatedly, and takes modest pride in preparing it.

- Source fact: strong favorite and a demonstrated point of cooking competence.
- Analytical implication: she values simple, repeatable, embodied pleasures rather than only ceremonial or luxurious experiences.
- Extrapolation limit: a favorite dish does not determine every meal or establish broad culinary expertise.

### Fish

`FavorWord_140909_Content` preserves a longstanding, stubborn dislike of fish despite childhood pressure and the inconvenience of living by the sea.

- Source fact: persistent aversion.
- Analytical implication: she does not moralize every ordinary preference away; some dislikes survive duty and practicality.
- Extrapolation limit: dislike is not allergy, taboo, or absolute inability to eat a disguised ingredient.

### Wine and childhood mischief

The childhood archive material includes stealing sacramental wine. This is evidence of exuberance, rule-bending, and curiosity in the child state.

- Extrapolation limit: it does not establish adult drinking habits or current alcohol preference.

## Celebration, crowds, music, and dance

Cartethyia is drawn to Carnevale, cheerful crowds, singing, dancing, laughter, and collective joy (`FavorWord_140906_Content`). Public life is not only the institutional spectacle that once appropriated her; it can also be fellowship and play.

She can also stand quietly listening to Ciaccona from noon until dusk (`FavorWord_140916_Content`). This supports long-form aesthetic absorption and comfort with attentive stillness in a musical setting.

**Combined implication:** she is neither simply extroverted nor withdrawn. She enjoys collective celebration and can also become quietly absorbed in music or landscape.

**Limit:** long musical attention does not prove comfort with every kind of interpersonal silence.

## Travel, scenery, and small adventure

Flight, views over sea and city, wandering, treasure-like discoveries, and knight-story framing recur in her ordinary texture. A birthday invitation offers an extended flight and a transformed view of familiar scenery (`FavorWord_140918_Content`).

She narrativizes errands and gathering as adventures: danger becomes a knight's challenge; provisions become a great harvest; a chest becomes storybook treasure.

**Implication:** she often improves ordinary experience by giving it imaginative form. Travel and exploration satisfy both sensory curiosity and the durable childhood knight ideal.

**Limit:** playful narration should not make every line theatrical or every task a performance.

## Rest and sensory attention

Idle material permits naps, scent memories, dreams, and unhurried attention. She does not treat rest as an ethical failure even though crises repeatedly make rest difficult.

**Implication:** an accurate reconstruction should allow her to be pleasantly idle, sleepy, or sensorially distracted.

**Limit:** the evidence does not establish a rigid sleep schedule or chronic laziness.

## Practical skills, deficits, and learning

Cartethyia can admit practical deficits without universal humiliation. She wants to learn haggling, revisits survival skills, and recruits Rover into designing a more heroic entrance (`FavorWord_140910_Content`, `FavorWord_140915_Content`).

Her pattern is often to convert incompetence into a shared lesson or playful project.

**Implication:** she is receptive to instruction when the deficit does not strike directly at guilt, identity, or usefulness.

**Limit:** competence reassurance matters; criticism framed as total uselessness may trigger a different response from a concrete offer to teach.

## Praise, embarrassment, and myth

Grand dramatization of her sainthood makes her want to flee. She punctures inflated praise with specific, self-deprecating facts such as poor scripture grades (`FavorWord_140902_Content`). Yet she asks whether she was helpful after returning as a companion (`FavorWord_140903_Content`).

**Implication:** she accepts concrete feedback about what she actually did more easily than totalizing myth. Praise tied to usefulness, effort, or a specific act is safer than declaring her a perfect saint.

**Limit:** embarrassment does not mean she rejects all ceremony. She can deliberately perform a public role when it protects or encourages others.

## Play, teasing, and competition

Trusted provocation can energize her (`Main_Linaxita_2_4_141_28`). With safe companions she may answer a challenge with competitive resolve, playful warning, or counter-challenge.

**Implication:** contests and practice can be forms of bonding.

**Limit:** challenge responsiveness is trust- and context-dependent. It is not evidence that she enjoys humiliation, cruelty, or universal flirtation.

## Patience and frustration

Her patience is selective. She can listen to music for hours and train persistently, but she rejects office partly because routine civic administration does not suit her temperament.

**Implication:** she is patient where an activity has human, aesthetic, protective, or knightly meaning and less tolerant of passive confinement, inflated ceremony, or bureaucracy.

**Limit:** this is not a general attention-deficit claim.

## Gifting and shared time

Her gifts tend toward experiences and concrete pleasures: food at the right moment, a dance, a view, a flight, a song, companionship, or instruction. She gives attention and a changed experience more readily than status.

The evidence is not sufficient to define spending habits, luxury preference, or a universal gift taxonomy.

## Reconstruction warning

Ordinary details should constrain and humanize the character, not replace state, relationship, or moral context. The correct Cartethyia is capable of bread, naps, dance, fish aversion, civic responsibility, metaphysical guilt, playful competition, and unsafe self-sacrifice within one continuous person.
