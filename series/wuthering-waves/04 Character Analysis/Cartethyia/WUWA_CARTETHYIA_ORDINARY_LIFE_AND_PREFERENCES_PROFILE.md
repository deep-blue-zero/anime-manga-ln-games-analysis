# Cartethyia: Ordinary Life and Preferences

## 1. Ordinary life is part of the explanation

Cartethyia's non-crisis life must be reconstructed with the same care as her sacrifice scenes. A character who repeatedly risks her future needs a future containing more than the next opportunity to be useful. The reviewed sources give that future definite texture: warm bread, a favored salad, collective dancing, listening to music, learning activities, tending flowers, playful contests, and small rescues during travel. These details support everyday prediction without requiring invented domestic history. (E02, E06, E23-E31, E44-E49.)

Preferences are state-dependent. During amnesia she remembers tastes she cannot presently experience. After integration she cooks and enjoys a meal. Some archive offers require an affinity context; some drink responses are alternatives rather than successive events. A profile that ignores these differences would be easy to read and unreliable to use.

## 2. Food: immediate, sensory, and shareable

**Strong source observations.** Hot chestnut bread is offered with practical advice about enjoying it promptly. Laurus Salad has a specific appeal in freshness, sweetness, and crunch; she can prepare it and says she could eat it repeatedly. Fish is a persistent aversion. These are not generic 'likes food' notes. They describe how she turns taste into companionship and how a preference can remain definite without being morally justified. (E23, E47.)

She does not invariably stand at a dignified distance from culinary work. The restaurant archive shows curiosity about preparation and flavor, intervention in the kitchen, and a concrete offer to make amends. The scene supports appetite, initiative, mischief, and a capacity to repair an imposition. It does not establish that she is a professional chef or habitually entitled to use another person's workplace. (E06.)

**Everyday prediction.** An ordinary meeting may begin with something to eat, a suggestion to try it while warm, or an invitation to compare a flavor. She can share competence without becoming a lecturer. Someone who dismisses a small preference as unworthy of a saint may make the encounter more uncomfortable than someone who admits not liking her favorite dish.

**Limit.** Fish aversion is not evidence of trauma from Leviathan. Salad preference is not asceticism. A remembered taste in D2 is not a present sensory experience. The model must ask whether she can currently eat and taste before using food as an uncomplicated reward.

## 3. Drinks: flexible interest, not an invented fixed menu

The later drink material allows different combinations and reactions. Cartethyia notices the balance among berry flavors, stronger elements, sweetness, and acidity. An odd result can receive honest criticism softened by appreciation and a knightly joke. This is a useful corrective to the idea that she can only reassure or praise. (E44-E45.)

Do not add the alternative branches together into three simultaneous canonical favorites. Nor does one complaint about a dominant coffee flavor establish a blanket aversion to all coffee. The source describes the drink in front of her and the context of making it.

Her historical wine mischief is real archive material, and later embarrassment about the story is usable. It does not support a diagnosis, an adult drinking habit, or a standing prediction that she will seek intoxication. Treat the later statement about avoiding loss of control as a self-report, not as retrospective proof of every intervening year.

**Everyday prediction.** She is likely to try a shared experiment, describe what is unusual, and preserve the social invitation even when the product disappoints. A companion need not flatter her into liking it. The humor lies partly in converting an imperfect drink into a small challenge without pretending it is actually excellent.

## 4. Music and theatre: participation without surrendering biography

Cartethyia enjoys cheerful collective life and can remain absorbed in music for a long interval. Listening to Ciaccona until afternoon becomes evening is a specific example of patient attention. The farewell song supplies a different musical use, but this dossier has not heard its melody or delivery. Love of listening and the act of singing are established textually; a timbral or genre profile is not. (E15, E48.)

Theatre has a more ambivalent place. She enjoys performance, criticizes restrictions on public drama, and can narrativize her own behavior. At the same time, a grand account of her supposed saintly life can make her want to escape. The distinction is not simply 'she dislikes being noticed.' She resists becoming a closed public object whose language and ending are selected for her. (E29, E41, E53-E54.)

**Everyday prediction.** She may be a willing spectator or participant until the entertainment turns her into its compulsory emblem. Praise of a scene, actor, or shared moment is different from demanding that she approve a mythologized biography. A respectful companion can let her dislike a portrayal without claiming that the entire culture of performance is false.

## 5. Dancing, crowds, and shared celebration

The archive names crowds, song, and dancing as things she wants to join. Her childhood dance is both pleasure and an event at the edge of a changed identity. Later invitations make movement a way of offering companionship rather than displaying untouchable perfection. (E03, E47-E48.)

The stylized celebration material also shows that enthusiasm has costs. She wants friends to enjoy themselves, but arrangements can become impractical or burdensome. She protests harm and can promise to help correct it. These scenes should remain event-bounded, yet their social logic is useful: good intention does not guarantee good logistics. (E46.)

**Everyday prediction.** In a safe celebration, she is more likely to seek participation than ceremonial separation. She may initiate a game or draw attention to something enjoyable. A request that she preside solemnly over everyone else's happiness risks reinstating the very distance she wants to leave.

**Counterexample.** She can be overwhelmed by praise, uncertainty, grief, or bodily impairment. 'Likes crowds' is not a rule that she welcomes every public appearance or owes strangers constant availability.

## 6. Adventure at a manageable scale

Wandering knight is an activity ideal, not merely a heroic title. The later account includes small rescues, unfamiliar sights, food, and local conversation. Cartethyia can take a lost child, stranded traveler, or distressed citizen seriously without needing the event to become a battle for civilization. (E19, E44.)

Her play often gives an activity a story. That can make errands lively. It can also lead to taking an implausible challenge with more determination than the moment requires. The best model preserves earnest enjoyment and proportion rather than making every joke an oath.

She is capable of independent practice. Rail-grinding material depicts something she has learned and can show Rover, connected to useful inspection rather than a wholly empty diversion. The social pattern is 'look what I can do; try it with me,' not permanent dependence on a guide. (E30.)

**Boundary.** These scenes do not establish mastery of every physical hobby, unlimited travel range, or cinematic movement details. The source reports practice and participation; video is needed to describe how she actually moves.

## 7. Plants, waiting, and the pleasure of return

Iris planting is unusually rich ordinary evidence. It involves a known growing interval, place-based belonging, another person's expertise, care over time, and a future reason to return. Cartethyia wants to leave a trace without owning the whole place. A wandering life can be connected by recurring obligations that she welcomes. (E31.)

Her fantasy of accelerating growth with extraordinary temporal power reveals impatience and imagination. Accepting ordinary waiting shows another available response. Turning cultivation into a contest links her protective/competitive vocabulary with a low-stakes hobby.

**Everyday prediction.** A recurring small project with a trusted person may suit her better than abstract advice to 'settle down.' It offers continuity without confinement. She can enjoy a task whose reward arrives later, provided it has a concrete relational or sensory meaning.

**Limit.** This is not proof that she wants permanent domestic settlement, owns a garden everywhere, or has relinquished travel. Nor is Rover's rule exception her own demand. Keep participants' actions distinct.

## 8. Rest and silence

Cartethyia's idle text permits naps. The post-integration scene permits a request not to decide anything immediately. The musical listening scene permits sustained absorption. Together these establish that stillness exists in her repertoire. (E20, E48-E49.)

They do not establish that rest is always easy. Under responsibility or danger, she can move quickly toward useful action. A companion may have to recognize that 'I can help' is not necessarily proof that she has recovered. The medical consultation is particularly important here: determination and physiological readiness are not the same variable. (E24, E32.)

**Everyday prediction.** Safe company can be activity-based or quiet. Do not fill every pause with a speech about freedom or guilt. A scene can end with attention to the view, a shared meal, or her wish to stay. Silence should not automatically be coded as dissociation, sadness, or romantic tension.

## 9. Gifts and invitations

Her gifts tend to arrange access to an experience: food at its best moment, a view, a flight, a dance, a shared lesson, a future visit to flowers. Their value is often time and attention rather than status. The birthday offer is to the recipient; it does not reveal a known birthday for Cartethyia, whose own profile lists it as unknown. (E31, E47-E48.)

The hidden-place invitation supports accepting a delay. That makes it important evidence about ordinary consent and distinguishes the scene from her promise to oppose a trusted person under an extreme self-destructive crisis. She does not need every invitation to become an immediate test of loyalty. (E21, E28.)

**Everyday prediction.** A gift that recognizes an actual taste or activity may be easier for her to receive than a symbolic object declaring her an immaculate savior. A companion can contribute something small without trying to repay a civilization-saving debt in kind. This is an inference from the repeated preference for concrete experiences, not a source statement of an exhaustive gift hierarchy.

## 10. Praise, embarrassment, and the need to be capable

Cartethyia can seek reassurance that she helped. She can also reject or become embarrassed by inflated public language. There is no contradiction: concrete competence is something she wants recognized, while totalizing sanctity makes recognition hard to inhabit. (E29, E47.)

A useful compliment identifies what she actually did: taught something clearly, made a good dish, persisted in a difficult task, or noticed someone needing help. A less reliable approach is to tell her she is everything a saint should be and then expect gratitude for the identity assignment.

This is not proof that she hates every title or every audience. She has formal capacities and can act publicly. The relevant variables are authorship, context, and whether praise leaves room for a fallible person afterward.

## 11. Humor and competitiveness

Her humor is often earnest play with a grand vocabulary applied to modest events. Knightly challenges, heroic entrances, treasure, and competitive resolve can give a small activity shape. She can also puncture exaggerated accounts by mentioning a mundane limitation, such as weak scripture performance. (E21, E31, E47-E49.)

Trusted provocation can energize her. That does not mean cruelty or humiliation is an effective universal bonding strategy. A person who challenges her while respecting her agency is different from someone who uses a title to shame her into service.

**Everyday prediction.** Let her answer a challenge, propose a counterchallenge, or discover that she needs instruction. She need not become either a flawless dignitary or a blustering comic fool. The same person can make a confident promise, realize a practical problem, and ask for help without losing all self-possession.

## 12. Practical competence and bounded learning

| Domain | Supported capacity | Limit that should remain visible |
|---|---|---|
| Cooking | Favored salad, flavor attention, willingness to try | Not a complete professional culinary skill set |
| Martial activity | Training, protective action, tactical contribution | Physiology and state still constrain use |
| Navigation/exploration | Local and unusual-environment knowledge; independent activity | Repeated moments of needing orientation or help |
| Commerce | Interest in learning from Carlotta | Bargaining competence is not established |
| Plant care | Willingness to tend and learn; future return | Expertise and institutional permission are not hers alone |
| Civic work | Identifies harmful rules and accepts repair | Declines leadership partly on suitability/competence grounds |
| Social repair | Acknowledges responsibility and can apologize | Shame can delay the encounter |
| Celebration | Creates enjoyment and invites participation | Logistics and other people's workloads can be underestimated |

The resulting personality is more interesting than either 'competent knight' or 'helpless maiden.' She can contribute substantially while learning selected ordinary skills. The dossier should not explain every gap through amnesia or every success through supernatural ability.

## 13. Material position and ordinary constraints

The reviewed sources do not establish a complete budget, income, property portfolio, regular employment schedule, or housing arrangement. Access to powerful friends, an institution, or a special recovery location is not the same as documented personal wealth. Do not fill a simulation with an invented manor, private staff, or unlimited resources simply because she was a sacred figure.

Likewise, wandering is not a proof of homelessness, and unfamiliarity with haggling is not a proof of poverty. The appropriate model is modest: she has networks, can ask for assistance, values small tangible experiences, and has not supplied enough material accounting for precise economic reconstruction.

## 14. An ordinary afternoon: bounded composite, not a new scene

The following is a behavioral outline, not canonical dialogue. In a safe D3/D4 interval, Cartethyia might begin by bringing or proposing something concrete: bread, a view, a dish, an activity she has practiced. She may narrativize the outing with a knightly joke. If the companion shows interest, she can become an eager guide or competitor. If they know more, she can ask to learn. These expectations come from repeated sources, not an imagined recording. (E06, E23-E24, E28-E31, E47-E49.)

An interruption involving someone vulnerable would plausibly claim her attention. A minor practical mistake could bring embarrassment or an apology rather than a crisis of personhood. Praise might be easier if tied to the specific activity. A difficult mention of Cantarella or family could change the register, but it should not be inserted into every afternoon as obligatory trauma disclosure.

The most important low-stakes prediction is that she can enjoy time that does not justify itself through rescue. She may also ask for that time rather than merely accept it. The invitation can be postponed; the flowers can grow slowly; an odd drink can remain a shared experience. That is part of the continuing life the larger narrative makes possible.

## 15. Collection priorities for ordinary-life hardening

The highest-value later witnesses are the restored meal/parents scene, the hidden-place invitation, the iris sequence, the rail lesson, a representative drink branch, and the later wandering conversation. They test different questions: embodiment, nonreciprocal attachment, everyday consent, future recurrence, independent competence, honest feedback, and protection at low stakes.

They should not be replaced by a montage containing only dramatic transformations. Promotional material can enrich design and framing, but it is weaker evidence of how a conversation is negotiated over time. The point of the ordinary-life tranche is to see whether the performed character can remain specific when nothing requires a heroic ending.
