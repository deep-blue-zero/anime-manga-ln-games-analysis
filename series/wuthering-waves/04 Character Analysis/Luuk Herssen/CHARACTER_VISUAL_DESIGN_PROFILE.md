## Individualized summary

Luuk Herssen has a polished clinical-research silhouette: long tied blond hair, red eyes, a white split-tail coat, teal and black technical layers, gloves, instrument canisters, an ID badge, and molecular/DNA-like ornaments. Small gold vein-like markings introduce controlled bodily strangeness beneath the professional surface.
