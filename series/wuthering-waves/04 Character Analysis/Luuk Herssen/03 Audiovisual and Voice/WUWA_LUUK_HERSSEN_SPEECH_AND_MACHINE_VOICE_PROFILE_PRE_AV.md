# Luuk Herssen — written registers and measured audio, pre-listening

**No performed-voice or visual acting assessment has been made for this draft.** A decoded FLAC, a duration or a median F0 can establish a technical sound object and signal summary; it does not establish intent, empathy, threat, seduction, pace of a particular clause or a voice actor's characterization. Source-time and narrative-medium partitions come before a “voice style” label. Exact text/scene locators live in the [matrix](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_LUUK_HERSSEN_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md); twenty four-dub joins are metadata-only in [the case file](AUDIO_MATCHED_SEMANTIC_CASES.json).

Written diction should not be collapsed across English and Chinese. The source Chinese line in `FavorStory_151004_Content` says the *great majority* of other recipients shared Rhein's fate, while English intensifies it to “without exception.” The model should retain the Chinese scope. A localization witness may alter register or punctuation without proving a different event or actor emotion.

Three more text checks affect downstream characterization. `FavorWord_151008_Content` presents the exam-stress candy as impossible to treat as an overnight solution and suggests a counseling conversation; Japanese makes Luuk's comparative judgment slightly firmer than the more questioning Chinese/English/Korean phrasing. `FavorWord_151018_Content` is an affectionate birthday commitment, but Japanese explicitly calls him **one of** Rover's close companions, so an English singular “your companion” cannot establish exclusivity across witnesses. Finally, `FavorWord_151006_Content` says he has moved on from the past in a joking turn, while `FavorWord_151010_Content` voices a lifelong anti-Fractsidus wish. That is a tension across his own archive statements, not evidence that any one dub's actor delivered a secretly insincere line [LUK-E25, E28; C23, C26].

The `flow#/15461/5/6` waffle response is a graph distinction before it is a prosody question: a player option jumps to an approving or critical Luuk route. Listening to both FLACs would not prove both happened in a single playthrough. The `flow#/15914/1/10` cat message is text-medium evidence that a waffle lure must not be eaten, not necessarily a spoken feeding scene. Human review should retain those route and medium labels when comparing delivery [LUK-E26–E27; C24–C25].

Those values are **descriptive of this analyzer and selected objects**, not an actor comparison or ranking of emotional intensity. In particular KO/ZH have far fewer qualified F0 objects under the same estimator and must not be treated as equivalent pitch samples. Durations include different linguistic phrasing, silence and possibly multi-channel content; do not infer that JA “speaks slower” from the larger median. Machine flags include 1,227 `pitch_edge_band_frequent`, 67 `multichannel_not_isolated_speaker`, 28 `pitch_parameter_sensitive`, 23 `long_object_check_subtitle_extent`, 18 `pitch_fewer_than_10_voiced_frames`, and two `full_scale_samples_not_proof_of_audible_clipping`. Flags are audit prompts, not interpreted defects. One object can have several flags. The [dedicated audio QC audit](../04%20Validation%20and%20Readiness/WUWA_LUUK_HERSSEN_AUDIO_QC_AND_COMPARABILITY_AUDIT.md) breaks out the language-dependent qualification disparity and the resulting performance-observation gate.

## Exact-action contrasts, not an acted transformation

The [eleven-cohort audit](../04%20Validation%20and%20Readiness/WUWA_LUUK_HERSSEN_SOURCE_DEFINED_AUDIO_COHORT_AUDIT.md) joins 127 distinct selected semantic lines to 512 distinct measured render/PCM objects. It partitions the `12313/3` conversation into Sigrika-facing conjectural care and Rover-facing pacing/disclosure; separates `12331/4` Architect challenge from `12332/3` post-confrontation inference; and treats the approving and critical `15461/5` waffle branches as alternative three-line runs. Two Japanese clinic occurrences and two English Rover/disclosure occurrences each have an extra PCM variant, so a “one text key, one sound per dub” analysis would erase material runtime choices. The eleven cohorts are 17.7% of the selected 719 lines, not full performance coverage [LUK-E10, E12, E14, E16, E32].

This matched-source check strengthens the *comparability warning* rather than an emotion claim. At the −45 dBFS active-frame gate, clinic versus Architect-challenge median levels are ZH −24.03/−23.56, EN −23.53/−23.44, JA −24.41/−24.85 and KO −24.00/−23.34 dBFS. The direction is not universal, and the action sets differ in text, scene mix and length. The conservative F0 gate accepts just 1/12 ZH and 0/12 KO clinic objects; after-shock care qualifies 0/6 in both. No cross-dub vocal-maturity ranking or doctor-to-opponent pitch arc should be derived from such selected residuals. Source wording still distinguishes diagnostic uncertainty from forensic challenge; machine nonconfirmation does not flatten those speech acts [LUK-E10, E14, E32, C11, C19, C31].

The praise/critique waffle route is a second negative control. Its −45 dBFS active-level median changes are modest in ZH, EN and KO but reverse direction in JA, with only three lines per branch per dub. One may later hear approval and disappointment differently; these descriptive numbers do not decide it. More importantly, the raw graph jumps from the player choice to Luuk's TalkIds 7 or 10, then rejoins at 13. The source fixes the alternative structure before a listener compares exact renders. Finally, `15474/1` and `15914/1` retain twenty accepted message turns but no selected playable Luuk voice; their invitation and cat-safety content cannot be treated as acoustic examples. This does not exclude all possible future client assets [LUK-E24, E26–E27, E32–E33, C24–C25, C32].

## What listening must answer

Listen to exact four-language renders of (1) Ichor/color self-description, (2) early doctor uncertainty, (3) revenge disclosure, (4) Architect challenge, (5) candy as anti-numbness, (6) mutually exclusive waffle praise/criticism as two separate three-line branch runs, (7) grave/support reflection and (8) birthday companionship. For each case record phrase-aligned text, heard voice owner, emotional contour, pause/turn boundary, accent/timbre only if confidently perceived, background/music bleed and discrepancies. A case with long or multichannel flags receives extra context playback. If a render is an event variant rather than the expected line, keep both IDs and mark unresolved rather than fitting the sound to the text. A twenty-case listen would be an initial calibration, not completion of all 719 selected lines.
