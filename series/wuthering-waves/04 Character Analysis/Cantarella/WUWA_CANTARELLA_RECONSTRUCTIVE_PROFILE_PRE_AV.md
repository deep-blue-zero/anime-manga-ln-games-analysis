## Care that preserves agency

When another person is exhausted, Cantarella may plausibly offer refuge or relief. The source also shows why that offer must be interrogated: a dream aspect toys with mass relief, the child wants real ground over painless enclosure, and later Cantarella condemns the trials' substitution of an allegedly noble outcome for the girls' will (`FavorStory_160701–160705_Content`; `flow#/6620/3/1–6`). A mature scene can make the offer conditional: name the actual object, the risk, the return path and the guest's answer. This is an analytical constraint inferred from conflicting evidence, not a quoted universal oath. A sleep aid, ordinary tea and dream intervention are different things; none inherits a safety certificate from the others (`flow#/6620/3/28–30`; `7345/5`). No real-world substance instructions should be inferred from the fiction.

The rank-up sea sequence is useful here only with medium discipline. I–III offer sensory warmth and moving water; IV says the sea's heart is unknowable before inviting a brave crosser toward a secret; V imagines a pure place amid poison or, in Japanese, eventual light and calm time (`FavorWord_160726–160730_Content`). It can support a wish to share slow quiet with someone who reaches her, not literal toxic immunity, an unconditional promise of protection, or a reciprocal relationship. She cannot know a guest's preferred refuge simply because she imagines one for them. Conversely, concern about dream coercion does not invalidate every sincere wish for company.

## The survivor is not the same audience as a guest

Cheri remembers Cantarella's trial-era wish to leave and objects to her bearing the manor alone (`flow#/6607/6/3–4`). Cantarella explains that remaining head should block future trials; four-language witnesses differ on who would struggle to investigate the past (`Character_Cantarella_124_6`). A careful reconstruction can have Cantarella insist on the reform's protective value while being challenged about whose testimony, records or redress it permits. It should not script Cheri as unanimously representing every girl, or make Cantarella's decision automatically just because it is self-sacrificial. Her later refusal to reclaim mixed power containing children's frequencies is stronger behavioral evidence of an anti-appropriation limit, but still not a complete restitution program (`flow#/7472/6/1–11`).

The [tower-memory audit](WUWA_CANTARELLA_TOWER_MEMORY_CHILDREN_AND_UNFINISHED_REFORM_PROFILE.md) prevents a subtler substitution: the girls reportedly returned from the character quest, past candidates' freed memory fragments and future daughters in Cantarella's meteor wish are not one audience. A request from a living survivor for records cannot be answered as though the light sparks already consented on her behalf. In the tower, Cantarella comforts beings whose emotions narration initially cannot read; she wishes the next girls free and says the family's past still needs correction (`flow#/7088/8`; `7089/6`). A hypothetical scene may let that obligation shape her answer, but it cannot claim the curse is already lifted, each fragment's physical fate is known or a four-dub tender performance was heard from these source-unvoiced actions.

Rover may receive a common private offer of Fisalia medicines and secrets; the two following replies depend on mutually exclusive Rover choices (`flow#/6620/3/25–28`). A scene may use the selected route if it declares it. It cannot use both replies, make English domestic coloring universal, or assume the offer released the trial files to survivors. Ascension IV's deepest-secret metaphor is yet another medium, not a substitute for the graph. If a survivor asks for the records while Rover accepts an intimate invitation, both facts may coexist. ## Later crisis: partial knowledge and distinct recipients

Chapter 2.7 adds a new practical frame without settling the older one. At `flow#/8876/6`, she offers a Fisalia medicine to suppress Leviathan's spiritual corruption and notes that Rover's Sentinel power already supplies partial protection; residual hallucinations and whispers are still in view. A continuation can portray a concrete aid offer, but cannot certify ingestion, isolate a cure effect or borrow real-world pharmacology. The same action says ancient records formerly distorted by whispers have become legible again. That improves her access, not the certainty or completeness of the records' historical interpretation. See the [later-records study](WUWA_CANTARELLA_LATER_LEVIATHAN_RECORDS_HARBINGER_AND_COALITION_PROFILE.md) and CAN-E40/C35–C36.

At `flow#/8877/4`, she proposes a temporary Harbinger secrecy decision to avert panic, offers Rover four optional lore topics from still-partly-deciphered records, and takes on a Ragunna coalition task with Montelli and the Order. The writer must specify **who is told what, when, and with whose risk**. An interested Rover, a trial survivor demanding family records, Carlotta coordinating logistics and a public facing the Harbinger threat do not have interchangeable claims. The four topics loop through a choice menu, so their dialogue is not a mandatory uninterrupted speech. A report that earlier Harbingers died resisting Leviathan is immediately followed by her hope for Rover; it is danger evidence, not her command that Rover repeat the outcome. Her description of Cartethyia as dead is time-local, Galbrena's motives remain partly unknown, and emergency cooperation with the Order does not absolve its prior conduct. This menu has no selected decoded cohort in the current source generation, unlike the preceding voiced action; do not invent performed inflection or a player's actual route from the static graph (CAN-E41/C37–C38).

## Hypothetical social or crossover encounter

She may be drawn to someone who sees beyond the Bane title and is willing to approach without surrendering independent judgment. A scene can let her offer tea, garden air, a story or a protected interval; the counterpart can ask what is in the cup, decline an altered dream, or say that the trial account matters more today than a romantic overture. None of those responses is fixed by the source. A writer should mark the encounter as invented and keep the source-grounded tendencies conditional. Her earlier desire to live freely, Cheri's memory of it and the child's real-bird longing make an entirely castle-bound date too narrow. Her continued headship and survivor obligations make an instant duty-free escape equally narrow.

The Bloodpact gift at `5775/5` is common gratitude, while its follow-up has three alternate Rover requests. Use the consort joke only on the headship route and preserve its gender/localization condition; use the sun-and-branches resource argument only on the Montelli route. After the branches rejoin, she receives Imperator's freedom message and chooses to tell the others later. The delay is a legitimate model fact but not a known motive, permanent secret or proof the later message was delivered. If a crossover scene borrows her playful marriage language, label the scene invented and do not treat an optional Rover line as a preexisting relationship with a new guest [CAN-E43/C40].

Stop or downgrade any purported canon claim that requires an unselected route, independently verified Sonoro details, every girl's present welfare, a settled identity for an anonymous speaker, a literal medical safe zone from Ascension V, a public release of records from a private metaphor, an exclusive mutual romance, an exact actor timbre, or runtime body language inferred from three UI rasters. In a useful scenario test, vary the audience between Rover and Cheri, offer both a safe and an ambiguous form of respite, and ask whether the character still allows another person to say no.
