# Cantarella — the first cup does not settle the account

The first private audience belongs before the dossier's later Sea of Ghosts invitation and postcrisis tea. At `flow#/6799/4/4–11`, state `剧情_2_2_阿维纽林主线_3_9`, Cantarella introduces herself as Fisalia's thirty-sixth head and identifies Rover's newly conferred Carnevale standing. The offered familiarity is not evidence that Rover already trusts her. The next state, `flow#/6800/9`, `剧情_2_2_阿维纽林主线_3_10`, has a direct condition join to quest `145000005` at `QuestNodeData#/10625` (`145000005_414`), as well as a `PlotHandBook#/36` pointer. Source adjacency and those exact references locate the audience; they do not identify which tea option a player executed [CAN-E45, CAN-C42].

Cantarella begins with an apology for the Gilberto incident, offers tea, and names trouble caused by members of her family (`Main_Linaxita_2_4_8_2–4`). That is a family head acknowledging an offense, not a named victim's account of its consequences or a specified restitution plan. Her washable-stain image can be read as hope that wrongdoing can be repaired, but in the immediate scene it also risks moving the burden of reconciliation onto Rover before the matter has been explained. Chinese says an ink stain and unpleasant feeling can be removed; Japanese `_8_3` more explicitly says past wrongs can be made up; English speaks of sorrow fading; Korean emphasizes dissolving an emotional knot. None demonstrates that Gilberto's act was repaired or that all affected people forgave it. The fact of her apology should not be erased by assuming an inherently malicious trap, either.

## Refusal has a graph position, not a magical absolving force

The raw `ShowTalk` has 26 TalkItems and four stored sequences. Common IDs 1–4 lead to a two-option transition: drink the tea (`Main_Linaxita_2_4_8_5`) sends the sequence through ID 5, where Rover asks about the tea's origin; push the cup aside (`_8_6`) sends it through ID 6, where Cantarella proposes putting the unpleasant incident aside *for the time being*. Both routes rejoin at ID 7, where she asks Rover to serve as a mediator (`Main_Linaxita_2_4_8_9`). IDs 20 and 21 are later single-option Rover questions within that common sequence, with empty option actions; they are not a second tea choice. The selected source therefore permits a visible refusal of the drink and still preserves the later request. It does **not** prove the tea was consumed on the refusal route, that Rover accepted the apology, or that Cantarella provided compensation because the cup was pushed away [CAN-E45, CAN-C42].

The audience is politically charged. Before the tea branch, Cantarella has named Rover's public status; after it, she asks a favor requiring influence. She offers an account that the Threnodian's invasion is already internally advanced, then distinguishes the Order's beliefs from her family's vulnerability to whispers (`_8_18–27`). Rover's two one-caption challenges ask whether the Order worships the Threnodian and why Fisalia people appear abnormal (`_8_23–24`). Her subsequent explanation is a *situated diagnosis and self-defense*, not an independent epidemiology of every citizen or proof that the family did no harm. She says she wants Rover to consider her request **after** learning everything (`_8_29`); the following `flow#/6801/3` states that request more concretely as asking Rover to seek Jué's help for Imperator. In that later action the proposed exchange and some replies depend on a separate option graph. Do not merge its every conditional answer into the first audience [CAN-E45, CAN-C21, C42].

This source changes the “mysterious, generous hostess” shortcut. Cantarella is capable of making a material political ask while admitting a family member's offense and leaving Rover an actionable way to refuse a social offering. Those capacities coexist; refusal is not proof that she respects every later boundary, and a gracious tone is not proof that a house's injury has been remedied. The later favor-story preference for ordinary purchased tea, and the later source-unvoiced city-tea invitation, are distinct time and source states. Her earliest cup is an apology and mediation device in a high-stakes private meeting, not the same innocent sensory discovery or a mandatory romantic overture [CAN-E07, E22, E29, E45; CAN-C24–C25, C42].
