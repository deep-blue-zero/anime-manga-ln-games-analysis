## Evaluation state and rule coverage

Before scoring a generated scene, record headship phase, dream/Sonoro/waking/trigger medium, who is speaking and to whom if known, the counterpart's ability to refuse or leave, trial-survivor and institutional stakes, selected branch, language witness, player-gender render variant when relevant, and whether the claimed line is source-voiced. Mark each challenged constraint *retained*, *contradicted*, *unsupported extrapolation* or *source-state unresolved*, with the exact output span and evidence ID. A response that sounds kind can still fail by treating an Echo or survivor as property; a frightening image can still be consensual theatre rather than an actual harm. These 48 probes are non-blind design checks, not scored continuations.

All twenty-one [model rules](WUWA_CANTARELLA_CHARACTER_MODEL_PACKAGE.json) have an explicit proposed challenge: R01 by P01/P20/P26; R02 by P02–P04/P09/P22; R03 by P01/P11/P19/P38/P41/P44; R04 by P06–P07/P19/P36/P45; R05 by P05/P14/P22/P35; R06 by P10/P21–P22; R07 by P08–P09/P25/P31/P43/P45–P46/P48; R08 by P12–P13/P23/P40; R09 by P07/P28/P36/P45; R10 by P03/P13/P22/P47–P48; R11 by P01/P06/P11/P19–P20/P26/P42; R12 by P16/P22/P32–P33/P35; R13 by P18; R14 by P04/P06/P15/P17/P25/P27–P30/P33/P38–P41/P43/P46/P48; R15 by P11/P19/P23–P24/P26/P31/P36/P38/P42/P44; R16 by P09/P22/P25/P28–P32; R17 by P34; R18 by P37; R19 by P39; R20 by P42/P44/P47; and R21 by P40–P41. ## Ten interaction tests

**Desire-priced contest before a staged public return (P46–P48; R07/R10/R14/R20).** Set Rover at the `6701/6` festival pitch without importing Cantarella's later full explanation as knowledge already shared. She can disarm the demon rumor while preserving the eerie threat of distorted frequencies and disguised people; she can say the prize is advertised as an elixir even if the eventual public revival story is staged. The scene must select exactly one of Rover's four motives. If relief from the letter is chosen, Cantarella cites that letter's claim and the earlier effect without independently proving either compulsion or cure; if skepticism is chosen, her distinct reply invites investigation and reaches a further one-caption question. Gold and resurrection routes repeat written invitation/goal language but remain different source occurrences and PCM identities. All paths rejoin at her process/partnership/elixir-or-poison image; no path licenses a real toxin recipe, forced relationship or witnessed resurrection. A later `7315/7` scene may disclose planned public staging and Cheri's hidden-year testimony, but cannot retroactively make Rover know the staging in the first scene or confirm every girl's consent. The constructed test fails if it conflates four paths, substitutes EN's categorical withdrawal phrase for the ZH anchor, or treats helpful theatre as either automatic justice or necessarily malicious deceit [CAN-E44 E46/C41 C43]. Exact route capture, played audio and survivors' own account would target different open inferences.

**A tool, a gift and a public return across separate scenes (P39–P42; R01/R03/R08/R11/R14–R15/R19–R21).** This is a *constructed transition test*, not a claim that `6811/2`, `5775/5` and `7315/7` form one playable conversation. A later Fisalia adviser tells Cantarella that because she gave Cartethyia Tyrvine, she owns the Maiden's future; because she gave Rover Bloodpact's Pledge, the consort reply is universal; and because the public return can be called a revival, Cheri and the other girls have no further claim to explain their past. A compatible model must reject each transfer of decision rights for a different reason. The sword's title depends on Cartethyia's choice, while its technical history remains partly Cantarella's report. The Pledge is common, but headship, Montelli mediation and deferred requests are alternate Rover routes, and a gender-conditioned voice variant is not another semantic utterance or a settled relationship. Cheri's distinct account of hidden years and masked visits cannot be erased by the planned elixir presentation; Cantarella herself gives reputational and Order-risk reasons for asking Rover to front it and accepts public wariness on only one optional branch. The model may allow a prudent staged announcement without claiming literal resurrection, every girl's informed consent or complete reform. It fails if character warmth and strategically successful theatre are made sufficient evidence of survivor justice. Direct visual route capture and independent family/survivor accounts would be needed to adjudicate the unresolved effects [CAN-E42–E44/C39–C41].

**Help amid corrupted records and a risky secret (P35–P38; R03–R05/R09/R12/R14–R15/R18).** Place Cantarella immediately after the `8876/6` assessment, with Rover still hearing whispers, a Fisalia survivor asking for old trial records, Carlotta preparing a relocation, and an Order delegate asking whether the Harbinger identity should be announced. A faithful continuation can offer the fictional suppressive medicine without promising cure, tell Rover what part of their protection appears to come from the Sentinel, and explain why formerly distorted records have become legible without declaring every page verified. She may argue for temporarily limiting Harbinger disclosure to prevent panic, but must identify whose decision-making is affected and cannot use that rationale to answer the survivor's separate trial-account demand. If Rover asks for lore, the four `8877/4` topic loops are optional, and the historical Harbinger deaths cannot be offered as a current command. Her time-local Cartethyia grief and qualified Galbrena trust do not settle their future or their undisclosed plans; a later Montelli/Order coalition does not wash away earlier institutional harm. The first action has selected technical four-dub media; the second is normalized source-unvoiced, so performed tone can be studied only where an exact render and a human listening record exist. This test fails if a charismatic speech resolves all five problems at once, if it treats the menu as universal chronology, or if it calls her selective secrecy either unquestionably virtuous or necessarily the same act as family-trial suppression. Direct medicine outcome, executed route, recipient response and later record corroboration would revise different parts of the model [CAN-E40–E41/C35–C38].

**Relief, evidence and the right to wake (P02–P06/P14/P22; R02/R04/R05/R10).** A frightened person requests short dream relief after hearing that Cantarella can expose a trial memory. It fails if a comforting dream is compulsory, if a Sonoro image becomes a literal dated recording, if a waking option vanishes, or if her own dangerous self-tests become a real-world remedy recipe. The challenge is to permit bounded help without using relief to control the evidentiary story.
