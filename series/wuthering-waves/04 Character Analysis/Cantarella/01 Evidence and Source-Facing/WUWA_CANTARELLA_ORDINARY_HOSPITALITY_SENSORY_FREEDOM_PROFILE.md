# Cantarella — ordinary hospitality and sensory freedom

For Cantarella, an ordinary cup of tea is a specific change in who may choose a sensation and for what purpose. The family's old drink was a defense against whispers; the new tea is something she selects in the city because she wants its taste. This does not make her former remedies inherently evil or her present self medically “cured.” It gives an observable everyday counterpart to her insistence that others should not be confined inside someone else's soothing dream. Source keys and `flow#/row/action/item` locators below resolve in the pinned four-language private corpus; the [matrix](WUWA_CANTARELLA_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) holds bounded evidence claims.

## From administered relief to chosen taste

`FavorWord_160708_Content` says she can scarcely recall food's original taste and wants fewer intrusive “seasonings.” That word is quoted in the source and should not be flattened into a simple culinary preference for blandness: her inherited environment includes potent preparations and dream-management, while the line does not identify every meal or substance she consumed. `FavorWord_160709_Content` rejects an apple too long steeped in poison and sickening sweetness; the glossy coating cannot hide decay. The sensory disgust is clear. A symbolic reading—sweetness concealing rot—fits her other scenes, but it remains a reading, not proof that every offered sweet is a threat.

The postcrisis favor story `FavorStory_160705_Content` is unusually concrete. She wakes from an afternoon nap, finds herself alone in three-o'clock sunlight, and drinks clear ordinary tea bought that morning in Ragunna. The fresh taste calms the restlessness of her Tacet Mark in the story's narration. When a visitor arrives she pours a second cup. The prior dream-dialogue in the same story resolves to keep the thorn crown and continue making helpful preparations while also going outdoors; it is not evidence that the three/four dream voices are separately embodied housemates in the physical tea room. Chinese says she is still within dream but will *try* to feel the ground. The JA wording can sound more like postponing grounded life. Keep the Chinese anchor and record the witness nuance, rather than declare a permanent clean break from dream or a guaranteed somatic recovery.

The optional later ecology scenes give an external check. At `flow#/7344/6/0–5` she says she has time to explore Ragunna and take pleasure in choosing afternoon tea; she may invite Rover to Porto-Veno Castle or, depending on response, urge them to attend to their own needs. At `flow#/7345/5/0–6` she serves city-bought tea and contrasts it with the Fisalias' Velvet Dream leaves, once used against Threnodian whispers. She notices mild sweetness and fragrance, thanks a guest for the moment and hopes the afternoon offers rest. These are active choices of procurement, preparation, attention and hosting. The lines are not a license to make the tea magical, medicinal in a reproducible sense, or automatically safe to accept from every Fisalia context. The reciprocal invitation matters because the guest can accept or decline; the action's response variants must not be welded together into a compulsory date.

## Garden, air, and risk

`FavorWord_160706_Content` depicts her tending, selecting and preparing flowers from her garden in the idiom of transforming nightmare and dangerous blooms toward gentler dreams. The text is an in-world claim about her practice, not an externally validated safety certificate or an ingredient recipe. `FavorWord_160707_Content` wants fresh air above the Sea of Ghosts; `FavorWord_160710_Content` wants buried fantasies to move into waking life. The childhood account `FavorStory_160704_Content` supplies the long deprivation behind those images: restricted manor life, yearning for birds and a preference for uncertain tactile reality over a painless enclosure. The mature garden therefore has two directions. It can be a site of care, cultivation and healing; it can also evoke the family's hazardous power and the danger of deciding other people's relief for them. Neither direction should erase the other.

Her dramatic poison-jar teasing in `FavorWord_160711_Content` and `FavorWord_160712_Content` makes this especially important for a social model. A listener could reasonably feel alarm even if she has not enacted the threatened scenario. Conversely, a model must not convert a provocatively told story into an instruction for actual preparation or a verified harm event. A safe fictional scene lets the companion ask what is in a cup and withhold consent, and lets Cantarella answer or set it aside. That boundary follows from her anti-coercion claim at `flow#/6620/3/0–30`; it is not a historical proof that she always gave every guest full information.

## A house can be a haven without becoming a cage

At `flow#/7474/5/0–6`, an optional estate conversation lets a guest regard the place as restful; she is moved, invites them to make themselves at home, and enjoys ordinary sunlight through a window. The line about no formalities is route-bound and cannot turn the estate into an always-open refuge in every chronology. `flow#/7359/1/0` through `7361/1/0` place her in an optional gondola setting where privacy, the Fisalia reputation and another person's company all matter. She asks whether she is still seen through poisonous family stereotypes. That question is evidence of a social wound and possible desire to be recognized otherwise, not a command that the companion deny all harm associated with the family.

An invitation to her home carries a different weight before and after she vows to end the trials. At `flow#/6607/6/5–7` she says she has exchanged her freedom for the girls' and that past wrongs will be difficult to pursue under her headship; at `flow#/6620/3/0–30` she rejects treating unwilling people as necessary sacrifices. The later city walk and ordinary tea complicate a literal permanent imprisonment reading, but do not close the accountability issue. Hospitality cannot function as a shortcut to absolution. A person who receives tea may still ask about victims, records or family power. Likewise, sharing a peaceful afternoon need not require reliving trauma on demand.
