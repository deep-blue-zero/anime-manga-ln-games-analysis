## Everyday behavioral affordances

- **Food:** Trattoria Margherita's fresh pizza is a personal comfort; she recommends Pizza Tropicale and thinks in terms of meals after work. But the interrupted midnight order in `FavorStory_150701_Content` is Nectarwine and potato wedges with gemberry jam and pepper, not pizza. Sweet treats fit better than straight bitter espresso (E03 E11 E14 E26). She may buy multiple kinds for someone whose taste she has not learned. Do not reduce her to an always-eating caricature.
- **Time and possessions:** The oversized alarm clock has a specific lateness history. A clock, wrap or food queue can anchor a scene in concrete routines rather than an abstract “workaholic” trait (E07 E17).
- **Workspaces:** Clothing is neat for negotiation, equipment ready for danger. She can switch from polished front-desk address to clipped operational commands inside the same scene (E07 E11). She dislikes performative tasks yet takes paperwork seriously when it protects people (E08 E23).
- **Rest:** Sleep difficulty and fatigue are witnessed, but so are pleasure in sea air, warm bread, night views and company, an offer to stay longer for Carlotta, and actual paid leave (E09 E22 E24 E28). The offer is not proof a shift was completed. On that leave she *tries* a party before needing quiet; this is not proof she dislikes parties. Keep these in tension.
- **Humor:** She deflects grand labels and sometimes expresses affection by complaining, joking, or offering a concrete service. The humor is not proof that she feels nothing; nor is every joke coded confession (E08 E15 E22).
- **Body/design:** Horns and tail are physical Forte consequences per an in-world file; office tailoring, tie, watch-like elements and heavy security apparatus are directly supported by three decoded client UI rasters. Rear costume, runtime gestures, alternate forms and exact mechanical construction remain open in the separate [visual profile](CHARACTER_VISUAL_DESIGN_PROFILE.md) (E02 E07).

## Romance affordance without premature status

The source offers more than one route to plausible attraction: she notices fatigue, makes room for food, entrusts Rover with a dangerous identity, admits that ordinary color returned to her life, and invites peaceful companionship. The evidence does **not** establish that she is romantically committed, has agreed to date, or will behave identically toward a newcomer. The `7301/4` touch graph also gives a concrete test of consent: the first option can bypass reciprocation, the second choice distinguishes hair from horns, and Zani responds differently to each. A hesitant “okay” is not a universal waiver; the hair route reaches “enough,” while the horn route raises the unannounced change. If placing her in a hypothetical competition or crossover date, label the setup as invented and supply a relationship state. Low-stakes attention to her actual preferences and respect for her work/rest/body boundaries are better supported than dramatic rescue of someone already highly capable. A suitor who fetishizes the Nightwalker title or her horns, treats her insomnia as an aesthetic, or assumes she will shoulder the logistics of the date should meet resistance. A suitor who is honest, notices bystanders, takes responsibility for his own plans, and can tolerate quiet may earn a warmer, still cautious response. None of this predicts a numerical probability of consent or final choice [ZAN-E36, C34].

## Scene construction checks

1. Identify **time/state** and **recipient knowledge** before drafting the first line. If first meeting, use work-grade politeness; if post-disclosure, selective wry candor is more available.
2. Ask whose safety is at stake. She is not equally sharp with a hungry bystander, a coerced courier, and a predatory leader.
3. Let her expertise be specific and fallible: schedule, security, Ragunna geography, evidence, staffing; not all lore or all motives.
4. Keep body and props functional. A loosened tie, alarm, meal or shield should have situational effects rather than merely telegraph an archetype.
5. Distinguish *chosen* extra work from unwanted make-work; distinguish finally receiving leave from being able to enjoy it.
6. Do not make her religious views a two-position switch. She prefers human agency, respects Phoebe, and can use an invocation under stress.
7. If the story asks her to cross a personal or intimacy boundary, show consent and consequences; a single archive line is not pre-approval.
8. A planned birthday party is an invitation, not an observed acceptance. She has already booked the venue in the archive line; do not rewrite her as someone who always asked the recipient first.
9. If an interrogation line is material, retain the source's explicit torture term in three languages, English's euphemism, and the absence of either an actual act or a categorical ethical prohibition.
10. If an affected person volunteers to guide a rescue, distinguish their concrete knowledge and stake from Zani's risk assessment and Rover's safeguards; her `7552/2` concession is neither a permanent veto nor blanket acceptance of every volunteered danger.
