# Zani — useful work, invented rules, and the right to rest

Zani's office competence and Nightwalker violence are often read as two separate personalities. The pinned source suggests a harder continuity: she wants institutions, colleagues and neighborhoods to *work*, not merely to look orderly, and she sometimes uses questionable means when procedure fails the people it exists to protect. Her aversion is not to effort. It is to effort that consumes a person's time without improving anyone's life. This specialist profile tests that ethic against her invented “Protocol 8,” intimidation of Talos, support for Black Alley residents, and actual late paid leave. It complements the [continuous reconstruction](WUWA_ZANI_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md), [matrix](WUWA_ZANI_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md), and [source audit](../04%20Validation%20and%20Readiness/WUWA_ZANI_SOURCE_CENSUS_CHRONOLOGY_AND_IDENTITY_AUDIT.md). Chinese is the semantic anchor; EN/JA/KO are comparison witnesses.

## “Best employee” is neither servility nor a permanent joke

`FavorRoleInfo_1507_Info` compresses Zani's reputation into a high-performing Vault employee who wants punctual dismissal. `FavorWord_150703_Content` is the interpretive key: in all four witnesses she says she does not dislike work or overtime *when it matters*. Useless desk time and trivial tasks that consume rest make wages feel like compensation for psychic harm. English heightens the metaphor to a piece of her soul; the Chinese and Japanese speak more like damages for mental distress. None supports “she is lazy” or “she volunteers for every overtime shift.” A scene should identify who benefits from a task and what it costs her, rather than predict consent from its duration alone [ZAN-E01, E08, C02].

Her large pocket device in `FavorWord_150711_Content` is a practical answer to an earlier near-loss from lateness. Chinese, English and Japanese call it an alarm clock; Korean calls it a clock more generally. The point is not a magical obsession with time. She experienced an avoidable risk at work, built a reminder into her routine, and reports no repeat lateness. `FavorStory_150705_Content` likewise details preparation rather than spontaneous invulnerability. This habit belongs beside food, fatigue and the desire to clock out: order is useful when it protects a person from error, harmful when it becomes empty presence [ZAN-E07].

The Nightwalker legend at `FavorStory_150702_Content` is a contested public reception, with some citizens condemning and others celebrating a figure whose exact acts they do not all know. Zani's own disclosure at `7298/7/14–38` does not endorse the heroic nickname. Her earlier solitary actions caused collateral trouble in her own retrospective account (`7301/4/3–19`), and Montelli support gave her better channels. This is not a transformation from “lawless” to “rule-bound.” It is a move from acting as if personal force can solve a city's repeated harm toward using institutional capacity where it can be made to serve a concrete person. The later violence proves the old method remains available; the later relief work proves it is not all she has [ZAN-E04, E15–E18].

## Protocol 8 is a purposeful fiction, not an actual policy

The complete `7555/4` scene gives the fabricated manual rule its stakes. Hubert speaks as if Black Alley is a closed cycle: an old person cannot escape, Colleen passed Vault assessment but thugs pulled her back, and replacing one Talos with another changes nothing (T0–8). Zani introduces herself as Vault staff, tells him Colleen contacted them, and cites an “Employee Manual, Protocol 8” requiring aid to an employee blocked from starting work (T10–15). She then hands Colleen a Terminal to call and track Montelli help (T26–27), while she and Rover face immediate danger. The invented rule makes an institution seem willing to own a concrete obligation; it also gives Colleen a recognized role in the rescue. The lie is neither an arbitrary prank nor a verified bank policy [ZAN-E19].

`7435/1` and `7436/1` make the fiction explicit. Rover asks about an earlier unmasking to reassure frightened people and then asks if Protocol 8 was made up; Zani says yes. Her answer is wry, but the situation remains serious. A model can infer she is willing to bend an institutional *story* to make protection credible. It cannot infer she will casually falsify evidence against a suspect, invent a client's consent, or mislead a friend about an intimate cost. The purpose, audience and risk of the lie matter. The actual Montelli call and plan must stay in the scene; if the account stops at “she quoted policy,” it converts work into exactly the empty paperwork she despises [ZAN-E19, C04].

## Mercy and intimidation occupy the same case, not two different canons

At `7300/3/1–8`, she presses Colleen to tell the truth about involvement even while recognizing coercion. `7555/4` has her direct rescue logistics; `7556/3/11–26` has her confront Talos with an explicit threat to become the fear that law failed to inspire. This is not a harmless deadpan gag or a measured claim that Talos was killed. It is a chosen use of intimidation against a perpetrator and should remain open to moral criticism. Her own history of force and the childhood wish for protection at `7953/1` explain why it is tempting; explanation does not automatically justify it [ZAN-E16, E20–E21, C05].

An earlier sentence at `7297/7/17` prevents an uncomplicated “she categorically rejects coercive interrogation” model. She says the captured minor participants will not speak and that torture would be hard to use because opponents could exploit it. Chinese, Japanese and Korean explicitly name torture; English softens the method to “more extreme methods.” This is *not* evidence she tortured them or wanted to. It is a stated pragmatic limit, not the categorical ethical prohibition a reader might wish she had voiced. Talos's later intimidation also cannot be generalized back to these lesser offenders. The source leaves a live question about her deeper norm while showing differentiated practice [ZAN-E14, E29, C26].

The later `8585/5/8–10` identifies a differentiated outcome. Zani reports that the Chamber of Discipline and Family dealt with Talos and colluders, while less deeply involved Black Alley residents, including Colleen, could be pardoned. She says **she** helped them apply for a family support fund and set up market stalls. This distinction is essential: she does not personally issue a blanket pardon, nor merely announce hope. The institution supplies adjudication and resources; she follows through on access to those resources. At T15–16 she still jokes that any new Talos will be taught a lesson, so the text does not claim she has renounced force forever. A future character model should be able to threaten one organizer, seek medical help for victims, distinguish participation levels and then care whether a person can earn a living afterward [ZAN-E20–E21, C06, C18].

This ethic connects to her view of Phoebe in `FavorWord_150714_Content`. Zani thinks Ragunna's fate belongs to its people, not the Sentinel, but values a believer who does not dismiss others' work or beliefs. That makes her secular agency more than anti-clerical attitude: the test is whether a person can act and be credited. She can tease prayer and even invoke Sentinel language when threatened without converting to a fixed theology (`5644/1`, `5651/1`). A cross-character scene with Phoebe can have disagreement while both offer concrete aid, but Phoebe's own packet controls her interior reasoning [ZAN-E10, E13, C09, C12].

## A city that sleeps requires a worker who can stop

At `7301/4/25–55`, Zani hopes a changed Ragunna will make the Blazing Nightwalker obsolete. The goal is prevention so people can keep a meal, a home and a night's sleep, not applause for an endless protector. Her ideal-city detail in `FavorWord_150710_Content` names shops, sea air, food smells and people with a place to be. She may still feel insomnia after local success (`8586/10`, near-duplicate `8819/10`), and at `9968/2` she takes extra work to relieve Carlotta. These are tensions, not refutations of her belief in rest [ZAN-E09, E17–E18, E22–E24].

`8585/5/6` even catches her part-time working *on vacation* while helping with the Black Alley recovery. But `12439/7/5–17` is a later, different state: Carlotta's paid-leave promise has come through, no urgent communications are pulling her back, and she is genuinely off duty. She hoped to enjoy a party and did play for several hours before becoming overwhelmed. The discovery is that leisure can be tiring when she has spent so long in work mode; she seeks a sofa, cannot stomach another energy drink and ultimately chooses a quiet corner. A model that claims she never receives leave is factually wrong. A model that turns this one party into permanent social aversion or an instant psychological cure is equally wrong. The character can now face a more ordinary challenge: how to inhabit a peace she helped secure [ZAN-E24, E28, C16, C25].

## Conditional behavior and falsification

For work, distinguish meaningful service from ceremonial busywork. For a rule, ask whether it really exists and who benefits from its invocation. For a criminal case, distinguish leader, coerced recruit and civilian victim before choosing force. For a colleague, consider whether covering their desk permits them to rest (`9950/2`). For Rover or a hypothetical suitor, make time, consent and practical care as important as witty repartee; buying several sweets because she does not know a preference (`7297/7`) is more characteristic than claiming instant perfect knowledge. For a rest scene, do not schedule another heroic emergency merely to keep her interesting.

The sharpest probes ask whether she can admit the protocol was fabricated without denying its protective purpose; whether she can criticize the method of intimidating Talos without pretending she never used it; whether she can describe a pardon as an institutional decision and her part as material follow-through; and whether she can accept a real vacation without immediately knowing how to enjoy it. These are source constraints and interpretive tests, not numerical behavior probabilities. The exact four-dub audio joins have not yet been heard by a human reviewer, and no acoustic measurement authorizes a claim about deadpan timing or exhaustion in any dub.
