## Ordinary-life ledger

Her food preferences are not arbitrary “cute traits”: red-date Loong buns at the morning market are a particular pleasure (`FavorWord_130408_Content`), and her refusal to waste food is tied to lived shortages (`130409`). Sanhua noticing a missed meal supplies a mundane cost of the ever-lit City Hall. She wants to hear Jinzhou Theater and especially cherishes a happy ending (`130406`; `FavorStory_130402_Content`), yet a story has her listening from the office window because she is still working. Childhood sea nightmares, remembered Mt. Firmament snow and Jinzhou neighbors helping one another brush away snow make winter both private memory and public value (`130411–130412`; `flow#/3153/4`). A handmade birthday lantern for Rover has more personal labor than a purchased trinket (`130418`). Moonlit Fair food, riddles and shared walking offer optional leisure (`flow#/4130/8–4133/4`); gameplay choice and favor address must be tagged separately.

Her self-report that worry rarely crosses her mind *now* (`FavorWord_130407_Content`) should be treated as a time- and audience-bound utterance. Mt. Firmament contains doubt, pain and resolve (`flow#/3223/3`). Neither statement cancels the other. Later travel curiosity (`flow#/20166/7–20167/7`) should coexist with a job that keeps her rooted in Jinzhou; it is not evidence of a completed world tour.

## Romance affordance and consent

The evidence supports a graded reading: **warmth and personal invitation are explicit**; possible romance is a scenario-compatible interpretation; an exclusive established partnership is *not* established by this selected corpus. Favor words compare aligned frequencies and, in Ascension V `FavorWord_130429_Content`, make room for the possibility of fate in meeting Rover; a birthday lantern asks Rover to join her, and fair dialogue can be read as attentive one-on-one time. ZH/JA/KO keep the fate allowance occasional or encounter-specific, while EN frames a stronger past-belief contrast; no witness records Rover's reciprocal answer or Jinhsi abandoning her civic self-rule project. These are different media and potentially different branches. In a generated comic dating-show scenario, give Jinhsi an explicit reason and voluntary decision to participate; her office, civic obligations and careful courtesy should still matter. She may ask questions, listen, decline, or accept a connection without treating affection as a reward for saving Jinzhou. Her tactical welcome of Rover should not be recycled as a generic manipulation script for courtship.

Useful counterreadings: formal diplomacy can sound intimate in translation; a chosen companion episode need not imply the same past in every player's route; the text contains gratitude and fate language but not an irrevocable contract. Revisit Chinese/Japanese/Korean lines and human-heard four-dub delivery before stronger claims.
