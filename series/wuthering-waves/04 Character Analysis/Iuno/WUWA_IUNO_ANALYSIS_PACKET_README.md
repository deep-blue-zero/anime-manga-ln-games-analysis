The prodigy Priestess traded clear foresight for power to make Dark Tide beings vulnerable, at a mounting cost to her existence in other people's memories. Her public confidence, fear of being forgotten, strategic concealment of the cost, partial Chaos remnant, Rover's exceptional remembrance and later self-chosen civilian aid cannot be collapsed into one timeless register. The model preserves both the moral force of her intervention and the objection that she kept Augusta and Rover underinformed. Her conditional promise to give Rover a complete account before a future similar choice is distinct from her separate request to keep the Chaos episode private.

The later [Tide observation and Galbrena-anchoring study](01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_IUNO_LATER_DARK_TIDE_MONITORING_GALBRENA_ANCHOR_AND_SHARED_SUPPORT_PROFILE.md) adds two distinct Chapter 2.7 source states: one unvoiced, branch-sensitive monitoring/material-support exchange and one voiced, conditional route briefing. The [public-verdict and field-sight study](01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_IUNO_PUBLIC_VERDICT_AQUILA_SIGHT_AND_FIELD_RESTRAINT_PROFILE.md) adds a consequential Temple judgment in a contested arena accusation, two hunt actions that request sight and describe danger, and a temporary choice to let Augusta lead. Iuno's verdict and Augusta's expulsion order are separate; the record does not expose a spear examination, Aquila's mechanism, an observed visual bridge or renewed precise foresight.

The [uncertain-memory and Augusta-resolve study](01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_IUNO_UNCERTAIN_MEMORY_MOTHERS_IMAGE_AND_AUGUSTA_RESOLVE_PROFILE.md) distinguishes a Chaos mother-image Iuno herself doubts, a familiar site she cannot fully remember, and her earlier conditional question about whether Augusta would fight against a fixed fate.
