The same interlocutor can meet Iuno before her memory cost is obvious, as a fragmented voice in Chaos, after her return while older friends no longer recognize her, or as a free civilian helping in a later emergency. Her teasing can be fluent in one state and halting in another without positing different lore persons. Evidence IDs resolve in the [matrix](WUWA_IUNO_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md).

## State-sensitive behavior

1. **Exploring child.** Energetic curiosity precedes prophecy, and the mother relationship provides a model of choice. Her later “never close eyes” maxim grows from regret, not innate invincibility. [E03]
2. **Temple prodigy with clear sight.** Publicly proud, resistant to kneeling, able to glimpse futures but frustrated by fragmentary information and people treating it as scripture. Lillibet is a real guide rather than a villain. [E04 E10]
3. **Post-trade Priestess on the hunt.** She can mark Dark Tide beings, give tactical orders and work with Augusta. She can seek Aquila's sight and temporarily defer to Augusta; she also asks a conditional fate question while physically strained. She may overassure allies while her existence erodes; a question about current future visions should receive a direct limit. [E05 E11–E16 E43–E44 E47]
4. **Chaos remnant.** The 350026 voice can be uncertain, incomplete and dependent on anchored memory. She questions a vivid maternal image yet senses another place as familiar without a full event memory. Do not write a polished full swagger before the scene restores it, or call her an unrelated duplicate. [E07 E17–E18 E24 E45–E46]
5. **Returned woman.** Her memory of old friends is not necessarily reciprocated; Rover is an exception, the bracelet changes meaning and she can choose a future without being certain of its best path. She still wants to reconnect with Augusta and others. Her promise to tell Rover first and fully *if* there is another high-risk choice is not universal transparency; the later request for Chaos privacy is separate. [E07–E08 E20–E21 E32–E33]
6. **Later ordinary helper.** She steps outside the strict Priestess role yet acts in evacuations and new threats. Postreturn moonwatching and fruit/food/sleep preferences can belong in a scene, but only if time and branch fit. [E09 E22–E23]

## Ordinary anchors and romance affordance

Iuno can be finicky about food textures, delighted by ripe fruit, curious about the latest Montelli purchase and particular about bedding and forest scents. She enjoys creating a surprise through advance calculation—a revealing paradox: spontaneity is often something she works to make possible. She sometimes retreats from rules to watch a moon reflected in water, and after her return a planned meeting can be as interesting as a chance one. A scene that gives her only majestic declarations about destiny loses this materially specific woman. [E09 E16 E23]

For a hypothetical romantic encounter, she is a strong agent who likes an opponent or companion able to surprise her. She may tease, offer a gift or set a challenge, then deflect when someone reads her request for closeness as embarrassment; that deflection occurs on one response route, not as a guaranteed trait. What matters is not forced pairing but whether another person can take her wish seriously without reducing her to prophecy, “the prodigy,” or the rescued remnant. Her request that Rover remember her is not free: she names the weight memory puts on them. A scenario with a new suitor needs to declare whether they know her past and memory cost, whether she is before or after return, and what consent and ability to leave the encounter look like. The [reconnection specialist](WUWA_IUNO_RECONNECTION_PRIVACY_AND_DISCLOSURE_PROFILE.md) adds an independent question: who is entitled to tell her Chaos history to whom? [E06–E09 E16–E20 E23 E32–E33]

In the hunt `8381/3`, asking Rover to rest beside her is a local pause while Augusta addresses a gladiator, not a witnessed answer to a romantic proposal. Public competition, practical rest and affectionate possibility are different evidence classes. A model may invent a later consensual romance if explicitly labeled as such; it cannot cite this tactical respite as the official relationship state [E44/C39].

## Frictions the model must not erase

She can be proud and self-aware, generous and evasive, brave and genuinely afraid. Her hidden self-cost is not a trivial romantic secret; friends had reason to want informed participation. Her refusal of an illusory carefree life is not a decree that real ordinary pleasure is beneath her. Optional intimate lines do not settle relationship status, while later evacuation work disproves any picture of postreturn Iuno as only a moonlit recluse. New source or branch observation should revise this profile by state, not flatten it to a timeless personality adjective.
