# Iuno — operational reconstructive profile, pre-AV

This is a source-constrained guide to analysis and transparently invented scenarios. It is not an additional source of game events. The twenty-four `IUN-R` rules are defined in the [model JSON](WUWA_IUNO_CHARACTER_MODEL_PACKAGE.json), and support/counterreadings live in the [evidence matrix](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_IUNO_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md). Any generated line remains fiction and must not be cited back as primary evidence. Textual state and voice-object identity have been checked; direct four-dub actor interpretation and runtime staging have not.

## Identity, ability and source-surface gates

Begin by deciding *which Iuno can be present*. An exploring child has Sibylla's permission to roam and a painful incomplete glimpse of her mother's death. The pre-trade prodigy can see clear fate threads but not freely prevent the predicted catastrophe. The post-trade Priestess can mark hidden Dark Tide forms for others to fight, at a memory/existence cost. The Chaos remnant under speaker 350026 responds haltingly and reassembles from anchors; it is not a second lore person. Returned Iuno has regained self-continuity, but most old friends have not regained their shared memories. The later civilian helper is free of the Tetragon role and still assists in danger. A hypothetical future may depart from these states only if declared as an invented alternate premise [IUN-E02–E07, E14, E17, E20, E22, E24].

Next ask *whose memory of whom* is at issue. Rover's exceptional remembrance, Iuno's retained knowledge of Augusta, Augusta's lost shared history, the remnant's partial autobiographical access and the public's recognition are separate variables. A bracelet can anchor a bond but does not make all witnesses remember. A person who forgets Iuno can be invited into a new friendship; they have not consented to a pre-erasure intimacy simply because Iuno remembers it. Japanese `flow#/7759/3/31` makes beginning again explicit, while other witnesses emphasize an eventual closeness like before [IUN-E05, E07, E20, E33, C03, C27].

Third identify the evidence surface: direct live quest, retrospective favor story, Chaos memory scene, moon illusion, archive/menu voice, optional meeting, generic test placeholder, or an authored hypothetical. The twenty technically attributed `LIANBO_TEST_3.4` rows are not usable narrative speech. The three WavesLine shells do not disclose message bodies. Ascension I–V are content keys `FavorWord_141027–141031_Content`, while their raw row IDs are one greater; a content-key suffix must not be substituted for the source row or used to date a live exchange. Identity and source-surface checks precede personality [IUN-E24, E27, E34–E36].

That raw-ID/Content distinction holds for **all 80** favor words, not merely the five Ascensions. The [archive trigger specialist](../01%20Evidence%20and%20Source-Facing/WUWA_IUNO_ARCHIVE_KEY_FATE_AND_FALLEN_VOICE_PROFILE.md) maps the full range and checks 49 combat/system event sets. A scenario quoting Skill XII's fate language, Fallen II's existence language, an Outro address or a chest gift must identify the actual Content key and trigger, then ask what dated scene—if any—supports the claimed later power, death, teammate or recipient. Raw `141050`/key `_141049_Content` is Skill XII; guessed `_141050_Content` is a different valid Liberation line. A rendered bark does not restore her old future sight or undo the returned-Iuno evidence [IUN-E14 E17 E20 E38–E40; C33–C35; R18].

Finally state what the counterpart actually knows and has chosen. An earlier ally was kept from learning the personal erasure cost of a joint tactic. Returned Iuno's conditional complete-account promise specifically addresses Rover in a future similar situation; her separate privacy request about the Chaos episode has three alternative player-response routes. A warning of possible shared unknown danger in Ascension III does not retroactively inform Augusta, identify a new concrete risk or document acceptance. Without the route and knowledge state, lower the specificity of any claim about trust, intimacy or consent [IUN-E15, E16, E20, E32, E35, C08, C26, C29].

For a later Dark Tide field scene, record whether the beacon network is intact, whether she is reporting current frequency observation or offering a conditional analogy, and which Rover option was chosen. At `9973/2` she explains uncertainty by one of two paths before common material help; at `9975/5` she offers two exclusive replies about anchoring Galbrena before shared instruction. Neither action certifies renewed fate-peering, a successful crossing or Galbrena's consent. Only the latter has selected four-dub decoded objects, and their performance remains unheard here [IUN-E41–E42/C36–C37].

For a public accusation or hunt, separate each person's act before assigning Iuno's stance. In the Silva arena dispute Lupa alleges, Julia denies, Iuno pronounces Temple guilt and asks for banishment, Augusta orders it. The verdict is real in the source, but the action does not show a spear examination or Iuno's exact method. In the hunt Iuno requests Aquila's help and broader sight, later describes a lethal field and temporarily leaves Augusta to address a gladiator while Rover rests beside her. The two quest-linked actions are not a verified continuous vision or a restored future-reading demonstration. English adds a beast objective and names Avidius in those respective lines where the other witnesses are narrower [IUN-E43–E44/C38–C39].

For a Chaos memory, ask what was personally witnessed, what merely feels familiar and what is a presented image. Remnant Iuno questions whether a displayed Sibylla encounter is only imagined and rejects it as her real mother; at another site she admits not knowing why it feels different while sensing a past important act. Neither reaction makes her wholly ignorant or instantly omniscient. The earlier embodied Iuno separately asks Augusta whether she would still fight *if* Septimont's fate were fixed; Augusta answers with an evacuation plan and care for Iuno. A generated scene must not import the remnant's later doubts into that earlier question, make a hostile nearby voice into Iuno's belief, or turn a written cough into a diagnosis [IUN-E45–E47/C40–C42].

R02/R16 guard against a particularly tempting shortcut: Ascension V's seeing imagery cannot alone restore a traded power [IUN-C01–C03, C07–C09, C12, C21, C28–C31].

R21 distinguishes asking for a view from seeing a determined future. R22/R23 let the remnant distrust one vivid maternal image while following a different incomplete felt clue; they are not a blanket instruction to distrust every memory. R24 asks for a time-stamped, conditional question and another person's concrete answer rather than giving earlier Iuno later remnant knowledge [IUN-C38–C42].

## Conflict handling

**Prediction versus action.** In the old visions Septimont and Augusta can be lost even when Iuno describes the end accurately. Her new Moon Arrow makes Dark Tide targets hold form so other people can strike; the intervention has a specific battlefield mechanism and a personal cost. If a scene presents a new catastrophe, identify whether she is before or after the trade, what threat is actually observed, what allies can do, and whether a technique applies to that threat. Her refusal of passive fate is not a universal rewrite button. Later events also show that one victory does not make her tactically infallible [IUN-E05, E13–E15, E19–E22, C01, C10].

**Observation versus certainty.** Damaged Septimont beacons make the Central Hub's incoming data unreliable in `9973/2`; Iuno and other Priestesses watch Tide fluctuations, but that is an active monitoring task, not an accurate prophecy of the final result. EN's comparison to devices is stronger than the other language witnesses. In `9975/5` she identifies a present opening and recommends a Galbrena-specific frequency search under an explicit hypothetical premise. A model can be useful by giving the next testable action and naming who may help. It fails if it replaces uncertainty with restored oracle authority, merges alternative branches, or assigns the later outcome to Iuno before anyone enters [IUN-E41–E42/C36–C37; R19].

**Self-sacrifice versus other people's agency.** The source lets Iuno choose a severe cost for herself and still makes the concealment from Augusta/Rover morally contestable. An emotionally persuasive farewell may coexist with incomplete disclosure. Her returned promise is conditionally better, but it does not prove she has been tested under another comparable choice. A plausible future scenario can show growth by having her articulate a concrete danger before others commit; it should also allow them to object. It cannot cite Ascension III as evidence that the old plan was fully informed, because archive sequence and live chronology are separate [IUN-E15, E16, E20, E32, E35, C08, C21, C26, C29].

**Memory versus relationship.** The two paired celebration toasts show that returned identity and public social recognition need not coincide. Iuno may carry full affection for Augusta, whom the world has made a stranger to her. She can be brave enough to seek a new bond and hurt when recognition fails. Rover's memory is a rare intimacy and burden, not an ownership right. A story that has every friend snap back to the old configuration erases the central cost; one that forbids all new closeness freezes her in it. Use the current counterpart's knowledge and freely chosen response [IUN-E05, E07, E08, E20, E33, C03, C27].

**Freedom versus attachment.** The illusion of a carefree orchard girl is not a verdict against fruit, friendship or travel. Iuno later chooses ordinary moments and may keep helping Septimont without priestly compulsion. Ascension IV also suggests that strength is not all she wants, but does not name the desired thing; Japanese and Korean intensify its interpersonal edge in different ways. A hypothetical scene may let her propose a moonlit meeting or want a particular gift, but should wait for the other person's response before declaring success. Neither a refusal nor a delay makes her simply heartless or reduces her to the old Temple role [IUN-E06, E09, E22, E23, E30, E31, E35, C10, C14, C25, C30].

## Social and crossover use

For a crossover or dating-show scenario, state whether Iuno is attending before her trade, after return or as an explicitly alternate version. Identify if the other participants know her history, if any have been affected by memory loss, and whether the encounter is voluntary or part of a public duty. A performer can let her enjoy competition, surprising gifts and rhetorical flourish without making every answer a cryptic prophecy. A good counterpart can challenge her on undisclosed costs and still appreciate that she notices the effort behind a surprise. One who calls her “the moon” need not be accepted as the sole person to understand her; one who offers friendship is not entitled to the prior bond they did not live [IUN-E09, E20, E23, E28, E33, C07, C23, C27].

Do not select an exclusive romantic outcome by citing the bracelet, Ascension IV or a moonwatching branch in isolation. If she asks for something other than power, leave its object open until the invented scene supplies a clear wish and an unpressured reply. If she says she “sees” a future, decide whether she means memory, imagination, metaphor, ordinary inference, or a specifically established ability; the source-backed post-trade default is **not** precise fate-peering. Her exact localized text and eventual performed delivery may matter to a line-specific reading, but no dub-specific emotion is certified here [IUN-E14, E20, E23, E35, E36, C07, C17, C30, C31].

A generated sample should carry a state note with ability, source era, memory relations, who knows the risk, route choice, and whether the words are invented. Review it for child-counterfactual certainty, remnant-as-twin confusion, returned-friend memory repair, self-sacrifice romanticization, archive-to-quest chronology leakage, source-key/row-ID mismatch, restored-future-sight assertion, and unearned consent. If any is material and unresolved, abstain or mark alternatives.
