# Iuno — working inside uncertainty after prophecy

Two later Chapter 2.7 states make Iuno's post-trade competence more specific than either “still an oracle” or “no longer able to help.” Their raw state keys are `剧情_2_7_黎那汐塔主线_上半_2_25_1` at `flow#/9973/2` and `剧情_2_7_黎那汐塔主线_上半_2_27_1` at `flow#/9975/5`. The retained quest-context reference links the latter state through PlotHandBook `#/57` to quest `158800019`; it does **not** provide an exact-state quest join for `9973`. Static state-key numbering and source-row proximity are not an executed route trace. The two actions should be read as local evidence of what she can say and do in this later crisis, not as an automatically continuous recording [IUN-E41–E42].

## Watchfulness is not renewed clear fate-peering

At `9973/2`, all eight accepted Iuno TalkItems are source-unvoiced: the raw items omit `PlayVoice` and the normalized selection has no decoded cohort. Rover's opening response forks. A sympathy question leads to T1, where she describes Priestesses seeking certainty inside Chaos. A question about Septimont's Central Hub instead leads through T2–4: the prior Tidespawn attack damaged multiple beacons, so transmitted data have error, while Priestesses are sensitive to Dark Tide fluctuations and assume observation work. T1 jumps to the common T5; T4 also jumps to T5. T5–7 turn from surveillance to Rover's workload: she notices Rover carrying too much, gives casting materials for camp blacksmiths to make useful weapons, and says others remain beside them. The two explanation routes must not be fused into a longer speech that one player necessarily heard; the material gift and group reassurance are common [IUN-E41/C36].

The line's epistemic structure matters. A damaged measuring network explains *why* human observers are needed in this crisis. It does not imply that all technology is intrinsically worse than priestly perception, nor that Iuno can perfectly predict the Dark Tide. EN at `Main_Rinascita_2_12_209_7` says the Priestesses are more attuned than any device; ZH/KO merely say they are more sensitive to fluctuations, and JA compares them with other people. The stronger EN device hierarchy should not silently become a cross-language law. In all four witnesses she is looking for a reliable signal amid uncertainty, not claiming the old clear sight of fate-threads that she explicitly says she traded away at `flow#/8386/4/19–20`. Her confidence is operational and collective: even the closing comfort uses “we,” not “only I will save you” [IUN-E14/E41, C01/C36].

## Advising an anchor is conditional field work

At `9975/5`, Iuno's eight named turns (speaker `250024`, T1–8) are raw `PlayVoice:true` and each has four selected PCM-valid render associations. These are eight possible semantic occurrences across a branching action, not eight lines proven to be heard in one route. Augusta's status question at T0 is hers, not Iuno's; her later reassurance to Rover at T11 is likewise Augusta's. Iuno reports a stabilizing frequency and says it is the best present window to enter. She compares the possible Leviathan-built Dark Tide world to an earlier experience of Chaos *if* the premise is right. She reasons that Galbrena, who has ties to the Tide and has chosen to hunt the Threnodian, is likely to know a path. This is a plausible inference from Galbrena's stated aim and experience, not proof that she can navigate every region safely or that she has already guided Rover in this action [IUN-E42/C37].

Iuno instructs Rover to find Galbrena's “existence” within disordered frequencies and meet her. At T5 Rover may infer that the task is to Anchor Galbrena (option `Main_Rinascita_2_12_21_7`, JumpTalk 7/T6) or ask how (option `_21_8`, JumpTalk 8/T7). T6 praises the inference; T7 reminds Rover of anchoring; both jump to TalkId 9/T8, where she gives the shared procedural aim: experience, feel and understand as much as possible about Galbrena, then recognize and hold a trace of her within Chaos. The option responses are mutually exclusive; the common instruction is not. ZH/EN/JA use anchoring language at T7 while KO calls it connection. That lexical fork does not itself prove a new physical ability in Iuno, a coercive claim over Galbrena, or a successful future reunion. The advice is to Rover and uses understanding of a distinct person as a clue, not permission to overwrite that person's will [IUN-E42/C37].

The apparent continuity with Iuno's own return is thematically fertile but mechanically incomplete. Earlier sources show Rover anchoring Iuno's fragmented autobiographical self; this later line asks Rover to locate another person's frequency trace. Both use an anchor vocabulary, but the target, crisis and evidence differ. It is tempting to write Iuno as an expert who can instantly diagnose the right path through any imagined inner world. The raw conditional “if,” unstable Tide, damaged observation system and route-dependent answers resist that competence inflation. They also resist the opposite reduction: losing clear prophecy did not strip her of perception, memory, tactical inference or the ability to teach [IUN-E17/E20/E41–E42, C36–C37].

## Retrieval, revisions and scene-use boundaries

The `9975/5` eight Iuno turns join to 32 selected four-dub PCM-valid render associations, but the per-render complete-line rows do not populate explicit Wwise event or numeric-media IDs. They establish a source→text→selected WEM/PCM/FLAC path at the retained mapping level, not independent event→bank proof or a heard performance. The `9973/2` common material offer has four-language text but no selected decoded cohort; it must not borrow Iuno's delivery from `9975/5`. A runtime review could resolve chosen Rover replies, entry timing, what instruments Iuno is actually using, how the gift is staged, and whether Galbrena is subsequently found. Exact four-dub human listening could refine speech register; it cannot change the raw branch topology or upgrade a conditional analogy into observed fact.

For a hypothetical scene, specify whether Iuno is monitoring the Tide, advising Rover before entry, or speaking after an actual navigation outcome. Let her state what is uncertain, use damaged-data context where relevant, rely on other Priestesses and blacksmiths, and offer help that does not make Rover's choice or Galbrena's personhood disappear. The most useful new model constraint is not “she is less prophetic” in the abstract. It is that she can act *intelligently without certainty* and can remind a companion that a dangerous burden belongs to a group, while declining to pretend she has verified the ending [IUN-E41–E42/C36–C37].
