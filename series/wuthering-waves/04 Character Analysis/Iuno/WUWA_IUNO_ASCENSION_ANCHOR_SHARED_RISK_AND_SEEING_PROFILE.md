# Iuno — an anchor, a warning, a wish, and the language of seeing

The five ascension addresses are a concentrated test of Iuno's model. They place growing power beside emotion, memory and connection; move through a lunar cycle; warn the addressee about a shared unknown; ask for something wanted beyond strength; and end with a montage of cold flame, Chaos and the addressee catching the moon. An English-only reading can make them a smooth upward arc from affection to restored prophetic vision. The pinned four-language evidence supports a more useful, less settled reading. These are archive progression lines, not a dated five-scene quest or a logged answer to the fourth address. The [evidence matrix](WUWA_IUNO_EVIDENCE_AND_FALSIFICATION_MATRIX.md) records IUN-E34–E36 and C28–C31; the [continuous deep dive](WUWA_IUNO_CHARACTER_DEEP_DIVE_PRE_AV.md) supplies the independent live-story controls.

## The exact source join matters

The first ascension's localized text key is `FavorWord_141027_Content`, at `favorword#/2566`, although its raw archive row ID is 141028. The other four content keys run `_141028` through `_141031` at `favorword#/2567–2570`, while their row IDs run 141029–141032. Treating row ID and content-key suffix as interchangeable would turn Ascension II into I, and a fifth request for `_141032_Content` would retrieve *Heavy Attack I*. The [selected audio case index](AUDIO_MATCHED_SEMANTIC_CASES.json) therefore carries both source locator and text key and joins on the semantic voice occurrence, not a guessed integer suffix. This is a provenance correction, not a literary inference [IUN-E34, E35, E36].

The five titles indicate menu progression. They do not show when a player unlocked each line relative to Iuno's field injury, temporary disappearance, return, later evacuation, or optional moonwatching. Some images recall those events, and the addressee is addressed as someone who has helped anchor her; that does not supply a dated conversation in which the addressee confirms the metaphor or consented to any later request. Source-surface and branch state must remain inputs rather than silently become chronology [IUN-C22, C28–C31].

## I–II: power is not detached from a remembered person

Ascension I links increasing manifestations of power to emotion, memory and connection, then lightly credits the addressee with becoming practiced at anchoring her (`FavorWord_141027_Content`). That is consistent with the Chaos recovery in `FavorStory_141005_Content` and the exact `flow#/7747/5` to `7764/3` sequence: Rover's remembrance and autobiographical cues help a partial Iuno cohere. It is not proof that one ascension increment caused her return, that memory can be restored to every friend through character growth, or that the game has disclosed a complete physical mechanism. Japanese notably phrases further power manifestation as something needed, while English, Chinese and Korean more directly describe it growing. The shared conceptual field does not erase that modal difference [IUN-E07, E17, E34, C12, C28].

Ascension II shifts to moon-to-moon change and an unmeasurable scale (`FavorWord_141028_Content`). Japanese makes the growth of *her power* more explicit than the Chinese anchor's imagery of one moon giving way to another. Korean also invokes a limit to measurement. None gives a numerical capacity, a claim of infinite lives already traversed, or a technical ledger of the circular device shown in three UI rasters. The line fits her pride and appetite for possibility, but her earlier practical coordination with Augusta and later ordinary civilian action prevent treating it as a new omnipotent mode. More power does not remove the need for allies or the possibility of error [IUN-E13, E22, E25, E34].

## III: the warning does not travel backward in time

Ascension III asks whether the addressee is sure about indulging her and suggests they *might both* fall into an unknown worse than Chaos (`FavorWord_141029_Content`). Unlike a simple triumphant level-up line, it notices another person's exposure. That concern has moral importance because in the dated plan before her disappearance Iuno minimized or concealed the cost to herself, and after returning acknowledged that omission (`flow#/8408/3`; `7759/3/17–26`). Her later conditional promise to give Rover a full account next time also shows a changed stated norm for shared decisions. Yet the archive warning has no retained story-state placement, specified operational plan, or response. It cannot prove she warned Augusta about erasure before the old plan, that Rover understood a concrete new risk, or that either accepted it. The risk is grammatically possible, not a reported completed joint fall [IUN-E15, E16, E20, E32, E35, C08, C29].

This distinction is useful for hypothetical scenes. Iuno need not be scripted as permanently incapable of warning people; the third address supports the possibility that she can make shared danger visible. Nor may a model let the presence of a warning substitute for the actual information another person needs. Ask what mechanism, likely cost and alternatives are known now, and what the counterpart can still decline. Her separate postreturn request to keep her Chaos experience private concerns publication of her past; it is not a veto on collaborators knowing material prospective risk. The [reconnection/privacy specialist](WUWA_IUNO_RECONNECTION_PRIVACY_AND_DISCLOSURE_PROFILE.md) holds that distinction against the three alternative Rover responses at `7759/3` [IUN-C26, C29].

## IV: wanting is not receiving

Ascension IV asks whether Iuno may request something she likes besides power and warns the addressee to consider an answer carefully (`FavorWord_141030_Content`). English and Chinese leave the desired thing unnamed. Japanese adds an image of not letting go of the addressee's hand until she obtains it; Korean uses a more forceful warning that she will not leave the addressee alone. These intensify proximity or insistence differently, but none supplies a selected answer, a filmed act of physical restraint, or proof that Rover is the unnamed thing she wants. The address can be playful, serious or mixed in performance; those acoustic alternatives remain untested. Its implication that power is not her only desire is supported more securely than any particular romance conclusion [IUN-E09, E23, E35, C10, C30].

An ethically faithful model can let Iuno be ambitious, teasing and direct about a wish. It should also let a counterpart decline. Her earlier interest in surprise, fruit, the sea, a moonlit meeting and new friendships gives many possible objects of desire, none of which should be selected by an analyst to close the archive's deliberate openness. A strong scene can make the difference between offer, negotiation and acceptance part of the drama. It should not use the more possessive Japanese or Korean formulation as a license to overwrite consent in all versions [IUN-E28–E31, E35, C23–C25, C30].

## V: what does “see” mean after the trade?

Ascension V names impossible-sounding cold flames, overturned completion, Chaos, illusion, reality, and the addressee catching the moon (`FavorWord_141031_Content`). The Chinese and English wording makes seeing feel present, while Japanese and Korean use past/perfective forms. This may be a poetic montage of what she has lived through, an image of a transformed future, a visionary register, or some combination. The line itself does not separate these readings. The strong counterweight is her direct quest explanation at `flow#/8386/4/19`: she says she traded fate-peering for a power that can resist/anchor the Dark Tide. Her later practical behavior does not independently demonstrate recovered exact future sight. It would be as unfounded to ban all visionary language from her voice as to certify a new clear-vision ability from the verb “see” alone [IUN-E02, E14, E21, E36, C01, C31].

The final address returns the addressee to the center of her imagery; it does not turn that person into the owner of the moon or a guaranteed romantic partner. In the live story Rover's exceptional remembrance matters, but other friends' memories remain broken and must be rebuilt prospectively. A model that treats Ascension V as proof all memory and uncertainty problems have been solved would erase the two postreturn toasts and her later admission that the future is not perfectly known. If later source material actually shows precise fate-peering again, C31 should be revised with that direct evidence and placed in time; this packet does not foreclose that possibility [IUN-E05, E07, E20, E33, E36].

## What the audio selection can and cannot add

All five keys now have exact four-language render joins: twenty added renders with explicit event/numeric-media IDs, source WEM and decoded PCM/FLAC hashes, and clean machine QC in the selected sample. That raises the [AV crosswalk](WUWA_IUNO_AV_HUMAN_RETRIEVAL_CROSSWALK.md) to 24 cases/96 renders, while 44 older rows still lack paired event/media IDs. The new timing differences—for example Ascension V runs roughly 15.8 s in English and 21.6 s in Chinese—cannot tell us whether the delivery is desire, warning, prophecy, self-irony or gratitude. A human reviewer should listen to each language against its *own* text, note pauses and inflection with timecodes, and keep the archive source surface visible. Until then, this specialist makes textual and provenance claims only, not an actor-performance profile.
