# WUWA narrative deep-reading protocol

## Unit selection

A deep reading must declare its bounded unit: patch, chapter, act, quest chain, quest, event, companion story, archive sequence, or another justified narrative unit. Stable scope notation should combine a human-readable title with technical IDs where available.

Do not assume one quest wrapper equals one coherent dramatic scene. Preserve branch, action, TalkSequence, option, and flow-state structure.

## Required front matter

Record:

- series and artifact type;
- stable scope and technical IDs;
- source generation/commit;
- release and diegetic chronology status;
- languages/modalities reviewed;
- authority and revision state;
- supersession fields.

## Reading sequence

1. Resolve exact story-unit evidence and source locators.
2. Reconstruct scene order and branching without forcing unresolved nodes.
3. Identify participants through occurrence-level identity decisions.
4. Read the complete local context before extracting claims.
5. Compare high-value semantic lines across official textual witnesses.
6. Route to voice and visual evidence only where the modality is available and directly reviewed.
7. Record character-state, relationship, world/faction, chronology, and open-question deltas.
8. Distinguish narrative fact, focalization, public myth, summary, inference, and speculation.

## Analytical lenses

Use only lenses warranted by the unit, potentially including:

- narrative architecture and causal sequence;
- character agency and constrained choice;
- identity, memory, role, and personhood;
- relationship-state change;
- institution, faction, legitimacy, and political economy;
- worldbuilding and metaphysics;
- rhetoric, localization, and speech;
- voice, music, staging, framing, animation, and editing;
- ordinary-life/coda material;
- rival interpretations and unresolved contradictions.

## Audiovisual honesty boundary

Text timing, subtitles, and clip existence are not visual review. Mark camera, gesture, gaze, blocking, lighting, music, editing, and acting claims `OPEN` until directly observed.

## Closeout

A reading closes only when it:

- states the exact covered and missing source surfaces;
- updates relevant longitudinal ledgers;
- records new or revised claims and open questions;
- preserves branch/identity uncertainty;
- provides exact evidence routes;
- identifies the next sequential unit without silently authorizing it.
