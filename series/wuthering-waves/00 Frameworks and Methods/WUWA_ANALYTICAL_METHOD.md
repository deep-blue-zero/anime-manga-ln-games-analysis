# Wuthering Waves analytical method

## Purpose

This method governs literary, character, relational, institutional, audiovisual, and longitudinal analysis of *Wuthering Waves*. It is designed for a live-service game whose evidence is distributed across story flow, profile/archive material, messages, localization witnesses, voice performances, audiovisual staging, patch chronology, and technical identity systems.

It does not require every analytical operation to produce the same artifact set. It requires every claim to have a clear scope, source boundary, authority state, evidentiary route, and revision behavior.

## Core commitments

### 1. Source before archetype

Public summaries, fandom labels, promotional shorthand, and genre expectations may nominate questions. They do not settle characterization. Start from the pinned source/evidence release and distinguish direct evidence from inference.

### 2. Identity must be adjudicated, not name-matched

A displayed name, technical speaker ID, role ID, alternate form, compound speaker, hidden generic ID, message ID, or runtime voice object is not automatically the same analytical person. Use the evidence bridge's identity crosswalk and exact occurrence decisions.

Global mapping of generic IDs is prohibited unless the source proves it. Ambiguous and compound occurrences remain typed as such.

### 3. State selection precedes personality prediction

A live-service character may differ materially across developmental periods, personas, alternate forms, operational contexts, memory states, public roles, or relationship states. Every reconstruction or prediction must select a state slice—or abstain.

Operational context is not automatically a developmental state. Temporary disguise, mission role, combat condition, or tactical secrecy should not be promoted into personality development without evidence.

### 4. Ordinary life is analytically material

Food, drink, rest, hobbies, leisure, crowds, solitude, practical skills, embarrassment, humor, aesthetic taste, routine, gifting, learning behavior, and low-stakes frustration are not decorative trivia. They often supply the strongest evidence for how a character behaves when crisis ethics are not controlling the scene.

These facts must still carry limits. A favorite food does not determine every restaurant choice; one childhood prank does not prove identical adult conduct; affinity-gated material does not define every relationship state.

### 5. Claims require counterevidence

Every major interpretive claim should record:

- supporting evidence;
- counterevidence or a falsifying condition;
- state and situation scope;
- relationship scope where relevant;
- confidence/epistemic status;
- alternative reading;
- extrapolation limit.

A contradiction is often the most useful modeling fact. Do not "solve" it by deleting one side.

### 6. Multilingual witnesses are distinct

`zh-Hans` is the current semantic anchor for the pinned normalized source. Japanese, Korean, and English are official localization and performance witnesses. Agreement across them can strengthen a semantic claim; divergence may reveal localization or performance choices.

No localization witness automatically replaces the semantic anchor. No dub automatically defines the universal character.

### 7. Machine measurement and human interpretation are complementary

Comprehensive machine analysis may process the complete usable voice corpus and discover distributions, contrasts, clusters, and outliers. It may not convert acoustic proxies into emotions or acting motives by itself.

Human review should interrogate machine findings, listen to selected exemplars and counterexamples, and record whether a proposed interpretation is preserved, strengthened, revised, downgraded, rejected, or left open.

### 8. Visual evidence must be directly reviewed

Dialogue and subtitles do not prove camera placement, gaze, blocking, animation, gesture, lighting, editing rhythm, or musical emphasis. Claiming those requires a bounded audiovisual witness and direct observation.

### 9. Live-service chronology is versioned

Distinguish:

- release chronology;
- diegetic chronology;
- epistemic chronology for the player;
- what the character knows at a given time;
- archive/profile unlock order;
- retellings, summaries, and retrospective framing.

A later patch may revise the current analytical model, but it does not erase the earlier source-bounded state.

### 10. Git and Drive have different responsibilities

Drive stores primary and deterministic derived evidence. Git stores analysis. Large evidence ledgers, raw flow states, normalized corpora, voice objects, and video do not migrate into Git merely because they are convenient.

Git artifacts should preserve exact evidence IDs, text keys, source locators, Drive routes, and source-generation identifiers sufficient to retrieve the underlying evidence.

## Analytical workflow

1. Resolve the WUWA current-state map and source boundary.
2. Choose a bounded analytical unit: character, quest/arc, relationship, institution, motif, performance question, or longitudinal claim.
3. Route through the narrowest Drive evidence package capable of supporting the work.
4. Audit identity and chronology before interpreting behavior.
5. Read the relevant source sequence in context, not as isolated quotations.
6. Separate source finding, machine measurement, human observation, inference, and speculation.
7. Draft claims with counterevidence and extrapolation limits.
8. Update the canonical topical home instead of creating a near-duplicate synthesis.
9. Update required indexes, ledgers, current-state records, and revision controls in the same Git change.
10. Run a fidelity or rival-reading check before calling a reconstruction usable.

## Authority states

- `canonical`: current governing method or accepted analytical authority for its declared scope.
- `active_provisional`: current but still open to material revision.
- `superseded`: replaced by a named successor; retained for provenance.
- `historical_legacy`: historically meaningful but not current authority.

Source freeze and analytical authority are separate. A source generation can be frozen while analysis over it remains provisional.

## Revision vocabulary

Use the following transitions for material reinterpretation:

- `PRESERVE`
- `STRENGTHEN`
- `REVISE`
- `DOWNGRADE`
- `REJECT`
- `OPEN`

Git history records that wording changed. A claim-revision ledger records why the interpretation changed.

## Proportionality rule

Architecture follows recurring analytical need. Do not make every character imitate Cartethyia's artifact count. Split a responsibility into a standalone document only when it accumulates enough evidence, supports repeated retrieval, or benefits from independent revision.
