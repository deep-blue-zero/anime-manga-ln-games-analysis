# Current authority for WUWA character packets

On 2026-09-09, the owner explicitly adopted the existing Chisa, Cartethyia, Aemeath, Denia, and Lynae character packets as current analytical authorities and requested corresponding changes to WUWA character-synthesis guidance. This is an owner-authorized authority adoption, effective upon governed integration into `main`; it is not a new source-reading or audiovisual pass.

## Decision and scope

All 55 packet documents now carry the complete current-authority quartet: `status: active_provisional`, `supersedes: []`, `superseded_by: []`, and `do_not_use_as_current_authority: false`. Any retained legacy `do_not_use_as_current_git_authority` field is also false. Local-draft authority/release fields are updated, and each document carries a publication note distinguishing its original preparation statements from the owner's current adoption.

The adopted packets comprise 12 Chisa, 12 Cartethyia, 11 Aemeath, 11 Denia, and 9 Lynae documents. Their current-state routers identify their scope. Nomination plans and retrieval crosswalks are current planning references, not reviewed AV evidence; fidelity probes are bounded tests, not independent accuracy measurements. The [manifest](WUWA_CHARACTER_PACKET_AUTHORITY_ADOPTION_MANIFEST.json) binds every authority edit to before/after hashes. Earlier import/hash records remain historical records of earlier bytes.

Missing AV is not a whole-model veto. Completed textual interpretation and actually reviewed audio measurements remain usable authority within their stated bounds. Direct acting, staging, gesture, camera, design, music, and editing findings require their corresponding evidence; those claims remain open when unreviewed. A material contradiction can revise dependent claims through the existing evidence/revision process.

## Coverage remains truthful

This adoption does not declare full machine-voice profiling where a packet reports only supplied metadata or partial processing. Chisa explicitly does not claim `machine_voice_profiled`; Lynae reports extensive but not protocol-complete processing and unresolved phone-message dispatch. Other packet-specific gaps, language coverage, missing context, historical-comparison limits, identity exclusions, and performance abstentions remain in force. No new recording was decoded or listened to, and no video was directly reviewed during this repository operation.

The owner's estimate that AV may contribute roughly 5–10% of model fidelity motivates prioritization; it is not an empirically established score, a guaranteed bound, or a percentage applied to individual claims. The guidance defines no automatic numerical penalty for missing modalities.

## Guidance and generation

The analytical method, character reconstruction protocol, character-folder contract, machine-voice protocol, and evidence-routing guidance now separate authority from modality completion. Future substantial accepted text/audio packets use the complete current-authority quartet at generation. Unaccepted drafts remain `draft_noncurrent`; owner-retired artifacts remain `historical_legacy`. Source language, exact audio coverage, evidence locators, and pending responsibilities remain mandatory.

This is a WUWA-specific guidance amendment. It does not alter repository-wide authority epochs, frozen migration records, validation code, global character-curation ownership, or Drive state. Generated character discovery continues through its designated curation agent. New enrollment may lag; affected existing references must remain valid before integration.
