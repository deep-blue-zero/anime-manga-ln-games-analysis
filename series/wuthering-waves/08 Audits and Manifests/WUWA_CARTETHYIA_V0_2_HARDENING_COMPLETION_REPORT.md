## Owner questions

### 1. How did the interpretation change?

The main change is from near-solitary self-authorship to relational authorship. Cartethyia still answers for inherited conditions and rejects roles that monopolize her future, but the evidence also shows that identity and moral agency are held through relationships capable of recognition and opposition. Her ordinary pleasures and limitations now sit inside the interpretation rather than appearing as decorative exceptions to crisis ethics.

### 2. Which material claims changed status?

The full transition record is `WUWA_CARTETHYIA_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md`. The most consequential transitions are:

- **REVISE** “anti-utilitarian” to particularist and anti-futility;
- **REVISE** love/romance-dependent rescue to reciprocal moral authority;
- **REVISE** solitary authorship to relationally negotiated authorship;
- **STRENGTHEN** duty-reinscription as the strongest counter-reading;
- **STRENGTHEN** ordinary personality and relationship evidence density;
- **DOWNGRADE** any universal personality inference made from one dub;
- **OPEN** the four missing states, direct staging, and four-dub acting centers.

No material character claim was rejected outright.

### 3. What direct audiovisual evidence was added?

Ten bounded clips were prepared from a full English client-recorded playthrough: initial presentation, inverted bridge, amnesia/truth, Blessed Maiden memory, grief, integration/self-erasure, particular rescue, name choice, reciprocal defiance, and ordinary coda. Exact spans, byte counts, and SHA-256 values are in `WUWA_CARTETHYIA_DIRECT_AUDIOVISUAL_REVIEW_LEDGER.md`. This adds reviewable evidence availability, not completed visual findings.

### 4. What human listening evidence was added?

The technical substrate is new and complete: 23 matched semantic moments across Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English; 92 provenance-bound FLACs; exact WEM and FLAC hashes; semantic and localized occurrence identities; and canonical PCM verification. No subjective listening result was added because the run lacked a human listening surface. The distinction is deliberate.

### 5. Did the proposed Maiden/Knight performance hypothesis survive?

It remains **OPEN**, not survived or falsified. The cohort is appropriately stratified to test it, but duration, punctuation, and waveform identity cannot answer an acting question.

### 6. Which dub is strongest or weakest under the hypothesis?

None can be ranked from the completed evidence. Korean was the least specified hypothesis, but that is not evidence that it is a midpoint or the weakest performance center. The listening protocol explicitly allows the midpoint hypothesis to fail.

### 7. Did the missing flow states change the reading?

No recovered content exists, so there is no justified change to the core textual reading. The four gaps may refine transition, optional-memory, or ending/coda chronology. `11_27` has the greatest potential staging materiality, but none presently contradicts the attested identity, integration, rescue, or repair claims.

### 8. Which ordinary traits were previously underrepresented?

Hot bread and homecoming, dancing and crowds, Laurus Salad and cooking competence, dislike of fish, scenic flight, quiet Ciaccona listening, naps and scent memories, knight play, embarrassment at dramatized sainthood and poor scripture grades, desire for competence reassurance, survival/entrance/haggling learning, contextual patience, and the limit on projecting childhood mischief into adulthood.

### 9. What major uncertainties remain?

Direct audiovisual staging; human-observed four-dub acting centers; the content and runtime placement of four absent flow states; the extent of repair with Cantarella; the stability of Galbrena intimacy beyond operational trust; and whether later strategic self-sacrifice ultimately weakens the claimed lesson of reciprocal rescue.

### 10. Is the package canonical?

No. It remains `active_provisional` and `mutable_active`. The pinned Arikatsu view is the canonical normalized semantic source; installed-client packages/audio are canonical raw evidence. Those authority layers remain distinct from the provisional analysis.
