## Artifact groups

- Frameworks/methods: analytical method, character reconstruction, character-folder contract, machine voice protocol, narrative deep-reading protocol, longitudinal architecture, evidence routing.
- Source control: source boundary, Drive release pointer, source-coverage audit.
- Sequential layer: naming/closeout contract; no completed readings.
- Longitudinal layer: character state, relationship network, world/faction, chronology, open questions.
- Character layer: **18 owner-adopted current character reconstruction packets** (`0` `draft_noncurrent`), tiered by coverage depth into **three `canonical` packets** (`Cartethyia` imported V0.2 package plus twelve-document rebuild, `Augusta`, and `Iuno`, each with substantial Chapter II main-arc AV resolution, four-dub ear calibration, and claim/model reconciliation; Cartethyia's retired older ordinary-life profile remains `historical_legacy` under `90 Legacy and Superseded/`) and **fifteen `active_provisional` packets** (six partial-AV Chapter II packets — `Brant`, `Cantarella`, `Phoebe`, `Qiuyuan`, `Shorekeeper`, `Zani` — plus nine text/machine-audio packets — `Changli`, `Jinhsi`, `Luuk Herssen`, `Yinlin`, `Sigrika`, `Chisa`, `Lynae`, `Aemeath`, `Denia`). A separate V0.2 static visual-design layer (`status: active_provisional`) contributes three Git artifacts for each of the 18 characters: individualized Markdown analysis, structured claim JSON, and an official-client provenance/reverse-index manifest. - Audit layer: analytical corpus audit, authority audit, bootstrap path/hash manifest, imported Cartethyia hardening report; `WUWA_PRE_AV_CHARACTER_PACKET_IMPORT.md`, `WUWA_CHISA_PRE_AV_REBUILD_IMPORT.md`, `WUWA_CHARACTER_PACKET_AUTHORITY_ADOPTION.md`, and `WUWA_SIGRIKA_V0_2_IMPORT.md` (with their manifests) record the earlier packet imports and adoptions. `WUWA_CHARACTER_VISUAL_PROFILE_V0_2_IMPORT.md` and its manifest bind the 18-character visual-profile layer to the read-only extraction receipt while excluding the 54 PNGs. `WUWA_PRE_AV_BRANCH_INTEGRATION_REPAIR_MANIFEST.json` records the historical Cartethyia branch repairs and owner-requested archival of the older ordinary-life profile. `WUWA_RINASCITA_CH2_SHARED_AV_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md`, `WUWA_RINASCITA_CH2_FOUR_DUB_VOICE_LEDGER.md`, and `WUWA_RINASCITA_CH2_SHARED_AV_RECONCILIATION_MANIFEST.json` preserve the historical Chapter II (*Rinascita*) 1080p60 English-dubbed shared audiovisual pass (`WUWA_RINASCITA_CH2_SHARED_AV_V01`, `483` crosswalk dispositions, `107` four-dub moments / `428` renders) at its original snapshot. `WUWA_CH2_AV_CHARACTER_PACKET_ADOPTION_2026_11_18.md` and `WUWA_CH2_CURRENT_SOURCE_VALIDATION_2026_11_19.json` record the owner's subsequent promotion of all remaining draft packets, coverage-depth `canonical` vs `active_provisional` tiering across all 18 packets, semantic and source-fidelity acceptance verification, and SHA-256 bindings for both historical draft validation runs and regenerated current-authority `VALIDATION_REPORT.json` files.
