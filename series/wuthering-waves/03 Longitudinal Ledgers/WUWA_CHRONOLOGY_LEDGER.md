# WUWA chronology ledger

## Chronology ontology

Each entry may distinguish:

- `release_order` — patch/publication sequence;
- `diegetic_order` — best-supported fictional sequence;
- `player_epistemic_order` — when the player learns the information;
- `character_epistemic_state` — what the focal character knows at that moment;
- `retrospective_or_reenacted` — memory, recording, play, summary, archive, or reconstructed event;
- `confidence` — certain, strong, provisional, conflicting, or open.

Array position and unlock order are not automatically fictional chronology.
