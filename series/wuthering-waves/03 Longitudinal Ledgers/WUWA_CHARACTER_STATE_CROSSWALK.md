- state ID and label;

- start/end evidence and chronology confidence;
- governing goals, constraints, and behavioral logic;
- transition cause;
- relationships materially changed;
- source generation;
- character-specific ledger/monograph route;
- unresolved alternatives.

Temporary mood, combat condition, costume, or mission assignment is not automatically a state.
