# WUWA world and faction ledger

This ledger is initialized as a schema and current-boundary record. It deliberately does not pretend that the Cartethyia character package constitutes a title-wide faction synthesis.

## Initial analyzed institutional relation

### Rinascita / the Order / divine authority

Current evidence from Cartethyia supports a bounded distinction between:

- people's deeds, hope, faith, and solidarity;
- institutional claims to sacred authority;
- divine election and ownership of office;
- civic responsibility after rejection of inherited entitlement.

Cartethyia's post-Leviathan position preserves human investment in faith while rejecting automatic divine rule. She declines the Primus title yet accepts responsibility for repairing the Order's wrongs. This is character-centered evidence, not yet a complete institutional history.

## Future entry contract

For each region, institution, faction, metaphysical power, or durable political-economic structure, record:

- stable entity ID and aliases;
- release and diegetic chronology;
- leadership/authority claims;
- membership and internal factions;
- resources, coercive capacity, legitimacy, and public narrative;
- relationships with Sentinels, Resonators, cities, and other factions;
- source versus propaganda/retelling distinctions;
- state changes by patch/story unit;
- disputed interpretations and open evidence;
- linked sequential and specialist syntheses.

Do not infer a faction's unified view from one representative unless the source warrants it.
