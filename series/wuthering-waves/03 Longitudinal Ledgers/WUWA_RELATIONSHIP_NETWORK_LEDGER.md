## Edge contract

Every future entry should identify:

- participants and continuity/state scope;
- relation kind: interpersonal, civic, institutional, antagonist, relation-to-self/form;
- evidence density;
- trust, intimacy, obligation, conflict, asymmetry, protection, secrecy, and reciprocity only where supported;
- transition points;
- alternate readings;
- unresolved labels such as romance;
- canonical character/sequential artifact route.

Do not turn co-occurrence or compound speaker identity into a relationship claim.
