# Story, disclosure and recurrence atlas

## Responsibility

This atlas relates source-established local units without replacing their full analyses. It preserves each unit's particular causal and interpretive problem, its disclosure frontier, local rules and possible connections. There are **no narrative units or recurrence findings** at INITIAL. The source's actual segmentation must be learned under an authorized run.

## Schemas

| Register / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Local unit `WW-U-nnnn` | Witness/version; verified chapter/section and boundaries; segmentation rationale; movements; full-reading route; source-established subjects/setting; central local question; causal conditions/options/consequences; closure/open endpoint; rival account; evidence; limits |
| Sequence constraint `WW-T-nnnn` | Two or more local/event positions; disclosure order; story/travel-time relation with uncertainty; publication/edition relation kept separate; supporting/contrary evidence; feasible alternatives; admitted frontier; mode |
| Knowledge position `WW-K-nnnn` | Narrator/focalizer/subject/reader; proposition; known/believed/rumored/withheld/unknown state; acquisition/disclosure; audience; source evidence; reliability of this act; later revision and retrospective limits |
| Recurrence edge `WW-X-nnnn` | Source units; relation type; exact identity or analogy evidence; meaningful variation; counterinstances; competing grouping; confidence; full comparative argument; allowed generalization scope |
| Local rule/condition `WW-LW-nnnn` | Unit/setting; asserted rule and speaker; observed operation/enforcement; exceptions; who benefits/is constrained; resources/choices; evaluation positions; evidence; whether any cross-unit generalization is supported |
| Open endpoint `WW-OE-nnnn` | Unit; unresolved event/motive/outcome; explicitly absent evidence versus genuine alternatives; character/reader knowledge; future test allowed only within run; linked claim/dispute; closure history |

Recurrence relation types are `SAME_REFERENT_CONFIRMED / POSSIBLE_SAME_REFERENT / SAME_EVENT_RETOLD / FORMAL_RHYME / THEMATIC_ANALOGY / SITUATION_VARIATION / LEXICAL_SIMILARITY / UNRESOLVED`. A candidate link cannot be silently promoted because a synthesis prefers continuity. Same title/name or similar social function is insufficient alone.

Epistemic modes are `PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY / RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY / EDITION_COMPARATIVE / UNRESOLVED`. Keep the earlier reader's uncertainty even after a later source resolves identity. A main/original comparison stores paired witness identities and cannot overwrite main-lane state.

## Full-argument discipline

Every local-unit record links to the developed full reading. Preserve supporting subjects, mundane activity, language, causal alternatives and ethical positions independent of their usefulness to a recurring subject's development. An atlas blurb is navigation only.

A recurrence proposal must explain selection and exclusion: which units were eligible, which differ, and whether the apparent pattern depends on cherry-picking. A local rule is not world law until evidence supports that scope. A shared structure may host contrary values or different causal mechanisms.

Chronological constraints retain unknowns; do not manufacture dates or one total travel order from disclosure sequence. An unresolved local endpoint remains an analytical result if the source withholds it. Do not search unauthorized later prose or an adaptation to complete the row.

## Update and review

Create unit keys only after actual segmentation and inspection. At unit/volume closeout, append local records, sequence and knowledge changes, local rules and candidate recurrence edges. Record a scoped no-change finding where a dimension was inspected but yielded no supported change. Add link versions; retain earlier edges and reasons when revised.

At checkpoint, retrieve the full local arguments behind central cross-unit claims. Test identity, chronology and scope separately. Review low-stakes/ordinary material and counterinstances, not only memorable conclusions. A material revision triggers the claim/dependency register and affected persona/specialist outputs.
