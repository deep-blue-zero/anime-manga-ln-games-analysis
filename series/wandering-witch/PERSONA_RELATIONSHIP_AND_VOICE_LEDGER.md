# Persona, relationship and voice ledger

## Responsibility and initial restraint

This surface accumulates source-grounded subjects, self-presentation, directed relationships, ordinary-life controls and Japanese written expression. The title identity “Elaina” does not supply a persona model, biography or roster. No subject has been analytically discovered; all characterization and reconstruction coverage are **NOT_ASSESSED**.

A one-encounter relationship is not automatically durable; repeated encounters are not automatically mutual intimacy.

## State change and generalization rules

Classify a proposed change cause as `DISPOSITION / KNOWLEDGE / RELATIONSHIP / CONTEXT / ROLE / RESOURCE / REVEALED_NOT_NEW / UNRESOLVED`. Link prior and revised states, retaining the disclosure boundary and rival cause. Do not treat later disclosure as later life without chronology evidence.

Ordinary-life examples are required controls where available. Preserve practical decisions and mundane exchanges in full prose; absence of drama is not absence of analytical content. A refusal needs a real opportunity; missing depiction does not establish avoidance. Crisis constraints stay attached to the evidence.

Written voice is situated action, not a list of mannerisms. Trace appraisal/objective/speech act/recipient/register and preserve alternative readings. A polished English paraphrase cannot stand in for consequential Japanese distinctions. No performed voice or audio inference belongs to this lane.

## Reconstruction firewall and evaluation boundary

Evidence may support a later conditional model, but no policy or synthetic test exists now. Models, monographs and evaluation records have separate homes. OBSERVED evidence does not become PROMOTED_POLICY without appropriate tests; candidate policies include inhibitors, exceptions, counterevidence and falsifiers.

The architecture specifies twelve descriptive coverage dimensions and six scenario families. A subject with inadequate ordinary/relationship/time coverage remains restricted or uncommissioned. Genuinely held-out tests freeze before source exposure; otherwise label RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent evaluators own readiness judgment and global registry outputs. Synthetic dialogue can never populate an evidence row or become a source quotation.
