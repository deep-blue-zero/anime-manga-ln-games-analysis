# Reading boundary and synthesis register

## Responsibility

This surface records actual source-facing transactions, preserved local arguments, downstream roles and integration gates. It must not turn the count of read volumes into a claim of synthesis readiness. No run or source frontier exists at INITIAL; no source admission is authorized.

| Record / prefix | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Reading transaction `WW-TX-nnnn` | Authorized operation/run; exact witness/unit; entry/exit frontier; local-unit coverage; full output; source/atlas/persona/argument deltas or scoped no-change; retrieval/image/paratext debts; name/reference sample/results; reviewer/disposition; next authorized operation or NONE |

| Role gap `WW-RG-nnnn` | Responsibility; local/cross-unit coverage; missing evidence/context/voice/relationships; effect; targeted return or specialist assignment; independent-study rationale or justified no-specialist decision; acceptance evidence |
| Handoff `WW-HO-nnnn` | Base snapshot; complete input/output paths/digests; source/lane/admission/knowledge boundary; authority; role/owner; unresolved obligations; transfer receipt; full returned files; change/evidence manifest; base-candidate-current drift and acceptance |
| Gate decision `WW-GR-nnnn` | W0–W12 gate; exact boundary/input files; evidence examined; reviewer/independence; defects; pass/hold/bounded acceptance; next permitted operation |

No source acceptance, hash, exposure status or model setting may be fabricated to fill a schema. An absent required value remains unresolved with an owner.

## Atomic closeout and full-output acceptance

A future reading closes only with its full text and synchronized registers. Verify actual source coverage, local-unit boundary rationale, central retrieval, modality debts, claim versions, recurrence identity and directed relationship state. Inspect the bounded name/reference sample required by method §9. A summary and a set of updated rows cannot replace a missing local argument.

A checkpoint preserves full readings and a frozen state at the admitted boundary. It separately audits local integrity, recurrence/generalization, chronology/knowledge and character/voice coverage. Source-complete never bypasses role-gap review or specialist convergence.

A receiving synthesis session gets full governing documents, complete local/volume readings and relevant ledgers. The returning candidate provides full substantive outputs and limitations. Follow the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md): compare the base, candidate and live target; preserve concurrent changes; retrieve consequential evidence; reject loss of causal steps, ordinary material, language ambiguity or competing arguments. One owner mutates this register.
