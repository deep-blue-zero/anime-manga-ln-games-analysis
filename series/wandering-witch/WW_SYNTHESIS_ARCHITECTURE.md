# Wandering Witch synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose, authority and governing question

The core design problem is **local integrity versus cumulative interpretation**. A future reader must preserve a local narrative's causal, linguistic, relational and ethical argument while testing how the numbered corpus connects, varies or refuses to unify such arguments. This design does not assume that the unread work actually has one stable episodic form or one consistently reliable narrator.

The source planning boundary is Japanese main V01–25 plus two separately cataloged 2014 original editions. No prose has been admitted. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the single present authority route; the [method](WW_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs source-facing reading. INITIAL canonical authority covers these rules and attributed metadata only. Owner acceptance, sequential authorization and literary/model readiness remain unclaimed.

The [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md), archive policy and long-series/handoff contracts retain their respective authority. No local convention overrides them.

## 2. Distinct source lanes and evidence precedence

The main numbered witnesses form the proposed anchor reading lane. Exact inspected Japanese text, within a locked witness and authorized boundary, supports literary claims; inventory and historical audit metadata support only source/provenance claims.

The 2014 Upper and Lower editions form a separate editorial-history lane, currently unadmitted. Neither title similarity nor an older publication date proves passage correspondence, causal continuity or an authorized prequel reading. Original Lower is not main V01. A future comparative study must establish an actual crosswalk and preserve unmatched/revised material. It cannot backfill the main lane without an explicit comparative claim.

Illustrations and paratext have their own component and modality boundaries. An image can disagree with a narrated account without automatically resolving it. Archived alternative copies remain provenance witnesses; the manifest's equivalence findings are historical assertions, not fresh byte/content certification. Adaptations, later acquisitions and secondary sources have no automatic admission.

The source register ranks an observed present listing for current presence, a locked receipt for byte identity, an actual inspected component for content, and a reviewed full reading for interpretation. A contradiction in the manifest is retained at the layer where it occurs. No convenient summary field can certify all underlying historical notes.

## 5. Sequential accumulation: local, volume, checkpoint

The prospective reading unit is a verified volume, with finer chapter/section frontiers. The local analytical unit is established from the text rather than presumed from the table of contents. Both identities are stored, so a local question can continue across a boundary without forcing premature closure.

Within an authorized unit, preserve a complete local analysis before using it as evidence of a series pattern. At volume closeout, assess how units are arranged and what the arrangement changes: juxtaposition is evidence of organization, not proof of chronology or a shared moral. Record both recurrence and purposeful variation as hypotheses with counterexamples.

An accepted transaction includes the full reading and synchronized source, atlas, persona, argument and boundary registers. Checkpoint cadence is set in the authorized run before reading and can react to a major recurrence, narrator reclassification or source identity issue. A checkpoint freezes the source frontier, earlier prospective states, full arguments and open questions. It does not summarize away local units to save transfer space.

No unit may enter because the previous unit was finished. The run's explicit scope and the current lock govern admission. The INITIAL lock is CLOSED, with no run record or next source candidate.

## 6. Temporal, epistemic and continuity model

The state key combines `source lane + witness version + admitted frontier + local-unit disclosure position + story/travel-time constraints + narrator/focalizer + subject information + context/role/resources + directed relationship`. Unknowns are explicit. An edition-comparative interpretation has its own mode and paired witnesses.

Keep `PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY`, `RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY`, `EDITION_COMPARATIVE` and `UNRESOLVED` distinct. A later account can identify an earlier figure or reinterpret an ending without granting earlier reader/subject knowledge. Preserve source-required anonymity and the exact point of disclosure.

Recurring names, locations, roles or motifs have separate relation types. “Same name,” “same referent,” “analogous function” and “same event retold” require different evidence. A local convention does not become a universal world rule merely because another setting has a similar feature. A change in self-presentation may be audience-dependent performance, new knowledge or newly disclosed continuity rather than character growth.

Chronology uses relative constraints where dates are unavailable. Preserve multiple feasible orderings when underdetermined. Publication/edition order never silently substitutes for travel chronology.

## 7. Contradictions and revision

Classify apparent conflict as source identity, stale audit metadata, edition revision, chronology/disclosure, narrator/report reliability, subject ignorance, audience/persona, local-rule scope, analyst disagreement, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Keep multiple explanations live until evidence distinguishes them. The current source audit discrepancy belongs to provenance, not fictional unreliability.

Use `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN`. Store old and new formulations, source/epistemic boundary, evidence, counterevidence, rival disposition and dependencies. A recurrence thesis revised at Vn does not rewrite the earlier local reading's valid uncertainty.

Dependency review checks the atlas edge, local argument, subject state, language inference, specialist and model separately. A source correction may affect some but not all. Preserve nonaffected interpretation; do not use “revision” as a pretext for wholesale compression.

## 8. Specialist and monograph readiness

A local-unit study is warranted when its causal/formal or evaluative problem needs sustained treatment beyond the source-facing reading. It must contribute an independent question, not a polished duplicate. A recurrence study needs a stated selection rule, diverse instances, counterinstances and plausible alternative groupings; it cannot stand on a few memorable examples.

A character monograph requires adequate time/context/relationship coverage and ordinary-life evidence, with an explicit statement of what remains inaccessible. It integrates the subject's narration/presentation, actions, language, development and literary role. A relationship study handles directional and systemic issues that cannot be responsibly owned by either monograph alone.

Potential specialists include narrative self-presentation, ordering and local-world variation, if evidence warrants them. They are **anticipated responsibilities**, not findings or mandatory empty directories. The role-gap review decides whether independent specialists are needed and records an evidence-based rationale if full readings and a qualified synthesis can discharge a role without a separate study.

The edition-comparison study is separately scoped because comparing a revised passage is not a sequential main-volume reading. It records omissions and unmatched material as carefully as matches. No official-history or authorial-intent conclusion can rest on title metadata alone.

## 9. Reconstruction as a distinct derived layer

Reconstruction is intended for source-established recurring or sufficiently evidenced subjects, not automatically for every local participant. No model exists and readiness is NOT_ASSESSED. Character discovery and global capability registry outputs belong to designated agents, not this bootstrap.

A future dossier separates evidence/retrieval, operational policy model and independent test record; any monograph remains its own literary artifact. Its identity is `subject@lane/witness-boundary/state`, with known/excluded sources and narrator/subject knowledge limits. Earlier-edition evidence cannot silently train a main-lane model.

Descriptive coverage spans twelve responsibilities: values/goals; appraisal/threat; motives/conflicts; knowledge/errors; attention/reasoning; affect/regulation; disposition/state distinction; directed relations; persona/role/audience; action repertoire/inhibitors; written Japanese voice; development/exceptions. Record evidence density and missing contexts without self-awarded readiness letters.

A policy includes trigger, stakes, current state, response family, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, support, counterevidence, rivals and falsifier. Each projected response records confidence, distance from observed contexts/relationships/states, and an abstention or multiple-plausible-responses rule where the requested situation lacks adequate support. Separate direct speech, behavior, narrated interiority, reports, role/observer presentation and inference. Use OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY; repetition alone does not promote a policy if contexts are homogeneous.

Test scenario families, when evidence permits, include ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, incomplete or false information, changed recipient/relationship, role/audience shift, and a consequential practical or ethical choice. Written voice testing distinguishes decision fidelity from linguistic surface. If a subject lacks adequate scenarios, keep the model restricted or uncommissioned.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before source exposure, with author/evaluator roles separated and latent familiarity disclosed. Once exposed, use `RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST`; it is useful but not prospective validation. An independent evaluator records SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED or UNRESOLVED. Include diagnostic perturbations, decoys, negative controls and baseline comparisons when meaningful. Denominators and confounds stay visible; no invented accuracy percentage.

Synthetic speech and scenario outputs are labeled derived test objects. They cannot become primary quotations, evidence rows, canonical characterization or independent corroboration. A persuasive monograph does not certify a model's predictive scope; independent assessment does not retroactively turn speculation into canon.

| W4 Evidence/claim stabilization | Central retrieval tested; rival readings retained; consequential revisions propagated |
| W5 Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap and coverage audit determines adequate inputs, missing work and justified independent study scopes |
| W6 Specialist/monograph completion | Complete developed outputs returned with evidence, limits and conflicts |
| W7 Specialist convergence | Cross-study conflicts and dependencies reconciled or retained; no-specialist choice requires explicit evidence-based rationale and assigned role coverage |
| W8 Integrated synthesis readiness | Full local arguments and specialist inputs available; scope, state and unresolved debts adequate for commissioning |
| W9 Continuous synthesis acceptance | Sustained integrated interpretation explaining connection and variation, not a list of lesson summaries |
| W10 Reconstruction evaluation | Independent held-out assessment for each model/scope, separate from literary readiness |
| W11 Final validation | Actual final files checked for source routes, authority, depth, name/reference clarity and unclosed obligations |
| W12 Release/freeze | Defined immutable corpus and limits, current routing, preserved history and designated integration acceptance |

Source-complete is not synthesis-ready. A partial main-boundary release is not full-work completion; an edition comparison can remain deferred without pretending its questions were answered. Genuinely unresolved interpretation may survive release with explicit limits; absent required evidence cannot masquerade as resolved uncertainty.

## 11. Execution and full-output handoff

Use SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS for future readings and interpretive ledger updates, DEEP_SYNTHESIS for checkpoints/specialists/monographs/models/adjudication, and PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST for architecture and continuous integration where false recurrence or edition conflation would propagate widely. Mechanical hashing is ROUTINE_FAST; settled locator mapping is BOUNDED_STANDARD. Follow the live [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md) without hard-coding a product label as authority.

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) prefers source-capable local sequential work and synchronized registers, fresh adequately supplied synthesis sessions and a designated integrator. This local metadata-only design does not assert primary-source transport, extraction or inspection capability. Verify transport, processing, TEXT/STILL_IMAGE inspection, interpretation and persistence separately under the [capability policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

A [handoff](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) transfers full exact files and digests, base snapshot, authority, witness/admission/knowledge boundary, role assignment and unresolved obligations. Include complete local readings and competing arguments; a synopsis is navigation only. Returned work includes complete outputs and a change/evidence manifest. The integrator checks base/candidate/current drift, retrieves consequential evidence and assesses preserved depth. One mutable owner per shared register applies; parallel specialists can own distinct files against a frozen input.

## 12. Extension, freeze and all sixteen duties

A later main volume extends the inventory, not an authorized run. An earlier-edition or adaptation expansion requires explicit source ranking, continuity and modality decisions. Add infrastructure only when recurring evidence creates a distinct responsibility; migrate schemas with an explicit mapping and backfill the affected admitted evidence proportionately. Do not replay the full corpus merely to populate a new table.

Current cumulative views are mutable, with append-only revision history. Accepted prospective readings and checkpoint snapshots are frozen. Later corrections use linked errata or superseding current artifacts with reciprocal authority metadata. Create a historical container only when genuine prior reasoning exists; preserve complete displaced arguments, not a compressed obituary.
