# Evidence, argument and revision register

## Responsibility

This register connects retrievable evidence to complete local arguments and carefully bounded cross-series propositions. It contains no literary claims, primary quotations or predictions at INITIAL. Metadata discrepancies remain in the source register; they cannot become narrative findings.

Warrant classes are OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED. Confidence is high/moderate/low/unresolved with a reason. Quotation states are EXACT_VERIFIED, WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION and PARAPHRASE.

Revision uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. A cross-series argument requires explicit selection and counterinstances, not merely several citations. A local argument can remain valid while a global generalization from it is rejected.

Dispute causes include WITNESS_IDENTITY, AUDIT_METADATA, EDITION_REVISION, CHRONOLOGY_DISCLOSURE, REPORT_RELIABILITY, KNOWLEDGE_ASYMMETRY, PERSONA_AUDIENCE, LOCAL_RULE_SCOPE, INTERPRETIVE_DIFFERENCE, MISSING_EVIDENCE, POSSIBLE_TEXTUAL_INCONSISTENCY and UNRESOLVED. Keep multiple causes live where needed.

## Update and analytical preservation

Develop the full argument in the reading or study before reducing its retrieval route to a row. Store exact evidence and retrieve central anchors in the locked witness. A broken locator remains a debt. A corrected extraction has a documented mapping; it does not silently redefine the source.

New evidence creates or revises claim versions. Preserve old formulations, reasons and full arguments, including rejected and low-confidence readings. Current pointers can change after acceptance; frozen source-facing prose cannot be overwritten. Notify every linked recurrence edge, subject state and specialist/model dependency.

A seemingly consistent pattern must retain alternative groupings and contrary local outcomes. Do not compress an ethically ambiguous unit to a moral proposition or a subject's self-description to a definitive trait. The register's evidence/support cells point to substantive prose rather than replacing it.

Freeze forecasts before opening the test material. If exposure has occurred, mark the exercise RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Synthetic outputs remain derived test artifacts and never constitute independent support, primary quotations or source facts.
