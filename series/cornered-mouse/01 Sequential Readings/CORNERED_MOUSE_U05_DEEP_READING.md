# CM-U05: 鼠、袋小路で考える

Kyoichi Otomo tries to turn attentiveness into a way out of the relationship. He reasons that treating Imagase with the consideration a partner deserves will make their arrangement troublesome, breaking its seductive ease. A deliberately offered compliment instead makes Imagase visibly pleased and produces a warm moment. The short's trap is not the absence of choice: an actual choice has an immediate effect different from its maker's purpose.

## Ease, gender and Kyoichi's argument with himself

Domestic detail gives ease an ordinary basis: laundry, a flexible dinner and leaving the house without an extended clothing consultation. These are not merely abstract confessions. They make the arrangement attractive through shared routine. Yet the comparison with an imagined woman is Kyoichi's gendered account. The miniature woman and supposed correct answer to her clothing question are comic constructions inside it, not evidence that all women demand this performance or that gay men inherently lack it (O001).

The black- and white-clothed selves dramatize a divided evaluation. One celebrates escaping the effort of anticipating women's reactions; the other objects that convenience does not justify drifting into this particular attachment or treating Imagase carelessly. Their labels and toy-hammer impact make self-rebuke visible without turning it into a second actual person, a psychiatric condition or the author's independent verdict. Even the corrective voice retains a gendered analogy: it proposes consideration like that given to a woman partner rather than demonstrating a nongendered account of this individual's needs (O002; CM-CL-007 v2).

Kyoichi then appropriates the correction for a distancing purpose. He will perform the troublesome consideration, discover how difficult gay dating is and wake himself from comfort. That aim is directly represented to the reader; Imagase is not told it. This is genuine initiative with an evasive desired outcome, rather than a clean change from passive to morally autonomous. It also limits any claim that the ensuing compliment transparently declares romantic love (O002–O003; CM-CL-001 v4/013).

## The plan meets a person

The experiment's first act is small and particular: Kyoichi notices a jacket, asks whether it is new and says it looks good on Imagase. Imagase's surprise and flushed reply occupy successive close panels, then both men share a larger decorative frame. The represented response makes the compliment consequential even though its private purpose was distancing. His ordinary capacity to receive appreciation is evidence distinct from sexual pursuit or strategic bargaining (O003–O004; CM-CS-017).

The final Gray Kyoichi label declines the easy moral binary established by the internal doubles. His own complaint is that a good mood has resulted. He recognizes immediate danger to the escape plan, not an externally measured transformation of his whole identity. The opening and closing apartment views enclose this internal reversal; repeated danger language changes its referent from comfortable drifting to the warmth produced by attempted correction (O001–O004; CM-CL-006 v5).

A credible alternative is that this is a momentary comic misfire rather than lasting developmental change. The page supports that limit: no subsequent decision, explicit couple contract, chosen woman or final orientation is supplied. Still, the response cannot be erased because Kyoichi meant something else. Care can acquire relational meaning in its reception before its maker can settle its purpose. This develops U04's knowingly repeated gift without pretending the two motives are identical: the gift could repair hurt; here the attempted route out is explicitly stated (O003–O004; CM-CL-013).
