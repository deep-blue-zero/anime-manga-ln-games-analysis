Promote an independent specialist when substantial distinguishable evidence spans several units, it owns an explanatory duty unowned elsewhere, it requires sustained reconciliation/argument, and it serves a downstream use beyond repeating readings. A compact integrated study may discharge several responsibilities if ownership and retrieval remain explicit. Review every anticipated domain after source completion; record justified consolidation, deferral or exclusion.

Character monographs and operational models have separate readiness gates. Kyoichi and Imagase are bibliographic candidates only. Other people earn independent work through their source evidence, not their assumed utility to the central relationship. No model, subject dossier, graded readiness or global character record is warranted now.

## State, claims and contradictions

Each state binds a unit/disclosure point, actual witness and source observations. Separate stable disposition from temporary state, reader/character/analyst knowledge, character account from shown event, and retrospective explanation from prospective inference. Event-time, publication-time and disclosure-time may differ. Do not silently merge original/revised Japanese text, translations, author commentary or adaptations.

Claim IDs retain earlier formulations and revision events: `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`. Record reason, new evidence, counterevidence, scope/confidence and every downstream document affected. Current interpretations may change; frozen readings/forecasts remain historical. A frozen error receives an attributable erratum or successor through the governing correction route. Neither a CLOSED admission lock nor a future run seal prevents authorized correction of current interpretation from already admitted evidence.

Classify apparent contradictions as chronology/disclosure, testimony/ignorance/deception, focalization, edition revision, translation, supplemental recontextualization, adaptation divergence or unresolved inconsistency. The claim ledger preserves the question and alternatives; the source map owns witness identity conflicts. A material unresolved conflict blocks only the downstream conclusion dependent on its resolution, with exact scope documented.

## Amendment, lifecycle and durable retrieval

Review after the first mainline component, the title-work/sequel boundary, a material source/edition discovery, mainline closure, and before specialists/full synthesis/release. Move INITIAL → EVOLVING → STABILIZED only when evidence supports it; owner-approved freeze is separate. Record material changes with version, reason, affected responsibilities, dependencies, source boundaries and any necessary backfill in this architecture and current map. Preserve prior authority history.

Promote a visual, dialogue, ethics or chronology ledger only when evidence recurs across units, accumulates independently, affects downstream synthesis and cannot be retrieved adequately through existing homes. Give it one responsibility and migrate references with a reviewed crosswalk; symmetry alone is insufficient. Backfill only material missing evidence from retained readings/sources under authorization, never fabricated old forecasts.

The current map, source map and cumulative ledgers remain mutable by targeted edits. Accepted sequential readings, prospective checkpoints and forecasts freeze; releases preserve exact versions/hashes and supersession links. Historical receipts record what actually occurred. No parallel current entrypoint, environment-branded model or duplicated nearly-final synthesis is introduced.
