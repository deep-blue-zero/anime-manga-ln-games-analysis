## Update contract

Enroll a subject only from admitted evidence, preserving secondary characters' independent agency and source-required anonymity. Append state/revision events; maintain a compact current projection keyed by character and source-state, with links to earlier records. Never overwrite old knowledge with later revelations. Allocate `CM-CH-...` IDs only with real records.

Default readiness is NOT_ASSESSED; advancement requires a scoped justification and evidence under the reconstruction protocol, never a count of mentions. Explicitly distinguish missing evidence from evidence of a limitation. A monograph-ready state cannot promote a behavioral model or domain prediction state. Global discovery/capability records remain untouched.
