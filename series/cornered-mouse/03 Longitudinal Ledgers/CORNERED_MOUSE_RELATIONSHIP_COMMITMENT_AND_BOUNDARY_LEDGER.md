## Update contract

Use `CM-REL-...` IDs once evidence warrants a relation. Append directional state transitions; a current projection links their histories. Unknown reciprocity remains unknown. Distinguish desire, affection, permission and durable commitment; later acceptance never substitutes for earlier consent. Track changes in power and vulnerability without a fixed controlling/vulnerable assignment.

The information ledger owns proposition, audience and causal leverage mechanics; this ledger links `CM-INF-...` / `CM-DEC-...` and owns consequences for boundaries, expectations and trust. Preserve other affected people as individuals when source permits. No verdict or future couple trajectory is prefilled.
