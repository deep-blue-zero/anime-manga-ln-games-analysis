# Information, leverage and decision state

**Information records: 0. Decision records: 0.** Separate ownership is warranted because consequential knowledge distribution and conditional options must remain retrievable apart from their relational effects. This is a planned responsibility, not a claim that any narrative event has been observed. Follow the [method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/CORNERED_MOUSE_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/CORNERED_MOUSE_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Record schemas

| Information field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `information_id`, `revision`, `prior_record_id`, `witness`, `unit` | Stable `CM-INF-...` identity and source boundary |
| `proposition`, `source_truth_status`, `knowledge_by_person`, `reader_state` | Known, believed, suspected, false, unknown; source/character/reader planes distinct |
| `higher_order_belief`, `disclosure_audience`, `withholding`, `timing` | Who believes that another knows; only where causally material |

| `decision_refs`, `relationship_refs`, `observation_refs`, `source_locators` | Causal and evidentiary retrieval |
| `alternatives`, `counterevidence`, `confidence_basis`, `unresolved` | Avoid unproved deception/omniscience claims |

| Decision field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `decision_id`, `actor`, `witness`, `unit`, `prior_state_ref` | Stable `CM-DEC-...` action/state identity |
| `available_information_refs`, `perceived_options`, `actual_options_status` | Character's perceived and analyst-supported options separately |
| `goals_avoidance_targets`, `constraints_costs`, `pressure_ref` | Stated versus inferred aims; practical exit and coercion variables |
| `choice_or_nondecision`, `immediate_result`, `later_disclosure_refs` | Observed action/inaction and time-bounded consequences |
| `counterfactual_uncertainty`, `counterexamples`, `claim_refs`, `source_locators` | Alternatives and limits; no invented available option |

## Update contract

Enroll only consequential propositions and choices. Append changes without retroactively granting knowledge. Separate deliberate lie, omission, strategic ambiguity, mistaken belief and unresolved motive. Record later explanation at its disclosure point. Non-action requires evidence of options/costs before being called a free choice.

This ledger owns causal mechanics; the relationship ledger owns trust/commitment/boundary consequences and links these IDs. Character state may reference a decision without duplicating the event. Hypothetical reconstruction outputs cannot become observations here.
