# The central relationship: access, recognition and chosen continuation

Kyoichi and Imagase's relationship develops through repeated failures to make access mean the same thing to both people. A suppressed report can purchase contact; a home can supply company; a gift can carry recognition; a declared choice can create a future obligation. None automatically answers whether the other person wants the same relation. The supplied edition's strongest resolution is a mutually desired continuation with imperfect communication and acknowledged risk. Its credibility depends on actual choices and ordinary receptions, while its limits remain visible in prior harm, outside obligations and unequal public recognition.

## Access is an inadequate first form of belonging

The opening bargain makes proximity possible through information leverage. Imagase desires this particular person and controls a cost Kyoichi fears. Kyoichi can refuse specific acts while accepting a bargain shaped by that fear. The later café exit changes the displayed leverage by accepting possible disclosure, but the subsequent reunion leaves classification unresolved: dating is refused while a trip and renewed contact become possible. These are distinct movements in the relation's terms. Eventual attachment cannot make the initial transaction retroactively mutual. [sp0012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o002); [sp0036](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o026); [sp0048](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o038).

U02's provisional companionship explicitly reduces the demand for a final identity or lifelong promise. That can create room for exploration and protect an immediate place, yet its gendered imagined termination already contains inequality: a future woman might count as the true inward love that ends Imagase's turn. Kyoichi can value domestic ease while avoiding the burden of deciding what he offers. Imagase can lower the request while hoping its acceptance will grow into more. Their shared presence therefore carries different futures. [sp0089](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o039); [sp0090](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o040).

## The receiver can change an act's meaning

The lighter, compliment and wine reveal a relational process more specific than escalating passion. A practical transfer is later explained as an intimate memory, then repeated with that explanation known. A compliment privately intended to help Kyoichi escape the arrangement produces a warm response he enjoys. A birthday bottle can be opened after the giver offers a future recurrence. These acts acquire force through what is communicated and received, not through a guaranteed purity of intention. [sp0135](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o039); [sp0140](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o003); [sp0221](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o017).

The distinction also preserves their misunderstandings. Imagase's computer discovery gives him a trace from which to imagine Kyoichi's concern, but not a confession. Kyoichi's actual thanks in U08 echoes a private household fantasy without sharing wifehood, surveillance, a child or a schedule. The connection can be real at the point of reception while the broader imagined life remains one person's. Intimacy enlarges knowledge without producing complete mutual access. [sp0093](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o002); [sp0259](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U08_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u08-o004).

## Actual absence tests the value of presence

U06 and U09 make separation more than a bargaining threat. In U06 Kyoichi's expectation of continuing routine fails against an empty household and a disconnected number. The two-month interval and retained cigarette remains expose an attachment his replacement-target explanation cannot adequately contain. At reunion both men have believed themselves unwanted, with different ideas of what would count as seeking contact. The discovery is not that one possessed perfect understanding; it is that both had acted within incompatible accounts. [sp0172](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o030); [sp0179](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o037); [sp0185](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o043).

U09's departure follows changed sexual participation and Kyoichi's newly felt responsibility. Imagase can say wishes have been fulfilled and still describe a dead end. This defeats a model in which a desired bodily act by itself secures the relationship. Kyoichi recognizes the former lover after the person has left. Recognition matters, but its delay permits another life to begin with Tamaki. The later reunion will consequently involve a third person's actual claim, rather than a simple return to a vacant original arrangement. [sp0305](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o045); [sp0326](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o066); [sp0337](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o077).

## Power and vulnerability move across different dimensions

Imagase initially controls information and often initiates bodily access; Kyoichi has the socially ordinary future Imagase fears losing to a woman. Neither asymmetry fixes control in every encounter. Imagase is vulnerable to another companion's violence, accepts one contextual rejection and actually leaves. Kyoichi initiates pursuit, makes conditions and later exercises force. Their sexual roles change without erasing these other dimensions. The source supports directional accounts tied to acts and situations, rather than a single hierarchy for the couple.

The distinction between wanting, asking and agreeing is particularly important to their communication. An invitation can express the wish for equal feeling without achieving equality. A request for force can precede a refusal that must still be read at its own time. A later statement of preference may describe reception while leaving earlier permission contested. The [agency/ethics study](CORNERED_MOUSE_AGENCY_COERCION_AND_ETHICS_STUDY.md) owns that normative analysis. Here its relational consequence is that intensity repeatedly fails to create a fully shared set of terms. [sp0246](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o042); [sp0411](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o073); [sp0415](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o077).

## A chosen bond has an external cost

U10 changes the arrangement through both promise and performance. Imagase asks Kyoichi to end the relation with Tamaki, then retreats when the agreement becomes credible. He fears responsibility for the cost of being chosen. Kyoichi insists on deciding his happiness, travels to the hospital and actually conducts the breakup. Tamaki determines part of its form by requiring him to sit and look at her. A central agreement now answers to another recipient whose grief remains in the text. [sp0432](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o094); [sp0435](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o097); [sp0452](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o114).

Their renewed bond also has limits within itself. The no-third-time warning refuses endless pursuit after another inadequacy rejection. Private bridge imagery accepts the possibility of distance and an ending, while ring/bed/trip talk projects continued life. The ring exchange is reciprocal in intention and unequal in public visibility: Kyoichi cannot wear his at work. A token makes a limited commitment communicable without supplying legal status, universal public acknowledgment or protection from future failure. [sp0484](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o146); [sp0491](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o153); [sp0497](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o159).

## Ordinary continuation gives the ending a scale

Hummingbird carries this chosen future into one small enacted encounter. Imagase reports continuing uncertainty at a friend's wedding. Kamimura's account of everyday welcome changes what the return home can mean to him. Kyoichi then actually welcomes him and removes rice from his hair; his practical explanation and Imagase's blush coexist. The scene gives received ordinary care a form that neither person needs to understand completely for it to matter. [sp0529](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o027); [sp0532](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o030); [sp0534](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o032).

The adjacent comic extends token possession and reported gifts while returning the characters to vanity and abrasive banter. It supports continuation, not an unbroken secure lifetime. The strongest counterreading is that these are temporary reassurances inside a repeated cycle. The source gives that risk substance through earlier ruptures and Imagase's continuing fear. It also supplies a meaningful difference: costly performed choice, an explicit further-rejection limit and an actual ordinary welcome. A reading of either perfect cure or empty repetition would discard part of that evidence.
