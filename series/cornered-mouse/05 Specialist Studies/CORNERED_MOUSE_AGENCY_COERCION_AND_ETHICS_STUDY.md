# Agency, coercion and interpersonal ethics

The work's ethical difficulty comes from the coexistence of real attachment, consequential choice and conduct that injures other people. Its strongest developmental moments do not cancel those injuries. A source-grounded assessment asks who can choose, what cost refusal carries, what was communicated at the time and whose interests remain outside the couple's account. This approach can recognize changed participation and sincere care while preserving specific responsibility.

## A bargain has a mechanism

Imagase's initial report bargain links suppressed marital information to bodily access. Kyoichi fears disclosure; Imagase controls it. The cost of refusal therefore belongs to the permission context even when Imagase kneels, professes devotion or presents the transaction as help. Kyoichi's refusal of particular expansions and Imagase's denial of his standing to give directions establish boundaries being overridden. The ethical judgment follows those acts and conditions, rather than a presumption that affection is absent. [sp0012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o002); [sp0019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o009); [sp0023](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o013).

Kyoichi later accepts disclosure risk and leaves. That is an actual exercise of an option, not proof that the option had no cost. The subsequent divorce explanation also limits the analyst's causal claim: Imagase's apology and Kyoichi's accusation do not establish that Imagase engineered every marital event or controlled Chikako's purpose. A precise coercion finding should identify the demonstrated leverage, without expanding it into omnipotence. [sp0036](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o026); [sp0040](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o030).

## Permission belongs to acts and turns

The source repeatedly distinguishes wanting contact, accepting a relation and agreeing to a particular act. In U02 Kyoichi denies having approved an earlier act and challenges the logic by reversing who could act without permission. Imagase's later protest during Kyoichi's initiative deserves the same attention. The scene's desire and changing initiative cannot supply every omitted answer. At U04 the morning finger-only correction limits the act itself, while intoxication, wait language and selective memory remain separate questions. [sp0056](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o006); [sp0083](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o033); [sp0135](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o039).

U10 puts the distinction under particularly strong pressure. Imagase requests force at411, then says to release/not touch and protests at413–416. Kyoichi restrains him while harboring a retaliatory wish. A prior request may establish a relevant expectation; it cannot automatically extinguish later refusals. Imagase's praise at443 is later reception and preference, not contemporaneous assent supplied backward. The source's conflicting signals are ethically material rather than an invitation to assign a secret meaning that makes every turn agree. [sp0411](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o073); [sp0413](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o075); [sp0416](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o078); [sp0443](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o105).

The reversed sequence matters equally. Imagase initiates during Kyoichi's sleep/waking, and Kyoichi asks him to wait421. Visible pleasure, later closeness or a changed sexual role does not erase that utterance. Nor does an unshown negotiation permit certainty that every other act was unwanted. The defensible account preserves the specific request, refusal, initiative and gap at its own moment. It refuses to distribute responsibility through one permanent active/passive role. [sp0420](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o082); [sp0421](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o083).

## Agency is more than an innocent choice

Kyoichi's recurrent self-account as responding to others is countered by confrontation, concealment, pursuit, conditions, gifts and performed breakup. Those choices establish responsibility as well as capacity. The hotel outburst is an initiated act resting on an inaccurate rival inference. The care-as-exit experiment is an initiated act with an evasive purpose and an unexpectedly warm result. His final choice is initiated and costly without making all intervening conduct justified. [sp0076](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o026); [sp0140](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o003); [sp0433](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o095).

Imagase's capacity to leave complicates an equally simple account of limitless dependence. He accepts a contextual rejection, cuts off access and later actually departs again. His longing and reported distress remain. Natsuki's plea to be actively chosen and Tamaki's offer to wait similarly show agency under painful constraints. Agency should not be measured by freedom from vulnerability or equated with a guarantee that a chosen action is wise. [sp0161](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o019); [sp0172](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o030); [sp0326](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o066); [sp0459](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o121).

## Information can serve care and control

Unauthorized phone/computer access gives Imagase information without granting the right to obtain it or certainty about its meaning. The birthday PIN is an especially exact conjunction: Kyoichi's care is part of the route through which privacy is breached. Imagase's explanation that a lock implies guilt confuses a boundary with proof of wrongdoing. Their not-dating classifications earlier in the work do not eliminate a privacy interest, while lack of an explicit exclusivity agreement limits the analyst's claim of a particular broken contract. [sp0092](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o001); [sp0300](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o040); [sp0303](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o043).

Professional help in U10 adds another distinction. Imagase can coordinate an investigation and be credited with rescue while confessing a harmful wish. A wish matters to moral self-understanding; it is not the aggressor's performed act or proof that he caused the fall. The pause's explanation arrives later, and Tamaki is not shown hearing it. Her gratitude cannot therefore be treated as informed forgiveness of that undisclosed thought. The source allows genuine help, jealousy, guilt and self-interested pleading to coexist. [sp0367](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o029); [sp0373](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o035); [sp0379](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o041).

## The people outside the renewed pair retain claims

Chikako's new partner and financially interested investigation complicate her position without erasing her complaint about a marriage requiring instructions. Nishimura's household account remains her testimony; the absent husband, son and mother-in-law cannot be invented to justify or condemn the affair completely. Natsuki's booking and unanswered call leave an obligation unresolved at the first title-work ending. Ethical understanding requires attention to these independent claims rather than allowing the central intimacy to decide whose life counts. [sp0039](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o029); [sp0064](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o014); [sp0200](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o058).

Tamaki makes the final breakup concrete by demanding a seated conversation. Kyoichi had wanted to protect her from a triangle, then became involved with Imagase before ending their relation. His subsequent truthful ending is better evidence of responsibility than a promise alone, but it does not undo that sequence. Her grief, offer to wait and friendship support remain costs of a choice he has the power to make. Loving her and being unable to continue can both be true. [sp0387](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o049); [sp0452](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o114); [sp0466](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o128).

Mina's protective concern also requires discrimination. Her insistence that harm to Tamaki matter is warranted by the scene; her assumptions about gay identity and female cover are interested stereotypes. Her bag strike is violence, and a gossip threat is not already disseminated gossip. She later chooses support and incomplete disclosure. Recognizing those distinctions keeps ethical scrutiny from becoming selective sympathy. [sp0467](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o129); [sp0469](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o131); [sp0473](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o135).

## Change does not require acquittal

The ending's ring offers, warning against another inadequacy rejection and ordinary welcome give the renewed bond responsibilities and received care. They establish something worth recognizing. They do not supply the missing permissions, cancel earlier deception or make devotion sufficient compensation for every harm. The credible positive development is more limited and more demanding: the men perform some choices, acknowledge uncertainty and communicate particular care while remaining accountable for what they have done. [sp0484](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o146); [sp0497](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o159); [sp0532](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o030).

A purely exculpatory reading would let the ending replace the history. A wholly static condemnation would make changed actions unintelligible. The supplied witness supports an account that retains both development and responsibility. This is a literary ethical judgment based on depicted and attributed conduct, not a clinical diagnosis, legal determination or portable behavioral certification. The [relationship study](CORNERED_MOUSE_CENTRAL_RELATIONSHIP_STUDY.md) owns the resulting terms of attachment; the [source audit](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/CORNERED_MOUSE_SOURCE_COMPLETION_AND_LONGITUDINAL_RECONCILIATION.md) preserves the uncertainties on which these judgments depend.
