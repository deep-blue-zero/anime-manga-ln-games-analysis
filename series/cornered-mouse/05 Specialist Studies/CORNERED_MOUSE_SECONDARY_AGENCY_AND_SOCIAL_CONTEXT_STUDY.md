# Secondary agency and social context: lives that the couple cannot absorb

The central relationship becomes intelligible through people whose wishes and costs exceed it. Chikako contests the kind of marriage Kyoichi offered; Nishimura seeks reassurance within a household known chiefly through her account; Natsuki demands active choice; Tamaki wants a recognized place. Former partners, coworkers and friends also interpret, withhold and act. Their incomplete interior access requires disciplined attention to what they actually do, rather than turning them into either reliable moral authorities or obstacles to the chosen pair.

## Chikako's account keeps a marriage from becoming scenery

Kyoichi initially experiences the investigation as a threat to a marriage he wants preserved. Chikako later explains a divorce/compensation purpose and reports a new partner. Her account changes the causal story available to the reader. She is pursuing an exit of her own; she is not merely a wife waiting to catch the central protagonist. The investigation can be financially interested and her criticism of waiting for instructions can still be compelling. [sp0038](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o028); [sp0040](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o030).

Her complaint identifies labor that agreeable responsiveness leaves to another person. Kyoichi's offer to do anything fails because it again makes her decide what he should do. The smoking concession and shared shopping pleasure preserve evidence that the marriage contained consideration. The problem is its adequacy and distribution, not proof that every moment was empty. Her own partner's perspective and the full marital history remain unavailable.

Imagase's later private fantasy calls her foolish for leaving a good man. That taunt is evidence about his desired household role and interested judgment. It cannot supersede her direct U01 account. Keeping both in view lets the manga show why Kyoichi is desirable to Imagase without making that desirability the measure of whether Chikako should have stayed. [sp0256](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U08_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u08-o001).

## Nishimura acts within a household the reader cannot fully enter

Nishimura, formerly Mizuno, initiates renewed contact, seeks reassurance, supplies a parents-home alibi and complains of husband, son and mother-in-law. These are actions and testimony. The source gives her dissatisfaction and wish to be cherished, while withholding the other household members' accounts. Neither invented abuse nor invented domestic harmony should fill that space. Her affair has responsibility on both sides even when Kyoichi treats it as a socially familiar restoration. [sp0059](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o009); [sp0064](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o014).

Her presence at Kyoichi's hotel outburst also makes her a witness to conduct the central pair cannot keep private. The man he attacks is explained as a client; his wrong inference and violence have effects before the relation between the men is clarified. Nishimura's shocked response belongs to her own encounter with that scene. No later full account of her marriage or the affair's aftermath is supplied. [sp0077](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o027); [sp0078](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o028).

The student-era kissing woman remains a different, unnamed person. Mention of Mika and Nishimura's earlier surname do not establish aliases. This limited historical role can expose the pain of Imagase's unrecognized desire without yielding a psychological monograph of the woman. The source-required anonymity is itself part of an honest social picture: the protagonists' memories do not give every other person a biography. [sp0065](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o015).

## Former partners have interests and limited knowledge

The unnamed U01 companion commits a visible act of violence while Kyoichi hears a noise and receives a minimizing cup explanation. Imagase's later breakup report is not the companion's account or proof of every agreement between them. The scene demonstrates vulnerability in another relation without absolving Imagase's conduct toward Kyoichi. It also prevents the phone's intimacy from making the other body in the room disappear. [sp0043](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o033); [sp0044](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o034).

Takasugi asks that Imagase be released from false hope and reports that his own reunion offer was refused because of Kyoichi. His interest in an alternative relation shapes that speech. Joint restaurant departure does not prove resumed sex; Imagase later says they separated immediately. The later concern/name-recovery call appears through Imagase's report, adding care to the portrait without supplying a direct present scene or diagnosis. [sp0107](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o011); [sp0114](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o018); [sp0384](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o046).

Natsuki's [independent monograph](../04%20Character%20Studies/CORNERED_MOUSE_NATSUKI_MONOGRAPH.md) owns her history of wanting active choice. Here her comparison with Chikako is useful but bounded: both contest adequate affection, with different histories and decisions. Her concrete holiday/call remains unresolved at U06, while the closing comic supplies a later marriage report. Those facts cannot be collapsed into either eternal exclusion or a retrospectively enacted clean settlement. [sp0200](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o058); [sp0535](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o033).

## Family and workplace distribute recognition unevenly

Kyoichi's father is available through a remembered explanation of loneliness and pity. Mother and sister appear within that memory, not as independently assessed psychological subjects. The recollection helps explain Kyoichi's wish to be liked; it does not certify that women as a class neglect good men. The form places a family account inside the adult narrator's self-understanding. [sp0208](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o004).

Yanagida's executive matchmaking and acknowledged daughter give another arrangement. Kyoichi initially admires responsibility through provision; Tamaki's funeral exclusion exposes what that measure misses. A loved father can exist privately while recognized family seats remain unavailable to a daughter. Executive rank can make a proposal awkward, but the source supplies no job-sanction bargain and Tamaki says she was not consulted. Material inequality and incomplete knowledge can be analyzed without inventing every institutional mechanism. [sp0266](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o006); [sp0269](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o009); [sp0297](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o037).

Tamaki's [monograph](../04%20Character%20Studies/CORNERED_MOUSE_OKAMURA_TAMAKI_MONOGRAPH.md) owns her full development. Her family exclusion and later desired romantic place resonate because she has experience of waiting, not because a father and a lover create identical rights. The office's girlfriend assumption and the ending's unequal ring visibility show other forms of recognition. The manga gives practical concern about family, work and livelihood without furnishing a general legal account. [sp0219](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o015); [sp0497](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o159).

## Professional roles do not establish romantic identities

The U02 client corrects a rival inference; the unnamed U10 colleague supports professional coordination. Neither role licenses an invented romance, private biography or comprehensive competence rating. Imagase's evidentiary caution is observed in a limited task. Okazaki, named as the former hairdresser/aggressor, is distinct from that colleague. Tamaki's help-seeking and capture/rescue report stay separate from an unshown complete fall mechanism and from Imagase's later confessed wish. [sp0077](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o027); [sp0359](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o021); [sp0369](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o031); [sp0379](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o041).

These distinctions matter socially as well as procedurally. A person can be interpreted as a rival because of proximity and then turn out to occupy a work role. The corrective pattern challenges confident inference without making every suspicious interpretation false by principle. Each scene still requires its own evidence.

## Mina supports a person beyond the breakup

Nogami Mina confronts Kyoichi because Tamaki's injury and loss count. Her concern has an ethical object, while her explanation through gay identity/female cover remains a stereotype and her bag strike remains violence. Threatened gossip is not already disseminated gossip. She later withholds the unfinished disclosure and offers company and pudding. These choices give her a changing response rather than a fixed protective function. [sp0467](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o129); [sp0469](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o131); [sp0473](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o135).

The friendship scene also gives Tamaki anger, humor and comfort outside the men's reunion. Mina's inferred identity is not automatically Tamaki's knowledge. Support can be real without completing every explanation or guaranteeing recovery. The source's willingness to remain with them after Kyoichi leaves is part of the ending's moral scale.

## Kamimura's friendship has its own wishes

Kamimura wants to draw an unusual bird, visit the exhibit and learn about Atsushi. She jokes about a conventional romantic script, rejects public humiliation, withdraws her crush and remains Imagase's friend. Her later wedding is her own event. The groom's partly cut name and shared visual traits do not justify merging him with Atsushi or Sakano. Her report of ordinary married happiness is testimony from a life she has chosen. [sp0510](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o008); [sp0514](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o012); [sp0523](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o021); [sp0526](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o024).

Atsushi and the unnamed teasing classmate are actual speakers in the public objectification, not a universal male nature. Sakano is shown teaching and becomes the object of private adolescent attraction; no teacher awareness or acted relation is established. Kamimura does not publicly name the desired person's gender. Her defense protects her own account of the outing while the delayed negation preserves the friendship. These limited roles belong to the social pressure of school, with no invented adult aftermath. [sp0518](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o016); [sp0521](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o019); [sp0524](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o022).

At the wedding her encouragement may help Imagase consider Kyoichi's effort, but it is not omniscient knowledge. She remains someone with a marriage, a history and a friendship, rather than a saint imported to approve the central bond. The paired-bird wedding bag carries that independent life into the final home image without making every form of belonging equivalent. [sp0531](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o029); [sp0534](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o032).

Across these lives, recognition has different stakes: receiving a partner's initiative, being believed, occupying a family seat, appearing available at work or being welcomed as a friend. The social argument gains force through their differences. Consolidating16 source-limited subjects here preserves their independent evidence and gaps; it does not certify operational models or claim that every person receives equal narrative access. The completed supplied witness permits a central relationship whose significance must remain answerable to the other lives around it.
