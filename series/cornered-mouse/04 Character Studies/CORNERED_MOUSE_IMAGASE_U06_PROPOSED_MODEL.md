# Wataru Imagase at the first-work exit

This **proposed model at CM-U06-EXIT** derives from observed behavior, speech, limited represented interiority and attributed history. It is neither a literary monograph nor a global capability grade. The [title-work review](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/CORNERED_MOUSE_TITLE_WORK_ARCHITECTURE_AND_READINESS_REVIEW.md) owns the immutable evidence bundle and common domain-gap matrix. ## State and accessible knowledge

CM-CS-019 follows CM-CS-002/006/011/013/017. Imagase has privately asked Natsuki to withdraw, accepted Kyoichi's café rejection without an initial counteroffer, made a separate final plea after Kyoichi reopened contact, and actually left. At reunion he reports painful waiting, learns that the cigarette remains were kept, cancels a proposed male visit under Kyoichi's condition and returns with him. Continued desire does not make the departure a bluff. His account of missed work/eating is testimony, not an independent clinical or employment record.

He knows his own contact routes and the private rival meeting, not necessarily Kyoichi's prior hotel confession to Natsuki or every holiday arrangement. The visible call from Natsuki at the end does not establish knowledge of all its implications. His investigation and digital monitoring have evidenced limits; reconstruction must not grant omniscience. His adolescent history remains attributed, non-graphic and irrelevant to invented consent or personality causes.

## CM-RULE-IMAGASE-001@v1 — personal reopening permits conditional reengagement

**Class:** CANDIDATE_POLICY; moderate confidence within this familiar attachment, unvalidated prospectively. **State:** U06 exit while attachment and relevant beliefs/commitments remain comparable. **Trigger:** after a rupture or ending-like exchange, Kyoichi voluntarily offers clear personal access or a meaningful affectionate gesture directed at Imagase. **Options/stakes:** accept, condition, defer or refuse renewed access; fear of unequal attachment and hurt remain. Apparent emergency rescue, incapacity, solely instrumental work contact, ambiguous accident or externally coerced invitation are ineligible.

**Predicted range:** before that interaction ends, Imagase accepts or actively negotiates the offered personal access rather than unequivocally refusing it and ending contact. A condition need not be sexual; the rule predicts no exact line, act, stable reconciliation or permission to override Kyoichi's boundaries. The range excludes a sustained outright refusal, but acceptance alone will not prove a unique motive.

**Basis:** U02 O029/sp0079, a home invitation answered with conditions; U04 O034/O039–O040, sp0130/0135–0136, response to Kyoichi's kiss and knowing renewed gift; U06 O042–O049, sp0184–0191, new information about retained traces, demanded call and renewed contact. The intoxicated U04 kiss is a retrospective comparison, not an independent eligible training instance for the sober rule. Stronger sober cases are the U02 invitation, U04 morning gift and U06 reunion. ## CM-RULE-IMAGASE-002@v1 — polite address can carry conflict

**Class:** CANDIDATE_POLICY for written register, moderate confidence in the familiar senior/junior relationship; no acoustic claim. **Trigger/state:** sober direct disagreement or pressure addressed to Kyoichi with their familiar relationship intact. **Range:** preserve at least some senior-address or polite-form language during the exchange; do not render the entire disagreement as undifferentiated casual hostility. It can coexist with sharp content, vulnerability, apology or an imperative. It never signifies ethical respect by itself.

**Basis:** U02 sp0056's renewed demand and U04 sp0128's objection, alongside U06 sp0182–0188's taxi interaction and sp0191's polite fearful reply. U06 sp0189 supplies a contrast: casual dismissal to an unheard man, not a transcript of that man's replies. This is an audience-conditioned contrast, not separate invented personalities.

**Counterexamples/limits:** one-sided casual call, emotionally sharp/direct content and changes within a single exchange prohibit an every-sentence politeness rule. Toward Natsuki he can also use pointed polite forms; politeness is not uniquely romantic. **Rivals:** age/university hierarchy or habitual form may explain the same surface, limiting discrimination. **Falsifier:** the first eligible complete exchange contains neither senior address nor polite-form language while sustaining direct disagreement. **Abstention:** impaired speech, quotation/dream, missing recipient, untranslated wording, emergency or materially changed relation/hierarchy. **Test:** CM-PR-006. A supported result would remain a narrow, low-discrimination register test, not behavior or ethical fidelity.

## Domain and generation limits

D0 retrieval may reproduce the attested action with its real knowledge boundary. D1 close variants remain proposals and must honor exclusions. D2 unfamiliar native contexts and D3 cross-series transfer are unsupported. Ordinary care, humor, working life and moral deliberation cannot be filled from a lover stereotype; unknowns require abstention. No generated case becomes canonical evidence. Distinguish reported history from shown behavior, private thought from speech, and intensity from permission. In particular, neither an always-resists-rejection rule nor an always-respects-refusal rule fits the admitted record. Tests and successor versions must preserve failures instead of inventing a permanent compliant or manipulative essence.
