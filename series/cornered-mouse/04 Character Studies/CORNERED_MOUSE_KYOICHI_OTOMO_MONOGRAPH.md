# Kyoichi Otomo: the cost of choosing a life

Kyoichi Otomo / 大伴恭一 learns to acknowledge a particular attachment through the consequences of decisions he repeatedly describes as other people's initiative. His development is neither an emergence from literal helplessness nor a replacement of his personality. He can initiate from the beginning. What changes is his willingness to recognize what his initiatives mean, to let another person's claim disrupt a convenient account of himself, and eventually to choose a bond whose costs he cannot control. The final choice is consequential precisely because his evasive manner and capacity to hurt people persist.

## Kindness as pleasure, habit and protection

Kyoichi's first explanation of his affairs distributes responsibility outward: women pressed, he could not refuse, the episodes were short or over. The account acknowledges conduct while softening authorship. Yet the same component supplies initiated confrontations, a shopping invitation, explicit refusals and a departure from the report bargain. The evidence supports a preference for responding and avoiding rejection, alongside a capacity for action when a situation matters to him. Treating him as incapable of choice would adopt the strongest version of his excuse. [sp0013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o003); [sp0036](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o026).

Chikako's objection is exacting because she contests the adequacy of the kindness he offers. She has waited for a partner who will initiate a shared life; his offer to do anything requires her to supply the instructions again. His reported smoking concession and the pleasurable shopping scene show consideration with real value. Her complaint still exposes a division of work within that consideration. He can make another person comfortable while leaving her responsible for deciding what the relationship should be. Her own new-partner/investigation account remains interested and limited; it does not cancel the force of what she asks him to recognize. [sp0039](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o029).

U07's remembered father gives this pattern a personal history. The adult Kyoichi recalls pitying a father ignored at home and wishing that he had married someone who loved him more. He connects the memory with listening to women and choosing people who like him. The scene explains how kindness can also protect the giver from rejection. Because it is remembered and narrated by Kyoichi, it cannot establish the whole family's motives or a single childhood cause for every affair. Its significance lies in the account he now makes of himself. [sp0208](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o004).

This makes his encounters with women distinct. Nishimura offers an admired former classmate and a socially familiar route for desire; Natsuki asks for the passion she felt absent from their earlier relation; Tamaki receives genuine attention and a contemplated family introduction. Their wishes meet different versions of his need to be liked. They also have independent choices and costs. His return to Imagase does not make his affection for Tamaki fictional, any more than Chikako's departure proves he never cared for her.

## Agency becomes visible when an account stops working

The U02 hotel confrontation is a strong counterexample to total passivity and a warning against equating initiative with virtue. Kyoichi sees Imagase with another man, assumes replacement, becomes violent and is corrected by the client explanation. The action gives his attachment practical force before he will give it a settled name. Its epistemic premise is wrong, and Nishimura witnesses consequences beyond the central pair. Jealousy can reveal investment while producing an unjust act. [sp0076](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o026); [sp0078](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o028).

His resistance to entering a male relationship also changes in content. Early ordinary-man language and the serpent fantasy frame intimacy as a departure from a familiar world. Later he describes a more particular fear: dependence on Imagase in a world where he imagines Imagase could leave for another man. That is an imagined asymmetry, not a verified map of gay life. It is nevertheless more revealing than a categorical identity claim alone. He is afraid of losing the protections supplied by being the person others choose. [sp0163](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o021).

U05 condenses the problem comically. He resolves to become more considerate so that the effort will expose the difficulty of the arrangement and help him escape it. A small compliment makes Imagase blush and leaves Kyoichi in a good mood. The care has an effect that exceeds its private distancing purpose. His initiative is real, his motive mixed and his pleasure inconvenient to his own plan. This is a more precise developmental unit than a general conversion from selfishness to kindness. [sp0140](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o003); [sp0141](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o004).

## Loss makes recognition harder to evade

The title-work choice costs him access. Imagase quietly accepts the contextual rejection, later leaves the household and disconnects the familiar number. Kyoichi had imagined that the old routine could continue despite the changed choice. Missing clothes, an empty home and cigarette remains expose the failure of that expectation. The subsequent two-month interval gives absence duration; distraction even intrudes into a limited work scene. His explanation that Imagase merely wanted a new straight target protects him from acknowledging this particular loss, but the retained ashtray makes that account increasingly hard to sustain. [sp0161](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o019); [sp0172](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o030); [sp0178](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o036).

At the taxi he initiates pursuit and admits he could not discard the remains. The indirect complaint about cigarette butts communicates attachment because it reveals what he retained. He then requires Imagase to settle the other arrangement before accepting renewed contact. That condition demonstrates both agency and an asymmetry: Natsuki's own unanswered call remains at the title-work ending. He can demand responsibility from Imagase before fully performing his corresponding responsibility. The reunion is meaningful without being ethically complete. [sp0184](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o042); [sp0187](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o045); [sp0200](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o058).

The sequel repeatedly tests the adequacy of receiving and giving signs. Kyoichi plans a birthday wine and offers another gift next year; Imagase can use what he feared losing. Kyoichi says he wants Imagase's company, apologizes, extends a hand and later initiates a fuller departing kiss. These are particular acts of recognition. His work declaration of having no girlfriend and Tamaki's broader reported no-partner wording show how private significance can remain publicly misreadable. The reader cannot compress all of these statements into one stable shared relationship definition. [sp0221](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o017); [sp0232](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o028); [sp0246](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o042).

U09's separation follows changed bodily participation and Kyoichi's new sense of responsibility. That order matters: a desired act and a felt transformation do not settle what Imagase needs. Kyoichi recognizes Imagase as a former lover after the actual departure. His grief makes received words and withheld words newly legible; recognition arrives late enough to be costly. His new intimacy with Tamaki starts after that departure, while her assumption that the former lover was a woman remains uncorrected on the page. [sp0305](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o045); [sp0327](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o067); [sp0337](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o077).

## A choice that leaves other people present

U10 gives Kyoichi a plausible life with Tamaki. He likes her, plans family contact and thinks of Imagase as part of the self now capable of offering care. His claim that he had been pretending sits beside his fear that meeting Imagase again would undo his control. These successive self-descriptions warrant attention to evasion and ambivalence, rather than selecting one sentence as his final identity. [sp0345](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o007); [sp0348](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o010).

The renewed intimacy retains acts for which he is responsible. Imagase's earlier request for force does not cancel later refusals; Kyoichi's hurt and private retaliatory wish do not supply assent. His own wait signal during Imagase's initiative requires the same temporal discipline. Choosing Imagase with his mind is an important declaration, but it neither justifies those acts nor resolves the intervening betrayal of Tamaki. [sp0413](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o075); [sp0421](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o083); [sp0426](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o088).

The hospital journey turns a declaration into an action with duration and an affected recipient. Tamaki makes him sit and look at her; he states an ending and declines to ask her to wait, despite genuine liking. He cannot keep both lives by announcing that he chooses his own happiness. Mina's condemnation and Tamaki's later grief remain in the text as consequences the central reunion does not absorb. This is the strongest evidence of changed choice: he performs an ending that hurts someone he values and accepts responsibility without gaining a clean self-image. [sp0433](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o095); [sp0452](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o114); [sp0460](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o122).

## A manner persists while a commitment changes

Kyoichi still calls himself a man carried by the strongest flow. That description at487 does not erase the performed breakup or ensuing pursuit, just as those actions do not eliminate his familiar evasions. His late private bridge imagery allows separation to remain imaginable while care remains worth giving. He does not claim a way to fill every gap or protect the relation from every future loss. The ring offer supplies a communicable sign, while his inability to wear the reciprocal ring at work preserves unequal visibility. [sp0487](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o149); [sp0491](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o153); [sp0497](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o159).

S01 then limits the grandeur of the ending productively. Kyoichi is eating chips, welcomes Imagase home and removes rice from his hair. The reader knows why ordinary welcome matters after the wedding conversation; Kyoichi has not been shown hearing it. His practical explanation does not undo the care received, and it supplies no new interior vow. The individual who could once make others wait for instructions now gives a small recognition that reaches its recipient. One encounter cannot certify a permanently repaired life. It can show the work of attachment occurring within a familiar, imperfect manner. [sp0532](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o030); [sp0534](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o032).
