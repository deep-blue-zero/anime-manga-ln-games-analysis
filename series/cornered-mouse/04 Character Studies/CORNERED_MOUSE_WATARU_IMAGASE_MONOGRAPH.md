# Wataru Imagase: pursuit, self-limitation and the wish to be ordinary

Wataru Imagase / 今ヶ瀬渉 wants his attachment to Kyoichi to become a place he can inhabit without continuously extracting evidence that it exists. His methods repeatedly undermine that wish. He can use knowledge, bodily pressure and lowered demands to obtain access, while recognizing that access obtained through pity or obligation cannot answer the question he most wants answered. Actual departures, private self-criticism, professional conduct and an enduring friendship give him capacities beyond relentless pursuit. They do not make his conduct ethically consistent or his love painless.

## Knowledge secures access without securing reciprocity

The opening report bargain establishes a specific mechanism. Imagase controls damaging marital information, Kyoichi expects disclosure to threaten his household, and suppression is exchanged for intimacy. Kneeling, senior-address and professions of devotion coexist with practical leverage. His refusal to respect stated limits matters independently of later pleasure or love. This is more exact than making every gesture a manifestation of a universal manipulative personality. The report gives him power in this relation under these circumstances. [sp0012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o002); [sp0019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o009).

His later drive account says he initially hoped to conquer or use Kyoichi to exhaust an ideal, only to find the renewed person more compelling. That testimony supports an interested strategy in his present explanation of the past. It does not prove that every stage was planned or turn the initial threat into innocent role-play. Sincere attachment and deliberate pressure remain compatible findings. [sp0313](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o053); [sp0314](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o054).

Surveillance reveals the same inadequacy of access. Checking the phone supplies information the sensory clues did not certify. Monitoring the computer makes a search term available but cannot establish who typed it or why. Later opening the birthday-keyed inbox gains unauthorized access while the birthday itself records importance. Each discovery produces interpretation rather than secure mutual knowledge. His inference that a lock proves guilt and the leap from Tamaki's message to replacement are actions of a fearful reader, not an omniscient investigator. [sp0068](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o018); [sp0093](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o002); [sp0300](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o040).

U03 gives his limit in private speech: reckless wounds are his responsibility, pity is not enough, and kindness does not entitle him to possession. The desired exception is Kyoichi wanting to love him. This self-rebuke has force because longing survives it. Turning off the computer ends that act of looking; it does not establish deleted history or abandoned future surveillance. Understanding his pain therefore increases the need for precision about his choices. [sp0094](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o003); [sp0095](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o004).

## An inferior place can be defended strategically

Imagase repeatedly invokes seniority, knowing his place and the possibility that Kyoichi will ultimately choose a woman. Such speech can express learned exclusion, protect him from an impossible demand and lower the immediate cost of remaining. Its emotional sincerity does not make it a stable policy. The promise of provisional company in U02, the wish to be taken by impulse in U04 and the later desire for equal feeling ask for different things at different boundaries. [sp0089](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o039); [sp0128](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o032); [sp0245](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o041).

Natsuki exposes the interest within his apparent self-sacrifice. He asks her to withdraw, admits that his old advice sought to separate her from Kyoichi, and fears he cannot win if she is serious. She refuses to become disposable because he is hurting. His idea that men need an exit protects Kyoichi's freedom while also protecting an arrangement in which Imagase can continue hoping. Natsuki's competing demand for a choice has its own harshness and gender assumptions; neither person is a neutral authority. [sp0151](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o009); [sp0155](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o013).

The café is decisive counterevidence to a necessary-objection account. Imagase quietly accepts the contextual rejection and the interaction ends. Later pain and a bath plea do not undo that first response. He subsequently leaves, changes the contact route and makes the domestic absence real. At reunion he reports waiting, not eating and missing work. Those reports indicate cost while remaining testimony. A capacity to withdraw and severe continuing attachment can coexist. [sp0161](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o019); [sp0172](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o030); [sp0179](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o037).

His later farewell does comparable work under changed conditions. He can value meals, sleep, appearance and listening, describe extraordinary happiness, and still declare a dead end. He also offers an imagined ordinary married future for Kyoichi that speaks from his own expectations. Packing and going turn those words into a loss of access. His compact luggage and the room's relative emptiness emerge through Kyoichi's later account; they cannot certify a hidden lifetime plan never to put down roots. [sp0317](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o057); [sp0325](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o065); [sp0326](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o066).

## Receiving care changes the question

Imagase's attachment is responsive to small acknowledgments. The explained lighter transfer gains meaning when Kyoichi repeats it knowingly. The jacket compliment in U05 surprises him; the birthday wine is difficult to use because consumption resembles losing a cherished object. Kyoichi's offer of another gift next year enables a different relation to the present one. The source gives actual responses without making any single gift a sufficient relationship contract. [sp0135](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o039); [sp0141](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o004); [sp0221](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o017).

He keeps asking what care means. Is Kyoichi choosing this company or compensating for being loved? Does being the sole man secure a place that a woman might otherwise take? The moth/spider account makes attraction and dependence coexist, while the offered hand answers through action rather than a complete definition. Imagase can hear a meaningful invitation and still remain afraid. His intensity is intelligible without treating every suspicion as accurate. [sp0233](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o029); [sp0242](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o038); [sp0246](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o042).

The household fantasy in U08 separates a desired role from actual recognition. Imagase imagines wifehood, a child, attention and monitoring; he also sees troubling aspects of the fantasy himself. None is a negotiated household agreement. Kyoichi's actual thanks reaches him without communicating the whole imagined life. This distinction is central to his desire: he wishes for an ordinary acknowledged place, while his attempts to protect it can convert care into control. [sp0256](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U08_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u08-o001); [sp0259](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U08_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u08-o004).

## Professional action and harmful thought

U10 gives Imagase a professional context in which adequate evidence and coordination matter. The colleague remains distinct from the stalking aggressor. Tamaki credits help; her fall and the full rescue are not continuously shown. Imagase then confesses his own harmful wish after a prolonged unexplained pause. The wish is disturbing evidence of jealousy and his moral self-evaluation. It is not a performed attempt to injure her, proof of pregnancy or a complete plan that explains the professional work. [sp0358](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o020); [sp0367](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o029); [sp0379](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o041).

His guilt and plea for a place can both be sincere; the sequence also allows guilt to become part of renewed persuasion. His claim that secret intimacy would hurt no one cannot guarantee that result. Likewise the rapid movement from asking to be chosen to retreating when Kyoichi agrees reveals fear of responsibility for the cost. The person who has demanded a nonzero bond can hesitate when he is offered a bond with consequences for Tamaki. [sp0387](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o049); [sp0432](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o094); [sp0435](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o097).

The bodily sequence preserves his vulnerability and agency in both directions. A request for force precedes later demands to be released; those later utterances retain authority at their own time. His subsequent praise cannot retrospectively supply assent. His own initiative during Kyoichi's sleep/waking includes Kyoichi's wait signal. A literary understanding of his ambivalence therefore requires identifying acts and turns, rather than decoding all refusal as one secret desire. [sp0411](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o073); [sp0415](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o077); [sp0421](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o083); [sp0443](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o105).

## A friend sees another facet

Hummingbird gives Imagase a history of liking Kamimura's company while distinguishing that affection from attraction toward Sakano. The teacher is shown teaching, with no enacted teacher–student relation. Imagase criticizes the boys' objectification and recognizes his own appearance-focused gaze. Self-criticism complicates his claim to superiority without equating private adolescent attraction with public humiliation. Kamimura defends herself; he is not made her sole rescuer. Their friendship survives and continues into adult contact. [sp0517](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o015); [sp0519](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o017); [sp0524](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o022).

This friendship changes the available portrait. Imagase can be curious, gentle, embarrassed and receptive outside the central struggle for assurance. Kamimura has her own crush, rejection and marriage; she is not present solely to interpret him. Her wedding account of ordinary welcome gives him a language for happiness that does not require a spectacular declaration. His fear that continuing effort will exhaust someone remains, and her encouragement is not omniscient knowledge of Kyoichi. [sp0527](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o025); [sp0530](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o028).

At home the welcome and rice-removal gesture actually reach him. Kyoichi's practical answer coexists with Imagase's blush. The paired birds arrive on a wedding bag, carrying another person's ceremony into his ordinary household without making it the same institution. The closing comic then permits vanity, gift boasting and abrasive friendship with Natsuki. Reported marriage/gifts and visible ring possession extend the aftermath while leaving earlier-call resolution and permanent security unshown. [sp0532](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o030); [sp0535](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o033).

Imagase's development makes room for being cared for without making his fear or harmful conduct disappear. His written politeness toward Kyoichi and abruptness elsewhere are relational evidence; an unvoiced manga supplies no acoustic performance. The [corpus reconstruction review](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/CORNERED_MOUSE_CORPUS_READINESS_AND_RECONSTRUCTION_REVIEW.md) preserves qualified historical tests and withholds a final universal response policy. The strongest literary account retains his capacity for departure, friendship and moral distress alongside pursuit, jealousy and the recurring temptation to secure love by controlling access.
