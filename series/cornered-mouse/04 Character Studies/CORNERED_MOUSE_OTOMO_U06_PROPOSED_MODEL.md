# Kyoichi Otomo at the first-work exit

This is a **proposed model of Kyoichi at CM-U06-EXIT**, not a monograph, global capability assessment or model of his eventual state. Its rules are derived interpretations; source observations remain in the readings. The [title-work review](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/CORNERED_MOUSE_TITLE_WORK_ARCHITECTURE_AND_READINESS_REVIEW.md) fixes the evidence bundle, dimension/scenario gaps and architecture decision. The [project protocol](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/CORNERED_MOUSE_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md) governs use. No rule is promoted policy. Later tests cannot silently change these bytes.

## State and accessible knowledge

The model binds CM-CS-018, with historical CM-CS-001/005/010/012/016 and the linked information/decision records. Kyoichi has rejected Imagase in a declared choice, then experienced his actual absence, retained the cigarette remains for two months, heard Imagase's account of waiting and pursued a reunion. He has voiced fear and required an end to Imagase's other arrangement. His own promised holiday with Natsuki remains unresolved in the depicted ending. His earlier normal-man framing is still evidence; no final orientation or total ethical transformation is declared.

Kyoichi does not inherit the reader's access to Imagase's U03 private computer checks or the U06 rival meeting before his arrival. Imagase's later reported waiting/work costs become available only when spoken. His inference that Imagase sought a new target and his fear of exclusive dependence do not become objective truths. A scenario must declare what he actually knows, not supply the reader's entire archive.

## CM-RULE-OTOMO-001@v1 — threatened particular access can prompt pursuit

**Class:** CANDIDATE_POLICY; moderate confidence within a narrow dyad, unvalidated prospectively. **State:** U06 exit, transferable only while the relevant attachment, beliefs and available choices remain comparable. **Trigger:** after perceived loss or rupture, Kyoichi unexpectedly encounters Imagase with an apparent competing male attachment in a space where he can address him. **Stakes/options:** perceived replacement, desire and self-image conflict; he can initiate contact, leave or delay. Actual danger, intoxication, restraint, an emergency or an imposed public woman-versus-man choice excludes this rule.

**Predicted range:** before the immediate encounter ends, Kyoichi actively initiates a personal interaction with Imagase rather than simply leaving without addressing him. The model excludes silent, uninterrupted acceptance as its prediction. It predicts neither assault nor any exact line, sex, reconciliation, exclusive agreement or morally justified conduct. His initial language may be indirect; action and stated explanation must be scored separately.

**Basis:** U02 O025–O029, sp0075–0079: imagined happy ending gives way to pursuit and a confrontation, followed by a client correction and home invitation. U06 O037–O046, sp0179–0188: taxi pursuit, complaint about the butts, admission of retention and a condition before kissing. Primary locators are `CM-JP-AIO2020-LOCAL::sp0076::xhtml=item/xhtml/p-0074.xhtml::image=item/image/i-0074.jpg::panel=P00` and `CM-JP-AIO2020-LOCAL::sp0181::xhtml=item/xhtml/p-0179.xhtml::image=item/image/i-0179.jpg::panel=P00`. P00 is the declared whole-page locator, not an invented balloon coordinate. [U02 O026](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o026), [U06 O039](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o039); CM-DEC-025/029/031, CM-CL-001 v5.

**Counterexamples/inhibitors:** he leaves the U01 café, does not call back after the U01 goodbye and fails to make effective contact during U06's two-month absence. At U06's imposed public choice he rejects Imagase and leaves with Natsuki. The rule therefore requires immediate opportunity and perceived competing attachment, not loss alone; even then it rests on only two episodes. These exclusions are training-bound declarations, never retrospective excuses added to a failed test.

**Rivals:** jealous possession, attachment, fear of being replaced and anger can produce the same initiation. The predicted action cannot identify a unique motive or prove love. **Falsifier:** a first eligible encounter ends with an available opportunity but no personal initiation. **Abstention:** missing knowledge/opportunity, changed commitments, material intervening state change, unfamiliar partner, work task or serious danger. **Test:** CM-PR-004 in the claims ledger, fixed before U07. No source accuracy percentage is warranted.

## CM-RULE-OTOMO-002@v1 — self-description does not eliminate agency

**Class:** OBSERVED_CONSTRAINT, high confidence against literal helplessness; no new prospective action rule. **State/context:** U01–U06, especially familiar intimate conflict. Kyoichi's accounts of drifting, kindness and normalcy must be compared with his actual options and decisions. A reconstruction cannot replace all his decisions with passive compliance, nor infer good intention solely from considerate appearance.

**Basis:** the U01 café exit (O026/sp0036), U02 rush and invitation (O026–O029/sp0076–0079), U05's intended care-as-exit experiment and contrary warm result, and U06 choice/retention/pursuit. CM-CL-001 v5 and CM-CL-013 retain distinct purposes. **Counterevidence:** coercive report pressure, intoxication and practical costs constrain particular choices; active action does not establish unconstrained autonomy. **Rivals:** fear, habit, self-protection and care may overlap. **Falsifier/limit:** correction of a cited decision revises its local basis; this is not a forecast that he must act decisively next time. **Abstain:** claims about professional competence, strangers, comprehensive ethics or a permanent identity from this constraint alone.

## Written register and use limits

At sp0191 Kyoichi addresses Imagase as お前 in a direct fearful statement; Imagase answers with polite forms and 貴方. At sp0175 Kyoichi responds to a colleague with a polite apology. Sp0189 distinguishes his private 俺 self-reproach from Imagase's spoken phone call. These attested contrasts support audience-sensitive written language, not a single aggressive template, an acoustic voice or permission to invent the unheard recipient's speech (U06 O033/O047/O049). A close variant may retain the interlocutor and register; precise novel dialogue remains lower confidence than the action family.

D0 attested responses can be retrieved. D1 close variants are proposals subject to the stated exclusions. D2 unfamiliar native situations and D3 cross-series transfer require abstention unless independently supported. Unfamiliar scenarios must fix this source-state, accessible knowledge, commitments and introduced facts. Synthetic consistency cases are noncanonical and cannot validate a source prediction. Any successor state needs a new identified model; this U06 proposal remains historical even if later findings contradict it.
