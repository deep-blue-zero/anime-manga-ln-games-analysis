# Loving across a gap: an integrated reading of the supplied All-in-One

*The Cornered Mouse Dreams of Cheese* and *The Carp on the Chopping Block Jumps Twice* make the wish to be loved inseparable from the difficulty of recognizing another person's love without controlling its terms. Kyoichi wants the safety of being chosen while describing himself as carried by others. Imagase wants an acknowledged place while using means that can secure access more readily than reciprocity. Their attachment changes them through repeated failures of interpretation, actual absence and small acts of received care. The supplied revised All-in-One ends with chosen continuation rather than a guarantee that these difficulties have been solved. Its achievement depends on allowing that choice to matter while retaining the history of harm and the lives it has affected.

The opening bargain already separates proximity from recognition. Imagase can suppress information Kyoichi fears and obtain contact through that power. Devotion is present in his speech, but the cost of refusal remains part of the scene. Kyoichi can experience pleasure, resist particular acts and still be acting under pressure. The ethical distinctions are embedded in the drama rather than applied from outside it: limits are stated, challenged and overridden. An eventual loving relation cannot answer those earlier turns on behalf of the people who spoke them. At the same time, naming the coercion does not require imagining that every later desire is false. The work asks the reader to hold both facts long enough for change to become assessable. [sp0012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o002); [sp0019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o009).

Kyoichi's account of being responsive is equally unstable from the beginning. He confronts Imagase, invites Chikako shopping, refuses, leaves and later pursues. His preference for letting someone else supply the initiative can have the pleasant surface of consideration; it can also protect him from deciding what he wants and taking responsibility for its cost. Chikako's complaint makes the distinction concrete. When he offers to do anything, she remains the person required to tell him what. His kindness is not worthless, but it leaves work undone within the relation. The later remembered father supplies an intelligible history for seeking the person who loves him, without becoming a total explanation of his life. What matters is the repeated protection afforded by receiving another person's desire before naming his own. [sp0039](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u01-o029); [sp0208](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o004).

Other people repeatedly identify this inadequacy without becoming interchangeable judges. Natsuki wanted passion from a man whose kindness did not make her feel loved. Imagase asks whether care is romantic feeling or compensation for being loved. Tamaki can receive genuine affection and still lose the future she wants. Each perspective has a different history, knowledge and interest. Their convergence gives Kyoichi's problem weight; their differences prevent one person's verdict from becoming the complete truth of him. The women's abrasive or self-interested choices do not disqualify their accounts any more than Imagase's devotion makes his every interpretation reliable. The manga's moral scale depends on preserving particular recipients of conduct. [sp0116](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o020); [sp0233](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o029); [sp0460](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o122).

Imagase's methods expose a related difficulty from another direction. Information can tell him where a threat might be, but cannot make him secure in the place he has. The phone search, computer trace and birthday-keyed inbox produce interpretations that exceed what the discoveries establish. A lock becomes imagined guilt; a message becomes replacement; an unfamiliar man becomes a rival in Kyoichi's mirrored error. These scenes make both men readers of incomplete evidence. The birthday key is especially exact: being important enough to furnish the code does not authorize taking access, and taking access does not deliver the desired assurance. Care and mistrust can inhabit the same material fact. [sp0093](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U03_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u03-o002); [sp0300](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o040).

The form makes this uncertainty an experience for the reader. A room is explained after its appearance; a kiss is later located in student memory; apparent romantic company becomes a client; a daughter correction reaches the men at different times. The page offers a compelling image and then tests what it seemed to mean. Close faces provide affect without a uniquely certified motive. Private monologue grants access to one person's account without giving that account to the other characters. The reader gains knowledge by tracking these distinctions, but never receives a license to make all minds transparent. That limit is also the central relationship's problem: visible presence, pleasure and kindness repeatedly carry meanings one participant cannot securely share.

Small acts begin to answer the problem without solving it abstractly. The lighter's first transfer has a practical reason and several histories; once Imagase explains what it meant to him, Kyoichi repeats the gift under changed knowledge. A compliment intended to help Kyoichi escape domestic ease produces a good mood instead. A birthday bottle can be opened after the offer of another gift next year. These acts are meaningful because someone receives them as particular care. Their force does not require a perfectly pure motive in the giver or a complete contract for the future. The work locates development in the sometimes inconvenient consequences of paying attention. [sp0135](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U04_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u04-o039); [sp0141](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U05_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u05-o004); [sp0221](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U07_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u07-o017).

Actual absence gives those ordinary presences their cost. Imagase's quiet acceptance at the café is followed by another encounter, a plea and a departure that Kyoichi had not expected to change the routine so completely. Missing clothes and a disconnected number make loss more than rhetoric. Cigarette remains survive the person, while Kyoichi says he could discard them whenever he wished. His delay is an action he has declined to name as attachment. At reunion both men have understood themselves as unwanted, using different measures of what would count as seeking contact. Their mutual fear is more revealing than Kyoichi's imagined division between the secure pursuer and the isolated man who enters his world. [sp0161](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o019); [sp0172](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o030); [sp0185](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o043).

That reunion is still ethically unfinished. Kyoichi requires Imagase to settle another arrangement while Natsuki's own booked holiday and unanswered call remain on the page. The title-work ending's rain and drowning question hold intimacy beside unresolved consequences. Desire has crossed a bodily boundary, but it has not thereby answered the obligations surrounding it. U09 repeats the lesson under changed conditions: a desired change in participation and Kyoichi's new sense of responsibility are followed by Imagase declaring a dead end and leaving. A bodily role cannot do all the work of recognizing a partner, and a powerful scene cannot establish a permanent policy. The successive endings prevent development from being measured by sexual escalation alone. [sp0200](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o058); [sp0305](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o045); [sp0326](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o066).

The language of an ordinary world supplies another inadequate solution. Kyoichi can find a woman reassuring and still be participating in an affair; he can choose a woman and remain frightened of losing Imagase; he can recognize a male lover and continue to like Tamaki. Identity, desire, conduct and commitment overlap without becoming identical. Imagase's wifehood fantasy likewise imagines recognition through a familiar role while keeping surveillance and self-critique within the imagined household. The eventual real thank-you reaches him without communicating the wife, child or schedule. The manga gives gendered scripts psychological force and shows their insufficiency in relation to actual people. It does not require one final orientation verdict to make those contradictions intelligible. [sp0059](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U02_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u02-o009); [sp0163](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U06_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u06-o021); [sp0259](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U08_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u08-o004).

Tamaki makes the social meaning of recognition especially sharp. The earlier affair inference yields to her history as Yanagida's daughter; the funeral shows a father who existed for her and a recognized place she cannot occupy. Her later desire for Kyoichi's available place resonates with that exclusion without making fatherhood and romance the same institution or right. She can care about his grief and welcome its opening. U10 then gives the courtship enough substance that ending it costs something. Her stalking consultation is an action, her fear of being suspected belongs to her account, and the aggressor remains responsible for his act. Imagase's later confession of a harmful wish adds another person's interior without transforming that thought into the cause of her fall or something she knowingly forgives. [sp0297](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o037); [sp0335](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U09_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u09-o075); [sp0359](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o021); [sp0379](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o041).

The renewed central intimacy retains the same need for temporal precision. Imagase's request for force does not cancel later release/refusal language. Kyoichi's hurt and retaliatory thought do not grant permission. Imagase's later praise cannot be delivered backward, and Kyoichi's own wait signal during the reversed initiative deserves equal attention. The source's contradictory utterances remain part of the relationship's ethical reality. The reader can understand their desperation and recognize desired contact without deciding that all resistance is secret assent. Development becomes more credible when its remaining failures are allowed to stay visible. [sp0411](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o073); [sp0415](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o077); [sp0421](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o083).

What distinguishes the final choice is consequently not the intensity of the reunion alone. Imagase asks for a breakup and retreats when Kyoichi agrees, revealing fear of responsibility for the cost of being chosen. Kyoichi insists that he decides his happiness, then travels to the hospital and performs the ending. Tamaki requires him to sit, look at her and speak; a promise between the men acquires an affected recipient who shapes its form. He genuinely likes her and cannot continue. Her offer to wait does not become a mutual reserve contract, and her grief is not absorbed into the men's reconciliation. Mina's later scene gives her humor, anger and actual comfort through friendship. Even that support preserves unequal knowledge: Mina's corridor inference is not a disclosure Tamaki is shown receiving. [sp0432](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o094); [sp0452](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o114); [sp0473](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o135).

Kyoichi's late claim that he still follows the strongest flow therefore cannot simply cancel development. His familiar manner persists, but the cost and object of the action have changed. He has chosen and performed something he cannot make painless by calling it someone else's initiative. The no-third-time warning gives the renewed bond a limit; the bridge imagery imagines care without permanently filling the gap. His private language can accept possible loss while his cooler public speech communicates less. Ring offers then provide a limited sign that can be received. Reciprocity does not erase the difference between Imagase possibly wearing the ring at work and Kyoichi being unable to do so. A chosen life remains socially uneven and incompletely spoken. [sp0484](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o146); [sp0487](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o149); [sp0491](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o153); [sp0497](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_U10_DEEP_READING.md#cm-u10-o159).

Hummingbird enlarges that life by returning to a friendship rather than only adding another trial of the couple. Young Imagase likes Kamimura's company while locating attraction elsewhere; she has her own crush, rejects humiliating public interpretation and remains his friend. The delayed negation preserves continuity where a familiar plot might have supplied estrangement. Her adult marriage is her own event, and her account of happiness gives ordinary conversation and welcome a value that Imagase can desire. The friend context also complicates the closed world he and Kyoichi sometimes imagine: a meaningful life has relations beyond their struggle for assurance. Kamimura's encouragement is helpful without granting her omniscience. [sp0519](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o017); [sp0524](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o022); [sp0527](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o025).

The last welcome home brings that account to a modest scale. Kyoichi snacks before dinner; Imagase has bought food. A hair gesture gives affectionate expectation and then the practical explanation of rice. The plain noun and ceremonial correction make room for comedy without deleting Imagase's response. The wedding bag brings paired birds from another person's ceremony into their household. It does not turn them into the same marriage or make every public barrier disappear. The ordinary act can matter without either participant possessing a complete explanation of why it matters. The strongest ending is located in that received particularity. [sp0532](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o030); [sp0534](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o032).

The adjacent comic keeps vanity and abrasiveness alive after solemn commitment. Visible ring possession and reported birthday gifts extend the tokens beyond an unperformed offer, while Natsuki's reported marriage gives her a life beyond the earlier choice. The scene does not supply every purchase, the earlier phone-call resolution or a secure lifetime. Creator commentary read separately places Hummingbird's adult present roughly three to four weeks after the sequel ending and before Cassis Orange. The old afterword invites imagined continuation. Those statements frame the edition's layered ending without authorizing invented future events or replacing fictional evidence of motive. The missing Reboot story remains outside this run, and no original-form equivalence is claimed. [sp0535](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/CORNERED_MOUSE_S01_DEEP_READING.md#cm-s01-o033); [creator note](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/CORNERED_MOUSE_SOURCE_COMPLETION_AND_LONGITUDINAL_RECONCILIATION.md#cm-para-o012).

A credible counterreading sees the final tenderness as another reassurance in a cycle that has repeatedly joined care to pressure and concealment. The earlier ruptures, continuing fear and comic deflation make that risk real. The work also supplies differences that a pure repetition account misses: an actual costly breakup, a stated limit on renewed pursuit, reciprocal signs with admitted constraints, and an ordinary welcome received after reflection on another person's effort. Neither side can prove the unseen future. The supplied ending asks whether a chosen relation is worth maintaining despite that absence of proof, while leaving its participants responsible for the ways they maintain it.

Across the specialists, this is the point of convergence. The character histories establish capacity for change without a total personality replacement; the relationship study distinguishes access from recognition; the sexuality study keeps social scripts from exhausting desire; the ethical study preserves act-specific responsibility; the form study explains the reader's incomplete access; the secondary study keeps the cost distributed among identifiable lives. The resulting interpretation does not certify simulation. The original prospective failures, qualified matches and non-discriminating window remain as recorded. Literary understanding can explain why these people surprise both one another and their own accounts without pretending to forecast every response.

The final movement is therefore from trying to secure love through a manageable account toward giving and receiving care across a gap that remains. This is an achieved change in the supplied witness, neither a promise that the gap will close nor permission to stop judging what happens across it. Its ordinary scale is its strength: a retained object, a difficult conversation, a future sign, a welcome and a grain of rice can matter because another person receives them. The lives around the couple and the history within it keep that significance answerable to more than desire alone.
