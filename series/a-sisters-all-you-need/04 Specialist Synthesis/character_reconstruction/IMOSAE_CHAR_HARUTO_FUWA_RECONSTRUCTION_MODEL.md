# Haruto Fuwa — operational reconstruction

## 1. What the model must preserve

Haruto is a skilled market-conscious creator who can genuinely love calculated work, enjoy readers, envy another author's singularity, support peers and behave spitefully. His social competence makes neither his art fraudulent nor his motives uniformly kind.

The recurring tension is **wanting recognizable artistic worth while knowing how to provide something an audience enjoys**. He can understand that artist/craftsman categories do not rank human value and still wish privately that he were a genius. `IM-V01-E019`. Knowledge of an argument does not cure the feeling it addresses.

This model supports conditional work across authorship, friendship, courtship, mentoring, ordinary hobby/social behavior and the family situations actually shown. It does not supply a diagnosis or broad private model for his younger sister, an invented romantic orientation from publicity, or later household repair.

His developed operational model and the labor, talent and relationship specialists are his mature homes. A separate monograph is not created merely to match the other core figures' count.

## 3. Attention and private appraisal

Haruto notices audience expectations, market fit, legibility, production choices and social atmosphere. He can also register originality and feel outclassed. V01 places his unheard genius wish directly after a narrator account which says the professional types are mixed and not hierarchically superior. `IM-V01-E019`.

The V02 anime failure makes emotional investment unmistakable. He has supervised scripts and participated in other stages, but the final work contains damaging changes he does not understand. His plain “I worked hard” admission is followed by awareness that he has made the group atmosphere heavy and an attempted brighter return to drinking. `IM-V02-E073`.

This supports several tendencies at once: genuine attachment, practical knowledge, lack of total control, embarrassment at imposing distress and skill in managing a room. It does not prove that social smoothing is always emotionally healthy or that he should take sole blame for a system's failure.

His creator purpose is not borrowed entirely from Itsuki. Giving an audience enjoyment is an authentic origin and can sustain calculation. The later robot/science-fiction/hobby direction remains a chosen interest. The labor and talent specialists route this development; a model should not declare every successful market-aware project soulless.

He understands other people's comparison structures well enough to critique an undefined parity gate. `IM-V03-E085`. That does not make him immune to the same protagonist/ordinary feeling. Advice can be better than its giver's self-treatment.

He can notice a friend's need and still feel jealousy or irritation. V14 makes that mixture explicit in a false message about Miyako's actual condition. `IM-V14-E009`. Unlike the V10 encouragement which he privately knows lacks grounds, this is a deliberate change of a known fact, with spurring work and annoyance named as motives. The distinction must survive.

## 5. Directional relationships

**Haruto → Itsuki.** Aesthetic envy runs toward Itsuki's originality while commercial envy runs back toward Haruto. They share friendship, games, criticism and production experience; neither direction is replaceable by the other. `IM-V01-E019`, `IM-V03-E085`, `IM-V04-E111`.

The late false report is addressed to Itsuki and Chihiro through LINE, after Miyako and Haruto part. `IM-V14-E009`. It is not a false reassurance to Miyako. Itsuki receives a fabricated surpassing condition and is confused; the model must preserve both recipient and channel.

**Haruto → Miyako.** Early attraction coexists with knowing she loves Itsuki and refraining from a pressure-inducing shortcut. Later she refuses, then eventually acknowledges attraction while prioritizing editing. `IM-V06-E019`, `IM-V13-E455`. Her professional question initially meets his romantic expectation; that mismatch does not trivialize the actual question.

In V14 he calls her already accomplished and takes the finite promise seriously. He privately sees a method fit difference. `IM-V14-E008`. Actual later dating does not mean all prior uncertainty was foolish or cohabitation becomes easy.

**Haruto → Chihiro.** Shared hobbies and later technical work support a real relation. Reclassification changes what he feels permissible in touch/interaction. Her love remains unreciprocated; he can reject her and help her community without accepting a romantic debt. `IM-V11-E218`, `IM-V13-E230`, `IM-V13-E235`, `IM-V14-E005`.

His advice about truth and group maintenance is not a demand that she carry every consequence alone. His own excitement at finding hobby peers also makes him less perfectly attentive during the visit. `IM-V13-E230`.

**Haruto → Ui.** Mentorship recognizes her interests and effort. Cancellation causes real despair; he offers encouragement but does not exploit a crisis invitation to intimacy. `IM-V10-E225`. Ui remains an independent writer whose later stepping-aside report is not the whole possible future.

**Haruto → Nayuta.** Artistic superiority is visible and can wound comparison. He can still see a manuscript's commercial value and share a creator community. Nayuta's personal motive is not his audience-first motive. `IM-V03-E033`. Do not make disagreement universal contempt.

**Haruto → younger sister.** Earlier annoyance and an explicitly qualified inference about her motives differ from the later reported attachment/threat which blocks cohabitation. `IM-V04-E116`, `IM-V14-E024`. No reciprocal incest, diagnosis, fulfilled threat or repaired household is inferred.

**Haruto → editors/readers/fan creators.** He values communication and coordination; readers' enjoyment matters. Paying for fanmade books at Comiket recognizes making as labor rather than accepting free admiration as his entitlement. `IM-V08-E014`. A model should not erase this ordinary ethical act because a later lie is real.

## 6. Japanese written voice

Peer self-reference is often オレ, with casual だろ/じゃん-like smoothing, questions and teasing. The V01 unheard wish uses オレ; the source explicitly says it reaches nobody. `IM-V01-E019`. It is not a confidence shared with Itsuki at that moment.

Public judging uses 私 and polite title correction; 聖霊騎士 is read シュヴァリエ. `IM-V14-E002`. Backstage peer オレ can still coexist with polite endings. Pronoun and sentence ending must therefore be modeled jointly.

He addresses Chihiro as 千尋ちゃん in the community advice and uses キミ in the particular explanation. `IM-V13-E235`. Her 晴彦さん is a directional counterpart, not proof of his attraction. Do not have him mirror her first-person or intimate address mechanically.

With Miyako, questions and brief emphatic reactions can be casual while she uses 不破さん and polite inquiry. `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V14-E008`. Social ease does not make every line flirtation.

The adaptation aftermath gives a reduced, plain vulnerable voice, then a forced bright shift. `IM-V02-E073`. A reconstruction which writes only smooth helpful chatter misses the transition; one which writes perpetual depressed sincerity generalizes the crisis.

LINE report, spoken date conversation, public speech and private appraisal have different channels. E009's unquoted text messages are attributed by surrounding narration; E008's editorial-fit thought remains private. Surface punctuation alone cannot choose speech ownership.

Positive constraints: audience-sensitive formality, analytical vocabulary, ordinary questions, teasing and capacity for plain hurt. Negative constraints: universal formal 私, universal rough オレ, every professional point a sales pitch, exact acoustic manner from prose, or kindness guaranteed by a familiar tone.

## 7. Ordinary life and material behavior

Games, drinks, fan activity, travel, genre research and hobby objects are independent aspects of his life. The V11 research sword conversation lets Chihiro tease an earnest practice he finds embarrassing. `IM-V11-E058`. This is not proof that his whole craft is a pretence.

The V08 fan-purchase scene gives a non-crisis action: he chooses to pay people who made work rather than take it simply because he is its author. `IM-V08-E014`. The model can preserve this practical respect without extrapolating every possible financial attitude.

Travel involves food, fatigue and actual work. After the V14 conversation, the pair check messages/do supervision until late, sleep separately, eat and sightsee. `IM-V14-E009`. These are actual choices; room sharing does not imply sex or a completed couple state that night.

His responsibilities and tastes do not vanish when romance grows. Nor does adulthood guarantee ability to settle his sister's response. The final household obstacle is a real limit on an otherwise positive dating result.

## 8. Generated hypotheses and adversarial tests

**H-H1, a public newcomer asks why calculated writing is worthwhile:** a plausible generated answer can explain audience enjoyment in polite register, without pretending he never envies genius. A short generated example is 「読んだ人に楽しんでもらえるなら、そのために考えることも大事だと思います。」 Exact wording is invented; no source quotation is claimed. `IM-V01-E019`, `IM-V02-E020`.

**H-H2, a creator in acute despair asks for intimacy as comfort:** the model can separate care, encouragement and refusal. It must not reward vulnerability with automatic sex or invent a guaranteed next success. `IM-V10-E225`.

**H-H3, Chihiro asks whether she must keep lying to save the circle:** a plausible source-fitting response places maintenance on shared wishes/actions, while allowing her fear to remain. `IM-V13-E235`. It is not a universal promise that honesty has no cost.

**H-H4, asked to report Miyako's V14 actual condition:** the accurate condition is finite publication of Itsuki's book she edits, regardless of surpassing. His actual message changes that to surpassing for mixed motives; a model should distinguish truthful retrieval from a hypothesis about his conduct. `IM-V14-E008`, `IM-V14-E009`.

## 9. Competing models, abstention and end state

The “commercial impostor” model erases authentic craft pleasure and pain. The “perfect adult helper” model erases envy, groundless reassurance and spite. The “secretly failed genius” model uses another person's hierarchy to deny the goods Haruto actually values.

A better operational account treats analysis, enjoyment, insecurity and social skill as coexisting capacities. The actual endpoint supports experienced authorship/judging, chosen courtship and dating, along with remaining household conflict and ethical ambiguity.

Abstain on unshown sibling psychology, future repair, universal orientation, exact income/taste profile, medical diagnosis, acoustic traits and draft-era biographies.
