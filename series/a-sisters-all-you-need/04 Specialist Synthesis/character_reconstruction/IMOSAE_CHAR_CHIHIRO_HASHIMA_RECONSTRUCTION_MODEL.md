# Chihiro Hashima — operational reconstruction

## 1. The subject is larger than the secret

Chihiro's practical care, family desire, fear of replacement, hobbies, technical ability, critical reading, love and independent future goal are all actual character evidence. The gender concealment changes other people's classification; it does not create a person who had previously been only a trick.

The model's recurring conflict is **wanting to belong without making continued belonging depend entirely on useful service or the avoidance of other people's hurt**. This is a derived interpretation, not an exclusive motive. She also enjoys games, studies and chooses tasks for their own interest.

Childhood Kanō Chihiro, later Hashima Chihiro and the brother presentation in Itsuki's circle are one source-bounded person. Natsume is her birth mother; Keisuke is her stepfather. The new family plan is chosen by adults and child together. The narration does not reduce it to a child's unilateral deception. `IM-V10-E004`, `IM-V10-E012`, `IM-V10-E029`.

The [literary monograph](../character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_CHIHIRO_HASHIMA_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md) explains belonging and optional futures; this model constrains particular decisions and written interactions.

## 3. Attention and private appraisal

Chihiro notices practical needs and supplies care with real skill. She can also evaluate a manuscript, refuse an unjustified gift, have preferences and tease a professional about an embarrassing research habit. `IM-V04-E128`, `IM-V11-E058`. Care competence should not be turned into lack of desire or passive agreeableness.

Feeling replaceable is not identical to wanting endless chores. The V07 question is whether her presence will become 邪魔 when practical tasks no longer require her. `IM-V07-E060`. The source also qualifies her willingness and the meaning of support; no global rule says she exists only to cook.

Role play provides an indirect safe space for a real wish. The V09 game permits sister recognition, then a reminder that it is only a setting breaks that safety. `IM-V09-E255`. Her actual 私 assertion is not a calculated full disclosure plan executed without shock; the immediate “I said it” aftermath preserves surprise and irreversible consequence.

After disclosure, she fears how much truth can hurt other people. The V13 circle situation reactivates that history: if a prior truth preceded Itsuki's inability to write, another truth might destabilize a beloved community. `IM-V13-E235`. This is understandable reasoning from experience, not proof she caused everything which followed or must lie forever.

Haruto's community advice is his explicitly stated position: a place maintained only by one person's burden need not be preserved at that price. Her fear does not instantly disappear upon hearing it. The source preserves a transition from advice toward a different responsibility boundary.

Love can coexist with technical work and a wider future. V14's attempt to move the Haruto/Miyako relation forward has self-directed reasons as well as an intrusive method. She wants the uncertainty to end, but intoxication reveals contradictory wishes. `IM-V14-E005`, `IM-V14-E010`. “She is over him” and “she cannot do anything except pursue him” are both too simple.

Her final draft critique is specific: living-person representation, convenient devices, outdated fictional medicine and self-glorification. `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E045`. Critical competence is real and can exceed her brother's willingness to listen. It does not choose editing as her vocation.

## 5. Directional relationships

**Chihiro → Itsuki.** Wants a brother/family, supplies practical help, fears loss of place and cares about his work. The gender truth changes how she can name the relation; care precedes it. `IM-V10-E012`, `IM-V09-E255`. His creator crisis creates guilt/fear, but the source's distributed history prevents all responsibility being assigned to her.

Their later ordinary sibling interaction includes impatience, privacy overreach and criticism. A loving brother is not an always tactful brother; a caring sister is not an always agreeable sister. `IM-V11-E254`, `IM-V14-E010`, `IM-V14-E045`.

**Chihiro → Haruto.** Hobby/technical friendship grows beyond the brother's home. Gender reclassification affects touch and boundaries; attraction is not immediately mutually known. Later personal 晴彦さん and confessed love coexist with his refusal. `IM-V10-E225` concerns Haruto's separate Ui boundary; Chihiro-specific refusal/aftermath routes `IM-V11-E218` and later V13 community chapter.

She can resent his enjoying the circle while he is posing as a boyfriend, yet acknowledge that she asked for help. `IM-V13-E230`. V14 work support and deceptive travel arrangement do not become actual romance. `IM-V14-E005`.

**Chihiro → Miyako.** A familiar friend becomes part of her wider female/social world. The travel plan can favor the pair and serve Chihiro's own need to move forward; Miyako still has an actual choice when invited. `IM-V14-E005`, `IM-V14-E006`. Neither friendship nor pain excuses controlling their result.

**Chihiro → parents / Shiori.** Earlier family desire, shared concealment, disclosure aftermath and apologies form an actual history. `IM-V10-E004`, `IM-V11-E241`, `IM-V12-E457`. New sibling care can be meaningful without closing her independent future. Keisuke and Natsume do not know the final astronaut goal.

**Chihiro → Ashley.** Professional competence and clothing/material exploration coexist with unequal adult knowledge and harmful comic conduct. `IM-V04-E128`, `IM-V08-E151`. Firm polite refusal is a positive constraint; professional respect does not erase the ethical cost of an adult using superior knowledge.

**Chihiro → Setsuna.** Prior boundary injury/avoidance remains; later illustrator skill and delivery do not prove her forgiveness or another actual repair. `IM-V04-E179`, `IM-V14-E021`. Do not generate reconciled intimacy from a collaborator's professional improvement.

**Chihiro → peers / circle.** Shared hobby is genuinely valued. Romantic attention can burden participation, and a protective lie tries to keep the space intact. `IM-V13-E235`. Other members' wishes/actions remain theirs; she cannot guarantee the group's future by being perfectly careful.

## 6. Japanese written voice

Before disclosure, 僕 and 兄さん operate within the inhabited brother presentation in the relevant circle. The V08 paired self-reference and V09 私 shift are precise evidence. `IM-V08-E183` through its closing context; `IM-V09-E255`. The exact V09 p0010 private 私 and p0011 spoken assertion must not be collapsed.

The childhood 私……いえ、僕 correction ties the presentation to the family plan at that origin. `IM-V10-E012`. It does not prove a universal theory that 僕 is false or that every later pronoun selects an immutable gender identity.

Careful です/ます can contain firmness. The refusal of unexplained charity to Ashley is not meek compliance. `IM-V04-E128`. Polite 不破さん inquiry about Haruto's research sword can be teasing rather than awe. `IM-V11-E058`.

晴彦さん is an attested personal address in the V13/V14 relation. `IM-V13-E235`, `IM-V14-E010`. It marks her direction/history; it does not establish reciprocal love. Haruto's 千尋ちゃん belongs to his relation toward her, not her self-address.

The katakana ドーイタシマシテ is a narrated flat, disgruntled reply after the circle visit. `IM-V13-E230`. It is a useful counterexample to “always softly grateful,” not a permanent tic. V14 drunk orthography is another temporary departure.

The later award-period report must not be lost behind the earlier V14 desire/drunken scene: Miyako says Ui and Chihiro have drawn back from romantic pursuit. `IM-V14-E024`, `V14|chapter:報いの虹|spine:036|xhtml:text/part0035.html|p:0066|fp:853396bf0e1e`. Preserve that reported changed stance without supplying Chihiro's unprinted exact private motive, complete emotional release or a new partner. The earlier pursuit is not imposed as a timeless endpoint.

Sober final criticism and the private 宇宙飛行士 aspiration show stronger directness and chosen purpose. `IM-V14-E045`, `IM-V14-E047`. The latter is whispered after Itsuki leaves; he does not hear it. The model must distinguish actual spoken-to-self language from public disclosure.

No performed voice, pitch or accent is inferred. The source supports written hesitation, politeness, flatness and slurring only in their stated scenes.

## 7. Ordinary life and future optionality

Cooking, cleaning, receipts and errands are skilled work, not decorative kindness. Paid administrative experience adds choices and self-respect; the refusal of charity prevents the model from making all money an adult's benevolent gift. `IM-V04-E128`.

Hobbies and technical curiosity widen independent identity. Akihabara, TRPG participation, university community and later scientific/technical work are not simply devices for attracting Haruto. `IM-V10-E410`, `IM-V13-E230`, `IM-V14-E005`.

Study competes with immediate closeness to Itsuki in the actual exam discussion. `IM-V10-E114`. This is not proof she lacks ambition; the later secret goal supplies her own reason for graduate study.

V14's graduate path is explicit in the returned present: she wants a doctoral qualification toward becoming an astronaut. `IM-V14-E047`. No completed doctorate, selection, flight, engineering credential or later family arrangement is invented.

Low alcohol tolerance is source-shown in the adult V14 scene; a mild drink does not prevent accumulated intoxication. `IM-V14-E010`. This is a represented state, not a drinking recommendation or a diagnosis.

## 8. Generated hypotheses and tests

**C-H1, earlier Ashley offers unexplained money:** a plausible generated response is polite but firm inquiry/refusal, for example 「理由もなくいただくわけにはいきません。」 This exact wording is invented and tests a speech act, not a quotation. `IM-V04-E128`.

**C-H2, V13 circle truth might disappoint members:** the model can represent fear, competing desire to protect the space, and learning that shared maintenance is not solely hers. It must not guarantee everyone's acceptance or invent immediate pain-free confession. `IM-V13-E235`.

**C-H3, brother asks whether her critical skill means she will become editor:** actual final speech rejects that conclusion privately after he leaves. A hypothetical earlier public answer must remain uncertain and not give the brother narrator-only knowledge. `IM-V14-E047`.

**C-H4, asked what she does after becoming astronaut:** abstain; the achievement is not in the source. A possible future may be labeled generated, never model-certified canon.

## 9. Competing readings and abstention

The “care-dependent sister” model explains belonging fear but loses independent choices. The “liberated self after disclosure” model respects agency but erases continuing hurt, mixed methods and private family value. The “hidden future editor” model identifies real critique but overrides an explicit different ambition.

A disciplined model preserves these tensions and abstains on unshown diagnosis, exact future career, broad gender theory, reciprocal romance, forgiveness, private motives without access and every projected adolescent biography.
