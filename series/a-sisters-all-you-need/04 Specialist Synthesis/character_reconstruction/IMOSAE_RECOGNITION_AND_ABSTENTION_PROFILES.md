# Recognition profiles and explicit abstention

## 1. What recognition permits

These profiles retain significant people whose evidence does not warrant an operational model at the full main-series horizon. A profile can identify a person, action, local speech or relation and route it to source. It cannot confidently supply a new private motive, general decision engine or unrestricted dialogue.

The local distinction is evidentiary rather than evaluative. A household participant or industry leader may be extremely important and still have little independent private access. Infants matter to the ending without supporting stable adolescent psychology. The [readiness audit](IMOSAE_CHARACTER_READINESS_AUDIT.md) records the overall decision; [protocol](IMOSAE_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md) controls use.

## 2. Creators and industry participants

### Manaka Kumano / Mana — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

熊野愛果 is Manaka; Mana is a nickname. V14 directly supports an adult partner relation with Kaiko, independent creative/doujin work, shared living space and the practical cost of noise/privacy around work. `IM-V14-E016`, `IM-V14-E017`.

The relation has two agents. Manaka is not simply a reward for Kaiko's career and must not be assumed to share every taste, desire or work schedule. Later reported family acceptance is a result with its own evidential ceiling, not a full record of all conversations.

The profile can answer who she is in the actual Comiket/household scenes and why those scenes matter. It cannot extrapolate all couple choices, jealousy, sexual preferences or private psychology from a selected intimate encounter. For exact voice, reopen the particular scene; no global first-person/catchphrase rule is assigned.

### Takashina Yūma — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

高科勇真 is the actor and substantive reader who explains how Nayuta's work changes attention toward other lives. His professional reasoning and confession/refusal are not merely a failed route introduced to flatter Itsuki. `IM-V12-E345`, `IM-V12-E410`.

The source supports serious work, independent values and a plausible alternative to Nayuta's concentrated love. Her refusal does not prove him objectively less worthy. The concentrated V12 window supplies too little ordinary/longitudinal voice variation for broad simulation.

Use the profile for the represented reader-effect/craft argument and the actual refusal. Abstain on later romance, routine, private resentment or a general actor psychology. Narrated/quoted positions are not performed audio.

### Yanagase Makoto — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

柳ヶ瀬慎 is a particular editor with source-shown creator fit, especially in the Kiso entry/working relation. The mature labor specialist and V08 reading preserve that differentiated contribution. His later industry presence does not supply a full private person. `IM-V08-E206` identifies the relevant creator/editor visual context; exact substantive interaction stays in the V08 reading.

A profile can identify assignment, represented practice and comparison with other editorial methods. It cannot confidently answer unrelated social/romantic questions or assume every author would receive the same treatment.

### Yoshihiro Kiso — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

木曽義弘 is read よしひろ, an older writer and Nadeshiko's grandfather. He receives late-entry professional recognition; V14 gives age72, while V06 describes the late-sixties cohort. No exact birthday/calendar is derived from those approximate states.

The V13 parody is presented through his attribution before its actual young author is understood; that prestige attribution changes Toki's reaction. `IM-V13-E345`. V14's account of Nadeshiko's early praise names the grandfather among the adults who encouraged her. `IM-V14-E011`.

Use these for creator generation, family opportunity and attribution effects. Do not infer a complete grandfather philosophy, daily domestic life or that every perceived kindness is professional evaluation. Neither Nadeshiko nor an invented Nagisa is his alias.

### Hoshiimo — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

干し芋 / Hoshiimo is the illustrator whose established reliability helps attract unsustainable workload. `IM-V08-E050`. This is direct counterevidence to a moral account in which every delayed artist lacks diligence.

The profile preserves reliability, over-allocation and production consequences. It does not invent a full burnout diagnosis, private contempt for clients, permanent incapacity or a universal scheduling method. The creator's significance is causal and institutional even without enough direct dialogue/interiority for operational extrapolation.

### Kirara Yamagata — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

山県きらら is the adaptation/publishing professional with candid production-network reasoning and sharp speech about asymmetric blame. `IM-V05-E029`. V09 also gives a situated assessment of Aoba's resemblance to Nayuta which can detect a problem without articulating a useful revision method. `IM-V09-E013`–`IM-V09-E018`.

These are distinct professional judgments, not proof of omniscient artistic evaluation or uniformly hostile personal behavior. The profile can route responsibility, mediation and the limits of “authenticity” vocabulary. It cannot supply private life, acoustic traits or an all-purpose editor simulation.

### Gōdo Satoshi — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

神戸聖 is read ごうどさとし. Kanbe/Kobe/Hijiri are unsupported current readings. Original V07 ruby also verifies the name in the older cohort's editorial history. `IM-V07-E164`.

He supplies a forceful cultivation position in V14, emphasizing the institutional resources required to develop creators. `IM-V14-E013`. The debate leaves costs and alternatives credible. His stern appearance is a depicted/focalized presentation, not criminal identity.

The profile permits source-specific institutional arguments and history. It withholds broad private motives, family life or a complete decision engine. His participation in Nadeko's professional feedback follows an explicitly changed feedback contract. `IM-V14-E011`.

### Shirogamine Nobunaga — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

城ヶ峰信長 is the Branch Hill institutional figure whose autonomy position contrasts with Gōdo's cultivation. `IM-V14-E013`. Practical costs, staff capacity and his represented creed are actual analytical material; they do not prove that independence is universally better.

Miyako's comment that she is surprised to see his firm conviction is **her** local appraisal. `IM-V14-E014`. The model must not turn a publicly stated philosophy into every private motive.

A profile supports institutional comparison and his source-shown mediation/management role. It cannot confidently simulate unrelated domestic or ethical dilemmas.

### Kamo Tadashi — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

加茂正 is a newcomer writer whose represented earlier life/employment circumstances and later self-presentation complicate a uniform creator cohort. V06's afterparty distinguishes him from the other novices rather than supplying one professional archetype. `IM-V06-E083`.

His later tax/industry talk is source-specific self-presentation, not current financial advice or a tax-professional identity. A profile may answer what he claims and how the scene uses it. No broad private motive, exact finances or universal work ethic is inferred.

## 3. Family and social participants

### Natsume — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

棗 is Chihiro's birth mother and Itsuki's stepmother, a participant in the new-family concealment plan and later apology/care. `IM-V10-E004`, `IM-V10-E012`, `IM-V11-E241`.

V11 directly shows ordinary 私 in a meal-ending line and moving her own dishes, while the father/son interaction continues. `IM-V11-E254`. Her domestic action is real, but not enough to reconstruct every private motive, role preference or marriage decision independently.

The profile preserves responsibility and care without blaming the child alone or making the mother merely invisible infrastructure. It cannot diagnose family members or guarantee later harmony. The late household contains several providers of care.

### Nodoka Hashima — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

羽島和花 is read のどか in original V10 ruby; she is Itsuki's deceased birth mother. The grave, remembered loss and Itsuki's changing family interpretation are actual. `IM-V10-E317` and its chapter context route the remembered relation, with the exact name witness in the correction layer.

Death is TF; a total trauma explanation for taste/romance is not. The profile cannot create an independent monograph of her private personality or predict what she would endorse after death. No guessed Waka name or unshown maternal instruction is admitted.

### Haruto's younger sister — RECOGNITION_PROFILE

Earlier care/annoyance and the V04 qualified inference about her feelings differ from the V14 reported attachment and threat which obstruct cohabitation. `IM-V04-E116`, `IM-V14-E024`.

A reported threat is not a performed act, diagnosis, reciprocal incest relation or completed repair. A single intense case is insufficient for general private psychology. The source can answer the obstacle's existence and how it limits adult closure; it cannot answer every future response.

### Other chiefly named family/industry associates — RECOGNITION only at exact scene

An isolated name, job, greeting or report can be indexed without becoming an operational subject. The source-significant roster above and the seventeen accepted models cover the people with developed evidence in this release.

A later question about another named participant should reopen their actual scenes before assigning a scope. The absence of a model is not a declaration that the person has no independent life. It is a limit of the admitted source and this reviewed evidence set.

## 5. Safe retrieval examples and adverse cases

“Who is Manaka in the actual final volume?” can receive the adult partner/creator relation with E016/E017. “What would she secretly feel in a new jealousy crisis?” must abstain or request a narrower hypothetical scope.

“What does Gōdo argue for?” can describe cultivation at E013. “Does he universally prefer control over people?” exceeds a public institutional position.

“Why does Hoshiimo's delay matter?” can identify reliability-induced overload. “What illness caused it?” is unshown.

“Did Shiori love the story?” can report the represented listener response and comprehension qualification. “Did she endorse it as a professional critic?” is a category error.

“Does Yū fear replacing Kasuka?” is answerable only about draft-Yū's represented interpretation. Actual young Yū's later response, the parents' actual naming motive and Kasuka's legal name are not established by that passage.

“Does the astronaut image prove Chihiro became one?” must refuse achieved biography; it is explicitly PF possibility. `IM-V14-E076`.

All these answers are bounded canon retrieval. None is an A–E capability assessment, a held-out prediction or authority to update global character files manually.
