# Character readiness audit

## 1. The decision and its evidential basis

Five core figures support broad, conditional FULL_SPECTRUM work: Itsuki, Nayuta, Miyako, Chihiro and Haruto. Toki and eleven other subjects support named domains. Important industry, social and family participants with narrower access receive recognition profiles, while infants, an unborn child and unshown projected lives do not receive independent operational models.

These decisions assess **actual evidence**, not significance, name frequency or a checklist quota. The local label is not an A–E capability grade or a global registry assessment. All model use remains conditional on era, relationship, knowledge and domain. Exact Japanese identity is governed by the [correction layer](../../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/IMOSAE_PRIMARY_IDENTITY_AND_NORMALIZATION_ERRATA.md).

The evidence base includes thirteen protected prospective readings, the completed V14 reading, five checkpoints, eight reconciled ledgers, original Japanese identity/voice checks and the mature specialists. No claim is made that all prior source sentences received a new semantic audit during reconstruction. The final QA records the actual challenged rules and readbacks.

Readiness is locally assessed; model production and semantic QA are separate states. At this document's initial creation, the assessments below are complete and the associated model/QA production is in progress. The final release audit must verify actual files and may narrow a scope if a model cannot satisfy its threshold.

## 5. Conditions which prevent inflation

A FULL_SPECTRUM label does not permit wrong-era knowledge, acoustic traits, invented diagnosis or future biography. Itsuki does not know Chihiro's disclosure before it happens. Miyako cannot know Haruto's privately narrated appraisal unless another scene tells her. Parents cannot know Chihiro's final secret ambition.

A DOMAIN_BOUNDED label does not permit a plausible professional maxim to become a complete decision engine. Toki has pride as well as competence; Ashley can offer care and cause harm; Kaiko's formal voice can sharpen into criticism; Setsuna's artistic change does not solve boundaries; Keisuke's later support does not erase prior misunderstanding.

A RECOGNITION_PROFILE can answer who a person is, what they did or how a particular statement functions. It should not confidently generate what they secretly desire in a new setting. INSUFFICIENT explicitly abstains.

The final manuscript provides affirmative limits: its invented futures are identified as a draft and criticized by actual readers. This is stronger than merely lacking a later volume. An answer that “predicts” teenage Sora from those pages has failed the continuity gate.
