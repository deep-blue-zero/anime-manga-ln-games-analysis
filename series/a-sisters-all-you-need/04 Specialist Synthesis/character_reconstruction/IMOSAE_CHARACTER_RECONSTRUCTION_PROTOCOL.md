# Project-local character reconstruction protocol

## 1. What this home does

This populated subdirectory of the existing 04 Specialist Synthesis home owns operational models, their readiness decisions and adversarial/evidence review. It extends the V1 architecture's character work without creating another series root. The sole series entrypoint remains [CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP](../../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md). The terminal character reference is a condensed navigation product, not a competing modeling protocol.

A literary monograph explains a person's trajectory and significance. An operational model constrains what that person might notice, infer, choose, say, hide or refuse in a specified situation. Neither inherits the other's conclusions without source routes. Mature monographs live in the architecture's character_monographs home, with links here.

The evidence horizon is Japanese V01–V14, with original EPUB as ultimate authority and frozen normalized/visual releases for retrieval. Source coordinates and identity corrections are exposed in the evidence indexes. Performed acoustic traits, unconsulted adaptations and unverified later life are outside the horizon.

Every model is an interpretive reconstruction with conditional application. No document licenses unlimited simulation. “Full spectrum” here describes breadth of source-tested domains, not certainty in every imaginable context.

## 2. The local scope vocabulary

| Local scope | Positive threshold | Required limitation |
|---|---|---|
| FULL_SPECTRUM | Repeated decisions, private access, relationship variation, ordinary behavior, written voice and contradictory states across materially different stakes | Select era, knowledge and interlocutor; abstain on unshown biography, radically novel domains and exact unsupplied speech |
| DOMAIN_BOUNDED | A coherent pattern of choices and written interaction within identified professional, family or social domains, with multiple diagnostic situations | Do not extrapolate the domain into a complete private person; missing mundane/intimate evidence remains a gap |
| RECOGNITION_PROFILE | Enough source to recognize a role, relationship, voice cue or local action, but too little variation/interiority for operational extension | Answer bounded canon questions; do not generate private deliberation or general decision rules |
| INSUFFICIENT | A name, infant presence, unshown person, narrow report or fictional projection cannot support a stable independent model | Identify what is known and refuse the reconstruction request |

These are **project-local** decisions. They are not corpus-wide A–E assessments, discovery upserts or population of the specification-only capability registry. The global curation agent retains its governed responsibilities.

Importance, name frequency, plot impact and amount of commentary do not alone establish readiness. A deceased historical figure can support a dated professional domain; an important editor can lack evidenced private life; an infant can be crucial to the ending while remaining unreconstructible as an adolescent.

## 3. Required scenario contract

Before applying a model, supply or recover:

1. Actual continuity or explicitly classified authored/performed level.
2. Era/state boundary, not merely “the character in the series.”
3. Interlocutor and directional relation at that boundary.
4. Facts the subject knows, facts only the reader knows and unresolved information.
5. Immediate setting, task, fatigue/intoxication if established and relevant practical constraints.
6. Stakes and whether the scenario stays inside evidenced domains.

If any missing item changes the answer materially, offer alternatives or abstain. Do not silently select the most dramatic era. A proposal for a hypothetical continuation must state that it is generated, not a later canonical event.

The model's output should distinguish likely attention, possible interpretation, preferred action, competing motive, written-speech constraints and uncertainty. The source sometimes supports a choice more strongly than an exact line. When voice evidence is narrow, paraphrase a likely speech act rather than inventing confidently canonical Japanese.

## 4. Evidence and attribution discipline

TF establishes represented facts; LF establishes source language; PF establishes paratextual statements; FR concerns formal recurrence; SI and IT are inference/interpretation; SP remains open. A model rule is generally SI/IT derived from multiple TF/LF scenes, not itself a new TF.

An anchor index resolves a source unit. It does not establish whether its caption is right. High-risk attribution receives contextual Japanese readback; registered adjacent context is explicitly identified where needed. The E023 correction shows why: Nayuta's request is shown, Itsuki's explicit reply is not.

Each decision rule requires at least one support route and a meaningful counterexample or boundary. Several rules may share a scene when they ask different questions. Repeating an anchor does not create independent evidence.

Private thought is not spoken speech and is not shared knowledge. Haruto's V14 appraisal of Miyako as accomplished and privately less suitable for his own process must not be inserted into her knowledge. The narrator's account of Nayuta's effort does not make every bystander know its amount.

Quoted content does not always mean immediate actual speech: scripts, games, novels and dreams quote voices. Conversely, an unquoted dash-led line can be spoken when attribution establishes it. Original ruby, gaiji and typography govern uncertain readings.

## 5. Temporal state and knowledge gates

The five checkpoints preserve historical interpretation. Models may use later evidence to explain an earlier state but may not give the earlier person later knowledge.

For the core ensemble, useful boundaries include early V01–V03 comparison/concealment, V04–V06 vocational/romantic action, V07–V09 maintenance and pressure, V10–V12 disclosure/collapse/repair, V13 independent professional practice and V14 actual adult states. These are retrieval segments, not claims that every character changes on the same schedule.

The individual model owns its finer segmentation. Toki's Izumi-related error, Haruto's anime disappointment and Chihiro's disclosure each require their own state transitions. A calendar reconstructed from volume number alone is prohibited.

V14 contains several time steps. Its opening ages, April publication, roughly a year to the award and a few months to the returned present must remain separate. Itsuki's June6 birthday is source-verified in V04; a supposed December birthday cannot be used to manufacture an age contradiction.

All adolescent futures in 青い小鳥たち stay inside Itsuki's draft. Actual Sora is two at the return. Actual Yū and Shiori are children; actual Kaede's represented teenage role is not an admitted adult/teen biography. Actual pregnancy does not prove birth, name or later relation.

## 6. Written voice is a conditional resource

Record first-person form, address, honorifics, politeness, sentence shaping, professional vocabulary, comic strategy and speech act **with an audience and era**. A catchphrase frequency list is insufficient.

Itsuki's theatrical 俺, Nayuta's polite extreme candor, Miyako's colloquial あたし with professional です, Chihiro's dated 僕/私 distinction, Haruto's public 私 and peer オレ, and Toki's withheld praise/casual directness are configurations. They do not define all speech.

Pronouns do not independently establish gender, authenticity or moral intent. The source's local self-corrections matter because a scene supplies their meaning. Politeness can carry refusal; casual reassurance can be a lie.

No model supplies actor timbre, pitch, habitual audible pace or accent from written prose. Narrated loudness or slurring remains a textual state cue.

Generated Japanese examples, when used, are short, visibly labeled hypotheses, tied to a speech act and never presented as quotations. A model can abstain from exact idiom when only a general action is supported.

## 7. Ordinary behavior and material constraints

A model must preserve non-crisis evidence where available: meals, work tools, games, errands, study, travel, reader encounters, paid labor, social preparation and fatigue. It must not invent favorites, daily timetables, diagnoses or universal drinking preferences.

Comfort is not total resolution. A parent can enjoy a sleeping child and still want to write; a rejected lover can participate in family meals while still hurting; an editor can remain employed after naming a missed masterpiece.

Competence is domain-specific. Chihiro's technical and critical abilities do not select editing as her vocation. Miyako's fit with Itsuki does not make her an ideal editor for every writer. Setsuna's improved deadlines do not establish that every boundary problem disappeared.

Care labor must have an agent and a cost. A model cannot answer “they would support the creator” without considering who cooks, pays, edits, waits, studies, rests or has permission to refuse.

## 8. Error, countermodel and repair

Every substantial model includes competing explanations, not a single formula which absorbs every outcome. An action can carry several motives: concern and spite, help and self-worth gratification, pride and institutional responsibility.

Repair is evidence of a specific changed relation or practice, not proof of a permanently cured personality. Itsuki's V12 integration and V14 childishness coexist. Miyako's increased professional authority retains finite capacity. Chihiro's later initiative retains unreciprocated love.

Evidence gaps and negative evidence differ. No shown romance for Toki is a gap, not proof that he cannot love. A knowingly false reassurance is affirmative counterevidence to a model of always honest Haruto. A draft-only career is not merely missing evidence; its actual status is explicitly contradicted by the frame.

## 9. QA procedure and meaning of PASS

The project QA asks source-routed adversarial questions after model authoring. Tests include wrong-volume knowledge, wrong addressee/register, private-to-public leakage, sister-aesthetic caricature, romantic accessory reduction, genius/effort erasure, universalized stress behavior, satire-as-endorsement and unsupported future achievement.

A PASS means the documented model can answer the specific challenge while preserving its source ceiling. It does not certify generated behavior as canon, replace human judgment or claim held-out predictive performance. These are retrospective fit and constraint tests using known evidence.

For each test record: subject/era, trap, expected constrained response, source anchor, reviewed model response/disposition and limitation. A successful abstention is a positive result when the source cannot answer.

Mechanical checks verify metadata, links, route existence, rule IDs and exact bytes. Semantic review checks speaker, level, motives, countercases and limits. Neither substitutes for the other.

## 10. Succession and retrieval

The V01–V06 Nayuta monograph remains byte-identical bounded history. A new V01–V14 literary monograph explicitly succeeds it **as the mature whole-series reading surface**, while the operational model supplies a separate task. Historical supersession is documented here and in the readiness/entrypoint map without editing the old document's frozen horizon.

The V01–V06 Toki monograph also remains byte-identical. Its mature DOMAIN_BOUNDED model is an explicit **complement**, not a replacement literary monograph. The creative-labor and talent specialists contain the full mature editorial argument and grief/capacity countercases. There is no silent broadened “old Toki monograph.”

New monographs are warranted for Itsuki, Nayuta, Miyako and Chihiro because their prolonged developmental problems otherwise recur at length across topical homes. Other figures retain developed operational/domain work and specialist routing. This is a responsibility decision rather than a symmetry quota.
