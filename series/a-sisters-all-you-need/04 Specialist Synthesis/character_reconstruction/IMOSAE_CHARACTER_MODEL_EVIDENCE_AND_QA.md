# Character-model evidence and adversarial QA

## 1. What was applied and what the result means

This is the completed project-local retrospective constraint review of seventeen operational subject models: five broad conditional models, Toki's bounded editorial/social model and eleven individually bounded secondary models. Recognition and insufficient profiles are reviewed for their ability to decline unsupported reconstruction. The governing procedure and scope vocabulary are in the [protocol](IMOSAE_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md); individual readiness reasons are in the [readiness audit](IMOSAE_CHARACTER_READINESS_AUDIT.md).

The analyst applied each written model to the scenario below, selected the relevant state and rule, recorded the constrained answer, and compared it with the source-supported boundary. These are manual applications to known evidence. They are not independently sampled model generations, an automated inference benchmark, a blinded assessment or held-out predictions. PASS means the particular recorded application retains the required boundary. It supplies no percentage estimate of real-world character prediction accuracy.

Fifty-eight cases were applied. Thirty-six preserve an affirmative source constraint, sixteen successfully abstain from an unsupported assertion, and six preserve a documented repair made during source review. A repair result does not make the initial mistake disappear: the failure and correction are exposed below. Further source checks refined Aoba's dream/awake routes without converting an abstention into new biography.

The cases cover four challenges for each of the five broad models, four for Toki, two for each of eleven secondary subjects, six recognition/insufficient checks and six cross-model source/authority checks. This distribution establishes coverage of the chosen hazards, not equal evidentiary richness across people. Several cases use the same scene; repeated use is not independent corroboration.

The table records scenario, applied response, rule and evidence. Rule identifiers refer to the individual model, not to a shared personality template. A source anchor resolves a unit; its exact endpoints appear below. Contextual extension is identified when the decisive statement lies beyond a registered anchor. Completed Phase F source, semantic, chronology and capability audits separately retain these local ceilings; repository publication/integration remains an exact-head responsibility.

## 3. Why the constraints matter beyond a single test

Wrong-volume knowledge destroys the temporal model even when a later fact is true. Early Itsuki does not know what the reader will learn about Chihiro; Chihiro's school audience and the apartment circle do not share a single knowledge state. A late confession or disclosure does not supply an earlier interlocutor with hindsight. `IM-V01-E041`, `IM-V09-E255`, `IM-V10-E029`.

Uniform voice destroys directional relations. Toki can privately value a manuscript while withholding a spoken compliment, and can use polite colleague speech when admitting loss. Haruto's public 私 and peer オレ do not create exclusive formal/informal politeness classes. Chihiro's drunken bravado is a state, not her universal critical register. `IM-V07-E043`, `IM-V14-E002`, `IM-V14-E010`, `IM-V14-E014`, `IM-V14-E045`.

Single-axis motives destroy the ensemble. Miyako can feel useful while carrying unequal care work; Haruto can encourage Ui without knowing the future; Ashley can cause harm and later make a serious risk-sharing offer; Kaizu can defend the right to stop while binding himself to a costly memorial obligation. These competing motives are retained, not reconciled into a slogan. `IM-V09-E021`, `IM-V10-E225`, `IM-V08-E151`, `IM-V12-E214`, `IM-V11-E131`.

Outcome knowledge is not universal competence. Itsuki's award does not predict gracious response to criticism. Miyako's fit with Itsuki does not make her perfect for every author. One child enjoying Nadeshiko's work does not reverse a professional rejection under another feedback contract. Each source result belongs to a particular person, audience and task. `IM-V14-E025`, `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V13-E379`, `IM-V13-E380`, `IM-V14-E011`.

The final draft is an affirmative continuity barrier. Its imagined adolescent interiorities cannot become actual children's operational biographies. Actual Sora is a toddler at the returned present; Chihiro's ambition is still private and unachieved; the unborn sister has no established life. The models can explain the draft's literary function while declining future simulation as canon. `IM-V14-E042`, `IM-V14-E047`, `IM-V14-E048`, `IM-V14-E076`.

## 6. Protected history and acceptance ceiling

All nineteen protected historical files were checked against the captured base snapshot: thirteen V01–V13 readings, four pre-V14 checkpoints and two V01–V06 provisional monographs. SHA-256 and byte counts match. The new fifth checkpoint and mature whole-series studies are additional artifacts. No old monograph horizon was broadened.

The mature Nayuta monograph succeeds the earlier bounded surface for whole-series reading while preserving its old bytes; the Toki domain model is a complement, with his mature literary problems routed to the labor and talent specialists. These are explicit retrieval decisions, not an unauthorized global registry operation.

The completed local release review accepts the seventeen models within their stated conditional scopes and the narrower profiles within their refusal limits. Repository publication/integration is a separate exact-head gate. No model is unrestricted. Recognition-only people and actual children do not inherit a stronger scope from the success of other cases. No global A–E capability rows or discovery upserts have been written.

Still outside this review: independently elicited model responses, held-out prediction, audio-performance fidelity, unconsulted supplements, unshown careers/private lives and universal ethical endorsement of the narrator or illustrations. These are stated limits rather than silent PASS claims.

The [terminal character reference](../../05%20Full-Series%20Synthesis/IMOSAE_CORE_CHARACTER_REFERENCE.md) exposes the accepted local scopes and navigation. Completed release checks verify its links, source boundaries, identities and chronology; the exact-byte manifest binds the released payload.
