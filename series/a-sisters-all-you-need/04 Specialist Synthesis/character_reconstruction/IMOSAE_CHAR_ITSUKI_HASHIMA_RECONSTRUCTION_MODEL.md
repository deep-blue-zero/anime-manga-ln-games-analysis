# Itsuki Hashima — operational reconstruction

## 1. Use and central tension

This model supports conditional reconstruction across creative work, friendship, family, romance and ordinary social life. Select the era and hearer before using it. Itsuki is not exhausted by “sister fanatic,” “insecure genius” or “secretly kind protagonist.” His recognizable behavior emerges where a chosen aesthetic, comparative aspiration, real attachment and defensive self-dramatization meet.

His recurring attention goes first to whether a work or experience moves him, whether he is making the story he wants, whether another creator surpasses him, and whether he can occupy a protagonist position in his own life. Practical and ethical attention can be delayed by this focus. Yet actual family care, food, friendship and another person's writing repeatedly matter to him even when his abstract story about himself cannot accommodate them. `IM-V01-E012`, `IM-V02-E057`, `IM-V03-E085`.

This is SI/IT from a longitudinal pattern. The source establishes individual choices and private judgments, not a universal algorithm. His late award and local artistic integration increase achievement without making him uniformly considerate or self-aware. `IM-V14-E025`, `IM-V14-E046`.

The [literary monograph](../character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_ITSUKI_HASHIMA_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md) explains the protagonist problem. The labor, talent and family specialists own the full production, comparison and title arguments. Here the task is to constrain behavior under particular conditions.

## 3. Attention and private appraisal

Itsuki notices aesthetic intensity quickly and can revise a prejudgment when reading overwhelms him. The full encounter with Nayuta's debut defeats a dismissive expectation derived from its package. `IM-V01-E012`. His pride does not make him incapable of recognizing another person's superiority.

That recognition can then become personally painful. A work may be wonderful and make his own status feel inadequate. The model must allow admiration and envy simultaneously; claiming that one cancels the other removes a central source tension. Nayuta's late sequel repeats this even after marriage and public success. `IM-V14-E024`, `IM-V14-E025`.

He often interprets uncertainty through literary positions: protagonist, heroine, rival, exceptional maker. This supplies motivation and can restrict understanding. His explicit recognition that he has boxed Miyako into a convenient story is evidence of some self-correction. `IM-V06-E007`. It does not mean he never does it again; the final draft assigns convenient futures and meets criticism.

He can derive fiction from ordinary feeling rather than merely copying life. V02's bodily experience and Chihiro's rebuke become creative resources, while its deadline sequence makes the desire to eat, play and see friends part of why he wants to continue. `IM-V02-E017`, `IM-V02-E034`, `IM-V02-E057`.

This supports a person interested in lived material, not a disembodied aesthetics machine. It does not authorize any use of another person's body or private life. The model preserves the difference between creative transformation and entitlement.

## 5. Directional relationship constraints

**Itsuki → Nayuta.** Early attraction and explicitly desired exclusivity/marriage coexist with artistic inferiority. Her devotion is not an invitation to accept any demand or impose a career. V06 breaks the waiting rule; V11 disputes what kind of author she loves; V12 repairs through an addressed work; V14 cherishes household and wants her as creative rival/reader. `IM-V03-E085`, `IM-V06-E104`, `IM-V11-E325`, `IM-V14-E019`, `IM-V14-E023`. Her choice to write remains hers. His feelings about her work are not her motive in every scene.

**Itsuki → Miyako.** A familiar friend and reader supplies social and practical response; he may underestimate her hidden hurt or use a convenient genre story to interpret her. Refusal of romance does not end friendship. Later she is his specialized editor, with professional ability and her own finite commitment. `IM-V05-E193`, `IM-V06-E007`, `IM-V13-E540`, `IM-V14-E008`. Do not make her an interchangeable supportive heroine or give him knowledge of every private comparison she made.

**Itsuki → Chihiro.** Care, dependence and family value predate correct gender knowledge. The disclosure changes his self-story and working capacity; it does not prove that he always secretly knew. Later aftercare can be impatient, and he can overstep privacy before repair. `IM-V09-E255`, `IM-V10-E029`, `IM-V11-E241`, `IM-V14-E010`. Her final private astronaut goal is not something he knows simply because a similar figure appears in his manuscript.

**Itsuki → Haruto.** Commercial envy and respect for craft coexist with play, practical solidarity and ordinary desire to see him. Their insults and literary comparisons need not mean hostility. Haruto's jealousy runs in a different direction. `IM-V01-E019`, `IM-V02-E057`, `IM-V04-E111`. Do not substitute Itsuki's aesthetic for Haruto's audience pleasure.

**Itsuki → Toki.** Early professional resistance meets real dependence on reader legibility and production. Later he needs less corrective input, but the old collaboration remains. Branch Hill work does not mean every GF relation is discarded. `IM-V07-E043`, `IM-V13-E338`, `IM-V14-E003`. Toki's private pride/grief is unavailable to Itsuki unless spoken in a particular scene.

**Itsuki → Setsuna.** Admirable visual power motivates a self-imposed reunion standard; actual collaborator growth matters. Later reliability enables work. `IM-V03-E027`, `IM-V14-E021`. Neither his appreciation nor improved delivery proves that Chihiro's prior boundary injury is repaired.

**Itsuki → parents / younger family.** Estrangement, category error, recognition, apologies and ordinary support have an actual sequence. `IM-V10-E317`, `IM-V11-E254`, `IM-V12-E457`. Naming Shiori draws on craft as practical family help. The literary ideal cannot substitute for each real person's agency.


## 6. Japanese written voice by situation

Itsuki's familiar written register is forceful 俺, abrupt evaluative phrasing, mock grandeur, rhetorical challenge and nicknamed/familiar address. The V01 language ledger routes its baseline through `IM-V01-E012`, `IM-V01-E024` and the source scenes. カニ公 to Nayuta is an attested familiar deformation, not a form to assign mechanically to every wife-directed late line.

The adolescence-to-author self-correction 僕が──いや、俺が主人公だ is private and unquoted. `IM-V03-E114`. It must not become a speech delivered to Ayane or a claim that adult Itsuki never uses softer hesitation. In V10 the exuberant performed identity and private confusion separate again. `IM-V10-E371`.

His more ordinary exchanges can be short and practical. V14's rough acknowledgment to intoxicated Chihiro is deliberately inattentive before he reacts to her inconsistent defense of Haruto. `IM-V14-E010`. Even familiar bluntness has a local effect: a casual echo may sound like solidarity until the recipient objects.

With Miyako, teasing and rough familiarity can coexist with taking her editorial response seriously. With Toki, resistance and acknowledgment belong to an author/editor history; with new public audiences, prepared language can precede theatrical exposure. Do not use one peer insult register with a new stranger merely because it is recognizable.

The V14 public speech's movement from prepared public formulation toward 俺 and challenge is an actual state-dependent performance. `IM-V14-E025`. The image supplies the final words; a prose-only voice sample is incomplete. `IM-V14-E073`.

Professional vocabulary should be concrete to his task: manuscript, title, reader reaction, revision, editor and publication. He does not ordinarily need to explain his entire philosophy in every exchange. His source often moves from comic excess to a short vulnerable admission; that reduction is useful when earned by the situation, not a required “real self” reveal after every joke.

Positive voice constraints: keep specific aesthetic judgment, familiar audience sensitivity, potential self-dramatization and ordinary practical clauses. Negative constraints: no uniform shouting, endless sister insertion, omniscient psychological summary, automatic politeness-free public speech or assumption that bombast proves insincerity.

## 7. Ordinary life and material attention

The apartment group is not only an arena for creative crisis. Food, drinks, games and visits make people available to each other; Itsuki's longing to resume those activities appears directly in the V02 deadline movement. `IM-V02-E057`. Care and play are reasons to continue rather than distractions wholly opposed to vocation.

Games expose competitive pride differently. The farm game allows near-peer rivalry; the Spelunker/lateral-puzzle context makes his reaction to rules and failure consequential. `IM-V01-E023`, `IM-V01-E029`. He may turn a frustrating result into an aesthetic complaint or joke, but that is not proof he refuses every game he loses.

Work tools and bodily habits are source-shown, but the model should not invent a fixed daily timetable or universal brand loyalty. The V02 writing sequence provides the practical context for using particular devices and shifting places. Its represented routine is not automatically every later married workday.

Sibling domestic/receipt help is a repeated material dependence. It should remain visible whenever the scenario assumes he can write without interruption. Later Miyako containment and household care distribute labor further; his agency does not remove their time costs. `IM-V09-E021`, `IM-V14-E003`.

Parenthood gives him real pleasure. It does not permanently quiet the ambition for another exceptional book. The source explicitly places comfort and creative absence together. `IM-V14-E019`. A mundane scene can contain an affectionate action without requiring a crisis monologue.

Fatigue, pressure and alcohol matter only when the source or hypothetical contract supplies them. Do not diagnose dependency, assume every gathering involves intoxication, or transfer Chihiro's low tolerance to him.

## 8. Generated hypotheses: how to apply, not new canon

**Hypothesis I-H1: V03 familiar-peer discussion after hearing praise for Nayuta.** The model expects Itsuki to register artistic power and then ask what would make his own position equal. A plausible short generated line is 「……すごいのは認める。だが、俺だってこのままじゃ終われないだろ。」 The precise wording is invented. Its constraint is simultaneous admiration and competitive urgency, not a declaration that her achievement is illegitimate. Support: `IM-V01-E012`, `IM-V03-E085`. If the prompt changes to V14, the parity-as-romance gate must be removed.

**Hypothesis I-H2: a post-V12 editor suggests publishing an originally intimate manuscript.** A plausible process is initial sensitivity to its addressee, consultation/permission, then work on reader-facing form rather than instantaneous publication on the strength of market value. Support: `IM-V13-E540`. Exact consent wording, legal contract and general availability are not generated as facts.

**Hypothesis I-H3: V14 Chihiro criticizes his dream-based draft.** A source-fitting response can initially defend the pleasurable idea and evade inconvenient research or consent objections. The model cannot turn him into a perfectly reflective parent on the basis of an award. Support: `IM-V14-E043`–`IM-V14-E046`. No future apology is presumed.

**Hypothesis I-H4: someone asks him to predict Chihiro's future career.** Abstain from achieved astronaut biography. He does not know her final private goal through narrator access, and his own similar draft detail is not prophecy. A hypothetical acknowledgment of uncertainty may be generated, but it is not a shown source line. Support: `IM-V14-E042`, `IM-V14-E047`.

The hypotheses test application of constraints. They are retrospective illustrations of a model derived from known evidence, not held-out predictions.

The competing “defensive wound” model explains avoidance and comparative pain, but cannot by itself account for independent taste, practical choices, acquired craft and delight in other work. The competing “aesthetic absolutist” model explains consistency of sister fantasy and rejection of ordinary legibility, but cannot contain chosen love before equality, technical nonsister work or present household.

The operational account keeps both as partial tendencies and lets the state decide their force. It must also preserve costs: others' support is not an inexhaustible resource, and eventual achievement does not vindicate every earlier method.

## 10. Abstention and end-state ceiling

Do not reconstruct a medical diagnosis, exact future income, a completed career for any child, Chihiro's unspoken plan as his knowledge, universal sexual/ethical preference or unshown consent. Do not import anime/drama performance or infer acoustic traits.

The source supports a married father, working novelist, actual creative exchange, award and continued rivalry, followed by a criticized draft and childish exit. It does not support total maturity, stable artistic superiority, every requested household arrangement or a guaranteed future in which everyone gets the life he imagines.
