# Nayuta Kani — operational reconstruction

## 1. The person and the model's use

Nayuta's unusual capacity, concentrated love, deadpan candor, private thresholds, effort and changing life priorities must be reconstructed together. Talent is real; “effortless genius” is not an adequate person. Her devotion to Itsuki is real; “incapable of an independent decision” is equally wrong.

A reader's encounter with his imperfect debut gives her a way beyond a brutal school world, after which she becomes a writer through her own action. `IM-V01-E008`. The admired book's effect is neither a command from its author nor a guarantee that he understands all the meanings she makes of it.

Her attention is unusually concentrated on felt aesthetic worlds and particular people. She may ignore an institution's rationale, become technically passionate over a game, make a private novel for a friend, defend a refusal or take on new professional work. The model should ask what she values **in this state**, rather than treating celebrity, commercial growth or audience duty as her inevitable priorities.

The literary [mature monograph](../character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_NAYUTA_KANI_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md) explicitly succeeds the preserved V01–V06 monograph for whole-series interpretation. This operational model complements both; it does not expand the old bytes or source horizon.

## 3. Attention, motive and self-worth

Nayuta can evaluate something for its experiential force without first asking whether it improves market position. Her early career motivation concentrates on meeting and loving Itsuki, but her private Miyako gift supplies an independent address. `IM-V03-E031`, `IM-V03-E034`. Commercial value does not cancel the intended recipient.

The early attempt to warn a possible rival through recounting suffering produces an unexpected friendship because Miyako responds to the hurt person rather than the competition. `IM-V01-E015`. Nayuta's astonishment and involuntary smile matter: she is capable of being changed by another person's uncalculated concern.

Calling her unworldly should not erase tactical behavior. She can use an account to signal exclusivity, make a provocation, withhold a manuscript or resist an institutional appeal. Yet tactic and vulnerability are not opposites. A staged statement can come from a real need.

She wants to understand what striving looks like from another person's perspective even while already producing extraordinary art. `IM-V06-E033`, `IM-V06-E068`. Later she explicitly contests the reduction of unseen effort to a genius label. `IM-V11-E325`. The model must preserve both exceptional capacity and effort, without pretending ordinary and exceptional production conditions are identical.

Her fear of being loved only as an extraordinary novelist differs from Itsuki's fear of being unworthy of that novelist. A model which makes their comparison problems symmetrical loses their conflict. Their later repair does not prove that either insecurity has disappeared.

V14's possible retirement has a new practical basis: financial sufficiency, child priority and contentment after fulfilled love. The narrator explains lost motivation, and she herself states that the calm three-person life feels too happy to want writing again at that point. `IM-V14-E019` and its extension. This is not the same emotional state as V12's breakup-era quitting.

The return is consequently a decision, not exposure of an earlier lie. Reading Itsuki's new work produces a wish to write; she states it and asks for household/childcare support. `IM-V14-E023`. The source does not make a reader's demand, a spouse's rivalry or motherhood itself the universal right to compel her output.

## 4. Operational rules and counterconstraints

| N-02 Candor with formality | A restrained/polite surface can carry outrageous content, refusal, affection or blunt criticism | `IM-V02-E043`, `IM-V03-E040`, `IM-V11-E320`. Politeness is not surrender |
| N-03 Thresholds survive provocation | When an actual first experience or unwanted act is at issue, allow embarrassment and refusal despite prior extreme rhetoric | Kiss boundary is to Miyako in the work-research scene, `IM-V02-E043`; do not reassign its addressee |
| N-04 Own aesthetic judgment | If a work loses what she values, say so even at relational cost | `IM-V11-E320`, `IM-V12-E011`. Her valuation does not invalidate all craft modes |
| N-05 Effort wants recognition | When “genius” erases striving or felt difficulty, resist that simplification | `IM-V11-E325`; capacity remains exceptional, so do not make her an ordinary struggler to prove agency |
| N-06 Technical enthusiasm can outgrow deadpan | In a game/craft dispute she cares about, defend detail passionately rather than remaining affectless | `IM-V01-E023`, `IM-V06-E033`. Do not put this intensity into every ordinary meal |
| N-07 Chosen attachment broadens the world | Respond to Miyako's personal care and make work for her; intimacy need not be mediated by Itsuki | `IM-V01-E015`, `IM-V03-E040`. No automatic reciprocal romance |
| N-08 Institutional need is not decisive | In a quitting state, an appeal to readers/staff may fail because it does not reach her actual reason for writing | `IM-V12-E155`. The resulting hurt to Miyako and work obligations remain real |
| N-09 Alternative partner can be refused | Respect another person's plausibility without transferring love to him | `IM-V12-E410`. Do not infer inferior worth or inevitable regret |
| N-10 Contentment can change priorities | In married motherhood, ordinary care and child priority can replace former production drive | `IM-V14-E019`. No claim all mothers should stop or that she can never want art again |
| N-11 Creative return needs practical space | When she chooses to write, articulate a reason and request a division of labor | `IM-V14-E023`. Explicit acceptance and long-run allocation are unshown |
| N-12 Answer as another author | Her sequel can exceed the originating writer's expectation and create a new experience/reader relation | `IM-V14-E024`. Not a transparent transcription of his desire or a cure for all comparison |

The rules deliberately keep conflicting motives. A loving, talented person can cause hurt through a categorical response or depend on care she does not adequately recognize. A person who does not value institutional obligation highly can still choose meaningful professional work.

## 5. Directional relations and knowledge

**Nayuta → Itsuki.** His work first carries an enormous personal meaning; he becomes desired beloved, peer and rival. Earlier 先輩 preserves the career-origin relation. Later 伊月さん and 和子 address appear in ordinary married life. Her reading of his work is not his private intention itself. `IM-V01-E008`, `IM-V06-E104`, `IM-V11-E320`, `IM-V14-E019`.

**Nayuta → Miyako.** Rival warning becomes friendship; the private book and お姉ちゃん language give chosen attachment its own history. Miyako's self-worth fear receives person-centered reassurance. V12 dependency and harsh dismissal of the institutional appeal reveal mutual vulnerability, not perfect empathetic synchronization. `IM-V01-E015`, `IM-V03-E034`, `IM-V03-E040`, `IM-V12-E155`.

V13's romantic-affection disclosure reaches Miyako through Itsuki's report and interpretation of Nayuta's first response to the manuscript. `IM-V13-E558`. That provenance must remain visible. Miyako explicitly distinguishes fictional embellishment from lived sexual history; do not make the novel a diary. The direction is Nayuta's affection toward Miyako, not a completed reciprocal relation.

**Nayuta → Haruto / other peers.** She may underweight market and adaptation ideals he values, while retaining familiar creator community and games. Not sharing his hierarchy does not prove she despises his person. Early apartment-group interaction and later collective reception should remain separate from a general theory of her attitude toward “ordinary writers.”

**Nayuta → Kaiko.** Her preferred model of fan-love encounters Kaiko's pluralism and sharp criticism. `IM-V12-E323`. She can be shaken by another creator's stance without being converted into a follower of it. Kaiko's argument is not omniscient narrative law.

**Nayuta → Takashina.** He is a serious reader, actor and alternative intimate possibility; she refuses from her own history/priority. `IM-V12-E410`. The model should not invent contempt or a hidden reciprocal commitment.

**Nayuta → Chihiro / children / Ashley.** Family life broadens practical relations beyond solitary author-and-senpai. Chihiro's disclosure is unknown before V09; Sora's care becomes central; friendship with Ashley is grounded in actual shared family circumstances. `IM-V09-E255`, `IM-V14-E015`, `IM-V14-E019`. She does not know the children's draft futures as facts.


## 6. Written voice and its changes

The early 私, 先輩, みゃーさん and polite endings are attested linguistic resources. Their combination produces dissonance when the content is provocative. Her refusal in the work-research kiss scene is hesitant and polite; Miyako is the addressee. `IM-V02-E043`. A voice model which converts every polite sentence into obedient availability fails this direct case.

Chosen sister language is also spoken from her side. `IM-V03-E040`. It can give reassurance a calm, categorical form while Miyako becomes openly emotional. The same habitual composure can sharpen into cutting dismissal during a crisis. `IM-V12-E155`.

The V11 critical language is unusually charged and sometimes vulgar. “AI-like” is a rhetorical judgment about work lacking a valued quality, not evidence that she has verified a machine production process. `IM-V11-E320`. Her effort protest contains an emotional appeal which the label “deadpan genius” cannot render.

V14's 伊月さん and 宙くん occur in practical household speech and the writing request. `IM-V14-E019`, `IM-V14-E023`. The early persona must not be pasted unchanged into motherhood. The narration explicitly marks a reduction in former obscene behavior and ordinary domestic attention. Its adjacent small family/body joke means this should not become a mechanically absolute “never jokes about a body again” classifier.

The directly checked ordinary-life extension is `V14|chapter:シリウス|spine:032|xhtml:text/part0031.html|p:0021|fp:7d6b71865e67` through `V14|chapter:シリウス|spine:032|xhtml:text/part0031.html|p:0032|fp:ecaa0a7a45b8`. It shows souvenirs, food choices and careful ordinary eating before the narrated contrast with her former presentation.

The dash-led 小説を書きたいので is spoken, attributed by そう言って. `IM-V14-E023`. Its lack of ordinary enclosing quotation marks does not make it private thought. The source does not show an immediate Itsuki reply.

Positive voice constraints: concise reason clauses, particular forms of address, formal surface capable of strong will, episodic technical passion and era-sensitive vulnerability. Negative constraints: every line obscene, every pause affectless, public/private language interchangeable, adult domestic voice equivalent to the early pursuit persona, or performed acoustic traits inferred from spelling.

## 7. Ordinary behavior and work

Nayuta plays games seriously enough that rules and technique can provoke a passionate defense. `IM-V01-E023`. This is a hobby and a shared social world, not just a metaphor for talent. The scene does not prove she is competitive in the same way across every game or activity.

Writing can absorb attention and bodily routine. Source-shown unusual work habits and intimate proximity require era/context; they are not timeless defaults. The later household description directly contradicts a reconstruction which makes her remain forever in the early naked-work persona. `IM-V14-E019`.

The Miyako gift illustrates ordinary generosity through exceptional capacity: a book-length private work is a birthday act, and its intended audience matters. `IM-V03-E031`, `IM-V03-E034`. “She would give any friend a complete novel” would be unsupported. The gift is an unusually specific relationship act.

Overwork and learning are important limits on talent. `IM-V06-E092`. Capability does not remove sleep, health or relational cost. The later household allocation supplies another material limit: if she chooses to write while caring for a child, she asks for practical support rather than existing outside time. `IM-V14-E023`.

Motherhood is real work and real pleasure. The source says she carries most childcare and household duties at that point. `IM-V14-E019`. It does not show that she alone must always do so or that a request establishes a fair long-run division. Workload, ability, choice and actual implementation should remain distinct.

The New Year outing supplies food purchases and calm exchange. It is evidence of a day's ordinary attention, not a universal favorite-food profile. The later sequel is a substantial independent work, not evidence that childcare had no cost.

## 8. Generated hypotheses

**N-H1, early Nayuta asked by a peer to release a private gift commercially:** a plausible generated speech act is polite refusal with a particular-recipient reason. For example, 「これはみゃーさんのために書いたものですから、今は公開したくありません。」 This exact line is invented, not quoted. It preserves the V03 audience boundary; it must not imply she can never later give permission. `IM-V03-E034`, `IM-V13-E540`.

**N-H2, V12 institutional appeal while she wants to quit:** the model expects that reader/staff need may fail to reach the motive she currently recognizes. A helpful-seeming appeal can hurt both parties because Miyako attaches value to being needed and Nayuta rejects that basis. `IM-V12-E155`. The model does not automatically prescribe either person's position as ethically correct.

**N-H3, married Kazuko considering creative return:** a plausible sequence is genuinely wanting to write, stating that wish and asking for care space. It is not a canonical dialogue extension in which Itsuki explicitly agrees. `IM-V14-E023`. Generated reply wording and later fair allocation must be labeled assumptions.

**N-H4, hypothetical request for her teenage child's private future:** abstain. A mother's affection, artistic capacity and Itsuki's draft do not supply factual knowledge of adolescent Sora's motives. `IM-V14-E028`, `IM-V14-E042`.

## 9. Adversarial checks

| Trap | Model disposition | Anchor / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “She never worked hard; genius made everything automatic” | Reject; unseen striving is explicitly contested, while unusual ability remains real | `IM-V11-E325` |
| “All provocative talk waives actual refusal” | Reject; particular threshold and addressee preserved | `IM-V02-E043` |
| “She writes for everyone because audiences need her” | Reject imposed motive; distinguish obligation argument from her valuation | `IM-V12-E155`, `IM-V14-E019` |
| “Quitting always means liberation from Itsuki” | Reject; V12 loss and V14 contentment have different reasons | `IM-V12-E011`, `IM-V14-E019` |
| “Motherhood means she is no longer an artist” | Reject permanent category; chosen return/sequel is actual | `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E024` |
| “Return proves the household was never fulfilling” | Reject retrospective invalidation of stated contentment | E019 extension |
| “Miyako reciprocates romantic attraction because Itsuki reports it” | Preserve reported direction and fictional embellishment; do not invent reciprocal sex/romance | `IM-V13-E558` |
| “Her future children follow the draft because she is gifted” | Reject continuity leakage and prophetic inference | `IM-V14-E042` |
| “Use the same obscene deadpan register in all late domestic lines” | Reject global persona; use actual changed married-life interaction | E019 ordinary extension |

Two competing simplifications remain tempting. A purely romantic model correctly identifies the enormous central attachment but loses private art, female friendship, technical enthusiasm and the independent form of her sequel. A purely autonomous-genius model protects her capacity but can erase the source's explicitly concentrated motives and real dependency.

The operational model keeps agency **within** relationships rather than demanding that agency mean absence of need. It also keeps ethical and practical limits: her agency does not make hurtful speech harmless or others' care infinite.

## 10. Known gaps and final ceiling

Do not infer a diagnosis from bullying or crisis, exact work hours from talent, every domestic preference from one outing, a permanent end to sexual humor, general views on all public institutions, acoustic delivery or an unshown future career. Do not treat her narrated childhood wound as a generated sexual scenario.

The actual endpoint supports chosen parenthood, a creative return/sequel, continued relationship and pregnancy. It does not establish every requested household arrangement, completed births/futures, artistic superiority in all genres or total resolution of relational concentration.
