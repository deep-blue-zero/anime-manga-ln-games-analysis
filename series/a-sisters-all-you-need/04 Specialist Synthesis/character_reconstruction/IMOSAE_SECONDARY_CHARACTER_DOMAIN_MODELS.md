## 1. Kaizu Makina / Akira Yasaka

**Identity and domain.** 海津真騎那 is the writer; 八坂顕 is his real name. The V14 family roster verifies the same person, spouse Ashley and their daughter 優羽 / Yū. `IM-V14-E015`. Permitted reconstruction covers author purpose, source-shown grief-linked appraisal and partner/parent priorities. Much routine and unrelated private life remains unshown.

**Development.** The V07 retrospective places him beside the exceptional Kasuka in an older creator cohort; comparison, compromised choices and remembered loss precede the vow of ordinary continued work. The V11 counsel to Itsuki argues that making can be a livelihood and leaving is legitimate. V12 reveals that his own moderate-continuation vow can itself become stubborn self-sacrifice, then Ashley offers shared risk. V13 marriage preserves memory; V14 new genre/film success and fatherhood give another sufficient value. `IM-V07-E160`, `IM-V07-E182`, `IM-V11-E131`, `IM-V12-E201`, `IM-V12-E214`, `IM-V13-E643`, `IM-V14-E015`.

The stages are not one steady conversion from envy to wisdom. He can defend an ordinary life to a younger writer while binding himself to an unusually hard promise. Later success does not prove his earlier view was a failed writer's rationalization; earlier self-sacrifice does not make its anti-destructive counsel worthless.

**Attention and conflicting motives.** He notices money, survival, comparative talent and the human cost of turning work into a sacred calling. He also wants to preserve what he received from Kasuka. The V11 direct speech uses 俺 and きみ, with deliberately forceful demystification. `IM-V11-E131`. That poison in the wording is part of its effect on Itsuki; it is not a neutral career pamphlet.

In V12 he recognizes that he is not uniquely chosen, yet insists on the ordinary older man's stubbornness. The context before E214 at spine024 p0093–0099 makes the contradiction visible. Ashley's offer does not merely reward a self-sufficient hero; it challenges unilateral endurance.

**Directional relations.** Kasuka's remembered significance remains separate from the living daughter's identity. His V14 imagined approval is a grief-linked construction, not a visitation or proof of what the deceased would actually say. `IM-V14-E015`. Ashley is an informed adult who chooses risk; he initially underestimates the need/value of her accompanying him. Their ten-year financial plan is a hypothetical future support contingency, not the duration of an existing marriage. `IM-V12-E214`.

Toward Itsuki he offers an alternative measure which can be useful and can become an anesthetic if borrowed against the recipient's remaining desire. Toward adaptation professionals he later delegates rather than insists on exhaustive personal control. V14's success still includes craft/fit and chance; no “money instead of art” caricature is warranted.

**Rules.**

- **K-01:** Under destructive creator-duty pressure, make exit/livelihood thinkable. Support E131; countercase: his own endurance can become an obligation.
- **K-02:** Under a request to abandon the memorial vow, weigh ordinary continuation as a received value, not proof of genius. E182/E214; no forecast that persistence secures every reward.
- **K-03:** A partner's informed offer can revise unilateral protection. E214; do not make Ashley incapable of understanding costs.
- **K-04:** When a new genre/adaptation works, value the practical family goods and delegated expertise as well as craft. E015; no guarantee of similar success next time.
- **K-05:** Keep memory and present relations together without making a child a substitute. E643/E015; draft-Yū fear is not actual parental intent.

**Written voice and ordinary evidence.** 俺, きみ/君 toward a younger interlocutor, reflective qualification and blunt aphoristic counterargument are attested in the V11/V12 scenes. To Ashley, confused questions and familiar address differ from the harsh counsel to Itsuki. `IM-V12-E214`. Do not write every line as a lecture about mediocrity.

Whisky, social meals, work conversation and later parent friendship provide actual ordinary settings. The specific drink scene does not prove a universal favorite or problem. V14 actual parenting/nearby friendship is stronger than an imagined teenage-family future for everyday reconstruction.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** In a known blocked-writer conversation, a plausible hypothesis offers another livelihood without declaring all artistic desire silly. Exact wording is not canon. If asked whether Kasuka died because of criticism, reject the causal collapse: terminal illness is independent source evidence. If asked his child's adolescent motive, abstain from the draft. If asked whether he must always keep writing, distinguish his vow from a general duty he explicitly denies.

**Ceiling.** No diagnosis, universal coping rule, fully known deceased-person response, future income or actual teen daughter's interiority. This domain is substantial because it supplies competing decisions, not because it covers all life.

## 2. Ashley Ōno / Ashley Yasaka

**Identity and domain.** 大野アシュリー later becomes 八坂アシュリー through the actual marriage. She is a tax/accounting professional and a source-shown peer/partner/mother. The model covers creator administration, unequal knowledge, selected social/family care and the decisions actually represented. It does not provide current tax advice, all private motivations or a clinical explanation for her comic conduct.

**Development.** Early expertise arrives in a controlled domineering comic register. Chihiro's secret gives Ashley superior knowledge which she can use harmfully; professional skill does not excuse that conduct. Older Kasuka/Kaizu history and private grief broaden the persona. V12 informed risk-sharing, V13 marriage/memorial and V14 parent friendship add genuine care without retroactively sanitizing earlier action. `IM-V01-E035`, `IM-V04-E128`, `IM-V08-E151`, `IM-V12-E214`, `IM-V13-E643`, `IM-V14-E015`.

The original V08 high-risk check was deliberately limited to two non-graphic attitude/attribution paragraphs, not a fresh display of surrounding explicit material. Critical interpretation preserves the abuse of unequal adult knowledge without reproducing sexualized minor content.

**Attention and motive.** Ashley notices financial/material conditions, plausibility of a plan, vulnerabilities and opportunities for control. In the charity exchange, Chihiro's polite refusal is firm enough to prompt an acknowledgment that the child is strict about money. `IM-V04-E128`. This is evidence against universal domination successfully meeting passive compliance.

Her V12 marriage directive uses ワタシ and アナタ while she accepts an eccentric, difficult life project. `IM-V12-E214` with preceding p0099. She is embarrassed and serious, not merely staging another sadistic joke. Practical ability becomes a way to share risk rather than only exert superiority.

**Directional relations.** With Chihiro, employer/professional support and private knowledge coexist with harmful boundaries. Keeping a secret does not make every use of it protective. With Kaizu, chosen accompaniment responds to his vow; she can understand costs and refuse his unilateral exclusion. With Kasuka, remembered attachment and grief remain source-shown rather than an invented private biography.

With Kazuko, pregnancy/birth support develops actual friendship; the V14 narrator explicitly contrasts this with an alternate-world competition. `IM-V14-E015`. That alternate reference does not install the bonus route in actual history. With Itsuki, the award speech evokes concern about dangerous intensity resembling Kasuka. `IM-V14-E025`. Concern does not authorize every intrusive intervention.

**Rules.**

- **A-01:** In creator administration, attend to records, payment/material constraints and the represented client's situation. E035/E128; source tax simplification is PF-qualified, not a current legal model.
- **A-02:** Controlled formality can carry a directive or provocation. E128/E214; do not make tone equal consent.
- **A-03:** Superior knowledge creates a harm risk. E151; preserve guilt/pleasure attribution instead of treating professional authority as ethical clearance.
- **A-04:** When choosing a partner's known difficult project, offer concrete shared risk rather than only reassurance. E214; her informed choice is not helpless rescue.
- **A-05:** Later family care broadens rather than erases the prior persona. E015; no “motherhood cures all cruelty.”
- **A-06:** Distinguish a resemblance-based concern from a prediction. E025; Itsuki is not guaranteed Kasuka's fate.

**Written voice and ordinary evidence.** ワタシ/アナタ, composed directive language and ～かしら-like resources belong to the early voice ledger; the original V12 risk-sharing scene verifies the capitalized forms directly. The V14 retrospective concern addresses 伊月クン in its actual source context. These differ from Itsuki's shouted theatricality.

Family gatherings, relocation of the office near the household, financial planning and parent friendship are supported ordinary context. `IM-V14-E015`. No model infers exact daily care allocation, wealth, drinking preference or domestic temperament from those facts alone.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** A hypothetical informed partner offer can sound high-handed yet accept real cost. It should not be written as secretly insincere because her comic persona is controlling. Conversely, a scene of professional skill cannot certify ethical entitlement to a younger person's body or secret. Exact hypothetical speech is generated, not canon.

Tests: reject “always sadistic,” “always secretly maternal,” “tax expert therefore always legally right,” “Kasuka memory proves child replacement,” and “alternate route proves actual rivalry with Kazuko.” Accept abstention on unshown motives or actual adolescent Yū.

**Ceiling.** Source-shown competence, conduct and care support this domain; diagnoses, comprehensive private psychology, acoustic delivery and current law remain outside it.

## 3. Kaiko Mikuniyama

**Identity and domain.** 三国山蚕 / Kaiko is the manga creator; her parents call her 絹恵 / Kinue, verified in original V05 ruby. Shortened “Miku” references in inherited analysis are not a second person. This model covers manga craft, fan/creator criticism and source-shown parental/couple negotiation. It does not supply all private life or turn a fetishized work theme into complete psychology.

**Development.** Entry through adaptation gives her a particular artistic ambition, not merely the role of eccentric guest. Medium-specific choices and the illustrator conflict test what a faithful adaptation means. Parental concern can be genuine while attempts to control vocation remain contestable. Later creator criticism shows a more articulate account of legitimate different modes, and V14 supplies actual independent work and partnership with Manaka. `IM-V04-E176`, `IM-V05-E209`, `IM-V12-E323`, `IM-V14-E016`, `IM-V14-E017`.

Her formality and unusual craft attention stay recognizable through these changes. Growth should not mean that all eccentricity disappears; eccentricity should not erase deliberate work.

**Attention and competing motives.** She notices a medium's affordances, material construction, representational detail and what kind of making she wants to do. This can be technically serious and erotically/comically framed at once. Professional research rhetoric does not authorize pressure on another person's body.

In V12 she explicitly respects passion-led and calculated craft, denying one absolute correct mode. Nayuta responds by defining love as correcting a beloved author back from a wrong path. `IM-V12-E323`. Kaiko can allow that stance and then become sharply critical. Her position is not “every choice is equally good” and not an omniscient narrator's ethical theorem.

**Directional relations.** With Itsuki, admiration of an original does not require mechanical imitation; adaptation is her authored work under particular responsibilities. With Setsuna, art/craft conflict is medium and client specific, not an all-purpose contest of whose art is real. With parents, practical concern and naming authority meet her chosen vocation. With Nayuta/Miyako, criticism addresses specific reader/editor roles rather than a universally licensed harsh method.

With Manaka, V14 actual adulthood combines intimacy, distinct creative work and ordinary shared-home privacy/noise. `IM-V14-E016`, `IM-V14-E017`. An earlier roomshare is not automatically the final couple status; later reported parental acceptance is not a full transcript of every negotiation.

**Rules.**

- **KM-01:** When adapting, choose meaningful medium-specific representation rather than merely copy the author's imagined picture. V04/V05 craft routes; constraint: contractual/creator relations still matter.
- **KM-02:** Treat technical detail as real labor, but do not let craft purpose itself supply consent. `IM-V04-E181`, `IM-V05-E206`.
- **KM-03:** Under parental control, defend a vocation while retaining the practical reasons for concern. E209 and Kinue witness; no invented belief that parents are wholly malicious.
- **KM-04:** In creator-mode argument, preserve plural legitimacy and distinguish her own fan stance from all possible fans. E323; her later sharpness is a contrary case to purely gentle pluralism.
- **KM-05:** In adult cohabitation, preserve two creators' work and privacy, not one partner absorbed into the other's vocation. E016/E017; no unshown complete domestic settlement.

**Written voice.** わたくし and formal balanced phrasing are directly verified in the V12 argument; a calm acknowledgment can precede a stern change. `IM-V12-E323`. Formality does not mean meekness or a fixed graceful mood. Research/fetish vocabulary is relevant only in a scene where her craft actually calls for it.

The model does not invent precise honorifics for every unsampled interlocutor. The parents' 絹恵 is their address to her, not her own professional signature in every setting.

**Ordinary evidence.** Working posture/material detail, adaptation tasks, independent drawing and late shared housing supply actual contexts. A depicted eccentric workspace does not define every day. V14's noise/privacy conflict is particularly useful because it tests ordinary constraint outside a grand art argument.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** A hypothetical adaptation proposal can use formal explanation and strong specific visual priorities. It must remain an imagined line, not a newly discovered source statement. In a parental disagreement, do not assume compromise means surrender or that artistic freedom eliminates cost. Reject “panties mean no craft,” “polite means always gentle,” “plural art means any method justified,” and “Manaka has no independent work.” Abstain on unshown intimate motives or exact future publications.

**Ceiling.** Her professional and selected family/couple domain is reconstructible. Acoustic traits, exhaustive private psychology, legal-name paperwork beyond parental testimony and every future relationship outcome are not.

## 4. Setsuna Ena / Puriketsu

**Identity and domain.** 恵那刹那 / ぷりけつ is an illustrator. Original V01 narration calls the age16 subject 少年/彼; V14 again uses 彼 at22. No later female reclassification is established. This corrects an unsupported new-draft assertion, not the already correct frozen V14 reading.

The domain is illustration/collaboration and source-shown peer play. Strong skill, admiration and practice do not supply a complete ethical/private-life model.

**Development.** Early popularity and visual power help Itsuki's work reach readers while making the author feel his prose loses to the pictures. The illustrator wants reunion and plays with him. Boundary violations, including pursuit of Chihiro, remain consequential. V06 fine-art conversion changes aesthetic language, not automatic ethical conduct. V14 practical communication, deadlines and sustained practice make renewed collaboration possible; obsession/puns survive. `IM-V01-E028`, `IM-V03-E027`, `IM-V03-E028`, `IM-V04-E179`, `IM-V06-E031`, `IM-V14-E021`.

**Attention and motive.** Setsuna notices visual/formal possibilities and may convert a desired subject into a supposed artistic resource. The model must distinguish legitimate observation/research from entitlement to access. Itsuki can value collaboration and still call the prior violation wrong.

The late response to Itsuki's acclaimed work intensifies practice because the illustrator fears pictures could now lose to prose. E021 directly shows a reversal of the earlier comparison. This is evidence against effortless perfection and against interpreting all visual ability as commercially interchangeable.

Accepting a long reserved schedule has an opportunity cost and is narratively marked as exceptional. It is not a standard workflow every artist can sustain. The narrator praises practical character/skill, but the surviving obsession limits ethical-redemption interpretation.

**Directional relations.** To Itsuki, desire for collaboration can become long patience and competition through making. To Miyako, the offer meets direct professional acceptance and actual reserved capacity. To Chihiro, no later forgiveness or response is supplied merely because the illustrator improves professionally; her absence at the award interaction must be respected.

To other creators, aesthetic experimentation can exceed client fit. Cubism/fine art does not make all commissioned work better. `IM-V06-E031`. Independence and collaboration can conflict without one being worthless.

**Rules.**

- **SE-01:** Under an admired author's growth, respond through practice rather than assume the original talent ranking cannot change. E021; outcome not guaranteed.
- **SE-02:** In a chosen collaboration, reserve attention/capacity exceptionally if the source-established commitment warrants it. E021; do not universalize the privilege.
- **SE-03:** Let visible skill coexist with scheduling/communication weakness in early states. E028; late phone/deadline practices are a real revision.
- **SE-04:** Aesthetic conversion is domain-specific, not ethical conversion. E031/E179; professional admiration cannot erase boundary harm.
- **SE-05:** Retain a recognizable pun/obsession where the source actually does, without putting it into every mature business line. E021; “no change anywhere” is wrong too.

**Written voice.** Early 自分, ～ッス and enthusiastic near-miss game complaint are directly verified at V01 神 p0025. `IM-V01-E029` opens the game context; E028's extension resolves the exact line. センセー is a creator-relation cue from the early ledger. Late professional acceptance includes plain 了解です-style language; one tic cannot be imposed on all later speech.

The star/body pun in シリウス persists in V14. It is a written joke and a source-described preference, not a claim of acoustic sound or proof the collaboration lacks sincerity.

**Ordinary evidence.** Games, travel/practice across art forms, answering a phone, smaller jobs while reserving time and work delivery are actual material behavior. E021 supplies this unusually rich work-linked ordinary evidence. It does not establish all tastes or a general off-duty personality.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** In a late offered collaboration, a hypothesis can combine prompt practical acceptance with artistic anticipation. It cannot guarantee deadline perfection forever. Reject “a genius never practices,” “changed clothes prove changed gender,” “good artist therefore entitled to a model's body,” and “returned partnership proves Chihiro forgave.” If asked what Chihiro thinks at the award, abstain: she is absent from that exchange.

**Ceiling.** Illustration/work/play extrapolation is conditional. No unshown gender transition, repaired boundary, medical diagnosis, broad romance or private future can be supplied.

## 5. Ayane Mitahora

**Identity and domain.** 三田洞彩音 is read みたほらあやね; Mitsudou is a legacy error. The model covers editing/mentorship and the documented early relation with Itsuki. It does not make her an all-purpose maternal figure or expose every motive behind institutional hostility.

**Development.** V03 retrospective adolescent familiarity and rejection affect Itsuki's chosen self-story, but his taste predates meeting her. V11 gives her own vocational history: an editor dream influenced by a boyfriend survives his betrayal/departure from writing because love of light novels and effort have become hers. V13 training/mediation exercises professional skill. V14 institutional seniority and a different editorial manner remain distinct from Miyako. `IM-V03-E096`, `IM-V03-E104`, `IM-V11-E275`–`IM-V11-E297`, especially her own account at `IM-V11-E292`, `IM-V13-E379`, `IM-V13-E380`, `IM-V14-E012`.

Relational origin is not lifelong ownership by the original relationship. Nor does successful vocation prove she never felt hurt or could always choose freely.

**Attention and decisions.** Ayane reads a particular author's communication history and channel. In the Izumi conflict she asks Miyako to withhold an angry email, then explicitly qualifies the preference for meeting: some people communicate better in writing, records can matter, and face-to-face is not always superior. E379/E380.

This is a genuine conditional professional model inside the source. She does not merely announce a universal “be kind” principle. Her guess is that this author becomes more combative by email; the choice depends on his behavior and their current conflict.

**Directional relations.** Toward Itsuki she once occupies an older-sister-like role he misreads through a protagonist fantasy; later he recognizes her separate story. Toward Miyako she teaches without supplying a personality to copy. 京ちゃん and colloquial guidance locate a mentor relation. Toward Izumi, 漬物先生 and mediation respect a creator whose revision behavior still needs work. Toward Shirogamine, hostility/history remain unresolved rather than generalized to all colleagues.

**Rules.**

- **AM-01:** Preserve vocation which has become one's own after the original lover/plan changes. V11 history; no proof all relationally begun goals must persist.
- **AM-02:** Before escalating a written dispute, reread how it will land and consider the specific author's channel. E379/E380; no universal ban on email.
- **AM-03:** Teach through diagnosis of a relation, not only a junior editor's formal right. E379; no guarantee the mediator is always correct.
- **AM-04:** Allow a junior's distinct editor ideal rather than requiring exact imitation. E012 plus V13 practice; strong influence is not total identity transfer.
- **AM-05:** Keep early intimate history and current professional identity dated. E104/V11; no retroactive claim she knew Itsuki's full later wound or directed his taste.

**Written voice and ordinary evidence.** The source verifies あたし, colloquial explanatory qualification and 京ちゃん / 漬物先生 in the mediation. E380's long balanced sentence admits exceptions, not just casual confidence. Earlier self-introduction's あたし has original name ruby; it belongs to an adolescent state, not every adult official interaction.

Reading emails, arranging meetings, training and professional conversation are supported mundane work. The source does not provide a full domestic routine, relationship inventory or favorite-food profile.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** A new author dispute can receive conditional advice about channel and trust, with several possible methods rather than a formula. Exact generated phrasing is hypothetical. Reject “always email is bad,” “Miyako should become Ayane,” “editor dream belongs forever to the boyfriend,” and “Itsuki's sister aesthetic originates only in her rejection.” Abstain on unshown motives or institutional private biography.

**Ceiling.** Strong professional/dated relational reconstruction; broader life remains bounded.

## 6. Ui Aioi

**Identity and domain.** 相生初 is read あいおいうい. Hajime is an inherited misreading, and Ui is a different person from Aoba Kasamatsu. The bounded domain is writing, peer/mentor interaction and source-shown disappointment/return. Her sharply concentrated evidence does not support broad domestic or private simulation.

**Development.** A newcomer with her own taste meets Haruto's mentorship and the field's comparison. Her earlier classroom contempt for templates and later recognition of her own isekai pleasure are Ui's history, not Aoba's. `IM-V07-E144`–`IM-V07-E158`. Later cancellation exposes the gap between working, caring and receiving a viable continuation. Haruto refuses crisis intimacy and asks her to fulfill the reader-facing work; encouragement permits another small attempt rather than guaranteeing eventual reward. The V14 opening reports a dedicated writer aged twenty-eight, three concurrent series and roughly ten books yearly. **Attention and motive.** She cares about making something she likes and about recognition from a trusted senior. Those are distinct from Haruto's own market analysis and from Aoba's rejection of contemporary templates. A model must not turn her into a passive recipient of a more talented person's method.

The cancellation scene's desert metaphor describes uncertainty, not a moral failure by her. She asks whether the rewarding moment will come for her; the reader knows Haruto's reassurance lacks grounds. She does not know that private qualification. The distinction is a knowledge gate.

**Directional relations.** To Haruto she uses 春斗先輩 and, in the stronger trust statement, 不破春斗先生. He is mentor/trusted writer, not an already reciprocal lover. The refused invitation and return to work remain separate outcomes. Toward Aoba, external ranking/criticism does not define her own taste or worth. Toward readers and editors, obligations are real but cannot promise that another series succeeds.

**Rules.**

- **U-01:** Preserve her own preferred fiction under mentorship rather than make her merely Haruto's technique repeated. `IM-V07-E144`–`IM-V07-E158`; source horizon remains professional.
- **U-02:** Under cancellation, allow real despair and uncertainty without inferring incompetence or laziness. E225 context.
- **U-03:** Trust can make weakly grounded assurance actionable, but does not make it true. E225 p0187–0192.
- **U-04:** A refused intimate appeal can redirect toward completing work rather than require total personhood collapse. E225 context; no guaranteed emotional cure.
- **U-05:** Separate the V14 high-output professional report from Miyako's later report of romantic withdrawal. `IM-V14-E002` p0035/p0049 and `IM-V14-E024` p0066. Do not invent permanent success, retirement, exact private motive or complete emotional release.

**Written voice and ordinary evidence.** 私, hesitant polite questions and the two Haruto address forms are directly verified. Exact extension: `V10|chapter:砂漠|spine:036|xhtml:OEBPS/Text/p-0036.xhtml.xhtml|p:0187|fp:60fb35c16f13` through `p:0192|fp:87fac1239d53`. Her “try a little more” is bounded, not heroic indefinite persistence.

After returning home she removes makeup/contacts and changes into ordinary clothes before the writing scene. E225 extension p0197–0199 supplies actual mundane self-presentation distinct from an intimate outing. This does not establish a whole daily routine or favorite fashion identity.

**Generated hypothesis / QA.** In a hypothetical mentor conversation after a failed series, a plausible speech act asks whether continuing can lead somewhere and expresses conditional willingness to try. Exact words are generated, not canon. Reject Aoba/Ui conflation, automatic sex as comfort, assurance as prophecy, market failure as no effort and unsupported retirement. Abstain on the high-output report's unshown private motives or unrelated domestic choices.

**Ceiling.** A coherent but concentrated creator/mentor model. No all-purpose romantic personality, diagnosis, acoustic traits or guaranteed career.

## 7. Sōta Izumi / Misaka Sōma / Tsukemono Steak

**Identity and domain.** 和泉颯太, former pen name 神坂蒼真 and current 漬物ステーキ identify one writer. Original ruby verifies みさかそうま; Kamizaka is another inherited hazard. The domain is young-author authority, trust, communication and revision.

**Development.** Newcomer recognition encounters an unwritten three-year expectation and GF rupture; withdrawal from writing then web return follows Aoba's second-work success. At seventeen in V13 he is a high-school senior and regrets a playful pen name becoming professional. He chooses Branch Hill conditional on Miyako as editor, resists revision/authority and eventually accepts mediated changes; V14 reports continuation at a later actual age. `IM-V10-E139`, `IM-V10-E140`, `IM-V13-E177`, `IM-V13-E379`, `IM-V13-E380`, `IM-V14-E003`, `IM-V14-E012`.

This is not a simple delinquent-to-compliant arc. The earlier opaque norm creates a real trust problem; later unreasonable communication remains his responsibility. Trust must be made usable through actual work.

**Attention and competing motives.** He notices whether professional authority will repeat a painful exclusion and whether this particular editor can be trusted. Pride in a returned work and admiration for successful peers coexist with defensive resistance.

The web pen name can free publication from a failed professional label while later becoming embarrassing. The source gives this naming problem a practical voice, not a metaphysical rebirth. It does not mean the former person disappears.

Miyako's conditionally selected role offers a relationship, not immunity from revision. Ayane's view that he becomes more combative in email is a situated diagnosis, with explicit alternatives to face-to-face communication. E380 does not make all email bad.

**Directional relations.** Toward Toki/GF, hurt and missing knowledge do not prove deliberate sabotage by every professional. Toward Miyako, conditional trust gives her responsibility and leverage, but she must learn his communication; he must not treat respect as absence of critique. Toward Ayane, mediation changes a conflict rather than erases it. Toward Aoba/Nayuta, another creator's success can motivate a return and even the naming coincidence can please him. `IM-V13-E177`.

**Rules.**

- **IZ-01:** Under authority uncertainty, ask whether a particular relation avoids the prior failure; do not substitute global institutional trust. E139/E140/E177.
- **IZ-02:** A changed platform/name can permit another attempt after withdrawal. E177; not proof no prior cost.
