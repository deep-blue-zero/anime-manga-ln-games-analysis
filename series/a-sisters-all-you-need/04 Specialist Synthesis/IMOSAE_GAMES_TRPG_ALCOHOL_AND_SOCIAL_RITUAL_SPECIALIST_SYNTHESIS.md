# Games, TRPG, alcohol and social ritual

## 0. What can a shared practice do that a direct declaration cannot?

**How do people find an occasion, a rule, a role or a material task through which they can remain together and make something, while their real desires remain unequal and partly hidden?**

Games, meals, drinking, birthdays, holiday gatherings, fan events and professional ceremonies are not interchangeable background decoration. They create different permissions and constraints. A card can invite a hypothetical answer; a GM can make a role inhabitable; an unfinished campaign can justify the next meeting; a gift can identify a specific reader; a shared drink can slow an awkward conversation; a public award can authorize a speech while exposing listeners to its pressure.

This specialist owns those mechanisms and the labor needed to sustain them. The relational study owns reciprocity and refusal; the talent study owns comparison's injury; the language study owns the full discourse apparatus; the industry study owns professional production. Here the question is what the practice structurally makes available and what it cannot decide.

The qualified conclusion is that rituals provide **a shared object and another occasion**, not automatic equality, consent or repair. The rules can constrain a writer's preferred ending; the host can secretly alter them; play can support concealed identity; its boundary can then fail. Food and hosting are material contributions, and being invited is not the same as having every need recognized.

The present study describes the games as represented in the frozen Japanese corpus. It is not an updated rulebook or a recommendation to buy a product or adopt a drinking practice. Afterword rule corrections, secret-GM commentary and publication collaborations are PF with their own speaker. The adolescent Grancrest-derived play is inside Itsuki's draft, not an actual achievement by the two-year-old Sora.

Direct readbacks include the lateral-puzzle chapter, illustrator/author farm-game pairing, Cat & Chocolate's vote and unused card, Once Upon a Time's narrator/ending mechanism and rule correction, TRPG protection and failed miracle, deliberate maintenance, Word Wolf's imperfect information, the sister-card game's tactical categories, Comiket payment, family meal/sake and year-end food. Exact contextual extensions beyond selective early E anchors are stated below.

## 1. Leisure is a creative condition and can be an excuse

V01 places Itsuki apparently relaxing with reading and games while seeking a new concept. Narration explains that ideas can arrive outside direct desk effort, then adds a comic plea to believe the work claim. `IM-V01-E023`.

The two voices matter. Leisure can genuinely supply attention and input; calling it work can also provide an author a useful explanation for idleness. The scene does not give the analyst a universal productivity rule under which every game hour is either wasted or professional research.

Haruto enters with actual meeting notes, finishes them and proposes a game. The transition is practical rather than a mythical inspiration arriving outside all labor. Another creator helps provide the shared activity through which Itsuki's thinking may move.

The recurrent apartment makes such transitions easy: objects, drinks, food, familiar rules and people are available. That apparent ease rests on contributed work and proximity. Itsuki does not create the entire setting simply by being the person whose room it is.

V05 is a useful contrary volume. The afterword describes a nearly continuous work turn with little room to play. `IM-V05-E197`–`IM-V05-E199`. That PF account supports the changing emphasis, not a claim that every numbered novel must contain the same game inventory. Sociality can be constrained or displaced by the industry work that sustains it.

## 2. Lateral puzzles make the listener part of the creator's judgment

### 2.1 Winning formally can still fail creatively

In ウミガメのスープ, the represented procedure is yes/no questioning toward a hidden explanation. Itsuki and Haruto often improvise their own puzzles and limit questions. A deliberately impossible answer could make winning easy, yet the group punishes an unsatisfying solution through attacks on the maker's professional pride.

The target is a question nearly but not quite solved within the allowed attempts, followed by an explanation listeners can accept. Winning and satisfying are therefore distinct. The person who owns the hidden answer still needs another person's judgment.

This is a particularly rich leisure/work miniature: learning, frustration, taste, ideation and failed professional transfer coexist.

## 3. Another competition can temporarily suspend another hierarchy

Itsuki and Setsuna play アグリコラ 牧場の動物たち, described as a two-person game with no random element, and enjoy close skill. The text presents a 37–35 result and Itsuki's unrestrained boasting. `IM-V01-E029`.

The juxtaposition with the illustrator-as-god metaphor is important. In the publication ecology Setsuna is someone whose contribution helps govern Itsuki's prospects. At the table they can be closely matched opponents. The temporary axis does not erase their professional difference.

The narration's formal classification of the game remains a represented description. The analytical use is not to certify an external game-theory taxonomy, but to identify why comparable skill matters to this pair's enjoyment.

This is also distinct from claiming all players are always equal. Other games involve knowledge, rhetorical agility, hosting experience, or professional pride that advantage some participants. A shared rule can create a common task without producing equal ability or resources.

## 4. Cat & Chocolate makes accepted imagination a social vote

V02's school-themed game lets players propose solutions using supplied resources and lets others judge. The mechanism makes imagination accountable to a group, rather than allowing the best-known author to appoint an explanation automatically successful. `IM-V02-E007`–`IM-V02-E012`.

The reopened scene shows a striking split. Nayuta proposes a drastic way of making a problem disappear; Haruto and Miyako reject it, Itsuki accepts, and she treats his vote as personal understanding. `IM-V02-E009`.

A game's validation thus crosses into attachment. The vote is about a proposed fictional solution, yet the participants have histories that make its meaning broader. Itsuki's agreement need not be the final ethical judgment on the scenario for the effect on Nayuta to be real.

The unused next card is more important still. It asks about falling for the same person as a close friend. Miyako sees her situation in it and cannot imagine an item-based solution. She conceals the reaction rather than making a public confession.

The practice has given her a mirror without giving her a method. Its structural limitation is explicit: a game can define a problem as solvable within a set of props while actual interpersonal desire supplies no equivalent clean move.

The scene is therefore not a blanket claim that games heal people by revealing truth. It reveals some vulnerabilities to the reader, lets a player hide another one, and ends with the actual problem uncompleted.

## 5. Once Upon a Time turns narrative ownership into contested procedure

V03 supplies story cards, a private ending, interruption and narrator transfer. Players must use the assigned material and steer the shared story toward an individually held conclusion. `IM-V03-E076`.

The incentives make several familiar professional problems visible in compressed form. Each narrator wants coherence and control; another can seize the thread; incompatible intended endings make apparently collaborative production adversarial. Local judgments of contradiction or unreasonable development depend partly on the group's mood.

The professional writers intensify the competition. The book's comic assertion that it is a peaceful game except for writers is a represented characterization of this group, not a verified universal population claim.

The resulting grotesque or unstable narrative has formal resonance with protagonist hunger. It does not cause every later desire or prove the card game is an allegory with fixed real-character equivalents. The subsequent serious Itsuki/Haruto discussion has its own stakes. `IM-V03-E085`.

The afterword explicitly corrects simultaneous-card use in the depicted play, acknowledging tempo and an earlier misunderstanding. `IM-V03-E118` and its final note. That correction is crucial: realistic named products and detailed explanation do not make every represented rule documentary authority.

The study uses the scene's actual narrative procedure and its admitted limit. Neither outside rules nor later adaptation knowledge may silently repair the depicted event into a different sequence.


## 6. クロニカクロニクル gives care a role and a rule

### 6.1 A protective fantasy becomes a mechanical cost

The first campaign makes Itsuki a female character, 月子, while Chihiro can occupy a sister relation inside play. When an attack threatens the adjacent character, the GM identifies 庇う as a available action and asks whether the player wants to take the damage instead. `IM-V01-E037`.

Itsuki's protection therefore becomes more than an authored declaration. It changes a game state and costs the acting character HP. The role also exposes an amusing language mismatch: he initially speaks through an older-brother protective idiom, then remembers the role is now an older sister.

This is not evidence of a new actual gender identity or literal kinship event. It shows how a person can inhabit another role while carrying habits from the ordinary self. Player, character, speaker label and fiction level remain separate.

Chihiro's response has another weight. A normally concealed family identity can be acknowledged in the local role without first changing what the other players know. The protective act is emotionally meaningful because the fiction makes something available that ordinary conversation cannot yet support.

Other players contribute distinct actions and proposed combinations. The campaign is not only Itsuki narrating his sister preference at a passive audience. Their decisions, the host's prepared world and the represented mechanics mediate what he can make happen.

### 6.2 V04 refuses to reward conviction with a miracle

The third session builds a mentor's lost-sister history and a protective commitment, then puts that commitment against a warning about damage. `IM-V04-E134`–`IM-V04-E135`.

月子's remaining HP is 15. The GM states the counterattack as 3D+10, and the player insists that feeling for the sister will produce a miracle. The displayed dice are three, two and five: ten before the added ten. The hoped-for survival fails.

The math is a diegetic rule application, not a universal judgment that conviction is foolish. Its interpretive force is the refusal to let an author-player's desired noble ending alone dictate outcome. The fiction has acquired constraints that are perceptible to the player before the choice.

The character's dying speech and other characters' responses preserve the choice's significance after failure. Meaning and victory are distinct. A protagonist's act can remain their own without securing the result they want.

The next volume or game cannot be silently used to retcon this local failure. Nor should the role-character's death be inserted into Itsuki's real biography. The event is an actual session and an embedded fictional death.

There is important contrary evidence to an ideal of perfectly impartial rules. The V06 afterword says Haruto's original-rule sessions work through intensive hosting and occasional secret changes to dice. `IM-V06-E109`. This PF account qualifies the general mechanism without authorizing the analyst to declare the specific V04 displayed roll falsified.

### 6.3 Deliberate maintenance requires a host's work

After confession/refusal changes casual visits, Itsuki, Miyako and Haruto need a reason to meet deliberately. The unfinished campaign supplies one, and playing roles offers a possible distance from the awkward real relations. `IM-V06-E044`.

The occasions align with Haruto's completed scenario preparation. Maintaining friendship therefore depends on someone doing concrete work. The act is not reducible to consuming a product or waiting for warmth to return.

The prior manner of gathering had been largely unplanned; the new situation requires an appointment. That distinction is developmental. A ritual's familiarity is useful precisely because the people cannot simply reproduce their old state by wishing.

The afterword's 接待プレイ description adds a less egalitarian layer. A skilled professional is sustaining the game for friends, sometimes protecting its flow through hidden control. Their apparent free coauthorship is supported by another person's expertise and choice.

This is a genuine care contribution and a limit on transparency. It makes the campaign effective while restricting claims that its outcomes measure every player's independent capacity. The adolescent draft will repeat that problem through concealed accomplished-player help.

### 6.4 The buffer can fail and change ordinary life

V09 makes the role boundary explicit. Chihiro refuses to be described as a sister only within game setting and declares the actual relation. `IM-V09-E255`. What had made a barred identity possible no longer contains its emotional force.

The transition does not occur because the game has an automatic therapeutic rule. Accumulated desire, a remark, private history and present anger combine. It is a specific causal sequence rather than an abstract proof that role-play safely reveals truth.

Nayuta lacks confirmed prior knowledge of the concealed identity. Ashley knows; Toki suspects without certainty. `IM-V10-E029`. The game has not supplied equal information to everyone at the table.

The afterword's explanation that TRPG was the world in which Chihiro could be the true self before Itsuki is strong PF compositional support. `IM-V09-E258`–`IM-V09-E276`. It does not outrank the mainline's particular timing, response and cost.

V10 then moves care into an actual future choice. Chihiro wants proximity; Itsuki worries about study and responsibility for obstructing it. `IM-V10-E114`. No game action can reduce this to a fixed HP transfer or establish which owned decision will produce a satisfactory future.

## 7. 妹が多すぎる。 makes a “true” identity a tactical product

V11's game is explicitly related to シンデレラが多すぎる through changed backstory, people and terminology. It concerns convincing an amnesiac writer that a candidate is the real sister. `IM-V11-E061`–`IM-V11-E064`.

The role's amnesia is part of game backstory; Itsuki does not thereby have amnesia. The boss-like figure in the story is also a parody within the game, not new evidence of the actual editor's crimes.

Players provide information, jointly accept it or expend a limited rejection, then choose a candidate who fits the remaining conditions. Rank affects which eligible candidate is most persuasive. `IM-V11-E064`–`IM-V11-E068`. The tactical target is not genealogy but the conditions under which someone can be made to believe.

The constraints include age, preference and body-coded attributes. They must not become the analyst's actual criteria for recognizing a living sister or judging a minor. The comedy and visual specialists retain the critical problem of the cards' framing without reproducing explicit material.

The game makes the process of supplying and vetoing information visible. A player may even reject self-supplied information or provide apparently adverse evidence to mislead opponents. The source explicitly says advanced play requires inferring which candidate another player is trying to promote.

This is distinct from Word Wolf. Here players manipulate a shared evidential environment toward a favored candidate; in the later game a player may not know which information class they personally occupy. The mechanisms cannot be collapsed into generic “social deduction.”

Miyako's interest in manufacturing cost and edition logistics before play also matters. `IM-V11-E062`. Her perception of the object now includes publication work. An item others see primarily as a game is another possible professional task to her.

The V11 afterword identifies the actual edition collaboration and visual/game-balance work. `IM-V11-E337`–`IM-V11-E345`. That PF route brings play into the real book's publication apparatus while leaving depicted outcomes and actual reader play separate.

## 8. Word Wolf tests uncertainty about one's own position

### 8.1 An occasion is needed again after vocational divergence

The V13 gathering is deliberately proposed by Haruto to celebrate education/employment and prevent distance as Miyako no longer routinely visits GF. Chihiro's cooking and several beers supply the table; the initial conversation concerns work and private feelings. `IM-V13-E100`.

The premise repeats V06 at another developmental boundary. It is no longer only refusal that threatens casual proximity; professions and study redistribute time and routes. Friendship requires a new occasion without proving anyone has stopped valuing it.

The game chapter therefore owns more than its rule explanation. Its social prelude and later conversation carry information the game will not itself settle. Chihiro's feeling for Haruto and Miyako's response remain actual relationship matters.

### 8.2 A player cannot begin by assuming which side they are on

Haruto explains the represented Word Wolf procedure: one participant receives a different word, but does not necessarily know that this is the minority word. Conversation should identify the different participant while avoiding an easy disclosure of the majority term, since a caught minority player can reverse the outcome by naming it. `IM-V13-E112`.

The host chooses words and distributes folded papers randomly; even the host does not know who has which word. That limit is stated. Haruto's responsibility does not confer omniscience over every private role.

The structure rewards cautious questions and inference from answers, but it can make a perfectly understandable personal statement appear suspicious when listeners assume a shared keyword. A player's uncertainty concerns both other people and the self's class.

This is a formal analogue to the ensemble's social misreadings, not proof that any particular unspoken love is equivalent to a secret word. The difference matters: game information is finitely assigned and ultimately revealed; actual desires may change and remain partly unknown.

Miyako's worry that the game will be difficult is acknowledged rather than replaced by a sudden universal competence. Chihiro can ask a pertinent rule question; Nayuta can tease; Itsuki can request the word-selection procedure. The same shared setting supplies different speech acts and perceptual priorities.

The later profession/romance conversation remains open-ended. Unlike a round's result, it cannot appoint a unique minority or announce that everyone's purpose has now become transparent.

## 9. Food, gifts and events make the social field materially available

The recurring room has particular food labor. V07's new couple does not abolish Chihiro's place, and Itsuki's reassurance partly rests on domestic reliance. `IM-V07-E060`. That is affection with a condition worth examining, not an abstract communal atmosphere.

V09's roomshare makes the cost more visible: Miyako increasingly undertakes the household work, feels gratitude and need-based satisfaction, and recognizes an imbalance. `IM-V09-E021`. A convivial setting can be real while its sustaining labor is unequal.

The flower gathering in the same volume extends an existing seasonal practice to a larger group. Chihiro's food, others' contributions, drinks and new participants make a changing circle rather than a fixed five-person tableau. `IM-V09-E025`–`IM-V09-E032`.

Birthday and private-gift scenes have another structure. Nayuta's V03 novel addresses Miyako as a particular recipient and refuses automatic market circulation. `IM-V03-E034`. The ritual identifies a reader through an owned gift; it does not make artistic value equal to saleability.

Comiket moves the relation between creator and audience into a public event. Haruto buys copies of his own work's fan creations, thanks the makers and refuses free provision merely because he is the original author. `IM-V08-E014`. Gratitude becomes material payment and attention.

That act changes the hierarchy without abolishing it. He is recognizable and receives special offers; the choice not to extract unpaid work matters because the asymmetry exists. The event is also work for people staffing the booth, including Miyako.

His joy at seeing her represent the work later becomes the example he offers Ui when encouraging continued writing. `IM-V10-E225`. The remembered ritual is part of an experienced good, while his promised future reward remains an ungrounded assurance expressly qualified by narration.

Fan participation, creator pride, employment and romantic attention thus converge in one event without becoming one purpose. The industry and relationships homes unpack those distinctions; this study owns the occasion and its concrete exchange.


## 10. Family meals permit speech without requiring a complete confession

The V11 family table gives apology a material occasion. Natsume prompts Keisuke's intended words before eating; he and she acknowledge the concealment; the seating and meal give the returned son a place within the household. `IM-V11-E241`.

The later sake conversation is hesitant. Itsuki wonders about a toast, follows his father's action instead, notices the drink's taste in relation to the food and hears that the delayed new book has been watched. Keisuke's brief encouragement matters within an awkward ordinary form. `IM-V11-E254`.

This ritual does not turn everyone eloquent or make the father understand every novel. It gives room for a small act of attention that contradicts the son's total image of indifference. Its restraint is part of its force.

V12's year-end return offers another limit. Itsuki uses a band-breakup phrase to describe his loss, asks not to be questioned deeply because it hurts, and Chihiro respects the request. The father's laughter at the changed hair is followed by actual food and conversation. `IM-V12-E294`.

Keisuke has learned cooking and made the year-end soba himself, with tempura Itsuki likes. Care is not fixed as women's or children's eternal function. The source gives a specific changed practice rather than an indefinitely egalitarian household audit.

Natsume's joking concern about being overtaken in cooking adds another mixed response to competence and being needed. The meal can sustain belonging without making every participant's motives perfectly generous.

The ritual's value is a place to return and a task that can be done now. It does not solve the breakup or guarantee that a full explanation must be given. A chosen boundary on disclosure is compatible with actual care.

## 11. Alcohol is a sensory object, a social convention and a state variable

The novels name drinks, tastes, strengths, pairings and ways of serving. This specificity prevents the group from becoming an abstract collection of people exchanging themes. They spend time with material pleasures and differences of preference.

Itsuki's V11 sake appraisal shows an ordinary taste judgment: he tends toward more distinctive acidic or fruity qualities, yet recognizes that the less assertive drink may fit daily food. `IM-V11-E254`. The difference from a blunt global “beer enthusiast” profile matters to reconstruction.

Haruto's V04 solitary drink after the final anime episode gives another function. He tries to preserve enjoyable making memories amid painful reception and chooses a drink because he cannot yet sleep or work. `IM-V04-E111`. The depicted coping act is neither a proven cure nor the same ritual as a group toast.

The V06 afterword says the author selects drinks personally tried and liked. `IM-V06-E109` and its neighboring Q&A. That is a PF taste account, not independent product verification, an external recommendation or evidence that all characters share the author's palate.

V14 Chihiro's low-tolerance intoxication and Itsuki's aftercare make bodily response part of the social event. `IM-V14-E010`. Different drinks and bodies do not produce uniform conduct. The altered written state cannot be used as privileged proof that one expressed resentment is the permanent true self.

The study therefore distinguishes a drink as an object of attention, drinking as a form of gathering, and intoxication as a condition affecting speech and care. One should not infer each from the others. A family table can matter before intoxication; a person can drink alone; a written drunken scene can remain painful rather than only convivial.

No claim here gives clinical guidance or promotes the fictional practice into a norm for actual readers. The evidence is the represented action and its particular consequence.

## 12. The final rituals grant several kinds of continuity

V14's family visits, Christmas meeting, New Year shrine visit, Comiket and award ceremony bring relationships into different scales of publicness. `IM-V14-E004`, `IM-V14-E015`–`IM-V14-E018`, `IM-V14-E025`.

Christmas links the two households through children, birthdays, parental friendship and Kaizu's different sufficient goods. The scene permits new interests among adults beyond the old apartment's professional/gaming roles.

Hatsumode gives Itsuki and Kazuko time to state or withhold desires while childcare is supported by other relatives. `IM-V14-E018`–`IM-V14-E020`. The occasion is ordinary and culturally specific; it does not make the desired artistic answer inevitable.

Comiket continues as both a commercial/creative event and a familiar place for intimate joking. Kaiko's literal promise fulfillment and Haruto/Miyako's exchange have different consequences from Haruto's earlier refusal of free fan work. `IM-V14-E017`. “The same ritual” does not mean the same ethical act every time.

The award ceremony gives Itsuki public standing and a microphone. His prepared polite speech changes to a personal declaration, with listeners whose reactions differ. `IM-V14-E025`. A ceremony makes a statement visible; it does not make its imperative universally appropriate.

That distinction is reinforced when the final draft borrows the declaration into a play. The shift from 俺 to 俺たち makes protagonist aspiration plural inside an imagined performance. `IM-V14-E037`. It is a transformation within an embedded story, not proof that the actual children will reenact their parents' creative circle.

## 13. The adolescent TRPG-derived play is a privilege test inside fiction

In Itsuki's draft, Sora's prized play originates in Grancrest GM work supported by accomplished parent/peer players. Their contributions include useful characters and foreshadowing. `IM-V14-E032`.

Sora initially treats discovery of this help as proof that he contributed nothing. The draft gives a more complicated accounting: he organized and made choices, while access to unusually skilled helpers mattered. Neither pure solitary originality nor total fraud describes the origin adequately.

The social ritual has produced work that crosses to another medium and reaches another reader. The later script requests add actors, staging constraints, revision and coaching. `IM-V14-E030`–`IM-V14-E035`. Shared play can become an input to authorship without providing a finished theatrical product automatically.

The hidden-support problem is a longitudinal echo of Haruto's hosted campaign. A participant can experience free creation while another skilled person supplies part of the favorable environment. Acknowledging that work reduces the claim of independence without erasing the participant.

There is also a stronger unequal-opportunity limit. The imagined children have access others would not possess. The narrative itself names the privileged coaching as a kind of cheat; the positive result does not prove a general method available to every young maker.

All these claims remain at the draft level. Actual Sora is two. `IM-V14-E042`. The manuscript's generational continuity is something Itsuki has constructed and other actual readers can judge, not an achieved sequel-history.

The final living-person critique then keeps the ritual thesis accountable. Having a close circle and making an engaging story together does not grant permission to narrate its members' entire future lives as if known. `IM-V14-E043`–`IM-V14-E049`.

## 15. Strong countertheses

### 15.1 The games may be an insider hobby catalogue

Frequent named products, drinks, jokes and explanation can look like accumulation of the author's interests rather than necessary form. That criticism is strengthened where a long rules inset interrupts another development.

The strongest response is scene-specific rather than a claim that every reference is indispensable. Cat & Chocolate's unused card, the failed protective miracle, V06's planned reunion, V09's disclosure and V13's new occasion perform work that a generic “they hung out” would lose. Other mentions may primarily thicken the setting or advertise taste. The study does not force equal thematic weight on all of them.

### 15.2 Hosted freedom may conceal control

A capable GM can keep a group comfortable by shaping information and outcomes. The afterword explicitly admits hidden intervention. One can therefore read the ritual's apparent equality as care mixed with management.

That objection qualifies rather than abolishes the choices shown. V04 grants a meaningful failed gamble; V06 requires participants to choose a meeting; V09 produces a disclosure that the host cannot neatly contain. The point is to recognize the support and its limits, not to declare every result either authentic or fabricated.

### 15.3 Conviviality may depend on unequal labor and resources

The attractive room, varied drinks, cooked food, talented host and available time are not universally distributed. Chihiro's and Miyako's work can disappear beneath the pleasure their companions receive.

The source itself gives the imbalance and caregiver gratification, later the father's cooking, and a direct request to redistribute care. These facts sustain criticism of the arrangement as well as recognition of its pleasures. A shared table is not proof of a fair division.

### 15.4 Fictional distance can become avoidance

Role-play, a hypothetical card or an authored story can let someone speak indirectly and avoid an actual answer. The later future draft is the strongest limit: imaginative insight does not replace permission or research.

Yet indirect forms also make some speech possible before direct discussion is bearable. The V12 request not to probe a painful breakup is respected, and the relation continues through a meal. There is no general obligation to confess everything whenever a ritual provides the opportunity.

## 16. Final disposition and neighboring responsibilities

The completed series makes social ritual a form of creative and relational infrastructure. Its objects and repeated occasions permit attention, role experimentation, local competition, disclosure, continuation and public recognition. They also distribute work, privilege, information and control.

The strongest conclusion is modest but substantial: a practice can give people another way to act together before they know how to resolve a direct problem. The practice cannot appoint the right feeling, guarantee the desired ending, make a private future known or turn every contribution into independent achievement.

This strengthens the earlier collaborative-play and maintenance accounts while narrowing any claim of automatic healing or equal freedom. The formal transition history remains in the checkpoint and claim ledger. The industry home follows supporting labor, relationships follows the chosen bonds, family follows actual identity, comedy follows boundary effects, narration follows rule/dialogue/fiction levels, and visual analysis follows the cards, sheets and publication form.
