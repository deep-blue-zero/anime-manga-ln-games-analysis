# Family, siblinghood and identity

## 0. What could “a sister is enough” mean?

The title raises a sufficiency claim whose object changes: **a fictional ideal, an actual younger family member, a wished-for secure relation, or a specifically chosen person who makes a life livable**. The fourteen novels test these meanings without permitting any one of them to absorb all the others.

This specialist owns genealogy, family roles, disclosure, domestic care, sibling language, grief, naming and the ethical limits of making another person's identity one's answer. Romance has its own home; authorship enters here when fiction is used to understand, protect, conceal or appropriate a family member.

Its governing distinction is between recognizing a relation and obtaining a person who satisfies a template. Chihiro was precious before Itsuki knew the concealed identity; discovering a literal sister does not complete his artistic or familial life. His later actual family contains care and disagreement, and his imagined future fails to respect everyone's own possible story.

High-stakes Japanese readbacks include the chronology of Itsuki's sister taste and Ayane's rejection, the three-person concealment decision, Chihiro's public declaration, the education/care dispute, parental apologies, a father's ordinary support and Shiori's naming scene. Correct identities follow the primary errata wrapper rather than older romanized summaries. Father is 羽島啓輔, Keisuke Hashima; 棗, Natsume, is Chihiro's mother and Itsuki's stepmother; his deceased birth mother is 羽島和花. The latter is kept in Japanese here without inventing a reading from the kanji.

The inferred family ethic is IT, not a statement that every narrated custom is right. Internal futures, metaphoric sibling roles and public pen names are explicitly different authorities from actual genealogy.

## 1. Competing title theses remain necessary

| Candidate interpretation | Evidence giving it force | Evidence restricting it |
|---|---|---|
| A literal younger sister supplies the missing good | Itsuki's repeated claim; Chihiro's real relation and later Shiori | He values Chihiro before knowing; disclosure destabilizes work; further artistic and intimate desires remain |
| Fictional sister fantasy is the sustaining refuge | Adolescent reading history and professional aesthetic | A real relative cannot be required to conform; fiction can mislead a parent or appropriate a child |
| A specific beloved person is sufficient | Formative reading, private gifts, addressed novel and adult family | Love does not abolish work, comparative desire, refusal, finite care or new aspirations |
| The title names a way to choose what matters while admitting lack | Actual final ないものねだり / これさえあればいい frame | The formulation is situated, not a universal minimum or a permanent cure for desire |

The matrix is a starting test, not a verdict selected before reading. The early texts already contain counterevidence to literal sufficiency. The later texts do not simply replace “sister” with “lover” and settle everything.

The particle さえ gives a concentrated minimum claim; the conditional いれば imagines sufficiency if the object is present. What matters is who defines that object and whether its actual person is allowed another will. The final formulation retains desire for what is absent alongside what can presently matter enough.

## 2. The first “sister” is an authored fantasy with a real readership

The opening's apparent extreme sibling scenario is a manuscript, disclosed through Toki's reaction. `IM-V01-E001`. That frame prevents attribution of the opening's events to Itsuki's actual family. It does not make the published work's erotic framing and use of youthful bodies disappear; the comedy and visual specialists retain that critical problem without reproducing explicit material.

Itsuki then declares that his life would be improved if he had a sister. `IM-V01-E006`. The proposition is comic, persistent and insufficiently explanatory. His real room already contains family care through the person he calls a younger brother.

The adjacent recoil from real sibling possibilities distinguishes a fictional ideal from lived relation. `IM-V01-E007`. Desire for a genre arrangement cannot be assumed to be a wish to sexualize an actual relative. Conversely, making that distinction does not justify treating a real relative's embarrassment as harmless because the author's private aesthetic is fictional.

V02's false excuse composition gives family another instrumental use. Itsuki and Miyako consider constructing a crisis narrative to delay work, while she resists implicating Chihiro. `IM-V02-E052`. A family member can become useful material for a persuasive story without consenting to that use. The final living-person manuscript will give the same professional competence a more substantial ethical test.

The early family question is thus already larger than a missing biological role. Who provides ordinary care? Who is perceived accurately? Who gets used as the object that makes another person's own story intelligible?

## 3. Sister taste precedes the rejection that intensifies it

The V03 retrospective origin describes literary taste before Ayane's rejection. Itsuki reads widely, turns toward light novels and comes to prefer sister characters among several appealing archetypes. He notices that they often remain secondary rather than central heroines. `IM-V03-E096`.

This chronology rejects a monocausal account in which disappointment with an older girl creates the entire preference. The taste is already present. Later rejection intensifies and reorders its meaning rather than inventing it from nothing.

Ayane, 三田洞彩音 / Ayane Mitahora, treats him as a younger brother while loving another person. His refusal of an unwanted “older sister” relation is partly a defensive response to wounded confidence. The source contrasts the possibilities of his later more confident self with what he could do then. `IM-V03-E104`.

He uses the younger-sister claim as a wall, while she points out that he has no sister. The gap between available reality and declared sufficiency is visible within the dialogue. His wish to become a protagonist also follows recognition that he is scarcely a marginal figure in another person's desired story.

The resulting artistic self is made from many feelings—desire, heartbreak, inferiority, anger, hope and prayer—not from a single sibling fetish. `IM-V03-E113`. A reconstruction that makes sister references explain every later response would repeat the convenient categorization the novels themselves criticize.

His father's apparent emotional absence after the birth mother's death belongs initially to Itsuki's perspective. V03's narration describes his interpretation of work absorption and lack of visible mourning. `IM-V03-E096`. V10 later opens other focal access. New evidence corrects the reader's account without changing what the grieving child experienced.

## 4. Family is precious before the category becomes true for Itsuki

V03's aquarium material gives Itsuki ordinary attention to Chihiro and identifies the existing family relation as important before he knows the concealed identity. `IM-V03-E069`. The care has not been created by the eventual discovery of a sister.

This is one of the strongest restrictions on the title's literal reading. The relation already does work; the new label changes knowledge, expectations and anxiety. It cannot be treated as the magical acquisition of a previously absent person.

The earlier Chihiro is competent and particular, not a household robot. V05's school, work and hobby routes give an everyday life beyond the apartment. `IM-V05-E018`–`IM-V05-E023`, `IM-V05-E128`–`IM-V05-E137`. Practical usefulness neither supplies complete recognition nor exhausts the child/young person's desires.

V07's fear of becoming an intrusion after Itsuki gains a girlfriend exposes the insecure basis of some belonging. His assurance rests partly on continued domestic need, qualified by Chihiro's willingness to provide it. `IM-V07-E060`. The relationship remains affectionate while he cannot fully explain the eagerness to care.

V06's mortality recognition likewise concerns specific lost and endangered people, including his mother and the writer who helped form him. `IM-V06-E092`. The importance of a person cannot be deferred until one has completed an artistic identity. Actual family and love put time limits on a protagonist's self-made conditions.

## 5. A real sister can criticize rather than embody an ideal

Haruto's younger sister supplies an early counterhousehold. After the painful anime, she angrily identifies failures and questions his behavior. Haruto experiences relief in someone being angry for a work he cannot freely denounce as a participant. `IM-V04-E116`.

The source marks part of his account as inference: perhaps she is voicing what his professional position prevents him from saying. The analyst should not promote that “perhaps” into complete access to her motives. What is directly shown is the criticism, his relief and his response.

Her hostile surface and apparent care coexist without proving that all hostility is loving. Haruto is reminded of Miyako crying with him; different reactions can perform a related recognition of pain. This is a relation that matters because the sibling has a response of her own.

The final cohabitation obstacle shows why that early comfort cannot become a globally benevolent sister profile. The V14 attachment and threat constrain his relationship with Miyako. `IM-V14-E024`. The text supplies no reciprocal incest, diagnostic explanation or completed repair.

Thus neither “real sisters are awful” nor “real sisters secretly love and therefore every hurt is wholesome” survives the full record. Family can supply powerful recognition and harmful pressure in different situations.

## 6. Concealment is a child's proposal carried by adults

V10's opening retrospective account adds material history to Chihiro's care: a small household, the mother's labor, practical cooking and longing for a father and older brother. The meeting identifies the younger child as 加納千尋, Chihiro Kanō, before the later Hashima family. `IM-V10-E004`, `IM-V10-E012`.

The family's birth is not only a disguise gag. Natsume cares for an intoxicated Keisuke, he encounters her existing household, and each brings a history and concern. The viewpoint alternates enough to prevent the new wife from being reduced to Itsuki's impression of an intruding stranger.

Keisuke's professional worry about Itsuki has real stakes and poor predictions. He expects the bottom prize grade and genre unfamiliarity to lead back to conventional life. `IM-V10-E012`. Itsuki's continuing authorship disproves that forecast; it does not prove a parent has no legitimate reason to worry about insecure work.

The concealment decision is explicitly joint. Keisuke, Natsume and Chihiro conclude they can sustain it together; the adults expect it to be temporary and hope Itsuki will change. Chihiro corrects 私 to 僕 while stating the desire for a family. `IM-V10-E029`.

Those facts assign agency and responsibility asymmetrically. The child proposes or owns an intense wish; the adults supply permission, resources and continued participation. Calling it solely Chihiro's lie would erase their causal role. Calling the child wholly passive would erase the direct decision and its motivation.

The adults' reading of Itsuki's debut contributes to fear that his fictional sibling aesthetic reveals a dangerous actual personality. The source's comic account of their judgment is not independent evidence of such a risk. It shows an interpretation governing a family arrangement.

The later favorable relationship cannot automatically justify the deception. It gives Chihiro a wanted family while requiring a long role performance and limiting recognition. The adults' eventual apology is significant precisely because they cannot erase the earlier choice by citing the good that followed.


## 7. Disclosure changes who may know, not what everyone always knew

Chihiro's final V09 declaration occurs when a game-only sister role is challenged. The distinction between role-play and actual family becomes intolerable, and 私 emerges in a public claim to be the real sister. `IM-V09-E255`.

The event is not a serene identity announcement with a preplanned response. Emotion, withheld history and a game situation combine. The other participants' expressions disclose that the knowledge is distributed, not universally shared.

V10's immediate return supplies the finer distinction: Ashley already knew; Toki had noticed without confirmation; Itsuki, Nayuta, Haruto and Miyako did not understand the declaration at first. `IM-V10-E029` and the following 俺の弟が妹だった件について opening.

This knowledge map is essential to chronology and reconstruction. Toki's suspicion is not full prior certainty. Ashley's knowledge does not mean that all professional adults knew. The reader's access to an earlier secret cannot give Itsuki the same access.

本当の妹 asserts a family identity beyond the game and disguise. It does not assert shared biological parentage with Itsuki. Their relation is through the remarried family; the declaration matters because “real” concerns lived kinship and self-identification as well as genealogy.

The later pronoun change is not a mechanical removal of one word. Chihiro has years of habitual speech, continuing 兄さん address, practical competence and uncertain responses from other people. Actual recognition permits new conduct without proving that every old habit or defensive strategy immediately disappears.

The language specialist owns the detailed written-voice segmentation. The operational model must retain both the early performed role and the private knowledge unavailable to most interlocutors at that time. Neither should be treated as a globally false or globally complete person.

## 8. Actual siblinghood creates obligations a fantasy cannot decide

The V10 school/care dispute places Chihiro's wish to be near Itsuki against study and an independent future. Itsuki worries that his need for company could obstruct her; she claims responsibility for her choices and rejects leaving his care entirely to Aoba. `IM-V10-E114`.

Aoba is an actual younger aspiring writer whose “older brother” mode is read through a sister-character archetype. She is not a literally embedded fictional character just because the chapter contrasts 義妹 and 妹キャラ. The contrast concerns performed or projected roles within the real ensemble.

The scene does not make either sibling entirely correct. Chihiro can own a choice and still be making it under intense need for the relation. Itsuki can recognize her future and still rely on a double standard about his own earlier education.

His concern is nevertheless a significant change from asking only how useful another person can be to his work. He wants the sibling not to lose a desired future because of him. Chihiro recognizes that this repeats the logic by which she had earlier concealed identity to avoid obstructing his writing.

Mutual protective reasoning becomes a conflict rather than automatic harmony. Each wants to support the other's path and also wants something that may burden it. Neither can settle the disagreement merely by saying “it is my responsibility.”

The artistic consequence is equally important. An actual sister does not fulfill the fictional sister ideal by simply appearing. Disclosure helps destabilize the boundary between professional imagination and lived identity; later writing-function repair and personal integration are separate developments. `IM-V10-E340`–`IM-V10-E376`, `IM-V11-E298`–`IM-V11-E336`.

Literal title fulfillment is therefore dramatically insufficient. The person has a life, education and romantic object beyond being Itsuki's sister. The writer has acquired a family truth and a new problem of authorship. He cannot solve either by requiring her to become a character.

## 9. A father can have been misunderstood and still owe an apology

V10's grave visit names the image of Keisuke inside Itsuki: cold, rigid, apparently unmoved by the birth mother's death. It then recognizes surprising conduct and the need to face him, while acknowledging that the visit home has not yet occurred. `IM-V10-E317`.

This is a prospective limit. An intention to talk is not a completed reconciliation. Later evidence cannot be placed inside the V10 freeze as if it had already happened.

V11 finally supplies an actual family table. Natsume prompts Keisuke to say what he had intended; father and stepmother apologize for the concealment. Itsuki is startled because such an apology from his father is unprecedented in his experience. `IM-V11-E241`.

The apology does not require choosing between “the parents were monsters” and “they did everything correctly.” They had motives and gave Chihiro a family; they also participated in a deception that merits acknowledgment. Understanding a cause is not erasing responsibility.

Natsume's prompting is itself a concrete family action. It prevents the father–son relation from being treated as two men spontaneously repairing a bond in a vacuum. Another person helps make the speech possible.

Later ordinary drink and conversation give support a less theatrical form. Keisuke has checked the delayed publication date, asks about work and tells Itsuki to continue trying. `IM-V11-E254`. His bluntness does not mean he has no attention to the writer's actual life.

The son's embarrassment and tearful response demonstrate the force of this acknowledgment. The source does not establish that every prior disagreement is now harmless or that the father understands all the books. A parent can support an actual career without acquiring every genre preference.

The ritual specialist reads the shared meal and sake as a form permitting speech; the family study reads the altered recognition and retained difference. This is a repair performed in behavior and words, not merely a claim that “family was always there.”

## 10. Shiori's name joins creative skill to a living person's future

V12's new baby makes siblinghood materially immediate. Itsuki and Chihiro touch a warm living infant; he addresses Natsume as mother; the family asks him to choose a name. `IM-V12-E457`.

Keisuke's elaborate specification makes the request comic and demanding: individual but not bizarre, suitable to contemporary life and a lifetime of being called. The novelist's naming experience is suddenly expected to serve a person who will live beyond his authorship.

Itsuki responds with actual craft history, not a mystical claim of gifted naming. He has named many invented people through different methods. The difficulty is that these available habits must now be directed toward someone whose identity he will not control.

The selected 栞 / Shiori brings the book-related meaning of a bookmark into family hope. `IM-V12-E457` and the remaining 栞 naming sequence, `IM-V12-E458`–`IM-V12-E478`. A bookmark marks a place and a possible return; it does not determine the entire future text.

The placement also pauses Itsuki's current artistic crisis before the addressed-novel act. A new family member is a meaningful interruption, not a device that automatically repairs writing capacity or romantic conflict.

The name can be interpreted as a modest alternative to insisting that a child become one's protagonist. It is an offered orientation rather than a complete story. The later imagined future will test how easily even this novelist can forget the distinction.

## 11. Metaphoric sisterhood gives friendship a chosen form

At Miyako's V03 birthday, Nayuta says the admired friend's value does not depend on the worldly sexual persona she had claimed. The chosen お姉ちゃん relation names affection for the person rather than for the façade. `IM-V03-E040`.

This is not literal kinship or an adoption event. Its meaning is supplied by the exchange: Miyako fears loss of admiration, Nayuta distinguishes a good person from the role she had performed, and the two recognize affection.

The adjacent ownership joke is itself contested. Words that treat a future kiss as belonging to Itsuki prompt his small objection. The narration and joking form can make a possessive convention appealing while the dialogue retains a limit to its authority.

The scene gives the title another sibling register. “Older sister” can be a language of friendship independent of Itsuki's imagined erotic younger-sister ideal. A literal-family-only account would miss that role.

The later roomshare puts such affection into daily labor. V09's unequal domestic work and Miyako's mixed satisfaction in being needed prevent chosen sisterhood from becoming an automatically fair household. `IM-V09-E021`. A loved role still needs conditions that people can sustain.

V08's parental acceptance of Miyako as a companion for Kaiko similarly depends on a history of action, not only a pleasant label. `IM-V08-E123`. The simultaneous demand that Kaiko succeed without returning after failure is a retained contrary case: trusted chosen support can coexist with harsh inherited expectations.

## 12. Becoming an adult family does not erase a creator's other identities

V13 gives actual graduation, marriage and professional changes. Chihiro's new community and study belong to a life beyond concealed family care; Ashley and Kaizu's wedding changes an already negotiated relation; Itsuki and Nayuta move toward their own household. `IM-V13-E001`–`IM-V13-E009`, `IM-V13-E195`–`IM-V13-E249`, `IM-V13-E567`–`IM-V13-E596`.

The sequence does not prove that family is the final superior destination for every creator. The novels preserve different vocational and intimate goods within these milestones. The talent and relationships specialists trace that plurality in greater detail.

Nayuta's identity requires particular care. 可児那由多 is the public writer; 本田和子 and later 羽島和子 identify personal/family states. The changing family name and preferred domestic attention do not mean the artist was a false earlier person. `IM-V14-E003`, `IM-V14-E019`, `IM-V14-E023`.

Her possible retirement is an owned possibility, not a biological law of motherhood. Itsuki's desire for the rival writer has no authority to decide it for her. When she returns to writing, she asks him to take the domestic tasks that make it possible.

Ashley and Kaizu provide another configuration. His writing becomes a means toward family life, and she brings practical expertise and a chosen willingness to accompany risk. `IM-V12-E201`, `IM-V12-E214`, `IM-V14-E015`. Neither spouse becomes merely the other's support role.

Haruto's pen identity and private family identity are likewise different contexts, not one exposing the other as unreal. His professional and romantic milestones coexist with a difficult sibling household. `IM-V14-E024`. The family does not become healthy simply because the romance advances.

The final ensemble therefore supports identities that coexist and can compete: author, agent, reader, spouse, parent, sibling, friend and person with a private ambition. Itsuki's central story cannot choose the ordering for everyone.


## 13. Actual family life is ordinary enough to keep changing

V14's Saturday household brings Chihiro and Shiori into Itsuki's family routines. Children, food, comments about work and familiar irritation share space with the acclaimed author's self-conception. `IM-V14-E003`–`IM-V14-E005`.

The scenes contain troubling sexualized jokes involving young characters. They must be analyzed critically and without graphic quotation or reproducing the relevant images. Their continued presence prevents a family thesis that declares the whole ending ethically cleansed by marriage and fatherhood. The comedy and visual homes carry the detailed critical treatment.

Chihiro's continued love for Haruto is part of the same present. Itsuki can supply advice and aftercare, and still cannot give her the romantic answer. `IM-V14-E005`, `IM-V14-E010`. A real sibling relation provides support without resolving every other desire in the supported life.

Kazuko's parent friendship with Ashley creates a new lateral relation between households. The children's close birthdays and shared hospital connect families without making them one biography. `IM-V14-E015`. The novels give details of ordinary connection rather than a genealogical proof that the entire creative circle must reproduce itself.

The household support surrounding Itsuki and Kazuko is important to the work they can make. It is also unequal opportunity. A broad “chosen family sustains creation” reading must retain the available relatives, proximity and resources that make this particular configuration possible.

The final actual siblings' conversation then reverses the expected permanent supportive younger-sister role. Chihiro criticizes the manuscript, Itsuki rejects the unwelcome judgment and leaves childishly, and she keeps another aspiration private. `IM-V14-E043`–`IM-V14-E047`.

Her goal of graduate/doctoral study toward becoming an astronaut is not a plan to become the editor his imaginary story assigns her. It remains a secret wish with an unachieved horizon. The actual person exceeds the familial-professional place he finds convenient to write.

## 14. An author cannot complete the children's identities through an imagined future

The entire adolescent sequence of 青い小鳥たち is reclassified by the actual return: Sora is two, and Itsuki has written a dream-based story. `IM-V14-E028`–`IM-V14-E042`. Apparent age, career, relationship and interior access in the sequence cannot populate actual character endpoints.

Inside the draft, Yū's name and resemblance produce a fear of being a replacement; Shiori has private wishes and cruelty; Sora thinks about privilege and ordinary life; imagined parents and mentors shape available opportunities. `IM-V14-E028`, `IM-V14-E029`, `IM-V14-E038`. These remain interpretations and inventions at that level.

The actual child's 優羽 / Yū and the deceased writer's pen name 関ヶ原幽 / Kasuka Sekigahara must not be conflated. Original ruby reads 幽 as かすか, not ゆう. Inside the draft, Yū says she received the parents' deceased friend's name; that neither verifies the actual naming history nor establishes the deceased writer's legal name. The drafted adolescent's fear is not evidence that the actual parents intended her to be a replacement. The mainline young child's own later response is unknown.

The draft's adult Nadeko career is similarly unauthorized as a fact about the real novice. The actual frame makes her anger visible after reading it. `IM-V14-E033`, `IM-V14-E044`. A plausible or revealing imagined future is still another person's story being appropriated.

The children's privilege in the draft matters analytically without becoming a forecast. Parental TRPG help and tailored performance coaching illustrate how inherited resources can disappear from a young person's account of achievement. `IM-V14-E032`, `IM-V14-E035`. They supply a generational counterexample to pure self-authorship within the author's scenario.

At the same time, the draft grants several young figures hidden perspectives that exceed Sora's first-person assumptions. Itsuki can construct this formal plurality and still fail to respect the real referents. Literary insight and ethical conduct are different evidence domains.

The actual frame makes that distinction sharp through 生モノ, living-person fiction, and inadequate toddler permission. `IM-V14-E043`. Family closeness does not supply consent to an adult biography and does not reveal every future private desire.

## 15. The omitted unborn sister is the title's final boundary test

Kazuko is actually pregnant with Sora's younger sister. That child is omitted from Itsuki's imagined future. `IM-V14-E048`. Pregnancy and the stated expected sibling are actual source-supported present facts; a birth, name and personality are not.

The omission matters because the author who insists on sister-centered imagination fails to perceive the family possibility already becoming real. His dramatic plot has selected one protagonist's needs and neglected another life he should have known about.

The joke and correction do not mean the title must finally refer only to this unborn sister. They make every total interpretation less secure. Literal sisterhood continues to emerge, but its future member is not an available finished answer to the adults' desires.

Chihiro hopes for gentler or happier futures than the draft gives several people. Those hopes are neither achieved repairs nor assured predictions. Her actual astronaut wish likewise cannot be promoted into the draft's achieved career.

The brief possibility of a prophetic dream is dismissed within the scene and left unproved. `IM-V14-E049`. The second afterword explicitly describes uncertainty about what future might actually occur. `IM-V14-E050`. Neither route grants the invented future authority over the actual open one.

The closing coexistence of wanting absent goods and finding something presently sufficient is therefore earned through the frame. One can own an aspiration or cherish a relation without completing all the lives implicated by it.

## 17. Strong contrary readings

### 17.1 The title's ideological center may remain an idealized sister

The series repeatedly places youthful female characters, domestic care and male creator aspiration in appealing proximity. Its troubling jokes and visual framing persist even in the final family. A reader can reasonably argue that critical counterexamples do not dislodge the central pleasure of the ideal.

The contrary evidence includes early real-family recoil, Chihiro's own choice and cost, the adults' apology, Haruto's sibling's independent anger, the final manuscript critique and the omitted actual unborn sister. These establish resistance within the work; they do not absolve every formal choice. The family's ethical interpretation remains narrower than its entire affective appeal.

### 17.2 The concealment may be a convenient plot machine

The long disguise can seem designed primarily for a reveal that destabilizes a novelist. Its plausibility and the adults' willingness are openly comic and extraordinary. That is a legitimate formal objection.

The retrospective chapter nevertheless supplies economic history, desire, distributed participation and distinct parental judgments. Those details make the arrangement more than a twist while leaving its ethical and practical convenience contestable. A close reading should neither naturalize the extreme choice nor discard the actual person whose life has been shaped by it.

### 17.3 Family sufficiency may obscure external inequality

Supportive proximity and resources make these families unusually available. The adolescent draft intensifies inherited advantage through access to major writers and performers. A warm general claim about belonging risks presenting a privileged arrangement as universal.

The study therefore retains who supplies time, household competence, money, access and opportunities. The text can depict real care without proving that family is sufficient for every unsupported person's path.

### 17.4 Naming and dreaming can still colonize another person's story

Shiori's book-related name is lovingly offered, and the draft supplies several independent viewpoints. Both can be read as forms of a novelist writing the world around him into his preferred language. The actual children's later acceptance is not shown.

The frame's criticism is the best test against a wholly benign authorship thesis. Itsuki is capable of imaginative plurality and capable of enjoying a real person's hurt. Chihiro's secret aim and Kazuko's corrective pregnancy keep actual life beyond the supplied fiction.

### 17.5 The final sufficient good may be an unstable consolation

The actual closing formulation can sound like a modest acceptance of an unfinished life; it can also sound like another temporary story by which people soothe lack. The novels do not give a future experiment that decides which interpretation is final.

Their strongest achievement is retaining both: concrete people and acts that matter now, and desires that will make further claims. A title-centered reading should leave that tension active rather than declare permanent contentment.

## 18. Final disposition and inter-home responsibility

Literal sisterhood, fictional sister aesthetic, chosen sibling language and family-making all remain distinct across the completed corpus. None can explain every artistic or intimate problem. The most defensible title thesis is consequently situated: a person may find something that makes a life worth continuing without acquiring a person who must satisfy every need.

This strengthens the earlier account of lived care while rejecting total literal sufficiency and a single-cause origin of Itsuki's taste. It revises any account of Chihiro as merely a hidden twist or perfect caregiver. It preserves the temporal freezes and the claim ledger's formal dispositions.
