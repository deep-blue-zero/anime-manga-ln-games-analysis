# Illustration, paratext and media reflexivity

## 0. How does the published object make reading happen?

The primary problem is **what the book's composite form lets a reader see, anticipate, mistake and reconsider**. Covers, color plates, monochrome narrative images, profiles, manuscripts, questionnaires, game sheets, advertisements and creator afterwords do different work. Their common presence in an EPUB does not give them identical narrative authority.

This specialist argues that the visual program repeatedly makes creation and social life material. It offers commercially legible attractive characters, selects intimate and professional encounters for attention, lets readers inspect fictional publishing objects, and sometimes performs an action which prose extraction alone cannot recover. Its strongest reflexive achievement is to make representation answerable to other readers. Its strongest ethical difficulty is that the commercial gaze can intensify the very boundary problems the story notices.

The creative-labor home owns production relations and economics; narration owns speaker/fiction-level attribution; comedy owns the detailed consent and vulgarity argument; this home owns the original package's visual and paratextual mediation. Reusing a scene here asks why its form and placement matter, not whether one topical essay can replace the others.

Evidence includes the frozen visual index, prospective readings, visual ledger and selected original assets. All 562 non-gaiji indexed routes received package/hash verification; that is not a claim of 562 new semantic image inspections in this retrospective pass. All35 V14 non-gaiji items were perceived during its prospective reading. This specialist additionally reopens twelve selected non-graphic original assets across V01, V04, V06, V07, V09, V11, V13 and V14. Their exact routes are recorded below.

No source artwork, full-page copyrighted text gallery or binary release package is added to Git. The analytical corpus supplies descriptions, locators and source identities. Critical treatment of sexualized young figures stays non-graphic. The primary-source package remains the source rather than an artwork collection recreated inside the analysis.

## 1. One package, several evidential kinds

| Material kind | Direct evidential use | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| Cover/front color composition | Published emphasis, character selection, visible relation/posture, package expectations | No exact scene chronology or access to private motive by appearance alone |
| Narrative illustration | Scene framing and a source-supported depicted moment | No proof that every apparent feeling is settled; no independent consent inference |
| Embedded manuscript/pitch/catalog | Material form of a represented text or production object | Fictional contents do not become the author's deeds or external industry documentation |
| Profile/questionnaire | Attributed written paratext and public/persona presentation | No audio traits, automatic chronology or unlimited private access |
| Game sheet/diagram | Represented role/system information and reader-facing rules apparatus | Game identity does not become actual genealogy; source explanation is not a current rulebook |
| Author/illustrator afterword | Named compositional claim, stated intention, dated personal comment | No omniscient canon override or verification of every claimed effect |
| Promotional/reception page | Historical marketing selection, announcement and package identity | No newly admitted external story or representative reception sample |
| Colophon | Edition provenance and publication data | No thematic verdict |

The frozen index's tier_1b classification is useful source administration. It does not forbid an image from carrying a narrative function. Conversely, an image located in a narrative chapter does not by itself authenticate everything it depicts as an event in the actual continuity.

The V14 challenge is the clearest positive example: ILL021 visibly supplies words required to complete a prose sentence. Its actual role is narrative continuation. `IM-V14-E073`. The astronaut afterword image supplies a different kind of assertion, expressly a possible future. `IM-V14-E076`. They should not receive the same use merely because both are drawn by the same illustrator.

A robust route therefore records asset ID, source member/hash, package class, placement, represented level, any speaker/text assignment and analytical use. Interpretation can amend a use while retaining the frozen inventory identity.

## 2. Covers distribute attention, but they do not dictate an arc

V01's cover visibly crops Itsuki at the left, places Nayuta in physical attachment to him, gives Miyako a mediating/supporting position and Chihiro a distinct space on the right. The ruled manuscript-page motif surrounds the ensemble. `V01-ILL-001`. This makes attractive relations and writing jointly legible before the narrative reveals how complicated those relations are.

Chihiro's soft and androgynous presentation can support retrospective foreshadowing, but the composition is not a spoken disclosure to Itsuki. The reader's later information and a character's knowledge remain different. The family specialist owns that disclosure history; the cover owns the anticipation.

V06's cover brings Itsuki and Nayuta's near-kiss gesture to the center while other figures watch or intrude from the margins. `V06-ILL-001`. This is compatible with the volume's eventual coupling, but it is not sufficient evidence for the exact timing, exchange or consent of a depicted gesture. Those depend on the hospital scene and proposal. `IM-V06-E125`.

V07 removes Itsuki from its cover and places the older creator/social relation at the center. `V07-ILL-001`. The black-haired figure's presentation is visually repeated in the photograph visible at the V13 wedding, where the source context identifies the remembered Kasuka relation (legacy analytical label Yuu). The V07 retrospective prose and illustration independently route that history through `IM-V07-E159` and `IM-V07-E221`. This is useful cross-volume visual continuity; hair and dress alone would not establish the full biography. Original ruby names 関ヶ原幽 as Kasuka Sekigahara; she is not the later child Yū Yasaka.

The contrast undermines a pure protagonist-only account of package hierarchy. Yet V14 returns to the original four-person configuration with an added child: Itsuki and Miyako engage with a manuscript, Nayuta sits with Sora, and Chihiro occupies the right. `V14-ILL-001`, `IM-V14-E053`.

The return is not a reset. Miyako's professional involvement is materialized through manuscript and pen; motherhood is visible without exhausting Nayuta's authored future; Chihiro's changed presentation does not erase family continuity. The shared manuscript-paper motif makes a series about makers recognizable across age and role changes.

These selected covers support variation and callback, not a monotonically measurable move “away from Itsuki” or proof that cover area equals narrative importance. One especially relational cover cannot settle whose story a volume ultimately becomes. The sustained ensemble argument depends on focalization and independent decisions as well as package composition.

## 3. Images can carry both comfort and unresolved desire

V14-ILL009 divides the image into parents looking into a bedroom above and the sleeping child below. Their attentive expressions and Sora's relaxed pose give parenthood a concrete emotional reward. `IM-V14-E003`, `IM-V14-E061`.

The prose preserves dissatisfaction around this warmth. Itsuki's creative longing and Kazuko's later uncertainty are not disproved by a comfortable image. A reader can see a genuinely valued household and still ask what making no new novel costs or whether something is absent.

The image does not need to hide unease for that tension to exist. Its selectivity is precisely the mechanism: one moment is made unusually available to the eye, while the prose supplies a wider temporal and interior context. This differs from calling the image deceptive.

The same distinction governs romantic travel. A pleasant visual moment between Haruto and Miyako does not erase Chihiro's deceptive arrangement or specify all subsequent consent. `IM-V14-E006`, `IM-V14-E063`. Once there, the pair make choices; the friendly frame should not make the setup automatically fair.

V14-ILL014 gives Toki and Miyako a street-level shared conversation. He scratches his head; she gestures while listening or speaking; coats, signs and walking space make editorial vulnerability an ordinary adult encounter rather than a disembodied industry theorem. `IM-V14-E014`, `IM-V14-E066`.

It is contrary evidence to the visual caricature of Toki as only a shouting taskmaster. It is not proof of uniform gentleness, nor of a private romance with Miyako. The surrounding professional grief and rival contributions supply the correct relation.

This selective materiality is one of the program's achievements. A career, a family or a friendship is allowed to have a body, a place and a mundane duration. The image cannot certify the total state of that life.

## 5. The reader sees a publishing process through its objects

V04-ILL008 is a tilted project sheet headed 企画書, not a portrait. The absurd sister-world concept is laid out through concept, characters and synopsis, while a smaller attached editor comment asks for pages to make an unclear possibility judgeable. `IM-V04-E172`.

The image makes an important distinction visible. An eccentric concept is not yet a readable novel; the editor's comment neither proves the work will succeed nor dismisses it solely for sounding strange. The simulated paper and attached note give revision a material relationship between proposal and response.

This form complements the prose's failed pitches and later negotiations. It does not establish an actual external submission template, nor is its fictional sister-world biography Itsuki's actual family. Treating its seventeen-year-old narrator as Itsuki himself would confuse author and designed speaker.

V05's pitch, office, finalist summaries and prize requirements extend the mechanism. `IM-V05-E203`, `IM-V05-E204`, `IM-V05-E211`, `IM-V05-E212`. The objects show books moving through designed evaluative systems: categories, conditions, comparison and human labor.

The prospective V05 judgment argument is particularly sensitive to this. Institutional form can make ranking look clean while the narrative preserves disagreement among judges and different kinds of originality. `IM-V05-E149`. The document is a rule/object within that process, not proof that its resulting order is a universal hierarchy of human value.

V06's casting and professional documents similarly place production before finished spectacle. `IM-V06-E118`, `IM-V06-E122`. Its fictional report manga is instructed to make cast harmony visible before that harmony exists. `IM-V06-E087`. This is a source-specific warning about documentary-looking representation: its purpose may be promotion rather than transparent reporting.

V08's father/publishing-object material makes the opposite reception problem visible. A parent can read a son's genre presentation as personality and fail to recognize what writing meant to him. `IM-V08-E211`. Package legibility creates knowledge, but it can create an overly simple classification.

V09's production and finished-anime visual material moves from mediation to reception. `IM-V09-E291`, `IM-V09-E304`. Itsuki's bodily response to the completed white-box screening is a creator event, not merely an item in a media-mix checklist. The previous discarded or difficult production labor matters to how that success should be valued.

V13-ILL015's work document makes the hybrid writing phase inspectable, and ILL021 materializes the private-to-public book. `IM-V13-E636`, `IM-V13-E642`. These forms prevent “he writes a new book” from becoming an abstract state change. A manuscript, a title, a treatment and permission circulate among people.

V14's catalogs do yet another job. Detective, doujin and fantasy books have distinct visual/publication objects. `IM-V14-E068`, `IM-V14-E070`, `IM-V14-E072`. The ensemble makes different kinds of work for different uses; no single cover style, award or genre contains all legitimate creation.

These recurring artifacts are one reason this creator-industry novel can examine the light novel as both art and manufactured object. The analysis should retain the individual production relations without taking simulated paperwork as documentary evidence about all real publishing.

## 6. Games make temporary worlds available for inspection

The V01 TRPG sheets and diagrams give shared fantasy a visible structure. `IM-V01-E049`. Players and readers can track roles, constraints and action rather than relying only on the host's summary. The sheets' authority is the represented game system.

V04's party/death/character material similarly puts contingency beside intention. `IM-V04-E183`, `IM-V04-E184`, `IM-V04-E185`. The prose's failed miracle has a rule-and-dice context; the death illustration gives the failure emotional weight. `IM-V04-E135`. It does not turn the fictional death into a real one or certify a host's universal refusal to intervene.

The V06 author PF says homebrew hosting may include intensive preparation and occasional concealed dice management. `IM-V06-E109`. That compositional account should be consulted alongside, not imposed as proof that the particular V04 failure was secretly arranged.

V09's dense TRPG cluster supplies a world in which sister recognition can temporarily feel safe. `IM-V09-E305`, `IM-V09-E311`, `IM-V09-E312`. The visual apparatus strengthens the contrast between a recognized role and an actual undisclosed identity. It cannot decide which real participants know the truth until the spoken scene does so.

V11 card sheets manipulate sister categories as inspectable tokens. `IM-V11-E064`. Their recurrence moves the title's fantasy through play and design without converting every card into actual genealogy. A physically available category can be funny, strategically useful and emotionally insufficient.

The V13 Word Wolf document is a different information apparatus. `IM-V13-E632`. Its relevance is less heroic embodiment than hidden classification: a player need not know which category the system assigned them. The ritual and narration homes explain that epistemic structure in detail.

Visual game documentation therefore performs a double move. It makes an invented world concrete enough for affective investment while displaying the constructedness which prevents literal transfer back to life. This is close to the final manuscript's problem, but play and portraiture have different permissions and audiences.

## 8. The wedding image preserves a past inside a continuing life

The picture-within-picture is also a compact authority lesson. Its represented past is present as a photograph/object in the wedding scene. That does not make Kasuka physically attend the wedding as a living person or prove a supernatural visitation.

The V14 Christmas/family scene gives actual parenthood and continued making another scale. `IM-V14-E015`. One could read the photograph as a comforting simplification of grief. The narrative does not show every cost of the new household. That is a valid ceiling. Yet the visual choice still defeats the narrower claim that continuation requires the old attachment to vanish.

## 9. Fanservice and critical distance can work against each other

The erotic color package and some narrative illustrations attract attention by making bodies unusually inspectable. They can exaggerate a joke, intensify embarrassment, privilege a gaze or select a vulnerable moment. Their commercial and formal roles should be named rather than sanitized.

The V02 forced clothing-removal image accompanies an explicit boundary violation. `IM-V02-E100`. The V04 avoidance image preserves the victim's memory of that encounter. `IM-V04-E179`. Later artistic development does not retrospectively supply consent.

Several examples involve minors. Their critical significance is retained here without explicit quotation or image reproduction. The existence of a comic explanation, character retaliation or later more serious plot does not neutralize the sexualizing frame.

The V05 direct handoff to Kantoku's swimsuit image turns composite-media production into a joke: prose openly delegates a scene's appeal to the illustration. `IM-V05-E207`. Formally, this is a revealing act of medium awareness. Ethically, awareness of the handoff does not by itself resolve what the selected depiction asks the reader to enjoy.

V06's fine-art transformation can give Setsuna a new artistic language while leaving boundaries distinct. `IM-V06-E115`, `IM-V06-E119`. An image which marks skill or aesthetic conversion should not be captioned as proof of ethical maturation.

V14 repeats the tension around Nadeshiko and the draft's adult/young-person encounter. `IM-V14-E065`, `IM-V14-E074`. The eventual frame criticizes irresponsible representation while the published book has already made an uncomfortable representation available. Both facts belong to the evaluation.

The best reading neither reduces every visual choice to exploitation nor excuses exploitation because the book can reflect on it. Narrative and visual framing may cooperate, diverge or pull the reader toward incompatible judgments. That instability is a finding, not an inconvenience to eliminate.


## 10. The book makes three endings materially different

The award scene reaches a prose clause which remains suspended. ILL021 provides the speaker's face, gesture, challenge かかってこい and conspicuous 完. `IM-V14-E025`, `IM-V14-E073`. The reader must cross from prose to image to recover the completed utterance.

The forward gesture and enlarged lettering make competition an invitation and a performance. This is an actual public event in the ensemble narrative, with actual listeners. It remains subject to the prose's differentiated responses and private qualification; the image does not turn every listener into an untroubled admirer.

An author afterword then appears. Its position and introduction of a future bonus encourage the expectation of an epilogue after a completed main story. `IM-V14-E027`. This is a publishing voice acting on the reader's expectations.

The long apparent future has its own characters, interiorities, illustrated moments and collective stage work. Its end is 続かない. `IM-V14-E041`. Only the return to the actual present identifies the preceding material as Itsuki's dream-based draft. `IM-V14-E042`.

The final mainline reflection and （完） occur after that criticism, the private astronaut goal and the actual pregnancy. `IM-V14-E047`, `IM-V14-E048`, `IM-V14-E049`. The work therefore closes after making its award-winning author answerable as a reader-facing maker, rather than at the award alone.

These closures cannot be counted as redundant decoration. They change which book, story or level a reader thinks has ended. The image ending, the framed manuscript ending and the returned present each establish something different.

One favorable interpretation is that the package makes the reader rehearse the danger of accepting an author's preferred future. Another is that the reveal exploits a reader's investment in what seemed to be genuine later lives. Both should remain available. The correct authority classification does not settle whether the trick is aesthetically satisfying.

The late return also prevents the award image from becoming a certificate of mature conduct. Itsuki can win, issue a memorable challenge, continue loving his family and then respond childishly to justified criticism. `IM-V14-E046`. The composite ending retains achievement and limitation.

## 11. Author and illustrator voices have narrower powers than they appear to

The source credits identify Yomi Hirasaka and Kantoku. That credits the published work; it does not tell the analyst who proposed each composition, selected every image or made every editorial decision. An undocumented production hypothesis stays a hypothesis.

Afterwords supply stronger evidence where they explicitly describe a choice. The V04 prose-rewrite notice identifies a changed relationship to a drama script; the V06 hosting account identifies stated composition practice; V08 commentary discusses adaptation priorities. `IM-V04-E159`, `IM-V06-E109`, `IM-V08-E217`. Their statements are valuable PF, separately classed from scene truth.

The V14 illustrator afterword describes the astronaut picture as Chihiro's future possibility. `IM-V14-E076`. The visible floating pose, equipment and Earth-like background make the aspiration imaginable. The written qualification prevents the image from promising that she completes training or enters space.

The same page advertises an art collection and invites looking back at illustrations from the first volume. That is historical marketing inside the source. It does not make the unconsulted artbook or its interview part of this release's admitted corpus.

The author afterword's statement that the bonus belongs with the main story can describe his compositional intent. `IM-V14-E050`. It cannot override the narrative's explicit revelation that the represented adolescent future is a draft. “Part of the novel's main composition” and “actual event in its continuity” are different propositions.

Questionnaires are another controlled voice. V14 ILL010, ILL012 and ILL015 present fictional written responses through portraits and prompts. `IM-V14-E062`, `IM-V14-E064`, `IM-V14-E067`. Only independently identified portraits receive names; timing is not always fixed. Neither a familiar face nor a witty answer authorizes a new dated biography.

Their usefulness is real but bounded. They can foreground professional creeds, social habits or attitudes in a compact form and contrast several people. They cannot supply performed timbre, and they should not flatten the longer narrative into a list of maxims.

## 12. Promotional surfaces are a record of mediation

The ebook preserves advertisements, anniversary/retailer galleries, selected congratulations, media-mix notices, recruitment and editorial information. These are evidence of what this edition marketed and how it presented a publication ecosystem.

They do not create mainline events. An adaptation notice does not import the adaptation's scenes, a bonus cover does not authenticate its implied pairing, and a congratulatory page is not a representative survey of reception.

The V14 ads ILL025–035 are explicitly outside mainline inference. `IM-V14-E077` through `IM-V14-E087`. A dated price or availability announcement is not a current shopping recommendation. Source inventory can record it without pretending it has been verified in the present.

Similarly, a represented industry statistic in a narrator's explanatory passage and a promotional page's sales claim require their own authority tags. The creative-labor specialist analyzes their literary use within the source boundary; this specialist does not silently upgrade them to independently established contemporary facts.

The Taiwanese signing scenes show that mediation also includes translation, events and reader-facing bodies. `IM-V10-E408`. The visual encounter supports the scene's embodied labor but cannot establish readership demographics beyond the text. Translation is part of making a book available, not an invisible neutral transfer.

Supplementary artbook, audio, anime, manga and interview research remain explicitly deferred. The source inventory and crosswalk list acquisition and classification tasks. Their absence limits claims about production collaboration, performance and reception; it does not block the fourteen-numbered-volume completion.

## 13. Countertheses which the visual account must keep

**The package still privileges attractive female bodies.** Documents and professional images do not make the erotic commercial apparatus disappear. Especially where young figures are sexualized, a reflexive frame cannot be treated as an ethical waiver. `IM-V02-E100`, `IM-V14-E065`, `IM-V14-E074`.

**Illustration selection simplifies lived complexity.** A warm family image, a striking confession or a triumphant author can stand in for a longer process in a reader's memory. The analysis must recover the surrounding state rather than accuse every selective image of being false. `IM-V05-E214`, `IM-V14-E061`, `IM-V14-E073`.

**Document form can simulate authority.** A prize rule, profile or report looks inspectable and can make a judgment feel objective. The V06 harmony-report instruction is direct contrary evidence to a transparent-document model. `IM-V06-E087`.

**The manuscript reveal can weaken apparent future investment.** The ethical critique earns its force by taking away a presumed future status. The reader may judge the strategy moving, manipulative or both. Its material sequencing must be described before its success is evaluated.

**The cover is neither irrelevant nor a complete interpretation.** It establishes emphasis and invites expectations, but it does not yield a numerical hierarchy of character importance. V07's older relation and V14's return to the original group demonstrate variation rather than one steady trajectory.

**Creators' intentions do not determine all effects.** An afterword explains a stated choice, not every reader's experience. The source contains several represented cases of effects beyond intention, including fiction changing another person's attention. `IM-V12-E345`.

**The illustrator's future picture remains a possibility.** It enriches the ending while withholding achieved closure. Treating it as prophecy would repeat the very category error the draft reveal exposes. `IM-V14-E076`.

## 14. Mature revisions and the qualified result

The early “illustration plus fanservice” account is too narrow. The full boundary supports a mixed program of bodies, institutions, social systems, fictional objects and page-level interruption. The strongest positive cases are the pitch sheet, TRPG apparatus, dark page, wedding photograph and image-completed challenge.

The hypothesis of a linear move from protagonist to ensemble is also too simple. The package redistributes attention and makes callbacks; it can foreground an older cohort or return to familiar figures in altered relations. Prose focalization remains essential to establish independent personhood.

The assumption that a visual future grants total closure must be rejected. V14 explicitly separates a depicted possible astronaut from achievement and an apparent adolescent future from actual biography.

The full source's visual achievement is therefore not that it provides a picture of everyone's completed life. It makes making, caring, reading and being looked at concrete while repeatedly requiring authority-sensitive interpretation. Its ethical limitation remains visible where commercial gaze and comic pleasure exceed the boundaries which the prose can acknowledge.
