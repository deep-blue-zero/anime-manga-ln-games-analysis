# Nayuta Kani: who may determine a gifted person's reasons?

## 1. Succession and the literary problem

The preserved V01–V06 monograph has a strict early horizon. This new monograph takes over **whole-series reader-facing interpretation**, while leaving that older bounded history byte-identical. This is explicit retrieval succession, not a silent expansion of its metadata or a claim that later findings were known at V06. The [operational model](../character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_NAYUTA_KANI_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) has a separate function.

Nayuta's literary problem is who may define what her extraordinary capacity should be for. Readers want books, institutions want output, Itsuki wants a rival/answer, Miyako wants her needed work to continue, and Nayuta herself wants particular people and experiences. Those wants intersect without becoming one legitimate command.

The complete series does not answer by proving she was never exceptional or never dependent. It makes exceptional talent and concentrated attachment real, then insists that neither contains all agency. She can choose professional expansion, private art, quitting, care and another work without becoming a different entity each time.

The strongest positive thesis is that her authorship remains an activity with chosen reasons rather than an inexhaustible gift owed to everyone. The strongest contrary thesis is that the story repeatedly organizes those reasons around the beloved man's work, leaving her artistic autonomy relationally concentrated. Both have source support.

## 2. The book which reaches her does not own her

The school wound is severe and source-shown; its critical treatment here is non-graphic. Itsuki's imperfect debut offers an imagined world with enough energy to change what she thinks possible. `IM-V01-E008`.

Her response is independent action. She enters authorship and produces extraordinary work; the book's author did not direct a program of rescue or command her career. A work can become life-changing beyond its creator's knowledge.

This keeps gratitude and obligation distinct. The later claim that he is responsible because he helped save her expresses an emotionally serious relation, but it is not a transferable rule that a writer owns or must indefinitely satisfy a reader's life. The V11 dispute makes that danger explicit. `IM-V11-E325`.

The ordinary-world encounter with Miyako establishes a second transformation. Nayuta intends an account of suffering partly as a rival warning; Miyako responds to the hurt person, becoming angry and tearful on her behalf. Nayuta is surprised into an involuntary smile. `IM-V01-E015`.

This is a meeting her original tactic does not fully predict. It complicates the portrait of a self-contained genius whose emotional world is simply Itsuki plus everyone else. A particular other woman can answer something in her with no need to outrank the writer.

## 3. Candor is not absence of boundaries

Nayuta's polite surface and outrageous content make an early recognizable comic voice. That voice can signal will, play, desire and provocation. It does not prove worldly experience or permanent availability.

The research scene's kiss refusal is specifically addressed to Miyako. She says it would be her first and turns away embarrassed. `IM-V02-E043`. The distinction between explicit rhetoric and an actual threshold is not a minor inconsistency to smooth away. It is evidence of a person with different stakes for language, action and first experience.

Her chosen お姉ちゃん language and private birthday novel answer Miyako's self-worth problem through attachment. `IM-V03-E034`, `IM-V03-E040`. Affection can use crude categories while valuing the person beyond a socially performed sexual persona. The comedy does real relational work.

At the same time, bold candor can hurt. The V12 institutional appeal meets a cold dismissal because she has not written for those institutions/readers as such. Miyako hears the devaluation of being needed, a value central to her own experience. `IM-V12-E155`. Politeness can cut as well as comfort.

Neither “shameless” nor “secretly innocent” is adequate. The source does not ask for one hidden true state beneath every performance. It provides a pattern of context-dependent thresholds, willingness and vulnerability which her recognizable voice can carry.

## 4. Talent does not contain experience

Nayuta's exceptional production is materially different from ordinary novice labor. Her work's scale, speed and effect are not imaginary compliments. The analysis should not democratize her by pretending everyone has the same capacity.

Yet the experience she wants from effort is not automatically supplied by that capacity. In V06 she wants the 景色 of striving visible to admired others, and professional encounters generate new work pleasure. `IM-V06-E033`, `IM-V06-E068`.

This challenges a simple hierarchy in which genius means having everything important in advance. Ability to make a world does not guarantee having lived another person's process. Exceptional quality can coexist with envy of another kind of experience.

The V11 effort protest then resists the opposite erasure: that her greatness proves she has never tried. The original context explicitly invokes striving out of sight. `IM-V11-E325`. Itsuki knows there is effort and still cannot stop comparative pain. Recognition of a fact and relief from an emotion are different.

Her work and body also have limits. Overwork has an actual cost at the earlier romantic threshold. `IM-V06-E092`. The source's extraordinary-capacity portrait cannot be used to make a boundless daily schedule or to imply that other people's care has no burden.

The complete account therefore preserves three distinct things: exceptional capacity, actual striving and desired experience. None is a substitute for the others. A comparison which sees only one will misread both creator and lover.

## 5. A private gift creates a different audience

The book for Miyako is not just unused commercial inventory. It is made for a particular person; Nayuta refuses an automatic move toward broader circulation. `IM-V03-E031`, `IM-V03-E034`.

Haruto can recognize its market value while the author recognizes its private address. The disagreement is not resolved by saying the market estimate is false. It is resolved locally by not treating commercial value as superior permission.

This private making gives Nayuta another authorship relation. Miyako is not merely a rival she tolerates for Itsuki's sake; she is an addressee who warrants a whole work and a chosen kinship term.

V13 adds further complexity. Itsuki reports Nayuta's first reaction to a manuscript modeled partly on Miyako as evidence of romantic affection. `IM-V13-E558`. The report's provenance matters. It is not direct reciprocal confession between the women, and Miyako explicitly distinguishes invented sexual history from lived history.

A careful monograph can retain a broader intimate possibility without treating every affectionate image as romance or every novel as diary. Nayuta's artistic representations carry feeling and invention together.

## 6. Entering a wider field remains a choice

Early professional expansion can be subordinated to proximity to Itsuki. Later initiative and encounters with other makers provide real creative pleasure. `IM-V06-E068`. The change is not proof that her original reason for writing was false; reasons can accumulate.

Itsuki's jealousy over pleasure he did not cause shows another danger of concentrated artistic romance. A loved person's work can generate experiences and attachments outside the partner's causal control. Accepting that matters as much as accepting her talent.

The later household also contains unequal practical care. Miyako finds gratitude and satisfaction in being needed while the arrangement is expressly not good as a division. `IM-V09-E021`. Nayuta's absorbed art exists within that ecology.

Agency cannot therefore be defended by ignoring dependence. She chooses; others make some choices materially possible. The relevant ethical question is how work, care, refusal and priority are negotiated, not whether a talented person can be declared independent of all relations.

This distinction is also why a general “she should write for society” answer is inadequate. Institutions can have legitimate obligations and other workers real needs, while the source still refuses a simple transfer of ownership over her reasons.

## 7. The creator dispute is serious and ethically unstable

V11's “AI-like” criticism is a metaphor for a work missing what she values, not evidence of actual machine authorship. `IM-V11-E320`. Technical construction has returned; the source does not deny that.

Her preferred author has changed his relation to a former aspiration, and she experiences that as a loss. The quarrel is not just envy or inability to appreciate ordinary craft. It concerns a specific work, remembered rescue and the person she loves.

The same specificity makes her demand dangerous. To say “this is not the work/person I need” can be a candid reading. To require him to remain the author who saved her can overreach into his freedom to change or leave.

Kaiko provides the strongest contrary position: different maker modes can be respected, and there is no one absolute correct answer. `IM-V12-E323`. That does not make Nayuta's stance illegitimate as one fan's stance. It limits its authority to dictate everyone else's work.

Kaizu's livelihood/exit argument also survives. `IM-V11-E131`. The later successful reconciliation must not be used as retrospective proof that harsh pressure was ethically necessary or that no different acceptable life existed.

This is one reason the literary monograph remains separate from an operational rulebook. It can explain why the hurtful demand is powerful in the story while refusing to prescribe it as a reliable method for lovers or editors.

## 8. Quitting has several meanings

V12 quitting follows the loss of the central romantic/creative relation. It is not automatically liberation from that relation. Her gaming, changed presentation and withdrawal carry actual pain, and the institutional appeal does not reach her motive. `IM-V12-E011`, `IM-V12-E155`.

Miyako's care changes from needing the author to valuing the person's continued existence. This matters precisely because it does not require a new book as proof that Nayuta deserves love.

Takashina offers a credible different relation, including serious reading and professional values. Nayuta refuses. `IM-V12-E410`. The refusal is her choice grounded in her own history; it does not establish his inferior worth.

The V14 possibility of retirement is different. Kazuko has fulfilled love, parenthood and a household she values. Financial sufficiency and child priority make writing unnecessary for income and, at that point, no longer desired. `IM-V14-E019` with original context p0039–0065.

A total trajectory which labels both moments “loss of agency” loses the difference between collapse and contentment. A total trajectory which labels both “free choice” equally complete loses dependency and pain. The source supplies different stated reasons, conditions and consequences.

## 9. The mother and the author are not mutually exclusive identities

V14's narration calls Kazuko a good wife/mother and immediately asks whether the change should count as growth. That question is not her declaration that motherhood is empty; the subsequent dialogue states her contentment plainly. E019 and its original extension.

The change in register is real. Ordinary souvenirs, meals, careful eating and 伊月さん / 宙くん differ from the early constant provocation. Yet a small adjacent family/body joke prevents a rigid “never a body joke again” classifier. The source's generalization describes a marked pattern, not an exhaustive future rule.

Most childcare and household work fall to her at that point. This makes a later wish to write materially consequential. Returning cannot simply mean that a hidden artistic essence reappears without anyone making time.

Itsuki longs for a rival but does not directly command her to rank unknown readers above their child or return to a punishing industry. His private desire and her spoken priority differ. That restraint has ethical weight without making him perfect.

The new fantasy reaches her as a particular reader. Her answer is to want to write and ask for practical help. `IM-V14-E023`. The unit shows her request, her reason and her rise as 可児那由多; it does not show Itsuki's explicit answer.

A later sequel is actual. `IM-V14-E024`. The exact sustained household arrangement remains unshown. The monograph can celebrate chosen return while retaining that care-labor gap.

## 10. An answering work is hers

The sequel extends the originating fantasy into different lives and forms of love. Its internal later setting is not an actual fourteen-year jump in the ensemble. `IM-V14-E024`.

Itsuki admires it as greater, and its publication changes the first work's reception. Her agency is visible as authorship with effects beyond the role of cheering a husband or reproducing his message.

Yet the reply remains relationally occasioned. This is the strongest countercase to a maximal independence thesis: the beloved man's work again helps generate her next desire. The source does not solve that tension by stripping it of value.

A more precise account says she owns the response even though another person occasions it. Relational origin need not make the work or choice his property. The private Miyako book, broader media pleasure and answering sequel show different addresses through which she becomes more than one fixed function.

Actual pregnancy remains at the final returned present. `IM-V14-E048`. The novel does not supply the next birth, complete work/care future or the teenagers inside the draft as authenticated destiny. Multiple roles continue rather than reaching one final resolved label.

## 11. Strong alternatives and the qualified conclusion

The romantic-determination reading rightly names how much her life is organized around Itsuki. It becomes false when it denies private art, chosen friendship, particular refusals, different experiences and independent form of reply.

The autonomous-genius reading rightly protects capacity and choice. It becomes false when it makes dependency, effort, hurt and concentrated reasons disappear.

The gender-role critique has real force: the story suspends a gifted woman's new writing around household contentment and lets a man's book occasion return. Its strongest answer is not that this pressure is imaginary. It is that contentment is stated, choice is shown, practical support is requested and the resulting work exceeds the man's valuation of his own.

That answer remains incomplete because sustained allocation and many ordinary costs are unshown. A new book does not erase the work of caring or settle what future choice will mean.

The full monograph therefore grants Nayuta neither limitless obligation nor perfect autonomy. She is an exceptional maker whose reasons change, whose love can overreach, whose effort deserves recognition and whose choices cannot be replaced by another person's theory of what a genius owes.
