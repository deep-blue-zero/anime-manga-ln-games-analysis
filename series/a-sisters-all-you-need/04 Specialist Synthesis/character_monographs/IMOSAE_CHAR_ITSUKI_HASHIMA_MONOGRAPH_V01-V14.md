# Itsuki Hashima: becoming a protagonist among other authors

## 1. The claim to a story of his own

Itsuki's central problem is not simply that he wants a sister. He wants a story in which his particular taste, effort, significance and beloved people can make him a protagonist worthy of admiration. The sister aesthetic is one highly visible medium of that wish; actual care, craft and relationships repeatedly exceed it.

The full series grants him more than a correction of taste. He can make successful work, choose love, accept family truth, acquire technique, reach a particular reader and win an award. It also withholds the inference that these achievements give him authority over other people's lives or complete ethical maturity. The final reader of his work criticizes an author who has already received public recognition. `IM-V14-E025`, `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E046`.

This monograph explains that trajectory. The [operational model](../character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_ITSUKI_HASHIMA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) separately constrains era-specific choices and written voice. The eight topical specialists retain their own full arguments; this home asks why the same person can learn, achieve and remain conspicuously limited.

Its strongest thesis is that self-authorship becomes livable when singular aspiration no longer requires making others supporting roles. Its strongest countercase is that the author never entirely stops doing so. The ending's critique is not a footnote to triumph; it changes what triumph can mean.

## 2. Taste, wound and the error of a total origin story

The V03 retrospective establishes sister-genre preference before Ayane's arrival and rejection. `IM-V03-E096`, `IM-V03-E104`. The rejection reorganizes a preexisting preference through the painful younger-brother position; it does not create all preference from nothing.

This matters for the tempting therapeutic reading. Mother loss, family estrangement, adolescent comparison and rejection can illuminate his defenses, but the source does not establish a single diagnosis which explains every aesthetic choice. A monograph should keep a causal hypothesis open rather than treat it as recovered clinical truth.

The creator-birth moment's 僕が──いや、俺が主人公だ is a chosen self-position. `IM-V03-E114`. He adopts a forceful authored identity after an experience of being peripheral. The sentence is private; its linguistic self-correction is local to this act, not proof that Japanese 俺 is universally more authentic than 僕.

The performance has productive power. It gives a young person something to do with hurt besides wait to be chosen. It also makes a protagonist hierarchy attractive: if he can author the story, perhaps he can secure a status another person denied him.

That hope is not wholly false. Writing actually changes his life and moves readers. Nayuta's encounter with an imperfect early book becomes a decisive part of her own life. `IM-V01-E008`. Yet her response is an independent creation of meaning, not something he planned or owns. The first great consequence of his writing already exceeds the author.

The false manuscript opening trains a parallel interpretive caution. A reader initially encounters a voice/scenario and later learns its authored level. `IM-V01-E001`. Itsuki's preferred story is present in the world as a made object, not automatically the world's truth.

## 3. Comparative worth is not a single ladder

Itsuki can be moved by exceptional work and injured by the comparison it invites. Reading Nayuta's complete debut defeats a dismissive expectation derived from title/cover/genre. `IM-V01-E012`. Recognition of greatness is real; it does not make his subsequent response gracious or easy.

Haruto is commercially stronger while envying Itsuki's singularity; Itsuki envies forms of success Haruto already has. `IM-V01-E019`. Their positions make an objective total ranking impossible. Each treats a different absent good as the measure of inadequacy.

Setsuna creates another version. The illustrator's work can make Itsuki feel that his prose is carried by superior pictures. Itsuki delays collaboration until he feels able to match it; later Setsuna reads the defining novel and fears his pictures might lose to prose. `IM-V03-E027`, `IM-V14-E021`. The relation changes through making rather than discovery of a permanent natural rank.

The parity condition imposed on romance in V03 is therefore especially revealing. Itsuki explicitly wants Nayuta, exclusivity and marriage but makes action depend on equal protagonist status. `IM-V03-E085`. The desired professional good becomes a permission gate for an intimate good already available.

Haruto's response exposes the metric problem. Equality among different creators has no simple objective finishing line. Waiting for it can turn a motivating comparison into a rule that indefinitely denies action.

The hospital/mortality threshold in V06 breaks the gate before the professional hierarchy is solved. `IM-V06-E092`, `IM-V06-E104`. This is meaningful learning: action can recognize another person's actual choice instead of demanding that the protagonist earn an imagined universal rank.

It is not the death of aspiration. He still wants exceptional writing and a protagonist story. The revised relation allows those desires to coexist with love rather than requiring them to be completed first.

## 4. Real attachment interrupts the convenient narrative

Ordinary life is not filler around his creative ambition. The V02 deadline movement makes food, drinks, games, travel and wanting to see friends part of why he wants to continue. `IM-V02-E057`. His imagined isolated artist depends on a dense material/social world.

Chihiro's care matters before Itsuki knows the correct sibling category. The aquarium scene gives ordinary pleasure and warmth; family-home refusal still follows. `IM-V03-E070`, `IM-V03-E071`. Care and avoidance coexist. A later secret should not make the early warmth retrospectively unreal.

Miyako supplies another correction. Her confession requires him to confront a person's desire which his convenient genre story has not adequately recognized. The refusal remains actual and painful, but friendship continues; he can later identify his own tendency to box her into a useful role. `IM-V05-E193`, `IM-V06-E007`.

This does not make him an omniscient reader of people. Recognizing one mistaken story is local growth, not possession of the whole friend's interior. Her later vocation and editor ideal belong to her; the work she does with him cannot contain all her purposes.

The father relation likewise moves from a felt hostile category toward an independent person with a history. A grave visit and intent to face the father are not yet completed repair; apologies and shared ordinary practices arrive later. `IM-V10-E317`, `IM-V11-E241`, `IM-V11-E254`.

These cases show why “he is secretly kind” is too easy. He can genuinely value people and still fail to understand, distribute care fairly or act promptly. Warmth is evidence of attachment; it is not a moral exemption.

## 5. Disclosure breaks an explanation, not the family itself

V09 Chihiro's spoken truth changes shared knowledge. `IM-V09-E255`. Itsuki's V10 exuberant reaction suppresses distress which he cannot initially understand. The later recollection explicitly describes effort to contain tears and perform high spirits. `IM-V10-E371`.

His actual affection for Chihiro does not disappear. What fails is a self-explanation organized around an imagined absence: the assertion that there is no real sister, the relation between fantasy and family, and the idea of having become protagonist by his own power.

This is why the crisis cannot be reduced to discovering he “got what he always wanted.” A literal category and a preferred fictional construction have different functions. Actual siblinghood brings a person, history, responsibilities and competing desires; it cannot be a collectible aesthetic object.

The source also keeps causal limits. The disclosure is consequential, but a full psychological/medical diagnosis is not supplied. Work capacity, intimacy and self-story interact; no single phrase should be turned into a universal theory of blocked creation.

Chihiro later fears that telling truth can make her responsible for another person's collapse. Haruto challenges that burden in a different community setting. `IM-V13-E235`. Itsuki's crisis therefore belongs not only to his monograph. It becomes part of another person's learning about the cost of disclosure, and that cost cannot be erased by celebrating his eventual recovery.

## 6. Technical recovery is real; borrowed sufficiency can still be evasive

V11 restores function. Years of technique can construct readable non-sister work even while a former desire is disconnected. `IM-V11-E320`. The recovery should not be called fake simply because Nayuta rejects the result.

The dispute is about what kind of author she loves and what he still wants, not whether all calculated craft is inherently worthless. Kaiko's V12 argument explicitly denies one absolutely correct creator mode. `IM-V12-E323`. Haruto is the longstanding counterexample to treating craft and sincerity as opposites.

Kaizu's counsel makes exit and ordinary livelihood legitimate. `IM-V11-E131`. A creator does not owe destructive sacrifice to an audience or ideal. That remains a serious anti-thesis to the protagonist's intense ambition.

The problem arises when Itsuki borrows a sufficient-life answer as an anesthetic for a desire which still hurts. The narrator gives plural protagonist lives; he converts that true insight into a diminished “this degree is enough” story for himself. `IM-V11-E296`. Plural worth need not require subtracting his own wish.

Nayuta's challenge has force because it identifies avoidance. It also has ethical danger: a lover's need for a particular author can become a command that he perform her salvific ideal. `IM-V11-E325`. Their later successful route does not prove every harsh demand was necessary or justified.

The mature account must therefore resist both simplifications. Technical recovery is genuine and legitimate as work. Local emotional/artistic integration is another achievement. Choosing one does not require declaring all other modes worthless.

## 7. An addressed work joins capacities without becoming a universal recipe

The V12 private manuscript reaches one particular reader through acquired technique, personal feeling and artistic purpose. Itsuki's body pays for the concentrated work; local success is not proof of a sustainable general method. The V13 discussion recognizes its exceptional quality while admitting that ordinary next work cannot simply reproduce the same state. `IM-V13-E332`.

The work begins as an addressed act, not public property. Miyako's later encounter changes her and leads to a publication proposal with permission and revision. `IM-V13-E540`. The private origin remains true even after public circulation.

This is a consequential new form of authorship. Itsuki can allow a work's relation to accumulate instead of treating one intended use as all it can ever become. Public readers are additional readers, not retroactive proof the intimate work was secretly always made for a market.

Miyako's contribution differs from causing every feeling which produced it. Toki later names grief about not being the relation which generated the defining work, while she distinguishes reading/proposing publication from making him write it. `IM-V14-E014`. The source distributes causation rather than assigning one savior.

Toki's old collaboration remains real. An author's development can need different people at different times without making previous labor worthless. That is a stronger account of ensemble creation than replacing the bad editor with the good friend.

## 8. Parenthood supplies happiness and a new absence

V14 gives Itsuki a valued household, child and continuing work. Seven well-selling standalones do not free him from comparison with the defining novel; a hostile 一発屋 label refers to repeated prestige, not proof that later books failed commercially. `IM-V14-E003`.

Kazuko's contentment creates another asymmetry. She can regard motherhood and fulfilled love as sufficient; he longs for her as creator/rival. The actual scene does not make her desire a disguised failure which he must repair. `IM-V14-E019` and its original extended context.

He refrains from directly demanding that she prefer unfamiliar readers to their child or return to a punishing work world. His held feeling remains real. The distinction between desire and authority is a substantial development from earlier role demands.

His new fantasy addresses her through fiction. She answers by wanting to write, requesting practical household/childcare space and making a sequel of her own. `IM-V14-E020`, `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E024`. E023 does not show his explicit agreement; future production is real while daily allocation remains unshown.

Her sequel can be greater in his judgment and expand the originating work's readership. The couple's artistic relation is not restored through her merely producing what he commands. Another author answers, and the answer can exceed him.

## 9. The award does not finish the ethical story

The public speech is an actual achievement and a chosen exposure of desire. Prepared language gives way to a challenge visually completed by ILL021. `IM-V14-E025`, `IM-V14-E073`. Listeners receive it differently: enthusiasm, recognition, concern and cost coexist.

Kasuka's memory shapes Ashley/Kaizu's worry about intensity; Chihiro hears a history which contains both warmth and painful concealment. Neither reaction cancels the public triumph. They prevent it from becoming one unanimously approved moral law.

The apparent adolescent future initially tempts the reader to accept a completed allocation of lives. It is then revealed as Itsuki's dream-based draft. `IM-V14-E028`, `IM-V14-E042`. The source changes the authority of those futures, not merely a date in a timeline.

Actual readers criticize unauthorized living-person portraits, convenient medicine/technology and the author's self-glorification. `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E045`. These objections differ. Better research would not automatically confer consent; consent would not make a lazy device convincing.

Itsuki responds with amusement at hurt and childish escape. `IM-V14-E044`, `IM-V14-E046`. This defeats the reading of the award as moral graduation. It also prevents a wholly flattering account of “fiction helps everyone understand life.”

The real family contains an unborn younger sister missing from the preferred draft. `IM-V14-E048`. Actual life exceeds the very author whose sister aesthetic appears to anticipate it. His imagination can be rich and selective, valuable and careless.

## 10. Competing readings and the remaining claim

An aesthetic-absolutist reading rightly sees stable taste and artistic ambition, but cannot contain technical non-sister work, chosen love before equality or actual family value. A trauma-defense reading illuminates pain and avoidance, but cannot establish a total causal diagnosis or explain away agency and craft.

A heroic-growth reading rightly names real achievements, but fails at the final criticized draft. An exploitation reading rightly names dependence and representational overreach, but cannot erase affection, skill, transformative reader effects or another creator's independent reply.

The full monograph therefore preserves a difficult result: Itsuki earns a more workable protagonist story without owning the story of everyone around him. Other authors, editors, friends, siblings and readers make that possible and continue to limit him. The ending leaves him capable of further work, not certified to write anyone's destiny.

This is a literary conclusion, not an operational guarantee. For a specific situation, the model must still select era, audience, knowledge and domain. For a future, the source must still be allowed to say that it does not know.
