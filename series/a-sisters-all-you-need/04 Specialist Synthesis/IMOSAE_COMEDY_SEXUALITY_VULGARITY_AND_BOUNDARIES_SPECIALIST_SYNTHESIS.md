# Comedy, sexuality, vulgarity and boundaries

## 0. What does a joke permit, expose or make easy to ignore?

The primary question is **how comic form changes the reader's relation to embarrassment, desire, harm and responsibility**. “It is a joke” names a formal condition, not a complete account of its ethical effect.

This specialist owns obscene and erotic humor, refusal and objectification in their comic forms, profanity as speech action, embarrassment, tolerated overreach and uneven consequence. The relationships study owns the actual directional bonds; the industry study owns practical obligations; the language and visual studies own the broader register and image apparatus. Here the problem is what their framing makes easier to say, feel, forgive or fail to notice.

The completed series supports a double conclusion. Comedy enables a group of creators to expose desires and vulnerabilities that respectable role speech can hide. It also protects some characters' invasions and evasions through talent, affection, rapid payoff or another joke. The later serious and domestic arcs do not cleanse the early form; the ending retains both more explicit choices and renewed discomfort.

Erotic framing involving minors is treated critically and non-graphically. This document does not reproduce explicit passages or such images. Short Japanese anchors concern register, refusal, attribution and wordplay. Newly reopened primary checks include professional coercion, illustrator “input” rhetoric, artistic conversion, Kirara's burden/profanity, V11's insulting serious dispute, V13's 本物 parody and V14's promise/consent frame. In the V08 Ashley episode, only two non-graphic attribution/attitude paragraphs were newly read back; omitted graphic material is not claimed as a fresh full-scene read. The existing frozen reading supplies the earlier full analytical intake.

TF, LF and PF are kept separate from IT. A focalizer's pleasure is not a narrator's universal ethical verdict; narrative affection is not proof that every affected person consents; a hypothetical uncomfortable reader is a counterreading, not a reception dataset.

## 1. The false opening produces distance and retains an appeal

The V01 opening initially asks a reader to inhabit an extreme sibling fantasy, then reveals a manuscript through Toki's response. `IM-V01-E001`. The frame converts apparent immediate narrative into the material of a professional argument.

That reversal has several simultaneous effects. It ridicules Itsuki's excess; grants the editor a correcting position; makes the reader recognize an interpretive mistake; and preserves the intensity and attention produced by the opening itself. Reclassification does not mean the preceding imagery was never supplied.

A sanitized account that calls the opening simply “a parody of sister novels” would lose this double address. The work can mock an excess and use that excess to mark a provocative commercial identity. The object of ridicule can also be part of the attraction.

Conversely, attributing every authored fantasy act to Itsuki's real life would reproduce the frame error. The distinction between a fictional manuscript and actual conduct is indispensable. His father will later make a related mistake when genre content becomes a personality judgment. `IM-V10-E029`.

The correction does not establish that all boundaries in the actual ensemble are intact. Subsequent bodily and professional intrusions are real acts at the mainline level. A frame that makes one extreme scenario fictional cannot make every later violation hypothetical.

The title's censored obsession term, rendered with a visible mask in 妹キ●ガイ variants, is another formal signal. The language is excessive and partly censored while the series repeatedly varies and foregrounds it. This is a performed comic identity, not a clinical category from which to derive all interpersonal psychology.

## 2. Polite form and vulgar content do different work

Nayuta's written voice often joins respectful address and polite endings with blunt desire, ridicule or bodily vulgarity. 先輩 and みゃーさん identify different relations; their persistence does not make the contents conventional professional politeness. `IM-V01-E011`, `IM-V03-E040`.

The mismatch makes a line comic because the form of deference does not regulate what is being proposed or said. It also gives Nayuta a way to act directly while retaining an intimate position toward another person.

This is not a license to model her as uniformly obscene. Her private gift, serious work interests, hidden-effort protest and later domestic choices supply different speech acts and concerns. A coarse gag is one register feature, not her whole attentional field.

Miyako's purportedly worldly persona is exposed at the V03 birthday. Nayuta separates liking the person from admiring the performed sexual experience. `IM-V03-E036`, `IM-V03-E040`. The vulgar labels name a feared social category while the exchange refuses to let that category decide friendship.

The adjacent ownership joke is revealing. Words treat a possible kiss as if it belonged to Itsuki, and he quietly objects to being assigned that ownership. The scene's sweetness and genre convention coexist with a stated limit. It should not be translated into an actual claim that he controls another person's consent.

Profanity can likewise give a worker's frustration a direct form. In V05 Kirara moves from a calm explanation of publication logistics to anger at receiving blame for an author's missed manuscript. `IM-V05-E029`. The insult is a sudden break in role performance whose causal content remains intelligible.

The joke does not make the burden unreal. It lets a professionally responsible person name the injustice of the allocation while still returning to the task. The industry's comedy often lives in that discrepancy between what a worker must do and what the worker can say about it.

## 3. Artistic attention does not authorize access to another body

Setsuna's V02 “input” argument begins with a recognizable creative problem: repeated illustration assignments require variation, and external observation can replenish an artist's resources. The source explicitly describes different degrees of author/editor instruction and illustrator freedom. `IM-V02-E013`–`IM-V02-E014`.

Itsuki can accept the general need for input and reject the specific entitlement inferred from it. The distinction is the episode's central boundary. A valid theory of creative work does not itself make someone else's body available as reference.

The subsequent intrusion involving Chihiro is recorded in the original V02 textual/visual reading and answered by retaliation; the later V04 avoidance is evidence that the event is not simply forgotten. `IM-V02-E100`, `IM-V04-E179`. The present critical account retains the violation and persistence without reproducing explicit source material.

The illustrated framing matters because visual attention can exceed the amount of an act necessary to understand refusal or retaliation. A scene can give the targeted person a response while also turning that person's embarrassment into a spectacle for the reader. Response and objectification can coexist.

Setsuna's artistic capacity is repeatedly real. V06's encounter with Cubism produces a genuine change in artistic ambition; Itsuki and Haruto try to preserve the commercially valued style and then cannot halt the new desire. `IM-V06-E031`–`IM-V06-E032`.

This is not an ethical redemption of the earlier act. The narrator's transformation into パブロ・プリケッソ magnifies the talent myth while sustaining the bodily joke. Creative growth and boundary respect remain different domains.

V14's improved communication, practice and deadline behavior continue that separation. Setsuna can become a more reliable collaborator and retain obsessive comic desire. `IM-V14-E021`. Professional maturation is evidence about tasks actually changed, not a certificate that earlier invasions were harmless.

## 4. Deadline farce exposes coercion and invites indulgence

Itsuki's V02 excuse writing uses real imaginative skill to construct an account others will believe. Miyako improves its plausibility and resists involving Chihiro in a fabricated family crisis. `IM-V02-E052`. The joke lets an author practice craft on professional accountability.

Toki's location tracking claim and the escalating confinement response make the same professional relation coercive. He explicitly claims willingness to cross a line to get a book made; Miyako finds the norm questionable. `IM-V02-E053`–`IM-V02-E054`.

Two opposed readings are possible. One sees comic discipline answering a persistent evader and honoring the people waiting on his pages. Another sees a professional with institutional power treating wrongdoing as lovable competence. The source supplies reasons for both reactions.

The resulting delivery does not decide between them. A useful outcome cannot certify the method. Equally, criticism of the method need not deny the genuine coordination Toki performs during the same period. `IM-V02-E061`.

The later genuine option to refuse adaptation provides a better contrast than an abstract rule. `IM-V04-E039`. Toki can preserve agency when he chooses to, even while representing an employer's desire. His role does not compel every intrusive method shown in farce.

V08's candor about poor conditions and the overloaded reliable illustrator further keeps the criticism concrete. `IM-V08-E006`, `IM-V08-E050`. The series does not lack workplace counterevidence; its warmth can still make readers forgive more than its critique would justify as policy.

## 5. Sexual-knowledge asymmetry can become a joke at the vulnerable person's expense

The V08 Ashley/Chihiro episode uses a knowledgeable adult's position to give misleading information and inappropriate material to a younger person. The frozen reading registers the asymmetry and boundary problem. `IM-V08-E146`–`IM-V08-E152`.

The newly checked non-graphic attitude paragraph explicitly joins Ashley's guilt to transgressive pleasure; the preceding selected speech uses apparent concern as an explanation for concealment. `IM-V08-E151`, `IM-V08-E152`. This is stronger evidence than a purely innocent accident or misunderstanding.

Her actual professional competence in other scenes cannot erase this specific act. Tax administration, friendship and later chosen family show another domain of the same person. They do not turn trust obtained there into an entitlement to manipulate a young person's knowledge.

The episode also shows why embarrassment is an inadequate proxy for consent. A person can be confused, grateful or eager to please while lacking the information needed to understand what has happened. The narratively comic tone does not supply that missing knowledge.

No invented medical or sexual claim from this episode is repeated here as advice. Its analytical use is the character's manipulation and the narrative's uneven response, not the factual truth of the misinformation.

A strong contrary reader may find this part of the series especially complicit: guilt and self-awareness are made interesting without a proportionate repair for the affected person. That judgment cannot be refuted merely by noting that the adult is funny or valued by friends.

## 6. Shared joking can recognize pain rather than dismiss it

Haruto's response to the failed anime shows that humor, games and drinking need not dissolve seriousness. His apparently calculated work has an owner who cares, and his friends encounter pain not settled by sales. `IM-V02-E075`.

The sister's later anger gives a related recognition. She criticizes the adaptation and his conduct; he feels relief in another person expressing what his professional position inhibits. `IM-V04-E116`. The familiar hostile surface can be meaningful without becoming a rule that every hostile act is loving.

V06's post-refusal male-friend exchange uses teasing and genre jokes to accompany hurt. Haruto recognizes Itsuki's limitations, admits spite in a small act and distinguishes the desired heroic protagonist from a standard oblivious romantic-comedy lead. `IM-V06-E019`–`IM-V06-E020`.

This is a productive comic mode because the joke does not deny the refusal or pretend the pain has vanished. It gives the men a form in which companionship can continue while their feelings remain unfinished.

The continuing TRPG then supplies an occasion to meet after awkwardness. `IM-V06-E044`. In-character distance can help friends act on maintenance without asserting that fictional play resolves every real conflict.

The ritual specialist owns the detailed mechanics. Here the case is a necessary counterexample to an exclusively condemnatory theory of comedy. Some joking allows people to see, say or accompany something otherwise difficult to sustain.


## 7. Couplehood gives erotic embarrassment another object

V07's first couple scene puts sincere embrace beside Itsuki's uncertainty about how to end it. He wants to continue, notices the partner's physical condition after collapse, cannot find the right moment, and encounters another person's practical interruption. `IM-V07-E001`.

The humor does not deny the chosen relationship. It lowers the distance between a heroic declaration and the mundane fact that the new boyfriend does not know how to conduct the next minute. Wanting a person is not the same competence as caring well in every embodied situation.

This is a useful alternative to both complete idealization and blanket condemnation of erotic comedy. Bodily pleasure and awkwardness can make adult affection ordinary and particular. The narration can also choose what to linger over, and that choice remains open to criticism rather than being exempt because the couple is mutual.

Nayuta's independent work, feelings and refusals continue after the label. The couple's later crisis is not erased by earlier playful intimacy. A joke about embarrassment does not prove that the lovers already understand the roles they will demand from each other.

The final Haruto/Miyako trip offers a second counterexample to assumed sexual payoff. They share potentially romantic circumstances, actual choice, work and sleep, while the source explicitly denies sexual consummation. `IM-V14-E006`–`IM-V14-E009`.

Narrative framing can invite expectation and then withhold it. The absence is not proof that the relationship is false or that one participant is withholding a debt. It preserves the difference between a situation, a desire and consent to a particular act.

## 8. Vulgarity can intensify a serious dispute rather than turn it into a gag

The V11 final argument is sometimes vulnerable to the series' surrounding comic reputation. It must be read as a damaging disagreement, not dismissed because the lovers use childish insults. Nayuta's rejection of the manuscript, Itsuki's response to the pain of regained function, and their incompatible responsibility claims are substantial. `IM-V11-E320`, `IM-V11-E325`.

Her protest against being explained away as a genius includes the claim of hidden effort. His acknowledgment does not end envy. Their insulting exchange reduces linguistic restraint while exposing rather than resolving the inability to understand each other's present demand. `IM-V11-E331`–`IM-V11-E334`.

The short insult うんこ is recognizable comic vocabulary; in this scene it does not make the emotional event harmless. A written voice can carry a habitual vulgar repertoire into serious pain. The analyst has to identify the scene's stakes, framing and aftermath rather than classify its tone solely from the word.

Likewise ゴミ is an evaluative attack on a manuscript, not a factual material class and not necessarily a total person-description. Its force depends on who says it to which writer. The most trusted reader can wound in a way an anonymous hostile stranger cannot.

The ＡＩ comparison belongs to Nayuta's frightened appraisal of work she experiences as alienated. It is not actual machine authorship. Treating it as a technological fact or a permanent ethical doctrine would turn situated rhetorical violence into external authority.

V12 later refuses to call the functional recovery entirely false. `IM-V12-E028`. That makes the V11 dispute harder, not easier: genuine recovered capacity and a lover-reader's refusal coexist. The later reconciliation does not declare every accusation necessary or correct.

## 9. Harsh maker rhetoric has a limit even when it helps

Kaiko's V12 challenge recognizes different ways of making work and then attacks Itsuki's evasive nonmaking. `IM-V12-E322`. It can be a consequential intervention without becoming a universal moral duty to produce.

The scene also distinguishes sharp speech from a generic humorous persona. A character accustomed to comic extremes can issue a serious accusation. Its usefulness in this case does not require the reader to enjoy or endorse that mode in every situation.

Kaizu's ordinary-work antithesis provides a direct restriction: stopping or choosing a more moderate life can be legitimate. `IM-V11-E131`. The difference between an owned limit and an evasion matters, and other people cannot always know it with certainty.

This is also why “friends are honest with each other” is insufficient as an ethic of harshness. Honesty must still concern a perception that can be mistaken and a hearer who retains choice. Calling a statement blunt does not establish its truth; calling it effective does not establish its fairness.

The comedy study consequently does not group every forceful intervention under a single category of loving teasing. It asks what is known, what is requested, what power the speaker has, how the affected person responds and what the narrative allows to remain afterward.

## 10. 本物 makes authenticity rhetoric comic without abolishing intimacy

V13's handwritten 本物 story uses impossible power totals, a hero named 八まん, a quest for “the genuine,” instant acquisition of true love and arbitrary reversal. It is explicitly an embedded amateur text, not an event in the mainline ensemble or another series. `IM-V13-E340`–`IM-V13-E345`.

The inflated numbers make mastery mechanically available; the final reversals make authenticity a word a narrator can hand out and retract without a developed relation. The parody targets the difference between claiming an absolute good and earning its meaning through form.

The surrounding attribution changes the adults' response. Toki first gives an appalled judgment; after Yoshihiro says Nadeko wrote it, he jumps to an absurd plan involving another label and 『俺ガイル』, then receives a call to calm down. `IM-V13-E345` and following paragraphs.

That reversal exposes private fondness inside professional appraisal. It does not establish an actual crossover publication or a real new Oregairu volume. The quoted title is intertextual speech within this corpus; external source events are not admitted by the joke.

The grandfather and editor also treat the child's cosplay/singing visits as ordinary. Narration points out their lack of surprise. This is a comic norm the work supplies, not evidence that every reader must regard the arrangement as a healthy model for adults and young people.

V14's actual professional feedback to Nadeko is more explicitly calibrated. Adults first inquire into her aim, criticize honestly, and a one-listener pleasure later remains separate from professional quality. `IM-V14-E011`. The later scene gives growth without pretending the earlier professional fondness was an impartial evaluation.

The narration/intertext specialist develops the parody's language and authority level. This document uses it to show comedy criticizing hollow authenticity claims while exposing its own group's selective judgment.

## 11. Literal compliance can be a method of evasion

The V14 Comiket episode fulfills a promised phrase through a deliberately narrow literal reading. `IM-V14-E017`. The joke belongs to the recurring problem of a person pointing to technical fulfillment while the listener expected a different practical act.

This is the same structure as an early author defending a plot event's bare presence or inventing a credible deadline excuse. Comic intelligence can recognize a loophole without honoring an understood agreement.

The professional and intimate forms should not be collapsed. A fan-facing joke has different stakes from deliberately changing another person's romantic condition. They share a mechanism—control of wording—rather than identical harm.

Haruto's adult sexual comment in the same episode receives Miyako's polite キモいです. The rejection of the line coexists with affection. It is a boundary speech act, not evidence that she secretly approves every vulgar proposal because they will later date.

The form matters. Polite surface does not dilute the refusal into playful assent. It can make the refusal efficient and comic precisely because the content is unambiguous.

The later altered report of Miyako's promise is a more harmful use of verbal flexibility. `IM-V14-E008`–`IM-V14-E009`. Narration names Haruto's encouraging and spiteful motives; the false comparative threshold must not replace her actual finite publication condition.

The favorable eventual outcome can make the lie feel affectionate. A mature analysis keeps that response possible and keeps the fact of distortion. A warm story can still be organized around information someone else did not authorize.

## 12. Intoxication changes written conduct; it does not certify the truth

Alcohol is a recurring comic and social resource whose specific ritual mechanics belong elsewhere. Here the important issue is its effect on restraint, embarrassment and a reader's temptation to treat unguarded speech as final truth.

V14 Chihiro becomes intoxicated with a low-strength drink and expresses conflicting wishes about the other pair. The altered written pronunciation/orthography marks a represented state. `IM-V14-E010`. It does not authorize acoustic reconstruction of an actor's timbre or turn one drunken utterance into the person's complete stable preference.

Chihiro sincerely wants people she values to be happy and continues to hurt. The contradiction is not necessarily hypocrisy concealed by sober competence. Several desires can remain active, and an intoxicated state can let them collide in one scene.

Itsuki's aftercare matters because the joke does not end with the written slurring. He provides a place to sleep and tells their father. The care is a concrete response to the state, rather than proof that the distress itself is harmless.

The gaiji/typography route must be respected. Nonstandard dakuten can mark such states and can also occur in author comic signatures. A graphic resemblance does not mean the speaker or authority is the same in every occurrence.

The source/linguistic audit separates these cases and the G005 semantic error. A normalized 吐 cannot be read as a vomiting gag at the draft coach's scolding passage simply because alcohol and bodily comedy recur elsewhere. 原字/ruby governs that particular reading.

## 13. The final novel retains troubling asymmetries

The V14 household jokes and the Nadeko episode continue sexualized framing of young characters. `IM-V14-E004`, `IM-V14-E011`, `IM-V14-E065`. Their critical treatment must be non-graphic, but they cannot be omitted from an ethical account because the adults now have families.

One consequence is that “the series matures from obscenity to wholesome adulthood” fails as a historical claim. More explicit professional and relational choices appear alongside renewed objectification. Growth is differentiated by character and domain; formal habits also persist.

The illustration in the Nadeko episode can amplify attention beyond what the story's failure/one-listener lesson needs. The visual specialist owns the asset and framing analysis; this document retains the ethical problem of the surplus attention.

Kaiko and Manaka's adult relationship provides another sexual/intimate register, with privacy, work and housing negotiation. `IM-V14-E016`. Its existence expands the household field without making every scene involving women's bodies consensual or reciprocal.

The effect is therefore not a uniform moral position on sexuality. Adult affection, aggressive flirting, harmful information asymmetry, bodily spectacle, embarrassment and polite refusal have different structures. One general “sex-positive” or “anti-sex” verdict would miss the distinctions the text permits and the contradictions it sustains.


## 14. A joke can cross a boundary the speaker does not know exists

The final V09 TRPG session makes this problem concrete. Nayuta's game-only sister remark becomes the trigger for Chihiro's real-family declaration. `IM-V09-E255`. The source does not give Nayuta prior confirmed knowledge of the concealed identity.

The effect therefore cannot be reduced either to malicious deliberate outing or to a harmless line because the speaker did not know. A joke can strike accumulated private vulnerability through a category the speaker treats as fictional.

The group responses are distributed. Ashley has knowledge and a comic reaction; Toki has suspicion and anxiety; the others are initially bewildered. V10 supplies the explicit distinctions. `IM-V10-E029`. The reader must not make that later information universally available beforehand.

The scene also qualifies the claim that games always provide safety. In-character space had enabled an identity that could not be expressed openly. The boundary between game and ordinary life then fails, with lasting consequences for relationships and writing.

The same mechanism recurs at a different level in the final future draft. Itsuki may regard a portrayal as funny or dream-derived while a real referent experiences it as injurious. Intent, genre and epistemic classification do not decide another person's response.

## 15. The award winner's final joke is an accountability failure

The actual frame after 青い小鳥たち makes Chihiro the reader who can object to living-person fiction, inadequate permission and careless research. `IM-V14-E042`–`IM-V14-E045`. Itsuki's success is not the last word.

Nadeko's actual anger is met in part by his enjoyment of her hurt face. His dream explanation tries to give the portrayal an origin that would reduce responsibility. `IM-V14-E044`. The source makes that defense contestable rather than automatically accepting “a dream made me do it.”

A toddler's enthusiastic response is likewise inadequate evidence of permission for the elaborate future identity written into the draft. `IM-V14-E043`. Familiarity with a child does not reveal the child's future private life.

The final childish exit has comic energy, but it is also a refusal to sustain criticism. `IM-V14-E046`. This is an important ending for the comedy specialist: the story can complete an artistic triumph and retain a protagonist whose coping repertoire includes evasion.

Chihiro's private ambition and Kazuko's corrective pregnancy then give actual people information and futures his manuscript did not govern. `IM-V14-E047`–`IM-V14-E049`. Another joke does not put them back inside his settled account.

This framing restricts an indulgent thesis that a talented creator's excess is justified by the joy it produces. The work itself offers an alternative reader who says the new work has costs. It also restricts a total condemning thesis, because the earlier achievement, care and chosen relationships remain real.

## 16. Paratextual joking has another authority

Author afterwords and the comic biography can use persona jokes, exaggerated self-description, provocation and qualifications. `IM-V14-E026`, `IM-V14-E050`, `IM-V14-E051`. Their speaker and evidential class differ from a character's dialogue.

An authorial statement of taste may illuminate why some formal habits recur. It cannot be used to make every narrator statement a sincere author belief, predict every character's future, or establish all readers' responses.

The afterword's claim that the apparent extra story is actually part of the main story supports treating its frame as structurally important. It does not change the internal invented adolescent events into actual canon. The joke about a misleading introduction is itself part of how the book handles a reader's expectations.

Advertising and colophon data have still another responsibility. Publication identity and marketed tone can be documented without treating the promotional address as moral adjudication. The visual/paratext home keeps those routes separate.

## 18. Strong contrary readings

### 18.1 Critical responses may be alibis for repeated spectacle

The strongest objection is that a refusal, punch, skeptical line or later avoidance supplies just enough resistance to let the series continue enjoying the violation. If the form lingers over a vulnerable body and then resolves the scene through comic retaliation, agency can become a framing convenience.

The available counterevidence is concrete persistence, not complete absolution: Chihiro avoids Setsuna, Miyako questions coercion, Ashley's improper pleasure is attributed, and Chihiro criticizes the final manuscript. Those details prevent an account of wholly invisible discomfort. They do not prove proportionate consequence or make the gaze neutral.

### 18.2 Ethical criticism can flatten stylized adult play

An exclusively condemnatory reading might treat direct flirtation, profanity, affectionate embarrassment and invasive acts as identical. That would erase chosen adult relations, effective refusal, companionship and the series' specific written voices.

The study resists that flattening through the V07 couple awkwardness, V06 maintenance ritual, Kirara's justified burden complaint and Miyako's clear polite boundary. These are different structures. Preserving their pleasures does not require endorsing every neighboring gag.

### 18.3 Good outcomes can retrospectively authorize bad methods

The book appears, the couple dates, the family reconciles, the artist gains fame. Those outcomes make earlier farce easy to forgive. The contrary reading is strongest when it asks whether the same method would still seem acceptable without the favorable result.

The source gives enough alternative choices to sustain that test: adaptation refusal can be genuine, a caregiver can be asked directly, a private reader can authorize circulation and a partner can request a practical division of work. The morally better method is not always unavailable within this world.

### 18.4 “Satire” can become a blanket escape from evaluation

The novels are plainly self-conscious and exaggerated. That fact cannot explain away every objectification or information abuse. Satire has an object and a direction; the analyst has to identify who is made ridiculous, who bears the cost and what remains valued.

The ending's living-person critique is especially valuable because it directs evaluation back at the successful creator. The protagonist cannot be exempt merely because the surrounding book knows he is childish.

## 19. Final disposition

The full-series record rejects both a sanitized maturation narrative and a single verdict that every obscene joke is the same harm. Comedy is a working language of this ensemble: it exposes guarded feeling, gives speech to labor resentment, lets friends remain close, performs desire and makes the creator's self-image vulnerable.

It also grants selective indulgence. Talent, professional necessity, affection, genre convention and successful outcome repeatedly soften boundary violations. The late family form keeps some of those habits while supplying clearer refusals and an unclosed criticism of the protagonist.

The mature conclusion is therefore double: **comic intimacy can make other people more perceptible, and comic permission can make their independence easier to ignore**. The actual final manuscript argument makes that instability part of the ending rather than a problem the award resolves.

This preserves earlier scene-specific judgments and narrows any total claim that vulgarity is either intrinsically liberating or intrinsically dismissive. The claim ledger retains formal dispositions; the neighboring specialist homes retain their own primary problems. Operational models must generate or abstain according to era, relationship and evidence, never equate a character's repeated gag with permission to enact it in every scenario.
