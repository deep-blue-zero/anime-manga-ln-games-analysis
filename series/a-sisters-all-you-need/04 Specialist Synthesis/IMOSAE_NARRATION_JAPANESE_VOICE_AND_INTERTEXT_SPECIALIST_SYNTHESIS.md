# Narration, Japanese written voice and intertext

## 0. Who owns a sentence, and what may a reader know from it?

The primary question is **how the series distributes speech, perspective and interpretive authority among people, invented works and the book's own publishing voices**. A word does not have the same evidential force because it is true of the source text wherever it occurs. Speaker, addressee, mode, developmental state and fiction level determine what it can establish.

This specialist owns focalization, withholding and reclassification, written idiolect/register, speech acts, ambiguity and literalism, ruby/gaiji-sensitive terms and intertextual function. The other topical homes interpret the work, intimacy, identity, comedy and rituals represented through these forms. The visual home owns the full asset/edition apparatus; here image-borne language matters where it changes what a sentence actually says.

The argument is that IMOSAE makes reading another person an activity with real successes and recurring limits. It opens several private perspectives, lets characters misclassify familiar people, makes fiction capable of illuminating and appropriating life, and ends by requiring the reader to reclassify an apparent future. Its voices remain recognizable through changes of context precisely because recognition cannot depend on one fixed catchphrase or one pronoun.

The evidence horizon is written Japanese V01–V14. No acoustic timbre, pitch, actor delivery or adaptation-only voice is reconstructed from prose. Narration may describe a delivery; that is a textual description, not a recording. Original XHTML/base/ruby checks and the current identity/normalization/attribution errata govern contested readings.

A coordinate/fingerprint resolves a passage. It does not decide who is speaking, whether the line is spoken, or whether an embedded event occurs in the actual continuity. The fresh E023 correction is an instructive example: its request is real, but the old register's added “Itsuki response” is not explicitly shown.

## 1. The corpus contains several speaking authorities

| Level or mode | What is directly established | What is not automatically established |
|---|---|---|
| Actual scene dialogue | A character says a line to an identified or inferable hearer | The speaker believes every word, tells the truth, or has omniscient information |
| Attributed thought/private appraisal | The source grants this figure an interior or a focal judgment | Another character knows it; the judgment is neutral fact |
| Narrator exposition/evaluation | The narration represents an explanation, qualification or evaluative position | A current external industry rule or a universally binding ethic |
| Character-authored novel, game role or script | A represented creator/text says or makes this within its level | Literal autobiography or an actual event in the ensemble |
| Itsuki's dream-based future draft | The published book gives a constructed possible world and its private voices | The actual children reach those ages, careers or relationships |
| Stage play within that draft | Another performance/text is made inside the constructed future | Actual Sora's performance or a new mainline family fact |
| Author/illustrator afterword | A named publishing participant makes a compositional or personal statement | Character omniscience, future occurrence or an independently verified reception/law statistic |
| Promotion/colophon | Edition identity or an advertisement is present | Mainline narrative expansion or ethical approval |

The actual book is one publication containing these levels, not one flat database of equally actual events. Its original package class for a chapter stays unchanged even when close reading identifies embedded authority inside it.

A compact map makes the ending's critical route visible:

~~~mermaid

  D --> P["Sora's represented play and TRPG origins"]

  A -. "does not certify occurrence" .-> D
~~~

The route from the actual readers back to the draft is decisive. They are not simply another generation within a verified future; they are the people who can judge the author's present treatment of them.

## 2. Withholding does not make the early scene unreal

The opening manuscript reveal shows that a reader can initially misassign an authored scenario to the immediate world. `IM-V01-E001`. The correction changes authority and speaker relation while preserving the text's material presence and affective force.

Chihiro's concealed identity is another kind of withholding. The cooking, care, speech and desire for belonging are actual before Itsuki learns the secret. The disclosure reclassifies an existing person and relation, rather than revealing that every earlier act was fictitious. `IM-V01-E005`, `IM-V09-E255`.

V10 supplies the knowledge distribution the shocked scene alone did not fully explain: Ashley knows; Toki suspects; the others lack confirmation. `IM-V10-E029`. A reader who now knows cannot silently give each prior participant the same information.

The paired origin chapters also alter how familiar roles are read. V03 gives the making of the writer's self; V10 gives the making of the family/disguise arrangement through several people, including perspectives Itsuki could not have supplied. `IM-V03-E096`, `IM-V03-E104`, `IM-V03-E114`, `IM-V10-E004`, `IM-V10-E012`.

The latter expansion corrects his total image of a cold father without invalidating the earlier child's experience. Focal judgment and later widened knowledge coexist. That structure is essential to a retrospective synthesis that preserves prospective freezes rather than rewriting them.

The V14 future reveal is more extensive. A long sequence supplies adolescent interiorities and narrative causality, then returns to a toddler and the author's manuscript. `IM-V14-E028`–`IM-V14-E042`. Everything may be coherent at the draft level while remaining unverified at the actual one.

This distinguishes withholding from a license to ignore text. The source's later reclassification must govern actual biography; the earlier represented scenes still matter as Itsuki's imaginative construction and as formal experience for the external reader.

## 3. The narrator opens private judgments without making them universal verdicts

Miyako's V05 confession separates visibly intense feeling from an unexpectedly clear inner appraisal. She interprets Itsuki's confusion, recognizes that he really missed her attraction and names what her earlier efforts meant. `IM-V05-E185`.

The movement between direct speech, narrated private thought and inference from his face makes her more than the object of his surprised gaze. It also preserves a distinction: her conclusion about his heart is a focal appraisal supported by the scene, not a general ability to read every person.

Itsuki later recognizes that his own convenient story had pressed her into a familiar category. `IM-V06-E007`. The phrase describes a professional novelist who can make a plausible account and use it to avoid seeing someone accurately. Narrative skill has a wrong interpersonal application as well as a useful artistic one.

Toki's V07 private pride offers a different contrast. The source directly grants pleasure in Itsuki's growth and says he withholds the statement because telling him would encourage vanity. His spoken closing line is a familiar plain-style だな. `IM-V07-E043`.

The private appreciation does not make Itsuki know it. The familiar ending also prevents a model from assigning Toki uniformly distant polite speech merely because he is an editor. Role, history and addressee are active constraints.

V14 Haruto evaluates Miyako's accomplished editor state and thinks her intuitive fit may be less suitable to his own analytic method. `IM-V14-E008`. That thought is not a statement she has heard or a global verdict that she cannot work with any analytic writer.

The industry dispute likewise gives Gōdo and Shirogamine serious conflicting explanations. `IM-V14-E013`. Treating one participant's forceful exposition as the narrator's final theorem would erase the other's sustained counterargument.

The work does sometimes give narrator evaluations with apparent authority, including warm/strict parent descriptions or sweeping professional explanations. Those can be critically tested against the represented acts. Narrative evaluation is evidence of framing; it does not make ethical disagreement impossible.

## 4. Quotation marks alone cannot classify spoken and internal language

Itsuki's adolescent declaration 僕が──いや、俺が主人公だ is unquoted, following inward conviction and the opening of the computer. The next sentence names the writer's birth. `IM-V03-E114`. The source does not show him announcing it to a present interlocutor.

In V14, however, the unquoted dash-led wish 小説を書きたいので follows Kazuko's household request and is attributed through そう言って. `IM-V14-E023`. It is speech despite the lack of an ordinary new pair of quotation marks around that paragraph.

The contrast is diagnostically important. A mechanical “quotes = speech, dash = thought” classifier would fail on one of the series' most consequential acts of agency. Attribution requires the surrounding syntax and action.

Long public speech can also continue across paragraphs without repeating an opening quote at every line. The award sequence includes such continuation. `IM-V14-E025`. A paragraph's isolated surface cannot always identify a new narrator.

TRPG speaker labels add another layer. 月子, 千, ミヤコ, デスマスク and GM indicate role/performance position, not necessarily the same linguistic baseline as the real player. `IM-V01-E037`, `IM-V04-E135`, `IM-V06-E044`.

Itsuki's momentary older-brother phrasing within a female role is evidence of a habit crossing a performance boundary, not proof that every role statement is literal self-identification. Likewise Chihiro's game sisterhood has real affective meaning while its actual identity cannot be inferred simply from every chosen character.

The source-routing and model protocols therefore need separate fields for actual speaker, represented role, mode, addressee, knowledge and continuity level. A single “character said” tag is too coarse for this corpus.


## 5. Recognizable voices are configurations, not verbal stamps

Itsuki's theatricality depends on an audience and a problem. His early 俺, bombast, familiar insults and insistence on protagonist status make embarrassment and comparative insecurity livable; they also give friends something to answer. The creator-birth self-correction from 僕 to 俺 is a local act of self-authorship, not a dictionary proof that one pronoun is more truthful. `IM-V03-E114`.

When an award platform first elicits a prepared public voice and then a personal challenge, the change is an action: he abandons a manageable script and exposes desire to a large audience. `IM-V14-E025`. It does not follow that private vulnerability has finally become transparent to everybody. The audience contains different recipients and concerns; the narration supplies regret and qualification which the recipients need not possess.

This limits both “the bombast is all a mask” and “the bombast is his whole personality.” He can believe in an aesthetic, use it defensively and hurt another person through it at the same time. The final draft dispute preserves the familiar pleasure of a provocative author while exposing poor research and the misuse of living people. `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E045`. Recognition of his voice must leave room for such unflattering continuity.

Nayuta's 私 and polite endings coexist with extreme content, terse composure, technical enthusiasm and emotional appeal. Politeness is not deference in the sense of surrender. Her affectionate みゃーさん and chosen お姉ちゃん make a relation with Miyako audible without creating legal siblinghood or routing her whole affect through Itsuki. `IM-V03-E040`.

The same scene answers Miyako's insecurity through deliberately undignified categories, then moves into unmistakable affection. Miyako's あたし, rapid objection and unguarded repetition differ from Nayuta's restrained permission. Their dissimilar delivery helps the reassurance land: one voice can hold a proposition still while the other loses composure. A flattened shared “cute girl” register loses the relation's structure.

Nayuta's final 伊月さん, rather than mechanically repeated 先輩, occurs in the household request. `IM-V14-E023`. It marks an attested current form of address, not proof that all earlier career-origin language disappears from married life. The desire to write is stated through an ordinary reason clause; the grammar makes it a practical request as well as an artistic turning point. A grand declaration would not necessarily be more faithful.

Miyako's ordinary colloquial voice is ordinary only in relation to more stylized peers. It is capable of professional politeness, embarrassed self-correction, jealous private ranking, affectionate outburst, firm refusal and controlled commitment. The V13 question to 不破さん about what authors want from their 担当編集者 is professional inquiry delivered inside a scene Haruto can initially misread as romantic. `IM-V13-E455`. Her あたし and です coexist; self-reference alone does not select the level of formality.

This is not a joke about her inability to speak professionally. She is asking a real question which matters to her emerging craft. The comic mismatch depends on Haruto's expectation, not the reader's right to ignore her vocation. The same distinction controls V14: her promise concerns publication of Itsuki's book which she edits, irrespective of whether it surpasses the prior one. `IM-V14-E008`. Haruto privately judges her already accomplished; that is not a judgment she hears word for word.

Haruto's early unheard wish to have been a genius follows narrator exposition that explicitly qualifies the artist/craftsman binary. `IM-V01-E019`. The source's general account and his individual dissatisfaction occupy adjoining paragraphs but are not the same authority. His オレ can be socially smooth, privately wounded or professionally analytic.

In V14 the public judging voice uses 私 and polite correction of a title whose 聖霊騎士 is read シュヴァリエ; backstage オレ still appears with polite language. `IM-V14-E002`. There is no simple rule that 私 always means professional truth while オレ means candid intimacy. Pronoun, sentence ending, hearer and speech act must be tracked together.

His subsequent LINE report to Itsuki and Chihiro knowingly changes Miyako's actual condition. `IM-V14-E009`. A familiar message can aim to spur work and cause annoyance at once. The narration explicitly names both motives; recognizable social fluency is not a certificate of honest content. Miyako is not the recipient of this false report.

Chihiro's careful language carries competence before it carries a revelation. Addressing Haruto as 不破さん and teasing his research sword uses polite inquiry to notice an amusing contradiction in an adult's self-presentation. `IM-V11-E058`. This is not reducible to timid service.

Her earlier 僕 and 兄さん operate within the inhabited younger-brother presentation, while the V09 disclosure changes the first-person position to 私. `IM-V09-E255`. The exact contextual extension is `V09|chapter:クロニカクロニクル最終章 ～そして爆発へ～|spine:070|xhtml:OEBPS/Text/p-0070.xhtml.xhtml|p:0010|fp:c01a64cbeec7` through `p:0012|fp:bbe726c1bf3a`. The first 私 is unquoted interior positioning; the next 私 is spoken assertion. Neither should be silently normalized into one undifferentiated English “I.”

The retrospective concealment-origin self-correction runs in the opposite direction, 私 toward 僕, as a child asks for family. `IM-V10-E012`. Together these transitions support a history of audience-sensitive identity work, not a universal psychological rule attached to the pronouns. The V08 paired self-reference belongs to the intervening pressure. `IM-V08-E183`.

V14 intoxication temporarily distorts Chihiro's orthography and self-control. `IM-V14-E010`. Repeated complaints, slurred forms and contradictory wishes are evidence of this state. Her final direct criticism of the draft is sober, technically observant and stronger than the earlier service persona might predict. `IM-V14-E043`. Modeling every late line as shy, or every late line as drunkenly aggressive, would each generalize the wrong sample.

Toki's directness is likewise relational. The V07 contrast between unspoken gratitude and casual spoken だな shows an editor protecting himself from an author he expects to become conceited. `IM-V07-E043`. The narrator supplies the withheld praise. The dialogue alone could look merely ungenerous; treating the private passage as a spoken compliment would make the exchange falsely tender.

His rough commands, professional vocabulary, drunken excess and wounded pride coexist with judgments about process and a later recognition that his contribution may have limits. `IM-V10-E150`, `IM-V13-E338`, `IM-V14-E003`. A reconstruction should neither erase the cost of intimidation nor convert every direct sentence into contempt. The degree of pressure, its object and the known history of the author are decisive.

These configurations are elaborated operationally in the character models. This specialist's linguistic conclusion is narrower: a voice is the patterned selection among available resources under relationship and state constraints.

## 6. Relationship terms can preserve a hierarchy while changing its use

先輩 does not settle Nayuta's agency. It can retain the career-origin relation while the speaker refuses, competes, teaches, reassures or asks. The term's persistence does not make the intimate relation identical to an employment hierarchy. Conversely, a married name does not erase the artistic rivalry. `IM-V01-E008`, `IM-V06-E104`, `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E024`.

カニ公 is recognizable familiar deformation from Itsuki, but its effect depends on what follows and what the recipient can answer. Familiar insulting address does not authorize limitless humiliation. Much of their important conflict uses recognizable intimacy while producing real hurt. `IM-V11-E320`, `IM-V11-E325`.

兄さん persists through Chihiro's reclassification. The address gives continuity to the relation rather than proving that every aspect of her self-description remains unchanged. Her felt family bond is not created from nothing on the day Itsuki learns a new category. `IM-V09-E255`, `IM-V10-E029`, `IM-V11-E241`.

Miyako's 不破さん and Chihiro's more personal 晴彦さん must not be exchanged casually. The former can position professional/social inquiry, the latter carries Chihiro's particular history and attraction. In V14 the personal address remains even when reciprocity is absent. `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V14-E005`, `IM-V14-E010`. Naming intimacy is evidence of the speaker's relation, not a guarantee of the addressee's love.

センセー and ～ッス make Setsuna's creator relation recognizable, but a tic is not a substitute for artistic attention. Earlier boundary violations, later practice, improved delivery and continuing obsession must remain separately legible. `IM-V06-E031`, `IM-V14-E021`. Original V01 narration calls the sixteen-year-old a 少年 and uses 彼; the V14 introduction again uses 彼 at22. No later female reclassification is established. Neither self-reference nor changed appearance should be used to invent one.

Ashley’s ワタシ and controlled directive language differ from Itsuki's theatrical shouting. The charitable-excuse exchange with Chihiro tests this contrast: Chihiro can respond politely and firmly to a domineering adult. `IM-V04-E128`. This is evidence against an automatic “politeness equals yielding” model.

Names are their own source problem. The live correction layer routes 白川京 to Miyako, 相生初 to Ui, 神戸聖 to Gōdo Satoshi, and the other verified identities through original ruby. A plausible romanization derived from the visible kanji is not sufficient. Name lookup, character identity and written register must share this correction layer rather than maintaining rival alias tables.

## 7. Literal truth can misdirect without becoming a lie

The series repeatedly separates what a sentence literally permits from what a listener reasonably expects. The V11 sister-game packaging can evoke its creator's wish while its actual rules work through cards, ranks and information. `IM-V11-E064`. A sister label in a game does not identify an actual biological sibling.

The associated publication play with an obi is a more ethically pointed version: formally defensible wording may still invite the wrong expectation. This is a case of pragmatic implication and packaging, not a license to classify every misleading expression as equally innocent. The prospective V11 reading and fiction-within-fiction ledger retain the full local route.

The distinction becomes sharper beside Haruto's V14 report to Itsuki and Chihiro. That message is knowingly false about Miyako's condition, according to the narration's explicit explanation. `IM-V14-E009`. Spurring work and causing annoyance are its named motives; neither makes the reported condition accurate.

Miyako's finite publication condition is different again. She names an action whose completion will release the waiting, including the possibility that the new book does not surpass the former one. The explicit 結果的に超えても超えられなくても prevents the promise from silently becoming an indefinite genius test. `IM-V14-E008`.

Nayuta's private book for Miyako and later private-to-public work also distinguish a manuscript's existence from a permission to circulate it. `IM-V03-E034`, `IM-V13-E540`. Saying a work is valuable does not by itself authorize publication or make its represented persons public property.

Finally, toddler approval is neither a mature ethical evaluation nor consent from every living model for a future draft. `IM-V14-E043`. The returned-present discussion makes this category error explicit. The same sentence “Sora liked it” can be true as a report of a response and inadequate as an answer to Chihiro's concern.

The narration therefore asks a reader to hold several classifications at once: literal proposition, intended speech act, hearer's inference, private motive and downstream permission. Collapsing them loses the source's most exact comedy and some of its most serious conflicts.


## 8. A vocabulary of making also distributes value

The early 芸術家 / 職人 distinction is narrated as a useful tendency and explicitly qualified, including mixtures and institutional preference for reliable output. `IM-V01-E019`. Haruto's unheard disappointment immediately resists the idea that a practical classification can resolve a creator's self-worth.

V12's explicit rejection of 絶対的に正しいただ一つの答え is Kaiko's speech, with her formal わたくし and carefully balanced respect for different ways of making. Nayuta answers with a passionate definition of what a real fan owes a beloved author. `IM-V12-E323`. Their voices stage disagreement about love and criticism; Kaiko is neither an omniscient theorem nor merely a mouthpiece for permissive relativism.

The sequencing matters. Kaiko first allows Nayuta's stance to exist, then changes expression and prepares a sharper objection. A quotation cut after “different stances are fine” would lose the conflict. Conversely, Nayuta's intensity does not prove that every creator should be corrected back toward a fan's favorite mode.

主人公, メインヒロイン, 同業者 and 対等 are likewise relational vocabulary. They can name aspiration, recognition or a gate to affection. Itsuki's equality condition turns a meaningful professional comparison into an obstacle to accepting love. `IM-V03-E085`. Later action revises that condition without erasing the desire to become the protagonist of an admirable story. `IM-V06-E092`, `IM-V06-E104`.

Miyako can hear 同業者 as a threshold of belonging while Itsuki hears the loss of specialness within the same general professional world. A translation that selects one universally favorable gloss for “colleague” cannot preserve both responses. The local focalizer and comparison matter.

景色 begins as part of Nayuta's fiction and becomes language for a form of experience she wants to share. The wish to see another person's world through effort is not identical to the ability to write exceptionally well. `IM-V06-E033`, `IM-V06-E068`. Her talent does not make the experiential wish empty; her wish does not prove she lacked all earlier effort.

担当 and 担当編集者 name an assignment and a relation rather than an interchangeable skill package. Miyako's V13 inquiry, Toki's uncertainty about his future contribution and the V14 privately judged fit with different writers make this concrete. `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V13-E338`, `IM-V14-E008`. The same formal role can be fulfilled through different combinations of analysis, pressure, listening and companionship.

生モノ in the final criticism is especially resistant to a purely literal gloss. Chihiro is identifying the ethical sensitivity of using living people in fiction. `IM-V14-E043`. A reader need not import an entire external fan-practice taxonomy to understand the scene's own problem: recognizable persons have not all consented to these portraits, and the author's amused response does not answer that concern.

ないものねだり and これさえあればいい name an ending which has to be heard through its remaining conditions. `IM-V14-E049`. What seems sufficient from one state can coexist with longing for something absent. The construction さえ can make an imagined possession sound like the whole solution, but the fourteen-volume narrative repeatedly tests what such sufficiency excludes.

This is a lexical argument about the source's repeated uses, not an exhaustive linguistic theory of these expressions outside the novels. Their interpretive weight comes from juxtaposition, changing speakers and outcomes.

## 9. Fiction transforms life; it can also appropriate it

In V02, bodily experience and a family rebuke become resources for fictional affect and dialogue. `IM-V02-E017`, `IM-V02-E034`. Transformation need not be transparent autobiography. A character can learn from a lived situation and write something with a different plot, relation and emotional use.

The early false opening, private gifts, commercially packaged novels, manga and anime scripts, TRPG improvisation, parody and the final draft therefore require different authorship tags. “Fiction within fiction” is a useful umbrella, but it is not an adequate description of every work's permission, audience or continuity.

Nayuta's work can change a reader beyond the creator's immediate design. Takashina's encounter with 銀色景色 helps shift attention from his assigned self toward other lives. `IM-V12-E345`. This prevents a purely intention-centered model of literary effect. An author cannot know or own every important use another person makes of a book.

The V13 本物 parody, presented under Yoshihiro Kiso's name, exploits attribution before its actual origin is understood. `IM-V13-E345`. Toki's response changes when the author attribution changes. The episode tests prestige, imitation and the audience's confidence, while its publishing gag remains an in-universe gag. It does not establish an actual new external Oregairu publication.

The V14 明日の君さえいればいい。 addresses a particular reader through an unusual fantasy rather than a direct message delivered as a letter. `IM-V14-E020`, `IM-V14-E022`. Nayuta's sequel answers by making her own work. Its later internal setting and plural forms of love are properties of that fantasy's world. `IM-V14-E024`.

The distinction is important at two scales. First, Itsuki's novel and Nayuta's sequel do not make their fantastic acts mainline biography. Second, her creative response is not reducible to a transparent transcription of what her husband wanted. Its success can increase the original's readership and leave him admiring and envying a work he considers greater than his own.

The dream-based 青い小鳥たち is more ethically unstable. Its actual author allocates privileged homes, adolescent wounds, performed personae, medical difficulties and public successes to recognizable future versions of living people. `IM-V14-E028` through `IM-V14-E041`. Its internally accessible thoughts are not evidence that the actual toddlers will have those thoughts.

The return reveals that the future was a text subject to criticism. `IM-V14-E042`. That revelation does not erase the embedded story's literary interest, including the collective play's transformation of solitary creator defiance. It relocates its authority. The actual reader can now ask whether its author researched, sought consent or installed himself as too convenient a source of solutions.

Chihiro's critique distinguishes craft weakness from the misuse of living people and from the author's enjoyment of others' discomfort. `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E044`, `IM-V14-E045`. These are related failures but not one charge. A technically improved draft would not automatically solve permission; permission would not make a lazy medical device convincing.

The apparent future's prose can thus be emotionally effective while its author's choices are criticized. A reading that must either accept everything as prophecy or dismiss the entire embedded story as meaningless misses the point of the return.

## 10. Intertext is a situated action, not an imported canon

Games and genre references let speakers borrow roles, manipulate expectation, compare craft and display shared knowledge. The ritual specialist owns the specific social mechanisms. Here the crucial issue is what authority a reference brings.

An invoked tabletop role can give Chihiro an indirect channel for a real desire. `IM-V09-E255`. It cannot prove that every in-game line is an explicit confession to its actual audience. The author afterword's explanation of the design is PF, separately useful as a claim about composition.

A familiar protagonist template can help Itsuki name an aspiration and can encourage him to misclassify people as roles. `IM-V06-E007`. The series does not require all genre vocabulary to be false in order to criticize a convenient story. The problem is the template's explanatory loss.

The adapted V04 drama scenario is discussed through its numbered-volume representation and introductory production notice. `IM-V04-E159`. That notice explicitly identifies a prose rewrite with differences from the audio. The source-bound specialist does not silently admit an unconsulted audio performance. A later drama-study task would need its own source lock, credits, continuity and voice evidence.

Industry references, game explanations and author anecdotes are historical represented claims at publication. They are not current rulebooks, tax advice, product recommendations or independently verified industry statistics. The same boundary applies to literal named external works: mentioning one does not import all its plots into this corpus.

The final draft's Grancrest session is a represented creative mechanism inside the draft. `IM-V14-E032`. Four expert players contribute, and the GM's deliberate facilitation matters. It is not documentary proof of actual Sora's adolescence or plagiarism. The draft's own explanation, not external system lore, supports this particular argument.

Intertextual competence is unevenly distributed. Some references bond insiders while confusing or excluding another participant; an explanation can then change the membership boundary. A reference is therefore an act between people, not just an item to recognize on a list.

## 11. Typography, ruby and gaiji alter what is recoverable

Ruby preserves intended readings of names and genre terms. 聖霊騎士 / シュヴァリエ in Haruto's public correction makes the printed title and the spoken reading two coordinated pieces of evidence. `IM-V14-E002`. The visible kanji alone would invite a different title pronunciation.

Name ruby likewise corrects plausible but wrong romanizations. Miyako, Ui and Gōdo must remain stable across character retrieval, evidence tables and final prose. The correction layer is analytical metadata; it does not authorize rewriting the frozen normalized source release.

Gaiji pose a different problem. At `V14|chapter:青い小鳥たち|spine:042|xhtml:text/part0041.html|p:0166|fp:f825ffa55e0b`, G005's frozen replacement 吐 is analytically wrong for the original scolding glyph/ruby しか. The current interpretation must use “scold,” retain the release identity and expose the exception. A matching hash certifies the asset, not the old replacement's meaning.

G063's LR symbol remains placeholder-sensitive rather than a confidently invented character. G004's intoxication-associated typography and G054's afterword manner belong to different speakers and states. Ornament IDs can mark a structural break without representing words spoken by a character.

Image-only language creates the converse hazard: a prose extraction can omit something indispensable. V14's prose ends a suspended challenge at 全員まとめて──; ILL021 supplies かかってこい and 完. `IM-V14-E073`. The image is a source-bearing continuation, not decoration which can be discarded without changing the ending.

The later 続かない at the draft's close and actual present's （完） create additional closure levels. `IM-V14-E041`, `IM-V14-E049`. They do not all close the same continuity. A one-field “final line” index would lose the book's structure.

The original-source readback helpers intentionally report missing base text when a paragraph is an illustration or gaiji placeholder. Such a mismatch is not by itself a normalization defect. Original XHTML, asset class, ruby and normalized annotation must be read together. Equally, a whitespace match does not certify speech attribution.

## 12. Strong contrary readings and limits of the argument

**A privileged narrator still guides the reader.** The many focalizers do not make the book neutral. Its explanatory passages, comic timing and selection of private access shape judgment. The claim here is not equal authority for every voice. It is that source ownership and local disagreement remain consequential even when the narrator supplies a strong frame.

**The late manuscript reveal can feel like retrospective manipulation.** A reader has been invited by chapter sequence and author PF to expect a future. Reclassification can be praised as reflexive reading or criticized for making investment depend on a withheld frame. Both responses have a textual basis. `IM-V14-E027`, `IM-V14-E042`. The specialist does not settle taste by declaring the reveal necessarily satisfying.

**Register shifts can invite an authenticity hierarchy.** Several emotionally important scenes reduce theatrical performance. `IM-V02-E073`, `IM-V03-E114`. Yet polite Nayuta can be forceful, theatrical Itsuki can mean his challenge, and familiar Haruto can lie. “Plain equals true” is too broad.

**Readers may understand more than a character does.** Private access can make an interpretation secure for the external reader while the hearer remains misinformed. That asymmetry is a feature, not a reason to populate everyone's model with narrator knowledge.

**The final critique cannot automatically absolve the published book.** It questions an author's portrayals while the book itself uses erotic and comic visual framing, including ethically troubling treatment of young figures. The comedy and visual specialists preserve that countercase. Self-criticism is evidence of an ethical question, not proof that the work escapes it.

**A beloved fictional vocabulary can still constrain life.** The series grants transformative reading and artistic response but keeps the risk of role assignment. Itsuki's final childish reaction shows that learning to make better books and winning an award do not guarantee that he welcomes another person's criticism. `IM-V14-E046`.

## 13. What the full boundary revises

The early hypothesis that a single hidden “real self” lies beneath every verbal performance must be narrowed. The full source supports layered, changing and audience-conditioned selves; some performances are chosen activities through which a person acts, rather than detachable false covers.

The idea that knowing a secret resolves perspective is also rejected. Chihiro's disclosure corrects a category, not every feeling, vocation or private ambition. Her final goal remains unknown to her family despite the reader's access. `IM-V14-E047`.

The claim that fiction reliably reconciles its author and reader is revised. The love-letter and sequel exchanges are real successes, but an unauthorized future manuscript provokes justified criticism. Publication, craft and intimacy do not give the creator limitless representational authority.

The strongest positive conclusion is that IMOSAE trains source-sensitive attention: hear the particular voice, recover its audience and level, let a later frame correct a classification, and preserve what remains unknowable. That practice explains why the final published state can be meaningful without supplying everyone's finished biography.
