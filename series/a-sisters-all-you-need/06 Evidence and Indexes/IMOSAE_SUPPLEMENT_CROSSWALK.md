The existence of an accessible internal source permits analysis at its class. It does not prove the contents of a related external source. Later acquisition should preserve this separation through stable SUPP/adaptation/performance IDs, not retrofit the numbered-series facts.

## 3. Future admission gate

A future task should record exact source/edition/hash, credits, acquisition and integrity status, continuity/tier, source-facing reading, analytical home and claim effects. Use the method's SF classification only after supplemental evidence is actually admitted. A continuation-compatible scene, alternate route, creator opinion and performed interpretation are different authorities.

Preserve the five numbered-series checkpoints and V01–V14 endpoint. A new source can strengthen or revise a retrospective claim with an explicit delta; it cannot make an earlier character know it, silently authenticate the manuscript's future, invent a missing reply or grade a prose model for actor timbre. Deferred work is outside the present completion boundary.
