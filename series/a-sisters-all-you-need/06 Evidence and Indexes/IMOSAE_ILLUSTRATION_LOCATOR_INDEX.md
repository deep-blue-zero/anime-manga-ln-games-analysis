## 2. Classes determine what a picture can establish

| Class family | Retrieval task | Authority limit |
|---|---|---|
| Cover/front title/color/profile/series map | Edition framing, recognizable cast and allocation of attention | Attractive depiction is not an unshown thought, consent or durable endpoint |
| Narrative illustration/profile | Which represented instant/person receives visual emphasis | Prose attribution and chronology still govern event/knowledge claims |
| Work/publishing document | Inspect an in-story made object and its production function | A picture of a document is not the same kind of evidence as a household scene |
| Game/diagram/formal device | Procedure, diagrammed options and typographic resources | Rules/play outcomes do not transfer to literal-family fact |
| Q&A/afterword document/art | Publication commentary, creator address or future possibility | PF is not secret omniscient access to every character or actual achieved future |
| Promotional/retailer/QR material | Source-discovery lead and classed edition material | Announced content is not read/admitted content |
| Inline gaiji | Exact glyph/ornament and ruby in its source block | Frozen replacement confidence is defeasible against the original; not a narrative illustration |
