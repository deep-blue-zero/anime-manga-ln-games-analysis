# Volume-by-volume evidence retrieval guide

## 1. What this ledger indexes

This is a compact coverage/retrieval ledger, not another sequential reading or fourteen-volume substitute synthesis. Each original reading retains its chapter analysis and immutable evidence register. The machine-readable evidence route index unifies those registers without copying their narrative excerpts. Later arguments belong to ledgers, specialists and terminals; frozen interpretation remains visible at its original horizon.

All fourteen raw identities/ZIPs and normalized sidecar identities were checked in the release audit. Paragraph counts below refer to the normalized release; E counts refer to analytical units, whose granularity differs markedly by volume. A larger number is not a better reading. V14 uses fifty-two textual/paratextual units and thirty-five visual units to route the whole prospective reading, not one unit per paragraph.

## 5. Use with the retrospective corpus

For a date-specific question, begin with the reading/checkpoint and distinguish what a person knows from what a later reader knows. For a whole-series mechanism, follow the appropriate independent specialist. For a concrete hypothetical behavior, use the character protocol and local scope rather than extrapolating from this coverage table. For an actual endpoint, the character reference and final checkpoint retain the manuscript/afterword ceilings.

The source audit checks 3,666 units and 3,729 coordinate references; repeated coordinates and shared scenes do not become independent corroboration. A route with correct bytes can still carry an old mistaken caption. The primary locator guide and current errata expose those exceptions, including E023's missing reply, verified name readings and both G005 occurrences.
