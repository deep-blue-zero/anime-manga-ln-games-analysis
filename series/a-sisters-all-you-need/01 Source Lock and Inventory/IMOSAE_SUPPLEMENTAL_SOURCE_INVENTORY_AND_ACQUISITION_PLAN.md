# 妹さえいればいい。 / A Sister's All You Need
## Supplemental Source Inventory and Acquisition Plan

## 0. Purpose and caution

The fourteen numbered Japanese light novels are sufficient for a complete mainline literary analysis. Supplemental material is valuable because this particular series is unusually concerned with:

- adaptation;
- media mix;
- performance of written characters;
- creator/illustrator collaboration;
- alternate romantic possibilities;
- games and social ritual;
- the light-novel industry reflecting on itself.

Supplements should therefore be acquired selectively for what they can **add**, not because completeness requires indiscriminately collecting every branded object.

The central authority rule is:

> **No supplement can silently become mainline novel evidence. Every source must have authorship, continuity, format, and analytical function recorded first.**

Status labels in this document:

- `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL` — existence/function verified from publisher/anime official material.
- `VERIFIED-SECONDARY` — existence/details found in a credible secondary database/reference but official credit verification is still desirable.
- `UNRESOLVED` — plausible or reported material whose exact credits/contents should be checked before analytical use.

# I. Priority 1 — `妹さえいればいい。カントク ART WORKS`

**Format:** official art book / creator paratext  
**Status:** `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL`  
**Acquisition priority:** **VERY HIGH**  
**Recommended integration:** after V14 end-state checkpoint, before illustration/paratext specialist synthesis.

Official publisher material describes the book as collecting the color illustrations through the final Volume 14 and additional material that had not been collected in the numbered novels, including anime/goods/work-within-work-related art. It also includes character commentary written by 平坂読 and a 平坂読 × カントク interview.

## Analytical contribution

This is probably the single most valuable supplement for the literary project because it can contribute simultaneously to:

- a complete visual chronology of the cast;
- cover/marketing emphasis across the series;
- scenes or characters selected for visual memorialization;
- erotic/comic/domestic/professional framing;
- images absent from the EPUB packages;
- Hirasaka's retrospective character commentary;
- creator/illustrator collaboration and division of interpretive labor;
- media-mix self-reflection.

## Limitations

- retrospective creator commentary should not be substituted for what the novels themselves establish;
- an illustration is paratext, not a prose retcon;
- promotional/goods images may reflect marketing priorities more than narrative hierarchy.

## Proposed source ID

`SUPP_ARTBOOK`

# II. Priority 1 — Volume 8 / `@comic` 4 / `外伝` 2 linked-purchase special booklet

**Format:** retailer-campaign special booklet containing newly written prose and comics  
**Status:** `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL` for existence and Hirasaka-written SS  
**Exact SS title:** **UNRESOLVED** in the official source reviewed  
**Acquisition priority:** **VERY HIGH**  
**Recommended integration:** after V14 end-state checkpoint; before full-series synthesis.

The official Gagaga editorial blog for Volume 8 records a linked-purchase campaign covering **Volume 8**, **`妹さえいればいい。@comic` Volume 4**, and **`妹さえいればいい。外伝 妹にさえなればいい!` Volume 2**. The reward was a special newly written booklet containing a **平坂読-written SS** together with newly drawn comics by the two manga artists. The editor specifically described Hirasaka-written purchase-bonus fiction as rare.

## Analytical contribution

This is the most clearly verified **extra prose fiction** found outside the numbered novels in the current research pass. It is potentially valuable for:

- canonical/supplemental character voice written directly by Hirasaka;
- late-middle-series relationship state around the Volume 8 publication period;
- comparison among prose, main manga adaptation, and spin-off manga inside a single coordinated campaign;
- evidence about what the publisher/author considered suitable for cross-media fan consumption;
- recovery of narrative material that may never have been collected in a standard volume.

## Required acquisition work

Because the official blog does not give the internal SS title in the retrieved text, acquire or locate a scan/physical copy and record:

- exact booklet title;
- exact SS title;
- page count;
- author credit as printed;
- which characters appear;
- whether continuity placement is stated;
- the two manga contributions and their credits.

## Proposed source ID

`SUPP_V08_LINKED_BOOKLET_SS`

# VI. Priority 1–2 — Television-anime Blu-ray audio dramas

**Format:** audio drama included with anime home-video releases  
**Status:** upper-box author-written scenario `VERIFIED-OFFICIAL`; lower-box route details discovered in secondary product metadata and should be package-verified  
**Acquisition priority:** **HIGH**

Official Bandai material for the anime home-video release identifies an upper-box audio drama, `お見舞いオールヒロイン`, with a scenario written by 平坂読. The same official material is also valuable because it identifies Hirasaka as deeply involved in the anime's series composition/scripts.

Secondary product information reports lower-box route dramas built around alternative heroine possibilities, including an Ashley route and a 三国山蚕 route.

## Analytical contribution

### `お見舞いオールヒロイン`

Useful for:

- ensemble interaction outside the novel's normal focal structure;
- comedy and care behavior;
- how Hirasaka writes character differentiation in pure dialogue/performance form;
- whether “heroine” framing exposes or satirizes route logic.

### Alternate-route audio dramas

Potentially exceptionally useful as **counterfactual tests**:

- What traits does the author foreground when constructing an explicitly alternate romantic route?
- Which mainline relationship assumptions are contingent rather than inevitable?
- How does the series' route-game vocabulary become an actual alternate narrative structure?

These should be classified as `COUNTERFACTUAL_OFFICIAL`, not mainline chronology, unless the source says otherwise.

## Proposed IDs

- `SUPP_ANIME_DRAMA_UPPER`
- `SUPP_ANIME_DRAMA_LOWER_ROUTE_ASHLEY`

# XIV. Supplemental-prose search state

As of the research pass underlying this document, **no separate collected prose side-story/bonus-novel volume has been verified**. However, at least one historical retailer campaign is now confirmed to have included a **平坂読-written SS**: the linked-purchase booklet associated with Volume 8, `@comic` Volume 4, and `外伝` Volume 2.

That confirmed booklet means additional uncollected retailer/event prose is a real possibility rather than merely a hypothetical one.

Retailer bonuses are difficult to exhaust retrospectively, so maintain an unresolved acquisition slot for:

- `特典小説`
- `書き下ろし小説`
- `ショートストーリー`
- `SS`
- booklet prose bundled with special editions or events.

### Acquisition rule

If a candidate is found, verify:

1. publisher/event provenance;
2. author credit;
3. title;
4. original release date;
5. bundle/retailer;
6. continuity status if stated;
7. whether it is actually prose rather than illustration/commentary.

Only then add it to the canonical supplemental inventory.

1. **`妹さえいればいい。カントク ART WORKS`**

8. **`@comic` manga adaptation**
9. **Television anime in Japanese audiovisual form** if an adaptation pass is desired
10. **Volume 11 card game** if the games/social-ritual study warrants material-culture depth
11. **Audiobooks** if a performed-reading study is desired
12. **Mini-anime/promotional ephemera**
13. **Other retailer/marketing material** only when it contains distinct analytical value.

If budget/storage is constrained, items 1–6 yield the highest marginal value beyond the novels.

# XVII. What supplements should *not* do

Supplemental completeness is not a goal in itself.

Do not allow supplements to:

- delay the fourteen-volume sequential pass indefinitely;
- turn the project into franchise merchandising inventory;
- replace exact Japanese novel evidence;
- retroactively make every ambiguity “solved” by creator commentary;
- treat alternate routes as secret mainline truths;
- treat anime vocal performance as if it existed in the novel text;
- elevate store bonuses above scenes in the core books.

The mainline novels remain the governing literary work.
