# IMOSAE Family, Identity, and Siblinghood Ledger

## V01 family facts

1. 伊月's father and 千尋's mother married roughly three years before V01, around the same time 伊月 debuted. **TF** — IM-V01-E004.
2. 伊月 lives separately despite being able to commute from home; he frames this as protecting writing time.
3. 千尋 repeatedly visits, cooks, cleans, shops, and organizes practical matters including receipts.
4. 伊月 publicly understands 千尋 as his younger stepbrother for nearly the entire volume.
5. 千尋 uses `僕` and addresses 伊月 as `兄さん`. **LF**.
6. 伊月 insists that life would be better if he had a sister, while reacting to the realistic possibility of his parents producing another child as `生々しい`. **TF/SI** — E006–E007.
7. The final chapter states that 千尋 is a girl and that 伊月 does not know. **TF** — E041.
8. The final title-like formulation repeats `妹さえいればいい。けれど、いない。` immediately after the reveal, creating dramatic irony. **TF/FR** — E042.

## V01 governing interpretation

**OPEN / SI:** The sister fixation functions partly as a controlled fictional category whose purity depends on distance from ordinary family materiality. The `生々しい` reaction and final 千尋 reveal support the distinction, but V01 does not establish trauma, repression, or a specific origin story.

## V01 key tension

**Idealized care vs lived care.** 伊月 imagines sisters as ultimate emotional/erotic figures while the real younger sibling in his life supplies mundane, sustaining care. The volume has not yet shown whether 伊月 can recognize those forms as related.

## V01 open questions

- What does 千尋 want beyond being useful and included?

## V07 additions

### Chihiro — usefulness and belonging become explicitly entangled

V07 materially revises the family-state model. The possibility that Itsuki's girlfriend might eventually make Chihiro's visits or domestic labor unnecessary triggers fear of becoming `邪魔`. Relief comes when Itsuki and Nayuta remain materially dependent on Chihiro's care. The robot-vacuum rivalry then literalizes the same structure: a machine can replace a task, and Chihiro experiences that practical substitution as a threat to relational place. This does **not** mean Chihiro loves Itsuki only because service purchases belonging. The remembered promise of an outing, independent preferences, paid work, family values, and ordinary desire for inclusion all contradict that reduction. The stronger claim is that a preexisting pattern of care has become one available proof that Chihiro still has a place in the family system.

### Lived siblinghood continues to precede correct classification

Itsuki remains unable to interpret why his presumed younger brother is so invested in his household, even while the relationship behaves in strongly sibling/family-coded ways. V07 therefore strengthens the title-level irony: relational substance continues to outrun Itsuki's category map. Evidence: IM-V07-E053–E064, E183–E192.

### Family care is not exhausted by efficiency

The robot vacuum can perform a household function, but it cannot settle the question Chihiro is actually asking: whether being replaceable at a task means being replaceable to a person. This opens a broader distinction between **care as labor** and **care as relationship enactment**. Claim route: `IM-C031` **OPEN**.

## V09 family/title transition

The title’s sister problem reaches its first explicit category break. **State correction:** the concealment was not a unilateral deception authored by Chihiro alone. It was a family-system decision in which a child proposed the mechanism and adults accepted responsibility for sustaining it.

### Disclosure reception — event closed

Itsuki accepts Chihiro as his actual younger sister. His primary relational argument is continuity: the truth cannot erase shared memories or Chihiro's care. Sister-fetish excitement coexists with, but does not replace, that continuity basis. Evidence: IM-V10-E033–E055.

### Real family / romance boundary

Itsuki tells Nayuta that actual family status is precisely why Chihiro cannot become a romantic object: `妹は妹、彼女は彼女`. Real-sibling recognition therefore narrows rather than expands sexual permission. Evidence: IM-V10-E056–E063.

### Chihiro's `原風景`

Chihiro's early desire for an older brother comes from observing an older boy protect a younger girl and imagining an ordinary happy family. Her formative sibling ideal is care/protection/completeness, not Itsuki's erotic fictional grammar. Evidence: IM-V10-E068–E079.

### Authoring siblinghood after correct classification

Chihiro and Itsuki experiment with `お兄ちゃん`, mock sibling hostility, and other stock behaviors, then reject forced imitation. They conclude that they should become siblings at their own pace and in their own way. Correct biological/category truth changes the relationship but does not specify its local script. Evidence: IM-V10-E080–E107.

### Category-security pressure

Aoba/Nadeshiko pressure Chihiro not only as substitute caregivers but as openly available `妹キャラ`. Chihiro's attachment insecurity therefore migrates from practical indispensability toward categorical uniqueness. Evidence: IM-V10-E108–E134.

### Gender presentation after concealment

Chihiro does not immediately replace her wardrobe or speech. Feminine presentation becomes newly available/negotiable. Shopping becomes a chosen sibling ritual rather than proof that earlier presentation was wholly unreal. Evidence: IM-V10-E182–E196.

### Keisuke: motive understood, authority unresolved

Itsuki's grave visit shows new understanding of Keisuke's fear and past behavior. Direct repair does not occur. The ledger must distinguish explanatory empathy from ethical vindication. Evidence: IM-V10-E314–E323.

### Family truth and creator rupture

The most important V10 complication is that correct family recognition preserves the Chihiro/Itsuki relationship while destabilizing Itsuki's creative self. Actual siblinghood makes his prior romanticized sister-fiction identification feel morally/affectively inaccessible. This is not evidence that Chihiro is rejected; it is evidence that family truth can produce consequences beyond the family relation itself. Evidence: IM-V10-E340–E376.

## V10 family/identity synthesis

V10 replaces the concealment-phase problem with a harder one: **truth does not automatically supply a script**. Family identity consists of category truth, accumulated care, boundaries, public recognition, and repeated local practice. Correcting a category can preserve a relationship while changing behavior and destabilizing other identities built around the prior misclassification.

## V11 — family, identity, and siblinghood delta

### Causality is not guilt

Chihiro worries that disclosure caused Itsuki's collapse. Toki tries to relieve that guilt; Haruto later accepts likely causality while insisting Itsuki must solve his own vocational crisis. Truthful self-disclosure can disrupt another person's identity without becoming wrongdoing or creating a duty to repair that identity. Evidence: IM-V11-E001–E020, E055–E088.

### First love exposes uneven adulthood

At eighteen, Chihiro needs help naming her first romantic love. Her prior male presentation contributed to limited experience being treated socially as a girl, but she retains adult agency: she chooses confession, anticipates rejection, and experiences the real pain as stronger than prediction. Evidence: IM-V11-E199–E223.

### Sibling ethics survive rejection of incest fantasy

Aoba prompts Itsuki to distinguish romantic-incest fiction from the ordinary ethical impulse to help a hurting sister. He rejects the former as personally inhabitable while acting on the latter by returning home to support Chihiro. Evidence: IM-V11-E224–E274.

### Family repair becomes repeatable practice

Keisuke and Natsume apologize; Chihiro names eating together as a family of four as a longstanding dream; Keisuke supports Itsuki's career; father and son talk at length; Itsuki intends to return again. Family truth is becoming meals, visits, conversation, apology, and recurrence rather than one revelation. Evidence: IM-V11-E224–E274.

### Protection still has limits

Keisuke's drunk call to Haruto on Chihiro's behalf violates Chihiro's romantic boundary. She sets a hard limit and he apologizes; Itsuki also owns his participation. Repair does not eliminate the care/control problem. Evidence: IM-V11-E224–E274.

### New half-sister

Natsume's expected child is a girl, making another real sister part of Itsuki's family future. V11 supports the fact and Itsuki's immediate response only; later consequences remain open.

### V11 family synthesis

Family becomes procedural: the same household can apologize, support, overstep, be corrected, and continue. Correct category recognition remains necessary but insufficient; durable family is increasingly defined by repeated ethical practice.

## V12 — family, identity, and siblinghood delta

### Post-disclosure siblinghood becomes ordinary crisis support

Itsuki and Chihiro now use their sibling relation during heartbreak, New Year, Natsume's labor, games, and waiting. The relation is no longer primarily a category problem; it is repeatable family practice. Evidence: IM-V12-E285–E310, E428–E478.

### `俺たち二人の妹`

The birth of Shiori lets Itsuki and Chihiro recognize a new younger sister together. Real siblinghood, which destabilized Itsuki's fantasy identity in V10, becomes a source of uncomplicated family joy in V12. Evidence: IM-V12-E448–E478.

### `お母さん`

Itsuki directly asks Natsume to use his given name and addresses her as `お母さん`. This is the strongest explicit maternal acceptance yet and should be preserved as a phase change rather than generalized into total family repair. Evidence: IM-V12-E452–E457.

### Shiori as bookmark

Itsuki names the child `栞` after the bookmark image: when advancing a story becomes tiring, a bookmark preserves the place and waits as a guide for beginning again. The metaphor makes rest compatible with continuity and becomes a family wish rather than merely a writer slogan. Evidence: IM-V12-E466–E472.

### Future family becomes imaginable

Seeing the expanded household leads Itsuki to want a family of his own and identifies Nayuta as the only person he can imagine in that future. This is desire/future imagination, not yet marriage outcome. Evidence: IM-V12-E472–E478.

### V12 synthesis

Family repair has moved from truth disclosure to ordinary recurrence and generational expansion. Correct categories matter, but durable kinship is increasingly meals, waiting, naming, support, apology, and chosen address.

## V13 — family, identity, and siblinghood delta

### Chihiro's gender truth becomes ordinary social life

Graduation travel with female high-school friends and university life move post-disclosure identity away from singular crisis. The important shift is normalization rather than proof of one fixed gender-expression script.

### Hobby belonging is less usefulness-driven

Chihiro values the university TRPG circle as a place entered because she likes the activity. This directly strengthens the movement from relational utility toward independently chosen interest.

### Community concealment repeats the burden problem in a new domain

Chihiro lies about having an older boyfriend to prevent romantic competition from destabilizing the circle. Haruto argues that a community that survives only by forcing one member to absorb concealment costs is already fragile and should be maintained by all participants' effort.

### Truthful existence does not create unlimited responsibility

Chihiro connects the lie to her earlier sibling/gender disclosure and Itsuki's writer crisis. Haruto insists that even if Itsuki had never recovered, adapting to the truth would remain Itsuki's responsibility. V13 therefore strengthens the distinction between causal impact and unlimited moral responsibility.

### Repeated rejection no longer threatens total belonging

Chihiro confesses to Haruto again and is rejected again, yet retains friendship, humor, university belonging, sibling identity, and a future orientation. Diversified identity buffers non-selection.

### Shiori becomes ordinary family presence

The deliberately ridiculous `アホ` chapter places baby Shiori inside ordinary household/sibling comedy. It is low-weight but useful continuity evidence: family reclassification has become daily practice.

### `本物` does not erase enacted pseudo-kinship

Itsuki's claim that he has graduated from treating unrelated girls as pseudo-sisters wounds Nadeshiko and triggers a metafictional `本物` story. Correct biological/category truth remains important, but V13 warns against retroactively declaring all enacted affection under a pseudo-category unreal.
