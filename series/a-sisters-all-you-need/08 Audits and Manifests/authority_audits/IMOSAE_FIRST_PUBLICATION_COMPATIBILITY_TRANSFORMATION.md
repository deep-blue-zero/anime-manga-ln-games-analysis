The related validation receipts additionally expose original-tranche and publication identities in explicit bookkeeping fields/notes.

They preserve the Phase A/B tranche representations; the publication hashes and final manifest separately bind the Git-compatible representations. This is an explicit first-publication transformation, not a silent retrospective rewrite or a new primary-source release.
