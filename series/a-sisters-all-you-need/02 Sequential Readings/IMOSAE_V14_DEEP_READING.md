## 1. Executive argument: a conclusion that makes its own conclusions answerable

V14 does not ask merely whether Itsuki can win, marry or become a protagonist. It tests what happens when earlier answers have become everyday conditions. Marriage, a child, money, an acclaimed book, an editor who believes in him and peers who recognize his ability are already present after the jump. They relieve real suffering without settling what he wants his next work to do. The earlier masterpiece becomes a public standard he cannot reproduce by professionalism alone. This is the first decisive limit on a simple recovery thesis: being able to work and having a good life do not make desire transparent. `IM-V14-E003` `IM-V14-E019`

The new response is an addressed creative act. Itsuki wants to encounter 可児那由多 as a writer again, although 羽島和子 is content in domestic life and is considering retirement. He knows he cannot simply command that return. He writes a novel capable of provoking the rival whose talent he loves and envies. The outcome depends on Nayuta's response and on a redistribution of housework and childcare. She does not merely resume an old identity: her sequel answers his work through new experiences of love. The award consequently recognizes one book while the writer privately judges her answering book greater. Competitive joy persists inside material interdependence; it is neither the annihilation of envy nor the reduction of art to family provision. `IM-V14-E019` `IM-V14-E020` `IM-V14-E023` `IM-V14-E024` `IM-V14-E025`

Other endings give different measures. Kaizu can make novels serve family without forfeiting craft or achieving Itsuki's aesthetic. Miyako can be an effective editor without becoming suitable for every writer. Haruto and Miyako can date without a successful shared household. Chihiro can still hurt over love while pursuing technical work and a future goal withheld from even close family. Toki can remain an editor after acknowledging a loss that no rival's reassurance can undo. These differences prevent the protagonist's eventual public language from becoming everyone's moral law. `IM-V14-E015` `IM-V14-E008` `IM-V14-E014` `IM-V14-E047`

The volume then stages a more dangerous temptation. The award speech and a visually completed challenge appear to end the series. An author afterword announces a future bonus; adolescent children seem to inherit the creator community's stories. Only after that lengthy narrative does the book reveal that this apparent future is Itsuki's dream-based manuscript. Chihiro reads it, questions its unauthorized portraits, rejects its convenient medicine and vague technology, and punctures its self-praise. The award winner responds childishly. The last reader inside the series therefore makes the protagonist answerable for a story that the external reader has just been encouraged to accept. `IM-V14-E073` `IM-V14-E027` `IM-V14-E042` `IM-V14-E043` `IM-V14-E045` `IM-V14-E046`

**IT:** V14's strongest closure is a continuing capacity to choose, make, read and contest stories among other people. Its strongest reservation is that this capacity does not confer ownership of their lives, guarantee ethical maturity or forecast their futures. The text grants real achievements and affectionate continuities while keeping refusal, specialist limitations, institutional cost, family conflict and unknowability alive. The final reflection's promise that persistence can secure one's own これさえあればいい remains a situated, ambitious formulation: the contrary evidence does not permit a universal guarantee that everyone who persists gets what they need. `IM-V14-E049` `IM-V14-E013` `IM-V14-E044`

## 3. ３年後: professional survival does not erase the work of surviving

### 3.1 Haruto's public fluency and the cohort's contingent survival

The opening awards ceremony is seen through a professional who has acquired the authority to judge newcomers. Haruto's ten years in writing and repeated judging show practiced public ease. He corrects the reading of 聖霊騎士 politely instead of humiliating a speaker, and his public 私 differs from the peer-facing オレ. The same character can perform institutionally appropriate warmth and retain private competitive or guilty thoughts. That difference is evidence of developed role repertoire, not an acoustic voice description or universal sociability. `IM-V14-E002`

Kaizu no longer judges; another veteran fills the slot. Later debuts have disappeared, while the six members of the familiar fifteenth cohort remain active. The narrated rarity resists the illusion that the reader's visible successful circle is a normal sample of the profession. These are represented industry conditions, not externally verified survival rates. The ceremony also brings together different viable allocations: Aoba gives university study priority, Ui writes several simultaneous series at high output, an older writer retains adaptation success while declining judging, and another writer finds an adult niche. Continued writing is neither one schedule nor one prestige ladder. `IM-V14-E002` `IM-V14-E003`

Haruto inherits a consoling professional role without simply adopting Kaizu's earlier way of distancing himself from judging. He can reassure writers and still privately consider the responsibility meaningful. Kamo's flirtation attempts are met by women's rejection and threats to record his behavior; the source does not establish that a recording was actually published. The harem joke belongs to his attempted script, and the women's cold response contests it. An operational model should therefore distinguish the practiced judge, the uneasy peer and the man who sometimes tells a useful or spiteful lie later in the volume.

### 3.2 Miyako's indispensability is both achievement and a concentration of burden

Itsuki misses the ceremony because Miyako has confined him to finish work. His hidden phone and game allusion create comic distance, but the scene depends on real manuscript pressure. A draft that can be revised is better than one indefinitely withheld: professional production has stages, and errors in an initial delivery do not negate its practical value. Haruto and Toki become participants in that work situation rather than neutral spectators. `IM-V14-E003`

Miyako's register changes with addressee. She can confront Itsuki bluntly while speaking professionally to other editors and writers. She handles both editing and cross-company agency; the arrangement nominally belongs to Branch Hill, but most fees reach her because she performs the work herself. The success makes her a real professional causal agent. It also leaves a critical open question inherited from IM-C041: who could sustain the system if this unusually committed person became unavailable? A contract and an effective relationship are evidence of infrastructure; a sole person's extraordinary effort is evidence of potential fragility inside it.

Toki remains involved with Itsuki's GF work. Increasing Branch Hill publication and Miyako's privileged role do not establish an exclusive replacement of Toki. Haruto's jealousy is about time and access despite his knowledge that Itsuki is married. Miyako does not necessarily know that private jealousy. Their few outings and her new 春斗さん address are developments within a still-undated relationship, not sufficient evidence that romance has already begun.

### 3.3 A masterpiece can become an obstacle without becoming a mistake

Seven later standalones sell well but do not equal 主人公になりたい in reception. A hostile 一発屋 label consequently describes a prestige hierarchy rather than an objective inability to sell or write. The slump combines comparison with the old masterpiece, audience pressure, adaptation work, scheduling and family responsibility. Reducing it to deficient talent would discard the causal plurality the chapter supplies.

Itsuki's late return to the household provides tangible relief. He has a wife, a young son, practical skills learned from Chihiro and nearby family support. The image of the sleeping child gives a persuasive domestic completion at the level of a moment. Yet his last unease concerns the story he tells about himself: is he merely a noble provider now? The question does not prove that family is insufficient for everyone. It shows that material security and affection do not automatically answer his particular relation to creative work. `IM-V14-E003` `IM-V14-E061`

## 4. おっぱい: ordinary family can correct a grand voice without cleansing every gag

The weekly gathering makes siblinghood habitual rather than exceptional. Chihiro has a chosen graduate-school path and a feminine presentation after disclosure; she still participates in childcare and ordinary family comfort. Shiori enthusiastically welcomes Itsuki, while Sora's address for Chihiro imitates the taught sister term although she is actually his aunt. Enacted address can express affection without altering genealogy. The scene neither restores the earlier concealment nor turns post-disclosure Chihiro into a wholly new person. `IM-V14-E004`

Itsuki launches a sophisticated account of good critics and hostile fault-finders. That account is his polemic. A child's interruption recognizes the concrete adult condition underneath it: tiredness. The family laughs, and Itsuki accepts the observation. The scale of judgment changes from defending an artistic self against public commentary to acknowledging a body that needs rest. This is a small but diagnostic correction by ordinary life. It does not invalidate all criticism or demonstrate that children possess a superior critical theory.

The same chapter keeps an uncomfortable sexualized gag inside a family setting. Its comic handling and others' reactions cannot prove safe boundaries, nor should analysis reproduce the details to make its argument. Its significance is precisely that domestic warmth and ethical discomfort share a scene. Later claims about adult maturity must retain that counterevidence. The child's sophisticated borrowed food-review language is imitation as comedy, not an established future profession or prodigy capacity; the closing possibility remains a possibility.

## 5. 不毛な恋 and 北へ。: moving on is a decision before it is an emotion

### 5.1 Chihiro chooses an action that her feelings have not caught up with

Chihiro has loved Haruto for four years and now supplies meaningful engineering-research support to his work. Neither sustained closeness nor technical usefulness has produced reciprocity. When she asks Itsuki to help advance Haruto and Miyako, the reason is not that she has stopped loving Haruto. She wants to stop living on a possible delay. She anticipates hurt and regret, yet chooses an action intended to change her own conditions. This distinguishes agency from immediate emotional success. `IM-V14-E005`

Itsuki initially mistakes stalled romance for good news on her behalf. Chihiro corrects that account of her interest. He then recognizes that manipulating other people's romance for one's own closure is questionable, although he also wants the pair to progress. His agreement is a mixed act of sibling solidarity and interested interference. The book does not supply an innocent motive that dissolves the ethical problem.

Chihiro's post-disclosure 私, 兄さん and increased capacity for direct or vulgar jokes coexist with the old affection. A model that treats her as either permanently self-effacing or suddenly liberated into every form of speech misses the relationship and context. Her professional usefulness is real; it does not entitle her to Haruto or explain the whole person. The final private astronaut goal will make this separation still sharper.

### 5.2 A manipulated opportunity can contain a real choice

The travel plan exploits genuine professional interests: Miyako needs a planning trip with Itsuki, and Haruto is interested in agricultural robotics. Different departure arrangements delay discovery. The apology is encoded through a familiar betrayal joke. Reader, schemers, Haruto and Miyako have unequal knowledge; later good intentions do not retroactively erase that inequality. `IM-V14-E006`

Haruto notices the danger of merely asking どうする as though he had no desire in the situation. His personal invitation makes his wish legible and leaves a practical chance to exit before the next stop. Miyako chooses to continue. This is an improvement in the local negotiation, not proof that the setup became fully consensual. Sunk costs and the limited time window still matter. The scene supports a layered conclusion: real assent can arise within an unfairly arranged opportunity, while the arrangement remains contestable.

The material details resist a frictionless romantic plot. GrandClass costs money; comfortable rest matters; beer and cider are situated pleasures; a crowded night view does not produce the expected atmosphere. The shared twin room requires another negotiation, and two separate beds are followed by late work rather than sex. Research information and restaurant help supplied afterward are care, but they do not authorize the earlier trick. `IM-V14-E007`

### 5.3 The true promise has a finite process boundary

Miyako now acknowledges that she has worked well professionally. She is no longer asking Haruto to wait until an indefinitely elusive 一人前 appears. She wants one more book attempt, specifically an attempt to surpass 主人公になりたい. Crucially, she promises to date **whether or not** it surpasses that work once the book is published. The editorial ambition and the relationship gate must be separated. `IM-V14-E008`

Her account of editing preserves an author/editor distinction: she can believe in Itsuki and do her best work, but she cannot write his novel or simply give him the missing self-knowledge. Haruto privately recognizes a settled editorial style and also suspects it would fit his more analytical writing less well. Romantic compatibility is not proof of ideal professional compatibility.

The next exchange deliberately damages the improved communication. Haruto tells Itsuki and Chihiro that dating depends on a superior book, and narration explicitly says this is false: encouragement and spite motivate the change. A retrieval system that records only his message will turn a lie into Miyako's condition. A thesis that describes the late ensemble as uniformly transparent will also fail here. Successful friends can still manipulate information, and Itsuki is placed under a pressure that the actual agreement does not require. `IM-V14-E009`

### 5.4 Alcohol makes ambivalence audible, without delivering a cure

Chihiro's low tolerance and repeated bottles of low-alcohol peach beer lead to garbled, repetitive complaints. She can insult Haruto and immediately defend him; she can want a finishing blow and still hope. Intoxicated written orthography and a nonstandard dakuten mark this temporary state. They are not a permanent idiolect or permission to infer a performed voice. `IM-V14-E010`

Itsuki hosts her, teases her and tells Keisuke where she is. This practical sibling care is sometimes irritating and unglamorous, unlike an ideal fictional sister role. The scene ends without proving that Chihiro has stopped loving Haruto. A chosen move toward release is the achieved development; emotional extinction remains unshown. This qualification survives the later report that she has stepped aside.

## 6. 吸血鬼爆誕: one reader restores an activity, not professional rank

Nadeko's amateur vampire fiction initially borrows the voice of a grand narrative. Repeated superlatives, formulaic assertion, abrupt training explanation and an explicit 中略 disclose the register of a novice writer, not missing source material. The reveal that she is reading to Shiori recasts the apparent supernatural account as a manuscript. No vampire powers enter the main continuity. `IM-V14-E011`

Her adolescent gothic persona and self-mythology belong to a chosen imaginative presentation; the text does not give a clinical diagnosis. Earlier family praise and her first prize attempt are not the same test. Toki and Gōdo ask whether she actually wants professional judgment before giving serious criticism. The courtesy reading is not a bypass around the prize. Hostile web response then damages her desire to keep writing.

Shiori's pleasure makes writing possible again even though the narrator qualifies how much she understands. One responsive listener can sustain an activity without authenticating literary quality, commercial promise or future professional success. This is a new local test of one-reader value that differs from Itsuki's technically developed love-letter novel. Their audience size can resemble each other while the work and competence differ.

The final gag and ILL013 sexualize young characters. They are strong counterevidence against a universal claim that the series' comedy has matured into reliable boundary care. A non-graphic critical account is sufficient; neither the joke nor image should be reproduced as an example to imitate. The actual endpoint leaves Nadeko an aspiring novice with renewed pleasure, not the future professional described much later inside Itsuki's draft. `IM-V14-E065`

## 7. 仁義なき業界: freedom, cultivation and the problem no company resolves

### 7.1 A professional style has an ecology and a failure range

Branch Hill's year-end party gives its autonomy a social form. A small group of editors with limited collective supervision can still develop solidarity through shared deadline pressure. Ayane's teasing and negotiation with the chief are professional acts of affective management. Miyako's specialized successes have become conspicuous enough to attract rival labels' nicknames and resentment. The writers' routes nevertheless differ: voluntary movement, bypass of an unwritten convention, a return after a failed debut, and rights retrieval are not one generic act of poaching. `IM-V14-E012`

The narrated account of printing inventory, rights and publisher incentives explains conflict within this fictional professional world. It should be cited as the novel's institutional representation. It does not license a factual account of current Japanese contracts or a recommendation about real rights disputes. The distinction matters because industry's apparent explanatory voice often sounds like direct documentary expertise.

Miyako has a demonstrated failure range. Vague direction, weak business handling in some cases and poor fit with particular creators coexist with her unusual success among difficult talents. This qualifies the flattering pirate or genius-handler label. It also resolves part of the entering 一人前 question: a professional identity can be substantial and self-recognized without becoming universally competent. Her method is increasingly her own, while Ayane's judgment and other editors' patience remain different resources.

The agency arrangement extends creator options across labels. Yet its concentration in Miyako and the exceptional favors surrounding Itsuki's next book keep sustainability uncertain. Greater freedom for a specific writer may require expensive discretionary labor by other people. Those costs should be included in creative causality rather than treated as proof that the institution has disappeared.

### 7.2 The debate preserves opposed forms of harm

Gōdo and Shirogamine disagree over newcomer discovery, publisher loyalty, market fit, author freedom and long cultivation. An exceptional writer might have found readers outside a prize route; this counterfactual does not establish that every less immediately marketable talent can do so. Gōdo's account of ordinary soil supporting exceptional growth identifies a real institutional contribution. Shirogamine's concern about survival and wasted years identifies the cost of continuing to cultivate a writer who cannot make a living. `IM-V14-E013`

The argument gives both immediate exclusion and prolonged support possible harms. A fast talent verdict can be arrogant and premature. Long encouragement can consume money, time and a writer's capacity to change terrain. Freedom to leave may protect a career; pressure to adapt may also injure it. No single benevolent intention removes those tradeoffs.

Both speakers acknowledge part of the other's concern, then continue arguing. The chapter therefore supplies a problem structure rather than a settled publishing policy. The strongest IT supported here is that professional ethics requires attention to duration, alternatives, explanation, resource limits and the particular writer. A universal preference for institutional patience or entrepreneurial exit exceeds the scene. IM-C003 and IM-C034 gain evidence for contestable, role-sensitive authority; IM-C041 gains a harder test of finite maintenance, not an uncomplicated PASS for the whole late system.

### 7.3 Toki names a loss without making Miyako the thief

Toki's private conversation with Miyako distinguishes a long editorial relationship from the special access that allowed 主人公になりたい to emerge. His pain is not merely that another label acquired a hit. Itsuki was his first assigned writer; he had expected to help make an epochal work, and the greatest reception event happened through a relationship he could not reproduce. He says the difference is that he was Itsuki's editor rather than his friend. That is Toki's account of a particular causal limitation. `IM-V14-E014`

Miyako answers without claiming to have written the love letter. She recognizes publication and editorial mediation as her contribution and imagines a future work that only a different particular relationship could enable. Her response preserves multiple kinds of causality. It does not prove that friendship is always superior to editing or that a future triumph will repay Toki's lost opportunity.

Toki remains a GF editor. Their reciprocal rivalry and the narrator's limited assurance of a lasting relationship establish continuity, while his expressed grief remains meaningful. This revises the entering succession question: the volume shows coexistence and changed standing rather than complete replacement. A character reconstruction must allow professional bluntness, pride, disappointment, care and vulnerability to vary with addressee. Uniform contempt cannot account for this scene.

## 8. ママ友とパパ友: two households create a different scale of sufficient life

The Christmas gathering makes the new family network concrete. Sora and Yū were born three days apart in the same hospital; nearby homes and offices sustain repeated contact. Kazuko and Ashley's friendship now includes pregnancy, childbirth and films they can imagine sharing with children. Such interests can grow alongside earlier tastes. No requirement appears that motherhood must erase eccentricity or that one favorite genre defines a lifetime. `IM-V14-E015`

## 10. シリウス: loving a contented wife and wanting a rival

### 10.1 Domestic ease places a new desire under pressure

New Year's shrine visit makes marriage materially ordinary: a child is cared for elsewhere, clothes and remembered earlier visits mark time, and familiar food becomes attractive after formal holiday meals. Kazuko's gratitude and pleasure are sincere. The household has achieved something that cannot be dismissed as a false ending. `IM-V14-E018`

Her possible retirement is also sincere. She has money, love, a child and no publication for more than two years. The disappearance of earlier vulgar habits is described through the question of growth. That question is a narratorial pressure point, not a warrant to pronounce a quiet mother inauthentic or to prescribe continued artistic productivity. The text can ask what happened to a writer's desire while accepting that domestic contentment has value. `IM-V14-E019`

Itsuki's reaction makes his own conflict legible. He loves the person and wants to read and fight the writer. He cannot demand a return for anonymous readers or use his own appetite as a claim to her labor. His unspoken wish nevertheless does not vanish because he recognizes that limit. Writing becomes the medium through which he can address a selfish desire without directly ordering her future.

That medium is not ethically neutral. A sufficiently affecting work can place powerful pressure on a loved person. The relevant distinction is between influence and ownership, tested by whether the other person can answer with her own choice and whether the material conditions allow that choice. IM-C039 therefore remains under pressure rather than closing as an innocent theory of artistic influence.

### 10.2 The target is a professional identity, and the response changes the household

Itsuki's proposal for 明日の君さえいればいい。 specifies 可児那由多 as its target. The name matters. The Sirius joke returns his old bodily obsession inside a polished professional portrait. New reliability has not erased eccentricity. ### 10.3 A challenging book produces uneven public response and a specific private act

## 11. 報いの虹: an award recognizes a book, while listeners keep judging the speaker

### 11.1 Actual relationship advances retain actual obstacles

At the award-era boundary Itsuki is twenty-seven. Miyako and Haruto did begin dating after the new book's publication, fulfilling the finite process promise. Their hoped-for cohabitation has been blocked by his sister's long attachment and threat of self-harm. The source gives no reciprocal sibling romance, no clinical diagnosis and no completed solution. Chihiro and Ui are reported to have stepped aside; an enacted decision does not prove their feelings extinguished. `IM-V14-E024`

Kaiko and Mana's family acceptance and cohabitation plans likewise do not deliver Miyako's immediate move. One pair's practical arrangement depends on another family's unresolved conflict. The ensemble's end state is relationally connected without being universally settled. Later Chihiro's friendship with Haruto's sister adds contact; it still does not authenticate a household repair.

Setsuna's handshake recognizes the joint work behind the awarded book. His renewed focus on Chihiro's body qualifies his apparent transformation, but Chihiro is absent from that exchange. Itsuki's unwillingness to grant brotherly approval is not her refusal, nor evidence that he has legitimate authority to authorize her relationships. Preserving whose boundary is actually expressed prevents a protective-family reading from appropriating her agency.

### 11.2 Nayuta answers as an author, rather than serving as a reward

どんな星空よりも、どんな思い出よりも is a sequel set fourteen years after Itsuki's fantasy. Its response broadens love beyond a private pair, including self, family, strangers and world. Publication proceeds through an explicit request for Itsuki's permission. That represented permission is evidence within the novel's professional world, not a legal ruling about real derivative works. `IM-V14-E024`

The sequel brings readers back to his original and increases its circulation. Nayuta is consequently both responding artist and causal contributor to his recognition. Itsuki considers her novel greater, while the award does not select it partly because it is derivative of another writer's work. Formal victory and comparative aesthetic judgment are thus directly disjoined in the winning writer's own account. He can enjoy winning and still feel that the rival has exceeded him. `IM-V14-E025`

The competition is newly pleasurable but remains competition. This qualifies IM-C025: there is evidence for a larger relational world, a chosen return, independent new art and negotiated labor; the entire earlier effort/lovability mechanism has not been experimentally resolved. A broad claim that Nayuta is either healed of all dependence or merely mobilized by her husband would outrun the opposed evidence.

### 11.3 Public defiance, private qualification and other people's readings

Itsuki begins in a formal public register and moves toward 俺 and direct challenge. The dropped script enacts the shift. He does not disclose Chihiro's private identity history as the explanation of his career to the whole audience. Public self-authorship does not require making every family member's story public. `IM-V14-E025`

Listeners receive different speeches. Chihiro remembers both the abnormal concealment and the warmth of those years. Nadeko hears a concrete invitation to keep going. Ashley and Kaizu recognize a resemblance to Yuu's dangerous intensity and worry that someone must continue looking after Itsuki. No single listener's response cancels the others.

His protagonist injunction can motivate someone, but its implied explanation of dissatisfaction can also shame or pressure people: if one dislikes one's life story, does that mean one has failed to change it? The manuscript that follows will test precisely the cost of compelling others toward a protagonist ideal. The award is therefore not a warrant to treat his public psychology as a universal moral theory.

He asserts a constant sufficient value through change, then privately qualifies the bravado about no regrets. Regret remains; speaking as though it can be overcome is part of chosen stance. The final inclusion of professionals, amateurs, artisans, geniuses, critics, fans and antagonists widens the set of possible protagonists without proving equal resources or equal outcomes.

ILL021 supplies the words that complete the final suspended clause. Its visual かかってこい and 完 matter because the prose itself stops at 全員まとめて──. This is a real formal ending at one level. ## 12. Afterword and 青い小鳥たち: the apparent future first repeats the ending's promise

## 13. The return to the actual present: a reader refuses the author's future

### 13.1 The manuscript reveal changes every earlier future claim

The return marker and Chihiro's “read it” locate the story in the parents' living room, a few months after the award. Sora is explicitly two. 青い小鳥たち is a short-story manuscript that Itsuki says reproduces a dream of a future. Chihiro has just read the text the external reader has encountered. `IM-V14-E042`

They cannot be added to the mainline endpoint. The draft remains evidence of represented possibilities and current Itsuki's act of imagining and showing it to people.

The original normalized content_class MAIN_NARRATIVE is a package-level location, not a claim that every event in the member is unnested reality. Preserving that frozen classification while recording narrative authority in this reading is more faithful than rewriting the source sidecar or trusting a coarse class to settle interpretation.

### 13.2 Permission, care and research become failures of the successful author

Chihiro begins with unauthorized use of real people. Itsuki shows a video of small children cheering when asked if they may be written about. She questions whether that is adequate permission and points out she never consented. Her marginal role does not settle her objection. The scene supports a critique of representational care without requiring a real-world legal conclusion about identifiable fiction. `IM-V14-E043`

Nadeko's permission was sought after writing, and she was furious. Itsuki admits enjoying her hurt expression. Chihiro connects that pleasure with the draft's cruel future Shiori. His dream account becomes an excuse that she doubts. The text gives neither a scientific account of dreams nor verified supernatural knowledge; writers' supposed movie-like dreams are characters' claims. `IM-V14-E044`

She then challenges lazy future devices, unresearched institutions, invented illness and authorial self-aggrandizement. “Future” does not exempt the work from research or responsibility to a reader. Itsuki admits part of the failure and offers publishing-compliance excuses for the illness. These excuses belong to him. The second afterword echoes the production difficulty but does not erase the criticism. `IM-V14-E045` `IM-V14-E050`

Itsuki's response is childish and insulting. He leaves without his belongings. The narrator directly contrasts this display with his acquired title. This is terminal counterevidence against the idea that artistic recognition completes moral or interpersonal growth. A successful creator can still require the specific reader who says no. `IM-V14-E046`

### 13.3 Chihiro keeps her own future, including from the author closest to her

After Itsuki leaves, Chihiro says she will not become an editor although she has some interest. She wants to become an astronaut and has pursued graduate study to obtain a doctorate toward that goal. The ambition has been told to no one, including parents, friends and Haruto. Her technical work and literary reading do not exhaust her vocational self-authorship. `IM-V14-E047`

The draft's coincidence with her secret desire briefly raises prophecy as a possibility, which she dismisses. Her confidence that she will achieve the goal belongs to her aspiration and self-belief; the corpus ends before achievement. The same limit applies to her hoped-for marriage and kinder predictions about Shiori and Nadeko.

The actual pregnancy offers a decisive mismatch: Kazuko currently carries Sora's younger sister, entirely absent from Itsuki's imagined future. A baby exists as an unborn child in the actual present; no birth, name or later personality is supplied. The author of the draft cannot claim to have captured even every present fact relevant to the future he invents. `IM-V14-E048`

The last reflection names ないものねだり and lists goods whose possession and desirability differ among people. The title's “all one needs” becomes a particular sufficient story pursued through that mismatch. This is a Chihiro-aligned reflection with narratorial generalization, not a controlled proof that persistence overcomes every inequality. Its value is the refusal to let even the beloved author finish other people's lives for them. `IM-V14-E049`
