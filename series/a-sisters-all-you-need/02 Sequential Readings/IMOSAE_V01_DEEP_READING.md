# 『妹さえいればいい。』Volume 01 — Deep Reading

## 1. Executive thesis

Volume 1 is most useful when read not as a simple romantic-comedy setup and not as a long joke about 羽島伊月's sister obsession, but as an **ensemble origin volume about unstable systems of value**. [IT] Across its episodic chapters, characters repeatedly discover that professional success, artistic greatness, human worth, romantic desirability, practical usefulness, and personal happiness do not line up cleanly. The book's own chapter titled 「メインテーマ」 makes the governing mismatch unusually explicit: what one person wants most is often possessed by somebody who barely values it, and the coincidence of wanting and possessing the same thing is described as something close to a miracle. [TF/FR]

This pattern organizes nearly every major introductory relationship:

- 伊月 possesses unusual creative drive, an established career, and the capacity to affect readers, yet compares himself unfavorably with the more obviously transcendent talent of 可児那由多 and illustrator ぷりけつ／恵那刹那. [TF/SI]
- 那由多 possesses extraordinary talent, sales momentum, and offers of adaptation, yet treats professional expansion as secondary to proximity to 伊月. [TF]
- 不破春斗 possesses greater commercial success, discipline, market awareness, an anime adaptation, and an actual younger sister, yet envies the kind of originality and life-changing artistic effect he sees in 伊月. [TF/SI]
- 白川京 possesses the outwardly ordinary social world that the creator characters mostly lack, but experiences precisely that normality as evidence that she has not chosen a meaningful axis for her own life. [TF/SI]
- 羽島千尋 supplies the practical domestic care that makes 伊月's independent creator life viable, yet is misrecognized by 伊月 at the most basic familial level. [TF]

The title fixation therefore enters V01 as more than a fetishistic punch line, but less than a solved psychological diagnosis. [SI] 伊月 repeatedly turns 「妹」 into an artificial absolute: a fictional form whose desirability can be endlessly intensified, formalized, and defended. Yet the volume also shows that he recoils when the possibility of a *real* new sibling becomes bodily and familial—「生々しい」—and ends by revealing that the “missing” younger sister is already materially present in his life, only unrecognized. [TF/IT] V01 does **not** explain the causal origin of his fixation, so readings of trauma, repression, or displacement remain OPEN rather than established.

A second organizing claim is that **creative autonomy is represented as radically relational**. [IT] 伊月 performs an ideology of the lone, uncompromising artist, but the actual production of his life and work depends on 土岐's editorial response, 千尋's domestic labor, friends who read and argue with him, games that generate ideas, travel that becomes research, readers who send feedback and fan gifts, illustrators whose work can exceed his imagination, and even accountants who translate the strange border between creator-life and business expense. V01 repeatedly turns “downtime” into creative infrastructure and “ordinary friendship” into artistic process.

Finally, the volume's form supports its ensemble thesis. [FR/IT] It opens by tricking the reader into inhabiting one of 伊月's manuscripts, then repeatedly shifts focal attention to 京, 春斗, 那由多, industry professionals, and finally 千尋. The book ends not with 伊月 learning something, but with the reader learning something **about the world that 伊月 does not know**. That asymmetry is the decisive V01 endpoint: the protagonist's worldview is structurally incomplete, and the narrative has already begun to exceed him.

### V01 thesis in compressed form

> **V01 establishes a world in which talent, success, love, usefulness, and happiness are non-equivalent forms of value. 伊月's sister ideal is his most extreme attempt to create an absolute value immune to that instability, but real people, collaborative work, and the final 千尋 reveal already show that life refuses to remain inside such a controlled fiction.** [IT]

## 3. Chapter and structural map

V01 is episodic, but not shapeless. Its fourteen chapters can be read as three linked movements: **ensemble/value introduction**, **social-professional expansion**, and **group integration plus withheld-family reveal**.

| Ch. | Title | Primary function in V01 |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 小説家は妹キチ●イ | False-start manuscript; 伊月/土岐 editorial conflict; creator baseline; 千尋 domestic/family baseline. |
| 2 | 天才で変態 | 那由多 introduction; art as rescue; talent asymmetry; romance blocked by artistic comparison. |
| 3 | 女子大生にもいろいろいる | 京 introduction; ordinary-life insecurity; hidden love; 京–那由多 friendship. |
| 4 | 今回は男友達もいる | 春斗 introduction; sales versus originality; author persona; peer envy without hostility. |

### 3.1 Opening condition

The first chapter deliberately lets the reader mistake an absurd incestuous fantasy for the novel's diegetic reality before 土岐's reaction reveals it to be 伊月's manuscript. [FR] This creates a formal rule immediately: **fiction inside the novel cannot be assumed to be transparent autobiography**, and first impressions are themselves objects the book can manipulate.

### 3.2 Middle expansion

Rather than drive toward a single conventional plot climax, V01 expands the network. Each new chapter places a different institution or ritual around 伊月's apartment: editorial work, rival authorship, family, board games, travel, publishing, illustration, fan response, adaptations, taxes, and TRPGs. [FR] The apartment becomes both home and informal salon: private space, workplace, social hub, and story laboratory.

### 3.3 Ending state

The TRPG chapter is the first point at which the major introductory ensemble is made to co-create one fictional situation together. That is why its function exceeds “long game chapter.” [SI] Role-play exposes personal material, repairs 春斗's old injury around gaming, and allows 千尋 to experience 伊月's declaration 「妹は俺が守る！」 in a form he can say safely because it is ostensibly about a character. The night then separates 千尋 from the group and hands the reader privileged information: 千尋 is a girl and 伊月 does not know. [TF]

The ending therefore transforms the title into dramatic irony. [FR/IT] “If only I had a little sister” is no longer merely an absence statement; by the last page it is a statement made by someone whose knowledge of his own family is defective.

## 4. Narration and focalization

### 4.1 The false first-person opening

The volume's opening “I” appears to belong to the novel's protagonist but actually belongs to the protagonist of 伊月's manuscript. [TF/FR] This is not merely a cheap bait-and-switch. It introduces the reader to 伊月 first through **his aesthetic output**, before the text lets us meet him as a person. The order matters: the obscene “sister world” is a constructed artifact under editorial review.

This formal move also establishes a recurring V01 distinction:

> **What 伊月 writes is evidence about him, but not literal biography.**

The rest of the volume repeatedly tests that distinction through his novels, his imagined sister concepts, and the TRPG character 月子.

### 4.2 Flexible third-person ensemble focalization

After the manuscript reveal, V01 primarily uses third-person narration with flexible access to different characters' interior states. [FR] 伊月 receives the largest share, but 京's self-comparison, 春斗's envy, 千尋's pleasure in being needed, and industry outsiders' responses to 那由多 all become independently legible. This is decisive evidence for an ensemble model even at the introductory stage.

伊月 is therefore not the sole interpreter of his world. The novel often lets another person's inward account contradict his surface reading:

- 伊月 experiences 春斗 as more commercially successful; 春斗 internally experiences 伊月 as the artist who possesses what he cannot manufacture. [TF]
- 伊月 reads 千尋 as an overcompetent younger brother; 千尋 privately values being necessary to him. [TF]
- 伊月 treats 那由多's pursuit as a comic nuisance, yet his own interiority reveals attraction and serious artistic awe. [TF]
- 京 presents herself as the socially normal university friend, but internally interprets her lack of vocation as deficiency. [TF]

### 4.3 Expository narrator as pseudo-essayist

Several chapters temporarily become miniature essays: what a light novel is, how packaging works, why an illustrator matters, how creators generate ideas, how taxation and expenses work, or how writers may be categorized as 「芸術家」 and 「職人」. [FR]

These passages carry more explanatory authority than ordinary character dialogue, but the method still requires caution. The 「芸術家／職人」 distinction, for example, is immediately qualified as a spectrum rather than an absolute binary. [TF] Likewise, the tax chapter is a comic representation of creator accounting practices, not a replacement for tax law.

### 4.4 Aphoristic generalization in 「メインテーマ」

The most conspicuously thesis-like narrator statement occurs after 春斗's interaction with his real younger sister. The text enumerates different desirable goods—talent, money, status, appearance, relationships, “sister”—then observes that a person's most desired possession is often held by someone who values it little, and that the alignment of possession and desire is rare. [TF]

Because the chapter is literally titled 「メインテーマ」 and because the pattern recurs across multiple characters, this statement has unusual organizing force. [SI] It should nevertheless remain a *textual* thesis rather than being automatically generalized into a metaphysical doctrine of the entire series.

### 4.5 Withheld knowledge as the final formal operation

The last reveal gives the reader information 伊月 lacks. [TF] This is not an incidental twist after the plot; it changes the epistemic structure of the story. [IT] From V01 onward, the reader knows that 伊月's most loudly asserted value system is based on an incomplete map of his own household. His certainty and the reader's knowledge now diverge.

## 5. Character-state baselines

## 5.1 羽島伊月

### Stated desire

伊月 says he wants sisters, sister heroines, and eventually an 「究極の妹」. [TF] Professionally, he wants to keep writing work that is unmistakably his rather than retreat toward generic market calculation.

### Enacted desire

His behavior shows a more complex hierarchy:

1. He wants **strong audience response**, even while insulting readers and editors; he asks 土岐 to read printed manuscripts in front of him so he can watch the reaction directly. [TF]
2. He wants **artistic distinction**, not merely sales. [SI]
3. He wants to be able to stand beside exceptional collaborators without feeling his prose is being carried by them. His refusal to reunite with 刹那 because 「俺の小説は、お前の絵に負けている」 is among V01's clearest admissions of insecurity. [TF]
4. He values **work coherence** enough to reject a financially convenient idea when it would degrade a novel. [TF]
5. He repeatedly returns to friends, games, food, and travel even though he rhetorically performs the solitary genius. [TF/SI]

### Pride and shame

He is proud of his speed, career, distinctiveness, and sister-centered aesthetic, and he weaponizes that pride in a comic grandiose register. But V01 repeatedly reveals comparative shame:

- 千尋 is more competent at ordinary life;
- 那由多 appears to operate on another artistic level;
- 刹那's drawing can exceed his imagined scene;
- reader feedback suggests his recent escalation has made protagonists harder to inhabit.

His aggression is therefore not evidence of uncomplicated narcissistic certainty. [SI] It functions at least partly as defense against a persistent fear that his singularity may be becoming self-defeating.

### Contradictions exposed

- **Anti-editor rhetoric / dependence on editorial reaction.** He insults 土岐 but wants immediate, embodied editorial response.
- **Anti-author-personality stance / status fantasy.** He resists selling books through author persona yet imagines symbolic rewards such as attention from voice actresses.
- **Sister absolutism / real-family recoil.** He idealizes sisters in fiction but reacts to the possibility of a real new sibling as too 「生々しい」.
- **Arrogance / genuine aesthetic humility.** He can say that 那由多's work is on a different level and that his prose loses to 刹那's art.
- **Leisure / work.** He lies around gaming and reading, yet the volume explicitly frames these activities as part of ideation.

### What he cannot yet say plainly

He cannot discuss his feelings for 那由多 directly without defensive jokes, even though he admits to 京 that the problem is made worse precisely because he does not feel nothing. [TF] He also cannot plainly articulate why the family home is uncomfortable enough that he would rather travel than return when his air conditioner breaks. [SI] V01 gives the avoidance, not its full explanation.

**V01 baseline:** a productive, energetic, imaginative young professional whose theatrical certainty masks intense comparative self-evaluation and whose artistic absolutism is already being challenged by the social/material world that sustains him.

## 5.2 可児那由多

### Stated and enacted desire

那由多 is unusually aligned in V01: she says she wants 伊月 and behaves as though proximity to him is more important than money or career expansion. [TF] She is sexually and romantically explicit rather than coy, but the volume also gives that attachment a history beyond lust.

### Wound and artistic origin

After severe bullying and withdrawal from school, she encounters 伊月's debut novel. [TF] The book does not rescue her by being refined or solemn; it gives her an overwhelming sense that the world contains forms of joy and possibility outside the school environment that had trapped her. She then writes, wins the same newcomer award, and leaves school. [TF]

This makes art in V01 materially consequential: a messy entertainment novel can alter a life even if the author who made it does not regard himself as the greatest artist in the room. [IT]

### Talent and career

Her first four 「景色」 books already surpass 伊月's cumulative sales, and the narrative repeatedly positions her as exceptional. [TF] Yet her relationship to success is almost anti-careerist. She lets her parents manage money and rejects a remarkable set of adaptation offers because increased busyness would reduce time with 伊月. [TF]

That choice must not be simplified into “she lacks ambition.” [SI] Her ambition is strongly artistic—she writes work that other professionals struggle to explain—while her *career-expansion preference* is weak. V01 separates making extraordinary art from maximizing professional opportunity.

### Voice and personality

Her central comic technique is register dissonance: calm, often polite `です／ます` speech and `先輩` address coexist with extreme sexual directness. [LF] The deadpan delivery turns vulgarity into a form of radical candor. Yet she is not emotionally flat. Her long defense of *Spelunker* and her improvisational intensity during TRPG show that she can become deeply animated when a rule system or imaginative situation matters to her. [TF/LF]

### Risk visible in V01

Because 伊月 is simultaneously rescuer, artistic origin point, romantic object, and preferred use of time, her value system is highly concentrated in one person. [SI] V01 does not establish that this becomes destructive, but the concentration itself is analytically important.

## 5.3 白川京

### Desire and insecurity

京 is drawn to 伊月 before she even understands his novels because she sees that he possesses a self-chosen priority stronger than university routine. [TF] Her own life feels comparatively unchosen. His abrupt `学校やめた` message hurts not only because he failed to consult her, but because his dismissive `時間の無駄` presses directly on her fear that her own university path lacks intrinsic necessity. [TF/SI]

### Social strength

She is the most socially legible member of the early group: university, fashion awareness, ordinary conversational competence, and emotional responsiveness. That “normality” is not presented as trivial by the actions around her. It is exactly her spontaneous empathy that converts 那由多's attempt at intimidation into friendship. [TF]

### Romantic self-erasure

京 loves 伊月 but supports 那由多. [TF] In Okinawa, she turns differences in talent and social value into a hierarchy of **human worth**, thinking that 那由多 is more valuable than she is and therefore has more claim to 伊月. [TF]

This is one of V01's most ethically important errors. [IT] The novel has already established that professional success, artistic talent, and personal desire are different variables; 京 collapses them back together. Her conclusion is a character belief generated by insecurity, not a narrator-endorsed moral ranking.

### Defensive strategies

She hides romantic desire under “friendship,” lies about sexual experience when embarrassed by 那由多's stereotype, and avoids reading 「景色」 partly because she fears that encountering 那由多's artistic greatness will destabilize her ability to treat her simply as a friend. [TF]

**V01 baseline:** relationally capable and ethically generous, but vulnerable to converting another person's talent into evidence against her own right to want things.

## 5.4 不破春斗

### Professional identity

春斗 is a commercially successful, market-literate writer whose `絶界の聖霊騎士` has reached anime adaptation. [TF] The book places him toward the 「職人」 side of its artist/craftsman spectrum: able to analyze demand, execute reliably, and work within recognizably marketable forms. [TF]

### Envy without malice

His most important contradiction is that the more successful writer feels defeated by 伊月. [TF] At their newcomer award, 伊月's manuscript seemed rough but unrepeatably individual, whereas 春斗 saw his own work as polished and trend-conscious. Seeing a real person—那由多—say that 伊月's novel changed her life makes the deficit feel even sharper. [TF]

This gives V01 a sophisticated creative rivalry: envy need not mean hostility. [IT] 春斗 can simultaneously care for 伊月, offer criticism, clean up after him, and want what he has.

### Public persona

His `ホモアピール` with 伊月 on Twitter is explicitly strategic author branding, partly intended to manage the assumptions of fans around an attractive male writer with voice-actress connections. [TF] Creator identity is therefore already divided into private self, professional work, and public performance.

### Social wound and recovery

The final TRPG session reveals that 春斗 once loved tabletop role-playing but abandoned it after a university circle was destroyed by relationship conflict. [TF] 伊月's solution is not therapeutic conversation; it is to make 春斗 run a game. The successful session gives him evidence that the activity can again be safe and pleasurable. [SI]

**V01 baseline:** disciplined, successful, socially smooth, and generous, yet artistically insecure and still carrying an old loss around collaborative play.

## 5.5 羽島千尋

### Publicly available identity inside V01

For almost the whole volume, 千尋 is presented to 伊月 and much of the ensemble as his younger stepbrother. [TF] He uses `僕`, calls 伊月 `兄さん`, cooks, cleans, shops, sorts receipts, and quietly administers the practical infrastructure of 伊月's life. [TF/LF]

### Enacted desire

千尋 repeatedly appears pleased to be needed. `やっぱり僕がいないと駄目みたいだね` carries both exasperation and satisfaction. [TF/SI] This is not merely service: care supplies a position in 伊月's world.

### Exclusion and family distance

千尋 is hurt when 伊月 travels to Okinawa with friends and Hokkaido with 刹那 without telling him, and points out that 伊月 could simply have come to the family home when his apartment became uncomfortable. [TF] 伊月 does not have a clear practical reason for avoiding that option in the scene. [SI] The family home is therefore already emotionally charged even before V01 explains why.

### Final reveal

The TRPG lets 千尋 occupy the role of 伊月's “younger sister” inside fiction. 伊月's in-character `妹は俺が守る！` produces a strong private emotional response. [TF] That night, the narration and illustration reveal that 千尋 is female, followed by the explicit statement `羽島千尋は女の子である` and that 伊月 does not know. [TF/PF]

V01 does **not** establish the complete meaning of 千尋's feelings. Jealousy, pleasure in being needed, and emotional reaction to “sister” language are clear; defining those feelings as romantic would exceed the volume's evidence. [OPEN]

**V01 baseline:** a highly competent caregiver whose apparently ordinary domestic role is actually the volume's largest reservoir of withheld information.

## 5.6 土岐健次郎

土岐 is both constraint and creative infrastructure. [IT] He rejects 伊月's escalating sister concepts, cites reader-response problems, manages manuscripts and creators, and later suffers the practical consequences of 刹那's missed deadlines. [TF]

V01 refuses a simple “artist versus censor” model. 伊月 may perform contempt for editorial interference, but he actively seeks 土岐's live reactions. [TF] The editor is an adversarial collaborator: someone empowered to say “no,” but whose resistance is incorporated into the author's creative process.

## 5.7 恵那刹那／ぷりけつ

刹那 is a sixteen-year-old illustrator whose ability is presented as extraordinary and whose professional reliability is poor. [TF] V01's “illustrator as god” joke holds both sides at once: the artist may determine the commercial and aesthetic fate of a book while creating enormous logistical costs for editors.

The crucial relation is not simple admiration. 伊月 thinks 刹那's image-making can surpass what his prose earns. [TF] His refusal to work together again until he improves turns collaboration into a benchmark for self-development. [SI]

## 5.8 大野アシュリー

Ashley enters late, but the tax chapter makes her more than a gag professional. She understands creator expense categories, reads clients' work, and translates the unstable boundary between life and labor into accounting decisions. [TF]

Her questioning also reveals 伊月's hierarchy of values. He can be persuaded to classify hobby objects as research when it saves money, but when a proposed textual alteration would lower the work's integrity, he chooses `作品の完成度` over tax optimization. [TF] Ashley therefore functions as a stress test that makes his aesthetic seriousness visible.

## 6. Relationship ecology

## 6.1 伊月 ↔ 那由多 — desire blocked by artistic hierarchy

The apparent surface is straightforward: 那由多 wants 伊月; he refuses. V01 complicates this immediately. 伊月 is physically attracted to her, does not “feel nothing,” and yet his encounter with `銀色景色` produces the stunned conclusion `格が違う`. [TF]

The strongest V01 inference is therefore that **artistic asymmetry has entered the emotional relation as a barrier**. [SI] The text does not state a clean formula such as “I cannot date someone more talented than me,” but it places the rejection in direct relation to reading her work and later lets him tell 京 to read 那由多 rather than waste time on his own books. His attraction and his inferiority judgment coexist.

This relationship is already reciprocal in a way the comedy can obscure. 那由多 was changed by 伊月's art; 伊月 is changed by hers. [IT] Each recognizes in the other a form of artistic value that destabilizes self-perception.

## 6.2 伊月 ↔ 京 — ordinary friendship carrying unspoken asymmetry

京 gives 伊月 access to a non-industry perspective and ordinary social feedback; 伊月 gives 京 a model of self-chosen vocation. [SI] The bond is close enough that his unilateral university withdrawal feels like exclusion, but not communicatively mature enough for 京 to state her love.

Her role cannot be reduced to “romantic loser.” [IT] V01 makes her a bridge between creator subculture and ordinary adulthood, and her emotional competence is what allows 那由多 to become a friend instead of remaining a rival.

## 6.3 伊月 ↔ 春斗 — rivalry as friendship

Their relation demonstrates that competition and care are not opposites. 春斗 critiques 伊月's inaccessible recent protagonists; 伊月 mocks 春斗's market-friendly success; both drink, play, and compare themselves. [TF]

The asymmetry is elegant: 伊月 envies the visible success that 春斗 has; 春斗 envies the originality and transformative reader impact that 伊月 has. [SI] Neither possesses the whole definition of “successful writer.”

## 6.4 伊月 ↔ 千尋 — dependence without interpretive recognition

千尋 performs extensive domestic labor, while 伊月's ability to live independently partly rests on that labor. [TF/SI] Yet the relation remains emotionally oblique. 伊月 dislikes being outperformed at ordinary life, avoids home, and does not know 千尋's gender. 千尋 likes being necessary and wants more inclusion in his social world.

This is V01's strongest example of **practical intimacy coexisting with epistemic distance**. [IT]

## 6.5 伊月 ↔ 土岐 — resistance as collaboration

Their arguments are not simply about censorship. 土岐 represents reader legibility, deadline reality, and publishing viability; 伊月 represents singularity and authorial conviction. [TF] The fact that 伊月 wants to watch 土岐 read makes conflict itself a creative resource.

## 6.6 伊月 ↔ 刹那 — collaboration as aesthetic competition

The author/illustrator relation becomes an aspirational rivalry. 刹那's art does not merely decorate 伊月's book; it can transform or exceed his image of a scene. [TF] The partnership therefore destabilizes singular authorship: the finished light novel is produced by multiple aesthetic agents.

## 6.7 京 ↔ 那由多 — friendship born from failed manipulation

那由多 initially deploys her bullying history partly as a deterrent in romantic competition. 京 responds not with retreat but with genuine grief and anger on her behalf. [TF] The tactic fails as manipulation and succeeds as intimacy. [SI]

This is one of V01's clearest examples of the text's recurring social mechanism: people say something for one reason and discover that another person hears it more seriously than expected.

## 6.8 The apartment group — a proto-creative community

Food, alcohol, games, reading, work talk, insults, sexual jokes, and professional advice create a space where the boundary between friendship and creative labor is continuously porous. [FR] V01's group is not a “found family” in any settled sense yet, but it is already a **distributed support system** in which emotional and professional life are difficult to separate. [SI]

## 7. Creative labor and industry

V01's industry material is not merely insider seasoning. It repeatedly changes how character, friendship, and value are understood.

### 7.1 Writing as production, not pure inspiration

伊月 has produced roughly twenty books in three years and maintains a high writing pace. [TF] Yet ideation is represented as diffuse rather than desk-bound: reading, games, baths, travel, food, and idle time all become sources of material. [TF]

The volume's practical model is therefore:

> **work → leisure/social experience → idea → pitch → editorial response → revision/rejection → new work**. [SI]

This cycle appears comically, but it makes “life” itself part of the creator's production system.

### 7.2 Editorial labor

土岐's work includes reading, rejecting, redirecting, managing creators, tracking audience response, and eventually physically locating and containing 刹那 long enough to complete a cover. [TF] V01 thus makes visible the hidden labor required to sustain the image of spontaneous creator genius.

### 7.3 Market feedback without market sovereignty

伊月 hears the same criticism from multiple directions: recent sister extremity has made protagonists difficult for readers to inhabit. 春斗 says it, reader commentary says it, and 土岐 cites questionnaires. [TF] This triangulation gives the criticism more weight than a single editor's taste.

But V01 does not conclude that market feedback is automatically right. [SI] 伊月 continues to value artistic identity, and the book elsewhere treats startling originality as a genuine virtue. The problem is not “market versus art” but how to prevent singularity from becoming communicative failure.

### 7.4 Sales, adaptation, and artistic worth separate

The ensemble provides several combinations:

| Creator | Commercial position | Artistic self/peer position | Other value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 伊月 | Established, moderately successful, some strong past sales | Distinctive but worried recent escalation is inaccessible | Has demonstrably changed at least one reader's life |
| 那由多 | Rapidly outsells 伊月; major adaptation interest | Treated as extraordinary / difficult to explain | Values intimacy over expansion |
| 春斗 | Higher sales, anime adaptation, strong market execution | Sees himself as lacking 伊月's singularity | Reliable, socially adept professional |
| 刹那 | Successful visual collaborator | Can exceed 伊月's imagination | Deadline instability imposes institutional cost |

The table is effectively V01's argument: **no single scalar called “success” can rank these people without loss**. [IT]

### 7.5 Author persona as product layer

春斗's calculated Twitter persona and the discussion of fan assumptions make public identity part of publishing. [TF] 伊月 rejects the principle that books should sell through author personality, yet his own bravado and status fantasies show that he is not outside persona-making. [SI]

### 7.6 Packaging and collaborative authorship

「神」 explicitly defines the light novel through an unstable combination of content, format, publisher, readership, and packaging, then gives unusual weight to cover/illustration visibility. [TF] The finished object is not reducible to prose. [IT]

This is why 刹那 matters thematically: the illustrator is both aesthetic collaborator and market agent, and the book can succeed partly because visual choices attract reader populations different from the writer's imagined core audience.

### 7.7 Reader affect

The Valentine chapter's fan chocolates reveal a particularly important distinction. Some gifts are addressed not to 伊月 as a celebrity but to characters in his works. [TF] He is genuinely delighted by them. Reader attachment to fictional people therefore becomes a form of professional validation independent of raw sales.

### 7.8 Adaptation as opportunity and burden

那由多 receives extraordinary cross-media interest but rejects it because of the time cost. [TF] The chapter also suggests that professionals doubt whether the specific experiential power of her prose can transfer cleanly to another medium. [TF]

The volume therefore refuses the standard career ladder “more adaptation = more success.” [IT] Expansion can be valuable, but it can also threaten the life structure the creator actually wants.

### 7.9 Taxes and adulthood

「くたばれ確定申告」 insists that creator life includes receipts, withholding, deductions, rent allocation, and the problem of deciding whether leisure consumption counts as research. [TF] 千尋's monthly receipt organization is a form of invisible professional support.

The chapter also produces one of the clearest ethical/aesthetic tests of 伊月's seriousness. When tax optimization would require him to distort a work, he refuses. [TF] His sister rhetoric can be absurd, but his belief in the integrity of the finished work is not a joke.

### 7.10 Afterword as paratextual confirmation, not diegetic proof

Hirasaka's afterword says that making entertainment professionally is neither easy nor always fun, but that professionalization creates new ways of seeing and—especially—access to peers with whom one can discuss fiction seriously. [PF] He describes the book as a highly personal “100,000-character love letter” and says it is fiction but “true in feeling.” [PF]

This is powerful paratextual support for reading creative community as a central concern. It does **not** authorize direct one-to-one identification between fictional characters and real creators.

## 8. Talent, effort, ambition, success, and failure

### 8.1 Talent is real in V01

The volume does not flatten skill differences into motivational egalitarianism. [TF] 那由多's work is presented as extraordinary by 伊月, sales, reviewers, editors, and adaptation interest; 刹那's visual ability shocks 伊月; 春斗 explicitly believes 伊月 possesses an originality he lacks.

### 8.2 Talent is not sufficient for a good life

At the same time, each gifted character has a deficit elsewhere:

- 那由多's social world is narrow and heavily concentrated around 伊月;
- 伊月 cannot manage ordinary domestic life and feels artistically inferior to peers;
- 刹那's deadline unreliability makes his talent costly to institutions;
- 春斗's market competence does not satisfy his desire for singularity.

Thus H2 receives **strong initial support**: talent exists, but it is unstable as a basis for self-worth. [IT]

### 8.3 Effort versus originality

春斗's self-conception is especially important because he represents the painful limit of effort. [SI] He can become skilled, reliable, and commercially successful through craft, yet believes he cannot manufacture the singular sensibility he saw in 伊月's award submission. The novel does not declare him objectively inferior; in fact, the narrator explicitly refuses a simple hierarchy between artist and craftsman. [TF]

### 8.4 Impact versus quality versus sales

那由多's origin story introduces a third axis: **reader-life impact**. [IT] 伊月 may think her books are far better than his, and 春斗 may outsell him, but 伊月's book nevertheless gave 那由多 the conceptual permission to imagine a life outside school. V01 thereby separates:

- artistic judgment;
- commercial result;
- transformative effect on a specific reader.

No one axis cancels the others.

### 8.5 Ambition is plural

伊月 wants artistic distinction. 春斗 wants originality as well as professional success. 那由多 wants to write, but does not want every available career expansion. 刹那 appears to want fun, art, and appetite more than professional discipline. [TF]

V01's implied question is therefore not “who is ambitious?” but **what object does each person's ambition actually target?** [IT]

## 9. Family, siblinghood, and identity

### 9.1 Fictional sister as aesthetic absolute

伊月's works repeatedly install younger sisters as central heroines, and he treats this not merely as preference but almost as artistic ontology. [TF] The escalation problem is already visible: because each new “sister” must exceed the previous one, his attempts become increasingly difficult for readers to inhabit.

This makes the sister ideal self-consuming. [SI] The more absolute it becomes, the less communicatively available it may be.

### 9.2 Real sisterhood produces discomfort

When 那由多 notes that 伊月's father and stepmother could have a child, the abstract dream of gaining a sister becomes an actual reproductive possibility. 伊月 recoils and calls it `生々しい`. [TF]

This is perhaps V01's strongest evidence that **fictional “sister” and real family are not equivalent objects for him**. [SI] Fiction gives him a controllable aesthetic category; real kinship entails bodies, parents, history, obligation, and lack of authorial control.

### 9.3 Stepfamily and debut are temporally linked

伊月's father married 千尋's mother roughly three years before V01, almost exactly when 伊月 debuted as a novelist. [TF] This temporal overlap is potentially important, but V01 does not establish causation. It should be retained as an OPEN longitudinal datum rather than converted into a psychological explanation.

### 9.4 千尋 as the absent/present sister

For most of the volume, the narrator and characters allow the “younger brother” frame to stand. Yet the illustrations often give 千尋 a soft androgynous or feminine presentation, and the cover places 千尋 alongside the principal female cast. [PF/SI] The final bath image then visually participates in the reveal immediately before the prose states it explicitly. [PF/FR]

The ending creates a title-level paradox:

- 伊月 believes: **I do not have a sister.**
- The reader now knows: **the person occupying the younger-sibling/caregiver position is a girl, and 伊月 does not know.**

That does not mean his ideal has simply been “fulfilled.” [IT] The entire point is that he has failed to recognize the real person already in front of him while endlessly producing imagined sisters.

### 9.5 Domestic care versus idealized care

伊月's fictional sisters are exaggerated erotic/affective ideals. 千尋's actual care is mundane: cooking, cleaning, shopping, accounting organization, worrying, inviting him home. [TF]

The contrast may become one of the series' central sibling questions: **what happens when an idealized category meets the unglamorous labor of a real relationship?** [OPEN]

## 10. Sexuality, comedy, and boundaries

### 10.1 Sex as framing device

V01 uses sexual extremity aggressively, beginning with the false manuscript opening. [TF] The first formal joke is that the most outrageous material is *produced fiction* being judged by an editor. This immediately asks the reader to distinguish the content of a character's fantasy from the ethical and social world surrounding its production.

### 10.2 那由多's frankness

那由多's sexual speech is comic because extreme content is delivered with composure and politeness. [LF] But reducing it to fanservice misses its characterization function: she refuses the ordinary layers of embarrassment through which other characters hide desire. Her explicitness often forces 伊月 or 京 to reveal their own evasions.

### 10.3 Desire and restraint coexist

The Okinawa material is important precisely because 伊月 is attracted to the women around him yet can choose not to act voyeuristically. [TF] The method distinction “desire ≠ entitlement” is supported early here. Sexual desire is not denied; behavioral restraint remains possible.

### 10.4 京's lie about experience

京's false claim to sexual experience emerges because 那由多 reads her through a stereotype of the stylish university woman. [TF] The joke is therefore partly about social performance: sexual identity is something characters may claim defensively when they feel observed.

### 10.5 TRPG boundary setting

The TRPG session deliberately allows sexual improvisation to become excessive, but the scene contains internal brakes: game-master limitation, another player's direct physical/social interruption, and a later author-afterword warning that extreme sexual jokes and harassment should be reserved for unusually close groups and that play should maintain moderation. [TF/PF]

The paratext does not erase potentially uncomfortable comedy; it does demonstrate that the text is aware of boundary conditions. [SI]

### 10.6 What V01 does *not* settle

V01's sexual comedy can produce candor, but it can also overwhelm scenes and reduce bodies to joke mechanisms. [IT] It is too early to claim that vulgarity consistently functions as critique, liberation, or evasion. H7 remains OPEN with evidence in both directions.

## 11. Games, food, alcohol, travel, and social ritual

## 11.1 Games as creator laboratory

The 「ウミガメのスープ」 chapter directly states that ideas often arrive while doing things that do not look like work. [TF] The group's puzzle contest then converts professional identity into play: even a silly lateral-thinking problem becomes an occasion to judge craft, fairness, originality, and explanatory payoff.

那由多's passionate defense of *Spelunker* is especially revealing. She argues from rule acceptance: once the game's rules are understood, failure is not necessarily evidence of bad design. [TF] That seriousness beneath the deadpan sexual persona expands her baseline.

## 11.2 Travel as freedom, intimacy, and research

The broken air conditioner produces an impulsive Okinawa trip; 刹那's appetite produces an impulsive Hokkaido trip. [TF] These scenes dramatize the temporal flexibility of young freelance creators, but they also become research and idea-generation events. 伊月 still writes while traveling and converts experience into pitches.

Travel therefore sits at the boundary of leisure and work rather than cleanly on one side. [SI]

## 11.3 Food as relational conversion

「南北春巻き」 is structurally simple but thematically efficient. 千尋 takes ingredients produced by 伊月's impulsive travels and turns them into a shared domestic meal. [TF] Experience acquired outside the home is literally recombined through care inside it.

## 11.4 Alcohol as lowered inhibition

Drinking repeatedly allows complaint, vulnerability, and comic collapse. 土岐 vents about creator deadlines; 伊月 becomes less grandiose; group hierarchies loosen. [FR] V01 is already using alcohol less as decorative “adultness” than as a social technology.

## 11.5 Valentine and fan-gift ritual

The Valentine gathering interweaves romance, friendship, industry, and readership: handmade/obligation chocolate, fan gifts, work talk, drinking, and gaming all occupy the same social space. [FR] The scene therefore refuses to isolate romance from the professional community in which it occurs.

## 11.6 TRPG as the strongest social technology in V01

TRPG does several things simultaneously:

- lets the group collaboratively author a world;
- gives each participant a safe fictional role through which to exaggerate or displace personal material;
- lets 伊月 become a “sister” character rather than merely desire one;
- lets 千尋 choose a sibling relation to 伊月 within fiction;
- allows care to be enacted mechanically through `庇う`;
- repairs 春斗's damaged relationship with the hobby;
- creates a future-oriented reason for the group to meet again.

[TF/FR/IT] This is the strongest V01 support for H4: games are not downtime from the “real story”; they are one of the forms through which the real relationship story becomes speakable.

## 12. Fiction within fiction and intertext

### 12.1 伊月's opening manuscript

The false opening establishes his aesthetic tendency through parody: sister desire intensified to absurdity until ordinary emotional identification nearly collapses. [TF] 土岐's reaction and later reader criticism show that the joke has a professional consequence. His “sister” art is not separate from craft problems.

### 12.2 `新世界の創妹記`

This earlier work functions as a counterexample to his recent excesses. It achieved substantial success, was produced during a period when he was more receptive to editorial guidance, benefited from 刹那's art, and attracted a meaningful female readership. [TF]

The work therefore complicates any simplistic “compromise ruins art” thesis. [SI] V01 suggests that constraint can expand communicative reach.

### 12.3 那由多's `景色` series

The internal critical description emphasizes the ability to make readers experience another life, with unusually immersive language and a recognizable 「可児節」. [TF] The magazine writer's problem is that he can feel the effect but cannot adequately explain the mechanism to somebody who has not read it. [TF]

This is a metacritical problem inside a metanovel: **how does criticism speak about art whose power seems irreducible to paraphrase?** [IT]

### 12.4 Fiction as life intervention

The series' strongest V01 statement about fictional power is not a manifesto but a causal event: 那由多 reads 伊月 and changes her life. [TF] The afterword's declaration that Hirasaka is happy to be around people who “take fiction seriously” provides paratextual resonance without collapsing story and author biography. [PF]

### 12.5 TRPG and self-insertion

When 伊月 creates 月子, the character biography reproduces recognizable personal complaints and desires. [TF] This provides a useful model for interpreting fiction-within-fiction throughout the series: not “fiction = autobiography,” but **invented material can refract its creator's concerns while transforming them into new structures**. [IT]

### 12.6 Real-media references

V01 freely references actual games, publishing practices, and works, culminating in the author's `宇宙兄弟` reflection in the afterword. [PF/FR] These references locate the novel inside an actual media culture while the internal fictional titles create a parallel industry world.

## 14. Illustration and publication-form analysis

V01 contains 51 raster assets total; 37 are non-gaiji visual assets in the canonical visual index. [PF] The image layer is not neutral decoration. Its placement repeatedly changes emphasis.

### 14.1 Cover — `V01|ILL:001`

The cover does not present 伊月 as a conventional central male hero. He is partially cropped at the left while 京 and 那由多 dominate the central social cluster and 千尋 stands separately to the right. [PF] The composition therefore markets the ensemble before the prose has explained its relationship structure.

Most interesting prospectively, 千尋 is visually grouped with the principal female characters while still retaining enough androgyny for the “younger brother” reading to survive. [PF/SI] The cover can therefore be reread after the ending without requiring that it explicitly spoiled the twist.

### 14.2 Front social plate — `V01-ILL-003`

A front color illustration labels 伊月, 春斗, 千尋, 京, and 那由多 around food and drinks. [PF] Before the novel develops a plot, the publication sells **the group hanging out**. This materially supports the reading that social ritual is part of the series' core appeal rather than connective filler.

### 14.3 Front Okinawa plate — `V01-ILL-004`

A second color spread strongly foregrounds the novel's erotic-comedy layer through 京 and 那由多 in the Okinawa swimming scene. [PF] Its frontmatter placement means fanservice is part of the commercial package, not merely an incidental joke inside prose. This should be kept distinct from the volume's more serious relationship analysis rather than allowed to erase it.

### 14.4 千尋's domestic visual coding — `V01-ILL-005`, `V01-ILL-008`

The front plate of 千尋 with food and the first monochrome prose illustration both emphasize softness, domestic competence, and a visually delicate/androgynous presentation. [PF] These images make the final reveal feel prepared rather than visually arbitrary, while still allowing 伊月's mistaken frame to govern the prose.

### 14.5 Character illustrations as perspective compression

The monochrome portraits of 那由多, 京, and 春斗 tend to choose scenes that crystallize their introductory social role: 那由多's intimate presence, 京's emotional reactivity, 春斗's relaxed competence/care. [PF] The images simplify complex characters into immediately legible poses; the prose later complicates those impressions.

### 14.6 `神` and collaborative creativity — `V01-ILL-020`

The 刹那 illustration presents creator play and visual production as energetic rather than ceremonious. [PF] This works against the narrator's joking “god” metaphor: the supposedly divine illustrator is also simply a young creator fooling around with 伊月. The contrast reinforces the chapter's dual view of artistic reverence and messy professional reality.

### 14.7 Ashley — `V01-ILL-023`

Ashley's visual entrance exaggerates interpersonal dominance and comic threat while situating her amid books/material culture. [PF] The image makes taxation into character comedy, but the chapter's actual technical argument remains serious: creator adulthood is administrative as well as imaginative.

### 14.8 TRPG sheets and diagrams — `V01-ILL-025` through `V01-ILL-029`

The book reproduces game material as visual paratext inside the narrative sequence. [PF/FR] This gives the session objecthood: the characters are not merely “talking about a game”; the publication itself temporarily takes on some of the documentary/formal texture of play.

### 14.9 TRPG action — `V01-ILL-030`

The illustration attached to the protective action gives visual weight to the moment in which role-playing converts 伊月's sister fixation into an enacted act of care. [PF] What is usually abstract fetish language becomes embodied defense inside the game.

### 14.10 Final 千尋 reveal — `V01-ILL-032`

The final bath illustration is placed **between** the prose's bodily clue and the following explicit declaration `羽島千尋は女の子である`. [PF/FR] This placement is formally precise: prose implies, image confirms visually, prose states. The publication uses the image as an active beat in the reveal rather than retrospective decoration.

### 14.11 Afterword art and authorial paratext

The author afterword and its art belong to a separate authority layer. [PF] They help establish the creator-community frame and the book's self-conscious tone, but they do not make authorial jokes into diegetic facts.

## 15. Counterevidence and competing interpretations

### 15.1 Counterreading A — V01 is simply an episodic industry romcom

A strong objection to the governing thesis is that many chapters are plainly structured as self-contained comic introductions: tax jokes, travel jokes, game jokes, sister jokes, publishing jokes. It would be easy to over-systematize them.

**Response:** the artifact should preserve that episodic texture. [IT] The claim is not that every joke encodes one hidden allegory. Rather, V01 itself supplies the possession/desire mismatch in a chapter called 「メインテーマ」, and multiple independent character arcs reproduce the same non-equivalence of values. The thematic pattern is therefore text-supported without requiring every scene to symbolize it.

### 15.2 Counterreading B — 伊月's sister fixation is only branding/gag

V01 never provides a clinical or traumatic origin. His absurd concepts are often treated primarily as comedy and professional branding.

**Disposition:** **OPEN.** [SP] It is valid to read the obsession at V01 as an aesthetic program with comic/fetish content. The distinction between fictional sister desire and real-family discomfort is strong evidence of a deeper structural function, but causal diagnosis would be premature.

### 15.3 Counterreading C — 那由多 is objectively “better,” so 京's hierarchy is rational

The narrative supplies remarkable evidence of 那由多's talent: sales, industry interest, critical response, and 伊月's judgment.

**Response:** these facts support exceptional artistic ability, not greater *human worth*. [IT] 京's move from “she is more talented” to “she is more valuable than I am” is exactly the kind of variable collapse the analytical method warns against.

### 15.4 Counterreading D — 春斗's artist/craftsman taxonomy ranks artists higher

春斗's envy may tempt the reader to adopt his hierarchy.

**Response:** the narrator explicitly says the categories overlap and neither mode is automatically superior; publishers often value stable craftsmanship. [TF] 春斗's sense of defeat is evidence about 春斗, not neutral aesthetic law.

### 15.5 Counterreading E — Afterword proves the characters are autobiographical

The afterword calls the novel a personal love letter and “true in feeling.” [PF]

**Response:** Hirasaka explicitly distinguishes fiction from direct real-world correspondence. “Feeling-real” supports thematic affinity with creative community, not one-to-one biography.

### 15.6 Counterreading F — 千尋's reveal means the title problem is already solved

A literal reading might say 伊月 wanted a younger sister and already has one, so the premise is resolved.

**Response:** V01's final line instead opens a problem. [IT] The relevant issue is not merely biological category membership but 伊月's inability to recognize the actual person occupying that relationship. The reveal destabilizes the ideal; it does not yet tell us how real siblinghood will relate to the fantasy.

### 15.7 Counterreading G — Sexual comedy automatically equals endorsement

The volume frequently sexualizes situations and sometimes places discomfort inside a joke.

**Response:** comedy is framing, not verdict. [IT] V01 also shows refusal, restraint, peer correction, and a paratextual warning around TRPG sexual joking. None of those automatically neutralizes objectionable material, but they demonstrate that the ethical picture cannot be inferred from explicitness alone.

## 19. V01 end-state

At the end of Volume 1, the ensemble is socially connected but interpretively unstable.

- 伊月 has a functioning creator life but no stable account of how artistic worth, sales, or personal desire should relate.
- 那由多 has made her romantic intention unmistakable but remains rejected for reasons entangled with art and self-comparison.
- 京 is embedded in the group yet hides both romantic desire and a serious crisis of comparative self-worth.
- 春斗 has recovered some access to a lost form of communal play, while his artistic envy remains unresolved.
- 千尋 has entered 伊月's friend group and has been symbolically recognized as a “sister” inside play, while the reader now knows that 伊月's literal family map is wrong.
- 土岐, 刹那, Ashley, readers, and other professionals establish that none of these creators operates alone.

The appropriate prospective conclusion is therefore not “the relationships are set.” It is the opposite: **V01 has built a network in which nearly every person's explicit self-description is already under pressure from another person's perspective.** [IT]
