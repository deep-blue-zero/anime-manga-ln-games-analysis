# IMOSAE V10-V12 Checkpoint

## 1. Purpose and freeze semantics

This checkpoint freezes the **fourth prospective analytical block** of the IMOSAE project after canonical close readings of Volumes 10, 11, and 12. It inherits the three earlier frozen checkpoints through Volume 09 as historical authority and adjudicates only the new pressure introduced by V10-V12.

The prospective boundary is strict:

- V10 inherits the three frozen checkpoints through V09 and uses V10 as its only new semantic evidence;
- V11 inherits those checkpoints plus canonical V10 and uses V11 as its only new semantic evidence;
- V12 inherits those checkpoints plus canonical V10-V11 and uses V12 as its only new semantic evidence;
- this checkpoint may synthesize the corpus through V12, but its **new adjudicative evidence is V10-V12**;
- **no V13-V14 event, reaction, resolution, retrospective explanation, marriage outcome, career outcome, or later creator-state information is used to settle a V10-V12 ambiguity.**

The V12 ending receives the same protection that the V09 disclosure cliffhanger received at the prior checkpoint. Itsuki and Nayuta have reconciled through a private one-reader novel and renewed their relationship. That is known. The durability of the new creative synthesis, any later marriage decision, Kyo's full-time employment state, Haruto/Kyo romantic development, the future effect of Shiori on Itsuki's sister fiction, and the long-term stability of Nayuta's broadened creative world remain unknown at this boundary.

This checkpoint freezes:

1. Chihiro/Itsuki's movement from disclosure reception to locally authored real siblinghood;
2. the collapse, technical recovery, and re-authorship of Itsuki's creator identity;
3. the mature revision of `主人公` away from exclusive exceptionalism and toward non-exclusive self-authorship that permits rest, dependence, and changed methods;
4. the distinction between **functional recovery** and **integrated identity/relational continuity**;
5. the plural legitimacy of artist-led, craft-led, and task-specific hybrid creative modes;
6. the distinction between influence, relational stake, obligation, and ownership;
7. role-sensitive care and intervention rather than a universal gentleness/force rule;
8. the dynamic ownership of vocation, including Kyo, Ayane, Nayuta, Kaizu, and Itsuki;
9. family repair as repeated practice, including Shiori's birth and the bookmark model of rest/continuation;
10. transformed autobiography, private one-reader art, reader plurality, and public communication ethics;
11. chosen reciprocal interdependence as compatible with self-authorship while unilateral protective exclusion can deny agency;
12. the checkpoint formulations of `IM-C001`-`IM-C040`;
13. the V13 watchlist and all unresolved questions carried beyond the block.

"Frozen" means later volumes must not silently rewrite this artifact. V13-V14 may strengthen, revise, downgrade, reject, answer, or split a V10-V12 formulation only through later ledgers/checkpoints/synthesis. Factual, locator, checksum, and metadata repairs remain permissible.

# 3. Executive synthesis of the fourth block

Volumes 10-12 form the series' strongest sustained examination so far of what happens when a self-authored role becomes **factually impossible to inhabit in its old form**.

V10 begins by resolving the V09 disclosure cliffhanger. Itsuki accepts Chihiro as his actual sister. Crucially, this does not retroactively invalidate the years in which they already behaved as siblings; correct biological/social classification and accumulated relational history coexist. But correct category truth changes what can ethically and imaginatively be done with the role. Itsuki and Chihiro try stock sibling scripts and reject them. They decide to become siblings `自分たちなり` and `自分たちのペースで`. The discovery therefore **preserves relationship continuity while forcing role re-authorship**.

The same truth destroys the imaginative distance on which Itsuki's `妹モノを心から愛している小説家` identity depended. He does not reject Chihiro. He loses access to the previous position from which sister romance was fictionally available. V10 thus supplies the block's first movement:

> **Reclassification can preserve a relationship while making an old self-model impossible. Truth may require re-authorship rather than restoration.**

V11 asks whether function can return before the self-model is repaired. The answer is yes. After failed attempts, Itsuki uses six years of technique to complete a professionally competent `妹すべ` V7 by disconnecting `魂`, `情熱`, pride, and autobiographical identification from the process. Toki judges the manuscript `普通に面白い`, `職人芸`, and something like a well-made `工業製品`. Nayuta rejects the work not because it is technically incompetent but because she does not recognize the writer who transformed her life inside it.

This seemed at first to imply that functional recovery was "false." V12 explicitly corrects that inference. Itsuki **did** recover from writer's block in V11. Craft-led writing is legitimate. Calculated broad-reader work can be good. The issue is not whether function was real; the issue is that function, self-recognition, relationship continuity, aesthetic distinctiveness, reader response, and vocational identification are different axes.

V12 therefore supplies the block's second movement:

> **Recovery is multidimensional. Function can return without resolving every identity or relational conflict, and personal integration can matter without invalidating technically successful work.**

The final one-reader novel then produces the third movement. Itsuki does not simply abandon the craftsman mode and return to old passion. For the specific task of communicating with Nayuta, the narration combines `芸術家の魂` and `職人の技術`, with love as an `乳化剤` that mixes normally opposed tendencies. The result is not presented as a universal best way to write. It is a task-specific re-authorship of method: the right mode is the one that fits the purpose the creator has consciously chosen.

This logic generalizes across V12. Kyo learns that being a friend and being an editor can require different interventions. Miku's harsher confrontation succeeds where Kyo's gentle acceptance did not, but the chapter is `正解のない道`, not "harshness is correct." Kaizu's ordinary-writer ideal remains admirable until he turns it into unilateral self-sacrifice and decides Ashley should be excluded from his precarious future for her own good. Ashley restores the missing element by choosing the risk herself and adding professional financial competence. Shiori's name gives the block its gentlest image of continuation: a bookmark is not an ending; it preserves the place from which a tired reader can return.

The fourth-block thesis is therefore:

> **Mature self-authorship is not purity, independence, or permanent consistency. It is the capacity to re-own a commitment after rupture: to distinguish function from identity, chosen purpose from defensive evasion, reciprocal dependence from imposed dependence, and legitimate stakeholder concern from ownership. Different creative, professional, relational, and life modes can be valid when the person understands why they are choosing them and respects the agency of the other people who bear their consequences.**

The block rejects a series of false binaries rather than replacing one hierarchy with another.

# 4. Block architecture: what each volume contributes

## 4.1 V10 — reclassification demands re-authorship

V10 resolves Chihiro's spoken disclosure while refusing the easy outcome that "truth fixes everything." Itsuki accepts Chihiro as his sister and differentiates `妹` from `彼女`. The relationship they built under concealment remains real, but the correct category changes ethical boundaries and public presentation. Their attempts to imitate stereotypical siblings feel artificial, so they choose to grow into siblinghood in their own way rather than copy a script. This substantially revises the family/title architecture: **category truth matters, but it does not mechanically dictate local relational form**.

The same reclassification breaks Itsuki's creator identity. His professional scorecard is stronger than ever—anime success, sales multiplication, overseas readers, praise—and yet he cannot write. External `主人公` success therefore fails as a repair mechanism because the crisis is internal to the imaginative role that made sister fiction available.

V10 also widens the institutional and aesthetic field. Taiwan demonstrates that translation can transform language without abolishing source authorship. Live-action adaptation similarly preserves governing priorities rather than surface identity. Publishing customs complicate the earlier critique of unwritten rules: informality can conceal asymmetric enforcement, but hard formalization can also destroy useful flexibility. Ethical administration therefore requires transparency, negotiability, accountable discretion, and attention to bargaining power rather than a simple formal/informal preference.

Kyo moves from desire to concrete institutional choice by choosing Branch Hill rather than the easier path through familiar people. Haruto moves from career breadth toward a personally meaningful robot project. V10 therefore makes **re-authorship** a cross-domain process: siblinghood, creator identity, vocation, adaptation, and institutional rules all survive by changing form.

## 4.2 V11 — function returns before the self is integrated

V11 turns the V10 creator rupture into an explicit professional problem. Toki's generic burnout explanation to Chihiro is a protective half-truth; privately he suspects the real-sister revelation is the stronger cause. Yet he also admits that no universal slump-recovery protocol exists. Different creators respond differently to rest, stimulation, pressure, environment, money, and encouragement.

The failed non-sister manuscript rules out the simplest cure. Itsuki cannot merely switch subject matter. Months of block have degraded fluency and control. When he finally writes V7 successfully, he does so by making writing `ただの、仕事だ`: technique survives while personal identification is intentionally minimized.

V11's strongest insight is therefore not "passion good, craft bad." It is that **performance cannot tell us every dimension of recovery**. Toki recognizes competent craftsmanship. Nayuta recognizes a loss of the writer-self she values. Both observations can be true.

The volume then pluralizes `主人公`. Ashley, Keisuke, Ayane, and unknown others have lives in which they are not supporting cast. Itsuki's correct insight—that everyone is a protagonist of some life—becomes harmful when he subtracts himself from the same principle and says `俺の物語はもう、この程度でいい`. The problem is not ordinariness but defensive resignation.

Ayane provides the opposite vocational pattern. Her editor dream begins through a boyfriend, survives the boyfriend's departure and betrayal, and becomes hers because her effort and love of light novels persist independently. Vocation therefore need not originate as pure prior desire to become authentic.

Chihiro's first love and Haruto's rejection supply a relational version of the same ethics. Chihiro's agency is real; Haruto's nonreciprocity is real; compassion cannot manufacture consent. Family support helps Chihiro survive without erasing the pain.

V11 ends by maximizing the conflict between **stake and ownership**. Kaizu says a creator does not owe a life to people who chose to dream because of the work. Nayuta says Itsuki bears responsibility because his work saved her, gave her a dream, and taught her love. The volume makes both positions intelligible without closing the boundary.

## 4.3 V12 — `正解のない道`: plural legitimate continuation

V12 refuses to resolve the V11 conflict by declaring one mode "authentic." Its afterword explicitly states that Itsuki had already escaped the slump. The technical V7 was real recovery. Passion-led work and calculated work cannot be placed in a universal hierarchy.

The volume instead asks which mode fits which chosen purpose. The unpublished one-reader novel for Nayuta requires a hybrid because the task is relationally specific. The work does not prove that every novel should be intimate confession; it proves that **restricted address can itself be an artistic value mode** and that craft and soul can be recombined when the creator knowingly wants that combination.

Kyo's arc provides the professional version. She first treats Nayuta as a friend and prioritizes safety and acceptance. Miku then demonstrates that challenge may be necessary when a creator is evading something. Kyo concludes that an editor may have duties unlike those of a friend. Yet there is no universal intervention algorithm. Role, expertise, power, reversibility, dependence, and the stakes of inaction all matter.

Kaizu/Ashley provides the autonomy version. Kaizu's desire for a sustainable ordinary writer's life is legitimate; his decision to exclude Ashley from his precarious future without allowing her to choose is not. Ashley's informed willingness to share risk demonstrates that interdependence can be fully compatible with self-authorship.

Shiori's birth and name provide the family/time version. A bookmark preserves the point from which one can continue after stopping. Rest therefore becomes compatible with `主人公` agency. A protagonist need not perform permanent forward motion to remain the author of a life.

The V12 endpoint reconciles Itsuki and Nayuta, but not by proving that every disagreement was mistaken. They restore relationship through a new communicative act after both have been changed by the rupture.

# 5. Major cross-block revisions

## 5.1 `主人公`: from exceptional monopoly to non-exclusive self-authorship

At the beginning of the project, Itsuki's `主人公` language carried a strong exceptionalist element: he would be one of the rare people who defeat ordinary probability and therefore deserve the life he wanted. V07-V09 had already weakened a rank-based reading. V10-V12 completes the strongest revision so far.

V10 shows that external success cannot make a person coherent from the outside. V11 shows that other people have equally dense private lives and that plural protagonisthood is real. V12 shows the positive form: one can remain protagonist while resting, depending on others, changing methods, losing, reconciling, or choosing a moderate life.

The checkpoint therefore freezes `主人公` primarily as **non-exclusive self-authorship**: an ongoing responsibility to own one's direction without requiring exceptional monopoly, guaranteed victory, or the reduction of others to supporting roles.

The risk of comparative use is not erased. Itsuki can still weaponize genius/ordinary categories against himself and others. But the mature block-level formulation no longer requires superiority.

## 5.2 Recovery: function, identity, relationship, and capacity must be separated

V10 opens a creative rupture. V11 restores professional function. V12 corrects the temptation to call that recovery false.

The checkpoint therefore freezes a multidimensional model:

- **functional recovery**: the capacity to perform the activity again;
- **technical recovery**: control of craft sufficient for competent output;
- **vocational identification**: the degree to which the person still recognizes the practice as part of a chosen self;
- **relational integration**: whether important relationships can coexist with the restored mode;
- **capacity sustainability**: whether the mode can be maintained without destructive overextension;
- **aesthetic specificity**: whether the work retains qualities a creator or reader regards as distinctively theirs.

These axes can diverge. No single axis automatically invalidates the others.

This is the frozen form of `IM-C038`.

## 5.3 Creative mode pluralism: artist and craftsman are not moral castes

V11's imagery could support a seductive hierarchy in which soulful art is human and calculated craft is dead. V12 explicitly rejects that conclusion.

The block instead distinguishes **mode from purpose**. Craft-led writing can be excellent. Passion-led writing can be excellent. Either can become unhealthy if treated as compulsory. A hybrid can be valuable for a specific task without becoming a universal ideal.

This strengthens `IM-C012`, `IM-C018`, `IM-C026`, and `IM-C038` simultaneously.

## 5.4 Vocation is dynamically owned, not permanently certified

Kyo, Ayane, Nayuta, Kaizu, Haruto, and Itsuki demonstrate different career trajectories.

Ayane's relationally seeded editor dream becomes hers. Kyo's incidental entry into editing becomes increasingly self-authored through practice, institutional choice, and enjoyment. Nayuta's writing is revealed to remain more relationally anchored in Itsuki than earlier confidence suggested, then broadens through work, readers, anger, collaboration, and other relationships. Itsuki's vocation temporarily loses identity coherence while professional technique survives. Kaizu deliberately treats writing as ordinary livelihood, but his life-design ideal becomes distorted when converted into solitary martyrdom.

The checkpoint therefore revises `IM-C035`:

> **Vocation ownership is dynamic. A desire can begin through another person, become self-authored, remain partly relational, collapse under changed conditions, or be re-authored through new practice. Authenticity is not guaranteed by origin, independence, intensity, or permanence.**

## 5.5 Influence creates stakes, not automatic ownership

V11 makes the conflict explicit through Kaizu and Nayuta; V12 expands it through Kyo, Miku, Yūma, readers, family, and collaborators.

A work can save, inspire, redirect, or sustain another person. That history is real. It can create reasons for confrontation, grief, gratitude, expectation, or professional interest. But causal importance is not identical to a property right in the creator's future labor.

The legitimate scope of intervention depends on the actual relationship: promises, dependency, expertise, role, power, reversibility, and the kind of harm at stake.

`IM-C039` remains OPEN because the block provides strong principles but no complete general rule.

## 5.6 Care is role-sensitive

V10-V12 supplies enough cases to reject both "care means leave people alone" and "care means force the right outcome."

Toki withholds part of his diagnosis from Chihiro to reduce guilt. Keisuke oversteps when he calls Haruto. Nayuta's confrontation is perceptive and also highly demanding. Kyo's gentle friendship response does not restart Nayuta; Miku's harsher challenge does. Kyo then realizes that an editor may need to do what a friend would not.

The checkpoint freezes `IM-C023` as a role-sensitive care/authority problem, still OPEN:

> **Concern and insight can justify attention but not unlimited control. The legitimacy of intervention depends on role, competence, power, dependence, reversibility, informed agency, and the cost of inaction. Neither gentleness nor force is universally ethical.**

## 5.7 Chosen interdependence versus protective exclusion

Ashley and Kaizu provide the clearest new V12 case, but the principle resonates backward through roomsharing, editing, family repair, translation, adaptation, and creator support.

Self-authorship does not require solitary burden-bearing. If risks are disclosed and another competent person freely chooses to share them, interdependence can expand rather than reduce agency.

Conversely, deciding for another adult that they must be excluded "for their own good" can become a form of control even when motivated by love.

This opens and checkpoint-preserves `IM-C040` as OPEN pending recurrence.

## 5.8 Private one-reader art becomes a supported value mode

`IM-C016` had remained open because V03's private gift-art case did not yet justify broader generalization. V12 supplies the strongest possible recurrence: Itsuki writes a work not for publication, market, reviews, or broad readership but for one reader whose reception is the point of the artifact.

The checkpoint therefore strengthens `IM-C016` into a supported bounded claim:

> **A creative work can possess serious value because of restricted address. Commodity circulation, scale, and public audience are not necessary conditions for artistic or relational significance.**

This does not imply that private address is superior to public art.

## 5.9 Family: truth, practice, and expandable kinship

The block begins with the consequences of Chihiro's truth and ends with the birth of Shiori.

Chihiro/Itsuki demonstrate that correct category recognition can preserve years of enacted care while changing boundaries. V11 converts family truth into repeated practice: apology, meals, return, help after rejection, father-son conversation. V12 expands the family with Shiori and supplies explicit maternal recognition of Natsume.

Family is therefore neither pure biology nor pure performance. Biological/category truth matters; enacted care matters; boundaries matter; repeated practice determines whether the relationship remains livable.

The Shiori bookmark metaphor adds time: interruption need not be abandonment.

## 5.10 Fiction, autobiography, and transformation

V10's creator crisis proves that fiction can be deeply self-invested without becoming literal confession. V11's Ashley/Yuu history then demonstrates direct real-life modeling and transformed intimacy. V12's one-reader novel is openly autobiographical and openly embellished.

The block therefore freezes a stronger `IM-C006`:

> **Fiction can contain substantial biographical, affective, or relational source material while transforming it through genre, invention, compression, perspective, and design. Material autobiography does not make fiction a transparent transcript or grant unlimited access to private life.**

This aligns with the broader transformed-fidelity architecture: identity can persist through meaningful transformation.

# 6. Character and relationship freeze at V12

## 6.1 Hashima Itsuki

Itsuki ends V12 neither restored to his pre-V10 self nor converted into a wholly different person.

He has:

- accepted Chihiro as his actual sister without repudiating their prior relationship;
- rejected stock sibling scripts in favor of locally authored sibling practice;
- experienced a creator-identity rupture after real sisterhood made his old imaginative distance unavailable;
- recovered professional writing function through accumulated craft in V11;
- learned that functional success, broad reader acceptance, and personal/relational integration are separate axes;
- recognized other people as protagonists of their own lives;
- revised `主人公` toward an owned life-story rather than an exclusive hierarchy;
- written a one-reader work combining passion and technique for a specific relational purpose;
- reconciled with Nayuta;
- participated more fully in family recurrence and Shiori's birth;
- remained vulnerable to comparison, overwork, and all-or-nothing self-narratives.

The checkpoint does **not** freeze the new hybrid writing mode as permanent or universally superior.

## 6.2 Kani Nayuta

Nayuta ends V12 with a more complicated rather than simpler autonomy profile.

The block shows that:

- she can be patient and non-instrumental during Itsuki's slump;
- she can perceive a genuine change in Itsuki's relation to his work;
- she can impose heavy moral language when she believes he is abandoning himself;
- breakup destabilizes her writing motive enough that she considers quitting;
- her creative world nevertheless broadens through Kyo, Miku, Yūma, readers, public work, and anger-driven independent movement;
- Yūma's serious confession establishes that alternatives exist, yet she still defines Itsuki as the person she loves;
- reconciliation occurs through a new work and reciprocal recommitment rather than a denial that the rupture happened.

`IM-C025` therefore remains OPEN. The effort/lovability/dependence structure is diversified but not proven dissolved.

## 6.3 Shirakawa Kyo

Kyo's V07-V09 chosen editorial desire becomes a more demanding professional identity in V10-V12.

She:

- chooses Branch Hill to open a path less dependent on existing talented friends;
- says editing is enjoyable;
- supplies a useful recovery reframing for Itsuki;
- initially treats Nayuta primarily as a friend during crisis;
- learns from Miku that professional stewardship may require challenge rather than unlimited acceptance;
- retains a strong care orientation while beginning to differentiate friendship, family care, and editorial responsibility;
- states that what she wants is Itsuki with Nayuta and Nayuta with Itsuki, providing the strongest self-authored closure yet around her old pursuit of Itsuki.

Her sustainable fit, workload boundaries, and Haruto relationship remain open.

## 6.4 Hashima Chihiro

Chihiro's V09 disclosure receives full block-level resolution.

Itsuki accepts her as his actual sister. Chihiro's broader life survives that reclassification and expands further through first love, rejection, family repair, education, and becoming an older sister to Shiori.

Her confession to Haruto is real adult agency; his refusal is real nonreciprocity. She survives rejection through a diversified identity rather than needing romantic selection to secure belonging.

This substantially strengthens `IM-C031`: broader reciprocal recognition can buffer attachment insecurity even though the underlying mechanism should not be declared erased.

## 6.5 Fuwa Haruto

Haruto remains a creator whose positive telos is plural rather than rank-based. V10 gives him a personally meaningful robot-project dream. V11 demonstrates ethical nonreciprocity with Chihiro. Through V12 he remains in love with Kyo but is not yet in a canonical mutual relationship with her.

His function in this block is partly to show that care, ambition, and refusal can coexist without every intimate relation becoming romance.

## 6.6 Kenjiro Toki

Toki's block-level significance is epistemic and professional.

He recognizes that no universal writer-recovery protocol exists, adjusts criticism to creator state, understands more of Itsuki's crisis than he tells Chihiro, and confronts the limit of editorial authority when a manuscript is technically publishable but appears personally alienated.

The block therefore deepens rather than resolves the editorial-care problem: good judgment does not automatically create jurisdiction.

## 6.7 Kaizu and Ashley

Kaizu's ordinary-writer ideal remains one of the corpus's strongest anti-prestige countermodels. V12 reveals its hidden self-sacrificial edge: he tries to protect Ashley by excluding her from an uncertain future.

Ashley does not deny the risk. She chooses it. Her accounting/financial competence also converts romance into practical shared infrastructure.

Their partnership is the strongest V12 basis for `IM-C040`.

## 6.8 Mitsudou Ayane

Ayane remains the strongest proof that relationally contingent origin does not invalidate later self-authorship. Her editor vocation survives the loss of the relationship that helped generate it because the effort and love of the field become hers.

This is important counterevidence against any model of authenticity requiring pristine independent origin.

## 6.9 Aoba Hajime

Aoba's creator path remains important as a permission/evaluation case and as a boundary case in Itsuki's V12 alternate-route fantasy.

The checkpoint must preserve the distinction between imagined sexual possibility and factual conduct. Awake, Itsuki explicitly recognizes the ethical boundary and tells Aoba to value herself.

## 6.10 Family system

By V12 the Hoshino/Hashima family has moved from concealment/conflict into imperfect recurrence:

- apology has occurred;
- Keisuke explicitly supports Itsuki's career;
- Itsuki returns home;
- Chihiro values repeated family meals;
- care/control failures still occur and are corrected;
- Natsume is explicitly recognized as mother;
- Shiori is born and welcomed by Itsuki/Chihiro as their younger sister.

The family is repaired enough to function as a recurring relationship but not idealized as free of boundary problems.

# 7. Creative labor, industry, and audience synthesis

## 7.1 Creative labor is multidimensional adult labor

V10-V12 strengthens the series' refusal to reduce creative life to artistic output. Translation rights, adaptation, workplace norms, taxes, packaging, marketing law, mental recovery, reader diversity, partnership finances, and family obligations all shape what it means to remain a creator.

Aesthetic agency and material dependence coexist.

## 7.2 Success remains non-equivalent across axes

The block repeatedly separates:

- sales;
- prestige;
- adaptation success;
- broad reader satisfaction;
- technical competence;
- distinctive authorial voice;
- health;
- vocational identification;
- bargaining power;
- relationship stability;
- private communicative success.

Itsuki can win on several public axes while failing on a private self-coherence axis. The V11 V7 can be commercially and technically successful while being rejected by Nayuta. The one-reader novel can be commercially irrelevant while relationally decisive.

## 7.3 Reliability and capacity remain institutional risk factors

The V08-V09 reliability-tax claim survives this block. V11 adds the absence of universal rehabilitation protocol. V12 adds the inverse warning that even an integrated creative breakthrough can involve dangerous short-term overextension.

`IM-C032` therefore remains a finite-capacity claim rather than an anti-work principle.

## 7.4 Public communication includes implication, access, and privacy

V10's international readership, V11's obi ethics, and V12's rumor/privacy problem all strengthen `IM-C033`.

Literal truth is insufficient. Responsible public communication requires attention to likely inference, context, audience segmentation, timing, correction costs, and privacy tradeoffs.

## 7.5 Institutional discretion needs accountability, not simple abolition

`IM-C034` can now move beyond OPEN.

V09 showed hidden selective enforcement. V10 showed that informal norms can preserve valuable flexibility. V11 showed formal consumer-protection law supplying a necessary floor against misleading discretion. V12 continues role-bounded judgment.

The checkpoint therefore freezes:

> **Discretion is ethically safer when expectations are legible, bargaining asymmetry is recognized, decisions are contestable, and formal floors protect against predictable abuse. Formalization and informality are tools, not moral categories.**

## 7.6 Readers are not one public

Taiwanese readers, anime-acquired readers, longstanding original readers, social-media spectators, interview audiences, rumor participants, and a single intended reader all occupy different relations to the work.

Public exposure and reader responsibility therefore remain ecological rather than scalar: "more audience" does not mean one homogeneous relation.

# 8. Family, romance, sexuality, and relational ethics synthesis

## 8.1 Truth changes boundaries without erasing history

Chihiro/Itsuki is the decisive case. Their relationship before disclosure was real sibling care under false category knowledge. Correct truth changes sexual/romantic impossibility, address, presentation, and family structure while preserving the accumulated relation.

This blocks both "biology alone creates family" and "biology does not matter" reductions.

## 8.2 Nonreciprocity is ethically meaningful

Haruto's rejection of Chihiro is painful but appropriate. Compassion cannot require romantic consent. Yūma's rejection by Nayuta serves a parallel function: the availability of a plausible alternative does not create obligation to choose it.

The block therefore treats refusal as part of relational ethics rather than mere failure.

## 8.3 Love does not solve jurisdiction automatically

# 10. Talent, worth, ordinariness, and life design

## 10.1 Talent remains real; totalization remains the error

V10-V12 does not deny genius difference. It shows repeatedly that talent is a poor total theory of personhood.

Itsuki uses `天才` to make empathy seem impossible. Nayuta rejects that reduction because it erases effort. Yūma demonstrates an alternative high-capacity path without automatically becoming the correct romantic choice. Kaizu deliberately values survival over genius mythology.

Talent can matter professionally without deciding who deserves love, rest, permission, or intelligibility.

## 10.2 Ordinary happiness remains a legitimate telos

V11 reveals that Itsuki can weaponize "ordinary enough" as resignation. V12 reveals that Kaizu can weaponize ordinariness in the opposite direction, turning sustainable life into a solitary martyr project.

The checkpoint therefore freezes `IM-C030` as:

> **Moderate, durable, ordinary happiness is a legitimate positive telos when genuinely chosen and livable. It becomes distorted when used to anesthetize unresolved desire or to justify self-erasing sacrifice. Rest, sufficiency, ambition, and dependence must be evaluated by ownership and sustainability rather than prestige.**

## 10.3 Shiori's bookmark adds pause to the theory of agency

The bookmark metaphor is not merely sentimental naming. It repairs a hidden assumption inside protagonist rhetoric: continuous forward motion is not required for continuation. A pause can preserve continuity.

That makes rest conceptually compatible with self-authorship rather than its opposite.
