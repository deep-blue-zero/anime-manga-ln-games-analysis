# V13–V14 End-State Checkpoint

## 0. Decision and boundary

This is the fifth and last **prospective block checkpoint** of the fourteen numbered Japanese novels. It freezes the V13–V14 end state before mature topical/full-series synthesis. It preserves the four earlier checkpoints and thirteen earlier readings at their historical horizons. It does not retrospectively make their uncertainty mistaken, and it does not certify every theme as universally true.

The relevant predecessor is [V10–V12 checkpoint](IMOSAE_V10-V12_CHECKPOINT.md). Local V13 analysis is [V13 reading](../IMOSAE_V13_DEEP_READING.md); local V14 analysis is [V14 reading](../IMOSAE_V14_DEEP_READING.md). The latter's [source validation](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/locator_validation/IMOSAE_V14_SOURCE_AND_READING_VALIDATION.md) gates this checkpoint. Source and primary-name corrections remain explicit in the [errata layer](../../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/IMOSAE_PRIMARY_IDENTITY_AND_NORMALIZATION_ERRATA.md).

This freeze records a specific completed-mainline horizon. It leaves actual future careers, cohabitation repairs, births, motives not given a POV and external supplemental events unknown. Mature synthesis may organize these states differently but must not promote a possibility into an event.

## 1. What the block adds to the fourth checkpoint

The fourth checkpoint had already rejected a simple recovery binary: functional technique, identity integration, relational reconciliation and self-worth were separate dimensions. Itsuki's privately addressed novel was a task-specific integration rather than proof that every project should use the same mode. Miyako had chosen editing but had not yet exercised sustained professional authority; Chihiro's expanded family identity survived rejection without becoming merely a support role; shared risk remained newly open.

V13 tests these achievements under institutions and circulation. Miyako gains a desk, assignments, collaborators and the capacity to injure trust through unexplained authority. Ayane's repair makes expertise legible rather than abolishing expertise. Haruto's communication minimum distinguishes ordinary repeatable responsibilities from extraordinary devotion. The private-origin manuscript becomes a publicly revised work through consented mediation, and its reception creates a new professional standard. Miyako and Haruto acknowledge attraction without dating. Ashley and Kaizu institutionalize chosen risk through marriage that includes Yuu's memory. Itsuki proposes; the answer remains elided. V13 then announces three years, supplying none of the later biography. (V13 register: IM-V13-E010–IM-V13-E099; IM-V13-E195–IM-V13-E249; IM-V13-E300–IM-V13-E339; IM-V13-E352–IM-V13-E426; IM-V13-E427–IM-V13-E491; IM-V13-E492–IM-V13-E596.)

V14 supplies those later facts and tests their maintenance. Marriage, parenthood, editorial success and acclaim are real. Itsuki still struggles with the work after the masterpiece. Miyako's style is effective and specialized, with a failure range and a concentrated agency burden. Haruto's actual dating promise acquires a finite publication boundary irrespective of artistic victory; he deliberately lies when reporting it. Toki's continuing role changes through grief and rivalry rather than disappearing. Kaizu's family-first writing differs from Itsuki's renewed need for artistic conflict. (V14 `IM-V14-E003`, `IM-V14-E008`, `IM-V14-E009`, `IM-V14-E012`, `IM-V14-E014`, `IM-V14-E015`.)

The new addressed book calls to 可児那由多. Her response is her desire to write and a request for material domestic support. Her answering sequel changes both her art and the reception of his book. An award recognizes Itsuki while his own judgment prefers hers. Achievement therefore closes an event question but leaves the multidimensional model intact. (`IM-V14-E020`, `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E024`, `IM-V14-E025`.)

The final frame makes the block's most substantial authority correction. An apparent adolescent future is a manuscript; the real reader challenges its portraits, research and self-praise. Itsuki's childish reaction survives his professional success. Chihiro's own secret astronaut goal and Kazuko's pregnancy occur in the returned present; the imagined careers, teenage interiorities and medical future remain inside the draft. (`IM-V14-E042`, `IM-V14-E043`, `IM-V14-E045`, `IM-V14-E046`, `IM-V14-E047`, `IM-V14-E048`.)

**Checkpoint IT:** self-authorship has become public, materially interdependent and answerable to other readers and participants. This is a bounded interpretation of the block's tested developments. It is not a universal claim that everyone has obtained ethical institutions, transparent desires or sufficient resources.

## 4. Closure is not universal repair

The block completes several concrete acts: proposals become marriage, a chosen editorial vocation becomes practiced authority, delayed love becomes actual dating, a new book is made, a reader answers with art, and formal recognition arrives. The acts remain worth recognizing even where consequences are mixed. Erasing them because ethical perfection is absent would reproduce the all-or-nothing recovery model already corrected in the fourth checkpoint.

Conversely, praising those acts cannot make remaining problems disappear. Haruto's false report, the concentrated labor behind successful agency, exceptional illustrator availability, amateur pain, young-character objectification and unauthorized future portraits remain contrary evidence. The same people who can care and collaborate can impose a cost on someone else. The freeze therefore records **bounded achievement plus unclosed obligations**.

The manuscript frame offers a particularly strong test. Its imagined collective stage can answer the lone-protagonist script, while privileged coaching and concealed parental contributions keep equality limited. Its father can imagine a child's objection to 人でなし aspiration yet still wound an actual young reader. Its final reader can expose the problem and decline the vocation he assigns her. Narrative self-criticism has force without proving the book escapes every pleasure it criticizes.

This distinction controls the final title language. Kaizu's family good, Itsuki's changing art, Nayuta's chosen return and Chihiro's private technical ambition give different sufficient directions. これさえあればいい cannot mean that one literal sister solves every person's life. Its last persistence formulation remains hopeful and contestable in a world of unequal resources and contingent survival. This freeze preserves both the formulation and the text's pressure on it.
