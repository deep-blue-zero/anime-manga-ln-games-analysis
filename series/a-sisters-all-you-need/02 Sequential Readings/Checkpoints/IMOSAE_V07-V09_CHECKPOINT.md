# IMOSAE V07-V09 Checkpoint

## 1. Purpose and freeze semantics

This checkpoint freezes the **third prospective analytical block** of the IMOSAE project after canonical deep readings of Volumes 07, 08, and 09. It inherits `IMOSAE_V01-V03_CHECKPOINT.md` and `IMOSAE_V04-V06_CHECKPOINT.md` as immutable historical-development states, then adjudicates only the new pressure introduced by V07-V09.

The prospective boundary is strict:

- V07 inherits the two frozen checkpoints through V06 and uses V07 as its only new semantic evidence;
- V08 inherits those checkpoints plus canonical V07 and uses V08 as its only new semantic evidence;
- V09 inherits those checkpoints plus canonical V07-V08 and uses V09 as its only new semantic evidence;
- this checkpoint may synthesize V01-V09, but its **new adjudicative evidence is V07-V09**;
- **no V10-V14 event, reaction, explanation, identity consequence, career outcome, or retrospective resolution may be used to settle a V07-V09 ambiguity.**

The V09 ending receives an especially strict freeze rule. Chihiro states that she is Itsuki's real younger sister. The fact of the statement is evidence. **Itsuki's reception, the ensemble's reaction, the stability of Chihiro's first-person register after disclosure, and the consequences for family structure are not known at this boundary.** The checkpoint therefore records the disclosure as **spoken / reception unknown**.

This checkpoint freezes:

1. the mature friendship, romantic, professional, and domestic ecology at the end of V09;
2. the block's distinction between continuity, role-inhabitation, and authored commitment;
3. career divergence and plural professional teloi;
4. the revised model of comparison, usefulness, recognition, and conditional worth;
5. the transition of Kyo's editing path from aptitude to presently chosen vocation;
6. the V07-V09 expansion of creator longevity into capacity, recoverability, public exposure, and audience responsibility;
7. the current family/sibling/title logic through Chihiro's spoken disclosure;
8. the role of games and fiction as social infrastructure, reductive models, and identity rehearsal;
9. the block's consent/boundary corrections without retroactive absolution;
10. the checkpoint formulations of `IM-C001`-`IM-C036`;
11. the V10 watchlist and all questions that remain unresolved at the disclosure cliffhanger.

"Frozen" means later volumes must not silently rewrite this document. Later evidence may strengthen, revise, downgrade, reject, answer, or split a V07-V09 formulation only through the live ledgers and later checkpoints/synthesis. Factual, locator, checksum, and metadata repair remain permissible.

# 2. Executive synthesis of the third block

Volumes 07-09 form a coherent sequence about what happens **after a person has chosen, acquired, or been assigned a role**.

V07 begins from completed romantic movement. Itsuki and Nayuta are now a couple; Kyo's confession has already been rejected; the apartment ensemble knows the relational geometry has changed. Yet the volume refuses to treat this as a reset. Itsuki still wants to become a creator who can stand beside Nayuta. Kyo still has to inhabit friendship after loss. Chihiro still organizes security around being useful. The creator community still gathers for games, food, work, and conversation. The volume's thesis is therefore continuity under change: **durability is not sameness**. A self, relationship, genre, or career persists by absorbing changed conditions without allowing one metric to define the whole.

V08 asks what that continuity costs when roles become ordinary practice. The couple has to live through work schedules, ambition, sex, travel, illness, publicity, and household arrangements. Kyo's editorial aptitude becomes increasingly undeniable but remains analytically separate from vocation. Nayuta's safer roomshare is meaningful but does not prove her effort/lovability pathology cured. Toki's work demonstrates both responsibility and capacity blindness. Hoshii's collapse shows how reliability can become a pathway to overload. Kiso's late debut demonstrates that a creative vocation can begin after another full professional life. Chihiro develops independent friendships and interests, weakening the equation between practical indispensability and relational necessity even as the false male-younger-brother category becomes more deeply embedded in social life.

V09 then converts these role problems into a question of authority: **who gets to decide what a role means?** Institutions assign market value, tacit rules, professional status, and bargaining power. Relationships assign usefulness and family category. Critics assign authenticity or derivative status. Yet none of those assignments automatically determines whether a person may create, whether a vocation is genuinely chosen, or whether a lived relationship is real. The volume separates **assigned value** from **authored commitment**.

Aoba is the clearest artistic case. Reception knocks her from grandiose self-certainty into the opposite totalization: if she is derivative or not talented enough, she has no right to write. Itsuki refuses both poles. He does not certify her as a genius or promise commercial success. He asks whether she wants to write and whether she loves what she made. The resulting principle is narrower and stronger than unconditional praise:

> **Evaluation can govern revision, publication, and professional judgment without becoming a tribunal over the basic permission to create.**

Kyo is the clearest vocational case. V06-V08 established that aptitude and institutional demand do not equal desire. V09 finally supplies the missing proposition: she presently wants to become an editor. But the same volume immediately prevents easy closure. Her roomshare labor has drifted toward disproportionate care, and she experiences a dark satisfaction in being indispensable to two brilliant creators. Her ideal of becoming an editor who can `一緒に泣く` with authors is admirable but ethically demanding. The block therefore freezes **chosen vocation without assuming sustainable professional boundaries**.

Chihiro is the clearest identity case. V07 makes practical replaceability feel like relational replaceability. V08 revises that model by giving Chihiro independent friendship, income, hobbies, group membership, and reciprocal recognition. V09 then shows that diversification buffers but does not erase insecurity: Aoba can reproduce a care function without malice and still reactivate Chihiro's fear. Pregnancy creates an even stronger anticipatory threat because a future child would possess an undisputed biological relation to Itsuki. The TRPG finale provides a temporary solution through fiction. Chihiro can inhabit the socially blocked self of a girl who is Itsuki's younger sister. Itsuki calls that in-game character the `最高の妹`; Nayuta innocently reminds Chihiro that the relation is only the game setting; the gap between enacted identity and recognized category becomes unbearable; and Chihiro shifts into `私` to state `私は、兄さんの本当の妹だもん!!`.

The block therefore ends not with identity resolved, but with **concealment becoming unsustainable**.

The creator-industry material supplies the same structure at institutional scale. V07 distinguishes engaged readers from broader exposure and makes moderate happiness a legitimate professional telos. V08 shows the reliability tax and treats publicity timing/form as creator-reader labor. V09 expands this into systemic capacity failure, broadcast audience responsibility, tacit employment conventions, selective enforcement, and adaptation under constraint. The anime can change wording, lose material, compress production, and still be recognized by Itsuki as his work because the adaptation preserves governing priorities rather than surfaces.

The third-block thesis is therefore:

> **Mature self-authorship is not freedom from roles, institutions, comparison, or dependence. It is the capacity to distinguish what others assign from what one actually commits to, and then to inhabit chosen commitments without converting talent, usefulness, market value, recognition, or category into total measures of personhood. Roles become ethically meaningful through maintenance, limits, reciprocal burden, and the possibility of revision.**

This block does not abolish excellence, professional judgment, family facts, institutional power, or category. It rejects their **totalization**.

# 3. Block architecture: what each volume contributes

## 3.1 V07 - continuity under change

V07 tests whether the V06 revisions survive ordinary life.

Itsuki's earlier equality rule is not restored as a condition of romance. He and Nayuta remain a couple while his `対等の主人公` ambition persists. This is a crucial distinction: romantic permission has been separated from professional comparison, but professional comparison has not disappeared. The block therefore carries forward a live risk that career hierarchy may continue to organize self-worth even after it loses jurisdiction over love. [IM-V07-E002-IM-V07-E012]

Kyo demonstrates relationship maintenance after romantic loss. Friendship survives through actual behavior rather than the mere declaration that everyone will remain friends. She supports the couple without pretending her prior desire never existed. The apartment group likewise persists through changed seating, teasing, games, food, and social routines. V07 therefore turns `IM-C024` from hypothesis into a durable relational principle: continuity is enacted. [IM-V07-E020-IM-V07-E032] [IM-V07-E082-IM-V07-E105]

Chihiro's robot-vacuum episode makes usefulness/security explicit. The fear is not anti-technology. It is that if the practical role through which belonging is secured can be automated, perhaps the relationship can be automated too. This opens `IM-C031`. [IM-V07-E116-IM-V07-E127]

The Aioi Hajime material strengthens aesthetic pluralism. Shared genre structures and `template` categories are real, but categorical similarity does not erase local intention, execution, or love of form. The block rejects both naive uniqueness and dismissive sameness. [IM-V07-E144-IM-V07-E158]

The Yuu/Kaizu history then widens creator-life evaluation. Genius, durable sales, media mix, prestige, influence, and audience love do not guarantee health or happiness. Public visibility can multiply hostile spectators as well as readers. Kaizu's desired life as an ordinary, moderately happy working writer becomes an affirmative professional telos rather than consolation for failing to become a genius. [IM-V07-E159-IM-V07-E182]

V07 therefore contributes the block's first principle: **continuity requires change without totalization**.

## 3.2 V08 - inhabiting roles and paying their material costs

V08 takes roles that V07 preserved and asks how they function as daily practice.

Itsuki and Nayuta live ordinary couplehood beside career asymmetry. Itsuki's shrine language of becoming a `相応しい` protagonist beside Nayuta shows that comparison remains psychologically active, but the couple's existence does not depend on first satisfying that status condition. [IM-V08-E022-IM-V08-E034] [IM-V08-E153-IM-V08-E167]

Kyo's editing path becomes more concrete. She is good at the work and the institution wants her. Toki's description of editing, however, makes clear that being suited to the job is not enough; it is a demanding occupation that a person must actually want. V08 therefore strengthens the aptitude/demand/desire distinction rather than closing it. [IM-V08-E001-IM-V08-E010]

The anime-announcement leak adds a new dimension to authorship. The prepared announcement is itself collaborative work involving prose, illustration, manga, animation, web infrastructure, timing, and reader address. The injury is not merely possessiveness about secrecy. The form and timing of public communication carry relational meaning. This opens `IM-C033`. [IM-V08-E035-IM-V08-E045]

Hoshii's collapse opens `IM-C032`. Reliability has made him a preferred recipient of additional work. His past competence becomes evidence that he can absorb more, until finite capacity is treated as effectively expandable. V08 therefore identifies a **reliability tax**: a system can reward dependability by consuming the dependable person's recovery margin. [IM-V08-E046-IM-V08-E055]

Kiso expands the series' career-time model. At sixty-seven, after a major non-literary career, he deliberately learns unfamiliar genre grammar and begins light-novel work. Late vocation is not framed as unserious imitation. Prior life experience becomes usable creative capital. [IM-V08-E056-IM-V08-E065]

Chihiro provides the block's most important self-correction. The robot vacuum does reduce the practical need for one domestic function, but V08 broadens Chihiro's social world: paid work, personal purchases, hobbies, friendship with Haruto, and group inclusion. Practical replaceability does not automatically produce relational erasure. `IM-C031` must therefore be revised away from a simple "usefulness equals belonging" rule. [IM-V08-E086-IM-V08-E101] [IM-V08-E168-IM-V08-E191]

V08 contributes the block's second principle: **a role is not proven by possessing the title; it is tested by ordinary burden, maintenance, boundaries, and material conditions**.

## 3.3 V09 - assigned value versus authored commitment

V09 turns the previous two volumes into explicit conflicts over who has authority to define value, vocation, and identity.

The tax opening converts creative success into uncertain probability rather than moral standing. A creator can have high current income without possessing a stable future income curve. Professional planning therefore requires uncertainty rather than the fantasy that artistic success is a permanent state. [IM-V09-E001-IM-V09-E012]

Aoba's reception crisis separates artistic evaluation from permission. Her editor may correctly perceive derivative elements while lacking useful revision language. Public response can also be genuinely negative without proving that Aoba lacks the right to write. Itsuki's intervention refuses to substitute unconditional quality certification for condemnation; he instead separates whether the work is good enough from whether Aoba wants to continue creating. [IM-V09-E013-IM-V09-E018] [IM-V09-E038-IM-V09-E046]

Kyo's roomshare and employment sequences link relational and institutional value. At home she compensates for perceived status inferiority by carrying disproportionate domestic labor and taking satisfaction in being indispensable. In employment she encounters contradictory conventions, predatory self-publishing, and the `3年縛り`: an unwritten expectation enforced against a weak newcomer but acknowledged as waivable for a creator valuable enough to retain. This opens `IM-C034` while also demonstrating why power must be tracked separately from the language of `常識`. [IM-V09-E019-IM-V09-E024] [IM-V09-E129-IM-V09-E190]

Toki's career history and Kyo's changing desire open `IM-C035`. A vocation does not have to arrive as pure childhood destiny. Contingent entry, competence, practice, social recognition, and repeated choice can become an authored commitment. This does not mean any imposed role becomes authentic merely through endurance. The decisive feature is later endorsement. [IM-V09-E129-IM-V09-E190]

The anime crisis expands `IM-C032` from one reliable creator into a system-level capacity claim. A production runner disappears, the planned episode structure becomes unsustainable, and the team has to recompress work under pressure. Discarded or failed plans are not equivalent to zero: process knowledge remains and can enable a new ending. [IM-V09-E047-IM-V09-E054]

The broadcast-regulation chapter expands `IM-C033`. Itsuki initially treats a requested wording change as injury imposed by a non-creator. Oshima answers from a different responsibility position: television has a low access barrier, accidental audiences exist, sponsors and station workers are part of the system, and version choice affects who can access the work. Itsuki chooses a rewrite in part because younger readers may not be able to buy the uncensored package version. The volume does not declare that choice universally correct. It establishes **bounded audience responsibility**. [IM-V09-E191-IM-V09-E205]

The white-box screening then supplies strong evidence for transformed fidelity. The anime contains compromises and altered surfaces, yet Itsuki recognizes it as unmistakably his work. Fidelity can therefore preserve identity through priority rather than literal surface replication. [IM-V09-E213-IM-V09-E218]

Finally, the TRPG sequence opens `IM-C036`. Fictional role-play gives Chihiro a temporary space in which a socially blocked identity becomes experientially inhabitable. Because the role is emotionally real enough to matter, the distinction between game setting and actual relation becomes intolerable. Role-play does not solve identity; it accelerates the pressure for recognition. [IM-V09-E219-IM-V09-E257]

V09 contributes the block's third principle: **assigned value can constrain a life without possessing final authority over what the person chooses to become**.

# 4. What changes from the V04-V06 checkpoint

## 4.1 From revisable self-authorship to authored commitment under uncertainty

The V04-V06 checkpoint revised self-authorship away from rigid consistency. Itsuki became more agentic by abandoning a rule whose continued enforcement would have subordinated finite relational life to an abstract equality condition.

V07-V09 go further. The series increasingly asks whether a person can **own a commitment without first proving that the commitment will succeed**. Haruto's TRPG philosophy is especially useful here: protagonists can fail, lose, suffer, and still remain protagonists when the action is genuinely theirs. Aoba can write without a certificate of genius. Kyo can choose editing before knowing whether a particular institution will hire her or whether the role will remain healthy. Chihiro can assert the truth of sibling identity before knowing how Itsuki will receive it.

At this checkpoint, `主人公` therefore no longer functions primarily as a status aspiration. It is best frozen as a language of **owned action under uncertainty**, while residual comparative uses remain a risk factor rather than the concept's whole meaning.

## 4.2 From post-confession relationship survival to mature ecology

V06 ended with major relational changes: Kyo's rejection, Itsuki/Nayuta coupling, and a stated intention to maintain friendship. V07-V09 demonstrate that these relationships do not persist automatically.

Maintenance occurs through food, games, roomshare rules, friendship after rejection, professional support, shopping, travel, advice, work, and play. The ensemble's stability is therefore not a static equilibrium. It is a continuously reproduced ecology. V08-V09 also show that explicit rules can drift. The roomshare begins with a reciprocity model and then slides toward Kyo carrying disproportionate labor. Relationship maintenance must therefore be evaluated by practice, not by initial agreement.

## 4.3 From aptitude versus vocation to a chosen but still untested vocation

The V04-V06 checkpoint froze `IM-C028` because Kyo's editorial aptitude was clear while her own desire remained unresolved.

V07 adds little closure. V08 strengthens both aptitude and institutional demand while preserving the question. V09 finally crosses the threshold: Kyo identifies editing as what she presently wants. The checkpoint therefore records a **partial resolution**: editing has become chosen vocation at the level of current desire.

What is not yet known:

- whether she secures a job;
- what institution she enters;
- whether the work remains self-authored under actual professional hierarchy;
- whether her empathy can remain bounded rather than becoming compulsory indispensability;
- whether `一緒に泣く` becomes sustainable professional care.

Aptitude, opportunity, chosen desire, employment, and sustainable fit remain separate axes.

## 4.4 From Chihiro-specific usefulness anxiety to a conditional cross-character mechanism

V07 opens the strongest usefulness/security formulation through Chihiro. V08 directly revises it: diversified reciprocal recognition gives Chihiro ways to matter that do not depend on one chore or even only on Itsuki. V09 then reactivates the mechanism when Aoba can substitute into a care role and shows a distinct parallel in Kyo, who experiences satisfaction in being indispensable.

The checkpoint therefore freezes a narrower and more general formulation:

> **Usefulness can function as attachment security when belonging is narrow, unequal, or role-contingent. Broader reciprocal recognition can buffer that mechanism, but direct substitution or status asymmetry can reactivate it.**

This is not a universal psychology of all characters, and Kyo's and Chihiro's mechanisms are not identical. `IM-C031` is a condition-sensitive relational claim.

## 4.5 From creator longevity to capacity, recoverability, and public ecology

V07's Yuu/Kaizu history makes longevity and moderate happiness explicit professional values. V08 adds Hoshii's reliability tax and Kiso's late entry. V09 expands these into production-system capacity failure, educational/career optionality, and process knowledge that survives discarded work.

The block therefore revises professional success away from peak metrics. A livable creator life may require:

- bodily sustainability;
- recovery margin;
- workload limits;
- alternative paths;
- social support;
- the ability to revise plans without treating discarded work as wasted life;
- resistance to defining professional worth by fame or output alone.

`IM-C032` is strong enough to freeze in bounded form: **systems become fragile when reliability is interpreted as infinite or fungible human capacity.**

## 4.6 From reader reciprocity to differentiated audience responsibility

V07 distinguishes engaged readers from the wider public exposure created by fame and adaptation. V08 shows that a coordinated public announcement is itself a creator-reader act. V09 then adds broadcast access, accidental audiences, sponsors, station labor, package-version differences, and wording constraints.

The checkpoint therefore freezes `IM-C033` in bounded form:

> **Public delivery is part of creative responsibility because medium, access, timing, wording, and audience composition alter who receives the work and under what conditions.**

This does not make every restriction correct. It requires competing responsibilities to be made explicit rather than treating all non-author constraints as aesthetically illegitimate by definition.

## 4.7 From concealed siblinghood to spoken reclassification

The previous checkpoint froze a long-running asymmetry: Chihiro already enacted sibling care while Itsuki's category map remained false.

V07-V08 make the secret harder to isolate. Chihiro develops independent social relationships under the male-younger-brother presentation. Correct disclosure would therefore not simply repair one factual error; it could reclassify multiple existing relationships. V09 then adds three escalating pressures: Chihiro's inference that disclosure might alter Itsuki's creative drive, Natsume's pregnancy and the fear of losing `居場所`, and TRPG role-play in which the desired sibling identity becomes directly inhabitable.

The block ends when the concealment can no longer be contained. Chihiro says she is Itsuki's real sister.

The checkpoint freezes the following distinction:

- **biological/category truth:** now spoken by Chihiro;
- **years of enacted sibling relationship:** already real before disclosure;
- **social recognition/reclassification:** unknown at the V09 boundary;
- **future relational consequences:** unavailable and protected from V10 backfill.

# 5. Frozen ensemble state at the end of V09

## 5.1 Hashima Itsuki - ambition after the equality gate

Itsuki remains ambitious, competitive, aesthetically opinionated, and strongly invested in becoming a creator who can stand beside Nayuta. Nothing in V07-V09 supports the claim that romantic maturity requires abandoning professional ambition.

What has changed is **jurisdiction**. Professional equality no longer determines whether he is allowed to love or be loved. V09 further pressures his protagonist model through Aoba: he can tell another creator that desire and love authorize continued practice without proof of exceptional value. Whether he can consistently apply the same non-totalizing rule to himself remains open.

His adaptation arc also matures his model of authority. He learns to negotiate among staff constraints, audience access, medium conditions, wording, and unfinished production rather than equating authorship with unilateral surface control. Yet the block does not turn him into a passive author. He continues to argue, judge, and choose.

Frozen V09 state:

- couple with Nayuta;
- professional comparison remains active;
- equality is not a romantic prerequisite;
- creative authority is increasingly responsibility-sharing rather than solitary control;
- protagonist rhetoric is better read as owned action than objective rank;
- Chihiro has just disclosed; Itsuki's reaction is unknown.

## 5.2 Kani Nayuta - secure love does not prove cured self-worth

Nayuta's relationship with Itsuki is stable across V07-V09. V08 gives her explicit present happiness and a safer shared-living arrangement after earlier health collapse. V09 also shows the power of direct reader recognition: Aoba approaches her not merely as a competitor but as someone moved into writing by her work.

At the same time, the checkpoint must not overstate recovery. The V06 effort/lovability pathology is not decisively resolved. Safer housing is infrastructure, not proof of transformed internal belief. Nayuta also possesses extraordinary bargaining power inside publishing systems, which separates her institutional experience from weaker creators.

Frozen V09 state:

- loved and coupled without professional equality being required;
- exceptionally powerful in market/institutional terms;
- capable of being moved by direct fan recognition despite large-scale fame;
- supported by safer domestic infrastructure;
- no warrant yet to declare compulsive effort/self-worth dynamics cured.

## 5.3 Shirakawa Kyo - chosen vocation, unresolved care boundaries

Kyo is one of the block's largest movers.

She maintains friendship after rejection, shares a household with Nayuta and Kaiko, develops editorial skill, actively consumes editor-centered fiction, seeks employment, challenges contradictory institutional conventions, and states that editing is what she presently most wants.

That is enough to move her from **aptitude without self-authorization** to **chosen vocation without occupational closure**.

But V09 also exposes the cost of her relational style. Roomshare rules designed to prevent one-sided burden drift toward Kyo doing most domestic work. She notices the imbalance and also feels satisfaction at being indispensable. Her future editorial ideal therefore carries a known risk: empathic care may become a means of purchasing belonging through overfunctioning.

Frozen V09 state:

- editing is presently self-endorsed vocation;
- employment/institutional fit unresolved;
- self-worth remains vulnerable to status comparison;
- care is a strength and a risk;
- Haruto/Hajime romantic direction remains unresolved;
- friendship with Itsuki and Nayuta remains intact through practice rather than erasure.

## 5.4 Fuwa Haruto - optionality, mentoring, and non-zero-sum creator identity

Haruto increasingly functions as relational and vocational infrastructure rather than merely Itsuki's rival.

He mentors Hajime, supports Chihiro's exploration of education and interests, and articulates a creator horizon that can include novels, games, scripts, lyrics, doujinshi, and other media. His TRPG role also gives the block a mature protagonist model: consequence and ownership matter even when victory is not guaranteed.

His attraction to Kyo remains real but unresolved, and V07-V09 do not justify turning social abundance or teasing into established reciprocal romance.

Frozen V09 state:

- mentor and host as durable identity axes;
- plural creator desire rather than one-medium purity;
- optionality treated as legitimate life design;
- rivalry with Itsuki is no longer sufficient to describe him;
- Kyo remains desired; reciprocity remains unknown.

## 5.5 Hashima Chihiro - concealment ends at the level of speech

Chihiro undergoes the block's clearest identity transition.

V07 makes usefulness/security explicit. V08 expands Chihiro beyond domestic service through work, hobbies, personal consumption, friendship, and social belonging. V09 then pressures the remaining category conflict through Aoba's substitute competence, career uncertainty, robotics interest, family pregnancy, and TRPG.

The decisive formal movement is `僕` toward `私` at the truth-claim threshold. The final statement `私は、兄さんの本当の妹だもん!!` ends the concealment phase as an event.

Frozen V09 state:

- actual sister relationship has been enacted for years before correct recognition;
- Chihiro possesses independent interests and relationships beyond service to Itsuki;
- practical replaceability still triggers anxiety under direct substitution;
- a future sibling triggers anticipatory `居場所` fear;
- fictional role-play makes the desired identity inhabitable;
- disclosure is spoken;
- reception, public reclassification, and future first-person register remain unknown.

## 5.6 Kenjiro Toki - professional realism, capacity sight, and acquired vocation

Toki continues to resist one-dimensional classification as gatekeeper or benevolent caretaker.

V07 emphasizes developmental editing: mature creators may require less direct control. V08 adds accountability and capacity blindness; he recognizes his role in a leak and in over-relying on a dependable creator. V09's career history shows that his vocation was not a pure original calling. Contingent entry became an endorsed professional identity through practice.

Frozen V09 state:

- adversarial caretaker remains a useful bounded description;
- institutional power and coercive leverage remain part of the record;
- he can communicate risk and admit responsibility;
- he sees creator and system constraints imperfectly, not omnisciently;
- his career supports the possibility of acquired vocation without proving all contingent roles become authentic.

## 5.7 Kasamatsu Aoba - permission without certification

Aoba moves from authenticity hierarchy toward a more defensible creative position.

Her imitation of Nayuta is real. Editorial criticism may also be real. V09 rejects the inference that derivative work or harsh reception removes permission to create. Itsuki does not certify Aoba as a hidden genius. He preserves desire as a valid reason to continue while leaving craft, voice, publication, and reception open to judgment.

Frozen V09 state:

- imitation is developmental evidence, not a final ontology;
- creative permission is separated from proven excellence;
- direct contact with Nayuta reframes hierarchy through fandom and recognition;
- an independent creative voice is not yet established.

## 5.8 Secondary figures with block-level structural importance

**Kaizu Makina** remains the block's strongest affirmative model of a non-peak creator telos: longevity and moderate happiness can be chosen achievements.

**Sekigahara Yuu** remains a retrospective warning that genius, influence, prestige, and audience scale do not guarantee psychological safety or health; her terminal illness prevents causal simplification of public hostility into a total explanation.

**Kiso Yoshihiro** establishes late-life creative entry and self-authored genre learning.

**Hoshii** provides the strongest individual reliability-tax case and helps open the wider capacity claim.

**Kaiko** demonstrates that commercial success can provide leverage for adult independence without making family support unconditional or autonomy complete.

**Setuna / Puriketsu** supplies a real consent/boundary improvement by accepting refusal and explicitly rejecting force; this does not retroactively absolve prior behavior.

**Ashley and Kaizu** show restraint and care in adult attraction, but V09 does not establish a completed relationship.

> **Status names relationships, but repeated practice sustains them. Practice can preserve a relationship, distort its burden distribution, or expose that the named status no longer matches the lived relation.**

# 7. Creative labor, career divergence, and institutional power

## 7.1 Career success is multidimensional

V07-V09 strongly reject a single career-success axis.

Relevant dimensions include:

- artistic craft;
- sales;
- prestige;
- influence;
- media-mix reach;
- income stability;
- longevity;
- health;
- autonomy;
- audience relationship;
- bargaining power;
- happiness;
- recovery capacity;
- ability to continue creating at all.

Yuu, Kaizu, Kiso, Hoshii, Aoba, Kyo, Itsuki, and Nayuta occupy different positions on these dimensions. The block does not permit them to be collapsed into one ranking.

## 7.2 Reliability can be an institutional risk factor

V08-V09 establish enough recurrence to freeze `IM-C032` in bounded form.

Hoshii shows the individual pathway: dependable performance generates confidence that more work can be assigned. V09's anime crisis shows the system-level pathway: production plans become fragile when workload assumes human capacity will remain available until it suddenly does not.

The frozen claim is not "reliable workers are always exploited." It is:

> **When institutions treat past reliability as evidence of expandable future capacity, the dependable worker can lose recovery margin and the system can become brittle.**

## 7.3 Institutions are neither neutral nor uniformly predatory

The block adds employment, publishing, adaptation, retail, publicity, and broadcast evidence.

Institutions can:

- create opportunities;
- match creators;
- teach genre knowledge;
- provide editorial feedback;
- coordinate adaptation;
- expose work to readers;
- conceal or naturalize expectations;
- selectively enforce conventions;
- transfer risk downward;
- reward market leverage;
- protect or fail to protect finite human capacity.

The `3年縛り` is especially important because the text identifies it as an unwritten norm whose application is discretionary. The fact that Nayuta would receive an exception makes market power visible inside the language of ordinary professional expectation. `IM-C034` therefore remains OPEN but high-priority: one block supplies a strong case, not yet a universal law of every institution in the series.

## 7.4 Vocation can emerge through practice

Kyo and Toki jointly strengthen `IM-C035`.

The corpus should reject two simplistic models:

1. **pure destiny:** a vocation is authentic only if it was always consciously desired;
2. **pure adaptation:** remaining in a role long enough automatically makes it self-authored.

The block supports a middle model: contingent entry can become vocation through competence, practice, social relation, reflection, and later endorsement. Kyo's current desire matters precisely because it arrives after aptitude was already visible.

## 7.5 Public communication and audience access are part of creative work

V07 opens an exposure ecology. V08 makes announcement form/timing collaborative labor. V09 adds broadcast regulation and differential access.

The checkpoint freezes a bounded version of `IM-C033`: creators do not communicate with an abstract undifferentiated audience. Different media create different access barriers, accidental exposure, economic constraints, and institutional responsibilities. A decision can therefore be artistically costly and still intelligible as audience responsibility.

## 7.6 Discarded work can leave durable process knowledge

V09's anime crisis strengthens the broader revision model. A failed or abandoned plan is not equivalent to returning to zero. People retain:

- interpretive understanding;
- coordination knowledge;
- failed experiments;
- constraint awareness;
- medium-specific insight;
- relationships built during the attempt.

This helps explain how transformed fidelity can survive production breakdown without romanticizing the breakdown itself.

# 8. Talent, comparison, permission, and definitions of success

## 8.1 Talent remains real; totalization remains the error

The block does not adopt a "talent is meaningless" position.

Nayuta is exceptional. Aoba can be derivative. Kiso has to learn unfamiliar grammar. Haruto and Itsuki possess different strengths. Editors make judgments that may be perceptive even when their language is inadequate.

The repeated error is converting one real judgment into a total human verdict.

At V09, `IM-C002` therefore receives a major revision:

> **Talent and effort are real but unstable when converted into total measures of worth, lovability, or basic permission to create.**

Professional institutions may still say no. Readers may dislike a work. A creator may fail. The series distinguishes those outcomes from the metaphysical claim that the person lacked the right to try.

## 8.2 `主人公` is no longer safely read as rank

Itsuki's comparative language persists, especially around Nayuta. That evidence prevents declaring status anxiety cured.

But V07-V09 increasingly separate protagonist status from superior position or guaranteed victory. The stronger block-level formulation is that `主人公` names **owned agency**: a person takes responsibility for choices inside uncertainty and consequence.

That definition remains compatible with failure, dependence, collaboration, or changing one's mind.

## 8.3 Moderate happiness is a positive telos

Kaizu's ordinary-writer aspiration, Kiso's late start, Haruto's optionality, and the repeated emphasis on bodily sustainability make `IM-C030` strong enough to preserve and strengthen.

The checkpoint rejects the assumption that the only serious creator-life goal is maximum prestige, sales, genius status, or cultural dominance. A long, ordinary, moderately happy creative life can be affirmatively chosen.

## 8.4 Recognition is plural

V07-V09 distinguish:

- award recognition;
- sales recognition;
- editor recognition;
- family recognition;
- direct fan gratitude;
- mass public visibility;
- hostile attention;
- one-reader impact.

These forms have different causal and psychological effects. Nayuta's response to Aoba's direct gratitude is not redundant with already having large-scale fame.

# 9. Family, siblinghood, and title logic

The third block forces the series' title problem from implication toward direct confrontation.

Itsuki continues to idealize fictional sisterhood while relying on an actual sibling relationship he does not correctly categorize. V08's Keisuke material demonstrates why fiction cannot be treated as transparent confession: a family member can interpret fictional obsession literally and cause real relational consequences without that interpretation being correct.

Chihiro's problem becomes more complex precisely because life outside Itsuki expands. The false category is not merely a secret inside one household; it is the category through which multiple friendships have been lived. Disclosure therefore becomes **relational reclassification**, not just factual correction.

V09 adds an important counterfactual. Itsuki says that having a real sister might satisfy some of the hunger driving his idealized sister-writing. Chihiro then reasons that disclosure could damage his vocation. The first statement is direct evidence; the causal conclusion is Chihiro's inference. This checkpoint does **not** freeze the claim that learning the truth will actually reduce or destroy Itsuki's creative drive.

Pregnancy then intensifies the title logic. Chihiro fears a future child who would be both a biological younger sibling and, in Chihiro's imagination, a more legitimate occupant of the family role. The fear is anticipatory: no rejection has yet occurred.

The TRPG finale converts the title problem into form. Fiction lets Chihiro inhabit the exact identity ordinary social life has blocked. The resulting disclosure establishes an event but not a resolution.

Frozen title-theme state at V09:

> **The series has now made literal siblinghood, fetishized fictional sisterhood, enacted family care, social category, and desired recognition collide in direct speech. It has not yet shown how the surrounding relationships will reorganize after that collision.**

# 10. Games, fiction, and social ritual

V07-V09 strengthen `IM-C004` and `IM-C024` while expanding what games can do analytically.

Food, alcohol, shopping, travel, apartment gatherings, roomsharing, Comiket, board games, and TRPG all function as low-formality environments in which difficult relations become observable.

The block's games have three distinct analytical functions:

1. **relationship maintenance:** people continue to spend time together after roles change;
2. **reductive modeling:** `Moteneba!` translates romance into optimization and thereby reveals what its mechanics omit about real desire;
3. **identity rehearsal:** TRPG permits Chihiro to inhabit an otherwise blocked self.

`IM-C036` remains OPEN because the identity-rehearsal mechanism is strongly evidenced but still primarily Chihiro-specific at this boundary. The checkpoint should not universalize one powerful scene into a theory of all fiction or play.

Fiction-within-fiction likewise remains non-transparent. Works refract desire, anxiety, professional learning, and social categories without functioning as one-to-one autobiography.

# 11. Comedy, sexuality, consent, and boundaries

The block preserves the corpus's mixed comedy model.

Itsuki/Nayuta's intimacy demonstrates that explicit sexual comedy can coexist with genuine mutual desire, awkwardness, care, and negotiated readiness. That does not convert all sexual comedy into harmlessness.

Ashley/Chihiro and earlier Toki hyperbole show why knowledge asymmetry and coercive framing still require scene-level ethical analysis. The checkpoint freezes two cautions simultaneously:

- **comic framing does not cancel coercion or boundary violation;**
- **prior misconduct does not make later improvement impossible or analytically irrelevant.**

Do not retroactively absolve earlier scenes because a character later behaves better.
