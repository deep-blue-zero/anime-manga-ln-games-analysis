# Core character reference

## 1. Scope, names and use

This reference condenses identity, desires, roles, written voice, directional relations and final source-supported states. It is a navigation surface; the linked models constrain hypothetical behavior, while monographs explain trajectories. The [continuous synthesis](IMOSAE_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) supplies the whole-series literary argument. The sole corpus entrypoint is [the current map](../CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md).

**No character is licensed for unrestricted extrapolation.** Five broad models support conditional application across several evidenced domains; twelve other subjects support specified domains. Recognition profiles answer bounded canon questions and decline a new private personality. Infants, unborn people and imagined adolescent futures remain insufficient for operational reconstruction. These local scopes do not populate or grade the separate corpus-wide A–E capability registry.

Before using a model select actual/drafted/performed level, era, interlocutor, knowledge and setting. A reader's later knowledge is not earlier character knowledge. Written voice is audience-conditioned; there is no performed acoustic evidence here. All exact new dialogue is a generated hypothesis, never a quotation from canon. The [protocol](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md), [readiness audit](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHARACTER_READINESS_AUDIT.md) and [58-case QA](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHARACTER_MODEL_EVIDENCE_AND_QA.md) provide the conditions and actual review.

Original ruby controls identity. In particular, 白川京 is **Miyako**, 相生初 is **Ui**, 関ヶ原幽 is **Kasuka**, 羽島和花 is **Nodoka**, and 木曽義弘 is **Yoshihiro**. Legacy English labels can still be search aliases; they must not create extra people or override Japanese. The [identity/normalization errata](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/IMOSAE_PRIMARY_IDENTITY_AND_NORMALIZATION_ERRATA.md) exposes old mistakes while preserving frozen prior artifacts.

## 2. Itsuki Hashima — 羽島伊月

**Persona and motive.** A professional novelist with theatrical sister-genre enthusiasm, genuine craft and a painful wish to be a protagonist worthy of admiration. He notices singular talent, genre possibilities and comparison. Care, friendship and family exceed the preferred aesthetic. Preexisting taste is not wholly caused by Ayane's rejection; remembered loss does not authorize diagnosis. `IM-V03-E096`, `IM-V03-E104`, `IM-V03-E114`.

**States.** Early comparison and a presumed younger-brother relation differ from the V06 romantic decision, the V10 disclosure destabilization, V11 functional recovery, V12 personally integrated work, V13 public hybrid and V14 husband/father/award states. Functional skill and self-integration can coexist differently by project. June6 is the verified birthday; V04 turns twenty-one. `IM-V04-E011`, `IM-V06-E092`, `IM-V11-E320`, `IM-V12-E323`, `IM-V13-E332`, `IM-V14-E025`.

**Directional relations.** Nayuta is beloved reader/creator/rival, not an award for greatness. Miyako becomes an editor with her own life after romantic refusal. Chihiro is already a cared-for family member before reclassification; her final goals are not his knowledge. Toki supplies critique and support without every private valuation being spoken. Haruto can be an envied friend and critic at once.

**Written voice and ordinary life.** Theatrical 俺 and genre vocabulary do not exclude tenderness, doubt or defensive evasion. The adolescent 僕→俺 self-correction belongs to a particular private act. Reading, games, manuscript delivery, food and family talk belong to the persona as much as crisis. `IM-V01-E002`, `IM-V01-E023`, `IM-V01-E029`, `IM-V03-E114`.

**Actual endpoint/limit.** A successful author, husband and father who can move Nayuta and still react childishly to Chihiro's critique. His final dream-based draft authenticates no child's future. Do not grant ethical completion, early disclosure knowledge or prophetic authority. `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E042`, `IM-V14-E046`. Use the [model](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_ITSUKI_HASHIMA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) for conditional behavior and [monograph](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_ITSUKI_HASHIMA_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md) for the protagonist problem.

## 3. Nayuta Kani — 可児那由多; personal 和子 / Kazuko address

**Persona and motive.** An exceptional writer and reader whose early relation to Itsuki's work helps organize career and desire. Polite extreme candor, enthusiasm, refusal and embarrassment coexist. The private Miyako book and desire for criticism prevent reduction to a devoted muse. Exceptional capacity includes effort; “genius” is not evidence of no work. `IM-V01-E008`, `IM-V03-E034`, `IM-V11-E325`.

**States.** Early career/先輩 desire; V06 experienced striving and chosen relation; later pressure on Itsuki's creative identity; V12 breakup/quitting and refusal of institutional reasons; renewed relation through addressed work; V14 wife/mother, possible retirement and a new wish to write. Stopping in one state is not a universal liberation or failure. `IM-V06-E033`, `IM-V06-E104`, `IM-V12-E155`, `IM-V12-E410`, `IM-V12-E513`, `IM-V14-E019`, `IM-V14-E023`.

**Directional relations.** Itsuki's intended work and her reading are different evidence. Miyako receives private art and chosen お姉ちゃん affection; reported attraction does not establish reciprocal Miyako romance. She can reject Takashina without treating him as objectively inferior. An editor's content intervention is not equally welcome to every writer. `IM-V02-E043`, `IM-V03-E040`, `IM-V05-E033`, `IM-V12-E410`, `IM-V13-E558`.

**Written voice and ordinary life.** Polite grammatical framing can carry extreme candor or a firm boundary. Earlier 先輩 and later 伊月さん belong to changed relations; a maternal voice has altered priorities without erasing the person. Games, gifts, intense work, food and children are source-shown, not a single endless flirtation. V14 reduces earlier obscenity but does not eliminate all body humor.

**Actual endpoint/limit.** After genuine household contentment, she asks for housework/Sora's care because she wants to write. E023 prints no explicit Itsuki reply. The later actual nominated book, 『どんな星空よりも、どんな思い出よりも』, continues Itsuki's new fictional world fourteen years later; GF obtains his publication permission. That assent is a different exchange, and the nested future is not actual child biography. `IM-V14-E019`, `IM-V14-E023`, `IM-V14-E024` p0118–0125. Do not infer a measured fair care routine, another guaranteed masterpiece or earlier hidden misery. The [model](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_NAYUTA_KANI_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) is broad conditional; the [mature monograph](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_NAYUTA_KANI_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md) explicitly succeeds the unchanged V01–V06 monograph as the whole-series reading surface.

## 4. Miyako Shirakawa — 白川京, しらかわみやこ; legacy Kyo alias

**Persona and motive.** An initially self-comparing university friend who wants to count and be wanted, then chooses editorial work she can claim as her own. Empathy and directness coexist with envy, performed sophistication and mixed satisfaction in being useful. Karate is real prior experience; its unchosen future is unknown. `IM-V02-E039`, `IM-V03-E036`, `IM-V09-E021`.

**States.** Persona/truth with Nayuta; direct confession and Itsuki's refusal; maintained friendship; materially unequal roomshare; vocational choice; independent editorial conflict/learning; finite dating gate and actual later relationship. The path neither proves secret genius nor makes her career a romantic accessory. `IM-V05-E185`, `IM-V05-E193`, `IM-V10-E150`, `IM-V13-E379`, `IM-V13-E380`, `IM-V14-E008`, `IM-V14-E024`.

**Directional relations.** She values Nayuta's existence beyond new output, while an institutional appeal to continue fails. Itsuki becomes her author, not a still-available romantic entitlement. Haruto's early offer/refusal and late attraction differ. Izumi's trust must survive legible revision and communication. Toki is a colleague/earlier opportunity, with a contribution she cannot simply replace. `IM-V12-E155`, `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V14-E014`.

**Written voice and ordinary life.** あたし, colloquial questions, embarrassment and dry corrections differ from professional polite forms; 不破さん can coexist with あたし. Work visits, food, care, job decisions and channel selection are diagnostic. She is less theatrically voiced than some friends, not psychologically generic. `IM-V03-E036`, `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V13-E380`.

**Actual endpoint/limit.** She acknowledges her own competence before the dating condition: next **Itsuki** book **she edits**, publication whether or not it surpasses the cherished work. Haruto's privately narrated view of fit with his own process is not her knowledge; his later false LINE goes to Itsuki/Chihiro. `IM-V14-E008`, `IM-V14-E009`. Use the [model](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_MIYAKO_SHIRAKAWA_RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) and [monograph](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_monographs/IMOSAE_CHAR_MIYAKO_SHIRAKAWA_MONOGRAPH_V01-V14.md); abstain on universal editorial success or completed cohabitation.

## 6. Haruto Fuwa — 不破春斗; private 晴彦 / Haruhiko form

**Persona and motive.** A commercially capable writer, reader, host and mentor who values enjoyment and has his own comparison wound. Skill, sociability and an articulate account of community do not make him invulnerable or always honest. He envies singularity while others envy his success. `IM-V01-E019`, `IM-V03-E085`.

**States.** Successful peer/adaptation anticipation; painful anime outcome and renewed reader purpose; early attraction/refusal sequence with Miyako; changed boundaries after Chihiro's disclosure; Ui crisis and Chihiro refusal; V13 maintenance/advice and chosen waiting; V14 accomplished public writer and actual partner with household limits. `IM-V04-E111`, `IM-V06-E019`, `IM-V10-E225`, `IM-V11-E218`, `IM-V13-E235`, `IM-V13-E455`, `IM-V14-E024`.

Ui's trust does not prove reciprocal attraction or make his reassurance prophetic. Chihiro's desire does not make friendship consent to romance. His sister's late reported obstacle does not authenticate her private whole personality.

**Written voice and ordinary life.** Public 私 and peer オレ vary with setting, while polite endings can coexist with either contextual role. Marketing homoerotic appeal does not establish private orientation. Care for fan creators, game preparation, meals, professional technique and fatigue matter alongside romance. `IM-V01-E016`, `IM-V08-E014`, `IM-V14-E002`.

**Actual endpoint/limit.** The Miyako relationship advances after the stated publication gate; cohabitation is not fully solved. ## 7. Kenjirō Toki — 土岐健次郎

**Domain and motive.** Editorial craft, institution-linked responsibilities, creator/colleague interaction and selected private work appraisal. He wants books made and authors developed, can recognize unusual achievement, and bears employer, collaborator and schedule pressures. No broad domestic/romantic model is supported.

**States and countercases.** Early manuscript critique is his E002 meeting, not Haruto's E017 peer review. Coercive deadline farce differs from actual adaptation refusal options. V07 private pride/withheld praise differs from spoken contempt. Izumi's opaque-norm problem exposes injury without proving every institutional rule malicious. V14 first-author grief differs from dismissal by a successor. `IM-V01-E002`, `IM-V02-E054`, `IM-V04-E039`, `IM-V07-E043`, `IM-V10-E140`, `IM-V10-E155`, `IM-V14-E014`.

**Direction/voice/ordinary work.** Familiar author speech can be casual and blunt. To Miyako he uses 白川さん, 私 and polite 悔しいですよ: actual respectful admission with frustration. Unspoken gratitude must remain unspoken. Meetings, reading manuscripts, explaining norms and making or withholding suggestions are work-linked ordinary behavior.

**Actual endpoint/limit.** He remains an editor and rival colleague while uncertain about making a different kind of future work possible. Miyako read/proposed publication of the private masterpiece; she did not make Itsuki write it. No guaranteed compensating masterpiece is shown. `IM-V14-E014`, extended end of chapter and following p0001–0007. The [domain model](../04%20Specialist%20Synthesis/character_reconstruction/IMOSAE_CHAR_KENJIRO_TOKI_DOMAIN_MODEL.md) explicitly complements the unchanged V01–V06 monograph.
