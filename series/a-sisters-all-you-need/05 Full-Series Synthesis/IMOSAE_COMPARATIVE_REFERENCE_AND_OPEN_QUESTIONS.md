# Comparative reference and open questions

## 1. Reusable comparison without a single master formula

The [full-series synthesis](IMOSAE_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) argues that singular aspiration becomes more livable among people who retain their own authorship, while preserving the contrary reading that the work remains indulgently author-centered. This reference supplies matrices and tests for reusing that argument. It does not replace the eight specialists, settle every disputed evaluation or authenticate later lives.

Comparison should identify an object and a measure before ranking. Artistic quality, output, income, recognition, audience significance, ethical conduct, reciprocal love and belonging are different dimensions. A comparison which turns them into a single score will lose the conflicts which drive this series. A difference in final status also does not establish a difference in personal worth.

The [character reference](IMOSAE_CORE_CHARACTER_REFERENCE.md) identifies people and their local reconstruction ceilings. The ledgers preserve changes by volume, while the five checkpoints preserve prospective interpretation. Here the primary unit is a contrast or a question: which mechanism differs, which evidence would weaken a claim, and what source could actually answer it?

The matrix preserves genuine excellence and difference. It does not declare all purposes equally successful under every criterion. A beginner can be permitted to make something and still need technical improvement. A master can make an important book and still owe a living reader respect. Evaluation should name the contract under which it operates.

## 4. Institutions and care systems: tradeoffs to compare

| Arrangement | Enabling mechanism | Cost / failure test | Primary route |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close author/editor collaboration | A reader diagnoses particular problems and changes intervention as ability develops | Coercion, opaque norms, employer demands and withheld legibility | `IM-V01-E002`, `IM-V02-E054`, `IM-V07-E043`, `IM-V10-E140` |
| Content-autonomous exceptional writer | Preferred process and strong independent judgment | Scheduling, contact, proofing and bodily limits remain | `IM-V05-E033`–`IM-V05-E045` |
| Coordinated publication network | Many people can produce/distribute a book reliably | One local delay externalizes costs; “care” cannot grant infinite capacity | `IM-V05-E026`–`IM-V05-E029`, `IM-V08-E050` |
| Miyako's specialized editing/agency | Directness, sensitivity, channel learning and particular author fit | Reliance on one person; not ideal for every author's process | `IM-V13-E379`, `IM-V13-E380`, `IM-V14-E003`, `IM-V14-E012` |
| Cultivation-oriented institution | Stated developmental commitment and support | Possible exclusion, paternalism and resource cost | `IM-V14-E013` |
| Autonomy-oriented institution | Stated creator freedom and commercial discretion | Unequal information, profit/growth pressures and exhaustion | `IM-V10-E155`, `IM-V14-E013` |
| Shared household/community | Food, money, hosting, childcare and a reason to return | Satisfaction may coexist with imbalance; gratitude may stabilize overwork | `IM-V09-E021`, `IM-V06-E044`, `IM-V14-E023` |

The last two institutional rows describe represented positions, not verified present-day industry policy or a universal winner. The comparison asks who has information, who can decline, how reasons become legible and who bears residual costs. Those questions remain meaningful when a spokesperson is likable and when an outcome succeeds.

| Source configuration | What it can establish | Wrong transfer to test |
|---|---|---|
| Itsuki's private 僕→俺 protagonist declaration | A local act of self-positioning after humiliation | 俺 universally means authentic maturity; private line is spoken, `IM-V03-E114` |
| Nayuta's polite extreme candor and firm refusal | Politeness can frame provocation, desire or a boundary | Her persona pre-consents to any act or fixes every maternal line, `IM-V02-E043`, `IM-V14-E019` |
| Miyako's あたし with 不破さん/professional polite forms | Casual self-reference can coexist with respectful question and vocational seriousness | Haruto's anticipated confession changes her actual professional question, `IM-V13-E455` |
| Chihiro's dated 僕/私 and later state changes | Audience-specific presentation, disclosure, sober/drunken contrast | One pronoun defines all identity; intoxicated speech is the universal “real” voice, `IM-V10-E029`, `IM-V14-E010`, `IM-V14-E045` |
| Haruto's public 私 and peer オレ | Public/private configuration with variable politeness | Pronoun fixes orientation or exclusive formal/informal class, `IM-V01-E016`, `IM-V14-E002` |
| Toki's casual author speech versus 白川さん/私/です | Direction-specific critique and respectful admission | Every private compliment becomes spoken; every line is contempt, `IM-V07-E043`, `IM-V14-E014` |
| Kaiko's わたくし and balanced-then-sharp criticism | Formality can support an owned judgment with a change in force | Graceful grammar means universal gentleness, `IM-V12-E323` |
| Setsuna's early 自分/～ッス | A particular youthful creator/game relation | Permanent tic or invented later gender event, `IM-V01-E028`, `IM-V01-E029`, `IM-V14-E021` |
| Nadeshiko's 我/persona and ordinary listener question | Deliberate pose distinct from everyday question | Literal vampire ancestry, diagnosis or guessed universal 私, `IM-V14-E011` |

Voice reconstruction should state era, audience, channel and speech act. A literary gloss is not an original master keyword. Written emphasis, narrated loudness or slurring do not supply actor timbre, pitch or accent. The [Japanese passage index](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/IMOSAE_JAPANESE_PASSAGE_AND_TERMINOLOGY_INDEX.md) gives exact routes and exceptions; the narration specialist owns the fuller argument.

| Level / example | Admitted meaning | Forbidden continuity transfer |

| V12 alternate/nightmare | Authored/dream boundary and its effect in context | An explicit alternate event becomes actual romance, `IM-V12-E064`, `IM-V12-E065`, `IM-V12-E068` |
| V13 bonus movie | Existing reading classifies it as work-within-work/alternate material | Its extraordinary scenario determines numbered-series end-state; see V13 reading and fifth checkpoint |
| V14 青い小鳥たち | Draft-level futures, privilege questions and formal distribution of interiority | Teen biographies or achieved careers enter actual endpoint, `IM-V14-E028`, `IM-V14-E042` |
| Author/illustrator afterword and publication art | PF: a creator's framing or imaginative possibility at that edition | A depicted astronaut possibility supplies achievement, `IM-V14-E050`, `IM-V14-E076` |

The governing method distinguishes TF, LF, PF, SF, FR, SI, IT and SP. SF would require admitted supplemental evidence; none of the eleven external groups is silently promoted here. Formal recurrence and strong inference can connect levels, but they do not erase the source class. A metaphor can be illuminating while its factual transfer is false.

## 8. Ordinary life as comparative evidence

Ordinary behavior tests whether a model has preserved the person outside the crisis which made them memorable. It must still distinguish a preference, skill, obligation, one-time choice and a narrated coping act.

| Ordinary scene | Question it can answer | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Itsuki reading/playing during idea search | How work and leisure can overlap | Every delay is necessary creative incubation, `IM-V01-E023` |
| Close Itsuki/Setsuna game | A temporary alternative competition and companionship | Permanent equality in all artistic dimensions, `IM-V01-E029` |
| Chihiro cooking/helping, then threatened by replaceability | How care and desire for a place coexist | Pure selflessness, objective rejection or a duty to serve forever, `IM-V07-E060` |
| Miyako's roomshare satisfaction/imbalance | How care can provide meaning while distributing work badly | Satisfaction makes a division just, `IM-V09-E021` |
| Ui changing out of outing presentation before writing | A particular mundane return from crisis | Exhaustive fashion identity or fixed schedule, `IM-V10-E225` p0197–0199 extension |
| Father/son meal and encouragement | Practical recognition without total confession | Perfect parenting or complete mutual understanding, `IM-V11-E254`, `IM-V11-E256` |
| Actual parent friendship | A new lateral relation and ordinary support | Guaranteed permanent family harmony, `IM-V14-E015` |
| Nayuta wanting childcare/housework support | A concrete enabling condition for art | Explicit agreement or proven implementation, `IM-V14-E023` |

The comparisons become most useful when the same person can be capable, caring, tired, defensive and limited without one state being declared the only true self. The local reconstruction QA tests precisely those anti-caricature boundaries.

Finishing the fourteen-volume assignment does not require making any of these disappear.

The strongest countertest for the capstone is representational: does another person's resistance actually limit the protagonist's authority, or is it safely made into another pleasure in his story? Chihiro's criticism/private goal, Nayuta's refusal/answer and Miyako's vocation supply resistance. Continued objectification, hidden care costs and softened consequences supply the contrary case. A useful comparative reading states which channel and standard it evaluates rather than awarding a single final ethical score.

## 11. Comparative lenses for other works

These are questions to carry to a separately source-grounded corpus, not asserted similarities from titles or genre reputation.

| Lens | IMOSAE diagnostic mechanism | Evidence needed in a comparison work |
|---|---|---|
| Creator-industry fiction | Private purpose versus coordinated production; editor fit; deadline cost | Actual work scenes, who contributes, refusal rights, scheduling/capacity and represented results |
| Genius and ordinary effort | Exceptional capacity with unseen effort; several local measures of success | Comparable assessment contracts, ordinary labor and counterexamples to each ranking |
| Ensemble adulthood | Refusal with maintained relation; vocation and love on different timelines | Directional desire, continuing mundane life, independent decisions and unreturned outcomes |
| Family and chosen affiliation | Category, practice and desired role diverge | Actual genealogy, care history, knowledge/disclosure and ability to refuse a role |
| Narrator/voice ownership | Private appraisal versus speech; polite candor; literal truth versus misleading report | Primary-language attribution, exact audience/channel, narrator qualification and work level |
| Social ritual | Shared procedure gives occasions, roles and costs | Specific rules or hosting practices; failed effects as well as successful gatherings |
| Ending and future | Actual closure versus draft projection; award versus criticism | Explicit frame, time boundaries, remaining questions and what the last image can establish |
| Ethics of representation | Unauthorized living-person futures alongside loving intent | Permission, objectification, independent interiority, consequences and disagreement across prose/images |

The actual precedent consultation informs the **craft of this corpus**. [Aobuta's synthesis](../../aobuta/V2%20Analysis/05%20Continuous%20Synthesis/AOBUTA_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md) was consulted in developed selected sections, with its V01–V15 plus canonical-bonus boundary preserved. [Oregairu's synthesis](../../oregairu/V2%20Analysis/06%20Continuous%20Synthesis/14_OREGAIRU_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md), supporting components and all eight character models were consulted for separated questions, developmental knowledge and conditional written voice. The LOGH [release guide](../../legend-of-the-galactic-heroes/05%20Final%20Release/00_README.md) and thirteen substantive specialists were used for independent topical responsibilities. [86's bounded V01–V14 synthesis](../../86-eighty-six/V2%20Analysis/05%20Final%20Synthesis%20and%20Release/18_FULL_SERIES_CONTINUOUS_SYNTHESIS.md) and selected evidence/character/adversarial components supplied audit practice; that boundary is not a claim the ongoing series ended there. The [Rudeus model](../../mushoku-tensei/04%20Character%20Analysis/rudeus/RECONSTRUCTION_MODEL.md) and V01–V15 monograph supplied the operational/explanatory distinction, not a complete Mushoku Tensei release.

Those consultations do not make benchmark formulations evidence for IMOSAE. They also do not authorize copying another project's folder conventions, assuming its old links are perfect, importing its source horizon or compressing several IMOSAE problems into one essay. Any comparative claim about another work's actual story needs that work's own admitted primary evidence and scope.

## 12. Supplemental and adaptation tasks remain separate

All inventoried external groups are deferred for this main-series release. The [supplement inventory](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/IMOSAE_SUPPLEMENTAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY_AND_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md) retains discovery/credit leads; the [crosswalk](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/IMOSAE_SUPPLEMENT_CROSSWALK.md) distinguishes deferred material from accessible embedded bonus fiction.

Useful future tasks would each admit an exact source before interpretation: compare a special-edition drama's script and performance with its prose rewrite; examine artbook interviews and visual context; classify retailer short fiction by authorship/continuity; compare anime/manga choices with particular novel scenes; inspect the standalone card game's actual rules; or study a performed audiobook register against written voice. A notice is a lead, not evidence that any such material has been read.

Future admission needs exact title/edition/credits, immutable source identity, continuity/tier, a source-facing reading, stable SUPP/adaptation/performance locators and an explicit mainline-claim delta. It must not silently complete Chihiro's career, the children's futures, a reported threat, a repaired relationship or a previously unshown voice. Where the new source is an alternate route, the mainline ceiling remains intact.
