## Work identity and availability

**僕の彼女はデッカワイイ** is published by KADOKAWA under Dengeki Comics NEXT and credited to sanorin. Its catalog copy identifies a school romantic comedy using the central couple’s difference in height and the privacy of their relationship. Treat the display title’s romanization as a route to this work. Publisher material is orientation for design and identity.
