# Episode 11 — primary audit targets

## 1. Private difference before public integration

Test how the soundtrack treats Miku and Riina during cohabitation and the discovery of private/public contradictions.

Questions:
- Does room tone or scoring leave their difference exposed, comicize it, or smooth it over?
- Are Miku's serious-professional stretches audibly different from her public cat-idol delivery beyond lexical `ニャ` density?
- Does Riina's domestic/family delivery materially contrast with her projected public rock register?
- Does the episode sonically privilege either private register as more authentic? The current prospective answer is **no**; audio should be used to test rather than assume that conclusion.

Relevant existing claims: `CG-CHAR-0024`, `CG-CHAR-0025`, `CG-MEDIA-0005`, `CG-VOICE-0008`, `CG-VOICE-0009`.

## 2. The serious unit-negotiation sequence

Target the 15:29–16:34 range where the pair tries to understand why Producer combined them and what continuation would mean.

Questions:
- Does music withdraw, thin, or remain active during the negotiation?
- Are pauses/breaths/nonlexical sounds carrying relational information not present in the transcript?
- Does the sound design frame the scene as reconciliation, experiment, professional negotiation, or something more ambiguous?

Relevant existing claims: `CG-PROD-0021`, `CG-REL-0012`.

## 3. Emergency live: difference becomes performable
