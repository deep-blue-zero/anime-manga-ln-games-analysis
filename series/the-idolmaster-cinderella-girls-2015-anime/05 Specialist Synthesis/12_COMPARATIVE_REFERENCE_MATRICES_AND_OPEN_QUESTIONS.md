# 12 — Comparative Reference Matrices and Open Questions

## How to reuse the completed *Cinderella Girls* model without letting comparison rewrite it

## Scope and responsibility

This document is intentionally different from Specialist Documents 01–11.

Those documents establish arguments. This document **routes** them.

Its job is to make the completed *THE IDOLM@STER CINDERELLA GIRLS* analysis usable for later comparative work without flattening its distinctions or silently importing concepts from other series. The governing rule is simple:

> **Comparison begins only after the object being compared has been described on its own terms.**

The primary *Cinderella Girls* reading is therefore closed under the existing source hierarchy: the Japanese audiovisual corpus, the sequential episode readings, the frozen E13 checkpoint, the post-final-sound 410-claim ledger, and Specialist Documents 01–11. External works may sharpen contrast. They cannot become evidence for what *Cinderella Girls* itself means.

This document has four tasks:

1. provide compact reference matrices for the stabilized internal model;
2. identify the correct primary documentary home for recurring comparative questions;
3. offer bounded cross-work comparisons where the project already contains sufficiently mature analysis;
4. separate **settled findings**, **deliberate interpretive limits**, **genuinely open questions**, and **deferred research questions**.

The result should function as a map for future comparative analysis, not as a second full-series synthesis.

# I. Comparative-use rules

## 1. Primary reading before analogy

A comparison is useful only when it preserves the category that made the *Cinderella Girls* claim specific.

For example:

- `persona` is not interchangeable with `voice`;
- `belonging` is not interchangeable with `unit membership`;
- `audience recognition` is not interchangeable with popularity;
- `professional success` is not interchangeable with personal fulfillment;
- `autonomy` is not interchangeable with adult non-intervention;
- `institutional plurality` is not interchangeable with institutional benevolence;
- `magic` is not interchangeable with fantasy as escape from labor;
- `maturation` is not interchangeable with becoming less stylized.

When another work appears similar, the comparison should ask **which mechanism is similar**, not merely whether the works share a theme.

## 2. Primary-home discipline

Every major *Cinderella Girls* conclusion already has a primary home. Document 12 should direct the reader there rather than reproduce the argument.

| Comparative question | Primary home | Compact stabilized answer |
|---|---|---|
| How does the series change structurally? | 01 | Solutions become premises of later problems; Cour 2 tests whether the ecology built in Cour 1 survives its own success. |
| What is New Generations? | 02 | A durable but non-exclusive professional home whose members become capable of divergent growth without ceasing to belong. |
| What does Producer learn? | 03 | Responsibility for possibility without possession of personhood. |
| What makes units work? | 04 | Different units solve different social/professional problems; there is no universal ideal unit form. |
| What is Cinderella Project collectively? | 05 | A developmental professional collective that begins as a cohort and matures toward networked community-of-practice behavior. |
| What kind of institution is 346? | 06 | A conversion infrastructure controlling selection, opportunity, visibility, risk, evaluation, branding and resource allocation. |
| What does performance do? | 07 | It makes possibility answerable to reality and produces multidimensional evidence rather than a single success verdict. |
| What counts as authentic image/persona? | 08 | Authored non-alienation inside professional mediation, not absence of construction. |
| What makes developmental risk legitimate? | 09 | Bounded, interpretable, supported and revisable challenge with continuing responsibility for consequences. |
| How do motifs work? | 10 | As elastic formal systems whose function changes with dramatic placement, not as fixed symbolic equations. |
| How does voice mature? | 11 | Through expansion of pragmatic repertoire inside a continuous marked voice, not normalization toward a plain “true” register. |

Representative routing claims include `CG-PERF-0004`, `CG-PROD-0029`, `CG-INST-0012`, `CG-REL-0017`, `CG-VOICE-0002`, `CG-MEDIA-0005`, `CG-CIND-0007`, `CG-MOTIF-0010`, and `CG-VOICE-0037`.

## 3. Comparison is diagnostic, not adjudicative

A comparison should clarify what *Cinderella Girls* emphasizes by asking questions such as:

- Does another work make performance reveal an already-existing truth, or generate new knowledge through action?
- Does another institution primarily select, educate, protect, rank, commercialize, preserve memory, or coordinate?
- Is group identity exclusive, familial, contractual, cohort-based, elective, or professionally permeable?
- Is persona treated as disguise, authored craft, aspiration, role, brand, or survival technology?
- Is failure evidence of unreadiness, moral error, market mismatch, incomplete knowledge, institutional injustice, or developmental opportunity?

The point is not to rank works by moral sophistication. The point is to identify **different answers to structurally similar problems**.

### Character-comparison guardrail

Do not compare characters by assigning them one abstract trait—“confidence,” “authenticity,” “leadership,” “trauma,” “kindness”—and then ranking intensity. The more useful unit is the **developmental transformation**:

> what problem the trait initially solves → what new problem that solution creates → what additional capacity later makes the trait usable.

That is why Mio's charisma, Miku's persona, Minami's responsibility, Uzuki's diligence and Rin's skepticism cannot be evaluated as static personality assets.

## Collective taxonomy: what Cinderella Project is and is not

| Term | Does it fit? | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Roster** | Yes, as administrative substrate. | Does not explain shared learning or obligation. |
| **Cohort** | Strongly, especially early. | Becomes too temporally narrow once careers diverge. |

This taxonomy is especially useful in cross-work comparison because many idol narratives default rhetorically to “family” or “team.” *Cinderella Girls* gives us a more discriminating vocabulary.

## 3. Performance as epistemic action

For future comparison, ask whether performance is functioning as:

1. **display** — showing an already established capability;
2. **test** — exposing whether a claimed capability survives public conditions;
3. **translation** — making a private or highly authored form receivable by others;
4. **experiment** — generating evidence the performer could not possess in advance;
5. **argument** — publicly contesting an institutional or relational claim;
6. **coordination technology** — producing common time among different performers;
7. **memory infrastructure** — preserving an event for later interpretation;
8. **audience formation** — creating the public with which the performance can then interact.

*Cinderella Girls* uses all eight. A comparative work may privilege only some.

### Portable authenticity test

When comparing persona across works, use four questions:

1. **Authorship:** Can the performer meaningfully claim the form as hers?
2. **Inhabitability:** Can she act through it without persistent estrangement?
3. **Revisability:** Can the form change when experience changes?
4. **Non-exhaustiveness:** Can other registers or selves coexist without being treated as exposure of fraud?

A public identity need not be spontaneous, private or naturalistic to pass this test.

## 2. Producer / manager philosophy

### CG2015 and U149

The closest family resemblance is not simply “both have Producers.” It is that both works reject a false choice between **protection and agency**.

CG2015 develops a domain-sensitive authority model across rookie performance, bodily safety, persona, long-term opportunity and crisis management. U149 makes the asymmetry more explicit because its performers are elementary-school children. In U149, age cannot be wished away by ambition: parental consent, legal hours, bodily supervision and adult contractual responsibility remain real. The useful contrast is therefore:

> **CG2015 asks how professional authority can create possibility without possessing personhood. U149 asks how adult authority can recognize child personhood without pretending childhood no longer creates special duties.**

The comparison strengthens neither work by making them identical. U149 places protection closer to the center of the premise; CG2015 places institutional pluralism and divergent career pathways closer to the center.

### CG2015 and SHINE POST

Naoki's lie-sensitivity gives *SHINE POST* a manager who can often diagnose protective self-deception with unusual precision. CG2015 Producer, by contrast, is repeatedly denied omniscience. His maturity partly consists in admitting that participation, peers, other producers and even Mishiro can reveal possibilities he did not foresee.

A useful comparative axis is:

> **diagnostic manager versus condition-building manager.**

Both care about truthful performer development. But CG2015 puts heavier emphasis on the producer's inability to know the final person in advance.

### CG2015 and Gakuen Idolmaster

Both treat production as developmental work rather than mere booking/logistics. The institutional difference matters: Hatsuboshi makes development explicitly educational, with producer training itself inside the system; 346 treats Producer as already professionally empowered inside a corporate hierarchy.

This makes Gakumas particularly useful for asking whether “production” is best understood as:

- pedagogy;
- coaching;
- opportunity design;
- career management;
- image translation;
- institutional advocacy;
- or some combination.

CG2015's distinctive answer is that the producer is a **professional author of conditions who remains subordinate to a larger institution and answerable to the performer's authorship**.

## 3. Group formation and belonging

### CG2015 and Love Live! Superstar!!

Both ultimately reject the idea that a group's maturity requires founders to remain its sole center. *Superstar!!* frames that transformation strongly through succession: Kanon's recovered voice becomes something Liella! can inherit and continue without her. CG2015 frames it through professional non-exclusivity: New Generations remains meaningful even when its members acquire additional units and opportunities.

A compact contrast:

> **Superstar!! asks whether a beginning can become inheritance. CG2015 asks whether belonging can survive plurality.**

Both resist fusion. They emphasize different temporal problems.

### CG2015 and IDOLY PRIDE

Hoshimi Production, especially in its mature extended form, is a useful comparator for “integration without domestication.” Units can remain stylistically and ethically abrasive, melancholic, elite, warm or eccentric while inhabiting one agency ecology. This resembles CG2015's claim that professional collective competence is ecological rather than uniform.

The difference is the historical burden. IDOLY PRIDE's units repeatedly orient themselves relative to Mana, memory and inherited miracle. Cinderella Project orients itself more strongly toward **future possibility, opportunity and continuing routes**.

## 4. Performance philosophy

### CG2015 and SHINE POST

Both treat performance as more than display, but their epistemologies differ.

*SHINE POST* often asks whether a performer can give hidden truth the right form. CG2015 is less confident that a pre-existing hidden truth is always waiting to be uncovered. Rin can discover desire only by performing; Mio can acquire perspective through acting; Uzuki can return before she knows what the return will finally mean.

Therefore:

> **SHINE POST often makes performance a truth-bearing form. CG2015 often makes performance an evidence-generating experiment.**

The distinction is not absolute, but it is analytically productive.

### CG2015 and Girls Band Cry

Both separate artistic/personal truth from market outcome. *Girls Band Cry* goes further in allowing principled self-definition to coexist with poor sales and objectively imprudent decisions. CG2015 is more committed to the possibility that professional infrastructure, audience relation and plural institutional support can make integrity and viable work coexist.

The comparison is strongest around the question:

> **What does commercial failure actually disprove?**

Neither work lets it automatically disprove personal value. Girls Band Cry makes the tension harsher; CG2015 devotes more attention to redesigning systems so that heterogeneous value can remain professionally usable.

## 5. Persona and image

### CG2015 and SHINE POST

Both reject the slogan “just be yourself,” but they take different routes. SHINE POST's lie-detection grammar makes self-deception structurally visible. CG2015's strongest persona cases—Miku, Ranko, Nana, Riina—argue that construction itself is not the problem.

The portable distinction is:

> **truthful form versus authored non-alienation.**

A persona can be highly artificial and still non-alienating in CG2015. What matters is whether the performer can own, inhabit and revise it.

### CG2015 and IDOLY PRIDE

IIIX provides a useful adult-professional comparison because its stage names, glamour, severity and antagonistic public surfaces function as survival and authorship rather than simple concealment. The broader Hoshimi model can integrate such forms without demanding sweetness. That parallels CG2015's rejection of normalization while adding a stronger adult/post-trauma dimension.

## 6. Failure, care and realism

### CG2015 and Maebashi Witches

Both works insist that encouragement does not end responsibility. Maebashi Witches arrives there through the ethics of helping customers pursue desires whose consequences continue after the inspirational episode. CG2015 arrives there through performers: adults may encourage, schedule, select and create opportunities, but they remain responsible for safety, information, recoverability and externalities after the motivating choice.

Shared proposition:

> **Respect for another person's authorship does not license the helper to disappear once the person chooses.**

Difference: Maebashi foregrounds accompaniment after advice; CG2015 foregrounds professional development under unequal institutional power.

### CG2015 and U149

U149 gives the risk question an especially sharp age-dependent boundary. CG2015's E13 already demonstrates that performer motivation does not overrule acute health risk; U149 makes such asymmetry systemic. Comparative analysis should therefore distinguish **general developmental paternalism** from **special adult duties toward children** rather than treating autonomy as age-neutral.

## 7. Voice

### CG2015 and Love Live! Superstar!!

The strongest convergence is methodological. Both audio-based analyses reject “confidence” as a sufficient vocal-development model.

Kanon moves from fragmented context-specific voices toward an integrated voice capable of surviving fear, leadership, intimacy and succession. CG2015's ensemble instead provides many variants of the same principle: Uzuki's phrase changes pragmatic function; Producer remains formal while expanding relational capacity; Miku and Kirari retain marked style; Nana demonstrates that deliberate stylization may be more fluent than imposed plainness.

The useful shared question is:

> **How many social and emotional functions can the same recognizable voice carry by the end?**

The difference is scale: *Superstar!!* concentrates this heavily in Kanon's founding/succession arc, while CG2015 distributes the phenomenon across a heterogeneous professional ensemble.

# XIII. Settled findings that should not be reopened without new primary evidence

The following are not “open questions” merely because later comparative work could frame them differently.

## 1. New Generations is not restored to exclusivity

The final state is plural. Additional units and opportunities do not erase the trio, and the trio does not regain legitimacy by becoming the exclusive container of each member's future. Any future comparison that describes E25 as restoration should be treated as a regression against the established evidence.

## 2. Producer does not defeat Mishiro in a moral referendum

Their models remain different. Producer's local/developmental resolution is vindicated in key cases, but Mishiro identifies real portfolio, timing and resource problems and discovers opportunities he missed. The institutional endpoint is contestable coexistence, not executive conversion into Producer's philosophy.

## 3. Persona is not equivalent to falsity

Miku, Ranko, Nana and Riina are sufficient counterexamples. Plain/private speech may reveal additional information, but it does not possess automatic ontological priority.

## 4. Audience size is not recognition

Mio's E06 error establishes the distinction, and later audience development only makes it more important. Scale, direct address, call-and-response, correspondence and operational engagement are different phenomena.

## 5. Professional maturity does not mean voices converge

Document 11's direct audio re-audit strengthens this. Marked voices gain range rather than being corrected toward a common adult norm.

## 6. Cinderella imagery does not support fixed one-to-one role mapping

Producer is not a stable prince/fairy-godmother equivalent, and “Cinderella” is distributed across performers, spectators, institutional structures and changing temporal positions.

## 7. E26 does not replace E25

E25 is the mainline ending. E26 is supplementary ordinary-life/paratext confirmation. Its different tone cannot be used to retroactively dissolve the stakes of the finale.

## 8. The Project is not best described as a family

Family language may capture intimacy in local moments, but it is analytically inferior to professional collective/network/community-of-practice language for the whole structure.

# XIV. Genuine open questions inside the completed anime

An open question is a question the source leaves meaningfully underdetermined—not simply one that the analysis has not yet answered.

## 1. How durable is the Producer–Mishiro equilibrium?

E25 establishes mutual correction without philosophical merger. It does not show how the organization handles the next serious conflict over resource concentration, brand coherence, slow development or performer-led deviation.

**What is known:** both perspectives remain institutionally present.

**What remains open:** whether 346 can repeatedly convert disagreement into productive pluralism rather than only doing so under the exceptional evidence of the Ball.

**Relevant claims:** `CG-INST-0012`, `CG-INST-0013`, later E25 institutional claims.

## 2. How will opportunity fairness work after permeability becomes normal?

Rin and Anastasia demonstrate that additional units can be legitimate. But a system in which members can pursue multiple pathways raises new questions of:

- scheduling;
- unequal visibility;
- resource competition;
- substitution burden;
- conflict between legacy unit obligations and new opportunities.

The anime proves that permeability is possible. It does not fully institutionalize a rule for distributing its costs.

## 3. What will Uzuki eventually call her professional specificity?

E24–E25 correctly refuse to force a false answer. Her broad desire and relational non-fungibility are legitimate. The source does not tell us whether later experience will produce a more differentiated artistic/professional self-description.

This should remain open because the point of her arc is not “Uzuki never needs specificity.” It is “absence of specificity at this developmental moment does not make her empty or disposable.”

## 4. How does the second generation alter Cinderella Project's collective identity?

E26 indicates reproducibility: the first cohort's developmental model does not terminate with itself. But the supplementary episode cannot establish whether the second cohort will reproduce unit structures, alter leadership, compete for resources or transform the first generation into mentors.

The open question is not whether continuity exists. It is **what form institutional memory takes when the original cohort becomes precedent rather than present tense**.

## 5. What is the long-term meaning of Cinderella “magic”? 

The source deliberately refuses exhaustive definition. E25 allows performers to name effort, inner light, bonds and feeling as experienced magic while the narration leaves the category open.

This is an interpretive limit, not an analytical failure. A future sequel could narrow the grammar, but the completed anime's mature position is that material infrastructure and experienced magic can coexist without one exhausting the other.

## 6. What does the wider audience know about these developments?

The anime increasingly gives audience sound and response interpretive importance, but most spectators remain aggregate or situational. We know much more about what performers learn from audiences than what particular audiences understand about unit changes, institutional conflict, persona or career plurality.

A reception-centered comparison should therefore avoid assuming that the viewer's knowledge is shared by diegetic fans.

## 7. How much bargaining power do different senior idols actually possess?

Kaede demonstrates substantial senior agency. Mika and others provide mentorship and professional modeling. The series does not give a complete institutional map of how senior popularity, contracts, departments or executive favor translate into formal bargaining power.

Document 06 therefore supports a plural senior-idol layer but not a general labor-law theory of 346.

## 8. How stable is “community of practice” once careers diverge further?

Document 05 identifies mature cross-unit learning and transferable competence. E25 shows plural professional continuity. The anime does not test the collective after years of increasingly unequal fame, external projects, adult life transitions or staff turnover.

The category is strongly supported at the ending horizon. Its indefinite durability is not.

## 9. How do performer-authored personas change under long-term market feedback?

Document 08 establishes that self-authorship and construction are compatible. It does not show every later stage where a once-authored persona becomes commercially obligatory or personally stale.

Nana and Uzuki demonstrate the possibility of alienation from a professional form; the series leaves open how often mature performers should revise, retire or strategically preserve highly successful identities.

## 10. How far can the multi-axis success model extend?

Commercial, technical, social and personal axes are clearly separable. Artistic and institutional success are sometimes useful additional categories. Future comparative work should test whether other performance forms require additional axes—political, pedagogical, communal, memorial—without retroactively overcomplicating CG2015 events where those categories are not evidenced.

# XV. Deliberate interpretive limits

These are not exactly open questions. They are places where the corpus imposes a ceiling on responsible inference.

## 1. E26 fine Japanese lexical analysis

The supplementary episode has Japanese program audio but an English supplied subtitle layer. Per Document 11, E26 can support bounded vocal-state observations but not the same level of fine Japanese lexical/register claim as E01–E25 without a new verified transcription layer.

## 2. Underdeveloped visual motifs

Doors, windows/glass, corridors, home and darkness occur meaningfully, but the 410-claim audit does not support elevating each to an independent full-series motif on the level of castle, stairs, shoes, stars or linked movement.

## 3. Hidden musical intention

The final sound audit supports dramaturgical claims about placement, recurrence, diegesis, audience sound, silence and technical sound. It does not justify inventing unverified harmonic/compositional intention or treating every lyric as privileged interior narration.

## 4. Offscreen corporate mechanics

The anime shows enough of 346 to analyze hierarchy, branding, resource allocation and project structure. It does not provide a complete legal, financial, contractual or labor-regulatory model of the corporation.

## 5. Creator intention

This specialist corpus is work-internal. Statements about director/writer intent, production-history causality or contemporary reception belong to the later external research phase.

# XVIII. Reusable comparison prompts

These are deliberately formulated so future work compares mechanisms rather than slogans.

## Producer / manager

- What does the adult know that the performer cannot yet know, and what does the performer know that the adult cannot know?
- Which domains justify directive authority, and which require performer authorship?
- Does the manager create answers or create conditions under which answers become discoverable?
- When the manager is wrong, what mechanism can correct them?

## Institution

- What conversion points does the institution control between private desire and public career?
- Does it primarily educate, select, protect, rank, commercialize, coordinate or memorialize?
- Which kinds of error does it tolerate, and who bears their costs?
- Can multiple production philosophies coexist inside it?

## Group

- Is belonging exclusive?
- Does group maturity require synchronization?
- Can members acquire additional affiliations without the original relation becoming false?
- What kind of knowledge travels across unit boundaries?

## Performance

- Does performance display truth, create truth, generate evidence, test a hypothesis, form an audience, settle a conflict or preserve memory?
- Which success axes align, and which diverge?
- What can the performer know after the performance that she could not know before it?

## Persona

- Who authored the public form?
- Can the performer inhabit it across multiple emotional conditions?
- Can she revise it without losing legibility?
- Does private contradiction expose fraud, or merely show that public identity is selective?

## Failure and care

- Who defines failure and against what reference class?
- Is failure recoverable, and what infrastructure makes it so?
- When does support become possession?
- When does non-intervention become abandonment?
- Who absorbs the externalities of another person's developmental choice?

## Audience

- Is the audience aggregate scale, direct address, fandom memory, market evidence, operational partner or moral witness?
- Can the performer interpret the audience response reliably?
- Does audience recognition create obligations or merely information?

## Voice

- Which lexical/register markers remain stable?
- What new pragmatic functions become possible?
- Does serious emotion require de-stylization?
- How do address terms, pause, silence, repetition and code-switching encode relational history?

## Dream / realism

- Does realism mean lowering aspiration, making aspiration administratively possible, or accepting uncertainty about outcome?
- When a dream becomes real, what new burdens appear?
- Does the work preserve wonder after revealing infrastructure?

# XIX. Compact comparative profiles

For quick future reference, the following one-sentence formulations are the safest high-level comparisons currently supportable by the project corpus.

### CG2015

> **Professional development becomes ethical and durable when institutions, groups and producers make heterogeneous possibility usable without claiming exhaustive ownership of the people who carry it.**

### U149

> **Professional adults must take ambitious children seriously without treating ambition as consent to adult burdens or treating childhood as disqualification from agency.**

### SHINE POST

> **Performance becomes truthful when private wound or desire is given a form others can receive without protective falsehood substituting for relation.**

### Love Live! Superstar!!

> **A recovered individual voice becomes a collective beginning, and mature leadership succeeds when that beginning can be inherited rather than permanently owned by its founder.**

### Gakuen Idolmaster

> **An educational institution can turn weakness, contradiction, fantasy and apparent unsuitability into communicable idol forms when development is plural rather than governed by one metric.**

### IDOLY PRIDE

> **Professional idolhood becomes inheritance after miracle: grief, rivalry and public memory must become new living songs rather than copies or shrines to the past.**

### Girls Band Cry

> **Artistic selfhood can remain valuable even when the person is morally mistaken, practically imprudent or commercially unsuccessful; refusal gains meaning through collective form rather than automatic vindication.**

### Maebashi Witches

> **Helping another person articulate a desire creates responsibility for accompaniment after the inspirational moment, without granting ownership over the person's future.**

These formulations are intentionally asymmetric. A good comparison preserves each work's own strongest verb: **develop, protect, reveal/form, inherit, educate/produce, remember/transform, refuse, accompany**.

# XX. Final reference synthesis

The completed specialist corpus makes *THE IDOLM@STER CINDERELLA GIRLS* especially reusable for comparison because it refuses several shortcuts that performance narratives often invite.

It does not define development as confidence.

It does not define authenticity as lack of construction.

It does not define audience response as moral truth.

It does not define belonging as exclusivity.

It does not define leadership as permanent centrality.

It does not define support as removal of risk.

It does not define institutional power as automatically corrupt or automatically benevolent.

It does not define failure as a self-evident event.

It does not define professional maturity as stylistic normalization.

It does not define Cinderella magic as either supernatural escape or cynical illusion.

Its most portable analytical contribution is therefore not one doctrine. It is a **method of preserving distinctions while people change**.

A performer can be constructed without being false.

A group can remain real without remaining exclusive.

An institution can be necessary without being omniscient.

A producer can exercise authority without owning a person.

A performer can fail on one axis and succeed on another.

An audience can matter without possessing the performer it recognizes.

A marked voice can mature without becoming plain.

A dream can become materially real without ceasing to feel magical.

A relationship can hurt because someone grows without that growth becoming a betrayal.

A professional collective can become resilient by making functions shareable while keeping persons non-fungible.

For future comparative work, the central question should therefore not be:

> **Which other idol anime is most like Cinderella Girls?**

It should be:

> **When another performance narrative confronts the same structural problem—selection, visibility, persona, failure, audience, group membership, authority, aspiration—what does it allow to remain distinct, and what does it require to become one?**

That question preserves the independent *Cinderella Girls* reading while making the corpus genuinely reusable.

And it identifies the remaining open horizon accurately: not whether the first Cinderella Project has “won,” but whether the practices it learned—plural opportunity, revisable authorship, recoverable failure, differentiated common time and non-exclusive belonging—can remain viable when the next performers, the next executives, the next markets and the next forms of success make different demands.
