- **Pre-Crisis Self-Limitation vs. Stated Catchphrase**: Twice in `TV01`, Karen recites the self-affirming slogan `"舞台少女 愛城華恋は日々進化中！"` (`03:33.7`, `09:10.2`), claiming she is evolving every day. Yet her demonstrated behavior at lunch contradicts that slogan: she already knows every line of *Starlight* by heart (`セリフ全部入ってる`, `07:09`) and deeply loves the play (`キレイなのに切なくて熱くなって`, `08:04`), **but preemptively abdicates competition for the leads**, assuming Maya and Claudine are untouchable (`いやあ 次も天堂さんとクロちゃんがメインでしょ ... やっぱ全然かなわないなあ`, `08:27`–`08:46`) and hoping merely for "a named role again" (`名前付きの役がもらえたらいいなあ`, `08:16`).
- **Conditional Trigger & Performance Identity Shift**: The Giraffe accurately diagnoses Karen's ordinary complacency (`朝も１人じゃ起きられない、主役になれなくてもいい`, `19:57`–`20:03`). What overrides Karen's passivity is **neither abstract institutional ambition nor solitary pride, but acute threat to Hikari and to their shared childhood vow** (`19:50`–`20:27`). Once triggered, Karen's physical competence undergoes an extraordinary leap (or theatrical transfiguration): the same girl disarmed by Futaba at `06:12` parries high-velocity arrows on the run and cleanly severs Junna's clasp mid-vault (`21:27`–`21:51`). Simultaneously, Karen exhibits **relational tunnel vision**: she tries to move Hikari into Mahiru's room without asking Mahiru (`13:37`), and she defeats Junna without grasping why Hikari screamed `ダメよ 華恋！` (`21:44`) or what losing the clasp costs Junna (`22:20`–`22:36`).
