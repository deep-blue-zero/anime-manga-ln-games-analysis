## 3. Visual & Theatrical Form Analysis (Method §4.B)

### 3.1 *In Medias Res* Spotlight Match-Cut & `龍虎図` (*Dragon and Tiger*) Dojo (`02:22`–`02:31`, `tv06_g01`, `tv06_s04`)

Storyboarder **Saeki Shōji (`佐伯昭志`)** and director **Yokouchi Kazuki (`横内一樹`)** build `TV06` around a two-stage Japanese theatrical architecture:
1. **The `02:22.4` Temporal Bridge (`tv06_g01`)**: Over young Futaba's sunlit Kamo River vow (`香子のことは私が—`), overhead theater fresnel lamps slam ON (`02:22.4`–`02:22.8`), cutting directly to a symmetrical *shoin-zukuri* pavilion flanked by **weeping willows (`柳` = `花柳`)** inside the Underground dome (`02:23.2`), so present-day Futaba completes her childhood sentence across ten years: **`—叩き直してやる！`** (`02:29`).
2. **`龍虎図` (*Ryūko-zu*) Fusuma (`02:24.5`–`02:26.0`, `tv06_s04`)**: Behind Kaoruko coils an ink-wash **Dragon (`龍`)**; behind Futaba roars an ink-wash **Tiger (`虎`)**. In classical Muromachi/Edo screen painting (especially Kyoto Zen temples), `龍虎` signifies **two supreme, co-equal forces locked in eternal parity**—visually refuting both girls' initial assumption that Kaoruko is the master (`お嬢さん`) and Futaba merely her attendant (`ファン第一号`).

### 3.2 Bespoke Railway Realism (`鉄道作監：高倉武史`) & The Shinkansen Door Threshold (`15:00`–`16:17`, `tv06_s19`, `tv06_s20`)

Just as `TV05` credited a dedicated `スズダルキャット作監`, `TV06` credits mechanical specialist **Takakura Takeshi (`高倉武史`) as `鉄道作監` (*Railway Animation Director*, `tv06_s32`)** solely for Kaoruko's flight to **Tokyo Station Platform `19`** (`tv06_s19`–`s20`):
- Every JR Central detail is rendered with documentary precision: the overhead LED board (`のぞみ 1283 | 12:23 | 新大阪 | 停車駅：品川・新横浜・名古屋・京都`, analog clock `12:04`), tactile yellow paving blocks, platform safety gates, and the **N700-series Shinkansen Car `9` (Green Car cloverleaf emblem — of course pampered `お嬢様` Kaoruko books Green Car even when running away!)**.
- Crucially, the Shinkansen sliding door functions as a **realist stage curtain**: sitting inside Green Car `9` eating Maya's `八ツ橋`, Kaoruko hears the departure chime, realizes running away forfeits Futaba's gaze forever, and **steps back across the threshold onto the Tokyo platform just before the doors seal**, turning `逃避` (*flight*) into voluntary return.
