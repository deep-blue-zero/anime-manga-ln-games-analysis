### 2.3 Spatial & Institutional Ontology of the Underground Theater and the Giraffe

Across `TV01`–`TV12`, the Underground Theater refuses reduction to either pure hallucination or mundane architecture:
- **Physical & Bureaucratic Reality**: It is accessed via Seishō's basement elevator (`TV01`, `TV11`) or a concrete stairwell behind the elevator (`TV03` `16:37`, `TV11` `17:27`); combat inflicts real bodily exhaustion, torn cords (`TV10` `20:38.5`), `-130g` mass loss (`TV08` `09:40.5`), and bleeding palms (`TV11` `01:07`); its four simultaneous Day 4 stages occupy one contiguous steel-truss hall (`TV05` `17:23.5`); and the Giraffe stamps official school documents (`退学届` `TV11` `03:39.0`) with his crimson hanko seal and arranges international transfers (`TV08` `14:33`).
- **Affect-Driven Stagecraft & Fourth-Wall Spectatorship**: Simultaneously, stage lighting, sound, and towering set-pieces (`勝手に動き出す`, `TV07` `13:55`) materialize combatants' memories and desires—turning cram-school masks into flashcards (`TV02`), solidifying light into wire-hung prisms (`TV07`), or crashing Tokyo Tower across desert sand (`TV12`). In `TV12` (`14:12`–`14:48`), the Giraffe's `90°` lens turn (`あなたと一緒に`) establishes his definitive ontological identity within the TV series: he is **not an external demon to be slain, but the personified appetite of the theatrical audience (`観客`)**—demanding unpredictable self-combustion (`永遠の一瞬`) yet satisfied without human sacrifice when Karen claims spectator agency (`観客席` → `ENCORE`) to author `結末の続き` (`CLM-0023`).

## 3. Differentiated Character Trajectories Across All Nine Stage Girls (`Non-Uniform Developmental Patterns`)

Rather than forcing all nine principals onto one developmental template, the twelve episodes construct **nine structurally distinct trajectories**:

1. **`愛城華恋` (`#1`, Sabre) — From Nostalgic Passivity to Collective & Spectator-Authorial `ENCORE`**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Unlike disciplined Maya or Junna, Karen begins in comic inertia—oversleeping, ceding leads without auditioning (`やっぱ全然かなわないなあ`, `TV01` `08:45`), and coasting on an unexamined childhood memory until `TV03`'s crushing eviction and `TV04`'s self-diagnosis (`足りなかったんだ、舞台少女の熱量には！`, `13:21`) completely overhaul her daily habits (`TV05` `00:58` dawn studio arrival, `追いつくからね`).
   - *Distinctive Mechanism*: Karen is the **only character whose power grows by expanding first-person grammar** (`私` → dual `私達` in `TV01`/`TV04` → cohort-wide `わたしたち` in `TV11` `16:55` → spectator-inclusive `ENCORE` in `TV12` `16:36`). Her `TV11` descent is explicitly **non-combative**, converting 7 peers' `舞台で、待ってる` into shared mandate, and her `TV12` kintsugi clasp (`18:05.6`) preserves visible fracture lines rather than resetting to unbroken innocence.

2. **`神楽ひかり` (`#29`, Corded Dagger → Star-Crossguard Sword) — From Unilateral Self-Sacrifice to Permitting Desire**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Already shattered before Episode 1 by her London `R.A.T.A.` defeat (`-130g` `燃料` loss, `TV08`), Hikari operates across `TV01`–`TV11` through **protective unilateralism**: banning Karen's replies (`TV08` `05:47`), begging the Giraffe to expel Karen (`TV02` `07:12`), locking Karen in a shed (`TV03` `08:43`), severing Karen's clasp from behind (`TV10` `22:05`), and slicing her own bare palm (`TV11` `01:07`) to become the solitary desert prisoner.
   - *Distinctive Mechanism*: Hikari's arc is **not** learning to work hard or care for others—she already sacrifices everything for Class 99-A—but overcoming the guilt-ridden belief that her wish requires solitary penance, finally allowing herself in `TV12` (`10:50`, `19:33`–`19:40`) to admit `会いたくなっちゃうじゃない` and **`これじゃあ華恋と、スタァライト… したくなるじゃない`**.

3. **`天堂真矢` (`#18`, Rapier) — From Solitary Apex Mandate (`嫉妬深い`) to Uncloaked Co-Elevation & Sovereign Generosity**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: `主席` Maya never suffers a training slump or domestic codependency. Instead, `TV03`–`TV04` peel back her serene `This is 天堂真矢` mask (`TV02` `23:55`) to expose **ferocious preemptive jealousy (`私、嫉妬深いんですよ。誰よりも`, `TV04` `15:19`)** and noblesse-oblige solitude (`一人でもスタァだ！`, `TV03` `21:03`).
   - *Distinctive Mechanism*: Paradoxically, **suffering her first Loop `N+1` uncloaking (`TV10` `18:37`) liberates Maya** from solitary perfectionism, allowing her to step into Claudine's French (`Avec toi — je peux aller toujours plus loin`, `20:10`) and graciously yield `Claire` in `TV12` to preside as the **7th Sovereign Goddess** (`22:16`).

4. **`西條クロディーヌ` (`#11`, Longsword) — From Ex-Prodigy `次席` Wound to Protective Self-Uncloaking**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Haunted since age 11 (`2012` `アリー` award) by fading child-genius dread (`成長したらただの人`, `TV03` `11:42`) and second-place fixation on Maya (`À terre Maya Tendo !`, `TV03` `10:02`), Claudine matures first horizontally by mentoring `#30` Futaba (`TV03`, `TV06`) and then vertically in `TV10` (`19:11`) when she **tears off her own unbroken cloak** to shield `"her Maya"` (`Ma Maya à moi`) from sole defeat—winning the equal recognition she chased since Year 1.

5. **`大場なな` (`#15`, `二刀流` Katana + Wakizashi) — From Maternal Time-Loop Director to Advocate of Ephemeral Fire**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Nana possesses **supreme martial dominance from the start** (repeatedly defeating Rank `①` Maya in `Timeline 0` and `5+` loops, `TV07` `15:39`), rooted in deep middle-school abandonment trauma (`第七中学校` empty coat hooks, `TV09` `05:53`). Her pathology is **protective anti-historicity**—using omnipotent victory to freeze `みんな` inside `2017`–`2018` so no one gets hurt or grows apart.
   - *Distinctive Mechanism*: Defeated twice (`TV08` by Hikari, `TV09` by Karen) and met as an equal by roommate Junna (`TV09` `20:12`), Nana discovers that her secret loop micro-edits (`少しずつセリフをいじったり`, `20:40`) proved she too craved live creation, culminating in her `TV11` (`20:03`) inversion of `再演`: **`いつか終わるからこそ、私たちは今を激しく燃やすのね`**.

6. **`星見純那` (`#25`, Bow) — From Borrowed Maxims & Honor-Student Desperation to Self-Authored Voice (`私の言葉よ`)**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Entering without natural elite pedigree (`TV02` `18:48` cram-school past), Junna initially armors her anxiety in overtraining, punitive hostility (`観客気分なら出てって！`, `TV02` `09:32`), and classical quotations (`THE CHEESE SHAKESPEARE`). 7. **`露崎まひる` (`#17`, Flanged Mace) — From Comparative Self-Erasure & Caretaker Codependency to Autonomous Warmth**:
   - *Baseline → Shift*: Despite genuine Two-Baton championship laurels (`TV05` `21:38`), Seishō's comparative pressure convinced Hokkaido eldest-sister Mahiru she had `"nothing"` (`私には何もないの！`, `18:25`), channeling her identity into maternal caretaking that secretly needed Karen to stay helpless (`お寝坊してよ、遅刻してよ`, `17:04`). Once Karen names Mahiru's authentic artistic warmth (`18:48`–`19:01`), Mahiru achieves stable, non-possessive warmth (`TV05` `20:20` `神楽さん` → `TV06` `04:09` `ひかりちゃん`; `TV11` English phone cue cards for Karen).

8. **`石動双葉` (`#2`, Poleaxe) & `花柳香子` (`#22`, Naginata) — Co-Evolving Breakout of *Iemoto*-Retainer Codependency**:
   - *Asymmetric Co-Evolution*: Admitted dead last (`#30/30`, `TV06` `18:05`) from a family serving Kaoruko's Kyoto `千華流` house, **Futaba** awakens autonomous `トップスタァ` ambition via *tate* drills with Claudine (`TV03` `13:18`), walks out on Kaoruko's quit-together demand (`TV06` `08:42`), and **loses `約束のレヴュー` on the scoreboard while winning its moral stakes** by blocking Kaoruko's self-cutting blade (`19:45`). Conversely, prodigy **Kaoruko** must have her slacking entitlement and flight/self-forfeiture reflex shattered (`TV06` Round-1 cut → Tokyo Station Platform `19` → `19:38` self-slash attempt) before dropping `-はん` for bare **`おおきに、双葉`** (`20:03`), passing Round 2 (`TV11` `06:11`), and challenging Maya (`負けまへん`, `TV06` `21:49`).

9. **Class 99-B (`眞井霧子` & `雨宮詩音`) — Institutional Co-Authors Outside the Underground Stage**:
   - Never summoned by the Giraffe, director Masai Kiriko and playwright Amemiya Shion nonetheless reject static Year 1 nostalgia (`舞台を育て進化させていく`, `TV05` `09:20`), enforce meritocratic Round-1 cuts (`TV06` `07:01`), draft and trim `100th` *Starlight* (`TV09` `00:08`, `TV11` `10:09`), and adapt the final production to embrace all **9** Class 99-A Stage Girls (`TV12` `22:07` wings).

## 6. Complete Musical Dramaturgy of the Television Corpus

### 6.1 Taxonomy of Revue Vocal & Acoustic Topologies (`TV01`–`TV12`)
