## Governance & Methodological Rules
- **Source Hierarchy**: Synchronized Japanese vocal performance in primary lossless FLAC / original audio is primary evidence. Japanese ASS files (`TV`, `MOVIE`) are verified against spoken delivery whenever wording is consequential. For `OVA01`–`OVA03` (which carry no Japanese text layer), transcriptions are derived strictly from direct Japanese listening, with uncertain syllables/phrases explicitly marked `[聴取不確定 / listening-uncertain]` rather than back-translated from English subtitles.
