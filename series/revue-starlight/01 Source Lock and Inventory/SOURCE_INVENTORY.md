# Revue Starlight Full-Series Synthesis — Source Inventory

## Scope

This inventory records the source boundary used to create the definitive animated-corpus synthesis.

The final delivery package contains **analytical documents and methods only**. It does not redistribute copyrighted episode, OVA, movie, audio, subtitle, screenshot, or contact-sheet files.

## Governing audiovisual corpus

### Television anime

- **12 television episodes** (`TV01`–`TV12`).
- Japanese audio used as the governing source for:
  - dialogue timing;
  - vocal delivery;
  - song entry;
  - music/speech boundaries;
  - choreography synchronization;
  - silence and sound design.
- Corrected Japanese subtitle/dialogue layers used for:
  - wording;
  - pronouns;
  - tense;
  - address forms;
  - repeated phrases;
  - lyric and title retrieval.
- Dense chronological retained frames, contact sheets, and scene indices used for visual navigation and direct frame inspection.

### OVA episodes

- **3 OVA episodes** (`OVA01`–`OVA03`).
- Complete Japanese audio and dense visual material.
- Available subtitle layer: **English**.
- Consequence for analysis:
  - visual, musical, and performed-vocal-state evidence remains strong;
  - exact Japanese lexical claims are made only where confidently recoverable from audio;
  - English subtitle wording is not silently treated as Japanese textual evidence.

### Sequel film

- 『劇場版 少女☆歌劇 レヴュースタァライト』.
- Supplied bundle contained:
  - complete Japanese movie audio;
  - Japanese analysis-oriented ASS/dialogue layer;
  - **1,670 indexed cues**;
  - **82 indexed scenes**;
  - **3,449 retained frames**;
  - **173 contact sheets**;
  - approximately **120:22** of complete audio.
- The movie was analyzed through:
  - whole-film formal reconnaissance;
  - movement-by-movement close reading;
  - major Revue music/action/staging concordances;
  - credits and post-credit inspection.
- The Japanese ASS is treated as a high-quality retrieval/transcription aid, not automatically as a publication-grade official screenplay.

## Narrative material treated as canonical evidence

- TV Episodes 1–12.
- OVA Episodes 1–3 as supplementary character/social evidence.
- Complete sequel film.
- Film ending credits where they provide:
  - career destinations;
  - travel;
  - messages;
  - first performances;
  - continuing relationships.
- Post-credit audition.

## Non-governing material excluded from silent use

The following are not used to govern conclusions unless explicitly labeled as later comparative questions:

- mobile-game continuity;
- stage-play continuity;
- manga adaptations;
- guidebooks not supplied to this analysis;
- cast interviews;
- production interviews;
- fan wikis;
- online plot summaries;
- secondary criticism;
- unrelated franchise promotional material.

## Source priority

1. Direct Japanese audiovisual evidence.
2. Japanese dialogue, lyric, title, and onscreen-text layers.
3. Credits/post-credit narrative evidence.
4. Navigation files such as contact sheets and indices.
5. Quantitative final-mix measurements as corroboration only.
6. Earlier episode/movement analyses as retrieval and hypothesis maps, not substitutes for the primary material.

## Verification rule

When an exact line, frame, musical event, or performance feature becomes load-bearing in future work, the original audiovisual source should be rechecked where available.

The final synthesis documents are designed as durable analytical bookmarks after the large source bundles are removed from active storage; they do not claim to replace the sources themselves.
