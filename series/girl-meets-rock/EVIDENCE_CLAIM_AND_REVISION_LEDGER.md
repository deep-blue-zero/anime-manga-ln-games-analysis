# Evidence, claim and revision ledger

## Responsibility and schemas

Route exact evidence to complete causal, relational, Japanese and graphic arguments, preserving serious rivals. No narrative claim, quotation, musical judgment or forecast exists now.

Warrant classes: OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION, UNRESOLVED. Confidence needs a reason. Revision: PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT, OPEN.

Dispute types separate witness/page map, timing/chronology, report reliability, knowledge asymmetry, group/role/audience, rendering, implied/heard channel, external-reference mismatch, interpretation, missing evidence and possible inconsistency.

## Update and preservation

Develop the full source-facing argument first. A song title, audience label, talent rating or group summary cannot replace it. Verify load-bearing routes in locked pages; pending locators remain unresolved. Quote/lyric excerpts stay minimal and source-verified.

Append versions with prior wording, boundary and full counterarguments preserved. Correcting a map retains old/new identity and tests equivalence; changed evidence needs substantive revision. Propagate across state, depicted expression, specialists and models independently.

Freeze prospective predictions before exposure; exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Synthetic output and imagined sound are never primary observations or independent support. Frozen prose and forecasts are not silently rewritten.
