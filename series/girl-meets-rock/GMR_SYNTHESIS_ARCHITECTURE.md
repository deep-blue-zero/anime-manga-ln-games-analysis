# Girl meets Rock! synthesis architecture

## 1. Purpose and INITIAL authority

This architecture supports a manga analysis of **depicted musical expression, group activity and individual relationships**, while keeping heard performance outside an evidence boundary that contains no audio. Bibliographic premise informs the questions; no particular song, scene, subject or instrument is established.

The planning range is Japanese manga V01–12. The [current entrypoint](CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) is the single current authority route; the [method](GMR_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) governs source-facing reading. INITIAL canonical authority covers rules and attributed metadata, not literary/music findings, owner architecture acceptance or model readiness.

The [initiation policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md), [generic architecture](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MULTI_DOCUMENT_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md), [reconstruction method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_METHOD.md), archive and execution/handoff policies retain authority over their respective responsibilities.

## 2. Source classes and absent-channel boundary

Locked, readable and actually inspected Japanese manga pages support claims about drawn action, lettering, depicted notation/lyrics, formal arrangement and assertions. A character's judgment about a performance, a drawn audience response and an analyst's inference are different evidence categories.

No supplied audio or AV witness exists. A page can represent sound and rhythm, but cannot establish heard timbre, precise performance timing, instrumental execution or acoustic delivery. This is an explicit source-scope limit, not a claim that an AV inspection task has been completed.

Real-world recordings, remembered songs, promotional clips or imagined delivery are not the fictional scene's admitted audio. If a source establishes a reference, external context may be separately scoped and labeled; it does not become direct scene evidence. New audio/AV would require its own witness identity, admission, modality method and capability review.

Metadata establishes reported identity/medium and bibliographic premise, not source contents. Covers, notes, bonus material and previews have separate component/spoiler boundaries. Other editions, adaptations and later acquisitions are excluded until explicitly reviewed. Primary images, recordings, lyric/text dumps and private source receipts remain outside Git.

## 5. Sequential and checkpoint contract

Verify numeric order and actual chapter/page/spread mapping before a run. A volume is the prospective source unit, with chapter/movement disclosure frontiers. Publication/source order, depicted chronology, subject information acquisition and analyst admission remain distinct.

An owner-authorized run states exact start/terminal scope, exclusions and checkpoint cadence. A provisional planning ceiling of three volumes between full reconciliations suits this twelve-volume inventory as an initial review proposal; it is not a story-arc claim or authorization. Dense group/state/form changes or source problems can trigger earlier checkpoints.

Accept complete reading and synchronized five-register updates atomically. Freeze entering/exit states and prospective predictions. No-change findings have an actual inspected scope; absent updates or unread pages do not establish no change.

Checkpoints retain full earlier readings, ordinary-life material, state histories and competing interpretations. Reconcile group, relationship, knowledge, graphic-expression and claim histories before role-gap review. Arrival at V12 inventory position cannot certify full-work completion, synthesis readiness or any audio capability.

## 6. Temporal, epistemic and continuity state

The state key is witness/version + admitted frontier + disclosure position + story-time constraints + subject/group knowledge + role/resources/condition + directed ties + interpretive mode. Modes are PROSPECTIVE_AT_BOUNDARY, RETROSPECTIVE_WITHIN_ADMITTED_BOUNDARY, EXTERNAL_CONTEXTUAL and UNRESOLVED.

A narrator/participant's performance evaluation, the reader's visible reaction evidence and actual heard execution are not interchangeable. Store the channel and warrant for every sound-related claim. Page rhythm is graphic pacing unless a narrower explicit source proposition supports something else.

Group membership and shared activity do not prove identical knowledge, goals or bonds. A later skill/role or relationship cannot be projected backward. State changes distinguish DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Ordinary contexts and actual opportunities constrain trait inference.

Page sequence may be simultaneous perspectives, reaction inserts or successive action; retain evidence and alternatives. A still instrument depiction does not show continuous technique. Lyrics may be quotation, performance text or another source layer, not automatically a subject's literal self-report.

## 7. Conflicts, revision and retention

Classify apparent contradictions as witness/page mapping, chronology/timing, knowledge asymmetry, report reliability, role/group/audience, rendering convention, implied-versus-heard channel, external-reference mismatch, interpretation, missing evidence or possible textual inconsistency. Do not resolve a gap by imagining how a scene sounds.

Use PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN. Record old/new formulation, exact source/knowledge/channel boundary, support/counterevidence, rival disposition, confidence reason and dependencies. Preserve full earlier arguments even when rejected.

A material revision reviews state/ties, depicted-performance/form, language, specialists and models separately. Frozen readings and forecasts retain historical bytes; later corrections use linked errata or superseding current claims with reciprocal authority metadata. A polished synthesis must not compress away ordinary behavior or serious alternative explanations.

## 8. Monographs and specialist convergence

A character monograph requires sufficient temporal, relational, ordinary-context and expressive coverage. It integrates motives/rivals, actions, knowledge, development, Japanese/graphic presentation and literary function; it is not a talent or personality profile.

Relationship/group studies earn independent homes when directed development, coordination, belonging, role/resource constraints or persistent interpretive tension require sustained treatment beyond one subject. A group result alone cannot establish every member's private aims or mutual feeling.

Music-depiction, page-rhythm, intertextual or institution specialists remain anticipated questions, not established findings. Commission only after recurring evidence demonstrates an independent load-bearing responsibility. A no-independent-specialist choice needs an evidence-based rationale and assigned role owner.

Specialists receive complete frozen readings and tested primary routes, return full arguments and source returns, and preserve unresolved alternatives. Convergence reconciles dependencies and disagreements before integrated readiness. Continuous synthesis connects people, groups, depicted expression, ordinary practice, language and form without inventing heard performance or concatenating abstracts.

## 9. Reconstruction and independent testing

Reconstruction is intended later for sufficiently evidenced source-established subjects, with behavioral and Japanese/graphic-expression scope. No model or readiness grade exists. Singing, instrumental execution, timbre and vocal imitation are outside this manga-only model boundary. Designated agents own global discovery/capability registries.

Separate evidence/retrieval, operational model, independent tests and any literary monograph. Identity is subject@witness/frontier/state with source/knowledge/channel exclusions and latent exposure recorded.

Twelve descriptive dimensions are goals/values, appraisal/threat, motive conflict, knowledge/errors, attention/reasoning, affect/regulation, disposition/condition, directed ties, role/audience, repertoire/inhibitors, written/graphic expression and development/exceptions. Coverage gaps constrain scope; dramatic performance scenes do not substitute for ordinary relational evidence.

Each conditional policy gives trigger/stakes/state → response family, inhibitors/overrides, exceptions, support/counterevidence, rivals and falsifiers. Distinguish disposition from temporary condition. Trace appraisal → decision → objective → recipient-conditioned expression. Record confidence, distance from observed contexts and abstention/multiple-response criteria. OBSERVED, CANDIDATE_POLICY and PROMOTED_POLICY remain separate.

Test ordinary interaction, pressure/conflict, uncertain/false information, changed recipient/tie, role/audience shift and consequential choice where evidence supports it. Separate decision fidelity from Japanese/graphic voice fidelity. No test may self-certify an absent acoustic channel.

Freeze genuine held-out predictions before source exposure; keep author/evaluator independent and disclose familiarity. Exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent outcomes are SUPPORTED, PARTLY_SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY, CONFOUNDED and UNRESOLVED, using diagnostic baselines, perturbations, decoys/negative controls and explicit opportunity denominators where meaningful. No invented accuracy percentage.

Synthetic dialogue, performances or scenarios remain derived tests, never primary evidence, canonical quotations or independent corroboration. Literary monograph quality does not certify predictive scope.

| Gate | Acceptance evidence |

| G5 Character/specialist readiness | Role-gap and ordinary/context/linguistic/visual coverage audit |
| G6 Specialist completion | Full scoped monographs/studies with source returns and limits |
| G7 Specialist convergence | Conflicts/dependencies handled; no-specialist route justified and owned |
| G8 Integrated synthesis readiness | Complete full inputs, adequate roles and stable channel/source scope |
| G9 Synthesis acceptance | Sustained integrated interpretation without imagined performance or lost local detail |
| G10 Reconstruction evaluation | Independent exposure-controlled model assessment at its actual channels |
| G11 Final validation | Actual files checked for source routes, authority, names/references, depth and debts |
| G12 Release/freeze | Defined immutable corpus, channel/scope limits, current routes, history and acceptance |

Source-complete is not synthesis-ready. Manga completion never certifies audio/AV coverage or official full-work completion. Interpretive uncertainty may remain explicit; missing required page inspection remains a hold.

## 11. Execution, full-output handoff and growth

SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS covers future readings/interpretive updates; DEEP_SYNTHESIS covers checkpoints, specialists, monographs and model/evaluator work; PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST covers architecture and continuous integration because false channel assumptions propagate. Stable indexing is BOUNDED_STANDARD, hashes ROUTINE_FAST. Resolve actual configuration via the [reasoning policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_REASONING_AND_MODEL_ROUTING_POLICY.md).

The [long-series protocol](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_LONG_SERIES_HYBRID_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) favors local source-capable readings/ledgers, fresh fully supplied synthesis, independent review and designated integration. Current capability is metadata/documents only. Verify transport, processing, STILL_IMAGE/TEXT inspection, interpretation and persistence under [capability routing](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md). No audio capability has been tested or claimed.

[Handoffs](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) include base snapshot, full exact input/output paths/digests, authority, source/channel/admission/knowledge boundary, owners and unresolved obligations. Return complete outputs and change/evidence manifests. Compare base/candidate/current, retrieve central evidence and check lost causal steps, ordinary life, Japanese/form detail and counterarguments. One mutable owner per register applies.

New sources extend inventory only after identity/scope review, not a run. An audio/AV expansion requires an explicit source/method/capability architecture revision and cannot retroactively certify prior manga readings as heard performance. Add schemas only for demonstrated responsibilities, with recorded migration and proportional affected-evidence backfill. Current registers preserve history; accepted readings/checkpoints freeze; real superseded work retains complete arguments and reciprocal authority metadata.
