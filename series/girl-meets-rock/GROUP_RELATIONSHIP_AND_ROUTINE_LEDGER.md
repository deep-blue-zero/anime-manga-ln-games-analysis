Group activity does not establish identical goals or knowledge. An audience reaction is not a direct skill measurement. A dramatic performance cannot define a subject's stable disposition without context and ordinary evidence.

## Update and depth obligations

Full readings own developed causal and relationship arguments; this ledger routes them. Create identities and roles only from admitted pages. Preserve source-required anonymity and later disclosure links.

After each accepted unit append actual changes and scoped no-change findings. Include routine conversation, practical arrangements and low-stakes choices when present. Negative evidence needs a real opportunity. Directed ties stay asymmetric rather than becoming one generic “friendship” label.

At checkpoints test whether memorable performance scenes obscure ordinary life, independent motives or group constraints. Review later explanations without rewriting earlier knowledge. Material changes propagate to depicted-expression/form, claims, studies and model coverage. One sequential owner maintains this surface.
