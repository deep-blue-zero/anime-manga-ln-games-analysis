# Girl meets Rock! analytical method

## 1. A manga method with a bounded music lens

Adapt the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) for supplied Japanese V01–12. The [architecture](GMR_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns accumulation. Bibliographic context identifies a music-club/band premise, but no primary narrative has been inspected. No cast, song, instrument, event or relationship may be filled from familiarity.

The source is manga, with **no supplied audio witness**. Analyze what pages depict or assert about music, expression and audience response; do not claim to hear it. This boundary protects rather than narrows literary work: graphic representation of sound can be analyzed deeply as form, causality, performance presentation and relational evidence.

Record prior exposure before a future run and exclude unadmitted knowledge. Fresh context is not automatic blindness. Keep chapter/movement disclosure frontiers so later material does not explain an earlier subject's motive or knowledge retrospectively.

## 2. Source lock and full page coverage

No narrative is authorized now. A future run needs an OPEN precondition and explicit bounded scope. Use `GMR-JP-Vnn` keys bound to immutable bytes, edition/language and versioned spine/image map. Presence, transport, verification, admission, inspection and closure are separate; changed bytes are a distinct witness pending equivalence.

Verify numeric order and actual chapters. Map all image occurrences including covers, inserts, narrative, notes, bonus, colophon and previews. Extras can carry later knowledge. Page states are REVIEWED_MATERIAL, NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED and UNRESOLVED_READABILITY, with exact extent/reason.

Inspect actual readable STILL_IMAGE pages, including silence, dense lettering, small diagrams or musical marks if present. OCR/search/contact sheets are retrieval aids, not coverage. Container integrity cannot certify legibility, completeness or correct order.

## 3. Locators and quotation discipline

Use witness/version + verified chapter + one-based image occurrence including covers + visible printed label if any + panel/balloon/region ordinal. Bind to immutable digest, spine/member and map version; state reading-order conventions and ambiguity. Crops keep coordinates and full-page context; spreads retain paired images and verification basis. Page-turn claims require actual facing layout.

Reopen the page for central Japanese, image, instrument or lyric/lettering observations. Mark EXACT_VERIFIED, WORKING_TRANSCRIPTION or PARAPHRASE and label glosses. Preserve consequential Japanese, ruby/base, address, ellipsis, modality and register. A recognized lyric or title in memory is not source verification. Use only the minimal eligible lyric/text excerpt needed for a claim, with exact page route; never copy bulk lyrics or dialogue into Git.

Test load-bearing retrieval. A failed/pending route remains RETRIEVAL_UNRESOLVED. Private IDs, source pages, OCR dumps and recordings stay in evidence/working storage.

## 4. Depicted performance is not heard execution

For a source-established performance passage, separate:

- Visible action/posture/instrument depiction and page arrangement.
- Printed lyric, notation, sound effect or descriptive text.
- Character/narrator statements about sound or skill.
- Audience behavior, reaction and reported judgment.
- Analyst inference about narrative effect, with alternative explanations.
- Any separately admitted external recording/reference, which is not the sound of this fictional scene by default.

Do not infer exact pitch, tempo, timbre, tuning, vocal technique, synchronization or executed movement from still images unless the source explicitly establishes a narrower proposition. A written evaluative claim is evidence of that judgment; a favorable reaction does not independently measure musical quality. Notation, if present, is depicted notation, not proof of actual rendition.

Graphic pacing or repeated panels can suggest rhythm; label it a page/form effect rather than measured musical timing. A song title can function as an intertextual reference if actually present, but remembered recordings cannot fill the scene's acoustic channel. External music context needs explicit scope and source labeling.

## 5. Causality, knowledge and group practice

Segment by source-established goals, configurations, places, information or formal shifts. Develop conditions → perception → appraisal → affect → options/constraints → action/non-action → consequence. Mark missing links and rival causes; adjacent reactions do not uniquely prove why an audience or participant responded.

Keep narrator presentation, each subject's knowledge/belief, reader information and analyst inference separate. Publication/source order, depicted chronology, information acquisition and admission are distinct. Later explanation cannot enter an earlier state.

If band/group roles, practice or institutions are established, distinguish membership, assigned role, actual contribution, resources, stated rule/custom and observed enforcement. A group result does not prove each participant's intent, knowledge or skill. Preserve coordination, labor and interpersonal constraints alongside individual action.

## 6. Ordinary character and directed relationships

Create subject identities only from admitted evidence, preserving aliases and concealment boundaries. Separate speech, behavior, thought text, reports and public role. A visible expression does not uniquely establish emotion or diagnosis.

Read ordinary conversation, practical arrangements, practice/rest, waiting, humor and low-stakes decisions as first-class evidence where present. A dramatic performance or crisis cannot define a whole person. Negative evidence needs a real opportunity; no depiction is not refusal or lack of ability.

Relationships A→B and B→A require independent information, expectations, conduct, access, obligation, trust/conflict and source-earned dimensions. Shared activity does not automatically prove mutual intimacy or identical goals. Preserve audience and group effects without flattening interpersonal motives.

Change causes are DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Distinguish temporary condition from durable tendency and retain counterexamples. Depiction, explanation, evaluation and endorsement remain separate.

## 7. Japanese voice and graphic form

Attribute speaker, thinker, narrator and recipient by tail, placement and context. Distinguish speech, thought, editorial caption, lyric/quotation, diegetic text and sound effects. A lyric's speaking position may differ from a character's own proposition; do not automatically treat quoted lyrics as autobiography.

Trace appraisal → objective → speech act → recipient/audience filter → register → words/lettering. Compare ordinary and pressured contexts. Preserve Japanese forms and alternative glosses. Graphic lettering expresses a convention, not measurable acoustic delivery.

Analyze panel/page/spread layout, framing, gaze, reaction sequence, gutters, silence, rendering shifts, image/text integration and implied rhythm. Explain observed feature → supported effect → rival. Silent pages can be central to a work about music. Symbolism, tone and recurrence need variation and counterinstances, not merely a striking image or recognized reference.

## 8. Claims, counterreading and revision

Warrant classes are OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTION, EXTERNAL_REFERENCE, EVALUATION and UNRESOLVED, with confidence/reason. Every unit's thesis has a serious rival and a disconfirmation condition.

Conflicts may be witness/page map, timing/chronology, report reliability, knowledge asymmetry, group/role/audience, rendering convention, implied-versus-heard channel, interpretation, missing evidence or possible inconsistency. Do not settle them by imagining a performance.

Revision uses PRESERVE, STRENGTHEN, REVISE, DOWNGRADE, REJECT and OPEN, keeping old/new formulations, source/knowledge/channel boundaries, evidence, rivals and dependencies. Frozen prospective states are not rewritten. Sensitive or contentious material is described only as needed for sourced analysis, without moral prefiltering or retrospective justification.

## 9. Full output and acceptance

Each future reading includes exact receipt/coverage; entering state/forecasts; developed thesis/rival; movement-level causal/page argument; subjects, directed ties and group state if established; ordinary controls; Japanese expression; drawn performance/lyric/audience/form analysis where earned; recurrence/counterexamples; claim transitions; five-register manifest; exit freeze; limits and completion decision.

Do not reduce a performance passage to a song list, a relationship to “bonding,” or a subject to a talent label. Full graphic, causal, linguistic and ordinary-life reasoning must survive at the needed length. Summary tables are retrieval aids, not substitutes.

Before semantic acceptance, promotion or integration, sample introduction, substantial middle, conclusion/exit and major relationships/ambiguous actors. Check names, purposeful roles, pronouns, concealment, speaker/thinker/recipient and knowledge frontier. Classify JUSTIFIED_ROLE_REFERENCE, SOURCE_REQUIRED_ANONYMITY, KNOWLEDGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRED, UNNECESSARY_GENERICIZATION, AMBIGUOUS_REFERENT or INCORRECT_IDENTITY_OR_ATTRIBUTION. Record sample/findings and repair supported defects.

Accept complete prose and synchronized registers atomically. Scoped no-change findings require actual inspection. Unreadable pages, untested central locators and missing updates remain holds. The absence of audio is a source-boundary limit, not a falsely closed AV debt.

### Current-unit story synopsis (initiation policy §5.3)

Each authorized plot-bearing volume reading must include a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near the front of its canonical artifact, after receipt/coverage and entering context, before the developed thesis/rival, extended interpretation and ledger inventories. Mark inherited developments as inherited and distinguish them from events or disclosures newly encountered in this volume.

Write coherent, accessible prose giving the opening situation, source-established participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, turning points/disclosures and the ending state. Preserve meaningful physical and scene mechanics: individual contributions, coordinated activity, practical interactions and audience actions where established. Include consequential ordinary scenes as well as any depicted performance. Follow verified chapters, causal movements and panel presentation, distinguishing successive action from reaction inserts or simultaneous viewpoints and marking inferred gutter transitions. Detail follows narrative density without a fixed length or mandatory chapter template.

Use only the current admitted unit and explicitly distinguished inherited context. Separate drawn events from speech/thought, lyrics or quotation, narrator presentation, reported judgments, conjecture, disputed attribution and unresolved outcomes. Newly disclosed earlier events are not new story-time events. Depicted action, printed musical expression and audience response do not establish heard timbre, tempo, delivery, synchronization or continuous instrumental technique. Do not turn a participant's evaluation into an independently demonstrated fact or a lyric into an unqualified personal statement. Give material events retrievable witness/chapter/image/panel locators under §3, or stable evidence references resolving to them, within readable prose. Later volumes, remembered songs, external recordings and adaptations cannot supply missing events or an acoustic channel.

Compose or finalize this unit-end, spoiler-bearing orientation after applicable prospective captures are frozen, and shield analysts assigned an earlier chapter, movement or volume horizon from the full spoiler-bearing reading. It is neither a prospective freeze nor independent evidence. Full causal, individual/group, directed-relationship, ordinary-life, Japanese, graphic-form and counterreading work, all five register updates and the explicit absent-audio boundary remain required. Only a genuinely non-narrative separately scoped pass may record inapplicability with a reason; a narrative performance passage cannot be exempted as musical material.

## 10. Reconstruction and full-output transfer

Future models are conditional behavioral/written-graphic models, distinct from monographs and performance simulation. Policies need trigger/stakes/state, response family, inhibitors, exceptions, counterevidence, rivals/falsifiers, confidence/evidence distance and abstention/multiple-response criteria. Distinguish disposition from condition; trace appraisal → decision → recipient-conditioned expression. A manga-only model cannot certify singing, instrumental execution or vocal imitation.

Freeze genuinely held-out predictions before exposure; exposed exercises are RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST. Independent evaluation owns outcomes/readiness; synthetic output never becomes primary evidence or canon. [Archive authority](../../governance/source-policies/ARCHIVE_AUTHORITY_AND_SUPERSESSION_POLICY.md) and the [handoff contract](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md) preserve complete readings, source/channel boundaries and counterarguments across sessions.
