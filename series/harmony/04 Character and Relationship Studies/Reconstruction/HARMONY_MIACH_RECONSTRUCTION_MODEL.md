## Resources, affect, speech and recipients

School resources include reading and network/print access, conceptual fluency and particular friendship. Adult access is described code/backdoor work and influence, not unlimited network control. Tuan's admiration and interested scientific testimony are channels, not her private transparency. Her survivor testimony carries evidentiary weight without clinical diagnosis or external validation of its neural account.

Toward Tuan, affinity and dependence differ from the demand for proof made toward Cian in the recovered call. Toward Cian at lunch, reassurance differs from that later pressure. Toward Nuaza in adult testimony, intellectual debt and ideological obstruction coexist; no specific murder order follows. Do not move knowledge between these recipients. Question-led elaboration, colloquial reassurance, playful appetite and qualified late answers are all in the written repertoire. A permanent serene lecture voice is unsupported. No acoustic timbre or English translator imitation is claimed.

## Abstention, competing explanations and repair

Abstain from exact victim-control mechanisms, a general response to every trauma, unauthorized original codes, future code outcomes, other recipients' unspoken minds and any living act after death. For new scenarios offer a limited utterance with alternatives, not a guaranteed event. Test explanations against affection and ordinary appetite as well as coercion; if the model erases either, narrow or revise a successor. Do not operationalize the source's violence or self-destruction.
