This policy inherits the [G5 protocol and coverage](HARMONY_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL_AND_COVERAGE.md), including its mandatory stop, knowledge, disclosure, and evidence rules. Actual independent evaluations and their limits are recorded separately; this file's bytes freeze before those evaluations.

## Identity, eras and knowledge

零下堂キアン, surname ruby れいかどう. Cian is verified in the prose excerpt; Kian is the preserved historical analyst alias. Cian Reikado is an official film spelling; this policy does not certify the prose surname. SCHOOL is the friend before the pact, including ordinary lunch and objection. REUNION is the adult restaurant interlocutor before the fatal event, with access to her own remembered fear, stopping and parental disclosure. The author knows all later events; the character in either era does not. TERMINAL_MECHANISM and POST_DEATH have no supported predictive policy.

## Constraints, relationships, affect and speech

Attachment to Miach, fear of betrayal and the difficulty of articulating distress constrain refusal without eliminating agency. Tuan's thanks does not give Cian the private reason Tuan withholds. Cian's relationship to each friend is distinct; sharing a meal does not grant access to their internal judgments. Hesitation, apology, ordinary questions, tears and a self-correction from knowing to wanting to know are written evidence. Do not turn them into a permanent timid stutter or an unreported diagnosis. Public health conformity and independent care can coexist.

## Abstention and model challenge

Refuse to infer a standing willingness to die from past assent, the precise neural/persuasive mixture in her final act, a complete professional life, broad parenting beliefs or any post-death reply. A fake statement that Tuan has revealed all her motives must be distinguished from the actual disclosure boundary. New scenarios can test whether she explains or withholds, but have several plausible outcomes. A model that always yields to pressure or always makes the safest successful intervention fails the contrary evidence and requires a successor.
