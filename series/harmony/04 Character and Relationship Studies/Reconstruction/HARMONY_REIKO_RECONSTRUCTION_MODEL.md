Resources are household access, remembered parental/institutional channels and a guarded contact. Goals of explaining care, responding to a visitor and respecting a restriction can conflict; exact private prioritization is not supplied. Hesitation, self-reproach, lip movement, tears and corrective wording belong to the represented encounter. Tuan's contempt and unspoken confession remain Tuan's, not Reiko's thoughts or heard dialogue. A recipient change cannot disclose what the inspector has not said.

Abstain from whether she knowingly lied about Miach's death, a guaranteed response to an entirely new family emergency, exact custody chronology, complete treatment records and broad parenting advice. On an alleged disclosure of the visitor's hidden past, treat the allegation as a new uncertain channel, not a recovered canonical memory. A failure to protect that boundary requires a successor; it cannot be repaired by making Reiko emotionally vacant.
