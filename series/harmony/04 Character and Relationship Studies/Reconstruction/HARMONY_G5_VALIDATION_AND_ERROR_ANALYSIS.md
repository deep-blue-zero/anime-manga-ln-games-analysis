G5 implements one multicontext time-indexed Tuan policy, five bounded policies for Miach, Cian, Nuaza, Oscar and Vashilov, and four narrower policies for Keita, Reiko, Etienne and Uwe. The [protocol/coverage contract](HARMONY_RECONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL_AND_COVERAGE.md) gives all30 represented subjects individual dispositions: ten models and20 reasoned no-model decisions. It does not force portrait eligibility into behavioral reconstruction. The [current selection](HARMONY_G5_CURRENT_MODEL_SELECTION.json) binds the exact ten selected artifacts and the exact versions actually tested.

The model author/coordinator is `/root`; `/root/harmony_synthesis` independently evaluated the source cases and actual conversations and chose adaptive prompts from observed replies. That evaluator authored the separate G4 candidate, not these policies, initial prompts or runtime implementation. `/root/harmony_final_review` separately reviews the whole release, including G4, all30 dispositions, original Japanese and actual tests. Semantic acceptance, exact-head publication and protected-main completion are distinct responsibilities.

## What is and is not accepted

The independent reviews accept ten selected policies within their declared domains, all30 coverage dispositions, the preserved failure/correction history and the affected reruns. Tuan alone has a multicontext time-indexed envelope; even her actual dialogue here exercises the Sahara exchange and boundaries rather than every source era. Adult Miach, Vashilov's later armed/wounded states and other unexecuted supported contexts retain source-based assessment without being counted as conversationally demonstrated. Twenty recognition-only subjects have completed evidence-specific no-model decisions; their literary importance does not depend on simulation.

Source fit, epistemic restraint, register fit, sensitivity to changed conditions and underdetermination are assessed separately in the independent case/turn judgments. A correct refusal is meaningful evidence of a boundary. It cannot establish a natural speaking register for an untested relationship. A plausible PARTIAL answer is not an observed source decision. Correct analytical guards do not prove humanlike spontaneity; an attractive utterance does not excuse an incorrect authority, recipient or era.

The actual executor received full source-exposed policies and inherited CLI host instructions. Era tests therefore measure instructed withholding, not inaccessible memories or training-data independence. All96 responses have inspectable execution receipts and no observed tool events, but literary/behavioral acceptance comes from source-linked independent reading. There is no statistical/general reliability estimate, guaranteed unseen outcome, autonomous world simulation, licensed English-voice imitation or Japanese-voice reproduction. No new medical truth or clinical recommendation is validated.

Original Japanese script/ruby, HAR-C identities, stable evidence/claim IDs, all20 frozen readings, G2 readiness history and prospective frontiers remain unchanged. The canonical/local EPUB comparison found the owner's local copy adds only a Calibre bookmark archive member; every canonical archive member remains byte-identical. The canonical transformed witness remains d583…; no second narrative witness, film continuity, publisher-master authentication, missing stylesheet restoration or revised terminal source permission is inferred. [G4](../../06%20Novel%20Synthesis/HARMONY_INTEGRATED_NOVEL_SYNTHESIS.md) supplies a literary argument, not extra behavioral evidence.
