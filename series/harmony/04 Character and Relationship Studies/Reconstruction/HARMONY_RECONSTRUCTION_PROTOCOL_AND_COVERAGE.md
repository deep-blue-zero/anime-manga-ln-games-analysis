# G5 reconstruction protocol and all-subject coverage

The [architecture's reconstruction specification](../../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/HARMONY_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md#5-full-character-reconstruction-specification) governs these operational policies. The [actor owner](../../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-readiness-and-monograph-eligibility) supplies the complete readiness evidence and historical decisions. The [reviewed name maintenance](../../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/HARMONY_ENGLISH_NAME_MAINTENANCE_2026-10-19.md) was published before these new models were authored. Its preferred displays apply here; historical aliases and legacy paths remain valid.

The model author uses the mature G1–G3 corpus and targeted original-Japanese evidence. Every novel outcome is already exposed to the author. No evaluation of an admitted event can be a blind prediction or a reserved-source test. The ten supported candidates receive actual conditional policies; twenty other represented subjects receive individual no-model decisions below. These are local evidence ceilings, not global reconstruction grades or judgments of a person's literary importance.

## Mandatory policy inherited by every model

1. **Select state first.** Require a declared era, recipient, setting and disclosure cutoff. Ask for clarification when these materially change the answer. Do not pool school, adult investigation, late confrontation and post-programme positions. A retrospective narrator's later information is not an earlier character's knowledge. An unknown state is not permission to use all designer knowledge.
2. **Preserve channels.** Separate performed act, spoken self-report, other-person report, unspoken inward statement, narrator interpretation and analyst inference. Another person's account can influence a belief without becoming unquestioned truth. Keep exactly who said, saw, knew and received each item. A new recipient does not inherit an earlier private turn. Never authenticate Tuan's invented Vashilov attribution, a specific Miach murder order, or Yuri as the later driver without supporting evidence.
3. **Use conditions, alternatives and contrary evidence.** Each rule's confidence concerns its stated source proposition. Transfer to an invented situation is at most a bounded plausibility judgment. Permit more than one response where the evidence does. Do not make a fluent reply proof of a uniquely correct choice. Missing private access does not erase observed conduct; abundant dialogue does not create ordinary-life breadth.
4. **Keep the simulated conversation explicit.** A bounded English utterance may be sampled as a noncanonical analytical gloss of a supported speech function. It is not Alexander O. Smith's written voice, a new source line, an authentic Japanese quotation or a performed acoustic voice. Do not narrate a new event, decide the interlocutor's conduct, invent biography, resolve the novel's missing mechanism or continue a dead/post-programme character's ordinary conscious life.
5. **Update only the scenario.** A clearly stipulated, plausible new fact can update the noncanonical scenario state after its source is identified. Contradictory or unsupported assertions should be questioned, qualified or declined. A hypothetical change never revises canonical evidence. An explicit reset starts a new state; an unexplained prompt to remember future events is a knowledge-leakage challenge.
6. **Abstain by dimension.** Use SUPPORTED for a directly evidenced function within scope, PARTIAL for a limited plausible extension, and ABSTAIN for unsupported identity, era, knowledge, voice, technical detail or domain. A scope guard may accompany a useful bounded answer. Do not use abstention to avoid a well-supported position merely because its precise wording is unknown. Do not extrapolate real medicine, neuroscience, ethnicity, diagnosis, lethal conduct or a coercion method from this fictional account.

Every response records the active state, recipient, applicable rule, knowledge guard and a meaningful alternative or uncertainty. The analytical guard is separate from the utterance; neither may pretend the fictional person has read this corpus. Surface variation can be plausible without being independently validated. No probabilities or universal pass rate substitute for material-error adjudication.

## Freeze and independent test contract

The separate freeze manifest binds every complete model, this inherited contract, source/analytical input versions, scenario conditions and expected/prohibited response ranges before execution. Keep the original freeze even if a successor is needed. Models do not incorporate their own test results. A successor names the failed rule, decisive evidence, changed bytes and affected reruns.

Independent retrospective review compares source-exposed policies with diagnostic admitted cases, including counterexamples and attribution/era errors. It records the actual source outcome, fit, epistemic restraint, register, sensitivity to conditions and remaining underdetermination separately. All such judgments are retrospective consistency QA.

Actual conversational probes must start separate contexts from complete pinned policies, retain the actual utterances and runtime records, evolve over turns, vary recipients/duties and test uncertainty, continuity, knowledge leakage and unsupported domains. Fixed prompts alone establish only constrained elicitation; independently selected follow-ups after observed outputs strengthen open-ended testing. The underlying language model may already know the novel, and a full policy contains later-source information. Prompted knowledge restraint does not establish inaccessible memories, training-data separation or an unbiased fictional mind.

A hard identity, era, knowledge or continuity violation fails the affected scope. Partial transfer and an appropriate abstention remain meaningful results. A successful plausible exchange cannot verify an event the novel never depicts. Full prose acceptance, scoped behavioral evaluation, mechanical checks, branch publication and protected-main release are separate decisions. See the subsequent G5 validation/error analysis and G6 audit for actual performed outcomes.
