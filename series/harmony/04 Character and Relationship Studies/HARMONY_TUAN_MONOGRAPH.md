# Tuan and the unequal ownership of choice

Tuan does not move cleanly from dependence to autonomy. She learns to secure room for herself by negotiating, withholding and using the very institutions whose access she resents. Her most consequential choices ask who may decide what another person's body or conduct means. At school she finds a language for alienation in Miaha; as an inspector she makes autonomy a practical privilege; at the end she accepts a world without choosing while insisting that one killing belongs to her alone. The development is real, but it is neither an uncomplicated emancipation nor proof that she never acts freely enough to bear responsibility.

The central tension is an unequal distribution of authorship. Tuan demands authority over her own body and self-account while sometimes denying comparable authority to others. Her narration exposes enough jealousy, concealment, self-disgust and care to make that inequality legible. Its selectivity is an action the monograph must explain, not a reason to disbelieve the entire narrated history. G2's [current arguments](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17) and [HAR-C001 readiness](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-readiness-and-monograph-eligibility) are the starting point; frozen readings preserve the earlier knowledge boundaries.

## Alienation before the charismatic explanation

The childhood ethics session prevents a simple origin story in which Miaha implants rebellion into an otherwise contented girl. Tuan remembers nausea at an ostensibly courteous demand to eliminate caffeine and shame at her father's defeat. Nuaza offers moderation; categorical assertions produce the atmosphere he cannot master. The adult teller explicitly dates the discomfort before meeting Miaha. It persists at school and during games at home. This is a represented ordinary-life history of alienation, not merely a later crisis-derived trait. [HAR-U011-E001–E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e001); CL072–CL073.

Miaha's recognition then has force because it articulates something Tuan already feels and has not expressed. Tuan admits the description strikes home: she joins groups and volunteers while preferring not to care about people she does not want to engage. Recognition offers relief, but Miaha's claim that they are made of the same material goes beyond what Tuan's pleasure can prove. The hand-kiss leaves a pleasant bodily after-effect despite her withdrawal. Later, at the desk, Tuan repeatedly refuses painful touching and is ignored. Attraction and violation are distinct encounters, not evidence that one retroactively consents to the other. [HAR-U002-E008/E013/E018–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e008); CL007–CL010.

The adolescent Tuan also manages information. She fears what Miaha would think of her parentage and conceals it; later disclosure brings nonrejection. Her attachment is therefore neither transparent sameness nor the simple absence of a self. She chooses what can be known about her while valuing someone whose accuracy has made her feel known. The suicide pact intensifies the contradiction: depicted assent and ingestion coexist with the older narrator's denial that the decision was free. Charisma constrains action without making action disappear. Survival can then feel like defeat because it breaks the story in which her body would have delivered an uncompromised refusal. [HAR-U002-E011/E017](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e011); [HAR-U003-E006–E008/E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e006); [HAR-U004-E006–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e006); CL009v2/CL015/CL022v2.

## The inspector's room to move

Adult Tuan obtains bodily refusal through organized dependence. Dangerous international service earns social credit and distance from ordinary community expectations. She trades treatment programs for tobacco and alcohol; aid and personal benefit occur in the same bargain. The Kel Tamasheq representative's invitation remains open after she declines, providing a specific less demanding kindness without establishing an innocent culture outside medicine. Her own stated fear of losing social regard helps explain why the available refuge is not a complete solution. She resents belonging and depends on it. [HAR-U003-E004/E009–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e004); CL013/CL016–CL017.

Her competence is materially enacted. In the pursuit she delegates driving, orders Etienne out of the gun position, destroys the WarBird, scans for further threats and then relinquishes vigilance while exhausted. `わたしがやるから` is a practical assumption of responsibility, with short directives and confidence calibrated to this task. It is not evidence that she masters every interpersonal situation. Dependence on equipment and others' labor is built into successful agency rather than exposed afterward as its negation. [HAR-U004-E003–E004/E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e003); CL020–CL021.

Oscar's sanction provides another test. Tuan uses the threat of scandal to obtain a secrecy commitment but cannot prevent recall to Japan. Her apparent success is partial even before Oscar later discloses factional use. The subordinate A-kun sees light punishment where she feels the loss of escape. The difference shows why a single outcome does not carry the same meaning for every recipient. Her impulse to strike him remains unperformed; drinking is the act she chooses. [HAR-U005-E002–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U005_PART_I_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u005-e002); CL025–CL030.

DummyMe makes this autonomy technically specific. It falsifies internal reports; it does not make the body invisible or end social judgment. Professional exemption, discovered through Uwe, is a different route: permission within the institution rather than deception of its monitoring. Tuan's surprise at this distinction is a countercase to treating an experienced inspector as institutionally omniscient. Her adult pattern is skilled selective participation, with real blind spots, rather than one absolute departure from the system. [HAR-U006-E002/E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e002); [HAR-U017-E005–E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e005); CL027v2/CL035/CL134–CL135.

## What competence cannot receive

With Kian, Tuan first tries to preserve the Miaha she needs. She withholds the imagined DummyMe lesson and substitutes a dry remark; she distinguishes comrades from friends and resists Kian's suggestion of loneliness. Later she expressly retracts the follower image when Kian explains her attempted balancing and rescue disclosure. The change is not a new performance of universal empathy. Tuan thanks Kian sincerely while withholding why she values the life Kian preserved. She can revise a mistaken relation without giving up every informational advantage. [HAR-U006-E005–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e005); [HAR-U009-E001–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001); CL034/CL037/CL054–CL055. The [Kian monograph](HARMONY_KIAN_MONOGRAPH.md) develops the friend's independent account.

Reiko's grief exposes a different limit. Tuan can negotiate with governments and armed groups but is unsettled by a raw parental confession. Her inward confession of having been one of the girls remains unspoken; she calmly encourages the mother to continue. A professional capacity to obtain facts does not ensure a capacity to receive another person's pain without contempt or discomfort. Reiko's admission that Miaha suffered outside her imagination also resists Tuan's reduction of the parents to empty protocols. They may have failed profoundly without having felt nothing. [HAR-U008-E011–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e011); CL051–CL053v2.

Tuan's reticence here is consequential because it distributes vulnerability unevenly. Reiko exposes grief while remaining ignorant of the visitor's participation and kinship. The account does not show equal disclosure as a necessary condition for every useful interview, but it makes the cost of that professional advantage visible. Tuan's alienation from communal confession cannot alone justify demanding such confession from someone else.

## The body claimed and the body entered

The Gabriel handshake is the sharpest practical test of Tuan's autonomy language. She implants listening molecules without shown informed consent, although she explicitly says Gabriel's behavior displayed nothing abnormal. The justification is that Gabriel is the only lead. The emergency is credible and the procedure has official authorization; neither changes whose body supplies the involuntary access point. This is a precise conflict between professional means and a demand for self-ownership. Calling Tuan a hypocrite names the contradiction but explains less than tracing the institutionally equipped decision. [HAR-U013-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003); CL090.

The next disclosure places her in the recipient's position. Vashilov had withheld information to use her visit as a stimulus. Later Oscar admits that both factions watched the same woman in hopes of exposing their respective targets. Tuan's choices remain efficacious enough to be useful to others. Being instrumentalized does not mean every move was scripted; it means the freedom she thought she had secured was tolerated for purposes she did not know. Her successful bluff produces true information because she risks a plausible invented attribution, not because she already possesses the knowledge she performs. [HAR-U013-E004–E005](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e004); [HAR-U016-E010–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010); CL091v2/CL124/CL126–CL127.

Her narration preserves this embarrassment, as it preserves her jealousy over Miaha's selection for Nuaza's experiment. `なぜ、わたしじゃなかったの` is not a noble objection to research ethics. She then distinguishes benefit from purpose and calls the experiment grotesque. Jealousy and valid criticism coexist. The least flattering disclosure is positive counterevidence to reading every retrospective qualification as a successful effort to appear innocent. It also does not make the self-account exhaustive. [HAR-U014-E010–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e010); CL103–CL105v2.

## Killing, gratitude and what the father still means

Nuaza's shielding action gives Tuan a debt that does not restore thirteen years of parental presence. Her grief is quieter than for Kian; she says he had become almost a stranger. She thanks him while questioning whether love, reflex or inherited bodily disposition explains his movement. The act's benefit is definite; its private psychological cause is not. This distinction also stops a simple equation between mental deliberation and moral value: the action can save her even if it is not a fully deliberated ethical judgment. [HAR-U015-E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e011); CL113–CL114.

The wounded Vashilov offers information for a final shot. Tuan promises it, then freezes before the first killing of a person she describes as hers. Relief from the public ultimatum, requested mercy and revenge appear as an excess of justifications that produces self-disgust. Her question about the evolutionary function of that feeling does not remove the feeling or supply a demonstrated command. She pulls the trigger. Later reporting confirms his death. The sequence supports a responsible but pressured actor, not a cleanly altruistic mercy killing and not a merely involuntary movement. [HAR-U015-E012–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e012); [HAR-U016-E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e008); CL117v2/CL124.

Her subsequent pursuit is explicitly personal. Oscar's encouragement disconcerts her because the world is not what she principally wants to save; she wants an ending with Miaha after Kian and her father's deaths. With Uwe she accepts logistics while insisting on going alone, borrowing authority more strongly than Oscar actually supplied it. These are recipient-specific decisions: strategic deference toward a superior, liking and resistance toward a colleague, and an exclusive claim on a friend turned adversary. They cannot be reconstructed as one register or one universal appetite for risk. [HAR-U016-E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e015); [HAR-U017-E002–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e002); CL131/CL133/CL139.

## The last assertion of ownership

At the bunker Tuan initially rejects the necessity of the deaths. Miaha's testimony then changes the motive story Tuan had carried there: professed world-love replaces hatred as the sufficient present explanation, without proving pure benevolence. Nuaza's resistance in the name of dignity revalues the old image of his humiliation. Tuan now mourns a conviction she had not known how to respect. The past event is not rewritten; its significance changes. [HAR-U018-E008–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e008); [HAR-U019-E004–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e004); CL128v2/CL147–CL156.

Tuan's `たぶん` followed by `異議は、ない` is qualified assent to the prospect of harmonics. Her revenge is not presented as saving humanity by stopping it. She promises Miaha the world she wants while withholding it from Miaha herself: `実現してあげる` turns an apparent benefaction into the premise for deprivation, followed by `あなたには、与えない`. The repeated giving language makes her claim on the friend's future exact. No evidence gives Tuan the elders' code authority or makes this promise a performed global command. [HAR-U019-E011–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e011); CL157–CL159/CL115v4.

She shoots Miaha, declares revenge ended, strokes her hair, carries her to the mountain view and agrees to watch the end of her consciousness. The strong contrary reading is reconciliation: recognition and last care appear to restore intimate reciprocity. That reading captures something real but cannot convert a request about dying into advance consent to being killed. The gun has denied Miaha a desired future before care answers her remaining particular requests. The same agent enacts both. [HAR-U019-E013–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e013); CL159–CL160.

`わたしが` repeats before the isolated `わたし`. Tuan claims the shots as no one else's will while the self's bodily boundary begins to fail. A reading of pure autonomy would ignore the dependencies, information manipulation and qualified assent that brought her here. A reading of total programming would discard the particular revenge, changed appraisals, hesitation and enacted care the narration supplies. The better account is narrower: she takes responsibility for this act at a threshold where the continuing owner of responsibility is becoming unavailable. Her claim is an important act of self-description; it is not an external experiment proving independence from every cause. [HAR-U019-E014–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e014); CL161v2.

## Voice, development and reconstruction limits

Tuan's Japanese varies with the work she is doing. Short practical directives in danger, ironic defiance with Oscar, performed official authority before Reiko or Gabriel, intimate questions to the friends, and the broken late first person belong to distinguishable situations. The distinction is between written represented speech, inward statement and retrospective prose; no acoustic timbre is inferred. Her first-person narration can disclose a fantasy and cancel it, enclose an unspoken confession, delay a covert act and revise a judgment. Those are demonstrable editing acts. They make the reader accountable for who knows what when, without proving that every statement is a lie.

The opening defeat/desertion categories consequently retain more than one plausible referent. They can bear the failed adolescent refusal, adult accommodations, loss of companions and final surrender of the choosing I. The text does not designate one exhaustive incident, and no military desertion or fixed shame diagnosis follows. The epilogue gives the last farewell fuller form while describing Tuan historically from a changed grammatical position. It supplies no continuously conscious post-programme Tuan, no biological-death proof for her and no new personal model state. [HAR-U001-E005/E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U001_OPENING_AND_PART_I_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u001-e005); [HAR-U020-E003/E007–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e003); CL003v2/CL163/CL167.

G2 therefore reasonably makes Tuan a MULTICONTEXT_CANDIDATE rather than treating incomplete omniscience as disqualification. School intimacy, ordinary pleasure, official bargaining, threat response, grief and late violence offer materially different conditions. A future authorized reconstruction must keep era, recipient, disclosure and public/private performance separate; all admitted outcomes are already exposed and cannot serve as blind holdouts for this author. No model, predictive rule set or validation is produced here. The monograph's supported conclusion is a developmental tension: Tuan learns to claim a choice, discovers the arrangements that let others claim her choices, and finally asserts her own act while consenting to the disappearance of the kind of owner she has spent her life defending.
