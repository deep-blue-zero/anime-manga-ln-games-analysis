# Miaha, the body that refuses to become a book

Miaha's development turns on a changed answer to a persistent problem. At school she insists that a body should not be replaced by the words through which a caring society administers it. In the final encounter she proposes to remove the I that suffers from being required to fit that society. The dissident becomes an advocate of social completion, but the earlier diagnosis of painful conformity remains. The change is consequential: the order once judged wrong for the person becomes the order to which the person must be remade. Her professed love of the world is therefore neither an irrelevant disguise nor a transparent acquittal. It names a form of rescue capable of destroying what the earlier girl claimed to defend.

This account begins from G2's current versions and [Miaha's substantial-monograph eligibility](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-readiness-and-monograph-eligibility), which retains `HAR-C002` as `DOMAIN_BOUNDED`. Literary importance and rich speech support a substantial monograph; they do not supply a complete private psyche or broad predictive model. Canonical claims remain in the [claims owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17), especially CL007–CL010/CL014–CL015/CL037/CL050v2/CL110v2/CL128v2/CL150–CL160. Technology and political authority remain in the [institutional owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTIONS_AND_COERCION_LEDGER.md#g2-current-institutional-and-mechanism-reconciliation). The monograph develops an argument about Miaha without turning her history into an illustration of a clinical type.

## The several Miahas the reader must keep in time

The reader first meets Miaha through Tuan's remembered adolescence and admiration. After the shared attempt, a bedside report says she could not be saved. For much of the adult investigation she persists as an absent leader, a projected voice, a remembered maxim and a disputed object of loyalty. Reiko's adoption and donation account, Keita's intermediary admission and Nuaza's experimental report then provide different access to her life. A retrieved call supplies a strongly recognized adult voice. Finally a bodily encounter, wound, conversation and watched death establish the present person. These are changing disclosure conditions, not interchangeable evidence of an unchanging essence.

Later living Miaha defeats treating the school-era death report as a complete final history. Nuaza's account of restored consciousness and the actual adult meeting make present survival clear. They do not recover every custody transfer, medical stage or the original report's cause. A knowing lie by Tuan's mother is not established. The older Tuan's language of Miaha going alone belongs to a history of loss and belief; it must not overrule the later narrated encounter. [HAR-U004-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e006); [HAR-U008-E013–E016](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e013); [HAR-U015-E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e002); [HAR-U018-E008–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e008); CL012v2/CL022v2/CL053v2.

Tuan's initial perception of the old girl's body in the bunker is likewise an emotionally situated recognition. Subsequent notice of growth prevents turning resemblance into a literal no-aging claim. Neither age, current physiology nor complete restoration history can be measured from the familiar presence. Miaha's actual final death is a different proposition: breath and heat cease after two bullets through the chest, and Tuan explicitly narrates consciousness disappearing through death. The evidence gives a bodily endpoint while leaving earlier medical chronology incomplete. [HAR-U019-E004/E013–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e004).

## Recognition makes her authority intimate

Miaha's first persuasive success is local and accurate. She recognizes that Tuan's public sociability does not express an uncomplicated wish to care for everyone. Tuan says the recognition strikes home—`図星だった`—and remembers pleasure in having an inarticulate feeling given precise words. Miaha does not merely broadcast a doctrine to any available listener. She selects a person whose apparent indifference to her conspicuous solitude reveals an affinity. The relationship begins in the tension between being seen and wanting some portion of the self left unclaimed. [HAR-U002-E012–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e012); CL007.

That success is easy for Tuan to expand into the belief that Miaha knows everything. The source supplies narrower channels: study, network access, downloaded and printed books, unusual historical knowledge and a gift for sharpening another person's discomfort. Even Miaha's material solitude is produced through a network library and paid manufacture. The heavy book creates a manner of attention and social separation within the technical world; it does not prove complete independence from it. Nor does the accurate reading of Tuan's alienation prove prior knowledge of her father. The eventual brief nonrejection after disclosure is a later outcome, not evidence of omniscience at the first meeting. [HAR-U002-E006/E011/E013/E017](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e006); [HAR-U008-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e006); CL009v2.

Her hand-kiss intensifies recognition into an embodied, ceremonious invitation. Tuan withdraws, yet describes a pleasantly lingering cold contact. Miaha's declaration that they are made of the same material is confident and selective; the pleasure makes it compelling without making it a demonstrated identity. Tuan's concealed family knowledge, different personal history and later choices remain. Charged intimacy is supported. A fixed sexual identity, complete mutual commitment or transparent equivalence of desire is not. [HAR-U002-E018–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e018); CL010.

The account discloses the later desk scene before this origin. Consequently the reader has already seen the danger inside Miaha's authority before experiencing its first pleasure. She continues painful touching despite Tuan's explicit refusal while explaining why no one should take ownership of another's body. The contradiction is enacted, not dependent on a guessed pathology: the lesson's recipient cannot control its delivery. `いつものように` gives the narrator's perception of recurrence, but does not provide an independent survey of their whole relationship. Familiarity and didactic provocation can explain functions of the scene; neither converts refusal into consent. The earlier pleasant kiss cannot authorize this different act. [HAR-U002-E007–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e007); CL008.

This is an early form of the ethical asymmetry the adult project enlarges. Miaha sees a person's distress clearly and then claims latitude to determine the experience that will explain or relieve it. The continuity is persuasive as a literary pattern. It must not become a deterministic claim that one adolescent violation already contains every later death. Institutional resources, research, ideological change and later choices are needed to explain that expansion.

## Appetite and reassurance interrupt the emblem of refusal

The roof lunch is crucial counterevidence to an exclusively ascetic or death-driven portrait. Miaha prepares her own large, rice-heavy meal, eats with conspicuous appetite, has a grain on her cheek, jokes and becomes the object of Tuan's laughter. Her physiological explanation is self-report; her enjoyment and playful exchange are narrated conduct. The scene allows ordinary bodily variation to exist beside political argument. It is neither a recovered comprehensive childhood nor decorative humanization added after the serious ideas. It shows what bodily self-direction can look like in practice. [HAR-U016-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e003); CL119.

When Kian apologizes for not being hungry at the collective lunch hour, Miaha and Tuan reassure her. Miaha then turns the mismatch into an account of discipline: a timetable makes physiology appear to be the guilty party. Her explanation is supported by this local apology, while its general history remains her political interpretation. The care in the reassurance is also actual. She does not only compel Kian; she can recognize and defend a difference that Kian experiences as fault. The same woman who overrides one refusal can tell another friend she need not apologize for her body's rhythm. A monograph must retain recipient and circumstance rather than average these into either benevolence or coercion. [HAR-U016-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e004); CL120.

Her knowledge here is explicitly limited. She says the effect of her refusal of designed meals on her mother's social assessment may or may not exist; she does not know. That admission checks Tuan's omniscience image. Her quoted writers and historical references work as recommendations, corrections and provocations to particular friends. They are not independent verification of the external works or permission to import those works' full arguments. The girl enjoys showing a friend another way to read. Her ideological authority is partly made through such ordinary interaction.

## The book funeral exposes the cost of her own programme of leaving

Miaha gathers books to burn on the day of the tablets. She fears their power to bind her to life because they have given her strength. The destruction therefore does not establish contempt for reading. It is preparation against an attachment capable of making departure harder. Her practical concern about transporting the books before bodily weakening makes the metaphor inseparable from a finite body. The reader sees an assembled pile and later fire; Tuan cannot independently verify the claim that these are all Miaha's novels. [HAR-U019-E001–E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e001); CL150–CL152.

The argument about description gives this ritual its particular stakes. Medical seeing reconstructs the body through technical language; WatchMe threatens, in Miaha's account, to make the body a book. She wants to die before that transition and insist her body is not such an object. This is not an external theorem that description literally replaces everything described. It is a girl's political and existential claim about who may author her bodily meaning. The original WatchMe/WacthMe spelling variation is a witness fact, not a second technology.

At the decisive moment she gives the igniter to Tuan, covers her hand and admits that lighting it herself would be too painful: `わたしにはきっと辛いから`. Miaha designs a ritual whose final action she cannot readily perform. This is meaningful dependence on the friend she otherwise leads. It need not be dismissed as calculated emotional manipulation, though the allocation of action is asymmetrical. Tuan consents to this concrete request and actually lights the books. Miaha's composure beside the fire does not erase the attachment she has confessed.

When asked whether the three deaths will change the world, Miaha replies that everything will change for them. That narrowing is consequential. The girl who speaks of striking frozen time does not here demonstrate a feasible global reform strategy. She moves from a public image of attack to the participants' own boundary. Her adult catastrophic programme later acquires a more elaborate causal route. Treating the pact as already the same technically effective plan would collapse development into retrospective destiny. [HAR-U003-E006–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e006); [HAR-U019-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e003); CL014–CL015/CL150–CL151.

## Kian makes the limits of Miaha's self-account visible

The school pact contains persuasion, trust and actual assent. Older Tuan explicitly denies that her choice had been free, identifying charisma and the belief that Miaha always possessed a method. That is evidence of a powerful constraint within the relationship, not proof the girls never act or that Miaha controls minds through childhood speech. Kian's hesitation and earlier objection to mass harm prevent the trio from becoming one will. [HAR-U003-E006–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e006); CL015.

The adult survivors disagree over what Miaha sought. Tuan wants comradeship and a military language of desertion; Kian proposes friendship and companionship. Tuan's move from probability toward necessity reveals the investment of her image, rather than settling Miaha's private motive. Miaha may have sought companions and comrades together. The later funeral request, convivial meal and recognition of particular friends make a purely instrumental recruitment account incomplete. They do not prove an exclusively innocent wish for company. [HAR-U006-E005/E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e005); CL037.

Kian's own account then revises the apparent follower. She stopped taking the medication and told her parents; she also says she hoped to act as a balancer who could relieve Miaha and prevent catastrophe. The asserted purpose is qualified retrospective self-interpretation, while the disclosed stopping and telling are affirmative conduct. This matters for Miaha's biography because a person she seems to lead is also attempting to care for and redirect her. Miaha's authority does not disclose whether she understood that aim. [HAR-U009-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001); CL054.

The retrieved call turns the rhetoric of courage toward a fatal proof of ownership. Its political argument proceeds through persistence, natural impermanence and a life held hostage by goodness. It then narrows from history to the girls who did not come to Miaha's side. The claimed injury—`すごく傷ついたしすごく悲しかった`—is followed by an urgent request for Kian to prove herself now. This makes belonging conditional on an act directed toward death. Tuan's recognition of voice and thought is emphatic; strong identification is warranted. No identified bodily location or complete survival chain follows from the recording alone. [HAR-U009-E017–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e017); CL061–CL062.

Later Miaha admits manipulating valuations and broadly acknowledges responsibility. These disclosures support pressure and causal participation more strongly than an innocent-call reading. They do not isolate the precise mixture of neural intervention and persuasion in Kian. When Tuan accuses her of needing conscious self-justification for a settled event, Miaha responds with uncertain questions, not a complete confession. The monograph must not borrow Tuan's certainty about the other's private mechanism simply because her accusation is powerful. [HAR-U019-E007/E011–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007); CL154/CL157–CL158.

## Her history arrives through other people's categories before her own voice

Reiko's adoption narrative places love, resource policy, treatment assurances and incomplete knowledge together. It is not proof that Miaha's childhood pain had been eliminated. Nuaza's later account classifies her acquired consciousness, heredity and unusual research value. His explanation is interested testimony from someone who selected and experimented on her. It may explain why researchers were fascinated without explaining all she is. Neither the rhetoric of imitation nor the claimed unusual neural site makes her a lesser person. [HAR-U008-E009–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e009); [HAR-U015-E005–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e005); CL050v2/CL110v2.

Her final testimony adds a survivor's channel. She identifies the bunker as the site of her captivity and sexual violence and describes the birth of consciousness there. The testimony is substantial evidence, even though no medical archive or complete custody history is displayed. Her smiling manner is not a credibility test. Tuan's tears and inability to understand how she can speak this way reveal the listener's distress and limits; they do not authorize a diagnosis of dissociation, emotional vacancy or a counterfeit mind. A gun's role in the remembered coercion also makes the present armed audience materially charged without reducing the current encounter to a repeated clinical pattern. [HAR-U019-E004–E005](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e004); CL110v2/CL152.

The rescue she reports involved a mixed MRS/volunteer force employed by Chechnya, followed by adoption through a life-government programme. Nuaza is not thereby made the soldier who personally rescued her. The unnamed population remains unnamed; its attributed neurological classification cannot be generalized to every Chechen or another ethnic group. Miaha reports what fMRI is said to show and what she believes happened to her. Those qualifications allow her testimony to matter without converting it into validated real-world neurobiology.

The neighboring boy's death supplies another formative event in her own account. Miaha was twelve; the boy's exact age is not given. His reported sense of having no place makes her perceive a violence produced by suppressing nature as well as by unleashing it. The adolescent conclusion that the system itself was wrong follows within this narrated history. Trauma alone does not dictate that conclusion, and no complete motive for the boy is available. Miaha has intellectual and political agency in what she makes of the events she reports. [HAR-U019-E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e006).

## World-love is a change of solution, with a disturbing continuity

The research interval makes another answer available. Nuaza reports ordinary behavior during consciousness removal and a restored Miaha's happy ecstasy without episodic memory. This is a participant's account of her reported experience, not a live demonstration or a complete phenomenology. It nevertheless gives a supported route by which absence of conflict can become a desired condition rather than only a death she resists. [HAR-U014-E009–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e009); [HAR-U015-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e001); CL103/CL105v2/CL106.

In the bunker Miaha explicitly rejects the premise that hatred explains her current chaos. She says she loves the world with all her strength and wants to save it from the invading I. CL128v2 correctly makes this the center of present motive reliance. It would be a failure of reading to leave continuing hatred as the default while relegating her actual new rationale to a negligible possibility. Her world-love is a professed motive, however, not omniscient evidence of pure altruism. Qualified homecoming, grief, violence and self-justification remain compatible components of an incompletely accessible mixture. [HAR-U019-E006–E008/E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e006).

The strongest continuity is structural. Formerly, a completed system makes particular bodies blame themselves for failing to fit. Miaha now proposes to eliminate the recalcitrant faculty so thoroughly that this blame and suffering no longer arise. Her image of fitting the body to the shoe states the direction of adaptation. This changes the adolescent ownership claim profoundly while answering its old pain. The proposal does not merely expose a hypocrite who never believed her earlier words. The intervening research and current explanation support a changed commitment whose retained problem makes the change intelligible.

Her power remains limited. She claims most source code, several-government backdoors and value manipulation; she expressly lacks the elders' final codes. A minority leader stages catastrophe to force the code holders' decision, then returns to former rescuers because she has fled the mainstream faction. These are choices under constraints, not omnipotence. The scope is several life-governments' systems and their members where specified, not a class of council office holders; technical access is separate from the elders' exclusive authorization. Her anti-suicide aim explains the strategy without justifying the deaths used as pressure. The assertion that no other solution remains is not independently proved. [HAR-U016-E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e013); [HAR-U018-E010–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e010); [HAR-U019-E007–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007); CL115v4/CL154–CL155.

## Tuan refuses to let Miaha choose the meaning of their reunion

The bodily encounter begins with Miaha's expectation that Tuan would come and the old card confirming a material route of recognition. It does not give her knowledge of every journey event or authenticate the whole note/courier chain. Her dance then makes Tuan a sole audience. Steps intensify, stop at an authority limit and resume with explanation. The prose makes thinking theatrical and bodily without supplying an actual soundtrack or diagnostic reading of movement. Miaha's apparent ease and Tuan's tears do not divide them into inhuman intellect and authentic feeling. [HAR-U018-E008–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e008); [HAR-U019-E004–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e004).

Nuaza occupies a sharply different relationship in Miaha's account: research collaborator and teacher of possibility, then leading obstacle to the final threshold. The father who secretly installed the network also defends the dignity of a conscious person. Miaha's ideological opposition and acknowledgment of his death do not demonstrate a specific murder order. Tuan learns enough to revalue the man she had remembered as humiliated, but cannot retrieve his last thought or erase his unauthorized intervention. The directional complexity prevents Miaha from being explained solely as the scientist's product or Nuaza solely as her benevolent restorer. [HAR-U019-E006–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e006); CL156–CL157.

When Tuan identifies revenge as exclusion from the desired future, Miaha is surprised. Tuan takes that response as familiar self-centeredness and feels relief at recognizing the girl again. Her interpretation has force because it encounters an expectation Miaha has failed to include. It still does not disclose everything Miaha anticipated. Her qualified assent to homecoming—`そう、なのかもしれない` followed by a stronger but still self-interpreting response—cannot become a uniquely proved motive for the whole project. [HAR-U019-E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e012).

After falling, Miaha asks whether she will be forgiven and then requests the mountain view and a witness to the end of her consciousness. These are new, constrained acts of agency by a dying person. They cannot be treated as free assent to having been shot. Her wish for a particular witness also makes the conscious end matter to the woman who has argued consciousness unnecessary. This is not a logical refutation of her programme: the desired painless future has been withheld, and ordinary death is not the same event as programme removal. It is a powerful particular counterweight to the claim that nothing of value attaches to an I's last experience. [HAR-U019-E013–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e013); CL159–CL160.

Tuan's final recognition refuses lesser personhood even under the attributed neural account: there is no great difference between the allegedly emulated consciousness and theirs. It is an ethical recognition in the narration, not a fresh brain examination. Miaha's death becomes the particular loss the subsequent population-wide account could otherwise absorb into technical completion. The epilogue's pairing of the women as last mourners gives her a historical function; it does not resurrect her, supply a new reciprocal exchange or prove that their intentions finally became identical. [HAR-U020-E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e008); CL110v2/CL167.

## Speech, variation and the limits of a reconstructible Miaha

Miaha's written speech repeatedly turns a recipient's ordinary answer into a larger problem. A question about lunch becomes discipline; a book's title becomes an analysis of Tuan's attention; a recalled maxim becomes a proof demanded of Kian. She shifts between quick colloquial replies, affectionate or ceremonious address, explicit recommendations and long explanatory sequences. `ねえ`, questions, `でしょ` and inclusive first-person forms make theories conversational. They invite agreement while the distribution of knowledge often gives her control of the next step. This is a supported literary repertoire across named scenes, not a universal dialogue generator.

Variation is equally important. The girl admits she does not know an assessment consequence, says the books will be painful to burn, reassures Kian and eats with amusement. The caller repeatedly asks for courage and describes hurt. Adult Miaha professes world-love, acknowledges code limits, stops dancing in frustration, qualifies her homecoming motive and asks to be forgiven after the attack. A fixed register of serene omniscience would lose this range. Tuan's soprano-like comparison and other auditory descriptions are written perceptions, not independently inspected acoustic timbre.

The supported reconstruction ceiling remains era-specific, relationship-conditioned persuasion and self-presentation, plus the stated limited-access pressure strategy. Independent adult ordinary conduct is sparse; most private motives and several relationships reach the reader through Tuan or interested reports. No broad policy about all trauma survivors, scientists, ethnic populations, lovers or political leaders follows. No clinical diagnosis, automatic death-seeking disposition, universal lack of empathy, code-execution model or certainty about every manipulated victim is warranted. Source exposure is already retrospective, and no model or validation has been produced here.

The substantial literary judgment is firmer than that predictive ceiling. Miaha can recognize suffering, provide genuine reassurance and value attachment while claiming authority to determine what another person's relief must require. She changes from defending a body against its administrative description to coding a system whose final completion removes the receiving self. Her watched death refuses to let that development end only as an idea: the person whose alleged unusual consciousness interested researchers is granted the same weight of loss as any other. That is why explaining her by trauma, hypocrisy, genius, pure love or pure hatred alone would weaken rather than complete her characterization.
