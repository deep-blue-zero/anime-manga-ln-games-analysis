# Relief and the end of the answering self

Harmony's most difficult proposition is that a society can become better at relieving suffering while becoming less able to hear what a particular sufferer says that relief means. Its medicine works. Its people can care sincerely. The final intervention really ends suicide and almost all conflict in the narrated world. The novel makes those accomplishments consequential, then asks what has happened to the person on whose behalf they are claimed. At the end, the system has removed the I that receives distress. Its success is therefore also a change in who, or what, may count as the recipient of a good.

The novel develops this question through a form that repeatedly makes an apparently understood person answer back. Cian's agreement becomes an attempted rescue; Nuaza's humiliation is reread through a conviction his daughter did not know; Tuan's hatred-only account of Miach meets Miach's professed world-love and its more radical accommodation; Tuan's demand for ownership of her life becomes a claim to dispose of another's future. These corrections do not uncover a final, fully transparent personality behind every mask. They make the earlier account insufficient. The final social harmony ends that recurring insufficiency by ending the ordinary subject who could experience it. Yet a subjective text remains, arranged to make the vanished position emotionally available to a later reader.

This synthesis argues that the political and formal movements belong together. The novel tests when a description of someone's condition becomes permission to finish that person's story. It gives refusal, belated understanding and particular care a value without making them infallible; it gives coordination and relief real benefits without allowing success alone to establish consent. Its ending preserves the pressure of a first-person history inside the account of a world that no longer requires first-person answers. This is an interpretation of the work's construction, rather than a doctrine attributed unchanged to Tuan, Miach or the final voice. The [accepted G3 studies](../07%20Audits%20and%20Handoffs/HARMONY_G3_CONVERGENCE_AND_HANDOFF.md#final-g3-substantive-convergence-and-role-gap-disposition) and the three cumulative ledgers remain its evidence-bearing dependencies; the following argument connects their findings rather than replacing their distinct responsibilities.

## The credibility of a world that protects

The remembered childhood rescue prevents the reader from dismissing the medical order as a fraud. Tuan falls into water, her father pulls her out, the wound is closed and medical molecules provide protection against infection. The technology later described as capable of violence is first effective care for an identifiable child. Miach herself acknowledges that the society has done well in eliminating most disease. This concession comes from the character who most powerfully articulates its suffocation. Her criticism cannot be reduced to ignorance of medical benefit, and the reader cannot answer the novel by assuming every beneficiary has merely been deceived. [HAR-U002-E015–E016](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e015).

What Tuan resents is the additional claim made through benefit. Her declaration that kindness demands kindness as its price places an emotional debt beside bodily protection. Public-resource consciousness makes the cared-for body important to others, while being important becomes an obligation to care in the expected way. Her volunteering and group membership are partly performances intended to secure some privacy. The resulting pressure does not require a secretly malevolent giver: benevolent attention can be sincere and still leave its recipient little room to dislike being attended. At the same time, Tuan's generalization remains the account of someone who feels constrained. It is not a census proving that every person experiences every kindness as suffocation. [HAR-U002-E009–E010/E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e009).

The distinction becomes visible in the interval between medical recovery and agreement. Treatment returns adolescent Tuan to health after the shared attempt, while she conceals her sense of defeat. A successful clinical result has not produced a reconciled account of living. That does not prove the clinicians knowingly ignore a disclosed truth; she does not disclose it. Reiko later supplies the contrary emotional perspective of a parent whose care has not reached her daughter. Her grief is real evidence of failed understanding, rather than proof of either perfect innocence or conscious oppression. When WatchMe displays a warning about Reiko’s measured state, she expresses gratitude for its internal supervision; Tuan recoils from the joining of physiological monitoring to acceptable social feeling. The passage does not show administered treatment or prove that the grief has been biochemically removed. The same intervention can be welcome assistance to one woman and an alarming jurisdictional claim to another. [HAR-U004-E006–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e006); [HAR-U008-E011–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e011).

Harmony consequently gives no warrant for making care and domination synonyms. A seat is offered to Tuan without an established hostile intention. The Kel Tamasheq representative leaves refuge available after she declines. Uwe helps while disagreeing about the danger of her plan. These encounters differ in scope and power, but each prevents her discomfort from being sufficient evidence that another person has no defensible purpose. The refuge offer is particularly useful because it preserves an observed refusal without requiring total independence from medicine: that community also uses WatchMe and obtains treatment. An alternative relation to assistance exists within the sampled encounter; no innocent, medically untouched civilization has been established. [HAR-U003-E009–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e009); [HAR-U006-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e009); [HAR-U017-E004/E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e004).

These local cases matter to the whole argument. They demonstrate that an effective benefit need not settle every difference between giver and recipient. Their modesty is their strength. None proves that every refusal can safely be honored, or that the institutions face no collective problem. They show the novel preserving room for help, gratitude, disagreement and incomplete understanding at once. The final programme will obtain something different: agreement that no longer has to be negotiated with an experiencing recipient.

## How a person exceeds the account made of her

Tuan's first-person narration begins by arranging a self before supplying its history. The parallel defeat and desertion terms culminate in `わたし`, giving the I the appearance of an equivalence reached through declared categories. This is a chosen self-position, not a mechanically verified identity. Later survival, institutional accommodation, lost companions and the abandonment of conscious choosing all give those terms possible bearings. No single episode is designated as their exhaustive solution. The opening makes the reader attend to the difference between a legible formulation of a person and an adequate account of what that person has done. [HAR-U001-E005–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U001_OPENING_AND_PART_I_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u001-e005).

Cian is the clearest test of that difference. Her objection to mass harm precedes an explanation of her position; Tuan initially hears it as empty. Later Tuan reads her as Miach's follower and as a person accommodated to the health order. Both pictures contain observed conduct, but neither accounts for the whole available person. At lunch she is not hungry at the prescribed hour and apologizes for a bodily rhythm of her own. As an adult she says she stopped taking the medication, became afraid of dying and eventually told her parents. Her qualified account of being a balancer describes assent as an attempt to keep Miach alive. The fact that she actually joined the dangerous attempt remains decisive contrary evidence to any portrait of an always effective protector. Her care was a purpose within a failed strategy, not a guarantee that the strategy was harmless. [HAR-U002-E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e007); [HAR-U016-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e004); [HAR-U009-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001).

The Japanese makes the limits of repair part of the encounter. Cian changes knowing she is not to blame into wanting to know it: `わかりたいんだけど`. Her account of adolescent motive is retrospective and qualified. Tuan's response abandons the follower image, but Cian does not simply accept the new praise as a final identity. Nor does Tuan make the relation transparent in return. She sincerely thanks the woman who preserved her life while keeping private the forbidden pleasures that make survival valuable to her. The scene achieves a consequential revision without achieving complete reciprocity. Its ethical force lies in allowing the previous image to become answerable to Cian's words, even when no final exchange can remove her burden. [HAR-U009-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001).

The disclosure order intensifies this achievement by making it late. The meal has already been associated with Cian's death when the novel returns to the earlier conversation. Her choice to live becomes fully audible to the reader after the outcome that can tempt us to make her only a victim. The visual record then lets Tuan experience being looked at by the dead, but the later telephone record changes the evidentiary meaning of the lowered gaze. A picture of a posture cannot settle what another person was doing; a different channel supplies an interaction the picture did not contain. These records sustain a viewpoint after its owner cannot answer again, while leaving the whole private causal history unavailable. The reader must learn from them without confusing preservation with living reciprocity. [HAR-U008-E002–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e002); [HAR-U009-E016–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e016).

This is also a limit on Tuan's narrative authority. An inward confession to Reiko is framed as a confession but never spoken; an imagined DummyMe lesson is withheld from Cian; a later disclosure establishes that the handshake with Gabriel concealed an actual intrusion. These devices differ. One is a canceled or unperformed utterance, another an act concealed in the original telling. The notation cannot make an unspoken statement heard, and selectivity cannot make every narrated event false. Tuan's adverse disclosures—jealousy, self-disgust, invasive conduct, incomplete gratitude—give the reader grounds to judge her beyond her scene-level performance. They also prevent a simple theory in which the account consistently arranges her acquittal. [HAR-U006-E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e007); [HAR-U008-E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e011); [HAR-U013-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003).

The novel's repeated reopening of a person is therefore an interpretive discipline rather than a promise of complete access. Tuan can be corrected without ceasing to be the narrator. Cian can have cared without having protected successfully. Reiko can have loved without having understood. A later record can warrant a stronger conclusion without recovering every reason. These distinctions give the final disappearance of interiority a specific cost: something more than unruly behavior is lost. The person whose outward conformity had been an insufficient account is no longer described as having an inward answer that the account might miss.

## The rebellion changes its remedy

Miach initially gives Tuan the pleasure of having an unarticulated discomfort recognized. This has force because Tuan's alienation predates the friendship. The caffeine session has already shown her an admired father unable to make moderation heard against courteous certainty. Miach names an existing injury; she does not create every ground of resistance. Yet local accuracy becomes an invitation to believe in deeper sameness. Tuan conceals her parentage and fears rejection, demonstrating how much remains unknown inside the apparent recognition. The first-person account makes the attraction compelling while preserving the unequal information on which it depends. [HAR-U011-E001–E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e001); [HAR-U002-E011/E013/E017–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e011).

Miach's authority is already tested by a present refusal. She teaches bodily ownership while continuing painful touching that Tuan asks her to stop. A didactic or intimate reading can explain the scene's functions, but cannot convert the explicit refusal into permission. The novel tells this scene before returning to the pleasurable origin of the relationship, so the reader encounters the limit of recognition before its charm. Later Miach's reassurance about Cian's appetite supplies an equally actual countercase: she can protect a friend's difference in one situation and override it in another. Her politics cannot be adequately reduced either to a fraudulent slogan or to consistent respect for every recipient. [HAR-U002-E007–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e007); [HAR-U016-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e003).

The books give her refusal a material form. They are made through downloaded texts and paid printing, so their use is a practice within the technical world. They offer attachment, knowledge and a way to sustain solitude. Burning them before the adolescent attempt is painful precisely because they have helped her live. She hands Tuan the igniter because she cannot readily perform the decisive destruction herself. This dependence complicates her command without making the request unreal. Her argument that the medically described body is becoming a book asks who has the power to write what the body means. The funeral of books is an effort to prevent an attachment or a description from determining the final act. It does not establish that death is a workable programme for reforming the world. [HAR-U002-E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e006); [HAR-U019-E001–E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e001).

Adult Miach changes the proposed remedy. Her final testimony places the experience of coercive violence beside the discovery that a protective society also fails people who cannot inhabit it. Research then makes it possible, in her account, to change the human participant rather than the social arrangement. Her professed love of the world is central to this development. She no longer proposes simply to refuse an order that cannot accommodate its members. She would remove the consciousness that makes accommodation painful, fitting the body to the social shoe. The continuity is a refusal to accept suffering as necessary; the change is who must be remade to end it. Her testimony supplies an intelligible intellectual and personal route, without making trauma a sufficient cause of every later choice or proving a pure private motive. [HAR-U019-E004–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e004).

Cian's recorded call makes the ethical continuity harsher. To prove the body belongs to oneself, the recipient is asked to display courage to Miach and return to their shared day. Ownership becomes something another person must certify through a fatal proof. The later code admission supports causal participation and manipulated valuation, but does not isolate its exact contribution from the intimate appeal in Cian's particular death. We should therefore resist both an entirely unpressured choice and a complete explanation through an unseen neural command. The speech itself exerts a visible demand. The uncertainty about total causation does not neutralize the appropriation of Cian's ability to answer. [HAR-U009-E017–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e017); [HAR-U019-E007/E011–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007).

Miach is thus neither the novel's unchanging spokesperson for freedom nor an inhuman exception to its concern for persons. Ordinary appetite, humor, reassurance, admitted uncertainty, attachment to books and the wish to be forgiven persist alongside coercive means. Her final request for someone to watch the end of her consciousness gives a particular last experience importance even as her programme denies the continuing need for such a subject. That is a counterweight to her abstraction, rather than a logical proof that she never believed it. Ordinary dying and the desired programme outcome remain different conditions. [HAR-U016-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e003); [HAR-U019-E012–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e012).

## Who may decide what another person knows

The social order acquires power through several channels that the novel keeps distinguishable. Nuaza's caffeine argument loses to an atmosphere without producing an explicit ban. Uwe later distinguishes contractual prohibitions from social assessment and reveals an occupational exception Tuan did not know. DummyMe falsifies bodily reports; professional permission works through the institution's rules. WatchMe also supplies identification for shopping, transport and home access, making disconnection materially costly. The resulting constraint is substantial without one uniform law or a single agent ordering every choice. Tuan's ignorance of a recognized exception is particularly important: skilled resistance gives her real room to move, but no privileged knowledge of the whole structure she resents. [HAR-U011-E002/E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e002); [HAR-U017-E005–E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e005).

Her official competence is nevertheless real. She directs colleagues and destroys the WarBird under fire. She negotiates with Oscar, obtains bounded concessions and later elicits a genuine membership admission through a knowingly false attribution to Vashilov. The successful bluff is an instructive small model of the novel's epistemic problem: a useful result does not make the asserted route to knowledge true. Oscar's admission warrants a revised institutional picture; it does not authenticate words the dead Vashilov never spoke. Nor does later factional involvement mean Oscar's every earlier question was feigned or her help unreal. Her condolence and practical assistance coexist with her use of Tuan's relationships. [HAR-U004-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e003); [HAR-U016-E010–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010).

Tuan crosses the line she contests when she enters Gabriel's body through listening molecules without shown informed consent. The explanation is professional urgency: Gabriel is the available lead, despite having done nothing outwardly abnormal. It is not necessary to deny the danger facing the investigation to recognize the involuntary access. Later disclosures put Tuan in the corresponding position. Vashilov hoped her unwarned visit would stimulate a useful reaction; Oscar says both factions watched her movement to reach their different targets. Her initiative is valuable enough to be exploited. That makes her neither sovereign author of the investigation nor an inert puppet. It shows how practical agency can operate inside purposes the agent is not permitted to assess. [HAR-U013-E003–E005](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003); [HAR-U016-E010–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010).

Nuaza's research extends this distribution of knowledge to whole populations. His earlier defense of science values ambiguity and repeated verification. He later admits that the group crossed the supposed brain barrier while sustaining the public misunderstanding of its protection. Relevant papers remained available but were buried in information. Public access was compatible with effective ignorance. The contradiction concerns who may test a premise: the people whose brains can be entered have not been given the same practical opportunity to judge that boundary as its custodians. This is a literary relation between scientific modesty and institutional secrecy, not a claim that Nuaza secretly engineered the daughter's earlier humiliation or that every public account is manufactured. [HAR-U011-E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e002); [HAR-U014-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e004).

His justification for experiments then returns to benefit: without intervention the endangered children might have died. Tuan distinguishes that result from experimental purpose, and Nuaza makes a qualified concession. Actual help cannot by itself establish that rescue governed the work. Equally, the experiment's instrumental purpose cannot establish that nobody was helped. This exchange is the novel's larger institutional dispute in a father-daughter conversation. Their relation gives him access to her attention, while her jealousy at Miach's selection prevents the criticism from becoming a flattering picture of her own moral purity. The valid objection is not disqualified because its speaker also has an injured, possessive motive. [HAR-U014-E009–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e009).

Nuaza's refusal of the last threshold is real, too. He fears the removal of self-awareness, recognizes that others will refuse it and professes belief in social remedies. His opponent later identifies his defense of conscious dignity as an obstacle. Yet he participates in installing the dormant capacity in recipients who were not asked. The man who preserves one boundary has already helped exclude objection to another. His final bodily protection of Tuan repeats the childhood rescue without answering whether love, reflex or kinship caused the movement. Its definite benefit and his unresolved private motive coexist. Gratitude neither erases his absence nor makes his scientific power innocent. [HAR-U015-E003–E004/E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e003); [HAR-U019-E009–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e009).

The catastrophe depends on this compromised infrastructure and on a meaningful difference among its custodians. Miach has backdoors into several governments' WatchMe controls and admits manipulating valuation; she does not possess the elders' exclusive activation codes. Vashilov's genuine Interpol identity assists a different factional purpose, and his preference for capturing Nuaza becomes acceptance of death as a useful fallback. Threatened people also act through articulated reasons, as Luigi's refusal and Franz's fearful killing show. These are related routes to pressure, not evidence that every violent act is an identical remote command. The faction creates conditions in which authorized opponents will feel forced to choose its preferred end. Power over the decision's circumstances substitutes for possession of the credential. [HAR-U015-E008–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e008); [HAR-U011-E009–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e009); [HAR-U016-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e006); [HAR-U019-E007–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007).

## The last choice does not rescue choice

Tuan reaches Miach without acquiring an unanswerable defense of consciousness. During the ascent, pain can feel like participation in living, yet medicine's actual achievements make a simple appeal to natural inviolability unstable. If diseases can be treated, why should the patchwork function of consciousness be sacred? The succession of once-needed faculties becomes a funeral-like list. These are Tuan's questions and fictional explanatory premises; the novel has not established a real-world neurological theorem. Their literary effect is to deny her the easy victory of a soul whose value follows automatically from its supposed independence of the body. [HAR-U018-E004–E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e004).

Miach consequently puts a serious case to her. A society has demanded internal discipline while continuing to produce people unable to bear it. Why preserve an inherited capacity simply because its suffering has been familiar? Tuan's qualified absence of objection, marked by `たぶん`, grants the prospect considerable force. Her revenge is not an effort to stop harmonics and restore a world of choosing selves. She says she will realize the desired world while withholding it from Miach. The structure of giving and refusal makes her target exact: this person's future, rather than the global intervention's occurrence. No code authority or performed command follows from that promise. [HAR-U019-E008–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e008).

Tuan's two bullets assign personal debts to Cian and Nuaza. They do not prove every causal allegation she has made, and Miach's broad responsibility does not supply a missing order for Nuaza's death. They are nevertheless an actual fatal attack, followed by an emphatic claim that the act belongs to Tuan. This insistence is the last development of a life spent negotiating whose account of her behavior will prevail. It is consequential without becoming proof of metaphysical independence from all causes. Her attachment, information, institutional resources and coercive history remain. The choice is neither purified by her declaration nor erased by those conditions. [HAR-U019-E011–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e011).

Her response to the dying Miach is equally actual. Tuan strokes her hair, takes her to the mountain view and agrees to witness the end of consciousness. A reconciliation reading has substantial evidence in this attentive intimacy. Its limit is that Miach's remaining requests cannot authorize the shots that made her die. Her surprise at revenge is counterevidence to a mutually arranged execution. The earlier delegation of the book burning offers a formal recurrence of Tuan's decisive action, not transferable consent across thirteen years. Killing and care therefore have to remain acts of the same person without one redeeming the other by definition. [HAR-U019-E001–E003/E012–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e001).

That last attention also resists the treatment of Miach as an inferior specimen. Tuan grants no ethically decisive difference between the consciousness described as emulated and the consciousness she calls theirs. The reader sees a particular life end through the ordinary process of bodily death. Tuan's own boundary with the cold then becomes uncertain, and the written farewell fragments before the word is finished. Biological death, formal self-fracture and programme activation are separate claims. The scene establishes the first for Miach and strongly constructs the second for Tuan; the global enactment is still within an even-if sequence. Preserving these differences gives the epilogue work to do. [HAR-U019-E014–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e014).

## A record remains after a recipient disappears

The epilogue supplies the later event. After Tuan descends, the authorized elders decide, enter codes and biometrics, and the programme reaches the billions described as having WatchMe installed. Suicide disappears and almost all conflict disappears. This is affirmative event-and-outcome narration. Treating the intervention as forever unverified would omit the final admitted source. At the same time, it does not make Tuan its displayed operator, supply a census of unimplanted people or validate the fictional neuroscience externally. The particular authority that still decides institutes a condition in which the recipients no longer choose or decide in the old way. Authentication identifies implementers; it does not record the recipients' consent. [HAR-U020-E004/E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e004).

The final voice explains why no one suffers from incorporation: the I receiving suffering no longer exists, and society becomes the unit. This is more radical than making everyone believe a persuasive argument. The difference between the earlier person's experience and the system's account has been removed with the experiencing subject. Outward life continues, including tears and shouts, while interiority is said to be gone. The change fulfils the prior experimental prospect rather than producing a species visibly inert. The benefit is therefore substantial on the new order's terms. The ethical question is whether substituting that order for the continuing beneficiary answers what was owed to those who suffered before it. The narration stages this question; its internal success does not by itself settle it. [HAR-U015-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e001); [HAR-U020-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e006).

## What the strongest alternatives preserve

The most serious defense of the ending grants its own standpoint. The ordinary subject was contingent, conflicted and often miserable; the new social whole abolishes suicide and most conflict while sustaining ordinary activity and retaining an exceptional route to emotional functions. On these premises, insisting that relief must belong to a continuing individual could appear to conserve the very form that generated the distress. The novel gives this interpretation real force through Miach's changed remedy, Tuan's failure to find an absolute guarantee and the affirmative epilogue. Its limit is not a secretly failed outcome. It is the substitution the defense accepts: earlier people do not remain to evaluate what has been done for them. The whole-novel argument must retain both that coherence and that cost.

A skeptical interpretation instead asks whether the final system's self-description should be trusted. Its claims of theory coinciding with reality and its evaluative happiness plainly belong to a situated account. The unprovided speaker identity and the language of perfection justify pressure on those claims. They do not furnish positive evidence that code entry and narrated relief were fabricated. The strongest skepticism here discriminates event, interpretation and evaluation. It questions whether successful coordination establishes goodness while preserving what the last narration actually says occurred. A universal unreliability theory would also cancel the earlier rescues and confessions on which criticism of the system depends.

A defense of individual resistance has its strongest evidence in Tuan's insistence that she fired, her continuing attachments and the particular care of Miach's death. It weakens when it turns that act into prevention of the programme, or treats revenge as consent to all its own means. A complete-determination reading has serious grounds in altered valuation, covert installation and the account of unconscious agency. It weakens when it erases represented hesitation, local knowledge changes, practical alternatives and relationship-specific reasons. Harmony makes ownership of an action difficult enough to require judgment; it does not make every judgment unnecessary in advance.

The resulting literary account neither sanctifies suffering nor treats the absence of a sufferer as an automatically sufficient cure. Its repeated corrections show why a person's presence matters even when she reasons badly, injures others or cannot explain herself completely. Cian's late account, Nuaza's unresolved protection, Miach's changed love and Tuan's compromised final claim all exceed a stable category assigned by someone else. The epilogue gives a world in which that excess has ceased to be a practical problem. The novel leaves us the organized record through which its relief and its loss can still be felt together. A society no longer has to ask its members what their lives mean; the book makes the disappearance of that question the matter of its last surviving address.
