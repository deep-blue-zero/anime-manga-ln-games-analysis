# The preserved I and the reader after it

Harmony makes a subjective account persist after the world that could ordinarily produce its subject has ended. That persistence is constructed throughout the novel, rather than appended as an abstract paradox. Tuan's retrospective voice gives events an order, exposes and withholds information, enters recorded viewpoints, and arranges judgments and affects inside visible enclosures. The epilogue then describes this participant's subjective story as a text that can still occasion emotional functions for a differently situated reader. The crucial formal development is from a first person presented as the result of a declaration to a first person preserved as an object of reading. A written I survives; the account explicitly says that the I which receives suffering does not.

This is a specialist argument grounded in G2's [current claims](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17), especially CL001v2/CL002v2/CL003v2/CL004v2 and CL161v2–CL169. The [actor owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-actor-and-directional-history-reconciliation) and [institutional owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTIONS_AND_COERCION_LEDGER.md#g2-current-institutional-and-mechanism-reconciliation) retain identities, relationships and implementation distinctions. The present account develops their implications without copying their ledgers or assigning the final voice a newly invented personal identity.

## A self is announced before it can be judged

The opening identifies the story through `敗残者` and `脱走者`, then arrives at `わたし`. The calculation declaration and parallel `pls` fields followed by `eql` deliberately make the I an equivalence reached through compromised categories. Neither a specifically military desertion nor a complete psychology of shame follows. What the reader initially possesses is a teller's chosen arrangement of terms. The events by which those terms might be assessed have not yet been supplied. [HAR-U001-E005–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U001_OPENING_AND_PART_I_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u001-e005).

Retrospectively, that arrangement becomes more exacting, not less. Adult Tuan survives through institutional accommodations she resents, imagines comradeship through desertion, rejects an adapted-Miaha double, and finally claims a killing as her own while accepting a world without choosing. These episodes give defeat and departure several supported bearings. They do not nominate one exhaustive referent for the opening. The initial equation remains an interested way of positioning the story, whose adequacy the story puts under pressure. CL003v2 preserves this distinction between a sustained retrospective interpretation and a purported single solution.

The first major exposition already refuses an easy alignment between categorical notation and settled authority. Tuan's theorem-like account is visibly corrected into a rule-like one, and becoming gives way to Japanese prohibitive and obligatory modalities. The enclosure does argumentative work: it displays a revision in how bodily transition should be understood. It does not make the proposition true by parsing it. This distinction is essential because the same novel contains fictional scientific papers, dictionary entries, scripts of bodily checking, memories and affect labels. The notation makes claims and their organization conspicuous; it does not give every enclosed proposition the same evidentiary rank. [HAR-U002-E001/E005/E010/E016–E017](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e001); CL005/CL011.

## Disclosure is a practical power of Tuan's narration

The narrator's selectivity is demonstrable at particular joints. It should be described there rather than inflated into a universal unreliability that cancels the narrated world. In the Sahara sequence Tuan first lets the reader imagine an unmedicalized outside, then explicitly discloses Kel Tamasheq use of WatchMe. The correction converts an attractive geography into a precise distinction among monitoring, treatment access and network membership. It tests the reader's readiness to turn a landscape into a political totality. Tuan's control over the timing contributed to that readiness. [HAR-U003-E009–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e009); CL016.

Other omissions concern her own conduct. The DummyMe lesson is imagined and then withheld from Kian. The confession to Reiko takes verbal form inside `〈confession〉` but is explicitly not spoken. The listener placed on Gabriel becomes known after the apparently ordinary handshake. These are different devices: an inward rehearsal canceled in the scene, an unperformed avowal, and an actual act disclosed late. Treating all of them as equivalent lies would lose what the novel carefully supplies. Treating the label confession as proof that Reiko heard it would be a direct attribution error. [HAR-U006-E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e007); [HAR-U008-E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e011); [HAR-U013-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003); CL034/CL051/CL090.

Tuan nevertheless exposes jealousy, self-disgust, fantasies, intrusive acts and the private reasons she withholds from companions. Her narrative is often less flattering than her scene-level presentation. That is counterevidence to a simple self-exoneration thesis, without making self-accusation automatically objective. The usable account is narrower: she controls disclosure to characters and to the reader in different ways, and the text gives the reader material with which to examine that control. Her responsibility as teller is not exhausted by whether a factual statement can be disproved.

Nor do prolepses grant earlier Tuan the teller's later knowledge. The narration announces Kian's death before returning to the Sahara scene forty-eight hours earlier in story time, and the early promise of Miaha's reunion coexists with reports of her lost life. The narration confidently knows more than the focal actor and initially gives the reader less explanation than outcome. The later bodily meeting resolves present reliance on a final school-era death while leaving the complete medical and custody chain unsupplied. That is a consequential withholding and subsequent reconciliation, not proof that the early assertions meant nothing. [HAR-U003-E001/E005/E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e001); CL012v2/CL022v2.

## A viewpoint can outlive its owner

The recordings make the later survival of a subjective text imaginable without demonstrating the same technical process. In Kian's visual record Tuan is the object at the center of someone else's gaze. The sentence `わたしは死者に見つめられている` reverses the investigator's usual relation to evidence: she is looking, yet experiences being looked at. Kian's preserved viewpoint is neither Kian's new living act nor an omniscient reconstruction of her mind. Tuan's first-person prose and Kian's optical position remain different layers. [HAR-U008-E002–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e002); CL046–CL048.

The later telephone record changes what that visual posture can mean. Tuan's own downward gaze during internal communication suggests a communicative explanation for the bowed head. Playback then supplies a strongly recognized Miaha voice and a solicitation immediately before Kian's apology. The investigation succeeds by changing medium and attribution, rather than by squeezing a final truth out of a visible expression. It reaches substantial interpersonal pressure; it does not isolate the exact contribution of persuasion and manipulated valuation to Kian's death. [HAR-U009-E016–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e016); CL060–CL062/CL154/CL158.

This provides a formal precedent for the epilogue. A retained record can expose a first-person field after its original owner can no longer answer. But analogy has limits. AR recording, a retrieved conversation and the subjective story's final ETML account are not stated to share one acquisition mechanism. It would be unsupported to infer a complete consciousness upload, to identify the story as an unedited brain recording, or to assign its compilation to a named machine. The novel establishes preservation and changed reception more strongly than it establishes the physical production of the preserved artifact.

## Four levels that must remain distinguishable

| Level | What the admitted witness establishes | What it does not establish |

| Fictional ETML reader | The epilogue conditionally describes installed emotion textures and tags as a way to occasion emotional and meta-textual functions | That our browser or any present reader has those textures installed; the exact embodiment of the fictional reader |
| Harmony intervention | Attributed installation and value manipulation, exclusive elder authorization, then affirmative epilogue delivery through WatchMe | That a literary tag itself performs global intervention or that the epilogue's output can identify its producer |

The distinction develops historically. In the opening, literary organization is certain while a fictional function is unexplained. In the epilogue CL004v2 can strengthen the functional account because the text expressly describes one. It is wrong either to backdate that explanation as knowledge available at the opening frontier or to preserve the early uncertainty as though the explanation had never arrived. [HAR-U001-E002–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U001_OPENING_AND_PART_I_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u001-e002); [HAR-U020-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e003).

The described reader makes context-dependent affect available after ordinary social survival rarely demands it. The construction `インストールしてあれば` is conditional; `「実感」` receives quotation marks. These expressions support a designed relation between text, reader and feeling, while leaving implementation and the quality of that feeling underdetermined. The emotional functions are described as remaining in the brain. It would therefore be inaccurate to say every affective capacity has been erased. It would be equally inaccurate to turn their retained availability into proof of an ordinary interior I. The text explicitly distinguishes those functions from the receiver of suffering and describes outward crying and shouting as imitation-like. CL166 owns this difficult compatibility, rather than an invented neuroscientific explanation resolving it.

## The transition preserves a sign while removing its ordinary guarantee

Part IV ends after Tuan repeatedly owns the shots: `わたしが` becomes the isolated `わたし`. Her body's boundary with the cold air then becomes uncertain, and the farewell breaks through `さよ`/`なら` to `わ`/`た`. The missing remainder is not printed. This is a strong formal dissolution after an emphatic claim to agency. The actual source-CSS final panel preserves the isolated fragments and blanks; the interpretation does not depend on treating accidental file truncation as authorial form. [HAR-U019-E014–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e014); CL161v2.

The separate interstice encloses the complete `わたし` in `〈null〉`. This is more demanding than a blank page. The sign remains legible while a no-value-like designation surrounds it. It can look backward at the fracture and forward toward the account of an absent receiving subject. It does not complete Part IV's missing syllable or show a biological death. Its three-line autonomy is formally significant without the EPUB's member boundary becoming evidence of a new scene. [HAR-U020-E001](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e001); CL162.

The epilogue repeats the language/version/encoding apparatus, but the repetition surrounds a different enunciative situation. Its current I/we/you describes Tuan and Miaha as past women and the participant's subjective story as an artifact. The grammatical first person is retained in `わたしはシステムの一部`, then the voice says the I receiving pain no longer exists. This is not an incidental contradiction that must be corrected by inventing a hidden conscious narrator. The text is using the capacity of language to preserve a position after redescribing what, if anything, occupies it. A collective or system position is the strongest formal description. A precise named author, body or consciousness is not supplied. [HAR-U020-E003/E007–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e003); CL163/CL167.

## What the final voice can and cannot certify

The strongest surviving-Tuan counterreading starts from continuity of apparatus and the preceding first-person story. A later framing could preserve Tuan's voice, or the form could itself be a reproduction of her account. These are plausible relations of textual continuity. They cannot override the historical third-person naming, the distant present and the explicit account of subject loss to prove a continuously conscious post-programme Tuan. The final quotation is attributed to Tuan at the WatchMe-online/unconsciousness-descending threshold; it does not identify her as the current framing speaker. CL163 and CL167 preserve that distinction.

A stronger skeptical reading treats the collective voice as a system's self-description and asks whether its perfect-agreement rhetoric is interested. It is interested: theory/reality coincidence and happiness are evaluations within its account. But the record offers no positive sign that the elders' entry and programme outcome were fabricated. Unspecified voice identity cannot indiscriminately return actual narrated enactment to the status of an unfulfilled faction forecast. The textual asymmetry is productive: the novel gives a definite transformation while withholding a fully personified teller authorized to make sense of it. [HAR-U020-E004/E006–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e004); CL115v4/CL164–CL169.

The widening final intervals make the voice's affirmation increasingly isolated. One blank paragraph, then two, then three separate the happiness statement and its two detached `とても。` sentences from closure. These are source paragraph events under the active vertical layout, not translated ellipses. Their strongest effect is to put pressure on the receiverless happiness the prose has just explained. That pressure can coexist with sincere post-subjective affirmation. It does not require an audible hesitation, a liar or a secret rebel. The book preserves the means to feel loss while its future voice accounts for the abolition of the ordinary loser. This is the specialist consequence for G4: form keeps the cost readable without requiring the programme's narrated benefits to be denied.
