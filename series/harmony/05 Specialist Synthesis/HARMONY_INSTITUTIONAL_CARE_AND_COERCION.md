# Care, jurisdiction and the removal of refusal

Harmony makes care dangerous through its success. Tuan's injuries heal; protective objects prevent accidents; ordinary people offer hospitality and concern. Those benefits give the institutions of life their practical credibility. The novel then asks how a capacity to preserve a person becomes a claim to regulate her, and how preservation can finally be redefined until it no longer requires a person who can receive, refuse or dispute the benefit. The institutional movement runs from care that asks for an answering attitude, through infrastructure that makes participation indispensable, to an emergency decision that abolishes the experiencing recipient of distress. This movement is supported by several kinds of authority and several routes to harm. A general word such as control becomes misleading if it conceals who is authorized, what actually happens and where refusal remains possible.

The argument here develops the reconciled [current claims](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17) and [institutional state](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTIONS_AND_COERCION_LEDGER.md#g2-current-institutional-and-mechanism-reconciliation). Those remain the owners of claim versions and HAR-K records. The present specialist explains their relationship; it creates no replacement event register. Its ending discussion concerns the political consequences of the narrated event. Exact final enunciation and ETML form require the complementary formal specialist.

## A benefit becomes a claim upon its beneficiary

The childhood fall gives the institutional argument a difficult starting point. Tuan's father pulls her from the water, repairs her wound and connects medical molecules that protect against infection. The narrator's vivid memory of blood does not invalidate the rescue. Nor does Miaha simply deny medical accomplishment: she grants that society has done well to eliminate most disease. The medicine that can become an instrument of violence has already helped a named child. Any reading that makes this care a sham removes the condition that makes the novel's problem demanding. People have good reasons to want these capacities. [HAR-U002-E015–E016](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e015); CL006.

Tuan's objection begins when receipt of kindness appears to require its emotional repayment. The groups approaching solitary Miaha need not be secretly hostile for their attention to restrict her. Tuan volunteers and participates partly to make herself inconspicuous; social warmth becomes a condition she must perform to obtain tolerable space. The body is called a scarce public resource, and the individual who dislikes such attention appears ungrateful for what keeps her alive. This is a jurisdictional conversion: successful care supplies a reason to claim authority over what its beneficiary should feel and do. Tuan's account is antagonistic and generalizes beyond its sampled encounters, but her actual accommodating conduct supports the pressure she describes. [HAR-U002-E009–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e009).

The hospital aftermath makes the separation between treatment and assent explicit. Medication and counseling return Tuan to health, while she hides her continuing sense of defeat. Her silence is a demonstrated limit on what the treatment setting knows. It supplies no evidence that clinicians knowingly suppress a confession she never makes, or that treatment cannot help anyone. It does show that recovery can be real while agreement with the order that defines recovery remains absent. A body restored to acceptable functioning is not sufficient evidence of a reconciled subject. [HAR-U004-E006–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e006); CL022v2/CL023–CL024.

Reiko gives that problem a different emotional direction. Her account of Miaha slipping through caring fingers expresses grief and a failure of parental understanding. Tuan, who privately withholds her own part in the adolescent pact, cannot receive the grief with the ease she brings to armed negotiations. Reiko's WatchMe warning then translates physiological disturbance into guidance about an acceptable interpersonal state. Reiko thanks it; Tuan sees body and norm placed on the same table. The source supports both response positions. Regulation can feel like welcome assistance to the person being regulated and still make social acceptability part of medical supervision. Reiko's gratitude cannot prove universal assent, while Tuan's disgust cannot cancel Reiko's use of the help. [HAR-U008-E008–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e008); CL051–CL052.

Care also exceeds this regulatory pattern. The train-seat offer to Tuan is a particular kindness, and the Kel Tamasheq representative offers refuge while allowing her refusal. The latter's community selectively obtains medicine through the very trade that offends the medical order. Its offer makes an alternative relation to aid thinkable: accepting a medical benefit need not entail adopting the whole server-connected way of life. The scene tests neither that community's response to every future refusal nor all its internal hierarchies. Its local countercase nevertheless matters. A supported criticism of compulsory care cannot become a claim that every act of attention appropriates its recipient. [HAR-U003-E009–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e009); [HAR-U006-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e009); CL017/CL033.

## Constraint travels through different channels

Harmony's public order does not need a single command center to make bodily conduct conform. The caffeine session offers a precise example. Nuaza attempts a professional-necessity and stress-reduction exception, while the unnamed woman speaks with impeccable restraint and categorical force. He cannot finish saying 適度に, in moderation. No formal prohibition follows; an atmosphere makes the drink a disreputable harm. The social victory belongs to a way of deciding what may be said and heard. It is consequential even though nobody orders an arrest. Because the episode precedes Miaha, Tuan's discomfort with that environment has an earlier, filial history: seeing her father defeated helps make the world unbearable to her before Miaha recognizes her dissatisfaction. [HAR-U011-E001–E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e001); CL072–CL073.

Uwe later specifies how atmosphere acquires material force. His reported survey finds explicit alcohol bans in twenty-six life-government agreement collections; elsewhere social assessment carries the common-sense prohibition. The source distinguishes formal contract, prevailing opinion and public credit. These layers can reinforce one another without being identical. The absence of an explicit ban therefore does not make Tuan's experienced constraint imaginary, and the existence of constraint does not authorize calling every unwritten expectation a law. Uwe's survey is a report by a character; no complete archive of contracts is inspected. Its literary force is to expose how far a capable inspector can mistake a norm's practical authority for one uniform legal source. [HAR-U017-E005](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e005); CL134.

Infrastructure adds a further dependence. WatchMe supplies identity for shopping, transport and access to a home. Going offline is technically distinguishable from finding a tolerable life without those services. Tuan's exasperated account calls complete withdrawal practically impossible. Her later outside visit shows a boundary can be crossed under conditions, including a waiver and loss of ordinary information services. It does not show an unlimited territory free of disease, poverty, contractual authority or danger. Dependence here is generated partly by useful integration: the system can make continuing membership necessary without a guard forbidding the off switch. [HAR-U011-E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e012); [HAR-U013-E005–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e005); CL080/CL092–CL095.

These distinctions make three routes to alcohol analytically important. Tuan's DummyMe falsifies reported bodily data. Going outside changes a connection and its associated services. Uwe's professional rule discounts the health risk of drinking during negotiations in a drinking region, with a report obligation. He explicitly calls it a safety net. It accommodates conduct within institutional assessment rather than defeating assessment. Tuan's surprise is a countercase to her image of herself as the person who knows how to escape: she mastered an illicit workaround while missing a recognized permission. That is a local knowledge correction, not proof that her former misconduct was unnecessary in every earlier circumstance. No completed report is shown at the meal. [HAR-U007-E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U007_PART_II_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u007-e011); [HAR-U017-E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e006); CL027v2/CL135.

The institutional order thus both constrains and makes room. Its exceptions favor people who know a professional repertoire and can justify their conduct through a useful role. Uwe's pleasure depends on this knowledge. Tuan's willingness to use superior rank, covert bodily access and official transport shows that agency can operate through the system one resents. The novel gives no pure, self-sufficient outside from which she could judge every institutional dependency. It gives choices made with unequal access to the rules.

## Expertise can protect a boundary and then conceal its removal

Keita makes the production of knowledge an encounter between people with different leverage. When Tuan names Miaha, he acknowledges having acted as Nuaza's agent and eventually supplies Gabriel's name after learning the investigation's stakes. Tuan then threatens a warrant to obtain a research explanation. His pointed delivery of a paper she cannot readily read makes technical access different from usable understanding; he admits the gesture is partly a deliberate provocation. He gives an actual explanation afterward. The scene supports guarded disclosure, pressure and responsive teaching, without proving every secret Tuan thinks he protects. [HAR-U009-E009–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e009); CL058v2.

His intellectual concessions also resist treating scientific authority as one fixed appetite for command. He corrects his own reward-as-pleasure shorthand toward motivation to repeat a choice. When Tuan suggests that mind rather than body might become obsolete, he laughs and recognizes his own lingering assumption of a sacred, singular soul. A teacher can be coercively pressed into disclosure and still revise an argument for reasons he recognizes. His subsequent suggestion that scientists pursue interesting problems rather than social purposes is hedged self-description and generalization. It explains an attraction without absolving the researcher of what a technology permits. Keita offers a focused portrait of expertise that remains personally answerable in conversation, alongside the later custodians' effort to insulate consequential decisions from comparable public challenge. His full private hierarchy, complicity and ordinary professional conduct remain unavailable. [HAR-U009-E011–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e011); CL058v2/CL059.

The changing brain-access account makes knowledge itself part of institutional power. In the first emergency briefing, Tuan can explain why WatchMe does not ordinarily monitor most of the brain. The liaison distinguishes external scanning, nanoscale resolution and the absence of a report after eight hours. The scene shows expertise and appropriate qualification, not generic official stupidity. Later Nuaza says that molecular passage across the barrier had already been achieved, and that his group deliberately sustained the public misunderstanding by burying relevant open papers among other papers. This is a stronger insider account that changes present reliance. It does not give earlier Tuan secret knowledge she did not possess. [HAR-U007-E007–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U007_PART_II_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u007-e007); [HAR-U014-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e004); CL042/CL099.

The particular method matters. Public availability of a paper is compatible with effective ignorance when an organization can determine what attracts attention. The group does not have to prevent every reader from opening the right document. It can preserve the social belief in an inviolable boundary while crossing it. This gives a political afterlife to the problem of salience in Gabriel's scientific explanation: what becomes available to conscious attention and what becomes available to public judgment are distinct processes, but the novel places selective attention at the center of both. This parallel is a literary inference, not a new technical claim that the group uses its neural program to curate papers. [HAR-U012-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U012_PART_III_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u012-e006); [HAR-U014-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e004).

Oscar's dispute with Tuan about certainty gives the epistemic asymmetry an ordinary supervisory form. Tuan says she has a route toward someone who may know the cause; Oscar presents this as already possessing strong evidence. When Tuan corrects the description, Oscar asserts jurisdiction over the meaning of certainty. Information sharing in a lethal emergency has a serious practical rationale. Yet a superior's need for information does not make a possible lead into accomplished evidence. The ethical problem is located in a specific inflation, rather than in a general demand that all institutions refrain from requiring reports. [HAR-U011-E013–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e013); CL081.

Tuan reproduces the same distribution of entitlement when she implants listening molecules in Gabriel during a handshake. The later disclosure establishes an actual covert act, while useful intercepted output remains unshown. Tuan says Gabriel behaved normally and justifies the intrusion because she is the remaining lead. The scientific interview's guardedness therefore never becomes proof of guilt. Emergency need explains why the investigator wants access; it does not supply the recipient's informed agreement. Her autonomy critique survives as a critique, but it cannot function as an exemption for her own professional practice. [HAR-U012-E002–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U012_PART_III_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u012-e002); [HAR-U013-E003](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003); CL090.

Gabriel warrants a bounded institutional portrait here. She negotiates an overview, guards unpublished work, tries to close the interview and gives a civic answer to the threat. The later financial allegation, her status as a covert-surveillance recipient and reported death increase the stakes without revealing her entire allegiance or proving a particular lie. A person who speaks through institutional limits remains a person with unobserved motives. The admission channels of Nuaza, Oscar and Miaha justify stronger conclusions about the working group; Gabriel's pauses do not justify the same strength about her private guilt. [HAR-U013-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e004); [HAR-U016-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e009); CL085–CL088/CL125.

## Office, faction and intimate access

The working group overlaps WHO, life governments, scholarship and medical industry. Overlap explains how exceptional capacities can inhabit ordinary institutions without making every employee a participant or every official act a faction order. Vashilov's Interpol enrollment is subsequently reported as confirmed. His allegiance to Miaha's faction is also explicit. His real office helps him obtain cooperation and information while his capture plan serves a different chain of purpose. The opposition between genuine and forged authority cannot settle the ethical question, because a genuine credential can be used instrumentally. [HAR-U010-E005–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U010_PART_III_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u010-e005); [HAR-U015-E008–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e008); [HAR-U016-E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e008); CL066v2/CL091v2/CL111/CL124.

Oscar turns Tuan's kinship and friendship into a parallel information route. Her private admission says both factions let the same woman move: the mainstream seeks Miaha, while Miaha's faction hopes Nuaza will approach his daughter. Tuan's relationships become infrastructure precisely because their members remain personally significant to one another. This is more exact than a claim that all trust is false. The tactic depends on real attachment and Tuan's actual initiative. Her scandal bargains do alter local decisions, yet she discovers that her continued movement also serves purposes she did not know. She is neither a wholly free investigator nor an inert puppet. [HAR-U016-E010–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010); CL127.

The admission itself is obtained by an epistemically dirty route. Tuan falsely attributes a statement about inspector membership to Vashilov. Oscar then admits her own senior membership. The second fact does not make the first speech true. A successful bluff can disclose a real arrangement while contaminating the story about how the interrogator knew it. This distinction is central to a novel in which institutional information and intimate trust repeatedly cross. Readers must track the asserted source as carefully as the answer it elicits. [HAR-U016-E010–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010); CL126–CL128v2.

Care within these relations remains effective. Oscar grants limited investigative permission, offers apparently sincere condolence and arranges Uwe's assistance. Uwe recognizes Tuan's personal motive and helps, then objects to her dangerous solo plan. His concern is not unconditional agreement, and her private aim does not make concern an invasion identical to the hidden neural installation. The novel represents differences of degree, means and consequence. It gives no warrant to dismiss a life-saving intervention simply because its recipient dislikes the claim it may place upon her. [HAR-U007-E010–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U007_PART_II_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u007-e010); [HAR-U016-E012–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e012); [HAR-U017-E004/E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e004).

## Several routes to death, one pressure upon final authority

The catastrophe becomes politically coherent when its causal routes are kept apart. The timed Network24 demonstration strongly corroborates the public claimant's ability to make the threat credible. The altered voice remains unidentified; rhetorical resemblance to Miaha does not authenticate a speaker. The recording demands murder as the price of survival, turning the language of escape into explicit coercion. Neither its claim of an ineradicable mechanism nor the timed event supplies the precise neural channel for every later act. [HAR-U010-E010–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U010_PART_III_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u010-e010); CL068–CL070.

Luigi's will and Franz's represented speech establish another route. One refuses to murder and dies under the threat; the other kills his wife and appeals to the absence of capital punishment. Both respond through articulated reasons. Fear and anticipation make the ultimatum productive before its deadline. These accounts do not prove complete absence of neural influence, but they positively support causal explanation through understood language and perceived alternatives. Describing both as simply programmed would discard the very evidence the novel supplies. Calling either unconstrained would discard the threat. [HAR-U011-E009–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U011_PART_III_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u011-e009); [HAR-U016-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e006); CL078–CL079/CL122–CL123.

Miaha later supplies a direct insider admission: she wrote most of the molecular network's code and left backdoors in the WatchMe controls of several life governments. She describes making death acquire a value greater than attachment to living. This is manipulation of the conditions under which a choice appears compelling. It differs both from a spoken threat and from authority to end consciousness. Oscar's earlier expression 幾つかの生府の合意員 describes members of several life governments; it does not identify a council-official target class. The institutional and access limits matter. Neither admission licenses a picture of universal direct control over every person at every moment. [HAR-U016-E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e013); [HAR-U019-E007–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007); CL154/CL115v4.

Miaha expressly lacks the elders' exclusive codes. That limit explains why she manufactures the emergency rather than simply executes her preferred end state. Nuaza's side has covertly installed a dormant capacity for catastrophe; her faction produces conditions meant to make nonactivation unbearable. Vashilov calls death and threats catalysts. His capture of Nuaza is intended to weaken resistance during the next decisive days; death becomes an accepted fallback after capture fails. The strategy attacks institutional judgment as well as bodily life. Creating a condition in which an authorized actor feels compelled to act can be a form of domination even when the instigator never acquires the actor's credential. [HAR-U015-E003–E004/E009/E012–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e003); [HAR-U019-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e009).

This does not turn the mainstream into innocent bystanders. The emergency solution already exists because they sponsored experiments, hid technical access and installed the network without recipient deliberation. Nor does it make the factions identical. Nuaza professes belief in social remedies, fears consciousness loss and is later described by Miaha as defending conscious dignity. The conflict concerns a real final boundary, while both sides share responsibility for building a world in which that boundary can be crossed secretly. The institutional tragedy depends on both common infrastructure and divergent restraint. [HAR-U014-E009–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U014_PART_III_05_DEEP_READING.md#har-u014-e009); [HAR-U015-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e003); [HAR-U019-E008–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e008).

## The authorized rescue removes the person who could contest it

The epilogue narrates actual code and biometric input by the elders after Tuan descends. Delivery reaches the described billions with WatchMe installed. This is an accomplished fictional event, stronger than the factions' earlier forecasts. No Tuan-issued order or newly acquired code is shown. No exact census of all unimplanted people is supplied. Distributed authentication proves who may implement the intervention; it supplies no consent from its recipients. [HAR-U020-E004/E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e004); CL164/CL115v4.

Its benefits are also positively narrated. Suicide disappears and almost all conflict disappears. Outward crying and anger continue, while the receiving I and interiority are said to be absent; residual affective functions remain. Treating the result as an unfulfilled boast avoids the novel's hardest proposition: coercive care can achieve the relief it promises. Treating relief as sufficient justification equally avoids the change in what counts as a beneficiary. The final polity solves the conflict between individual and system by making the system the unit. It does not report persuading each person that the solution is good. [HAR-U020-E003–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e003); CL163–CL166.

The strongest defense of the intervention is the catastrophe it stops and the distress ordinary life had already failed to answer. Nuaza's fearful restraint and Miaha's professed anti-suicide concern have textual substance. The strongest objection is equally specific: recipients are excluded from a decision that removes their capacity to dispute what is done for them. The source does not establish that every less invasive remedy had been exhausted, or prove a real-world welfare theorem. It stages the disturbing fulfillment of an institutional aim. Care begins by restoring a body able to go on living; it ends by preserving coordinated life without the subject who might answer the gift.

That end gives the earlier small exceptions their final importance. A delayed appetite, a moderate drink, an unwelcome emotion, a secret grief and a refusal of rescue are forms in which a person differs from the order attending her. Some are dangerous, some ordinary, some morally compromised. The novel does not make their outcomes equivalent. It shows what is lost when the institution's final answer eliminates the need to negotiate any such difference with an experiencing person. Its affirmative relief and its loss of refusal must be read together.

## Scope and interpretive limits

This specialist uses the completed exact transformed Japanese witness, with the source map's unresolved edition and conversion lineage. It does not authenticate first-print equivalence, external neuroscience or a universal account of care. Reported rule collections, research results, faction rosters and private motives retain their actual channels. The final voice's precise producer and absolute future irreversibility remain unresolved. The control distinctions here are literary and causal analysis; no behavioral model or validation is supplied.
