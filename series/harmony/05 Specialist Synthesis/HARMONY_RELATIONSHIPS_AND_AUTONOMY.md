# Recognition, care and the authority to refuse

Harmony's relationships make recognition an unstable source of authority. To know someone's pain can enable care, but it can also become a reason to decide what that person must do. The novel tests this conversion in intimate persuasion, parental rescue, professional cooperation and final violence. Its strongest countercases are not people untouched by dependence. They are particular acts that leave a refusal standing, offer practical help without complete agreement, or answer a limited request without claiming that the whole person has been understood.

The specialist argument is therefore directional. It asks who recognizes whom, what access that recognition grants, and whether the recipient can revise the terms. It does not classify every caring relationship as coercion or every dependency as the loss of agency. The [actor and relationship owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-actor-and-directional-history-reconciliation) retains the thirty identities and sixty-three directional IDs; the [claims owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17) remains the sole cumulative interpretation history. This study develops the relations among those findings, not a duplicate registry or G4 account of the novel as a whole.

## Recognition that identifies and recruits

Miaha's first successful reading of Tuan offers the pleasure of being found: she identifies a private indifference hidden beneath volunteering and group membership. Tuan confirms the recognition, not every claim Miaha makes about her. `同じ素材` then turns local accuracy into a proposal of deeper identity. The friendship acquires an epistemic asymmetry: Miaha appears able to name what Tuan has not named herself, while Tuan conceals her parentage and worries about being rejected. Accurate recognition supplies a reason for attachment without establishing omniscience or complete reciprocal access. [HAR-U002-E011/E013/E018–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e011); CL007/CL009v2/CL010.

The unwanted-touch scene tests the politics of that intimacy against an actual refusal. Miaha argues for bodily ownership while disregarding Tuan's request to stop. Tuan's rejoinder says she cannot bear what Miaha's hand is doing; it redirects the abstract question to the present agent. An autonomy lesson thereby reproduces the access it condemns. Intimacy, pleasurable earlier contact or a didactic purpose may explain the gesture's place in their relation. None makes a later explicit refusal disappear. The scene supports a precise consent conflict, rather than a conclusion that every aspect of the friendship is false. [HAR-U002-E007–E008/E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e007); CL008.

Book burning makes the allocation of acts visible in another way. Miaha assembles the ritual but hands Tuan the igniter because burning the beloved books herself would hurt. Tuan acts partly in anticipation of the role Miaha expects. The task is neither a wholly imposed motion nor an event Miaha performs alone. It is delegated participation sustained by pleasure in agreement. The future shooting repeats Tuan's decisive bodily role near Miaha's death, but the recurrence cannot supply consent across thirteen years: asking a friend to light books does not authorize that friend to kill one's adult body. [HAR-U019-E001–E003/E013–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e001); CL150–CL152/CL159–CL160.

## The third friend changes the geometry

The triangle is not adequately represented as a leader and two identical followers. Kian objects to mass harm before Tuan understands her grounds; she later describes agreement as an attempt to keep Miaha from the brink. Tuan first takes Kian's assent as evidence of emptiness and later calls that a serious misunderstanding. Her retrospective revision proves the inadequacy of the first attribution, not Kian's immunity from failure. Kian actually joined the attempt and now blames herself for the rescue's timing. [HAR-U002-E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e007); [HAR-U003-E006–E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e006); [HAR-U009-E001–E002](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001); CL054.

`友だち` and `同志` distinguish competing forms of belonging rather than two objectively exhaustive motives. Tuan's military image organizes her own fidelity and deserter anxiety. Kian's suggestion of companionship resists that image. Miaha's later recorded request joins political proof to a return to their shared day, so affection and ideological demand remain entangled. The strongest reading is not that either friend alone knows the complete true Miaha. It is that the meaning of participation depends on which relation each person believes she is preserving. [HAR-U006-E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e011); [HAR-U009-E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e019); CL037/CL062.

The recorded appeal turns bodily ownership into a test addressed to another person: show courage *to me*. That demand has special force against Kian's prior sense of betrayal, but no secure private account lets us reduce the final act to that vulnerability alone. The manipulation project is admitted later; its precise individualized interaction with persuasion stays unresolved. What is firm is that a language of freeing the body is used to demand a proof with irreversible bodily costs. The autonomy claim cannot justify itself merely by naming autonomy. [HAR-U009-E017–E019](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e017); [HAR-U019-E007/E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007); CL154/CL158.

## Care can remain real when understanding fails

Kian tells Tuan about fear, stopping medication and parental disclosure. Tuan gives sincere thanks without revealing her own private valuation of survival through forbidden self-injury. The scene offers an actual improvement in understanding and preserves incomplete reciprocity. Kian's self-blame does not prove her failure to care; Tuan's withheld reason does not make gratitude a lie. These distinctions matter because the same social world pressures people to make interior life publicly shareable. The novel can value a disclosure without presenting complete transparency as the only possible intimacy. [HAR-U009-E001–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U009_PART_II_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u009-e001); CL054–CL055.

Both mothers supply consequential counterevidence to a simple story of hostile caretakers. Tuan's unnamed mother cries, gives news and accompanies her home. Tuan's inferences about guilt and apparent failure to notice grief do not establish a knowing lie or deliberate disregard. Reiko exposes parental effort and the limits of her imagination while Tuan withholds involvement in the pact. Her institutional response may have been inadequate or suffocating, yet its failure does not make her suffering a performance. These are asymmetries in access, not a warrant to invent a mother's private innocence or guilt. Later living Miaha qualifies the early death report without proving what Tuan's mother knew. [HAR-U004-E006–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U004_PART_I_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u004-e006); [HAR-U008-E008–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U008_PART_II_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u008-e008); CL022v2/CL050v2–CL053v2.

Nuaza's relation to Tuan is equally irreducible. Successful childhood rescue, long absence, scientific attraction and secret technical installation all precede the final shielding action. Tuan receives its bodily benefit without recovering an uncomplicated father. Her gratitude is direct; her speculation about love or reflex remains speculation. Later learning of his resistance to removing consciousness changes the meaning of his earlier humiliation. That is a change in the daughter's relation to memory, not an erased record of abandonment or a posthumous confession by Nuaza. [HAR-U002-E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e015); [HAR-U015-E003–E004/E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e003); [HAR-U019-E009–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e009); CL107v2/CL113–CL114/CL156.

## Refusal-respecting alternatives are local and consequential

The Kel Tamasheq representative leaves refuge available after Tuan declines. This is positive evidence of a particular invitation not withdrawn as punishment for refusal. His interest in resources and in Tuan as a woman limits a romantic picture of a power-free outside, and no actual migration tests the community's wider practices. The care countercase remains meaningful at the scope observed. Neither global innocence nor broad reconstruction readiness is necessary for the offered alternative to matter. [HAR-U003-E003–E004/E009–E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U003_PART_I_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u003-e003); CL017.

Uwe offers a different case inside professional life. He recognizes a personal mission, conditions help, supplies an institutional exception, facilitates paper contact and disputes Tuan's solitary risk before arranging transport. Their liking does not make him agree with her judgment; her acceptance of logistics does not mean she accepts his preferred protection. The relation can contain assistance and disagreement together. Compared with the demand that recognition authorize the recognizer's whole plan, this negotiated partiality supplies a practical alternative. It does not establish Uwe as universally brave, selfless or free of self-interest. [HAR-U017-E002–E010](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U017_PART_IV_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u017-e002); CL131–CL139.

The unnamed seat-offering woman similarly supplies an actual act of concern without a private biography. Tuan's rejection and inward hostility reveal how unwanted proximity can feel coercive without proving the giver's hostility. Recipient distress and giver intention answer different questions. That distinction prevents autonomy analysis from becoming a device for declaring every kindness secretly violent. [HAR-U006-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U006_PART_I_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u006-e009); CL033/CL036.

## Professional agency repeats intimate asymmetry

Tuan implants Gabriel with listening molecules while claiming the privileges of investigation. Gabriel's unchanged outward behavior does not mean she consented, and Tuan explicitly supplies no abnormal act as cause. Vashilov then reveals his own informational use of Tuan; Oscar later admits both factions tolerated and watched her movement to reach their targets. The structure repeats across different technologies and motives: one person's activity is made useful under an arrangement the person does not know. This is a supported analogy, not proof of identical moral weight, office or mechanism. [HAR-U013-E003–E005](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U013_PART_III_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u013-e003); [HAR-U016-E010–E011](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e010); CL090/CL091v2/CL126–CL127.

Oscar's actual help after disclosure and Vashilov's vulnerable plea complicate any fixed division into caring ally and coercive enemy. Genuine institutional identity coexists with factional pursuit; a requested fatal shot is obtained through an informational bargain made in pain. Tuan's self-disgust records pressure and mixed justification without canceling her act. Professional responsibility and personal grievance require separate judgments; neither a badge nor a request supplies complete moral absolution. [HAR-U015-E008–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e008); [HAR-U016-E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e015); CL111/CL117v2/CL124.

## A final request is narrower than a redeemed relation

At the end Tuan accepts the prospect of a world without a choosing I while deciding that Miaha will not enter it. The two chest bullets are assigned to Kian and father, making absent relations the measure of present revenge. Tuan declares revenge finished, answers Miaha's requested mountain view and agrees to witness consciousness's end. The transition from killing to care can be read as an intimate reunion, and that reading has real evidence in touch, conversation and attention. Its limit is consent: Miaha asks what happens now, after being shot. Nothing makes her later requests an authorization of the earlier deprivation. [HAR-U019-E011–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e011); CL157–CL160.

This scene also prevents care from becoming simply the opposite of violence. Tuan can be the cause of dying and the person who listens to the dying person's particular wish. The acts must remain together without being averaged into a balanced moral score. Recognizing Miaha's consciousness as not materially lesser than their own refuses a dehumanizing justification for what Tuan has done. That recognition intensifies responsibility rather than dissolving it. The epilogue's historical co-naming supplies no new reciprocal exchange, resurrection or consent to the shots. [HAR-U019-E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e014); [HAR-U020-E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e008); CL160/CL167.

Across these relations, autonomy is not established by being free of every influence. It becomes assessable through the available refusal, what is concealed, who controls implementation and who bears its cost. Recognition is most dangerous when local accuracy claims the right to finish another person's story. It is most defensible, within the observed countercases, when it can remain partial and answerable to a particular recipient. That is this specialist's cross-relationship conclusion; the institutional, formal and ending studies retain their separate responsibilities, and an integrated G4 argument remains subsequent work.

No relationship here supplies a general behavioral model. Tuan's broad candidacy and the bounded readiness of Kian, Miaha, Nuaza, Oscar, Vashilov and Uwe remain the actor owner's independently scoped judgments. The present study does not manufacture reverse intentions, individualize every death mechanism, generalize from one kindness to a culture or call already read outcomes blind tests.
