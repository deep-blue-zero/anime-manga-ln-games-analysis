# A successful ending and the loss within success

Harmony's ending obtains the relief its intervention promises. Suicide disappears, almost all conflict disappears, and people continue outwardly ordinary lives. The narrative does not leave this as a proposal waiting forever for verification. Its ethical difficulty arises because this successful outcome is achieved by removing the self that receives suffering and makes a conflict its own. The ending therefore cannot be understood adequately as technological failure, a simple triumph of individual resistance or an unqualified utopia. The relation between benefit and recipient has changed. What the new order calls happiness no longer answers the same subject's claim for a less painful life.

That argument begins from G2, especially CL115v4/CL128v2/CL161v2–CL169 in the [claims owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_CLAIMS_PREDICTIONS_AND_REVISIONS_LEDGER.md#g2-current-arguments--complete-admitted-novel-2026-10-17). The [institutional owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTIONS_AND_COERCION_LEDGER.md#g2-current-institutional-and-mechanism-reconciliation) owns the separate technical and authorization channels; the [actor owner](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_ACTOR_RELATIONSHIP_AND_READINESS_LEDGER.md#g2-actor-and-directional-history-reconciliation) owns the personal histories. This study tests how those channels converge at the ending without claiming to be the G4 integrated novel synthesis.

## The end arrives in more than one register

There are at least four consequential closures. Miaha dies by an ordinary bodily process after Tuan's fatal attack. Tuan's first-person account breaks at a formal threshold. The epilogue subsequently narrates authorized programme delivery and its effects. A final historical voice places the two women inside a story of the I's disappearance. These events belong together, but reducing them to one instant would produce errors about agency, chronology and death.

At Part IV's end, global code entry is still inside Tuan's even-if sequence. She is attending to Miaha despite what may be happening elsewhere. Miaha's white breath stops and the narration describes bodily and brain heat leaving; consciousness disappears through death. This is a definite narrated death. Tuan's later bodily boundary confusion and clipped farewell are strong formal evidence of self-fracture. They do not independently establish her biological death, an observed elder command or a world result already seen from the mountain. [HAR-U019-E013–E015](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e013); CL159–CL161v2.

The epilogue adds an actual later account: shortly after Tuan descends, authorized elders enter codes and biometrics, and the programme reaches the WatchMe-installed population. Keeping that addition is necessary to completion. Continuing to say only that activation might happen would erase the last admitted source rather than preserve caution. Conversely, the epilogue does not retrospectively make Tuan a witness to code entry at the bunker or turn her promise into a displayed control operation. [HAR-U020-E004/E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e004); CL115v4/CL164–CL165.

## Catastrophe makes one authority choose what it had refused

The strategy is coercive precisely because Miaha is powerful without being sovereign. She claims to have written most of the installed network's source code and to possess backdoors into several life-governments' control systems. That access permits value manipulation. It does not give her the several authorization codes known only to the elders. Her own correction of the button shorthand specifies this limit. The catastrophe makes authorities who have refused the removal of consciousness face conditions under which they will authorize it. [HAR-U019-E007–E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e007); CL154–CL155/CL115v4.

The institutional scope must remain exact: `幾つかの生府の合意員` means members of several life governments, not a council-office target class. Nor does several-government server access imply universal access or access to the elders' codes. The prospective outsider accounts, current insider admission and final enactment each add different support. Technical reach, office membership, factional loyalty and authorization are not the same power. [HAR-U016-E013](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e013); the [attributable wording erratum](../03%20Longitudinal%20Ledgers/HARMONY_KNOWLEDGE_INSTITUTIONS_AND_COERCION_LEDGER.md#g3-attributable-wording-erratum--2026-10-18) preserves the earlier mistaken English scope.

The catastrophe also includes a social route. Franz's stated reason for killing is fear of the announced threat and the hope of preserving his own life within a continuing legal order. Such conduct can be caused through anticipation and articulated reasons without each act being a separately demonstrated neural command. This matters because the ending's solution answers both manipulated valuation and the disorder generated around its threat. It would be reductive to turn the whole intervening society into puppets whose stated reasons never count. [HAR-U016-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U016_PART_IV_01_DEEP_READING.md#har-u016-e006); CL122–CL123.

The elder decision is then a final exercise of the very faculty the programme removes. The epilogue says `決断した`, and the resulting world has no hesitation, choice or decision. The privileged authorize a state in which the recipients no longer need or possess the same form of authorization. Biometric authentication proves an implementing authority's identity within the fiction; it does not prove the recipients' consent. This asymmetry is not canceled by a beneficent outcome. It is also not created only by the final event: secret installation has already excluded recipients from deliberation. [HAR-U015-E003–E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e003); [HAR-U020-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e004); CL107v2/CL164.

## Tuan's revenge accepts the future and excludes Miaha

Tuan's final act does not save humanity from Miaha's programme. Her ascent supplies reasons for defending experienced life—pain felt as participation, attachment to particular people and the effort required to reach Miaha—while also undermining her ability to give consciousness an absolute foundation. The five once-needed faculties become a funeral-like list. She reaches the encounter as someone still feeling and choosing while wondering whether those capacities are obsolete. [HAR-U018-E004–E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e004); CL143–CL145.

At the bunker she initially insists that nobody needed to die. After hearing the proposal, however, she says she probably has no objection to the described world. Her final threat distinguishes realizing that world from giving it to Miaha: `あなたの望んだ世界は、実現してあげる` is followed by the refusal of its personal beneficiary. This is qualified assent paired with exclusionary revenge. It does not prove knowledge of every possible consequence, but neither can it be paraphrased as a refusal of harmonics. [HAR-U019-E010–E012](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e010); CL156–CL158.

The two chest bullets are assigned to Kian and Nuaza. That distributive accounting makes Tuan's reasons particular. Miaha's broad acknowledgment of responsibility does not authenticate every individualized causal chain, and the father was not shown to be another random neural target. Nevertheless, the novel plainly performs a fatal attack, then lets Tuan insist that it belongs to her. The ownership is not a neutral demonstration that all coercive histories have vanished. It is a first-person claim made at the limit of first-person life. Her intention to settle personal debts survives her acceptance of a future in which such a personal creditor will no longer be the unit. [HAR-U019-E011–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e011); CL157–CL160.

Afterward Tuan responds to the dying woman's requests. She strokes Miaha's hair, carries her to the mountains and agrees to witness the end of her consciousness. This is actual care performed by the person who has denied her future. It cannot acquit the shooting, but it cannot be dismissed as unreal because it follows violence. The difference between denying Miaha's desired outcome and honoring a concrete last request makes the encounter ethically irreducible. It gives the mass intervention a particular person whose own end is watched rather than merely counted.

Miaha's own final request does not establish prior consent to the shots. Her surprise at revenge is material counterevidence to an arranged execution reading. The school-era request that Tuan light the books likewise does not authorize a later killing. Both scenes put a decisive movement in Tuan's hands, but a repeated pattern is not a transferable permission. [HAR-U019-E003/E012–E014](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U019_PART_IV_04_DEEP_READING.md#har-u019-e003).

## The strongest case for the new world is allowed to stand

An adequate counterreading must grant the programme its narrated benefits. Disease has already been substantially conquered, medical care actually rescues people, ordinary kindness has value, and the final intervention abolishes suicide and almost all conflict. Miaha's claim that suffering need not persist solely to honor an inherited faculty has real pressure inside this world. Tuan cannot simply defeat it with a surviving theological guarantee or a timeless proof that natural functions are inviolable. [HAR-U002-E015–E016](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U002_PART_I_02_DEEP_READING.md#har-u002-e015); [HAR-U018-E004–E006](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U018_PART_IV_03_DEEP_READING.md#har-u018-e004); [HAR-U020-E006–E007](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e006).

A sincere post-subjective affirmation of relief is therefore a textually strong reading. No one any longer suffers from incorporation into the system because the receiver of that suffering is absent. From the standpoint the epilogue describes, continuing to insist on an abolished subject's loss might seem to repeat the outdated categories that generated the problem. Historical first persons and heroes can be remembered without being practically needed. The text even offers ETML as a route through which emotional functions can still be occasioned. The endpoint is not simply a silent, inert species.

But this defense reaches its limit exactly where it is strongest. It does not show the earlier sufferers accepting a remedy on terms they themselves can continue to evaluate. It substitutes a social whole for the individual unit and removes the receiver to whom the injury was owed. The remedy's internal coherence cannot settle whether such substitution is justified. The novel gives that cost formal and personal force through Tuan's account, Kian's belatedly recognized care, Miaha's watched death and the requiem. The argument does not require a claim that all suffering is intrinsically valuable. It asks what follows when relief ceases to belong to a continuing beneficiary.

The text does not delete every emotional function. It distinguishes residual functions, expressive behavior and interiority, without providing a complete theory reconciling them. Continued tears cannot alone prove a hidden conscious resister; the stated subject-loss cannot erase the retained ETML-triggerable capacities. The tension belongs to the fiction's account and its formal purpose. No external neuroscience is necessary to recognize it, and no external neuroscience is established by recognizing it. CL166 and the [narration study](HARMONY_NARRATION_ETML_AND_FINAL_VOICE.md#four-levels-that-must-remain-distinguishable) preserve those levels.

## Requiem and affirmation inhabit the same closure

The music field, five enclosed hallelujahs, angels, soul, requiem, heaven and ascent make system completion speak through sacred and funerary images. The same exact Messier identifier and fivefold pattern appeared earlier inside Tuan's anger at unchosen installation. Its repetition does not have to carry precisely the same emotional stance: a bitterly heard promise and a later account of fulfillment can use the same apparatus. Their relation makes the reader hear both the offer of salvation and the removal it requires. The source provides no actual soundtrack or external composer identification. [HAR-U015-E004](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U015_PART_III_06_DEEP_READING.md#har-u015-e004); [HAR-U020-E005/E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e005); CL108/CL168.

The historical pairing of Miaha and Tuan as the I's last mourners converts their unequal actions into a shared memorial function. It does not say that they wanted the same thing in every respect or acted with reciprocal consent. Miaha is already bodily dead; Tuan's final farewell is newly quoted at the online/unconsciousness-descending threshold. `もう二度と会うことはないでしょう` gives parting its felt finality, but it remains parting speech, not an inspected proof of absolute technical irreversibility. The final speaker's attribution supplies a fuller quotation; it does not repair the earlier clipped text or sharpen the timing into certainty that Tuan was still offline while speaking. [HAR-U020-E008](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e008); CL167.

The final `とても。` sentences repeat happiness while becoming increasingly separated by blank paragraphs. This positive formal construction produces strong ironic pressure, especially after a requiem for the receiver of happiness. Its irony need not be voiced by a covert rebel: a sincere or ritual affirmation can become ironic through placement and what the reader has been made to remember. The conditional heaven statements and `おそらく` also qualify the evaluative ascent. The novel does not require us to call the global outcome false in order to hear its happiness as troubling. [HAR-U020-E009](../01%20Sequential%20Readings/HAR-U020_INTERSTICE_AND_EPILOGUE_DEEP_READING.md#har-u020-e009); CL169.

## Three limits that do not cancel the ending

| Question | Warranted result | Limit retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the intervention occur? | Yes, in the epilogue's affirmative event/outcome narration: elder codes and biometrics, delivery to WatchMe-installed billions, stated consequences | No exhaustive census of unimplanted people, no complete operating logs or external scientific validation |
| Who produces the final voice? | A changed collective/system enunciative position retrospectively frames the subjective participant story | No exact personal name, body, compiler, runtime or continuously conscious Tuan identified |
| Is restoration absolutely impossible? | The novel ends in a settled subject-loss condition and marks parting as final within the represented history | No demonstration of impossibility under every future intervention; earlier experimental restoration also prevents treating all such loss as inherently technically irreversible |

The second and third limits must not be used to undo the first. Equally, a definite event cannot close every question about who reports it or what is eternally possible. Earlier restoration was a bounded experimental report; it does not establish a present available remedy after the final intervention. The final farewell and historical distance make loss decisive as an ending without becoming universal engineering laws. CL105v2/CL107v2/CL115v4/CL163–CL169 retain these different strengths.

The resulting specialist judgment is that Harmony locates the ending's loss within success itself. Tuan's particular revenge demonstrates a last capacity to distinguish whose future matters; the elders' authenticated decision closes that kind of question for the described population. The surviving text lets a reader apprehend a subjective history whose society has declared the subject unnecessary. G4 must preserve the actual relief, the forced substitution of its recipient, and the unresolved identity of the voice together. Removing any one would simplify the novel at the cost of its evidence.
