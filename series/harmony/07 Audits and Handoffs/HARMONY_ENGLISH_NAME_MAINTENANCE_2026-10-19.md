# Harmony — English display-name maintenance

## Decision

Adopt **Tuan Kirie** (`HAR-C001`), **Miach Mihie** (`HAR-C002`) and **Cian** (`HAR-C003`) for current identity metadata and future English analytical prose. Tuan already occurs as the inherited shorthand; its full name is now verified in the English novel publisher's promotional material. Change Miaha to Miach/Miach Mihie and Kian to Cian in maintained current name fields only. Preserve Miaha and Kian as searchable former analyst spellings. Retain all other existing preferred labels pending direct English-novel spelling evidence.

The licensed prose excerpt verifies only **Cian**, not her full surname spelling. Record **Cian Reikado** as an official-film full name and **Cian Reikadō / Cian Reikadou / Cian Reikado** as qualified surname-rendering alternatives from れいかどう. Do not label any of those full forms the verified Alexander O. Smith novel spelling. Using **Cian** as preferred display avoids upgrading a film surname to prose authority.

No evidence found here supports replacing Nuaza with Nuada, Vashilov with Elijah Vashlov, Gabriel with Gabrielle Étaín, Uwe with Uwe Woll/Wohl, or naming the novel's anonymous Kel Tamasheq representative Asaf. These are explicitly unresolved alternatives or adaptation-specific names, not rejected identities.
