## An arrival first imagined as destruction

The opening reverses the apparent geography of freedom. Japan is the desert into which Tuan falls; harmony is an antlion trap. Her approaching PassengerBird becomes the setting of a violent fantasy, whose destruction resembles the WarBird she destroyed in Sahara. The embedded Magritte note supplies an attributed visual comparison for falling passengers, then the ground population grotesquely abandons its benevolence. Block 9 explicitly cancels the imagined event: while she has been entertaining this pointless fantasy, the aircraft has landed. An actual return follows an unreal destruction (E001). The distinction matters because the unit later does depict an abrupt bodily emergency. It trains the reading to preserve the narrator's transitions between fantasy, scene and later knowledge (HAR-CL031).

Arrival also restores an informational environment. The same augmented field attaches user ratings to a café and social assessments to Tuan and Kian: the café and Tuan have four stars, Kian three. Shared display grammar brings persons and services into comparable forms of evaluation, without proving identical criteria. Kian's friendly concern about Tuan's skin immediately turns bodily appearance into a social judgment (E002–E003).

The reader now learns the withheld name DummyMe and its stated function: installed in Tuan's body, it continually sends false internal information, preventing WatchMe from alerting her contracted counselor about her unhealthy conduct. This resolves a specific reader-level information gap left at HAR-U005. Her skin nevertheless supplies a visible trace. Data evasion has not made her body socially unreadable. Nor does this explanatory narration mean that she has told Kian or Oscar the method (HAR-CL031/CL035).

## The insult and the body it names

The health list makes ordinary adulthood an accumulation of pills, schedules, food, consultations and medical molecules. Tuan's ensuing equations turn rough skin into failed self-control and dark circles into insufficient resource awareness. Compulsory adult disclosure of histories, including medical records in many life-governments, gives social assessment an ostensibly open basis (E003). The passage connects standards, visible bodily evidence and published information rather than relying on surveillance as a single undifferentiated cause.

The enclosed question and answer identify an old insult for a fat person; the following account distinguishes the word's decline because it hurts people from the near-disappearance of both fatness and excessive thinness under continuous health care. ## Protection that anticipates experience

Tuan experiences the airport's avoidance of monumental authority as a politically charged accomplishment. Its absence of an overt smell of power makes her infer a large technical effort to manufacture gentleness. `慈母によるファシズム` is her pointed formulation, not an independently delivered institutional self-description (E004). Her account of a profiling sheet in the server then gives a concrete mechanism: accumulated preferences, ethical tendencies and therapy history allow artworks to be filtered or warned about before she encounters them. The protective double takes over what she dislikes.

Miaha's remembered Hiroshima art story expands this concern from reception to possible creation. Both girls call the described act in bad taste. Miaha nevertheless values the older work's capacity to wound and upset, and claims that anticipatory filtering now prevents both audiences and artists from reaching such possibilities. That last extension belongs to her argument; the story does not independently demonstrate the disappearance of every offensive idea. Its force here comes from the conjunction of acknowledged hurt and the imagination she envies (HAR-CL033).

The unit also supplies material benefits of this social order. Food distribution, shelter and employment support qualify Tuan's observation of a low-rated cleaner; her confidence about his particular living conditions remains a supposition. Later, an unnamed woman offers Tuan a seat out of concern. These details keep care as actual provision and interpersonal action within a reading of coercion. Tuan's objection is sharpened by kindness that works, not sustained by the proposition that every helpful act is a disguise.

## The absent leader and the double she leaves

Walking beside Kian creates a missing position ahead of them. Tuan likens the two survivors to a temple robbed of its image, calls Miaha their charisma and locates the loss thirteen years earlier. Recollection enclosures, an “absent” Miaha who seems to speak, and Tuan's fruitless backward glance mark memory and projection explicitly (E005). The passage does not stage a bodily return of Miaha. Her repeated full name makes absence insistently present.

The adulthood definition gives WatchMe installation, life-government membership, server connection and community participation a nested formal order. Against it, an imagined Miaha reiterates bodily ownership. Tuan then says she cannot decide whether continuing to carry this loss is shameful or precious (E006). That uncertainty complicates a triumphant account of fidelity to rebellion.

Her later explanation to Kian makes Tuan feel like Miaha imparting forbidden knowledge. She imagines teaching DummyMe through repeated `ねえキアン`, then expressly replaces this performance with a dry remark about being treated as a sociopath. A knowledge transfer rehearsed in her thought remains withheld in the scene. The retrospective history of improved grades and gathered knowledge supports her description of becoming Miaha's doppelganger, while this refusal shows that resemblance does not mechanically reproduce every act (E007/E009; HAR-CL034).

## Two accounts of the same recruitment

Lunch keeps both women inside the health infrastructure. WatchMe pays the transit fare through linked credit; restaurant warnings present calories and genetic-damage probabilities as a courtesy before diners choose within consultant limits. Tuan calls these warnings routine, something customers are satisfied to receive and then let pass. Risk communication persists in this tightly regulated meal (E008/E010).

The sentiment enclosure places Miaha, Kian and Tuan against one city view: the one Miaha tried to wound, Kian accommodated and Tuan escaped. At the table, Kian offers a different picture of the girl who first approached them. Perhaps Miaha was waiting for someone to spend time with. Tuan rejects that picture because it does not fit her image of Miaha; she distinguishes `友だち` from `同志` and develops a military analogy. Her insistence moves from “probably” to “must,” but the evidence remains an interpretation of recruitment rather than access to Miaha's private intention (E011).

Kian asks whether they failed to be the soldiers Miaha expected. Tuan imagines them as deserters and wonders whether a surviving Miaha would forgive them; she ends by saying she cannot know. Friendship and comradeship can overlap, as Kian suggests. Tuan's categorical distinction serves her current account of fidelity and failure. The competing reading is consequential because it offers loneliness and companionship alongside ideological selection, without reducing either possibility to an established motive (HAR-CL037).
