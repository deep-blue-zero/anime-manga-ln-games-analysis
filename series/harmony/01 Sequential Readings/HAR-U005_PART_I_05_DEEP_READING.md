## Colors that place rebellion inside an office

The narrator introduces the medical army through the admiring image of a maiden's cheek, then describes its pink uniforms, equipment and camp (E001). The color presents armed organization through a convention of gentle bodily health. Against this pale field, the inspector's scarlet coat makes Tuan conspicuous. Oscar wears the same institutional red. Their conflict therefore takes place between two people visibly authorized by the same office; color does not simply sort rebel from institution.

Tuan leaves the distribution of the goods and profits to Etienne, taking her own and A-kun's share. Her inference that they cannot be taking everything rests on the credits she periodically receives, while she expressly declines to learn the full accounting. That selective interest gives adult escape a practical scale: enough cigars and wine matter more to her than mastering the whole trade. The lung/liver list names the desired goods by their capacity to harm her body. Repeated `みみっちい` (analyst's gloss: petty or paltry) measures this available rebellion against the adolescent fantasy. She has obtained a small bodily refusal, and knows how diminished it is.

That sequence supports a reading of defiance remaining entangled with the institutional body rather than occurring in an untouched outside (HAR-CL025).
