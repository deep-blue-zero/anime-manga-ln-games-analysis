## A report that can become a weapon

The opening declaration's second item, `少なくとも、みなにそう思われている` (analyst's gloss: at least, that is what everyone thinks), prevents the first item from being treated as a neutral job description (E001). Tuan subsequently explains a genuine causal power: after complaints from several medical administrations, the content of her inspection report can lead to conflict (E002). That is an institutional consequence, qualified as a possibility. She expressly says the Sahara inspectors have not chosen a side. The unit therefore does not depict her writing a particular report or authorizing Niger's fighting.

Her history distinguishes the bureau's original genetic audit from a greatly expanded mandate to preserve the right to life. Even the earlier task is retrospective institutional knowledge: `業務だったはず` marks what she understands its business to have been, before her service. The narrative explains the symbolic spiral without supplying an independent institutional archive. Her impatience with the inherited mandate and her remembered Miaha maxim frame this explanation as an interested account. Nevertheless, her job's practical power and danger receive more support than a vague allegation that medical care is oppressive.

The dictionary block defines 生命主義/Lifism through a political priority and a three-part medical lifestyle (E002). It is a displayed attributed definition inside this fiction, not an analyst's universal theory. The surrounding narrative turns its criteria into a dispute over recognition: the Kel Tamasheq cite their own WatchMe use against Niger's charge. Health technology can thus function as evidence in a political argument about who may intervene, without making network membership, treatment access and coercive incorporation identical. This develops HAR-U003's selective-use distinction rather than erasing it.

Tuan calls this dangerous apparatus her only escape. She receives the room to evade a society's ordinary protections by working within an institution empowered to judge other people's protection. This is a specific dependence, supported by her adult conduct and stated motive; it does not prove that every inspector secretly wants violence. The list of twelve inspectors' deaths in twenty years belongs to her narrated professional account. Its varied causes give danger a countable form, before the scene shows how that danger can become her own capacity to act.

## Enacted force, uncertain surveillance

Tuan is now explicitly twenty-eight and a senior inspector with continuing weapons training (E003). Etienne's appeal reverses a merely physical hierarchy: the muscular companion whom she describes as lacking combat experience needs her intervention. His alarm is depicted; a permanent cowardice diagnosis would exceed it. Tuan's competence is evidenced by action, not only by her self-description. She takes over the defense and destroys the aircraft (E004). The weapon was a gift from the warrior, adding another practical dependency to the trade whose pleasures she wants.

Before acting she asks whether the pursuer is autonomous. Etienne answers probabilistically, with `たぶん` and a likelihood judgment based on known electronic countermeasures (E003). Tuan's objective is conditional: nontransmission would protect the transaction from identification. The subsequent explosion verifies destruction in the narrated scene; it does not independently verify that no image reached a headquarters. Her later scan establishes that she sees no further low-level pursuit and explicitly leaves vigilance in place. Treating the episode as complete proof of invisibility would turn a tactical inference into omniscience.

The list of WarBird components divides wings, body, biological brain fused with a neural network, and armament before their destruction (E004). These are the account's fictional embodied features, not external engineering verification or proof of the outer ETML's operation. The explosion adds orange to the previously blue/yellow landscape. Violence is incorporated into Tuan's aesthetic noticing rather than given only a solemn moral description. Her later bodily collapse and quiet inward clamoring qualify a simple portrait of effortless mastery: effective action is followed by depleted attention and reliance on Etienne to finish the journey. Neither the pleasure motive nor the combat competence makes her independent of other people.

## Two awakenings and an earlier failure

Sleep joins the adult aftermath to the adolescent hospital without an initial date label (E005). The sentence about opening her eyes and knowing she has failed initially permits continuity with the preceding scene. The machines, child's monitor, mother and dependence on Miaha establish the earlier recovery. The last awakening then returns explicitly to the twenty-eight-year-old beside the contraband boxes (E010). This is a controlled disclosure structure; the account can represent a remembered sequence across sleep without certifying its psychological mechanism as a literal dream.

Survival is first focalized as failure. Tuan's recollection also discloses an earlier admission before meeting Miaha, after an eating disorder, while carefully denying an equally definite wish to die at that earlier time (E005). She describes an undifferentiated desire rather than a single clear prior intention. Her later dependence on Miaha's guidance is explicit, and the teller calls that dependence other-reliance (E006). This supports the existing charisma account without explaining the actual rescue sequence. The narrative does not show who found the girls, which intervention saved Tuan or precisely what happened to Miaha.

The disappointment enclosure joins excess and deprivation as two failed routes through the body. It makes Tuan's evaluative position visible, while the child-monitor explanation preserves the established distinction between a medical port and adult WatchMe. A hospital scene with external monitoring is not a contradiction of the adult implant rule. Tuan recognizes survival through bodily apparatus before she receives reliable information about her friends.

## A mother's report and an anger she cannot voice

Tuan's first mutter about failing may be unheard or poorly voiced; the text itself offers that alternative (E006). Her mother's tears cannot therefore securely prove deliberate disregard of the remark. Tuan infers from the mother's puzzled expression that the subsequent Miaha question has been heard, repeats it, and receives a report: Miaha could not be saved. Tuan then says that Miaha died and the mother nods (E007). Kian is reported safe and receiving treatment at another center. These are attributable scene-level reports, stronger evidence of what Tuan is told than an unspecified retrospective assertion, but not direct inspection of either friend's body or outcome. The earlier reunion promise remains unresolved.

The mother's bitten lip looks to Tuan `まるでそれが自分の罪だとでも言うように` (analyst's gloss: as though it were her own fault). The comparison marks interpretation, not access to a confession. Tuan's anger is directed at a social atmosphere that makes strangers feel responsible for preventing every death. Her claim that the mother and Miaha had no connection is also her claim, not proof that the mother's grief is meaningless. A parent's grief for a daughter's friend and alarm at the daughter's near death are material compatible readings; the text gives no private account settling them.

The anger block marks a forceful inward address that the exhausted Tuan cannot actually deliver. Her voiced response is only the acknowledgment of death. This gap matters for the relationship ledger: ideological rejection is not a completed confrontation, and the mother's nod is not agreement with Tuan's critique of public feeling. The source-required anonymity of this mother is retained; no personal name is invented.

## Recovery without assent

Medication and counseling return Tuan to health, through a list that names the bodily conditions from which she is brought back (E008). That is substantial care, consistent with the earlier successful rescue. At the same time she deepens her sense of defeat and conceals it before the counselor: `おくびにも出さず` is explicit non-disclosure, not absence of the feeling. The account therefore gives both effective treatment and an epistemic limit on its social interpretation. It does not establish that counseling never helps anyone, nor that clinicians deliberately ignore a known dissent. It shows this patient's ability to withhold her continuing judgment while undergoing treatment.

The taxi scene gives that judgment a date, **2060-06-12**, and an urban surface (E009). The pastel buildings are not said to obey an explicit color law: `誰が法律で定めたわけでもない` is the relevant qualification. Their uniform mildness provides Tuan an image of a consensus that needs no named legislator to shape experience. The narrator's limitless hospital is a metaphorical totalization of that vista, not an independently measured description of the entire planet. Its force comes from her perceiving a built environment whose refusal to disturb appears to leave nowhere outside it.

Her hopelessness is not identical to a new embrace of its values. She learns to get by within disappointment and regards Miaha's death as the cost of that lesson. Regret follows anger: the earlier objection to the mother's possible guilt coexists with Tuan's own apology to Miaha and `わたしにはできなかった` (analyst's gloss: I could not do it). This is an affective tension, not proof the narrator's social critique is simply invalid. Nor does her figurative sacrifice to a medical god establish that doctors killed Miaha. The mother appears not to notice the taxi tears; that remains a perception, distinct from deliberate coldness.

The final adult awakening beside cigars and wine gives the remembered accommodation a practical afterlife: Tuan has survived into the risky institutional escape already shown. It does not settle every cause connecting the two eras. The unit's affect labels—disappointment, anger, hopelessness and regret—organize shifts in an account whose speech, private response and retrospect remain distinguishable. Confidence in that deliberate formal work does not resolve the apparatus's still-open diegetic status.
