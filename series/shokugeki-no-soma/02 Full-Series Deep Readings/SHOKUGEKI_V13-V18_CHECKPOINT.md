## 3. Literary development across the third movement

### 3.1 Completion becomes an obligation to leave a usable world

V13 completes Soma's Autumn Election dish through a second-stage soy-milk ojiya, then gives Hayama the championship through a cook-specific specialty criterion. Soma and Kurokiba remain joint runners-up whose complete systems are not invalidated by placement. Defeat opens an authorship question: Soma must seek cooking beyond Joichiro's footsteps if inheritance is to become something he can call his own.

The stagiaire translates that question from plate to workplace. Soma and Hisako do not pass merely by surviving a rush. They must leave Mitamura with a service system that works after they depart. Reservation design, information flow, owner choice, inherited menu, and an individual regular become culinary evidence. Hisako's return to Erina likewise reframes growth as movement from “behind” toward “beside” without pretending the hierarchy is already balanced.

V14 intensifies reciprocal transfer at SHINO'S. Soma initially harms course flow because he sees his station rather than the future state of the whole kitchen. Observation, preparation, questions, and French-method study restore useful participation. His whole-quail oyakodon remains authored even when Shinomiya rejects and revises it. Growth is neither obedient copying nor solitary originality; it is contribution that can survive expert correction and travel into a later task.

### 3.2 Public competition expands from plates to operating systems

V15 turns Elite Ten distance into a problem of resources and legibility. Kuga's Sichuan operation coordinates brand, architecture, trained reproducibility, synchronized labor, appetite, and volume. Soma and Megumi initially answer with a technically sound product that customers cannot imagine. Day-one fortieth place and day-two thirty-fifth place are exact adverse results, not proof of culinary emptiness.

Mobile service, visible dough-to-noodle transformation, small portions, customer-reaction cascades, and regional/sensory advice reframe the shop. V16 completes the answer as time-release mapo-curry noodles whose changing bowl is served by an attributable coalition. Soma wins Central's day-four interval; Kuga remains cumulatively ahead, and the final aggregate is withheld. Competition now evaluates a public system while preserving the difference among daily rank, cumulative rank, solvency, Food War verdict, and Elite Ten seat.

Kuga's response supplies a clean adverse-state test. He recognizes the bounded loss, loses the condition for a Tsukasa rematch, feels frustration, and claims future growth. Tsukasa's solo nine-course service supplies a different elite production model: concentrated authorship can describe itself as self-erasure while retaining total operational control.

### 3.3 Culinary authority becomes constitutional power

V16 changes scale when six Elite Ten members appoint Azami director through a valid council mechanism. Procedure makes the office operative; it does not erase Azami's contempt for ordinary diners, coercive treatment of Erina, or unilateral removal of Hisako. Erina's childhood material reconstructs present fear as conditioned abuse rather than ideological consent or timeless personality.

V17 turns office into program. Central abolishes autonomous organizations, reserves creation for selected cooks, and calls prescribed reproduction freedom from unequal talent and struggle. Polar Star becomes a target because its independent-selection tradition shelters ordinary self-organization. Eizan preserves the Food War's form while buying judges and removing tasting, then synchronizes Soma's challenge with direct property force.

Resistance is correspondingly distributed. Alice authors Erina's immediate escape route; Erina chooses movement while afraid; Hisako and Ryo preserve continuity; classmates provide transit; Polar Star provides privacy and shelter. Residents defend the dorm while Soma cooks elsewhere. At the table and in the match, invited criticism, reciprocal service, and actual tasting reopen relations that Central tries to preclose.

### 3.4 Judgment can be repaired before the institution

V18 gives the interrupted Food War a complete form. Ketchup-based sweet-vinegar ankake, Parmesan, heat sequence, and accumulated Polar Star practice complete Soma's chicken-wing gyoza. All three judges vote for him. The 3-0 result rescinds the dorm order and stops demolition. It is a valid, material victory with an exact limit: no seat changes and Central remains.

Isshiki converts that opening into a procedural constraint. Negotiated themes/dates, randomly selected external judges, anti-contact invalidation, and survival on victory make future matches more answerable. Yet the society did not choose abolition, and loss still means dissolution and possible expulsion. Fairer procedure exists inside a coercive option set.

Central then wins four simultaneous matches 3-0. Momo's cake receives detailed independent-panel praise. The block therefore ends with a demanding proposition: valid culinary judgment can advance an illegitimate institutional purpose. Skill does not confer political legitimacy, and political illegitimacy does not make skill unreal.

### 3.5 Frozen block thesis

> **Volumes 13-18 move from completing a dish to asking what completion owes beyond the plate. Growth must survive departure, transfer must survive correction, a public dish must become legible to an audience, a coalition must preserve attribution, refuge must widen choice without claiming cure, and judgment must answer to tasted evidence. The block's central warning is that excellent craft and valid local verdicts can inhabit coercive systems; institutional legitimacy depends on who may author, refuse, remain, and answer after the result.**

## 8. Ledger synthesis

### Craft and pedagogy

The block moves from specialty and professional transfer through public legibility to institutional authorship. Technique becomes durable when it can survive correction, departure, scale, and an answering eater. Central's error is not elite technique; it is converting technique into a prescribed answer whose recipients may not author alternatives.

### Institution and power

Totsuki's evaluation systems multiply: Election result, stagiaire pass, sales board, Elite Ten seat, constitutional vote, Food War, and remnant-hunt rule. Each produces real evidence under distinct conditions. Azami's rise shows that valid procedure can create illegitimate authority; V18 shows that valid culinary verdicts can serve or resist that authority.

### Competition and evaluation

Results remain local. Hayama's championship, Soma's Kabutoyama win, Kuga's sales positions, Soma's day-four win, Eizan's corrupted Kushiyama result, Soma's valid 3-0, and Momo's valid 3-0 are not interchangeable scales. Outcome quality depends on task, evidence, panel, time boundary, and stake.

### Visual form

The six volumes move through second bowls, winner boards, crowded service, reservation screens, course timing, whole-bird interiors, ordered seat portraits, empty stalls, breaking meatballs, six-signature paper, ladders, barricades, refused dishes, completed vote boards, historical photographs, arena grids, cake layers, and an unresolved bandana tableau. Closure is repeatedly made visible at one scale and withheld at another.

## 13. Checkpoint closure

Volumes 13-18 begin by refusing to let a first serving, one tournament result, or temporary rush performance count as complete. They end by refusing to let a fair Food War count as a fair academy. Across that movement, the series repeatedly makes local success exact and then tests what remains unrepaired around it.

The checkpoint closes with two valid but politically opposed 3-0 structures behind it: Soma's verdict preserves one autonomous home; Momo's verdict advances the purge of another society. Ahead, Ryo and Rentaro have only entered the arena. The next volume must begin from that exact asymmetry and incompletion.
