# SHOKUGEKI_V03_DEEP_READING

## 1. Authority, horizon, and reading rule

This is the canonical V2 full-volume reading of *Shokugeki no Soma* Volume 3 at the V03 publication boundary. The reading was produced directly from the original Japanese manga pages after the V03 visual-reading pipeline passed its representative-page validation gate. Automated Japanese OCR was used only as a discovery aid; dialogue correction, reading order, speaker attribution, and visual claims were established from direct page inspection.

Volume 3 was part of the V1 sample. In accordance with the V2 fresh-reading rule, the earlier V1 V03 character reading was not consulted before this document's volume argument and evidence structure were established. V1 comparison belongs in the crosswalk after this V2 unit is stable.

No event after Volume 3 is used to resolve the final challenge. The last serialized page establishes only Soma's proposed condition: if he defeats Shinomiya in a Shokugeki, Megumi's expulsion must be withdrawn. It does not establish acceptance, rules, judges, outcome, or reinstatement.

The central V03 finding is:

> **Volume 3 separates culinary authority from culinary accountability. It places several genuinely skilled professionals inside the same attritional institution, then tests whether their authority remains legitimate when ingredients, time, service conditions, or students require adaptation. Megumi's successful adjustment to damaged cauliflower becomes punishable only because Shinomiya makes obedience to his recipe more important than responsible judgment.**

Two reciprocal developments organize that conflict.

First, rivalry becomes educational. Soma recognizes Takumi as a peer only after seeing hands formed by real restaurant service; Takumi, in turn, needs Soma as the rival who gives continued direction to his own mature technique. Second, collaboration becomes a route to autonomy. Soma and the Polar Star residents name Megumi's concrete contribution to the wilderness dish; she internalizes that recognition and solves Shinomiya's damaged-ingredient problem alone.

## 3. Structural movement - from selection pressure to contested authority

Volume 2 ended by announcing an attritional camp. Volume 3 turns that announcement into a layered professional simulation:

1. graduates acquire immediate power to expel;
2. students work as if they were employees in graduate-run restaurants;
3. assignments test sourcing, menu design, execution, and mass-service throughput;
4. judgments are individualized, rapid, and ordinarily unreviewable;
5. Shokugeki becomes the only available procedure by which a student can contest a graduate's discretionary sanction.

The structure initially appears coherent. Shinomiya's first expulsion is tied to a sensory service concern: a student's strongly scented hair product could interfere with diners' perception. Inui rejects forgettable dishes because the assignment asks for something that satisfies her, not merely something edible. The fifty-portion test makes real production speed visible.

But the final assignment exposes a contradiction. Students are told to choose stock from a shared rush in which late access predictably yields damaged cauliflower. Megumi diagnoses the defect, preserves the ingredient's appearance with white wine vinegar, and produces a coherent terrine. Shinomiya recognizes exactly what she did, then fails her because she altered his recipe without authorization.

The camp therefore does not reveal a single unified standard called `professionalism`. It reveals competing answers to a harder question:

> **When conditions depart from the ideal, is a subordinate cook responsible for preserving instructions or preserving the dish?**

Megumi chooses the dish. Shinomiya chooses the instruction. Soma contests the legitimacy of making only the subordinate bear the cost of that disagreement.

## 4. Volume thesis - professionalism is accountable adaptation

V03 grants professional credibility to several distinct practices.

Takumi's credibility comes from real restaurant hands. He can butcher a duck quickly, coordinate Isami's prep, and synthesize Tuscan and Japanese flavor functions. Soma does not recognize him because of elegance, imported identity, or school status. He recognizes `現場を知ってる手`—hands that know a working kitchen (`V03 / Ch. 16 / CBZ images 03-043–03-046`).

Soma's credibility comes from reading constraints as information. In the wilderness task he treats Inui's snack, a loose chicken, a fresh egg, and the fenced boundary as a usable system. His kaki-no-tane coating is novel, but the governing checks remain practical: can it replace flour or starch, will it remain crisp, can the pair finish within a diner's patience, and does every component belong to the place?

Megumi's credibility comes from careful attention that initially appears subordinate. She gathers mountain plants without instruction, performs fine prep, and completes the egg-yolk sauce. Later, when no assistance is permitted, the same disposition becomes independent diagnosis. She sees that the cauliflower will discolor, remembers an acid technique, and adjusts.

These modes are not identical, and the volume refuses a simple winner between the first two. Inui withholds judgment between the Aldini duck and Soma's river-fish fry. Soma finishes the fifty-portion service before Takumi, but Takumi says his own dish has more flavor. Each claim answers a different professional demand.

Shinomiya's failure is therefore not lack of skill. It is the conversion of one professional good—recipe control—into absolute authority over every other variable. His dish concept is coherent, and his criticism that extra acid changes the intended harmony is intelligible. The legitimacy breaks when he excludes his own responsibility for damaged stock and punishes the student who protects the ingredient.

V03's stronger proposition is:

> **Professional authority remains legitimate only while it answers for the conditions it controls and permits competent response to the conditions it does not.**

## 5. Chapter architecture

### 5.1 Chapter 15 - graduate prestige becomes immediate jurisdiction

The resort's luxury first resembles reward. The camp announcement converts it into selection architecture: five nights and six days of daily evaluated work, with failure producing immediate expulsion (`V03 / Ch. 15 / CBZ images 03-010–03-016`).

Graduates enter as the tiny survivor class that reached Totsuki's summit. Restaurants and career accomplishments establish real expertise. The institution then makes that expertise jurisdiction: their on-the-spot judgments determine whether students remain enrolled.

Shinomiya's first expulsion fuses professional perception and punitive excess. He detects citrus in a student's hair product and argues that scent can corrupt a diner's experience. The concern is not absurd. The sanction is maximal, immediate, and unreviewable (`V03 / Ch. 15 / CBZ images 03-018–03-019`).

Dojima states the governing metaphor: the graduates are owner-chefs, and students should work as employees in their businesses (`V03 / Ch. 15 / CBZ images 03-021–03-023`). The volume will later hold that metaphor against Shinomiya: an owner-chef cannot invoke employee obedience while disclaiming responsibility for ingredient management.

Alongside the hierarchy, the chapter preserves alternative social rhythms. Soma proposes a late-night card game; he and Megumi welcome their pairing. Takumi's ceremonially aggressive shoe-grinding is reduced by Soma's literal analysis to a strange physical fact. This comedy is not inattentiveness. Soma notices concrete behavior precisely while refusing the prestige script attached to it.

### 5.2 Chapter 16 - peer recognition requires evidence of service

Inui's wilderness assignment asks pairs to gather ingredients from a bounded field and make one dish that satisfies her. Time pressure sends most students toward obvious river fish and simple salt grilling. The field seems open, but urgency narrows perception (`V03 / Ch. 16 / CBZ images 03-030–03-039`).

Takumi and Isami return with a duck. Their response exposes what others failed to notice: fish was never the only animal available, and a Japanese frame was never required.

The subsequent cooking sequence demonstrates professional fluency through process. Takumi butchers, seasons, sears, renders, and finishes while Isami performs delegated prep. Soma names the evidence correctly: these are hands formed by an actual work site. Isami explains that the brothers worked from childhood in the family trattoria.

Takumi then reframes his theatrical rivalry. He is a cook who has protected a working `castle`, and Soma is the same kind (`現場で城を守ってきた料理人だ / キミと同じさ`). Only at that point does Soma accept the challenge. Peer recognition is occupational, not status-based.

### 5.3 Chapter 17 - cultural synthesis is functional and collective

The Aldini flashback binds family, place, and vocation. The brothers worked at Trattoria Aldini from age five and entered Totsuki to acquire every possible technique before returning to Italy. Takumi experiences school competition as representation of the family restaurant and wagers the title `professional` on the contest (`V03 / Ch. 17 / CBZ images 03-050–03-052`).

The mezzaluna gives that inheritance a material signature, but Isami operates it. Their duck dish is possible because the brothers form a brigade rather than because Takumi performs alone.

Their green sauce is not arbitrary fusion. Each substitution answers a culinary function: sweetfish `うるか` supplies fermented bitter-salty depth; shiso and mitsuba supply fresh aromatic structure; yuzu pepper supplies citrus heat; soy, mustard, black pepper, and honey complete the glaze (`V03 / Ch. 17 / CBZ images 03-057–03-061`).

Inui passes them. Megumi quietly wants Soma to win. Soma, still without a visible dish, inventories the whole environment and asks whether anything within the fenced area is permitted. The answer makes the evaluator's snack part of the ingredient field.

### 5.4 Chapter 18 - invention remains answerable to service

Soma borrows Inui's bag of `柿の種`. Megumi independently gathers plants. A loose chicken leads Soma to a freshly laid egg. Each ingredient enters through perception and circumstance, but none remains a mere gag.

With fifteen minutes left, Soma translates deadline pressure into restaurant ethics: a diner who has already waited that long may leave. He assigns Megumi fine vegetable prep and egg separation. Crushed rice crackers become a flavored coating for river fish; the egg yolk and tree buds become sauce; gathered mountain vegetables reinforce the site (`V03 / Ch. 18 / CBZ images 03-075–03-084`).

Soma's prior oyster experiment at Yukihira supplies the analogy. Innovation is recombination of tested memory under new constraint. Inui calls the refusal to fear limitation a professional disposition because Soma keeps seeking his own cuisine inside the assignment rather than outside it.

The pair pass. Inui does not name a winner against the Aldinis.

### 5.5 Chapter 19 - rivalry and throughput expand the definition of work

Takumi asks Inui to judge. She begins praising Soma's idea and the powerful texture it gives the char, then declares the result `in her keeping` (`この勝負…私の預かりとします`). The absence of a verdict is evidence, not a gap to fill. The volume preserves multiple valid professional strengths.

Takumi proposes a future Shokugeki. On the bus he admits that he had expected to win but now imagines a one-on-one settlement after further growth. Soma privately realizes that staying only at the Yukihira shop would have prevented him from knowing a peer like Takumi (`V03 / Ch. 19 / CBZ images 03-094–03-097`). School has become a source of encounter, not only exclusion.

The return to the hotel produces a second assignment: each student must make fifty complete beef-steak set meals for visiting sports clubs within sixty minutes. Soma finishes first. Takumi remains at forty-five while claiming his dish has more flavor; Soma replies that he should move his hands if he can still complain.

The exchange is not a definitive hierarchy. Takumi may optimize the plate; Soma optimizes viable service flow. The bodybuilder clients, continuous order calls, depleted mise en place, and steam-filled panels make production capacity part of culinary competence.

### 5.6 Chapter 20 - recognition becomes internal support

The corridor encounter between Soma and Erina begins as embarrassment comedy. Erina refuses help and insists she can stand; she calls the camp trivial except for the question of when Soma will drop out. Soma replies that she sounds excited. Her attention is unmistakable, but it remains antagonistic and status-conscious rather than reconciled (`V03 / Ch. 20 / CBZ images 03-109–03-113`).

In the bath, Dojima notices Soma as the first student to finish the fifty-portion task. He contextualizes Erina's cultivated `God Tongue` history and warns that Totsuki is deep. His authority operates through observation and counsel rather than immediate punishment.

The central scene occurs after the Polar Star gathering. Megumi says Soma's support got her through the day but discounts her own work. Soma does not offer empty encouragement. He states that the dish could not have existed without the ingredients she gathered and that her cooking completed it. Ryoko and Yuki reinforce the assessment (`V03 / Ch. 20 / CBZ images 03-122–03-124`).

Megumi's resulting desire—remain with everyone and become better at cooking—binds belonging to vocation. The next day's expulsion threat therefore attacks both at once.

### 5.7 Chapter 21 - the supreme recipe produces an accountability crisis

Shinomiya assigns his nine-vegetable terrine. Every student must reproduce it alone; information exchange, advice, and teams are banned. Ingredient selection occurs through a rush he anticipates.

Megumi reaches cauliflower after others have taken the best stock. The remaining heads are bruised and beginning to discolor. She almost reaches for Soma's protective charm as a substitute for action, then stops: she cannot keep relying on him. The charm becomes a cue to calm herself. She recalls that acid can protect color and uses white wine vinegar (`V03 / Ch. 21 / CBZ images 03-131–03-137`).

Shinomiya tastes the completed terrine, correctly identifies the vinegar, and acknowledges its effects: preserved whiteness, delicate acidity, and a fit with cauliflower sweetness. He expels her because no one authorized a change to his recipe.

Soma exposes the suppressed condition. The shared stock included damaged cauliflower; late-arriving students could not reliably obtain good ingredients. If students are employees, the owner-chef bears responsibility for ingredient management. Shinomiya answers that the head chef's recipe cannot be altered.

Megumi tries to end the conflict by calling it her own problem. A silent eye-and-sensation montage allows Soma to see the injury she is minimizing. He then proposes a Shokugeki whose stake is withdrawal of her expulsion (`V03 / Ch. 21 / CBZ images 03-139–03-147`).

The challenge is both personal defense and institutional appeal. Cooking is again constitutional procedure, but now the procedure is invoked against the judgment of an authority who controls the ordinary evaluation channel.

## 6. Character-state reconstruction

### 6.1 Soma Yukihira

V03 strengthens a distinction already visible in V01-V02: Soma resists prestige, not expertise. He jokes through Takumi's theatrical aggression, yet becomes serious when he sees restaurant-formed hands. He shakes Dojima's hand casually before knowing the director's status, then listens to the warning about Totsuki's depth.

His professional model broadens in three ways:

- **peer exposure:** school gives him access to cooks he could not encounter if he remained inside the family shop;
- **service scale:** he demonstrates that speed across fifty set meals is a real culinary skill;
- **institutional argument:** he extends kitchen accountability upward, insisting that a chef who controls stock and rules owns their consequences.

The final intervention is not impulsive in the sense of lacking reasons. Soma first identifies ingredient degradation, argues management responsibility, distinguishes adaptation from incompetence, and only then escalates to Shokugeki.

### 6.2 Megumi Tadokoro

Megumi moves from secure partnership to independent adaptive judgment without ceasing to value relationship.

During the wilderness task she gathers useful ingredients on her own and executes careful prep. She nevertheless discounts that work until Soma itemizes it. The Polar Star group's recognition produces a modest increase in self-trust.

The terrine assignment tests whether that recognition can survive isolation. Megumi cannot ask for advice. She recognizes damaged cauliflower, remembers an acid technique, and completes a visually precise dish. The evaluator's rejection cannot erase the demonstrated capability.

Her attempt to stop Soma should not be reduced to passivity. She tries to keep the consequences on herself and says it is her matter. That self-effacing responsibility remains dangerous because it makes unjust authority easier to absorb, but it is an active ethical stance rather than simple helplessness.

### 6.3 Takumi Aldini

Takumi's grand gestures overlay real professional discipline. His identity is structured by the family trattoria, representation before customers, and fear of stagnation. The supplementary story makes the developmental gap explicit: rapid improvement has left him without a further goal, and a rival is prescribed as the missing pressure.

Soma becomes that rival because Takumi recognizes another service-formed cook, not merely because he is difficult to beat. Inui's withheld judgment intensifies rather than resolves the bond.

### 6.4 Isami Aldini

Isami is not only Takumi's biographical witness. He performs the mise en place that makes Takumi's speed possible, operates the culturally distinctive mezzaluna, and asks the question that reveals why Takumi postpones an immediate challenge. His support is technically productive and interpretively important.

### 6.5 Erina Nakiri

Erina's cultivated sensory authority is expanded through Dojima's account. The `God Tongue` is not framed as an effortless magic alone; it has been trained through early exposure to professional kitchens and repeated consultation.

Her antagonistic interest in Soma persists. She imagines his expulsion with visible animation, and he treats that investment as excitement. No evidence yet supports softened trust or acknowledged respect.

### 6.6 Hinako Inui, Gin Dojima, and Kojiro Shinomiya

The three graduates form a spectrum of authority:

- **Inui** is severe about distinctiveness but dialogic about reasons. She asks what techniques do and rewards coherent difference.
- **Dojima** observes performance, contextualizes talent, and issues a warning without demanding ceremonial deference.
- **Shinomiya** has technically intelligible standards but treats authorship as jurisdiction. His own accurate tasting confirms Megumi's competence before his rule converts it into failure.

The volume does not divide skilled from unskilled adults. It divides modes of exercising skill-derived power.

## 7. Relationship reconstruction

### 7.1 Soma and Megumi

Their relationship changes from practical safety to reciprocal capability recognition.

Megumi explicitly wants Soma to win against the Aldinis. Soma delegates work that suits her precision and later credits that work as indispensable. She internalizes the credit and acts alone in the terrine test. He, in turn, reads her tearful self-dismissal and risks his own standing to contest the expulsion.

The relationship should not be summarized as rescuer and rescued. V03 shows a loop:

`trust -> contribution -> explicit recognition -> independent action -> protective escalation`

Soma's final protection matters because Megumi has just demonstrated autonomy, not because she failed to.

### 7.2 Soma and Takumi

The rivalry begins asymmetrically: Takumi performs hostility while Soma declines its significance. It becomes reciprocal only when each recognizes the other's restaurant labor.

The pair then test different strengths without a final ranking. Their future Shokugeki is a developmental promise. Takumi's need for declared rivalry and Soma's refusal of prestige ceremony remain stylistically distinct even after professional recognition.

### 7.3 Takumi and Isami

The siblings operate as a mature brigade. Isami's loyalty does not erase judgment or skill; he explains, questions, and executes. Takumi's individual professional identity is materially enabled by this shared system.

### 7.4 Polar Star ensemble

The dorm group survives together, reunites, and provides interpretive support. Their gathering is not merely relief comedy. Ryoko and Yuki help convert Megumi's invisible labor into socially confirmed evidence, while the group's presence gives her a reason to persist.

## 8. Culinary ideology, craft, and pedagogy

### 8.1 Functional synthesis over decorative fusion

The Aldini duck maps each Japanese substitution to a Tuscan sauce function. Soma maps rice-cracker flavor and structure to breading. Megumi maps acid treatment to color preservation. In each case, creative variation succeeds because it answers a material problem.

### 8.2 Creativity is constrained and accountable

V03 does not celebrate novelty for its own sake. Soma's dish must fit the boundary, time limit, available equipment, texture window, and diner's patience. Megumi's change must protect the terrine's appearance and preserve flavor coherence. The question is never only whether a cook changed something, but whether the change remains answerable to purpose.

### 8.3 Replication has value but not sovereignty

Shinomiya's recipe embodies real composition. Exact replication can test control and expose students to a superior structure. The failure is making fidelity dispositive after the evaluator has also manufactured unequal ingredient conditions.

### 8.4 Dialogue distinguishes rigorous evaluation from arbitrary judgment

Inui's standards are demanding, and many students must redo dishes. Yet she articulates why food fails and why successful choices work. Shinomiya also articulates flavor logic, but refuses to treat his own stock design as relevant. Explanation alone is therefore insufficient; accountable pedagogy must allow reasons to travel in both directions.

## 9. Institution, power, and legitimacy

The camp converts external professional success into school authority. That conversion is only partly justified by expertise.

The owner-chef/employee metaphor supplies realism but also imports a hierarchy. When Dojima uses it, it frames the assignments as professional practice. When Soma applies it to Shinomiya, the metaphor becomes reciprocal: owners bear obligations for stock, working conditions, and outcomes.

Shinomiya's terrine test combines three powers:

1. he authors the recipe;
2. he designs the ingredient-allocation environment;
3. he personally judges and expels.

No ordinary review appears. This concentration makes Shokugeki the only visible appeal channel. The institution therefore contains a constitutional mechanism from V02, but access to justice still requires a student willing and able to wager through cooking.

The final challenge does not yet prove that the mechanism will constrain graduate power. It proves only that Soma can name and invoke it.

## 10. Competition, evaluation, and growth

V03 distinguishes three competitive forms:

- **informal comparison:** Inui compares the Aldini and Soma/Megumi dishes but withholds a winner;
- **throughput race:** Soma finishes fifty set meals first, revealing service capacity without settling total culinary superiority;
- **formal Shokugeki challenge:** Soma proposes enforceable stakes to contest Megumi's expulsion.

These forms should not be merged into one win-loss record. The first has no verdict, the second has a measurable speed result under a specific menu, and the third remains unresolved at the volume boundary.

Growth also operates differently for each central student. Soma expands his peer field; Takumi acquires a developmental rival; Megumi converts recognition into independent technique. None of those changes requires a clean defeat.

## 11. Japanese voice and register

### 11.1 Soma

Soma's casual speech continues to puncture prestige: `おー！`, `しばらくぶりー！`, and conversational replies to graduates refuse ceremonial distance. Under service pressure, the register sharpens into practical imperatives. In the final dispute, `納得いかないっすね!!` remains colloquial but reasoned; the informality does not weaken the accountability claim.

### 11.2 Takumi

Takumi alternates polished self-presentation with direct competitive commands: `逃げるなよ`, `必ず受けろ`, `いずれキミに食戟を申し込む`. The combination makes rivalry both theatrical and binding. His use of `キミ` preserves controlled distance even while granting professional parity.

### 11.3 Megumi

Megumi's speech is deferential and self-minimizing. `私は何も偉くねぇんだけど…` is visually attributed to her and combines rustic/colloquial negation with embarrassment; it should not be normalized into generic politeness. Her internal `私ひとりでちゃんと乗り切らなきゃ!!` marks a stronger self-directed imperative than her spoken register permits.

### 11.4 Shinomiya

Shinomiya's authority is compressed into categorical judgments: `退学だ`, `誰がルセットを変えていいと言った？`, and the claim that the head chef's recipe may not be altered. The economy makes rule and person nearly indistinguishable.

### 11.5 Inui and Dojima

Inui's gentle surface register coexists with strict evaluation and comic evasiveness. Dojima speaks with relaxed directness despite institutional rank. The graduates cannot be assigned one shared authoritative voice.

## 12. Visual, food, body, and comedy grammar

V03 repeatedly uses scale to decide how authority and pressure are felt.

- Graduate introductions occupy low-angle, spacious compositions; student reactions are crowded and fragmented.
- Takumi's workflow uses slanted process panels, enlarged blades, and isolated hand close-ups. Competence is visualized as uninterrupted economy.
- The mezzaluna cuts across panel geometry, giving family craft an inherited material icon while remaining in Isami's hands.
- Soma's ordinary snack bag receives a prestige reveal. The joke also makes value-in-use visible.
- Tasting fantasies translate flavor through bodies: the Aldini duck becomes operatic embrace; the kaki-no-tane coating encloses and floods the fish body. Erotic excess functions as sensory metaphor but should still be distinguished from corridor/bath fanservice.
- The athlete clients become monster-scale bodies. Their exaggeration communicates consumption volume and turns throughput into visible pressure.
- Megumi's V03 resolution and expulsion are both staged with white space. In the first, isolation protects an inward hope; in the second, it magnifies the institutional distance of the verdict.
- The silent eye montage before Soma's challenge refuses explanatory dialogue. Perception becomes the transition from argument to action.

Comedy is structurally mixed. It deflates Takumi's grandeur, relieves service exhaustion, and produces embarrassment around Erina. It does not erase the professional or institutional claims surrounding those scenes.

## 13. Contradictions, uncertainty, and evidence discipline

The following boundaries remain frozen:

- Inui does **not** declare Soma, Megumi, Takumi, or Isami the winner of the wilderness side contest. `預かり` is a withheld decision, not a tie.
- The fifty-portion result establishes Soma as first to finish that task. It does not establish overall superiority to Takumi.
- Megumi's terrine is technically coherent and visually completed, but the volume does not supply a neutral judge who declares it superior to Shinomiya's intended flavor design.
- Shinomiya's motive in mixing damaged cauliflower into stock is inferred by Soma as deliberate pass-group narrowing and is strongly supported by the test structure; Shinomiya does not verbally confess the motive in V03.
- Megumi's emotional expression supports hurt, fear, and self-effacement. The silent montage must not be converted into unspoken dialogue.
- Soma proposes a Shokugeki, but acceptance and outcome remain open.
- Sekimori Hitoshi is visually named among the graduate evaluators and is the likely caller who addresses Inui as Hinako; the call itself need not carry major claims beyond ending her delay.

## 14. Claim-state implications at V03

### Strengthened

- Soma grants legitimacy to demonstrated work rather than prestige.
- Soma's creativity is service-accountable recombination.
- Megumi's precision and environmental attention are substantive culinary competence.
- Polar Star belonging supports rather than substitutes for individual growth.
- Totsuki concentrates expertise and opportunity while exposing students to coercive, unevenly accountable authority.
- Shokugeki can function as political procedure and a potential appeal mechanism.

### Newly established

- Takumi is a durable professional peer/rival whose motivation is tied to restaurant representation and fear of stagnation.
- Megumi can independently adapt technique under isolation and pressure.
- Graduate authority divides internally among dialogic, observational, and sovereign modes.
- Throughput is a distinct dimension of culinary performance.

### Not yet established

- that Soma defeats or reforms Shinomiya;
- that Megumi's expulsion is rescinded;
- that Erina's attention has become trust or respect;
- that the institution recognizes its own accountability failure;
- that Soma and Takumi have a settled competitive hierarchy.

## 15. Frozen prospective register for Volume 4

The following predictions are frozen before reading Volume 4:

1. **Shokugeki authorization prediction:** because graduate authority exceeds ordinary student standing, the proposed contest will require authorization, mediation, or rule modification by a higher institutional figure rather than proceeding solely on Soma's declaration.
2. **Megumi agency prediction:** if the dispute is converted into cooking, Megumi's demonstrated competence will need to remain active; a resolution in which Soma simply replaces her would contradict V03's autonomy movement.
3. **Shinomiya stress prediction:** the conflict will test not whether Shinomiya can cook, but whether his recipe sovereignty can respond to another cook's coherent judgment.
4. **Dojima legitimacy prediction:** Dojima's observational, non-punitive authority positions him as a likely mediator or witness capable of distinguishing skill from procedural domination.
5. **Soma consequence prediction:** Soma's willingness to enter the sanction's reach will expose him to a real institutional stake, even if the exact form is modified.
6. **Takumi continuation prediction:** the unresolved rivalry will persist as a future-development line rather than being settled incidentally during the Shinomiya conflict.
7. **Erina attention prediction:** Erina will continue monitoring Soma's survival or performance, but V03 does not justify predicting open alliance.
8. **Pedagogy prediction:** the next resolution will likely distinguish faithful replication from adaptive hospitality more explicitly, because V03 has already established that Megumi's technique worked.

These predictions may be confirmed, revised, or falsified only by evidence available at the V04 publication boundary.

## 16. V2 closure at the V03 boundary

Volume 3 is not primarily a story about whether elite cooks deserve authority. Takumi, Inui, Dojima, Erina, and Shinomiya all possess real expertise. The volume asks what expertise authorizes them to do and what obligations follow from that authorization.

Its culinary sequences answer with plural professionalism: practiced hands, cultural-functional synthesis, improvisational perception, precise assistance, mass-service speed, and ingredient rescue. Its social sequences show how peers and communities help cooks see capacities they had not credited in themselves. Its institutional climax then demonstrates the danger of a chef who recognizes a successful adaptation but treats authorship as more important than accountability.

At the V03 boundary, Soma's Shokugeki challenge is therefore not merely hot-blooded rescue. It is a demand that culinary authority submit itself to culinary proof under enforceable stakes. Whether Totsuki will permit that demand, whether Megumi remains an agent within it, and whether Shinomiya can be moved remain unresolved.
