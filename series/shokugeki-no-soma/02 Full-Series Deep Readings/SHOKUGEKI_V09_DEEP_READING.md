## 1. Authority, horizon, and central finding

Most members are two-page scans read in Japanese right-to-left order. Contact sheets were navigation aids only.

Volume 9 completes Soma versus Alice and Kurokiba versus Megumi. It begins Hayama versus Hisako but ends after Hisako's dish is tasted and Hayama starts his answer. No later verdict is imported. The volume also reveals Mimasaka's surveillance practice without yet completing his culinary model or match with Takumi.

The central finding is:

> **Volume 9 makes competition diagnostic without making it total: a verdict selects who advances, while the losing dish can still expose the winner's limits, preserve its maker's authorship, and remain a valid form of knowledge. Strength is not one flavor register but the capacity to make an answer legible under opposition.**

That proposition governs both completed results. Soma's science-informed nori pearls defeat Alice without turning science into the losing ideology. Kurokiba defeats Megumi, but her clear, care-centered ramen forces him to taste, answer, and acknowledge a strength unlike his own.

## 4. Structural movement — from category correction to plural strength

Volume 8 ended on a proposition: a bento must carry more than deliciousness. Volume 9 tests that proposition immediately. Soma's hidden device is neither homely sincerity nor rejection of Alice's toolkit. It is a scientific method learned cheaply and translated into seaweed-flavored pearls inside an ordinary nori bento. The resulting surprise is technical, mnemonic, playful, and fitted to a delayed recipient event.

After the verdict, the volume changes the diagnostic question. Ramen has no single canonical intensity. Kurokiba's port-derived extraction, command, and shellfish violence become one complete system. Megumi's hometown kozuyu, clear broth, dried components, and measured layering become another. The story initially lets Kurokiba define strength as aggression; then Megumi makes strength accumulate quietly enough that he must recognize it.

The final pairing extends the problem without resolving it. Hisako makes nourishment sensorially extravagant through softshell turtle. Hayama's smell promises another specialist answer. Mimasaka's data collection introduces a troubling possibility: intimate knowledge of a cook can be extracted without relation or consent and converted into competitive leverage.

## 5. Chapters 66-67 — Soma wins without making Alice invalid

The black spheres on Soma's rice are formed with sodium alginate and calcium chloride. The remembered children's kit matters because it locates advanced transformation outside a prestige monopoly. Soma has observed a technique, retained it, and altered its purpose. The spheres release seaweed/soy flavor and textural surprise; they make familiar food more interactive without ceasing to be nori bento (`09-004`-`09-010`).

This is contextual design rather than anti-scientific virtue. Alice's temari sushi remains exact, beautiful, preserved, sequenced, and pleasurable. Soma's win identifies a narrower difference: his answer integrates the social use of bento—recognition, transport, opening, affordability, recipient, and fun—more completely in this panel's judgment.

Soma is explicitly announced as winner (`09-012`). No exact vote count is imported. Alice retastes his work, remembers the conditions that made an individual weapon necessary, cries at her first defeat, and reasserts ambition (`09-013`-`09-018`). Her response supplies fair-loss evidence without sentimentalizing it. Defeat hurts; she does not deny the dish, abandon science, or accept a lower permanent identity.

Soma's response also matters. He does not erase the result to protect her feelings. Losing is painful, and the answer is further competition. This preserves respect because it treats Alice as someone capable of changing and returning.

## 6. Chapters 67-70 — port kitchens make authority and coercion inseparable

Megumi versus Kurokiba begins with ramen. Megumi can now say that cooking feels enjoyable even while she is nervous. Soma tells her to do her best; he neither selects her concept nor enters the workstation (`09-018`-`09-024`).

Ramen is presented as a system of stock, tare, aromatic oil, noodles, and toppings. Megumi chooses dried scallop adductors, chicken, vegetables, and an Aizu kozuyu lineage. Kurokiba chooses fish frames, shrimp heads, shellfish, lobster, and concentrated extraction (`09-024`-`09-035`). Their culinary differences are visible before judgment.

Kurokiba's childhood port-pub history explains the social organization of his food. He is entrusted with accounting, procurement, kitchen, staff, and service timing despite his age. He makes a failing room function through absolute command. His competence is not imagined, but neither is the coercion: survival authorizes a kitchen dictatorship in his own account (`09-035`-`09-044`).

His `スープ・ド・ポワソンラーメン` concentrates seafood stock, rouille/cheese richness, and lobster-shell powder. Violent motion, dark fields, crushing shell imagery, and combat fantasy make the eater experience his dish as domination (`09-044`-`09-054`). The volume validates its power without yet allowing it to define every kind of strong cooking.

## 7. Chapters 71-72 — Megumi loses, and her dish remains evidence

Megumi's `こづゆ鶏醤油ラーメン` initially appears clear and quiet. Dried scallop, chicken, dried vegetables, and layered stock produce increasingly forceful umami. A concentrated side bowl changes the soup during eating. The dish is designed as generous service, but its generosity is technically accumulated rather than merely emotional (`09-055`-`09-062`).

Her decisive claim is that she wanted to make strong ramen. Kurokiba tastes it and recognizes a direct challenge. His aggressive register and her careful register become mutually legible without collapsing into sameness. He is forced to become serious; she does not need to imitate his body, commands, or flavor density to reach him.

Kurokiba is explicitly declared the winner (`09-066`). That consequence remains exact. Megumi's applause, the judges' praise, and Kurokiba's recognition do not make her co-winner or undo elimination. Conversely, elimination does not make the praise false. The volume's evaluative maturity lies in maintaining both propositions.

Megumi is disappointed and bows. Her friends support rather than overwrite her reaction (`09-067`-`09-070`). This is a stronger reconstruction point than a generic resilience label: she can experience adverse judgment, retain authored work, and remain in relation to the field.

## 8. Chapters 72-74 — obligation, surveillance, and medicinal appetite

Hayama's desire to reach the summit remains tied to Jun Shiomi. His gift has a research home, guardian, and purpose. Takumi and Isami preserve their ambition and fraternity while the bracket threatens any promised future meeting. None of these relational attachments makes the cook derivative; each does create an obligation that may later conflict with independent judgment (`09-070`-`09-074`).

Mimasaka's earlier reputation finally receives method evidence. He conceals himself, watches entrants, and retains fine-grained bodily, schedule, and history data. This is competitive profiling, not yet a complete culinary theory. The epistemic contrast with Megumi's recipient care is severe: both attend closely to another person, but one does so for service and the other extracts information without consent for leverage (`09-075`-`09-084`).

The fourth pairing is Hayama versus Hisako on hamburger. Hisako butchers softshell turtle, uses its meat and blood, incorporates processed shell material, and coordinates extraction, sauce, patty, and bun. Her burger is rich, glossy, appetite-producing, and physiologically framed. Medicinal cuisine does not mean sensory restraint (`09-079`-`09-093`).

Hayama identifies its structure through smell and begins his answer. Volume 9 stops at `09-094`. It supplies no completed Hayama dish, comparative tasting, vote, winner, or elimination.

## 9. Character-state reconstruction

### Yukihira Soma

Soma gains a clean, explicit tournament win. Its reconstructive value lies less in the fact of winning than in how the final mechanism works: he notices, stores, and recomposes knowledge from an inexpensive science kit into a task-specific recipient system. His innovation remains dependent and authored at once.

With Alice, he practices honest rivalry. He neither mocks her pain nor declares her work worthless. His weak point remains possible bluntness, but here directness preserves her standing as a competitor rather than converting her into someone who must be comforted through a lie.

### Nakiri Alice

Alice receives her first explicit adverse verdict. She retastes the winner's dish, connects the loss to her history of needing a weapon, cries, and renews ambition. This is credible fair-loss resilience because it includes injury and reflection. Her science, labor, knife skill, and sensory achievement remain intact.

### Tadokoro Megumi

Megumi's agency survives direct competition. She selects a regional ramen concept, develops it, explicitly seeks strength, serves it, loses, bows, and remains recognized. Her care-centered craft is not support-only identity; it can challenge an opponent in a formal match.

### Kurokiba Ryo

Kurokiba's port childhood gives his bandana-state more than spectacle. Command, service timing, extraction, and survival are one learned system. His ability to recognize Megumi's challenge is an important exception to pure domination: another register can compel him.

### Hayama, Hisako, Mimasaka, and Takumi

Hayama's specialist smell and obligation to Jun continue into the hamburger match, but no result exists here. Hisako's medicinal cuisine becomes a full appetite system and a self-authored competitive answer; its result is open. Mimasaka becomes reconstructable as a surveillant profiler but not yet as a complete cook. Takumi's promised match with Soma remains vulnerable and unresolved.

## 10. Relationship dynamics

Soma and Alice move from category conflict to reciprocal rivalry. The result is unequal, but recognition survives it. Alice's promise to continue and Soma's refusal to soften the fact of loss create a more durable competitive relation than mutual politeness would.

Megumi and Kurokiba acquire mutual legibility through food. He initially discounts enjoyment/care as play; her ramen makes that judgment untenable. The relationship is not friendship, equality, or reconciliation. It is a bounded correction delivered by the losing cook to the winner.

Alice and Kurokiba remain entangled across hierarchy and rivalry. She knows the frustration of defeat and expects him to win; he refuses to exist merely as commanded extension. Jun and Hayama likewise combine care, research dependence, obligation, and ambition without yet resolving possible conflict.

Mimasaka creates a negative relational model. Attention without reciprocity can become appropriation. The difference between knowing an eater and surveilling an opponent will require later testing, but Volume 9 supplies the first explicit evidence for the distinction.

## 12. Culinary ideology, craft, and pedagogy

### Science can be vernacular

Spherification is learned through children's material and adapted to cheap seaweed bento. Technical legitimacy does not require elite apparatus, but it does require functional transformation.

### Category fit can decide between excellent dishes

Alice's work remains excellent. Soma's answer wins because the panel judges its whole relation to bento more complete. Task fit is comparative without becoming universal worth.

### Strength has more than one sensory grammar

Kurokiba makes strength immediate, dense, and violent. Megumi makes it cumulative, clear, nourishing, and changeable. Her defeat does not refute the plurality; his forced acknowledgment demonstrates it.

### Regional memory is active design

Kozuyu is not reproduced as nostalgia. Megumi alters its structure for ramen, coordinates dried ingredients with noodles and stock, and adds a concentrated stage to the eating sequence.

### Medicine and appetite can coincide

Hisako's softshell-turtle burger is nourishing precisely through a rich sensory event. Health function does not excuse poor pleasure; pleasure carries the medicinal system.

### Attention has an ethics

Soma studies a category and remembers service; Megumi studies a recipient's experience; Mimasaka studies private habits. The craft value of attention cannot be separated from how access is obtained and whom the knowledge serves.

## 13. Institution, power, and legitimacy

The Election's bracket produces exact advancement while showcasing plural craft. Soma and Kurokiba advance; Alice and Megumi do not. Public recognition for losing food is possible, but it does not change institutional scarcity.

Kurokiba's childhood port role is an informal institution built under economic precarity. Responsibility over procurement, accounting, staff, and service gives him real authority; the absence of checks turns it coercive. Competence can legitimize command locally without making domination normatively complete.

Alice's overseas memory shows gender, age, and nationality shaping the burden of proof inside elite research space. Her individual weapon is partly defensive adaptation to exclusion. Losing now does not remove the historical need that produced it.

The fourth match keeps elite sorting open. Hisako's relation to Erina and Hayama's relation to Jun each produce motivated, specialist authorship. The volume does not decide which institutionally advances.

Mimasaka's surveillance exposes a governance gap. The tournament rewards prepared performance but, so far, supplies no visible protection against non-consensual profiling of peers.

## 19. V2 closure at the V09 boundary

Volume 9 resolves two contests while resisting the fantasy that a winner contains all culinary value. Soma's pearls complete a bento designed for an absent eater and prove that vernacular craft can absorb scientific process. Alice loses without becoming false. Kurokiba's seafood system wins through overwhelming integration. Megumi loses without becoming weak, derivative, or technically irrelevant.

The completed verdicts therefore do two jobs. They enforce institutional scarcity, and they expose new relations among valid forms. Alice can learn from a cheaper technology without abandoning her own. Kurokiba can be forced to recognize strength that does not look like command. Megumi can leave the bracket with disappointment and authorship intact.

The volume's final unfinished field makes the rule prospective. Hisako's medicinal burger is already real; Hayama's answer and the verdict are not. Mimasaka knows disturbing amounts about his peers; the culinary consequence is not yet established. At the V09 boundary, competition is a sharp instrument of selection and an incomplete instrument of truth.
