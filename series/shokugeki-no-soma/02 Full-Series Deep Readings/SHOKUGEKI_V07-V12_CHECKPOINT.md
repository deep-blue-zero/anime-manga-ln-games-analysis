## 3. Literary development across the second movement

### 3.1 Ranked individuality becomes recipient relation

V07 converts a field of methods into publicly scored systems. Local memory, medical training, fermentation, smoke, molecular apparatus, restaurant labor, and scholarly knowledge matter only when they become coherent dishes under a shared task. The exact preliminary results create scarce consequences without becoming a universal character hierarchy.

V08 then changes the unit of culinary thought from the autonomous plate to the route between cook and eater. Hayama's enclosed aroma, Soma's curry-risotto omelet, Alice's ordered jewel box, Soma's thermal nori bento, and the children's class all ask how timing, delay, sequence, fear, memory, and social cost shape what food can do. Technique does not oppose heart; technique is how relation becomes material.

### 3.2 Competition becomes diagnostic without becoming total

V09-V10 make adverse results explicit. Soma defeats Alice and Kurokiba defeats Megumi while the losing dishes remain technically valid and character-revealing. Hayama defeats Hisako through a complete burger system rather than smell alone. Mimasaka then proves that non-consensual attention can become real culinary craft: he predicts Takumi's dessert, anticipates its divergence, wins unanimously, and takes the mezzaluna.

The ethical and epistemic problem therefore sharpens. Accuracy is not care; consent does not automatically legitimate an extreme wager; and a verdict can select advancement without exhausting the defeated person. Soma's decision to expose the true Yukihira beef-stew baseline refuses secrecy as the foundation of authorship and makes future revision the field of comparison.

### 3.3 Authorship and judgment acquire temporal depth

V11 locates Soma's answer in years of failed practice that a recent external trace cannot exhaust. His separately optimized and recombined beef cuts defeat Mimasaka unanimously, all 99 tools return, and post-match action refuses vocational annihilation as the proper meaning of loss. The other semifinal exposes a different boundary: Hayama and Kurokiba make two complete systems that split four judges while Sonoka refuses a false fifth rank. The institution revises the bracket rather than manufacturing consensus.

V12 extends both arguments into ingredient time. Hayama and Kurokiba initially exceed Soma through trained procurement senses. Soma improves through repetition, then rejects technically successful maturation because it still fails the competitive comparison. Rice-bran preservation becomes meaningful only when it is integrated with grilling, rice, and repeated service. Kurokiba's sealed concentration, Hayama's calibrated aromatic strike, and Soma's unfolding meal are three different temporal forms. The volume ends before the third form completes.

### 3.4 Frozen block thesis

> **Volumes 7-12 argue that culinary excellence is a relational and temporal system made answerable under comparison. Competition can disclose task fit, craft, deficit, and growth, but remains legitimate only when it distinguishes a local result from a total person, preserves the evidentiary value of losing work, and admits when its own judging mechanism has not—or cannot yet—completed the comparison.**

## 8. Ledger synthesis

### Craft and pedagogy

The block strengthens a non-romantic model of authorship: local memory, inherited technique, elite apparatus, specialist senses, scholarship, copied data, repeated failure, peer feedback, and regional preservation can all enter genuine craft. The controlling question is whether those resources become a coherent, task-responsive eating system for which the cook can answer. Pedagogy likewise depends on the learner's condition and perceived cost of failure, not only the teacher's intention.

### Institution and power

The Election is more procedurally visible than earlier expulsion tests, yet visibility does not settle legitimacy. Scores, themes, panels, stakes, and bracket exceptions are disclosed; private tool/livelihood wagers and covert surveillance remain weakly governed. The Hayama-Kurokiba non-ranking shows accountable discretion, while Eizan's manipulation shows concentrated power escaping accountability.

### Competition and evaluation

Results remain exact and local. Preliminary scores, first-round winners, one unanimous Food War, one split/non-ranking semifinal, and one intermediate final comparison all have real consequences. None authorizes a permanent hierarchy. Losing dishes remain evidence; body spectacle remains sensory rhetoric; an unfinished eating sequence remains an unfinished judgment.

### Visual form

The six volumes move from scoreboards and apparatus fields through containers, route diagrams, mirrored workstations, returned tools, split placards, market repetition, moonlit architecture, sealed steam, weapon metaphors, clay-pot service, and interrupted closure. Objects and page sequence make consequence and temporality visible. Eroticized or comic body reactions amplify experience but never replace explicit adjudication.

## 13. Checkpoint closure

Volumes 7-12 begin by assigning scores to culinary individuality and end by refusing to finish a dish after one serving. Across that movement, the series repeatedly turns apparent oppositions into structured coexistence: science and care, influence and authorship, loss and valid craft, gratitude and rivalry, technical accuracy and ethical violation, institutional decision and epistemic humility.

The checkpoint closes at a deliberately unstable moment. Two finalists have completed their first service, one bounded comparison favors Hayama over Kurokiba, and Soma insists that the panel's temporal model of his dish is wrong. The next volume must begin from that exact incompletion. No remembered champion, later status, or retrospective hierarchy may cross the boundary early.
