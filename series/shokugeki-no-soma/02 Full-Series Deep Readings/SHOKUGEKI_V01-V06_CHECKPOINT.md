## 3. Literary development across the first movement

### 3.1 From a threatened shop to a public field

V01 begins with Yukihira as a service relationship under threat. Soma's practical ingenuity protects a diner whose value comes from repeated contact with customers, then Totsuki relocates him into a hierarchy that claims the right to classify nearly everyone as discardable.

V02 makes that hierarchy political. The Elite Ten, research-society resources, and Shokugeki procedure show that culinary institutions distribute space and legitimacy as well as grades. Soma's donburi answer succeeds through affordability, speed, service fit, and visible preparation—not pedigree denial or ingredient spectacle alone.

V03 and V04 move from peer comparison into the problem of authority. Takumi supplies a skilled rival who need not become an enemy. Megumi demonstrates independent adaptive judgment, yet Shinomiya's recipe sovereignty converts that judgment into expulsion. The mock Shokugeki then preserves both truths: Shinomiya wins the technical tasting 3-0, while Megumi's authored care and future potential justify continued enrollment and reopen Shinomiya's closed professional self.

V05 expands reception from plate to ecology. Soma's souffle fails as a buffet object despite tasting good; recovery requires visibility, timing, equipment, iteration, and diner choice. The Sumire roll extends that situated imagination through packaging, commuter routes, household use, price, collective production, and neighborhood memory.

V06 turns the same outward intelligence toward inherited knowledge. Joichiro, Jun, Hayama, Polar Star history, and the Moon Heaven Hall all show that expertise is accumulated through people and institutions. Yet inheritance does not deliver a finished answer. Soma must transform acknowledged deficiency and a month of experiment into something that can survive public comparison.

### 3.2 Frozen block thesis

> **Across Volumes 1-6, cooking becomes legitimate when skill remains answerable to a recipient, a situation, and the people whose knowledge makes the dish possible. Competition is most developmental when it renders differences exact without turning one result into a total human hierarchy.**

This thesis preserves the strongest counterevidence. Totsuki's coercive pressures sometimes create real learning; elite cooks often possess genuine mastery; Soma can overgeneralize from service values; care does not erase technical defeat; adaptation does not guarantee dominance; and collaboration can coexist with clear individual authorship.

## 6. Relationship-state transitions

- **Soma / Megumi:** anxious classroom pairing -> reciprocal capability recognition -> Megumi-authored hierarchy with Soma as support -> productive separation and mutual competitive expectation.
- **Soma / Joichiro:** familiar father-son rivalry -> benchmark relation complicated by unknown biography, institutional non-completion, and international reach.
- **Soma / Takumi:** unilateral challenge -> mutually recognized professional rivalry whose promised formal settlement remains open.
- **Soma / Mito:** coercive formal opponents -> post-loss social contact -> collaborators whose disagreement preserves domain expertise -> simultaneous competitors.
- **Soma / Erina:** status/palate conflict -> recurrent antagonistic attention and exact benchmark asymmetry; no trust, alliance, or parentage recognition yet.
- **Soma / Eizan:** local collaborative service versus managerial domination; personal threat remains procedurally unresolved.
- **Megumi / Shinomiya:** unjust evaluator/student -> authored corrective encounter with real technical loss, future investment, and partial rescission; power asymmetry remains.
- **Dojima / Shinomiya:** senior-junior history -> evidence-responsive intervention into defensive professional closure.
- **Hayama / Jun:** nominal professor/assistant pair -> reciprocal research, culinary, and caretaking interdependence.
- **Polar Star:** residence as developmental shelter -> specialist knowledge network -> internal public comparison under scarcity without yet demonstrated fracture.

## 7. Voice stability and reconstruction constraints

The first six volumes do not support generic confidence rewrites.

- Soma's colloquial compression remains stable across teasing, loss, command, envy, and ambition. Increased maturity appears as more exact diagnosis, not formalized speech.
- Megumi's soft, deferential, regionally inflected voice can carry competence and authority. Her behavior often advances before her self-description.
- Mito's forceful specialist register persists through ideological revision; flexibility must not become meekness.
- Erina's polished categorical register can carry both genuine expertise and status defense. Private affect is frequently visual and should not be invented as confessional monologue.
- Takumi's ceremonially charged rivalry language makes competition future-binding, while occupational technique keeps it from becoming empty grandiosity.
- Shinomiya's partial correction remains audible through abrasive phrasing; reform does not instantly sanitize voice.
- Dojima's low-heat declaratives exercise great procedural power. Authority is not indexed by loudness.
- Joichiro's teasing familiarity and concise challenges allow affection without sentimental exposition.
- Hayama's formal address to Jun coexists with blunt commands, caretaking, and sensory certainty.
- Jun's flustered/apologetic social speech coexists with exact scholarly exposition.

## 8. Ledger synthesis

### Craft and pedagogy

The first movement advances from service knowledge, through affordable/contextual design, to accountable adaptation, distributed authorship, and research-practice conversion. Its strongest pedagogical rule is that knowledge becomes durable when a cook encounters a concrete difference—an eater, opponent, failure, place, or specialist—and changes practice without erasing the source of correction.

### Institution and power

Totsuki is neither exposed as empty nor vindicated as meritocracy. Its experts are real; its opportunities are valuable; its selection and expulsion powers are concentrated and sometimes weakly accountable. Shokugeki can constrain conflict when authorized, but V04's remedy depends on Dojima's exceptional intervention. The Election offers clearer public rules while retaining selection and judge discretion.

### Competition and evaluation

Results must remain typed. A technical vote, future-investment vote, throughput threshold, sales change, selected status, private family rivalry, and public preliminary score answer different questions. The corpus rejects both sentimental equalization and total rank inference.

### Visual form

Bodily tasting fantasy persists, with meaningful gender asymmetry and male/comic variants. The manga also develops a wider formal grammar: empty/crowded service space, hands and tools as occupational evidence, coins as differentiated judgment, packages as moving community identity, negative space as interior pressure, aroma as spatial reach, montage as duration, and architecture as institutional memory.

## 9. V1-to-V2 reconciliation

V1 sampled Soma readings overlap V01 and V03 inside this checkpoint. They remain frozen bounded provenance rather than mutable ensemble authority. The V2 crosswalk records the relevant transitions.

- V01 V2 broadly confirms the V1 model of service-grounded identity, practical recombination, low-cost experimentation, resistance to unsupported hierarchy, and affective reliance on cooking. It narrows claims that could otherwise overgeneralize from one transfer exam or from spectacle.
- V03 V2 preserves Soma's competence-sensitive rivalry and protective action while reallocating more authorship to Megumi, differentiating graduate authority, and treating the Shokugeki proposal as procedural appeal rather than an already won reform.
- V02, V04, V05, and V06 contain no same-volume V1 Soma character reading. Their evidence extends the V2 model prospectively and must not be described as holdout validation of V1.

The checkpoint therefore preserves V1's value without letting its sampled architecture substitute for the unread full-series sequence.

## 13. Checkpoint closure

Volumes 1-6 establish a story in which cooking repeatedly becomes a way to make hidden relations measurable: between flavor and timing, price and dignity, recipe and authority, care and technique, inheritance and authorship, or confidence and acknowledged lack. The series' competitions are valuable when they expose those relations precisely. They become dangerous when a result or office is treated as permission to totalize a person.

The checkpoint closes before the first Election score. That is analytically useful. The field, methods, ambitions, and histories are now visible, but the corpus has not yet learned who advances. All future claims about V07 must therefore begin from this unresolved state rather than from remembered outcome.
