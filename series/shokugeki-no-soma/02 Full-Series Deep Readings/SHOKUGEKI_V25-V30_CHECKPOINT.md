## 3. Literary development across the fifth movement

### 3.1 Craft matters only when a cook can make it available now

V25 completes the first Régiment bout by refusing a simple tradition-versus-innovation hierarchy. Nene's nine-to-one handmade soba is excellent, and Julio's inherited Italian eel service is coherent. Their losses arise when authentic systems become closed to present conditions or other culinary categories.

Soma learns from Nene's own demonstration, selects aromatic `sanbanko`, uses an eight-buckwheat/two-wheat `nihachi` route, and builds heat, char, duck fat, sauce, and cup-yakisoba-derived delivery around a cold room that suppresses delicate soba aroma. Diner practice provides both readiness and a stopping rule: the relevant question is whether this eater can be served in this environment.

Isshiki makes the same argument through chosen obligation. Polar Star ingredients and experiments are genuine material inputs to his `hitsumabushi — Polar Star Dorm flavor`; he remains responsible for transforming them into a controlled three-stage plate. `My cooking` names accountable synthesis rather than isolated originality. His 3-0 win over Julio protects living contributors instead of a hereditary category.

The first-bout result is exact: Soma defeats Nene, Isshiki defeats Julio, and Megishima defeats Kaburagi. The rebels sweep 3-0 and retain eight active cooks against Central's five. Megishima/Kaburagi's result is canonical while their undisclosed theme, plates, tasting, and vote remain unavailable.

### 3.2 Team capacity emerges as attention crossing separate cards

V26 denies Soma an automatic place in the next field. Erina selects Kuga, Megishima, and Mimasaka after reading fatigue and tactical need. Winner eligibility does not override bodily availability or team judgment.

The second bout makes cooperation culinary before it makes it victorious. Mimasaka's `Perfect Trace Flash` reduces copying lag and frees attention for smoked soy sauce and tea-smoking support at Kuga's station. Kuga accepts the help and remains author of his green-tea Sichuan plate. Megishima enters after Soma repeatedly loses at ramen but persuades him that culinary plurality requires material defense. Rindo, Tsukasa, and Saito meanwhile receive full specialist credibility: field research, temperature-specific ingredient service, and fairness through blade sharing are not invalidated by Central alignment.

V26 ends with six completed plates and no results. Capacity, fairness, purpose, strain, and mutual attention exist before a ballot. V27 then supplies the exact Central sweep: Tsukasa defeats Kuga 3-0, Rindo defeats Megishima 3-0, and Saito defeats Mimasaka 3-0.

The bodies of the winners prevent unanimity from becoming an effortless-dominance story. Tsukasa describes Kuga's pressure through crossed blades; Rindo collapses after defeating Megishima; Saito recognizes how far Mimasaka's tracing advanced. The losing cooks do not receive fractional wins, and the winners do not become weak. Attritional cost becomes team evidence at a different scale from the ballots.

Kuga's recognition problem receives a precise answer. He does not reverse the hierarchy, but Tsukasa can no longer treat him as forgettable. Recognition arrives through experienced cost, not score revision.

### 3.3 Assistance, chronology, and material error remain attributable

The third-bout preparation differentiates mentorship: Joichiro works with Soma, Gin with Takumi, and Shinomiya with Megumi. Erina replenishes Megumi without solving her problem. The first-year formation moves ingredients and information quickly across stations, but each official plate retains one entrant.

Eizan's cynarin attack reveals that culinary evaluation is temporal. His roast beef and artichoke legally alter how Takumi's later pizza will taste. Takumi answers by adopting the imposed chronology: a yuzu-miso shigure-ni half uses the sweetened baseline, and a four-cheese half creates a second transition. The total becomes a `hop, step, jump`, and Takumi wins 3-0. He credits Eizan's pressure without sharing the official result.

Megumi converts Shinomiya's transfer pedagogy and attacking spirit into apple-and-white-bean dorayaki with apple butter and an ambitious confiture center. She earns one WGO vote and loses 1-2 to Momo because slight bitterness and final imbalance remain. Growth, material error, opponent excellence, and exact adverse judgment all coexist.

Saito's support for Central begins in a real gendered institutional injury to his mother's sushi practice. Standardized merit offers intelligible protection while reproducing exclusion through concentrated authority. His seafood/butter bowl and placement knowledge remain serious. Soma defeats him 3-0 with butter-pilaf inari forged from attributable cohort lessons and insists that a `single blade` can be the product of the 92nd class rather than an isolated maker.

The third bout closes at Takumi 3-0 Eizan, Momo 2-1 Megumi, and Soma 3-0 Saito. The rebels win two cards and Central one, leaving four active rebels against three Central cooks. Erina moves from maintaining the field to voluntarily entering it.

### 3.4 Perfected mastery is tested as both ceiling and relation

V29 gives three different mechanisms to the same 3-0 score. Erina defeats Momo by attempting a 120-point black-sugar soufflé after treating Megumi's failed risk as instruction. The loss preserves Momo's commercial-aesthetic authority, soy-sauce counterpoint, and roll-cake fortress while exposing a safe perfected ceiling.

Tsukasa defeats Isshiki with hare `à la royale`, but Isshiki's `序破急` bowl, Nene-linked joy, and refusal of subordinate service remain outside the winner's possession. Tsukasa accurately understands that coercion cannot remove culinary pleasure; the recognition sharpens rather than excuses his political contradiction.

Rindo defeats Takumi with pirarucu-centered wild causa. Takumi's calamari ripieni remains credible, and his adverse position lets him perceive a narrow margin, high expenditure, and fear in the winner. The fear is evidence; its single cause remains unstated through V30.

Azami becomes head judge with WGO first-class executors Decora and Courage under Senzaemon's approval. Their professional local authority does not make him politically disinterested. After the fourth-bout result leaves Soma/Erina against Tsukasa/Rindo, Senzaemon changes the unit of judgment from two possible cards to one appetizer-and-main course per team.

The format makes relation part of the dish without erasing the two cooks. One plate changes the eater who receives the next. Erina assigns Soma the appetizer and retains the main, accepting that another person's work will alter the conditions of her own judgment.

### 3.5 Relational authorship defeats the cage

V30 gives Central a real hundred-point course. Rindo's mushroom mille-feuille uses ant-derived formic acid as a precise appetite-opening contrast. Tsukasa's salt-crust venison completes the specialité that previously defeated Soma. Their coordination aligns two perfected culinary identities.

The rebel pair instead creates a reciprocal transformation loop. Erina rejects Soma's first route and uses the God Tongue to correct successive trials. Soma turns cohort memory, familiar sauce, pâté, meat, vegetables, and injected consommé into `時限式・生意気小僧風原始肉`, a changing appetizer that remains his. He then calls the course a shokugeki between teammates and demands that Erina surpass it.

Erina abandons her preset main, selects a new route, and orders Soma to complete a tightly timed contribution. Her oyakodon reconstruction uses chicken, egg-and-cream turmeric sauce, rice cracker, bread-based `croûte`, squid tentacles, and melting chicken-aspic `furikake`. `楽園から飛び立つ真の美食 ～不良娘風～` is neither a copy of Soma nor an isolated expression untouched by him.

Authority moves with the task. Erina assigns and corrects Soma; he changes the conditions of her authorship; she then commands his labor inside her answer. Cooperation is agonistic, hierarchy reversible, and influence attributable.

Azami's doctrine gains its strongest explanation. He saw Joichiro's collapse and experienced culinary wandering as pain, then made the God Tongue a sovereign compass and exclusion a protective cage. Erina answers that relationships, changed values, and the desire to hear another person say `delicious` made her plate possible. His involuntary stripping pulse spreads through judges, opponents, and audience, making sensory recognition exceed ideological refusal.

Hisako names herself Erina's friend and defines help as enabling another person's own step. Soma answers Azami's permanent-suffering story with concrete testimony that Joichiro found happiness after meeting Soma's mother. No one person becomes the cure; relation matters by restoring capacity to act.

The rebel alliance wins. Erina's `お粗末!!` transfers a diner-facing register position without making her into Soma. The ending joins collective result, distinct authorship, and relational change while leaving governance and family accountability open.

## 8. Ledger synthesis

### Craft and pedagogy

The block distinguishes possession of technique from access to it under present conditions. Cold-room aroma, rare-ingredient acid, salt crust, cynarin, rice placement, aspic melting, and course order make environment and sequence part of the dish. Instruction succeeds through transfer and iterative correction, not reproduction. Low-prestige foods and industrial delivery systems remain legitimate archives when their functions are re-authored.

### Institution and power

WGO judging, tool fairness, exact ballots, and bounded expert criticism supply local legitimacy inside a coercive political contest. Central's strongest justification is protective rather than technically empty: it promises to prevent gifted cooks from wandering or being excluded by arbitrary custom. Its failure is the conversion of protection into one evaluator's sovereignty and the disposal of alternate futures.

### Competition and evaluation

The six volumes preserve five distinct scales: plate mechanism, ballot, bout aggregation, embodied attrition, and negotiated institutional consequence. A 3-0 can coexist with a difficult win; a 1-2 can mark elite growth and exact defeat; a team can benefit from a losing plate without owning part of its ballot; a shared course can win once while retaining two plate authors.

### Relationship and recovery

Recovery is ecological rather than possessed. Nene's practice gives Isshiki a reason to continue; Soma's losses and argument move Megishima; Erina's care preserves Megumi's work; Mimasaka supports Kuga; Megumi's failed risk teaches Erina; Soma and Erina correct and provoke one another; Hisako names friendship; Soma remembers family happiness. No helper becomes sole author of the beneficiary's action.

### Visual form

Cold festival space, doubled sushi boards, exhausted winners, three-stage pizza moons, apple roses, boxing dorayaki, single-blade cohort imagery, black-sugar fortress, changing bowls, white armor, crude meat, golden melting cubes, cages, flight, and stripping pulse turn abstract claims into visible structures. Body comedy supplies sensory evidence while exact locators, ballots, and publication boundaries keep spectacle from becoming an unlimited verdict.

## 13. Checkpoint closure

Volumes 25-30 begin with cold air exposing the limit of a perfect inherited noodle and end with residual heat melting aspic into a dish its controlling evaluator tried to reject on sight. Between those points, the Régiment repeatedly grants excellence to opponents and failure to allies. That discipline lets the final political claim rest on something stronger than protagonist entitlement: authorship survives when people may observe, disagree, borrow, fail, correct, provoke, and still answer for what they make.

The frozen block therefore rejects two false choices. Tradition need not be dogma, and collective causation need not be anonymous.
