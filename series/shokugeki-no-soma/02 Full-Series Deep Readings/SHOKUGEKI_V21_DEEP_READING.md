## Assessment becomes unequal before anyone cooks

The first exam names an apparently ordinary standard: groups of five must make one salmon dish the examiner considers delicious. The material conditions have already decided whom the institution expects to fail. Soma's resistant group receives nutritionally depleted post-spawning `ホッチャレ`, while the other groups receive excellent salmon. An apparent escape clause permits procurement, but Totsuki's distribution power has absorbed the nearby supply.

The distinction is procedural rather than semantic. The task still uses culinary words, and a successful plate still requires skill. Yet formally equal instructions do not create equal opportunity when the assessor assigns unusable inputs and controls the outside market. Volume 21 therefore shows assessment capture before the judge can issue a biased taste verdict.

The second stage scales the same logic. Students may make any noodle cuisine and procure what the room lacks, but resistant groups enter last, after earlier groups empty the pantry. A blizzard makes the market thirty kilometers away unreachable. The institution can now describe expulsion as a failure to complete an open-ended problem even though it engineered the student's practical world to contain no ordinary solution.

## Erina's teaching is proven by dishes she did not invent

The resistant students answer the salmon trap by remembering brine freezing. They do not possess the fresh salmon Central monopolized; they infer that frozen inventory may preserve an alternative. `時鮭 / トキシラズ` is valuable because it has not yet spent its nutrients on autumn reproduction, and rapid brine freezing protects cellular quality. Erina's prior instruction supplies a property chain from season, biology, and storage to procurement.

The heavy-snow-udon sequence tests the pedagogy more sharply. Soma, Takumi, and Megumi recover flour, potato starch, potato, onion, and scraps, then turn potato's starch, sweetness, and textural range into noodle, broth, and topping. Other resistant groups produce their own local noodle solutions. When the examiner accuses Erina of having armed them with the answer, she makes the volume's authorship rule explicit: she taught ingredient characteristics; they decided what to cook.

This is not laissez-faire romanticism. Erina's preparation was severe, technically dense, and designed for crisis. Its legitimacy comes from where authority stops. She uses expertise to increase the number of people capable of formulating an answer rather than making recognition depend on reproducing hers.

## Resistance becomes a network of procurement, memory, and labor

Volume 21 repeatedly denies a sole-protagonist rescue structure. Alice recognizes the exhausted salmon and seasonal problem; Ryo articulates treatment limits; the five-person group searches, returns, and executes together. Other groups independently survive their own unequal allocations. In the noodle test, Soma, Megumi, and Takumi share a strategy while each remains accountable for a required serving, and distant rooms generate distinct dishes.

Soma's contribution remains specific. He refuses premature surrender, treats procurement struggle as ordinary diner work, and recognizes useful remnants. Those actions matter without absorbing the knowledge, search labor, execution, or results of the surrounding cooks.

Kuga later extends the same distributed structure into bear-meat preparation. He supplies spices, a counterexample hotpot, local knowledge, assistants, lodging, and access to the mountain. He does so for self-interested reasons: a stronger Soma could reopen challenges against the Elite Ten. Support can be consequential, strategic, and conditional without becoming ownership of the future plate.

## Recovery enters ordinary time

Volume 20 made Erina's recovery visible in public emergency leadership. Volume 21 asks whether that widening can enter scenes not organized solely by crisis. On the train, friends bring brown-rice tea, offer massage, and worry that their teacher has not rested. Soma relays their concern and makes an intentionally crude comparison between crisp salmon skin and the night sky. Erina laughs and privately notices that speaking with him is no longer so unpleasant.

Sapporo gives the question more room. Mito understands successor pressure and tells Erina that her dignity under greater burden earned real respect. Megumi receives thanks for tea but rejects any repayment account: studying, reading manga, and spending time together are enough grounds for friendship. Erina lingers before the illuminated city and realizes that she has never truly taken part in sightseeing like this.

These are meaningful changes because they let her receive attention that is not tasting labor, family duty, or rescue ownership. They remain bounded. She does not speak the frozen-egg bowl's requested `delicious`, confront Azami, secure durable safety, cook an independent present-tense dish, or establish a post-crisis life. Ordinary pleasure becomes imaginable before it becomes stable.

## Hayama is neither a simple traitor nor a simple captive

Hayama's provisional Ninth Seat creates the volume's most difficult agency problem. Central has power over the Shiomi Seminar and can make Jun's position part of its leverage. Hayama also speaks in first-person ambition. He condemns Soma for hesitating, says he moved first, and turns protection, upward motion, and prior Election rivalry into one test.

Reducing him to voluntary doctrinal conversion would erase institutional pressure. Reducing him to a puppet would erase his contempt, competitive pride, and choice to use the offered route. Volume 21 preserves the mixed state: constrained agency can remain agency, and ambitious action can still be shaped by coercive options.

His new rank is provisional, not a completed proof over Soma in the current task. His Fall Election victory supplies relevant history, and Dojima's endpoint observation supplies a present skill warning. Neither is the bear-meat result before the dishes exist.

## Bear odor becomes an epistemic problem

Soma's first salt-seared bear meat appears promising: rich sweetness and fat arrive early. The animal odor emerges later in chewing. This delay matters because it turns first impression into incomplete evidence. The body discovers a problem after the eye and first taste have already formed a favorable expectation.

Ordinary game treatment suggests simmering and strong aromatics. Kuga's bear hotpot proves that miso and Chinese spices can make the meat delicious. It also clarifies the harder task: if every difficult smell is removed, bear meat becomes a generic carrier for other flavors. Deodorization can solve acceptability while failing expression.

Soma's comparison to natto and cheese reframes `臭さ` as a possible root of `旨さ`. Ingredient identity is not whatever survives after all resistance has been erased. The cook must distinguish destructive odor from the sensory force that makes the ingredient itself worth choosing.

Kuga's fieldwork principle completes the shift. His Sichuan practice came from travel, eating, and the air of a place. To cook bear, Soma should encounter the habitat rather than only manipulate a vacuum-packed portion in a hotel kitchen. Technique becomes ecological attention: smell, terrain, animal life, storage, spice, and eater response belong to one problem.

## A fair matchup can remain inside an unfair institution

Rindo describes the third stage as direct competition against the Elite Ten. Dojima serves as a neutral official, the theme is drawn from established advancement-exam material, both cooks receive bear meat, and three preparation days are allowed. These features can support a valid plate comparison later.

They do not cleanse the surrounding structure. Central first assigns unusable salmon, stages pantry depletion, benefits from blocked transportation, sends a required train away early, and then directs identified resisters into expulsion-bearing contests against its highest-ranked cooks. A later honest tasting would answer which plate is stronger under that task. It would not prove that the students freely chose the stakes or that the promotion exam is legitimate.

Volume 21 thus extends Volume 20's result/consequence distinction into result/structure. Culinary evidence can be real within a coercive institution. The ethical error begins when the institution treats task-specific superiority as authority to eliminate a person's education or cooking life.

## Rindo joins assistance to threat

Rindo arrives after the early train departure with supplemental tickets, prevents immediate removal, and reveals the more dangerous test awaiting the group. Her register remains playful even when the consequence is expulsion. She later eats casually with students and speaks openly about matchups, then disappears from the local scene while Central's plan proceeds.

The bonus comics supply low-weight historical texture: a younger Rindo pursues instructors for more tasting and imagines herself as Second Seat beside Eishi as First. They make competitive companionship and appetite plausible continuities. They do not resolve why present Rindo supports Central, assists stranded opponents, enjoys danger, or relates her own goals to Eishi's authority.

## Voice and register

Soma's speech remains vocational and concrete. He names procurement as diner work, greets impossible assignments with questions about materials, and treats Hayama's rank as a reason to test rather than a result. His confidence survives a new warning without pretending the bear problem is solved.

Erina's range continues to widen. She issues exact corrections, accepts friends' worry, thanks Megumi, explains why student invention belongs to the students, and briefly permits silent wonder in Sapporo. The voice is not softened into generic warmth; categorical culinary authority now coexists with reciprocal social attention.

Hayama speaks with clipped certainty and greater distance. His claim that he has already moved ahead turns institutional rank into personal accusation. Kuga's teasing self-interest carries real technical generosity, while Rindo's sociable play carries real threat. As elsewhere in the Central arc, surface warmth cannot determine moral or procedural position.

## Visual and formal argument

The salmon task contrasts boxes of rich fish with gray depleted bodies, then replaces scarcity with search routes, frozen stock, coordinated hands, and a luminous finished plate. The visual answer is not magic abundance but a changed map of where usable material may exist.

The blizzard test makes infrastructure visible as weather and distance. Empty bins, a thirty-kilometer sign, stopped transport, and a white exterior turn nominal freedom to procure into a cruel joke. Returning students then fill separate kitchens with differently organized motion. Heterogeneous production becomes the visual proof of Erina's pedagogy.

Erina's Sapporo sequence slows the institutional rhythm. Steam, street lights, soft serve, winter clothing, and walking bodies create a civic space in which food need not be test evidence. The early departure abruptly converts that ordinary interval back into administrative danger.

Hayama's reveal uses a high balcony, provisional badge, and sharp vertical separation. Bear preparation then moves from sealed blocks and late-emerging odor imagery to Kuga's overflowing spice jars and finally the mountain horizon. The volume expands the ingredient from meat sample to habitat but stops before either cook makes a final plate.

- **Yukihira Soma:** leads without monopolizing group work, converts procurement and remnants into dishes, accepts a new direct test against Hayama, and reframes bear odor through fermentation analogy and field study. He has no completed bear answer or third-stage result.

- **Kuga Terunori:** turns prior gatekeeping into strategic support, contributes spices, hotpot, field knowledge, and logistics, and seeks renewed Elite Ten conflict. He does not own Soma's future dish or result.
- **Tadokoro Megumi:** contributes to salmon and noodle survival, offers care without repayment, and articulates friendship as enough. Her gentleness remains operational and authored.
- **Mito Ikumi:** translates family-successor pressure into precise recognition of Erina and maintains genuine respect without surrendering her own perspective.
- **Rindo Kobayashi:** combines rescue from immediate administrative exclusion with announcement of a more dangerous Central test. Her complete motive and political relation to Eishi remain unresolved.
- **Dojima Gin:** accepts a neutral oversight role, preserves the difference between an expressive bear dish and simple deodorization, and issues an adverse present assessment of Soma's chances.
- **Central:** fails to expel resistant students through two rigged stages, then escalates into targeted Elite Ten matchups while retaining control of route, stakes, and progression.
