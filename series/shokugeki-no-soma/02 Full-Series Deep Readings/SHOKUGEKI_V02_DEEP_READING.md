# SHOKUGEKI_V02_DEEP_READING

## 1. Authority, horizon, and reading rule

This is the canonical V2 full-volume reading of *Shokugeki no Soma* Volume 2 at the V02 publication boundary. Volume 2 was not part of the original eight-volume Soma sample, so there is no same-volume V1 character reading to consult after the fresh V2 pass. The only inherited analytical material allowed to structure the read is the V2 method, the V01 local state, and the frozen V01 prospective register.

No event after Volume 2 is used to resolve open questions here. In particular, the final pages only establish departure for the residential training camp; no Volume 3 camp outcome is imported.

The central V02 finding is:

> **Volume 2 shifts the opening argument from who is entitled to judge culinary value toward what makes excellence appropriate to the actual form, eater, community, and institutional problem. It repeatedly grants elite technique and premium ingredients genuine value while rejecting the inference that value in one dimension should dominate every other variable.**

This is why the volume can admire Isshiki's refined sawara dish, establish Mito Ikumi as a technically formidable meat specialist, and still let Soma oppose both rank worship and ingredient supremacy. The opposition is contextual, not anti-elite.

A second movement is equally important. Totsuki stops being only an anonymous selection machine and becomes a contested social ecology. Polar Star Dormitory supplies communal production and distributed expertise; the Council of Ten and the Shokugeki system formalize student power; Erina's campaign against the Donburi Research Society shows how culinary ideology can become resource politics; and the residential training announcement restates attrition as an explicit educational mechanism.

# 3. Structural movement - from outsider to ecology

Volume 1 ended with Soma and Megumi surviving a sabotaged classroom task. Volume 2 begins by asking what happens when Soma acquires a place inside Totsuki rather than merely fighting its gates.

The answer unfolds through four nested social structures:

1. **Polar Star Dormitory** - entry is still earned through cooking, but the result is admission to a household rather than a grade. Knowledge is distributed among residents and materially embedded in fields, smoking, fermentation, animal husbandry, and ordinary meals.
2. **The Council of Ten** - student culinary excellence is explicitly convertible into institutional decision power. Isshiki is both friendly dorm senior and Seventh Seat; status does not require ceremonial social distance.
3. **Shokugeki** - disputes become contractual culinary contests with mutually agreed stakes, recognized judges, and enforceable consequences. Cooking is political procedure as well as craft.
4. **Residential training** - the school reasserts the opposite side of communal belonging: first-years are sent into an explicitly attritional camp in which failure can mean immediate expulsion.

This is not a simple movement from bad institution to good community. Polar Star exists inside Totsuki and draws value from its concentration of ambitious specialists. Isshiki himself is a major institutional actor. The volume instead distinguishes forms of authority and learning that coexist inside the same school.

# 4. Volume thesis - excellence must fit the whole problem

The Mito duel supplies the volume's cleanest culinary argument.

Mito's starting proposition is close to ingredient sovereignty: valuable meat is the center around which all other ingredients should subordinate themselves. The manga does not expose this as empty boasting. She possesses unusually strong meat knowledge, can select and handle high-end beef, and demonstrates precise embodied control of cooking temperature. Her so-called "meat sonar" is stylized, but the underlying point is practical expertise built from repeated contact with meat.

Soma's objection is therefore not "expensive beef is fake." His response is more demanding:

> **A cook should not mistake the market value or intrinsic luxury of one component for the success of the complete dish.**

The Shokugeki theme is not steak. It is donburi. The actual object is a one-bowl meal whose parts have to work together through repeated bites. Mito's A5 wagyu is spectacular, but the richness makes the accompanying rice increasingly passive. The meat dominates the bowl rather than completing it.

Soma constructs his Chaliapin steak don around the opposite logic. Cheap supermarket beef is tenderized with onion; a rich onion-based sauce gives the meat weight; ume-flavored rice creates a sharp counterforce; the flavor cycle encourages another bite. The bowl is designed as a sequence, not a pedestal for one premium component.

Soma states the principle explicitly at the decisive moment: **a donburi is complete in one bowl.** The rice is not scenery. The eater's continued appetite is part of the design criterion.

This gives V02 a broader formal principle:

> **Culinary excellence is relational. A technically excellent component can be the wrong answer if its excellence prevents the dish, service context, price structure, or eater from functioning as a whole.**

That principle also explains why Soma refuses the easy ideological shortcut of answering premium beef with different premium beef. If he wants the Shokugeki to defend donburi as an ordinary, flexible form, winning through another luxury ingredient would fail to test the claim he actually cares about.

# 5. Chapter architecture

## 5.1 Chapter 6 - Polar Star admission converts evaluation into hospitality

Polar Star first appears visually as an old, somewhat ominous building far from the elite-modern image of Totsuki. Dorm manager Daimido Fumio nevertheless reproduces the school's basic evaluative grammar: a new resident has to cook something that satisfies her.

The conditions differ from Erina's transfer exam. Fumio gives Soma an ordinary pantry and leftovers rather than a prestige arena. Soma makes a canned-mackerel hamburger set from what is available. The dish triggers a strongly nostalgic response in Fumio, reconnecting present taste to her younger self.

The test therefore evaluates more than technical impressiveness. It asks whether an improvised dish can speak to the person who eats it.

Fumio then admits Soma to room 303. The gate has not disappeared, but the reward is incorporation into a shared household.

The immediate bath accident with Megumi also introduces a recurring distinction the visual ledger needs to preserve. Body exposure can serve several different functions in this manga: sensory metaphor, sexualized fanservice, embarrassment comedy, or ordinary dorm intimacy. They should not be treated as one undifferentiated device.

## 5.2 Chapter 7 - the "lawless zone" is a knowledge network

The welcoming party rapidly establishes Polar Star's ensemble: Marui Zenji, Ibusaki Shun, Yoshino Yuki, Sakaki Ryoko, Aoki Daigo, Sato Shoji, Megumi, Isshiki Satoshi, and Fumio occupy a noisy space whose social temperature is almost the inverse of the entrance ceremony.

The important revelation is Isshiki. He behaves like an unusually relaxed older dorm resident, then turns out to be the Seventh Seat of the Council of Ten. His rank does not arrive through ceremonial staging inside the dorm. It is discovered after familiarity has already been established.

This matters for Soma's V01 model. Learning Isshiki's title does not create deep deference. Soma becomes more interested in what Isshiki can actually cook.

The chapter also explains the Council of Ten as a student body whose culinary ranking carries major institutional decision power. That link is essential for later political analysis: at Totsuki, demonstrated culinary status is not merely prestige. It is convertible into governance.

## 5.3 Chapter 8 - spring dishes and reciprocal expertise

Soma and Isshiki's informal sawara comparison gives V02 its first clean test of the V01 frozen higher-status-competence prediction.

Isshiki serves grilled sawara with sansho and spring-cabbage puree. Soma immediately recognizes the dish's refined balance and compares it to high-level Japanese restaurant cooking. He does not depreciate the technique because Isshiki is an institutional elite, nor does learning the Seventh Seat title cause him to surrender his own authorship.

Soma replies with `Yukihira secret menu No. 20 revised`, sawara onigiri ochazuke. He uses a French-style searing mechanism inside a casual rice-and-tea format. The dish demonstrates the prestige permeability already visible in V01: techniques can travel across cuisine, social status, and meal form.

The two cooks then describe each other's dishes through reciprocal seasonal imagery: spring breeze, snowmelt, freshness. Competition becomes a mode of communication rather than a zero-sum status verdict.

Soma asks how one acquires a Council seat and whether defeating Isshiki would simply transfer Seventh Seat to him. Isshiki explains that the structure is not that simple. Soma responds by declaring that he intends to take the seat eventually. Isshiki is delighted rather than socially threatened.

This is the volume's first durable example of **rank without prestige submission** and **rivalry without interpersonal hostility**.

The scene is not a fair-loss test. Later dialogue implies Isshiki may not have shown his full capacity. No V2 score should pretend that Soma suffered or processed a clean defeat here.

## 5.4 Chapter 9 - Shokugeki turns food into constitutional procedure

The threatened Donburi Research Society moves the analysis from individual evaluation to institutional resource conflict.

Erina's project is presented as active reorganization: research societies she regards as unnecessary can lose space and resources. Mito acts as the culinary enforcer of pressure against the Donburi Research Society.

Isshiki explains the Shokugeki system. It originated as a student mechanism for settling disputes. Both sides must put up stakes judged commensurate; the conditions must be agreed; official judging is required; and the result carries institutional force.

This is important because Shokugeki is neither arbitrary violence nor neutral sport. It is **contractualized culinary politics**.

The procedure can create legitimacy by converting a power dispute into a bounded contest, but it cannot erase asymmetry in what the parties possess or can afford to wager. A weak club may be forced to risk its existence merely to resist a stronger faction. Formal consent therefore has to be analyzed together with the distribution of institutional power.

## 5.5 Chapter 10 - Mito's ingredient hierarchy

Mito Ikumi is introduced through sexualized visual emphasis and an aggressive culinary identity, but the character should not be reduced to either.

She is a serious meat specialist. The series gives her both technical authority and ideological rigidity. Her key proposition is that valuable meat should cause the other ingredients to bow before it.

Soma directly objects that rejoicing only in the value of the ingredient disgraces the role of the cook. Again, this is not anti-meat or anti-luxury. It is an authorship claim: ingredients do not relieve the cook of responsibility for composition.

Soma volunteers to take the Donburi Research Society's Shokugeki. The stakes become severe: his expulsion if he loses; preservation of the club plus Mito's membership if he wins.

This is a new form of Soma's actionability. He is not defending a childhood institution or existing friend. He adopts a dispute he has only just encountered because the culinary and power claims are concrete enough to act on.

## 5.6 Chapter 11 - affordability is not an aesthetic excuse

Preparation for the duel makes the material problem literal. Soma lacks the budget to answer Mito's A5 beef with equivalently priced meat.

He and Konishi test multiple approaches and study the Donburi Research Society's recipes. Soma eventually reaches an important constraint: if the point is to make Mito recognize the value of donburi, winning with another premium beef product would hollow out the argument.

Megumi contributes to the search, and Soma adopts the Chaliapin steak mechanism: onion enzymes tenderize inexpensive beef. He then builds a sauce and rice system around it.

The critical point is not "cheap always beats expensive." Cheap beef creates additional technical problems that have to be solved. Affordability is treated as a real design constraint, not moral purity.

## 5.7 Chapter 12 - restaurant service remains Soma's fear baseline

The official Shokugeki begins in a large arena with a hostile crowd. Soma has earned much of that hostility himself through his opening-ceremony provocation.

Megumi worries about the pressure. Soma's answer is one of the strongest V02 character locators: compared with real restaurant service, where the shop's name rests on the cook's work in front of actual customers, this contest is not especially frightening.

This directly strengthens the V01 customer-facing craft claim. Service is not nostalgic background that disappears once tournament spectacle begins. It is the experience against which Soma measures institutional pressure.

Mito, meanwhile, treats the match as an opportunity to demonstrate how far premium meat can be pushed. She is not relying on price alone; she intends to extract its maximum potential.

## 5.8 Chapter 13 - the quiet bowl and the eloquent bowl

Mito's A5 wagyu don is a legitimate high-level dish. Her tactile control of internal heat, resting technique, surface handling, and deep meat knowledge are all treated as real competence. Judges respond with extreme pleasure.

Soma's cheap steak initially looks ridiculous beside it. The reversal therefore cannot be "the supposedly skilled elite cook was never skilled." It is a comparison between different definitions of the task.

The judges find that Soma's bowl becomes easier to continue eating. The sauce is rich, but the ume-seasoned rice cuts and redirects that richness. Each component makes the next bite possible.

Mito's weakness is not bad meat. It is that she had conceptually demoted the rice. Her garlic rice cannot keep pace with the A5 beef's dominance.

Soma names the formal difference: donburi is complete in a single bowl.

He then serves his bowl directly to Mito with `oagariyo`. The competitor becomes an eater. This is structurally important because the argument is no longer won only through third-party judges; Mito must experience the competing culinary logic herself.

Her response is unusually revealing. The bowl seems to tell her to eat freely, to shovel it in according to her own appetite. The "eloquent" donburi communicates a relation to eating that her prestige hierarchy had suppressed.

A childhood flashback adds a tentative psychological layer. Young Ikumi receives a soft toy from her mother, while a harsher family demand tells her to think only about being strong. The volume does not yet warrant a complete family model, but it makes the duel's emotional meaning larger than ingredient theory: the food reaches a self whose cultivation has emphasized hardness, value, and strength.

Soma wins unanimously.

His post-win behavior matters just as much as the score. He immediately asks to taste Mito's donburi too. Victory does not make her skill epistemically worthless. He teases her with the `Nikumi` nickname and converts the defeated opponent into an ongoing social/culinary relation.

Erina's response is different. The cooking room previously granted to Mito is ordered withdrawn after the loss. This is the clearest V02 evidence that patronage and culinary usefulness can condition access to resources.

Mito nevertheless follows the Shokugeki term and reports to the Donburi Research Society. The loser is not narratively erased; the contest redistributes affiliation.

## 5.9 Chapter 14 - Megumi's garden and the alternate school inside the school

Chapter 14 temporarily steps away from arena spectacle and reveals the material infrastructure of Polar Star.

Residents produce knowledge with their hands:

- Ibusaki makes his own smoking chips;
- Ryoko works with shio-koji and maintains dedicated production space;
- Yoshino raises poultry and dreams of developing a Polar Star branded chicken;
- Isshiki cultivates a large garden containing many vegetables and fruits;
- residents trade ingredients, techniques, labor, and meals.

Isshiki states the pedagogical principle directly: knowing how ingredients grow is itself part of learning to cook.

This is not presented as an official alternative curriculum, but it behaves like one. Polar Star converts residence into a workshop, farm, pantry, and peer-learning network. The joke that Isshiki has become so absorbed that he has not been attending class properly pushes the contrast to comic excess: informal craft study can become so rich that it competes with formal schooling.

Megumi's three onigiri then supply a major reconstruction correction.

In V01, most of her evidence came from expulsion anxiety and a sabotaged graded exercise. Here, without immediate evaluation threat, she prepares a deliberately varied lunch for the group. The fillings are thoughtful rather than flashy: chicken with green-onion salt sauce, kombu tsukudani with cheese, and a pork-and-garlic preparation marinated with miso, sake, and honey. She also considers what drink accompanies the meal.

Isshiki identifies the distinctive quality of her cooking as its ability to calm the eater. Soma recognizes that the work is careful and asks why she cannot simply reproduce this in class. Megumi answers that intense nervousness makes her mind go blank and causes mistakes.

This materially revises the naive proposition "Megumi lacks cooking competence." V02 supports something more specific:

> **Megumi already possesses coherent craft knowledge and recipient-sensitive cooking, but evaluative threat interferes with reliable access to that competence.**

That is enough to promote her V2 readiness from `tracked` to `emerging`, while still leaving high-stakes independent performance unresolved.

The chapter closes by announcing residential training. All first-years will face repeated severe cooking trials at a mountain lodging; failure can cause immediate expulsion. Isshiki describes it as the beginning of Totsuki's serious "selection of jewels." Students remember years in which the cohort was cut dramatically, even below half.

The volume therefore ends by placing Megumi's newly demonstrated ordinary competence directly in the path of the institution that most disrupts it.

# 6. Literary lens I - community as distributed expertise

Polar Star changes the meaning of ensemble writing.

The dorm residents are not simply friends waiting to cheer Soma in future matches. V02 gives several of them distinct material domains. Smoking, fermentation, livestock, cultivation, home cooking, and restaurant-style refinement occupy the same household.

This matters because the series' knowledge system is becoming networked. No single prestige hierarchy exhausts culinary competence. A Seventh Seat can learn through farm labor; Megumi can offer a home-derived combination nobody in the room dismisses as lesser; Soma can use French technique in ochazuke; Mito can possess elite beef mastery while being wrong about the total logic of donburi.

The emerging V2 proposition is:

> **Culinary authorship develops through permeability to specialized people, materials, and contexts rather than through isolation of a pure individual style.**

That claim remains open, but V02 supplies the first ensemble architecture capable of supporting it.

# 7. Literary lens II - institutional power becomes legible

V01 established that Totsuki concentrates authority. V02 specifies several mechanisms.

## Council of Ten

Culinary achievement carries governance power. A high-ranked student can be both elite cook and institutional decision-maker.

## Shokugeki

Disputes can be formalized as contractual culinary contests with agreed stakes and official judging. This gives cooking a constitutional role inside student politics.

## Research-society resource conflict

Clubs possess rooms, budgets, identity, and vulnerability. Erina's preferred reorganization can threaten groups she regards as unproductive. Institutional culinary ideology therefore has material consequences.

## Patronage

Mito's access to a cooking room is shown as something Erina can withdraw after failure. Relationship to a powerful patron can condition resources.

## Residential training

Attrition is reiterated as pedagogy rather than accident. The school does not only judge completed dishes; it designs environments intended to eliminate students.

The resulting claim strengthens `SH-INST-001` from V01:

> **Totsuki's legitimacy cannot be evaluated by asking only whether its strongest people are competent. The institution deliberately fuses expertise, selection, contractual risk, resource control, and concentrated student authority.**

---

# 8. Literary lens III - competition as conversion, not annihilation

V02 supplies two very different competitive forms.

Soma and Isshiki cook informally. There is no official stake, and the exchange produces mutual description and future challenge. Competition functions as knowledge exchange.

Soma and Mito enter an official Shokugeki with expulsion and club survival on the table. Yet even this harsher contest does not end in total social domination. Soma wants to taste Mito's dish after winning. Mito enters the Donburi Research Society under the agreed terms. Her meat expertise survives the defeat.

This suggests an important relationship rule for later testing:

> **A culinary result can redistribute status, obligation, or affiliation without making the defeated person's competence socially or epistemically worthless.**

Erina's post-loss withdrawal of Mito's room supplies the countermodel: institutional patronage can treat failure more transactionally than Soma treats rivalry.

# 9. Manga form - food argues through bodies, page rhythm, and contrast

## 9.1 V01 bodily truth grammar persists

V02 strongly confirms the frozen V01 visual prediction. High-impact tasting continues to externalize evaluation through impossible bodily/fantasy imagery.

The pattern broadens:

- Fumio's canned-mackerel dish produces nostalgic embodied transport;
- Polar Star residents react intensely to Soma and Isshiki's spring dishes;
- women continue to receive strongly eroticized fantasy staging;
- Isshiki also receives a conspicuous male nude/idealized sensory reaction;
- Mito and the Shokugeki judges receive highly sexualized responses to the donburi contest;
- ordinary non-tasting body comedy includes the bath encounter and Isshiki's near-naked farm labor.

The correct longitudinal formulation remains asymmetric rather than exclusive: female eroticization is more frequent and more conventionally sexualized, but male bodies and male sensory fantasy are part of the formal system rather than absent exceptions.

## 9.2 Ingredient prestige becomes visual scale

Mito's A5 beef is staged with mass, marbling, shine, precision, and spectacle. Soma's supermarket steak is visually embarrassing by comparison. The manga lets the prestige hierarchy win the first visual comparison before reversing the evaluative frame through how the bowl behaves over repeated bites.

This is structurally related to V01's delayed furikake transformation. The reader is first encouraged to see why the elite classification is compelling, then shown the variable it omitted.

## 9.3 The one-bowl argument is spatial

The donburi duel is not only explained in dialogue. The composition repeatedly emphasizes the entire bowl: meat, rice, sauce, garnish, and the movement of chopsticks through them. Mito's meat can dominate the frame; Soma's solution repeatedly returns attention to the system beneath the meat.

The title "quiet bowl / eloquent bowl" becomes formal rather than decorative. Soma's bowl "speaks" because its organization tells the eater how to continue eating.

## 9.4 Dormitory space changes pacing

Polar Star chapters use ensemble panels, shared tables, work areas, fields, bedrooms, bath space, and food preparation to create a lower-pressure rhythm between evaluation arcs. This ordinary-state material is analytically valuable because it exposes behavior that tournament pressure can mask.

# 10. Character reconstruction delta - Yukihira Soma

## 10.1 Current V2 model through V02

V02 substantially strengthens the V01 model without promoting Soma to `substantial` yet.

New or strengthened rules:

1. **Demonstrated competence overrides prestige skepticism.** Isshiki's Seventh Seat title matters less than the cooking Soma can taste. Once skill is demonstrated, Soma studies and praises it.
2. **Rank recognition does not require identity submission.** He can call Isshiki `senpai`, listen to the Council explanation, and still announce intent to take the seat.
3. **Technique is portable across prestige boundaries.** French poele technique can be metabolized into a Yukihira ochazuke form.
4. **Affordability can become a first-class design constraint.** Soma does not romanticize cheap beef; he engineers around its limitations because the problem demands an accessible donburi.
5. **Service remains his pressure benchmark.** A formal arena with expulsion at stake feels less frightening to him than serving real customers under the Yukihira name.
6. **Victory does not end epistemic interest in the loser.** After defeating Mito, he immediately wants to taste her bowl.
7. **Provocation is relationship-shaped.** With Mito he moves rapidly from ideological challenge to nickname teasing. This is not ceremonially gracious sportsmanship; it is a casual attempt to normalize continued contact.

Necessary counterevidence remains:

- V02 still does not supply a clean non-family fair loss for Soma;
- Isshiki may have held back, so the informal spring comparison cannot be scored as a defeat test;
- voluntarily risking expulsion for the Donburi Research Society may reflect admirable intervention, thrill-seeking, confidence in his own skill, or some combination. Later volumes must distinguish these motives.

# 11. Character reconstruction delta - Tadokoro Megumi

Megumi receives the largest readiness change in V02.

V01 made her easy to misread because almost all evidence was filtered through imminent expulsion. V02 provides ordinary social space and independent cooking.

Her onigiri demonstrate:

- multi-component technical planning;
- preservation/marinating knowledge;
- concern for what is pleasant to eat during shared work;
- home-derived food memory used without embarrassment;
- recipient-calming rather than prestige-maximizing design.

Soma's surprise is analytically useful: even he had not inferred this competence from her classroom behavior.

Current conditional formulation:

> **Megumi's early weakness is not absence of culinary knowledge. High evaluative threat appears to disrupt access to competence that is much more visible in ordinary, relational cooking.**

Readiness transition: `tracked -> emerging`.

The next decisive test is whether she can recover that competence under high-stakes conditions without relying on Soma to structure the task.

# 12. Character reconstruction delta - Isshiki Satoshi

Isshiki enters V2 at `emerging` rather than merely `tracked` because Volume 2 gives him several distinct states:

- genial dorm senior and host;
- Seventh Seat institutional elite;
- refined cook;
- informal rival who welcomes Soma's challenge;
- explainer of Council and Shokugeki structures;
- agricultural laborer who treats ingredient cultivation as culinary education;
- communal caretaker who expects the Polar Star first-years to return safely from the training camp.

His authority style is strikingly low-ceremony. Rank is structurally real but socially underperformed in the dorm.

Open conditions:

- how much skill he withheld in the spring dish exchange;
- whether geniality persists when Council interests conflict with dorm loyalties;
- how he responds to his own failure, correction, or status threat.

# 13. Character reconstruction delta - Mito Ikumi

Mito also reaches `emerging` from a single unusually dense volume.

V02 establishes:

- real meat expertise rather than prestige bluffing;
- ingredient-supremacy ideology;
- aggressive and sexualized public self-presentation;
- relation to Erina as patron/authority;
- willingness to stake and perform in formal Shokugeki;
- emotional destabilization after a fair culinary loss;
- childhood pressure toward strength and suppression of softness;
- compliance with the wager after defeat;
- capacity to enter a new social group rather than disappear after losing.

The defeat does not prove a complete personality conversion. Her embarrassment, anger at the `Nikumi` nickname, and continued pride remain visible.

A strong open hypothesis is:

> **Mito's hardness may be both genuine competitive temperament and an identity reinforced by family/status environments that reward strength and premium-value thinking.**

Later evidence is required before treating that as causal certainty.

---

# 14. Character reconstruction delta - Nakiri Erina

Erina remains `emerging`.

V02 does not instantiate the frozen low-prestige tasting condition, so `SH-V2-P003` remains untested. The volume instead adds institutional behavior.

She appears to use Mito as an agent in the Donburi Research Society conflict and can withdraw a cooking room previously granted to her after defeat. This suggests a patronage model in which culinary usefulness, loyalty, and access to resources may be linked.

That should not yet be reduced to "Erina abandons people who lose." The evidence is one institutional relationship under a specific project. What V02 does establish is that her power is materially consequential beyond verbal snobbery.

# 15. Japanese voice and register delta

## Soma

V02 confirms the V01 architecture: `ore`, direct colloquial syntax, low ceremony, quick admission of practical constraints, and shallow senior politeness.

With Isshiki, titles such as `Isshiki-senpai` coexist with open challenge. With Mito, plain speech and nickname teasing appear rapidly. Serious culinary claims remain concrete: bowl, rice, meat, price, service, eating to the last bite.

The post-match `oagariyo` is especially important. Soma uses a service formula toward the defeated opponent, turning the rivalry back into cook-to-eater relation.

## Megumi

V02 supplies the first strong ordinary-state contrast. Her speech remains soft and relational, but the content becomes much more assured when she explains food she prepared herself. Anxiety therefore affects more than behavior; it alters how much of her culinary competence is audible in speech.

## Isshiki

Isshiki uses a warm, inclusive, lightly teasing senior register, commonly addressing juniors by name plus `-kun`. He explains institutional structures without adopting bureaucratic stiffness. His linguistic authority is socially soft even when his formal rank is high.

## Mito

Mito's register is blunt, aggressive, and identity-marking. Imperatives and superiority claims fit the "meat master" persona. The nickname `Nikumi` becomes a recurring face-threat trigger; Soma's continued use of it after the match creates a deliberately asymmetrical teasing relationship.

## Polar Star ensemble

The dorm broadens the voice corpus toward peer banter, work chatter, food-sharing, and group rallying. This ordinary-state material is essential for later reconstruction because tournament speech alone would overestimate competitive intensity.

# 16. Relationship states through V02

## Soma <-> Megumi

`cooperative peer bond -> ordinary familiarity + reciprocal craft visibility`

Megumi is no longer only the anxious partner Soma rescues in a crisis. She contributes ideas during Shokugeki preparation and later feeds the group food that reveals an independent craft identity. Soma begins learning what she can do outside evaluation threat.

## Soma <-> Isshiki

`dorm senior/junior + mutual culinary interest + aspirational rivalry`

Soma recognizes Isshiki's skill, learns his rank, and responds with challenge rather than distance. Isshiki welcomes that challenge.

## Soma <-> Mito

`ideological opponent -> formal rival -> post-match teasing/epistemic continuity`

The relationship begins over ingredient hierarchy and resource coercion. After winning, Soma still wants her food. Competition therefore opens rather than closes a future relationship.
