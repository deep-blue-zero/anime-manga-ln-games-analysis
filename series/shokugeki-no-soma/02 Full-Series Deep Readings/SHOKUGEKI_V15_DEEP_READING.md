## Leading formulation

> **Volume 15 turns institutional distance into a problem of operation and legibility. Kuga's rank is powerful because it coordinates money, memory, labor, technique, reputation, and appetite into one reproducible public system. Soma cannot answer by intensifying a copied flavor. He must learn how a small mobile shop makes craft visible, lets customer response alter the next act, and recombines many credited contributions into an unfinished counterstrategy.**

## Seat power is a culinary infrastructure

The volume first refuses to treat the Elite Ten as a purely symbolic honor. Tsukasa is explicitly first seat, and the other nine positions are printed in full: Rindo second, Megishima third, Momo fourth, Saito fifth, Nene sixth, Isshiki seventh, Kuga eighth, Eizan ninth, and Erina tenth. The map closes V14's uncertainty about seat order without pretending to reveal later alliances or a common ideology.

Kuga explains what the order controls. A seat gives access to ingredients, craftsmen, equipment, recipes, archives, money, and permissions; higher position expands the range of available action. Totsuki's claim that “everything is cooking” therefore joins culinary evaluation to material governance. Ability does not merely receive a certificate. It is translated into the capacity to command resources and determine what can be built.

The people occupying the hierarchy do not sound alike. Tsukasa speaks softly about responsibility and discomfort with public command; Kuga teases him for failing to embody the top seat's expected confidence. A light register can still gatekeep, and an anxious register can still occupy first place. Voice, personality, institutional authority, and later commercial performance remain distinct variables.

Kuga refuses a conventional seat wager but offers a narrower test: show him one culinary dimension worth contesting. Soma's response is not to claim a right to rank. He looks for a foothold through the approaching Totsuki school festival.

## The festival makes cuisine inseparable from political economy

The Moon Festival is not merely a larger cooking match. Around 500,000 visitors, approved shops, food tickets, hygiene rules, location zoning, guidebook publicity, staffing, utilities, price bands, and daily sales rankings turn it into a temporary economy.

Its three areas encode different service theories. Main Street emphasizes inexpensive stalls and movement. Central supplies specialized kitchens and variable shop scale. The Mountain area supports expensive restaurant experiences, often attached to elite names and reservation-like scarcity. A dish cannot be evaluated apart from the customer flow and cost structure that make it reachable.

The institution intensifies the experiment through danger. Students budget ingredients, equipment, labor, and utilities against revenue; ending in deficit carries expulsion risk. Daily rankings therefore combine evidence, spectacle, and coercion. They show who has converted a plan into sales, but they are not a neutral measure of intrinsic culinary worth. Existing reputation, infrastructure, location, labor, and audience familiarity all shape the comparison.

The festival's commercial boards also clarify that rank is plural. Trattoria Aldini leads Main Street on day one. Kuga leads Central. Momo leads the Mountain area while Erina is second, Nene third, Saito fourth, Tsukasa fifth, Rindo sixth, and Isshiki seventh. None of those placements renumbers the Elite Ten. A first seat can finish below a fourth seat in one area-specific sales interval without ceasing to be first seat.

## Kuga's “one taste” is both authorship and organization

Hojo brings Soma and Megumi into the Chinese Cuisine Research Society, where Kuga's power becomes visible as trained repetition. A large crew performs synchronized wok work and produces multiple plates of mapo tofu with effectively identical dimensions and flavor. Kuga can design one taste and distribute its execution through other bodies.

That reproducibility is not a defect. The society has returning customers, media recognition, a practiced service routine, and the capacity to produce roughly the thousand daily servings needed to compete near the top. Kuga's authored taste becomes an institution because it survives delegation.

The content of that taste is equally organized. Kuga centers Sichuan `麻` and `辣`: numbing Sichuan pepper and chili heat. He describes diners whose faces redden but who continue eating, while Erina identifies the cultivated cycle as a form of control. The body comedy is therefore not separate from his culinary politics. Pain, craving, spectacle, and repeat consumption are made into a public rhythm.

Volume 15 does not reduce Kuga to coercion. His mapo tofu is excellent; his crew's consistency is real craft; his restaurant serves multiple Sichuan dishes; his brand accurately promises an intense experience. The ethical and institutional question is how that competence is joined to concentrated command and sensory domination, not whether the food secretly lacks value.

The same distinction limits generalization. Kuga is eighth seat, not a complete model of every council member. Tsukasa's reserve, Rindo's sociability, Nene's formality, Erina's diagnostic help, and the Mountain ranking already make the council heterogeneous.

## More heat is not an answer

Soma's first response shows the danger of mistaking a visible signature for a complete system. He makes mapo tofu and adds chili and Sichuan pepper, but Erina says both `麻` and `辣` are overwhelmingly insufficient. The problem is not solved when he simply increases them. Trials become painfully hot without acquiring Kuga's composition.

Erina supplies a crucial conceptual correction: physiological spiciness is pain, not one of the five canonical tastes. She and Megumi then widen “Chinese cuisine” beyond one generic flavor. Sichuan, Hunan, Guizhou, and other regional traditions carry distinct condiments, histories, and relations to heat. Kuga has mastered a deliberate selection within that plurality. Soma cannot surpass it by treating the category as a jar of stronger powder.

The correction network is distributed. Hojo explains the society's specialization. Megumi recalls regional knowledge and later diagnoses public imagery. Mito suggests richer seasoning and speaks from meat practice. Erina distinguishes taste, pain, and culinary region. Earlier Hayama experience informs spice handling. Shopping-district cooks supply a stone-kiln method. Nikumi and the Don RS offer labor. None of this erases Soma's authorship because the narrative keeps contribution and decision boundaries visible. He selects, tests, rejects, and recombines the advice; other people remain named sources rather than becoming invisible raw material.

## The pepper bun fails twice: first as flavor, then as image

Soma adapts a shopping-district stone kiln to a Taiwanese `胡椒餅`. The bun is technically promising: a crisp surface, hot meat juice, pork, scallion, sesame, and black-pepper aroma. Its form is suited to a cart and to walking customers.

The first version is too weak inside. Mito's input and Soma's own work improve the filling with sauces, aromatics, sesame oil, and toasted pepper. The result becomes genuinely delicious. Yet almost nobody approaches. Technical correction has solved one problem while exposing another.

The exterior does not tell an unfamiliar visitor what experience it contains. Kuga's restaurant needs no such explanation. Its architecture, queue, media identity, visible woks, staff uniforms, and established “fiery Sichuan” image make flavor imaginable before the customer eats. Soma's bun hides its best qualities behind a form that most passersby cannot read.

This distinction is the volume's sharpest revision of meritocratic spectacle. A good dish does not automatically become public evidence of goodness. Access, recognition, visibility, and other diners' reactions mediate whether anyone encounters it.

The first-day result is exact. Kuga is Central first. Soma is fortieth of forty and deeply in deficit. He does not joke the loss away. Megumi panics because the financial stakes are real; Soma's expression makes clear that he understands the scale of failure.

## A cart can observe and move

Day two begins with partial improvement, not miraculous recovery. Soma adds menu items and rises only to thirty-fifth. Kuga remains first. The gap is still immense.

The mobile cart nevertheless possesses capacities the palace-like restaurant lacks. It can move to where people gather, expose cooking as performance, and alter its offer quickly. Sadatsuka Nao's exaggerated smell sensitivity and wandering curiosity provide a useful first test. Soma recognizes that customer reaction can become part of service design rather than a score received only after the fact.

He hand-cuts pepper-bun dough into noodles and builds a small bowl from shrimp-shell and fish-like broth, minced pork, dried shrimp, green onion, and the bun's existing filling. The resulting Yukihira-style `担仔麺` transforms inventory, portion, texture, and presentation at once.

The bowl is lighter than the bun, visibly made, easy to eat while walking, and familiar enough to communicate “noodle” before tasting. Its size invites one more purchase rather than requiring a full meal commitment. Customers' pleasure becomes audible and visible to nearby passersby. The crowd begins to gather.

Soma explicitly links the mechanism to the shopping district's summer festival. There, the rarity of a stone kiln first drew attention; the response of one eater called others; the mobile operation could orbit the attraction. This is not a nostalgic flashback laid over an elite event. It is transfer of a working-cook observational method into a different economy.

Mobility is not automatically superior to scale. The cart can listen and move because it is small; Kuga can produce enormous consistent volume because his operation is fixed and trained. Volume 15 makes those capacities commensurable only through the festival's temporary sales contest, not through a universal theory of restaurant excellence.

## Megumi's diagnostic contribution

Megumi enters the project voluntarily because Soma lacks workers. She is not summoned as an interchangeable assistant. She provides remembered regional knowledge, performs shop labor, receives the preparation-room key, observes passersby, and finally names the remaining problem.

People who already know pepper buns can imagine the crisp shell and hot filling. Most festival visitors cannot. Kuga's Sichuan identity, by contrast, projects a powerful sensory image across the courtyard. Megumi's insight translates the competitive problem from “make a stronger dish” into “make the strength imaginable before purchase.”

This contribution is neither sole authorship nor passive support. Soma immediately thinks with it; Megumi does not cook the final counterstrategy for him. Their working relation strengthens through differentiated competence. The evidence supports trust and operational reciprocity, not automatic romance or equality in every domain.

## Alice's correction: spectacle must answer an audience

Alice briefly offers the most Alice-like solution: an elaborate, technologically controlled, expensive showpiece. Her own calculation makes the flaw visible. Festival visitors are not attending to buy a private high-price laboratory experience from Soma's cart. They need a dish that can be understood, enjoyed, and repeated within this event.

She withdraws the proposal herself and says they should think from what will make the visiting customers happy. The moment extends her V08 bento correction. Molecular technique remains valid, but brilliance that ignores the assigned form or recipient is not automatically the best answer. Audience calibration becomes part of her practical judgment rather than a principle imposed from outside.

The parallel booth structure matters. Alice, Kurokiba, and Hayama work under the Shiomi Seminar banner, partly as an offering to Jun. Takumi and Isami build a Trattoria Aldini branch with classmates and lead Main Street. Festival collaboration multiplies without making every team a version of Soma's. Post-stagiaire growth is visible in different organizational forms.

## The unresolved return to mapo tofu

By the night of day two, Soma understands that his pepper bun and danzai noodles cannot simply out-Kuga Kuga. Kuga's mapo is a mature specialty embedded in an institution. Soma nevertheless announces that his next move will be a new Yukihira mapo tofu.

The apparent reversal is the correct endpoint. If “mapo tofu” meant only stronger `麻辣`, the previous failures would make the choice incoherent. The intervening chapters have changed what the dish must do. It must project an image before tasting, work through the cart's customer circuit, retain recognizable relation to Kuga's stronghold, and still emerge from Soma's recombinatory archive.

Volume 15 does not show whether he can satisfy those conditions. It supplies no dish image, recipe, service, sales result, financial recovery, or Kuga judgment. The counterstrategy is a hypothesis, not a victory.

## Visual and formal argument

The volume repeatedly measures distance through scale. The Elite Ten appear as a ranked diagram backed by craftsmen, archives, and money. Kuga's headquarters fills pages with synchronized bodies and identical woks. His festival shop becomes a palace of pillars, tables, and queues. Soma's cart occupies a sliver of empty ground.

Tables and boards perform institutional work. The exact seat map fixes one hierarchy; the daily area rankings produce another. By placing Tsukasa fifth in the Mountain sales interval while retaining him as first seat, the pages make the reader hold two rankings apart.

Kuga's bodily dramaturgy turns pain into abundance. Red faces, sweat, fire, open mouths, compulsive return, and repeated dishes make mass appetite look like a force he conducts. Soma's first-day failure is visualized through emptiness: calls into open space, tiny ticket piles, and the widening physical gap between cart and restaurant.

The day-two noodle sequence reverses that isolation through hands and motion. Dough is cut in public; broth, toppings, steam, and small bowls move across the cart boundary; one eater's response becomes the next person's evidence. The mechanism is rendered socially before it is rendered statistically.

The school-song spread offers a deliberately comic common ritual, gathering rivals and collaborators into one institutional voice. The pages that follow immediately fracture that unity into separate shops, prices, queues, and rankings. Belonging to the same academy does not mean occupying the same economy.

The endpoint returns to the moon and the mobile wheel. Instead of a winner tableau, the volume gives Soma in transit with an untested proposition. Motion, rather than closure, is the final image.

## Character-state consequences

- **Yukihira Soma:** his low defensiveness now operates across marketing and service failure. He can accept last place, distinguish flavor quality from public legibility, use customer reaction as evidence, and revise product plus operation. The V15 endpoint remains a proposed test.
- **Kuga Terunori:** moves from casual gatekeeper to substantial character evidence. His craft, mass-training system, public reputation, sensory ideology, and organizational command are demonstrated, while adverse response and behavior outside dominance remain open.
- **Tadokoro Megumi:** gains stronger operational and diagnostic evidence. She enters by choice, contributes knowledge, shares responsibility, and identifies an audience problem Soma had not seen.
- **Nakiri Erina:** expert diagnosis remains bounded. She supplies taste/pain/regional distinctions and later temporal advice without taking over Soma's shop.
- **Nakiri Alice:** begins converting recipient/context correction into her own judgment by withdrawing an ill-fitted luxury solution.
- **Takumi and Isami Aldini:** first-place Main Street performance makes post-stagiaire operational growth visible without changing the Autumn Election record.
- **Elite Ten:** exact seat order is now authoritative, but council position, personality, sales rank, and ideology remain separate records.
