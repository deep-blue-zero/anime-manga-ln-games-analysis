# Ending, BLUE, and `Le dessert` end-state synthesis

## Boundary first

The final volume contains four distinct narrative times:

1. serialized Chapters 309-315, ending at `36-145`;
2. `Le dessert 1: Présent`, `36-149`-`36-178`;
3. `Le dessert 2: Passé`, `36-179`-`36-208`;
4. `Le dessert 3: Futur`, `36-209`-`36-243`.

All are canonical. They are not simultaneous, and later facts must not be backdated into the serialized ending.

## What BLUE actually resolves

The semifinal receives complete judgment. Asahi's `バスティー` is coherent and delicious. Soma's five-cuisine fried rice is then tasted, linked to Tamako and failure, and preferred by all three judges. Soma wins 3-0 (`35-168`-`36-075`).

The result establishes:

- Soma advances to the final;
- socially accumulated own taste defeats Asahi's centerless borrowed repertoire in this task;
- Asahi's losing plate remains genuine craft;
- one gifted knife is returned.

It does not establish universal rank, Mana's cure, Erina's consent, or complete Cross Knives accountability.

The final itself is not shown as a completed ballot. Erina recovers Mana as chosen recipient, cooks, and resumes rivalry with Soma before serialized `完` (`36-084`-`36-145`).

`Présent` later identifies Erina as `BLUE GRAND CHAMPION` and Soma as runner-up (`36-153`-`36-157`). No final score, vote allocation, full dish comparison, or judge reasoning is supplied. A responsible end-state account must state both the exact placement and the missing mechanism.

## Erina is not the semifinal prize

The BLUE arc repeatedly risks converting Erina into a stake: Asahi's marriage claim, Mana's authorization, Megumi's protective wager, and Soma's claim about her happiness (`32-191`-`35-111`).

The final reverses that structure. Erina becomes the author who chooses Mana as recipient and then wins the tournament. Soma's semifinal victory does not give him Erina; her final victory places her above him in BLUE's last recorded ordering.

Romantic implication remains elsewhere: in their unique evaluative relation, Erina's irritation at his absence, her private desire for his return and food, and his repeated promise to win her `美味い` (`36-168`-`36-176`; `36-224`-`36-243`).

There is no explicit mutual confession, dating relationship, partnership, engagement, or marriage.

## Mana: reintegration, not cure

Mana's pleasure at both semifinal dishes and her future-facing response to Erina reopen appetite and relation (`36-004`-`36-073`; `36-140`-`36-142`). In `Présent`, she anticipates Erina's cooking while still physically affected (`36-171`-`36-176`). In `Futur`, she joins a family table (`36-221`-`36-224`).

The series supplies no medical assessment or cure declaration. `Relational reintegration with improved access to eating` is supportable; `cured by Soma/Asahi/Erina` is not.

## Azami and Asahi: explanation, kinship, and compressed repair

Azami explains Central through Jōichirō's collapse and Mana's despair (`36-011`-`36-023`). `Passé` establishes Asahi as his biological son, and Erina prevents Azami from repeating departure as solitary atonement (`36-197`-`36-205`).

Erina and Mana approach Asahi as family. Adult evidence places him inside Nakiri and as a Tōtsuki instructor (`36-204`-`36-223`).

These are clear changes. They are not a full accountability narrative. The epilogue omits public restitution for Central, apology for abduction and coercive courtship, item-by-item tool return, and appointment safeguards. Family belonging should not be rewritten as absolution.

## `Présent`: immediate consequence

One year after BLUE, the cohort advances, another Election occurs, alumni judge, and culinary-school interest grows (`36-149`-`36-168`). Soma travels overseas under Jōichirō's strength condition and communicates intermittently. Erina's annoyance and worry make absence relationally visible.

The segment supplies placement and near-term motion. It does not yet show adult settled lives.

## `Passé`: causal history

Jōichirō narrates rehabilitation through Tamako and Yukihira. Her imperfect cooking, customer attention, and affection restore his relation to cooking (`36-179`-`36-197`).

Asahi's grave visit and parentage disclosure connect another child to family absence. Biological truth enters beside chosen pedagogy, not above it (`36-197`-`36-207`).

The segment's function is not nostalgic completion. It shows that the future depends on relationships the main plot previously left partly invisible.

## `Futur`: distributed adulthood

The adult montage gives cooks different destinations. Erina leads; Isshiki preserves Kyoto cuisine; Kuga runs restaurants; Megumi travels; Hayama teaches; Takumi and Isami work across locations; Alice and Ryo combine international operations, cooking, and marine science; other former students become owners, reviewers, and chefs (`36-209`-`36-227`).

Soma is internationally sought and intermittently opens Yukihira. The diner remains home without becoming confinement.

Montage breadth has a cost: labor conditions, institutional safeguards, family accountability, and ordinary relationship maintenance remain compressed. The ending should not be asked to prove more than it shows.

## The last dish

Erina privately wants Soma's return and food. Friends then arrive, Soma serves an experimental dish, and everyone calls it `不味い!!!!` (`36-234`-`36-242`).

The failure performs four closures:

- adult prestige does not become infallibility;
- romance does not absorb the ensemble;
- negative judgment does not withdraw belonging;
- Tamako's failure pedagogy survives into adulthood.

Soma treats the bad result as a new area and begins again. Erina smiles privately; the desired positive verdict remains withheld. `おあがりよ!!` closes the series as an offer, not an achieved summit (`36-241`-`36-243`).
