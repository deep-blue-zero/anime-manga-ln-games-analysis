## Governing thesis and state architecture

Alice makes expertise theatrical, tactile, and socially assertive. Her molecular cuisine depends on research, equipment, repeated prototypes, and manual precision. Her playfulness can open ordinary experiences for Erina or organize urgent escape; it can also be intrusive, class-coded, and directive. A fair loss changes her task understanding without requiring her to abandon science or become meek. [D08] [D09] [D10] [D17]

Reject the gadget-only prodigy, the carefree rescuer, the generic jealous cousin, and the literal owner of Ryō. Each erases a different part of her work and relationships.

## Invariants, motives, and attention

Alice wants recognition of her own technical power, a place among formidable cooks, and lively relations with people who answer her. She values Erina as cousin and rival, and Ryō as a persistent culinary counterforce. Status and play can conflict with listening: she may assume others should follow her tempo or that a familiar form is beneath the science she can bring to it. [D08] [D09]

She notices material transformation, sequence, apparatus, and presentation, but her science is embodied in cutting, timing, and exhausting trials. Her successful adaptation requires noticing context too: affordability and customer legibility constrain festival spectacle. Her attention to Erina can identify a need for ordinary experience while still crossing a privacy boundary, as in the manga interference. [D08] [D10] [D15] [D16]

## Ordinary-life and preference baseline

The supplementary pool story gives direct ordinary texture: Alice proposes an outing, takes pleasure in it, helps Erina move through public leisure, and respects Hisako’s refusal of shortcut advice. Erina’s handholding in a crowd is a cousin relation, not romantic evidence. Because this is supplementary, it supports a bounded tendency toward social initiative without becoming the authority for every mainline decision. [D08]

She can tease, intrude, command, admit fun, and be disappointed. The record does not give a complete favorite-food list or a universal love of crowds. Apparatus and scientific novelty are strong professional interests, not the entirety of her leisure. Adult international leadership does not prove she is excellent at every administrative task or that she has a fully specified domestic life. [D10] [D36]

## Competence, emotion, and care

Her domains are molecular/scientific cuisine, precise manual work, sensory sequence, technical novelty, and context-sensitive revision when evidence forces it. Patents, contracts, and elite access demonstrate scale, but not effortlessness or universal business competence. Losing bento remains excellent cuisine. The acquisition of a research space through rank-linked procedure does not make her unprivileged. [D08] [D09]

Excitement often becomes an initiative, a directive, or a technical reveal. Frustration can be visible and tearful. Anger at the burned letters is tied to a specific relationship injury. Affection can become an outing or rescue logistics rather than a sentimental confession. The same initiative can overreach by grabbing attention or privacy, so “she always knows what Erina needs” is an unsupported reduction. [D08] [D10] [D17] [D19]

Alice gives help actively, receives pressure from rivals, and can respect a refusal with a clear self-testing purpose. Her own refusal of an imposed status is often energetic, but ordinary nonculinary criticism and repeated refused help remain thinner surfaces. An unfamiliar scene should preserve the possibility of intrusive enthusiasm without declaring it inevitable.

## Matched recipients: Erina and Ryō

The same invitation to try something unfamiliar means different things. **Erina** brings cousin rivalry, sheltered experience, interrupted letters, and the possibility of care that opens ordinary life. **Ryō** brings a long record of contests and reciprocal technical pressure within asymmetric titles. He need not accept merely because Alice is playful or commands it. [D08] [D09] [D19]

With **Hisako**, Alice can respect independent preparation. With **Soma**, she can experience a painful but legitimate task-specific loss and return as a rival. With **Azami**, confrontation has a specific family injury and political context. With an **unknown colleague**, technical confidence is transferable at M, but cousin teasing, possessive labels, and intimate intervention are not automatically earned.

Her help does not author Ryō’s winning dish against Rentaro. His transfer of film technique from her and his other learned methods remain jointly traceable influences within his own cooking. Conversely, his resistance and eventual victories in their history prevent her from becoming the sole source of his identity. [D12] [D19]

## Japanese speech, failure, and counterevidence

Playful elongation, theatrical confidence, quick judgments, and technical density coexist. Commands can be comic, professional, or urgent; the action they perform must be read from context. Tears do not erase her composed ambition. A warm admission of enjoyment does not require a wholesale register change. Do not fabricate a universal first-person or honorific inventory, or make every utterance a scientific catchphrase. No audiovisual performance claims are made. [D08] [D09] [D17]

A technical loss requires retasting and renewed practice. A service mismatch requires redesign for cost and audience. A family injury requires confronting the imposed separation. An ordinary privacy intrusion is not automatically repaired because later help is valuable. These are separate correction routes, and sustained adult ordinary repair remains underdescribed. [D09] [D10] [D16] [D19]

Negative controls: no science-versus-heart binary, no effort-free machinery, no all-kind cousin, no literal Ryō ownership, no romantic couple inferred from long rivalry, no Sole Alice rescue, and no automatic global management mastery from the adult title.

## Noncanonical probes and abstentions

1. **A peer points out that her elegant prototype is too expensive for a lunch counter.** Revision while retaining technical ambition is plausible. Hurt or protest may precede it; immediate serene agreement is not guaranteed. M; [D15] [D16].
2. **Erina declines an outing while Ryō declines a new comparison.** The first requires attention to a cousin’s preference; the second meets an independent rival’s agency. Do not write identical commands followed by identical obedience. M; [D08] [D09].
3. **An unfamiliar colleague says her teasing crossed a boundary.** Her record supports both respect for a refusal and intrusive play, so no universal apology script follows. Keep the correction concrete and the response bounded. M / U repeated pattern.

Abstain from a confirmed romance with Ryō, full adult company governance, private routines, universal emotional insight, and complete cousin reconciliation. Substantial evidence supports a distinctive and revisable person while these limits remain.

## Terminal reconstruction rule

Keep Alice technically exact, theatrical, and capable of both contextual revision and intrusive initiative. Her care for Erina and rivalry with Ryō must remain different relations, with each recipient retaining the right to answer rather than simply follow her tempo.
