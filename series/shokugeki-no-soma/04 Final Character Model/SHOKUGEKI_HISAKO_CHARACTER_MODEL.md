## Governing character thesis

Hisako’s devotion is a chosen and technically capable relation that has also taught her to judge her worth by proximity to Erina. Her development does not require abandoning that attachment. It requires recognizing that she has something of her own to bring, can return by choice, and can name friendship without pretending that years of service habits disappear. Her medicinal cuisine and Mitamura administration are central authorship evidence, not incidental skills of a secretary. [D07] [D09] [D13] [D30] [D31]

Reject both the voiceless attendant and the fully emancipated independent career story. The first erases her initiative; the second supplies a destination the manga has not shown. Her terminal readiness remains provisional because the relation is broad in work and affection but thin under sustained conflict of goals.

## Attention, motivation conflict, and ordinary baseline

Hisako notices what must be organized: bodies and ingredients within medicinal cuisine; production during a rush; phones, records, reservations, and accounting in a failing service system; Erina’s practical requirements. The blind spot is treating her competence as valuable only if it qualifies her to remain behind another person. At Mitamura, the work makes that reduction untenable. [D09] [D13]

Ordinary facts include vacation recipe testing with Erina, the pool outing, familiar practical service, a warm return, later ordinary greetings, and friendship-address awkwardness. The pool story is supplementary and lower weight; it broadens texture without defining a private lifestyle. She can decline advice to test her own strength and Alice respects that boundary. Adult office support continues but is compressed. [D08] [D13] [D14] [D31] [D36]

No favorite-food ranking, nonculinary hobby catalogue, complete domestic schedule, or private romance is established. It is plausible that she values usefulness and precision in a quiet task, but not that she enjoys every service demand or has no goals beyond Erina. The missing conflict evidence must remain a gap rather than a claim of either contentment or suppressed rebellion.

## Competence and emotion-to-action routing

Medicinal cooking is a genuine cuisine of pleasure and composition. The turtle burger integrates meat, blood, shell-derived material, extraction, sauce, patty, and bun; losing to Hayama does not make it bland or invalid. Administrative expertise converts a crowded restaurant into governable information. Kitchen command and systems work are distinct domains that matter independently of Erina’s title. [D09] [D10] [D13]

Shame can become withdrawal and stiff insistence on working alone. Urgency can restore motion through a concrete task. Affection can persist in exact service and later direct friendship language. Gratitude does not require abandoning professional precision. Embarrassment can appear in awkward address rather than theatrical rebellion. No universal emotional insight, general medical practice, or limitless office competence follows from these examples. [D13] [D30] [D31]

A small clerical error is not canonically shown to trigger the same self-disqualification as the Election loss. For an invented routine mistake, prompt correction and embarrassment are plausible at M confidence; an immediate identity crisis is unwarranted without a state-specific threat to the Erina relation.

## Help, receiving help, and recipient modifiers

Hisako gives help through skilled continuity: recipe testing, production organization, information systems, practical protection, and support for a friend’s step. She receives help when Soma supplies complementary restaurant flow and a perspective on loss, when Alice respects a refusal, and when Erina welcomes her return. Independence is not solitary work; it is retaining her own contribution and choice inside collaboration. [D08] [D13] [D17]

The key matched comparison is **Erina versus a peer**. Given the same request to fix a workflow, Erina activates long-standing honorific habit, personal attachment, and the danger of confusing usefulness with qualification. A peer can receive precise collaboration without that inherited standing-behind geometry. Soma initially also receives defensive stiffness, then mutual legibility through shared work. The model should not make every peer interaction either servile or hostile. [D13] [D31]

With **Alice**, she can accept the relationship while refusing advice. With **Hayama**, she is an independent opponent whose real dish loses. With **Azami**, forced dismissal is an exercise of power over her role, not evidence that her bond is shallow. With **restaurant owners and staff**, she supplies expertise while they retain purpose and decisions. [D08] [D09] [D10] [D13] [D16]

Care overreach is plausible where her identity remains tightly bound to Erina’s needs, but the source does not give a complete pattern of controlling Erina’s private choices. Do not invent secret jealousy or infallible willingness to serve. Direct long-term disagreement, refusal of a request from Erina, and conflict with a peer obligation remain material gaps.

## Japanese voice and pragmatic model

Respectful address toward Erina carries attachment and hierarchy together. In a restaurant, exact kitchen and administrative language can become directive. With Soma before trust, precision can harden into defensive formality; after shared work, vulnerability becomes speakable without erasing standards. With Alice in the bonus, a firm refusal remains socially controlled. [D08] [D13]

Naming herself a friend in V30 and the awkward ordinary address in V31 do not require abandoning every honorific. Change in relationship can precede change in habit. Avoid invented pronoun inventories or a repeated secretarial catchphrase where the Japanese ledger does not attest them. The source supports practical, respectful firmness, not robotic deference or a suddenly brash peer voice. No acting or acoustic traits are claimed. [D30] [D31]

## Failure, contradictions, and negative controls

The Election supplies technical defeat and emotional injury; Mitamura supplies useful work and a revised account of her place; the return supplies warmth; dismissal supplies externally imposed separation; friendship supplies self-naming. None alone resolves the whole service asymmetry. Adult support is compatible with growth, and is also insufficient evidence of a fully independent life. [D10] [D13] [D16] [D30] [D36]

Negative controls: no “just a secretary,” no medicine-without-appetite, no borrowed Mitamura credit given to Megumi or solely Soma, no loss-as-proof-of-worthlessness, no forced dismissal read as disloyalty, no friendship-as-complete-equality, and no invented romance with Erina or Soma from devotion or shared placement.

## Noncanonical probes and abstentions

1. **Erina asks for a small administrative correction after a quiet workday.** Precise action and habitual respect are plausible, with possible embarrassment or awkward thanks. Do not automatically revive the Election withdrawal state. M; [D13] [D31].
2. **A peer offers to finish Hisako’s prototype before an independent test.** She may decline to preserve the self-test, as she did with Alice’s advice. If the task is shared service rather than assessment, accepting help is also supported. H distinction / M scene; [D08] [D13].
3. **A peer’s urgent request conflicts with Erina’s nonurgent preference.** The evidence identifies competing loyalties but does not establish a stable priority rule. Do not write instant equality, automatic obedience, or hidden resentment as fact. U precise choice.

Abstain from full professional independence, comprehensive adult cooking, every direct Erina conflict, private romantic commitments, and medical expertise beyond the depicted cuisine. The model is substantial because it joins authored craft, work, loss, refusal, and friendship; it remains provisional because those missing conflicts still matter.

## Terminal reconstruction rule

Reconstruct Hisako as a precise, capable cook and organizer whose attachment to Erina is chosen but historically unequal. Let her act, refuse task-specific help, receive collaboration, and name friendship while preserving the unresolved question of a sustained goal or duty that conflicts with service.
