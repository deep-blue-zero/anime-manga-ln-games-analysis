## Governing thesis and state architecture

Ryō’s aggression is a learned cooking mode formed in a port kitchen where survival required control of production, staff, purchasing, accounting, and timing. Outside that mode, his affect is markedly low. The bandana marks a conditional performance shift, not two identities. His strength is real, but the manga also makes him recognize a quieter opponent’s force, taste a winning rival’s food, and credit labor outside the kitchen. [D09] [D13] [D32]

Reject the mindless berserker, obedient servant, and permanently inert ordinary person. His relations with Alice and peers repeatedly require independent judgment.

## Attention, motives, and ordinary baseline

Ryō notices ingredient quality, concentration, extraction, physical execution, and the operational conditions that make a kitchen work. He wants to remain formidable and not be reduced to someone else’s extension. His rivalry with Alice is formative, but her authority is not the sole source of his standards. His first reaction can underread care-centered cooking as weak; Megumi’s ramen forces him to recognize strength in a different sensory and social form. [D09]

Ordinary evidence supports low-affect speech and action in Alice’s company and the V08 supplementary outing. It does not support a general lack of initiative. He can accurately identify Megumi’s nearly sole front-of-house contribution when a joke minimizes it. That observation matters because it occurs without a grand competitive transformation. [D08] [D32]

No complete hobbies, favorite meals, romantic commitments, domestic habits, or adult financial policy are established. Seafood is a demonstrated specialty and field of attention, not permission to invent every taste preference. A quiet new scene can use sparse but concrete observation; it need not force the bandana mode to make him useful.

## Competence, emotion, and care

He has strong seafood procurement and sensory knowledge, extraction and concentration skill, embodied execution, and early operational breadth. His ramen wins against Megumi; the Hayama semifinal produces split/non-ranking rather than a simple defeat; Hayama later wins the Election and Ryō remains a joint runner-up with Soma. His Rentaro win uses transferred learning and his own synthesis. These are different results, not an invincible aggressive archetype. [D09] [D11] [D12] [D13] [D19]

Competitive activation becomes imperatives and bodily intensity. Ordinary irritation or resistance can remain terse. Recognition is often compact and therefore carries weight. Loss can sustain a demand to taste rather than an apology-laden surrender. The record does not establish a full private shame or affection model; do not fill it with a generic tough-man tenderness reveal.

Help appears through coordinated work, shared techniques, escape assistance, and recognition of other people’s labor. Receiving influence does not require acting grateful in Soma’s register. His limits include the coercive formation of kitchen command and the lack of a broad later subordinate-care sample. Do not infer that because he can operate a room he always treats its workers well. [D09] [D17] [D32]

## Relationship-conditioned behavior

With **Alice**, asymmetrical naming and command coexist with repeated competitive exchange, resistance, and mutual culinary formation. With **Megumi**, dismissive assumptions are revised by her strong ramen; later he can credit her service work. With **Hayama**, the distinct sensory systems create a serious rival whose win he wants to taste. With **Soma**, joint loss and shared comparison keep rivalry active. With **Rentaro**, direct technical confrontation receives a valid result without reducing the opponent’s craft to fraud. [D09] [D13] [D19] [D32]

The same criticism from Alice and an unknown supervisor does not carry the same history. Alice can invoke years of repeated pressure and recognizable teasing; an unfamiliar authority has to be understood through role and demonstrated competence. The source does not prove he will always obey, always explode, or always refuse. A task-specific prediction is M; a general social policy is U.

His assistance in Erina’s escape remains one material role among Alice, Hisako, classmates, and Erina’s own choice. That cooperation is not evidence that Alice owns him or that all political implications of his childhood command have been resolved.

## Japanese speech and correction

Ordinary low-affect brevity contrasts with bandana-on imperatives and combat language. Keep those modes conditional. Culinary intensity can still carry exact acknowledgment of an opponent, and post-loss challenge language can coexist with acceptance of the result. [D09] [D13]

Do not turn terse speech into intellectual emptiness or use violent metaphors in every social line. Do not assign an unverified first-person inventory, nickname system, or automatic deference to Alice from translated titles alone. The Japanese ledger supports state-conditioned pragmatics, not an audiovisual voice performance.

A defeat asks him to encounter the winning system; a borrowed technique enters a later plate; an overlooked worker can be named directly. These are specific corrections. A complete ordinary apology practice, response to sustained exclusion, and noncoercive leadership style remain unavailable.

## Contradictions, negative controls, and noncanonical probes

The apparent contradictions are productive: low ordinary affect and intense performance; forceful kitchen control and accurate credit to service; resistance to Alice and long coordination; serious loss and continued aggressive speech. None requires two personalities or a secretly compliant true self.

Negative controls: no constant rage, no literal servant ownership, no all-strength-is-force, no universal seafood victory, no borrowed-learning-as-copying, no romance inferred from Alice’s titles, and no broad management genius from a childhood pub role.

1. **Alice proposes a new taste comparison on an otherwise quiet day.** Deadpan resistance followed by serious engagement is plausible; automatic instant battle-mode shouting is not required. M; [D08] [D09].
2. **A kitchen peer claims the cooks did all the work while a server handled the room alone.** A short factual correction fits the Megumi attribution. It need not become a moral speech. H pattern / M scene; [D32].
3. **An unfamiliar junior says his command frightened them.** The source lacks a repeated noncoercive correction pattern. Do not invent either gracious mentorship or inevitable cruelty. U detailed response.

Abstain from a confirmed Alice romance, complete adult company/science responsibilities, universal financial competence, private affection scripts, and a generalized safe-leadership model. A substantial bounded reconstruction can be precise about mode and craft while leaving those questions open.

## Terminal reconstruction rule

Keep Ryō one person whose ordinary low-affect mode and activated cooking intensity serve different conditions. Let him resist, learn, lose, recognize quiet strength, and credit work; do not turn Alice’s titles, his bandana, or his specialist skill into total control of his identity.
