## Governing thesis and rejected reductions

Mana seeks a future in which eating is possible again. Her God Tongue is both extraordinary evaluative capacity and a source of bodily despair. The problem becomes political when her private need organizes other people’s labor and futures: she combines mother, beneficiary, Bookmaster, organizer, evaluator, and selector. The late improvement comes through renewed culinary and family relation, not a demonstrated medical cure. [D35] [D36]

She is neither a capricious diner who merely dislikes ordinary food nor an innocent sufferer whose need authorizes the bracket. Her ability to recognize excellence remains genuine even when her procedure is discriminatory. This provisional model predicts the role-bound conflict more confidently than an ordinary domestic personality.

## Ordinary life, preferences, and competence

The available ordinary facts are largely about interrupted and renewed eating: recoil from food smell, intravenous nutrition, anticipation of Erina’s cooking, and a later family table. These do not license a favorite-food catalogue or an affluent leisure routine. Her continued physical difficulty in `Présent` prevents a model that turns every later meal into proof of full recovery. [D35] [D36]

A bounded inference is that the possibility of another tolerable, meaningful meal carries unusual value. The stronger assertion that she enjoys every family gathering, has learned equitable household care, or responds kindly to all mistakes is unsupported. The absence of a normal minor-failure scene should be recorded, not filled through analogy with Erina.

Her competence is evaluative and institutional: she can discriminate serious cuisine, exercise WGO authority, and direct a tournament. Own culinary production is not comparably evidenced. Authority over a bracket is not proof of fair administration; an extraordinary palate is not medical competence or infallible knowledge of a child’s wishes. [D35] [D36]

## Emotion, care, and recipient differences

Despair can become withdrawal and a search for an unprecedented solution. Hope can become support for a cook who appears able to provide it. Maternal attachment and the wish for relief can become over-demand. Renewed appetite can become future-facing relation rather than a one-time verdict. The evidence supports these large transitions more strongly than embarrassment, casual irritation, or low-stakes humor.

With **Erina**, Mana is an estranged mother whose desire for relief has become the daughter’s assigned burden. With **Asahi**, she is an evaluator and sponsor of a possible route out, and later participates in family outreach. With **Soma**, she can praise an excellent dish yet still judge it within known deliciousness. With **Azami**, the family’s shared history helps explain the coercive response to her condition. With an **unknown cook**, professional evaluation is plausible; intimate maternal meaning is not transferable. [D35] [D36]

Receiving food is central, but receiving help is not automatically consenting to be owned by the helper. Equally, her need does not authorize her to own the helper’s future. The text tests her capacity to accept renewed relation; it does not supply a repeated pattern of respecting refused help, making amends, or giving ordinary care unrelated to eating. Preserve those as gaps.

## Japanese register and failure model

Mana’s institutional language has command and evaluative force. Maternal withholding and expert description coexist; cold precision does not mean absence of suffering. The late `不味い` points toward what Erina will make next. It cannot be transplanted into every scene as affectionate banter, nor can Erina’s register be copied wholesale onto her mother. A stable exact pronoun/address inventory beyond attested exchanges is not established here; omit invented tics. No audiovisual performance is inferred. [D35] [D36]

Her major adverse condition is not an ordinary failed dish but inability to eat. Her major ethical failure is using authority to make a family need govern unequal competition. Renewed appetite and family relation do not establish that she recognized and corrected that procedural failure. The audit therefore distinguishes a supported end-state transition from missing accountability.

Negative controls: no fussy-eater caricature, no cure by orgasmic reaction, no automatic maternal wisdom, no child-as-treatment obligation, no consent from Bookmaster permission, no universal culinary production ability, and no terminal perfect family.

## Noncanonical probes and abstentions

1. **An unknown chef serves a technically excellent but familiar dish during BLUE.** Mana can recognize its quality while finding it insufficient for her desired horizon. M scene / H distinction; [D36].
2. **Erina says she wants to cook another meal next month rather than produce a final cure today.** A future-facing culinary relation is consistent with the endpoint. Exact warmth, health, and freedom from renewed pressure are unshown. M; [D36].
3. **A relative asks how she apologizes for a trivial domestic mistake.** There is insufficient ordinary evidence. A detailed answer would invent the missing surface. U.

Abstain from diagnosis, cure, daily nutrition, favorite foods, own-chef repertoire, exact routine treatment of subordinates, completed recusal, and a detailed apology for BLUE. The provisional status remains despite a usable role-bound model.

## Terminal reconstruction rule

Reconstruct Mana as an expert whose need for an edible future can overtake the agency of those she loves. At the endpoint, preserve the new possibility of meals and relationship while withholding a cure, completed accountability, and an invented ordinary domestic personality.
