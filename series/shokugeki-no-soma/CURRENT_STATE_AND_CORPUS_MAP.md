## V2 character reconstruction target

V2 does not assume every named character will receive a monograph.

Readiness states are:

`background -> tracked -> emerging -> substantial -> monograph_ready -> validated_model`

Promotion depends on coverage across voice, behavior, relationships, ordinary/high-stakes states, emotional activation, competition/failure, authority, craft cognition, counterevidence, and exception conditions — not popularity or raw appearance count.

The full reread is expected to determine which characters genuinely support substantial reconstruction. Soma's V1 model supplies a strong existing baseline; Erina and other major ensemble members are not pre-promoted without V2 evidence.

## Dual-horizon contamination control

Because V1 already analyzed Soma in V01/V03/V08/V13/V19/V25/V30/V36, V2 cannot honestly claim total ignorance of those volumes.

For each overlapping scope:

1. perform the fresh V2 whole-volume reading first;
2. stabilize local-boundary literary/character conclusions;
3. only then consult the matching V1 Soma reading/model;
4. record differences in `06 Evidence and Indexes/SHOKUGEKI_V1_TO_V2_CROSSWALK.md`;
5. do not count V1-known material as a fresh prospective prediction.

V2 prospective validation is therefore described as **procedurally prospective, not epistemically blind**.
