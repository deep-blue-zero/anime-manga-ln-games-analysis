## Purpose

Cumulative source-grounded record of Yukihira Soma's Japanese linguistic voice. This ledger tracks forms and pragmatic functions rather than merely collecting catchphrases.

Current coverage: **V01, V03, V08, V13, V19, V25, V30, V36**.

V01 mainline Chapters 1-5, V03 Chapters 15-21, V08 Chapters 58-65, V13 Chapters 102-109, V19 Chapters 155-163, and V25 Chapters 209-217 are primary within their publication boundaries. `倉瀬さんの日記` is supplementary to V01. ## 1. First person and core self-reference

| Feature | V01 observation | Evidence | Confidence |

`ウチ` is important because Soma often frames culinary identity collectively through Yukihira rather than only as an isolated individual chef.

## 2. Casual masculine morphology

Repeated or diagnostic forms include:

- `～ねー`
- `～じゃねー`
- `～だろ`
- `～よな`
- `～だぜ`
- `～だな`
- `～っしょ`
- contractions such as `食わねー`, `作れねー`, `分かんねー`

**Interpretive note:** This creates a low-ceremony, physically relaxed voice. It must not be equated with low conscientiousness; V01 repeatedly pairs the register with precise technical self-monitoring.

## 3. Semi-polite adaptation

Soma uses `～っす` / related casual-polite forms in contexts where some public/formal accommodation is useful without moving into full deferential keigo.

Examples include:

- public entrance introduction;
- certain exchanges with adults/strangers.

**Current hypothesis:** Soma's register adaptation is functional and shallow: he can mark social context without surrendering his colloquial identity.

**Status:** OPEN — Medium confidence.

## 4. Theatrical cook register

| Form | V01 function | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `おあがりよ` | dish presentation / invitation to eat | recurring cook-to-diner performance marker |
| `御粗末！` | post-result closure | playful inversion after successful service/judgment |
| `薙切試験官どの` | mock-formal address | theatrical rather than genuine subordination |

These should be modeled as **pragmatic performance forms**, not detachable catchphrases.

## 5. Direct information-seeking

Soma often prefers short operative questions:

- what is the actual requirement?
- who is this person?
- what does this term mean?
- what exactly is wrong with the dish?

Diagnostic V01 behavior includes openly saying he does not know and asking to be told.

**Voice implication:** ignorance is often lexicalized directly rather than hidden through hedging.

## 6. Challenge language

V01 challenge speech tends to convert social conflict into a concrete result:

- satisfy the tongue;
- say whether it is good or bad;
- let the dish decide;
- fulfill the stated wager.

He frequently uses direct imperatives or directive forms without much interpersonal cushioning once cooking has become the adjudicative frame.

## 7. Teasing and de-escalation

Soma's teasing can sound provocative without necessarily indicating sustained hostility. With Erina, after provoking a reaction, he can immediately soften into casual joking and tell her to eat slowly. With peers, revolting experimental dishes are used as social play.

**Open question:** determine later whether he reliably reads when teasing has gone too far.

## 8. Public speech

The entrance ceremony demonstrates a mixed register:

- casual-polite self-introduction;
- provocative propositional content;
- little ceremonial humility;
- closing politeness sufficient to mark a public address.

Crucially, he later admits being nervous about delivering it. The linguistic confidence of the speech is therefore not proof of affective ease.

## 9. Textual prosody

V01 repeatedly uses:

- elongated vowels for comic surprise or relaxed speech;
- abrupt short clauses during diagnosis;
- emphatic exclamation in challenge mode;
- quick transitions from comedic looseness to clipped serious directives after the headband is tied or a solution is identified.

Later readings should track whether this "loose → compressed/commanding" shift remains stable under pressure.

## 11. Senior-facing casual politeness

V03 establishes a distinctive pattern that should be preserved in future reconstruction.

Soma can acknowledge hierarchy morphologically while refusing epistemic submission.

Examples:

- `どうぞ 乾先輩 冷めないうちに`
- `あざっす 堂島先輩`
- `納得いかないっすね!!`
- `四宮先輩！すんません！`
- `質問なんすけど…`
- `～適用されるんすよね？`

This is not random inconsistency between polite and rough speech. The recurring structure is:

> **relationship/status marking at the surface + unusually direct propositional content underneath.**

Soma can therefore be confrontational without dropping every politeness marker.

**New claim SV-007:** Soma's casual-polite register can carry strong disagreement rather than functioning only as deference.\
**Status:** OPEN; Medium confidence after V03.

## 12. Dry deflation of theatrical provocation

Takumi's early challenge elicits:

> `そっかよ…！ よかったね おめでとう！`

before Soma tells him to remove his foot.

This is pragmatically different from ordinary teasing. Soma reduces the emotional grandeur of the other person's performance rather than matching it.

**New claim SV-008:** Dry congratulatory/underreactive language is one possible Soma response to overperformed rivalry or posturing.\
**Status:** OPEN; Medium confidence.

## 13. Social perception can be verbalized bluntly

With Mito, Soma notices that her hostile superiority performance conflicts with her visible excitement about the hot-spring trip:

> `よく言うぜ ウキウキで歩いてたくせに`

The line matters for voice modeling because it shows that his bluntness may arise from **accurate observation**, not absence of social perception.

**New claim SV-009:** Soma may directly name the contradiction between a person's performed stance and their observable affect, with little face-saving cushioning.\
**Status:** OPEN; Medium confidence.

## 14. Competition-to-seriousness register shift

Takumi's status as an actual rival is not linguistically granted immediately. After Soma recognizes `現場を知ってる手`, his register shifts to compressed competitive excitement:

- `おもしれー…`
- `かかって来な！`

This suggests that strong rivalry language follows substantive activation rather than every challenge cue.

**New claim SV-010:** Soma's rivalry register is selectively activated by perceived competence.\
**Status:** OPEN; Medium confidence.

## 15. Internal problem-solving voice

The clearest V03 internal self-command is:

> `考えろ… 今 この場所で 俺が作れる 最高の料理は 何だ――？`

This formulation is spatially and temporally constrained: *here*, *now*, *what can I make*. It is a useful generative template for high-pressure internal reasoning.

## 53. V25 is a saturation volume rather than a voice discontinuity

The late-series competitive setting does not produce a new prestige register. Soma continues using the same basic architecture established much earlier:

- `俺` as default first person;
- colloquial contractions;
- shallow `～っす` accommodation toward seniors/professionals;
- direct questions when information is missing;
- blunt semantic content under polite morphology;
- and service-performance markers from Yukihira.

This stability is itself important for reconstruction: later institutional status has not linguistically transformed him into an elite-school persona.

## 54. `～っす` remains the main social accommodation layer

Diagnostic V25 forms include:

- `俺の番っすね`
- `俺にだって あるんすよ`
- `大丈夫すか？`
- `いいこと言うじゃないっすか`
- `分かんなかったですけど`
- `違和感あったんすよね`
- `いいすかね`
- `できて当然っすよ`

The semantic content can remain extremely direct. With a WGO judge he can effectively say that he initially thought she was merely arrogant, while the morphology still carries light social accommodation.

**SV-003 — STRENGTHEN VERY STRONGLY.**\
**SV-007 — STRENGTHEN VERY STRONGLY.**

## 55. High-stakes identity language remains ordinary

Two of V25's most important self-definitions are linguistically simple:

> `積み上げてきた 時間がね`

and:

> `お客相手は 毎日が真剣勝負でしょ`

Neither uses elevated language for craft, vocation, or philosophy. Soma's most important abstractions continue to be expressed through ordinary possessive/temporal/service vocabulary.

**SV-011 — STRENGTHEN VERY STRONGLY.**

## 56. Mechanism explanations move from concrete object to principle

The cup-yakisoba explanation is a highly diagnostic discourse pattern.

Soma begins with a concrete object (`カップ麺`, `カップ焼きそば`), names a functional property (`機能美`), points to observable variation in seasoning/materials, recalls his own experiments, and then connects the mechanism to the present dish.

Voice/model implication:

> **Soma is more likely to explain an abstract culinary idea through a handled example and an operational chain than through generalized culinary theory first.**

**New claim SV-020 — concrete-object explanation chain. Confidence: HIGH within cooking explanation.**

## 57. Self-correction is linguistically cheap

When Isshiki overstates the completeness of Soma's foresight, Soma immediately narrows the claim:

> `温度が原因だったとは 分かんなかったですけど`

He then says only that he noticed `なんか違和感` and that the smell did not arrive as strongly as remembered.

This is important for generative voice. Soma does not need to preserve the flattering version of the story. He can casually say what he **didn't** know and then explain what he did perceive.

**New claim SV-021 — low-cost epistemic self-correction. Confidence: HIGH.**

This strengthens the older “ignorance is cheap” pattern from V01/V13.

## 58. `なんか` and sensory approximation are analytically meaningful

The phrase:

> `なんか匂いがグッとこねーなぁって`

shows a characteristic tolerance for approximate sensory language when precise causal terminology is unavailable.

He does not wait until he has a formal technical label before treating the sensation as useful information.

For simulation, avoid making every Soma explanation sound like a textbook chef's lecture. His cognition may be exact in **function** while his verbal description remains colloquial and phenomenological.

**New claim SV-022 — approximate sensory phrasing can carry actionable certainty. Confidence: Medium-High.**

## 59. Service formulas survive tournament escalation

Soma continues to present with:

> `おあがりよ`

and ends the adjudicated match with:

> `御粗末!!`

These are now extremely stable markers across multiple sampled eras.

**SV-005 — STRENGTHEN to HIGH longitudinal confidence.**

## 60. Prestige surprise does not produce prestige performance

On discovering the WGO judges' status, Soma reacts with surprise and admits that he may simply not know the organization. The discovery does not cause a deep register shift.

His initial address to a judge is:

> `あの おねーさん 大丈夫すか？`

while he is cooking.

Later:

> `偉そうなだけの人かと思ったら いいこと言うじゃないっすか`

This is a strong late-series confirmation that formal prestige can change his **information state** without deeply changing his **social voice**.

## 61. Post-victory rival language remains invitational

After defeating Nene, Soma does not deliver a superiority speech. He asks:

> `俺にも先輩のそば 食わせてもらって いいすかね`

and adds:

> `俺のそばも どんどん食べて いいすから！`

The pragmatic function is reciprocal exchange. The senior marker remains, but victory does not become a linguistic dominance claim.

**SV-012 — STRENGTHEN.**

## 62. Group warmth is often packaged as teasing

After the rebel wins, Soma easily high-fives Takumi and Megumi. He tries to pull Erina into the ritual through playful elongated speech:

> `あれあれあれ～～？ 薙切っちは しないのお？`

This fits a broader pattern in which relational intent can be warmer than the surface teasing format.

**New claim SV-023 — playful ritual invitation as low-ceremony affiliation. Confidence: MEDIUM.**

## 70. Status does not formalize the endpoint voice

By `Le dessert 3 Futur`, Soma is 25, a former Totsuki First Seat, and internationally sought after. None of this produces an aristocratic or deeply polite persona. He returns to Yukihira and addresses Erina in the same familiar low-ceremony register, immediately talks about what he has learned, and frames the encounter through the old challenge of making her acknowledge his food.

**Locators:** `V36 / Le dessert 3 Futur / 36-232 to 36-235`.

**Final voice rule:**

> Do not model mature Soma as becoming linguistically formal merely because he becomes prestigious. His maturation is expressed much more through diagnostic range, technical vocabulary when needed, and relationship-specific precision than through ceremonial register elevation.

## 71. Serious emotional content remains linguistically plain

V36 gives emotionally weighty material—Tamako, Joichiro, Asahi's belonging, Erina's burden, and the future attachment to a privileged recipient—without changing Soma into an abstract or lyrical speaker. Even when the narrative around him becomes highly symbolic, his own speech remains concrete: cook this, taste this, this is boring, who do you want to feed, I will make you say it is good.

This strengthens the long-running distinction:

**plain diction is not shallow affect.**

## 72. Erina-specific endpoint register

The mature Erina register combines:

- familiar surname-address / direct peer address;

**Representative locators:** `V36 / Ch313-Ch315 / 36-099 to 36-145`; `Le dessert 3 / 36-232 to 36-242`.

**Locators:** `V36 / Le dessert 3 / 36-237 to 36-242`.
