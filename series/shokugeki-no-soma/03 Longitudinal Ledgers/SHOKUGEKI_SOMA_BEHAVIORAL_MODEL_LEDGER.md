- **HIGH**: repeated within multiple V01 contexts or exceptionally direct evidence; still longitudinally provisional.
- **MEDIUM**: meaningful repeated evidence but context may be narrower than it looks.
- **LOW**: plausible pattern with too few independent observations.
- **OPEN**: interpretive question explicitly retained.

# Domain summaries after V01

## Competition and status

- Competition is comfortable even with repeated defeat when the standard is accepted.
- Rank/pedigree has little automatic authority.
- Demonstrated skill and customer-facing proof matter more.
- Totsuki's difficulty can motivate him once linked to a meaningful benchmark.

## Learning and curiosity

- openly asks when he does not know;
- reads recipes/books;
- transfers underlying mechanisms rather than copying surface forms;
- tests alternatives empirically.

## Risk and uncertainty

- willing to accept severe stakes when his cooking can adjudicate them;
- uncertainty creates visible fear before he has a model of the problem;
- actionability appears to reduce distress.

## Hierarchy and authority

- institutionally irreverent;
- authority must survive contact with competence/evidence;
- no evidence yet that he rejects expertise itself.

## Care and social support

- likely action-mediated in kitchen context;
- keeps anxious partner useful rather than replacing her;
- evidence outside cooking remains insufficient.

## Conflict

- often converts conflict into challenge/wager;
- can be teasing rather than vindictive after victory;
- will enforce agreed conditions.

## Failure

- honest losses appear metabolizable;
- dishonest/opaque negative evaluation is more destabilizing.

## Emotional activation

Strong V01 triggers:

- threat to Yukihira;
- loss of father-rival pathway;
- invalid judgment of cooking;
- unfamiliar institutional scale before he can act on it.

## Cooking cognition

Provisional process:

`observe/taste -> define function -> inventory constraints -> retrieve prior knowledge -> modify -> serve -> use response as feedback`

## 4. Domain summaries after V03

### Competition and status

- A challenge does not automatically create a rival.
- Demonstrated technique, service history, and procedural control can make a rival interesting very quickly.
- Soma appears less invested than Takumi in immediate formal ranking closure.
- Fair-loss behavior remains insufficiently tested.

### Learning and perception

- Soma reads expertise from how people work, not only from what they claim.
- Totsuki begins to expand his horizon because it exposes him to serious cooks he would not meet at Yukihira.
- He can recruit someone else's knowledge without needing to subsume it under his own expertise.

### Risk and uncertainty

- Fifteen minutes becomes psychologically ample once translated into diner service time.
- He will risk his own standing when an authority decision against a peer seems illegitimate.

### Hierarchy and authority

- Status is acknowledged linguistically (`先輩`, `～っす`) but not treated as epistemic surrender.
- He challenges Shinomiya using the logic of cooking conditions and then the institution's own Shokugeki mechanism.

### Care and social support

- notices Megumi's tension;
- makes her expertise useful;
- gives executable tasks under pressure;
- explicitly names her contribution when she discounts herself;
- moves into overt protection when the threat comes from an external authority.

### Social perception

V03 rejects the easy equation `blunt = socially unaware`.

Soma can detect:

- Takumi's awkwardness;
- Mito's concealed excitement;
- Megumi's sustained tension;
- Takumi's real professional background from workflow.

His social roughness may reflect **what he chooses to prioritize**, not what he fails to see.

### Cooking cognition

Current process is better represented as:

`observe conditions -> identify operative function -> read available people/resources -> clarify rules -> allocate tasks -> improvise -> serve -> use embodied response/result as evidence`

The addition after V03 is **people as expertise-bearing resources without reducing them to tools**: Soma can identify what Megumi knows, trust it, and later credit it as hers.

## 5. Frozen predictions for V08

1. **Credible competitor activation:** public reputation alone should not be enough; demonstrated cooking should matter more.
2. **Fair adverse result:** if a genuine fair loss/inferiority occurs, Soma should investigate/adapt rather than deny.
3. **Procedural reading:** he should continue inferring strength from process and choices rather than only credentials.
4. **Pressure translation:** time/resource stress should be converted to operational constraints rather than sustained panic.
5. **Conditional care:** peer anxiety -> useful participation/concrete credit; unfair external threat -> direct advocacy.
6. **Face management:** he should show awareness of posturing/embarrassment even if he punctures it.
7. **School as epistemic environment:** competition should increasingly represent access to techniques/people/problems, not just ranking.
8. **Culinary adjudication:** serious disagreement should be pulled toward concrete cooking evidence where possible.

These predictions are frozen prospectively before V08 close reading.

## 3. Domain summaries after V08

### Competition and status

- Soma explicitly wants to win and can expect very high performance from himself.
- A fair one-point deficit is acknowledged as real rather than reframed away.
- Competitive pain becomes productive when it reveals a credible knowledge gap.
- He does not need to deny an opponent's excellence to preserve confidence.
- Reputation and pedigree remain weak compared with demonstrated culinary effect.

### Learning and curiosity

The V08 post-Hayama sequence provides the clearest current learning rule:

`credible gap -> identify unknown domain -> seek broader exposure -> acquire more tools -> recombine them into future dishes`

The desired exposure explicitly includes `食材`, `調理法`, and `現場`.

### Risk, uncertainty, and pressure

- formal competitive spectacle does not obviously destabilize him;
- no V08 scene replaces V03 as the strongest acute time-pressure test;
- confidence should still not be modeled as absence of disappointment or uncertainty.

### Hierarchy and authority

- no new authority dispute materially changes V03;
- Alice's elite background does not induce deference;
- expert technique itself does attract attention and praise.

### Care and social support

Current conditional model:

- **execution uncertainty:** create an actionable role;
- **competence denial/self-minimization:** name the person's concrete contribution or effect;
- **ordinary reluctance/pride:** challenge or tease may mobilize action;
- **shame/fear of ridicule:** default teasing can misfire; once visible, lower stakes and absorb consequence;
- **unfair external threat:** direct advocacy remains available from V03.

### Social cognition

Do not model Soma as generally oblivious.

Evidence now supports:

- recognition of posturing and awkwardness;
- deliberate use of provocation;
- self-conception as reasonably capable at conversation;
- recognition of Megumi's changed/resolute demeanor;
- a narrower weakness around shame-based vulnerability and face-sensitive reassurance.

### Failure

V08 substantially strengthens a two-part rule:

1. **credible failure/adverse comparison:** information about where to grow;
2. **opaque or evidence-inconsistent judgment:** likely to provoke challenge/confusion.

### Cooking cognition

Expanded provisional process:

`observe/taste -> define task/category function -> model eater/use-context -> inventory constraints -> retrieve prior knowledge -> modify/recombine -> account for storage/time/transport -> serve -> use response/result as feedback`

This is broader than V01's ingredient-substitution model and V03's service-time model.
