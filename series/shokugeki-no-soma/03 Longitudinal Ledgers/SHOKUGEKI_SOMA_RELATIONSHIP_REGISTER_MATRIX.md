# Relationship states

## Joichiro — V01 state

**Function:** father + benchmark + trainer + rival.

Soma does not need to reduce Joichiro to preserve self-esteem. He can acknowledge hundreds of losses and still structure his future around surpassing him.

**Open test:** whether separation from Joichiro changes the rivalry from concrete daily competition into a more abstract identity standard.

## Erina — V01 state

**Function:** elite palate/judge whose social hierarchy Soma does not automatically accept.

The first relationship is organized around a mismatch:

- Erina claims evaluative authority through status and palate;
- Soma insists sensory response should be stated honestly and should answer the dish.

**Do not import:** later affection, trust, rivalry development, or romantic interpretation.

## Megumi — V01 state

**Function:** first meaningful peer collaborator at Totsuki.

Soma treats her as capable of contributing under pressure even while she is panicking. He does not yet display extensive personal knowledge of her.

**Open test:** whether later care becomes more verbally/emotionally explicit or remains primarily practical.

# Construction freeze: relationship model through V25

## High-confidence relationship rules

1. Competitive results do not automatically create interpersonal hierarchy.
2. A rival/opponent can remain worth tasting and learning from whether Soma wins or loses.
3. Seniority tends to produce shallow register accommodation, not deep submission.
4. Demonstrated competence matters more to relational attention than title alone.
5. With peers, warmth is often enacted through food, concrete praise, teasing, high-fives, and practical participation rather than elaborate declarations.
6. Once another person's shame/problem becomes legible, Soma can become more tactful than his blunt baseline suggests.
7. Erina is a particularly strong site for teasing-as-inclusion rather than explicit relational naming.

## Lower-confidence / OPEN relationship dimensions

- formal leadership of a genuinely interdependent cooking team;
- accepting peer correction when it directly threatens his assigned team role;
- collective-scale care/agency restoration;
- division of authorship across several cooks producing one dish.

# V30 holdout relationship update

## Erina — MAJOR STRENGTHEN / REVISE

V30 is the first sampled volume to place Soma and Erina inside **genuinely interdependent culinary production** rather than parallel rivalry, ordinary peer interaction, or group affiliation.

### Register

The relationship remains strongly low-ceremony:

- casual peer address;
- complaints and challenge language;
- direct criticism;
- teasing rather than status cushioning;
- compressed task responses when one temporarily directs the other.

Greater trust does **not** produce a general shift into polite or sentimental language.

### Relationship mechanism: agonistic trust

The most diagnostic Soma formulation is:

> `こーゆう方が 俺ららしいだろ？`

**Locator:** `V30 / Ch259 / 30-113 to 30-114`.

The antecedent is their conflict-heavy preparation and the current situation in which Soma has deliberately challenged Erina to produce a stronger dish. He identifies this frictional mode as characteristic of `俺ら`.

Current formulation:

> **Soma-Erina trust can be expressed through confidence that the other person will answer provocation with authorship rather than withdrawal. Their relationship does not require rivalry to disappear in order for cooperation to become deep.**

### Bidirectional competence use

Soma repeatedly asks Erina to taste/calibrate his components. He accepts her corrections immediately and uses the God Tongue as a high-precision instrument.

**Locator:** `V30 / Ch257 / 30-079 to 30-083`.

Yet he adds an independent final arrangement. Erina therefore influences the dish without becoming its author.

Later the direction reverses. Soma challenges the inadequacy of Erina's existing plates; Erina discards the plan and authors a new main; then she orders Soma to become her hands and feet. He accepts immediately and executes.

**Locator:** `V30 / Ch258-259 / 30-103 to 30-117`.

This creates a reciprocal relationship structure:

> correct me when your competence is useful -> let me challenge your ceiling -> take ownership when the problem becomes yours -> I will support your execution.

### Competition is internal to cooperation

Soma explicitly reframes the shared final as:

> `俺とお前の食戟`

**Locator:** `V30 / Ch258 / 30-103 to 30-106`.

This is not an attempt to sabotage shared victory. He states first that he desperately wants the rebels to win. The internal contest is a method for forcing both dishes toward decisive quality.

### Post-result register

After the rebel victory, Soma's interaction with Erina remains teasing. He tells her that since she has already copied various things from him, she should also use the closing phrase. Erina ultimately delivers `御粗末!!`.

**Locator:** `V30 / Ch262 / 30-183 to 30-185`.

Victory does not establish a permanent superior/subordinate relationship between them.

### Current Erina relationship function after V30

**rival + high-trust culinary peer + sensory calibrator + collaborator + mutually provocative authorship catalyst**.

**Confidence:** High within the sampled V30 state.

**Do not overread:** V30 does not by itself establish a generalized romantic register. The strongest source-grounded result is culinary/relational interdependence expressed through rivalry, correction, reciprocal influence, and dynamic task hierarchy.
