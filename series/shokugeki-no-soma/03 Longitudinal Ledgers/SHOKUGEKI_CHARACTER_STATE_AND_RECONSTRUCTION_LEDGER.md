## V02 integration - contextual excellence, ordinary competence, and post-competition continuity

### Yukihira Soma

V02 strengthens rather than replaces the V01 model.

- `SH-CHAR-SOMA-003` STRENGTHEN: after learning that Isshiki is Seventh Seat, Soma responds to demonstrated cooking skill rather than title alone. He praises the spring dish and turns rank into a future challenge.
- `SH-CHAR-SOMA-005` OPEN / MEDIUM-HIGH: victory does not automatically terminate epistemic interest in a skilled opponent. After defeating Mito, Soma immediately asks to taste her donburi.
- Customer-facing service remains an affect benchmark: before the official Shokugeki, Soma explicitly treats real restaurant service under the Yukihira name as more frightening than the arena.
- Affordability becomes a first-class constraint in the Mito problem. Soma refuses to defend donburi by merely substituting another luxury beef product.
- Post-win teasing of Mito (`Nikumi`) adds a relationship-specific register: rivalry can normalize into casual contact without ceremonial winner/loser distance.

Counterconditions remain live: V02 still does not supply a clean non-family fair loss for Soma, and the Isshiki exchange is not scored as one.

### Tadokoro Megumi

`SH-CHAR-MEGUMI-001` REVISE / SPECIFY.

V02 demonstrates independent ordinary-state competence that V01's evaluation crisis obscured. Megumi's three onigiri show coherent preservation, seasoning, pairing, and recipient-sensitive design. Isshiki identifies a calming quality in her cooking; Soma explicitly wonders why she cannot reproduce this level in class. Megumi states that intense nervousness makes her mind go blank and causes mistakes.

Current formulation:

> Megumi already possesses meaningful craft competence and recipient sensitivity; high evaluative threat appears to disrupt reliable access to that competence.

New claim `SH-CHAR-MEGUMI-002` OPEN / HIGH within V02: recipient-calming, home-derived cooking is an independent strength rather than merely an adjunct to Soma's plans.

### Isshiki Satoshi

New claim `SH-CHAR-ISSHIKI-001` OPEN / HIGH within V02.

Isshiki combines Seventh Seat authority with unusually low-ceremony social behavior, refined cooking, dorm caretaking, institutional explanation, and direct agricultural labor. He welcomes Soma's challenge rather than defending rank through distance. Current model is intentionally provisional because only one volume has tested him.

### Mito Ikumi

New claim `SH-CHAR-MITO-001` OPEN / HIGH within V02: meat expertise is genuine while ingredient-supremacy ideology narrows contextual composition. Her A5 handling, temperature control, and embodied meat knowledge are treated as real competence.

New claim `SH-CHAR-MITO-002` OPEN / MEDIUM: fair defeat can produce visible emotional destabilization without immediate withdrawal from culinary or social participation. She honors the Shokugeki condition and enters the Donburi Research Society while retaining pride and irritation.

Her childhood flashback opens, but does not prove, a possible conditioning relationship between family demands for strength and her adult hardness/value hierarchy.

### Nakiri Erina

V02 adds institutional behavior rather than a new palate test. Erina appears to use Mito as an agent in the Donburi Research Society conflict and orders withdrawal of a cooking room previously granted to Mito after the loss. This supports a provisional patronage/resource model but does not justify a general rule that she abandons every subordinate who fails.
