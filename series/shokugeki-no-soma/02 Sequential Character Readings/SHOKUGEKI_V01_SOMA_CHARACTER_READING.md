# SHOKUGEKI_V01_SOMA_CHARACTER_READING

## 1. Purpose and evidentiary boundary

This is the first sequential reading in the Yukihira Soma character-modeling project. Its purpose is not to summarize Volume 1 as a plot object. It establishes the earliest source-grounded model of:

- Soma's Japanese linguistic voice;
- behavioral defaults and exception conditions;
- cooking cognition;
- relation-specific register;
- emotional activation points;
- responses to hierarchy, uncertainty, failure, and conflict;
- and claims that later sampled volumes must attempt to falsify.

The governing rule for this document is intentionally prospective: **later knowledge is not used to make early Soma more coherent than Volume 1 itself makes him.** V03 and later samples may preserve, strengthen, revise, downgrade, or reject the hypotheses below.

### Primary canonical evidence

Mainline Chapters 1-5:

1. `1-1 果て無き荒野`
2. `1-2 神の舌`
3. `1-3 「化ける」ふりかけ`
4. `1-4 魔王、玉座を語る`
5. `1-5 その料理人は笑わない`

### Supplementary evidence

The short bonus `番外編「倉瀬さんの日記」` is used conservatively because it depicts Soma in the same middle-school/Yukihira social environment as the opening mainline material.

### Excluded from the canonical model

The `特別読切 食戟のソーマ` included later in the tankobon is an earlier prototype/one-shot version of the premise. It is preserved as publication-history material but is **not mixed into the canonical serialized Soma model**. Its dialogue, characterization, or relationship configurations must not be used to resolve ambiguities in the mainline character.

# 2. Executive baseline

Volume 1 does not support the shallow formulation that Soma is simply an arrogant prodigy who is too confident to feel fear.

A better initial model is:

> **Soma is a working-cook identity organized around iterative failure, customer-facing proof, practical improvisation, and a highly informal social style. He can be startled, anxious, angry, or nervous, but once a situation becomes an actionable cooking problem he tends to convert affect into experimentation, challenge, or service. Status has little automatic authority over him; demonstrated competence, sensory truth, and the obligation to put a dish in front of someone matter much more.**

Several features that look contradictory on the surface are already compatible once their conditions are separated.

He can proclaim that the entire Totsuki cohort is merely a stepping stone while privately admitting that the speech itself made him nervous. He can accept his 489th loss to Joichiro without excuse while becoming baffled by Erina's dishonest rejection of a dish he correctly reads as successful. He can treat prestigious culinary education as a waste of money, then immediately accept Totsuki as a serious proving ground once Joichiro makes survival there relevant to surpassing him. He can make highly refined food for customers and deliberately make revolting experimental food for friends.

The strongest Volume-1 distinction is therefore not confidence versus insecurity. It is **actionable versus non-actionable pressure**.

When the problem has an operational form—cook this, satisfy this diner, rescue this dish, make something from what remains—Soma becomes unusually stable. When the problem removes his chosen field of action—Yukihira closing, an apparently arbitrary institutional judgment, the scale of Totsuki before he understands it—he can be visibly disoriented.

That distinction should be tested aggressively in later volumes.

# 3. Baseline before Totsuki: defeat without humiliation

The first important fact about Soma's competitive psychology is that the story introduces him as someone who **loses constantly**.

After another cooking defeat by Joichiro, Soma does not deny the outcome or invent an excuse. He acknowledges that he lost that day. When teased that he must be approaching five hundred losses, his correction is comically precise: it is only the 489th.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-011`

### Observed fact — AC / JT

- Soma accepts Joichiro's judgment of the result.
- He keeps an implicit or explicit running score of repeated defeats.
- The emotional register is irritated/playful rivalry, not shame or withdrawal.

### Initial inference

Soma does not appear to need an undefeated self-image in order to remain competitive. Losing to a person he accepts as a valid judge and superior cook is compatible with continuing the rivalry.

### Important limit

This is **not yet evidence that Soma accepts every adverse verdict calmly**. Erina later supplies the counterexample: he is deeply confused by a verdict that contradicts what he believes the sensory evidence showed. The distinction may be between **credible defeat** and **opaque or dishonest evaluation**, not between winning and losing per se.

**Claim status after V01:** OPEN, medium-high confidence.

# 4. Experimentation: failure is material, not contamination

The Yukihira opening establishes an unusual relationship between competence and failure. Soma and Joichiro are not only willing to fail; they sometimes deliberately make food that they already know may be terrible and inflict it on familiar people as a joke or experiment.

Soma's grotesque combinations are therefore not evidence that he cannot tell good food from bad food. The humor depends on the opposite: he often knows exactly how bad the experiment is.

The more important cognitive pattern appears in two forms.

## 4.1 Failed dishes remain useful

Soma's later successful techniques explicitly emerge from earlier failures. The potato-and-bacon pseudo-roast in Chapter 1 is connected to experimentation with failed potato-salad work, while the honey technique in Chapter 5 grows from reading about pineapple enzymes and then testing accessible substitutes.

### Initial inference

For Soma, a failed result does not automatically invalidate the attempt. A bad dish can reveal a property, technique, combination, or constraint that becomes useful later.

This gives his experimentation a distinctly **iterative** rather than romantic form. He is not merely creative because he has flashes of genius. He accumulates reusable information from unsuccessful trials.

## 4.2 Practicality matters to invention

In the honey example, he does not stop at remembering that pineapple can tenderize meat. He reasons from the underlying function, searches for alternatives, and evaluates them partly through mundane operational criteria: honey keeps well and is easy to have around.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-5 / CBZ 01-146`

This is a major clue about his cognition:

> **Soma's innovation is constrained by service reality.**

He is interested in what works with ingredients a working kitchen can actually keep, obtain, and deploy—not merely what is theoretically elegant.

**Claim status:** OPEN, high confidence within V01.

# 5. Casual surface, serious internal audit

Soma's casual demeanor should not be mistaken for a casual standard of work.

After normal service at Yukihira, he privately thinks that his fried rice should have received three more wok movements. The detail is small but disproportionately valuable because no rival, teacher, or audience is forcing the evaluation.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-016`

### Observed fact — CC / AC

Soma spontaneously performs fine-grained post-service self-evaluation.

### Initial inference

His strongest standards may be internally procedural rather than socially ceremonial. He can sound loose, use slang, and treat elite etiquette irreverently while still tracking technique in granular detail.

This prevents a common misread:

> casual speech ≠ casual craftsmanship.

The same page ties his desire to finish middle school to the possibility of spending more time training. At the beginning of the volume his near-term ambition is not elite education. It is more time in the restaurant and eventual ability to surpass Joichiro.

**Claim status:** OPEN, medium-high confidence.

# 6. Yukihira as identity, laboratory, home, and standard

Before Totsuki, Soma's ambition has a concrete local object:

- surpass Joichiro;
- become good enough to carry Yukihira;
- continue cooking in the place where his competence was formed.

This matters because his later declaration that he will take the top of Totsuki can otherwise be misread as evidence that prestige was always his natural objective.

It was not.

When Joichiro abruptly closes the restaurant, Soma is more emotionally destabilized than he was by the property developer's threats or by personal insults. He protests that he intended to remain there and surpass his father there.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-045 to 01-047`

### Initial model

Yukihira is not simply a family business in Soma's background. It is the environment through which he presently understands:

- training;
- succession;
- competition;
- customer contact;
- experimentation;
- and his route toward adulthood.

When the environment disappears, he temporarily loses the map by which his ambition was organized.

This is one of the clearest cases where Volume 1 contradicts the idea of an emotionally unshakeable Soma.

# 7. The property confrontation: anger becomes culinary adjudication

The property-development conflict gives the first strong behavioral template for Soma under hostile pressure.

He does not respond to sabotage by turning the conflict primarily into a physical or rhetorical confrontation. Once the opponent's demand can be converted into a food problem, he defines a culinary wager: if he can satisfy her tongue, she must leave the restaurant alone.

**Key locators:**

- `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-019 to 01-020`
- `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-027`

The important behavior is not merely "Soma likes challenges." It is more specific:

> **He transforms contested authority into an outcome that cooking can adjudicate.**

He also sets very high stakes on himself. Earlier he says that if he cannot supply what a customer wants, he will accept closing the restaurant. This may suggest high risk tolerance, but the hypothesis needs later testing because in V01 he is principally staking **his own identity/future and the family restaurant**, not making decisions that impose severe costs on vulnerable third parties.

## 7.1 Constraint-solving rather than literal compliance

When the meat stock has been destroyed, he does not interpret "make me meat" as a requirement to possess the obvious ingredient. He inventories what remains and reconstructs the desired sensory experience through potato and bacon.

The cognition appears to be:

1. identify what the diner is actually seeking;
2. identify surviving resources;
3. build a functional substitute;
4. make the opponent's own appetite verify the result.

This is an early example of **problem reframing**.

## 7.2 Victory does not make him conciliatory

After she clearly wants more, Soma withholds the plate until she honors the condition of the wager. He is playful, but he does not dissolve the boundary merely because the opponent has been defeated.

This suggests that his friendliness should not automatically be equated with softness or conflict avoidance.

# 8. Fear exists; status alone does not govern him

Totsuki initially unsettles Soma.

He thinks paying money to learn cooking sounds foolish. He is surprised to learn there is an entrance examination. He becomes visibly anxious when he sees the scale of failure and hears that the school is an elite institution with a very low graduation rate.

**Locators:** `V01 / 1-1 / CBZ 01-049 to 01-052`

This is useful counterevidence against "fearless Soma."

But the fear changes quickly once Joichiro gives the institution a personally meaningful criterion:

> If Soma cannot survive there, surpassing Joichiro is laughable.

Soma's response is immediate acceptance of the challenge.

### Initial interpretation

Institutional prestige is not intrinsically motivating to him. It matters when translated into a test relevant to a standard he already cares about.

That distinction is reinforced during the transfer examination. Candidates discuss prestigious restaurants and family lineages; Soma simply identifies Yukihira as a small neighborhood diner. He does not inflate it or show shame.

When another candidate's insult targets Soma personally as a low-status commoner, Soma absorbs it with comparatively little escalation. When the insult targets **Yukihira's quality**, he becomes directly angry: the speaker has never eaten there and therefore has no basis to determine its rank.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-2 / CBZ 01-069 to 01-072`

This may be one of the most important early distinctions:

> **Soma is less sensitive to status insult than to epistemically illegitimate judgment about cooking.**

That claim remains provisional until later volumes test it.

---

# 9. Ignorance is not a face threat

Soma repeatedly admits when he does not know something.

When the other candidates invoke Erina's status, he does not bluff. His response is essentially: he does not know who she is; tell him.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-2 / CBZ 01-076`

When the class is later assigned `boeuf bourguignon`, he initially does not know the term. Once he understands the functional structure, he reduces it to something like a beef-sinew stew and consults the recipe.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-5 / CBZ 01-139`

### Initial inference

Soma does not appear strongly motivated to conceal gaps in declarative knowledge. His self-image is sufficiently procedural that "I don't know that term/person" is not automatically humiliating.

This should be tested later against domains in which he **does** claim expertise or against ignorance revealed publicly by a rival.

**Claim status:** OPEN, medium confidence.

---

# 10. Erina: social hierarchy versus sensory truth

Erina is the best V01 test of Soma's relationship to elite authority because she combines:

- formal institutional power;
- inherited prestige;
- recognized sensory expertise;
- and a personal tendency to use all three hierarchically.

Soma's behavior toward her is strikingly under-deferential even after he learns who she is.

He asks what the rules are. When she asks whether he really intends to take the test, he reduces the problem to its operative criterion: he only has to make her say the food is good, correct? Then of course he will take it.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-2 / CBZ 01-079 to 01-082`

His register can even become playfully theatrical: `薙切試験官どの` is far more performative than genuinely deferential.

## 10.1 He trusts the reaction more than the status declaration

During the furikake-rice sequence, Soma observes Erina's appetite and bodily response. When she ultimately declares the dish bad, he does not immediately internalize her verdict as authoritative truth. He is stunned because the declared judgment conflicts with the evidence he believes he just observed.

**Locators:**

- `V01 / 1-3 / CBZ 01-096 to 01-106`
- `V01 / 1-3 / CBZ 01-108 to 01-109`

Later, after receiving an acceptance notice, he tells her directly that if she thought it was good she should simply have said so.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-4 / CBZ 01-124`

This is the strongest qualification to the earlier "accepts defeat" hypothesis.

Soma accepts Joichiro's repeated victories because he appears to accept the contest and judgment as real. Erina's rejection feels different because Soma thinks her own response falsifies the verdict.

Possible general rule:

> **Soma can tolerate losing more easily than he tolerates an evaluation he believes is disconnected from observable reality.**

V03-V13 should test this aggressively.

# 11. What "Yukihira cooking" means in V01

Soma's explicit formulation during the entrance test is unusually useful.

After turning an ordinary furikake rice into something Erina cannot stop eating, he defines Yukihira cooking around the ability to transform an apparently ordinary menu through ingenuity.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-3 / CBZ 01-101`

The surrounding confrontation clarifies what he rejects about Erina's hierarchy. He concedes that Yukihira is a small diner and that the elite world may genuinely eat more expensive food. His counterargument is not that class distinctions are imaginary. It is that sitting above others does not automatically produce everything that can be made through actual cooking.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-3 / CBZ 01-105 to 01-106`

This is an important difference from generic anti-elitism.

Soma's argument is fundamentally **productive and epistemic**:

- What can you make?
- What can you make someone want to keep eating?
- What have you learned by working?
- Can your judgment survive direct encounter with the dish?

He is not yet offering a developed social philosophy about class. The safest V01 reading is that **pedigree has weak authority when detached from production and sensory proof**.

---

# 12. The entrance speech: bravado with disclosed nervousness

Soma's opening-ceremony speech is often easy to remember as pure arrogance. The immediate aftermath makes that reading incomplete.

He introduces himself in casual semi-polite Japanese, says he unexpectedly transferred in, declares that he does not intend to lose to people who have never stood in front of customers, and says the school is only a stepping stone because he intends to take the top.

**Locators:** `V01 / 1-4 / CBZ 01-121 to 01-122`

The content is genuinely provocative. Nothing in the text suggests he did not mean it.

But afterward, he admits that he was nervous because he has never done something like that and asks how he came across.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-4 / CBZ 01-123`

### Behavioral consequence

Confidence in the proposition and comfort with public performance are separate variables.

This gives us a useful anti-caricature rule:

> **Soma may sincerely make an audacious claim while still experiencing ordinary performance anxiety about delivering it.**

His bravado should therefore not be modeled as permanent emotional invulnerability.

---

# 13. Totsuki as instrument rather than sacred institution

At the start of the volume Soma wants to surpass Joichiro and inherit/carry Yukihira. Totsuki is imposed on him by Joichiro's decision.

Once he accepts the challenge, however, he does not respond halfheartedly. He converts the institution into a new competitive metric and announces his intent to take the top.

The best V01 formulation is therefore:

> **Totsuki is initially instrumentalized into Soma's pre-existing ambition.**

He does not yet appear to desire Totsuki's prestige for its own sake. The school becomes valuable because conquering its standards becomes evidence relevant to surpassing Joichiro and proving himself as a cook.

Later volumes may substantially revise this as Totsuki becomes a social world rather than a mere obstacle.

# 14. Megumi: action-based stabilization rather than verbal consolation

Megumi provides the first clean example of Soma interacting with a frightened peer whose stakes differ from his own.

He notices her nervous ritual and asks about it directly. He does not initially understand the E-grade system and simply asks what it is. When she explains that one more E means expulsion, he does not produce a long reassurance speech.

During the exercise his affect remains strikingly relaxed. He explores the pantry, comments on the quantity of seasonings, and treats the unfamiliar French dish as a solvable problem.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-5 / CBZ 01-135 to 01-139`

When classmates sabotage their stew with salt, Megumi immediately spirals toward the conclusion that the dish must be remade despite insufficient time. Soma instead:

1. identifies the problem by tasting it;
2. retrieves replacement ingredients;
3. accepts the time constraint;
4. recalls the instructor's principle that a cook must still produce the dish;
5. turns serious;
6. gives Megumi concrete tasks and keeps her inside the solution.

**Locators:** `V01 / 1-5 / CBZ 01-140 to 01-143`

His key formulation is that before being students they are cooks, and **the food has to go out**.

### Initial care hypothesis

Soma's support style may be strongly **action-mediated**.

He does not primarily calm Megumi by telling her not to be afraid. He makes the crisis legible, assigns useful work, and creates a path to a valid result. In practical terms this also restores her agency.

This is promising but remains LOW-to-MEDIUM confidence because one scene is not enough to generalize his emotional-support style.

# 15. Cooking cognition in the sabotage recovery

Chapter 5 supplies the clearest V01 view of Soma's problem-solving sequence.

## 15.1 Detect

He notices that the lid has been moved and directly tastes the abnormal substance. The diagnosis—salt—is immediate.

## 15.2 Do not waste time on indignation

He recognizes sabotage, but the story does not show him spending the remaining time confronting the perpetrators. The output problem takes priority.

## 15.3 Retrieve usable resources

He obtains reserve ingredients.

## 15.4 Reframe the time constraint

A conventional bourguignon requires longer tenderization. Soma substitutes a method that accelerates the relevant physical result.

## 15.5 Retrieve cross-context knowledge

He remembers reading that pineapple juice tenderizes meat, then recalls his own experiments seeking an easier-to-stock alternative.

## 15.6 Optimize for kitchen reality

Honey's usefulness includes storage and accessibility, not merely chemistry.

## 15.7 Reintegrate the partner

He gives Megumi concrete preparation tasks rather than treating her as dead weight.

This pattern should become a major longitudinal target:

> **Does Soma consistently solve problems by identifying the underlying function, transferring prior knowledge, and privileging usable kitchen solutions over canonical form?**

**Claim status:** OPEN, high confidence for V01 evidence; longitudinal confidence not yet available.

# 16. Humor and social play through food

Soma repeatedly uses food as a social object even when the point is not good eating.

He gives friends horrible experimental food. After helping Megumi earn an A, he casually proposes that they mark becoming acquainted by having her try his newest creation—then produces squid soaked in honey.

**Locator:** `V01 / 1-5 / CBZ 01-150`

The bonus `倉瀬さんの日記` reinforces the same pattern in middle school: Soma makes bento experiments for classmates, receives strong reactions, and continues adjusting.

**Supplementary locator:** `V01 / bonus / CBZ 01-204 to 01-206`

### Initial interpretation

Cooking is not compartmentalized into "serious professional work" versus "private life." Soma socializes through making people eat things, including intentionally dubious things.

This may later matter for modeling intimacy and friendship: food may function as teasing, experimentation, challenge, hospitality, and attention depending on context.

# 17. Japanese voice baseline

The linguistic ledger contains the systematic record; the most important V01 observations are summarized here.

## 17.1 First-person and self-positioning

Soma's default first person is **俺**.

The form fits the wider voice: masculine, informal, direct, and unceremonious without necessarily being aggressively hostile.

## 17.2 Contraction and colloquiality

Recurring forms include:

- `～ねー`
- `～じゃねー`
- `～だろ`
- `～よな`
- `～だぜ`
- `～っす`
- `～っしょ`
- colloquial negative contractions such as `食わねー` / `作れねー`

His speech is often rhythmically compressed and action-oriented.

## 17.3 Semi-polite `～っす`

Soma can adapt minimally to public/formal settings without moving into highly deferential keigo. The entrance speech uses casual-politeness forms such as `～っす`, producing a hybrid voice: formally addressing a crowd while sounding unmistakably like himself.

This is a useful register marker. His politeness adaptation may be **shallow but functional**, not absent.

## 17.4 Playful/theatrical cooking register

Several expressions are almost ritualized:

- `おあがりよ`
- `御粗末！`
- mock-formal or theatrical address such as `試験官どの`

They should not be treated merely as catchphrases. They mark a performative cook-to-diner or cook-to-judge stance.

## 17.5 Direct pragmatics

Soma often reduces speech acts to operative questions or commands:

- he asks what the actual requirement is;
- admits he does not know and asks to be told;
- tells Megumi to help;
- asks the judge to say whether something is good or bad;
- challenges opponents to let the food decide.

The voice is therefore not just "casual Japanese." It is pragmatically **low-ceremony and outcome-oriented**.

# 18. Relationship-specific baseline

## Joichiro

### Register

- father addressed as `親父`;
- no deferential distance;
- competitive banter;
- willingness to protest strongly when Joichiro changes Soma's life plan.

### Behavioral relationship

Joichiro is simultaneously:

- father;
- training authority;
- benchmark;
- repeated victor;
- and the person most able to motivate Soma by turning an environment into a test of whether he can surpass him.

Soma's desire to surpass him does not require denying Joichiro's current superiority.

## Erina

### Register

- initially treats her as an unknown girl/young examiner rather than a sacred authority;
- very limited deference even after her status is explained;
- teasing and direct challenge;
- occasional theatrical pseudo-formality.

### Behavioral relationship in V01

Soma treats her palate as meaningful but her status claims as nonbinding. His conflict with her is initially a conflict between **sensory response and hierarchical judgment**.

Do not import later relational developments.

## Megumi

### Register

- direct, casual, quickly uses surname without honorific-heavy distance;
- openly asks about her strange nervous habit;
- gives simple operational instructions in crisis.

### Behavioral relationship in V01

The first cooperative bond is formed through shared kitchen work. Soma does not yet display deep personal insight into Megumi; he simply treats her as a usable partner and keeps her involved in the solution.

That modest formulation is safer than retroactively reading later closeness into the first class.

## Ordinary customers and peers

Soma's speech is relaxed and familiar. He appears comfortable being evaluated by ordinary diners and classmates. The social world of Yukihira normalizes direct reactions to food, including negative ones.

This may help explain why Erina's status performance feels alien to him: Soma's baseline evaluative culture is comparatively direct and embodied.

# 19. Counterevidence ledger: claims V01 itself prevents us from making

## Rejected as premature: "Soma is fearless"

Counterevidence:

- Totsuki's selectivity visibly alarms him;
- Yukihira's closure destabilizes him;
- he admits being nervous during the public entrance speech;
- Erina's apparently failed verdict leaves him confused.

Better hypothesis: he often regains stability once pressure becomes an actionable cooking problem.

## Rejected as premature: "Soma does not care about status"

Counterevidence:

- he does react to Totsuki's institutional difficulty once he understands the practical stakes;
- he sets a new goal of taking the top.

Better hypothesis: status has weak **intrinsic** authority; demonstrated standards and operational consequences matter much more.

## Rejected as premature: "Soma only cares whether food tastes good"

Counterevidence:

- he deliberately makes disgusting experimental dishes;
- he values learning, novelty, teasing, and technique outside immediate taste maximization.

Better hypothesis: taste is decisive in service/judgment contexts but experimentation has broader purposes.

## Rejected as premature: "Soma's arrogance means he is unaware of how others see him"

Counterevidence:

- after the opening speech he asks how he did and acknowledges nervousness.

Open question: he may understand that others evaluate him while still badly predicting *which* social effects his statements will produce.

## Rejected as premature: "Soma is naturally emotionally supportive"

Evidence is too thin. With Megumi, effective support occurs primarily through action and task structure. Later volumes must determine whether he can identify and address emotional needs directly.

# 21. Predictions to carry into V03

These predictions are deliberately falsifiable.

## P01 — competent authority versus status authority

If Soma encounters a higher-status person whose actual skill is obvious, he should show more substantive attention to the demonstrated skill than to the title itself.

**Would falsify/complicate:** strong deference produced solely by institutional rank.

## P02 — honest defeat

If he loses or is clearly outperformed under conditions he accepts as legitimate, he should remain oriented toward learning/retry rather than protecting his ego through denial.

**Would falsify/complicate:** blame-shifting or persistent excuse-making after an unambiguous fair loss.

## P03 — ignorance

When confronted with an unfamiliar ingredient, technique, or term, he should be willing to ask, observe, or experiment rather than conceal ignorance.

**Would falsify/complicate:** status-threat causes him to bluff expertise.

## P04 — kitchen crisis

Under time/resource pressure he should move quickly toward functional decomposition of the problem and a usable substitute.

**Would falsify/complicate:** freezes or remains attached to the canonical recipe when an alternate solution is available.

## P05 — peer support

When another student becomes anxious during cooking, Soma is more likely to give them something useful to do or reframe the problem than to offer elaborate emotional consolation.

**Would falsify/complicate:** repeated direct verbal soothing becomes his primary support mode.

## P06 — status insult

Mockery of Soma's own social pedigree should be easier for him to ignore than an attack on the validity of his cooking/Yukihira that is made without evidence.

## P07 — experiment as play

Soma should continue treating experimentation—including ugly failures—as intrinsically useful or amusing rather than as damage to his identity as a skilled cook.

## P08 — social-performance mismatch

Soma may continue to make socially provocative statements sincerely while underestimating or accepting their interpersonal consequences.

# 22. Model delta after Volume 1

There is no earlier source-grounded Soma model to revise, so V01 creates the baseline rather than transitioning prior claims.

All major claims therefore enter the cumulative ledger as **OPEN** with varying confidence.

The most important thing V01 establishes is not a trait list but an organizing contrast:

> **Soma's confidence is strongest when competence can be made concrete.**

He is not emotionless, socially omniscient, or immune to pressure. He is unusually good at turning pressure into something his hands can solve. His working-cook identity gives him a stable response grammar:

**taste → diagnose → reframe → improvise → serve → let the result answer.**

Whether that grammar survives larger institutional, relational, and competitive pressures is the central question for the sampled volumes that follow.
