## Source, state and contradiction

The 14 numbered EPUBs are prospective mainline candidates in volume order. Each exact witness advances through `INVENTORY_VISIBLE`, `STRUCTURALLY_VERIFIED`, `ADMITTED`, `INSPECTED`, `CLOSED` only on actual verification and complete transaction. `INSPECTED` requires narrative/page inspection, never just EPUB packaging. The V10 separate booklet remains a supplemental/paratextual object with undetermined continuity and placement. Bonus material included with a numbered edition is tagged but not automatically used in its mainline reading; a future transaction identifies which components it actually admitted. Anime, localization, interviews, promotion, and reception are separate lanes. No adaptation dialogue can establish manga speech or performance.

Keep **available inventory**, **admitted source**, **narratively inspected**, **completed sequential**, and **independently verified published** boundaries distinct. V14 is the current inventory ceiling, not an ending claim. If a V15 appears, add its identity and prospectively admit it after the previous freeze; do not rewrite earlier exit states. A later flashback or conflicting testimony receives a new retrospective record linked to the earlier event and prior claim. Test temporal order, speaker knowledge, role play, deceptive report, edition differences, and genuine contradiction before harmonizing. Chronology certainty and interpretation certainty are separately marked. Other continuities require explicit cross-source comparison rather than silent merger.

The claim ledger uses `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`. Every transition points to prior claim state, new evidence, counterevidence and downstream artifacts to amend. Historical freezes retain what was knowable; mutable ledgers and the current entrypoint carry the latest bounded account. Superseding an entire artifact follows the global authority/supersession graph; routine claim changes are not a reason to erase its history.

## Atomic VNN transaction after the lock opens

1. Check authorization and current head; verify/admit **one** exact Japanese numbered witness, its pagination/locator convention, and any limitations.
2. Record the entering state from the previous frozen volume and reproduce only the necessary prior developments; V01 has no narrative entering state.
3. Inspect the complete admitted mainline volume, both written text and pages/form. Mark any excluded bonus component or missed content explicitly.
4. Write `WATAYURI_VNN_DEEP_READING.md` with source receipt, synopsis/developments from the previous volume when applicable, close reading, state changes, evidence, alternative readings, and prospective exit state.
5. Update every **materially affected** cumulative ledger; add/revise claims and adjudicate predictions, leaving no fabricated zero-change entry.
6. Reassess character evidence density, relationship synthesis need, and reconstruction readiness if the evidence bears on them.
7. Advance the witness's inspection state, update the sole current entrypoint, freeze the volume endpoint, and identify the next candidate operation without automatically authorizing it.
8. Validate and commit the complete transaction under the repository checklist. A partial reading does not advance the high-water mark.

The continuation policy's `single_operation` default applies unless a future owner instruction expressly authorizes a bounded continuous run. The owner-approved lock is OPEN for a future V01 transaction; no sequential unit has yet begun.

## Synthesis dependencies and promotion

Complete and reconcile a meaningful frozen source tranche before claiming a specialist is ready. Stabilize contested claims and locators, then commission a specialist only when independent evidence density and a nonduplicated question justify it. Anticipated domains include public/private performance, Liebe's social script, secrecy/disclosure, attachment/jealousy/exclusivity, memory/grievance/repair, queer desire/self-conception/classification, manga staging, and eventual ending or continuing-series state. These are questions and possible homes, not promised documents or results. A relationship specialist needs reciprocal and directional evidence, not merely a memorable scene.

Character evidence ledger, operational reconstruction model, and monograph have separate promotion thresholds in the [reconstruction specification](WATAYURI_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_SPEC.md). Cross-series scenario portability is separately assessed; a rich monograph does not grant unbounded simulation. Global character routing and capability assessment remain with their governed curation process.

Dependency order for a bounded synthesis: closed sequential tranche → ledger/claim reconciliation → role-gap and architecture review → evidence-supported character/relationship/specialist work → cross-specialist contradiction review → bounded cumulative synthesis → release audit. A full-series synthesis needs an actual justified series boundary, coverage reconciliation, and specialist convergence; sequential completion alone does not suffice. A release freeze records source set, claim state, authority and exclusions. A continuing series can publish a scoped checkpoint without implying closure of the whole work.

## Amendment, execution and retrieval

Review the architecture after the first meaningful tranche, a new source class, a persistent uncaptured dimension, major retrospective recontextualization, and before substantial synthesis. Add or merge a ledger only when independent recurring retrieval warrants it. Record a material amendment and its effect on prior artifacts in the entrypoint; backfill earlier evidence only if existing readings and source routes cannot repair a material gap. Never silently revise frozen prospective states.

For workload routing, metadata reconciliation is `BOUNDED_STANDARD`, mature sequential reading and interpretive ledger updates are `SUBSTANTIVE_ANALYSIS`, architecture/major cross-volume synthesis can warrant `DEEP_SYNTHESIS` or `PREMIUM_QUALITY_FIRST` based on propagation risk. These are reasoning classes, not hard-coded model names. A fresh synthesis session can receive a pinned Git state and source boundary under the corpus handoff contract; the receiver validates full files, hashes, omissions and branch drift before integration. An adequately capable Work/Codex environment may own sequential source inspection and repository integration. Source modality and tool capability must be checked at the actual stage; present bootstrap used metadata only.

A future reader begins with the entrypoint, then method, architecture, source map, appropriate ledgers and last closed reading. The mutable entrypoint and ledgers reflect current state; each completed VNN reading remains a frozen prospective record.
