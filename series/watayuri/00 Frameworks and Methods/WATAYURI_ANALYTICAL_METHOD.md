## Witness and prospective discipline

The primary proposed witness is the Japanese numbered manga in the [source map](WATAYURI_SOURCE_AND_SCOPE_MAP.md). Inventory visibility is not admission or reading. Before V01, verify the exact source identity and practical page/locator scheme; record any inability to verify the Drive bytes against the manifest's **local-source** SHA-256. A remote file name and size are weaker than a byte-matched download. Keep a record of the pages/sections actually inspected and gaps in visual resolution, translation, or packaging.

For each authorized VNN, write an **entering state** from the immediately preceding frozen state (V01 begins with no narrative claims), read only the admitted numbered volume, record observations and bounded inferences, adjudicate prior predictions, then freeze the **prospective state after VNN** before opening V(N+1). The `WATAYURI_VNN_DEEP_READING.md` is immutable as a record of what was knowable then except for explicit errata. Later volumes can explain, strengthen, or overturn claims in cumulative homes, but never silently rewrite a prior freeze. Maintain separate columns or labeled prose for (a) prospective state at VNN, (b) later retrospective explanation, and (c) current mature synthesis.

## Evidence discipline

Use stable locators comprising Drive witness ID or source-map key, VNN, chapter if verified, and a **page/image index defined by the actual EPUB spine or publisher pagination**, plus panel/balloon when helpful. State which convention was used; do not assume digital indices equal print page numbers. Minimal Japanese snippets may be quoted only when needed for linguistic reasoning; prefer precise paraphrase and locator. Record observation, speaker/observer, context, competing explanations, inference strength, and potential disconfirmation separately. A character's speech is evidence of an utterance, not automatically of belief, truth, or narrator endorsement. Visual staging can qualify or contradict spoken presentation; do not infer a unique emotion from a single expression if alternatives fit.

Preserve witness levels: manga panel/lettering and edition paratext; later numbered manga; quarantined supplementary objects; adaptation; creator comment; translation; reception. A lower or different witness may illuminate a question but cannot silently become mainline manga evidence. When a contradiction arises, test timing, perspective, omission/deception, remembered versus shown events, editorial or edition differences, and genuine inconsistency; leave it open when unresolved.

## Watayuri observation questions

Capture the *setting and audience* of consequential behavior: public/front-of-house, backstage/private, or another evidenced context; performed role and how conscious its adoption appears; script or institutional pressure; leakage and deviation; whether another person recognizes the act; and whether performance and private attachment change each other. Performance may carry sincere emotion, concealment, play, obligation, and instrumental purpose at once. Never equate performed with fake.

For relationships, distinguish directional belief and desire from reciprocal status. Test affection, attraction (romantic or sexual when evidenced), admiration, friendship, dependency, jealousy, exclusivity, resentment, rivalry, possessiveness, and chosen-family attachment independently. An expressed label, public script, and private state can diverge; a declaration by A does not establish B's view. Separate desire from entitlement, affection from control, proximity from reciprocity, apology from subsequent changed behavior, and avoidance from resolution. Track actual options and costs before calling non-action a choice; intensity alone is not agency.

Track consequential propositions as information states: represented truth, each person's knowledge or suspicion, false beliefs, higher-order belief where behavior depends on it, audience and timing of disclosure/withholding, and later correction. Distinguish deliberate lie, omission, strategic ambiguity, mistaken belief, and in-role speech. Do not generate bookkeeping for inconsequential facts. For chronology, distinguish present event, established earlier event, a character's memory or account, later revelation, the character's present interpretation, and the reader's evidence. Chronological certainty and causal/emotional interpretation receive separate confidence assessments.

Written Japanese speech permits a model of self-reference, address, honorifics, politeness, sentence endings, register switches, lexical habits, indirectness, teasing, hesitation and confrontation. Note role-conditioned versus private speech when the page supports it. Manga lettering does **not** establish acoustic timbre, pitch, breath, or voice-actor delivery; do not import anime performance. Inspect manga form as well as dialogue: gaze and averted gaze, body orientation, approach/touch and response, isolation/proximity, panel scale/sequence, reaction and withheld face, page turn, costume, front/backstage space and repeated compositions. Distinguish depicted fact from an interpretive reading of form.
