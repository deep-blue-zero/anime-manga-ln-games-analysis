# Choices, boundaries and conflict mechanisms

**Canonical responsibility.** Track initiative, refusal, avoidance, confrontation, pursuit, withdrawal, boundary setting/crossing, disclosure, apology, forgiveness, cost and changed or repeated behavior. Separate emotional intensity from feasible action and repair attempt from repaired relationship.

**Record schema.** Event ID; VNN; actors; concrete act/non-act; available options and evidential basis; explicit/implicit boundary; whose consent or refusal; power/role constraint; immediate consequence/cost; rupture state; repair attempt and audience; subsequent behavior; alternative causal account; confidence; locator and pair/claim IDs.

**Boundary and exclusions.** Do not assign a global “toxic” label or infer meaningful non-action without a demonstrated opportunity/expectation. Relationship-level attachment remains in the relationship ledger; proposition disclosure state remains in information.

**Evidence convention.** Each future entry references a source-map key and exact Drive witness ID, volume, verified chapter and image/print-page scheme, and panel/balloon where relevant. Cite the associated frozen `WATAYURI_VNN_DEEP_READING.md` observation and claim ID when one exists. Separate depicted fact, character report, and analyst inference. Do not insert raw page images or long source passages into Git.

**Update and revision rule.** Update when choices or boundary/repair trajectory materially change. Do not close a rupture merely because an apology occurred. Link later behavior as a dated continuation and retain the earlier prospective assessment. Make targeted edits to mutable current-state rows while preserving dated prior states, IDs and frozen prospective readings. Use the claim ledger's explicit transitions for changed interpretations; an artifact-wide supersession requires the global authority procedure. The [architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/WATAYURI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns cross-ledger routing.
