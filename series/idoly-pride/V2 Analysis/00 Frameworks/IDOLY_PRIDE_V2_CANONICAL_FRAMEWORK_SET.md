## 3. Precedence rule

Precedence is determined by analytical specificity, not file length.

- The two specialized audiovisual protocols govern anime sequencing, voice acting, music, performance, and audiovisual selection whenever their instructions are more specific than the older general framework language.
- The source/evidence protocol governs source identity, evidence classification, ledgers, and claim routing.
- The synthesis architecture governs file placement and document architecture.
- The analytical method governs general procedure where no more-specific protocol controls.

When two canonical documents appear to conflict, preserve the conflict and resolve it by this specificity rule before proceeding.
