# 1. Tranche-level result

C1-A does not replace the established series architecture of **answerable interdependence**, `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM`, `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`, `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`, or `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`.

It does, however, justify one new cross-card temporal/memorial refinement:

> **`LEGACY_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_RESTORATION`**
>
> A lost, unavailable, failed, or superseded relational configuration can remain genuinely constitutive without being literally restored. Its meaning may continue through re-authored work, embodied practice, later vocation, public transmission, or care for another person.

This is not a claim that every loss becomes productive, nor that grief is valuable because it can be repurposed. It is a narrower structural observation about how *IDOLY PRIDE* repeatedly prevents inheritance from becoming resurrection.

C1-A demonstrates four distinct forms:

- **Kana:** the original family-restoration goal fails, but the visibility practice built around it acquires new fan-facing meaning;
- **Haruko:** literal co-performance with Mana is impossible, but a later musical lets Haruko inherit Mana's role and lead in her own way;
- **miho / hair:** Yō's recognition becomes part of miho's own embodied self-valuing practice rather than a static relic obligation;
- **miho / `Friend Glass`:** Sundance cannot be recreated, so IIIX re-authors the song while causing new listeners to seek the preserved original.

The tranche therefore strengthens the project's larger anti-imitation principle while adding a more exact temporal grammar: **continuity can be real even when restoration is impossible or undesirable.**

# 2. Companion-message refinement

## 2.1 Kana — public account does not waive private jurisdiction

`message-card-kan-05-fest-02` provides an unusually clean jurisdictional distinction.

Haruko reads Kana's public interview and sincerely believes the public story: she tells Kana she is glad Kana got to meet her father. Kana finds the sympathy uncomfortable but says it is preferable to being treated as pitiable. She then explicitly orders Makino not to tell the others the actual paternal situation.

Admit as a strengthening of:

> `PUBLIC_SELF_NARRATION_AS_DEFENSIVE_AUTHORSHIP`

and:

> `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`

Rule:

> **A person may deliberately publish a constructed but fact-bearing account of a private wound without thereby authorizing insiders to circulate the fuller private truth.**

This is important for Kana because her professional skill is precisely the production of visibility. The card/message pair shows that visibility is not equivalent to surrendering interpretive ownership.

## 2.2 Suzu — maternal acceptance is explicitly provisional

`message-card-suz-05-anml-00` removes any ambiguity created by the card's emotional ending. Suzu states directly that the study-abroad matter is only `保留` — on hold — and that her mother may again take a hard line.

Admit:

> **`MATERNAL_ACCEPTANCE_REMAINS_PROVISIONAL`**

The comic proposal to become a “world-class idol” in order to satisfy both idol work and foreign-study expectations also reveals priorities clearly: Suzu wants a solution that preserves idol activity. When Makino translates the grand idea into harder English/performance requirements, Suzu immediately scales the fantasy back to caring for her Japanese fans first.

The message therefore strengthens rather than weakens the card's core transition from defensive rebellion into self-authored vocation.

## 2.3 fran — perspective must be designed for bodies other than one's own

`message-card-kor-05-fest-02` has fran seek Chisa's opinion on new designs because fran recognizes that her own unusually tall, model-proportioned body is a poor sole reference point for smaller wearers.

Admit this as direct strengthening of:

> **`CROSS_ROLE_COMPETENCE_TRANSFER_WITH_CONTEXTUAL_ADAPTATION`**

and support for:

> `MARKET_DISCIPLINE_WITHOUT_ARTISTIC_SURRENDER`

The point is not that fran suddenly becomes humble. It is that serious authorship includes recognizing when one's own embodied baseline is not representative of the intended user.

## 2.4 Haruko — succession without impersonation

`message-card-hrk-05-link-00` materially sharpens the source-native musical reading.

Haruko asks how Mana behaved when Mana led the same production. At first this risks reproducing the old pattern: Haruko may treat Mana's precedent as the answer to how she should lead. But the conversation turns when Haruko realizes that hospitality/care is already her own domain of competence. She resolves to lead the cast `私らしく` — in her own way.

Admit:

> **`SUCCESSION_WITHOUT_IMPERSONATION`**

Rule:

> **Inheritance may use a predecessor as reference without making imitation the criterion of legitimacy. The successor answers the inherited role through her own established competencies.**

This is the most exact C1-A expression of the difference between inheritance and replacement.

## 2.5 miho — memorial ownership remains private even when the image becomes public

`message-card-mhk-05-pajm-00` shows miho irritated that fran and Kana discovered the personal origin of her hair attachment after the commercial became public and viral. The comedy is aggressive, but the jurisdictional point is clear: public use of the hair as image does not make the intimate memory origin common property.

Admit as strengthening of:

- `PRIVATE_MEMORY_WITHIN_PUBLIC_IMAGE_LABOR`;
- `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`.

## 2.6 miho — re-authorship actually produces archival circulation

`message-card-mhk-05-fest-02` supplies a small but concrete downstream result of the IIIX cover. Shizuku approaches miho to praise the cover and has also watched footage of miho and Yō.

This strengthens:

> **`LEGACY_TRANSMISSION_THROUGH_PRESENT_REAUTHORSHIP`**

The claim remains modest: the message proves at least one present Hoshimi junior engaged both the cover and Sundance archive. It does not quantify broad public rediscovery.

# 3. Historical transition audit

## 3.1 Kana — `REVISE`: “paternal abandonment” is too categorical

Historical V1/Tier-H analysis repeatedly summarized Kana through formulations such as:

- “Kana's central wound is paternal abandonment”;
- “Kana turns abandonment into visibility strategy.”

The second formulation contains a strong insight about visibility, but the first is now too coarse to remain current authority.

The C1-A card establishes simultaneously that:

- Kana and her father lived apart;
- they remained in some contact;
- he sent gifts;
- he watched her screen work and sent reactions;
- she sent him messages/photos;
- they nevertheless rarely met;
- he had never even taken a photo together with her;
- Kana continued to perform a “good child” version of herself to him;
- he formed another household;
- Kana's hoped-for family future did not occur;
- Kana herself concludes that, despite years of effort, they did not become a family in the sense she wanted.

Transition:

> **REVISE** `PATERNAL_ABANDONMENT_KANA` / categorical “abandonment” shorthand
>
> → **`CONTACT_WITHOUT_FAMILY_RECONSTITUTION`**

Supporting proposition:

> `PATERNAL_CONTACT_WITHOUT_CORESIDENCE_KANA`

This is not a rehabilitation of the father into a normal co-parent. It preserves deprivation while describing its mechanism more accurately: **mediated contact existed, but contact did not produce shared family life.**

## 3.2 Kana — `STRENGTHEN`: visibility as signal flare, then as authored public utility

Historical analysis correctly identified performance visibility as a signal flare directed toward Kana's father. C1-A provides direct support: he watched her work and his reactions mattered to her; she had wanted him to see her live since childhood.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN** historical visibility thesis
>
> → `PATERNAL_VISIBILITY_AS_ORIGINAL_VOCATIONAL_MOTIVE`

But the card also proves that this original purpose is not exhaustive. Once the desired family future fails, Kana receives fan testimony from someone enduring a painful household who uses Kana's broadcasts as support.

Admit:

> **`VOCATIONAL_MEANING_CAN_OUTLIVE_FAILED_ORIGINAL_PURPOSE`**

This is a genuine revision to simplistic causal accounts of Kana's career. A failed founding motive does not retroactively void the vocation.

## 3.3 Kana — `STRENGTHEN`: authored construction is not equivalent to falsehood

Historical analysis already treated Kana as a producer of visibility who rejects naïve authenticity. C1-A supplies one of the strongest concrete mechanisms: she does not answer a tabloid by exposing everything. She produces a strategically timed counter-public narrative that mixes factual contact with an idealized family framing while keeping the painful remainder private.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN** `ANSWERABLE_CONSTRUCTION`
>
> through `PUBLIC_SELF_NARRATION_AS_DEFENSIVE_AUTHORSHIP`

Do not call the public interview a transparent documentary account. Do not call it mere lying either. It is a bounded act of public authorship under hostile media pressure.

## 3.4 Kana — `RESOLVE`: Kuromi/card corroboration

The historical open proposition `OPEN_VERIFY_KANA_CARD_CORROBORATION` asked whether later ordinary card material independently remembered the earlier dream-amusement-park/Kuromi encounter.

`card_kan_014_st-card-kan-05-snro-00` does exactly that: Kana explicitly recalls the dream amusement park, says she met Kuromi there, and links that encounter to recovered positive memory of a childhood amusement-park visit with her father.

Transition:

> **RESOLVE** `OPEN_VERIFY_KANA_CARD_CORROBORATION`

Scope of resolution:

> **The later ordinary card independently remembers Kana meeting Kuromi and attributes meaningful memory recovery to the encounter.**

Not established:

> the ontological status of every crossover mechanism, supernatural implication, or other guest-property event as ordinary mainline fact.

The resolved claim is deliberately narrower than a blanket crossover-continuity ruling.

## 3.5 Kana — `STRENGTHEN/ADMIT`: memory can be transferred without false rescue

Historical Kana analysis recognized that she is unexpectedly good with children because she understands runaway fantasies, shame, adult control, and the importance of good memory. The later amusement-park card makes the mechanism explicit.

Admit:

- `CARE_WITHOUT_FALSE_RESCUE_PROMISE`;
- **`MEMORY_AS_TRANSFERABLE_RESILIENCE`**.

Kana does not promise to fix the residential-care child's structural environment. She listens, grants a bounded extra experience, and insists on safe return. She then links her own durable positive childhood memory to the desire to give the child one good memory as well.

This is care without savior fantasy.

# 4. Suzu transition audit

## 4.1 `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`: family structure and initial rebellion

Historical Tsuki analysis already modeled Suzu's family as emotionally uneven: a doting father, a demanding mother, extensive lessons, and loneliness. It also correctly framed her original parental conflict as a test of whether idolhood was a durable commitment rather than another short-lived enthusiasm.

C1-A preserves that model.

Admit/strengthen:

> **`SPLIT_PARENTAL_AUTHORIZATION_SUZU`**

The father has converted into exuberant idol support; the mother remains a conditional evaluator.

## 4.2 `STRENGTHEN`: rebellion becomes positive vocation

Historical analysis had already identified the America card as a pivot from rebellion to positive vocation. The primary read confirms the transition in Suzu's own terms: she says earlier opposition to study abroad included stubbornness, while present commitment rests on having performed with the others, stood on large stages, and received fan support.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN**
>
> **`DEFENSIVE_ENTRY_TO_SELF_AUTHORED_VOCATION`**

Rule:

> **A vocation can begin partly as resistance to control and later become independently chosen through lived practice, relationships, and audience reciprocity.**

## 4.3 `ADMIT`: conditional authorization can generate proof-seeking overwork

When Suzu believes her mother may force study abroad because her solo visibility is insufficient, she demands all available jobs and adds practice. Makino repeatedly offers rest and offers to reject work; the schedule later returns to normal once the threat recedes.

Admit:

> **`PROOF_SEEKING_OVERWORK_UNDER_CONDITIONAL_AUTHORIZATION`**

This is evidence about Suzu's response to conditional family approval, not evidence that Hoshimi institutionally requires unsustainable labor.

## 4.4 `STRENGTHEN`: ordinary jobs become legitimate idol work

Historical Suzu analysis already emphasized her maturation from craving only spectacular jobs toward recognizing mall events, flyers, character shows, merchandise assistance, and small fan-facing work as real idol labor.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN** `SMALL_WORK_AS_PROFESSIONAL_LEGITIMACY`

Her growth is not reduced ambition. It is a more professional understanding of how recognition is built.

# 5. fran transition audit

## 5.1 `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`: failed authorship remains the governing wound

Historical fran synthesis identified ERFOLG as failed authorship rather than mere debt: she wanted to move from model/object to designer/author, and material business failure exposed the difference between aesthetic talent and sustainable production.

C1-A strongly preserves this model.

The new card does not show fran abandoning fashion after the American arc. It shows her restarting ERFOLG while continuing IIIX and explicitly aiming for the top in both roles.

Admit:

> **`PLURAL_VOCATION_WITHOUT_HIERARCHY_FRAN`**

This revises any residual early shorthand that treats idolhood only as a temporary instrument for returning to fashion. At this stage, both domains are serious active projects.

## 5.2 `ADMIT`: prestige does not automatically cross domains

fran's modeling/idol fame does not grant designer legitimacy. Buyers/industry actors can still treat her as an artist or model playing at fashion, and her lack of conventional training remains socially legible.

Admit:

> **`CROSS_DOMAIN_STATUS_RESET`**

Rule:

> **Recognition in one professional field may create access without granting standing in another. A mature plural career can repeatedly expose the person to novice or outsider status.**

## 5.3 `STRENGTHEN`: failure teaches market discipline without extinguishing aesthetic authorship

Historical fran analysis correctly argued that “beauty is production”: design, materials, labor, money, branding, and distribution must coexist. The C1-A card is the cleanest later test of whether she has actually learned this lesson.

She explicitly recognizes that the earlier strategy overprivileged what she wanted to make. The restart requires recognition, customer trust, target-market perspective, and sales practice.

Admit/strengthen:

- **`MARKET_DISCIPLINE_WITHOUT_ARTISTIC_SURRENDER`**;
- **`FAILURE_REVISES_STRATEGY_NOT_VOCATIONAL_COMMITMENT`**.

The story does not turn her into a market servant. It makes her a more competent author because she now treats reception and sustainability as part of authorship.

## 5.4 `STRENGTHEN`: skill transfer requires translation

The trade-show recovery uses idol-domain skills: stage presence, performance video, personal selling, customer interaction, and Makino's own merchandise-sales experience. Yet those skills work only after being adapted to a buyer/trade-show problem.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN** the post-B2-B transfer rule
>
> through **`CROSS_ROLE_COMPETENCE_TRANSFER_WITH_CONTEXTUAL_ADAPTATION`**.

This is the cleanest positive case yet of the rule that **competence transfers more easily than form**.

# 6. Haruko transition audit

## 6.1 `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`: delayed flowering / delayed fulfillment

Historical Haruko synthesis already identified her as the idol of delayed flowering, postponed stages, and transmissible encouragement. That model survives intact.

The musical card gives it unusually exact temporal form:

- early Haruko auditions mainly to stand beside Mana;
- she fails;
- Mana tells her the work will not be wasted if she keeps moving;
- years later, Haruko enters a revival;
- she does not receive the old friend/supporting role she once sought;
- she becomes the lead in the role Mana once played.

Admit:

- **`FAILURE_MEMORY_AS_LONG_HORIZON_MOTIVATION`**;
- **`DEFERRED_PERFORMANCE_SUCCESSION`**.

## 6.2 `REVISE`: “fulfilling the promise to stand beside Mana” must not imply literal restoration

Some historical prose understandably described Haruko as eventually fulfilling a promise to stand beside Mana. C1-A requires more precise wording because the late musical itself explicitly marks literal co-performance as impossible.

Transition:

> **REVISE** literalized promise-fulfillment shorthand
>
> → **`PROMISE_FULFILLMENT_THROUGH_ROLE_INHERITANCE_NOT_COPRESENCE`**

The promise survives by changing form. Haruko occupies a stage lineage Mana once occupied; she does not recreate the unavailable dyad.

The companion message further adds:

> **`SUCCESSION_WITHOUT_IMPERSONATION`**

Haruko may ask what Mana did, but her mature answer is to lead through Haruko's own care, hospitality, and relational competence.

## 6.3 `STRENGTHEN`, not close: plural performance vocation

The successful musical demonstrates that Haruko's performance identity is not exhausted by idol work.

Admit:

> `PLURAL_PERFORMANCE_VOCATION_WITHOUT_IDOL_EXIT_HARUKO`

This materially advances `OPEN-14` by widening Haruko's demonstrated adult performance horizon. It does **not** close the separate question of long-run material/economic viability or whether she eventually leaves, scales back, or transforms idol work.

# 7. miho transition audit

## 7.1 `STRENGTHEN`: Yō's recognition becomes miho's own embodied value

Historical miho analysis already emphasized that Yō was the person who saw miho before miho could see herself as someone worthy of being seen. The hair card supplies a precise embodied mechanism.

miho says she originally did not care much about the long hair. A longtime friend praised it; that praise made miho happy; miho then began to like the hair herself and care for it seriously.

Admit:

> **`RELATIONAL_RECOGNITION_BECOMES_SELF_AUTHORED_EMBODIED_VALUE`**

This is more precise than saying “miho keeps long hair because Yō liked it.” The value has provenance in Yō's gaze but later belongs to miho as well.

## 7.2 `ADMIT`: private memory may inhabit public image labor

The current hair is also professional image labor: maintenance, commercial styling, and a public beauty asset. Its private memorial origin does not make the public function false, and the public function does not erase private provenance.

Admit:

- **`PRIVATE_MEMORY_WITHIN_PUBLIC_IMAGE_LABOR`**;
- **`MEMORIAL_PRACTICE_WITHOUT_STATIC_RELIC`**.

Do not infer that any future haircut would constitute rejection or betrayal of Yō.

## 7.3 `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`: `Friend Glass` remains the mature memory hinge

Historical miho analysis strongly argued:

- Sundance cannot be recreated exactly;
- `Friend Glass` must change if it is to live;
- fran and Kana do not replace Yō;
- the cover can create renewed attention toward Sundance/Yō;
- miho learns to let memory move rather than sealing it.

The C1-A source-native pass independently reproduces this architecture.

Transitions:

> **PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN**
>
> `MEMORIAL_REPERFORMANCE_WITHOUT_REPLICATION`
>
> **`LEGACY_TRANSMISSION_THROUGH_PRESENT_REAUTHORSHIP`**
>
> **`NEW_COMPANIONSHIP_WITHOUT_REPLACEMENT`**

No historical downgrade is required here.

## 7.4 `STRENGTHEN WITH SAFEGUARD`: guilt over professional absence

The card makes miho's guilt concrete: she toured Taiwan while Yō was hospitalized, expected recovery, received reassuring photos, and returned to find severe deterioration.

Admit:

> **`GUILT_OVER_PROFESSIONAL_ABSENCE_DURING_IRREVERSIBLE_LOSS`**

But reject stronger causal moralizations not established by the source.

**REJECT AS UNSUPPORTED:**

- miho knowingly chose career advancement over a dying Yō;
- management knowingly sent miho away to deprive Yō of her presence;
- miho possessed information that would have made the outcome obvious and ignored it.

Her guilt is real. The stronger culpability story is not established.
