# 1. `愛歌う星の継承者` — Suzu, Mana, and succession through affinity

## 1.1 Historical routing decision survives, but its scope was too narrow

The post-E1 reassessment correctly demoted this event from early E2 execution because E1-A/E1-E now provide stronger mature authority for Mana provenance, inheritance without substitution, plural memory, and person-versus-legend distinctions.

That demotion is **PRESERVED**.

However, describing the event as useful mainly for “Suzu/Mana imitation-to-rival chronology” undersells one mechanism that later sources do not replace: **Suzu's right to participate in Mana's legacy arises through chosen attention and artistic affinity rather than kinship or formal succession.**

Transition:

> **`REVISE_BY_SCOPE`** — remain support-deferred, but preserve a unique succession mechanism.

Admit:

- `AFFINITY_BASED_INHERITANCE`
- `MULTIPERSPECTIVAL_LEGACY_KNOWLEDGE`
- `ADMIRATION_TO_PEERHOOD`

## 1.2 Historical anti-imitation thesis is strengthened

Historical V1/V2 prose already argued that the dead must not be preserved through exact imitation and that living performers must transform inherited material. Suzu begins from almost the opposite instinct: because she reveres Mana, she assumes faithful reproduction is the respectful goal. Her repeated listening makes the technical gap feel larger rather than smaller, and her attempts at intensified study eventually damage her own ability to sing normally.

Makino's annotated score then demonstrates that Suzu's intense fandom is genuine knowledge but not exhaustive knowledge. His managerial/professional memory notices dimensions of Mana's singing and dimensions of Suzu's own voice that Suzu cannot recover merely by replaying recordings.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`** — the anti-imitation thesis gains a non-kin successor case and a concrete epistemic mechanism.

The mature authority nevertheless remains E1-A/E1-E. This event should be cited when the synthesis needs to explain **how a fan becomes a legitimate inheritor without becoming a copy**.

## 1.3 Suzu's aspiration changes category

The strongest event-local transition is Suzu's declaration that she remains Mana's enormous fan while now recognizing herself as an idol standing on the same stage. Reverence therefore ceases to function as permanent status subordination.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + REVISE_BY_PRECISION`**

Preferred formulation:

> **Admiration can remain intact while the successor moves from worship toward professional peerhood.**

The later desire to become greater than Mana is not rejection of Mana. It is evidence that the inherited figure has become a professional interlocutor rather than an untouchable ceiling.

## 1.4 Makino/Suzu romance inference remains low-authority texture

The event contains a deliberate romantic misunderstanding when Suzu hears Makino's statement about what she “likes more than anyone” as if he were referring to Suzu rather than to Suzu's love of Mana. Suzu's reaction is valid character texture and may support attraction on her side.

But the source explicitly grounds Makino's selection in professional criteria, and the surrounding characters reject the idea that he would use romantic favoritism in the work decision.

Transition:

> **`DOWNGRADE / OPEN_CHARACTER_TEXTURE`** for any mutual-romance claim derived from this event.

Do not use this source as romance canon.

# 2. `君と願う月灯の祝祭` — anniversary re-authoring without erasure

## 2.1 The support demotion remains correct

Later E1-E material is the stronger authority for plural-person memory, polyphonic memorial authorship, and the mature relationship between inheritance and living continuation. This event should not reclaim those broad theses merely because it contains them in earlier form.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE`** the support-deferred priority.

## 2.2 The event contains a unique temporal mechanism that later memory theory does not replace

The strongest independent contribution is Kotono's relationship to Christmas/her birthday. The date is already burdened by `あの日のこと`; the story does not demand that grief disappear before Kotono can celebrate. Instead, the Tsuki surprise, audience response, retrospective album, letter, and song add new lived meanings to the same recurring date.

Admit:

> **`ANNIVERSARY_REAUTHORING_WITHOUT_ERASURE`**

This is more precise than generic “healing through new memories.” The date itself functions as a recurring temporal container in which old grief and newly authored relational meaning can coexist.

The event therefore deserves independent retrieval whenever the mature synthesis discusses **time, anniversary, ritual, or repeated dates as structures of memory**.

Transition relative to prior routing:

> **`STRENGTHEN + REVISE_BY_SCOPE`** — support status remains, but the event owns a distinct temporal-re-authoring mechanism.

## 2.3 Kotono's understanding of Mana remains epistemically bounded

Kotono says the analogous birthday surprise lets her feel that she understands **a little** of how happy Mana must have been. The wording matters. She does not claim to possess Mana's interiority or become identical with her.

Admit:

- `ANALOGICAL_EXPERIENTIAL_INHERITANCE`
- `EPISTEMICALLY_BOUNDED_RECOGNITION`

This strongly stabilizes the current bounded-jurisdiction model of memory and inheritance.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

## 2.4 The birthday letter recognizes Kotono beyond functional role

Tsuki explicitly admits that its members cannot fully know the burden of continuously running at the group's center, while still recognizing Kotono's effort and telling her they are glad she was born.

Admit/stabilize:

- `EXISTENTIAL_RECOGNITION_BEYOND_ROLE`

This is supporting evidence for `NON_PERFORMATIVE_RECOGNITION` and `RELATIONAL_INDIVIDUATION`, not a new replacement doctrine.

The temporary hurt caused by the secret surprise remains a bounded texture point:

- `BENEVOLENT_SECRECY_RELATIONAL_COST`

Do not generalize it into a prohibition on surprise or temporary information asymmetry.

# 3. `舌戦開幕！賛美の湯` — IIIX hostile intimacy with real costs

## 3.1 Historical “ugly but durable bond” survives

Historical IIIX synthesis correctly identified `腐れ縁`, hostility, mutual legibility, professional cohesion, and refusal of clean friendship language as structural to the unit. The onsen event strongly supports that description: forced conventional friendliness creates awkwardness, ordinary antagonism restores their familiar rhythm, and outsiders can read the bond as closeness even when the members do not use that vocabulary.

Transitions:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

Admit as precursor/stabilization:

- `IIIX_RECOVERY_TOURING_BASELINE`
- `COMFORTABLE_ADVERSARIAL_DISTANCE`
- `PRAISE_WITHIN_ADVERSARIAL_GRAMMAR`

Later E2-B1 remains the stronger mature authority for `TRUST_WITHOUT_FRIENDSHIP_LABEL` and `CURATED_ADVERSARIAL_AUTHENTICITY`.

## 3.2 Historical “harshness as disguised care” requires a hard qualification

Historical synthesis often usefully observed that Fran, miho, and kana disguise care as business, correction, teasing, efficiency, or self-interest. That remains true in many scenes.

But the onsen event proves that this cannot become a universal decoding rule.

miho's drunken complaints are not merely affection in abrasive costume. She identifies:

- garbage and dressing-room cleanup that falls disproportionately on her;
- a rehearsal mistake that the others have mocked for months;
- frustration that natural competence allows the others to underprepare without immediate performance failure.

Admit:

- `ADVERSARIAL_GRAMMAR_WITH_REAL_FRICTION`
- `INVISIBLE_UNIT_MAINTENANCE_LABOR`

Transition:

> **`REVISE / QUALIFY`**

Preferred rule:

> **A durable antagonistic relationship can contain genuine care and genuine grievance at the same time. Not every attack should be translated into hidden affection merely because the relationship survives it.**

Any universal historical shorthand that makes IIIX “secretly caring underneath everything” should therefore be:

> **`DOWNGRADE`**

The mature relation is more difficult and more credible if actual resentment remains part of it.

## 3.3 miho's stabilizing role gains material texture

Historical prose already treats miho as IIIX's strategist, risk manager, disciplinarian, and person who makes the unit survivable. The onsen event adds mundane maintenance work to that model. Her burden is not only abstract emotional or strategic labor; it can include literal cleanup and compensating for the others' habits.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

This advances `OPEN-05` modestly by showing that internal burden can be distributed unequally even without explicit managerial exploitation.

# 4. `灼熱のBEACH ～イライラMAX ⅢX～` — media agency, tactical solidarity, and the ethical limit of persona

## 4.1 Historical IIIX media/materiality thesis is strongly corroborated

Historical analysis correctly treats IIIX as unusually conscious of television, SNS circulation, ratings, contracts, sponsorship, image, and institutional leverage. The beach event supplies unusually concrete evidence:

- IIIX is rebuilding non-live exposure after its earlier program ended;
- Fran identifies the need for broader media visibility;
- all three deliberately train against opponent-specific weaknesses;
- miho studies the program's recurring patterns;
- kana proposes a live, unscripted wager;
- miho uses ratings pressure and real-time social-media attention to make refusal costly.

Admit:

- `POST_FALL_MEDIA_REBUILD`
- `SELECTIVE_DELIBERATE_PRACTICE`
- `LIVE_MEDIA_LEVERAGE`

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

This is excellent source evidence for IIIX's media sophistication, though later sources remain better authority for the mature public-authenticity model.

## 4.2 Early victory-coordinated solidarity remains temporally bounded

Fran's statement that IIIX shares one justice—winning—accurately describes this stage's coordination logic. It corroborates the older “survival through proof / absolute victory” model.

Admit:

- `VICTORY_COORDINATED_ADVERSARIAL_SOLIDARITY`

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + REVISE_BY_TEMPORAL_MATURATION`**

The later U.S. dissolution/re-authorization material remains stronger authority on why IIIX ultimately chooses to remain together. Do not project the mature reason backward.

## 4.3 Public professionalism may conceal conduct that is not ethically answerable

Kana's PK sequence is especially useful as a negative control. She is openly furious at Kano, mutters `くたばれ` immediately before the kick, strikes Kano in the face, appears to the manager to celebrate, and then performs intense public concern.

The source does not contain a direct confession that she deliberately aimed for Kano's face, so the correct evidence state is:

> **STRONG INFERENCE — `RETALIATORY_INTENT_BEHIND_PROFESSIONAL_MASK`**

Do not promote this to textual fact.

However, the sequence still changes the authenticity model. A public mask can be professionally effective and internally coherent while concealing retaliatory or manipulative intent.

Historical prose that said persona is not inherently false remains valuable, but effectiveness/coherence alone cannot be the test.

Transition:

> **`REVISE + STRENGTHEN`**

The preferred current rule remains E2-B1's **authenticity as answerable construction**.

E2 support-deferred adds a further limit:

> **`AUTHENTICITY_WITHOUT_ETHICAL_EXONERATION`**
>
> **A representation may accurately express a person or relationship without making the represented conduct good, harmless, or beyond criticism.**

And:

> **`AUTHENTIC_GRAMMAR_WITHOUT_MORAL_SANITIZATION`**
>
> **IIIX's antagonistic relational grammar can be genuinely theirs without every antagonistic act becoming affectionate, constructive, or ethically acceptable.**

These are not replacements for `CURATED_ADVERSARIAL_AUTHENTICITY`; they are guardrails that stop authenticity from becoming moral absolution.

## 4.4 Friendship denial remains precursor evidence, not final vocabulary

The event again ends with all three rejecting Makino's suggestion that they are close: `仲良くないってば！！`.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`** as precursor evidence.

Later E2-B1's explicit distinction—`信頼` accepted, `友情` denied—remains the preferred mature relational vocabulary.
