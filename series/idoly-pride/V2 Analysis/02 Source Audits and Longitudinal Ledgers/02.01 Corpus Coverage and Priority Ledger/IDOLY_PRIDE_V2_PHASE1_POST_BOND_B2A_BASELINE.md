# 7. Labor and care model after B2-A

B2-A sharpens an important distinction.

There are at least three analytically different routes into unhealthy workload:

1. **institutional pressure or exploitation**;
2. **wound-driven self-overwork**;
3. **self-authored devotion overload**, where multiple genuinely valued commitments accumulate beyond bodily sustainability.

These can coexist but should not be collapsed.

Current rule:

> **Authorship is relevant to legitimacy; sustainability is a separate question.**

This prevents two errors:

- treating every exhausting schedule as externally coercive;
- treating a chosen schedule as safe merely because the person chose it.

Rui/Rio's six-hour run is support evidence for mutually escalating professional pride. Shizuku provides the stronger plural-role mechanism.
