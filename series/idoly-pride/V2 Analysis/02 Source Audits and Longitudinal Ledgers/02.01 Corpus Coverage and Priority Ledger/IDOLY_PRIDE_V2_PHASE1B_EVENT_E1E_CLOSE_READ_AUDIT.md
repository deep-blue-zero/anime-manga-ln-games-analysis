# 1. Executive result

The strongest cross-event result is:

> **Maturity in IDOLY PRIDE does not mean eliminating hierarchy, grief, rivalry, ideology, or differentiated roles. It means making them contestable without making the person who contests them disposable.**

This produces a late-series constitutional form of the project’s governing ethic of **answerable interdependence**.

Across E1-E:

- Tsuki retains a center office while making access to that office genuinely contestable among all five members;
- losing the center does not abolish Kotono or Suzu’s legitimacy;
- winning the center does not require Nagisa to become Kotono or Suzu;
- supportive members can admit painful ambition without betraying care;
- Kotono’s relationship to Mana becomes more plural without becoming less intimate;
- Sakura can explicitly deny present bodily indwelling while preserving meaningful felt presence;
- DoriKyun can articulate a serious anti-exploitation diagnosis while their coercive purification method remains morally objectionable;
- SUNNY PEACE can voluntarily seek adversarial pressure, use it, defeat it, and remain SUNNY PEACE;
- DoriKyun themselves begin to admit that industry change may emerge through the existence of strong counterexamples rather than through their exclusive power to destroy the unworthy.

The resulting preferred concept is:

> **ANSWERABLE CONSTITUTIONALISM**

This is an analytical term, not source vocabulary. It describes mature structures whose offices, inherited meanings, competitive rules, and ideological claims can be challenged from within without requiring total institutional collapse.

# 2. Event 2026_002 — ぶつかり愛のCenter Battle！
## Center as contestable office rather than personal property

## 2.1 The decisive move is procedural

The event’s most important development is not merely that Nagisa becomes center.

Kotono proposes that if everyone’s desire remains intact, the center for the new song should be selected from **all five** Tsuki members. Suzu reiterates the principle that center belongs to the idol best suited to occupy it and that anyone may challenge.

That converts what had earlier been a volatile personal succession problem—Kotono versus Suzu—into a **repeatable internal procedure**.

The mature Tsuki position is therefore stronger than:

> Kotono can let Suzu be center.

It is:

> **No current member possesses center by ontological right. All five retain standing to claim it, lose it, reclaim it, or support another member occupying it.**

This is the strongest evidence so far for resolving `OPEN-15` within Tsuki no Tempest.

### Claim transition

Historical formulation:

> Center is no longer exclusively Kotono’s; Suzu proves the office can move.

E1-E transition:

> **STRENGTHEN / GENERALIZE WITHIN TSUKI** — center contestability is not merely a Kotono/Suzu exception. It is now a five-member constitutional principle.

Do **not** yet generalize this as a universal rule for every IDOLY PRIDE unit.

---

## 2.2 Nagisa — relational individuation rather than separation

Nagisa’s most important interior statement is that she wants to stand at center:

> not as Kotono’s replacement, but **as one idol herself**.

That matters because Nagisa’s development has never required emotional disengagement from Kotono. Her mature selfhood does not come through `Kotono離れ` in the strong sense of detachment.

Instead:

> **Nagisa becomes more individuated while remaining deeply relational.**

The center win therefore confirms a major historical reading: support becomes rivalry, and rivalry becomes proof that attachment no longer requires subordination.

Historical V1/V1.5 treatment already understood Nagisa’s center victory as evidence that she can stand beside and even surpass Kotono without ceasing to love or support her.

E1-E transition:

> **PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN**

V2 adds a procedural layer: Nagisa’s selfhood is not only emotionally recognized. It is institutionally ratified by winning a contest whose legitimacy the previous center figures accept.

---

## 2.3 Kotono — losing office without losing self

Kotono’s response to Nagisa is especially mature:

> she is frustrated, but has no complaint; this time Nagisa truly surpassed her.

The important word is not kindness. It is **recognition**.

Kotono does not convert defeat into:

- humiliation;
- identity crisis;
- loss of leadership standing;
- proof that her previous centerhood was false;
- an obligation to withdraw.

This is a clean late-series instance of the E1-D competitive correction:

> **defeat reports an outcome; it does not acquire jurisdiction over the loser’s right to continue.**

Kotono remains a leader, rival, and future claimant.

---

## 2.4 Suzu — office as aspiration rather than possession

Suzu’s response similarly prevents the earlier center-transfer arc from being flattened into a permanent replacement narrative.

She recognizes Nagisa’s victory and intends to win again later.

Thus Suzu’s earlier center legitimacy is neither revoked nor frozen into permanent ownership.

The mature office has memory without hereditary right.

---

## 2.5 Mei — support without self-erasure

Mei supplies the event’s sharpest emotional counterweight to easy ensemble harmony.

After congratulating Nagisa, she privately breaks down and admits:

> she did not merely want to be “part of” the center structure; **she wanted to be the center**.

This is crucial because Mei is often coded as emotionally generous, playful, and relational. The story refuses to make those traits proof that she lacks competitive desire.

Preferred concept:

> **NON_ZERO_SUM_CARE_AMBITION**

Mei can:

- love Nagisa;
- celebrate Nagisa;
- feel hurt by losing;
- admire Suzu and Mana;
- still want the central position herself.

Care and ambition are not mutually exclusive moral identities.

This is a useful correction against ensemble readings that inadvertently make the most supportive characters ethically admirable only when they want less.

---

## 2.6 Saki — contribution outside center without renunciation

Saki states that there are ways to contribute other than center while still retaining future competitive intent.

This is a similarly important distinction:

> **acknowledging plural forms of contribution is not the same as renouncing access to prestigious office.**

The event therefore avoids a consolation hierarchy in which non-centers are told that they should simply enjoy invisible support work.

After the selection, other members retain meaningful authorship over:

- staging;
- presentation;
- MC;
- costume/performance support;
- the unit’s final realized form.

Center is differentiated authority, not total authorship.

---

## 2.7 Final event judgment

`ぶつかり愛のCenter Battle！` is **CORE confirmed**.

Its mature contribution is not merely “Nagisa center.” It establishes:

> **CONTESTABLE_CENTER_OFFICE + RELATIONAL_INDIVIDUATION + NON_ZERO_SUM_CARE_AMBITION**

`OPEN-15` is therefore:

> **RESOLVED FOR TSUKI NO TEMPEST / OPEN FOR CROSS-UNIT GENERALIZATION**

# 3. Event 2026_003 — あなたに捧ぐWith your songs
## Mana as plural person, reciprocal sister, and memorial co-author

## 3.1 The event’s question is not whether Kotono still loves Mana

Kotono is writing `With your songs` as an answer to Mana’s `song for you`.

The event does not ask Kotono to weaken sisterly attachment. It asks whether a mature answer to Mana can include **more Mana than the sister Kotono privately knew**.

Kotono eventually articulates the distinction herself:

- she had been imagining Mana as the family member who loved her;
- but Mana was also an idol who made many people smile and became meaningful to lives Kotono did not witness.

This produces:

> **PLURAL_PERSON_MEMORY**

A dead person may remain one person while legitimately existing in multiple relational archives.

This strongly converges with E1-A’s memorial pluralism and E1-C’s memory-palimsest model.

---

## 3.2 Suzu as epistemic bridge

Suzu is uniquely suited to help because she knows an aspect of Mana that Kotono cannot fully access from sisterhood alone:

> Mana as adored idol.

Kotono, conversely, knows domestic Mana in ways Suzu cannot.

Their musical collaboration allows two incomplete but legitimate views to meet.

Suzu’s fandom is therefore not merely comic worship. At mature scale, it becomes **epistemic contribution**.

This preserves the distinction between:

- loving someone privately;
- knowing someone professionally/publicly;
- receiving someone through art.

No single position monopolizes the whole person.

---

## 3.3 The piano memory humanizes Mana without diminishing her

The childhood piano material reveals that Mana was not naturally proficient enough to effortlessly teach Kotono. She practiced privately, then made the sheet music playful so her younger sister would want to continue.

This is a particularly good example of how E1-E treats demythologization:

> **showing labor does not destroy miracle; it gives miracle human structure.**

Mana’s care is neither pure effortless genius nor abstract sainthood. It involves work, frustration, adaptation, and attention to another person.

---

## 3.4 The pendant reverses Kotono’s grief asymmetry

The pendant sequence provides one of the most consequential late corrections to Kotono’s self-conception.

Mana had told Kotono that even if the form changed, their matching relationship remained. Later, Kotono finds Mana’s corresponding pendant in the bag Mana carried at the NEXT VENUS final.

Kotono realizes:

> **she had been one of Mana’s supports too.**

This revises a grief structure in which Kotono repeatedly imagines herself as:

- the abandoned younger sister;
- the needy one;
- the one who failed to understand;
- the one who must repay or complete something afterward.

The relationship was already reciprocal before Mana’s death.

Preferred term:

> **RECIPROCAL_SISTER_SUPPORT**

Historical Tsuki and Kotono synthesis already identified this as a major reversal.

E1-E transition:

> **STRENGTHEN**

The primary event fully supports it.

---

## 3.5 Haruko, Rio, and Sakura reconstruct idol Mana

Kotono seeks testimony from people positioned differently around Mana.

Their memories supply important corrections to impossible legend:

- Mana could be frustrated;
- Mana could be competitively shaken by Rio/LizNoir;
- Mana trained late;
- Mana had private difficulty she did not show to fans.

Rio’s insight is especially important: Mana’s ability to produce songs that could `寄り添う`—stay beside hurting people—may have depended on understanding pain rather than existing above it.

This does not require the corpus to condemn professional concealment or celebrate it uncritically.

It does require rejecting:

> perfect public image = absence of private struggle.

E1-C already established that public performance and private truth are not simple opposites. `With your songs` applies that framework retrospectively to Mana herself.

---

## 3.6 Polyphonic memorial authorship

The final answer song includes multiple people touched by Mana.

Kotono explicitly frames the result as:

> **my feelings, our feelings**.

This yields:

> **POLYPHONIC_MEMORIAL_AUTHORSHIP**

The answer to Mana is not democratized into ownerlessness. Kotono remains the initiating sister-author. But the song becomes stronger by admitting:

- Suzu’s fan relation;
- Haruko’s colleague/friend relation;
- Rio’s rival relation;
- Sakura’s bodily/artistic inheritance relation.

This is fully compatible with E1-A’s rule:

> provenance may expand without becoming erased.

Historical V1/V1.5 interpretation that Kotono’s final answer becomes plural is therefore:

> **PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN**

---

## 3.7 Sakura and the supernatural boundary

Sakura supplies one of the most important metaphysical guardrails in the entire corpus:

> Mana no longer dwells in her heart, yet her chest feels intensely warm, **as if** Mana were beside her.

The sentence does two things at once.

It **narrows** one supernatural interpretation:

> Sakura’s current heart should not be described as an ongoing container in which Mana presently resides.

And it **preserves** phenomenological openness:

> Sakura experiences Mana-like presence in a meaningful embodied form.

Haruko and Suzu similarly speak or think in continuing-address language.

The correct analytical classification is:

> **FELT_PRESENCE_WITHOUT_BODILY_POSSESSION**

This is neither:

- proof that Mana’s ghost no longer exists;
- proof that Mana literally remains beside them;
- proof of current heart possession.

`OPEN-17` therefore remains open, but its bodily-container branch is materially downgraded.

### Historical stress-test result

Historical prose already noticed the crucial line and correctly treated it as late Mana-inheritance evidence. The V2 improvement is not reversal but **metaphysical precision**.

Transition:

> **STRENGTHEN / QUALIFY**

---

## 3.8 Final event judgment

`あなたに捧ぐWith your songs` is **CORE confirmed**.

Its mature contribution is:

> **PLURAL_PERSON_MEMORY + RECIPROCAL_SISTER_SUPPORT + POLYPHONIC_MEMORIAL_AUTHORSHIP + FELT_PRESENCE_WITHOUT_BODILY_POSSESSION**

It does not complete Mana by replacing her.

It demonstrates that Mana can remain loved, plural, and artistically active in memory without retaining unilateral jurisdiction over the living.

# 4. Event 2026_007 — PRIDE貫く頂点への道標
## DoriKyun’s anti-exploitation ideology and its late self-limitation

## 4.1 SUNNY PEACE seeks the pressure voluntarily

The opening is critical because DoriKyun does not arrive to terrorize an unsuspecting Sunny.

Sakura repeatedly requests joint practice.

Her reasoning is explicit:

- Tsuki grew through its collision with DoriKyun;
- TRINITYAiLE also developed through DoriKyun pressure;
- SUNNY PEACE has become BIG4, but Sakura does not believe BIG4 status equals the final summit.

This gives us a clean case of:

> **VOLUNTARY_ADVERSARIAL_TRAINING**

Sunny is not naïvely inviting abuse. It is seeking a known difficult stimulus because it has evidence that the stimulus can reveal developmental gaps.

That matters for judging DoriKyun’s usefulness.

---

## 4.2 Sakura rejects zero-sum developmental logic

When DoriKyun interprets Sunny’s request as using them as a stepping stone, Sakura rejects the simplification.

Her desired model is:

> both units become stronger and thereby create better live battles.

This yields:

> **RECIPROCAL_ADVERSARIAL_DEVELOPMENT**

Sakura preserves competition while refusing the idea that only one party can benefit from the collision.

This is structurally continuous with:

- competition without erasure;
- non-terminal defeat;
- reciprocal rivalry;
- answerable interdependence.

---

## 4.3 DoriKyun remains coercive even when invited

The event must not be sanitized merely because Sakura requested training.

DoriKyun imposes:

- a five-match format;
- public humiliation stakes;
- reputational threats if Sunny refuses;
- their own adversarial rules.

The pedagogy remains coercive and image-sensitive.

Thus the event does **not** prove:

> harsh humiliation is the optimal or necessary way to develop idols.

It proves only that Sunny can sometimes deliberately enter this pressure environment and extract value from it.

---

## 4.4 SUNNY PEACE wins by remaining plural

Sunny initially loses, then wins through capacities distributed across the unit:

- Shizuku’s extraordinary idol-domain memory;
- Sakura/Chisa coordination;
- prior survival/problem-solving experience;
- mutual support;
- adaptive use of distinct member strengths.

DoriKyun ultimately recognizes Sunny as `本物のアイドル`.

The recognition is important because Sunny does not become harder by imitating DoriKyun’s cruelty.

The event therefore strengthens a long-running franchise argument:

> **softness is not the opposite of strength when softness is embodied by skill, memory, courage, coordination, and durable will.**

Historical SUNNY PEACE analysis already described the unit as five forms of received and returned support.

E1-E transition:

> **STRENGTHEN / LATE VALIDATION**

BIG4 does not terminate Sunny’s development, and adversity does not require identity conversion.

---

## 4.5 DoriKyun’s stated industry program

E1-E gives direct source-language support for a claim earlier historical analysis had often inferred from behavior.

DoriKyun explicitly targets:

- adults who treat idols merely as money-making tools;
- idols who gather around and reproduce that system;
- an industry they believe requires `浄化`—purification;
- a future in which idol work becomes the world’s greatest entertainment.

This is not generic villain rhetoric.

It contains a serious diagnosis:

> **idol labor can be commodified by adults whose interests are not identical to the performers’ flourishing.**

But the proposed cure remains dangerous:

> eliminate/crush the weak or compromised actors and purify the field through adversarial force.

Preferred analytical category:

> **AUTHORITARIAN_ANTI_EXPLOITATION**

The label preserves both halves:

- the anti-exploitation motive is real;
- the purificatory jurisdiction is authoritarian and dispositional toward other people’s futures.

Historical DoriKyun analysis already understood the duo as hostile to industry disposability and interested in world-scale entertainment.

E1-E transition:

> **STRENGTHEN** — the diagnosis moves from strong behavioral inference to explicit stated program.

---

## 4.6 The largest historical revision: useful is not necessary

Earlier historical prose repeatedly used language such as:

> DoriKyun as **necessary external pressure**.

That formulation now requires revision.

E1-E shows:

- DoriKyun have been useful to Tsuki;
- DoriKyun have been useful to TRINITYAiLE;
- Sunny itself seeks their pressure because of that demonstrated usefulness;
- DoriKyun can identify weakness and force specificity;
- DoriKyun can become a valuable adversarial training environment.

But the event also shows DoriKyun beginning to consider that idols like Tsuki, TRINITYAiLE, and SUNNY PEACE may themselves alter the industry.

This undermines the stronger claim that DoriKyun’s destructive/cultivating pressure is **necessary** for industry maturation.

The revised conclusion is:

> **DoriKyun is a powerful and sometimes uniquely useful developmental catalyst, but not a universal necessary condition of growth.**

Claim transition:

> **REVISE / DOWNGRADE NECESSITY CLAIM**

This directly advances `OPEN-06`.

Preferred status:

> `OPEN-06` = **PARTIALLY RESOLVED — USEFUL_BUT_NOT_NECESSARY**

What remains open is the wider question of how often their particular pressure mode produces net benefit versus avoidable harm.

---

## 4.7 Counterexample reform

DoriKyun’s most significant late ideological movement is not repentance.

They do not become warm, join Hoshimi, apologize for their entire history, or abandon combat.

Instead, strong rivals create a counterexample to their monopoly claim:

> perhaps the industry can change because more idols become powerful, serious, and self-authored—not because DoriKyun alone purifies it.

Preferred term:

> **COUNTEREXAMPLE_REFORM**

A rival can change an ideology not by persuading its holder into similarity, but by existing as successful evidence that the ideology’s exclusive causal story is incomplete.

This is a particularly mature form of rivalry.

---

## 4.8 DoriKyun / Makino / Hoshimi boundary

DoriKyun acknowledges Makino’s ability to develop real idols and tells him, in effect, to continue doing so.

Yet DoriKyun still refuses Hoshimi absorption.

This preserves the historical conclusion that some rivalry remains productive precisely because it stays external.

But the preferred wording after E1-E is no longer:

> Hoshimi **needs** DoriKyun outside.

It is:

> **Hoshimi can benefit from an external rival it does not absorb, and DoriKyun can recognize Hoshimi’s developmental legitimacy without surrendering independence.**

Transition:

> **PRESERVE STRUCTURAL EXTERNALITY / REVISE NECESSITY**

# 5. Cross-event synthesis
## Answerable constitutionalism

E1-E reveals a structural family among three apparently different problems.

### Tsuki center

A prestigious office remains legitimate because:

- it can be contested;
- loss does not erase prior legitimacy;
- non-holders retain authorship and future standing.

### Mana memory

A beloved dead person remains meaningful because:

- no single witness owns the whole person;
- memory can be expanded through other witnesses;
- inherited songs can be answered without replacement;
- felt presence need not become metaphysical coercion.

### DoriKyun ideology

A strong rival remains valuable because:

- its diagnosis can be answered;
- its method can be refused;
- its pressure can be entered voluntarily;
- successful counterexamples can revise its ideological monopoly.

The common rule is:

> **A mature system preserves strong forms while preserving routes of answer.**

This is the late-event constitutional form of `answerable interdependence`.
