# 1. `event_2022_004` — 愛歌う星の継承者
## Primary classification: `STABILIZATION + UNIQUE_SUCCESSION_MECHANISM + CHARACTER_CHRONOLOGY`

### 1.1 Mana inheritance is not restricted to blood, formal succession, or obvious public symbolism

In `st-eve-2204-contest-001`, Suzu is introduced as an extreme Mana admirer whose daily routine includes repeated viewing of Mana performances and whose favorite Mana song is `Precious`.

When Hoshimi receives an offer for a Mana tribute program, the obvious public-facing candidates would include:

- Kotono, Mana's younger sister;
- Sakura, who has a deep connection to Mana's legacy;
- Rio, Mana's historical rival.

Yet Makino selects Suzu for the solo `Precious` slot.

The decisive explanation arrives in `st-eve-2204-contest-005`:

> `すずは麻奈の曲を星見プロの誰よりも聴いていた`
>
> `そして、麻奈を心の底から尊敬して、憧れている`

and:

> `いつか聴いた、すずが歌う『Precious』は……俺の心に響いたんだ。だから指名した`

This supports:

> **`AFFINITY_BASED_INHERITANCE`**

Definition:

> **A legacy may pass through chosen attention, love, study, and artistic response rather than only through kinship or formally privileged proximity.**

This does **not** displace the existing Mana inheritance architecture. It gives one specific mechanism by which the inheritance field remains open to a non-kin successor.

### 1.2 Fandom expertise is real but partial

Suzu initially assumes that intense exposure to Mana makes faithful reproduction the correct goal. In `st-eve-2204-contest-002`, she and Mei repeatedly watch `Precious`; the more Suzu listens, the more Mana's technical superiority destabilizes her confidence.

By `st-eve-2204-contest-005`, Makino's annotated score contains details about:

- song characteristics;
- technical approaches;
- Suzu's own vocal habits;
- observations derived from Makino's long proximity to Mana's singing.

Suzu realizes that despite believing she understood Mana's music perfectly, she had not exhausted its meaning or technique.

Admit:

> **`MULTIPERSPECTIVAL_LEGACY_KNOWLEDGE`**

A fan's long attention, a manager's professional memory, a sibling's memory, and a rival's history can all be genuine without any single position exhausting the legacy.

This strongly stabilizes bounded epistemic jurisdiction and provenance-without-ownership already established elsewhere.

### 1.3 Suzu moves from worship toward professional peerhood

The most important character transition is not successful imitation.

After performing `Precious`, Suzu states in `st-eve-2204-contest-006`:

> `私は麻奈様の大大大ファンですけど――`
>
> `今は同じステージに立つアイドルだということに！`

She then declares that she intends to become an idol greater than Mana and asks Makino to build the next legend with her.

Admit:

> **`ADMIRATION_TO_PEERHOOD`**

Definition:

> **A predecessor can remain genuinely revered while the successor ceases to treat reverence as a reason to remain permanently below her.**

This is preferred early evidence for Suzu's transition from Mana worship toward competitive professional authorship.

It stabilizes later succession, non-disposable standing, and inheritance-without-substitution arguments rather than owning their mature formulation.

### 1.4 Overstudy becomes self-defeating

Suzu stays awake repeatedly replaying the performance and arrives unable to sing normally. Makino stops the session when her voice is not functioning as usual.

This is minor but direct stabilization for:

- chosen effort becoming self-defeating;
- professional care requiring interruption when functioning is impaired;
- `OPEN-03` proportional intervention at a low-severity boundary.

Do not elevate this small case above the stronger Mana/Sumire intervention evidence already admitted.

### 1.5 Romantic misunderstanding is character texture, not a governing romance claim

Suzu misparses Makino's statement `すずが、誰にも負けないくらい好きだからだ` as a confession to her, when the intended object is **Suzu's love for Mana**.

The misunderstanding creates genuine embarrassment and hints at Suzu's own attraction, and Rio's closing joke leaves additional teasing ambiguity.

However:

- Makino's selection is explicitly professional;
- Kotono and Rio both reject the idea that he would insert romantic favoritism into the work decision;
- the event does not establish a mutual romance.

Classify as:

> `CHARACTER_RELATIONAL_TEXTURE / DO_NOT_PROMOTE_TO_ROMANCE_CANON`

# 2. `event_2022_012` — 君と願う月灯の祝祭
## Primary classification: `UNIQUE_TEMPORAL_REAUTHORING + STABILIZATION + UNIT_MEMORY`

### 2.1 Mature Tsuki collaboration already contains procedural memory

In `st-eve-2212-race-001`, the members explicitly compare the new Christmas-song writing process with `恋と花火`. The new process is smoother because accumulated collaborative experience now exists.

This is useful stabilization for:

- distributed creative authority;
- unit process memory;
- maturation through repeated co-authorship.

No new mature creative-authority doctrine is needed here.

### 2.2 Benevolent secrecy can still temporarily injure belonging

The four non-Kotono members prepare a birthday surprise while hiding it from Kotono. The motive is affectionate and the surprise succeeds.

But the secrecy produces a real interim relational cost. In `st-eve-2212-race-003`, Kotono asks why nobody will tell her anything and withdraws in visible hurt.

This supports a bounded caution:

> **`BENEVOLENT_SECRECY_RELATIONAL_COST`**

Good intention does not erase the lived experience of being excluded from a shared communicative field.

This should **not** be inflated into a prohibition on surprise, secrecy, or temporary information asymmetry. The source resolves the tension successfully and retrospectively explains the exclusion.

### 2.3 Kotono understands a piece of Mana's happiness through analogous experience

During the live birthday surprise in `st-eve-2212-race-004`, Kotono recalls Mana once being surprised by an audience on her birthday and later receiving a properly planned celebration.

Kotono says:

> `みなさんのおかげで、お姉ちゃんがその時にどれぐらい幸せだったのか、少し分かった気がします`

Admit:

> **`ANALOGICAL_EXPERIENTIAL_INHERITANCE`**

Definition:

> **A living successor may understand part of a deceased person's remembered experience by entering an analogous situation, without claiming identity with that person or exhaustive knowledge of her interiority.**

The phrase `少し分かった気がします` is epistemically restrained and should remain so.

### 2.4 A painful anniversary becomes plural rather than erased

In `st-eve-2212-race-005`, Kotono explains that Christmas and her birthday had never been uncomplicated sources of joy because the date also made her remember `あの日のこと`.

The event does not require that older meaning to disappear. Instead, the new celebration adds:

- a public memory with Tsuki and its audience;
- a private party;
- a retrospective album of the group's path;
- a shared letter;
- a song containing the phrase `この世に生まれてくれて本当にありがとう`.

Admit:

> **`ANNIVERSARY_REAUTHORING_WITHOUT_ERASURE`**

Definition:

> **A date burdened by painful memory can acquire additional authored meanings through later relationships without requiring the original grief to be denied or overwritten.**

This is the strongest unique contribution of the event.

### 2.5 Recognition of Kotono exceeds her center function

The letter explicitly acknowledges that the other members cannot fully know what it is like to keep running at the group's center:

> `グループの中心で走り続ける大変さを私達は知ることが出来ません`

Yet they can witness her effort and say:

> `琴乃ちゃん、生まれてきてくれてありがとう`

Admit two bounded stabilizations:

- **`EPISTEMICALLY_BOUNDED_RECOGNITION`** — one can recognize effort without pretending to inhabit another person's burden;
- **`EXISTENTIAL_RECOGNITION_BEYOND_ROLE`** — Kotono is affirmed as someone whose existence matters, not merely as Tsuki's center or Mana's sister.

These stabilize `NON_PERFORMATIVE_RECOGNITION`, `RELATIONAL_INDIVIDUATION`, and bounded epistemic jurisdiction already owned by stronger material.

# 3. `event_2022_011` — 舌戦開幕！賛美の湯
## Primary classification: `IIIX_RELATIONAL_PRECURSOR + UNIQUE_FRICTION_TEXTURE + RECOVERY_CHRONOLOGY`

### 3.1 IIIX is already in a recovery-and-touring phase

The opening manager narration establishes that IIIX is touring, its popularity is recovering, and venues are filling.

Admit:

> **`IIIX_RECOVERY_TOURING_BASELINE`**

This is a chronology locator, not a mature IIIX thesis.

### 3.2 Forced conventional friendliness is less natural than antagonistic contact

The supernatural/comedic premise forces IIIX to avoid arguing for one night. The result is not comfortable intimacy but strained silence.

Mei observes that merely not arguing is different from actually getting along. Haruko later concludes:

> `そのくらいの距離が楽なのよ、きっと`

The event therefore provides strong precursor evidence for what later E2-B1 names `CURATED_ADVERSARIAL_AUTHENTICITY` and `TRUST_WITHOUT_FRIENDSHIP_LABEL`.

Preferred precursor term:

> **`COMFORTABLE_ADVERSARIAL_DISTANCE`**

Do **not** translate this into conventional friendship simply because Mei and Haruko use `仲がいい` as an outside interpretation.

The members themselves return to ordinary conflict and later sources become the stronger authority for their own relational labels.

### 3.3 Compliments can be absorbed into the adversarial grammar without replacing it

A forced praise exercise initially becomes another venue for kana to provoke the others. Yet it reveals real observations:

- kana notices Fran's private presentation discipline and miho's self-care;
- Fran acknowledges kana's popularity;
- miho acknowledges kana's ability to learn difficult choreography quickly.

At the next day's venue, Fran and miho spontaneously compliment each other's hair/skin before kana complains about being excluded from the praise.

Admit:

> **`PRAISE_WITHIN_ADVERSARIAL_GRAMMAR`**

Positive recognition need not convert IIIX into a conventionally affectionate unit. It can become another move inside the same combative relational language.

### 3.4 The hostility contains real grievances and invisible maintenance work

The event is valuable precisely because it prevents romantic over-reading of IIIX conflict.

While drunk, miho complains that:

- she is the one who cleans up garbage when the unit leaves dressing rooms;
- the others continue teasing her about a rehearsal mistake for months;
- she resents their lack of serious practice because their natural competence means they still do not fail onstage.

Admit:

> **`ADVERSARIAL_GRAMMAR_WITH_REAL_FRICTION`**

and:

> **`INVISIBLE_UNIT_MAINTENANCE_LABOR`**

Definition:

> **IIIX's antagonistic relational style can be authentic and durable while still containing genuine irritation, unequal maintenance burdens, and grievances that should not be sentimentalized into harmless affection.**

This is a significant qualification to later mature IIIX relational theory.

It does not contradict `TRUST_WITHOUT_FRIENDSHIP_LABEL`; it prevents trust from being mistaken for absence of interpersonal cost.

### 3.5 Professional cohesion is compatible with personal conflict

Even after the chaotic night, the members immediately return to performance orientation:

> `いつだって全身全霊で――`
>
> `最強を証明し続ける`
>
> `全ては絶対勝利のために`

This stabilizes the earlier success-conditioned IIIX constitution already preserved by E2-C1.

# 4. `event_2023_007` — 灼熱のBEACH ～イライラMAX ⅢX～
## Primary classification: `IIIX_MEDIA_CHRONOLOGY + PRECURSOR_RELATIONAL_GRAMMAR + ETHICAL_TEXTURE`

### 4.1 IIIX is actively rebuilding non-live media exposure

The event places IIIX below the rapidly rising `恋々Poison` in current rank and shows Fran explicitly stating that IIIX wants more exposure outside live performance because its former program ended after the Himeno affair.

Admit:

> **`POST_FALL_MEDIA_REBUILD`**

This is useful chronology between the earlier fall/recovery state and later sophisticated IIIX media strategy.

### 4.2 Shared hostility can create rapid tactical solidarity without becoming ordinary friendship

All three members are individually provoked by the rival unit. Once miho is insulted as well, Fran states:

> `スリクスが共有する正義はただ一つ――勝つことだけよ`

The group then trains, plans, and executes collectively.

Admit as a precursor state:

> **`VICTORY_COORDINATED_ADVERSARIAL_SOLIDARITY`**

This is **not** the mature constitutional reason IIIX remains together after the later U.S. re-authorization arc.

It is evidence that competitive hostility already functioned as a reliable coordination mechanism before that maturation.

### 4.3 IIIX competence includes deliberate preparation, not effortless talent alone

Fran trains swimming because her assigned opponent has competitive experience. Kana uses a coach for the PK challenge. miho studies prior broadcast patterns and likely question forms.

This supports:

> **`SELECTIVE_DELIBERATE_PRACTICE`**

IIIX's high competence should not be narrated as effortless natural ability. Their self-image may foreground talent and superiority, but when stakes demand it they deliberately prepare around weaknesses and opponent structure.

### 4.4 IIIX exploits live-media constraints as institutional leverage

The unit arranges for kana to propose an unscripted regular-appearance wager on live television. miho then uses the show's declining ratings and real-time social-media trend as leverage when the producer objects.

Admit:

> **`LIVE_MEDIA_LEVERAGE`**

Definition:

> **IIIX understands that a live public format can constrain institutional refusal and strategically converts audience attention into bargaining power.**

This is effective professional agency, but the source does not present it as procedurally clean. It should not be converted into a general endorsement of rule-gaming.

### 4.5 Public professionalism and private retaliation remain sharply separated

kana is openly enraged by Kano's earlier insult. Immediately before the PK she mutters `くたばれ`; the ball then strikes Kano in the face, and the manager thinks he sees kana celebrate before she performs extreme public concern.

Classify cautiously:

> **STRONG INFERENCE — `RETALIATORY_INTENT_BEHIND_PROFESSIONAL_MASK`**

The sequence strongly suggests intentional retaliation, but the text does not supply a direct confession that kana aimed for the face.

This matters because it prevents later `STRATEGIC_AUTHENTICITY` from being projected backward as if every public/private IIIX split were already ethically answerable. Here, the polished public performance may actively conceal retaliatory intent.

### 4.6 The event repeats the friendship-label problem in an earlier form

After IIIX wins the three-part contest and then the live battle, Makino comments on their apparent unity. All three immediately answer:

> `仲良くないってば！！`

The closing fight resumes almost instantly.

This is strong stabilization for the later, more precise E2-B1 rule:

> preserve IIIX's own distinction between functional/trusting interdependence and ordinary `友情` language.

Do not let Makino's `仲がいい`-style outside reading become final relational authority.

# 5. Cross-tranche prospective synthesis

The support-deferred tranche does not require a replacement series thesis.

Its strongest archival contribution is to distinguish four functions that later synthesis should preserve:

## 5.1 Succession mechanism

`愛歌う星の継承者` shows that Mana's inheritance is open to **affinity-based participation**. Suzu's legitimacy comes from attention, admiration, response, and eventually the willingness to stand beside rather than permanently beneath the predecessor.

## 5.2 Temporal re-authoring

`君と願う月灯の祝祭` shows that relational healing can add new meaning to a painful recurring date without rewriting history or eliminating grief.

## 5.3 Relational friction under authentic antagonism

`舌戦開幕！賛美の湯` shows that IIIX's conflict style is not merely disguised sweetness. It includes real recognition **and** real maintenance burdens, resentment, and irritation.

## 5.4 Tactical solidarity before mature re-authorization

`灼熱のBEACH` shows IIIX functioning as a highly coordinated competitive coalition before the later U.S. crisis forces a deeper answer to why the unit should continue.

The resulting governing archival warning is:

> **Later maturation should not sanitize earlier states.**

A mature formulation may become stronger authority without erasing:

- the insecurity that preceded it;
- the strategic deception or masking that preceded it;
- the burdens hidden inside a durable relationship;
- or the narrower motives that once held a unit together.
