# 1. Governing thesis after E1-A

The Tier-A thesis remains:

> **answerable interdependence**

E1-A adds its inheritance/memory form:

> **INTERPRETATION — provenance matters, but origin does not grant permanent unilateral jurisdiction over the living future.**

People may receive:

- songs;
- bodies/hearts;
- institutions;
- rivalries;
- promises;
- professional opportunities;
- memories;
- public legends;

without being required to reproduce the origin exactly.

The ethical test is whether the living retain standing to:

- answer;
- reinterpret;
- refuse;
- compete;
- share;
- revise;
- continue.

# 2. Mana — revised frozen state

Mana remains:

- historical founder-level figure;
- beloved dead person;
- public legend;
- possible supernatural witness;
- source of professional language and inherited ethics.

E1-A adds:

### 2.1 Mana is not self-originating

Her idol vocation is materially scaffolded by:

- Saegusa's scouting;
- Rio/Aoi LizNoir's performance;
- Haruko's friendship, seniority, and rivalry;
- Makino's management;
- Hoshimi's institutional platform.

Therefore the preferred model is:

> **Mana as a highly consequential node inside reciprocal inheritance.**

Not:

> Mana as sole origin from which all later idol meaning flows.

### 2.2 Mana's memory may be plural

Hoshimi contains legitimate positions including:

- sister;
- manager;
- friend/senior;
- rival;
- heart recipient;
- later entrant/non-witness;
- skeptic.

Shared ritual does not require shared emotional intensity.

### 2.3 Literal supernatural persistence remains unresolved

E1-A supplies subjective experiences of guidance/presence and meaningful coincidence, but also explicit uncertainty and ordinary physical framing.

`OPEN-17` remains `OPEN`.

# 3. Hoshimi Production — revised frozen state

Tier-A description remains:

> **plural professional home/platform**

E1-A sharpens this to:

> **plural professional continuity platform**

This means Hoshimi can preserve provenance while allowing:

- membership to expand;
- former rivals to join;
- units to retain rivalry;
- memories to differ;
- songs to gain new performers;
- unfinished work to gain new authors;
- older idols to remain active while younger idols inherit stewardship;
- institutional scale and location to change.

### 3.1 Positive institutional evidence now admitted

- IIIX receives a professional re-entry route after reputational collapse.
- Former-star privilege is not automatically preserved.
- Hoshimi does not require IIIX to abandon competitive identity.
- Current idols collectively recognize staff/management labor through performance.

### 3.2 Limits

Do not infer that Hoshimi has solved:

- overwork;
- internal prestige pressure;
- contractual fairness;
- health risk;
- compensation;
- every form of paternalism or exploitation.

`OPEN-05` remains open.

# 5. LizNoir / Rio / Mana — revised inheritance state

E1-A admits the following:

- Rio initially treats Mana-song inheritance as protected lineage.
- She refuses because she fears inappropriate appropriation, not because Mana means nothing to her.
- `星の海の記憶` had already become part of Rio's own survival/return-to-song history.

> **Rio/Aoi LizNoir helped cause Mana's decision to become an idol, while Mana's song later helped Rio continue being one.**

Preferred model:

> **reciprocal lineage**

Not:

> one-way Mana → LizNoir inheritance.

E1-A supplies three admissible inheritance cases:

## 8.1 `song for you`

Original provenance remains, while later characters experience multiple legitimate directions of address.

## 8.2 `星の海の記憶`

> **Songs may become transferable without becoming ownerless.**

# 10. Memorial/performance ontology

E1-A allows the following high-confidence distinction:

> **Performance can be dialogic memorial practice without proving literal supernatural communication.**

- a song as answer;
- a stage as reunion;

without the analytical corpus converting those experiences into objective metaphysical facts.

This rule should govern later Mana/Yō/dead-person performance analysis unless a later primary source crosses the evidentiary threshold.
