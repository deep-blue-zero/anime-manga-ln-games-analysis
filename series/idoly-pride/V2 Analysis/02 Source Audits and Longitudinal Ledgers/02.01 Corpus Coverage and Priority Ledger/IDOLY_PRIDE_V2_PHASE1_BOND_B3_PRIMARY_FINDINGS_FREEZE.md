# 1. Tranche-level result — support density without architectural disruption

B3 does **not** overturn the post-B2-B architecture. Its main value is to make several mature propositions more concrete at the level of ordinary life:

- professional care often appears as teachable practice rather than abstract encouragement;
- idol labor coexists with school fatigue, leisure, hobbies, domestic labor, and private experimentation;
- public persona can be intensely constructed without exhausting private identity;
- asymmetrical roles remain answerable to context, consent, and reciprocal care;
- admiration and relational fantasy can be textually strong without proving reciprocal romance or exclusive relationship status;
- fan desire, performer self-consciousness, and audience reciprocity are visible in mundane settings rather than only major live/event arcs.

Admit no new universal constitutional thesis at tranche level. The correct B3 result is **stabilization and mechanism enrichment**.

# 2. 井川葵 — embodied mentoring, reciprocal vulnerability, and work/leisure permeability

## 2.1 Mentoring is practical, individualized, and confidence-bearing

`st-company-bond-aoi-01-01` and `01-03` place Aoi beside Ai in dance practice. Aoi does not treat Ai's current difficulty as proof of low ceiling; she explicitly rejects Ai's self-description as talentless, praises her physical capacity, and teaches through repeated embodied practice.

Admit as support for existing mentorship architecture:

> **`EMBODIED_MENTORING_WITHOUT_DEFICIT_FIXATION`**

Aoi's teaching style can be over-demanding because her own baseline is unusually high, but she notices fatigue, apologizes when she has pushed too far, and changes activity. This is not perfect pedagogical calibration; it is **answerable practice**.

## 2.2 Protector status is locally reversible

In `01-05`, Aoi begins by promising to protect Chisa during a dark-location visit. When a dog appears, Aoi becomes frightened and hides behind Chisa. Chisa answers that she will protect Aoi and suggests that the two protect each other.

Admit as support:

> `RECIPROCAL_PROTECTION_WITHOUT_ROLE_ERASURE`

This is a low-stakes but clean instance of post-B2-B `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`: Aoi can remain the physically capable senior while Chisa has legitimate standing to protect her in the domain where Aoi is vulnerable.

## 2.3 Professional fatigue and self-directed practice share the same body

`01-06` explicitly shows Aoi falling asleep outside bed and saying that recently she may spend almost all non-work time sleeping; she also jokes that she has fallen asleep during a shoot. `01-08`, by contrast, shows her going out to dance on an off night because her body feels underused.

Admit as support:

> `WORK_RECOVERY_AND_SELF_DIRECTED_PRACTICE_COEXIST`

Do not flatten this into either exploitation or pure workaholism. The source shows both accumulated fatigue and genuine desire to move/practice.

## 2.4 LizNoir style is internalized but not totalizing

Aoi playfully repeats formulations such as `リズノワに笑顔はいらない` in mundane contexts and then immediately talks about convenience-store ice cream, errands, domestic teasing, and Sakura's amusing reactions.

Admit as voice/persona support:

> `UNIT_STYLE_AS_INTERNALIZED_REGISTER_NOT_TOTAL_SELF`

The cool LizNoir idiom is available to Aoi even offstage, but ordinary warmth and absurd humor remain equally native to her.

# 3. 白石千紗 — consent, craft competence, and growing ordinary-world agency

## 3.1 Adultized idol presentation is framed through performer choice

`st-company-bond-chs-01-01` has Chisa and Shizuku looking at a photobook whose adult members use more revealing swimsuit imagery. Chisa wonders whether they may someday do something similar. Shizuku replies that Hoshimi Production respects the person's feelings and asks whether Chisa herself wants to.

Admit as support-level institutional evidence:

> **`PERFORMER_CHOICE_IN_ADULTIZED_PRESENTATION`**

The scene establishes that sexualized/adultized presentation is imaginable within professional idol work while performer preference remains normatively relevant. Chisa's answer is unresolved: she does not yet know.

Do not use the scene to infer a future gravure decision, nor reduce her joking body-growth anxiety to a stable body-image pathology.

## 3.2 Chisa has private craft expertise that produces self-confidence

`01-02` shows sewing, embroidery, wool-felt work, an already-used eco bag, and an ambitious shared-house tablecloth. Praise from Shizuku gives Chisa visible confidence and motivates a new mascot project.

Admit as support:

> `PRIVATE_CRAFT_COMPETENCE_AND_SELF_EFFICACY`

This is another instance of `PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`: competence and pride can form outside stage performance and still feed the person's broader self-concept.

## 3.3 Peer critique becomes easier as Chisa gains standing

`01-05` explicitly contrasts earlier Chisa—who might have refused Rui's invitation out of fear of disturbing her—with the present Chisa, who accepts joint practice and directly asks for severe criticism. Her calibration is shaped by Rei's familiar harshness, but she does not merely imitate Rei; she actively requests the feedback form she finds useful.

Admit as support:

> `SELF_AUTHORED_FEEDBACK_CALIBRATION`

The scene strengthens `TRUSTED_CONFLICT_CAPACITY` and the post-B2-B distinction between influence and ownership.

## 3.4 Ordinary social autonomy remains effortful but real

`01-08` shows Chisa entering a fashionable cafe alone despite visible anxiety about ordering conventions, unfamiliar tea names, and attracting attention. She struggles, makes mistakes, and nevertheless completes the outing. Kana helps indirectly without converting the episode into rescue.

Admit as ordinary-life support:

> `ORDINARY_WORLD_AGENCY_THROUGH_SMALL_RISK`

This is not a grand independence breakthrough; it is useful evidence that Chisa's development includes mundane social competence beyond sisterhood and the stage.

# 4. 赤崎こころ — deliberate persona construction, self-study, and status-aware restraint

## 4.1 School and idol labor are materially concurrent

`st-company-bond-kkr-01-01` shows Kokoro sleeping during school lunch because of work stretching into the previous night, doing schoolwork during location downtime, and openly wishing to graduate so she can focus on idol activity.

Admit as support:

> `SCHOOL_WORK_OPPORTUNITY_COST_KOKORO`

This establishes preference and fatigue at the present moment, not a universal claim that school is meaningless to her or that idol work should consume every life domain.

## 4.2 The cute persona is an object of deliberate technical study

`01-02` shows Kokoro analyzing natural versus calculated cuteness and observing Sumire as a model. `01-03` shows her independently studying VENUS Program scoring on an off day after hearing from miho that the system has quirks.

Admit:

> `SELF_DIRECTED_PERSONA_AND_PERFORMANCE_ANALYSIS`

Kokoro's theatrical self-presentation is not evidence of superficiality. She treats cuteness and scoring as crafts that can be observed, modeled, measured, and improved.

This strengthens `ANSWERABLE_CONSTRUCTION` / `MULTIREGISTER_AUTHENTICITY` without giving B3 thesis ownership.

## 4.3 Status difference changes what conduct is permissible

In `01-04`, Kokoro worries about working with a child actor. Rio has warned her that playing the same pranks she plays on peers could become `パワハラ` when directed toward the child. Kokoro takes the warning seriously enough to rehearse a different interaction style with Sumire.

Admit as support for bounded jurisdiction and professional safeguards:

> **`STATUS_AWARE_BEHAVIORAL_RESTRAINT`**

Rule:

> **A behavior tolerated among familiar peers does not automatically remain acceptable across age, status, or workplace-power differences.**

This is unusually explicit lower-tier evidence for contextual ethics inside entertainment labor.

## 4.4 Solitude does not equal social incapacity

In `01-03`, Kokoro first defensively insists she has friends, then admits that she often spends off days alone except for occasional LizNoir outings. Sumire immediately finds a shared pattern and invites her shopping.

Admit as texture:

> `SOLITUDE_WITHOUT_SOCIAL_INCAPACITY`

Do not infer severe loneliness or social isolation beyond what is stated.

## 4.5 Image labor and hobby skill remain part of ordinary self-authorship

`01-05` foregrounds hair maintenance and the extent to which Kokoro experiences a badly set hairstyle as materially disruptive to public self-presentation. `01-06` shows her developing genuine DIY skill out of prior event production work.

Admit as support:

- `EMBODIED_IMAGE_LABOR_KOKORO`;
- `PRODUCTION_SKILL_TO_PRIVATE_HOBBY_TRANSFER`.

The latter is a clean inverse of hobby-to-professional transfer: professional production can seed a private craft that remains enjoyable outside its original work use.

# 5. 伊吹渚 — domestic devotion, fantasy, and the limit of relationship inference

## 5.1 Nagisa's attention to Kotono is extraordinarily dense and habitual

`st-company-bond-ngs-01-02` reveals a near-daily `毎日琴乃ちゃん観察日記` recording Kotono's small behaviors, expressions, school mistakes, and moments of physical proximity. `01-08` adds a self-made eight-hour compilation of Kotono's especially cute performance moments.

TEXTUAL FACT:

- Nagisa devotes substantial private time to observing, archiving, and celebrating Kotono;
- the attention spans school and idol performance;
- Nagisa openly enjoys sharing that knowledge with others.

Admit as strong relational-texture support:

> `KOTONO_FOCUSED_PRIVATE_ARCHIVING_NAGISA`

Do not automatically classify this as reciprocal romance.

## 5.2 Romantic/domestic imagination is textually explicit on Nagisa's side

`01-03` establishes that Nagisa enjoys conventional romance narratives and says that, in a kissing situation, she would prefer the other person to initiate. More importantly, `01-08` explicitly describes Rio/Aoi's longstanding domestic closeness as Nagisa's `憧れ` and says she fantasizes about eventually cooking with Kotono, bathing together, sleeping in the same futon, and waking together. She asks Rio for advice `二人の将来のために`.

Admit:

> **`NAGISA_KOTONO_DOMESTIC_ROMANTIC_FANTASY`**

Evidence classification:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** Nagisa explicitly fantasizes about intimate/domestic future scenarios with Kotono.
- **STRONG INFERENCE:** the fantasy is strongly yuri/romance-coded and exceeds neutral teammate admiration.
- **OPEN / NOT ESTABLISHED:** Kotono's reciprocal romantic intent; a canonical dating relationship; exclusivity; actual enactment of these fantasies.

This is the tranche's clearest case where support mining adds meaningful relationship evidence while still requiring conservative category boundaries.

## 5.3 Aoi/Rio analogy is aspiration, not equivalence

`01-06` and `01-08` encourage Nagisa to analogize her history with Kotono to Aoi/Rio's childhood bond. But Aoi's own description includes a childhood age/caretaking asymmetry, including Rio bathing or caring for her when she was young.

Rule:

> **Nagisa's use of Aoi/Rio as an aspirational model does not establish structural equivalence between the two relationships.**

Admit as interpretive safeguard rather than new proposition.

## 5.4 Domestic competence is relationally shaped without losing authorship

In `01-06`, Nagisa notes that the sweets she makes have gradually shifted toward Kotono's tastes because she has long cooked for her. Aoi reports an analogous shift toward Rio's preferences.

Admit as support:

> `RELATIONAL_PREFERENCE_SHAPING_WITHOUT_SELF_ERASURE`

This is a small but useful mechanism for `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`.

# 6. 成宮すず — theatrical voice, audience desire, and reciprocal everyday care

## 6.1 The ojou-sama register is ordinary-life identity, not stage-only speech

Across all eight stories Suzu repeatedly uses the same aristocratic/theatrical speech cluster in school, dorm leisure, food secrecy, travel, fan interaction, and karaoke: `ですわ`, `ですの`, `くださいまし`, `ごきげんよう`, and related forms.

Admit as voice support:

> **`SUZU_OJOUSAMA_REGISTER_AS_PERSISTENT_PERSONAL_VOICE`**

This does not prove that the register is wholly unperformed; it establishes that it is not confined to formal idol presentation.

## 6.2 Sister language with Rei is affectionate analogy, not literal kinship

`01-01` has Suzu call Rei `私のお姉さま`; Rei explicitly rejects recognizing Suzu as a younger sister while still lending clothes and fixing her hair.

Admit as texture:

> `AFFECTIONATE_SIBLING_ANALOGY_WITH_EXPLICIT_NON_KINSHIP`

This parallels prior sibling-analogy cases and should not be promoted into family ontology.

## 6.3 Fan attention begins as status competition and becomes genuine exchange

`01-07` begins with Suzu envying Yu's crowd and trying theatrically to redirect attention toward herself. Once fans approach, however, Suzu becomes absorbed in conversation, recognizes Moon Tempest fans, signs, shakes hands, and loses track of time because she is enjoying the exchange.

Admit as support:

> `RECOGNITION_DESIRE_CAN_CONVERT_INTO_AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY`

The source does not erase vanity; it shows that the desire to be noticed and genuine pleasure in fan relation can coexist.

## 6.4 Everyday care is reciprocal and often comic rather than solemn

`01-05` shows Suzu bringing Sakura a forgotten lunch before work; Sakura then routes Suzu through a school tour mainly to give her a popular cutlet sandwich for the commute. `01-03` turns Suzu's boredom into extended game play with Rei, who becomes more competitive than Suzu expected.

Admit as ordinary-life support:

> `RECIPROCAL_CARE_THROUGH_LOW_STAKES_EXCHANGE`

These stories reinforce Hoshimi's dense interpersonal life without requiring a family ontology.

## 6.5 Mana devotion remains active performance practice

`01-08` depicts Suzu using an off-work karaoke visit for a self-chosen Mana song marathon, including repeated `First Step`, and reacting enthusiastically when Yu sings Mana material.

Admit as support:

> `MANA_DEVOTION_AS_ACTIVE_REPERFORMANCE`

This is not merely memorial sentiment; Mana's repertoire remains something Suzu physically sings, repeats, and shares. Do not infer that admiration dictates Suzu's vocational identity or removes her own authorship.

# 7. B3 relationship and authority cautions

1. Nagisa's Kotono-centered archive and domestic fantasy are **strong textual evidence about Nagisa**, not automatic evidence about Kotono's reciprocal romantic category.
2. Suzu's `お姉さま` language toward Rei is explicitly resisted as literal sisterhood by Rei.
3. Kokoro's power-harassment concern is a context-specific professional ethics example, not proof that every peer prank is benign.
4. Chisa's swimsuit/photobook conversation establishes the relevance of performer preference, not the final decision of any individual idol.
5. Aoi's fatigue does not by itself establish exploitative scheduling, nor does her voluntary practice prove adequate recovery.
6. Ordinary-life comedy remains evidentiary when it clarifies voice, habits, role flexibility, and relationship mechanics, but B3 should not make comedy carry a series-level thesis it cannot support.
