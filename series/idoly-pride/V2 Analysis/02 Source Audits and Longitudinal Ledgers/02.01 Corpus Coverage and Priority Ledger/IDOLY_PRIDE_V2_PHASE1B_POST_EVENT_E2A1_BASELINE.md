# 1. Governing thesis remains intact

Series-level:

> **answerable interdependence**

E1 constitutional refinement:

> **ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM**

E2-A1 does not replace either.

It adds a jurisdiction rule:

> **A true partial judgment becomes domination when it claims authority over dimensions it did not actually establish.**

Preferred terms:

> **NON_DISPOSABLE_STANDING**

and:

> **EVALUATIVE_JURISDICTION**

A person does not lose standing to participate, choose, continue, contribute, or be taken seriously merely because she is not maximally useful, frictionless, young, institutionally legible, technically dominant, or independently self-sufficient.

An evaluative system is authoritative only over the dimensions it can actually judge.

# 2. Rei — chosen vocation and bounded parental authority

Rei's father now enters the governing model as a serious rather than straw counterposition.

His concerns include:

- social usefulness;
- entertainment precarity;
- replaceability;
- post-idol career uncertainty.

The event does not refute those concerns as factual categories.

It limits their jurisdiction.

Rei's explicit governing line:

> `私の幸せは、私が決めたいの。`

supports:

- `VOCATIONAL_SELF_AUTHORSHIP`
- `BOUNDED_PARENTAL_AUTHORITY`
- `RELATIONAL_SOCIAL_VALUE`

Current rule:

> **A vocation need not be socially indispensable or guaranteed to succeed in order to be legitimately chosen and capable of real relational value.**

Rei's post-idol career remains unknown.

Do not use this event to claim lifetime economic sustainability.

# 3. Rui / Shizuku — mentorship requires both goal consent and process authorship

Rui's recognition of Shizuku's talent is preserved as correct.

The event now requires a distinction between:

- correct professional diagnosis;
- legitimate authority over another person's development.

Rui initially performs:

> **BENEVOLENT_GOAL_SEIZURE**

when she treats Shizuku's hidden capacity as permission to define the goal for her.

The healthier sequence is:

> overreach → Shizuku answers → Rui withdraws → Shizuku proposes duet → collaboration is reauthorized.

Preferred term:

> **WITHDRAWAL_BEFORE_REAUTHORIZATION**

A second distinction is now governing:

- `GOAL_CONSENT`
- `PROCESS_AUTHORSHIP`

Shizuku's consent to sing with Rui does not imply consent to Rui's default TRINITYAiLE workflow.

Rui's explicit recognition of senior-career arrogance supports:

> **EXPERTISE_WITH_BOUNDED_EPISTEMIC_JURISDICTION**

Mature model:

> **COAUTHORED_MENTORSHIP**

Expertise remains valuable. It becomes legitimate by remaining answerable to the person whose development is at issue.

# 4. Chisa — self-disposability revised into safeguarded participation

Chisa's survival-event problem may no longer be summarized merely as fear.

The stronger mechanism is:

> **PREEMPTIVE_SELF_DISPOSABILITY**

When she anticipates needing accommodation or creating friction, she attempts to remove herself before others can judge whether she is worth carrying.

SUNNY PEACE answers without denying unequal capacities.

Preferred term:

> **DIFFERENTIAL_CAPACITY_WITH_EQUAL_BELONGING**

Chisa's decorative shells/sea glass also support:

> **NON_INSTRUMENTAL_CONTRIBUTION**

Group value is not exhausted by mechanical usefulness.

The final crossing is now governed by:

> **SAFEGUARD_ENABLED_AGENCY**

Chisa remains afraid, but participation becomes possible through:

- disclosure;
- practice;
- safer construction;
- appropriate equipment;
- team support;
- her own explicit desire.

Therefore:

> **Support can be part of agency rather than evidence that agency has not yet arrived.**

This is a direct lower-tier qualification against any autonomy model that requires frictionless independence.

# 5. IDOLY MATCH — plural idol institutions and metric jurisdiction

`野良アイドル` are admitted as genuine idol evidence outside VENUS registration.

Preferred term:

> **PLURAL_IDOL_INSTITUTIONALITY**

The underground arena demonstrates that a non-dominant institution can contain:

- performers;
- audiences;
- recurring rules;
- local prestige;
- judgment;
- community continuity.

It is not treated as the only authentic idol world.

The protagonists return to VENUS by choice while affirming the underground as a legitimate home for others.

Preferred term:

> **NON_EXCLUSIVE_INSTITUTIONAL_LEGITIMACY**

Current rule:

> **One institution can be the right home for a person without being the only legitimate institution for everyone.**

# 6. Score/rank ontology — evaluative-regime pluralism

E2-A1 substantially advances `OPEN-07`.

The protagonists themselves distinguish:

- likely victory under VENUS scoring;
- loss under an audience/judge framework asking a different question.

Preferred term:

> **EVALUATIVE_REGIME_PLURALISM**

Do not infer:

- VENUS metrics are fake;
- skill does not matter;
- audience enjoyment is the only legitimate criterion.

Instead:

> **Different evaluative regimes may accurately measure different dimensions without any one of them exhausting total idol value.**

This is the lower-tier institutional counterpart to the E1 rule:

> a result has jurisdiction over the result, not unlimited jurisdiction over the competitor.

# 9. Makino / safety / intervention

`OPEN-03` remains open but advances.

E2-A1 demonstrates a useful non-acute model:

- management can initially refuse burdensome work;
- risk can be disclosed;
- safeguards can be built;
- participation can remain voluntary;
- support can increase rather than replace agency.

Preferred continuing Makino model:

> **risk-aware co-authorship**

with the added E2-A1 clarification:

> **safeguard legitimacy should be evaluated partly by whether it preserves or enlarges authored options rather than simply transferring the final decision to the manager.**
