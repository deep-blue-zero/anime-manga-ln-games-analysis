This is the compact prospective reference state inherited by all lower-tier Phase-1B work after completion of:

- 12/12 anime episodes;

# 1. Governing Tier-A thesis

> **INTERPRETATION — *IDOLY PRIDE*'s Tier-A narrative concerns how people remain authors of their lives when those lives are profoundly shaped by inheritance, relationships, institutions, competition, audiences, gratitude, memory, and bodily limits.**

The early Mana problem supplies the founding form:

> receive a miracle without becoming its copy.

The later main story generalizes it:

> receive other people, institutions, pressure, care, and obligation without granting any one of them unilateral jurisdiction over what a life is for.

Preferred compact term:

> **answerable interdependence**

Autonomy is not isolation. Dependence is legitimate when participants retain standing to answer, refuse, revise, compete, leave, return, and continue.

# 4. Global stabilized propositions

## 4.1 Mana

Mana is:

- historical founder;
- beloved dead person;
- public legend;
- possible supernatural witness;
- source of professional language and inherited ethics.

She is **not** the permanent sovereign definition of idolhood.

The current direction is:

> **provenance and inheritance without continuing jurisdiction.**

Literal supernatural ontology remains unresolved.

## 4.2 Competition

Competition is legitimate.

Scores, rankings, BIG4 status, I-UNITY, and Grand Prix results are real professional evidence.

The rejected proposition is **metric sovereignty**:

> a score cannot unilaterally decide whether a person, group, or dream deserves a future.

## 4.3 Dependence

Self-authorship is compatible with relational causation and continuing dependence.

Healthy dependence preserves:

- answerability;
- refusal;
- revision;
- contribution;
- role contestability where applicable;
- future change.

## 4.4 Care

Tier-A repeatedly distrusts **unilateral benevolence** when the beneficiary is excluded from deciding what help means.

This does not abolish emergency/professional intervention; the threshold remains open.

## 4.5 Performance

Performance is communicative, but not pure interior expression.

It is produced through:

- technique;
- bodily discipline;
- staff and management;
- image and media;
- money/resources where evidenced;
- audience relation;
- staging and repetition;
- personal orientation.

Feeling without form is insufficient; form without meaningful orientation is also insufficient.

## 4.6 Body and futurity

The body remains a morally and professionally real limit.

Stellar adds explicit long-duration vocation: idol life may be imagined over decades.

Therefore:

> **present intensity cannot automatically erase the future performer from consideration.**
