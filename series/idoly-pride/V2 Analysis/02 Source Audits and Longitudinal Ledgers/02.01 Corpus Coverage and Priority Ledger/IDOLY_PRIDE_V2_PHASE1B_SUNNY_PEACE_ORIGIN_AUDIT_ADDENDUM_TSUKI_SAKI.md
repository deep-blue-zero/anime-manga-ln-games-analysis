# SUNNY PEACE Origin Audit — Tsuki/Saki Cross-Tranche Addendum

## Purpose

The completed SUNNY PEACE origin audit treated Chisa's childhood, near-drowning, suppressed idol desire, failed audition, and separation from Saki as the primary-source basis for her burden-avoidance and individuation arc.

The subsequent Tsuki no Tempest origin tranche disclosed **Saki's side of the same childhood system**. That material materially strengthens the causal model of Chisa and therefore requires this addendum.

Nothing in this file changes what the SUNNY primary-source freeze originally contained. The correct archival operation is supplementation, not retroactive rewriting.

---

## 1. Prior SUNNY finding that remains valid

From Chisa's origin:

- Chisa was active enough as a child to disobey warnings and enter the water alone.
- The near-drowning terrified both sisters.
- Saki promised she would never stop watching over Chisa.
- Chisa responded by promising to become a `いい子` who would no longer cause Saki trouble.
- Chisa subsequently became more withdrawn and increasingly treated spontaneous desire as something that could burden Saki.
- She secretly wanted idolhood but suppressed the desire partly because she did not want to drag Saki into difficulty.
- Makino later tested whether she still wanted to become an idol if remaining beside Saki were removed from the bargain.

The SUNNY audit therefore concluded that Chisa's reserve is relational rather than merely temperamental and that SUNNY moves her from exclusive dependence toward distributed interdependence.

**That finding remains fully valid.**

---

## 2. New Saki-side evidence

Saki's Tsuki origin adds several missing pieces.

After the accident, Saki tells herself:

`私がお姉ちゃんなんだから、しっかりしなきゃ！`

As the sisters grow older, parental praise for Saki's academic performance and requests that she keep supporting Chisa reinforce two internal rules:

`優等生でないといけない`

`千紗を支え続けないといけない`

Yet Saki also has a longstanding idol dream.

The key self-recognition is:

`私……千紗のそばにいることと`

`アイドルの夢を諦めることを一緒に考えてたみたい`

This matters because it demonstrates that Chisa was not the only sister whose desires had become organized around avoiding harm to the other.

---

## 3. Revised bilateral causal model

The two origin bundles now support this sequence:

### Chisa's rule

> **If I want something carelessly, Saki may be frightened, burdened, or hurt. Therefore love means being easy to protect and asking for less.**

### Saki's rule

> **If Chisa is vulnerable, I must be maximally reliable. Therefore love means remaining available to protect her, even if my own dream must be subordinated.**

These rules interact recursively.

- Chisa sees Saki sacrifice -> Chisa concludes she should impose less.
- Saki sees Chisa become smaller/dependent -> Saki concludes Chisa needs more protection.
- More protection reinforces Chisa's belief that she cannot safely stand alone.
- Chisa's dependence reinforces Saki's belief that dream-sacrifice is responsible.

The result is not a villain/victim relation and not a conscious bargain.

It is a **reciprocal sacrifice loop**.

---

## 4. Canonical cross-tranche formulation

The strongest joint finding is:

> **The Shiraishi sisters were trapped not because either demanded self-erasure from the other, but because both had learned to interpret self-limitation as proof of love.**

Their individuation therefore does not require reducing love.

It requires changing the evidence by which love is recognized.

Healthy later sisterhood becomes:

- I can want something without making that want an accusation against you.
- I can support you without deciding your life for you.
- I can remain available without making your dependence the proof that I matter.
- I can accept help without treating my own agency as betrayal.
- We can move separately and still remain sisters.

This makes the Hoshimi `Dear My Sister` material structurally stronger: both `姉離れ` and `妹離れ` are necessary because the unhealthy system is jointly maintained even though neither sister intended harm.

---

## 5. Effect on Chisa's SUNNY PEACE function

The Tsuki disclosure strengthens rather than weakens the original SUNNY thesis.

SUNNY PEACE does not simply give Chisa "replacement people" after separation from Saki. It teaches a new model of support:

> **support can be plural, horizontal, and non-exclusive.**

Shizuku can take Chisa's hand without becoming Saki.
Sakura can encourage Chisa without becoming her protector.
Haruko can guide her professionally without requiring permanent dependence.
The group can help her while still expecting her to develop a voice and role of her own.

Thus Chisa's move into SUNNY is not anti-family individualism. It is the social diversification that makes healthier family intimacy possible.

---

## 6. Effect on Saki's Tsuki function

The same causal history helps explain why Saki is useful to Tsuki no Tempest.

She knows how to:

- plan;
- structure;
- notice practical needs;
- sustain routines;
- take responsibility;
- hold multiple obligations together.

These are not false traits produced by trauma. They are genuine competencies.

Her risk is that care can become a justification for over-responsibility or for quietly suppressing her own claim to desire.

The mature Saki therefore functions best when her organization is **service she chooses**, not proof she must constantly earn the right to be loved or useful.

## 8. Interpretive limits

Do not infer that:

- Saki's parents intentionally parentified her in a clinically defined sense;
- Saki resents Chisa;
- Chisa manipulated Saki through vulnerability;
- separation is always healthier than closeness;
- either sister must become independent of the other;
- all later support behavior repeats the childhood loop.

The source supports a narrower and stronger claim: **love became overidentified with sacrifice, and both sisters eventually learn that love can survive self-assertion.**
