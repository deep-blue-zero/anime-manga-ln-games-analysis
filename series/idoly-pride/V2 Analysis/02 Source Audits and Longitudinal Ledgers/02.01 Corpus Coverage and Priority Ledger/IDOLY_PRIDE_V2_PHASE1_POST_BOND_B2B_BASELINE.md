# 2. Care, influence, and ordinary-life option space

Admit three tranche-level refinements:

### `CARE_FORM_CAN_CHANGE_WITHOUT_RELATIONAL_WITHDRAWAL`

Care can become reversible, shift domains, or be withdrawn in one familiar form to recognize competence without becoming abandonment.

### `RELATIONAL_INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_VOCATIONAL_OWNERSHIP`

Family, mentors, peers, books, and inherited advice can shape judgment and vocation without permanently owning what the person later practices, integrates, or chooses.

### `ORDINARY_LIFE_AS_OPTION_SPACE`

School, university, hobbies, family work, former aspirations, and alternate professional pathways are not merely recovery texture around idolhood. They preserve unselected or parallel futures and keep the person larger than the current role.

These extend rather than replace:

- `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`;
- `PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`;
- `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`;
- `RECIPROCAL_STANDING`.
