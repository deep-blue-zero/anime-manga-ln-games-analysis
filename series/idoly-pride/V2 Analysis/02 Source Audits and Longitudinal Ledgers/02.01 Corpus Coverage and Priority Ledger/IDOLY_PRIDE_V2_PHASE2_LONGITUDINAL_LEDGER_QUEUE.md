# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — PHASE 2 LONGITUDINAL LEDGER QUEUE

## 0. Purpose

Phase 2 reconstructs **change through time** from the Phase-1 source/routing state. It is evidence-bearing infrastructure, not polished synthesis.

The Phase-1 final baseline remains inherited authority until an individual ledger explicitly revises a proposition through source-grounded longitudinal comparison.

Default rule:

> **ledger first, synthesis later.**

---

# P2-0 — Ledger, freshness, and formal-dependency infrastructure

Complete before character-ledger production begins at scale.

1. `SOURCE_DELTA_LEDGER.md`
   - baseline snapshot: `IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A`;
