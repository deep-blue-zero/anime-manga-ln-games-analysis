# 1. Cross-source prospective synthesis

## 1.1 `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`

**TEXTUAL FACT + STRONG INFERENCE**

B2-A materially strengthens the proposition that the manager/idol relationship is not a one-way care hierarchy.

- Rui wants Makino's praise and prepares food, drinks, and snacks for a full day of solo work with him.
- Sakura notices that Makino had replaced dinner with an energy drink and buys him a tonkatsu sandwich.
- miho notices that Makino routinely remains at work late enough that she privately questions whether his schedule is healthy or efficient.
- these acts do not erase the professional role boundary; they show that the person occupying the managerial role can also become an object of concern, gratitude, and preparation.

Admit:

> **`BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`**
>
> **Professional asymmetry does not require care to flow in only one direction. A manager may retain legitimate role authority while the idols retain standing to notice his needs, prepare for him, feed him, worry about him, and value his recognition.**

This is compatible with `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM` because reciprocal care does not mean role collapse.

## 1.2 `MANAGERIAL_AFFIRMATION_AS_RELATIONAL_RESOURCE`

**TEXTUAL FACT**

Rui's `st-company-bond-rui-01-01` is unusually explicit. After hearing TRINITYAiLE's song requested at school, she writes a message to Makino describing how happy she is and ends with:

> `もっと頑張るので、もっと褒めて下さい！`

She becomes embarrassed by the sentence and accidentally sends it to Rio instead.

The source establishes that Makino's praise matters to Rui **as desired recognition**, not merely as objective performance evaluation.

Admit:

> **`MANAGERIAL_AFFIRMATION_AS_RELATIONAL_RESOURCE`**

The manager can become a trusted audience whose recognition helps an idol organize effort and self-understanding.

Do **not** infer romance from the desire for praise.

## 1.3 `AFFECTIVE_SELF_CONSCIOUSNESS_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_SETTLEMENT`

**TEXTUAL FACT + INTERPRETATION**

Rui becomes specifically self-conscious when:

- she imagines Makino receiving her request to be praised more;
- Yu threatens to send Makino a playful photograph of Rui being fed cake;
- Haruko teases her about the carefully prepared gifts for the next day's work with Makino.

In `01-08`, Rui says the gifts are `あくまで感謝の気持ち` and later wonders why she became so flustered when she has `何もやましいことはないはず`.

Admit only:

> **`AFFECTIVE_SELF_CONSCIOUSNESS_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_SETTLEMENT`**

The relationship carries private emotional salience and self-consciousness. B2-A does **not** settle its category as romantic.

## 1.4 `CARE_FLOWS_ACROSS_ROLE_HIERARCHY`

**STRONG INFERENCE**

Sakura's food gift and Rui's preparation show two modes of care directed upward across the professional hierarchy:

- corrective/nutritional concern toward a self-neglecting manager;
- anticipatory gratitude toward a manager accompanying solo work.

Admit:

> **`CARE_FLOWS_ACROSS_ROLE_HIERARCHY`**

Care is not the monopoly of the formally responsible adult. Its legitimacy depends on answerability and proportionality, not on occupational direction.

## 1.5 `SELF_AUTHORED_DEVOTION_OVERLOAD`

**TEXTUAL FACT**

Shizuku's `st-company-bond-szk-01-07` shows a dense collision of self-chosen commitments:

- late professional work/rehearsal;
- real-time idol viewing at 5 a.m.;
- early-morning fan-community meetings;
- late-night idol viewing followed by immediate choreography imitation;
- family night fishing;
- continued professional work.

When Ai asks why she does not simply rest, Shizuku says:

> `ライブに挑みたいから、休んでる時間、ない……！`

Admit:

> **`SELF_AUTHORED_DEVOTION_OVERLOAD`**
>
> **Overwork can emerge from the accumulation of genuinely chosen roles, pleasures, fandom practices, hobbies, and professional ambitions rather than from external coercion alone.**

This is not evidence that those interests are pathological. It is evidence that self-authored commitments can still outrun bodily limits.

Support:

- `ROLE_ACCUMULATION_RISK`;
- `OPEN-05`.

Rui/Rio's six-hour competitive running in `rui-01-02` is a separate support example of self-escalating professional overwork, but Shizuku provides the stronger B2-A mechanism because the load comes from multiple chosen life domains.

## 1.6 `DIRECT_RELATIONSHIP_SUPERSEDES_PARASOCIAL_EXTRACTION`

**TEXTUAL FACT + STRONG INFERENCE**

Shizuku has been a LizNoir fan since elementary school. In `st-company-bond-szk-01-05`, she collects Rio's discarded or used objects and uses them to infer preferences and habits because she wants to understand her idol more deeply.

Rio responds:

> `今後は普通に聞いてちょうだい。知りたいことは話すわ`

Admit:

> **`DIRECT_RELATIONSHIP_SUPERSEDES_PARASOCIAL_EXTRACTION`**
>
> **When a former fan gains reciprocal relational access to the person she admired, direct answerable conversation becomes a more legitimate route to personal knowledge than unilateral extraction from traces.**

This does not erase Shizuku's fan identity. Her fandom persists after peer access becomes available.

Support term:

> **`FAN_IDENTITY_PERSISTS_AFTER_PEERHOOD`**

## 1.7 `UNSENTIMENTAL_AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY`

**TEXTUAL FACT**

In `st-company-bond-mhk-01-08`, Mei asks miho whether she also receives energy from fans. miho first answers defensively, then says:

> `中には、ずっと応援してくれている人もいるし`

and when Mei paraphrases that as caring about them:

> `そうね。大事よ`
>
> `ただ、当たり前のことだから、わざわざ口にしないだけ`

She later tells herself:

> `ファンの笑顔のために、お仕事、頑張らないと`

Admit:

> **`UNSENTIMENTAL_AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY`**
>
> **Reciprocal audience attachment does not require a Sunny-Peace-like sentimental public vocabulary. miho can treat long-term fans as genuinely important while regarding that importance as too obvious to verbalize routinely.**

This supports a plural model of idol–audience reciprocity across unit/personality registers.

## 1.8 `MULTIREGISTER_AUTHENTICITY`

**TEXTUAL FACT + STRONG INFERENCE**

miho's `01-01` sharply juxtaposes:

- courteous, grateful speech toward a producer who knew her as a rookie;
- private embarrassment about her earlier career;
- private exasperation toward fran and kana;
- genuine acknowledgement that their talent is extraordinary and that meeting them is meaningful.

The public/professional register is not reducible to deception simply because her internal monologue is harsher.

Admit:

> **`MULTIREGISTER_AUTHENTICITY`**

This strengthens, without replacing:

- `PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`;
- `STRATEGIC_AUTHENTICITY`;
- `AUTHENTICITY_WITHOUT_ETHICAL_EXONERATION`.

# 2. 天動瑠依 — recognition, reciprocal preparation, and unresolved affective category

## 2.1 Makino's praise matters privately

`rui-01-01` establishes that Rui's professional motivation includes a desire for specific recognition from Makino. The line `もっと褒めて下さい` is not delivered to an audience or used strategically; it is drafted privately and then retracted out of embarrassment.

Admit:

- `MANAGERIAL_AFFIRMATION_AS_RELATIONAL_RESOURCE`;
- support-level `PRIVATE_DESIRE_FOR_PROFESSIONAL_RECOGNITION`.

Do not infer that Rui is dependent on praise for basic self-worth. The source shows desire, not incapacity without it.

## 2.2 Gratitude becomes anticipatory care

`rui-01-08` shows Rui independently preparing multiple forms of sustenance for a solo job where Makino will accompany her all day. She protects those items from Haruko because they are a carefully prepared gift for someone who routinely helps her.

Admit:

- `RECIPROCAL_MANAGERIAL_PREPARATION`;
- `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`.

The explicit source framing is gratitude. Haruko supplies romantic teasing, but teasing is not narrator-level settlement.

## 2.3 Manager-specific embarrassment is real but categorically open

Rui's reaction to Yu threatening to send Makino a playful photo (`rui-01-06`) and her reaction to Haruko's teasing (`rui-01-08`) show manager-specific self-consciousness.

Admit:

- `AFFECTIVE_SELF_CONSCIOUSNESS_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_SETTLEMENT`.

Do not promote:

- canonical Rui/Makino romance;
- romantic confession;
- exclusivity;
- desire for a relationship outside the professional bond.

## 2.4 Self-authored professional excess remains a risk

`rui-01-02` shows Rui and Rio turning a casual joint run into roughly marathon distance over six hours because neither wants to admit fatigue or lose to the other. Rio explicitly says `オーバーワーク上等`.

Admit at support level:

> **`MUTUALLY_ESCALATED_SELF_OVERWORK`**

This is a comic extreme but source-textually reinforces that professional pride can erode self-limitation without managerial coercion.

# 3. 川咲さくら — the manager as someone worth feeding and protecting from self-neglect

## 3.1 Sakura observes Makino's bodily neglect

In `st-company-bond-skr-01-01`, Sakura remembers that Makino had again replaced dinner with an energy drink and purchases an additional tonkatsu sandwich for him.

Admit:

> **`MANAGER_AS_CARE_RECIPIENT`**

and:

> **`RECIPROCAL_NUTRITIONAL_CARE`**

The source matters because Sakura does not merely appreciate Makino abstractly. She recognizes a bodily need created by his work habits and takes practical action.

## 3.2 Care does not become authority seizure

Sakura's action is additive rather than coercive. She buys food and hopes he will enjoy it; she does not claim a right to control his schedule or diet.

This is a small clean instance of:

> **care without goal seizure**.

It supports the broader B2-A rule that reciprocal care across professional hierarchy need not erase role standing.

## 3.3 Makino's voice becomes an internalized encouragement model

In `skr-01-05`, after falling asleep in class and missing test preparation, Sakura imagines what Makino would tell her:

> `さくら、いつまでも落ち込んでちゃダメだ`
>
> `一緒に頂点を目指して突き進もう！`

Evidence status: **INTERPRETATION**.

Admit only at support level:

> **`INTERNALIZED_MANAGERIAL_ENCOURAGEMENT`**

Makino's relational role has become legible enough to Sakura that she can simulate an encouraging response and use it to restart herself. This is not evidence that Makino literally said these lines on this occasion.

## 3.4 Sakura/miho remains a pressure-through-warmth relationship

Several stories show Sakura repeatedly entering miho's relational space through enthusiasm, invitations, fandom promotion, and ordinary shared activity. miho resists the framing but repeatedly becomes engaged despite herself.

This is support/texture for their established relationship and should not be promoted into a new B2-A doctrine.

# 4. 兵藤雫 — fandom after peerhood and self-authored labor collision

## 4.1 Fan and colleague coexist in one person

Shizuku can interact with Rio as a professional peer while still experiencing her as the idol she admired from childhood.

In `szk-01-05`, this produces an outdated epistemic habit: collecting artifacts and inferring Rio's preferences as a fan rather than simply asking the person with whom she now has direct access.

Rio's correction does not tell Shizuku to stop being a fan. It tells her to use the reciprocal relationship she now possesses.

Admit:

- `FAN_IDENTITY_PERSISTS_AFTER_PEERHOOD`;
- `DIRECT_RELATIONSHIP_SUPERSEDES_PARASOCIAL_EXTRACTION`.

## 4.2 Fan knowledge can become professional/public knowledge

Shizuku's fishing channel (`szk-01-02`) and detailed idol/merchandise knowledge show private interests becoming public/professional resources without ceasing to be genuine hobbies.

Support:

> `CROSS_ROLE_FERTILIZATION`

and:

> `PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`.

Her fish-channel performance is curated precisely because fishing is genuinely hers.

## 4.3 Self-authored commitment can still become unsafe

`szk-01-07` is the strongest B2-A evidence for `SELF_AUTHORED_DEVOTION_OVERLOAD`.

The load is not reducible to idol-industry scheduling. Shizuku combines:

- work;
- practice;
- fandom;
- fan community;
- fishing;
- family activity;
- immediate imitation/learning from other performers.

Her own phrase `休んでる時間、ない` is therefore analytically important.

Admit:

- `SELF_AUTHORED_DEVOTION_OVERLOAD`;
- `ROLE_ACCUMULATION_RISK` strengthened;
- `OPEN-05` advanced.

Do not infer that Hoshimi ordered the sleep deprivation.

## 4.4 Reciprocal influence crosses seniority

In `szk-01-06`, Rio begins fishing because she watched Shizuku's fishing program and found it appealing. Shizuku, the longtime LizNoir fan, becomes the person whose work inspires the former object of fandom.

Admit at support level:

> **`REVERSED_FAN_INFLUENCE`**

This is a compact example of reciprocal influence after hierarchy becomes relationally flatter.

# 5. miho — professional longevity, private memory, and audience care without softness

## 5.1 More than five years of professional continuity

`mhk-01-01` gives a direct internal chronology:

> `最後にお会いしたのは、もう五年以上も前でしたっけ`
>
> `デビューした頃は、よくご一緒したんですけどね`
>
> `今もなんとかアイドルを続けてます`

Admit:

> **`MULTIYEAR_PROFESSIONAL_CONTINUITY_MiHO`**

This establishes at least five-plus years of idol career continuity from her debut period to the bond's present state. It does not by itself settle miho's exact age, later retirement horizon, or industry-wide long-career viability.

## 5.2 Public professionalism and private irritation can both be genuine

The same story shows miho speaking politely and gratefully to an earlier-career producer while privately dreading his stories about her rookie mistakes and considering absurd retaliatory schemes before rejecting them herself.

Crucially, the polite register contains real gratitude:

> `数年ぶりに仕事をご一緒出来て、嬉しいです`

and she describes fran and kana's talent sincerely despite private complaints.

Admit:

- `MULTIREGISTER_AUTHENTICITY`;
- support for `PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`.

## 5.3 Yō remains privately protected memory

In `mhk-01-02`, miho remembers waiting until both she and Yō were twenty so they could drink together. She becomes alarmed at having spoken this memory aloud even though Haruko appears asleep.

Admit at support level:

> **`SELECTIVE_VULNERABILITY_PRIVACY`**

The memory remains emotionally available but is not freely public property.

Do not use B2-A to infer a full chronology of miho/Yō beyond what the source itself states.

## 5.4 Fans matter even when miho does not perform softness about it

`mhk-01-08` is direct evidence that long-term fans matter privately to miho and that their smiles motivate continued work.

Admit:

- `UNSENTIMENTAL_AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY`;
- support for `RECIPROCAL_AUDIENCE_COAUTHORSHIP` at the level of affective motivation, not literal creative jurisdiction.

Do not make miho's relation to fans identical to Sunny Peace's public relational idiom.

## 5.5 Professional care extends beyond her own unit

miho repeatedly notices and instructs younger Hoshimi idols:

- recognizing Suzu's preparation for radio work (`mhk-01-05`);
- giving surprisingly specific acting criticism (`mhk-01-06`);
- worrying about Mei being out late (`mhk-01-08`).

Admit at support level:

> **`CROSS_UNIT_SENIOR_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`**

Her care often arrives through correction, irritation, or practical instruction rather than overt tenderness.

# 7. Prospective prohibitions for historical comparison

Do not allow Tier-H to:

1. convert Rui's desire for praise, embarrassment, or prepared gifts into settled romance;
2. treat Haruko's romantic teasing as narrator-level classification;
3. infer exclusivity between Rui and Makino;
4. turn reciprocal care into role collapse or erase Makino's managerial responsibilities;
5. treat Sakura's imagined Makino speech as a literal quoted historical interaction;
6. attribute Shizuku's sleep deprivation to Hoshimi coercion when the bond shows a large self-authored component;
7. pathologize fandom simply because self-authored fandom contributes to overload;
8. erase Shizuku's fan identity after professional peer access becomes possible;
9. make Rio's willingness to answer Shizuku a general entitlement to private information;
10. treat miho's polite professional register as false merely because her internal monologue is harsher;
11. treat miho's private hostility as the sole authentic register;
12. equate miho's audience relation with Sunny Peace's public relational style;
13. use five-plus career years to close long-horizon idol economics or aging questions;
14. force undated bond material into later event chronology without independent evidence.

# 8. Prospective B2-A thesis

> **The bond layer shows that answerable professional relationships become deeper not by dissolving roles, but by making recognition and care more reciprocal. Makino can remain manager while becoming someone Rui wants praise from and someone Sakura wants to feed. Shizuku can remain a fan while becoming Rio's peer, but peer access changes what forms of knowing are legitimate. miho can remain abrasive and strategically professional while privately caring about fans. The corresponding danger is that self-authored devotion can generate overwork even where institutional coercion is absent.**

This thesis is frozen prospectively. Historical comparison may classify older formulations but may not retroactively rewrite the primary-source findings recorded here.
