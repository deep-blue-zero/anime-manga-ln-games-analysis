# 2. IIIX extension admitted after Tokyo

The post-I-UNITY bridge now adds the following facts before BIG4 begins.

## 2.1 Institutional position

- IIIX no longer operates under Preta Porte.
- Preta Porte has ceased to function as a stable idol-industry home for the unit.
- IIIX has become freelance/independent.
- The members are attempting self-production and must absorb administrative functions previously supplied by the agency.
- Their public image and work volume have been materially damaged by association with the I-UNITY scandal even though the unit itself did not directly execute Himeno's sabotage.
- IIIX has lost its prior BIG4 seat by the opening of `big4_001`.

## 2.2 Unit continuity

- Dissolution was a real option after I-UNITY.
- Each member could have continued idol work without continuing IIIX.
- The three nevertheless choose continued unit existence.
- The choice does not require friendship language; `腐れ縁` is the strongest self-description supplied by miho.
- Their hostile/adversarial relational grammar therefore survives the institutional break.

## 2.3 Competitive and professional identity

Do **not** infer that post-I-UNITY IIIX has become anti-competitive or conventionally warm.

The admissible state is:

- competition still matters;
- professional output still matters;
- ranking and proof still matter;
- the members remain abrasive and status-conscious;
- the reduced-live experience expands their evidence about audience recognition without replacing technical or competitive value.

## 2.4 Miho's memory politics

Miho's encounter with surviving memory of Yō is admissible as a destabilizing event.

It is **not** admissible to say that:

- her resentment toward Mana is fully resolved;
- her project of public memory has become ethically settled;
- or the BIG4 arc begins after a complete miho redemption.

That remains open.
