# 1. Tranche-level judgment

The frozen E2-B1 thesis survives Tier-H comparison:

> **E2-B1 extends answerable interdependence into answerable roles. Kokoro learns that a real past mistake does not create an unlimited future debt; LizNoir demonstrates that center is a prestigious but contestable and delegable office; Shizuku and Suzu show that apparently zero-sum role competition can sometimes be answered by changing the form itself; and IIIX discovers a public authenticity based neither on false harmony nor total exposure, but on selective, strategic presentation of a genuinely antagonistic relationship that nevertheless contains explicit trust.**

Preferred tranche term:

> `ANSWERABLE_ROLE_CONSTITUTION`

Definition:

> **A role remains legitimate when the person who occupies or inherits it retains standing to answer it: to contest, delegate, revise, multiply where the form permits, refuse false obligations, perform it without being reduced to it, and control the terms under which it becomes public.**

This is a refinement of `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM`, not a replacement.

The tranche also clarifies a distinction that must remain stable in later synthesis:

- `CONTESTABLE_SINGULAR_OFFICE` — a function may legitimately remain singular while its occupant is challengeable and replaceable;
- `ADAPTABLE_PLURAL_ROLE` — an apparent one-person role may sometimes be re-authored so that multiple non-interchangeable embodiments remain present.

Do not derive a single anti-hierarchy rule from these two cases.

# 2. Historical comparison — `心愛溶けるビターチョコレート`

## 2.1 What V1 already understood

Historical LizNoir analysis correctly treated this as a foundational Kokoro/Ai event. It identified:

- Kokoro performing confidence while hiding shame;
- Ai as the person able to reach Kokoro when self-punishment becomes isolating;
- the movement from regretting the past alone toward talking about the future together;
- the bitterness/sweetness concept as a fusion of Kokoro's emotional structure with LizNoir aesthetics;
- `Darkness sympathizer` as a new LizNoir frontier that Rio and Aoi could not have authored alone.

These claims are strongly supported by the primary close read.

### Transition

> **PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN**

## 2.2 V2 mechanism correction

The older formulation is emotionally accurate but under-specifies why Kokoro's guilt becomes dangerous.

The new evidence model distinguishes:

1. a **real mistake** — Kokoro collided with Rio and Aoi was injured while protecting Rio;
2. an **unsupported causal expansion** — Kokoro treats that injury as if it may explain LizNoir's defeat even though Aoi explicitly denies this;
3. a **self-created reparative debt** — Kokoro decides she must compensate the seniors through exceptional work;
4. **solitary labor escalation** — roughly one hundred discs, online research, notebooks, continued lessons, sleep collapse, refusal of help;
5. **protective guilt monopolization** — Kokoro withholds the issue from Ai so Ai will not feel responsible;
6. **future-facing repair** — Ai and Aoi refuse to let the past own Kokoro's future effort.

Admitted terms:

- `GUILT_CAUSAL_OVERREACH`
- `UNILATERAL_REPARATIVE_OVERWORK`
- `PROTECTIVE_GUILT_MONOPOLIZATION`
- `FUTURE_FACING_REPAIR`
- `REPAIR_WITHOUT_DEBT_OWNERSHIP`

The mature rule is:

> **Past harm may create reasons for apology, learning, care, and changed practice without granting the past permanent jurisdiction over future labor.**

This is not absolution. It is a limit on debt ownership.

### Transition

> **REVISE BY PRECISION + STRENGTHEN**

## 2.3 Overwork implication

Historical character analysis usually described the event primarily through shame and relational repair. V2 makes it a major `OPEN-05` source because the damaging pressure is substantially **self-authored**.

No hostile manager commands the overwork. The story therefore blocks an overly narrow exploitation model in which labor harm exists only when an institution explicitly coerces it.

> **STRENGTHEN `OPEN-05`: chosen devotion, guilt, ambition, and gratitude can generate harmful work even inside a broadly supportive institution.**

A performed role can become a medium through which a real relationship is rehearsed or made legible without making fiction identical to autobiography.

> **Construction and truth are not opposites.**

# 9. Post-E2-B1 governing synthesis

E2-B1 should be carried forward as the tranche in which **role constitution** becomes explicitly plural.

The corpus now distinguishes at least four ethically different forms of role relation:

1. **Debt-role overreach** — a past mistake is allowed to assign future labor (`GUILT_CAUSAL_OVERREACH`).
2. **Contestable singular office** — center remains singular but challengeable, delegable, and performance-justified.
3. **Adaptable plural role** — production form changes so distinct valid embodiments can coexist.
4. **Curated public role** — public identity remains constructed and strategic while becoming more answerable to the actual relationship.

Compact final principle:

> **A role is not made ethical by disappearing. It is made ethical by losing the power to become unquestionable ownership of the person.**

This principle is consistent with the existing V2 architecture of answerable interdependence, bounded jurisdiction, non-totalizing vocation, and answerable constitutionalism.
