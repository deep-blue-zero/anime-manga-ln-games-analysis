# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — POST-STELLAR GOVERNING BASELINE

## 0. Purpose

This document is the prospective state inherited after completion of the entire currently catalogued Tier-A game main-story spine.

It freezes what may be assumed when the project begins the **Tier-A Main Narrative Consolidation Audit** and, only after that audit, the remaining event/card/bond/message passes.

It is intentionally narrower than a full-series synthesis. It records stabilized state, not every interpretation the project may eventually endorse.

The admitted chain is:

> anime endpoint -> Hoshimi game expansion -> audited unit origins -> Tokyo -> BIG4 -> Stellar

No later event, card, bond, message, telephone, or retrospective character-synthesis proposition becomes prospective fact merely because it is already familiar to the analyst.

---

# 1. Global narrative state

## 1.1 The main story has moved beyond succession

The early narrative asks whether Mana can be inherited without replacing the living. By the end of Stellar, that problem has been distributed into a wider ecology.

Mana remains foundational, but no current major unit is well modeled as "the next Mana."

The main story has produced multiple incompatible but legitimate idol philosophies:

- Sunny Peace — shared sunlight, love, and burden circulation;
- Tsuki no Tempest — reciprocal moonlight, rivalry, and recoverability;
- TRINITYAiLE — elite aspiration propelled by reciprocal gratitude;
- LizNoir — continuing pride, four-person authorship, and long-horizon excellence;
- IIIX — hostile intimacy, authored image, experimentation, and `絶対勝利`;
- DoriKyun — external antagonistic pressure and entertainment-as-combat.

The main story therefore ends its current run with a **plural field**, not one final answer to idolhood.

## 1.2 Competition remains legitimate

The governing main-story position is not anti-ranking or anti-victory.

Scores, losses, ranks, BIG4 status, and championships remain meaningful evidence of performance.

What the story repeatedly rejects is **metric sovereignty**:

> a score does not acquire unilateral authority to determine whose dream, group, or future deserves to continue.

This is now supported by Tokyo, BIG4, and Stellar.

## 1.3 Self-authorship is compatible with dependence

The cumulative Tier-A spine now strongly supports:

> **autonomy is not isolation; healthy authorship can be socially produced so long as the person retains standing to answer, refuse, revise, and continue.**

This is visible across:

- Sakura and Sunny Peace;
- Kotono and Tsuki;
- Rui/Yu/Sumire;
- Rui/Asakura;
- IIIX's internal rivalry;
- Hoshimi's institutional support;
- Makino's management.

# 2. Hoshimi Production endpoint

## 2.1 Institutional identity

Hoshimi is now a major multi-unit professional institution and a nationally legible competitive force.

Characters use home/family-like language, but the governing model remains:

> **plural professional home/platform**

rather than literal family.

Its main-story legitimacy comes from supporting different authored forms of idolhood without requiring one emotional style.

## 2.2 Institutional strengths now established

Hoshimi can:

- host distinct unit philosophies;
- support inter-unit rivalry;
- absorb displaced units without requiring identity surrender;
- make temporary hiatus materially survivable;
- redistribute professional obligations;
- create multi-unit cultural projects;
- provide managerial/staff/medical infrastructure;
- sustain long-horizon career imagination;
- turn internal difference into creative and competitive development.

## 2.3 Institutional risks still open

Hoshimi's success creates risks of its own:

- overwork;
- public expectation;
- prestige pressure;
- role obligation;
- internalized duty to win;
- confusion between love for work and unlimited bodily availability.

The Hoshimi Festival and special Grand Prix award do not eliminate these risks.

---

# 3. Makino Kouhei endpoint

Makino remains the continuing manager identity under the governing project decision.

His best current professional model is:

> **risk-aware co-authorship**

Operationally this means:

- discover what the idol actually wants;
- expose relevant risks and consequences;
- do not use managerial fear to author the idol's life in advance;
- build logistical, medical, staffing, contractual, and relational support;
- intervene when necessary without treating intervention as morally costless;
- remain accountable for conditions even when the idol authors the decision.

The model is not final or infallible. Sakura's semifinal decision remains a genuine ethical stress case.

Makino's final Stellar pride is not that he produced a champion. It is pride in managing this plural Hoshimi constellation.

---

# 4. SUNNY PEACE endpoint

## 4.1 Unit state

Sunny Peace is an established BIG4 unit and finishes Stellar as a Grand Prix semifinalist.

Its mature main-story constitution is explicitly five-person:

> `五つのピース`

The center remains important, but the unit rejects a model where Sakura alone carries responsibility, pressure, or rescue labor.

## 4.2 Sakura Kawasaki

Established:

- Sakura's brightness remains authored and genuinely hers.
- Mana's heart/voice do not give Mana jurisdiction over Sakura.
- Sakura nevertheless inherits a dangerous version of Mana's audience ethic: every stage may be someone's only chance.
- BIG4 success intensified that ethic into duty to win and difficulty admitting burden.
- overwork caused real accumulated physical damage.
- proper rest is medically important.
- another serious overexertion collapse could, in the worst case, force idol retirement.
- before the semifinal she received current-condition medical clearance and extensive safeguards.
- she chose to perform.
- Sunny Peace and Makino supported that choice.
- she lost without treating the stage as meaningless.

Open:

- how sustainable `全てのステージが、私にとってのファイナル` can be over a long career;
- where future intervention thresholds should lie;
- the exact causal status of the heartbeat/miracle event.

Do **not** prospectively assume later events have solved these questions.

## 4.3 Other Sunny members

Rei, Haruko, Shizuku, and Chisa have become active co-bearers of Sunny Peace's professional and emotional load. They should not be reconstructed as supporting orbit around Sakura.

---

# 5. Tsuki no Tempest endpoint

Tsuki enters Stellar from the BIG4 constitutional refoundation and exits as VENUS Grand Prix finalist.

The BIG4 constitution remains stable under new pressure:

- Kotono is a legitimate major center without sovereign ownership of centerhood.
- Suzu retains center legitimacy and competitive standing.
- Nagisa's care preserves selfhood and refusal.
- Saki remains a procedural/care voice.
- Mei remains a resilience/recoverability voice.
- rivalry with friends does not destabilize the unit.
- losing to TRINITYAiLE does not erase Tsuki's future or status.

Tsuki now has evidence of **durable reciprocal constitution**, not merely successful reunion.

---

# 6. TRINITYAiLE endpoint

TRINITYAiLE wins the VENUS Grand Prix.

The championship must not be treated as final proof that the group is universally or permanently strongest. Rui explicitly rejects terminal summit status.

Its mature Stellar answer is `恩返し`:

> **received support becomes a freely authored reason to create, compete, and return new experience to those who supported the unit.**

This answer is not servile debt.

The group now explicitly recognizes:

- it cannot fly entirely by itself;
- technique alone is insufficient;
- leadership burden belongs to all three;
- family wounds and professional identity cannot be solved by silence;
- gratitude can include rivals and painful criticism;
- victory is a new starting point.

Rui/Asakura has moved from unspoken professional/familial contradiction toward explicit mutual recognition, but future relationship development remains open.

---

# 7. LizNoir endpoint

LizNoir remains BIG4 and a central Hoshimi professional unit.

Its current four-person form is not a temporary extension of Rio/Aoi. Ai and Kokoro are constitutive members.

The Grand Prix loss confirms rather than destroys its pride:

- the unit reaches a new self-best;
- recognizes continued room for growth;
- expands its horizon beyond a domestic tournament;
- treats ideal idolhood as potentially decades away.

Mana is no longer LizNoir's only or terminal horizon.

---

# 8. IIIX endpoint

IIIX remains Hoshimi-affiliated and retains:

- `絶対勝利`;
- abrasive internal relations;
- professional self-authorship;
- hostility to sentimental domestication.

Stellar adds:

- willingness to experiment with a deliberately imperfect stage;
- internal member rivalry as performance differentiation;
- recognition that audience enjoyment can matter even in defeat;
- defeat as usable information rather than identity annihilation.

This is **hostile intimacy becoming a creative resource**, not IIIX turning into a conventional friendship unit.

---

# 9. DoriKyun endpoint

DoriKyun remains outside Hoshimi.

Its post-BIG4 role now includes:

- adversarial rival;
- critic of overprotection/complacency;
- diagnostic evaluator of serious idols;
- brutal cultivator capable of provoking stalled talent into development.

This is not redemption into warmth.

Its elimination ethic remains ethically suspect; its critique of stagnant effort, institutional weakness, and the need for serious pressure can still be instrumentally valuable.

---

# 10. Mana endpoint

Mana remains:

- historical founder;
- private beloved dead person;
- public legend;
- possible supernatural presence;
- source of inherited language and professional ideals.

But the current main story increasingly demotes Mana from **causal jurisdiction** to **witness/inheritance**.

Sakura explicitly learns not to expect Mana to rescue her competition. Makino sees Mana's `想い` diversified across many idols rather than reproduced in one successor.

The next-generation audition confirms the main-story direction:

> inheritance creates new desire rather than copies.

---

# 11. Grand Prix endpoint and long-duration vocation

TRINITYAiLE is champion.

Hoshimi's units receive special institutional recognition.

No major group treats the tournament as the final horizon.

The end-state explicitly imagines:

- continued challenges;
- rematches;
- global ambition;
- twenty- or thirty-year development horizons;
- continuing idolhood while passion/audience/desire remain;
- new younger aspirants entering the field.

This must qualify every synthesis of `青春全部かけて`.

The main story values intensity **and** duration.

---

# 12. Unresolved Tier-A claims requiring consolidation audit

The following must remain explicit open items:

1. **Sakura health/autonomy:** how to distinguish admirable present commitment from future-destroying presentism.
2. **Heartbeat/miracle causality:** supernatural residue versus embodied/psychological mechanism remains unresolved.
3. **Makino intervention threshold:** risk-aware co-authorship is current best model, not a closed decision algorithm.
4. **Hoshimi family metaphor:** useful character language, but potentially over-totalizing if treated as literal institutional description.
5. **DoriKyun developmental necessity:** valuable in specific arcs; not proof that cruelty/pressure is universally necessary.
6. **Grand Prix score interpretation:** outcomes are real but not complete moral/personhood rankings.
7. **Long-career viability:** the ending imagines decades, but main-story evidence does not yet establish how every unit handles aging, contracts, family, health, or changing markets.
8. **Audiovisual final-live claims:** exact formal analysis remains incomplete for `stellar_011` and `big4_013`.
9. **Later-source chronology:** events/cards/messages must be routed without silently rewriting the Tier-A endpoint.
