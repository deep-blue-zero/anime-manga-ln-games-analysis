# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — POST-BIG4 GOVERNING BASELINE

## 0. Purpose

This document freezes the maximum state that may be inherited after `big4_014_epilogue`.

The next main-story pass may use the propositions below as established history without re-proving them. It may **not** import later Stellar, event, card, bond, message, or later historical conclusions as though BIG4 had already established them.

The inheritance chain is:

`POST_TOKYO_BASELINE`
→ `IIIX_POST_IUNITY_TO_BIG4_ENTRY_BRIDGE`
→ `BIG4_ENTRY_GOVERNING_BASELINE`
→ `BIG4_001-014_AUDIT`
→ **POST_BIG4_BASELINE**
→ `STELLAR_001-011`

The baseline is intentionally narrower than the full BIG4 audit. It records what the next tranche is allowed to know, not every interpretation developed during analysis.

---

# 1. Hoshimi Production / Makino

Hoshimi remains a materially expanded multi-unit professional institution whose strongest demonstrated governance principle is **structured autonomy**.

Established through BIG4:

- idols may make consequential professional choices without the producer authoring the answer for them;
- managerial respect for autonomy does not mean withholding logistics, risk information, media support, rehearsal infrastructure, or alternatives;
- Hoshimi can tolerate serious internal disagreement without making membership conditional on ideological sameness;
- IIIX can be given a professional place without requiring prior harms to be declared forgiven;
- unit stability is not treated as a higher value than the members' ability to choose what the unit should become;
- when Kotono leaves, the remaining Tsuki members receive a real choice between continuing and disbanding rather than an emotional command to preserve the group;
- when they choose to continue, Hoshimi supplies the resources necessary to make that choice materially real.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Makino/Hoshimi exercise legitimate professional authority when they protect the conditions under which idols can make authored choices and live with their consequences.**

The customizable game manager remains Makino Kouhei under the governing project identity rule.

# 2. SUNNY PEACE

## 2.1 Competitive status

SUNNY PEACE is a BIG4 unit.

Its BIG4 status is already established at the beginning of this tranche and remains intact at the endpoint.

## 2.2 Unit state

No BIG4 development overturns the mature Tokyo formulation:

> **SUNNY PEACE converts shared light into recursive recognition among members and fans.**

The group's function in BIG4 is increasingly comparative rather than remedial. SUNNY has already found an operational answer to its own idolhood problem while Tsuki must undergo a more painful reconstitution.

## 2.3 Sakura

Sakura remains self-authored and relationally central to SUNNY.

At the endpoint, she and Kotono are explicit living rivals. Both Hoshimi sun and moon now occupy BIG4-level space without one needing to replace or complete the other.

Do not infer later Stellar developments from this status.

# 3. Tsuki no Tempest

## 3.1 Competitive status

Tsuki no Tempest defeats DoriKyun in the final BIG4 Challenge and becomes a BIG4 unit.

This is a legitimate competitive result within the text.

`big4_013_the_moonlight` retains an audiovisual/formal backfill requirement for claims about the exact live staging, arrangement, choreography, vocal delivery, camera, lighting, and crowd sound. The textual result and dialogue are established.

## 3.2 Constitutional state

The original five-member roster has returned, but the pre-departure internal structure has **not** simply returned.

The governing inherited unit model is:

> **Tsuki no Tempest is a five-person reciprocal system with contestable center authority.**

Established:

- all five members have experience of a viable four-person Tsuki without Kotono;
- Suzu has an earned center history;
- Kotono can carry center but does not possess it as an unchallengeable identity right;
- Nagisa has an autonomous idol desire and can directly oppose Kotono;
- Saki expects group-level decisions to be made through accountable process;
- Mei understands the unit as capable of losing without necessarily breaking;
- all five retain growth acquired during the separation;
- center configuration can be chosen according to performance need, communication need, and competitive fit;
- the group can contain rivalry among its own members without treating rivalry as betrayal.

The pre-BIG4 hierarchy "Kotono as moon / others as supporting orbit" is therefore no longer sufficient as a full model.

## 3.3 Mature performance philosophy

The strongest established formulation is:

> **Each member can illuminate and be illuminated; the unit becomes one storm by combining members who are individually capable of becoming light.**

Kotono's own answer to idolhood is to illuminate another person's path.

BIG4 modifies that answer reciprocally: the people she once illuminated can also become the light by which she finds a path home.

## 3.4 Competition

Tsuki rejects DoriKyun's elimination ethic without rejecting competitive seriousness.

The inherited norm is:

> **winning matters, but defeat does not extinguish personhood, vocation, or the right to continue.**

Kotono explicitly asks DoriKyun to continue after Tsuki wins.

# 4. Kotono Nagase

Kotono has completed a major but non-teleological development.

Established:

- she feared that Tsuki's kindness and especially Nagisa's support had become a form of dependence;
- she left the unit and pursued individual development under/around DoriKyun;
- the departure caused real relational and professional harm;
- she gained genuine individual capacities during the wrong/overcorrected route;
- she later accepts that the route was not simply "correct" and does not use improvement to retroactively justify the harm;
- she accepts criticism from the members she hurt;
- she accepts Suzu as a legitimate center rather than treating center as her property;
- she can take center when it is entrusted to her for a particular performance objective;
- she can understand part of DoriKyun's anger without inheriting their doctrine;
- she defines her idol answer as illuminating people's paths;
- she can distinguish understanding an opponent from becoming the opponent.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Kotono is now an accountable center rather than a solitary or proprietary center.**

Open for later arcs:

- how stable this reconstitution remains under new high-altitude pressure;
- how her BIG4 status changes rivalry and self-comparison;
- how the new center constitution behaves when another unit member surpasses or challenges her.

Do not answer those questions from later material before Stellar earns them.

# 5. Nagisa Ibuki

Nagisa's BIG4 development is established as a movement from asymmetrical support toward reciprocal partnership.

Established:

- she refuses to leave with Kotono despite the emotional importance of the invitation;
- she explicitly wants to remain an idol;
- she values Saki, Suzu, Mei, and Tsuki independently of Kotono;
- she wants to shine herself;
- she wants eventually to stand beside Kotono rather than remain behind her;
- she can confront Kotono sharply when Kotono's self-story becomes dishonest;
- she can promise to surpass Kotono without treating rivalry as loss of affection;
- she welcomes Kotono back only after the relationship has changed.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Nagisa's care has acquired selfhood and the right to refuse.**

The tranche supports exceptional intimacy and romantic/yuri coding, but does not establish explicit canonical romance.

---

# 6. Suzu Narumiya

Suzu now has an earned rather than purely aspirational center identity.

Established:

- she volunteers to center the four-person Tsuki;
- she carries public and internal responsibility during the unit's most unstable period;
- the other members recognize that she helped keep the unit functioning;
- after Kotono returns, Suzu remains the formal center of the comeback live;
- she lends Kotono one song so Kotono can communicate to the audience through performance;
- later, before the decisive DoriKyun battle, she entrusts the center to Kotono because tested performance evidence shows the Kotono-center formation has the better chance to win;
- she explicitly says she still intends to challenge for and retake center in the future.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Suzu understands center as a burdensome professional office that can be contested, exercised, lent, or entrusted without determining whether she herself matters.**

Do not reduce her BIG4 role to "temporary replacement center."

# 7. Saki Shiraishi and Mei Hayasaka

## 7.1 Saki

Saki carries a strong procedural-care function.

Established:

- she supports continuation of four-person Tsuki;
- she develops independently within it;
- she objects to unilateral group-level decisions made by Kotono;
- she treats professional coordination, communication, and accountability as part of care.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Saki's support is increasingly procedural: people deserve not only affection, but standing in decisions that affect their shared future.**

## 7.2 Mei

Mei embodies resilient continuity.

Established:

- she contributes actively to four-member growth;
- she recognizes Suzu's center labor;
- before the final battle she can imagine defeat without imagining existential collapse;
- she believes the five can begin again even if they lose.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Mei's mature strength is recoverability: the future remains available after failure.**

# 8. DoriKyun

## 8.1 Competitive state

DoriKyun loses the decisive BIG4 Challenge to Tsuki no Tempest and therefore loses that BIG4 position to Tsuki.

They continue as DoriKyun rather than disbanding because Kotono rejects the elimination demand and asks them to keep going and fight again.

## 8.2 Philosophy

Established:

- they treat idol entertainment as combat, pressure, spectacle, and proof;
- they believe weak/half-hearted idolhood is disposable or should be crushed;
- they understand publicity and pre-battle narrative as part of entertainment;
- they respect strength and meaningful results;
- they can teach useful technical/strategic lessons without becoming benevolent mentors;
- they are capable of recognizing a worthy opponent after defeat.

## 8.3 Historical wound

Miho reports a history in which:

- a powerful producer attempted to induce a rigged loss;
- DoriKyun pretended to accept and instead crushed the favored opponent;
- the producer retaliated;
- Celestial Pro abandoned them rather than protect them;
- they lost their work and then forced a path back through public live battles.

This account is meaningful but retains its diegetic epistemic qualifiers:

- Miho says she does not know them in detail;
- much of the account is industry rumor/history available to her;
- her claim about DoriKyun's ultimate purification vision is explicitly her inference.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **DoriKyun possess a real critique of an exploitative industry, but universalize that wound into an elimination ethic the story rejects.**

## 8.4 Relation to Hoshimi/Tsuki

DoriKyun remains an external adversarial rival.

Do not yet import later event or Stellar claims about exactly how that rivalry develops.

# 9. IIIX

The BIG4 entry bridge remains valid and is now supplemented by BIG4 main-story developments.

Established at this endpoint:

- IIIX has left Preta Porte and become freelance before BIG4 begins;
- Saegusa offers IIIX a place at Hoshimi;
- IIIX accepts a reversible/trial professional affiliation rather than an immediate sentimental merger;
- the trio continues living separately and explicitly avoids ordinary Hoshimi-style closeness;
- Makino does not declare the anti-Mana erasure project forgiven;

- IIIX can function as a high-level internal rival/training resource;
- the trio remains caustic, self-interested, and recognizably IIIX;
- Miho explicitly becomes interested in evaluating **Nagase Kotono as an idol**, not merely Mana's sister.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **IIIX has entered Hoshimi's professional ecology without being domesticated into Hoshimi's relational style.**

Do not import later event-based "mature IIIX" or American-arc conclusions at the Stellar starting line unless Stellar itself reaches them.

# 12. Mana Nagase and inheritance

Mana remains historically causal but increasingly decentered as a terminal standard.

BIG4 strengthens the transformation from inheritance-as-copying toward inheritance-as-refraction.

Established:

- Kotono's current crisis is genuinely her own rather than merely a repeat of sister grief;
- Kotono's mature idol answer becomes moonlight that can illuminate a path;
- Makino can recognize solar and lunar modes of inherited radiance without requiring either SUNNY or Tsuki to reproduce Mana;
- Miho's evaluation of Kotono can move from "Mana's sister" to Kotono as an idol.

Preferred inherited formulation:

> **Mana's legacy is now a vocabulary the living can transform, not a role they must inherit whole.**

# 13. Competition and professional strength

BIG4 establishes a mature distinction among forms of power.

## 13.1 Performance strength

Technique, stamina, precision, center fit, scoring, spectacle, and pressure tolerance are real and consequential.

## 13.2 Public legitimacy

Media framing and audience judgment can lag behind actual improvement. Labels acquire inertia.

## 13.3 Institutional protection

Strong performers can still be vulnerable when agencies or powerful adults withdraw protection or manipulate opportunity.

## 13.4 Relational resilience

A unit can become stronger by developing the capacity to disagree, lose, revise, and continue.

Preferred inherited competition principle:

> **The franchise does not oppose strength. It opposes giving strength unilateral jurisdiction over who deserves a future.**
