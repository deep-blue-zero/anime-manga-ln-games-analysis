# 1. Tranche-level result

C1-B does not replace the established series architecture:

- **answerable interdependence**;
- `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM`;
- `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`;
- `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`;
- `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`;
- `LEGACY_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_RESTORATION`.

It admits two subordinate refinements.

## 1.1 `SELF_AUTHORSHIP_AS_REFLEXIVE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE`

> **Self-authorship can be epistemic as well as expressive: making a song, performance, or public formulation can help a person discover, organize, or accept an inner state that was not yet fully available to conscious self-description.**

Strongest instances:

- Rui writes feelings into lyrics before she can accept the relational category those words imply;
- Shizuku turns the erased fan identity `ぷにもちどろっぷ` into `drop`, using authorship to integrate rather than repudiate her former self;
- Nagisa's retrospective narration and live experience help her articulate a new relational goal: support Kotono, shine herself, and stand beside her as an equal.

Safeguard:

> Artistic production is not automatically transparent autobiography. This mechanism applies only where the source itself shows creative work changing or clarifying self-understanding.

## 1.2 `DIFFERENTIATED_RECIPROCITY`

> **Reciprocity does not require identical personalities, histories, needs, or relational functions. A relationship can be genuinely mutual when each person retains independent agency and still chooses the other or the group as constitutive support.**

The Nagisa/Kotono pair is the clearest C1-B instance:

- Nagisa still wants to support Kotono but refuses to remain only her support;
- Kotono proves she can carry a stage alone but defines her chosen future through five-member Moon Tempest;
- mature mutuality therefore means **capacity without isolation and reliance without self-erasure**.

This is subordinate to `RECIPROCAL_STANDING` and `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`; it does not itself establish romance.
