# Hoshimi Anime ↔ Game Expansion Audit — TRINITYAiLE Origin Addendum

## Purpose

This addendum records TRINITYAiLE-origin facts disclosed after the twelve-episode anime endpoint and the Hoshimi anime/game expansion audit were frozen.

It does **not** revise the prospective information state of the anime and does not silently rewrite the parent cross-media audit.

The governing rule remains:

> **Later origin disclosure may explain an earlier presentation retrospectively; it may not become evidence that the earlier viewer already knew the explanation or that later developments had already occurred.**

TRINITYAiLE is especially sensitive to this distinction because its six origin bundles span childhood, unit formation, the Grand Prix, and post-Grand-Prix family/institutional material.

---

## A1. Rui's idol vocation predates paternal motivation

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_001_white_resolve`.

Child Rui explicitly says:

`わたし、アイドルになりたいの！`

Only later does her mother reveal that Asakura Kyoichi is Rui's biological father.

After that disclosure Rui's dream acquires the `絶対的なアイドル` proof structure:

`私達を捨てたこの人すら、絶対に振り向くほどの――`

### Revision to parent audit

The parent Hoshimi/anime interpretation that Rui's vocation should not be reduced to paternal recognition is **strengthened**.

Canonical formulation:

> **Asakura does not create Rui's idol dream. Paternal abandonment changes the criterion by which Rui believes the dream must be validated.**

Do not backfill the childhood chronology as anime-native disclosure if the anime itself did not state it at the same point.

---

## A2. `絶対的なアイドル` is not a literal universal-superiority category

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_003_light_my_fire`.

In a controlled three-song performance contest, Rui wins two songs narrowly, but Yu wins the aggregate total:

`優ちゃんの合計点が上だった`

`純粋なパフォーマンス勝負で、優ちゃんの勝ちだったよ`

Rui confirms:

`三本中の二本は僅差で私が勝っていたみたい`

### Revision to parent audit

Anime-era language around Rui as an "absolute" idol/center must not be converted into a claim that she is objectively superior to Yu in every performance dimension.

Canonical refinement:

> **Rui's center role is a differentiated role within TRINITYAiLE, not a mathematical theorem of universal individual supremacy.**

This supports the frozen anime reading that Yu and Sumire already constitute real relational infrastructure around Rui rather than being decorative attendants.

## A3. Hierarchy and equality are co-original in the unit

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_001_white_resolve`.

When Yu offers to be used instrumentally, Rui answers:

`「利用する」って考え方は好きじゃないの`

`組むのなら真剣に、対等でいたい`

### Cross-media significance

The anime's visually obvious Rui-centered hierarchy can now be retrospectively read against an explicit formation ethic:

> **TRINITYAiLE allows unequal roles while demanding equal standing as participants.**

Later conflict arises when Yu's self-suppression violates that founding premise from inside the relationship.

---

## A4. Yu's support role contains a hidden self-suppression history

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_003_light_my_fire`.

Yu has learned to read other people's reactions and remain second to avoid envy and distorted attention. Her desire to support Rui is genuine, but she initially expresses it by withholding her own full power.

Rui's correction is explicit:

`優は、全力を出したっていいの`

`私もすみれも、優の全力を受け止めて……`

`だから……私達の実力を信頼して、委ねてほしい`

### Revision to parent audit

The anime's Yu-as-support/relational-member reading is **strengthened but qualified**.

> **Support becomes healthy when it is chosen from full agency, not when Yu preemptively shrinks herself because she assumes Rui needs less competition.**

Do not reinterpret every anime-era act of support as pathology; the origin describes a developmental history, not a permanent diagnosis.

---

## A5. Sumire's absence proves competitive sufficiency is not the same as unit identity

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_004_r_aliser`.

Weather prevents Sumire from appearing. Rui and Yu perform as two and achieve a record score.

Sumire's fear is grounded in real evidence:

`二人でも……勝てちゃうんだ`

`私がいなくても本戦行けちゃうんだもん`

### Cross-media significance

The trio's later insistence on `三人でトリエル` should not be justified by claiming a duo is incapable of elite performance.

Canonical refinement:

> **TRINITYAiLE's three-person identity is chosen and constitutive, not merely a technical necessity proved by score.**

This makes anime-era Yu/Sumire relational importance stronger, not weaker: their significance is not reducible to marginal score contribution.

## A6. Mana becomes a benchmark partly through Asakura

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_002_shoot_for_the_sky`.

Asakura explicitly directs the emerging unit toward Mana as the professional standard, using LizNoir/Rio's orientation toward Mana as context.

### Revision to parent audit

The anime correctly presents Rui as intensely concerned with Mana and with whether she can surpass the legendary benchmark. The origin adds provenance:

> **Mana's role as TRINITYAiLE's summit is partly installed through Asakura's professional pedagogy and Rui's recognition structure.**

This is materially different from SUNNY PEACE or Tsuki no Tempest's internally lived relation to Mana.

## A7. Grand Prix defeat releases the benchmark without abolishing ambition

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_004_r_aliser`.

After losing to SUNNY PEACE, Rui moves toward:

`誰かを追いかけるのは止めて、自分達の道を行く`

`長瀬麻奈に縛られることのない、まだ見ぬ高みへ`

### Cross-media significance

This strongly supports the frozen anime endpoint interpretation:

> **TRINITYAiLE's defeat humanizes perfection by restoring open futurity.**

The unit remains competitive and ambitious. What changes is the jurisdiction of a dead benchmark over the meaning of "higher."

## A8. Rui/Asakura recognition is sequenced after greater self-direction

**Primary source:** `origin_tri_004_r_aliser`.

After the unit turns toward a future less governed by Mana, Asakura tells Rui:

`いいステージだった`

`いいアイドルになったな`

Rui later says:

`……パパ……`

### Revision to parent audit

The origin strengthens the anime-era father/professional reading while preserving a guardrail:

> **Asakura's professional recognition arrives after Rui has begun making recognition less central to the unit's future. That timing is thematically meaningful, but it does not resolve the paternal relationship.**

Producer competence and father adequacy remain separate analytical axes.

## A9. `Réaliser` origin placement supports, but cannot substitute for, Phase-5 song analysis

The `Réaliser` origin bundle places Sumire's belonging crisis and the Grand Prix defeat inside the same developmental movement later associated with the song's post-defeat anime placement.

### Cross-media significance

The correspondence is **STRENGTHENING contextual evidence** for the existing anime interpretation of `realiser` as a relational post-defeat epilogue.

### Guardrail

Do not infer lyrical meaning solely from shared title/context. The Phase-5 audiovisual/music audit must independently analyze the song's Japanese lyrics, arrangement, vocal distribution, and placement.

## A10. Post-Grand-Prix sisterhood and Yamagata material must not be backfilled

**Primary sources:** `origin_tri_005_because_sisters`, `origin_tri_006_violette_blooming_proudly`.

The mature three-sisters metaphor, Sumire's brother/brewery crisis, `全部欲しい`, and the Yamagata institutional solution are later developments.

### Chronology protection

They may inform the **future longitudinal TRINITYAiLE model**. They may not be treated as completed character knowledge inside the anime's earlier prospective state.

The safe sequence is:

> anime/Grand Prix formation and defeat → post-defeat reorientation → later explicit sisterhood/family crisis → mature `全部欲しい` institutional action.

This protects the anime's uncertainty and prevents mature unit philosophy from being projected backward as if it had always been consciously articulated.
