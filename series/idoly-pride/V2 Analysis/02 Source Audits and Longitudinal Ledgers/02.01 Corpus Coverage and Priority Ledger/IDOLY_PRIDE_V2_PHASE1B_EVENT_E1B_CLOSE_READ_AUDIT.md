# 3. Tranche-level adjudication

The independent E1-B thesis survives historical comparison:

> **E1-B asks what must remain answerable when ambition becomes optimization.**

The four stories are not anti-ranking, anti-BIG4, anti-victory, anti-professionalism, or anti-ambition. Every focal unit continues aiming upward. What the tranche pressures is the conversion of a legitimate goal into a totalizing metric that no longer has to answer to:

- bodily limits;
- unit identity;
- member disagreement;
- audience meaning;
- the purpose of the work;
- or the person whose future is supposedly being optimized.

The mature E1-B correction is therefore:

> **Ambition remains legitimate when its means, identity, relationships, bodily costs, and artistic purpose remain contestable from inside the relationship or unit.**

This is best understood as a lower-tier extension of the Tier-A concept of **answerable interdependence**.

A second tranche-level claim also survives:

> **Rank and victory are necessary but incomplete languages of idol value.**

E1-B never pretends results do not matter. Results influence rank, work, survival, public legitimacy, and access to BIG4. But each event produces information that the scoreboard alone cannot express.

# 4. Event 1 audit — 遥か頂に向かう秀麗
## Rei, Haruko, SUNNY PEACE, ambition, body, and the right to brake

## 4.1 Historical claim recovered

The historical event map classified this story as essential for Rei/Haruko and summarized its core as:

> Rei's ambition and Haruko's humane braking function collide; SUNNY PEACE needs both urgency and care for limits.

The historical SUNNY PEACE material also consistently treats Rei's danger as over-control and Haruko as mature warmth/caretaking rather than lack of seriousness.

## 4.2 Revision status: **STRENGTHENED + QUALIFIED**

The old macro-reading is correct. The fresh close read makes its mechanism much more exact.

The event is not merely an abstract balance between ambition and rest. Rei's problem is that an abstract summit — `トップアイドル` — becomes **metric capture**. Because she cannot yet specify the path from present BIG4 success to the top, she starts treating acceleration itself as evidence of seriousness:

- more practice;
- later practice;
- practice on days off;
- comparison with other units' visible intensity;
- suspicion of ordinary professional workload as stagnation;
- resentment toward Haruko's insistence on rest.

miho's advice is not itself pathological. She explicitly says different people and groups require different growth methods and Rei must experiment. Rei converts open experimentation into self-pressure.

The V2 correction is therefore:

> **Rei is not simply too ambitious. She temporarily turns uncertainty about development into self-exploitation.**

## 4.3 Institutional consequence: **STRENGTHENED**

This event materially refines the project's labor ledger.

The source explicitly supplies protective conditions: SUNNY PEACE receives rest, Haruko enforces breaks, and Haruko will forfeit a competitive opportunity when Rei is injured. Yet Rei still pushes herself to dizziness/injury.

Therefore:

> **idol overwork can be endogenous to high-achievement culture even when management is not directly imposing the excess.**

This does not absolve institutions. It changes what protection requires. Merely granting time off is insufficient if the culture of achievement makes rest feel like moral failure.

`OPEN-05` should now distinguish:

- management-imposed overwork;
- schedule-derived overwork;
- member-generated self-exploitation;
- relational safeguards that make rest practically usable.

## 4.4 Haruko: historical "humane brake" becomes a stronger model

Historical analysis was directionally correct to call Haruko a brake. The source shows that "brake" must not be confused with weak will.

Rei initially makes exactly that mistake. Haruko:

- ignores insults directed at her own old career;
- discourages retaliatory fighting;
- asks Rei to rest;
- forfeits a tournament after Rei is hurt.

Rei concludes that such gentleness cannot reach the top.

The decisive reversal occurs when rivals degrade Rei. Haruko immediately demands an apology and states:

> `競い合い、励まし合うことはあっても / わざわざバカにして、貶める必要なんてない`

This is a clean source-level norm:

> **competition and mutual stimulation are legitimate; deliberate degradation is not necessary to competition.**

Haruko's gentleness is therefore **selective restraint backed by boundary capacity**.

Revision status: **STRENGTHENED**.

This also independently extends the Tier-A BIG4 judgment of **competition without erasure** beyond the DoriKyun crisis. It appears here as ordinary professional ethics.

## 4.5 Haruko's care: **QUALIFIED**

A too-clean historical "Haruko supports Rei" summary misses the means.

Haruko cannot get direct access to Rei's worry, so she:

- consults miho privately;
- engineers more one-on-one time through the two-person unit battle;
- gives Rei a surface professional reason for the pairing;
- does not immediately disclose the full care motive.

This is **structured indirection / benevolent concealment**.

It is not coercive in the strong sense: Rei can disagree, remains an active partner, and later initiates closeness herself. But the event should not be cited as pure transparent communication.

The project should preserve the distinction:

> effective care can still contain mild paternalism without becoming ownership.

## 4.6 Rei's endpoint: **STRENGTHENED**

The event does not teach Rei to become less ambitious.

Its endpoint is:

> **private optimization pressure -> collectively answerable SUNNY PEACE strategy.**

Rei brings her future-oriented ideas back to all five members. She distinguishes "do not panic" from "do not change." Her urgency becomes discussable rather than self-punitive.

That is more precise than a generic "learn to rely on others" reading.

## 4.7 Open item preserved

Rei asks Haruko what Haruko's own idol goal is. Haruko begins to answer, but the event does not disclose the answer.

`OPEN-E1B-01 — HARUKO_OWN_IDOL_GOAL` remains **OPEN**.

Do not fill it from later events or character synthesis.

`adv-live-eve-2309-backside-04` is unavailable.

# 5. Event 2 audit — 翔けぬけるVictoire　誓いのplume
## TRINITYAiLE, victory dependence, risk, and horizontal co-authorship

## 5.1 Historical claim recovered

The historical priority layer called this a Core TRINITYAiLE story and summarized it as:

> the winning streak becomes a cage, and the unit rediscovers that risk, loss, and challenge matter more than sterile invincibility.

Historical unit synthesis already framed TRINITYAiLE as exceptional girls learning that reaching the top does not require cutting away the human attachments that make them whole, with Sumire's former-child-performer history especially important to her fear of self-burden and failure.

## 5.2 Revision status: **STRENGTHENED + QUALIFIED**

"Winning streak becomes a cage" survives.

"Risk/loss/challenge matter more than winning" is too absolute if taken literally.

The primary text insists that winning still matters:

- the 99-win streak carries public and career value;
- a 100th win creates leverage to challenge higher-ranked opponents;
- BIG4 access is materially important;
- Rui and Yu have nontrivial reasons to fear major failure.

The better V2 formulation is:

> **TRINITYAiLE has turned victory from a goal into a safety behavior.**

## 5.3 Post-scandal precarity is causal, not decorative

Rui and Yu's loss aversion is grounded in career history. The unit has experienced a fabricated scandal capable of destroying Rui and damaging Yu/Sumire through association. Yu explicitly understands that one sufficiently bad public outcome can end an idol career.

Therefore the 99-win streak is not mere complacency or arrogance. It is also proof that the unit has rebuilt legitimacy after precarity.

Revision status: **STRENGTHENED**.

The V2 unit model should preserve the causal sequence:

> **public precarity -> repeated victory -> regained safety -> safety becomes a cage.**

## 5.4 Sumire's disagreement: **STRENGTHENED**

Sumire's child-actor history gives her a different empirical lesson. She once did what was requested, avoided mistakes, and performed reliably — and still watched work disappear.

So Sumire knows something Rui/Yu temporarily lose sight of:

> **low variance can also erase a performer.**

Her argument for challenge is not adolescent thrill-seeking. It comes from professional experience with the limits of safety.

## 5.5 Fan-survey result: **NEW V2 PRECISION**

Makino preserves old fan surveys. When the members examine them, the recurring answer to "TRINITYAiLE's most memorable activity" is not their long winning streak. It is the NEXT VENUS semifinal loss to SUNNY PEACE.

Sumire's conclusion is explicit:

> `ファンは勝敗じゃなくて私達を見てるんだよ`

This must not be inflated into "winning does not matter."

The correct distinction is:

> **competitive result and remembered performance value are overlapping but non-identical variables.**

A loss can remain an artistic peak in fan memory even while remaining a competitive loss.

## 5.6 Makino's management: **STRENGTHENED**

Makino does not order the challenge.

He:

- presents the opportunity;
- allows refusal;
- encourages discussion until the members are satisfied;
- uses fan evidence to change the information environment;
- leaves the final decision with the unit.

This is a high-quality example for `13_MANAGER_PRODUCTION_AUTONOMY_AND_THE_ETHICS_OF_GUIDANCE.md`:

> **guidance by evidence and framing rather than outcome seizure.**

## 5.7 Performance architecture: **NEW / STRENGTHENED**

The unit's renewed challenge is not just rhetorical.

They alter the production itself:

- new song;
- revised lyrics;
- new choreography;
- Yu rejects language that places members simply "behind" Rui;
- the preferred image becomes `三人で肩を並べて` — three standing shoulder to shoulder;
- Rui proposes putting Yu/Sumire more visibly forward rather than reproducing the ordinary center-heavy staging.

This is not abolition of Rui's center or leadership.

The safest formulation is:

> **TRINITYAiLE moves toward horizontally co-authored performance while preserving differentiated roles.**

That dovetails with, but is more specific than, the historical "communal ambition" thesis.

## 5.8 "The best TRINITYAiLE is now": **STRENGTHENED**

Rui's statement:

> `いつだって、トリエルの一番は今`

rejects two traps at once:

- nostalgia for the emotionally famous Sunny loss;
- fetishization of the 99-win streak as completed identity.

The past can instruct without becoming the unit's sovereign version.

## 5.9 Open item preserved

The unit narrowly defeats the top-five opponent. Rui nonetheless privately recognizes that their present ceiling may still be below BIG4 and that a time may come when `絆の力だけではどうにもならない` — bond alone may not close an objective capacity gap.

`OPEN-E1B-03 — TRINITY_OBJECTIVE_CAPACITY_GAP` remains **OPEN**.

Do not resolve it by importing later TRINITY events.

# 6. Event 3 audit — すれ違いのディソナンス
## LizNoir, commercial heteronomy, sponsor power, and the unfinished purpose of "top idol"

## 6.1 Historical claim recovered

The historical event map called this an essential LizNoir identity-crisis story asking whether becoming top requires abandoning "LizNoir-ness," especially under sponsor pressure toward cuteness.

The detailed historical LizNoir reading framed the central dialectic as:

- Rio: change is necessary; interpreting others can be growth;
- Aoi: not every change is growth; some changes become self-betrayal.

It also correctly identified Aoi as a necessary counterweight to Rio's over-adaptive leadership.

## 6.2 Revision status: **STRENGTHENED + SPLIT**

The historical reading is strong, but "authenticity versus commercial compromise" is still too broad.

The primary text supports a sharper distinction:

> **change versus dispossession.**

Aoi is not against external direction as such. Rio is not in favor of surrendering identity. Their disagreement concerns when professional interpretation of someone else's intention remains authored adaptation and when it becomes substitution of external desire for performer agency.

The event therefore supports three analytically separate forms of change:

1. **developmental change** — the performer gains capacity without losing standing to answer the work;
2. **adaptive change** — the performer interprets an unfamiliar professional brief while retaining co-authorship;
3. **dispossessive change** — the performer's identity is treated as material another actor may rewrite without meaningful answerability.

This three-way model should replace a simple authentic/fake binary in later professional-labor synthesis.

## 6.3 The initial "cute" problem is about specificity

Aoi's objection is not simply "LizNoir should never be cute."

The generic poses, smiles, and lyrics could be assigned to almost any idol unit. His professional question is effectively:

> if anyone could perform this, why hire LizNoir?

That makes `リズノワらしさ` a claim about **specificity and authorship**, not merely preservation of a cool aesthetic.

Revision status: **STRENGTHENED**.

## 6.4 Sponsor-fan capture: **NEW V2 EMPHASIS**

The external producer eventually reveals that the facility owner's son is a major LizNoir fan who uses sponsor leverage to request:

- his own lyrics;
- a particular "cute LizNoir" image;
- Kokoro as center;
- his preferred version of the unit.

This is a highly important institutional case because the coercive pressure originates in affection rather than hostility.

The event therefore yields a new distinction:

> **fandom as reception is not the same as fandom fused with jurisdiction.**

A fan may love an idol intensely. When money/institutional power gives that love authority over casting, lyrics, center assignment, or image, admiration can become **performer capture**.

This should enter both the audience and professional-industry ledgers.

## 6.5 External producer failure: **NEW V2 PRECISION**

The external producer privately disagrees with the sponsor but transmits the sponsor's demands downward while concealing the dispute.

Aoi states the producer's proper role explicitly:

> stand between sponsor and performer.

The producer's error is therefore not merely bad taste. It is a failure of **mediation and upward representation**.

He protects himself from sponsor conflict by transferring the conflict cost to the performers.

This is useful comparative evidence for Makino's role:

> a producer is not merely an efficient conduit for institutional demand; the producer must remain answerable in both directions.

## 6.6 Rio's resolution: **STRENGTHENED**

Rio does not choose pure defiance or pure obedience.

She insists on direct dialogue with the sponsor and helps convert the brief into a co-authored campaign. This vindicates her belief that unfamiliar external intention can produce growth — **but only after Aoi's resistance forces the underlying power structure into the open**.

Thus the final professional lesson is reciprocal:

> Rio's flexibility without Aoi risks surrender.  
> Aoi's resistance without Rio risks premature exit.  
> Together they create negotiated authorship.

Historical analysis already saw this reciprocity; V2 grounds it more precisely in producer/sponsor structure.

## 6.7 Consent/formal caution

The final campaign benefits from candid footage of the group playing in the pool, and the members like the result. The source describes the camera as having continued `こっそり`.

The text does not provide enough procedural detail to turn this into an affirmative general case study of consent-respecting candid production.

Record:

`OPEN-E1B-05 — CANDID_CAMPAIGN_CAPTURE_CONSENT_DETAIL` = **UNRESOLVED / narrow formal-ethical caution**.

## 6.8 Commercial success does not resolve the actual LizNoir conflict

This is the largest correction to any reading that treats the campaign as the event's resolution.

After the successful CM, LizNoir almost loses a live battle to a rookie unit because Rio and Aoi are still out of sync.

Aoi asks whether Rio is prepared to discard even `僕達らしさ` for the summit, then asks:

> `誰のために / なんのために、トップアイドルを目指すの？`

Rio cannot answer.

Therefore:

> **the commercial problem is solved; the teleological problem is not.**

The event deliberately ends with LizNoir's top-idol purpose unresolved.

`OPEN-E1B-04 — LIZNOIR_TOP_IDOL_PURPOSE` remains **OPEN**.

Later Aoi/Rio material may answer or mature this question, but it cannot be projected backward into the E1-B freeze.

## 6.9 Historical later synthesis contamination warning

Historical LizNoir documents contain later-event formulations in which Rio/Aoi reach more mature definitions of freedom, passion, battle, and LizNoir identity. Those are useful future comparison targets, but they are **not admitted here as the answer to `OPEN-E1B-04`**.

This is exactly the kind of later-canon backfill the V2 method is designed to prevent.

# 7. Event 4 audit — 君を照らすムーンライトロード
## BIG4-period Tsuki addendum, separation, reciprocal illumination, and non-prescriptive idol influence

## 7.1 Chronology adjudication: **CORRECTION / HARD GUARDRAIL**

Despite the 2024 release date, the story explicitly frames itself around:

> `月のテンペストとどりきゅんの決戦前夜`

and reconstructs the two-week run-up to the DoriKyun rematch.

It is therefore a **BIG4-period retrospective/addendum**, not evidence of later post-BIG4 maturation merely because of release chronology.

The historical detailed reading correctly recognized this Dori-rematch placement. Revision status: **CONFIRMED**.

This finding must be recorded in the chronology ledger so later event ordering does not silently over-mature Tsuki.

## 7.2 Kotono's departure: historical "hospitality" thesis

The earlier detailed reading correctly noticed that Sakura did not passively approve Kotono leaving. Sakura opposed it. The remaining four Tsuki members also wanted Kotono to stay, but ultimately decided to respect a serious resolved choice, trust her, and preserve a place she could return to.

The historical formulation called this a kind of hospitality:

> build a stronger moon so Kotono has a place to return.

Revision status: **STRENGTHENED**.

V2 makes the ethical structure more precise:

> **agency-respecting separation.**

Mei's line is explicit:

> `本当の友達なら、琴乃ちゃんを信じて、応援して / 送り出すべき`

Love does not grant permanent jurisdiction over Kotono's choice.

At the same time, Suzu's `琴乃の帰る場所` is not a frozen preservation project. The four-member unit must become strong enough that Kotono can meaningfully want to return.

Therefore:

> **a return home is living infrastructure, not a museum of the old relationship.**

This integrates cleanly with the Tier-A BIG4 thesis that Tsuki is reconstituted rather than simply restored.

## 7.3 Sakura's influence on Kotono: **STRENGTHENED**

Kotono remembers Sakura's earlier confidence that SUNNY PEACE could make an ambitious new song under pressure. At the time, Kotono interpreted Sakura as simply unusually strong.

Now Kotono can say something similar because she understands that Sakura's confidence was grounded in trust in other people.

This is not personality imitation.

It is:

> **internalized relational competence.**

Kotono learns a way of trusting from Sakura without becoming Sakura.

## 7.4 Public backlash and live reintegration: **QUALIFIED**

Kotono faces accusations that her departure/return was manipulative, attention-seeking, or proof of insufficient ability. The live begins with hostility mixed with support. During the performance, hostile noise diminishes, blue penlights increase, and she receives `おかえり` calls.

This demonstrates one audience transition.

It does **not** establish the universal claim that a strong live automatically resolves public scandal or criticism.

Record the narrower conclusion:

> **performance can alter the social meaning of a contested return when an audience remains open to being answered by the stage.**

## 7.5 Reciprocal illumination: **CONFIRMED + STRENGTHENED**

The historical reading correctly identified Kotono's central realization:

> she wanted to illuminate other people's roads, but she herself has been walking a road illuminated by others.

The primary text makes this explicit through her internal recognition during the live.

This moves Tsuki's moonlight philosophy away from unilateral beneficence.

> **The guide is also guided. The idol who illuminates is also a recipient of light.**

That is a strong unit-level maturation already available inside the BIG4 period.

## 7.6 Non-prescriptive illumination: **STRENGTHENED / CANONICAL TERM**

This is the strongest E1-B addition and one of the most useful event-level philosophical results in the corpus.

After Tsuki's performance, two former/current idols interpret the same stage in opposite ways:

1. an idol whose ambition had already been damaged by DoriKyun uses full-force defeat by Tsuki to let go and retire without lingering attachment;
2. a former idol working as venue staff realizes she still wants the stage and decides to try returning.

The event itself connects both outcomes to Tsuki illuminating a path.

Therefore the correct mature formulation is not:

> idols inspire people to keep chasing dreams.

It is:

> **idols can help make a person's path perceptible without dictating which path must be chosen.**

Canonical E1-B term:

> **NON-PRESCRIPTIVE ILLUMINATION**

This is ethically stronger than generic hope because it preserves the receiver's authorship.

The same performance can support:

- continuing;
- stopping;
- returning;
- changing direction.

Its value lies in clarification, not command.

## 7.7 Defeat becomes plural

The two post-live encounters also demonstrate that defeat has no single developmental meaning.

For one idol, defeat is closure.
For another observer, the same performance is reopening.
For Tsuki, hostile prior defeat becomes material for defining a new performance philosophy.

This strengthens the tranche-wide rule:

> **failure becomes information only when a person or relationship retains enough continuity to interpret it.**

## 7.8 Song concept: source-native unit philosophy

Tsuki's vague desire for a song expressing the "new Tsuki" becomes concrete only after these encounters.

Kotono articulates an idol who lights the road like moonlight so another person can feel or discover something and move forward. Nagisa distinguishes that light from the overwhelming brightness of the sun.

This is not merely post-hoc thematic commentary. The observed effects on other idols become material for the unit's own song concept.

Thus:

> **performance philosophy becomes recursively authored from performance consequence.**

## 7.9 Hoshimi inter-unit support: **STRENGTHENED**

SUNNY PEACE and other Hoshimi members assist Tsuki during the two-week preparation window. Rei supports choreography, Shizuku prepares practice material, and Sakura coordinates broader help. Rei/Shizuku explicitly frame this as reciprocal because Tsuki previously helped SUNNY PEACE.

Sakura states:

> `ユニットは違っても / 私達はデビューからずっと一緒の仲間`

This strongly supports the post-E1-A model of Hoshimi as a **plural professional continuity platform**:

- units remain distinct;
- rivalry remains real;
- support crosses unit borders;
- help creates no demand for sameness.

## 7.10 Sakura/Kotono rivalry: **STRENGTHENED**

Kotono explicitly admits long-standing envy of Sakura's brightness/strength. Sakura refuses the comparison as identity substitution: Kotono is not Sakura because Kotono is Kotono.

Kotono then names sun and moon as different rather than hierarchically interchangeable and still insists she wants to surpass Sakura in a decisive rematch.

Therefore:

> **rivalry matures through differentiation, not de-escalation.**

The ethical solution to envy is not to stop competing. It is to stop treating the rival's identity as the form one must become.

This is one of the cleanest lower-tier extensions of BIG4's **competition without erasure**.

## 7.11 Historical comparison result

The detailed historical Moonlight Road reading was unusually strong. It had already recovered:

- Sakura's prior opposition to Kotono leaving;
- the remaining four preserving and strengthening a place of return;
- Kotono learning the meaning behind Sakura's confidence;
- reciprocal illumination;
- one idol retiring and another returning;
- moonlight as clarification rather than universal command.

Revision status overall: **STRENGTHENED**, not overturned.

V2's gains are primarily:

- harder chronology routing;
- agency-respecting separation as the ethical category;
- living return infrastructure as distinct from restoration;
- formalizing **non-prescriptive illumination**;
- audience-conversion caution;
- cross-unit reciprocal labor;
- rivalry-through-differentiation.

# 9. E1-B additions to the V2 conceptual vocabulary

The following terms are admitted as useful working concepts.

## 9.1 Metric capture

A legitimate objective becomes so dominant that proxy behaviors begin substituting for the actual developmental purpose.

Primary E1-B case: Rei converts uncertainty about how to reach the top into escalating work intensity.

## 9.2 Victory dependence

A unit treats repeated victory not only as achievement but as the condition of safety, making high-value risk increasingly intolerable.

Primary E1-B case: TRINITYAiLE after public precarity.

## 9.3 Developmental / adaptive / dispossessive change

A three-way distinction for professional transformation:

- **developmental** — increased capacity with retained agency;
- **adaptive** — interpreting external demands while retaining co-authorship;
- **dispossessive** — external desire substitutes for performer authorship.

Primary E1-B case: LizNoir.

## 9.4 Performer capture

Audience/fan desire becomes institutionally empowered to dictate the performer's identity, image, lyrics, center, or role.

Primary E1-B case: Tokyo Seaside sponsor-fan.

## 9.5 Non-prescriptive illumination

A performance helps another person perceive or choose a path without defining what the correct destination must be.

Primary E1-B case: Tsuki's same live contributing to one idol's retirement and another idol's return.

## 9.6 Living return infrastructure

A relationship/unit preserves the possibility of return by continuing to develop, rather than freezing itself in the departed person's old form.

Primary E1-B case: four-person Tsuki preparing a place Kotono can meaningfully choose to return to.

# 17. Final E1-B governing synthesis

E1-B is a tranche about **ambition after legitimacy**.

These are not beginners asking whether they deserve to be idols. They are established units with real success, real rankings, real audiences, and real professional identities. Their problem is what happens when the language that helped them rise becomes too narrow to govern the next stage.

Rei learns that urgency cannot become a private tax on the body.

TRINITYAiLE learns that a winning record cannot become a shelter from every consequential challenge.

Rio and Aoi learn that professional flexibility is valuable only while the performers remain participants in authoring what change means.

Kotono learns that idol light is ethically strongest when it does not decide another person's future for them.

The tranche therefore adds a governing lower-tier proposition:

> **The series does not ask its idols to choose between ambition and care, professionalism and selfhood, competition and relationship, or influence and autonomy. It asks whether each side remains capable of answering the other.**

This is why E1-B belongs in the Core event layer.
