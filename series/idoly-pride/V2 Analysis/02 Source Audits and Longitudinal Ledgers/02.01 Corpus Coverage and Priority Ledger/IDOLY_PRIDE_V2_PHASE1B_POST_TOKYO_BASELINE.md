# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — POST-TOKYO GOVERNING BASELINE

## 0. Purpose

This document freezes the **maximum inherited state after `tokyo_014_with_beyond_the_miracle`**.

The next governing main-story pass may use these propositions as established history without re-proving them, but it may not import later BIG4, Stellar, event, bond, card, message, or historical conclusions as though Tokyo had already established them.

The baseline is intentionally narrower than the full Tokyo audit.

---

# 1. Institutional state

## 1.1 Hoshimi Production

Hoshimi is now a materially expanded multi-unit agency and has demonstrated a recognizable governance ethic.

The strongest established principle is Makino's:

`アイドルは事務所の道具じゃない`

`むしろアイドルが事務所を利用して輝いてくれればいい`

The preferred institutional formulation is:

> **Hoshimi's authority is legitimate when it enlarges the idols' capacity to make informed, authored professional choices rather than treating them as instruments of managerial design.**

This does not mean Hoshimi is flat, leaderless, or conflict-free.

It means idols retain standing in decisions whose consequences they must live.

## 1.2 BanPro / Asakura

Asakura has been cleared/released after evidence demonstrates that Himeno fabricated/manipulated the embezzlement case.

His earlier termination of TRINITYAiLE and LizNoir is retrospectively understood as protective career separation rather than simple abandonment.

Do **not** infer that Rui's paternal wound is therefore repaired. Professional vindication and adequate fatherhood remain separate questions.

## 1.3 Saegusa

Saegusa's disappearance during early Tokyo is retrospectively explained as covert evidence-gathering/protective work.

He returns as an active senior professional.

His secrecy may still warrant later ethical scrutiny; Tokyo does not establish that withholding information was the only or ideal method.

## 1.4 Himeno / I.E.A.

Himeno's attempt to capture institutional authority is defeated.

Her failure does **not** establish that industry coordination or regulation is unnecessary.

The governing institutional question carried forward is therefore:

> **How can collective industry structure exist without becoming one actor's jurisdiction over idol value and professional opportunity?**

---

# 2. Makino Kouhei

Makino now operates as more than Mana's former manager or the caretaker of Hoshimi's original ten.

Established Tokyo developments:

- he accepts institutional responsibility after Saegusa's disappearance;
- he shelters displaced elite units;
- he negotiates transfers and industry crises;
- he explicitly treats complete information and idol choice as managerial duties;
- he refuses the premise that idols are agency tools;
- he challenges Rui's attempt to decide unilaterally that she is a burden;
- he creates intermediate options rather than forcing false binaries;
- he allows idols and colleagues to support him;
- he delegates crisis work rather than centralizing every solution in himself;
- he can celebrate SUNNY PEACE going beyond Mana.

Preferred baseline formulation:

> **Makino exercises fiduciary authority: real professional power whose legitimacy is organized around preserving and widening idol agency.**

The customizable game manager remains Makino Kouhei under the project's governing identity rule.

---

# 3. SUNNY PEACE

## 3.1 Competitive status

SUNNY PEACE wins I-UNITY and produces a stage treated within the narrative as legendary.

This result is later procedurally preserved after investigation of Himeno's interference.

Do **not** infer universal repeatable technical supremacy over IIIX. IIIX explicitly distinguishes SUNNY's superiority **in that singular moment** from its own belief about repeatable ordinary conditions.

## 3.2 Unit philosophy

The current mature SUNNY formulation is:

> **SUNNY PEACE converts shared light into recursive recognition among members and fans.**

The group's explicit logic is:

- receive the fans' feelings;
- put them into song/performance;
- return them;
- receive the audience's renewed response;
- combine the five members' and audience members' "pieces" into widening bonds.

The a cappella final proves this philosophy is operational under pressure: the audience becomes an active participant in maintaining the live.

## 3.3 Sakura

Sakura remains strongly self-authored.

Her older heartbeat/Mana language has not disappeared entirely; she can still describe herself as having been guided by Mana's heart.

This is currently best treated as **provenance/habit language**, not restored jurisdiction.

Her Tokyo growth is from singular illuminating center toward a distributed system in which others can illuminate her and all five members can illuminate one another.

---

# 4. Tsuki no Tempest

Tsuki has now experienced major elite-level defeat.

The unit has incorporated two lessons:

1. perfectionism/fear can destroy the freedom necessary for excellent performance;
2. defeat is painful but can become professional and moral knowledge rather than evidence of personal invalidity.

Kotono explicitly states that her idol activity now has little to do with Mana and that she needs to face fans rather than her sister.

This is strong evidence that Mana is no longer the operating authority over Kotono's vocation.

### Open state

Kotono experiences Sakura/SUNNY as suddenly `遠く` after SUNNY's I-UNITY ascent.

Do not yet decide whether this is:

- rivalry;
- insecurity;
- fear of relational distance;
- admiration;
- self-comparison;
- another structure.

The next main-story arc must earn that interpretation.

---

# 5. LizNoir

LizNoir has:

- experienced meaningful loss to TRINITYAiLE;
- refused scapegoating after defeat;
- affirmed happiness in being LizNoir and being idols;
- remained at Hoshimi by choice after BanPro is restored;
- gained SUNNY PEACE as a **living** high-level rival.

Preferred baseline formulation:

> **Tokyo restores rivalry to reciprocal futurity.**

Mana was an unavailable dead benchmark. SUNNY is a rival who can change, answer back, win, lose, and be met again.

LizNoir's mature pride is therefore compatible with losing without identity collapse.

---

# 6. TRINITYAiLE

TRINITYAiLE has survived:

- abrupt BanPro termination;
- coercive Preta Porte affiliation;
- Rui's paternal scandal exposure;
- fabricated public scandal;
- suspension/removal from I-UNITY;
- loss of ordinary access to the stage.

The trio's governing Tokyo ethic is an extension of its origin structure:

> **care cannot require one member to unilaterally reduce or remove herself while deciding for the others what risks they are allowed to accept.**

Yu and Sumire share Rui's burden rather than allowing Rui to become a sacrificial shield.

Makino later makes the principle explicit: Rui cannot decide alone whether helping her counts as unacceptable burden.

TRINITY also refuses match-fixing.

At the Tokyo endpoint, the trio remains at Hoshimi by choice even after BanPro's restoration.

# 7. IIIX

This section must be kept especially precise because the origin corpus crosses Tokyo chronology.

## 7.1 State actually reached by the Tokyo endpoint

Established:

- IIIX remains an elite, technically controlled, strongly score-literate unit;
- it values full-strength performance and real onstage comparison;
- it knowingly benefits from some of Himeno's institutional manipulation;
- it distinguishes its own performance from the surrounding corruption;
- Miho escalates her anti-Mana grievance into a concrete cultural-erasure project;
- fran/kana can support strategic displacement of Mana without sharing Miho's grief logic;
- IIIX refuses or limits interference when corruption threatens the evidentiary meaning of its own performance;
- IIIX barely defeats Tsuki;
- SUNNY PEACE defeats IIIX in the I-UNITY final in a singular exceptional stage;
- IIIX acknowledges SUNNY's superiority in that moment;
- IIIX believes its repeatable normal-condition model remains stronger;
- IIIX still explicitly says victory is the only thing the three share.

Preferred ethical diagnosis:

> **IIIX's Tokyo failure is responsibility compartmentalization: it demands fairness inside the stage while trying to treat corruption of the conditions surrounding the stage as somebody else's moral account.**

## 7.2 What is NOT yet inherited

Do **not** import the later `Re-Polished` endpoint into this baseline unless the next arc's chronology independently reaches it.

Specifically, Tokyo does not itself establish as its endpoint:

- completed voluntary dissolution/re-formation;
- mature freelance self-management;
- the small-live re-encounter with audience intimacy;
- the fully articulated later "second founding" state.

Those findings exist in the full IIIX origin corpus but have a later inheritance boundary.

# 8. Mana Nagase

Mana remains:

- historically causal;
- loved;
- professionally legendary;
- a contested object of public memory;
- possible supernatural residue.

But Tokyo materially weakens her status as final horizon.

Key established developments:

- Kotono's idolhood is no longer principally Mana-directed;
- SUNNY PEACE is explicitly described as going `その先` beyond Mana;
- Rio/Aoi distinguish living SUNNY rivalry from an unreachable dead wall;
- Miho's administrative plan to overwrite Mana is displaced symbolically by a new legend created through performance.

Preferred baseline formulation:

> **Mana has become history rather than terminal horizon.**

## 8.1 Supernatural status

Makino, Sakura, and Kotono experience a Mana-like reassuring voice around the I-UNITY final.

This supports supernatural interpretation but does not independently verify an objective return.

Even under a supernatural reading, Mana does not regain authorship of the living idols' choices.

# 9. Competition and VENUS/metrics

Tokyo strongly rejects an anti-competition interpretation.

Established:

- rivals can co-produce higher performance;
- victory creates responsibility rather than permanent ontological superiority;
- defeat creates pain and knowledge rather than invalidation;
- technical measurement can be meaningful;
- repeatable excellence and singular event excellence are both narratively real;
- institutional manipulation corrupts the meaning of results by attacking the comparison itself.

Preferred baseline thesis:

> **The franchise critiques metric sovereignty and institutional capture more strongly than it critiques measurement, ranking, or competition.**

A score can be evidence without becoming the complete ontology of value.

---

# 10. Idol ontology

Tokyo does not establish one universal answer to `アイドルとは何か`.

Different units continue to embody distinct grammars.

The strongest meta-rule carried forward is:

> **Idolhood becomes meaningful through authored relations among person, craft, audience, rivals, institutions, and future—not because one authority supplies the correct definition from outside.**

SUNNY PEACE's reciprocal fan circuit is a major answer, not necessarily the only possible one.
