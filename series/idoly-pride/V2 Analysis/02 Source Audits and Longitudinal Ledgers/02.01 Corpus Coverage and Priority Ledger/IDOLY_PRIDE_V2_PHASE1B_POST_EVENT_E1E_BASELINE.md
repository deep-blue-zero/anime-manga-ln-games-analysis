# 1. Governing model after full E1 completion

The series-level ethical thesis remains:

> **answerable interdependence**

The E1 event layer now supports a constitutional refinement:

> **Strong roles, memories, institutions, rivalries, and ambitions remain legitimate when the people living inside them retain standing to answer, contest, revise, refuse, lose, return, reinterpret, or continue.**

Preferred late-stage term:

> **ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM**

Do not read “constitutional” as a literal legal institution. It is an analytical description of repeatable relational/professional structures whose authority is bounded by procedures of answer and revision.

---

# 2. Competition / defeat — final E1 governing rule

Competition remains constitutive.

E1-A through E1-E collectively reject both:

- anti-competition sentimentality;
- winner-sovereignty.

Current governing distinctions:

- rank affects opportunity, prestige, and career;
- center affects visible artistic responsibility;
- victory can matter deeply;
- defeat can reveal real comparative difference;
- none of these automatically exhaust personhood, artistic value, or future standing.

Preferred rule:

> **A competitive result has jurisdiction over the result, not unlimited jurisdiction over the competitor's right to continue becoming.**

This is now demonstrated in:

- IIIX's non-terminal absolute-victory model;
- Tsuki's five-person center contest;
- Kotono/Suzu accepting Nagisa's win without ontological displacement;
- SUNNY PEACE treating BIG4 as a milestone rather than final summit.

# 10. DoriKyun — final E1 ideological state

## 10.1 Explicit diagnosis

DoriKyun now directly states opposition to:

- adults who treat idols primarily as money-making tools;
- idols who reproduce that system;
- an industry they believe requires purification.

They aim to make idol work the world's greatest entertainment.

The anti-exploitation diagnosis is therefore no longer merely inferred.

## 10.2 Ethical category

Preferred term:

> **AUTHORITARIAN_ANTI_EXPLOITATION**

Their diagnosis contains legitimate concern about:

- commodification;
- disposability;
- weak performer agency;
- industry stagnation.

Their method remains dangerous because it claims excessive jurisdiction over:

- which idols deserve to continue;
- whose dreams may be destroyed;
- what counts as legitimate strength;
- how industry reform must occur.

## 10.3 Usefulness vs necessity

Historical wording that DoriKyun are **necessary external pressure** is no longer current authority.

Current rule:

> **DoriKyun can be an unusually powerful developmental catalyst, but their pressure is not a universal necessary condition of growth.**

> **PARTIALLY RESOLVED — USEFUL_BUT_NOT_NECESSARY**

## 10.4 Counterexample reform

DoriKyun begins considering that strong idols such as Tsuki, TRINITYAiLE, and SUNNY PEACE may themselves change the industry.

Preferred term:

> **COUNTEREXAMPLE_REFORM**

The rival changes DoriKyun's ideological model by succeeding without becoming DoriKyun.

## 10.5 Externality remains

DoriKyun still refuses Hoshimi absorption.

Current relationship:

> **productive external rivalry without institutional domestication**

Do not upgrade this into a claim that Hoshimi requires DoriKyun to exist.
