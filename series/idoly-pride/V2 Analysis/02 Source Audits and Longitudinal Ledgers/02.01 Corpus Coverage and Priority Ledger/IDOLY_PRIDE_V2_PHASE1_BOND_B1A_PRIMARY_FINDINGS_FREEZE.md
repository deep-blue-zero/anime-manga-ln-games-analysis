# 1. Tranche-level result

B1-A validates the bond layer's distinct analytical function. Its highest-value evidence is not large public plot movement but **private jurisdiction over identity, memory, family access, and future possibility**.

The tranche supports the following umbrella refinement:

> **`SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`**
>
> **Public visibility, professional role, family connection, inherited memory, and present success may create relationships and obligations without granting indiscriminate access to the person's private self, future vocation, or relational boundaries.**

This is not a replacement for `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM`; it is its private-scale extension.

B1-A repeatedly shows people deciding:

- who may contact them;
- which family ties still possess standing;
- which memories can be carried forward;
- how a predecessor can remain loved after differentiation;
- which public persona is strategically useful without being wholly false;
- which current professional resources should be converted into future optionality.

A second tranche-level rule follows:

> **`PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`**
>
> **The private register is not automatically the only true self, and the public/professional register is not automatically false. Authenticity remains a question of answerability across registers, not proximity to secrecy.**

kana and fran both display extreme register-switching. Yet the sources also show genuine enjoyment, care, aspiration, and competence operating through public/professional presentation. Their private hostility or cynicism therefore cannot simply be treated as the truth that invalidates all polished public behavior.

# 2. kana — family access, industry counterpower, and strategic public self

## 2.1 `st-company-bond-kan-01-01` — disturbing contact and opaque counterpower

The story begins with kana encountering an outside producer, Sakamoto. Her internal narration recalls that miho previously complained that he sent her `キモいメール` (creepy/disturbing messages). The source does **not** specify the exact content of those messages, so B1-A does not classify the conduct more narrowly than the text permits.

kana then deliberately mentions Hoshimi's manager and Saegusa. Sakamoto visibly changes demeanor, and kana remembers:

> `三枝社長が / 裏から手を回して、こいつのこと黙らせたんだっけ`

TEXTUAL FACT:

- kana understands Saegusa to have used behind-the-scenes influence to make Sakamoto stop or become quiet after the prior problem involving miho;
- the exact method, authority, threat, bargain, or institutional mechanism is not supplied.

This yields:

> **`INFORMAL_PROTECTIVE_COUNTERPOWER`**
>
> A powerful actor may protect vulnerable professionals through informal industry leverage even when the procedure by which that leverage operates remains opaque.

The result is protective in this source context, but its opacity matters. This is **not** evidence that all unreviewable informal power becomes legitimate when used benevolently.

Companion caution:

> **`PROTECTIVE_OUTCOME_DOES_NOT_RESOLVE_PROCEDURAL_ACCOUNTABILITY`**

This materially advances the Saegusa/institutional-power side of the open register without resolving how such power is governed.

kana then infers that Sakamoto may now be bothering another idol, Reia, based on a recent consultation from her. She uses the information asymmetry to secure a television guest appearance and plans to contact Reia to learn more and teach her countermeasures.

This is not passive victimhood and not transparent institutional reporting. It is a peer using informal information and leverage against someone she regards as dangerous or exploitative.

Admit:

> **`PEER_PROTECTIVE_INFORMATION_NETWORK`**

and, more narrowly:

> **`COUNTERLEVERAGE_WITHOUT_FORMAL_PROCESS`**

The second term is descriptive rather than celebratory. kana's countermeasure may help another woman while still operating through the same opaque ecosystem of favors, reputational threats, and private knowledge that makes the industry ethically difficult.

## 2.2 `st-company-bond-kan-01-04` — retaliation is psychologically real without becoming accomplished fact

kana privately fantasizes about sending another producer's messages to a weekly magazine and to his wife, explicitly imagining job loss and family collapse.

The important evidence is that her abrasive private register can include **genuine retaliatory ideation**, not merely harmless teasing.

Do **not** infer that every threatened retaliation is carried out. Do **not** sanitize the scene into secret affection.

This strengthens the existing E2/E3 rule:

> **`AUTHENTICITY_WITHOUT_ETHICAL_EXONERATION`**

A private behavior can be authentic to kana and still be vindictive, disproportionate, or ethically contestable.

## 2.3 `st-company-bond-kan-01-08` — selective family access

The story first shows kana in a warm public mode during a rural-location shoot. Elderly viewers, children, and other locals recognize her; she agrees to photographs and handshakes. After the shoot she privately concludes that it was a genuinely good day and that the townspeople were kind enough to make her feel emotionally refreshed.

This is important because her public warmth is not reducible to a totally false mask. The behavior is professionally performed, but the positive affect survives after the audience disappears.

Admit:

> **`STRATEGIC_PUBLIC_REGISTER_WITH_GENUINE_AFFECT`**

The same story then creates a sharply different family-access problem. A producer tells kana that a person named Kojima has been trying to obtain her contact information. kana initially hopes:

> `もしかして……お父さん……？`
>
> `やっとkanaのことを見つけてくれたんだ！`

When she learns the caller is her grandfather, she immediately rejects the contact:

> `絶対にそいつには教えないで下さい`
>
> `kanaの祖父を名乗ってるだけで、もうとっくに縁は切ってるんで`

She ends the story wishing:

> `お父さん、早くkanaを見つけてよ`

The source therefore distinguishes **visibility** from **access** and **kinship** from **relational entitlement**.

Admit:

> **`KINSHIP_WITHOUT_CONTACT_ENTITLEMENT`**
>
> **Biological relation does not by itself establish a right to private access when the person has withdrawn relational standing.**

and:

> **`SELECTIVE_FAMILY_VISIBILITY_LONGING`**
>
> **A person may want recognition or contact from one family member while rejecting another member's attempt to obtain access. Family estrangement need not be psychologically or ethically uniform across the kin network.**

This is high-value evidence for kana because her public visibility is unusually large. Fame makes her findable; it does not convert being findable into consent to contact.

Chronology remains unresolved. The source does not permit B1-A to say whether the expressed wish for her father precedes, survives, or is superseded by later United States father material.

# 3. 長瀬琴乃 — ordinary family memory and differentiation-enabled inheritance

## 3.1 `st-company-bond-ktn-01-01` — Mana as ordinary sister before icon

Kotono dreams that she has returned to elementary-school age and is singing with Mana to their mother's piano. She calls it something that seemed ordinary at the time but may have been deeply precious:

> `何気ないことだったけど、大切だったのかも`

This expands Mana's afterlife in Kotono beyond:

- idol predecessor;
- grief object;
- symbolic legacy;
- competitive standard.

Admit:

> **`ORDINARY_MEMORY_REHUMANIZES_LEGACY`**
>
> **A dead predecessor can remain most intimate through mundane family memory rather than only through public achievement or traumatic loss.**

The dream also affects Kotono's sleep, but the source does not establish a clinical sleep disorder or pathological grief state.

## 3.2 `st-company-bond-ktn-01-05` — the clearest private differentiation statement

Kotono privately builds a Mana playlist, carefully ordering songs and making `Gemstones` the finale. She is visibly delighted by it.

Then she thinks:

> `お姉ちゃんとは違う道を選んで、自分なりに答えを`
>
> `見つけて。時間は掛かっちゃったけど……`
>
> `少しは胸を張れるようになって、改めて思う`
>
> `でも……やっぱりお姉ちゃんの歌、大好きだな……`

This is exceptionally direct evidence for a mechanism the larger corpus has often inferred:

> **`DIFFERENTIATION_ENABLES_REAPPROPRIATION`**
>
> **Choosing a path different from the predecessor can make it possible to return to the predecessor's work as something loved rather than something that must be copied, defeated, or escaped.**

This strongly supports existing:

- `TRANSFORMATIVE_FIDELITY`;
- `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`;
- `VOCATIONAL_SELF_AUTHORSHIP`.

It also shows that differentiation does not require repudiation. Kotono can become more distinctly herself **and** become more freely a fan of Mana again.

Admit companion concept:

> **`POST_DIFFERENTIATION_FANDOM_RECOVERY`**

This source should remain a private-state home for Kotono even if later E1/E2 evidence continues to own the mature series-level inheritance thesis.

## 3.3 Kotono and miho — analogical grief recognition, not identity collapse

When miho recognizes the playlist as Mana's work, she says that even if she disliked Mana, she will no longer demean a dead person in front of family and admits that doing so was wrong.

Kotono then internally notices that miho also lost an important person and thinks they may share more than she expected:

> `大切な人を亡くして、時に道を間違えて、後悔して`
>
> `それでも前に進もうとしてる`

Admit:

> **`ANALOGICAL_BEREAVEMENT_RECOGNITION`**

with an evidentiary limit:

- it is TEXTUAL FACT that Kotono makes the comparison;
- it is an interpretation to claim the two grief histories are equivalent;
- the source itself emphasizes partial analogy rather than sameness.

This is a small but meaningful bridge between two previously opposed Mana-memory regimes.

## 3.4 `01-04`, `01-06`, `01-08` — fandom, astronomy, and private life

Kotono's reaction to Suzu's Mana archive in `01-04` confirms that her recovered relationship to Mana includes ordinary fan behavior: discovering unseen photographs, wanting memorabilia, and enjoying a predecessor's public archive without making that archive a command to reproduce Mana.

`01-06` and `01-08` establish astronomy/home planetarium as a genuine private interest linked to an ordinary childhood memory of watching the post-typhoon sky with Mana.

Shizuku immediately sees performance possibilities in the planetarium, while Kotono repeatedly insists that she wants Shizuku to look at the stars themselves. Kotono eventually agrees to sing and finds singing under the projected stars fun.

Admit only at support level:

> **`VOLUNTARY_CREATIVE_REFRAMING_OF_PRIVATE_MEMORY`**
>
> **A private memory or hobby may become performance material without its private meaning having existed merely in order to become content. The transition is strongest when the subject retains standing to say yes, no, or not yet.**

This extends `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION` without implying a strict private/professional wall.

## 3.5 Nagisa relationship texture

`01-02` and `01-03` preserve unusually relaxed Kotono/Nagisa intimacy: physical closeness, teasing use of `デート`, embarrassment, shared reading, and Kotono's admission that she already keeps many photographs of Nagisa.

The material is meaningful relationship texture, but B1-A does **not** promote it into a settled romantic category. Existing `ROMANTIC_LANGUAGE_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_IDENTITY` remains the correct discipline.

# 4. fran — future optionality as active professional practice

## 4.1 `st-company-bond-kor-01-01` — rebuilding a fashion network

fran attends a fashion-industry party despite disliking such parties because she wants to make herself known to industry figures. She explicitly tells contacts that she previously owned a brand, and her private narration identifies her purpose:

> `ブランド復活って目的がなければ`
>
> `こんな所、絶対に来ないんだから！`

This is not vague “maybe fashion someday” characterization. It is present professional behavior undertaken to rebuild a future option.

Admit:

> **`VOCATIONAL_OPTIONALITY_BUILDING`**
>
> **A current idol career can be used deliberately to create future professional options without those options invalidating present commitment to idol work.**

Companion:

> **`CROSS_ROLE_CAPITAL_CONVERSION`**
>
> **Fame, contacts, skills, and reputation acquired in one role can be deliberately converted into opportunity in another authored role.**

This substantially strengthens `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`, `PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`, and `CROSS_ROLE_FERTILIZATION`.

## 4.2 `st-company-bond-kor-01-07` — present action as investment in future authorship

fran stays up late designing new IIIX costumes. She distinguishes ordinary/business fashion from stage-costume design and recognizes that Chisa knows her former brand, `ERFOLG`.

She encourages Chisa to pursue fashion while her current popularity gives her leverage:

> `やるなら、人気のある今がチャンスなのよ？`
>
> `自分自身が広告塔になれるもの`
>
> `現在の行動は未来への投資よ`

This is one of the corpus's clearest statements that present fame can be treated as **time-limited capital for future self-authorship**, not as a total identity.

Admit:

> **`PRESENT_FAME_AS_FUTURE_OPTIONALITY_INFRASTRUCTURE`**

The important ethical qualification is that future-planning remains self-authored. fran encourages Chisa strongly, but Chisa's uncertainty about whether fashion should become work must remain her own question.

The story also shows fran beginning to occupy a mentoring role outside idol performance. Her assistance is mixed with self-interest—she asks Chisa to publicize fran's contribution—but mixed motive does not make the mentoring unreal.

Support concept:

> **`RECIPROCAL_CROSS_ROLE_MENTORSHIP`**

## 4.3 financial practice and glamour

Across `01-02`, `01-05`, `01-06`, and `01-08`, fran:

- studies stocks and bond-market conditions;
- buys growth stocks;
- takes surplus location meals home;
- saves electricity;
- worries about bill deadlines;
- retrieves a single yen;
- collects campaign stickers for household goods;
- fantasizes about absurdly profitable businesses.

Do not diagnose poverty or financial precarity from this material alone. The secure claim is that **financial calculation is a persistent part of fran's private self and future planning**.

Admit at support level:

> **`FINANCIAL_OPTIONALITY_AS_PRIVATE_PRACTICE`**

This gives additional texture to why fran treats present action, networking, and fame instrumentally without reducing her entire vocation to money.

## 4.4 public/private registers without a true-self hierarchy

`01-01` gives a particularly clear contrast between fran's impeccably polite networking persona and her internally contemptuous reaction to many attendees. Other stories show hidden anime fandom, thrift, and irritation.

However, this does not prove that polite fran is a lie while irritable fran is “the real one.” The networking self is a competent professional self chosen in pursuit of a real goal: brand revival.

This strongly supports:

> **`PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`**

The same person can authentically possess:

- a strategic professional register;
- a hostile internal monologue;
- serious fashion ambition;
- childish fandom;
- economic calculation;
- cooperative mentorship.

No single register automatically nullifies the others.

## 4.5 Mana without universal reverence

In `01-04`, fran watches a Mana concert Blu-ray borrowed from Suzu. Her reaction is basically that it was fine rather than transformative, although her strong memory lets her recall choreography after one viewing.

This is useful negative evidence:

> **`LEGACY_RECEPTION_WITHOUT_DEVOTION`**

Mana can be structurally important to Hoshimi history without every later idol being required to experience personal reverence. The ecosystem can inherit Mana unevenly.

This complements, rather than contradicts, affinity-based inheritance and multiperspectival legacy knowledge.

# 5. Cross-bundle synthesis

## 5.1 Visibility does not imply access

kana's family story provides the clearest private-scale version:

> **Being publicly visible does not make a person generally accessible.**

An idol may be recognizable to millions and still retain jurisdiction over:

- who receives private contact information;
- which relatives possess relational standing;
- which relationships she wants repaired;
- which relationships she has ended.

This extends `PRIVATE_LIFE_JURISDICTION_RESTRAINT` beyond romance and management into family contact.

## 5.2 Differentiation can deepen rather than destroy attachment

Kotono's Mana playlist gives the tranche's strongest inheritance principle:

> **A successor may need to become different enough from the predecessor before attachment to the predecessor becomes freely lovable again.**

This is a major private-state clarification of transformative fidelity.

## 5.3 Non-totalizing vocation requires positive alternatives, not merely escape

fran changes the meaning of `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION` by providing a positive mechanism. A vocation is non-totalizing not merely because the person could theoretically leave it, but because she can actively cultivate:

- other skills;
- other networks;
- other professional identities;
- financial resources;
- future options.

Thus:

> **`OPTIONALITY_IS_AN_ACTIVE_PRACTICE`**

Future authorship is partly built in the present.

## 5.4 Informal care and informal power require different ethical tests

B1-A also reveals two forms of informality:

- informal peer protection and information-sharing;
- informal executive/industry leverage.

Both may produce protective outcomes. Neither should therefore be treated as automatically legitimate.

Carry forward:

> **`PROTECTIVE_OUTCOME_DOES_NOT_RESOLVE_PROCEDURAL_ACCOUNTABILITY`**

This is especially important for Saegusa's unexplained ability to make another producer back down.
