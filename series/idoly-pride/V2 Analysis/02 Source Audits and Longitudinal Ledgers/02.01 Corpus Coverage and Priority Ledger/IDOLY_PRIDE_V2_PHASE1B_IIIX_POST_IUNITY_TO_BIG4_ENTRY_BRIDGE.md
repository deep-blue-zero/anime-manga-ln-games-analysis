# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — IIIX POST-I-UNITY TO BIG4 ENTRY BRIDGE

## 0. Purpose

This document resolves the chronology question deliberately left open by the frozen post-Tokyo baseline:

> **At what exact point do the post-I-UNITY IIIX origin findings become admissible before the BIG4 main story begins?**

The answer is now fixed.

The Tokyo opening correction remains valid and unchanged. The mistake in the earlier IIIX gate was not that the later IIIX material was analytically unusable; it was that its **endpoint was admitted too early**. The correct remedy is therefore not to redo the IIIX origin audit and not to rewrite the frozen Tokyo baseline. It is to insert a narrow chronological bridge after I-UNITY and before `big4_001`.

This bridge admits only the primary material needed to reconstruct IIIX's state at BIG4 entry.

---

# 1. Chronology decision

## 1.1 Boundary anchor — `st-group-thrx-01-01-17`

`st-group-thrx-01-01-17` remains the **I-UNITY final boundary anchor**.

Its relevant endpoint is already earned by the Tokyo pass:

- IIIX rejects Himeno's contaminated route to victory while preserving its commitment to direct competitive excellence;
- IIIX reaches the I-UNITY final against SUNNY PEACE;
- IIIX loses;
- miho recognizes a meaningful distinction between her own orientation toward Mana and SUNNY PEACE's attempt to surpass Mana.

Nothing in this bridge retroactively changes the Tokyo audit.

## 1.2 Newly admissible scope — `18–20`

The following stories are now admitted **after** the Tokyo endpoint and **before** BIG4:

1. `st-group-thrx-01-01-18` — `お元気で`
2. `st-group-thrx-01-01-19` — `獅子搏兎`
3. `st-group-thrx-01-01-20` — `この約束だけはずっと`

These three stories form the missing state transition between the frozen post-Tokyo baseline and the state presupposed by `big4_001_dark_of_the_moon`.

# 2. Primary-source reconstruction of the transition

## 2.1 Defeat does not immediately produce a stable new IIIX

The I-UNITY loss removes the result around which the first IIIX organized itself.

The immediate post-final state is therefore not best described as "second founding" already completed. The unit has lost:

- the I-UNITY title;
- Himeno's institutional project;
- the security of Preta Porte's idol operation;
- and the assumption that victory will automatically preserve each member's private objective.

The correct chronological sequence begins with **institutional and relational uncertainty**, not with mature re-authorization.

## 2.2 Preta Porte ceases to function as a usable institutional home

Stories 18–19 establish material consequences of the I-UNITY scandal and institutional collapse.

The primary text makes the practical condition explicit: Preta Porte is no longer functioning normally, new work is no longer arriving through the office, while already-contracted work continues to bind the members.

This is important because IIIX's transition is not an abstract change of attitude. It is produced under deteriorating professional conditions:

- programs and jobs disappear;
- new work acquisition becomes uncertain;
- previously secured commitments still have to be honored;
- the unit's status and market position weaken;
- the organization that assembled and administered IIIX stops supplying the infrastructure that made the first formation possible.

## 2.3 Dissolution becomes a real option

The members do not simply glide from corporate IIIX into freelance IIIX.

They treat separation as plausible. The post-I-UNITY text allows the unit to end without any member ceasing to be an idol.

This distinction is load-bearing:

> **Continuing as idols is not the same thing as continuing as IIIX.**

The later choice therefore has interpretive weight precisely because dissolution is available.

## 2.4 Residual work creates a final practical test

Story 19 places the apparently dissolving unit inside obligations that remain after institutional collapse.

The resulting smaller/reduced live is analytically important but must not be overread. It does not suddenly convert IIIX into SUNNY PEACE or prove that audience warmth replaces technical/competitive value.

What it does establish is narrower:

- IIIX can still perform when prestige and institutional guarantees are reduced;
- fan recognition survives some of the reputational collapse;
- miho encounters evidence that Yō has not vanished from public memory as completely as her grievance structure assumed;
- the unit's professional competence remains usable even when the machinery around it is failing.

Miho's anti-Mana memory project is **destabilized, not resolved**.

## 2.5 Voluntary continuation is finally explicit in story 20

Story 20 supplies the decisive factual transition.

Miho observes that Preta Porte may be finished, but nobody has actually ordered IIIX to dissolve. Fran answers that they are free to continue on their own. The members then choose continued association while explicitly refusing sentimental friendship language.

Miho's `腐れ縁` formulation is the clearest relational marker: the bond is acknowledged as durable without being renamed into conventional affection.

The factual conclusion is therefore secure:

> **IIIX voluntarily continues after the collapse of the institution that originally authored it.**

This is the point at which the later origin endpoint becomes admissible.

# 3. Fact / interpretation separation

## 3.1 Established primary-text facts at BIG4 entry

The following may be inherited as established history:

- IIIX lost to SUNNY PEACE in the I-UNITY final.
- Preta Porte's idol operation ceased to function as IIIX's stable institutional base.
- IIIX experienced canceled/lost work and weakened professional standing.
- Dissolution was treated as a genuine option.
- The members remained individually committed to idol work.
- Existing contractual obligations continued after institutional collapse.
- The three members ultimately chose to continue IIIX rather than separate.
- Their continued unit does not depend on claiming that they have become ordinary friends.
- By BIG4 entry IIIX has left Preta Porte, become freelance/independent, and taken on self-production/administrative burdens.
- IIIX has lost its prior BIG4 position by the opening of `big4_001`.

## 3.2 High-confidence interpretation, not bare fact

The following formulation remains analytically useful but must be labeled as interpretation:

> **The post-I-UNITY continuation functions as a second founding / re-authorization of IIIX.**

Why the interpretation is strong:

- the first IIIX was institutionally authored before it was relationally authored;
- after defeat and institutional collapse, continuation becomes optional;
- the same three people choose the unit again under worse material conditions;
- the relationship survives without requiring purified motives or sentimental relabeling.

Why it must remain interpretation:

- the text does not announce a formal "second founding" doctrine;
- the members themselves emphasize continuity (`これまで通り`) as much as transformation;
- later BIG4 development must remain free to complicate what this re-authorization means.

The governing wording is therefore:

> **Fact:** IIIX voluntarily continues and becomes freelance.  
> **Interpretation:** that voluntary continuation is best understood as re-authorization / a second founding.
