# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — PHASE 1B POST-E1 E2 REASSESSMENT / FINE-ORDER RERANK
## 0. Executive finding
The original E2 tier was generated against the frozen Tier-A main-narrative baseline. That ordering is no longer sufficient after all **16/16 E1 events** have been close-read, prospectively frozen, historically stress-tested, and admitted through `IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_PHASE1B_POST_EVENT_E1E_BASELINE.md`.

This reassessment therefore asks a different routing question:

> **After E1, which E2 events still possess unique analytical leverage, and which have become primarily confirmatory because E1 now supplies stronger evidence for the proposition that originally made them important?**

The strongest post-E1 promotions are not the most spectacular plots. They are sources that still address unresolved constitutional questions: who gets to define viable idolhood, how long a career may remain meaningful, when guidance becomes imposition, how care interacts with trauma and self-expendability, and whether the VENUS system exhausts the ontology of the profession.

The strongest demotions are likewise principled rather than dismissive. Several IIIX, Mana-memory, rivalry, and center-related events remain useful evidence, but E1 now supplies stronger and later authority for their central claims. They are preserved and routed later rather than treated as disposable.
## 1. Integrity disclosure and method
This pass is **not blind**. The analyst necessarily knows the frozen original rerank and the E1 findings. The correct integrity label is therefore:

`POST_E1_TARGETED_REASSESSMENT_WITH_PRIOR_RERANK_EXPOSURE`

The operation had four steps:

1. recover the exact 26-event E2 pool and original score/rationale from the frozen event rerank;
2. compare each old rationale against the **post-E1-E open register** and admitted E1 concepts;
3. increase the redundancy penalty where E1 now supplies stronger evidence, and increase open-question/revision weight where the event remains unusually diagnostic;
4. perform targeted raw-source rereads on candidates whose expected movement was large or whose new placement would determine the first E2 tranche.

This is a **routing audit, not an E2 close read**. No event-level proposition below is admitted to the governing baseline merely because it influenced routing. The post-E1-E baseline remains current authority until an E2 close-read tranche is independently frozen.
## 2. Reassessment rubric
The same basic dimensions are retained for comparability with the frozen first rerank: `S` state change, `O` open-question relevance, `C` character/relationship coverage, `I` institution/labor value, `R` revision potential, `J` Japanese voice/register value, `H` chronology/dependency, and `D` redundancy penalty.

The important change is **what counts as redundant**. `D` is now measured against the entire admitted E1 corpus rather than Tier A alone. Likewise, `O` is measured against the **post-E1 open register**, not the pre-event register.

For execution, the surviving pool is split into:

- **E2-A — PRIORITY CLOSE READ (`17–18`)**: best remaining leverage; read first.
- **E2-B — MATERIAL EXTENSION (`14–16`)**: still important, but not a dependency for the first E2 checkpoint.
- **E2-C — LATE IMPORTANT (`13`)**: valid E2 evidence whose main function is now chronology/recursion/controlled extension.
- **SUPPORT-DEFERRED (`10–12`)**: originally E2 but now execution-demoted to support because E1 supplies substantially stronger authority for the core claim.

No event is deleted. Execution demotion means **later and more targeted**, not analytically worthless.

## 4. Material promotions
### 4.1 `event_2026_004` — IDOLY MATCH / alternative professional ecology

This is the largest conceptual promotion. The raw source explicitly defines `野良アイドル` as idols outside the VENUS Program for multiple reasons, including non-affiliation, dislike of AI evaluation, hobby/side-work status, and other career arrangements. The underground venue is a protected stage for that population. The winner is `BEIJU`, an idol unit with an **average age of 88**. Rei, Aoi, and Mei lose; Aoi observes that under VENUS Program scoring they probably would have won, while Rei accepts that the actual question in this venue was which performers were the more compelling idols.

Routing implication only:

> this event may be the clearest lower-tier source for **institutional pluralism, VENUS metric non-sovereignty, extreme career longevity, and the question of who counts as an idol when the dominant certification system is removed**.

That combination makes it the new No. 1 E2 candidate.
### 4.2 `event_2021_005` — Rei and the social legitimacy of idol labor

The father/daughter conflict is more valuable after E1 because `OPEN-08` remains open. Rei explicitly argues that idols can save people, that abandoning dance/idolhood would leave her with regret even if success is uncertain, and that this is the work she chose. Her father ultimately describes idolhood as a short-lived occupation yet concedes it may nevertheless be work worth staking a life on.

This is not a generic “follow your dream” episode. It directly distinguishes **career duration, social utility, replaceability, chosen vocation, and life-worthiness**.
### 4.3 `event_2024_007` — Chisa, trauma, and self-expendability

E1 establishes answerable care but does not exhaust Chisa’s burden-avoidance problem. Here she discloses that she nearly drowned as a child and has not swum in the ocean since. Under pressure she repeatedly proposes that the others leave her behind. The group explicitly tells her she does not need to force herself; she later practices under supervision and eventually asks to go together.

The candidate value is therefore not “Chisa conquers a fear.” It is the recurrence and revision of a deeper rule:

> **when Chisa anticipates becoming a burden, she converts difficulty into permission to remove herself from the group.**

That remains important after E1 and justifies the strongest upward movement in the pool.
### 4.4 `event_2025_010` — LizNoir style after dispossessive-change critique

E1-B gives the governing distinction `change vs dispossession`; this event is now more valuable because it can test what a mature positive answer looks like. Aoi’s working conclusion is not that LizNoir must obey a fixed recognizable formula, but that **LizNoir-like dancing is what emerges when the four members dance as themselves**. Combined with a successful overseas-tour context, the event is a direct candidate for `OPEN-13` and for the mature LizNoir authorship model.
### 4.5 `event_2021_003` — Ai and four-person LizNoir constitution

Ai remains less fully covered than Rio/Aoi. The raw event ties her inferiority to her prior fandom of the original two-person LizNoir, shows her mistaking Rio’s criticism for a demand to suppress emotion, and then distinguishes emotional intensity from neglecting group/audience awareness. This is unusually concentrated evidence for how a successor member becomes constitutive without erasing the inherited form.
## 5. Material demotions
### 5.1 IIIX precursor/hostile-intimacy cluster

`event_2024_004` remains important for chronology: IIIX can pursue Las Vegas work while deliberately retaining its Hoshimi contract. But E1-D is now the much stronger autonomy/global-identity test because it takes IIIX all the way through dissolution and self-authored re-authorization. The earlier event therefore drops from 18 to 13.

`event_2022_011` and `event_2023_007` likewise remain excellent Japanese relational-grammar sources, but their central “hostile intimacy is real and durable” function is now better established by E1-A, E1-C, and especially E1-D. They move to support-deferred status.
### 5.2 Mana-memory cluster

`event_2022_004` and `event_2022_012` remain legitimate developmental evidence. Their execution priority falls because E1-A and E1-E now establish reciprocal inheritance, plural-person memory, provenance without jurisdiction, and polyphonic memorial authorship more completely. These events should later be mined for **Suzu/Kotono-specific chronology and voice**, not used as if the mature inheritance thesis still depended on them.
### 5.3 Rivalry/metric precursors

`event_2023_004` remains useful relapse/recursion evidence for comparative distance and result fixation, but E1-B and E1-E now supply substantially stronger models of metric capture, non-terminal loss, contestable ambition, and reciprocal adversarial development. It remains E2 only at the threshold.
