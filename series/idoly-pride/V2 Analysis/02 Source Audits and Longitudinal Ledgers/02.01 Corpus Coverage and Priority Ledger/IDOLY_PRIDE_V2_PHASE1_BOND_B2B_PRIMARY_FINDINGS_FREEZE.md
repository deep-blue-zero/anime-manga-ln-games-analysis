# 1. Tranche-level result — role continuity without role captivity

The five bundles repeatedly show characters carrying durable roles — older sister, daughter, sibling, senior, student, caretaker, professional — without those roles becoming total jurisdictions over the person.

Admit as tranche-level synthesis:

> **`ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`**
>
> **A formative or durable role may remain genuinely constitutive while its obligations become reciprocal, context-sensitive, revisable, or shared. Development need not require abolishing the role; it may require preventing the role from monopolizing the relationship.**

This extends the inherited `ANSWERABLE_ROLE_CONSTITUTION` and `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION` model into family and developmental relationships.

Cross-source examples:

- Haruko remains an idol while still imagining unrealized student/adult possibilities;
- Saki remains Chisa's older sister while allowing Chisa to care back toward her;
- Rei remains a daughter who wants parental trust while recognizing concern need not equal rejection;
- Sumire can admire an older brother who combines family work with an unfinished creative vocation;
- Rio initially treats seniority as a reason she must manage Aoi, while Aoi already possesses standing to notice and support Rio's exhaustion.

The core rule is therefore:

> **Constitutive roles can persist without remaining one-directional.**

# 2. 佐伯遙子 — idolhood has opportunity costs without owning the whole life

## 2.1 College life is real, not decorative background

`st-company-bond-hrk-01-01` begins with Haruko finishing a university report after missing class for work. A friend preserved the handout for her.

TEXTUAL FACT:

- Haruko is actively maintaining university obligations alongside idol work;
- work has directly caused at least one missed class;
- peer relationships outside idol work remain materially useful to her student life.

Admit:

> **`VOCATIONAL_OPPORTUNITY_COST_WITHOUT_TOTALIZATION`**

The source establishes cost, not regret. Idol work can crowd out other domains without thereby invalidating the vocation.

## 2.2 Counterfactual ordinary life remains imaginable

Haruko watches club members and imagines what she might have done had she joined a club: camping, magic, athletic-team management, friendship extending decades into the future, even joking about marriage and popularity.

Admit:

> **`COUNTERFACTUAL_ORDINARY_LIFE_IMAGINATION`**

Interpretation:

> Haruko's idol identity has not erased her ability to imagine socially ordinary adult/student futures. Her subjectivity remains larger than the work she currently performs.

Do **not** infer that she wishes she had quit idolhood or that any imagined club life is a hidden preferred future.

## 2.3 Ordinary adult texture remains active

Across the bundle Haruko maintains:

- university friends;
- ruins/haikyo travel as a serious hobby;
- cooking and household labor;
- drinking/socializing with adult peers;
- independent interests unrelated to idol ranking.

Admit at support level:

> **`ADULT_ORDINARY_LIFE_CONTINUITY_HARUKO`**

The significance is cumulative: Haruko's aging/career question cannot be analyzed as though idol labor is the only meaningful time axis in her life.

## 2.4 Aging self-awareness is present without self-expulsion

`01-05` explicitly frames Haruko as twenty, contrasting her current body with the literal high-school uniform she once wore. `01-08` contains comic body/weight self-consciousness.

Admit as support:

> `AGE_AWARE_EMBODIED_SELFHOOD`

Do not convert comedy about a tight skirt or weight anxiety into evidence that Haruko is professionally obsolete. The source only establishes awareness of bodily/time change.

## 2.5 Open consequence

`OPEN-14` should advance through a sharper distinction:

- **career continuation** and
- **the opportunity cost of continued idol commitment**

are different questions.

The bond supplies unusually direct evidence for the second.

# 3. 白石沙季 — sisterhood remains constitutive while care becomes reversible

## 3.1 Saki's older-sister role is deeply internalized

In `st-company-bond-ski-01-05`, Saki struggles to imagine playing a younger sister precisely because she has spent her life thinking:

> `お姉ちゃんとしてしっかりしなきゃ`

TEXTUAL FACT:

- being Chisa's older sister is not a superficial label;
- Saki has organized self-expectation around being dependable from childhood.

Admit:

> **`INTERNALIZED_SIBLING_ROLE_CONSTITUTION`**

This supports the inherited sibling-role architecture without proving that the role must remain permanent or exhaustive.

## 3.2 Private sisterhood becomes reciprocal

`st-company-bond-ski-01-03` begins with Saki offering Chisa lap-pillow/ear-cleaning care in a familiar older-sister mode. Chisa perceives that Saki may herself want to be relied on. Chisa then insists on returning the same care, and Saki discovers that being cared for is embarrassing precisely because she is unused to occupying that position.

Admit:

> **`SIBLING_CARE_ROLE_REVERSIBILITY`**

Current rule:

> **Reciprocal sisterhood does not require Saki to stop being the older sister. It requires the relationship to permit Chisa to become a legitimate caregiver as well.**

This is private-scale evidence for `RECIPROCAL_STANDING` and `SIBLING_ROLE_DETOTALIZATION`.

## 3.3 Influence can become one's own judgment

`st-company-bond-ski-01-06` is unusually explicit. Saki gives Sumire advice and then minimizes her authority by saying it is partly borrowed from Makino and books. Sumire replies:

> `最初は誰かの意見でも、実践したり、吸収すれば`
>
> `それはもう沙季さんの意見ですよ`

Admit:

> **`ASSIMILATED_INFLUENCE_BECOMES_OWN_JUDGMENT`**

This is direct lower-tier evidence for `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`.

Rule:

> **The origin of an idea does not permanently own the person who has tested, practiced, integrated, and re-articulated it.**

The source does not erase provenance; it distinguishes provenance from present authorship.

## 3.4 Cross-role transfer requires contextual fit

`st-company-bond-ski-01-01` shows that idol experience makes Saki more sensitive to audience response during school assemblies. She tries importing an idol-stage delivery style wholesale into student-council communication and produces a comically inappropriate result.

Admit as support:

> **`CROSS_ROLE_SKILL_TRANSFER_REQUIRES_CONTEXTUAL_FIT`**

This refines `CROSS_ROLE_FERTILIZATION`:

- experience can transfer;
- form cannot always transfer unchanged.

A useful capacity acquired in one role must be adapted to the receiving institution rather than simply copied.

# 4. 一ノ瀬怜 — family care requires negotiated trust, not either obedience or severance

## 4.1 Father/daughter tension is about trust as much as control

In `st-company-bond-rei-01-03`, Rei complains that her father uses their rare conversations to worry about her studies and says:

> `少しぐらい、信頼してくれてもいいのに`

Saki proposes that the father's concern may partly be an awkward attempt to have something to talk about.

TEXTUAL FACT:

- Rei experiences his repeated educational concern as insufficient trust;
- Saki offers a less adversarial interpretation;
- Rei does not resolve the issue within the story.

Admit:

> **`PARENTAL_CONCERN_TRUST_FRICTION`**

Interpretation:

> Care can become relationally frustrating when concern is repeatedly expressed through supervision rather than demonstrated trust.

Do not infer that the father is simply controlling or that Saki's charitable interpretation is objectively correct.

## 4.2 Support can retreat in one domain without becoming abandonment

In `st-company-bond-rei-01-04`, Saki eventually refuses to keep waking Rei as though she were a child and tells her that self-management is part of being a professional idol. Yet Saki has already prepared breakfast for her and packages it for later.

Admit:

> **`SUPPORT_WITHOUT_PERMANENT_DEPENDENCE`**

Rule:

> **Recognizing competence can require withdrawing a familiar form of help while continuing care in other forms.**

This is another private mechanism by which support avoids becoming destiny or ownership.

## 4.3 Rei carries multiple unrealized/alternative vocational histories

`st-company-bond-rei-01-06` establishes that Rei previously worked at a supermarket and had once aimed for medical school.

Admit at support level:

> **`ALTERNATIVE_VOCATIONAL_HISTORY_REI`**

The source does not explain why she changed direction or whether medicine remains a live future option. It does establish that current idol identity is historically non-totalizing.

## 4.4 Sibling creative vocation becomes a family comparison point

`st-company-bond-smr-01-08` reveals that Rei's older brother, once academically oriented toward medicine, is now pursuing a guitar career in the band `MYSELFish`. Rei makes a support uchiwa and is visibly proud of him even while defensively insisting that his band is gaining popularity.

Admit:

> **`SIBLING_CREATIVE_VOCATION_RECOGNITION_REI`**

This places creative risk-taking inside Rei's family rather than making idolhood the only unconventional professional path available in her relational world.

# 6. 神崎莉央 — mature LizNoir reciprocity grows out of an earlier asymmetrical constitution

## 6.1 Pre-debut chronology is internally anchored

`st-company-bond-rio-01-01` explicitly begins:

> `――LizNoirデビューの少し前`

This permits a stronger chronology claim than most bond bundles.

At that point Rio thinks:

> `あの子年下なのよね`
>
> `私がしっかり指導しなきゃダメね`

Admit:

> **`PREDEBUT_SELF_ASSIGNED_SENIOR_CARETAKER_ROLE`**

Rio initially interprets age difference as a reason she should discipline, guide, and protect Aoi professionally.

## 6.2 Reciprocity already exists inside the asymmetry

Aoi arrives at Rio's school because she has noticed Rio is tired. She offers practical care, even joking about carrying her. Rio resists because she is older and believes she should be the responsible one.

Admit:

> **`RECIPROCITY_PRECEDES_ROLE_EQUALIZATION`**

Rule:

> **A relationship can contain reciprocal concern before the participants possess a mature language for equal standing.**

This matters for later LizNoir development: reciprocity is not invented only after later four-person maturation; it is already pressing against the founders' early senior/junior role structure.

## 6.3 The senior role repeatedly fails to monopolize competence

`st-company-bond-rio-01-03` begins with Rio thinking she is supervising Aoi at the arcade. She then becomes stuck in a crane-game sunk-cost spiral and Aoi retrieves the prize immediately, producing Rio's private acknowledgment:

> `悔しいけど頼もしい`

Admit:

> **`DEPENDENCE_WITHIN_SENIORITY`**

A senior role can remain meaningful while the nominal junior is locally more competent and becomes the helper.

`st-company-bond-rio-01-08` repeats the structure with Saki. Rio tells Saki that relying on others is necessary, then gets confused while shopping and eventually accepts Saki's help, saying:

> `私も、もっと素直に頼ることにするわ`

Admit:

> **`RECIPROCAL_DEPENDENCE_WITHOUT_SENIORITY_ERASURE`**

## 6.4 The founding dyad remains special after group expansion

`st-company-bond-rio-01-04` explicitly recalls that Rio and Aoi once ate together alone all the time. Aoi says Rio is special to her; Rio reciprocates the continuing bond.

Admit:

> **`FOUNDING_DYAD_PERSISTS_WITHOUT_UNIT_EXCLUSIVITY`**

The mature four-member LizNoir does not require the founding Rio/Aoi relationship to become generic or disappear.

Do not infer that their special dyad overrides Ai/Kokoro's equal membership, current four-person unit constitution, or future-authoring rights.

## 6.5 Early public identity was negotiated rather than singularly authored

`st-company-bond-rio-01-02` shows Rio trying to introduce LizNoir through formal speech, while Aoi argues that singing/dancing is the truest introduction to the unit. Their accidental live stream turns disagreement into public exposure.

Admit as support:

> **`EARLY_UNIT_SELF_REPRESENTATION_NEGOTIATION`**

This is precursor evidence that LizNoir's public form was never simply dictated by one member. The source is not sufficient to establish the mature four-person creative constitution by itself.

# 7. Cross-source family/vocation model

B2-B adds three distinctions worth preserving for later synthesis.

### 7.1 Care is not identical to permanent assistance

Saki/Rei and Saki/Chisa show different routes:

- support may become **reversible**;
- support may be **withdrawn in one domain to recognize competence**;
- continuing love does not require maintaining the same help forever.

Admit umbrella:

> **`CARE_FORM_CAN_CHANGE_WITHOUT_RELATIONAL_WITHDRAWAL`**

### 7.2 Family influence does not own vocational identity

Rei and Sumire's brothers provide non-idol examples of career redirection and parallel creative aspiration. Saki explicitly receives outside ideas and makes them her own through practice.

Admit umbrella:

> **`RELATIONAL_INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_VOCATIONAL_OWNERSHIP`**

### 7.3 Ordinary life is analytically consequential even when it is not the chosen primary vocation

Haruko's university/clubs, Sumire's rural/family world, Rei's former medical aspiration, and Rio/Saki's school histories are not filler around idolhood. They are alternative role systems that shape what the characters can imagine, transfer, lose, preserve, or return to.

Admit:

> **`ORDINARY_LIFE_AS_OPTION_SPACE`**

The ordinary is not merely recovery from professional life; it also preserves **unselected or parallel futures**.
