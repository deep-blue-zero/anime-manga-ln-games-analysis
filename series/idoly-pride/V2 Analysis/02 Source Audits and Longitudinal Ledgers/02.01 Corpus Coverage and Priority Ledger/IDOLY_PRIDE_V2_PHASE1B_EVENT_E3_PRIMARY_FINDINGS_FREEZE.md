# 2. Tranche-level prospective result

The seven sources do **not** displace the mature E1/E2 architecture. Instead they make three broad contributions.

## 2.1 Professional identity can include ordinary selfhood without being dissolved by it

Several E3 stories show that professional maturity is not equivalent to continuous professionalized self-presentation.

- Sumire has missed ordinary school experiences because of work and initially tries to become a Rui-like organizer rather than act from her own present capacities.
- Sunny Peace's creators explicitly use ordinary hometown interaction and rest as material for a major competitive song.
- LizNoir's juniors want ordinary summer experiences with their seniors and discover that the mature four-person unit is meaningful outside work as well as inside it.

The inherited `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION` and `ORDINARY_LIFE_RECOVERY` therefore receive substantial support.

A genuinely useful incremental term is:

> **`EMULATION_WITHOUT_SELF_ERASURE`**
>
> **A person may aspire toward another's admired capacities while recognizing that growth must proceed through her own present identity rather than by performing as a substitute version of the admired person.**

Sumire supplies the clearest source-native statement:

> `だって、私は私で、瑠依ちゃんじゃないんだから`
>
> followed by
>
> `だから私は、このままの私で、頑張ります`

She does **not** renounce Rui as a model. She changes the relationship to the model.

## 2.2 Care and protection remain illegitimate when they seize the protected person's authorship

`守れ！純潔のベーゼ` is comic in register but unusually explicit about a serious E1/E2 problem.

Yu describes her own conduct as potentially:

> `うちの愛の押しつけ`

while still claiming it must benefit Rui and TRINITYAiLE. Her conduct escalates from objection to attempted interference with the work itself.

The story therefore gives a strong counterexample to any interpretation in which sincere protectiveness automatically creates jurisdiction.

Current support rule:

> **Protection may be sincerely motivated and still become goal seizure when the protected person's informed professional choice is displaced by the protector's preferred outcome.**

This stabilizes `BENEVOLENT_GOAL_SEIZURE`, `GOAL_CONSENT`, `AGE_AWARE_EQUAL_STANDING`, and the broader consultation requirement.

## 2.3 Authenticity and ethical approval are separate questions

E3 adds another boundary case to the mature `answerable construction` model.

Romantic address, intimacy, and emotional performance can be:

- professionally scripted;
- tailored to an idol's recognizable public character;
- convincingly enacted;
- enjoyed by the audience;
- and still not constitute a private romantic declaration.

This is explicit in Sunny Peace's public confession contest and in Rui's treatment of the kiss scene as acting.

Prospective support term:

> **`PERFORMED_INTIMACY_WITHOUT_PRIVATE_CLAIM`**
>
> **A performer can truthfully inhabit an intimate or romantic role without the performance asserting an equivalent private relationship or desire.**

This supports `ROLE_MEDIATED_RELATIONAL_TRUTH` and `AUTHENTICITY_WITHOUT_ETHICAL_EXONERATION`: successful role performance neither proves private disclosure nor ethically validates every surrounding institutional or interpersonal choice.

# 3. Source 1 — ぱじゃまパーティー！～夢見る少女と眠り姫～

## 3.1 Sumire's professionalized childhood and age-appropriate self

The opening study session makes the dual-role problem explicit. Sumire tells Suzu and Kokoro:

> `アイドルだからって、ううん、アイドルだからこそ 学業をおろそかにしちゃダメ`

because declining grades could worry parents and lead to restrictions on idol activity.

This is not school-life sentimentalism. It is a practical model in which adolescent education, parental authority, and professional activity remain simultaneous constraints.

Later, during the overnight planning session, Sumire says that she missed school trips and similar experiences because she was working:

> `そういう学校の行事って 私、いつも仕事で行けなかったな……`

The pajama-party atmosphere therefore functions as a partial recovery of an ordinary developmental domain previously displaced by work.

This supports:

- `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`
- `TIME_BOUNDED_LIFE_DOMAIN`
- `ORDINARY_LIFE_RECOVERY`

without adding a new mature thesis.

## 3.2 Sumire's failed Rui imitation is productive rather than humiliating

Sumire repeatedly tries to organize the group because she has internalized Rui's model of responsibility. She notices fatigue, tries to discipline the meeting, and interprets the inability to lead like Rui as personal inadequacy.

Chisa and Shizuku recognize that she is straining too hard and deliberately redirect her toward the shopping group and ordinary enjoyment.

The decisive endpoint is Sumire's recognition that she is not Rui. Her growth is therefore not abandonment of aspiration but **self-differentiated emulation**.

Admit:

- `EMULATION_WITHOUT_SELF_ERASURE`

Do not infer that Sumire no longer wants to acquire Rui's leadership capacities. She explicitly still hopes to grow toward them.

## 3.3 "Natural self" becomes professionally curated material

Satomi's unit concept emerges from seeing the idols during the overnight stay. She says the members' trained professional selves are already known and proposes emphasizing their `素` / ordinary selves instead.

This is important for the authenticity model because the event does **not** preserve a simple binary of:

- professional performance = constructed;
- private self = authentic.

The ordinary self becomes the **concept of a new professional unit**.

Admit as support:

> **`ORDINARY_SELF_AS_PROFESSIONAL_RESOURCE`**
>
> An ordinary or less-professionalized mode of selfhood can become material for deliberately constructed professional performance without thereby ceasing to be grounded in the performer.

This is support-level evidence for `answerable construction`, not a replacement definition.

## 3.4 Center assignment follows concept fit, not entitlement

Satomi selects Sumire as center because Sumire most strongly embodies the limited-unit concept. Suzu contests the outcome but accepts the current judgment while promising to challenge again.

This stabilizes the already-admitted center framework:

- center can remain prestigious and singular;
- selection can be justified by role/concept fit;
- losing the office does not erase standing to challenge later.

No new center doctrine is required.

## 3.5 Satomi as delegated creative authority

The manager delegates the creation of a new unit concept to Satomi. The idols then become collaborators rather than passive inputs, and Sumire ultimately calls Satomi the unit's "sixth member."

This is bounded support for:

- distributed creative authority;
- representative/adjacent professional authority;
- institutions enabling authored action through delegated responsibility.

Do not infer that every manager occupies member-equivalent relational standing.

### E3 classification

**HIGH-VALUE SUPPORT** — ordinary-life recovery, developmental self-differentiation, authenticity construction, and limited-unit center evidence.

Formal dependency: `adv-live-eve-2303-race-005` missing. Do not infer exact live form.

# 4. Source 2 — 守れ！純潔のベーゼ～imposition of love～

## 4.1 The manager's initial protective instinct is age-aware but consultation-seeking

The manager initially intends to reject Rui's kiss scene because of image/fan considerations and because Rui is a seventeen-year-old high-school student. Crucially, he still consults her rather than silently closing the opportunity.

Rui interprets the proposed refusal as age-based professional limitation and insists on attempting the work.

The source therefore gives a useful boundary case for `AGE_AWARE_EQUAL_STANDING`:

- age/development can create legitimate safeguarding considerations;
- age does not automatically nullify the performer's standing to be consulted;
- consultation does not imply that every risk must ultimately be accepted.

The event does **not** establish a universal rule for intimate scenes involving minors; it establishes a character/institutional negotiation about professional agency.

## 4.2 Rui treats staged intimacy as work

Rui repeatedly describes the kiss as an acting problem that can be professionally separated from private romantic meaning:

> `あくまでもお芝居よ。きちんと割り切るのも仕事`

This is strong support for:

- `PERFORMED_INTIMACY_WITHOUT_PRIVATE_CLAIM`
- role-mediated relational truth;
- professional persona not being reducible to private confession.

## 4.3 Yu's protection becomes explicit authorship seizure

Yu initially objects out of attachment and concern. The problem is not the existence of concern but the transition from concern into attempted control.

She asserts that because Rui is important to her, preventing the kiss must be good for Rui and TRINITYAiLE even if it is an `愛の押しつけ`.

This is unusually direct support for the already-admitted proposition:

> **Sincere benevolence does not create jurisdiction over another person's authored goal.**

It also provides a comic but clear negative example of what happens when a protector refuses to accept the protected person's stated professional choice.

## 4.4 The story does not ultimately canonize Yu's control as normatively correct

Rui calls Yu troublesome, tells her to restrain herself, and continues to treat the work as her own professional challenge even after the actual kiss is removed from the final shoot.

Sumire temporarily joins Yu's "protective" project, so the event is not a clean didactic condemnation. The safest reading is therefore:

- Yu's attachment is genuine;
- the unit bond is genuine;
- the protective impulse becomes controlling;
- the narrative repeatedly exposes the excess through comedy and Rui's resistance.

Do not infer that the actor's eventual unavailability was caused by Yu. The text does not establish the mechanism.

### E3 classification

**COUNTEREXAMPLE-RICH SUPPORT** — protection/goal seizure, age-aware standing, intimate role performance, TRINITYAiLE relational texture.

# 5. Source 3 — 感謝を伝えるLovely Valentine's Day

## 5.1 Public romantic performance is not private romantic disclosure

Sunny Peace stages romantic confession scripts written by Nagisa to fit each member's recognizable personality. The performances are designed to make fans react emotionally, and the event explicitly calls them acting.

This is a clean positive case for:

- `PERFORMED_INTIMACY_WITHOUT_PRIVATE_CLAIM`
- `ROLE_MEDIATED_RELATIONAL_TRUTH`
- curated public persona that remains grounded in recognizable individual traits.

The performance can be "their" Sunny Peace while not being a literal confession by the performer.

## 5.2 The manager articulates a bounded private-life jurisdiction

When the manager incorrectly believes all five Sunny Peace members have private romantic partners, he says:

> `恋愛をするなとは言いません`

and later thinks:

> `私生活には口出ししないようにしていた`

His professional anxiety concerns reputational consequences if an idol's relationship becomes public, not a claimed right to forbid private attachment itself.

Admit as useful institutional support:

> **`PRIVATE_LIFE_JURISDICTION_RESTRAINT`**
>
> **Professional management may recognize reputational and operational risks around private conduct without thereby claiming ownership of the performer's private relational choices.**

This is a source-native description of Makino's stated posture, not a universal industry norm.

## 5.3 Gratitude to the manager remains reciprocal rather than debt-based

Sunny Peace's handmade chocolate is framed as gratitude for being found, supported, given a place, and helped to become the current unit.

The endpoint is continued relationship:

> `これからも笑顔で、よろしくお願いします！`

not debt discharge or permanent obligation.

This stabilizes the mature gratitude model from E2-A2 rather than changing it.

## 5.4 Sakura's "friend chocolate" restores ordinary adolescent experience

Sakura says exchanging friend chocolates was on the list of things she wanted to do after recovering from illness. Her classmates help realize it.

This is bounded support for:

- `TIME_BOUNDED_LIFE_DOMAIN`
- `ORDINARY_LIFE_RECOVERY`
- adolescent social belonging coexisting with professional idol identity.

### E3 classification

**SUPPORT** — private/professional jurisdiction, romantic role performance, manager gratitude, ordinary-life texture.

# 6. Source 4 — 雨上がりの太陽と共に

## 6.1 Haruko's fear is partly about belonging, not only weather

The weather-jinx plot eventually reveals a deeper insecurity. Haruko describes herself as:

- plain;
- lacking star power;
- unlucky at important moments;
- uncertain whether she belongs in Sunny Peace.

The unit's response is not to prove she secretly possesses the same kind of brilliance as Sakura or another member. They tell her that she is **needed as herself**.

This is strong support for:

- `DIFFERENTIAL_CAPACITY_WITH_EQUAL_BELONGING`
- `NON_INSTRUMENTAL_CONTRIBUTION`
- `RECIPROCAL_STANDING`

and for the broader claim that belonging is not reducible to possessing the group's most visible form of talent.

## 6.2 Yu recognizes a parallel comparative wound

Yu tells Haruko that she understands the experience of standing near extraordinary talent, feeling comparatively ordinary, and continuing to work without becoming embittered.

This is a useful cross-unit recognition scene. It does **not** make Haruko and Yu psychologically identical, but it gives direct evidence that professional comparative insecurity can become a basis for respectful peer recognition rather than only rivalry.

Support term:

> **`COMPARATIVE_INSECURITY_AS_PEER_RECOGNITION`**

Use narrowly; do not promote it to a series-level thesis without broader evidence.

## 6.3 Positive re-experience changes Haruko's relationship to Okinawa

The unit does not erase the historical failures that produced Haruko's fear. Instead, they accompany her through a new Okinawa experience whose successful performance adds a different memory to the same place.

This is structurally analogous to the later `ANNIVERSARY_REAUTHORING_WITHOUT_ERASURE` mechanism, but applied to place rather than date.

Support-only formulation:

> **`PLACE_REAUTHORING_WITHOUT_ERASURE`**

A place associated with failure can acquire new authored meaning without the old event becoming unreal.

## 6.4 Haruko's care role is chosen but not exhaustive

Haruko repeatedly cooks, organizes, reassures, and acts as Sunny Peace's "older sister" / quasi-maternal stabilizer. The others value this, but the story's final belonging claim is broader than usefulness as caregiver.

Do not reduce Haruko's membership to maternal service.

### E3 classification

**HIGH-VALUE CHARACTER SUPPORT** — Haruko belonging, comparative insecurity, cross-unit recognition, memory/place re-authoring.

# 7. Source 5 — 導きのファンファーレ

## 7.1 Sunny Peace participates in production without pretending professional self-sufficiency

The manager invites the idols into the creation of a major competitive song:

- professional composers retain composition responsibility;
- Sakura/Haruko/Shizuku collaborate on lyrics with a professional lyricist;
- Rei contributes choreography;
- Chisa contributes costume design;
- professional creators turn suggestions into finished work.

The reason is both artistic and operational: early participation increases understanding of the work and can improve performance quality under compressed rehearsal time.

This gives unusually direct support for:

> **`PARTICIPATORY_PRODUCTION_AS_PERFORMANCE_KNOWLEDGE`**
>
> **Creative participation can improve not only authorship but embodied understanding of the work that performers must later realize.**

It also strongly stabilizes:

- `DISTRIBUTED_CREATIVE_AUTHORITY`
- self-authorship without self-sufficiency;
- professional interdependence as compatible with artistic ownership.

## 7.2 Rest is treated as part of creative work rather than absence from it

Sakura initially experiences guilt at stepping away while other members continue working. Shizuku and Haruko insist that incomplete rest is ineffective:

> `休む時は休む……徹底的に`

The eventual lyric breakthrough occurs during a non-work encounter with local children.

Admit as useful support:

> **`REST_AS_CREATIVE_INFRASTRUCTURE`**
>
> **In creative labor, stepping away can be an enabling condition of good work rather than a failure to work, especially when continued effort has stopped producing useful movement.**

This is distinct from but compatible with `PROTECTED_LEISURE_AS_PROFESSIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE`.

## 7.3 Creativity is socially scaffolded, not a solitary miracle

Rei initially describes Sakura as possessing an almost natural ability to pull answers toward herself. Sakura rejects the individual-genius formulation and names the other members, the children, and the advice to rest as causal contributors.

This strongly supports the existing socially scaffolded miracle/creativity model.

Do not treat Sakura's insight as spontaneous proof of isolated genius.

## 7.4 Hoshimi City's early fans remain part of Sunny Peace's self-understanding

The local children say they remain proud of Sunny Peace but feel lonely after the group's move to Tokyo. Sakura says Hoshimi City and its supporters raised them and have not been forgotten.

This gives early, concrete evidence for:

> **`ORIGIN_COMMUNITY_RECIPROCITY`**
>
> **Professional expansion can create distance from the community that helped constitute the performer; continued success does not erase that community's standing in the group's self-narration.**

This is support-level evidence for later fame-scale externality, audience reciprocity, and institutional scale findings.

## 7.5 Haruko rejects seniority as governing internal hierarchy

When Haruko apologizes for failing to guide Sakura and Shizuku despite greater professional experience, Sakura answers that Sunny Peace began together without upper/lower standing.

This does not erase Haruko's experience. It limits seniority's jurisdiction over internal authorship.

### E3 classification

**HIGH-VALUE SUPPORT** — distributed creation, rest/creativity, local audience reciprocity, non-sovereign seniority.

# 8. Source 6 — 熱中☆ハプニングサマー

## 8.1 Early four-person LizNoir still overidentifies work with relationship

Ai and Kokoro want to spend summer leisure time with Rio and Aoi but believe the seniors are too work-focused to ask directly. The seniors indeed convert "spending the day together" into more training.

Rio and Aoi are shown rehearsing on off days, neglecting food, avoiding air conditioning in the name of understanding summer, and becoming physically depleted while trying to produce a song.

This is strong support for `OPEN-05`:

> **Professional devotion can become self-neglect even when no manager is coercing it.**

The source therefore strengthens self-authored overwork as an internal exploitation risk.

## 8.2 Ordinary shared experience becomes part of four-person unit integration

The juniors covertly create an Okinawa trip partly because they simply want to play with the seniors. Ai eventually confesses this.

Aoi answers:

> `最初からそう言ってくれれば良かったのに`

and says that invitations from Ai and Kokoro would make both seniors happy.

At the end:

> `仕事抜きでも、僕達は大切な仲間でしょ？`

Rio agrees:

> `この四人は特別`

This is important precursor evidence for mature four-person LizNoir. The unit becomes more than a professional arrangement without becoming a literal family.

Support term:

> **`NON_WORK_RELATIONAL_INTEGRATION`**
>
> **A professional unit can become more durable when members learn that shared life outside direct production also belongs to the relationship rather than being a distraction from it.**

Later E2-A2 remains stronger mature authority on four-person LizNoir constitution.

## 8.3 Experience feeds artistic differentiation

Rio and Aoi initially struggle to create a conventional "summer idol song," partly because the default romantic template does not fit the unit's present experience.

The eventual idea emerges through frustration with romance-saturated summer convention:

> `今の私達には恋より、大切な夢がある`

The song becomes possible when LizNoir stops trying to satisfy the genre's presumed romantic center and organizes summer through its own current priorities.

Support term:

> **`GENRE_EXPECTATION_REFUSAL`**
>
> **A unit's authorship can become clearer when it refuses a genre convention that does not fit its actual orientation rather than treating convention as mandatory authenticity.**

This is support-level evidence for plural idol philosophies and authored relation to convention.

## 8.4 Juniors already contribute care and creative causality

Ai and Kokoro are not merely recipients of founder expertise. They notice senior self-neglect, provide food/water, organize the trip, create the experiential conditions that finally produce the song, and become part of the reason Rio says the resulting idea could only arise from this situation.

This is clear precursor evidence for reciprocal succession and emergent unit identity.

### E3 classification

**HIGH-VALUE PRECURSOR SUPPORT** — early four-person integration, self-authored overwork, genre/authorship, juniors as constitutive contributors.

# 9. Source 7 — 昇る初陽に咲く笑顔

## 9.1 Sunny Peace already frames audience support as reciprocal obligation

The year-end/new-year material repeatedly describes the new song and performance as `恩返し` / repayment or return for a year of audience support.

Because later sources provide far stronger mature audience-reciprocity evidence, this event is best retained as early chronology/stabilization rather than thesis ownership.

## 9.2 Professional growth increases role fragmentation without dissolving the unit

The members note that individual jobs have increased and they spend less time moving as a five-person group. The New Year's trip therefore preserves the ordinary relational layer underneath growing professional differentiation.

This is texture-level support for plural professional identity and non-totalizing vocation.

## 9.3 Sakura's family demonstrates concern without vocational rejection

Sakura's parents remain worried about her health history but continue to support her idol work. When they ask whether she burdens the unit, the others answer that Sakura is indispensable and that they are grateful to have met her.

This is bounded support for:

- non-disposable standing;
- relational value beyond health vulnerability;
- family concern not automatically becoming vocational prohibition.

Do not infer that all later medical risk questions are resolved by this scene.

## 9.4 Institutional care is distributed beyond Makino

The members describe Satomi as an indispensable behind-the-scenes worker who handles multiple talents and protects field operations. During the rail disruption, Makino notices the problem remotely and dispatches Satomi, who physically solves the logistics problem and gets Sunny Peace to the live.

This provides early support for Hoshimi as a distributed professional institution rather than a manager-centered household.

## 9.5 Age-specific professional rules coexist with equal idol legitimacy

The members cannot remain for the live midnight countdown because all are teenagers, so their participation is recorded in advance.

This is a small but concrete example of age-aware professional accommodation that does not imply lesser artistic standing.

### E3 classification

**SUPPORT / TEXTURE** — early audience reciprocity, Sakura family support, distributed institutional labor, age-aware operational practice.
