# 1. `音色の輝石が紡ぐ未来` — comparative distance, reconnection, and non-terminal repair

## 1.1 Unique source-native contribution

The story places Sunny Peace and Tsuki no Tempest after their joint NEXT VENUS victory but on visibly divergent professional trajectories: Sunny has reached BIG4 while Tsuki is publicly described as struggling. Kotono's jealousy is therefore not treated as a free-floating character defect. It has a real professional object.

E2-C1 freezes this as:

- `COMPARATIVE_STATUS_DISTANCE`
- `COMPETITIVE_PROXIMITY_INHIBITED_DISCLOSURE`
- `INTERGENERATIONAL_COMPARATIVE_DISTANCE_PATTERN`

The communication problem is unusually precise. Kotono explicitly trusts Sakura, but Sakura is also the person whose success most painfully embodies the gap Kotono is measuring. Emotional closeness therefore **inhibits** rather than automatically facilitates disclosure.

Haruko's account of her own distance from Mana establishes an earlier-generation analogue. The two cases must not be collapsed into identical histories, but the recurrence is meaningful: when one Hoshimi peer rises dramatically faster than another, professional comparison can make the trusted peer the hardest person to face honestly.

## 1.2 Repair technique without forced disclosure

Haruko transmits Mana's old repair technique to Sakura: lower the pressure, do something ordinary together, and make the relationship inhabitable before demanding an explanation. Sakura consciously repeats the form through the tonkatsu outing.

The key ethical feature is that Sakura says the outing would still have mattered even if Kotono never disclosed the jealousy. The care is therefore not conditional on successful extraction of confession.

Admitted terms:

- `INTERGENERATIONAL_REPAIR_TECHNIQUE_TRANSMISSION`
- `NON_EXTRACTIVE_RECONNECTION`

Governing rule:

> **Reconnection can create the conditions for truthful disclosure without making disclosure the price of care.**

This is a useful precursor to later answerability frameworks because it demonstrates that another person's interiority cannot be treated as something care earns the right to extract.

## 1.3 The relation repairs before the result-dependence does

Kotono finally names the jealousy directly and Sakura answers with her own competitive anxiety. Their rivalry becomes mutually legible rather than silently corrosive. E2-C1 therefore admits:

- `COMPARATIVE_AVOIDANCE`
- `RECIPROCAL_RIVAL_ANXIETY`

The decisive archival point comes after the successful performance. Kotono is genuinely glad she can speak normally with Sakura again; nevertheless, her final interior monologue says the actual professional gap remains, that she still cannot smile from the heart, and that she needs **more results**.

That yields:

- `RELATIONAL_REPAIR_WITHOUT_COMPARATIVE_CURE`
- `OUTCOME_DEPENDENCE_RECURSION`

The event is therefore **not** authority for the claim that mutual rivalry cures Kotono's result-dependence. It proves nearly the opposite: relational repair can occur first while the deeper evaluative dependence remains available for later recurrence.

## 1.4 Mana/Haruko inheritance

The old song Mana wanted to sing with future Hoshimi juniors also gives Haruko a small but important inheritance movement. During Mana's life, Haruko lacked the confidence to join the imagined future. In the later festival, she can finally answer that invitation from a changed self.

Admitted term:

- `DEFERRED_PARTICIPATION_IN_INHERITANCE`

The dead person's invitation is not reproduced at the original time. It becomes answerable later, after the living person has changed enough to enter it without pretending the missed past can be restored.

# 2. `不屈のChallenger～Roll the dice～` — early IIIX abroad, contractual scaffolding, and victory-conditioned togetherness

## 2.1 The Hoshimi contract already exists before the later United States crisis

The event gives a clean early chronology point: IIIX is already operating under Hoshimi Production while remaining highly autonomous in culture, ambition, and self-presentation. Makino negotiates with the American promoter, tries to preserve a three-member opportunity rather than sending kana alone, and worries about the implications of expanded overseas activity for IIIX's Hoshimi contract.

Admitted terms:

- `HOSHIMI_CONTRACTUAL_SCAFFOLD_WITH_UNIT_AUTONOMY`
- `UNIT_PRESERVING_OPPORTUNITY_EXPANSION`

This materially strengthens `OPEN-10` chronology without replacing the later mature conclusion about IIIX autonomy. Hoshimi support predates the later reauthorization crisis; the crisis therefore cannot be read as the first moment in which IIIX possesses institutional support.

## 2.2 Prestige does not automatically travel

The American promoter refuses a larger venue because IIIX lacks American recognition, and ordinary Las Vegas visitors do not know them. This is not evidence that their Japanese status is unreal. It is evidence that prestige is jurisdictionally and geographically bounded.

Admitted term:

- `NONPORTABLE_PRESTIGE`

This gives a modest new extension to the metric/jurisdiction thesis: ranking, fame, and institutional status carry information within particular fields but do not become universal social capital merely by crossing a border.

## 2.3 miho's pre-crisis logic: win now, or togetherness loses its justification

The event preserves a particularly important pre-E1 statement from miho:

> `今勝たなくては、私達が一緒にいる意味はないんだから`

This is not merely ordinary competitiveness. It makes immediate victory the legitimating condition of the unit's continued togetherness.

Her explanation that she tolerates Fran and Kana because she is constantly shown talent greater than her own further supports an early coalition logic structured by ability and competitive usefulness.

Admitted terms:

- `IMMEDIATE_VICTORY_JUSTIFICATION`
- `TALENT_JUSTIFIED_ADVERSARIAL_COALITION`
- `SUCCESS_CONDITIONED_RELATIONAL_SECURITY`

This state must remain distinguishable from the later E1-D United States dissolution/re-authorization chain. Later IIIX explicitly has to answer whether the unit remains worth choosing when direct individual routes to each member's desires are available. E2-C1 shows **why that later problem was already latent**: IIIX's earlier account of itself had not yet secured a reason to remain together that survived failure or the disappearance of competitive utility.

## 2.4 Global ambition is preexisting, not invented by the later U.S. arc

IIIX's global orientation is already textual here. kana's overseas virality opens the invitation; Fran and miho discuss future international activity; Fran has multilingual/cosmopolitan competence even though English is not one of her strong languages; the group intends eventually to make its claimed top status real.

Admitted term:

- `PREEXISTING_GLOBAL_AMBITION`

The later U.S. arc is therefore a **test and restructuring** of an already international ambition, not the origin of that ambition.

## 2.5 Strategic status misrepresentation is a counterexample to overbroad authenticity language

Fran's Las Vegas scheme depends on the fact that local audiences do not know IIIX. She proposes calling themselves Japan's top idols because the audience cannot easily verify the claim, then uses planted hype to create public expectation around that status.

The resulting performance is genuinely successful. The promoter is impressed and offers a better future show. On return to Japan, circulating reports repeat the “Japan's top idols” framing, and IIIX recognizes that the resulting overseas praise can be exploited promotionally.

E2-C1 therefore freezes both sides:

- `STRATEGIC_STATUS_MISREPRESENTATION`
- `PERFORMANCE_BACKED_STATUS_BOOTSTRAPPING`
- `TRANSNATIONAL_REPUTATION_FEEDBACK_LOOP`

The success does **not** retroactively make the originating claim true at the time it was made. Conversely, the deceptive tactic does **not** erase the reality of the successful performance or the subsequent opportunity it creates.

This becomes a useful negative control for `OPEN-16`:

> **Construction and strategy are not sufficient conditions for authenticity.**

Later E2-B1 `CURATED_ADVERSARIAL_AUTHENTICITY` is more mature precisely because the selected representation is answerable to IIIX's actual relational grammar. The Las Vegas tactic is effective strategic construction, but its founding status claim is knowingly misleading.

# 3. Cross-event finding — success-conditioned relational security

The two stories unexpectedly preserve parallel early problems:

- Kotono restores communication with Sakura but still experiences professional equality as something she must obtain through results.
- miho frames IIIX's togetherness as meaningful only if the unit can win now.

Preferred term:

> `SUCCESS_CONDITIONED_RELATIONAL_SECURITY`

Definition:

> A relationship may be genuinely valued while the participant still treats competitive success as necessary evidence that the relationship or her place within it remains legitimate.

This is not admitted as the mature series answer. Later E1 material provides stronger authority against allowing victory or rank to become sovereign. E2-C1 is valuable because it documents the earlier state against which that maturation becomes legible.

# 4. Historical/Tier-H stress test

## 4.1 Sakura/Kotono historical formulation

Historical synthesis correctly identified Sakura and Kotono as the two central living responses to Mana and correctly treated their mature relationship as **rivalry rather than replacement**. It also correctly framed rivalry as a way for inheritance to become forward motion rather than frozen mourning.

E2-C1 does not reject that reading. It introduces a necessary temporal qualification.

The 2023 event shows that rivalry can become speakable and relationally healthy **before** Kotono has stopped treating professional results as necessary proof of equality. The later mature formulation should therefore not be back-projected as though the comparative wound disappeared at the first successful reconciliation.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + REVISE_BY_TEMPORAL_PRECISION`**

Current routing:

- use E2-C1 for the rupture/repair mechanism and explicit persistence of outcome-dependence;
- use later E1 for the stronger mature theory of rivalry, rank, and growth.

## 4.2 Haruko/Mana / inheritance historical formulation

Historical analysis correctly treats Mana's inheritance as something the living must transform rather than copy. E2-C1 strengthens this through an unusually ordinary mechanism: a meal-based repair technique passes Mana → Haruko → Sakura, while Haruko later enters a future-facing song invitation she once could not accept.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

This event is especially valuable as evidence that inheritance in *IDOLY PRIDE* includes **methods of care and relationship repair**, not only songs, biological continuity, public memory, or professional aspiration.

## 4.3 IIIX historical “absolute victory / proof” model

Historical IIIX synthesis accurately identified early IIIX as an antagonistic professional unit organized around absolute victory, strategic image-making, media/contract awareness, and “survival through proof.” E2-C1 strongly corroborates that model with unusually direct evidence: miho makes present victory the condition under which being together has meaning.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + REVISE_BY_TEMPORAL_MATURATION`**

The revision is necessary because the historical formulation can sound like a timeless unit essence. E1-D later establishes a stronger mature state in which IIIX's continuation must be re-authorized after its members encounter alternative routes to their individual desires. `IMMEDIATE_VICTORY_JUSTIFICATION` is therefore an **earlier constitution**, not the final account of why IIIX remains together.

## 4.4 IIIX persona/authenticity historical formulation

Historical analysis was right to reject the naive equation “constructed persona = false self.” That remains important for Fran, miho, kana, and the later mature unit.

However, E2-C1 demonstrates why the broader version of that claim requires discipline. The Las Vegas publicity strategy includes a knowingly false status claim. It is strategic, effective, professionally intelligent, and eventually followed by a real performance success—but it is not thereby authentic.

Later E2-B1 gives the stronger positive case: IIIX publicly curates an antagonistic relational form that is selected and edited but remains answerable to how the members actually relate.

Transition:

> **`REVISE + STRENGTHEN`**

Revised rule:

> **Persona, editing, strategy, and commercial motive can coexist with authenticity, but construction becomes authentic only insofar as the representation remains answerable to the person or relationship it claims to represent.**

E2-C1 is the useful counterexample showing that **effective construction alone is insufficient**.

## 4.5 Hoshimi / IIIX integration historical formulation

Historical synthesis already emphasized that Hoshimi does not need to domesticate IIIX into sweetness in order to integrate them. E2-C1 strengthens the chronology of that claim: Makino is already providing contractual and negotiating infrastructure while IIIX retains its hostile self-conception and independent international ambition.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

This remains subordinate to the current V2 Hoshimi ontology: Hoshimi is a professional institution, with “home/family” retained only as a relational metaphor.

# 5. Redundancy and authority routing

E2-C1 should **not** be promoted merely because it contains themes that later become central. Its durable value is more specific.

## `音色の輝石が紡ぐ未来`

**Retain as important precursor authority for:**

- Sunny/Tsuki post-Grand-Prix professional divergence;
- Kotono's explicit jealousy and comparative-distance mechanism;
- trusted-rival disclosure inhibition;
- Haruko/Mana intergenerational precedent;
- meal-based non-extractive reconnection technique;
- explicit evidence that relational repair does not eliminate result-dependence;
- Mana's future-junior song intention and Haruko's deferred participation.

**Do not use as preferred final authority for:**

- mature rivalry doctrine;
- final rank/metric philosophy;
- Kotono's final relationship to results;
- full Mana-inheritance synthesis.

Those are better governed by later E1/current synthesis states.

## `不屈のChallenger～Roll the dice～`

**Retain as important precursor authority for:**

- first explicit IIIX overseas opportunity in this event sequence;
- pre-crisis Hoshimi contractual scaffolding;
- Makino's attempt to preserve a three-member opportunity;
- portability limits of domestic prestige;
- preexisting global ambition;
- immediate-victory justification of IIIX togetherness;
- talent-based early coalition logic;
- strategic status misrepresentation and reputation feedback.

**Do not use as preferred final authority for:**

- mature IIIX reason for continuing;
- mature IIIX autonomy after U.S. reauthorization;
- mature public authenticity;
- final Hoshimi/IIIX relationship ontology.

Those are governed more strongly by E1-D/E1-E and E2-B1.

Classification recommendation for both sources:

> **Keep as `IMPORTANT`, not redundant.**

Their value lies in developmental contrast and chronology, not mature-thesis supremacy.

# 9. Audit conclusion

E2-C1 confirms the reason the queue demoted these events from mandatory mature-thesis work to precursor-sensitive close reading.

Neither source is redundant.

Neither should govern the mature formulation where later E1 is stronger.

Their durable contribution is developmental contrast:

> **Relationships can be repaired before their governing insecurity is repaired, and institutions can support a unit before the unit has decided what makes continued togetherness legitimate.**
