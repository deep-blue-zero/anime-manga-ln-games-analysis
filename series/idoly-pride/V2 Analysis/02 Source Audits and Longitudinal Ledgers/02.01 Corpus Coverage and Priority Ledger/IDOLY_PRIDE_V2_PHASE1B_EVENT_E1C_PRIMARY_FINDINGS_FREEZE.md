# 2. E1-C prospective governing thesis

The three events converge on a distinction the prior baseline had only provisionally approached:

> **Constructed identity is not the same thing as false identity; factual truthfulness, relational authenticity, artistic authorship, and public legibility are separate variables.**

E1-C repeatedly presents deliberately manufactured public forms:

- IIIX hides or stylizes private conflict for a documentary;
- IIIX converts real internal disagreement into entertaining public material;
- Hoshimi deliberately revisits a place saturated with Mana history while making a new song and new memory;
- TWINkle fabricates a friendship history, strategically changes speech register, scripts intimacy, solicits fan-defined concepts, and manipulates the final vote through misleading emotional theater;
- Kokoro uses her idol smile in school as a chosen social technology rather than as proof that her idol self is fake;
- fan participation literally helps author TWINkle's concept without fully possessing the performers.

The prospective E1-C model therefore separates four questions:

1. **Representational truth:** Is the public claim factually accurate?
2. **Authorship:** Did the performer meaningfully choose, shape, negotiate, or revise the form?
3. **Relational reality:** Does the performed form correspond to, reveal, or causally create a real relationship/state?
4. **Jurisdiction:** Who has power to impose, revise, or terminate the form?

A public performance may score differently on each axis.

This is already enough to reject a simple binary of:

> authentic/private/real vs performed/public/fake.

But E1-C does **not** imply that all constructed personas are ethically harmless. TWINkle's vote-manipulating final MC is explicitly challenged by Makino because it misleads fans. Authorship alone does not erase duties of truthfulness.

# 3. Event 2024_008 — `偽りのREALISM ～密着 新曲会議！？～`

Do not import later mature US-arc motives into this state.

**TEXTUAL FACT**

> `そこで私達の素を見せなければいいんだから`

The cameras may enter backstage spaces, but IIIX can simply avoid showing its `素`—its unguarded/private state.

This directly establishes that documentary proximity does not equal transparent truth.

Yet the event does not conclude that the resulting broadcast is therefore meaningless or wholly fake.

## 3.3 Public professionalism is performed—and real

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

When the camera arrives unexpectedly for kana and miho, all three immediately become polished professionals:

Their public behavior is constructed. It is also a real professional capacity.

A useful distinction:

> **performed professionalism is still professionalism.**

The fact that they are suppressing insults or irritation does not make their camera discipline counterfeit.

## 3.4 IIIX uses private hostility as a resource while refusing raw exposure

**TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE**

The members continue fighting through channels the camera cannot easily capture:

- private messages;
- foot stepping;
- calculated physical annoyance;
- fake sweetness;
- revenge gestures;
- strategic positioning.

The documentary therefore captures neither pure public fiction nor pure private reality. It captures a **curated layer produced under surveillance**.

This is important for `OPEN-16`.

Makino proposes that IIIX create its summer song together because he hopes a common goal will make them cooperate during filming.

This should not be rewritten as a clean example of ideal manager-guided autonomy.

The initiation mechanism includes:

- managerial miscalculation;
- camera pressure;
- pride;
- competitive comparison with other units.

The members later choose to own the task, but **later authorship does not erase the compromised initiation condition**.

Potential analytical category:

> **SOFT PUBLIC-IMAGE COERCION** — an option remains technically rejectable, but refusal becomes materially difficult because the performer is already being watched and publicly framed.

This category remains provisional pending comparison with later sources.

This yields a unit-level finding:

> **IIIX collaboration is modular rather than fusion-based.**

They do not need emotional unanimity or constant co-presence to produce collective work.

This is **curated authenticity**, not raw access.

## 3.9 IIIX's “realism” is the truthful form of their falsity

# 4. Event 2024_010 — `お待たせしました里帰り～星見凱旋記～`

## 4.1 Minimal chronology

**TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE**

The event is post-BIG4 for both major Hoshimi units represented.

- Sakura/Kotono refer to a previously promised joint hometown live;

## 4.2 The homecoming promise survives professional success but is endangered by success-induced busyness

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

Sakura and Kotono want a joint hometown live, but increased BIG4 work repeatedly makes it difficult even to talk.

This creates a modest but important institutional point:

> success can threaten the ordinary relational practices that made success meaningful.

The response is not retreat from professional success. The members preserve the promise through planning, remote conversation, and eventually institutional scheduling.

## 4.3 Set-list debate shows cross-unit fandom as reciprocal rather than hierarchical

**TEXTUAL FACT**

Sunny Peace members enthusiastically debate their favorite Tsuki songs; Tsuki members respond by debating their favorite Sunny Peace songs.

The scene is comic, but structurally important:

- rivalry does not suppress fandom;
- unit difference does not prevent genuine admiration;
- professional peers can be each other's audience.

This is a low-stakes reinforcement of **competition without erasure**.

## 4.4 Sea Paradise is a layered memory site, not merely a repeated venue

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

The event explicitly establishes at least three temporal layers at the same place:

1. Mana's childhood/family visits, including sitting with Kotono as audience;
2. Mana's own hometown live and collaboration;
3. the later Sunny Peace/Tsuki homecoming and new song.

The location therefore becomes a **memory palimpsest**: the same physical site accumulates different relations to idolhood rather than freezing one canonical event.

## 4.5 Mana's local identity is familial, professional, and civic at once

**TEXTUAL FACT**

In the flashback Mana says:

- Sea Paradise is part of her childhood/family memory;
- standing on the stage she once watched with her sister feels uncanny and moving;

Mana's public identity is therefore not only national legend. She is also a local daughter of Hoshimi City with place-specific memories and audiences.

This is relevant to `OPEN-18`: the Mana myth has a **local civic layer** beneath later franchise-scale legend.

## 4.6 Haruko's historical marginality is preserved rather than retrospectively erased

**TEXTUAL FACT**

At Mana's old handshake event:

- Mana immediately attracts a long line;
- Haruko initially has nobody;
- Haruko experiences the humiliation directly;
- Makino deliberately becomes her first attendee so others may find it easier to follow;

This is important counterevidence against any sanitized “founding generation was already a harmonious shared success” reconstruction.

Haruko is a current future-bearing idol in the E1-A baseline, but her history includes **material asymmetry of public recognition** beside Mana.

## 4.7 Makino's intervention is a small-scale example of audience coordination rather than talent substitution

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

Makino cannot manufacture Haruko's popularity. He can reduce the social barrier to being first in line.

Haruko jokingly calls it `サクラ`, but the intervention is structurally closer to **coordination seeding**:

> management can sometimes create conditions under which latent audience interest becomes expressible without determining the audience's final response.

This is distinct from performer capture and from outcome seizure.

The category is provisional.

## 4.8 Mana/Makino intimacy is present but should not swallow the institutional reading

**TEXTUAL FACT**

The flashback contains explicitly intimate Mana/Makino material:

- Mana says she likes amusement parks/aquariums because she can see the smiles of people she loves;
- she implies Makino as a `大好きな人` in context;
- she takes his hand under the transparent pretext that they might get separated;
- they promise to hold another hometown live there.

This is meaningful relational evidence.

However, E1-C's principal structural function is not merely romance/backstory. The promise becomes historically tragic precisely because Mana never returns for that second live.

The later generation then returns to the same place without pretending they can literally fulfill Mana's interrupted dyadic promise for her.

## 4.9 Kotono inherits local memory without reenacting Mana's performance

**TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE**

After hearing the old story, Kotono wants the new live to express:

- gratitude to the people of Hoshimi City;
- the growth of the current idols;
- continuity with what Mana meant to the place.

Crucially, the current generation does **not** simply reproduce Mana's `星の海の記憶` hometown set.

They make a **new song**, `パラダイス！`, for the current homecoming.

This is one of E1-C's strongest inheritance cases:

> **functional inheritance without formal duplication.**

Mana's prior function—turning local attachment and gratitude into public performance—is inherited. The exact song and exact performance are not treated as mandatory sacred forms.

## 4.10 `お姉ちゃんの分まで` must be read carefully

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

At Mana's autograph, Kotono privately says she will deliver gratitude to Hoshimi City's supporters `お姉ちゃんの分まで`.

This does not require a replacement reading.

The phrase can support:

- continuation of an interrupted gratitude relation;
- sisterly stewardship;
- acknowledgment that Mana can no longer reciprocate publicly herself.

It should not automatically be inflated into:

> Kotono becomes Mana's substitute or owns Mana's public memory.

The surrounding event instead emphasizes multi-member and cross-unit authorship.

## 4.11 Public memory becomes place-based reciprocity

**INTERPRETATION**

The homecoming event shifts “memory” away from private grief alone.

Hoshimi City fans have supported the idols through growth. The idols return with:

- a live;
- collaboration;
- local references;
- new merchandise/activities;
- a purpose-built song;
- gratitude;
- a renewed promise to return again.

Preferred term:

> **CIVIC MEMORY RECIPROCITY** — a local community remembers and supports performers, while performers return that recognition through renewed public work rather than static commemoration.

## 4.12 Memory becomes iterative rather than terminal

**TEXTUAL FACT / INTERPRETATION**

The event closes by deliberately repeating the promise form:

- Mana/Makino once: “someday again, here”;
- later Sakura/Kotono: “someday again, in this city, in this place.”

But the later promise is not identical in subject or meaning.

This gives the strongest E1-C memory formulation:

> **A living memorial relation creates another future appointment.**

The past is not preserved by stopping time at Mana's last appearance. It is preserved by making the place capable of hosting new memories.

> **kana reads intimacy itself as a communicative medium.**

That does not prove her own emotions are fake. It proves she can deliberately engineer the signs by which emotions are publicly inferred.

A speech-register change can be **performed before it is relationally earned**.

Therefore later linguistic analysis must not infer intimacy from register shift without context.

The event therefore models school isolation as a **recursive hostile system**, not a clean innocent/aggressor binary.

## 5.9 The idol smile is social technology, not automatically false selfhood

**TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE**

Later Kokoro uses her `アイドルスマイル` to ask a classmate for missed notes.

Kokoro insists the girl is still a `クラスメイト`, not a friend.

This is significant:

> **A deliberately performed idol register can create a small real-world opening without requiring the performer to pretend the resulting relationship is deeper than it is.**

The smile is strategic. The interaction is nevertheless real.

This is one of the strongest E1-C counterexamples to “performance equals falsity.”

## 5.10 Fake friendship produces real care before either character renames the relationship

**TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE**

But the private behavior has changed materially.

Preferred freeze:

Do not prematurely force a neat “they were secretly friends all along” interpretation.

## 5.11 Performance can be constitutive, not merely expressive

**INTERPRETATION**

TWINkle provides unusually strong evidence for **constitutive performance**:

> sometimes a role does not merely express a preexisting relationship; repeated performance of the role creates practices through which a new relationship becomes possible.

This is not magical role absorption.

The causal mechanism is ordinary:

- time together;
- joint labor;
- mutual observation;
- disclosure;
- practical help;
- shared stakes;
- rehearsal;
- collaborative creation.

The false public premise creates the conditions under which real private familiarity can form.

## 5.12 The final vote manipulation remains ethically unresolved and should not be romanticized

# 6. Cross-event synthesis — Persona

## 6.1 E1-C rejects “private self = real self”

**STRONG INFERENCE / INTERPRETATION**

The tranche supplies three distinct persona mechanisms.

### IIIX documentary

Private hostility is partly hidden; professional charm is deliberately produced; staged conflict can reveal real unit grammar.

### Kokoro's idol smile

A public-performance register is deliberately deployed in school and creates a real social opening.

### TWINkle

A fabricated friendship persona creates the conditions for genuine reciprocal behavior.

Therefore:

> **Private behavior is not automatically the privileged truth, and public performance is not automatically ontologically false.**

A better framework asks what the performance:

- conceals;
- reveals;
- creates;
- permits;
- manipulates;
- and who controls it.

## 6.2 New analytical distinction: representation vs constitution

Preferred vocabulary:

### `REPRESENTATIONAL_PERFORMANCE`

A performance claims to represent an already-existing fact/relationship/self.

It can be truthful or deceptive.

### `CONSTITUTIVE_PERFORMANCE`

A repeated performed form helps create the relationship/identity/practice it initially only staged.

TWINkle is the clearest case.

These categories can overlap.

## 6.3 Curated authenticity

For IIIX, the preferred term is:

> **CURATED_AUTHENTICITY**

A public form can be selectively produced while accurately revealing structural truths about the performers.

This is different from:

- raw documentary access;
- literal factual transparency;
- complete fabrication.

## 6.4 Truthfulness remains independently important

E1-C explicitly blocks the relativistic conclusion that “if performance creates something real, lying does not matter.”

Makino's objection to the TWINkle final MC preserves a separate ethical obligation:

> **Public performance may be constructed, but factual claims used to mobilize audience action remain answerable to truthfulness.**

# 14. Strongest counterevidence and interpretive limits

TWINkle's final vote manipulation is self-authored and still ethically questionable.

IIIX's camera professionalism and Kokoro's idol smile produce real professional/social effects and express genuine capacities.

TWINkle becomes more behaviorally real because the role creates sustained contact, disclosure, and cooperation. This does not prove every fabricated intimacy will become genuine.

## 14.4 Against “fans should co-author idols” as a universal norm

TWINkle voluntarily invites distributed fan input. LizNoir's sponsor event demonstrates why concentrated fan power can become capture.

The Hoshimi homecoming creates new memory; it does not reverse Mana's death or literally complete her old promise.

## 14.6 Against “institutional success solves recognition wounds”

Kokoro's BIG4 status is the clearest counterexample in this tranche.

## 14.7 Against “IIIX documentary shows the real IIIX”

The event explicitly foregrounds concealment and production. Any claim of raw documentary transparency would contradict the text.
