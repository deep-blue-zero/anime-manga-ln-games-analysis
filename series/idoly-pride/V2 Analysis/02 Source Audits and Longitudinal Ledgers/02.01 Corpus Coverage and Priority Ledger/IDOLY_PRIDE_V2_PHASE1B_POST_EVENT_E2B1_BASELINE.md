# 1. Governing thesis

Series-level:

> **answerable interdependence**

Constitutional refinement:

> **ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM**

E2-A1 jurisdiction refinement:

> **A true partial judgment becomes domination when it claims authority over dimensions it did not actually establish.**

E2-A2 identity refinement:

> **Identity is not purity from influence; it is the authored organization of influence through a continuing self or group.**

E2-A3 vocation refinement:

> **A vocation can be central without being total.**

E2-B1 adds:

> **A role is not made ethical by disappearing. It is made ethical by losing the power to become unquestionable ownership of the person.**

Preferred umbrella term:

> `ANSWERABLE_ROLE_CONSTITUTION`

Definition:

> A legitimate role may be inherited, contested, delegated, multiplied where the form permits, revised, performed, or selectively disclosed while remaining answerable to the person and relationships it organizes.

# 7. Authenticity — current model

`OPEN-16` remains open at full-series scale but is now substantially constrained.

Authenticity is **not** adequately defined as:

- unconstructed spontaneous self;
- purity from influence;
- fixed inherited style;
- compulsory public disclosure;
- absence of strategic or commercial motive;
- elimination of performance or editing.

Preferred current formulation:

> **Authenticity is answerable construction: authored control over what is performed, inherited, edited, withheld, disclosed, and represented, constrained by the people and relationships the representation claims to express.**

Performance can mediate truth. Strategy can coexist with truth. Privacy can coexist with truth. Construction becomes ethically suspect when it erases the person or relationship it claims to represent or when another actor gains unilateral representational jurisdiction.
