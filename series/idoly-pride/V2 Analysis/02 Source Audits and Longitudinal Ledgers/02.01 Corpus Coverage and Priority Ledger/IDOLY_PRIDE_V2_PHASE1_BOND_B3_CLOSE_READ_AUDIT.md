# 3. 井川葵 — embodied mentorship and reciprocal protection

Historical LizNoir analysis already treats Aoi as a dancer whose embodied intelligence, freedom, and relational perception stabilize LizNoir's severity. It also treats mature LizNoir as a four-person unit in which juniors become genuine co-creators rather than passengers.

B3 preserves this architecture while giving lower-pressure demonstrations of how Aoi teaches and receives care.

## 3.1 Teaching without deficit fixation

Aoi repeatedly rejects Ai's tendency to reduce herself to lacking dance talent. Rather than answer with vague reassurance, Aoi makes her practice beside her, gives specific movement advice, and notices when her own standard has become excessive.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN_BY_MECHANISM`**

Admit:

> `EMBODIED_MENTORING_WITHOUT_DEFICIT_FIXATION`

The relevant mechanism is not merely "Aoi is a good senior." She treats competence as improvable through bodily calibration while refusing to let a junior's comparative inferiority become an identity.

## 3.2 Protection can reverse locally

When Aoi initially promises to protect Chisa but is frightened by dogs, Chisa proposes that they protect each other: `二人で、守り合いっこしましょう`.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

Admit:

> `RECIPROCAL_PROTECTION_WITHOUT_ROLE_ERASURE`

This is compact support for `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`: the more capable or senior person need not remain the only legitimate protector.

## 3.3 Recovery and voluntary practice coexist

Aoi describes sleeping through much of her non-work time and even a prior shoot incident, while another story shows her choosing to go out and dance on an off night because her body feels underused.

Transition:

> **`OPEN / SUPPORT ONLY`**

Admit:

> `WORK_RECOVERY_AND_SELF_DIRECTED_PRACTICE_COEXIST`

Do not collapse the pair into either "Hoshimi is exploitatively exhausting Aoi" or "Aoi freely chooses all overwork." The source supports coexistence of fatigue, recovery need, and intrinsic desire to move.

## 3.4 LizNoir's cool register has become ordinary play

Aoi can jokingly deploy `リズノワに笑顔はいらない` in mundane contexts while behaving warmly or absurdly.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN_AS_VOICE_TEXTURE`**

Admit:

> `UNIT_STYLE_AS_INTERNALIZED_REGISTER_NOT_TOTAL_SELF`

This supports the governing authenticity model: stylized persona can be a real, usable register without exhausting the person's private range.

## 6.1 Private archiving of Kotono is unusually dense attention

Nagisa keeps a near-daily `毎日琴乃ちゃん観察日記` and has assembled extensive compilations of Kotono's cute expressions and performance moments.

Transition:

Admit:

> `KOTONO_FOCUSED_PRIVATE_ARCHIVING_NAGISA`

## 6.2 Explicit domestic romantic fantasy

The strongest B3 relationship evidence is `st-company-bond-ngs-01-08`. Nagisa describes Rio/Aoi's longstanding closeness as an `憧れ`, imagines a future with Kotono involving cooking together, bathing together, sleeping in the same futon, and waking together, and asks Rio for advice `二人の将来のために`.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHENED / RELATIONSHIP-CATEGORY PRECISION`**

Admit:

> `NAGISA_KOTONO_DOMESTIC_ROMANTIC_FANTASY`

Current classification:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** Nagisa explicitly imagines an intimate domestic future with Kotono.

- **OPEN:** reciprocal romantic intent from Kotono, canonical dating status, exclusivity, and enactment of the imagined future.

This is stronger evidence for Nagisa-side desire than a generic "they are close" formulation, but it is not permission to collapse the relationship ledger into confirmed mutual romance.
