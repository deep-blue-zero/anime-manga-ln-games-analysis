# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — PHASE 1 BOND STORY PRIORITY AND SAMPLING AUDIT

## 0. Governing boundary

This is a **routing audit**, not a bond-findings freeze. It inherits the frozen post-E4 state and independently reranks all 20 character bond bundles after the Tier-A spine and all 60 events have stabilized the mature public/professional model.

**No bond claim is admitted by this document.** Until a selected bond tranche is prospectively close-read and frozen, `IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_PHASE1B_POST_EVENT_E4_BASELINE.md` remains the current lower-tier analytical authority.

The bond corpus contains **20 bundles × 8 stories = 160 granular stories**, totaling **6,341 utterances**. All twenty raw bundles were scanned directly; no flattened historical character-dialogue file was allowed to substitute for the source bundle. No bond bundle declares a missing audiovisual/formal asset.

The audit deliberately asks a different question from event priority: **what does private, low-pressure, character-specific material reveal that the public/unit-facing corpus cannot establish as well?**

Provisional routing rule:

> **`PRIVATE_SCALE_DOES_NOT_EQUAL_LOW_IMPACT`**
>
> A bond story can be analytically important without changing plot architecture when it uniquely exposes private aspiration, family memory, manager–idol reciprocity, relationship asymmetry, professional future-planning, or the continuity of a wound after public resolution. Conversely, ordinary comedy does not become important merely because it is character-specific.

## 1. Rerank method

Each bundle was evaluated on seven positive dimensions plus an explicit redundancy penalty. Numeric score is **reading-order guidance only**, not an epistemic-confidence score.

| Code | Dimension | Range |
|---|---|---:|
| `M` | Makino/manager/producer relational leverage | 0–4 |
| `Q` | open-register or unresolved-claim leverage | 0–4 |
| `C` | private character / biographical delta | 0–4 |
| `R` | relationship-network delta | 0–3 |
| `P` | professional / institutional / labor delta | 0–3 |
| `H` | chronology / background leverage | 0–2 |
| `V` | voice / private-register leverage | 0–2 |
| `D` | redundancy against Tier-A + 60-event model | 0 to −3 |

Execution bands:

- **B1 / `CORE`** — score ≥15: mandatory first close-read; potentially changes a load-bearing private-state or open-register model.
- **B2 / `IMPORTANT`** — score 10–14: mandatory targeted close-read after B1.
- **B3 / `SUPPORT`** — score 6–9: support-mining tranche; preserve exact mechanisms without concept inflation.
- **B4 / `TEXTURE`** — score ≤5: indexed ordinary-life/voice material; no mandatory standalone close-read unless later ledgers expose a specific need.

The old Pass-A treatment assigned all 20 bonds `IMPORTANT / PROVISIONAL_PASS_A`. That uniform label is now superseded **for routing only**. It remains provenance, not current priority.

## 3. B1 — mandatory first close-read

### 3.1 `bond_kan_001_kan` — kana

**Why promoted:** this bundle contains the strongest private family/visibility material in the bond layer and unusually direct industry-power evidence. `st-company-bond-kan-01-08` distinguishes the unwanted grandfather contact from kana's still-unresolved wish for her father to find her; `st-company-bond-kan-01-01` depicts a producer whom kana remembers sending miho disturbing messages and whom Saegusa had apparently pressured into silence.

This can materially affect:

- kana's father/visibility wound;
- `OPEN-12` BanPro / industry ethics adjacency;
- Saegusa's offstage institutional power;
- IIIX public/private register;
- `OPEN_VERIFY_KANA_CARD_CORROBORATION`.

The last item is especially important: E4 located a crossover-only father/amusement-park memory and Tier-H suggested a later kana card may independently corroborate it. The bond does **not** itself prove that amusement-park memory, but it makes kana's family-source chain high priority before card sampling.

### 3.2 `bond_ktn_001_ktn` — 長瀬琴乃

**Why promoted:** `st-company-bond-ktn-01-05` is unusually explicit about Kotono loving Mana's music **after** choosing a path different from her sister and becoming able to hold that love without replacement logic. `st-company-bond-ktn-01-01` preserves an ordinary childhood dream with Mana and their mother.

This is high-leverage private evidence for:

- inheritance without imitation;
- family memory;
- post-grief self-authorship;
- Kotono/miho recognition through partially analogous bereavement.

Later E1/E2 material may still own the mature thesis. The bond is promoted because it can clarify the **private interior mechanism** beneath that thesis.

### 3.3 `bond_kor_001_kor` — fran

**Why promoted:** the bundle makes fran's non-idol vocational future unusually concrete. `st-company-bond-kor-01-01` shows deliberate fashion-industry networking to rebuild contacts and revive a former brand; `st-company-bond-kor-01-07` frames present action as investment in future optionality while fran continues designing IIIX stagewear.

Potential leverage:

- `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`;
- `PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`;
- IIIX private/public code-switching;
- fashion authorship as a genuine professional domain rather than decorative characterization;
- Mana's diffusion across people who were not direct successors (`st-company-bond-kor-01-04`).

## 7. Manager–idol sampling result

The bond layer does not behave like twenty symmetrical Makino routes. Many stories are cross-character/private-life scenes with Makino absent. The manager-specific value is concentrated.

Highest direct manager/player leverage appears in:

- Rui — affirmation-seeking and reciprocal gift/preparation;
- Sakura — concern for manager nutrition/self-neglect;
- Shizuku — manager-to-Sakura message and boundary between observation/fandom;
- kana — references to current manager plus external producers and Saegusa power;
- several lower-level cases where `{user}` is used as a practical professional coordinator.

Therefore the bond layer should not be synthesized as “twenty romances” or twenty equivalent one-to-one routes. Its strongest producer evidence is about **recognition, care, trust, professional coordination, and boundary-sensitive intimacy**, with relationship-category claims remaining source-specific.
