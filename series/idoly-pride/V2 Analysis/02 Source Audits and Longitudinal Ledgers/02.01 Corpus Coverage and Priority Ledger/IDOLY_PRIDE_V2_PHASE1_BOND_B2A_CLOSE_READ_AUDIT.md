# 2. Cross-source transition — Makino from protector to participant in reciprocal care

## 2.1 Historical thesis: Makino as witness, interpreter, and safeguard

Historical synthesis correctly derived Makino's management ethics from Mana: the manager must know when to support an impossible stage and when to prevent a performer from burning herself alive. It also characterized his mature role as helping each idol find a form she can inhabit rather than replacing Mana or controlling the living.

B2-A does not reject this. It expands the architecture.

Rui prepares food, drinks, and snacks for a full day of solo work with Makino. Sakura buys Makino a meal because she remembers that he had again substituted an energy drink for dinner. miho privately notices how late he works. These are not cases in which the idols seize his managerial role. They are cases in which the person occupying the managerial role becomes visible as a body and person who can himself require care.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN / REVISE_BY_RECIPROCITY`**

Admit:

- `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`;
- `CARE_FLOWS_ACROSS_ROLE_HIERARCHY`;
- `MANAGER_AS_CARE_RECIPIENT`.

Current formulation:

> **A legitimate professional care relation need not be symmetrical in authority to be reciprocal in human concern.**

Makino retains occupational responsibility. The idols retain standing to notice him, care about him, and act on proportional concerns.

## 2.2 Reciprocity is not role collapse

Historical Hoshimi synthesis already insisted that Makino's mature role is not ownership. B2-A strengthens this by demonstrating the inverse proposition: allowing idols to care for him does not make them his managers.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

Carry forward:

> **Reciprocity changes the direction of care without automatically changing jurisdiction.**

# 3. 天動瑠依 — elite self-authorship can include desired recognition

## 3.1 Historical Rui: from isolation through command toward attachment

Historical TRINITYAiLE analysis consistently identified Rui's early danger as solitary perfection: she wants to become undeniable without relying on pedigree or allowing other people's support to contaminate achievement. Her maturation rejects false choices between excellence and human attachment.

B2-A strongly supports that architecture.

Her privately drafted request to Makino — `もっと頑張るので、もっと褒めて下さい！` — shows that a mature, highly self-directed Rui can still want recognition from a specific trusted person.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

New V2 private-state term:

> `MANAGERIAL_AFFIRMATION_AS_RELATIONAL_RESOURCE`

Qualification:

- praise matters;
- the source does not show that Rui cannot function without it;
- wanting recognition does not negate self-authorship.

No historical source consulted after the freeze supplies a stronger canonically explicit relationship category that B2-A itself lacks.

> **`OPEN / NEW V2 PRIVATE-STATE EVIDENCE — DO NOT PROMOTE`**

Admit:

> `AFFECTIVE_SELF_CONSCIOUSNESS_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_SETTLEMENT`

Do not admit:

The correct analytical result is **more emotional specificity without category closure**.

## 3.3 Rui/Rio overwork

Transition:

# 5. 兵藤雫 — fandom remains constitutive, but reciprocal access changes its ethics

## 5.1 Historical thesis: fan identity becomes creative power

Historical Shizuku synthesis was already strong: she does not cease being a fan when she becomes an idol; fandom becomes audience intelligence, empathy, archival attention, project design, and creative power.

B2-A strongly confirms this. Shizuku remains a serious LizNoir/Rio fan after becoming a professional peer. She also becomes someone whose own fishing work can inspire Rio.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRONGLY STRENGTHEN`**

Admit:

- `FAN_IDENTITY_PERSISTS_AFTER_PEERHOOD`;
- support-level `REVERSED_FAN_INFLUENCE`.

Her development is not fan → ex-fan → idol. It is fan knowledge entering a reciprocal professional relationship.

## 5.2 Historical boundary correction: attention is not unlimited access

Older Shizuku synthesis celebrated her attention-archivist function: she notices, remembers, preserves, and reads meaning from objects. That remains correct. But B2-A creates an important jurisdictional boundary.

When Shizuku gathers Rio's discarded/used objects to infer preferences and habits, Rio does not reject her affection. Instead she tells Shizuku to ask directly and promises to answer what she wants to know.

Transition:

> **`REVISE_BY_BOUNDARY + STRENGTHEN`**

Admit:

> `DIRECT_RELATIONSHIP_SUPERSEDES_PARASOCIAL_EXTRACTION`

Current rule:

> **Fandom can remain constitutive after peerhood, but reciprocal access changes what forms of knowing are answerable. Where direct conversation is available, unilateral extraction from traces need not remain the preferred route.**

This is not condemnation of fan culture. It is a maturation of relational jurisdiction.

## 5.3 Shizuku overwork — new V2 risk evidence

Historical material emphasized Shizuku's fandom as refuge, language, expertise, and creative resource. B2-A adds its strongest material-risk qualification so far.

Her schedule collision contains only things she genuinely values:

- idol work;
- idol fandom;
- fan community;
- choreography learning;
- fishing/family activity.

Yet she concludes she has no time to rest because she wants to challenge herself at the live.

Transition:

> **`OPEN / NEW V2 RISK EVIDENCE + STRENGTHEN ROLE_ACCUMULATION_RISK`**

Admit:

> `SELF_AUTHORED_DEVOTION_OVERLOAD`

`OPEN-05` advances materially.

Crucial qualification:

> **The absence of coercion does not eliminate labor risk, but the existence of labor risk does not make the underlying chosen activities pathological.**

This is one of the cleanest sources yet for separating **authorship** from **sustainability**.

## 5.4 Shizuku/Makino

Historical Shizuku synthesis explicitly framed Makino as safe managerial recognition and gentle encouragement rather than primarily romantic structure.

B2-A does not contradict that. Its strongest Makino-related development is indirect: the tranche establishes a broader bidirectional professional-care architecture, while Shizuku's most distinctive bond findings remain fandom/peerhood and workload.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE`**

# 6. miho — professionalism, mentorship, and audience care without softening

## 6.1 Historical thesis: miho's care is correction and stewardship

Historical miho analysis consistently identified her as an adult risk sensor and unwilling mentor whose care arrives as practical correction: food, health, preparation, scheduling, technical standards, and refusal to let younger idols treat the stage carelessly.

B2-A directly confirms this through her interactions with Suzu, Mei, and others.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

Support:

> `CROSS_UNIT_SENIOR_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`

miho's severity remains part of the care form rather than something that must disappear for her maturation to count.

## 6.2 Historical professionalism as dignity and wound

Historical analysis also correctly held that miho's professionalism can protect the stage while failing to protect miho herself. B2-A does not replace that with a softer story.

Her observation of Makino's late work, the broader B2-A overwork pattern, and her continued professional seriousness fit the existing model that high competence does not guarantee healthy self-limitation.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

## 6.3 Audience reciprocity beneath an unsentimental register

Historical synthesis strongly emphasized miho's responsibility to audiences and her transformation of memory into public stewardship. B2-A adds unusually direct private-language evidence that long-term fans are personally important to her:

> `そうね。大事よ`
>
> `ただ、当たり前のことだから、わざわざ口にしないだけ`

and:

> `ファンの笑顔のために、お仕事、頑張らないと`

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + EXPAND`**

Admit:

> `UNSENTIMENTAL_AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY`

Current series-level refinement:

> **Audience reciprocity has multiple emotional grammars. Sunny Peace can articulate warmth openly; miho can treat fan importance as obvious enough not to verbalize and still organize work around their smiles.**

This prevents the synthesis from using Sunny's vocabulary as the universal test for audience attachment.

## 6.4 Public/professional versus private register

Historical miho analysis already treated her composure as an operating system rather than proof of emotional emptiness. B2-A supplies another direct case: she speaks politely and gratefully to an old producer while privately feeling embarrassment and irritation, yet both layers are answerable to real aspects of her experience.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

Admit:

> `MULTIREGISTER_AUTHENTICITY`

This strengthens `PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`.

Do not replace one simplistic binary with another. Her internal hostility is not automatically "more true" than her professional gratitude.

## 6.5 Multiyear career continuity

miho says it has been more than five years since working with a producer from near her debut and that she remains active as an idol.

Transition:

> **`OPEN / NEW V2 CHRONOLOGY EVIDENCE`**

Admit:

> `MULTIYEAR_PROFESSIONAL_CONTINUITY_MIHO`

Do not use this to close:

- `OPEN-08B` long-horizon mainstream viability;

- universal claims about idol career duration.

## 6.6 Yō privacy

miho's private memory of drinking with Yō and her immediate concern about whether Haruko heard it fits historical analysis of selective transmission: miho increasingly makes memory public where useful without surrendering every private layer.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN_AS_SUPPORT`**
