# 1. Tranche-level source-native thesis

E2-A2 supplies a coherent answer to a question that the earlier corpus repeatedly approached from different directions:

> **How can a unit remain recognizably itself when its members, roles, influences, working conditions, and creative authority change?**

The four events reject the idea that mature unit identity is preserved by keeping a fixed stylistic template, reproducing the founding members, maintaining a one-way hierarchy, or purifying the group from outside influence.

The strongest source-native formulation is:

> **A mature unit remains itself by making change answerable to the people who constitute it.**

This appears in four different forms:

1. Ai can become fully LizNoir not by suppressing what differs from the old two-person unit but by contributing an expressive strength and revising the inherited `The Last Chance` choreography.
2. Sumire's value inside TRINITYAiLE is not proved by performing self-sufficiency or by successfully surprising Rui and Yu; they already recognize her value and reciprocate her care.
3. Aoi eventually abandons the search for an externally specifiable “LizNoir-like” form and concludes that the four members dancing **as themselves** is what makes the dance LizNoir.
4. Sumire later realizes that a TRINITYAiLE song can openly carry influence from other artists and still become unique when the three members genuinely make it theirs.

The provisional analytical vocabulary supported by this tranche is therefore:

- `EMERGENT_UNIT_IDENTITY`
- `CONSTITUTIVE_SUCCESSION`
- `DISTRIBUTED_CREATIVE_AUTHORITY`
- `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`
- `NON_PERFORMATIVE_RECOGNITION`
- `RECIPROCAL_GRATITUDE_CYCLE`
- `BENEVOLENT_RECIPROCITY_PRESSURE`
- `PROTECTED_LEISURE_AS_PROFESSIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE`
- `STEWARDSHIP_THROUGH_WAITING`
- `RIVAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_WITHOUT_ASSIMILATION`

These are analytical terms, not source-language terminology.

# 2. `芽吹く黒ユリの蕾` — Ai, legacy, and the right to alter what one inherits

## 2.1 Source boundary

Stories:

- `st-eve-2109-backside-001` — `LizNoir失格`
- `st-eve-2109-backside-002` — `私じゃダメ`
- `st-eve-2109-backside-003` — `合宿スタート`
- `st-eve-2109-backside-004` — `莉央の真意`
- `st-eve-2109-backside-005` — `The Last Chance`

No declared missing asset.

## 2.2 Rio's criticism is narrower than Ai's self-condemnation

The opening conflict begins with a real performance failure.

Rio tells Ai that emotional expression itself is not the problem:

- `感情を前面に出して踊るのがダメとは言わないわ`

The problem is that Ai becomes so excited that she stops seeing the rest of the stage and disrupts the collective performance:

- `でも興奮して周りが見えなくなるなんて論外よ`
- `貴方は自分の楽しさを優先して / 観に来てくれたファンをないがしろにしたの`

Rio then uses the severe phrase:

- `LizNoir失格よ！`

Ai converts this **specific professional judgment** into a judgment about total standing:

- `やっぱり私はリズノワにふさわしくないのかな……`
- `アイドルに向いてないのかな……`
- `こんな私がリズノワの一員なんて / 最初から荷が重かったんだよ……`

This is an E2-A2 extension of E2-A1's `EVALUATIVE_JURISDICTION` problem.

A real failure in synchronization and audience-minded professionalism does not logically establish that Ai is unfit to be an idol or unfit to belong to LizNoir.

Provisional rule:

> **A valid criticism of a performance dimension does not automatically have jurisdiction over membership identity.**

This does not mean Rio's wording is harmless. The source explicitly allows Ai to hear `失格` as a threat of total exclusion, and Rio later apologizes for having been too harsh.

## 2.3 The correction is not “emotion should be suppressed”

Ai initially believes professional maturity means obeying Rio and performing the old LizNoir form more perfectly:

- `莉央さんに言われた通りにちゃんとやらなきゃ……`
- `リズノワらしい完璧なパフォーマンスを……`

That reading is directly corrected in `st-eve-2109-backside-004`.

Rio says:

- `パフォーマンスで感情をストレートに / 表現出来るのは愛の長所よ`
- `そこに高い技術が加われば、愛はさらに輝ける……`
- `愛がリズノワをさらなる高みへ引き上げてくれる……`

This is not assimilation.

Rio's mature demand is **integration of Ai's distinctive expressiveness with collective technique**, not replacement of Ai's expressiveness by the older unit's restraint.

Preferred provisional term:

> **DIFFERENCE_AS_UNIT_CAPACITY**

A member's difference can become an expansion of the unit rather than evidence of imperfect conformity.

## 2.4 `The Last Chance` is inheritance, not a museum piece

Ai's relation to `The Last Chance` is especially important because the song is a two-person LizNoir legacy work.

Ai explains that the song sustained her before she joined the group:

- `リズノワがいる場所に私もいつか行くんだと / 曲を聴いて自分を奮い立たせていたんです`
- `辛い時に心を支えてくれた宝物のような曲なんです`

This makes the later four-person performance an inheritance problem.

Ai initially fears that someone judged `LizNoir失格` has no right to sing it.

The event's actual answer is the opposite.

Ai not only gains the right to sing the inherited song; she gains the right to **revise its form**.

Her proposal is:

- `オリジナルメンバーの莉央さんと / 新メンバーの私が前に出て一緒に踊ることで`
- `新生リズノワが歌うThe Last Chanceに / ふさわしい表現になると思うんです`

Ai is even willing to surrender her solo spotlight because representing the **new four-person LizNoir** matters more than maximizing her individual showcase.

Rio's response is decisive:

- `いいわ、愛のアイデアを検討しましょう`
- `新しい振り付けを考えるから愛も手伝って頂戴`
- `LizNoirが最高のパフォーマンスをするために / これからも思ったことは遠慮無く言いなさい`

This supports:

> **CONSTITUTIVE_SUCCESSION**

Definition:

> A successor becomes a legitimate bearer of a legacy not by preserving the inherited form unchanged, but by acquiring standing to reinterpret it from inside the continuing institution or relationship.

The old work retains provenance. The new members acquire authorship over its living continuation.

## 2.5 Ai's mature relation to Rio: follower → adjacent co-author

Ai explicitly names the developmental transition:

- `今まで私は莉央さんについて行くことばかり考えて`
- `ずっと莉央さんの背中を追いかけてた……`
- `これからは後ろじゃなく、隣に並び立てるような……`
- `時には莉央さんを / 引っ張っていけるようなアイドルに私はなりたい`

This is stronger than “Ai gains confidence.”

It is a change in **constitutional standing** inside LizNoir.

She moves from:

> admiration → obedience anxiety → speech → proposal → co-authorship.

Her mature aspiration is not to overthrow Rio but to stand beside her and sometimes lead.

Preferred term:

> **ADJACENT_AUTHORITY**

Leadership here is compatible with continued admiration and hierarchy-sensitive respect.

## 2.6 Care around Ai is distributed, not monopolized by the leader

Kokoro and Suzu both help create the conditions in which Ai can recover speech.

Kokoro's most important observation is that Ai is characteristically imperfect but persistent:

- `愛は不器用なんだから`
- `失敗しても何度も練習して / 最後には必ずやり遂げる`

Suzu provides space, social support, and rival recognition.

This matters because LizNoir's internal repair is partly scaffolded by relationships **outside** the original Rio/Aoi dyad.

The unit's mature form is therefore not evidence that the founding pair has become unnecessary. It is evidence that the four-person unit and broader idol ecology can distribute developmental labor.

## 3.2 Hoshimi/BanPro difference becomes operationally explicit

The event states a concrete workload difference between TRINITYAiLE's former and current agencies.

Yu says:

- `バンプロにいた頃は / 姫野さんがえげつない量の仕事を入れてたからなぁ`
- `星見プロではうちらに負担が掛かり過ぎないように / {user}さんが調整してるらしいわ`

Sumire immediately recognizes this as an institutional difference:

- `そういう所にも事務所の違いが出るんだね`

This is direct lower-tier evidence for both `OPEN-05` and `OPEN-12`.

The event does **not** prove that Hoshimi never schedules aggressively. A real Christmas Eve job is later inserted at short notice.

The stronger, source-bounded claim is:

> **Hoshimi management explicitly treats workload burden as something to be managed rather than as an unlimited resource to monetize.**

Preferred term:

> **PROTECTED_LEISURE_AS_PROFESSIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE**

Protection of rest is not merely kindness external to professional management. It is part of the work of sustaining performers.

## 3.3 Sumire has been occupationally formed around constant availability

Sumire says that a completely free Christmas period is unfamiliar because she worked from childhood:

- `子役の頃からイベントや撮影とかあって`
- `アイドルになってからもグランプリとかがあったから……`
- `この時期に丸一日予定が空いてるのは久しぶりで`
- `何をしたらいいのか分からないんだよね～`

This is stronger than “Sumire is busy.”

Her ordinary-time competence has partly been shaped by continuous professional scheduling.

When the new job destroys her planned party, she repeatedly converts disappointment into professional duty:

- `お仕事だから仕方ないよね`
- `予定通りにいかないのは慣れてるから`
- `アイドルが忙しいのはいいことだもんね！`

Yet her concentration visibly deteriorates, and she later admits:

- `私、自分で思ってたよりずっと / 楽しみにしてたみたい……`

The event therefore distinguishes:

> professional acceptance of changed obligations

from:

> absence of personal cost.

Do not infer exploitation solely from this disappointment. Do not infer harmlessness solely because Sumire accepts the job.

## 3.4 The unit does not require Sumire to prove worth through competence theater

Sumire's original plan is not only a party.

She wants to repay Rui and Yu and show that she can surprise them for once:

- `二人はいつも凄くて、レッスンやライブでは / びっくりさせられてばかりだったから`
- `たまには私の凄いところを見せて / 二人を驚かせたかったの`

Rui and Yu answer with a crucial form of recognition:

- `サプライズなんてしなくても / すみれが凄いってことはよく知ってるわよ`
- `すみれちゃんにはいつも驚かされっぱなしや`

Preferred term:

> **NON_PERFORMATIVE_RECOGNITION**

Definition:

> A member's standing and value need not be re-earned through a successful display every time insecurity activates.

This does not remove performance standards from idol work. It limits their jurisdiction over relational belonging.

## 3.5 Gratitude moves in both directions

Sumire prepares the party as thanks to Rui and Yu.

Rui and Yu independently prepare gifts and a surprise because they are grateful to Sumire.

The result is not a settled debt ledger in which one member finally repays another.

It is a circulation:

> support → gratitude → attempted return → reciprocal return → deeper recognition.

Preferred term:

> **RECIPROCAL_GRATITUDE_CYCLE**

This supports the larger series rule that gratitude can motivate action without turning a relationship into creditor/debtor ownership.

# 4. `let's 湯けむり dancing！` — Aoi and the emergence of LizNoir-ness

## 4.2 The early Ai transition has become a stable four-person practice

At the opening, Ai and Kokoro proactively propose formation and choreography changes.

Ai explicitly links this to a mature post-BIG4 commitment:

- `莉央さんと葵さんを追いかけるだけじゃいけない……`
- `これからは私達がリズノワを背負って立つんだ`
- `莉央さんや葵さんのことも私達が / 背負うくらいの気持ちでこれからも頑張りますから！`

Aoi recognizes the change:

- `二人ともたくましくなったね`
- `自分から積極的に提案することも増えて、頼もしいよ`

This makes `芽吹く黒ユリの蕾` more than a one-time confidence episode.

The earlier right to speak has matured into routine creative participation.

## 4.3 Aoi voluntarily seeks creative responsibility

Aoi asks to lead the new-song process because he has been reflecting on what he wants to do **inside LizNoir**:

- `僕はリズノワで何がしたいのか……`
- `リズノワのために何が出来るのか……`
- `僕は自分を試してみたいんだ`

This is not coerced responsibility.

It is chosen authorship.

Yet the responsibility generates unfamiliar pressure:

- `僕のせいでリズノワを台無しにしちゃいけないって`
- `プレッシャーを感じて、ずっと緊張していたんだ`

This is important because Aoi is usually associated with freedom and low attachment to imposed obligation.

The event shows that freely chosen responsibility can still produce anxiety precisely because the object matters.

## 4.4 Solitary authorship becomes distributed authorship without becoming failure

Aoi initially asks the others to let him solve the creative problem himself.

They respect that boundary while remaining available:

- Ai: `頼ってもらえることが、私もこころも嬉しいんですから`
- Rio: `私達はいつでもそばにいるから / 助けがほしくなったら声を掛けてちょうだい`

Later Aoi explicitly asks for their help:

- `そのためには、みんなの協力が必要だ`
- `莉央、愛、こころ。手伝ってもらえるかな？`

The transition is:

> self-chosen lead → protected experimentation → recognized limit → voluntary request → collaborative completion.

Preferred term:

> **DISTRIBUTED_CREATIVE_AUTHORITY**

A lead creator can retain responsibility while distributing authorship.

Needing collaboration does not retroactively make the initial initiative illegitimate.

## 4.5 The decisive LizNoir formulation: no fixed LizNoir-like form

Aoi's central insight is explicit:

- `リズノワらしいダンスの形があるんじゃない`
- `僕らが僕ららしく踊るのがリズノワらしいダンスなんだ`

He immediately expands it member by member:

- `莉央らしく、僕らしく、愛らしく、こころらしい`

This is the strongest source-native basis in the tranche for:

> **EMERGENT_UNIT_IDENTITY**

Definition:

> A mature unit's recognizable identity can emerge from the continuing interaction of distinct members rather than from conformity to a fixed style that exists independently of them.

This does not mean LizNoir has no history, conventions, or aesthetic tendencies.

It means those tendencies are not sovereign over the people who constitute the current unit.

## 4.6 Four-person LizNoir is explicitly treated as a real beginning, not an appendix

Rio remembers the American public-square dance and says:

- `愛とこころのおかげで / 私と葵は忘れかけていた情熱を取り戻した……`
- `本当の意味で四人体制のリズノワが始まったのは / この場所からかもしれないわね`

The new members do not merely inherit an already-complete institution.

Their arrival changes what the founding pair themselves can recover and become.

This supports:

> **RECIPROCAL_SUCCESSION**

The successors are changed by the legacy, but the legacy-bearers are also changed by the successors.

## 4.7 Rio/Aoi dyad remains privileged without becoming exclusive

Rio still possesses unusually deep knowledge of Aoi:

- `何年の付き合いだと思ってるの？`
- `相棒の行きそうな場所くらい察しが付くわよ`

Their old ritual of cheek-squeezing also functions as remembered regulation before the new-song debut.

But the event does not turn that privileged dyad into an exclusive constitutional core.

Aoi ultimately says he relaxes more with **everyone** than in the hot spring:

- `僕は、温泉よりもみんなといる時の方が / リラックス出来るみたいだ`

The mature formulation remains:

> Rio/Aoi is a historically privileged constitutive dyad inside a genuinely four-person LizNoir.

## 5.2 TRINITYAiLE now has a non-terminal loss grammar

Before the retrospective body of the event, the unit reflects after a semifinal against SUNNY PEACE.

Rui says that even if they lose, she will not retain the regret she felt after NEXT VENUS because they completely expressed what they could do:

- `もし今日、サニピに勝てなかったとしても / 一切の後悔はないって`
- `出し切れたって胸を張れるの`

Yu agrees:

- `やれることはやってやって、やり切った`

This does not make victory irrelevant.

It shows that mature competition can separate:

> desire to win

from:

> the proposition that only victory can validate the performance or unit.

This is consistent with, but does not supersede, E1's `NON_TERMINAL_ABSOLUTE_VICTORY` logic for IIIX.

## 5.3 Gratitude is repeated, not discharged

TRINITYAiLE initially defines reaching BIG4 as a major repayment to the manager.

But when DoriKyun points out that gratitude toward the manager should come first, Yu articulates the key principle:

- `恩返しは一回だけなんて / 決まりはあらへんもんね`

This supports:

> **RECIPROCAL_GRATITUDE_CYCLE**

and more specifically:

> **NON_DISCHARGEABLE_GRATITUDE**

Here “non-dischargeable” does **not** mean endless debt obligation.

It means gratitude in a living relationship need not terminate after one balancing transaction because the relationship itself continues generating new gifts, support, growth, and opportunities for voluntary return.

The manager does not demand repayment.

TRINITYAiLE chooses it.

That distinction is essential.

## 5.4 Gratitude can still become pushy

The DoriKyun exchange introduces an important qualification.

DoriKyun says their preferred repayment is simple: become stronger and confront them seriously onstage.

Rui insists that DoriKyun also accept **TRINITYAiLE's preferred form** of gratitude:

- `なら、私達のしたい恩返しも受け取って下さい`
- `じゃないとフェアじゃありません！`

DoriKyun explicitly describes this as overbearing:

- `押し付けがましいんだよお前らの恩返し……！`

The scene is comedic, but the jurisdictional point is real.

Preferred provisional term:

> **BENEVOLENT_RECIPROCITY_PRESSURE**

Definition:

> A sincere desire to repay care can itself become a pressure on the recipient when the giver assumes unilateral authority to decide what form gratitude must take.

This **qualifies**, rather than rejects, the series principle that gratitude can motivate without debt ownership.

Mature reciprocity must remain answerable in both directions.

## 5.5 Self-produced gratitude is authorship, not self-sufficiency

TRINITYAiLE decides to surprise the manager by creating a new song themselves.

Their division of labor is deliberate:

- Sumire: melody / song concept
- Yu: choreography and practical coordination
- Rui: lyrics

But Sumire explicitly acknowledges that actual production still requires professional collaboration:

- `実際は作曲家さんと詰めないとだけど……`

Later the unit also needs:

- a professional studio;
- recording equipment;
- DoriKyun's infrastructure;
- a live venue;
- eventual management/agency handling of the studio expense.

The event therefore rejects any equation:

> self-authorship = doing everything alone.

Preferred formulation:

> **Authorship concerns whose purposes and decisions organize the work; interdependence concerns how the work becomes possible.**

This is a particularly clean lower-tier enactment of `answerable interdependence`.

## 5.6 Sumire's originality problem converges with Aoi's LizNoir problem

Sumire initially worries that her melodies resemble SUNNY PEACE or Tsuki no Tempest.

She interprets influence as potential failure of originality:

- `ただの真似っこになってる……ってことだよね？`
- `真似っこじゃない唯一無二の曲を作れるでしょ？`

Her brother's message provides the conceptual break:

- `唯一無二じゃないといけない、って考えに / 縛られなくていいんじゃないかな`
- `オリジナリティーって呼べる要素は多くて一割程度`
- `あとは既存楽曲の影響を受けて作曲してると思うよ`

Sumire reaches the mature formulation:

- `影響を受けちゃっていても`
- `それが、私達三人で紡ぎたいって思える曲なら……`
- `『トリエルの曲』っていう、唯一無二に仕上がる`

Preferred term:

> **INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS**

This converges strongly with Aoi's realization that no abstract “LizNoir-like form” exists outside the members' actual self-expression.

Tranche-level rule:

> **Identity is not purity from influence; it is the authored organization of influence through a continuing self or group.**

## 5.7 Rui's lyrics: intimate gratitude without settled romance proof

Rui's lyric-writing draws on explicitly personal memories involving the manager and produces language Yu reads as romantic.

Rui initially denies that interpretation and jokingly redirects the “love” toward Yu and Sumire.

The source therefore supports:

- intimate attachment;
- embarrassment;
- personally charged gratitude;
- an awareness that the lyrics can plausibly be heard as romantic.

It does **not** by itself establish a settled explicit romantic relationship with Makino/the manager.

Classification:

> **CONFLICT / AMBIGUITY — romantic reading invited but not textually settled.**

Do not force the scene into either “purely professional gratitude” or “explicit confession.”

## 5.8 DoriKyun as infrastructure without absorption

DoriKyun provides studio and venue access, but its rationale remains its own:

- reform the idol industry;
- produce stronger rivals;
- obtain a better future battle;

# 7. Cross-event synthesis — gratitude without ownership

E2-A2 also makes gratitude more complicated.

The tranche contains three forms:

1. Sumire wants to thank Rui and Yu; Rui and Yu simultaneously want to thank Sumire.
2. TRINITYAiLE wants to thank Makino/the manager through self-authored growth; he did not demand repayment.
3. TRINITYAiLE wants to thank DoriKyun even after DoriKyun explicitly says a serious future battle is enough.

The first two support reciprocal care without debt ownership.

The third shows the risk that gratitude itself can become jurisdictionally overconfident.

Therefore the source-native refinement is:

> **Gratitude is ethically mature when it remains voluntary for the giver and answerable to the recipient.**

A recipient cannot own the giver through debt.

A giver cannot own the recipient's preferred form of receiving thanks either.

This is a direct E2-A2 extension of E2-A1's bounded-jurisdiction rule.
