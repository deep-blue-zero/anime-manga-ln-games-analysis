# 1. Governing exit standard

The closure audit applies four governing principles.

## 1.1 The method defines Phase 1 by survey, priority, coverage, and controlled expansion

`IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md` defines Phase 1 as:

> corpus priority, coverage, and anime-era expansion audit

and requires the ordered Phase-1B dependency sequence through origins, Tokyo, BIG4, Stellar, remaining source classes as required, and independent event reranking.

Phase 2 is separately defined as the longitudinal reconstruction layer for:

- characters;
- relationships;
- units;
- themes/institutions;
- linguistic voice/register;
- performed voice requirements;
- formal dependencies;
- the **Makino Player-Branch Canon Ledger**.

The closure test must therefore ask whether Phase 1 has produced a stable source-routing state from which those longitudinal ledgers can be built. It must not demand Phase-3 synthesis work in advance.

## 1.2 Breadth and depth are deliberately asymmetric

`IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_SOURCE_EVIDENCE_AND_LEDGER_PROTOCOL.md` explicitly rejects equal-depth treatment of the entire live-service corpus. It requires broad coverage across every source region and selective depth where analytical load warrants it.

Accordingly:

- leaving B4 bonds indexed is not an omission if they were sampled, classified, and generated no unresolved major claim;
- leaving C2/C3 cards outside mandatory close reading is not an omission if all cards were routed and no C1 result promoted a lower-tier source;
- routing ordinary messages at story level is sufficient unless an exact message becomes claim-bearing;
- a missing audiovisual asset does not block text-ledger work unless a current claim depends on that formal evidence.

## 1.3 Open questions may survive closure

The governing method requires ambiguity to survive when evidence does not settle it. A clean phase boundary is not permission to manufacture answers.

Open relationship, career, grief, family, and formal-dependency questions may therefore pass into Phase 2 if they are explicitly named and correctly bounded.

## 1.4 Live-corpus closure is snapshot-bounded

`IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A` remains the source frontier. Phase-1 closure does not claim that later IDOLY PRIDE material does not exist. Any later source extraction enters the source-delta workflow and must not silently backfill this closure state.
