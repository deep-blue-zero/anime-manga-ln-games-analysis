# 1. Governing thesis

Series-level:

> **answerable interdependence**

E1 constitutional refinement:

> **ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM**

E2-A1 jurisdiction refinement:

> **A true partial judgment becomes domination when it claims authority over dimensions it did not actually establish.**

E2-A2 adds:

> **A mature unit remains itself by making change answerable to the people who constitute it.**

And:

> **Identity is not purity from influence; it is the authored organization of influence through a continuing self or group.**

Constitutional refinement:

> **A mature unit is not merely a constitution that can be contested. It is a constitution whose identity is partly produced by the continuing practice of contesting, revising, inheriting, and remaking it.**

# 4. Makino — stewardship through waiting

`OPEN-03` remains open.

E2-A2 admits one additional legitimate managerial mode:

> **STEWARDSHIP_THROUGH_WAITING**

Definition:

> Waiting can be active management when it preserves trust, standing, resources, and a route of return while declining to seize the idol's authorship.

The late TRINITY event explicitly shows Makino doubting whether trusting and waiting counted as doing enough. Rui answers that his continuing presence as their manager helped make their return possible.

This does not imply:

- waiting is always ethical;
- non-intervention is always respectful;
- acute medical/safety situations should be handled by waiting;
- managers are absolved from building safeguards.

Continue to use the broader Makino model:

> **risk-aware co-authorship**
