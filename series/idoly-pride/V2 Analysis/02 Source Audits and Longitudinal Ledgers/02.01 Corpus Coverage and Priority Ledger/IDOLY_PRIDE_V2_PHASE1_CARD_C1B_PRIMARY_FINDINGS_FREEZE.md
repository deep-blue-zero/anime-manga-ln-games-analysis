# 1. Tranche-level source-native result

C1-B does **not** require replacing the inherited architecture of answerable interdependence, answerable construction, reciprocal standing, role continuity without captivity, or legacy continuity without restoration.

It does materially sharpen two subordinate mechanisms.

## 1.1 Candidate refinement — `SELF_AUTHORSHIP_AS_REFLEXIVE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE`

Across Nagisa, Shizuku, and Rui, acts of expression do more than communicate a fully formed inner self.

- Nagisa writes out her past motives and then discovers a new formulation of her relationship goal through lived performance;
- Shizuku turns her former fan identity and old handle into the title and conceptual grammar of a self-authored song/live;
- Rui discovers that lyric-writing has articulated a feeling she had not yet been able to accept consciously.

Candidate rule:

> **Self-authorship can be epistemic as well as expressive: making a song, performance, or public formulation can help a person discover what she already feels but has not yet successfully named.**

This does not mean every artistic product is a transparent confession. The mechanism requires source-specific evidence that the act of making actually changes or clarifies self-understanding.

## 1.2 Candidate refinement — `DIFFERENTIATED_RECIPROCITY`

The Nagisa/Kotono pair demonstrates that reciprocity need not mean identical relational functions.

- Nagisa explicitly retains the wish to support Kotono while also insisting on shining herself and standing beside her;
- Kotono demonstrates independent solo competence yet experiences visible relief when the other four arrive and repeatedly defines her future through all five members;
- their mature mutuality therefore operates through **independent capacity plus chosen reliance**, not symmetrical personality or identical roles.

Candidate rule:

> **A reciprocal relationship need not erase asymmetry of temperament, history, or function. What matters is whether each person remains an agent who can stand independently and still choose the other as constitutive support.**

This is a relationship refinement, not evidence of romantic reciprocity by itself.

# 2. 伊吹渚 — relationship self-definition after BIG4

## 2.1 Nagisa has an increasingly independent public/professional identity

The card opens after Moon Tempest has been BIG4 for some time. Nagisa now receives solo work connected to her openly discussed love of romance novels and says that this has made her world feel broader.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** her personal taste generates work that is not reducible to being Kotono's supporter.

This matters because the relationship material that follows cannot be read as simple reversion to the earlier self-erasing Nagisa. Her own professional horizon is expanding at the same time that she attempts to define what Kotono means to her.

Candidate admission:

> **`INDEPENDENT_SELF_EXPANSION_WITHIN_RELATIONAL_COMMITMENT_NAGISA`**

## 2.2 Nagisa explicitly narrates her developmental sequence

Nagisa gives a compact retrospective:

- when she became an idol, she thought only about supporting Kotono;
- when Moon Tempest became four members, she decided to support the unit;
- now she also wants **herself** to shine.

She then immediately insists that her feelings for Kotono have not disappeared. She names Kotono as both a member of the same group and her `親友` — best/close friend — and says that conflict has allowed them to understand one another more deeply.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** the current relationship category named by the card is friendship, specifically `親友`, even while the emotional intensity exceeds ordinary teammate language.

Candidate admission:

> **`SUPPORT_DESIRE_PERSISTS_AFTER_SELF_DIFFERENTIATION_NAGISA`**

The key developmental structure is not “supporting Kotono was false.” It is that support is no longer allowed to monopolize Nagisa's identity.

## 2.3 Romantic analogy is explicit, but so is the friendship distinction

Nagisa searches for language to describe the post-separation, post-reunion relationship. A recent romance novel comes to mind: an ex-couple separates, confronts the reason for the breakup, carefully rebuilds the relationship, reconciles, and ultimately marries.

The Manager explicitly answers that there may be useful parallels **despite the difference between lovers and friends**: `恋人と友人っていう違いはあっても`.

Nagisa then drifts into imagining Kotono's eventual marriage and what kind of partner she would permit.

Evidence classification:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** Nagisa uses a romantic reconciliation narrative as an analogy for structural aspects of her and Kotono's separation/reunion.
- **TEXTUAL FACT:** the same scene explicitly distinguishes the pair from lovers and places them in the friend category.
- **STRONG INFERENCE:** the ease with which Nagisa moves from relational self-definition into marriage imagination adds to the already intense romantic/yuri legibility of her Kotono attachment.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** the analogy is not a declaration that Nagisa and Kotono are dating, are mutually in love, or are destined to marry.

Candidate mechanism:

> **`ROMANTIC_ANALOGY_WITH_EXPLICIT_FRIENDSHIP_CATEGORY`**

This source therefore intensifies coding **without converting coding into textual mutual romance**.

## 2.4 Fan reception recognizes the dyad as a special pairing

Nagisa says that a significant number of fans tell her they like “the relationship between me and Kotono.” Later, when she takes Kotono's hand onstage, the crowd erupts and shouts `ことなぎー！`.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** the audience perceives and names the dyad as a distinctive public pairing.

Candidate admission:

> **`AUDIENCE_RECOGNIZED_DYAD_KOTONAGI`**

Boundary:

> audience shipping/cheering is evidence about public reception, not proof of the pair's private ontology.

## 2.5 “I need Kotono, and Kotono needs me” is Nagisa's interpretation, not yet Kotono-side romantic testimony

After the rain-disrupted festival, Nagisa sees Kotono's expression change when the other four members arrive. She later reports that Kotono said seeing **all four of them** made her relax and feel deeply reassured.

Nagisa draws a personal conclusion:

> `私には琴乃ちゃんが必要で……琴乃ちゃんも私のことが必要なんだ`

She then formulates her mature goal:

- she still wants to support Kotono;
- she also wants to shine herself;
- she wants to stand beside Kotono with pride;
- she wants them to support each other;
- she wants to become someone who can illuminate Kotono from closest beside and be illuminated in return.

Evidence classification:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** Nagisa explicitly claims mutual need and articulates reciprocal closest-adjacency as her goal.
- **TEXTUAL FACT:** the immediate observational basis was Kotono's relief at the arrival of the four-person remainder of Moon Tempest, not a private Kotono statement singling out Nagisa.
- **STRONG INFERENCE:** Nagisa's formulation is not arbitrary; the long relationship history makes a special Nagisa/Kotono bond highly legible.
- **OPEN:** whether Kotono independently defines Nagisa as uniquely necessary in the same way, and whether any such necessity is romantic.

Candidate admission:

> **`EQUAL_ADJACENCY_WITHOUT_SUPPORT_ERASURE_NAGISA`**

and evidence-limited:

> **`MUTUAL_NEED_AS_NAGISA_SELF_DEFINITION`**

## 2.6 “Never be separated again” is an intense future commitment, not a settled exclusivity claim

During the MV, Nagisa remembers drawing another girl beside Kotono's figure after Mana's death and writing `いつまでも一緒だよ`. She recalls that they nevertheless became separated and could have lost one another permanently.

Her present internal vow is to light Kotono's path so that Kotono can move forward without getting lost, to continue the road together, and:

> `もう二度と、離れたりしない`

She concludes that she will walk forever with **Moon Tempest and Kotono**.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** Nagisa makes a very strong durable-attachment vow.

Boundary:

- the vow includes both Kotono and the unit;
- it is not a legal/social exclusivity statement;
- it does not settle marriage, dating, or Kotono's independent future choices.

# 3. 長瀬琴乃 — paired late-state authority

## 3.1 BIG4 responsibility is answerability to supporters, not only status acquisition

Kotono says that being BIG4 means being needed by more people. She remembers envying Sunny Peace when their workload first exploded, but now frames Moon Tempest's own expanded demand through gratitude toward fans who remained through painful periods.

She repeatedly says she wants to answer those feelings and therefore requests more work.

The Manager notes that job acceptance is checked with her each time; Kotono confirms that this is true.

Candidate admissions:

- **`FAN_ANSWERABILITY_AS_SELF_AUTHORED_RESPONSIBILITY_KOTONO`**;
- **`WORKLOAD_CONSENT_WITHOUT_INFINITE_CAPACITY_KOTONO`**.

Boundary:

> voluntary acceptance does not erase fatigue or health risk; the Manager continues to monitor rest and later adjusts the schedule.

## 3.2 Kotono can now stand alone without making aloneness her ideal

When the other four cannot reach the festival in time, Kotono volunteers to fill the slot alone. The Manager initially resists because of weather and workload. Kotono supplies a concrete plan: solo songs, Moon Tempest songs adapted for one voice, and MC until the others arrive.

She frames the decision as answering the people waiting in the rain and says she will protect the Moon Tempest stage until the others come.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** late-state Kotono possesses enough self-trust and performance competence to take autonomous responsibility for a crisis stage.

Candidate admission:

> **`SOLO_CAPACITY_WITHOUT_SOLO_IDEAL_KOTONO`**

This is important because reliance on the group cannot be reduced to incapacity.

## 3.3 The paired card locates Kotono's relief primarily at the five-member level

Kotono successfully performs alone. When the others finally arrive, the transition to five members is treated as the restoration of Moon Tempest's full stage. The next day, at Mana's grave, Kotono emphasizes:

- she stood alone in the rain;
- her solo performance did not make the weather clear;
- when **all five** sang, the rain eventually stopped;
- being able to visit Mana as **all five members together** makes her happy;
- she intends to travel, meet people, and deliver **their** songs.

Her final internal statement is:

> `月ストの五人で` she will clear rain clouds and emotional fog and continue anywhere without hesitation.

Candidate admission:

> **`FIVE_MEMBER_RELIANCE_AFTER_INDIVIDUALIZATION_KOTONO`**

and:

> **`GROUP_COMPLETENESS_WITHOUT_PERSONAL_INCAPACITY`**

## 3.4 Kotono-side evidence does not settle Nagisa-side romantic reciprocity

This paired card is particularly valuable because it prevents overreading the preceding Nagisa card.

Kotono:

- worries about `渚達` — Nagisa and the others collectively;
- takes responsibility until `渚達` arrive;
- is relieved when the four arrive;
- celebrates five-member performance;
- visits Mana's grave with the full group;
- defines her forward path as the five-member Moon Tempest.

What the card does **not** contain:

- a Kotono statement that Nagisa is uniquely necessary above the other members;
- Kotono romantic language toward Nagisa;
- a private future fantasy with Nagisa;
- a dating confession or exclusivity statement.

Prospective adjudication:

> **The paired Kotono card strongly establishes group-level reciprocal reliance and mature trust. It does not independently establish reciprocal romantic intent toward Nagisa.**

Therefore the Nagisa-side romantic/yuri evidence can strengthen while **mutual romance remains unresolved**.

## 3.5 Mana remains constitutive without governing Kotono's present identity

Kotono still wants to report achievements to Mana and speaks at her grave. But the direction of address has changed from searching for the route Mana would have taken to reporting the route Kotono and Moon Tempest have actually made.

Her final thought is not “I will become you.” It is:

> `私ね、もう迷わないよ`

and a commitment to move forward with Moon Tempest.

Candidate admission:

> **`MEMORIAL_ADDRESS_WITHOUT_VOCATIONAL_SUBORDINATION_KOTONO`**

This fits the inherited anti-replication architecture: Mana remains loved and addressed without remaining the sovereign measure of Kotono's future.

# 4. 兵藤雫 — fan identity becomes creative authority

## 4.1 Shizuku moves from performer to multi-layer creative author

For her first solo song and solo live, Shizuku immediately asks to participate in:

- lyric writing;
- costume design;
- live staging.

She understands that this will add workload but insists because she wants the first solo project to contain her own thoughts and to be something she herself builds.

Candidate admission:

> **`MULTILAYER_CREATIVE_AUTHORSHIP_SHIZUKU`**

Her admired retired idol is an influence because that idol also self-produced songs/staging, but Shizuku's content is grounded in her own lived history.

Boundary:

> influence on the *mode of authorship* does not make the resulting work derivative in content.

## 4.2 The pre-idol fan self is reincorporated rather than discarded

Shizuku explicitly recounts her former life:

- low self-confidence;
- social withdrawal;
- idol fandom as emotional support;
- gaining enough energy from idols to step outside;
- eventually becoming an idol herself.

The song title `drop` comes partly from the image of a small droplet growing, falling, shining, and bursting outward. It also deliberately reincorporates her old fan-account name:

> `ぷにもちどろっぷ`

The account no longer exists, but Shizuku wants the feelings of that period inside the song.

Candidate admission:

> **`PAST_FAN_SELF_AS_CREATIVE_MATERIAL_SHIZUKU`**

and:

> **`IDENTITY_INTEGRATION_WITHOUT_ROLE_FIXATION_SHIZUKU`**

Rule:

> **Becoming an idol does not require Shizuku to repudiate the fan who preceded the idol. The old role becomes material for present authorship without remaining her only identity.**

## 4.3 Fandom gives Shizuku unusually concrete audience knowledge

At the handshake event Shizuku remembers:

- recurring fans' faces;
- their names;
- social-media posts;
- earlier letters;
- the circumstances described in those letters;
- even a frequent letter-writer she has never physically met before.

She reassures a socially anxious first-time attendee by explicitly connecting that person's difficulty speaking to her own past fan experience.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** Shizuku's former fan position becomes professional relational competence.

Candidate admission:

> **`FAN_EXPERIENCE_AS_AUDIENCE_RELATIONAL_COMPETENCE_SHIZUKU`**

Boundary:

> remembering fans and empathizing with them does not erase the idol/fan role distinction or convert all parasocial relationships into private friendship.

## 4.4 Fan↔idol support is explicitly reciprocal

Shizuku says she used to understand idols as support for fans. After becoming an idol, she realizes the reverse is also true:

> fans can become emotional support for idols.

She says she loves handshake events and fan interaction because repeated meetings produce increasing comfort and because being visited and supported makes her happy.

Candidate admission:

> **`RECIPROCAL_FAN_IDOL_SUPPORT_WITH_ROLE_DIFFERENCE`**

The reciprocity is affective but not symmetrical in institutional position.

## 4.5 Audience relation becomes part of creative design

The handshake experience helps Shizuku clarify what she wants from the solo show. The Manager proposes leaving the fixed stage space and flying above/into the audience. Shizuku adopts the idea because it allows her to approach the audience physically and symbolically, to sing as one body with them.

Her costume concept — an angel with wings — is also her own design response to the flying staging.

The result is therefore neither solitary auteurism nor passive execution of management direction.

Candidate admissions:

- **`AUDIENCE_RECIPROCITY_AS_CREATIVE_GOVERNANCE_SHIZUKU`**;
- support for **distributed creative authority** with Shizuku as the project's central author.

## 4.6 “I am no longer alone” is a present relational claim, not retroactive erasure

While flying over the crowd, Shizuku thinks:

> `私にはみんながいる……私はもう、一人じゃない……！`

She tells fans that their support allowed her to fly higher and invites them toward the future together.

The source supports a transformation from isolated fan to socially embedded performer/creator.

It does **not** support saying the earlier isolated Shizuku was unreal or that current belonging permanently eliminates vulnerability.

# 5. 天動瑠依 — creative authorship and romantic self-recognition

## 5.1 Rui's disturbance is affect-specific, not generalized professional collapse

The opening repeatedly shows Rui becoming flustered around words such as `恋`, physical proximity to the Manager, and the topic of what she had been writing privately. She otherwise remains professionally functional and focused on TRINITYAiLE's Grand Prix campaign.

A flashback makes the semantic field explicit:

> `想い……気持ち……愛情……恋……っ……`

Rui immediately rejects the last category to herself: `そういうんじゃないから`.

**TEXTUAL FACT:** Rui is actively resisting a romantic interpretation of a feeling connected to her lyric-writing process.

Candidate admission:

> **`ROMANTIC_SELF_RECOGNITION_RESISTANCE_RUI`**

## 5.2 Songwriting begins as concealment and becomes a newly claimed competence

When the Manager unexpectedly finds Rui writing, she lies that she is composing poetry and reacts defensively when he suggests that the practice could support future lyric writing.

After the song is completed, she openly reverses that defensiveness. Rui says she found lyric writing enjoyable, describes the satisfaction of turning feelings into words, and says she would like to do it again if given another opportunity.

Candidate admission:

> **`DISCOVERED_CREATIVE_AUTHORSHIP_RUI`**

This is late-state authorship in two senses: Rui gains a new creative role, and the role gives her a language for private affect.

## 5.3 Professional gratitude and private attachment coexist without collapsing into each other

Rui repeatedly frames TRINITYAiLE's new song and hospitality as gratitude toward the Manager for his support. She also says she likes his refusal to unfairly privilege one Hoshimi unit while preparing all of them to perform at their best.

The script then moves beyond professional gratitude. Rui asks him to watch without looking away and later requests a promise that, after the Grand Prix, he will answer **her own real selfish wish**.

The exact content of that wish is not disclosed in the card.

Candidate admission:

> **`PRIVATE_DESIRE_EMERGES_WITHIN_PROFESSIONAL_GRATITUDE_RUI`**

Boundary:

> do not infer the exact requested action from the existence of the promise.

## 5.4 Rui eventually accepts the feeling she had been refusing to name

Immediately before the semifinal, Rui explains what had been happening:

- while writing lyrics she had been thinking about something continuously;
- she could not put it into ordinary language at first;
- the lyric-writing process did in fact make it linguistically available;
- she could not accept what the words implied;
- that denial caused her exaggerated reactions;
- once she faced the feeling directly, it became “very simple.”

During the performance, her inner monologue states that the more she faced the lyrics, the less she could deny her own feeling. After accepting it, her performance gained depth. She then says internally:

> `だからもう、偽るのは止めにするんだ`
>
> `詞を書いていた時の想いは……紛れもない本物`
>
> `この人への想いも――偽りじゃない`
>
> `{user}さん、私ね`
>
> `ずっと、あなたのことが……`

Evidence classification:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** Rui connects the previously resisted `恋` semantic field to a genuine feeling directed toward the Manager and stops denying that feeling.
- **TEXTUAL FACT:** she begins an internally addressed confession construction `ずっと、あなたのことが……`, but the predicate is cut off in the available card text.
- **STRONG INFERENCE:** the intended category is romantic attraction/love toward Makino; the repeated `恋` framing, proximity embarrassment, denial/acceptance structure, and confession formula make a non-romantic reading markedly less plausible.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** the exact omitted predicate, whether Rui subsequently speaks a confession aloud, what her “real selfish wish” specifically is, whether Makino reciprocates romantically, and whether a relationship begins.

Candidate admission:

> **`RUI_TO_MAKINO_ROMANTIC_ATTRACTION_STRONGLY_ESTABLISHED`**

with evidence safeguard:

> **The source strongly establishes Rui-side romantic attraction; it does not establish a completed mutual romance.**

## 5.5 `SELF_AUTHORSHIP_AS_REFLEXIVE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE` is strongest in Rui

Rui's case provides the cleanest source-native proof for the tranche-level mechanism.

The lyric is not merely a vehicle for publicly expressing a feeling Rui already understands. Writing it forces an affective category into words before Rui is emotionally prepared to accept what she has written. Her creative product becomes evidence she must interpret about herself.

Candidate rule:

> **Artistic authorship can externalize an unaccepted inner state and thereby create a feedback loop through which the author comes to know herself.**

This is analytically distinct from simple “authentic self-expression.”

# 10. Frozen prospective conclusion

C1-B's strongest source-native conclusion is not that every intense relationship becomes romance. It is that the late game increasingly allows characters to **author relationships and selves from positions of differentiated agency**.

Nagisa can remain deeply oriented toward Kotono without surrendering her own desire to shine. Kotono can stand alone in crisis while still locating her chosen future in the five-person Moon Tempest. Shizuku can turn the fan she once was into the creative intelligence of the idol she has become. Rui can discover through writing that a private feeling she tried to deny is real.

The four cards therefore converge on a shared late-state principle:

> **Maturity is not the deletion of formative attachments. It is the capacity to reinterpret them, reciprocate them without self-erasure, and author from them without remaining captive to their earlier form.**

This prospective conclusion is now frozen before message and Tier-H comparison.
