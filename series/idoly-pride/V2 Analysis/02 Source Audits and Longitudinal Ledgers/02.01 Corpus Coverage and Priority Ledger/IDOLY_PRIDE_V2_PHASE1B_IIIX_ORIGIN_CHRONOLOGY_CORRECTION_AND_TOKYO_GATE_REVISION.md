# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — IIIX ORIGIN CHRONOLOGY CORRECTION AND TOKYO GATE REVISION

## 0. Correction statement

A chronology-control error was discovered **during the Tokyo Phase-1B source-native pass, before the Tokyo findings were frozen and before any historical/V1 Tokyo analysis was opened**.

The earlier document `IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_PHASE1B_IIIX_PRE_TOKYO_BASELINE.md` v1.0 treated the endpoint of all four IIIX origin bundles as if it were wholly anterior to `tokyo_001_new_wind`.

That was incorrect.

The IIIX origin sequence is not a pure prequel. It crosses the Hoshimi and Tokyo/I-UNITY timeline. In particular:

- `origin_thrx_001_diamond_mining` and `origin_thrx_002_house_of_cards` are securely pre-Hoshimi/pre-Tokyo formative material;
- `origin_thrx_003_gears_go_awry` begins after Hoshimi's Grand Prix emergence and then enters the I-UNITY crisis itself;
- `origin_thrx_004_re-polished` continues through the I-UNITY semifinal/final and then proceeds into **post-I-UNITY** institutional collapse and IIIX's later freelance continuation.

Therefore, the full-origin endpoint cannot govern the epistemic state at `tokyo_001`.

The original v1.0 baseline is **not deleted or silently rewritten**. It remains an archival record of the error. The corrected prospective baseline is v1.1.

---

# 1. What caused the error

The Phase-1 architecture correctly distinguished:

- `diegetic_position`;
- `disclosure_position`;
- `prerequisite_main_story_state`;
- `retrospective_targets`;
- `forward_inheritance_point`.

The IIIX tranche failed to apply that distinction at sufficiently fine granularity.

The four bundles were correctly read as one **longitudinal IIIX development sequence**, but the subsequent gate document incorrectly equated:

> "read before Tokyo for analytical dependency"

with:

> "all depicted events occurred before Tokyo."

Those propositions are not equivalent.

Reading IIIX first was still methodologically necessary because IIIX is a major Tokyo actor. But only the **pre-Tokyo portions** of that origin sequence may be inherited at the opening of Tokyo.

# 4. Claims retained at the Tokyo opening baseline

The corrected gate may securely inherit:

- IIIX was assembled by Preta Porte as a prestige commercial project rather than through a shared friendship-origin story;
- Miho, fran, and kana began with distinct and partly instrumental motives;
- victory became their principal common denominator after an early loss;
- Miho's original idol vocation with Yō was audience-facing;
- Yō died and explicitly told Miho not to remain trapped by her;
- Mana's subsequent death and overwhelming public mourning intensified Miho's grievance about Yō's lack of public recognition;
- Miho developed a project of surpassing/overwriting Mana's records and traces;
- fran's first vocation is fashion, and financial/debt pressure materially shaped her participation in IIIX;
- kana's visibility motive includes professional branding, genuine pleasure in attention, competitive status, and the hope that her absent father might see her;
- the members may dislike one another while still taking one another's competence seriously;
- the victory compact is real and remains unresolved at the Tokyo gate;
- IIIX has already reached BIG4-level professional status;
- Miho has learned that Hoshimi will field new groups including Mana's sister.

A compact Tokyo-entry formulation is therefore:

> **IIIX enters the admissible Tokyo baseline as a highly successful corporate unit whose members share victory more clearly than they share affection or vocation, and whose strongest unresolved internal driver is Miho's attempt to turn competitive supremacy into a correction of public memory.**

This is intentionally less mature than the post-I-UNITY IIIX model.

# 5. Effect on the full IIIX origin audit

`IDOLY_PRIDE_V2_PHASE1B_IIIX_ORIGIN_AUDIT.md` remains a valid **multi-period longitudinal audit** of all four origin bundles.

Its substantive findings do not need to be discarded.

The correction is instead:

> **Do not treat the audit's endpoint as the state of IIIX at the beginning of Tokyo.**

The full audit's concepts should be temporally partitioned:

- **corporate construction / victory compact** — pre-Tokyo;
- **Himeno capture and I-UNITY complicity/conflict** — Tokyo-concurrent;
- **defeat, dissolution option, and voluntary freelance re-formation** — post-I-UNITY / post-Tokyo-endpoint development.

The prior compact thesis "authenticity through re-authorization" remains useful for mature IIIX, but **must not be used to interpret early Tokyo IIIX as if that re-authorization had already happened**.

# 6. Effect on Tokyo prospective integrity

This correction cannot erase the analyst's prior exposure to the later IIIX-origin material.

Accordingly, the Tokyo primary-source pass must not be labeled `PRISTINE`.

The correct integrity status is:

> **`CONTROLLED_RECONSTRUCTION_WITH_DISCLOSED_PRIMARY_SOURCE_HINDSIGHT`**

What has **not** occurred:

- no historical/V1 Tokyo synthesis has been consulted;
- no later BIG4 or Stellar main-story material has been opened for this pass;
- no event/bond/card/message corpus is being used to decide Tokyo meanings.

What **has** occurred:

- all four IIIX origin bundles were previously read before Tokyo;
- some of those origin stories depict concurrent or later I-UNITY outcomes.

Mitigation protocol:

1. replace the v1.0 gate with v1.1;
2. re-read/reconstruct every Tokyo block against only v1.1 plus earlier Tokyo blocks;
3. flag claims whose salience may have been increased by the earlier IIIX exposure;
4. never call a later IIIX-origin fact "known" before the Tokyo block independently establishes it;
5. preserve contradictions instead of using future knowledge to smooth them away;
6. label the Tokyo freeze's integrity status explicitly.

This is analogous to the disclosed non-pristine episode treatment already used elsewhere in the project: contamination is **bounded and documented**, not concealed.
