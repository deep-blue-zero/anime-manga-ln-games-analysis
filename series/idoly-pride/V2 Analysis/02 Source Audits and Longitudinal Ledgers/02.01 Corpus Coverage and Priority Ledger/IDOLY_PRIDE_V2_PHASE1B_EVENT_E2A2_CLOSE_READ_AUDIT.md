# 2. `芽吹く黒ユリの蕾` — Ai and constitutive succession

## 2.1 Source-native finding

Rio's opening judgment is severe but dimensionally specific. She does **not** say that emotional expression itself is incompatible with LizNoir. She says Ai became so excited that she lost stage awareness, synchronization, and audience orientation. Ai then performs the crucial overgeneralization: `LizNoir失格` becomes, in her own mind, evidence that she may be unfit for LizNoir or even unfit for idolhood.

The event itself corrects both the technical problem and the totalizing interpretation. Rio later apologizes for excessive harshness and explicitly identifies Ai's direct emotional expression as a strength:

- `パフォーマンスで感情をストレートに表現出来るのは愛の長所よ`

Rio's demand is therefore not emotional assimilation to the old two-person LizNoir. It is the integration of Ai's expressive difference with enough technique and situational awareness that the difference can enlarge the unit.

The decisive succession evidence comes through `The Last Chance`. Ai once used the old two-person song to sustain herself as an aspiring idol. When the current four-person unit prepares it, Ai does not merely earn permission to reproduce an inherited museum piece. She proposes a new formation in which original member Rio and new member Ai stand forward together because that arrangement better expresses **new LizNoir**. Rio accepts the idea and tells Ai to help design the choreography and to keep speaking freely for LizNoir's best performance.

This is `CONSTITUTIVE_SUCCESSION`:

> **Successor legitimacy includes standing to reinterpret inherited form from inside the continuing unit.**

Ai's own language makes the constitutional change explicit. She no longer wants only to follow Rio's back. She wants to stand beside her and, at times, pull her forward. This is `ADJACENT_AUTHORITY`, not overthrow.

## 2.2 Historical stress test

Historical V1 already recognized the event as important for Ai and correctly summarized its broad emotional lesson: emotion is not the enemy of LizNoir discipline; shaped emotion can become strength. It also characterized Ai's larger arc as learning to speak, contribute, and lead without betraying reverence for the seniors.

Those claims survive.

What V1 under-described is **how succession becomes legitimate**. “Ai and Kokoro can change LizNoir without destroying it” is directionally correct, but E2-A2 supports a stronger formulation:

> **The right of later members to change inherited LizNoir forms is not merely tolerated by the real unit; it is one of the practices through which the current unit becomes real.**

The harshness of Rio's opening correction also needs qualification. Because Rio later apologizes and because the event distinguishes correct diagnosis from overbroad wording, the story does not establish cruelty as a necessary developmental method.

## 2.3 Transition ledger

- V1: emotion can become strength when disciplined -> **STRENGTHEN**
- V1: Ai learns to speak/contribute/lead without betraying respect -> **STRENGTHEN**
- V1: juniors may change LizNoir without destroying it -> **REVISE + STRENGTHEN**
- Any broad reading of `LizNoir失格` as justified total membership judgment -> **REJECT**
- Any claim that harshness itself is the necessary cause of growth -> **DOWNGRADE**
- New mechanism: `CONSTITUTIVE_SUCCESSION` -> **ADMIT**
- New mechanism: `ADJACENT_AUTHORITY` -> **ADMIT**

# 3. `羽休む聖夜のサプライズ` — leisure, workload, and recognition without proof

## 3.1 Source-native finding

The event creates one of the clearest early institutional contrasts between BanPro and Hoshimi.

Yu remembers Himeno scheduling an `えげつない量の仕事`, while at Hoshimi the manager is understood to adjust the schedule so the members are not burdened excessively. That does not make Hoshimi workless or perfectly protective: a real last-minute Christmas Eve job still destroys Sumire's planned day off.

The distinction is therefore not “bad agency works / good agency rests.” It is:

> **Workload is recognized as a legitimate management problem rather than treated as an unlimited proof of professionalism.**

This supports `PROTECTED_LEISURE_AS_PROFESSIONAL_INFRASTRUCTURE`.

Sumire's reaction is especially important because she has worked since childhood. A full free Christmas is unfamiliar to her. When the job intrudes, she repeatedly translates disappointment into professional acceptance:

- `お仕事だから仕方ない`
- `予定通りにいかないのは慣れてる`
- `アイドルが忙しいのはいいこと`

The event does not portray these statements as lies, but neither does it equate them with zero cost. Her concentration deteriorates and she eventually recognizes how excited she had been about the private plan.

The party's emotional resolution is therefore not that Sumire successfully proves how useful or impressive she can be. Rui and Yu explicitly tell her that they already know she is impressive even without the surprise. They have prepared care for her at the same time she is preparing care for them.

This supports:

- `NON_PERFORMATIVE_RECOGNITION`
- `RECIPROCAL_GRATITUDE_CYCLE`

Sumire does not need to successfully stage a proof of value each time insecurity activates. Care can circulate in multiple directions instead of functioning as a debt ledger.

## 3.2 Historical stress test

Historical TRINITYAiLE analysis already identified Sumire's danger with remarkable accuracy: be bright, be useful, do not complain, do not burden others. It also described the unit's mature answer as refusing false choices and letting Sumire claim her own dream rather than existing mainly to support other people's dreams.

E2-A2 strongly confirms that psychology at a smaller, earlier scale.

The new contribution is institutional. Historical summaries tended to frame this Christmas event as loneliness, gratitude, and private care. The primary text also gives it a labor-ethics dimension: Sumire's self-silencing occurs inside a profession where scheduling can consume private time, and Hoshimi is explicitly contrasted with a prior management regime that overloaded TRINITYAiLE.

The event still does **not** establish that BanPro is uniformly exploitative or that Hoshimi never overworks idols. It establishes a concrete difference in scheduling philosophy and a reason to track rest as part of professional infrastructure.

## 3.3 Transition ledger

- V1: Sumire loneliness + TRINITY private gratitude/care -> **STRENGTHEN**
- V1: Sumire's brightness can conceal “be useful / do not burden” self-management -> **STRENGTHEN**
- Hoshimi as humane integration/refuge -> **STRENGTHEN WITH WORKLOAD MECHANISM**
- Broad claim “Hoshimi scheduling is always safe” -> **REJECT**

# 4. `let's 湯けむり dancing！` — Aoi and emergent LizNoir identity

## 4.1 Source-native finding

This is now one of the most important unit-ontology events in the corpus.

Ai and Kokoro have reached the point where they routinely propose formation and choreography changes. Ai explicitly says that after BIG4 they agreed they cannot merely chase after Rio and Aoi; they have to help carry LizNoir and sometimes carry the seniors too.

Aoi then takes unusual personal responsibility for a new song because she wants to answer two questions:

- what does she want to do **in** LizNoir?
- what can she do **for** LizNoir?

Her initial attempt to own the choreography is legitimate, but it becomes isolating. Aoi searches for a specifiable “LizNoir-like” style and becomes increasingly stuck.

The breakthrough occurs through the founding dyad and the later members together. Rio finds Aoi because their long relationship gives her unusual epistemic access. The public-square memory then links the four-person unit back to an earlier moment when Ai and Kokoro helped Rio and Aoi recover something the founders themselves had forgotten.

Rio's line is constitutionally decisive:

- `本当の意味で四人体制のリズノワが始まったのは / この場所からかもしれない`

Aoi's conclusion is even clearer:

- `リズノワらしいダンスの形があるんじゃない`
- `僕らが僕ららしく踊るのがリズノワらしいダンスなんだ`
- `莉央らしく、僕らしく、愛らしく、こころらしい`

This is `EMERGENT_UNIT_IDENTITY`:

> **A unit's recognizable identity can arise from the members' continuing relation to one another rather than from conformity to an externally fixed style template.**

It also supports `RECIPROCAL_SUCCESSION`: the later members do not merely receive LizNoir from the founders; they alter what the founders are able to recover and become.

Finally, Aoi abandons unilateral authorship without abandoning the fact that this was her initiative. She asks Rio, Ai, and Kokoro to help complete the choreography.

This is `DISTRIBUTED_CREATIVE_AUTHORITY`.

## 4.2 Historical stress test

Historical V1 ranked this as a core Aoi event and already identified the central movement: “freedom” and “passion” are not abstract concepts Aoi manufactures but things recovered through moving as LizNoir.

That reading is strongly confirmed.

Historical LizNoir work also correctly treated Rio/Aoi as a privileged founding dyad while increasingly recognizing Ai and Kokoro as real members rather than junior intruders. The E2-A2 primary text forces the next precision step:

> **Rio/Aoi remain historically and relationally privileged, but they are no longer sufficient as the exclusive constitutional essence of LizNoir.**

The public-square scene is explicit that the four-person form has its own beginning and that the younger members altered the seniors.

# 5. `羽ばたけ！恩返しのAile` — gratitude, influence, waiting, and rival infrastructure

## 5.1 Source-native finding: gratitude is plural and repeatable

TRINITYAiLE's late gratitude language is not a one-time debt settlement.

DoriKyun defines the repayment it wants: become stronger and answer onstage. TRINITYAiLE accepts that but also insists on expressing gratitude in its own preferred form. Rui's wording is revealing:

- `なら、私達のしたい恩返しも受け取って下さい`
- `じゃないとフェアじゃありません！`

Yu later says:

- `恩返しは一回だけなんて決まりはあらへん`

This supports `NON_DISCHARGEABLE_GRATITUDE` in a specific sense: gratitude need not be extinguished by one equalizing exchange. It can recur as relationship.

But the DoriKyun response supplies the ethical limit:

- `押し付けがましいんだよお前らの恩返し`

TRINITYAiLE's desire to give thanks is sincere and prosocial, yet it can become `BENEVOLENT_RECIPROCITY_PRESSURE` when the giver treats its own preferred form of gratitude as something the recipient ought to accept.

The mature rule is therefore:

> **Gratitude is ethically mature when it remains voluntary for the giver and answerable to the recipient.**

The recipient may not own the giver through debt; the giver may not own the recipient through benevolence.

## 5.2 Self-authorship does not mean self-sufficiency

TRINITYAiLE decides to create a song as a surprise for Makino and distributes creative work:

- Sumire: melodic/conceptual seed;
- Yu: choreography and practical coordination;
- Rui: lyrics.

The source explicitly preserves professional boundaries. Sumire says the actual composition still has to be worked out with a composer. The group also forgets to secure adequate studio/venue infrastructure and ultimately depends on DoriKyun's facility.

This is crucial because it prevents “self-authored song” from becoming a romantic fiction of total independence.

> **Authorship can be real while production remains collaborative and institutionally dependent.**

DoriKyun itself makes the infrastructural politics explicit. Lemon says that escaping hollowed-out bad customs requires controlling production themselves. The facility is not charity; Raimu insists they will charge money. Makino later offers to keep using it commercially.

This supports `RIVAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_WITHOUT_ASSIMILATION`:

> **An ideological rival can provide useful professional infrastructure and remain an external rival rather than being absorbed into Hoshimi's identity.**

This strengthens DoriKyun's usefulness without restoring the already rejected claim that DoriKyun's pressure is universally necessary.

## 5.3 Sumire and influence without derivativeness

Sumire worries that her melodic ideas resemble Sunny Peace or Tsuki and therefore may be mere imitation.

Her brother provides the corrective: creators do not need to be purified of influence to make something distinctive. Sumire's mature answer is that if the three of them genuinely want to weave those influences together, the resulting song becomes `トリエルの曲`.

This supports `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`:

> **Originality is not the absence of influence; it is the authored organization of influence through a continuing person or group.**

This converges directly with Aoi's LizNoir ontology. Both events reject purity models of identity.

## 5.4 Makino: stewardship through waiting

The most important Makino passage arrives after the performance. He cries and admits he repeatedly wondered what he could do as manager. Even after deciding to trust and wait, he worried that “waiting” might be all he could offer.

Rui rejects his self-condemnation. She says they reached this point because he remained their manager.

This supports `STEWARDSHIP_THROUGH_WAITING`:

> **Waiting can be active managerial labor when it preserves trust, standing, resources, and a route of return without seizing the idol's authorship.**

This does not close `OPEN-03`. Waiting is not always correct; it is plainly inadequate in some acute medical or safety situations. The event establishes it as one legitimate managerial mode, not a universal rule.

## 5.5 Rui's lyric intimacy: open, not settled romance

Yu teases Rui's lyrics as `恋心`, and Rui eventually playfully redirects the wording toward Yu and Sumire. The scene intentionally makes intimate attachment and romantic language available as a reading.

The event does **not** establish an explicit manager/Rui romance fact.

Any stronger historical romantic formulation should remain `OPEN` or be downgraded to interpretive rather than textual status.

## 5.6 Historical stress test

Historical TRINITYAiLE analysis already understood the unit's mature movement from solitary perfection toward communal ambition and gratitude. Historical message analysis also recognized TRINITYAiLE as a serious creative unit capable of collaborative lyric/craft work.

Those theses are strongly confirmed.

E2-A2 supplies four mechanisms the earlier synthesis did not fully articulate:

1. gratitude can itself overreach;
2. influence does not negate originality;
3. responsible management can include waiting rather than intervention;
4. rival infrastructure can support development without moral or institutional assimilation.

Historical DoriKyun language that called them “necessary pressure” remains rejected by E1-E. Nothing in this event reverses that correction. Their infrastructure and adversarial incentives are useful; they do not gain monopoly jurisdiction over growth.

# 6. Cross-event synthesis

## 6.1 LizNoir: from inheritance to living constitution

The two LizNoir events form a clear longitudinal sequence.

Early Ai:

> “I may be unfit for LizNoir.”

The event answers:

> Your difference can become unit capacity, and belonging gives you standing to alter the legacy you inherited.

Late Aoi:

> “What is the correct LizNoir-like form?”

The event answers:

> There is no independent template prior to the living relation among Rio, Aoi, Ai, and Kokoro; their recognizable identity emerges from the way those four particular people move together.

This is stronger than “the juniors are accepted.”

The mature four-person LizNoir is **co-constituted**.

## 6.2 TRINITYAiLE: gratitude matures from proof into circulation

The Christmas event catches Sumire trying to prove value through a secret gift and impressive execution. Rui and Yu tell her that value was already recognized.

The late event retains gratitude but distributes authorship across all three members and directs it toward Makino, DoriKyun, one another, and the future. Gratitude becomes a recurring practice rather than a debt balance.

The remaining danger is precisely the unit's strength: because they take gratitude seriously, they can become pushy about giving it.

Thus:

> **Gratitude is not a debt to be discharged and not a license to force benevolence. It is a recurring relational practice whose form remains answerable to both sides.**

## 6.3 Identity and influence

Aoi and Sumire arrive independently at the same mature ontology.

Aoi:

> LizNoir identity is not a fixed dance form; it is the four members dancing as themselves.

Sumire:

> TRINITYAiLE originality does not require purity from influence; borrowed musical languages can become theirs through authored recombination.

The post-audit rule is therefore:

> **Identity is not purity from influence; it is the authored organization of influence through a continuing self or group.**

This materially advances `OPEN-16` beyond the persona/authenticity problem encountered in E1-C.

## 6.4 Constitutional refinement

E1's `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM` can now be sharpened:

> **A mature unit is not merely a constitution that can be contested. It is a constitution whose identity is partly produced by the continuing practice of contesting, revising, inheriting, and remaking it.**

This does not imply unlimited fluidity. Provenance, role history, expertise, repertoire, and founding relationships still matter. It means none of them receives permanent unilateral jurisdiction over what the unit may become.
