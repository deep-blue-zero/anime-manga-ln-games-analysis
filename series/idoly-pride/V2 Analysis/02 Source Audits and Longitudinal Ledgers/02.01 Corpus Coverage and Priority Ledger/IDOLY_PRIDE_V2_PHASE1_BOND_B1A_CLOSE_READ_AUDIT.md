# 2. kana — visibility, selective family access, and informal industry power

## 2.1 Historical thesis: abandonment becomes visibility strategy

Historical analysis consistently treated kana's childhood abandonment as the psychological origin of her pursuit of visibility. Older synthesis described fame, television, SNS, and circulation as a signal flare aimed partly at the absent father: if kana became impossible to miss, perhaps he would find her.

B1-A strongly supports rather than replaces that thesis. The bond ending:

> `お父さん、早くkanaを見つけてよ`

is source-native evidence that desired recognition from the father exists as a distinct relational category from unwanted grandfather access.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

V2 gain:

- visibility is not generalized openness;
- wanted recognition by one relative can coexist with explicit severance from another;
- `KINSHIP_WITHOUT_CONTACT_ENTITLEMENT` and `SELECTIVE_FAMILY_VISIBILITY_LONGING` make the family-access structure more precise.

## 2.2 Historical chronology claim: the father longing survives the United States father arc

A historical full-transcript analysis explicitly interpreted the late bond story as showing that kana's father wound remains active **after** the later father confrontation/rejection arc.

That emotional interpretation is plausible, but the raw bond bundle does not provide a reliable diegetic date. B1-A therefore cannot certify the relative ordering of:

- `st-company-bond-kan-01-08`; and
- the United States IIIX father material.

The bond securely establishes the state:

> kana rejects grandfather contact while desiring father recognition.

It does **not** securely establish:

> this state occurs chronologically after the U.S. father arc.

Transition:

> **`DOWNGRADE + REVISE_BY_CHRONOLOGY`**

Carry forward:

> **`BOND_TEMPORAL_PLACEMENT_UNFIXED`**

Historical developmental readings may use the bond as compatible evidence only after chronology is independently fixed.

## 2.3 Historical thesis: public persona is not merely fake

Older analysis was already sophisticated here. kana's sugary public register, media manipulation, camera awareness, and harsh backstage speech were treated as different professional/private registers rather than a simple false-self/true-self binary.

B1-A strengthens this through the rural-location story. kana's public friendliness serves professional work, yet after the audience is gone she privately acknowledges genuine enjoyment and warmth toward the town and its people.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN / REVISE_BY_ANSWERABILITY`**

The V2 formulation is narrower than “a constructed persona is true if chosen.” Construction and authorship matter, but authenticity remains constrained by answerability to the person and relationship represented.

Admitted refinement:

> **`PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`**

The polished register is not false merely because the private register is harsher.

## 2.4 Historical “useful devil” reading

Historical bond analysis called kana a useful devil: she mocks, threatens, exploits, teaches, protects, and often performs care while denying it is care.

The B1-A sources support this as descriptive relationship texture, especially around Kokoro, Ai, and the producer interactions. However, V2 should not let the phrase become moral absolution. `01-04` contains genuine retaliatory ideation, and `01-01` shows counterleverage operating through opaque informal channels.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE_AS_CHARACTER_TEXTURE + QUALIFY`**

Carry forward:

> authenticity or affection does not make retaliatory or coercive conduct automatically ethical.

## 2.5 Saegusa and Sakamoto — new V2 institutional evidence

Historical synthesis broadly understood Saegusa as an industry actor with substantial influence, but the B1-A source supplies unusually concrete private-scale testimony:

> `三枝社長が / 裏から手を回して、こいつのこと黙らせたんだっけ`

TEXTUAL FACT:

- kana attributes a prior protective outcome involving Sakamoto to Saegusa's behind-the-scenes intervention.

Not established:

- exact legal/contractual basis;
- threat, bargain, sanction, blacklist, or formal disciplinary mechanism;
- whether Sakamoto's conduct was reported through an official process;
- whether Saegusa's intervention was reviewable or accountable.

Historical transition:

> **`OPEN / NEW V2 EVIDENCE`**

Admit:

- `INFORMAL_PROTECTIVE_COUNTERPOWER`;
- `PROTECTIVE_OUTCOME_DOES_NOT_RESOLVE_PROCEDURAL_ACCOUNTABILITY`.

Open-register consequence:

- `OPEN-18` advances materially;
- `OPEN-12` gains industry-ethics adjacency but is not resolved, because the source is not itself BanPro evidence.

# 3. 長瀬琴乃 — differentiation makes attachment freer, not weaker

## 3.1 Historical thesis: from Mana's sister toward Kotono herself

Historical synthesis correctly identified Kotono's arc as movement:

- from Mana-mediated identity toward self-authored idolhood;
- from grief-vow toward chosen project;
- from replacement logic toward carrying Mana without becoming trapped by her.

B1-A supplies one of the clearest private first-person confirmations of that model:

> `お姉ちゃんとは違う道を選んで、自分なりに答えを`
>
> `見つけて。時間は掛かっちゃったけど……`
>
> `少しは胸を張れるようになって、改めて思う`
>
> `でも……やっぱりお姉ちゃんの歌、大好きだな……`

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRONGLY STRENGTHEN`**

The historical architecture was correct; the bond gives direct private-state evidence rather than requiring the mature synthesis to infer the mechanism from public arcs.

## 3.2 Historical simplification: individuation as movement away from Mana

Some older shorthand can sound like a linear movement from “Mana's sister” to “Kotono herself,” as though stronger individuation necessarily means weaker attachment to Mana.

B1-A shows the opposite mechanism is possible:

> choosing a different path and finding her own answer allows Kotono to return to Mana's music as something she can simply love.

Transition:

> **`REVISE_BY_PRECISION`**

Admit:

- `DIFFERENTIATION_ENABLES_REAPPROPRIATION`;
- `POST_DIFFERENTIATION_FANDOM_RECOVERY`.

Current rule:

> **Differentiation need not destroy inheritance. It can remove coercive or substitutive pressure so attachment becomes freer.**

This strengthens `TRANSFORMATIVE_FIDELITY` and `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`.

## 3.3 Mana as ordinary sister rather than only myth

Historical Mana/Kotono analysis already recognized that Kotono's wound is familial before it is institutional: Mana is the sister whose idol life created distance and whose death froze an unfinished relationship.

`st-company-bond-ktn-01-01` adds an especially quiet corrective to public myth. Kotono's dream centers on childhood singing with Mana to their mother's piano and her realization that an apparently ordinary moment was precious.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

Admit:

> **`ORDINARY_MEMORY_REHUMANIZES_LEGACY`**

The bond is a canonical private-state home for remembering Mana as family rather than only as predecessor, miracle, or grievance.

## 3.4 Kotono and miho — analogous bereavement without equivalence

Historical analysis already noticed that miho can become a dark mirror to Kotono because both organize parts of idolhood around a dead person.

The bond gives Kotono's own internal recognition:

> `大切な人を亡くして、時に道を間違えて、後悔して`
>
> `それでも前に進もうとしてる`

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + QUALIFY`**

Admit:

> **`ANALOGICAL_BEREAVEMENT_RECOGNITION`**

Do not infer equivalent losses, motives, culpability, or recovery trajectories. The evidence is that Kotono perceives a meaningful analogy.

## 3.5 Kotono/Nagisa category discipline

Historical material elsewhere can read Kotono/Nagisa with strong emotional or romantic coding. B1-A certainly provides intimate texture: physical closeness, playful `デート` language, embarrassment, and a large private photo archive of Nagisa.

But this tranche does not settle the relationship category.

Transition:

> **`OPEN / DO NOT PROMOTE`**

Carry forward:

> `ROMANTIC_LANGUAGE_WITHOUT_ROMANTIC_IDENTITY` remains the governing evidentiary discipline unless stronger sources explicitly settle the category.

# 4. fran — optionality, cross-role capital, and the active future

## 4.1 Historical thesis: failed authorship and ERFOLG

Historical analysis already strongly established:

- fran's fashion identity;
- ERFOLG as failed authorship rather than mere vanity;
- money as production infrastructure;
- the desire to regain creator status;
- IIIX as a second field of aesthetic authorship.

B1-A does not overturn this. It materially strengthens its **present-tense professional mechanism**.

In `st-company-bond-kor-01-01`, fran attends a fashion-industry party specifically to rebuild contacts and thinks:

> `ブランド復活って目的がなければ`
>
> `こんな所、絶対に来ないんだから！`

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRONGLY STRENGTHEN`**

New V2 mechanism:

> **`VOCATIONAL_OPTIONALITY_BUILDING`**

fran is not merely retaining a latent fashion dream. She is actively building the conditions under which a future fashion route could become actionable.

## 4.2 Idolhood and fashion as mutually convertible capital

Historical synthesis already argued that idolhood becomes a living fashion infrastructure for fran rather than a detour from fashion.

B1-A adds a complementary outward conversion:

- current fame creates networking leverage;
- a performer can become her own advertising surface;
- present popularity can support later authorship;
- costume work keeps design practice alive inside IIIX.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN / REVISE_BY_MECHANISM`**

Admit:

- `CROSS_ROLE_CAPITAL_CONVERSION`;
- `PRESENT_FAME_AS_FUTURE_OPTIONALITY_INFRASTRUCTURE`;
- `OPTIONALITY_IS_AN_ACTIVE_PRACTICE`.

This sharpens `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`: plural vocation is not only theoretical freedom to leave. It can be materially prepared in advance.

## 4.3 Historical abrasive-mentor reading

Historical full-transcript analysis already identified fran as an abrasive mentor, especially toward Chisa. That reading is directly supported by `01-07`: fran encourages Chisa to exploit present popularity if she wants fashion work, offers advice, and immediately wraps the advice in self-promotion.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

Support term:

> `RECIPROCAL_CROSS_ROLE_MENTORSHIP`

Qualification:

- the advice does not settle Chisa's vocation for her;
- mixed motive does not make real help unreal;
- fran's desire for credit remains part of the interaction rather than something to sentimentalize away.

## 4.4 Historical “persona is true when authored” formulation

Older fran analysis used a strong and useful formulation: a persona is false when imposed and true when authored.

After E2-B1, E2-C1, SUPPORT-DEFERRED, and now B1-A, that is too absolute.

Authorship is important but not sufficient. A deliberately authored image can still:

- misrepresent status;
- conceal retaliation;
- exploit audience assumptions;
- be ethically contestable;
- fail to remain answerable to the person or relationship represented.

B1-A does, however, reject the opposite simplification that the private internal monologue is the only real fran. Her polished networking self is strategic but also competent, chosen, and genuinely directed toward brand revival.

Transition:

> **`REVISE + STRENGTHEN`**

Current formulation:

> **Private register has no monopoly on authenticity; authored construction remains subject to answerability.**

## 4.5 Financial condition — source-scope correction

Historical prose sometimes describes fran as broke, debt-ridden, or financially anxious. Other primary origin material may substantiate debt history. B1-A itself securely shows:

- stock-market interest;
- careful spending;
- surplus-food use;
- utility saving;
- deadline sensitivity;
- small-sum retrieval;
- campaign optimization;
- persistent financial calculation.

The B1-A bond does **not by itself** prove fran's current balance sheet or that every thrift behavior arises from acute poverty.

Transition:

> **`DOWNGRADE_BOND_LOCAL_SCOPE / PRESERVE_BROADER_HISTORY_WHERE_PRIMARY-SOURCED`**

Admit at B1-A support level:

> `FINANCIAL_OPTIONALITY_AS_PRIVATE_PRACTICE`

## 4.6 Mana reception

Historical series analysis understandably emphasizes Mana's gravitational importance. fran's bond provides useful negative evidence: she can watch a Mana live, recognize competent performance, remember choreography, and still not become personally reverent.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN_BY_BOUNDARY_CASE`**

Admit:

> `LEGACY_RECEPTION_WITHOUT_DEVOTION`

Hoshimi's inheritance ecology does not require uniform emotional worship of Mana.

# 5. B1-A synthesis after historical stress test

The prospective umbrella survives:

> **`SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`**
>
> **Public visibility, professional role, family connection, inherited memory, and present success may create relationships and obligations without granting indiscriminate access to the person's private self, future vocation, or relational boundaries.**

The tranche additionally strengthens a more precise authenticity rule:

> **`PRIVATE_SELF_IS_NOT_AUTHENTICITY_MONOPOLY`**
>
> **Private speech is not automatically the only true self. Public and professional registers can be genuinely authored parts of a person while remaining subject to accuracy, consent, relational fit, and ethical answerability.**

And a future-authorship rule:

> **`OPTIONALITY_IS_AN_ACTIVE_PRACTICE`**
>
> **Future self-authorship is not preserved only by retaining abstract freedom. It can be built through present skills, networks, resources, relationships, and alternative professional capacities.**

These are private-scale refinements of the series' existing answerable-interdependence framework rather than rival theses.
