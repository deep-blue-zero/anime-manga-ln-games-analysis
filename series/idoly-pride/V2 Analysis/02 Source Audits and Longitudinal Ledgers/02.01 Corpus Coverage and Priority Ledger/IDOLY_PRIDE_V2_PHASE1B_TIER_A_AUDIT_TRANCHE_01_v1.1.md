# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — PHASE 1B TIER-A SEMANTIC AUDIT
## Tranche 01: anime-era game continuity and Mana origins

## 1. Purpose

Phase 1A had already established an exhaustive bundle-level census. This tranche begins **semantic Pass B** with the Tier-A material most directly constrained by the newly frozen TV-anime endpoint:

- 23 `st-original-cmn` / Hoshimi main-story bundles, 122 granular scenes;
- three Mana unit-origin bundles, 15 granular scenes.

The question here is not yet “what is the definitive character synthesis?” It is narrower:

> **Does each source block materially change, deepen, contradict, or merely repeat the reference model enough to justify its V2 priority and later routing?**

No later game arc is used to overwrite the anime-era meaning during this tranche. The frozen anime endpoint is the comparative reference surface.

---

# 2. Tranche-level findings

## 2.1 The game anime-era story is not a redundant transcription of the anime

**Disposition: CORE as a whole; cross-media expansion audit mandatory.**

The most important Pass-B correction is that `st-original-cmn` repeatedly provides **first-person manager interiority** that the anime cannot supply in the same form. Already in `st-original-cmn-01-01-01` / `Shine Purity`, the manager narrates that Mana was a bright, popular classmate while he occupied the social “shadow” side of the classroom; that they were not initially especially close; and that his relation to her begins through observation before it becomes vocation.

Across later blocks the same POV becomes a persistent analytical resource: the manager internally evaluates group balance, interprets member behavior, worries about professional responsibility, distinguishes his grief from his duty, and narrates the second loss of Mana. This is not neutral recap. It is **cross-media character evidence for Makino Kouhei**.

Therefore the game retelling must never be globally classified `REDUNDANT` simply because the anime covers the same broad incidents.

## 2.2 The manager/player label is interface parameterization, not identity uncertainty

The source preserves `{user}` and labels player dialogue `Manager (player)`, but the governing V2 identity decision is now explicit: **the game manager is the continuing Makino Kouhei**. The customizable name is a player-facing interface affordance layered onto a character who occupies Makino's Hoshimi role and retains his established history, visual identity, and voiced presentation.

The remaining analytical problem is therefore **branch canon**, not character identity. Pass B routes manager material according to four statuses:

- identity-invariant Makino fact;
- branch-invariant Makino action/dialogue;
- mutually exclusive player-selected Makino expression;
- interface parameterization (`{user}`, custom name, equivalent affordance).

The future **Makino Player-Branch Canon Ledger** will preserve that distinction. It must not silently treat every selectable line as a fixed event, but neither should it demote optional authored responses into evidence for a separate protagonist. Repeated character traits across independent branch options can legitimately strengthen the inferred bounds of Makino's characterization.

## 2.3 The game retelling often makes latent anime propositions more explicit

Examples visible in this tranche include:

- Makino internally identifying Mei as the element missing from an overly serious group;
- Sunny's early “sun” identity being described from the manager's working perspective;
- Kotono's `私たちの頂上` and Sakura's self-authorship being placed inside extended internal/external narration;
- the heart/Mana/Sakura problem being narrated through the manager's own uncertainty;
- Makino's second grief receiving far more explicit first-person language than the anime permits.

This can **strengthen understanding**, but it also creates a methodological risk: an explicit game narration must not be projected backward as something an anime-only viewer could already know prospectively.

## 2.4 Mana's unit origins are genuinely FOUNDATIONAL

The three Mana origin bundles do not merely repeat the fact that she became an idol and died. They preserve:

- Makino's pre-idol perception of her and his lack of prior idol investment;
- Mana's request that he become involved;
- the practical difficulty of school/idol dual life;
- the debut as a transformation witnessed by both manager and audience;
- the changing Mana/Kotono sister relationship during Mana's rise;
- Mana's professional routines and overwork;
- LizNoir/Mana competitive context;
- the management ethic formed around an exceptional performer;
- the interruption of Mana's trajectory and the way her story becomes available for continuation.

These bundles are therefore **FOUNDATIONAL** for documents on Mana, Makino, inheritance, audience, miracle/labor, management ethics, and the historical ecology of LizNoir.

### Tranche judgment

**No `st-original-cmn` bundle is demoted to REDUNDANT.** The anime remains the audiovisual authority for staging, voice, music, blocking, and prospective adaptation knowledge. The game retelling is independently necessary for manager POV, branching/player language, extended narration, and cross-media continuity.

This does **not** mean every game-retelling sentence is superior to anime evidence. It means the two source surfaces answer different analytical questions.

# 5. First Makino game-continuity and branch-canon routing entries generated by this tranche

These are **routing records**, not yet the full Phase-2 Makino synthesis.

## `CLM-MAKINO-P1-001` — Mana selected an ordinary evaluator, not an idol devotee

- Source region: `Shine Purity`, `First Step`.
- Evidence class: TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE.
- Working claim: game Makino's initial value to Mana is partly that he occupies outside-the-spotlight social space and does not automatically approach her through idol/fan reverence.
- Anime relation: compatible expansion; anime establishes that Mana selected him and formed his management career but gives less sustained first-person interior narration.
- V2 routing: Makino character ledger; manager ethics; audience/recognition; Mana/Makino relationship.

## `CLM-MAKINO-P1-002` — management becomes an acquired vocation

- Source region: early `st-original-cmn`, Mana origins.
- Evidence class: TEXTUAL FACT / STRONG INFERENCE.
- Working claim: Makino is not introduced as someone with a pre-existing idol-management calling. Continued observation of Mana's development makes the work itself meaningful to him.
- V2 routing: vocation, labor, manager identity, Mana/Makino.

## `CLM-MAKINO-P1-003` — the game retelling substantially increases manager interiority

- Source region: all 23 `st-original-cmn` bundles.
- Evidence class: FORMAL/TEXTUAL SOURCE COMPARISON.
- Working claim: first-person parenthetical narration repeatedly reveals evaluations, anxiety, attraction, grief, responsibility, and professional reasoning unavailable in equivalent density from the anime.
- Guardrail: player-choice branches require claim-by-claim branch-status checking before an optional line is treated as a fixed event; `{user}` itself is interface parameterization and does not create identity uncertainty.
- V2 routing: cross-media methodology; manager synthesis; claim-revision ledger.

# 6. Cross-media guardrails established here

1. **Do not call `st-original-cmn` “the anime transcript.”** It is a game narrative retelling/expansion with its own viewpoint affordances.
2. **Do not let explicit game interiority rewrite the anime's prospective ambiguity.** The Episode 1–12 frozen ledger remains historically valid for the adaptation.
3. **Treat the player-facing manager as Makino Kouhei.** Custom naming/`{user}` is interface parameterization; only explicit primary-source contradiction can reopen identity.
4. **Do not universalize branch-specific phrasing without checking branch structure.** Stable recurring behavior carries more weight than an isolated selectable response.
5. **When exact wording becomes load-bearing, descend to `idoly-ingest`.** The analysis bundle is the reading surface; granular source remains the provenance authority.

# 8. Next Phase-1B tranche

Tranche 01 established **priority and non-redundancy** for the anime-era Hoshimi game blocks. It did not yet exhaustively catalogue every difference between the anime and the game's telling. The next immediate tranche is therefore:

## Hoshimi Anime/Game Expansion Matrix

For all 23 `st-original-cmn` blocks, map the relevant anime episode(s) and record:

- `DIRECT_RETELLING`;
- `EXPANDED_MAKINO_POV`;
- `EXPLICITATED_MOTIVE_OR_PROFESSIONAL_REASONING`;
- `ADDED_SCENE_OR_CONTEXT`;
- `BRANCH_PARAMETERIZED_MAKINO_EXPRESSION`;
- `REFRAMING_OR_EMPHASIS_SHIFT`;
- `ANIME_ONLY_AUDIOVISUAL_FORM`;
- `CONTINUITY_TENSION`.

The aim is to identify **how the game expands the anime** while keeping the Episode 1–12 prospective baseline intact.

After that matrix is frozen, proceed through the **post-anime governing main story**, in canonical sequence:

1. `tokyo_001_new_wind` through `tokyo_014_with_beyond_the_miracle`;
2. `big4_001_dark_of_the_moon` through `big4_014_epilogue`;
3. `stellar_001_to_soar_high` through `stellar_011_all_my_youth`.

Only after the post-anime governing narrative is stabilized should event/card/message priority be finalized, because those materials need a reference model against which “changes the character” can be distinguished from “illustrates the character.”
