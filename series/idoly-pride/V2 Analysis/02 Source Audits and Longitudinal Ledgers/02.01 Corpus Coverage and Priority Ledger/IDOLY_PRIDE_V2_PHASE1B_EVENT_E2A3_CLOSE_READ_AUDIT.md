# 1. Tranche-level conclusion

E2-A3 establishes a new limit on professional identity:

> **Idolhood can be a central chosen vocation without acquiring total jurisdiction over the person.**

The preferred term is:

> **`NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`**

This does not weaken the professional seriousness of *IDOLY PRIDE*. The four events instead show what a mature professional ecosystem must be able to preserve around serious idol work:

- private creative practice;
- voluntary privacy;
- family inheritance without career predestination;
- professional collaboration without inherited competence assumptions;
- nationally scalable agency procedure;
- reciprocal support of management;
- school citizenship and ordinary peer belonging;
- time-bounded developmental experiences;
- cross-role transfer of skill.

The companion term is:

> **`PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`**

A person may genuinely author several roles at once. But E2-A3 also refuses the easy conclusion that voluntary plurality makes workload harmless. Yu's repeated overextension means the tranche's full proposition is:

> **Multiple authored roles are legitimate; unlimited role accumulation is not.**

This makes E2-A3 simultaneously an autonomy tranche and an institutional-care tranche.

# 2. `おしごとシークレット` — private authorship and the right not to disclose

## 2.1 What the event establishes

Yu's doujin activity is not merely a secret hobby used for comedy. It is recurring collaborative creative labor with her father: deadlines, drawing, production, event participation, and a community of people connected by shared attachment to the same works.

Yu explicitly treats both idol work and this creator life as precious. The event therefore establishes `PRIVATE_CREATIVE_VOCATION`: the idol profession is not her only serious authored practice.

The scandal plot then separates three things that should not be collapsed:

1. **shame-driven concealment**;
2. **legitimate privacy**;
3. **public branding**.

After the threat has passed, Yu no longer believes she needs to hide her love of manga/anime. But she still declines the suggestion that her doujin activity must be announced publicly:

> `隠す必要はなくなったけど、今まで通り楽しみたい`

The important result is:

> **`SELECTIVE_DISCLOSURE_WITHOUT_SHAME`**

Authenticity does not require compulsory transparency.

## 2.2 Self-removal as a familiar ethical failure

Yu's response to threatened reputational harm is to decide that she no longer has standing to perform:

> `ステージに上がる資格、うちにはもうないから`

She is trying to protect Rui, Sumire, Hoshimi, and the doujin community, but she converts concern into unilateral self-disqualification.

Admitted term:

> **`ANTICIPATORY_SELF_DISQUALIFICATION`**

This connects directly to E2-A1's `NON_DISPOSABLE_STANDING`: even benevolent self-removal becomes ethically suspect when the person treats her prediction of others' interests as sufficient authority to decide for them.

Rui, Sumire, and Makino do not deny that reputational risk exists. They deny Yu's exclusive jurisdiction to determine that the group is better without her.

## 2.3 Historical comparison

Historical Tier-H material had already classified `おしごとシークレット` as important because Yu's fan-creator/doujin identity becomes something she can stand openly with rather than merely hide.

Transition:

> **`REVISE + STRENGTHEN`**

Preserve the core claim that Yu stops treating this part of herself as disqualifying. Revise the word **openly**. The event does **not** make total disclosure the mature endpoint. Yu's actual endpoint is stronger and more liberal:

> **I do not need to be ashamed of this, and I still decide how public it becomes.**

That distinction should govern later persona/authenticity synthesis.

# 3. `開演！ぷりてぃー★エンジェル` — lineage as resource rather than destiny

## 3.1 Family inheritance does not own vocation

Yu is explicitly the daughter of famous actor **鈴村久美子**. The event makes family pedigree highly visible and then systematically denies it sovereign force.

Yu's own vocational origin is anime fandom and the desire to stand onstage in beautiful costumes. Kumiko tells her directly:

> `優の進む道は、優が決めてええんや`
>
> `ママが役者さんやからって、優もそうなること、ない`

Admitted term:

> **`INHERITED_CAPACITY_WITHOUT_INHERITED_VOCATION`**

Family can transmit access, habits, knowledge, resources, confidence, and usable expertise. It does not thereby acquire ownership of the child's destination.

## 3.2 Pedigree does not transfer competence

The stage director's judgment is devastating precisely because Yu has reason to expect acting aptitude:

> `あなたド下手。頑張ってこれじゃ、公演は絶対無理`

Yu comes to recognize that childhood praise was at least partly filtered through being “鈴村久美子の娘.”

Admitted term:

> **`PEDIGREE_NON_TRANSFERABILITY`**

Prestige can generate expectation and opportunity. It cannot stand in for present demonstrated ability.

## 3.3 Fidelity requires imagination

Yu's source knowledge initially makes her good at copying known scenes and weak at inhabiting the character outside already-given material. Kumiko and Sumire both lead her toward a richer model: the performer must imagine the character's ordinary reactions, unshown history, and possible behavior.

Yu's doujin practice becomes useful here because secondary creation has already trained her to extend a fictional person beyond the explicit source.

Admitted term:

> **`TRANSFORMATIVE_FIDELITY`**

Faithfulness can require generative extension rather than surface duplication.

This also reinforces E2-A2's `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS` at the level of adaptation.

## 3.4 Returning to the parent is compatible with independence

Yu initially treats asking her mother for help as a possible failure of independence. The source rejects that binary. Kumiko defines her own role narrowly:

> `私に出来るのは、方向を示すことだけ。あとは優次第や`

Admitted term:

> **`VOLUNTARY_REENTRY_INTO_LINEAGE`**

Independence means retaining authority over when and how inherited expertise is used, not refusing inherited help categorically.

## 3.5 Makino's non-centrality is itself positive evidence

Makino is mildly disappointed that Yu did not rely on him, but he explicitly accepts that people cannot do this work alone and that her independent route through other relationships is good.

This strengthens the existing model of **risk-aware co-authorship** by making it less manager-centric. A competent manager need not become the exclusive gate through which every developmental problem is solved.

## 3.6 Historical comparison

Historical Tier-H triage recognized the event as important for **Yu's fan/adaptation ethics** and for acting-professionalism material, but it underweighted the family architecture.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + REVISE_BY_EXPANSION`**

Preserve the adaptation/fandom reading. Add as coequal core functions:

- parental non-ownership;
- pedigree non-transferability;
- voluntary re-entry into family expertise;
- lineage as resource rather than destiny.

The event is substantially more important to Yu's character than a narrow “adaptation ethics” label implies.

# 4. `Stars Journey` — Hoshimi becomes procedurally scalable

## 4.1 The strongest institutional result

The full twenty-idol national tour pushes Hoshimi beyond what can plausibly be described only through home/family language.

The challenge is operational:

- aligning twenty successful performers' schedules;
- national transport;
- lodging;
- publicity;
- local appearances;
- song production;
- costume production;
- budgeting;
- maintaining other work while touring.

Hoshimi succeeds without dissolving the units into one homogeneous brand.

Admitted term:

> **`SCALABLE_PLURAL_INSTITUTION`**

This event is decisive for `OPEN-04`.

## 4.2 Managerial care becomes reciprocal

Makino initially treats the tour as his old dream and then begins talking as though he must solve its impracticality himself. The idols answer:

> `全部一人でなんとかしようだなんて、水くさいですわよ`
>
> `無理しないで、もっと私達を頼って下さい`

Admitted term:

> **`RECIPROCAL_MANAGERIAL_SUPPORT`**

Makino's role is not weakened by being supportable. The institution is more mature precisely because care can move upward as well as downward.

## 4.3 Procedure, not intimacy alone, makes scale possible

Twenty people cannot continuously deliberate every creative question together. Ai proposes smaller representative groups, with unit representatives participating in song and costume development.

Admitted term:

> **`REPRESENTATIVE_CREATIVE_GOVERNANCE`**

This is a major maturation of the Hoshimi model. The agency's pluralism is not sustained merely by everyone liking one another. It is sustained by a procedure that lets difference enter production without requiring total simultaneous participation.

## 4.4 Ai and non-sovereign leadership

Ai becomes song-team leader while openly recognizing that miho is more decisive and technically useful in several dimensions.

Ai's distinctive leadership act is to identify the shared orientation—`感謝`—that can organize the twenty-person song.

Admitted term:

> **`NON_SOVEREIGN_LEADERSHIP`**

Leadership does not require being the most expert actor in every domain. It can consist in synthesis, direction, and articulation of common purpose while remaining dependent on distributed expertise.

## 4.5 All members as protagonists

Ai's late formulation is explicit:

> `星見プロのアイドルは、全員が主役なんだって……`

Admitted term:

> **`PLURAL_PROTAGONISM`**

The institution need not identify one definitive idol philosophy, one definitive center, or one legitimate route to professional excellence.

## 4.6 Material limits remain visible

Individual hotel rooms are too expensive; shared rooms are used. The final party is held at the dorm because the tour has exhausted the available budget. Schedule alignment remains difficult.

This is important negative evidence against treating Hoshimi as infinitely resourced benevolence. The institution succeeds by working within material constraints.

## 4.7 Historical comparison

Historical Tier-H analysis was already unusually strong on this event. It identified:

- representative teams by unit;
- Ai's leadership;
- miho's superior technical decisiveness in parts of the process;
- gratitude as Ai's agency-scale contribution;
- cross-unit ecology;
- every Hoshimi idol as protagonist;
- constellation imagery;
- Hoshimi as an organization that can hold incompatible idol philosophies.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

The V2 gain is chiefly institutional precision. “Hoshimi as constellation” now has an administrative mechanism:

> **pluralism scales through representative procedure, distributed expertise, budgeted logistics, and reciprocal support—not only through warmth.**

# 5. `DOTABATA SCHOOL FESTIVAL` — ordinary life as a protected domain

## 5.1 Student life has irreducible temporal value

Yu runs for student-council president despite already carrying serious idol work. Her explicit reason is:

> `学生として「今」しか出来ひんこともあるやん`

Admitted term:

> **`TIME_BOUNDED_LIFE_DOMAIN`**

Some forms of life cannot simply be deferred until after professional success. Their value is partly constituted by the developmental period in which they are available.

This is the tranche's strongest evidence for `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`.

## 5.2 Civic authority is also plural-protagonist authority

Yu's campaign promise is:

> `あなたの学校生活の主人公は、あなたなんです`
>
> `あなたが輝くためのお手伝いをさせて下さい`

Admitted term:

> **`CIVIC_PROTAGONISM`**

Her model of student leadership echoes Hoshimi's plural institutional ethic: authority should help other people inhabit their own projects rather than turn them into extensions of the office-holder.

## 5.3 The story does not romanticize doing everything

Yu rapidly accumulates:

- idol duties;
- school greetings;
- student requests;
- administrative approvals;
- festival coordination;
- writing;
- musical preparation.

Rui explicitly warns that Yu is working too much and that collapse would defeat the point. Yu's instinct is to answer by working harder.

Admitted term:

> **`ROLE_ACCUMULATION_RISK`**

This is crucial to the ethical balance of E2-A3:

> **Voluntary commitments can still become self-exploitation.**

Consent is necessary to legitimate a role; it does not prove the workload is sustainable.

## 5.4 Kotono reclaims ordinary peer belonging

Kotono notices that classmates who once used `琴乃ちゃん` now address her more distantly as `長瀬さん` after professional work kept her away from school.

She acknowledges that she once treated school as secondary to idol work. She later came to see student life as meaningful and voluntarily joins the festival committee.

Admitted terms:

> **`ORDINARY_LIFE_RECOVERY`**
>
> **`FAME_DISTANCE`**

Professional success can erode ordinary relational ease without anyone actively rejecting the performer. Re-entry requires participation rather than merely expecting earlier closeness to persist automatically.

Nagisa independently supports the same principle: limited school attendance makes substantive participation more, not less, valuable.

## 5.5 Mana's school-stage legacy is inherited without duplication

Mana's old school-festival performance has become a school legend. Kotono chooses to stand on the same type of stage with Nagisa, not to reproduce Mana but to create something of their own that can equal or surpass the inherited memory.

This is another application of the already-governing rule:

> **inheritance is invitation, not jurisdiction.**

## 5.6 Cross-role fertilization

Yu's acting experience, doujin imagination, fandom literacy, idol experience, and student-council work all become usable in the festival musical.

Admitted term:

> **`CROSS_ROLE_FERTILIZATION`**

Plural roles need not remain sealed. They can strengthen one another without collapsing into a single master identity.

## 5.7 Historical comparison

Historical Tier-H triage already called this event very important for **Yu as protagonist-maker** and **Kotono reclaiming ordinary student belonging**.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`**

The prior claim remains correct. V2 adds the mechanism and the warning:

- Yu's protagonist-making is a civic theory of office;
- studenthood is a time-bounded life domain;
- Kotono's estrangement is partly `FAME_DISTANCE`;
- plural-role authorship can become `ROLE_ACCUMULATION_RISK`.

# 6. Historical-series claims revised by the tranche

## 6.1 “IDOLY PRIDE is professional idolhood, not school-idol youth”

Historical synthesis correctly distinguished *IDOLY PRIDE* from school-idol narratives by emphasizing agencies, contracts, ranking systems, professional schedules, media work, and industrial stakes.

Transition:

> **`PRESERVE + QUALIFY`**

The series remains structurally a professional-idol work. E2-A3 adds an important limit:

> **Professional identity is not entitled to consume the characters' remaining ordinary or student life.**

The difference from a school-idol work is institutional form, not a judgment that school life is narratively inferior.

## 6.2 “Hoshimi's family model must survive scale”

Historical analysis already asked whether Hoshimi's intimate/family-like moral ecology could survive media scale, contracts, outside units, and institutional crisis.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN + PARTIALLY_RESOLVE`**

The national tour shows that Hoshimi does not merely survive by scaling the family metaphor. It develops **professional procedure**.

Therefore `OPEN-04` should no longer be framed as a live either/or between “family” and “institution.”

Current answer:

> **Hoshimi is ontologically a professional institution. “Home” or “family” names some relationships and affective functions inside it; it does not define the institution's full governance model.**

## 6.3 “Yu's danger is disappearing into usefulness and polish”

Historical TRINITY synthesis had already identified Yu's tendency to support, connect, and self-manage until her own needs become secondary.

Transition:

> **`STRENGTHEN`**

E2-A3 demonstrates that the same pattern can migrate across domains. Yu does not merely over-function inside TRINITYAiLE; she can accumulate idol, creator, student, civic, and production responsibilities because each one genuinely matters to her.

The problem is therefore not false vocation. It is **too many true vocations being permitted to become simultaneous obligations**.

# 11. Final E2-A3 synthesis

E2-A3 adds a second dimension to the project's core term **answerable interdependence**.

Earlier tranches focused heavily on whether relationships, units, managers, rivals, memories, and institutions could shape a person without acquiring permanent jurisdiction over her.

E2-A3 asks the same question of **vocation itself**.

The answer is:

> **A vocation can be central without being total. A family can be formative without being destiny. An institution can be intimate without being a family. Privacy can coexist with authenticity. Leadership can guide without monopolizing expertise. Several roles can coexist without becoming one identity—but only if their accumulated demands remain contestable.**

This makes the professional world of *IDOLY PRIDE* more convincing rather than less serious. Maturity is not proved by sacrificing every non-idol part of life to the stage. It is proved by building relationships and institutions capable of letting serious idol work coexist with the rest of a human life.
