# 1. Governing thesis

Series-level:

> **answerable interdependence**

Constitutional refinement:

> **ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM**

Existing mature refinements remain active:

- bounded evaluative jurisdiction;
- non-disposable standing;
- answerable succession and unit identity;
- non-totalizing vocation;
- answerable role constitution;
- authenticity as answerable construction.

E2-B2 adds:

> **RECIPROCAL_STANDING**

Definition:

> **A relationship or group structure in which asymmetries of age, skill, role, fame, or vulnerability can remain real while all affected parties retain standing to assert wants, contest methods, contribute care, and alter the shared form.**

Compact care rule:

> **Help is most legitimate when the person being helped remains a co-author of what the help is for.**

Reciprocity does not require symmetry.
