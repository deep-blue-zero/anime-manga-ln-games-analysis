# 8. Cross-source historical result

B2-B adds three durable umbrella refinements without inflating them into replacements for stronger mature sources.

### 8.1 `CARE_FORM_CAN_CHANGE_WITHOUT_RELATIONAL_WITHDRAWAL`

Care may become reversible, move to another domain, or be withdrawn in one form to recognize competence. None of those changes necessarily means abandonment.

### 8.2 `RELATIONAL_INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_VOCATIONAL_OWNERSHIP`

Family, mentors, peers, books, and inherited advice can shape a person without acquiring permanent ownership over the resulting judgment or vocation.

### 8.3 `ORDINARY_LIFE_AS_OPTION_SPACE`

School, university, hobbies, family work, former aspirations, and alternate professional pathways matter not only as rest from idolhood but as **unselected or parallel futures** that keep the person larger than the current role.

These refine `NON_TOTALIZING_VOCATION`, `PLURAL_ROLE_AUTHORSHIP`, `INFLUENCE_WITHOUT_DERIVATIVENESS`, and `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION` without displacing their stronger E1/E2 authority.
