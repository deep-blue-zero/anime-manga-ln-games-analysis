# Hoshimi Anime ↔ Game Expansion Audit — SUNNY PEACE Origin Addendum

## Purpose

This addendum records origin-derived provenance discovered after completion of the Hoshimi anime/game expansion audit. It does **not** overwrite the parent audit or the frozen anime endpoint.

> **Origin disclosure changes the retrospective model; it does not change the frozen information state of anime episodes in which these facts were not yet available.**

The addendum is required because the five SUNNY PEACE origin bundles materially clarify recruitment logic, Hoshimi institutional history, and the pre-Hoshimi causal chain behind Sakura’s arrival.

---

## A1. Chisa recruitment and separation provenance

**Source:** `origin_sun_001_chisa_s_memories`, especially `st-group-sun-01-01-04` and `st-group-sun-01-01-05`.

### Added fact

During Chisa’s audition, Makino explicitly worries that her dependence on Saki will leave her hidden in Saki’s shadow and asks internally whether she can proceed without relying on her sister.

After Chisa fails but discovers that she genuinely wants idolhood, Makino contacts her and asks whether she still wants to become an idol if she cannot remain in the same agency/group configuration as Saki. He scouts her only after she answers yes, while warning that Saki will receive priority and they likely will not share a group.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent audit’s bilateral-individuation finding remains valid but gains earlier provenance. The later SUN/MOON separation does not merely *happen* to force Chisa’s individuation; a concern about sister-dependence is already present in recruitment.

### Guardrail

Do not claim that Makino predicted the complete later Saki/Chisa arc or possessed a fixed final unit plan at the first audition. The evidence supports developmental risk recognition and a deliberate separation test.

## A2. Shizuku and explicit SUNNY formation logic

**Source:** `origin_sun_003_shizuku_s_memories`, especially `st-group-sun-01-03-05`.

### Added fact

Makino explicitly describes the intended new group as people with potential who cannot shine alone:

`素質はあるのに一人では輝けない……そういう子達を集めて、新しいグループを作りたい`

He gives the local complementary example of a girl who knows nothing about idols and a girl whose knowledge itself prevents movement:

`何も知らない子と、知っているが故に前に進めない子が手を組んで、一歩一歩進んでいく`

He also treats Shizuku’s fan knowledge as positive idol competence because she can understand and value fans unusually well.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent audit’s “formation partly experimental” finding is **strengthened**, not overturned. Experimentation occurs inside a more intentional developmental design than the Hoshimi retelling alone exposed.

Canonical refinement:

> **SUNNY PEACE is deliberately designed around complementary non-self-sufficiency: people with real potential whose isolated traits prevent sustainable forward movement.**

### Guardrail

This does not make Makino a therapist or omniscient character designer. It is recruitment architecture, not proof that he fully understands every member’s history or future.

## A3. Haruko is Hoshimi Production’s first idol

**Source:** `origin_sun_004_haruko_s_memories`, especially `st-group-sun-01-04-03`.

### Added fact

Saegusa tells Haruko:

`キミは星見プロの未来に必要な人材だ`

`キミが、星見プロのアイドル第一号だ`

Haruko is therefore not merely a pre-Mana veteran or a living bridge to the old agency. She is literally **Hoshimi Production’s first idol**.

### Institutional significance

Saegusa selects her partly because her combination of kindness toward others and refusal to give up may be exactly what future Hoshimi idols will need as support.

This means a major strand of Hoshimi’s collective ethos predates Mana’s recruitment. Mana becomes the agency’s miracle and public myth, but the first idol selection already contains a different institutional proposition: kindness and endurance can themselves be professional idol strengths.

## A4. Saegusa management accountability

**Source:** `origin_sun_004_haruko_s_memories`.

### Added fact

Saegusa later accepts responsibility for failing to create enough opportunities/work that drew out Haruko’s potential.

### Revision to parent audit

Career outcome must not be modeled as pure transparent merit. Hoshimi’s own management recognizes that producers shape whether an idol’s strengths become legible to the public.

Route this fact to:

- professional labor/industry ledger;
- manager/production ethics ledger;
- Haruko longitudinal ledger.

## A5. Haruko is a SUNNY design requirement, not veteran decoration

**Source:** `origin_sun_004_haruko_s_memories`, `st-group-sun-01-04-05`.

### Added fact

Makino places Haruko in the member list without waiting for her to ask, explaining:

`一人では輝けない彼女達を包み込んで、教え導いてあげられる人が、このグループには必要`

and:

`遙子さんの持つ経験と、優しさと、諦めない強さと、アイドルへの熱い想いが必要`

`遙子さんなしに、このグループはありえません`

### Revision to parent audit

Haruko’s senior/infrastructural role is not merely something SUNNY develops after formation. It is part of the initial design rationale.

This strengthens the line of institutional continuity between Saegusa’s first-idol selection and Makino’s later unit formation.

## A6. Sakura’s pre-Hoshimi heart-guidance trajectory

**Source:** `origin_sun_005_sakura_s_memories`, especially `st-group-sun-01-05-04` and `st-group-sun-01-05-05`.

### Added facts

After transplant, Sakura learns during rehabilitation to interpret a strong heartbeat as encouragement when she wants to give up. Two years later, while visiting Tomo’s grave, a heartbeat response to Haruko at Mana’s grave contributes to Sakura telling Haruko not to quit; Haruko decides to continue. Three years after transplant, bodily intuition leads Sakura toward Hoshimi Production, where Haruko mistakenly treats her as an audition candidate.

The story later states that without Haruko and Mana the future in which all of SUNNY PEACE gathers would not have existed.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent audit’s medical/information-order safeguards remain fully in force. This origin **does not newly disclose donor identity** and must not be cited as an explicit donor statement.

What it adds is the phenomenological history that makes Sakura’s later trust in heartbeat intuition psychologically intelligible.

Canonical refinement:

> **Sakura’s “heart oracle” is not born only from later Mana resemblance. Trusting bodily intuition had been adaptive within her rehabilitation and pre-Hoshimi life. The anime endpoint’s eventual self-authorship therefore requires her to revise a once-useful survival epistemology, not merely reject a superstition.**

## A7. Tomo anti-substitution provenance

**Source:** `origin_sun_005_sakura_s_memories`, `st-group-sun-01-05-05`.

### Added fact

Sakura receives Tomo’s completed `やってみたかったリスト` and immediately assumes she should fulfill Tomo’s wishes in her place. Tomo’s written message explicitly anticipates and rejects this. The wishes were Tomo’s; Sakura should pursue Sakura’s own desires. Remembering Tomo is encouraged, delegated identity is not.

### Cross-media significance

This is a pre-Mana local instance of the same ethical problem that later structures Sakura and Kotono’s relationship to Mana:

> **The dead may remain loved and causally present without acquiring authorship over the living person’s future.**

Route this to the later grief/memory/inheritance synthesis. Do not backfill it as conscious Mana theory during anime episodes where Sakura does not yet know the donor situation.

## A8. Net effect on the Hoshimi audit

No parent finding is overturned.

The addendum produces four principal upgrades:

1. **formation intentionality is stronger** — especially in Chisa, Shizuku, and Haruko recruitment;
2. **Hoshimi institutional history begins before Mana** in a philosophically meaningful sense through Haruko’s first-idol selection;
3. **production ethics become more explicit** through Saegusa’s accountability and Makino’s developmental recruitment;
4. **Sakura’s Hoshimi arrival has a longer embodied prehistory** than the anime/Hoshimi retelling alone can show.

The parent audit remains the canonical comparison of the frozen anime with `st-original-cmn`; this file is its controlled Phase-1B origin addendum.
