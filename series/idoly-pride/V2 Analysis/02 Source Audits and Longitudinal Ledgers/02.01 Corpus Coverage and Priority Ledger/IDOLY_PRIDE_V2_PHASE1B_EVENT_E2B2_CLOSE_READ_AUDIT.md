# 1. Tranche-level historical verdict

The source-native E2-B2 thesis is:

> **Mature interdependence requires reciprocal standing: people must be able to support one another without converting support into self-erasure, paternal ownership, or an obligation for the recipient to remain passive.**

Preferred umbrella term:

> `RECIPROCAL_STANDING`

Historical analysis had already seen most of the important local character movements:

- Chisa and Saki become healthier when both stop yielding and admit a desire to shine;
- Mei and Suzu mature when conflict becomes speakable and their temporary unit becomes genuine co-authorship;
- Sunny Peace circulates received encouragement back toward fans and local communities;
- Sumire's belonging in TRINITYAiLE is compatible with receiving help and remaining a challenger.

What historical analysis did **not** yet have was a single sufficiently precise cross-event account of the ethical mechanism. E2-B2 supplies that mechanism.

The strongest synthesis is therefore not “support is good” or “mutual support matters.” It is:

> **Support remains legitimate when the supported person retains standing to define the goal, answer the method, return care, and refuse being reduced to the object of another person's benevolence.**

This is a major `STRENGTHEN` operation on the series-wide `answerable interdependence` thesis.

It also qualifies reciprocity itself. E2-B2 repeatedly rejects the assumption that reciprocity requires symmetry. Older sister/younger sister, stronger dancer/weaker dancer, nationally popular idol/fan, and older/younger TRINITYAiLE member are not equal in every relevant dimension. The ethical demand is **equal standing within relevant authorship**, not fictional sameness.

# 2. `心紡ぎ合う輝きの競演` — historical transition

## 2.1 Historical claim preserved

The strongest historical analysis already described this event as the Shiraishi sisters learning to stop yielding to each other. It correctly identified that:

- both sisters are accustomed to support functions;
- both initially step back for the other's sake;
- the resulting limited unit becomes weak because neither claims enough space;
- Chisa's confrontation recovers Saki's own desire to shine;
- the mature unit consists of both sisters trying to shine at once rather than one becoming permanent support.

The older formulation that their healthiest sisterhood becomes **care-as-permission-to-shine** remains excellent.

Transition:

> `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`

## 2.2 What V2 adds

V2 makes four mechanisms explicit.

First, the initial failure is not ordinary dependency. It is `RECIPROCAL_SELF_ERASURE`: both people voluntarily accommodate the other until the relationship loses expressive force.

Second, Saki's competence as a balancing/support member does not establish her permanent preferred role. Her explicit admission that she wants Tsuki no Tempest's center makes this distinction unusually clear:

> `SUPPORT_COMPETENCE_WITHOUT_SUPPORT_DESTINY`

Third, the sister-role itself undergoes `SIBLING_ROLE_DETOTALIZATION`. “I am the older sister” remains meaningful, but it stops functioning as a total office whose duty is unilateral support.

Fourth, the repaired relationship becomes `CONFLICT_TOLERANT_AFFECTION`. Chisa had feared that saying everything they thought might damage the bond; the event demonstrates the opposite. The relationship becomes safer when honest disagreement no longer threatens its existence.

The event therefore gives a more precise formulation than “independence from one's sister”:

> **The Shiraishi sisters mature not by reducing attachment, but by making attachment compatible with mutual ambition and reciprocal correction.**

This is an `EXTENSION` and `STABILIZATION` of the historical reading, not a contradiction.

# 3. `迷走ピリオド 涼やかな青春` — historical transition

## 3.1 Historical claim substantially preserved

Historical Mei/Suzu analysis was already exceptionally strong. It correctly recognized that:

- Suzu assumes she should become the stabilizing/organizing core;
- Mei proves much more practically organized than expected;
- Suzu's heavily researched proposals repeatedly lose to Mei's proposals;
- Suzu experiences this as humiliation and fears Mei is holding back for her;
- Mana's memory gives Mei permission to express anger rather than convert all discomfort into cheer;
- the final limited-unit concept combines both members instead of maximizing one at the other's expense;
- their closeness becomes stronger because it can now survive conflict.

The historical phrase “partnership is not ‘I lead and you shine,’ nor ‘you lead and I disappear’; it is co-authorship” remains fully compatible with the prospective freeze.

Transition:

> `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`

## 3.2 One historical shorthand requires revision

Some earlier prose summarized the conflict as:

> **Suzu believes effort, study, and preparation should win; Mei keeps producing better-fitting ideas through intuition and emotional feel.**

This captures **Suzu's experience** of the conflict, but it risks becoming an objective binary that the source does not fully establish.

Mei is not simply effortless intuition. She remembers schedules, prior unit experience, practical production needs, fan imitation, costume direction, choreography, and joint concept formation. Her proposals may feel intuitive from Suzu's perspective, but they are still forms of thought and work.

The better V2 distinction is:

> `UNILATERAL_OTHER_OPTIMIZATION` versus `COAUTHORED_UNIT_SYNTHESIS`.

Suzu's problem is not that she values effort. It is that she defines seriousness partly as maximizing Mei, then assumes she can privately absorb the cost of catching up. Mei objects because this would make the unit comfortable for Mei while leaving Suzu to struggle alone.

Historical “effort versus intuition” is therefore:

> `REVISE_BY_PRECISION`

not rejected, but demoted from narrator-level diagnosis to **Suzu's phenomenology**.

## 3.3 Jealousy and anger as relationship information

Historical analysis was also right that Suzu's jealousy is not villainous and that Mei's anger becomes developmentally important.

V2 formalizes this carefully:

- `JEALOUSY_AS_AUTHORSHIP_SIGNAL` — in this event, jealousy identifies an unmet need for constitutive creative standing; this is not a universal claim that jealousy is healthy;
- `KINDNESS_ACCUMULATION_RISK` — Mei's habitual accommodation can conceal frustration until the relationship becomes harder to navigate;
- `TRUSTED_CONFLICT_CAPACITY` — a relationship becomes more mature when disagreement can occur without being interpreted as abandonment;
- `RECIPROCAL_VULNERABILITY_DISCLOSURE` — both members eventually admit hurt, loneliness, dependency, and affection.

The historical reading is therefore **strongly preserved**, while V2 gives it a more reusable ethical vocabulary.

# 4. `笑顔のSUNNY 繋げるPEACE` — historical transition

## 4.1 Historical Sunny thesis preserved

Historical analysis identified this event as the strongest event-level definition of Sunny Peace's social function: Sunny does not merely project cheerfulness but **circulates hope through fans, family, locality, and reciprocal gratitude**.

The historical Sunny synthesis similarly described the unit's performance relation as circular rather than vertical:

> audience supports idol → idol receives that feeling → idol returns it as warmth → audience is encouraged in turn.

E2-B2 strongly confirms this.

Transition:

> `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`

## 4.2 V2 adds audience authorship without granting audience sovereignty

The national-tour fan-dream project is more than generic fanservice. Fan wishes become structured inputs into Sunny Peace's work, and the idols explicitly understand themselves as returning dreams after having received dreams and support from fans.

Preferred terms:

- `RECIPROCAL_AUDIENCE_COAUTHORSHIP`
- `CIRCULATING_DREAM_RECIPROCITY`
- `GENERATIVE_AUDIENCE_CIRCULATION`

However, `coauthorship` here must remain bounded. Fans contribute desires, meanings, memories, and destinations for performance. They do **not** thereby acquire full professional or personal jurisdiction over the idols.

This is important in light of E1-C's audience-coauthorship work and E2-B1's audience relational reframing.

## 4.3 Sunny's warmth now has scale externalities

Historical summaries emphasized the positive circulation of warmth. E2-B2 adds a necessary qualification: national popularity changes the surrounding environment.

The tour produces:

- large crowds;
- local tourism and “sacred site” effects;
- business consequences;
- media attention;
- inconvenience and loss of ordinary spatial anonymity.

Preferred term:

> `FAME_SCALE_EXTERNALITY`

This is not a refutation of Sunny's warmth. It is a professional-scale correction to any reading where popularity is treated as costless emotional good.

Transition:

> `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN + QUALIFY`

## 4.4 Haruko and Sakura open-question pressure

Haruko meeting a younger aspiring idol who chose the profession because she admired Haruko materially strengthens Haruko's present-tense legitimacy as an intergenerational professional model.

Preferred term:

> `INTERGENERATIONAL_IDOL_MODELING`

This advances `OPEN-14`, but does **not** settle the economics or bodily feasibility of Haruko's very-long-horizon career.

Sakura's promise to a hospitalized fan that she will keep singing so Sunny can continue giving strength is likewise important. The text supports:

> `PRESENTLY_AUTHORED_CONTINUATION_COMMITMENT`

It does not prove that Sakura has fixed her entire future around permanent idol activity. `OPEN-01` therefore advances but remains open.

# 5. `迎春！翼に込める躍進の一念` — historical transition

## 5.1 Historical core preserved

The historical priority map called this an essential TRINITYAiLE/Sumire story because Sumire's “equality wound” becomes explicit: receiving support does not make her a lesser member, and challenge need not be surrendered in order to accept care.

That core survives.

Transition:

> `PRESERVE + STRENGTHEN`

## 5.2 Equality needs a sharper definition

The older shorthand can sound as though the solution is simply to stop treating Sumire “as a child.” E2-B2 shows that this is too coarse.

Rui and Yu possess legitimate reasons to think differently about Sumire's training load and injury risk because she is younger and still growing. The text does not require them to pretend those facts are irrelevant.

The ethical problem appears when Sumire experiences the safeguards as evidence that the others do not truly recognize her standing inside TRINITYAiLE.

Preferred terms:

- `CARE_RECEPTION_GAP`
- `DEVELOPMENTAL_ASYMMETRY_WITHOUT_STATUS_SUBORDINATION`
- `AGE_AWARE_EQUAL_STANDING`

Current rule:

> **Equal membership does not require identical risk assumptions. Developmental safeguards become paternalistic when they erase the younger member's standing to understand, contest, and author the goal.**

The historical equality formulation is therefore:

> `REVISE_BY_DISTINCTION + STRENGTHEN`

## 5.3 The story does not validate every risk Sumire chooses

Sumire's choice of the hardest marathon is partly a genuine aspiration and partly an attempt to prove that she is not a child.

Preferred term:

> `STATUS_PROVING_RISK_TAKING`

The event's resolution should not be generalized into “letting her continue is always the respectful answer.” Her fall, disorientation, and the members' anxiety keep the risk question active.

Makino's eventual mode is especially useful: he checks the injury and then accompanies her rather than immediately taking control of the goal.

Preferred term:

> `ACCOMPANIMENT_WITHOUT_GOAL_SEIZURE`

Placed beside the Mana overwork memory in `迷走ピリオド`, E2-B2 now gives two contrasting intervention cases:

1. after actual collapse and a too-rapid attempt to return, forceful confrontation is justified;
2. during a difficult but still actively chosen challenge where the performer remains capable, accompaniment and monitoring may preserve more authorship than unilateral extraction.

This supports:

> `PROPORTIONAL_CARE_INTERVENTION`

Definition:

> **Legitimate intervention intensity should rise with credible risk, impairment, and loss of decision capacity rather than caregiver anxiety alone.**

`OPEN-03` is therefore **strongly advanced but remains open**. The corpus still lacks a universal threshold rule for acute medical, safety, or incapacity cases.

## 5.4 Rank is real but non-sovereign

Rui's failed glider record attempt is another clean test of metric sovereignty. The record matters; the team tries seriously to win. But when the wing breaks, Rui still treats the collaborative influence and bonds created through the attempt as valuable beyond first place:

> meaningful influence and bonds can be more gratifying than the number-one result.

Preferred term:

> `MEANINGFUL_SUCCESS_BEYOND_RANK`

This is `STABILIZATION` of the already-strong `OPEN-07` position: scores and ranks are real professional information, but they do not possess sovereign authority over meaning.

## 5.5 Care becomes reversible

The event ends by reversing the presumed care direction. Sumire, previously the younger member being protected, becomes the person worried about Rui and Yu and offers to remain with them.

Preferred term:

> `RECIPROCAL_CARE_REVERSIBILITY`

This is the cleanest confirmation of the tranche-level `RECIPROCAL_STANDING` thesis. Sumire does not become equal by ceasing to need help. She becomes legibly equal because the relationship permits her to **receive and return care while retaining challenge and voice**.

# 8. E2-B2 synthesis delta

E2-B2 strengthens the mature series model in a way that is subtle but broadly reusable.

The series does not oppose dependence with independence. It repeatedly opposes **unanswerable dependence** with **reciprocal standing**.

That distinction explains why:

- Saki and Chisa become healthier while remaining intensely sisterly;
- Mei and Suzu become closer by learning to fight rather than by eliminating friction;
- Sunny Peace can be supported by fans while also returning something that changes fans' lives;
- Sumire can remain younger and sometimes need age-aware protection without becoming a subordinate member of TRINITYAiLE.

Current formulation:

> **INTERPRETATION — Mature care does not ask “how can I make myself unnecessary?” It asks “how can I help while preserving the other person's standing to want, answer, contribute, and change the relationship?”**

This should be carried into later relationship, managerial, institutional, and full-series synthesis work.
