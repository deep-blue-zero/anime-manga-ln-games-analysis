# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — PHASE 1B STELLAR MAIN STORY AUDIT

## 0. Executive audit result

The Stellar main story has now been read sequentially from `stellar_001_to_soar_high` through `stellar_011_all_my_youth`, covering 56 granular stories and 6,301 utterances. The complete source-native finding set was written and SHA-256 frozen before the historical Stellar analysis was deliberately opened.

The most important result is that Stellar should not be reduced to either of the two broad summaries that its tournament structure invites:

1. **"TRINITYAiLE finds its emotional core and wins the VENUS Grand Prix."**
2. **"Hoshimi becomes a family whose different units sharpen one another."**

Both are substantially true. Neither is sufficient.

The stronger V2 formulation is:

> **Stellar is the main story's system-wide audit of reciprocal dependence after success. It asks how idols, rivals, managers, units, and institutions can support one another without turning support into control, gratitude into debt, competition into erasure, or present intensity into destruction of the future self.**

The arc generalizes several lessons previously earned locally:

- BIG4 taught Tsuki no Tempest that attachment becomes healthy when it is reciprocal and contestable rather than sovereign.
- Tokyo taught Hoshimi that legitimate institutions enlarge the space for authored professional choice rather than treating idols as instruments.
- Stellar brings those principles into conflict with **bodily finitude, public success, championship pressure, gratitude, rivalry, family, and management risk**.

Its largest movement is therefore:

> **protection -> supported risk**  
> **technical perfection -> authored purpose**  
> **victory as proof -> competition as reciprocal development**  
> **inherited miracle -> living continuation**

The historical V1 Stellar analysis was strong and is mostly **CONFIRMED** or **STRENGTHENED**. Its principal weaknesses were not wrong plot understanding but rhetorical compression at exactly the points where the primary story remains ethically unsettled. The most important corrections are:

- Sakura's semifinal defeat creates necessary recovery time, but should not be described as proving that the risky performance decision was justified or as literally saving her from imminent death.
- Makino's final management position is not a solved formula of "intervene when bodies are at risk, otherwise trust." It is **risk-aware co-authorship under unresolved uncertainty**.
- TRINITYAiLE versus IIIX is not warm feeling defeating cold technique. Both units are technically elite; IIIX deliberately experiments with an *imperfect* stage precisely because calculation alone has become a ceiling.
- `恩返し` is better modeled as **reciprocal gratitude converted into forward motion** than as debt-service or obligation to supporters.
- `青春全部かけて` must be read together with the same ending's explicit twenty- and thirty-year horizon. The arc celebrates total commitment without canonizing self-annihilating presentism.

These corrections materially improve the eventual series synthesis because Stellar is the point at which *IDOLY PRIDE* comes closest to staging a direct disagreement between **living fully now** and **protecting the conditions for a future life**.

# 2. Sequential audit

## 2.1 `stellar_001_to_soar_high` — when success becomes a public story

### Primary result

The documentary opening changes Hoshimi's narrative position. Hoshimi is no longer merely an inside space composed of office, dorm, rehearsal room, and local history. It has become an object of national public narration.

Characters describe Hoshimi as `帰って来る場所` and its people as `家族のような仲間達`. These are meaningful self-descriptions, but the V2 pass retains a guardrail: Hoshimi should not be flattened into literal family. IIIX's continued transactional and adversarial behavior demonstrates that the institution can create belonging without emotional uniformity.

The VENUS Grand Prix raises the external stakes, while TRINITYAiLE's desire to fight Sunny Peace creates the first internal test. Makino's hesitation is important because his post-Mana protective ethic begins turning into risk avoidance. The fear is not merely "we may lose" but "a serious defeat could break someone."

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED**

The historical reading correctly called the documentary a transformation of Hoshimi into **public myth** and correctly observed that success itself can become an identity trap. V2 strengthens this by tying the public-myth problem directly to later Sakura burden and to Hoshimi's emerging institutional responsibility for managing the consequences of its own success.

### Correction

The old shorthand "Hoshimi becomes a family" is useful only with qualification. The stronger formulation is:

> **Hoshimi becomes a chosen professional home whose members can use family-like language without requiring one common affective or vocational style.**

## 2.2 `stellar_002_nemophila` — protection becomes distrust when it preempts the idol's answer

### Primary result

DoriKyun's post-BIG4 role changes immediately. Saegusa has not recruited them into Hoshimi; he has told them not to retire because the industry still needs them.

Their criticism of Makino is unusually direct:

- `心配も度が過ぎると害悪です`
- `信じて見守る`

Haruko supplies the indispensable counter-history: the present idols are not Mana. Makino's failure is not caring too much in the abstract. It is importing the risk model of an irreversible past into decisions for living people who have their own capacities and reasons.

The Sunny Peace/TRINITYAiLE live then disproves the obvious training solution. TRINITYAiLE performs extremely well—apparently at or near a self-best—and is still crushed. Miho's `核になるもの` diagnosis therefore matters because technique has already reached a level at which merely repeating the same effort cannot answer the problem.

Sakura's reaction to victory opens the other half of Stellar: she has begun converting BIG4 recognition into debt. She treats the existence of defeated opponents and public expectations as reasons she must continue winning and must not admit burden.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED**

The historical reading correctly identified Makino's Mana-shaped overprotection and the significance of DoriKyun's "trust and watch" correction. It also correctly identified Sakura's increasingly hollow victory smile.

### V2 qualification

`信じて見守る` must not be translated into managerial passivity. Later Stellar scenes will show that watching rightly includes medical consultation, staffing, workload reduction, contingency planning, and the willingness to say no. The phrase means **do not confiscate the idol's authorship merely to calm the manager's fear**.

## 2.3 `stellar_003_to_fly_higher` — responsibility is redistributed before performance changes

### Primary result

TRINITYAiLE's first breakthrough is social rather than technical. Rui overtrains because she believes the center/leader must carry failure; Yu and Sumire worry that they failed to support her. The unit instead reaches a shared account: three people win and lose together.

Nagisa becomes a particularly important cross-arc witness. Having learned through BIG4 that care for Kotono cannot mean replacing her own selfhood, she can tell Yu that every member must be able to pull her own weight.

Sumire and Suzu provide the arc's most important anti-grandiosity check. When Sumire wonders whether she is unsuited to idol work, she returns to ordinary love—singing with Rui, dancing with Yu, penlights, cheers, making people smile. Suzu treats continued love and desire as sufficient evidence that the vocation remains real.

TRINITYAiLE's hiatus then becomes a conscious acceptance of professional risk. Crucially, Hoshimi's peers cover work and obligations. "Friends" here is not merely a feeling. It becomes operational infrastructure.

Meanwhile, Sakura converts the hiatus into more debt: Sunny Peace must work and win on behalf of those who stepped away.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED**

The historical account correctly understood that Hoshimi can be such a good home that TRINITYAiLE may need temporary distance to grow. V2 strengthens the institutional side: the hiatus is only practicable because the agency and peers absorb its professional consequences.

### V2 refinement

The correct contrast is not "home versus independence." It is:

> **A legitimate home can make temporary distance survivable.**

That is a more robust institutional claim.

## 2.4 `stellar_004_hoshimi_s_festival` — culture cannot substitute for labor governance

### Primary result

Hoshimi Festival demonstrates the agency's plural authorship better than any prior main-story block. LizNoir, Sunny Peace, Tsuki, IIIX, staff, and mixed relationships all contribute different skills and motives.

The event validates Hoshimi as a place where contribution does not require personality convergence. Fran and Chisa can create; Kana can exploit publicity; Nagisa can design programs; LizNoir can participate from pride; IIIX can remain self-interested.

The festival does help Sakura recover spontaneous enjoyment—but she collapses immediately after it.

That ending is methodologically important. If the arc ended only on collective gratitude and `Gemstones`, an analyst might overstate "Hoshimi culture heals Sakura." The collapse refuses that answer.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED**

The historical analysis correctly called the festival Hoshimi's attempt to heal through culture and correctly recognized IIIX's participation without sentimental domestication.

V2 strengthens the labor conclusion:

> **Belonging and joy can protect meaning; they cannot repeal workload, recovery requirements, or bodily finitude.**

This principle must feed directly into Document 11, `PROFESSIONAL_IDOLHOOD_LABOR_MONEY_AND_INDUSTRY`.

## 2.5 `stellar_005_we_are_the_sun` — Sunny Peace distinguishes inheritance of ethics from inheritance of identity

### Primary result

The doctor diagnoses overwork rather than a new transplant problem. Sakura nevertheless repeats one of Mana's most dangerous professional propositions: the audience may only have one chance, therefore the idol must not lightly relinquish a stage.

This is a major correction to any synthesis that treats the Mana/Sakura problem as solved once Sakura establishes that her heart and voice do not give Mana jurisdiction over her selfhood.

Stellar shows a subtler inheritance route:

> **Sakura does not become Mana's identity; she risks inheriting Mana's self-consuming audience ethic.**

Sunny Peace's answer is collective burden distribution. The four members first attempt to work without Sakura and themselves become overloaded. The solution is neither "Sakura must do everything" nor "the other four can simply replace her." The explicit formulation is that the `重荷` belongs to all five; pain can be divided and joy multiplied.

The day-off material and Nemophila scene matter because they recover ordinary friendship as part of idol sustainability rather than as time stolen from the profession.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED**

V1 strongly recognized "shared sunlight" and correctly treated Sakura's collapse as a repetition of Mana's dangerous self-consuming tendency.

V2 adds an important distinction:

- **Identity repetition:** largely rejected.
- **Ethical/professional repetition:** still possible and presently dangerous.

That distinction should become canonical.

## 2.6 `stellar_006_will_of_the_underdogs` — DoriKyun's philosophy mutates without becoming gentle

### Primary result

Saegusa's intervention forces DoriKyun to examine the limit of pure elimination. If the strong only remove the weak, the industry does not necessarily become richer; cultivating stalled talent can increase the level of the field.

DoriKyun's technical diagnosis of TRINITYAiLE is decisive: at the level of singing/dancing/performance technique, the group may already be superior to Sunny/Tsuki and possibly competitive with LizNoir/IIIX. Repetition therefore cannot be assumed to be productive merely because it is hard.

The distinction between real effort and `惰性` is one of Stellar's most useful professional claims.

The family clue appears, but the text deliberately does not let `家族` become a complete answer. The three have different family wounds and motives. Rui's father relationship is the most unresolved.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / RECONTEXTUALIZED**

The historical phrase "DoriKyun evolves from destroyers to brutal cultivators" remains a good **INTERPRETATION**. V2 preserves the key guardrail: this does not mean DoriKyun adopts Hoshimi's care ethic. It expands its definition of how predatory pressure can serve entertainment.

### V2 correction

The old formulation "warmth without pressure can become comfort; pressure without warmth becomes destruction; true growth may require both" is rhetorically useful but too symmetrical if treated as a general law. TRINITYAiLE needed DoriKyun's specific pressure at this point; the text does not establish that every idol needs humiliation or adversarial coaching.

## 2.7 `stellar_007_repaying_kindness` — gratitude becomes a core when it remains plural

### Primary result

DoriKyun's "荒療治" makes each TRINITYAiLE member expose a vulnerable family register:

- Sumire asks Asakura for a childlike souvenir.
- Yu must tolerate embarrassment rather than hiding behind safe competence.
- Rui must call Asakura `パパ`.

Rui's reconciliation with Asakura is one of the main story's strongest adult/child institutional scenes. The problem is not simply that Asakura did not care. He cared but attempted to preserve professional fairness by suppressing fatherhood and assumed Rui would understand the boundary. Rui correctly identifies that silence as a failure of communication.

The unit's breakthrough is then explicit:

- `私達は、私達だけじゃ高く飛べない`
- `支えてくれた人達の数だけ形の違う恩返しを`

TRINITYAiLE accepts that excellence is socially produced. Their answer is not to become less ambitious, but to transform received support into a performance capable of returning something to supporters.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / QUALIFIED**

The earlier formulation "gratitude as wings" is excellent and should be retained.

However, V1 sometimes glossed `恩返し` through the language of **debt** and **obligation**. V2 recommends a narrower governing formulation:

> **reciprocal gratitude converted into propulsion**

The difference matters. Debt-service can imply that supporters acquire ownership of the idol. The source instead emphasizes many differently shaped forms of giving back while preserving the idols' own goal of flying higher.

### Makino historical claim — **QUALIFIED**

V1's observation that Makino prepared new costumes because he trusted the group is confirmed. The stronger lesson is not passive faith but **preparatory belief**: he cannot create their answer, but he can build infrastructure ready to receive it.

## 2.8 `stellar_008_opening_of_grand_prix` — competition becomes a language of gratitude

### Primary result

TRINITYAiLE returns transformed. It chooses Tsuki as a pre-Grand-Prix opponent partly to repay Suzu and Nagisa for earlier support. The live battle itself becomes an act of recognition.

The group defeats Tsuki narrowly. Rui refuses to translate that single result into a permanent hierarchy; Suzu and Nagisa accept the score and demand another match.

This is one of the clearest main-story examples of **rivalry without erasure** after BIG4.

The Grand Prix speeches then stage plural idol philosophies without reducing them to a single doctrine:

- TRINITYAiLE: deliver `想い`, become the protagonist.
- IIIX: polished public dignity plus `絶対勝利`.
- DoriKyun: explicit rejection of friendly-competition rhetoric.
- LizNoir: four-person continuation of a formerly two-person dream.
- Tsuki: experiences, including pain, become nourishment; carry those unable to stand there.
- Sunny Peace: `大好きの頂点`, the summit of love.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED**

The historical reading correctly called the tournament a **constellation** in which each group carries a different answer to idolhood.

### V2 refinement

Do not turn the bracket into a simplistic philosophical elimination tournament. A victory is still a scored competitive result mediated by audience response and performance conditions. The themes explain why the confrontations matter; they do not convert every score into proof that one philosophy is morally superior.

## 2.9 `stellar_009_challenge_for_friend` — IIIX experiments with non-optimality

### Primary result

DoriKyun's account of IIIX confirms a highly rationalized live-battle model: if raw ability is similar, IIIX's scoring optimization is expected to create an advantage.

Rui then thanks Miho for the earlier `核` criticism. This is the new TRINITYAiLE ethic in action: even painful rivalry can become something worth returning through performance.

Miho's decision is the crucial IIIX development. She deliberately attempts:

> `スリクス初の――完璧ではないステージ`

The three members compete *inside* the performance rather than suppressing individual collision for exact synchronization. The stage becomes less optimized but more volatile and personally expressive.

Both units achieve self-best scores; TRINITYAiLE narrowly wins.

Miho does not abandon the score. She still says results matter. But she also concedes that if the audience enjoyed the stage, the loss was not meaningless. Fran and Kana immediately treat defeat as information for another attempt.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / QUALIFIED**

The older "diamond fractures correctly" formulation is a strong metaphor and survives as interpretation.

The older shorthand **"gratitude defeats calculation"** should be qualified. It creates a binary the primary source itself has already destabilized:

- TRINITYAiLE is technically elite.
- IIIX consciously introduces `想い`, internal rivalry, and imperfect individuality into its own performance model.

The better formulation is:

> **TRINITYAiLE's newly articulated purpose narrowly defeats an IIIX that is itself experimenting beyond pure optimization.**

### IIIX endpoint

This is not friendship conversion. The trio's hostile intimacy remains intact. Internal rivalry becomes a productive source of differentiation while `絶対勝利` persists as an organizing ambition.

## 2.10 `stellar_010_miraculous_beat` — Sakura's miracle becomes an ambiguity, not a doctrine

### LizNoir

LizNoir explicitly describes its present four-person form as a miracle that requires exactly these four people. Ai and Kokoro are not junior attachments to an essentially Rio/Aoi unit; they are constitutive members.

After losing narrowly to Sunny Peace, LizNoir refuses finality. The unit has produced its best stage and still sees growth ahead. The horizon moves toward the world rather than collapsing around this tournament.

Historical V1's reading of rebuilt four-person pride is **CONFIRMED**.

### Sakura's health

The doctor states:

- Sakura is healthy enough for ordinary life.
- idol work has accumulated a large bodily load.
- proper rest and avoidance of overexertion should be sufficient.
- another collapse from overexertion could, in the worst case, require retirement from idol work.

Sakura explicitly says she cannot promise not to push herself because the Grand Prix matters.

During the LizNoir match, her body improves dramatically after an unstable period. The story permits Sakura to wonder whether Mana caused a miracle. Kotono rejects a simple supernatural answer: Mana would not interfere in a fair fight; Sakura's voice and heart are Sakura's; the performance may reflect Sakura's accumulated work and desire.

### Epistemic freeze

The causal status of the sudden recovery is **CONFLICT / AMBIGUITY**.

Do not canonize either:

- "Mana supernaturally powered Sakura," or
- "nothing supernatural occurred."

The stronger narrative movement is that Sakura no longer expects Mana to act as a rescuing agent. Mana can remain witness without retaining jurisdiction.

### Rui/Sakura argument

Rui uses the candle metaphor: Sakura appeared to be shining like a flame immediately before burning out. Sakura admits the medical warning but refuses withdrawal. Her argument is existential:

- the future is uncertain;
- there may not be another identical chance;
- she wants to do what she wants before it becomes impossible;
- she wants to live fully now;
- she wants to compete with people she loves and respects.

Rui's counterargument is future-oriented:

- there will be more opportunities;
- irreversible damage would destroy those future opportunities;
- friendship creates standing to intervene.

This is a **real normative collision**, not a scene where one side is obviously stupid.

### Historical V1 status — **CONFIRMED / STRENGTHENED**

V1 correctly recognized that Sakura's self-consuming sun logic is the arc's most serious danger. V2 makes the conflict more explicit: **present authorship and future authorship can point in opposite directions.**

## 2.11 `stellar_011_all_my_youth` — supported autonomy wins no philosophical finality

### Rui discloses the risk

Rui tells Makino. Yu and Sumire support the disclosure once informed. This is important because the act can be read as paternalistic betrayal or responsible friendship. The text does not erase the tension.

### Makino and Sunny Peace deliberate

Makino acknowledges his past overprotection. Sunny Peace asks him not to simply prohibit effort, but to:

- `見守って`
- `支えて`

The final decision is that Sakura will perform.

### Safeguards

The decision is explicitly supported by professional risk controls:

- Makino consults Sakura's treating physician remotely.
- Sakura receives present-condition clearance.
- an emergency-response arrangement with the physician is established.
- staff are informed.
- the other four Sunny Peace members commit to supporting Sakura.
- Makino commits to doing everything he can to make safe maximal performance possible.

This matters enormously for the governance reading. The scene is **not** a simple triumph of desire over medicine.

### The unresolved remainder

The earlier warning has not vanished. Sakura still says:

> `全てのステージが、私にとってのファイナル`

and frames every stage as one she cannot surrender.

That ethic remains dangerous if universalized. The text mitigates the current risk through clearance and support; it does not establish that every future stage deserves the same answer.

### Semifinal outcome

Sunny Peace posts an extraordinary score. TRINITYAiLE narrowly wins.

Sakura is disappointed but does not interpret the loss as proof of moral or personal failure. She can say that she expressed everything she had.

This is a major maturity marker. Earlier Stellar Sakura treats victory as debt. The semifinal permits **full commitment without victory becoming the sole proof that the commitment was meaningful**.

### Historical V1 claim: "the loss saved Sakura" — **WEAKENED / QUALIFIED**

DoriKyun explicitly notes that defeat gives Sakura a recovery period and, in that practical sense, calls the outcome fortunate. That is source-supported dialogue.

However, the historical rhetoric that Sunny Peace's defeat "saves Sakura from burning herself to death in real time" is too strong as governing analysis.

Primary evidence establishes:

- accumulated physical damage;
- a serious warning about possible forced retirement after another overexertion collapse;
- Rui's perception that Sakura looked like she was burning her life;
- present medical clearance before the semifinal;
- professional safeguards;
- no catastrophic event during the semifinal;
- the practical benefit that defeat creates rest.

It does **not** establish that Sakura was imminently dying, nor that the favorable result retroactively proves the decision to compete was ethically correct.

The correct classification is:

> **The loss contingently reduces subsequent exposure and creates recovery time. It does not convert a risky decision into a retrospectively necessary or validated one.**

### Final: TRINITYAiLE vs Tsuki no Tempest

TRINITYAiLE wins the VENUS Grand Prix against Tsuki no Tempest.

This is narratively apt because the expanded Hoshimi ecosystem, rather than only the original sun/moon pair, now produces the champion. TRINITYAiLE's path passes through IIIX, Sunny Peace, and Tsuki.

But `st-main-cmn-03-01-53` has missing processed live asset `adv-live-main-cmn-03-01-53`. Therefore this textual pass freezes the outcome and stated reactions while deferring detailed formal claims about the final live.

Historical language such as "white wings pass through sun and moon" is retained only as **INTERPRETATION**, not audiovisual fact.

### Award and long horizon

Hoshimi's multiple units receive a special award. This is strong evidence of the institution's public emergence, but not proof that every Hoshimi policy or philosophy has been vindicated.

The ending then explicitly rejects tournament finality:

- Rui says winning does not mean they reached the absolute summit.
- Sakura says titles/rankings do not change the challenger's feeling onstage.
- Kotono calls them still developing.
- Rio imagines the ideal idol as perhaps twenty or thirty years away.
- Fran jokes that thirty years would normally mean retirement.
- Rui, Rio, Sakura, and others imagine continuing while passion, audience, excitement, and desire remain.
- Kotono closes: `青春全部かけて、アイドルの最高峰へ！`

This combination is essential.

### Historical V1 status — **STRENGTHENED / QUALIFIED**

V1 correctly read `All My Youth` as refusal of finality and continuation beyond the championship.

V2 strengthens the temporal counterweight:

> **"All my youth" is not equivalent to "consume the future for the present." The same ending imagines idol life across decades.**

That tension should remain active in the final synthesis rather than being resolved into a motivational slogan.

### Epilogue

The three unknown girls who see Hoshimi and imagine themselves becoming idols, followed by the next-generation audition, complete the inheritance movement.

Mana's original miracle has not been reproduced as another Mana. It has become an ecology capable of generating new desires in people who have not yet entered the story.

The historical phrase:

> **"Mana's miracle has finally become a sky."**

is retained as a strong **INTERPRETATION**.

# 3. Character and unit endpoint audit

## 3.1 Sakura Kawasaki — **STRENGTHENED, ethically unresolved**

### What is now established

- BIG4 responsibility pushed Sakura toward self-silencing and overwork.
- Her Mana inheritance recurs at the level of audience-oriented professional ethics, not just heart/voice identity.
- Sunny Peace explicitly redistributes the burden across five people.
- Sakura can accept support without ceasing to be the center or losing ambition.
- She can lose a major tournament match without treating the loss as invalidation of the stage.
- She does not expect Mana to rescue her causally.

### What remains unresolved

Sakura's proposition that every stage is effectively a final can become a durable ethic only if the institution continues placing limits around it. The text does not fully solve how many times the present can be privileged before the future is structurally destroyed.

**Canonical V2 formulation:**

> **Sakura's mature Stellar problem is not learning whether to live fully. It is learning whether full present commitment can remain compatible with preserving a future self who will still be able to choose.**

---

## 3.2 Makino Kouhei — **QUALIFIED maturation**

Makino's main-story endpoint is more sophisticated than either control or laissez-faire.

He learns:

1. protection can become overprotection when it preempts another person's authored answer;
2. trust does not abolish risk governance;
3. management can prepare infrastructure without manufacturing the idol's internal reason;
4. bodies, schedules, staff, medical advice, and contingency planning remain managerial responsibilities;
5. disagreement with an idol does not automatically authorize confiscation of the idol's choice;
6. neither does autonomy erase professional duty.

**Preferred formulation:**

> **Makino practices risk-aware co-authorship: protect the conditions of choice, make consequences legible, build safeguards, and remain willing to intervene, while refusing to turn fear into jurisdiction over the idol's entire life.**

Historical V1 sometimes treated this as a solved mature formula. V2 keeps it **provisional**, because Sakura's case remains genuinely difficult.

---

## 3.3 TRINITYAiLE — **STRENGTHENED**

TRINITYAiLE's Stellar architecture is:

> elite technique -> ceiling -> responsibility redistribution -> hiatus -> vulnerability -> family confrontation -> `恩返し` -> transformed performance -> championship without finality

The group's answer to "what is an idol?" is now source-explicit enough to freeze:

> **An idol receives support and turns it into a performance that returns something different to each supporter while flying toward a view they can share.**

This is gratitude without servitude and dependence without loss of ambition.

Rui's father reconciliation also prevents "professional fairness" from becoming an excuse for relational silence.

---

## 3.4 Sunny Peace — **STRENGTHENED**

Sunny Peace's mature Stellar constitution is not "Sakura plus supporters." It is an explicitly five-part system in which burden, risk, joy, and performance correction circulate.

`五つのピース` is therefore organizational as well as symbolic.

The unit still values winning. Shared sunlight is not retreat from competition.

---

## 3.5 Tsuki no Tempest — **CONFIRMED post-BIG4 constitution**

Stellar does not reopen Tsuki's constitutional crisis. Instead, it demonstrates the durability of the BIG4 refoundation:

- members can compete with and lose to friends without unit destabilization;
- Suzu can recognize Sumire as a top idol and still demand a rematch;
- Tsuki reaches the Grand Prix final;

# 4. Institutional audit — Hoshimi after Stellar

Stellar makes Hoshimi Production the broadest institutional protagonist of the game-era main story so far.

The strongest evidence is not the special award by itself. It is the cumulative capacity to support incompatible forms of idolhood:

- Sunny Peace's collective warmth;
- Tsuki's reciprocal disciplined rivalry;
- TRINITYAiLE's gratitude-driven elite ambition;
- LizNoir's adult pride and global horizon;
- IIIX's hostile professionalism;
- individual hiatus, overwork recovery, festival creation, medical contingency, and internal live battle.

The agency's main-story legitimacy is best modeled as:

> **plural professional infrastructure that allows idols to author different lives while remaining accountable to material reality and to one another.**

This is stronger than "Hoshimi is a family" and more precise than "Hoshimi is a good agency."

### Institutional counterevidence

The Sakura collapse is evidence against institutional complacency. Hoshimi's culture is excellent enough to generate belonging, but success still produces:

- excessive workload;
- public expectations;
- role pressure;
- internalized obligation;
- difficulty distinguishing devotion from overextension.

Therefore the special award should be interpreted as recognition of what Hoshimi has built—not certification that the institution has solved the ethics of professional idol labor.

# 5. Main thematic findings stabilized by Stellar

## 5.1 Dependence versus self-erasure

Stellar makes the series position increasingly clear:

> **Self-authorship does not mean self-sufficiency.**

The desirable state is not isolation but reciprocal dependence in which each participant remains capable of refusal, contribution, revision, and future change.

This is visible in:

- Sunny Peace's burden sharing;
- TRINITYAiLE's `私達だけじゃ高く飛べない`;
- Tsuki's stable reciprocal rivalry;
- IIIX's internal collision;
- Hoshimi's logistical support of hiatus and health;
- Makino's shift from preemptive protection to supported choice.

## 5.2 Gratitude without ownership

TRINITYAiLE's `恩返し` and Sakura's burden expose two different forms of response to what one has received.

Healthy gratitude:

> received support becomes a freely authored reason to create and return something.

Dangerous debt:

> because others supported or lost to me, I lose the right to admit limits or choose differently.

This distinction should become a major axis in the eventual thematic synthesis.

## 5.3 Competition without erasure

BIG4 established this morally. Stellar proves it at system scale.

The Grand Prix produces:

- winners who refuse terminal superiority;
- losers who retain meaningful futures;
- rematch demands;
- new experiments;
- global horizons;
- friendship expressed through full-force competition.

Competition remains real. Scores remain real. Losing is not cosmetically denied. The central rejection is that a score should possess **ontological jurisdiction** over whether a person, unit, or dream deserves to continue.

## 5.4 Miracle becomes interpretation rather than command

Sakura's heartbeat remains an intentionally unstable phenomenon. The narrative allows supernatural residue without allowing it to determine Sakura's choices.

Mana's role continues moving from causal sovereign to witness/history.

## 5.5 The body remains the veto that ideals cannot rhetorically abolish

Mana's origin already made the body the unresolved limit of miracle. Stellar returns to that problem explicitly through Sakura.

The series does not settle for:

> "follow your dream no matter what."

Nor does it settle for:

> "health professionals/adults should always make the decision."

It instead stages the hard zone where:

- medical knowledge;
- present condition;
- future risk;
- personal desire;
- team obligation;
- institutional duty;
- and uncertainty

must coexist.

## 5.6 Youth as duration, not only intensity

`青春全部かけて` could easily be read as glorification of consuming oneself for idolhood. The same ending resists that simplification by imagining ideal idolhood twenty or thirty years into the future and by having multiple adults/young adults imagine continuing as long as passion and audience remain.

The canonical tension is:

> **Give the work your youth—but build a form of work in which youth can become adulthood rather than ashes.**

This is an **INTERPRETATION**, but one strongly supported by the juxtaposition of Sakura's risk and the endpoint's longevity rhetoric.

# 11. Final tranche thesis

> **Stellar is the point at which IDOLY PRIDE stops treating dependence as an embarrassment that strength should overcome and instead asks how dependence can become reciprocal, chosen, professionally sustainable, and performance-generating.
