# Hoshimi Anime ↔ Game Expansion Audit — Tsuki no Tempest Origin Addendum

## Purpose

This addendum records origin-derived provenance discovered after the Hoshimi anime/game expansion audit was completed and after the television-anime endpoint had already been frozen.

It does **not** revise the prospective information state of Episodes 1–12 and does **not** silently rewrite the parent cross-media audit.

The governing rule remains:

> **Later disclosure may explain earlier behavior; it may not be treated as evidence that the earlier viewer or earlier analytical pass already possessed the explanation.**

The five Tsuki no Tempest origin bundles materially refine the causal history of Saki, Suzu, Mei, Nagisa, and Kotono and therefore require controlled routing back into the Hoshimi comparison layer.

---

## A1. Saki — competence has pre-Hoshimi moral provenance

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_001_saki_s_memories`, especially `st-group-moon-01-01-02` through `05`.

### Added fact

Saki's competent older-sister/honor-student presentation is not merely a stable personality package. After Chisa's near-drowning and repeated parental reliance, Saki internalizes the rules:

`優等生でないといけない`

`千紗を支え続けないといけない`

She has a real idol dream but treats that dream as incompatible with the role structure she believes love and reliability require.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent Hoshimi audit's reading of Saki as structured, responsible, and deeply implicated in Chisa's individuation is **strengthened** but gains a causal guardrail:

> **Saki's responsibility is not merely a virtue or a later leadership style; it is also the form through which she once restricted her own permission to want.**

### Routing

Send forward to:

- Saki longitudinal ledger;
- Saki/Chisa relationship ledger;
- labor/sustainability ledger;
- leadership/control ledger.

### Guardrail

Do not reclassify all Saki organization as pathology. Her origin resolution preserves planning, academic ambition, and care by making them chosen components of an integrated life.

## A2. Shiraishi sisters — bilateral sacrifice loop now has two-sided primary evidence

**Primary sources:**

- `origin_sun_001_chisa_s_memories`;
- `origin_moon_001_saki_s_memories`.

### Added fact

The two origins provide complementary causal accounts.

Chisa learns that spontaneous desire can frighten or burden Saki and therefore tries to become a `いい子` who creates less trouble.

Saki learns that loving Chisa means remaining a maximally reliable protector and therefore treats her own idol dream as something that may need to be relinquished.

Neither sister asks the other to erase herself.

### Canonical refinement

> **The Shiraishi dependency problem is a reciprocal sacrifice loop: both sisters interpret self-limitation as evidence of love, and each person's sacrifice becomes a reason the other continues sacrificing.**

### Cross-media significance

The anime/Hoshimi sister-individuation arc is therefore not adequately described as only "Chisa must become less dependent on Saki" or only "Saki must become less overprotective."

It is bilateral re-authoring of care.

## A3. Suzu — Mana fandom has a direct encounter and an anti-imitation causal core

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_002_suzu_s_memories`, especially `st-group-moon-01-02-02` through `05`.

### Added facts

Suzu's attachment to Mana includes:

1. witnessing Mana's Hoshimi Festival live under threatened cancellation/bad weather;
2. a brief direct post-live encounter in which Mana personally validates Suzu;
3. later using Mana's performances as emotional refuge during loneliness;
4. hearing Mana urge viewers not to lie to their own feelings and to walk on their own feet;
5. applying that advice first by telling her parents that she is lonely and wants more family time;
6. only after Mana's death deciding that she wants to become an idol who can push someone else's back the way Mana pushed hers.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent Hoshimi audit correctly treats Suzu as a major Mana-inheritance figure. The origin changes the causal sequence and therefore the interpretive weighting.

Canonical refinement:

> **Suzu's Mana inheritance begins as truthful self-disclosure and only later becomes vocational imitation. Her desire to "be like Mana" contains from the beginning a Mana-derived instruction to move on her own feet.**

### Guardrail

Do not collapse this into "Suzu was already fully self-authored." Her worship remains intense and successor-like. The point is that imitation and self-direction are structurally entangled from the start.

## A4. Suzu's family conflict is a persistence test, not simple anti-idol prohibition

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_002_suzu_s_memories`.

### Added fact

Suzu's mother explicitly treats idolhood as a legitimate and admirable profession. The objection is directed at Suzu's history of quitting difficult commitments and at her romanticized understanding of the labor behind Mana's image.

### Revision to parent audit

Suzu's flight to Hoshimi should not be reduced to "rebellious heiress escapes controlling parents." The parents are controlling in method, but the central narrative challenge is also substantive:

> **Can Suzu convert declaration into durable work?**

Later parental attendance/recognition functions as evidence that she eventually answered that challenge.

## A5. Mei — `本気` and the prehistory of Tsuki's affective regulation

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_003_mei_s_memories`, especially `st-group-moon-01-03-03` through `05`.

### Added facts

Mei's origin establishes:

- childhood social adaptation around unusual perceptions;
- broad adolescent social usefulness without one deeply chosen place;
- competitive cheer as a serious pre-idol performance practice;
- an explicit dispute over `本気` (serious/wholehearted commitment);
- Mei's synthesis that sustained effort and genuine enjoyment are both required;
- Suzu's promise of togetherness as the decisive particular bond behind Mei's choice of idolhood.

### Revision to parent audit

The Hoshimi retelling's Mei-as-intuition/social-solvent reading is **strengthened**, but the origin prevents the trait from being mistaken for simple unseriousness.

Canonical refinement:

> **Mei's lightness is partly a professional/relational competence: she has already learned that a performance culture can become self-defeating when seriousness destroys the capacity to enjoy and transmit the performance.**

### Guardrail

The origin does not explicitly classify all childhood unusual friends as ghosts. That belongs to later supernatural cross-checking.

## A6. Nagisa — Mana's request does not authorize coercion

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_004_nagisa_s_memories`, especially `st-group-moon-01-04-03` and `04`.

### Added fact

Mana asks Nagisa:

`これからもずっと、琴乃の味方でいてね`

Nagisa promises that she will.

However, Nagisa does not use the promise as authority to force Kotono into reconciliation. When trying to bring Kotono toward Mana's performance, she leaves the decision with Kotono.

### Revision to parent audit

This materially strengthens the project's emerging **accompaniment** concept:

> **Good care creates conditions for another person's decision; it does not convert concern into authorship.**

This origin-level Nagisa behavior should be compared later with mature Makino management ethics.

### Guardrail

Later Nagisa can over-observe or over-worry. Do not project those later boundary problems backward onto every early act of persistence.

## A7. Notebook continuity — relational succession without person substitution

**Primary sources:**

- `origin_moon_004_nagisa_s_memories`;
- `origin_moon_005_kotono_s_memories`.

### Added fact

Kotono's notebook begins with a lonely girl. Mana adds a second girl holding the first girl's hand. The sisters continue this visual exchange during periods of absence.

After Mana's death, Nagisa later adds herself beside the lonely figure and writes:

`いつまでも一緒だよ`

### Cross-media significance

This is a compact visual/literary model of one of IDOLY PRIDE's central inheritance ethics:

> **A living person may inherit a relational function without becoming a replacement person.**

Nagisa can renew "you are not alone" without becoming Mana.

### Routing

Send to:

- Kotono/Nagisa relationship ledger;
- Mana/Kotono ledger;
- grief/memory/inheritance motif ledger;
- visual/object motif index.

## A8. Kotono — the diary does not command proxy completion

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_005_kotono_s_memories`, especially `st-group-moon-01-05-05`.

### Added facts

Mana's diary tells Kotono that Mana:

- genuinely loved idolhood;
- regretted hurt and distance with Kotono;
- continued to love Kotono;
- wrote `大好きだよ琴乃`;
- retained Kotono's birthday in the final entries.

Kotono then concludes:

`お姉ちゃんがやり残したことを私が代わりに果たさなきゃいけない`

and that singing the unfinished song on the final stage is `私のやるべきこと`.

### Revision to parent audit

The parent cross-media audit already treats Kotono's early idolhood as Mana-governed and the anime endpoint as self-authorship. The origin now supplies the exact causal bridge:

> **Mana leaves loving evidence and unfinished work. Kotono herself converts those facts into a delegated commission that Mana never explicitly issues.**

This makes Episode 11's refusal to seek Mana's evaluative authorization more consequential. Kotono's anime endpoint undoes the interpretive structure that founded her idol career.

### Guardrail

Do not imply that Kotono's inference is arbitrary. Mana did leave unfinished work and intended to communicate through song. The correction is about **authority**, not causal disconnectedness.

## A9. Kotono's social guardedness predates bereavement alone

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_005_kotono_s_memories`.

### Added fact

Classmates approach Kotono partly for access to famous Mana; when Kotono refuses favors, some relationships reveal themselves as instrumental and become ostracism/bullying.

### Revision to parent audit

Anime-native social distance can now be retrospectively understood as produced by at least two interacting sources:

1. grief and guilt after Mana's death;
2. earlier learned distrust that apparent friendship may actually mean interest in Mana.

This is explanation, not retrospective anime disclosure.

## A10. Kotono's effort schema

**Primary source:** `origin_moon_005_kotono_s_memories`.

### Added fact

Kotono's intense effort to recover academically and enter the same high school as Nagisa succeeds. She states:

`努力は絶対報われる、頑張れば夢は叶うんだって思えるようになった`

and identifies this as part of what made idol pursuit possible.

### Interpretive routing

Record as a **longitudinal hypothesis**, not final diagnosis:

> Successful effort may help explain why Kotono later reaches for more effort/control when confronted with grief, rivalry, or inadequacy.

Counterevidence is essential: effort genuinely works for many of Kotono's professional problems. The later question is where an adaptive rule becomes overgeneralized.

## A11. Tsuki origin structure is polycentric

The parent Hoshimi material and later historical analysis can make Kotono appear to be the source of Tsuki's entire identity.

The origins show a more complex starting topology:

- Saki -> Chisa/family responsibility;
- Suzu -> family/Mana;
- Mei -> cheer/Suzu;
- Nagisa -> Kotono;
- Kotono -> Mana/Nagisa.

### Canonical refinement

> **Kotono becomes a major unit center, but Tsuki no Tempest's origin causal architecture is polycentric.**

This matters for later character synthesis because it protects each member's vocation from being reduced to Kotono's grief story.

---

## A12. Net effect on the Hoshimi audit

No parent cross-media finding is overturned.

The Tsuki origins produce five major upgrades:

1. **causal precision** — Saki, Suzu, Mei, Nagisa, and Kotono now have explicit pre-Hoshimi developmental mechanisms;
2. **inheritance precision** — Suzu and Kotono show two radically different transformations of Mana influence;
3. **care/agency precision** — Nagisa's promise to Mana and notebook role support accompaniment without replacement;
4. **unit topology precision** — Tsuki is polycentric at origin even if Kotono later becomes a major center;
5. **grief/self-authorship precision** — Kotono's proxy mission is a self-issued commission inferred from Mana's diary, not an explicit command from the dead.

The parent audit remains canonical for anime↔`st-original-cmn` comparison. This addendum is the controlled Phase-1B provenance layer added after Tsuki origin disclosure.
