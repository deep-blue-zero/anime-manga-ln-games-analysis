# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — IIIX PRE-TOKYO BASELINE v1.1

## 0. Purpose and correction boundary

This document is the **only IIIX state that the Tokyo main-story audit may inherit at `tokyo_001_new_wind`**.

Version 1.0 incorrectly treated all four IIIX origin bundles as diegetically prior to Tokyo. Version 1.1 corrects that error by admitting only the ten securely pre-Tokyo stories from `Diamond Mining` and `House of Cards`.

The following later material is deliberately excluded at the Tokyo opening gate:

- Himeno's present-tense Preta Porte/I-UNITY role;
- I-UNITY institutional knowledge;
- IIIX's Tokyo-concurrent complicity/conflict with Himeno;
- Sunny's later I-UNITY performance;
- Himeno's exposure/arrest;
- Preta Porte's withdrawal from idol operations;
- IIIX's later dissolution option;
- the small live;
- the voluntary freelance re-formation;
- the mature "second founding" / re-authorization state.

Those facts must be earned again at their proper chronological boundary.

---

# 1. Corporate formation

IIIX is securely known to have been assembled by **Preta Porte** as an elite commercial project.

Its name derives from `3EX / Triple Excellent`, a diamond-cut grade. The metaphor is structurally important: three already valuable people are selected and cut into a premium professional object.

The first IIIX therefore has **institutional authorship before relational authorship**.

The members are not recruited because they already share an idol philosophy or friendship.

---

# 2. Member motives at the gate

## 2.1 Miho

Securely admissible facts:

- Miho had an earlier idol vocation with her close friend 藤代葉.
- Their shared ideal included making people happy through stage performance.
- Yō died after illness.
- Yō asked Miho to continue rather than remain trapped by her absence.
- Mana's later death generated overwhelming public mourning while Yō's death received little comparable attention.
- Miho experiences that asymmetry as painful injustice.
- She subsequently develops a project of surpassing Mana's records/traces.

The pre-Tokyo project is already severe:

`長瀬麻奈の記録を塗り替えてやるのよ`

and:

`長瀬麻奈の痕跡が残らないくらいに`

**Admissible inference:** Mana has become the symbolic carrier of a public-memory hierarchy Miho experiences as erasing Yō.

**Do not yet assume:** how far Miho will operationalize the project in rights, institutional, or archival terms during Tokyo.

## 2.2 fran

Securely admissible facts:

- fashion/modeling is fran's primary vocation;
- she has high-level fashion-world experience;
- business failure/debt materially pressures her;
- IIIX offers income, visibility, and professional leverage;
- she is highly competitive and strongly dislikes losing.

**Do not yet assume:** that idolhood has already become a genuine secondary vocation in the mature post-I-UNITY sense.

## 2.3 kana

Securely admissible facts:

- kana enjoys attention and is highly skilled at self-branding;
- visibility is professionally useful to her;
- public exposure also carries a personal hope that her absent father may see and contact her;
- family exploitation has made her sensitive to status, money, and control;
- she strongly dislikes losing and fears disappearing from public notice.

**Do not yet assume:** how that exploitative-adult literacy will map onto Himeno before Tokyo establishes their relationship.

---

# 3. Relationship state

IIIX has already suffered a serious defeat and responded by making private motives explicit to one another.

Their new compact is not friendship-centered.

fran's formulation is essentially:

- private motives may remain private or even disagreeable;
- the members may continue to dislike one another;
- what matters is that all three genuinely commit to becoming stronger and refusing another loss.

The secure pre-Tokyo relational model is therefore:

> **instrumental coalition with increasing professional trust, held together most clearly by the shared demand for victory.**

The more mature term **adversarial interdependence** remains useful for later longitudinal synthesis, but at `tokyo_001` it should be treated as a hypothesis to test rather than a fully completed state.

Do **not** inherit post-I-UNITY voluntary re-selection as proof of loyalty yet.

---

# 4. Performance philosophy

Securely admissible:

- IIIX is extremely high-control and technically ambitious;
- the members respond to defeat through intensified practice;
- they treat measurable victory as professional proof;
- they seek near-perfect execution and large score margins;
- they have already defeated STROBOLIGHTS decisively and entered BIG4.

Miho explicitly thinks:

`勝てるなら他には何も望まないわ`

The gate therefore preserves a strong unresolved possibility:

> **Victory may still be the only shared value IIIX can name without qualification.**

Do not import the later post-I-UNITY audience-based revaluation.

---

# 5. Mana and Hoshimi at the gate

By the end of securely pre-Tokyo story 10, Miho learns that Hoshimi Production is debuting two new groups and that one includes **Mana's sister**.

This matters because it establishes that Hoshimi becomes relevant to Miho **through the Mana legacy before direct Tokyo conflict**.

However, the gate does not yet know:

- IIIX's eventual evaluation of Sakura;
- direct contact with Sakura/Kotono;
- the precise I-UNITY structure;
- any later institutional plot involving Hoshimi;
- whether Miho will distinguish the living Hoshimi idols from Mana's symbolic legacy.

Those are Tokyo questions.

---

Securely known:

- IIIX has already become BIG4;
- the unit is professionally successful within the established VENUS ecosystem.

Not admissible at the opening gate:

- Himeno's later producer role;
- I.E.A.;
- I-UNITY;
- Asakura's arrest;
- Preta Porte's later withdrawal;

The Tokyo text must establish those developments in order.

---

# 7. Ethical state at the gate

The pre-Tokyo corpus does **not** yet establish a mature distinction between:

- legitimate scoring optimization;
- tolerating institutional manipulation;
- rejecting corrupted victory conditions.

What it does establish is a group intensely invested in **winning through professional improvement**.

Therefore Tokyo must be allowed to reveal, rather than merely confirm, how IIIX behaves when outside actors offer them structural advantages.

This is especially important because the analyst has already seen later IIIX-origin material. The corrected baseline intentionally removes those answers.

---

# 8. Questions Tokyo is allowed to test

1. Does the victory compact survive success, pressure, and institutional temptation?
2. How does Miho translate the anti-Mana record project into concrete conduct toward Hoshimi?
3. How far will fran and kana support Miho's legacy project when their motives differ?
4. Does IIIX distinguish its own performance ethics from agency/institutional conduct?
5. What forms of manipulation will IIIX tolerate, reject, or rationalize?
6. Will direct contact with Hoshimi alter IIIX's understanding of audience value?
7. Is IIIX's relational structure merely mutually useful, or does harder loyalty emerge under pressure?
8. Does the group have a telos beyond victory at this point?
9. How do living rivals change Miho's relation to a dead benchmark?
10. What happens when technically reliable excellence meets performances whose audience response exceeds the controllable model?

These are deliberately **open** at `tokyo_001`.
