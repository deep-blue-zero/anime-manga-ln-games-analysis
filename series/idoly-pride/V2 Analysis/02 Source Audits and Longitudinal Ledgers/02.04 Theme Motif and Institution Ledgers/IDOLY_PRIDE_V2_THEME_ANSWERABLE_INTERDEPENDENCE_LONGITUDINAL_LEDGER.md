# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — ANSWERABLE INTERDEPENDENCE LONGITUDINAL LEDGER

## P2-E-01 — reciprocal standing and constitutional authority

---

# 0. Analytical responsibility

This is the first P2-E theme/institution ledger.

Its responsibility is not to repeat the five unit ledgers or to turn a Phase-1 slogan into a polished thematic essay. It asks whether **answerable interdependence** survives longitudinal stress across different kinds of relationships, roles, units, institutions, and failures, and if so, what the mechanism actually means once its limits and counterexamples are preserved.

The inherited Phase-1 baseline carried forward:

> **answerable interdependence**

with several related formulations including `ANSWERABLE_CONSTITUTIONALISM`, `SELECTIVE_PERSONAL_JURISDICTION`, `BIDIRECTIONAL_PROFESSIONAL_CARE`, `ROLE_CONTINUITY_WITHOUT_ROLE_CAPTIVITY`, and `DIFFERENTIATED_RECIPROCITY`.

Phase 1 explicitly warned that these were **not permission to force every character or relationship into the same mechanism**. P2-E therefore has to answer four narrower questions:

1. What recurring problem is shared across otherwise different cases?
2. What counts as legitimate asymmetry rather than domination or self-erasure?
3. What does “answerable” require without flattening role, expertise, age, centerhood, management, intimacy, or competition into sameness?
4. Where does the model stop?

This ledger owns the **cross-unit mechanism**. It does not take ownership of the character changes, relationship categories, or unit constitutions that supply its evidence.

# 2. Governing thematic model

The cross-unit evidence supports the following P2-E formulation:

> **`ANSWERABLE_INTERDEPENDENCE_WITHOUT_JURISDICTIONAL_CAPTURE`: IDOLY PRIDE repeatedly treats dependence, attachment, expertise, centerhood, leadership, management, seniority, institutional assistance, and shared ambition as legitimate and often necessary. The recurring failure does not arise from dependence itself. It arises when causal importance is converted into jurisdiction: because I support you, lead you, formed the unit, know more, love you, manage you, protect you, carry the burden, or make your success possible, I therefore acquire unilateral standing to decide your body, exit, information, desire, vocation, role, or future. Mature relations do not solve this by abolishing hierarchy or demanding equal roles. They redistribute and clarify standing. Centers remain centers, managers retain professional duties, veterans keep expertise, younger members can receive age-sensitive care, founders retain historical particularity, and institutions can provide real resources. What changes is that authority becomes bounded, contestable where appropriate, responsive to those affected, and unable to convert causal contribution into ownership. Interdependence is therefore strongest not when nobody needs anyone, but when need can survive refusal, dissent, disclosure, changed roles, credible exit, reciprocal care, and the continued authorship of more than one person.**

A complementary formulation is:

> **`DIFFERENTIATED_AUTHORITY_WITH_RECIPROCAL_STANDING`: equal standing does not require equal function. A professional constitution may distribute authority asymmetrically while preserving the affected participants' status as authors rather than instruments.**

The shortest rule is:

> **causal importance creates duties of answerability; it does not automatically create ownership.**

# 3. Definitions and anti-definitions

## 3.1 Interdependence

Here **interdependence** means that another person, role, audience, or institution is genuinely causally relevant to one's ability to act, develop, perform, survive, interpret oneself, or sustain a shared project.

It does **not** mean:

- inability to function alone;
- moral obligation to remain together forever;
- codependence as a diagnostic category;
- symmetrical need at every moment;
- fusion of motives or identity.

## 3.2 Answerability

**Answerability** means that power, care, influence, or specialized judgment remains open to some combination of:

- explanation;
- challenge;
- disclosure of shared stakes;
- negotiation;
- correction;
- refusal;
- reassignment;
- reciprocal recognition;
- bounded intervention;
- responsibility for consequences.

The exact form depends on the role. A manager and an idol do not possess identical professional authority. A center and another member do not have identical stage function. A doctor-like emergency safety decision would not require a popularity vote. Answerability is therefore not procedural sameness.

## 3.3 Reciprocal standing

**Reciprocal standing** means that the other participant remains a person whose authored interests count in the constitution of the relation or shared project.

Reciprocity can coexist with:

- unequal skill;
- unequal seniority;
- unequal responsibility;
- unequal visibility;
- unequal emotional dependence;
- unequal institutional power.

It fails when asymmetry becomes a reason to treat the lower-power participant as an object whose future can be authored from above.

## 3.4 Constitutional authority

A **constitutional authority** is a role that possesses real, bounded decision competence inside a shared system.

Examples include:

- centerhood;
- unit leadership;
- management;
- domain-specific creative expertise;
- bodily safety duties;
- scheduling and professional coordination.

The key distinction is:

> **office is not personhood.**

The authority attaches to a domain and purpose, not to an unlimited claim over the whole person.

## 3.5 Jurisdictional capture

This ledger uses **jurisdictional capture** for the recurring failure in which one legitimate source of standing expands beyond its defensible domain.

Common conversions include:

- center -> sole causal ownership;
- leader -> control of another member's future;
- support -> self-reduction;
- care -> secrecy or beneficiary exclusion;
- expertise -> veto over aspiration;
- seniority -> authorship of the younger person's goal;
- founder status -> permanent monopoly over unit identity;
- institutional assistance -> ownership of unit style;
- audience support -> entitlement to private life;
- result seriousness -> sovereignty over futurity.

These are not all morally identical. They are structurally comparable because each takes a real source of importance and expands it into a larger jurisdiction than the evidence justifies.

# 4. Longitudinal grammar across the corpus

The completed Phase-2 ledgers reveal a recurring seven-stage grammar.

## Stage I — real dependence or asymmetry forms

A relation begins with something genuinely unequal or causally concentrated:

- Mana selects and trains Makino;
- Sakura becomes SUNNY PEACE's center;
- Kotono becomes Tsuki's center and directional focus;
- Rio/Aoi found LizNoir before Ai/Kokoro join;
- Rui remains TRINITYAiLE's center and leader;
- Preta Porte assembles IIIX and assigns Fran centerhood;
- Nagisa enters idolhood substantially to support Kotono.

The text usually does **not** reveal these structures as fake or inherently corrupt.

## Stage II — the asymmetry works

The arrangement produces real value.

Support helps someone survive. Expertise raises quality. Center concentration gives clarity. Management converts desire into executable professional strategy. Institutional resources permit work at scale. Founder knowledge preserves continuity.

This functional success is important because it explains why capture can remain invisible.

## Stage III — causal importance expands into jurisdiction

The working asymmetry begins to imply a stronger claim:

- the center carries every failure;
- the supporter remains behind permanently;
- the caregiver withholds shared information;
- the manager acts without consultation because the motive is protective;
- the founder treats formal continuity as requiring old form;
- the institution defines the unit rather than enabling it;
- the younger member must prove equal standing by rejecting care;
- victory becomes proof of legitimacy rather than one professional result.

## Stage IV — rupture exposes the hidden constitution

Defeat, injury, separation, overload, disagreement, or institutional collapse makes the jurisdiction visible.

Crucially, rupture is not always proof the relationship should end. It is often diagnostic evidence that the **terms** of interdependence need revision.

## Stage V — refusal or dissent restores standing

A member says no, leaves, contradicts, discloses, demands consultation, competes for the center, rejects a sacrifice, or insists on her own goal.

The important developmental move is not mere rebellion. It is the emergence of a participant who can answer back while remaining inside—or potentially returning to—the relation.

## Stage VI — differentiated authority is rebuilt

The mature settlement usually preserves meaningful differences:

- Sakura remains center;
- Suzu and Kotono can both hold center competence;
- Rui remains TRINITYAiLE center;
- Fran remains IIIX's meaningful default center;
- Rio/Aoi remain historically special founders;
- Makino remains a manager with real duties.

What disappears is the equation between specialness and sovereignty.

## Stage VII — continuity becomes reauthorization rather than inertia

The strongest mature relations can survive:

- changed roles;
- temporary separation;
- credible exit;
- defeat;
- different external vocations;
- disagreement;
- receipt of care;
- institutional change.

Continuation becomes evidence because the participants retain enough standing that continuation is still a choice.

# 5. Makino — managerial authority as the foundational professional test

Makino provides the clearest role-based version of the mechanism.

His P2-A ledger rejects two equal simplifications:

1. the manager is merely a neutral helper with no real authority;
2. because the manager has expertise and duty of care, he has superior standing over the idol as a person.

The mature formulation is:

> professional authority without personhood superiority.

Early Makino can intervene for defensible professional reasons and still produce a relational injury by acting unilaterally. `origin_mna_003_goodbye_to_goodbye` is load-bearing because the protective motive is real **and** consultation failure remains real.

Later sources increasingly introduce:

- explanation;
- negotiation;
- delayed intervention;
- privacy boundaries;
- explicit recognition that some answers are not his to make.

`hoshimi_005_the_sun_moon_and_stars` supports the principle that waiting can itself be active management when immediate intervention would prevent the idols from diagnosing and authoring their own solution.

At `hoshimi_023_pray_for_you`, the mature limit becomes explicit: the manager can prepare conditions, but the decisive outcome ultimately belongs to the performers.

The P2-E implication is not anti-management.

> **Managerial competence becomes more legitimate when it can distinguish domain authority from authorship of the other person's future.**

The detailed ethics of bodily intervention, confidentiality, safeguarding, and professional care belong to P2-E-02. P2-E-01 uses Makino only to establish the general constitutional distinction.

# 6. SUNNY PEACE — centerhood becomes stronger when burden can circulate

SUNNY PEACE is the strongest warm-unit demonstration that reciprocal standing does not require role flattening.

Its P2-D master model is:

> `CIRCULATED_LIGHT_AS_DISTRIBUTED_ANSWERABILITY_SUNNY_PEACE`

with the explicit center model:

> `CENTER_WITHOUT_SOVEREIGNTY_SUNNY_PEACE`

Sakura is not demoted from center. The unit instead becomes capable of carrying meaning through Rei's technical judgment, Haruko's veteran/care infrastructure, Chisa's relational sensitivity, Shizuku's audience literacy and creative leadership, and Sakura's affective/center function.

## 6.1 Haruko — belonging cannot decide exit for the member

`hoshimi_008_make_up_her_mind` is a useful early limit case.

Haruko matters greatly to the group, but indispensability does not entitle the unit to make her career decision for her. The constitutional lesson is:

> **being needed creates a reason to speak; it does not create ownership of another member's exit.**

## 6.2 `stellar_005_we_are_the_sun` — center burden becomes jurisdiction over the self

Sakura's overload crisis is not selfish domination of others. It is a different capture pattern: she converts center responsibility into a private claim that she must personally absorb the unit's burden.

The other four then partly reproduce the same error by answering her overwork with their own overwork.

The repair is not “nobody is responsible.” It is that responsibility becomes shareable and suffering becomes discussable.

This yields:

> **`BURDEN_RESPONSIBILITY_WITHOUT_BURDEN_MONOPOLY`: a differentiated role may carry more responsibility without giving that role exclusive ownership of failure, suffering, or rescue.**

## 6.3 Supported risk

`stellar_010_miraculous_beat` prevents the framework from becoming safety paternalism.

Answerability does not require eliminating risk whenever a vulnerable person chooses a demanding goal. It requires that risk be treated as a shared professional reality rather than either:

- abandoned to the individual as proof of autonomy; or
- seized from the individual as proof of care.

SUNNY PEACE therefore supplies a general pattern:

> **care can preserve authored risk rather than abolish it.**

# 7. Tsuki no Tempest — attachment fails when it becomes unequal jurisdiction

Tsuki provides the strongest unit-scale falsification of the claim that maturity equals independence.

Its P2-D formulation is:

> `ATTACHMENT_TO_AUTHORSHIP_THROUGH_CONSTITUTIONAL_REFOUNDATION_TSUKI_NO_TEMPEST`

The early unit is genuinely effective, but its constitution overconcentrates direction around Kotono.

The problem is not that the other members care too much. The problem is that care, symbolic centrality, and initiative become organized so that Kotono is expected to carry the hardest decisions while others—especially Nagisa—are more legible as support.

## 7.1 Separation is developmental because the old relation cannot simply be intensified

BIG4 rupture matters because it gives both sides capacities that the old structure did not require.

Nagisa's development is particularly diagnostic:

> support Kotono -> support Tsuki -> also shine herself.

Her refusal to follow Kotono does not erase attachment. It changes what attachment permits.

The relationship ledger names this:

> `RELATION_PRESERVING_DIFFERENTIATION_KOTONO_NAGISA`

and later:

> `CONTESTATORY_CARE_KOTONO_NAGISA`.

This is one of the clearest cross-unit rules in the corpus:

> **mature support includes the standing to oppose the person being supported.**

## 7.2 Refoundation does not mean rolelessness

After reconstitution, centerhood remains real.

Suzu's center competence is preserved. Kotono can receive center authority for a particular performance configuration. Later material allows center position to become contestable without claiming that every member is equally suited to every center assignment.

Tsuki therefore distinguishes:

- **contestability** from role flattening;
- **attachment** from obedience;
- **reliance** from incapacity;
- **repair** from restoration of the old constitution.

The mature unit is stronger because Kotono can rely on others and because the others can say no.

# 8. Rio / Aoi and LizNoir — particularity is strongest when it does not become ownership

The Rio/Aoi relationship supplies a relationship-level constitutional case that later becomes unit-level infrastructure.

Its governing model is:

> `CHOSEN_PARTNERSHIP_THROUGH_ANSWERABLE_DIFFERENCE_RIO_AOI`

and its core rule is:

> **agreement is not the condition of partnership; answerable engagement is.**

## 8.1 Aoi's protective secrecy

`origin_liz_003_impatience_of_hollyhock` is a canonical failure example.

Aoi's motive is protective. Rio is genuinely special to her. Yet Aoi withholds information about stakes that materially belong to both of them.

The Rio/Aoi ledger correctly separates:

- trust as feeling;
- trust as shared jurisdiction.

The failure is not that Aoi cares too much. It is that care temporarily becomes unilateral authorship of Rio's decision environment.

> **`PROTECTIVE_CARE_WITHOUT_BENEFICIARY_EXCLUSION`: care cannot normally justify editing another competent participant out of a decision whose stakes are jointly theirs.**

The appreciation of motive does not ratify the method.

## 8.2 Founder particularity without founder monopoly

The exact-four LizNoir constitution then prevents the founding pair's specialness from becoming ownership of unit identity.

Rio and Aoi remain historically singular to each other while Ai and Kokoro acquire full present standing.

This is important because reciprocal standing does not require historical equivalence.

> **people can be unequal in provenance and equal in present membership.**

## 8.3 LizNoir hierarchy

The LizNoir unit ledger contributes:

> hierarchy without unilateral jurisdiction.

Experience, leadership, and founder status remain meaningful, but challenge, reinterpretation, creative authorship, and succession expand.

The unit therefore demonstrates that constitutional maturity can preserve pride and elite standards while making the object of loyalty **revisable form rather than frozen form**.

# 9. TRINITYAiLE — asymmetry of role without asymmetry of personhood

TRINITYAiLE states the mechanism most cleanly.

Its P2-D governing model is:

> `ANSWERABLE_HIERARCHY_WITHOUT_PERSONHOOD_SUBORDINATION_TRINITYAILE`

The unit remains overtly hierarchical in function:

- Rui is center and leader;
- Yu is strongly support/strategy-oriented;
- Sumire is younger and later-entering.

P2-D does not flatten any of those differences.

The unit instead establishes:

> **asymmetry of role is legitimate only when all three retain standing as authors of what the unit becomes.**

## 9.1 `origin_tri_003_light_my_fire` — full-strength challenge strengthens hierarchy

Yu can beat Rui in a direct contest and Rui can remain center.

This matters because it rejects a hidden premise common to brittle hierarchy:

> if the subordinate can outperform the leader locally, the hierarchy has been invalidated.

TRINITYAiLE instead makes centerhood a professional office whose legitimacy can survive evidence that another member is stronger in a local contest.

## 9.2 `stellar_003_to_fly_higher` — center responsibility without causal monopoly

After defeat, Rui initially absorbs loss into sole-center responsibility and more solitary effort.

Yu rejects the causal monopoly: TRINITYAiLE advances, wins, and loses as three people.

This is not a denial of Rui's differentiated responsibility. It is a refusal to let centerhood become sole ownership of causality.

## 9.3 `origin_tri_005_because_sisters` and later care — love does not authorize unilateral sacrifice

The family/sacrifice material establishes a recurring danger: someone can attempt to solve another person's problem by excluding the beneficiary from the decision.

Later evidence repeatedly moves toward help that expands rather than replaces Sumire's authorship.

## 9.4 `event_2025_001_st-eve-2501-race` — equal standing does not mean identical treatment

Sumire's age/body status is a particularly useful counterexample to simplistic equality.

Rui and Yu's concern is not automatically hierarchy abuse. Sumire's desire to prove equal status is not automatically mature autonomy.

The better rule is:

> **equal standing permits relevant asymmetrical care, provided care questions, accompanies, or proportionally intervenes without seizing the younger member's goal merely to relieve the caregivers' discomfort.**

This case should be inherited by P2-E-02 and P2-E-06 as well.

# 10. IIIX — self-authorship requires institutions and members to remain mutually non-sovereign

IIIX broadens the mechanism beyond warm relational idioms.

Its P2-D master model is:

> `SELF_AUTHORED_COMPETITIVE_PLATFORM_WITHOUT_RESULT_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_CAPTIVITY_IIIX`

IIIX is important because it demonstrates that answerable interdependence does **not** require friendship conversion, softness, or low conflict.

## 10.1 Preta Porte — a working institution can still own too much of the constitution

The original IIIX is substantially authored around the members by an external prestige structure.

Preta Porte supplies:

- concept;
- infrastructure;
- center assignment;
- professional scale;
- a common reward structure.

The arrangement works. That is precisely why its constitutional weakness matters.

When institutional and result security collapse, the members can continue as idols without continuing as IIIX. The later freelance continuation therefore transfers constitutional location from approximately:

> institution -> unit -> members

into:

> members -> unit -> chosen institutional relations.

This is not independence from institutions. It is a change in who possesses the standing to define the relation.

## 10.2 Himeno — optimization becomes capture when causality is no longer answerable

The Himeno material is a strong negative case because external assistance and optimization are not automatically illegitimate.

The failure occurs when winning can no longer be cleanly attributed to a process the unit itself can answer for.

IIIX's unit ledger formulates the relevant boundary:

> **winning only proves IIIX if IIIX remains causally answerable for the performance and result.**

## 10.3 Hoshimi — affiliation without domestication

Hoshimi then becomes a positive institutional contrast.

The IIIX ledger names:

> `AFFILIATION_WITHOUT_DOMESTICATION_IIIX`.

IIIX can accept a professional ecology, resources, and mutual benefit while remaining recognizably IIIX because affiliation does not demand relational assimilation.

This is exactly why a separate P2-D Hoshimi ledger is unnecessary here: the load-bearing unit-level proposition is already IIIX's. Its cross-unit significance belongs to P2-E-02.

## 10.4 Domain-specific creative authority

The 2024 creative-work material shows another mature form of differentiated authority.

Kana, Miho, and Fran need not be equally authoritative in every creative domain. Specialist jurisdiction can be real while local decisions remain contestable and answerable to shared work.

> **`DOMAIN_AUTHORITY_WITHOUT_TOTAL_CREATIVE_SOVEREIGNTY`: specialization can distribute authority without fragmenting the unit or turning expertise into universal veto.**

## 10.5 Credible dissolution and reauthorization

The 2025 U.S. arc proves that self-authorship does not make IIIX indissoluble.

All three can genuinely agree that dissolution is rational. Continued IIIX later becomes stronger evidence precisely because exit was real.

This supports a general rule:

> **continuation is most constitutionally meaningful when the participants retain credible standing to leave.**

# 11. Relationship-scale refinements

## 11.1 Kotono / Nagisa — support becomes adjacency

The pair begins with costly recognition and later develops a strong support asymmetry.

The source does not retroactively declare Nagisa's support false. Instead it shows why permanent rear-position support becomes too narrow once Nagisa's own vocation expands.

The mature relation is better described by the ledger's:

> `EARNED_ADJACENCY_THROUGH_DIFFERENTIATION_KOTONO_NAGISA`

than by a static supporter model.

This is important for P2-E because it demonstrates that answerability can require **more differentiation**, not less relation.

## 11.2 Rio / Aoi — shared stakes create standing, not possession

Rio's protest against Aoi's protective secrecy is constitutional because the relevant stakes belonged to both partners.

That is different from claiming Rio owns Aoi's body or choices.

The question is:

> who has standing to participate in this decision?

The answer varies by domain. Partnership creates duties of response around shared stakes; it does not create unlimited jurisdiction over the partner.

## 11.3 IIIX internal triad — trust without friendship conversion

The IIIX triad relationship ledger is an essential guardrail against emotional homogenization.

The trio repeatedly reauthorizes adversarial interdependence. Later material supports trust while explicitly resisting a conventional friendship category.

Therefore:

> **answerable interdependence is compatible with relational forms whose legitimacy comes from repeated professional choice, trust, conflict, and coauthorship rather than intimacy labels.**

This prevents the theme from degenerating into “everyone learns to become friends.”

# 13. Failure-mode taxonomy

## 13.1 Burden monopoly

A high-responsibility person treats suffering or failure as something only she is entitled or obligated to carry.

Primary examples:

- Sakura in `stellar_005_we_are_the_sun`;
- Rui after defeat in `stellar_003_to_fly_higher`;
- old Tsuki's directional concentration around Kotono.

## 13.2 Support captivity

A supporter allows care to become the main or sole authorization of her own role.

Primary example:

# 20. P2-E-01 completion result

The inherited Phase-1 phrase **answerable interdependence** survives Phase-2 longitudinal stress, but in a narrower and more operational form than a slogan.

The strongest current formulation is:

> **Interdependence is not the opposite of autonomy. In this corpus, autonomy repeatedly becomes durable through relations in which power, care, role, attachment, and support remain real while losing the right to become total jurisdiction.**

The mature pattern does not flatten difference.

It produces:

- center without sole causal ownership;
- leadership without futurity ownership;
- support without self-reduction;
- care without beneficiary exclusion;
- expertise without universal veto;
- founder particularity without founder monopoly;
- institutional affiliation without capture;
- competition without result sovereignty;
- audience relation without unlimited access;
- continued attachment with the right to refuse;
- credible exit without automatic relational invalidation.

The model can therefore be summarized as:

> **`ANSWERABLE_INTERDEPENDENCE_WITHOUT_JURISDICTIONAL_CAPTURE`**

with the constitutional corollary:

> **`DIFFERENTIATED_AUTHORITY_WITH_RECIPROCAL_STANDING`**.

These models are **current through `IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A`** and remain subject to the open registers above.
