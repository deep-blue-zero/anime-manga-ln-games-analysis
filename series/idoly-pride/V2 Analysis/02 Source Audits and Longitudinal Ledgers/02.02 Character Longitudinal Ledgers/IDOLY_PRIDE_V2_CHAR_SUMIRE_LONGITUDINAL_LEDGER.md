# 0. Responsibility and authority

This ledger is the current Phase-2 character authority for **Okuyama Sumire / 奥山すみれ / character code `smr`** through `IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A`.

Sumire is unusually easy to flatten because several descriptions of her are locally true while none is sufficient as a master model. She is the youngest member of TRINITYAiLE. She enters with less singing and dance experience than Rui and Yu. She is bright, affectionate, expressive, family-oriented, professionally experienced from child acting, and often treated as the trio's little sister. She also repeatedly worries that older or stronger people will leave her behind, tries to be useful, suppresses disappointment, volunteers for burdens, and sometimes makes risky choices because success would prove that she deserves equal standing.

The obvious summary would be “the insecure youngest member becomes confident.” That is too weak.

Sumire's central longitudinal problem is **proof captivity**: when belonging, equality, love, professionalism, or originality feels uncertain, she repeatedly tries to settle the uncertainty by producing evidence. She can attempt to prove her place by being useful; prove professionalism by swallowing private disappointment; prove family love by sacrificing a vocation; prove adult standing by accepting disproportionate physical risk; prove artistic legitimacy by imagining that originality must be purified of influence; or prove that she is needed by becoming difficult to replace.

These strategies are understandable because the underlying uncertainty is not imaginary. Rui and Yu really can win without her. Sumire really is younger. She really does make a professionally consequential scheduling decision during the qualifier. Her family's business really is under material pressure. Her older brother really did sacrifice part of his own trajectory. Her child-acting career really did decline despite competent, mistake-avoiding work. The narrative therefore does not mature Sumire by telling her that every fear was false.

It changes the question.

The mature Sumire increasingly learns that:

- **competitive substitutability does not determine constitutive belonging**;
- **age-aware care does not have to imply lower standing**;
- **professional accountability does not require carrying every conflict alone**;
- **family love does not become purer when desires are made mutually zero-sum**;
- **asking for help can be an authored act rather than evidence of weakness**;
- **several genuine wants can coexist before the mechanism for reconciling them is known**;
- **risk can reopen a stagnant life, but risk does not become obligatory proof of adulthood**;
- **originality can consist in authored recombination rather than purity from influence**;
- **a person's place in a relationship need not be re-earned through continuous performance of usefulness**.

The capstone is therefore:

> **`PLURAL_AUTHORSHIP_WITHOUT_PROOF_CAPTIVITY_SUMIRE`: Sumire matures from a person who repeatedly tries to secure belonging and protect loved ones by proving usefulness, self-sufficiency, maturity, success, or sacrifice into a person increasingly able to state several wants at once, ask others to enter the problem with her, accept developmental or role asymmetry without translating it into inferiority, and create from inherited relationships and influences without treating her right to belong as something that must be continuously re-proven.**

Short form:

> **Sumire does not become equal by making herself impossible to replace. She becomes equal by acquiring standing to want, choose, ask, contribute, receive, contest, and remain.**

This ledger does **not** claim that the proof reflex disappears. The 2025 marathon is specifically valuable because it shows the reflex in a mature environment: Sumire intellectually knows Rui and Yu regard her as an equal and still privately wants a hard physical achievement to make that equality undeniable. Development changes her available responses and relational institutions more reliably than it abolishes vulnerability.

The artifact also preserves the new architecture boundary established in method/architecture v2.5. Sumire's ordinary-life material is rich: toys, food, fashion, kendama, school behavior, message style, domestic rhythms, hobbies, fandom-adjacent interests, and comic habits appear across cards, bonds, messages, and specials. Those sources remain canonical evidence, but a full experiential inventory belongs to the later **Phase 8.5 holistic modeling profile** unless a detail is necessary to the longitudinal argument. This ledger therefore records only texture that changes the causal model.

# 4. Close reading I — childhood entertainment labor and the usefulness genealogy

## 4.1 Sumire's first dream is somebody else's future

The family arc later reveals that Sumire entered child entertainment partly because she wanted to support her older brother's overseas piano education. She describes the original dream in relational terms: the brother becoming a professional pianist.

That does not make the labor unreal or coerced in the simple sense. Sumire likes entertainment work, is proud of her skills, and carries real professional experience forward. But the genealogy matters because it teaches a durable rule:

> **my effort can convert love into material support.**

This is a powerful and often prosocial rule. It also creates vulnerability. If work is a way of loving, inability to work, declining jobs, or choosing a desire of one's own can feel morally charged rather than merely vocational.

## 4.2 The child-actor decline teaches the wrong lesson before it teaches the right one

Later Sumire remembers that her acting was praised for stability. She avoided mistakes and did what she was told. Work nevertheless disappeared until she no longer had a place in that part of the industry.

The first consequence is insecurity: competent performance does not guarantee continued recognition. The later consequence is more productive: **failure avoidance can itself become a dead end**.

This memory becomes crucial in 2023 when TRINITYAiLE's 99-win streak tempts the unit to choose only contests it can reliably win. Sumire recognizes the structural resemblance before Rui or Yu do because she has already lived through a career in which stable competence stopped generating a future.

Thus her past is not merely a wound to escape. It becomes **professional epistemic capital**.

## 4.3 Brightness is not evidence that sacrifice costs nothing

Childhood material also establishes Sumire's habit of making work-related disappointment appear manageable. She can say she likes work, minimize the loss of ordinary school experiences, and keep moving. The 2021 Christmas event later repeats the same pattern in a new setting.

This matters for modeling her family sacrifice. She is not simply a naturally selfless child who never wants anything else. She has a practiced ability to make costly adaptation look cheerful.

The correct inference is therefore not “her cheerfulness is fake.” It is:

> **Sumire's cheerfulness can be authentic while also functioning as a high-capacity container for disappointment.**

Both can be true at once.

# 5. Close reading II — recruitment: unique expressive surplus without exceptionalist mythology

## 5.1 Sumire enters technically behind

When Rui and Yu encounter Sumire practicing, she is explicit that she has little prior singing/dance background and therefore feels she must practice more than others. Her technique is not presented as secretly equal to Rui's from the beginning.

Yu nevertheless notices expressive power in her dancing. Rui states that the technique remains rough but that Sumire possesses something Rui and Yu do not: `あなたにしかないもの`.

This becomes the first positive answer to Sumire's place question.

She is not recruited merely because she is:

- a former child actor;
- commercially marketable;
- younger and cute;
- an accessory to Rui/Yu.

She adds a real expressive capacity.

## 5.2 Sumire's relationship to her own past is pragmatic rather than purity-driven

Asakura bluntly recognizes the publicity value of “famous actress's daughter + former child prodigy.” Rui worries that this amounts to using Sumire. Sumire's answer is revealing: she would rather use the history than hide it, because hiding it would feel like running away from her own past.

This is an early form of a mature theme that returns in later creation:

> **authorship does not require purity from history.**

Her former identity may be commercially useful without becoming the whole of her identity. Her later music may contain visible influence without becoming derivative. In both cases, the question is not whether prior material exists; it is whether Sumire has standing to decide what it means now.

## 5.3 The danger: expressive difference can become another proof obligation

It would be easy to stop here and claim Sumire's security comes from discovering the “special thing only she can do.” The later qualifier disproves that as a complete solution.

A group can value her expressive surplus and still demonstrate that it can win without her. If Sumire's place were grounded only in irreplaceable function, every successful two-person performance would reopen the wound.

The narrative therefore must move from:

> **I belong because I uniquely produce X**

into:

> **I contribute X because I belong, and my belonging is not reducible to a single function.**

That distinction is central to the entire ledger.

# 6. Close reading III — the qualifier: competitive dispensability versus constitutive belonging

## 6.1 Sumire's fear is evidence-based

Weather disruption prevents Sumire from reaching the final qualifier. Rui and Yu perform as two and achieve the highest score of the tournament.

Sumire's immediate interpretation is devastatingly rational at the local level:

- `二人でも……勝てちゃうんだ`
- `私がいなくても本戦行けちゃうんだもん`

She then turns a performance result into a judgment of personhood and membership: if the two can win without her and she is the one who causes inconvenience, perhaps there is no meaning in her being there.

That final step is the error.

## 6.2 Rui and Yu do not solve the problem by lying about performance

The story does not make the two-person result vanish. Rui and Yu **can** succeed without Sumire in that specific competitive task.

The correction is constitutional rather than empirical.

They choose three-person TRINITYAiLE. The later three-person performance exceeds the two-person score, but even that should not become the new proof. The important statement is that `三人でトリエル` names the form of group they want.

Thus:

> **competitive dispensability and constitutive non-removability are different dimensions.**

The group is not a machine whose membership must be recalculated after every output test.

## 6.3 Equality is not identical contribution

Sumire explicitly says she thought she had to be equal to Rui/Yu because they are older and more professional, and she feared being left behind. This reveals a mistaken equation:

> equal personhood = equal maturity + equal competence + equal burden capacity + equal output.

TRINITYAiLE gradually rejects that equation.

Rui remains center. Yu has different strategic/social competencies. Sumire is younger and may require age-specific safeguards. Those asymmetries do not automatically create lower membership standing.

The mature unit principle, from Sumire's side, is:

> **equality means equal standing to matter, speak, contest, choose, receive the others, and be received by them—not identical performance profile.**

# 7. Close reading IV — family duty and professional accountability without solitary jurisdiction

## 7.1 The missed qualifier is not morally costless

The scheduling failure is not explained away as pure bad luck. Sumire had chosen to help prepare the family brewery against severe weather after her father was incapacitated. Her mother had told her to go to Tokyo, but Sumire could not leave the family problem untouched.

Sumire later says directly that as a paid professional idol she got the priorities wrong.

Rui agrees that the professional-priority issue is real.

This matters because the V2 ethic must not become “family love excuses all professional obligations.” It does not.

## 7.2 The second error is unilateral burden management

Rui's more important correction is that Sumire should have consulted them. Sumire had assumed the relevant decision was hers alone:

- family needs help;
- therefore I must provide it;
- therefore I must personally absorb the professional conflict;
- therefore I must evaluate whether I still deserve my place.

The mature alternative is not irresponsible dependence. It is **answerable interdependence**:

> bring the conflict into the relationship early enough that other legitimate stakeholders can help define the options.

The unit can still tell Sumire she made a bad professional call. Consultation does not guarantee agreement. But it prevents care from becoming secret jurisdiction over everyone's consequences.

## 7.3 Family love and professionalism are both real claims

Sumire's arc is strongest when the story refuses to make either side unreal.

She genuinely loves her family and is not wrong to care about the brewery. She genuinely chose professional idolhood and is not wrong to owe colleagues reliability. The developmental task is therefore not choosing which value was “actually” authentic.

It is learning to govern **value plurality**.

This becomes the foundation for the later `全部欲しい` ethic.

# 8. Close reading V — brother, reciprocal sacrifice, and the collapse of the one-dream-at-a-time model

## 8.1 The sibling relationship is built around mutual protection

Sumire's brother is not a simple beneficiary of her sacrifice. He also sacrifices.

When family finances deteriorate, he gives up overseas study and later prepares to inherit the brewery so Sumire can continue idolhood. Sumire responds by considering the inverse sacrifice: leave idolhood, protect the brewery, and reopen his piano future.

Each wants the other to be free.

Each attempts to create that freedom by becoming less free personally.

This produces **reciprocal sacrifice recursion**:

> A sacrifices for B -> B experiences A's sacrifice as debt/injustice -> B sacrifices for A -> A experiences B's sacrifice as debt/injustice -> repeat.

The problem is not lack of love. It is that self-denial has become the default language in which love proves itself.

## 8.2 Sumire acquires a second dream that cannot be reduced to the first

Sumire is explicit that her first dream was her brother's piano future. But TRINITYAiLE generates a dream of her own: `三人でトップアイドルになる`.

This is a major longitudinal transition.

Idolhood began partly as an instrument for another person's future. It becomes intrinsically hers.

The transformation does **not** require her to stop caring about the brother's dream. Instead, the narrative confronts her with the harder proposition that two real desires can coexist.

## 8.3 The attempted withdrawal is proof logic in sacrificial form

When the family crisis intensifies, Sumire decides she must leave TRINITYAiLE. Her reasoning repeatedly invokes the danger of burdening others or interfering with their dreams. She treats the two-person qualifier evidence as proof that Rui and Yu can continue without her.

The same wound returns:

> if I can be removed and the system still works, removal may be the morally responsible choice.

This is precisely why exact-three identity must become more than performance utility. Rui and Yu are not asking whether they can technically operate without Sumire. They are asserting standing to prefer a life and unit that includes her.

# 9. Close reading VI — asking for help as self-authorship

## 9.1 Rui/Yu intervention initially risks overreach

Rui and Yu travel to Yamagata and mobilize resources. Their desire to help is sincere. But Sumire correctly notes that their presence alone does not solve a business with declining customers and inadequate money.

More importantly, Sumire objects that she never asked for help.

This is a real authority challenge.

Care does not automatically become legitimate because the caregiver has a good plan.

## 9.2 The decisive moment restores Sumire's choice

Rui eventually states the governing condition: if Sumire does not need help, Rui will leave. Sumire must choose `自分の意志で`.

Only then does Sumire say the full thing:

- she wants to sing with Rui and Yu;
- she wants to help her family;
- she does not know how to reconcile the two;
- `私を、助けて`.

The developmental importance is enormous.

Earlier Sumire acts as though a desire is not morally speakable until she has already solved its cost to everyone else. Asking for help reverses the sequence:

> **first state the true plurality of wants; then let the social system participate in solving the constraint.**

That is not childish dependence. It is a more adult form of authorship because it acknowledges both limits and stakeholders.

## 9.3 `全部欲しい` is constrained optimization, not fantasy

Rui's rhetoric—want all of it—could become sentimental if the plot resolved the problem by miracle. Instead, the source gives material mechanism:

- inspect business conditions;
- distinguish product quality from market-access/demographic problems;
- use professional visibility to create demand;
- work with local institutions;
- obtain production assistance;
- draw sponsors;
- expand the brewery's labor/economic capacity.

The mature proposition is therefore:

> **before treating a painful choice as metaphysically zero-sum, test whether collective action can change the constraint set.**

Some conflicts remain tragic. This one proves that not all apparently tragic conflicts are fixed.

## 9.4 Help does not erase contribution

Sumire does not become passive after asking. She performs, organizes, works, brings local knowledge, involves family, and publicly inhabits the resulting project.

The point is not “others solve things for Sumire.”

It is:

> **Sumire's authorship can include receiving infrastructure she did not create alone.**

That becomes important later when creative work similarly depends on composers, production spaces, rivals, managers, and collaborators without ceasing to be authored work.

# 10. Close reading VII — sisterhood, age, and reciprocal care

## 10.1 Chosen sisterhood is emotionally real

Rui and Yu increasingly call Sumire their little sister. Sumire calls them her older sisters. The language follows demonstrated acts of care rather than functioning only as decorative unit branding.

When Sumire's father is hospitalized, Rui/Yu alter schedules and travel with her. Sumire can finally admit that she is frightened rather than only reassure others.

This relationship becomes one of the mechanisms through which proof captivity weakens: there are people who continue to claim her when she is not currently producing the best performance.

## 10.2 Sister language also contains a hierarchy risk

The same language can create a care-reception problem. Rui and Yu are older. Sumire is still physically developing. They may have legitimate reasons to impose different training assumptions.

But “little sister” can be heard by Sumire as:

> protected because not fully equal.

The mature architecture cannot resolve this by banning protection or pretending age is irrelevant.

It needs **age-aware equal standing**.

## 10.3 Care must be reversible

A strong sign of maturation is that Sumire does not remain the permanent recipient. In the 2025 challenge aftermath she worries about Rui/Yu, manages practical details, and jokes that she will have to stay with them because their future concerns her.

The direction of care can reverse.

That is important evidence that sisterhood has not frozen her into permanent child status.

# 11. Close reading VIII — Christmas, professionalized disappointment, and recognition without proof

## 11.1 Sumire has been working long enough that ordinary leisure is unfamiliar

The 2021 Christmas event establishes a mundane but load-bearing fact: an entire Christmas Eve without work is unusual for Sumire. She has worked through seasonal events since child acting and continued doing so as an idol.

When she finally has a day off, she initially does not know what to do with it.

This is not merely cute workaholic texture. It shows that professional identity has occupied developmental time usually available for ordinary peer rituals.

## 11.2 The private party becomes a gratitude project

Sumire becomes excited by the possibility of a real private Christmas party and decides to surprise Rui/Yu. She wants to prepare the cake, decorations, and gifts herself because she wants to express gratitude.

That motive is affectionate and authentic.

Yet the familiar proof pattern appears: she refuses help because the care will mean more if she can personally produce it.

Her gratitude becomes a small authorship test.

## 11.3 Work cancels the plan and Sumire translates pain into professionalism

A last-minute job interrupts the day off. Sumire repeatedly tells herself:

- work is work;
- sudden schedule changes are normal;
- busy idols are fortunate;
- she should change her feelings quickly.

But she cannot fully do so. Concentration suffers; she privately admits she had wanted the party far more than she expected.

The developmental point is not that professional discipline is wrong. Sumire **does** need to perform the accepted job well.

The problem is the extra premise:

> if I am a real professional, disappointment should cease to count.

# 15. Close reading XII — age-aware equal standing and the marathon proof trap

Her warmth should not be modeled as compliance.

- experience with public expectations;
- risk literacy about stable performance;

The mature character remains useful but becomes less dependent on usefulness as the proof of place.

- former actor;

Her child-acting decline teaches that avoiding failure is not enough. The 99-win arc teaches that winning can become defensive. The marathon demonstrates real risk. None of this means she ceases to care about results.

Sumire remains competitive.

The mature rule is:

> **a result is information and consequence, not a total verdict on standing.**

## 18.8 She increasingly tolerates dependence that preserves decision standing

The phrase “ask for help” is easy to sentimentalize. Sumire's actual mature pattern is more specific:

- she can receive help;
- she retains voice in whether help is accepted;
- she contributes to execution;

That is **dependent action without dependent personhood**.

# 26. P2-B4 Sumire conclusion

Sumire enters TRINITYAiLE already carrying a long professional history, but that history does not grant secure standing. It has taught her nearly the opposite. Work can disappear even when done correctly. A child can be praised yet still lose a place. Love can be converted into labor. A family member's dream can become a reason to work harder. Cheerfulness can make costs easier for everyone else to bear.

Rui and Yu recruit her because they recognize a real expressive capacity they lack. That gives Sumire a positive professional reason to join them, but it cannot by itself solve the deeper problem. The missed qualifier proves that the two can win without her. Sumire then makes the characteristic proof-captive inference: if the system can function without me and I create inconvenience, perhaps my presence lacks meaning.

TRINITYAiLE's answer is not that she is technically indispensable to every task. It is that **the group is not only a task**. `三人でトリエル` names a chosen constitution. Sumire's presence has meaning because three people repeatedly decide to build a shared professional and relational life in which each has standing beyond immediate utility.

The family arc makes the same problem larger. Sumire and her brother love each other so strongly that each becomes willing to kill a personal future to protect the other's. Sumire initially assumes mature responsibility means choosing which desire must die. Her decisive development comes when she stops pretending she can solve the entire moral equation before speaking. She says that she wants to sing, wants to help her family, does not know how to do both, and asks for help.

That request becomes a model for mature authorship.

The resulting `全部欲しい` ethic is not a guarantee that every conflict can be solved. It is a procedural refusal to declare constraints fixed before relationships, institutions, professional knowledge, money, labor, and alternative arrangements have been tested. Sumire can receive collective help because the desire remains hers and the decision to accept help is restored to her.

Later arcs show that the old proof reflex survives in subtler forms. At Christmas, she wants gratitude to be expressed through a successful secret project and initially treats disappointment as something a professional should simply suppress. After defeat, role seriousness can still become self-blame. When Rui/Yu protect her younger body, she understands their love but still wants a difficult marathon to prove she is not a child. When writing music, she can still imagine that a legitimate idea ought to prove originality by being free of visible influence.

The mature answer is increasingly consistent across domains:

- she can be younger without being lower;
- she can be helped without being replaced;
- she can be responsible without owning every outcome;
- she can be useful without usefulness being the rent she pays for belonging;
- she can inherit without being derivative;
- she can take risks without turning risk into a compulsory adulthood test;
- she can love her brother without either sibling purchasing the other's future through self-erasure;
- she can hold family, idolhood, past acting, current creative work, and ordinary youth as several real parts of one life.

Her 2023 intervention in TRINITYAiLE's win-streak doctrine is especially important because it shows the younger member becoming the group's historical expert. Rui and Yu know excellence and strategy; Sumire knows what it feels like when stable, mistake-free performance ceases to open a future. She can therefore tell the unit something its older members cannot tell themselves as quickly. “Youngest” no longer means epistemically junior in all domains.

The 2025 marathon likewise refuses a simplistic emancipation story. Sumire's drive to finish is admirable and partly proof-captive. Rui/Yu's fear is loving and partly status-threatening. Makino's accompaniment helps without taking the goal. Sumire finishes partly because other people wait for and accompany her. She can finally say that shared enablement does not make the achievement less hers.

Late creative work completes the pattern at the level of art. Sumire does not need a melody purified of Sunny Peace, Tsuki no Tempest, family history, industry collaboration, or prior experience. If she and the others choose how to arrange those inheritances, the result can become theirs.

That is the final meaning of plural authorship:

> **Sumire's adulthood is not a future moment when she needs nobody, owes nobody, resembles nobody, and can do every task alone. It is the growing capacity to remain the author of a life that contains other people.**
