# 4. Formation constitution — complementary incompleteness becomes reciprocal illumination

The origin tranche supplies the most important anti-archetype correction:

> **SUNNY PEACE is not formed from five people who are already “sunny.”**

Its members instead carry different forms of interrupted or misrecognized light.

- **Sakura** has affective generativity but enters idolhood with little formal knowledge and a history in which smiling was once compulsory burden-management.
- **Chisa** possesses real desire but has learned to moralize independent desire as danger to Saki.
- **Shizuku** has extraordinary idol/fan literacy but has learned that public visibility can become punishment.
- **Rei** has elite discipline but initially uses idolhood instrumentally as professional proof.
- **Haruko** has experience, care, endurance, and institutional memory but years of weak recognition have eroded belief that she herself can still shine.

Makino's formation logic in Shizuku's origin explicitly seeks people whose strengths and insufficiencies can move one another forward. Haruko is not veteran decoration; she is structurally necessary because the younger members need someone who can teach, encompass, and persist.

The later unit history validates the origin interpretation of **circulated light**. The strengths are not merely additive. They become corrective:

- expertise corrects naivete without extinguishing enthusiasm;
- audience literacy corrects inward professionalism;
- relational sensitivity makes vulnerability speakable;
- care makes ambition survivable;
- Sakura's affective ignition gives technical and institutional competence a reason to reach outward.

This is why the mature unit is not well described as “balanced personalities.” Balance is static. SUNNY PEACE is **dynamic mutual answerability**.

# 5. Center and leadership — differentiated role without sovereignty

SUNNY PEACE has a real center structure.

In early Hoshimi material Shizuku can say:

> `センターはさくらちゃんだから……`

The later Tokyo songwriting crisis also gives Sakura an explicit leadership designation when Rei says:

> `さくらは私達のリーダーなんだから`

Those facts should not be flattened away in the name of equality.

But the unit simultaneously refuses center sovereignty. Sakura's own early response to the idea that only the center matters is:

> `そういうのは関係ない`

and she grounds her force in the others' smiles.

The later chronology clarifies what this means constitutionally.

Sakura's role includes:

- visible center function;
- affective ignition;
- ritualized unit calls;
- integrative leadership;
- unusually strong audience-facing charisma.

It does **not** include:

- sole technical authority;
- sole creative authorship;
- unilateral control of member career choices;
- exclusive responsibility for unit morale;
- unlimited bodily obligation to protect the unit's rank.

The cleanest mature formulation is therefore:

> **`CENTER_WITHOUT_SOVEREIGNTY_SUNNY_PEACE`: Sakura is a differentiated center whose authority is strongest when it remains answerable to the specialized judgment, bodies, desires, and support of the other four.**

# 6. Early developmental ecology — vulnerability is a shared work object

`hoshimi_007_drop_of_smile` provides a small-scale model of how SUNNY PEACE treats member difficulty.

Shizuku's inability to smile naturally is not read as proof that she is constitutionally unfit for idolhood. Sakura proposes training. Rei evaluates the problem in practical terms. Haruko supplies concrete methods. Chisa eventually becomes the key peer because she recognizes Shizuku's actual idol knowledge, seriousness, and fear.

Chisa's position matters. She does not help by presenting herself as a superior normal person repairing a deficient member. She says, in effect, that if Shizuku is disqualified by awkwardness, then Chisa is too. The developmental move is:

> **shared incompleteness -> relational practice -> increased capacity.**

This is a precursor to later unit governance. The group does not become safe by lowering standards. It becomes developmental because standards can be pursued **without making present weakness a verdict on personhood or membership**.

# 7. Haruko and the nonpossessive unit — indispensability does not equal ownership

`hoshimi_008_make_up_her_mind` tests whether SUNNY PEACE's language of need can become possessive.

Haruko receives a credible musical-theater opportunity and seriously considers leaving idolhood. The members want her to stay. The manager recognizes that she is indispensable to a still-consolidating SUNNY PEACE. Yet Rei states the governing limit: the decision belongs to Haruko.

This produces an important unit principle:

> **A member can be structurally important without becoming unit property.**

Haruko eventually chooses idolhood again because she recognizes that people still believe in her and because she wants to reach the important moment together with the group:

> `今はみんなとアイドルしたいって思ったの`

and:

> `その瞬間を、私はみんなと一緒に迎えたい`

That is not coerced retention. It is **membership reauthorization**.

The unit is stronger because Haruko can leave in principle and chooses continuation in practice.

# 8. Differentiation without dissolution — Tokyo and collective creative governance

By `tokyo_003_shiny_melody`, SUNNY PEACE has acquired a new problem: professional growth means the five no longer spend all of their time together.

The danger is not open hostility. It is gradual **loss of communicative access**.

Sakura interprets the distance through center/leader responsibility and worries that she must hold everybody together. The others reveal parallel anxieties. The repair is not to reverse professional differentiation or demand identical schedules. They restore shared low-pressure time, talk, and then use that renewed access to produce work.

The songwriting process is especially important because it moves SUNNY PEACE from **collective training** into **collective authorship**.

Different members want different things:

- Shizuku values continuity with the idol tradition/style she knows deeply;
- Rei wants challenge and novelty;
- Chisa can see the merits and practical limits of both positions;
- Sakura wants the work to feel recognizably bright and SUNNY PEACE-like;
- Haruko helps maintain an environment where disagreement remains usable.

No one simply wins the argument. The final work emerges by combination and late-night five-person conversation.

The constitutional transition is:

> **difference stops being evidence that the unit is drifting apart and becomes material from which the unit can author something none of the members would have produced alone.**

This supports:

> **`DIFFERENTIATED_BELONGING_THROUGH_RENEWED_COAUTHORSHIP_SUNNY_PEACE`.**

# 9. Audience reciprocity — from performance philosophy to unit practice

Tokyo supplies the clearest explicit shared idol philosophy.

SUNNY PEACE distinguishes its goal from both pure victory and one-way entertainment. The members describe receiving fan feeling and returning it through song. The manager summarizes the structure as a circle/bond between fans and performers.

This is more precise than “SUNNY PEACE wants to make people smile.”

The unit's mature audience relation is a loop:

> **audience feeling -> performer reception -> authored performance -> audience reception -> returned feeling -> renewed performer meaning.**

`event_2024_003_st-eve-2403-race` then turns the philosophy into institutionally organized practice.

The national tour is built around `繋ぐ` — connecting:

- SUNNY PEACE with fans across Japan;
- members with fan dreams;
- fans with one another and with shared places;
- private letters with public action.

Shizuku's fan-side knowledge is especially important to the project concept, but the tour does not remain “Shizuku's fan project.” The five participate in one another's fan encounters. A Shizuku fan can enjoy Sakura/Rei; Haruko's fan becomes a SUNNY PEACE fan; a hospitalized group fan receives a live performance; places touched by the group become continuing fan sites and memories.

The unit learns that the audience has not merely consumed SUNNY PEACE. Fans have:

- sustained the members;
- carried songs into schools and hospitals;
- built local communities;
- made performances meaningful enough to alter the performers' own understanding of idolhood.

By the tour endpoint the title's “connection” is reciprocal rather than promotional rhetoric.

# 10. Competitive ambition is constitutive — but rank is not sovereign

SUNNY PEACE is not an anti-ranking or anti-competition unit.

From formation onward the five choose to aim at major competition. Later they become BIG4, want the top, seek stronger rivals, and in 2026 explicitly state that BIG4 does not mean they have reached the summit.

The unit therefore rejects a false opposition:

- **warmth / audience care**, or
- **competitive excellence / victory**.

It wants both.

The actual problem is **jurisdiction**: when may a metric, rank, or result decide what the unit is allowed to do to its own members?

That problem becomes visible twice—first through Rei, then through Sakura.

# 11. Rei's BIG4 crisis — metric capture and non-degrading competition

In `event_2023_009_st-eve-2309-backside`, BIG4 success creates a new danger. Rei fears stagnation and receives the message that even their current best is insufficient for the summit. Her historically understandable result-orientation turns the unit goal into a private optimization problem.

She escalates training, hides weakness, and collapses.

> `私達はみんなを喜ばせ、楽しませるために アイドルをやっているの`

> `苦しいことも楽しいことも みんなで分かち合えるから……一人で悩まないでね？`

> `みんなで ――サニピで、少しずつ前に進んで行きます`

She returns new training/strategy ideas to the group and still says she wants SUNNY PEACE to go higher.

This establishes a unit governance rule:

> **Ambition is legitimate; privately converting shared ambition into unilateral bodily debt is not.**

# 12. `stellar_005` — the constitutional crisis of center burden

`stellar_005_we_are_the_sun` is the clearest P2-D stress test.

Sakura collapses after sustained overwork. The other four immediately tell her they can cover for her. This initially appears to validate distributed responsibility.

But the four then begin overworking themselves in order to prove that they can compensate.

That second failure is crucial. It prevents the unit ethic from being reduced to:

> “support the center harder.”

The real problem is structural. If every member may destroy herself whenever another member needs rest, then the group has merely **distributed self-sacrifice**, not solved it.

Makino shuts the group down and rest becomes unit-wide infrastructure.

The subsequent conversation makes the constitutional rule explicit.

Rei asks Sakura to let all of SUNNY PEACE carry the burden:

> `その重荷はサニーピースの全員で背負わせてちょうだい`

Shizuku says pain should be spoken and divided while joy is multiplied:

> `辛いことは話して、五人で分け合って…… 喜びは五人で何倍にも膨らませる……`

Chisa states:

> `サニーピースは五人で一つだよ`

Later:

> `私達は五人揃ってサニーピース`

The mature claim is therefore not “SUNNY PEACE prevents overwork.” It plainly does not.

It is:

> **SUNNY PEACE acquires a norm by which overwork can be identified as a unit failure even when the motive is noble, and by which burden-sharing must include the right to interrupt shared self-destruction.**

# 13. Care must include the caregiver

The same crisis generalizes beyond Sakura.

Haruko is often the member who notices, feeds, paces, reassures, and interrupts. But her own origin and career-choice story prevent the unit from treating care as her permanent utility function.

Shizuku's later creative leadership likewise prevents “quiet support” from becoming her professional destiny. Chisa's development prevents care from meaning self-suppression. Rei's crisis shows that a technical/disciplinary authority must be supportable. Sakura's collapse shows that the visible center must be supportable.

Thus:

> **Every recurrent support role in SUNNY PEACE is constitutionally reversible.**

A member can give care without forfeiting the right to receive it, compete, lead, specialize, rest, or pursue independent desire.

This is the unit-scale version of the origin freeze's proposition that **care can include the caregiver**.

# 14. Supported risk — answerability is not risk abolition

The post-`stellar_005` material does not produce a utopian “never strain yourself” contract.

The members explicitly refuse to promise that they will never push too hard. Instead, they ask Makino to watch them and help preserve the conditions under which their own seriousness does not become uncontrolled self-harm.

That distinction becomes decisive around Sakura's health in `stellar_010` and `stellar_011`.

Sakura wants to continue the VENUS Grand Prix with the other four despite medically serious risk. The manager and unit do not treat her autonomy as unlimited permission, but neither do they reduce professional care to automatic prohibition. The eventual arrangement is **supported risk**:

- medical reality remains real;
- Sakura's desire remains real;
- the four members commit to support her;
- Makino commits to monitoring/support;
- the performance occurs under bounded conditions.

The ethical model is not “the group lets Sakura sacrifice herself for them.”

It is:

> **the group makes a consequential choice answerable to health information, reciprocal commitment, and continuing monitoring.**

# 15. Five pieces — mutual support without center erasure

Immediately before the TRINITYAiLE semifinal in `stellar_011`, Sakura corrects the idea that only she is receiving support:

> `私だけが支えられてる訳じゃない……`

and:

> `私達は五つのピース、みんながみんなを支えてる……`

> `五人で支え合って、影響を与え合ってきたから`

> `私達がさくらちゃんを支えるから`

> `さくらちゃんの笑顔で私達を支えてね`

This is the mature answer to the center question.

Sakura's smile still has differentiated force. She is not demoted into one arbitrary interchangeable member. But her force is **one support inside a reciprocal system**.

The five-piece model therefore rejects both:

1. **heroic-center monopoly** — Sakura carries SUNNY PEACE while the others reinforce her; and
2. **anti-center flattening** — because personhood is equal, distinctive center function must be denied.

The text permits **differentiated role + reciprocal standing**.
