# 4. Longitudinal relationship reconstruction

## 4.1 Makino is not Rui's vocational origin

The first necessary boundary is causal.

Rui's ambition is older than this relationship. Her elite discipline, paternal proof structure, TRINITYAiLE leadership, fixation on the summit, and desire to surpass inherited standards all precede Makino's later importance.

Accordingly, the relationship should not be written as “Makino teaches Rui to become herself” or “Rui becomes an idol because of Makino.”

Makino's later role is more specific:

> **He becomes a trusted witness whose judgment Rui voluntarily makes personally important.**

That distinction protects Rui's authorship while still allowing genuine dependence and attachment to develop.

## 4.2 2022 — trust, contrast, and the request for privileged witness

`card_rui_004_st-card-rui-05-date-00` provides the earliest load-bearing compact in this ledger.

Rui states plainly:

> `大丈夫ですよ。{user}さんのことは信頼していますから`

Her comparison with her former manager matters. She remembers that the previous manager did not get closer than necessary and recalls that before transferring she had already imagined:

> `移籍する前から、この人が私のマネージャーだったら`

Once Makino actually is her manager, the difference becomes experiential. Rui says no manager has ever devoted himself to supporting her in this way. The relation changes what she can notice about herself:

> `一人では気付けなかったことも`  
> `{user}さんと一緒なら気付けるんです`

and culminates in two explicit requests:

> `だから、これからも私の支えになって下さい`

> `私の努力が実を結ぶ瞬間を一番近くで見ていて下さい`

This is already personally exceptional. Rui is not simply asking for competent scheduling. She is assigning Makino a preferred witnessing position in relation to her effort and future achievement.

Yet the same source prevents premature romance settlement. Rui invites him to walk together and notes that the place is famous as a `デートスポット`, but she immediately disclaims deep meaning. P2-C therefore preserves the scene as **dyadic salience plus explicit category non-settlement**, not as a canonical date.

## 4.3 Praise becomes personally wanted rather than merely deserved

The Rui bond adds another structural change.

Rui drafts a message intended for Makino:

> `期待に応えられるよう、私、もっと頑張ります！`  
> `もっと頑張るので、もっと褒めて下さい！`

She becomes intensely embarrassed when she accidentally sends it to Rio.

The point is not that Rui suddenly requires praise to function. Her competence remains intact. The development is that Makino's recognition has become something she can **want**, not merely something a sufficiently excellent result will compel.

That matters against Rui's broader proof architecture. Earlier Rui often treats achievement as the mechanism through which recognition must be forced into existence. Here she wants relational affirmation directly.

The same bond later shows Rui preparing drinks, snacks, and food for a solo workday in which Makino will accompany her. When Haruko asks whom the carefully prepared `心を込めた` offering is for, Rui insists it is only gratitude yet admits:

> `言っても何もやましいことはないはずなのに`  
> `妙に焦ってしまったわ……一体なんなのかしら……`

Exact bond chronology is not safely fixed. P2-C therefore uses it as **pre-category affective self-consciousness** without inserting it mechanically between dated cards.


## 4.4 2023 Valentine — romantic-category salience before romantic self-settlement

The 2023 Valentine card is the most important intermediate state because it blocks retrospective flattening.

Rui begins from genuine experiential absence:

- she has not dated;
- she has not confessed;
- she has difficulty making a romantic CM and song feel personally inhabited;
- she has tried romance fiction as research rather than drawing on lived experience.

Makino's advice is simple: think of Valentine's Day as a day to convey feeling to an important person.

Rui's response is revealing because she says the force of the advice depends partly on its speaker:

> `誰が言ってくれたかが重要ですから`

However, the card explicitly preserves a non-romantic prospective reading of Rui's **public performance method**. Asked what she imagined during the CM, Rui says she thought about important members and fans and adds:

> `といっても、恋愛的な意味ではないんですけど`

P2-C treats this sentence as information-frontier authority. It prevents the 2026 endpoint from rewriting the entire 2023 performance as a transparent romantic confession.

The private Makino-facing behavior is different. Rui tries to give him chocolate before Valentine's Day because she worries that giving it on the day could imply:

> `『そういう』意味`

She ultimately gives it on the day. Her spoken content remains substantially professional and relationally grateful: Makino lets TRINITYAiLE work freely, all three members trust him, and they can continue walking together with him.

Then Rui repeats his formulation that today is a day to convey feeling to an important person and immediately says:

> `勘違いしないで下さいね？`

Makino answers:

> `え……何を？`

This is an unusually useful asymmetry marker. Rui has become conscious that the situation is **romantically interpretable**. Makino's fixed response shows that he does not even understand the category she is trying to deny at that moment.

The correct 2023 proposition is therefore not “Rui is already consciously in love.” It is:
