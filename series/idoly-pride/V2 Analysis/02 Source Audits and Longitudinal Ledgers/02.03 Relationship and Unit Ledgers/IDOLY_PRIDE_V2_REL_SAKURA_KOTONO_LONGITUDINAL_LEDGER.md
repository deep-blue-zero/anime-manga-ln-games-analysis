# 0. Responsibility, authority, and central problem

This ledger is the current Phase-2 relationship authority for **Kawasaki Sakura / Nagase Kotono** through `IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A`.

The pair is unusually easy to flatten because Mana's legacy is present at the moment Sakura and Kotono become narratively important to one another. Sakura has a voice the public hears as Mana-like; Kotono has built her vocation partly around completing Mana's interrupted future; Sakura later learns that the transplanted heart in her body belonged to Mana; and Kotono can literally hear that heart beating in Sakura's chest. Those facts are real. They do **not** mean that the mature Sakura/Kotono relationship is fundamentally “Mana's sister relating to Mana's replacement.”

The relationship changes category across time.

1. **successor-pressure relation:** both girls are being measured through Mana, though in opposite ways;
2. **proxy-burden relation:** Kotono asks Sakura to perform the Mana-role Kotono cannot reproduce;
3. **anti-replacement negotiation:** Sakura rejects that role and Kotono accepts Sakura's jurisdiction over her own song;
4. **living peer/rival relation:** they begin to matter to one another as Sakura and Kotono rather than as two routes back to Mana;
5. **comparative-distance crisis:** Sakura's success becomes painful precisely because Kotono trusts and values her;
6. **non-extractive repair:** Sakura restores ordinary companionship without making confession the price of care;
7. **differentiated rivalry:** Kotono stops wanting to become Sakura while continuing to call Sakura special and wanting to defeat her;
8. **plural witness relation:** late Kotono asks Sakura for Sakura's perspective on Mana rather than asking Sakura to reproduce Mana.

Several reductive models fail against the full record:

- **“Sakura is just Mana's replacement for Kotono.”** This describes an early pressure structure, not the mature relationship.
- **“Kotono always respected the boundary between Sakura and Mana.”** She articulates that boundary early and then violates it by asking Sakura to reproduce Mana's unfinished song.
- **“Sakura agreed, therefore there was no imposition.”** Sakura's consent is real; Kotono later explicitly says she pushed everything onto Sakura.
- **“The donor heart gives Kotono a special claim over Sakura.”** It gives Kotono a grief object and Sakura a bodily history. It does not create jurisdiction.
- **“Once Mana is released, the relationship becomes uncomplicated friendship.”** Living rivalry creates a new problem: Sakura becomes a comparison target whom Kotono trusts too much to discuss the comparison easily.
- **“Jealousy means the friendship is false.”** Kotono explicitly loves/values SUNNY, trusts Sakura, envies her, and wants to defeat her at the same time.
- **“Repair cures competitive insecurity.”** The 2023 event explicitly leaves Kotono still demanding more results after relational repair.
- **“Sun/moon differentiation ends rivalry.”** It removes imitation while preserving the desire for a decisive future contest.
- **“`特別` or the playful `デート` language proves romance.”** The locked text establishes exceptional peer/rival importance and a joke explicitly denied as literal dating, not a romantic relationship state.

The strongest model is therefore:

> **`DIFFERENTIATED_RECIPROCAL_RIVALRY_AFTER_PROXY_RELEASE_SAKURA_KOTONO`: Sakura and Kotono first meet each other inside a Mana-shaped successor field. Kotono initially recognizes that Sakura should not have to manage Kotono's grief, but later recruits Sakura's Mana-like voice into the memorial project that Kotono herself cannot complete. Sakura accepts out of care, and Kotono later recognizes the burden as something she pushed onto Sakura. The donor-heart revelation intensifies the temptation to treat Sakura as continuity, but Sakura rejects replacement and Kotono accepts Sakura's right to decide what she sings. Their relationship then becomes genuinely living: each can want the other beside her and against her, support the other's unit, learn from the other's strengths, experience painful status comparison, repair distance without demanding disclosure, and compete without requiring identity convergence. By the mature 2024 frontier, Kotono no longer wants to become Sakura; she can name Sakura as personally special while describing sun and moon as different non-inferior forms and still promising to catch and overtake her. By 2026 Sakura can contribute to Kotono's understanding of Mana as one independent witness among several rather than as a vessel expected to make Mana present.**

Short form:

> **They become able to compete seriously because neither is required to become the other—or Mana.**

# 3. Relationship phase I — two successor problems, not one

The earliest pair dynamic is structurally asymmetric.

Kotono is **trying to move toward Mana**. Sakura is **trying not to be forced into Mana**.

That asymmetry matters because a generic “both girls struggle with Mana comparison” formulation is true but insufficient.

Kotono's problem is aspirational and memorial. Mana is a standard she actively tries to reproduce because her dead sister's interrupted path has become part of Kotono's reason for being an idol.

Sakura's problem is involuntary. She does not imitate Mana's voice on purpose. Public comparison and Kotono's grief create the possibility that simply singing naturally will be interpreted as assuming an identity she did not choose.

This yields the first relationship paradox:

> **Kotono is capable of recognizing Sakura's right not to carry Kotono's grief before Kotono is capable of ceasing to ask Sakura to carry it.**

In `hoshimi_014`, Kotono tells Sakura:

- `お姉ちゃんのことは、私とお姉ちゃんの問題`;
- `さくらは関係ない`;
- `自由に歌って`.

Those are not lies. They show real boundary recognition.

Later in the same narrative, however, Kotono admits that Sakura's voice shocked her because it did what Kotono could not. She says `どうして私じゃないんだろう`, then converts the difference into a proposed solution: `さくらがいてくれれば / お姉ちゃんの歌を再現出来る`.

This is not simple hypocrisy. It is a conflict between two true commitments:

1. Sakura should be free to be Sakura;
2. Kotono desperately wants Mana's interrupted song to reach the stage.

At this point the second commitment wins.

# 4. Sakura's consent and the ethics of proxy burden

Sakura agrees.

She says that if singing the song is Kotono's real wish, she will work with everything she has.

That fact prevents a model in which Kotono simply commandeers Sakura. Sakura is not passive material. She chooses to help a person she cares about.

But the inverse simplification also fails. **Consent does not change the origin of the role.** The role exists because:

- Mana died;
- Kotono believes the unfinished song should be reproduced;
- Kotono cannot reproduce Mana's voice;
- Sakura can approximate the missing function.

The later Kotono origin settles the accountability question more directly than retrospective analyst moralization could. Kotono says:

`……結局私は何もかもさくらに押しつけた`.

The mature ledger therefore uses:

> **`CONSENT_WITHIN_PROXY_PRESSURE`**

Sakura's choice is real. Kotono's imposition is real. The relationship becomes healthier by eventually preserving the first while dissolving the second.

# 5. The donor heart intensifies intimacy without creating jurisdiction

The heart revelation creates one of the pair's most emotionally intense scenes.

Kotono asks to hear Sakura's heartbeat. Sakura permits it. Kotono places her ear against Sakura and hears the living rhythm of the heart that once belonged to Mana. She cries. Sakura offers to let her continue listening.

Three propositions must remain separate.

### Bodily provenance

Mana was the donor. This is history.

### Grief meaning

Kotono can experience the heartbeat as a surviving physical connection to Mana. This is psychologically and textually real.

### Personal jurisdiction

Sakura is the living person whose body the heart now sustains. Donor history does not authorize Kotono to decide Sakura's songs, career, identity, or future.

The text immediately tests the distinction. Sakura says she will search for `川咲さくらの歌` and stop functioning as `長瀬麻奈の再来`. Kotono asks whether that means Sakura will not sing Mana's song. Sakura says yes.

Kotono's answer is the constitutional hinge:

`さくらが決めることだから`.

The line is not exuberant approval. It is more important than that: **Kotono recognizes the decision belongs to Sakura even when she dislikes the outcome.**

This ledger therefore rejects both mystical replacement and relational ownership.

---

# 6. Proxy release produces a new dyad rather than ending the old one

Sakura does not use self-authorship to sever Kotono.

After refusing the Mana role, Sakura asks Kotono to find her own song too. She says:

- `琴乃ちゃんと一緒に頑張りたいの`;
- `ううん、琴乃ちゃんと一緒じゃなきゃ出来ない`.

The mature significance is not literal dependency. The surrounding scene is about both girls ceasing to live as extensions of Mana. Sakura is saying that **differentiated persons can still choose a shared future**.

Mana herself formulates rivalry as one possible shape of that future, saying Sakura and Kotono should find their own songs, refine one another as rivals, and aim at the top.

The later corpus matters because it prevents this from remaining merely Mana's wish. Sakura and Kotono repeatedly **choose rivalry themselves** after Mana's authority has been reduced.

Thus:

> **`PROXY_RELEASE_ENABLES_LIVING_RIVALRY`**

The rival relation is not a leftover command from Mana. It becomes a self-authored relation between the living.

---

# 7. Relationship phase II — when the trusted person becomes the comparison target

The 2023 event is the most useful contradiction source after the Hoshimi anti-replacement sequence.

SUNNY PEACE has become BIG4. Tsuki is struggling. Kotono watches Sakura become more natural and competent in public work and tells her she is impressive.

Sakura correctly senses that Kotono is strained and offers to listen.

Kotono says two things that are easy to misread if separated:

- `さくらのことは信頼してる`;
- `さくらには、話せない`.

This is not evidence that trust is fake.

The exact problem is that Sakura is both:

- a trusted close peer;
- and the person who most directly embodies the professional status gap Kotono is trying not to feel.

Haruko names the mechanism more generally: sometimes the people who are closest are precisely the people to whom one cannot speak, especially when both take professional pride seriously.

The pair-specific model is:

> **`COMPETITIVE_PROXIMITY_INHIBITED_DISCLOSURE`**

The relationship has matured enough that Sakura matters too much for Kotono to discuss Sakura-comparison cheaply.

---

# 8. Non-extractive reconnection — care before explanation

Sakura's response is one of the strongest relational-care scenes in her corpus because it does not demand confession as payment.

Haruko tells her that Mana once repaired a similar distance through an ordinary curry outing. Sakura does not reproduce the relationship literally. She transfers the method: get the relationship inhabitable again first.

Her room entry is forceful and comedic. It should not be universalized as an always-correct method for withdrawal. But the later outing establishes the ethical center clearly.

When Kotono finally discloses her jealousy, Sakura is glad. Yet Sakura later tells her that the outing would still have mattered if Kotono had said nothing:

`琴乃ちゃんと久しぶりにゆっくり / お話し出来れば……それだけで嬉しいから`.

That makes the repair **non-extractive**.

Sakura creates room in which disclosure can happen. She does not define the outing's value by whether she successfully obtains Kotono's private account.

For the pair, this matters because their earlier Mana phase repeatedly involved questions of who has the right to ask another person to carry a private burden. The 2023 care scene exhibits a more mature jurisdictional grammar.

---

# 9. Jealousy becomes speakable without becoming shameful

Kotono says the difficult word directly:

`私、ずっとサニピに……さくらに嫉妬してた`.

Then she corrects the tense:

`ううん、今もしてる`.

The repair is not accomplished by pretending the jealousy is gone.

Sakura's answer is equally important. She does not reassure Kotono by saying competition does not matter. She says SUNNY also watches Tsuki's rapid growth and feels pressure. She has never regarded BIG4 status as proof that SUNNY is categorically above Tsuki.

Then both say they do not want to lose to the other.

This gives the relationship a stronger reciprocity than “successful Sakura comforts insecure Kotono.” The specific anxieties are not symmetrical—Kotono carries the heavier explicit envy/status wound—but the competitive seriousness is mutual.

The mature pair can therefore say:

> **I care about you enough to want you close, and I take you seriously enough to want to beat you.**

Those are not contradictions in this relationship.

---

# 10. Repair is not comparative cure

The 2023 event refuses a convenient endpoint.

Kotono feels better. She is glad to talk naturally with Sakura again. The performance succeeds. The dyadic rupture is substantially repaired.

Yet she still concludes that she needs more results.

That is why `RELATIONAL_REPAIR_WITHOUT_COMPARATIVE_CURE` remains necessary at this frontier.

A relationship can stop being broken before a person's self-evaluation system becomes healthy.

This prevents two opposite errors:

- blaming Sakura for Kotono's remaining status anxiety;
- crediting Sakura with curing a problem the text explicitly leaves active.

The later 2024 event is therefore a genuine **second-stage development**, not merely a repetition of the tonkatsu repair.

---

# 11. The BIG4 separation tests whether Sakura can care without overruling

When Kotono leaves Tsuki, Sakura's initial reaction is strong. She wants to go to Kotono and ask her not to leave. She says a world in which Kotono is gone from Tsuki is unacceptable to her.

If the scene stopped there, Sakura might simply repeat an interventionist care grammar.

Instead, Tsuki's members ask Sakura to trust them and trust Kotono. They explain that Kotono's decision is serious and that preserving a place for her is part of their own relationship with her.

Sakura changes course.

She later tells Kotono:

`琴乃ちゃんと月ストのみんなを / 信じることに決めたんだよ`.

This is important because non-intervention is not coded as indifference. Sakura cares enough to oppose the choice and enough to accept that care does not automatically grant veto authority.

When Kotono returns, the compressed exchange:

- Sakura: `おかえりなさい、琴乃ちゃん`;
- Kotono: `ただいま、さくら`;

marks restored belonging without pretending the separation never occurred.

---

# 12. Peer learning without identity imitation

Kotono's earlier envy contains an implicit imitation question: **why can I not be like Sakura?**

The 2024 event shows a more mature alternative.

Kotono remembers an earlier crisis in which Sakura said `絶対にうまくいく`. At the time Kotono read that confidence almost as a trait Sakura possessed and Kotono lacked.

Later she realizes that the confidence was relational: Sakura trusted the group acting together.

Kotono can now say to Tsuki:

`私達が力を合わせれば必ず間に合う`  
`絶対にうまくいくわ`.

This is not Kotono becoming Sakura. It is Kotono learning a **transferable practice of confidence** and translating it into her own leadership.

The distinction is crucial for the whole pair:

- replacement copies a person;
- peer learning adopts a competence while preserving personhood.

The pair's mature relationship strongly prefers the second.

---

# 13. Support acceptance and reciprocal institutional care

The same event extends the dyad into a broader Hoshimi network.

Sakura, Rei, Shizuku, and others offer help with Tsuki's new song and choreography. Kotono initially objects that it is Tsuki's battle. Sakura answers through the history of reciprocal support: Tsuki helped SUNNY before; now SUNNY can help Tsuki. Different units remain companions who debuted and developed together.

Kotono later says Sakura's intervention helped her accept the support.

This produces another relationship-level correction:

> **receiving help from a rival does not dissolve competition.**

Sakura can contribute to Tsuki becoming stronger and still want SUNNY to defeat it. Kotono can accept Sakura's help and still promise to overtake her.

That combination is a mature form of reciprocal standing rather than either dependency or hostility.

---

# 14. Sun and moon — differentiation without devaluation

The strongest mature Sakura/Kotono scene comes after the practical support work.

Kotono finally states the identity question directly. She had repeatedly wondered:

- how to become as strong as Sakura;
- how to shine like Sakura;
- why she could not do what Sakura could do.

Sakura gives a simple answer:

`私みたいになれなくて当然だよ`  
`だって琴乃ちゃんは琴乃ちゃんなんだから`.

Kotono accepts it and provides the metaphor that best describes the mature pair:

`眩しい太陽。優しい月明かり。どちらにも良さがあって`  
`どちらかが劣っている訳じゃない`.

This is **not** an equality claim in the sense that rank, popularity, skill, or current results are identical. It is a personhood/value claim: difference does not require inferiority.

The relationship has now solved two successive imitation problems:

1. Kotono does not need to become **Mana**;
2. Kotono does not need to become **Sakura**.

Sakura likewise does not need to become Mana or become less Sakura-like in order to protect Kotono.

---

# 15. `特別` — exceptional importance without unsupported romance

Immediately after the sun/moon differentiation, Sakura asks whether Kotono no longer thinks about SUNNY.

Kotono says that would be false:

`月のテンペストにとってサニーピースは特別で`  
`長瀬琴乃にとって川咲さくらは特別だから`.

This is strong relationship evidence.

It establishes Sakura as an exceptional person in Kotono's professional and relational world. It should not be diluted into “just another coworker.”

But the source immediately defines what Kotono does with that specialness:

- she no longer wants to become SUNNY;
- she wants Tsuki to create its own songs/stages;
- she wants Tsuki to become BIG4;
- she asks Sakura for a future decisive `月と太陽` contest;
- she promises to catch and surpass SUNNY.

The canonical category is therefore:

> **`SPECIAL_TRUSTED_PEER_RIVAL`**

not “romantic partner.”

The 2023 `デート` joke does not overturn this. Sakura explicitly says she is imitating Rio's invitation style, and Kotono answers `デートって……そんなんじゃないでしょ`.

Current relationship-category verdict:

- **TEXTUAL FACT:** trust, care, exceptional dyadic importance, rivalry, admiration/envy, mutual professional recognition, chosen continued relation;
- **TEXTUAL FACT:** playful date language exists and is denied as literal dating in context;
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** romantic attraction, confession, dating, exclusivity, reciprocal romantic self-identification, enacted couple status;
- **FORMAL OPEN ONLY:** future audiovisual review may refine affective intensity but cannot by itself rewrite textual status without evidence.

---

# 16. 2026 — from proxy vessel to plural witness

The 2026 answer-song event provides a late and unusually clean test of the model.

Kotono realizes that knowing Mana as her sister does not mean she fully knows **idol Nagase Mana**. She decides to solicit other perspectives.

She asks Haruko, Rio, Suzu—and Sakura.

Sakura initially wonders whether she is really the right person. Kotono answers that Sakura encountered Mana's feelings through the transplanted heart and is also an idol; therefore Sakura's account matters.

The crucial difference from early Hoshimi is what Kotono asks Sakura to do.

### Early

> reproduce Mana's unfinished song for me.

### Late

> tell me what **you** experienced and understood of Mana.

Sakura then contributes a perspective about competitive sensation and motivation grounded in her own embodied experience.

The late relation is therefore better described as:

> **`FROM_PROXY_VESSEL_TO_PLURAL_WITNESS`**

Sakura's donor history remains relevant. It no longer cancels Sakura's epistemic independence.

---

# 17. Relational asymmetry and reciprocity

The pair should not be flattened into mirror images.

### Sakura more often carries:

- early concern about harming Kotono;
- initiation of low-pressure reconnection;
- explicit reassurance that Tsuki remains a real rival;
- willingness to support Tsuki materially;
- a tendency to trust collective effort and offer confidence outward.

### Kotono more often carries:

- explicit envy;
- comparative status pain;
- fear that Sakura's growth proves Kotono inadequate;
- the earlier proxy imposition;
- later need to differentiate herself from Sakura.

Yet reciprocity is still real.

Sakura also wants to beat Kotono. Sakura is distressed by relational distance. Sakura initially wants to stop Kotono leaving. Kotono worries about the burden placed on Sakura, accepts Sakura's autonomy, learns from her without consuming her identity, welcomes mutual competition, and later asks for Sakura's independent perspective.

Thus:

> **reciprocity does not require identical wounds, identical roles, or identical amounts of visible insecurity.**

# 25. Capstone

The mature Sakura/Kotono relationship is not best understood as the bond between Mana's sister and Mana's successor. That formulation captures the field in which they first become entangled, but not what they build.

Kotono first tries to protect Sakura from her grief, then asks Sakura to carry that grief professionally. Sakura accepts because she cares, then later refuses to let care require self-replacement. Kotono accepts the refusal. Once that proxy structure breaks, the pair becomes capable of something harder and more durable: living rivalry.

Living rivalry introduces its own wounds. Sakura's success can make Kotono feel left behind. Kotono can trust Sakura and still be unable to tell Sakura why she is hurting. Sakura can want disclosure and still learn that care does not entitle her to it. Their repair therefore comes through ordinary time and food before explanation, then through the ability to speak jealousy without treating jealousy as betrayal.

The final differentiation is not “we are equal because we are the same” and not “we no longer compare.” Kotono explicitly continues to compare. She simply stops treating comparison as a demand to become Sakura. Sakura is the sun; Kotono chooses the moonlight. Neither metaphor makes the other inferior, and neither abolishes the scoreboard.

That is why `特別` can coexist with a promise to overtake.

> **Sakura becomes special to Kotono not because Sakura can reproduce the dead person Kotono lost, but because Sakura is a living person Kotono can trust, learn from, envy, support, be supported by, and still sincerely want to defeat.**

And Sakura's reciprocal choice is equally important: she does not protect Kotono by becoming less successful or less herself. She makes room for Kotono while continuing to run.

The pair's mature achievement is therefore not harmony without competition. It is **difference without devaluation, closeness without ownership, care without extraction, and rivalry without replacement.**
