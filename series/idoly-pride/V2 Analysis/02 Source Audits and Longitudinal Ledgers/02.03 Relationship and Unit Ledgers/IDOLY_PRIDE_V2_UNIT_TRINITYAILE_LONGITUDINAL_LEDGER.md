This is the canonical Phase-2 unit ledger for **TRINITYAiLE** under `IP-V2-SNAPSHOT-2026-08-13-A`.

Its object is TRINITYAiLE as an evolving **professional constitution**: the rules by which centerhood, support, age, expertise, care, risk, responsibility, competition, creative authorship, institutional help, and audience recognition are allowed to coexist across three non-interchangeable members.

It does not concatenate Rui, Yu, and Sumire into a group profile. The character ledgers remain authoritative for person-level causality. Rui/Makino remains owned by its relationship ledger. This document owns propositions that only become fully legible when the unit itself is treated as a changing institutional object.

The central architectural risk is to mistake one of two true observations for the whole unit:

1. **Rui really is center and the most explicit directional leader.**
2. **TRINITYAiLE repeatedly insists that Rui, Yu, and Sumire stand as full persons whose strength, desire, dissent, and responsibility cannot be subordinated to their role labels.**

The mature model requires both.

TRINITYAiLE does **not** mature by becoming egalitarian in the sense of erasing differentiated roles. Rui remains center. Yu remains unusually oriented toward support, strategy, translation, and enabling others. Sumire remains younger and is often treated through little-sister language. What changes is what these asymmetries are allowed to authorize.

The unit increasingly rejects the following conversions:

- center -> sole causal owner of victory or defeat;
- support -> permission to become smaller;
- seniority -> jurisdiction over a younger member's goals;
- youth -> epistemic inferiority across all domains;
- expertise -> authority to decide another person's aspiration;
- care -> unilateral sacrifice or rescue;
- winning -> proof that the unit deserves to exist;
- gratitude -> debt ownership by supporters;
- influence -> derivativeness;
- assistance -> loss of authorship.

The unit's mature constitutional principle is therefore:

> **TRINITYAiLE permits asymmetry of role only when all three members retain symmetrical standing as authors of what the unit becomes.**

This ledger does not make Phase-5 claims about vocal blend, camera hierarchy, choreography, arrangement, or stage blocking unless the underlying proposition is already textual. Formal performance questions remain routed to the audiovisual phase.

# 4. Formation constitution — complementarity before equality

TRINITYAiLE does not begin as three interchangeable peers.

Rui and Yu first form a two-person relation around a productive difference. Rui is direct, intensely achievement-oriented, and capable of recognizing Yu outside inherited social framing. Yu is socially adaptive, observant, and tactically sophisticated. Their pairing matters because each supplies something the other does not naturally generate.

The important early correction is that complementarity is not evidence of instrumental partnership. Rui refuses a model in which another member exists merely as a component of Rui's ascent. This distinction later becomes essential when Sumire enters.

Sumire is not recruited because the group needs a marketable younger face in the abstract. The origin explicitly gives her an expressive surplus Rui and Yu lack. She joins a hierarchy, but the hierarchy is already supposed to be justified by what each person actually contributes rather than by a fixed rank of human worth.

The formation constitution is therefore:

> **different people may occupy different professional positions because they bring different capacities; those positions do not decide whose desire counts.**

At this stage the proposition is aspirational. All three will later violate it in different ways.

# 5. Rui's center — legitimate office, dangerous proof machine

Rui's centerhood is not an illusion that later development needs to expose.

She wants the center. She is exceptionally capable. She supplies directional pressure, standard-setting, and a strong public identity. The text continues to treat her center as real long after Yu proves she can outperform Rui in a controlled contest.

The danger lies elsewhere.

Rui's history makes achievement unusually vulnerable to **proof conversion**. Because success becomes entangled with paternal recognition and abandonment, centerhood can become more than a job. It can become a place from which Rui tries to prove that she is sufficient, undeniable, and causally responsible for whether the whole structure survives.

This produces two predictable distortions:

1. **self-sufficiency:** if Rui truly deserves the summit, perhaps she should be able to carry it herself;
2. **burden monopoly:** if Rui is center and leader, perhaps defeat is principally her failure and recovery principally her obligation.

TRINITYAiLE's development does not remove Rui from center. It repeatedly narrows the jurisdiction centerhood is allowed to claim.

Mature principle:

> **center gives Rui special obligations and special visibility; it does not give her ownership of every outcome or of her teammates' permissible strength.**

# 6. Yu's secondness — from defensive ceiling to authored support

Yu's support role is equally easy to misread.

She is not merely the member who lacks the ambition to stand forward. Her origin establishes that attention and visible talent have social costs in her history. Choosing second can reduce friction. Once Rui recognizes Yu as herself, secondness acquires a positive object: Yu genuinely wants to stand beside and support Rui.

That does not automatically make the role healthy.

Yu begins to control her own visibility in ways she believes will make Rui or TRINITYAiLE shine better. The decisive crisis in `origin_tri_003_light_my_fire` is therefore not a center succession story. Rui's demand is more radical:

> **do not decide that loyalty requires you to be less capable than you are.**

Yu's narrow aggregate victory over Rui is constitutionally decisive. It proves that:

- Yu can defeat Rui;
- Rui can remain center;
- the relationship can survive full-strength contest;
- support can remain chosen without requiring inferiority.

This is why “Rui center / Yu support” remains a valid mature shorthand only if the second clause is understood as an authored professional orientation rather than a ceiling.

Yu later expands this support logic into production, acting, school leadership, and creative work. The unit-level implication is that support becomes increasingly **generative**: Yu creates conditions in which several people can be more fully present at once.

The constitutional guardrail is that Yu must remain one of them.

# 7. Sumire's entry — expressive surplus and the danger of usefulness as rent

Sumire enters from a different vulnerability.

She has professional history and real expressive value, but also a learned association between place and usefulness. The two-person qualifier/victory evidence makes her fear rational rather than merely insecure: Rui and Yu can in fact produce a successful TRINITYAiLE performance without her.

The mature unit could resolve this only by refusing a false argument.

It does **not** claim:

> “the unit literally cannot function without Sumire.”

Instead it moves toward:

> “the unit we choose to be is the three-person unit that includes Sumire.”

That distinction is foundational.

If belonging requires proof of technical irreplaceability, every member is permanently one bad comparison away from expulsion. Exact-three identity becomes stronger when it is admitted as a normative choice rather than disguised as an engineering necessity.

This also makes Sumire's later dissent possible. Once she does not have to rent her place through usefulness, her past can become knowledge the unit needs rather than merely evidence that she deserves to stay.

# 8. Full-strength reciprocity — Yu can beat Rui without abolishing hierarchy

`Light My Fire` is the clearest early constitutional test.

Yu's self-reduction is not corrected by putting her in Rui's place. Rui does not discover that the hierarchy was always fake. What changes is the hierarchy's compatibility with challenge.

The durable principle is:

> **a legitimate center must be able to remain center in a unit whose other members are permitted to reveal that they can surpass her in particular dimensions or moments.**

This is different from equalizing all skills or declaring every member equally suitable for every role.

It also creates a model for later Sumire development. Rui explicitly treats center as something Sumire can contest if she wants it. Rui will fight to keep it. That is not egalitarian rotation; it is contestable office.

The unit's mature hierarchy is therefore neither hereditary nor ceremonial. It remains connected to actual professional performance while losing metaphysical status.

# 9. Exact-three identity — chosen non-substitutability after two-person success

The Sumire absence crisis is one of the most important anti-sentimental pieces of evidence in the unit corpus.

Rui and Yu prove that they can win without her. The story refuses the easy reassurance that every member is physically or competitively indispensable at every moment.

TRINITYAiLE instead makes a stronger claim:

> **competitive sufficiency and constitutive belonging are different categories.**

The group can sometimes be operationally sufficient with fewer than three while remaining normatively exact-three.

This becomes the basis for later distributed careers, temporary separation, individual work, and differentiated expertise. Exact-three does not require constant physical co-presence or identical workload. It means the three have standing to define the unit and standing to refuse a future in which one member is silently optimized away because a narrower configuration can still function.

Transition:

`member value as indispensability proof`

becomes:

`member value as chosen coauthor standing`.

# 11. Family crisis — care must preserve beneficiary jurisdiction

The Sumire family sequence generalizes the unit's ethics beyond performance.

Sumire treats withdrawal as a way to protect her brother and teammates from the costs of her desire. Rui and Yu reject the decision because Sumire has made a life-restructuring bargain for others without letting them determine whether that bargain is what they want.

Rui initially risks the opposite mistake: comprehensive rescue can also become unilateral if the recipient's own choice is lost.

The decisive moment is Sumire asking for help and naming plural desire.

The unit's mature care rule is therefore not “never intervene.” It is:

> **intervention becomes more legitimate when it expands the recipient's viable choices and returns decision standing to the person whose life is at issue.**

The Yamagata solution depends on money, scheduling, business, staff, local institutions, and celebrity leverage. This permanently revises Rui's early self-sufficiency ideal. Authentic agency can require infrastructure.

The Kiriko motive guardrail remains: helpful institutional action can coexist with instrumental motives. Outcome alone does not prove pure benevolence.

# 12. Tokyo burden sharing — sacrifice without consultation is not automatically love

Later Tokyo material retests the same principle after the origin lesson is already known.

Rui's tendency under threat is to protect others by absorbing consequences herself. Yu and Sumire refuse the structure. The exact content varies by crisis, but the recurring unit claim is stable:

> **one member does not acquire unilateral jurisdiction over which person should bear shared risk merely because her motive is protective.**

This is a constitutional development because the unit can now criticize a behavior that once looked like exemplary leadership.

Protection remains valued. Self-sacrifice remains sometimes admirable. What is rejected is burden monopoly masquerading as care.

The mature alternative is **answerable interdependence**:

- name the risk;
- preserve relevant expertise and hierarchy;
- allow the affected members to answer;
- share consequences where the consequences are genuinely shared;
- escalate intervention when serious harm or impaired agency makes ordinary noninterference irresponsible.

# 13. Stellar defeat — responsibility without causal monopoly

`stellar_003_to_fly_higher` gives the unit one of its clearest explicit constitutions.

After defeat, Rui overtrains and argues that because she is leader and center she must pull the others forward. She then converts the result into sole causal blame.

Yu rejects the conversion: center, leader, and public gaze do not make Rui the entire unit. TRINITYAiLE is three people moving forward together, and victory or defeat belongs to all three.

The important distinction is not “nobody is individually responsible.”

A mature unit can still say:

- Rui made a poor decision;
- Yu failed in a particular support judgment;
- Sumire underperformed a task;
- one member has more responsibility for a role-specific failure.

What it rejects is:

> **role importance -> total causal ownership.**

This changes the recovery behavior from solitary overwork to shared inquiry.

`uncertainty -> one member works harder alone`

becomes capable of:

`uncertainty -> three members examine the problem together`.

# 14. The 99-win crisis — unit-level proof captivity

`event_2023_011_st-eve-2311-marathon-raid` is the clearest later test of whether the unit has really escaped proof logic.

After instability and earlier loss, TRINITYAiLE accumulates a ninety-nine-win streak. The record provides objective evidence of excellence, but Rui and Yu begin treating preservation of victory as protection against collapse. Rui states that they cannot fall to the bottom again and must continue winning.

The problem is not competitiveness.

The problem is that winning becomes a **safety behavior**:

> if we keep winning, recognition and legitimacy will remain secure.

This logic starts changing opponent selection. Sumire notices that they are increasingly fighting opponents they expect to beat and that challenge has become secondary to record preservation.

Her dissent is constitutionally significant because it reverses age hierarchy. Sumire is the youngest, but her earlier child-actor career gives her specialized historical knowledge of what safe, reliable stagnation feels like.

She knows that:

> **stable competence can be real competence and still stop opening a future.**

The mature unit must therefore be able to receive epistemic authority from the member whose biography gives her the best access to the problem, regardless of age or center position.
