# IDOLY PRIDE V2 — ANIME ENDPOINT LEDGER
## Episodes 01–12 prospective baseline

## Purpose

This document freezes the cumulative state of the *IDOLY PRIDE* television anime after Episode 12 **before later game-era narrative, origin material, events, cards, messages, official Q&A, or mature cross-media synthesis are allowed to reinterpret it**.

It is the Phase-0.5 handoff object for later V2 work.

The governing sequence was:

> episode primary source -> independent prospective interpretation -> frozen episode state -> historical V1 retrieval -> adversarial comparison

The ledger therefore records what the anime itself has earned by its endpoint, not what later franchise material can eventually explain.

# II. Full-anime executive thesis

Across twelve episodes, *IDOLY PRIDE* moves from a world organized by an interrupted singular idol toward a world capable of carrying that idol's effects without requiring her replacement.

Mana's death initially creates multiple substitution systems:

- Kotono tries to follow Mana's route and complete the summit she never reached.
- Sakura is interpreted through unexplained Mana-like voice and later literal bodily inheritance.
- Rio attaches self-recognition to surpassing Mana.
- Makino's vocation remains emotionally organized around the idol he lost.
- the NEXT VENUS Grand Prix is remembered through the final Mana never performed.
- public memory converts Mana into a miracle/record against which living idols are measured.

The anime gradually transforms those structures into relations among the living:

- Kotono develops `私たちの頂上` and ultimately rejects both substitution and Mana's judgment as final authority.
- Sakura learns that inherited impulse is not instruction and names her final heartbeat `私自身のドキドキ`.
- SUNNY PEACE and Tsuki no Tempest become distinct artistic communities rather than alternative Mana replicas.
- Rio learns that the rivalry she thought was unilateral was reciprocal.
- Makino becomes a top-idol manager by producing idols who are not Mana.
- the Grand Prix final produces two winners and then a ten-person Hoshimi stage rather than a singular successor.
- Mana's final unresolved regret turns out to be unsaid interpersonal speech, not an unfinished performance task.

The endpoint answer is:

> **let what the dead gave become material for lives they no longer control.**

# III. Character endpoint states

## Makino Kouhei / 牧野航平

### Established

- Began as an ordinary, comparatively unobtrusive boy whom Mana selected to become her manager.
- Learns management by witnessing Mana's transformation into an exceptional idol, then learns its ethical limit through losing her.
- Develops a post-Mana group-production theory rather than treating Mana's solo miracle as a universal template.
- Treats preparation, honest evaluation, accompaniment, health, rest, privacy, and stage-boundary restraint as management responsibilities.
- Is willing to let Sakura individuate even when that appears to hasten Mana's disappearance.
- Repeatedly redirects from memorial reproduction toward living performers.
- Saegusa recognizes him in Episode 12 as finally becoming `一人前`.
- Learns Mana loved him and that her deeper relational dream was to make him the manager of a top idol.
- Continues after Mana's second disappearance.

### Interpretive endpoint

> **Makino becomes a manager by learning that faithful witness does not mean preserving the conditions of the first miracle.**

His mature anime role is to create conditions under which other people can cross the stage threshold as themselves.

### Unresolved

- Long-term grief after Mana's second disappearance.
- Exact future balance between professional witnesshood and emotional attachment to stage transformation.

---

## Nagase Mana / 長瀬麻奈

### Established identities

1. ordinary schoolgirl;
2. charismatic emerging idol;
3. professional miracle;
4. public dead legend;
5. Kotono's older sister;
6. Makino's formative idol and romantic beloved;
7. Rio/LizNoir's rival;
8. recorded/archived ordinary person;
9. Sakura's organ donor by narrative revelation;
10. ghost with continuing consciousness.

### Established traits

- playful, assertive, socially skilled, ambitious, proud, audience-oriented, selectively awkward and capable of overwork.
- rejects artistic substitution: `私の歌は 私だけのものだから`.
- admits Rio mattered as a rival even though pride made that difficult to show.
- loves Kotono but has sometimes loved paternalistically, deciding what Kotono can handle without asking her.
- explicitly says she wanted personally to become top idol.
- reveals the deeper relational dream of making Makino a top-idol manager.
- identifies failure to verbalize that dream as her `唯一の心残り`.
- disappears after witnessing the new generation become self-authored.

### Interpretive endpoint

Mana is neither saint nor plot device. The anime continually increases the number of Mana forms rather than choosing one “true” memorial image.

> **Her final ethical function is not to supply the living with the correct answer; it is to become an inheritance rich enough that they can answer differently.**

### Unresolved

- exact ghost mechanics;
- whether disappearance is directly caused by the heart becoming fully Sakura's, as Makino hypothesizes.

---

## Nagase Kotono / 長瀬琴乃

### Episode movement

- **E01–02:** sister-defined vocation and severe post-death withdrawal.
- **E03:** solitary discipline begins becoming reciprocal discipline; `私` starts becoming `私たち` in professional grammar.
- **E04:** recognizes her tension changes other people's working conditions; identified as the group's engine.
- **E05:** leadership becomes obligation to living peers before confidence.
- **E06:** training becomes genuinely `楽しい`; Sakura becomes direct rival.
- **E07:** rivalry becomes care; verifies Sakura's health rather than treating her only as competitor.
- **E08:** Mana-copy project empirically collapses; Tsuki supplies a non-substitutional reason to sing; `私たちの頂上`.
- **E09:** can grieve bodily against another living person while hearing Mana's heart in Sakura.
- **E11:** refuses to ask Mana for approval/disapproval; `自分で決着`; stops chasing or replacing Mana.
- **E12:** `お姉ちゃんがいてくれてよかった`; `お姉ちゃんがアイドルでよかった`.

### Endpoint

- Mana remains loved and formative.
- Mana is no longer destination, judge, or profession-template.
- Kotono's seriousness remains; recovery does not turn her into Sakura or Mei.
- Leadership becomes a way of making intensity useful rather than deleting intensity.
- Tsuki is a living relational identity, not a memorial assignment.

### High-confidence formal finding

> **Kotono's visual recovery is tracked more reliably by what her body can do, how she crosses thresholds, and how close she can remain to other bodies than by palette alone.**

Cool/night spaces migrate from enclosure -> activity -> shared stage -> relational grief -> authored performance.

---

## Kawasaki Sakura / 川咲さくら

### Episode movement

- Begins with unexplained `ドキドキ` and a Mana-like singing atmosphere.
- Voice change and chest history accumulate as clues before explanation.
- E07 makes the surgery cardiac.
- E08 confirms transplant; donor remains confidential/unknown publicly.
- E09 narratively confirms Mana as donor while maintaining institutional privacy distinction.
- Learns gratitude can become debt and that inherited life does not imply inherited identity.
- Says idolhood may have begun through heart-guidance but `今は違います`.
- E10 rejects heart-oracle at a decisive moment and grounds confidence in Chisa, Haruko, Shizuku, Rei.
- E12 returns to heartbeat and calls it `私自身のドキドキ`.

### Endpoint

> **The heart has provenance without retaining jurisdiction.**

Sakura's selfhood does not require denying Mana's bodily history or purging resemblance. It requires authoring what the inherited material means now.

Her leadership method remains relational inquiry: lower the cost of disclosure, ask before categorizing, reduce interpersonal unknowns.

### Unresolved

- precise supernatural relation between heart, voice, intuition, and ghost;
- whether final near-perception of Mana generalizes.

---

## Ibuki Nagisa / 伊吹渚

- Longstanding close relation to Kotono predating Hoshimi membership.
- Idolhood partly inspired by watching Kotono work.
- Major function is relational translation: she makes Kotono's privately revised judgments and unperformed affection legible to others.
- Distinguishes Kotono's grief-specific withdrawal from stable low social fluency and refusal to counterfeit emotion.
- Provides a safe relation for selected vulnerability (`心細かった`, gratitude, uncertainty).
- Does not normally author Kotono's answers for her; Episode 8 gives permission rather than replacement purpose.

**Endpoint:** relational interpreter, friend, and stabilizer whose importance lies in making honesty transmissible without demanding theatrical emotional performance.

---

## Shiraishi Saki / 白石沙季

- Converts ambiguity into procedure: rules, organization, checklists, review structures.
- Practical care and control share a common root.
- Grand Prix creates an uncertainty preparation cannot eliminate.
- Episode 7 lets her admit fear to Chisa, showing procedural competence is also a self-regulation strategy.
- Learns that protecting Chisa need not require permanent older-sister invulnerability.

**Endpoint:** a responsibility-centered organizer becoming capable of reciprocal vulnerability.

---

## Narumiya Suzu / 成宮すず

- Strong `お嬢様` linguistic register; theatrical self-mythologizing is stable across emotional contexts.
- Intensely reveres Mana and carries public miracle mythology into Hoshimi.
- Sincere fandom can unintentionally flatten private grief, especially Kotono's.
- Ordinary footage of Mana expands rather than destroys reverence.
- Grandiosity is simultaneously comic defense, public brand, genuine ambition, and performance energy.

**Endpoint:** public myth becomes more humane without requiring Suzu to stop being a fan.

---

## Hayasaka Mei / 早坂芽衣

- Extremely permeable cognitive/social style: perception -> preference -> speech -> action.
- Makes preferences public and changes room dynamics through kinetic immediacy.
- Helps Hoshimi discover that technically good balance can require productive unpredictability.
- Can see Mana independently, strengthening the ghost's intersubjective reality.
- Gives Mana ordinary female social companionship after death (including the movie outing).
- Embodied learning and intrinsic fun provide a counterforce to overcontrolled professional urgency.
- Finale: visibly grieves not having said goodbye; continuation does not cancel grief.

**Endpoint:** catalyst whose spontaneity becomes useful because it is embedded in a disciplined collective.

---

## Saeki Haruko / 佐伯遙子

- Pre-Mana/new-generation bridge; remembers Hoshimi before myth froze around Mana.
- Long unsuccessful/quiet career gives her a different authority from miracle talent.
- Publicly fluent, domestically competent, good at environmental regulation and pacing.
- Care is infrastructural: food, rest, material comfort, social smoothing, perspective, sustainable rhythm.
- Does not oppose ambition; makes ambition inhabitable.

**Endpoint:** living institutional memory without shrinehood.

---

## Ichinose Rei / 一ノ瀬怜

- Introduced first as disciplined body/form, then as precise professional language.
- Chosen dance life faced legitimacy pressure from family; performance success becomes evidence that choice was serious.
- Fear is managed through technique, repetition, visible improvement, and control.
- Initially risks universalizing her coping system onto Chisa.
- Learns pacing, recovery, and another person's limits can be part of rigor rather than concessions to weakness.
- Remains demanding; development expands discipline rather than replacing it with softness.

**Endpoint:** professional form becoming relationally adaptable.

---

## Shiraishi Chisa / 白石千紗

- Quietness initially tied to fear, self-exposure, inferiority and reliance on Saki.
- Learns to act while afraid rather than trying to become fearless first.
- With Rei, discovers mutual negotiation: she raises the standard for herself while asking Rei to teach more gently.
- With Saki, separation creates enough space for same-stage aspiration and reciprocal fear.

**Endpoint:** timid sincerity becoming usable agency.

---

## Hyoudou Shizuku / 兵藤雫

- Quiet, observation-heavy serious idol fan.
- Recording practice becomes important evidence infrastructure in Episode 3.
- Economy of speech differs from Chisa's anxiety; she often uses the minimum needed to establish position.
- Fan/spectator knowledge crosses into performance authorship.

**Endpoint:** the watcher becomes performer without ceasing to understand the ethical and emotional importance of being watched.

---

## Kanzaki Rio / 神崎莉央 and Igawa Aoi / 井川葵

### Rio

- Early professional severity is tied to practical origins, self-legitimation and interrupted rivalry.
- Mana's performance teaches Rio a form of idol power beyond the economic reason she first entered the field.
- Surpassing Mana becomes a way to prove to herself that she is a real idol.
- Episode 8's attack on Kotono is partly accurate diagnosis and partly self-implication.
- Episode 11 reveals Mana did call Rio a rival; factual correction restores reciprocal relation.
- Losing to Tsuki does not collapse LizNoir; Rio can say the performance was fun.

### Aoi

- Embodied, low-reactivity counterweight whose presence makes Rio less solitary.
- Recognizes Rio/Mana pride symmetry immediately: `莉央と同じだね`.

### Endpoint

LizNoir remains elite senior competition, not an obsolete predecessor. Rio is warmer and historically recontextualized but not demonstrably fully independent of Mana as a standard.

The Rio/Aoi bond supports strong dyadic and yuri-adjacent interpretation without explicit anime romance labeling.

---

## Tendou Rui / 天動瑠依, Suzumura Yu / 鈴村優, Okuyama Sumire / 奥山すみれ

### Rui

- Idol dream predates learning Asakura is her father.
- Wants to become `絶対的アイドル`.
- Entering Asakura's professional world entangles genuine vocation with paternal/professional recognition.
- Believes surpassing Mana is necessary for Asakura to recognize her as top idol.
- Defeat strips away undefeated-record proof but not vocation.
- Recognizes Sakura's voice reached her because it was Sakura's, not because it resembled Mana.
- Asakura's `いいステージだった` is meaningful professional recognition but not complete paternal repair.
- Rui's controlled language survives until her body finally sobs after the task is complete.

### Yu / Sumire

- Provide the relational answer Rui's singular ideal lacks: `背中預けて`; “that is what TRINITYAiLE is for.”
- Demonstrate that the elite unit was never actually a one-person miracle.

### Endpoint

TRINITYAiLE's perfection is humanized, not debunked. A first great loss reveals the relationships that made excellence sustainable.

---

## Saegusa Shinji / 三枝信司 and Asakura / 朝倉

### Saegusa

- Senior professional architect connecting Mana era to current Hoshimi project.
- Restored Grand Prix is partly institutional response to unresolved history.
- Episode 12 acknowledges Makino's professional maturation.

### Asakura

- Demands standards and explicitly warns Rui that record fixation prevents reaching Mana.
- Introduces `物語` as something idols need beyond records.
- Has a professionally legible but emotionally/paternally incomplete relation to Rui.

### Endpoint

Their finale dialogue makes the decisive temporal point: the idol field continued moving after Mana. Even the adults who chased the phantom final can imagine stronger living futures now.

# IV. Relationship endpoint ledger

## Mana / Makino — light, shadow, vocation, love

**State:** resolved enough for farewell, not erased.

- Mana selects Makino and makes management possible as a vocation.
- Makino's formative joy is watching effort transform into public radiance.
- Mana's death creates grief, archive, ghost relation, and ethical risk of permanent backward gaze.
- Mana repeatedly redirects him toward living idols.
- Makino lets Sakura become herself even at the probable cost of losing Mana.
- Finale reveals explicit romantic attachment (`牧野くんを好きになって`) and Mana's dream for Makino's professional future.
- No conventional fulfilled romance is granted; farewell occurs through incomplete speech, teasing near-kiss language, dissolution, and grief.

**Endpoint thesis:** love becomes ethically mature when it stops asking the beloved's continued presence to be the condition of one's vocation.

---

## Mana / Kotono — sisterhood after jurisdiction

- Loving childhood and increasing career strain culminate in a cruel unfinished final argument.
- Kotono spends years turning grief into vocation and guilt into attempted substitution.
- Viewer learns Mana continued loving Kotono; Kotono deliberately does not use that knowledge.
- Episode 11 refuses both approval and disapproval as final authority.
- Episode 12 allows full gratitude: glad Mana existed; glad Mana was an idol.

**Endpoint thesis:** Kotono does not love Mana less; she gives Mana less jurisdiction over the future.

---

## Mana / Sakura — history inside another body

- Initially mysterious bodily/vocal resemblance.
- Eventually literal organ inheritance.
- Public and private actors repeatedly risk turning Sakura into successor/reappearance.
- Mana herself rejects this category.
- Sakura moves from bodily oracle to authored desire and names the final heartbeat as her own.

**Endpoint thesis:** provenance can survive without possession.

---

## Sakura / Kotono — from Mana-mediated threat to living rivalry

- First relation is saturated by Mana resemblance/inheritance.
- Direct rivalry emerges after debut.
- Rivalry becomes care, radical honesty and bodily trust.
- Kotono can confide in Sakura; Sakura can confront Kotono; each wants the other strong enough to meet at the summit.
- Final competition is genuinely between them/their units, not a proxy fight over Mana ownership.

**Endpoint thesis:** rivalry is a form of recognition: each person's future matters to the other's.

---

## Kotono / Nagisa — interpretation without appropriation

- Nagisa knows what Kotono privately revises but struggles to say.
- Helps others distinguish Kotono's lack of social performance from lack of feeling.
- Gives Kotono relational permission without prescribing a new identity.

**Endpoint:** one of the anime's clearest safe-intimacy relationships.

---

## Saki / Chisa — protection becoming reciprocity

- Saki's competence initially defines the asymmetry.
- Unit separation forces Chisa to act independently and lets Saki admit fear.
- Same-stage aspiration replaces simple sheltering.

**Endpoint:** sisterhood can remain close while becoming less hierarchical.

---

## Rei / Chisa — standards become pedagogy

- Rei sees urgency and objective technical need.
- Chisa experiences pressure as confirmation of inadequacy.
- Understanding Rei's history increases Chisa's willingness to work; understanding Chisa's needs increases Rei's ability to teach.

**Endpoint:** responsibility is distributed rather than won by one temperament.

---

## Rio / Mana — rivalry restored as reciprocal history

- Rio misreads apparent indifference as lack of recognition.
- Death makes surpassing Mana impossible in ordinary reciprocal time.
- Kotono supplies the missing fact: Mana called Rio rival.
- The correction changes the past's meaning but does not resurrect the lost contest.

**Endpoint:** factual closure can be liberating without giving the past authority over the future.

---

## Rio / Aoi — paired professional intimacy

- Aoi remains the person capable of underreacting to Rio's command surface while seeing what matters beneath it.
- LizNoir visual language repeatedly pairs them through proximity, gaze and matched movement.

**Endpoint:** extremely close dyadic partnership with strong yuri-adjacent coding; no explicit TV-anime dating label.

---

## Rui / Yu / Sumire — absolute center carried by a triad

- Rui's singular rhetoric contrasts with the actual three-person structure sustaining her.
- Yu and Sumire explicitly tell Rui to entrust her back to them.
- Defeat is collectively borne.

**Endpoint:** the unit has already learned something Rui's summit criterion is still catching up to.

---

## SUNNY PEACE / Tsuki no Tempest — plurality after differentiation

- Split begins as Makino's professional hypothesis.
- Each unit develops distinctive visual, vocal, relational and performance grammar.
- They become rivals without severing Hoshimi belonging.
- Tie gives both winner status.
- Ten-person encore restores a larger `we` without undoing unit specificity.

**Endpoint thesis:** professional differentiation does not require social separation, and larger belonging does not require homogenization.

# V. Unit and performance baseline

## SUNNY PEACE

### Anime-native artistic grammar

- warm/direct/outward light;
- everyday vitality becoming stage radiance;
- invitation and accessibility;
- increasing distribution of center/agency across five bodies;
- private feeling converted into audience-facing encouragement.

### Founding-to-endpoint movement

- Episode 5: sun metaphor is managerial hypothesis.
- Episode 6: debut song becomes aspirational constitution.
- Episodes 8–10: Sakura's Mana resemblance is challenged; unit explicitly refuses overreliance on exceptional voice.
- Episode 10: new song proves five-person authorship at summit level.
- Episode 12: Sakura's heartbeat is explicitly her own; unit shares victory rather than becoming singular heir.

**Endpoint:** distributed sunlight is strongly supported as anime-native prototype, but full mature game philosophy must not be projected backward.

---

## Tsuki no Tempest

### Anime-native artistic grammar

- nocturnal/reflective light;
- controlled intensity;
- darkness authored rather than escaped;
- identity discovered through relation and mutual illumination;
- seriousness as propulsion rather than permanent enclosure.

### Founding-to-endpoint movement

- Episode 5: moon metaphor begins as leadership hypothesis.
- Episode 6: blue/nocturnal performance reclaims Kotono's grief palette.
- Episode 8: `私たちの頂上` and moonlit relational space become explicit self-authorship.
- Episode 11: unit constitution increasingly matches lived biography.
- Episode 12: Kotono can thank Mana inside nocturnal performance without returning to substitution.

**Endpoint:** Tsuki is a living community that makes Kotono's intensity relationally productive.

---

## LizNoir

### Grammar

- embodied authority;
- paired Rio/Aoi intensity;
- senior professional confidence;
- rivalry as self-definition;
- darker saturated performance language.

### Endpoint movement

- Initially appears as proof of what “real idols” can do to a room.
- Rio's Mana wound is gradually made legible.
- Loss to Tsuki does not invalidate senior status or artistic identity.
- Returning black-lily imagery reads differently once the people inside it are known.

**Endpoint:** predecessor excellence can remain real without blocking successor originality.

## TRINITYAiLE

### Grammar

- precision;
- synchronization;
- wing/celestial imagery;

- Unit explicitly redistributes burden through trust.

**Endpoint:** perfection becomes human without becoming fraudulent.

# VI. Music and dramaturgy endpoint

## `First Step`

**Function:** inherited beginning.

At first it is both gift and trap: Mana's repertoire and old location give Kotono/Sakura a stage before they possess their own forms. By the finale the first-step concept has generalized into renewable start lines.

## SUNNY PEACE debut song

**Function:** founding constitution of outward light.

Everyday feeling -> self-recognition -> desire to illuminate another -> five pieces making a star.

## Tsuki no Tempest debut song

**Function:** founding constitution of relational nocturnal light.

Darkness, trembling, receiving light, accepting fluctuation, shining because another illuminates.

## `Daytime Moon`

**Function:** identity without static symbolism.

Tsuki/Kotono can remain lunar while occupying warm daylight; health does not require becoming a different archetype.

## `song for you`

**Function:** inheritance as re-performance.

- Mana's intended Grand Prix piece;
- last attempted private bridge to Kotono;
- Sakura/Sunny inherited composition transformed through present feeling;
- Mana/Makino farewell lens.

The anime's treatment demonstrates that an art object can carry provenance while every performance remains newly authored.

## `realiser`

**Function:** TRINITYAiLE post-defeat interior epilogue.

Its language of not chasing another's back, trusting felt warmth, and realizing dreams together gives musical shape to a relational future beyond Rui's current record/recognition fixation.

## `サヨナラから始まる物語`

**Function:** Phase-0.5 terminal song and renewable-beginning thesis.

Ten-person stage follows two independently matured units. Goodbye is not denied; action creates another starting line.

## `Pray for you`

**Function:** ordinary-life epilogue.

Memory, current living, tomorrow, pride, pages of a new story. Credits return grandeur to rooms, food, school, staff, photos and everyday continuation.

# VII. Formal and motif endpoint

## 1. Action-within-space > color-alone

**Status: HIGH-CONFIDENCE FORMAL FINDING.**

Kotono's early cool/dark room is pathological because of enclosure, immobility and isolation, not because blue is intrinsically negative. Later blue/night spaces become rehearsal, relation, open sky and stage.

## 2. Distance from other bodies

One of the clearest longitudinal Kotono variables:

> maximum isolation -> shared work -> accepted group touch -> requested bodily proximity -> self-directed grief in relation -> full group performance.

## 3. Stage threshold

The manager prepares, watches, judges, protects and accompanies. The performer must cross into public relation herself. Mana's influence becomes infrastructure for new crossings rather than a template for one correct performance.

## 4. Watching / witness / judgment

Watching can make performance possible. But mature witnessing stops short of authorship.

- Makino watches Mana and becomes manager.
- Mana says being watched gives strength.
- Kotono initially needs Mana as judge.
- Episode 11 refuses judgment while preserving Mana as witness.
- Episode 12 lets Mana witness what is already self-authored.

## 5. Heart

Unexplained `ドキドキ` -> scar -> heart surgery -> transplant -> donor provenance -> successor threat -> gratitude/debt -> own desire -> interpersonal trust -> `私自身のドキドキ`.

## 6. Sun / moon

Not moral binary. Not simple extrovert/introvert binary. Two performance-relational grammars whose distinctness survives shared institutional belonging.

## 7. `奇跡` / miracle

At least two anime-native uses must remain distinct:

- Makino: Mana as extraordinary professional exception (`奇跡みたいなもの`).
- Suzu/public myth: Mana as miraculous legend (`星降る奇跡`).

The finale neither debunks nor reproduces the miracle. It distributes what the miracle made possible.

## 8. Record / score / story

VENUS and tournament results are real professional constraints. The work repeatedly insists they do not exhaust artistic value, personhood or memory. Asakura's `物語` language and the tie sharpen this without making metrics meaningless.

## 9. Diary / recording / archive

- Mana diary reconnects Kotono to dead private language.
- Shizuku rehearsal recording turns living peers into epistemic resources.
- Mana backstage archive humanizes legend but raises consent questions.
- Songs themselves become mobile archives capable of new authorship.

## 10. Public / professional / private Mana

The anime's memorial ethics depend on refusing collapse among:

- public miracle;
- professional exception;
- private sister/friend/rival/beloved;
- recorded ordinary girl;
- conscious ghost.

## 11. Goodbye / starting line

The final motif synthesis.

Episode 1: death turns a beginning into rupture.

Episode 12: farewell is still painful, but social/institutional inheritance is robust enough that rupture can become another beginning.

# VIII. Anime-endpoint unresolved questions

These questions are deliberately carried forward rather than solved with later canon:

1. **Ghost ontology:** what exactly made Mana visible/persistent, and why to Makino and Mei in particular?
2. **Sakura threshold perception:** what precisely happens when Sakura thinks she sees/hears Mana near disappearance?
3. **Heart causality:** how literal are the links among transplant, voice, intuition and ghost beyond the anime's melodramatic supernatural grammar?
4. **Unit maturation:** how do Sunny/Tsuki philosophies develop once the Grand Prix origin arc is over?
5. **Center structure:** how stable are the leadership/center roles established in the anime?
6. **Rio after recognition:** does factual closure about Mana materially change Rio's self-evaluative framework?
7. **Rui after defeat:** how does she disentangle genuine idol ambition from Asakura/Mana recognition logic?
8. **Makino after second loss:** what does management look like once Mana is no longer an active conversational presence?
9. **Industry scale:** how do Hoshimi, BanPro, LizNoir, later rivals and VENUS structures evolve?
10. **Memory ethics:** how do archives, posthumous image, songs and inherited narratives continue to be negotiated?

# X. Final anime-endpoint definition

The TV anime begins with a miracle interrupted before its answer.

Its characters spend most of the season treating that interruption as a problem of succession:

> Who finishes Mana's dream?
>
> Who sings her song?
>
> Who carries her voice?
>
> Who reaches her summit?
>
> Who receives her approval?
>
> Who defeats her record?

By Episode 12, those questions have been progressively displaced.

Mana's heart can live in Sakura without making Sakura Mana.

Mana's sister can love her without living as her continuation.

Mana's rival can learn that the rivalry was reciprocal without finally defeating a dead person.

Mana's manager can fulfill her dream by managing top idols whose forms she never dictated.

Mana's Grand Prix can end with two winners.

Mana's agency can place ten living idols on the winner's stage.

Mana can disappear.

And the story can continue.

That is the anime's final answer to inheritance:

> **The dead remain part of the living person's history. They cease to be the author of the living person's future.**

Or, in the finale's own temporal grammar:

> **`サヨナラから始まる物語`**
>
> **a story that begins from goodbye.**
