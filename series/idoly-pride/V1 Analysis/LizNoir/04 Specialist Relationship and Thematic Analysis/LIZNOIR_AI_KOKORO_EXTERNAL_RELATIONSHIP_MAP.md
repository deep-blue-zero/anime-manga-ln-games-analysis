## Ai outside LizNoir

**1\. Ai and Suzu — probably Ai’s most important outside-LizNoir relationship**

Suzu matters because she meets Ai at the level of **idol faith**. In *芽吹く黒ユリの蕾*, when Ai is crushed by Rio calling her “LizNoir失格,” Suzu is the one who hears Ai’s reverence for old LizNoir and immediately understands it through her own Mana devotion. Ai says *The Last Chance* was a treasure-like song that supported her when things were hard; Suzu answers that she understands because Mana’s songs served that role for her. That makes Suzu one of the first outsiders who can validate Ai’s “fan who becomes idol” wound without reducing it to simple low confidence. IMPORTANT\_003\_\_event\_2021\_003\_s…

Suzu also becomes a practical accomplice in Ai’s recovery: Kokoro brings Suzu into the training-camp scheme precisely because Suzu cares about Ai and can help push the situation from the outside. So Suzu is not just a friendly cameo; she is an outsider who helps Ai remember her origin and re-enter LizNoir with courage. IMPORTANT\_003\_\_event\_2021\_003\_s…

The clean formulation:

> **Suzu is Ai’s fellow believer.**
> Ai loves LizNoir the way Suzu loves Mana: not casually, but as a song-image that carried her through weakness.

**2\. Ai and Yu — school-life femininity, visibility, and ramen friendship**

Yu is very useful for Ai because she pulls Ai out of the LizNoir performance frame and into school-life normalcy. Their bond stories show Ai’s classroom visibility anxiety, her ramen bento world, her admiration for Yu’s refined femininity, and her tendency to compare her own muscular/body-functional femininity against Yu’s softer, polished presence. Yu catches Ai preparing a ramen bento, points out that classmates were watching her, and later admires Ai’s abs while Ai admires Yu’s scent/style/body line. bond\_ai\_001\_ai

Yu therefore acts as a mirror for Ai’s “I am seen more than I think” problem. Ai assumes she is peripheral or embarrassing; Yu shows her that people notice her, and that her strength can be admired rather than treated as unidol-like.

Best formulation:

> **Yu helps reveal Ai’s ordinary-girl self-consciousness: the part of Ai that wants to be strong, but also pretty, graceful, fragrant, and socially visible in a way she does not apologize for.**

**3\. Ai and Nagisa — romance ignorance, softness envy, and ramen misunderstandings**

Nagisa is a gentle contrast partner for Ai. Their train scene is great because Nagisa brings romantic-literary imagination, while Ai panics and bodily overacts the scene, literally pulling Nagisa off the train. Later, Nagisa asks Ai to “train” her after overeating ramen, and Ai misunderstands this as ramen-making training, turning the whole thing into stockpot and湯切り practice. bond\_ai\_001\_ai

Nagisa matters because she shows Ai’s romance ignorance and soft-femininity gap without mocking her harshly. Ai sees Nagisa’s airy romantic mode as something she lacks, but the story lets Ai’s own absurd sincerity produce warmth anyway.

> **Nagisa brings romance-script softness; Ai accidentally translates it into muscle, food, and action.**

**4\. Ai and Rei — practical chaos and local helpfulness**

Rei’s “shortcut” bond story shows a different side of Ai: her weirdly effective practical/local-world intelligence. Ai’s shortcut to school is absurd — winding streets, steep paths, even passing through an elderly woman’s house because Ai once helped her — but it somehow works. bond\_ai\_001\_ai

This is meaningful because Rei is a disciplined, order/propriety-coded character; Ai’s route looks irrational to her, but it reveals that Ai builds a map of the world through embodied routine and kindness. She knows places because she has helped people there.

> **Rei lets us see that Ai’s “dumb physicality” is also a social geography built from accumulated good deeds.**

## Kokoro outside LizNoir

**1\. Kokoro and kana — her most important outside-LizNoir mirror**

You are absolutely right to identify kana. This is Kokoro’s richest outside-LizNoir relationship by far.

Their TWINkle event begins as fake friendship, mutual contempt, and pure opportunism: Kokoro wants a crown program, kana wants the CM/visibility, and both understand that “friendship” can be manufactured for fan-facing performance. But because both are media-native, sharp-tongued, defensive girls who understand attention as survival, the fake bond becomes strangely honest. The event’s logic of becoming **“本物よりも本物の仲良し”** is extremely revealing: they know that idol friendship is partly production, but production can still generate real intimacy. kana matters because she mirrors Kokoro’s ugliest and most useful traits: calculation, bite, performance, attention hunger, loneliness, and the ability to turn emotional damage into public image. Unlike Ai, kana will not simply receive Kokoro’s vulnerability with open sincerity. She answers Kokoro with cynicism, aggression, and craft. That makes her a different kind of confidante.

The key formulation:

> **kana is Kokoro without LizNoir’s moral cushion.**
> She shows what Kokoro’s media intelligence and defensive bite look like when they are sharpened by older wounds.

**2\. Kokoro and Suzu — reaction chemistry, rivalry, and ojou-sama foil**

Suzu is meaningful, but not in the same confessional/mirror way kana is. Suzu is a **reaction-rich foil**. Kokoro calls her “すずにゃん,” provokes her, targets her with pranks, and values her because Suzu gives elegant, high-energy, theatrical outrage. In Kokoro terms, Suzu is a premium reaction partner.

But there is more than gag there. Suzu also works with Kokoro when Kokoro is trying to help Ai. In *芽吹く黒ユリの蕾*, Kokoro consults Suzu about a training-camp location because she wants to push Ai forward; Suzu agrees because Ai’s back needs pushing, then becomes part of Kokoro’s scheme. > **Suzu is not Kokoro’s emotional mirror; she is Kokoro’s ojou-sama reaction stage and occasional co-conspirator.**
> Kokoro pokes Suzu because Suzu reacts beautifully. Suzu tolerates Kokoro because beneath the teasing, Kokoro often does understand what will move people.

**3\. Kokoro and Sumire — ordinary loneliness and “natural cuteness”**

Sumire is quietly important for Kokoro because she catches the school/off-day side of Kokoro: the part that is often alone, studying, or trying to understand how other girls are liked. Kokoro studies Sumire’s “beloved younger sister” aura because Sumire has a natural receive-care cuteness that Kokoro’s calculated cuteness lacks. This is a meaningful contrast: Kokoro is not unaware that her cuteness is engineered. She studies Sumire because Sumire shows a softer, less weaponized mode of being loved. bond\_kkr\_001\_kkr

Sumire also gives Kokoro a gentle peer outside LizNoir who can notice her loneliness without forcing a major confrontation.

> **Sumire shows Kokoro the kind of cuteness that does not need to conquer the room first.**

**4\. Kokoro and Rui — prank grammar breaking against alien common sense**

Rui is less emotionally central, but very good for Kokoro’s comedy logic. Kokoro’s pranks depend on the target reacting within ordinary social grammar. Rui often defeats that grammar because her own common sense is stranger than the prank: a painful foot-massage mat or bitter food can become health/food eccentricity rather than punishment. bond\_kkr\_001\_kkr

This relationship matters because it shows the limits of Kokoro’s control. Kokoro can manipulate normal reactions, but Rui is hard to script.

> **Rui is a Kokoro-prank countermeasure: too composed, too strange, or too off-axis to be controlled normally.**

**5\. Kokoro and Nagisa — support texture / dorm-life gentleness**

Nagisa is not as defining for Kokoro as kana or Suzu, but she matters in the daily-life layer. She helps with hair, dorm trouble, and moments where Kokoro’s self-authored visual identity collapses. Kokoro’s hair/horn silhouette is part of how she maintains “Akazaki Kokoro,” so someone like Nagisa helping restore it is not trivial; it is care for Kokoro’s performed self. bond\_kkr\_001\_kkr

> **Nagisa supports the maintenance of Kokoro’s image without turning it into a battle.**

## Ranking them by importance

For **Ai**, I would rank outside-LizNoir relationships roughly:

1.  **Suzu** — fellow idol believer; helps Ai recover her origin.

2.  **Yu** — school visibility, femininity/body contrast, ramen friendship.

3.  **Nagisa** — romance softness and Ai’s physical misunderstanding comedy.

4.  **Rei** — disciplined foil to Ai’s local/practical body-world.

5.  **Sakura / Mei / Rui / miho in Stars Journey** — less intimate individually, but crucial for Ai becoming agency-scale gratitude.


For **Kokoro**:

1.  **kana** — by far the most important outside-LizNoir mirror.

2.  **Suzu** — strongest recurring reaction foil/co-conspirator; very meaningful but more comedic/social than confessional.

3.  **Sumire** — school loneliness and natural-cuteness mirror.

4.  **Momo** — prank soulmate/rival.

5.  **Rui** — anti-prank/common-sense-disruption foil.

6.  **Nagisa** — soft support for image maintenance and dorm-life texture.


The compact final version:

> **Ai’s outside relationships tend to reveal her sincerity by giving it new objects: Suzu gives idol faith, Yu gives school/femininity visibility, Nagisa gives romance softness, Rei gives practical discipline, and the Stars Journey team lets her gratitude scale to all Hoshimi.**
>
> **Kokoro’s outside relationships reveal how her performance-self works: kana mirrors her defensive media intelligence, Suzu gives her a perfect reaction stage, Sumire reveals her loneliness and cuteness anxiety, Momo speaks prank as friendship, and Rui/Nagisa expose the limits and maintenance of Kokoro’s self-produced persona.**
