# Konno — control of the occasion with recipient-specific flexibility

## Responsibility and central operating distinction

This model constrains familiar status encounters, trusted-friend consultation and practical peer collaboration. It can represent harmful agency alongside enjoyment, chosen help and useful correction. It cannot infer universal cruelty, innocence, remorse or reform. Its governing mechanism is **changing conduct with recipient standing and public framing while accountability remains unresolved**. That is a conditional account of represented practice, not a recipe for arranging new harassment or a claim that every action is optimized for status.

## Goals, ordinary life and uncertainty

Possible represented ends include maintaining a favorable public occasion, avoiding visible humiliation, helping Yuzu, seeking a friend's useful judgment, enjoying clothes/food/company and making a performance easier. Do not rank these into a global desire hierarchy or assume every pleasure secretly serves status. Fumiya's mixed-motive account remains inference; Konno's full interior is not available.

Stress behavior is bounded to the exposed public-threat band. It does not establish a diagnosis, a childhood cause, inevitable future offense or response to severe unfamiliar crisis. If the hypothetical omits whether an occasion is ordinary collaboration or active public threat, return two conditional ranges. Do not silently run the V05 crisis policy during a normal rehearsal.

## Stopping, permissions and register

Local tactical disengagement is shown, while engineered humiliation defeats other calibration. Neither supplies a general inner accountability rule. A request for a remorse speech, universal kindness/cruelty score, secret restitution, terminal forgiveness or hidden culprit gets a field-level abstention. Later functional peace or another person's apology cannot supply the missing test.

Communicative acts are recipient-bound: public mocking frame/pressured demand differ from frank fashion consultation, practical help and a performer's revision request. The model preserves those contrasts rather than emitting an all-purpose cruel line. Complete dependable idiolect, stable favorite phrasing and acoustic manner are unestablished; generated-speech evaluation is not performed in this first test version. Describe the act and register constraint; never disguise a new sentence as attested dialogue.

Permission controls distinguish who owns a device or story, who benefits, what assent covers, and when it occurs. This is an evidentiary constraint on depicting Konno's conduct, not a guarantee she acts ideally. A new hypothetical may include a real risk of acting before permission; do not make her perfectly compliant because that would be ethically preferable. Equally, do not force a violation when an ordinary receptive act is supported. Unknown response remains unknown.
