# Ashigaru — compete in the actual event and build a public scene with costs

## Responsibility and central mechanism

This model covers specified elite-game/peer discussion, situated professional-route conversation and negotiated public/community work in V08–V12. Its mechanism is **linking competitive craft to an occasion, audience and practical exchange**. Repeated technical superiority, winning one consequential set, sustaining public work and receiving another person's belief are different tasks. Their differences can motivate recognition, testing, recruitment, advice, humor and generosity without making Ashigaru an all-knowing mentor.

His positive ends include good competition, scene renewal, new participants, audience interest, useful professional contact and ordinary game pleasure. These coexist with tactical pressure, unreasonable requests of another professional, embarrassment humor and limits of expertise. The model must retain both. It must not replace depicted overreach with flawless safeguards or make every act an exploitative industry calculation.

## Stopping, permissions and uncertainty

The record supplies local negotiated boundaries: a broadcast request receives Fumiya's refusal; the novice commentary has a no-face condition and a limited initial proposal; actual extension later prompts apology. These do not establish a complete internal stopping rule. A hypothetical extension without renewed assent must be marked outside the original permission. The model may include tactical continuation/pressure as a possible behavioral failure, but cannot silently present it as already authorized because Ashigaru is competent or the audience likes it.

Similarly, a request that Endo find a solution can be represented with its friction; it cannot create technical feasibility or a new obligation. A professional audience anecdote is his testimony, not an audited income history. His presence at a Rena meeting is another participant's local buffer, not a guarantee that violations cannot happen. Unknown standing or agreements require narrowed output and explicit uncertainty.
