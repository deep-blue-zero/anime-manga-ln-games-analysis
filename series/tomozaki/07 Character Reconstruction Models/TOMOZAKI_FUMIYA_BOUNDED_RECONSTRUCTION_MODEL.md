## Responsibility and causal argument

This is a manual prose decision/constraint model of the separately admitted finite-era learning, competition, familiar collaboration, ordinary company and particular partner-negotiation domains. It is not an autonomous inference program, a new literary conclusion or a late-state personality applied to every earlier scene. The mature monograph is the literary authority: 184,500 bytes, SHA-256 `1be90778f0f65cab18096758b4c3bd94d2d84aadb8fde4290ed1b53e5b50ab60`. Its TF-E, TF-P and TF-V12-E handles, together with the exact dyad/state/relationship/voice/claim/locator/audit instances in the dependency packet, ground the constraints below. FM mechanisms are derived operational hypotheses, not additional source testimony.

Fumiya often makes a difficulty tractable by looking for its mechanism, experimenting, practicing and translating a learned form into another task. That capacity does not itself decide what the task should be. He can learn to fit another person's wish, use a former assignment for a memory he independently wants, and collaborate effectively. He can also mistake his own successful destination for another person's need, convert helpfulness into a credential of unique worth, or use urgency to exceed informational standing. The same mechanism-seeking capacity can therefore yield useful care, a mistaken intervention or retreat into a technically framed problem. Select the actual state, desired end, recipient and stakes before deciding which branch is supported.

Game pleasure, curiosity, being with particular people, love, shared craft and an expressive professional aspiration are positive ends. They are not all proxies for saving Hinami or becoming popular. Neither does a self-owned end become ethically sufficient merely because it is sincerely his. A model that always explains him through optimization, pure authenticity, pathology, romance or rescue has erased counterevidence.
