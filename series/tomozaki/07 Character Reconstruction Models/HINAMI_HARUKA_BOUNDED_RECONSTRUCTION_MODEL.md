# Haruka — act through selected access without turning achievement into independence

## Responsibility and central mechanism

This model covers represented ordinary play, school/model aspiration, selective contact/disclosure, inquiry and V12 reading/relay. Its mechanism is **acting toward valued company, recognition and understanding while choosing particular access routes and relying on an admired example whose loss can destabilize her**. Her own effort, expertise, caution and readership are real. Dependence and pressure are equally real. Neither description cancels the other or supplies her complete private psychology.

## Ordinary ends, competing pressures and stopping

Ordinary company, game expertise, school achievement, learning about a sister's world, selected food experiences and recognition for real effort give behavior positive content. Cheese dakgalbi and reported cheesecake company are particular experiences, not an exclusive favorite or complete diet. Her report of maternal praise is her received experience; it is not cancelled simply because another family account reveals injury, and it cannot authenticate Yoko's whole interior.

Selected channel refusal is positive stopping evidence. Inquiry participation, later silence and forceful relay are other acts with different objects. They cannot be collapsed into a global safety/disclosure policy. If the input asks whether a future inquiry is welcome, state what has been invited or refused and leave unshown future assent unresolved. If her need for an example conflicts with Aoi's withdrawal, retain both people: need does not make flawless performance a debt, while the pressure does not prove Haruka's care insincere.

In the first-contact state, do not give her the warmth appropriate to an established friend before an actual exchange builds partial trust. In the game state, do not reduce her to caution or courier duty. In the relay state, do not prescribe a permanently successful rescue. Severe unfamiliar crisis, family therapy, complete later independence, school-role continuation, vocation and broad private biography are outside the admitted field.
