# Hinami Haruka — pursuing an example, choosing what to share

This account is accepted at its stated bounded literary scope through the admitted V12 boundary. See the [implementation review](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_CHARACTER_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md) and [source and review manifest](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_LITERARY_EVIDENCE_AND_REVIEW_MANIFEST.json) for independent acceptance, source returns, corrections and remaining limits. A bounded literary role is separate from a derived reconstruction model or aggregate release.

Haruka makes an admired example productive through her own effort. She also suffers when the person supplying that example becomes unavailable and incoherent to her. These are related facts, rather than rival descriptions of an active girl and a dependent girl. Her student-council achievement, questions, gaming invitation, guarded first encounter, selective contact channel and independent reading all require an individual account. Her sister's later response does not complete an independent basis of value for her.

This is a proposed **B bounded dossier**. It gives Haruka a substantial home for her actions, recipients, pleasures and unresolved conflict. It does not claim the broad interior and multi-domain coverage of a principal monograph or a generally predictive character reconstruction. Earlier bucket cells marked UNKNOWN are not evidence that she was absent from those books. The full admitted corpus supplies the retrospective horizon; later family knowledge must not be assigned to an earlier actor or disclosure boundary.

## 1. Evidence and the three clocks

**SCENE** means represented speech/action at its stated story time, including retrospective scenes. **REPORT** means a character's report about an earlier act. **INTERIOR** identifies whose thoughts the reader receives. **INFERENCE** is the dossier's interpretation or another actor's explicitly identified model. **OPEN** is withheld or conflicting evidence. A childhood SCENE is not a present interview with Haruka, and Aoi's reading of her expression is not Haruka's exhaustive inner account.

The V08.5 family unit presents childhood and middle-school retrospection inside a later frame; package order is not the story's date. Its childhood game first makes the three sisters visible to the reader. The V09 restaurant encounter and V10 opening birthday-video cutoff have their own narrower horizons. V11 makes Haruka a current direct interlocutor and supplies her reports about effort, uncertainty and the family. V12 represents her later reading and message, largely seen through Aoi and then through the DM Fumiya receives.

The full V11 family-history sequence is disclosure-framed but moves into third-person Aoi-centered presentation and first-person interiority. Haruka is not present at every remembered exchange, and Fumiya does not automatically hear every intervening thought as a verbatim confession. V12 p0022 is Aoi's private reader-facing account. Its description of Haruka's actions is evidence; its judgments that she was deceived by their mother or that pressure is dangerous are Aoi's judgments.

## 2. Ordinary play before the admired-model problem

In childhood Haruka is the smallest spectator beside Aoi and Nagisa. She does more than watch a contest whose meaning belongs to the older girls: she reacts to Nagisa's loss, chooses to cheer her, turns Aoi into the comic great demon king, celebrates the reversal and receives Nagisa's thanks. The temporary alliance is playful and local. It does not prove permanent allegiance against Aoi, a global family hierarchy or an elaborate strategic disposition. It does show that her relation to each sister is differentiated and that shared enjoyment permits teasing the strongest player. [HH-E01]

The later invitation matters as Haruka's initiated activity. After Aoi's basketball defeat, she asks her to play Atafami. Haruka has more experience in this game and defeats her, teaches the name of a dodge and teases her for studying too much. Aoi's age does not automatically give her the local competence advantage. Haruka's willingness to play without holding back makes her a particular companion rather than an apparatus whose sole job is to supply reassurance. The text offers possible concern for Aoi as an explanation for the invitation, but does not settle that intention. Calling it a consciously designed rescue would exceed the evidence. [HH-E02]

The shared time continues after Aoi overtakes her. Haruka protests the returning great demon king, jokes about tactics and eventually recalls how the sisters used to play. The missing third controller gives the exchange a history of loss without making every playful act an unconscious symptom. The narration retains alternatives for why they keep playing: avoiding loneliness, recovering pleasure, or both. Her enjoyment, earlier expertise and initiative survive even if Aoi eventually uses the game for measurable individual proof.

## 3. Current company, caution and selective access

The V09 restaurant encounter is brief ordinary evidence. Prompted by her mother, Haruka introduces herself politely to her sister's classmates. Nervous formality is present beside the family's easy banter. Fumiya sees a warm family and recognizes that Aoi shows them a public-facing form. These observations can coexist. The scene neither establishes that Haruka knows all Aoi's other modes nor proves that warmth is counterfeit. Her exact present age is not independently established by the observer's estimate here. [HH-E03]

V11 makes her caution much more specific. Encountering an unfamiliar older boy outside the house at night, Haruka asks why he is there, steps back and grips a personal alarm. She relaxes only through the exchange, shared concern and evidence that he actually knows her sister's gaming world. Her initial politeness does not erase suspicion. Fumiya's comic narration of the alarm is his experience; her observed choice to keep it available is her protective act. This is evidence against a universally trusting younger-sister type. It is not enough to infer a comprehensive personal-safety policy. [HH-E04]

She later agrees to further conversation because she wants to know Aoi's school life. When he suggests LINE, she refuses it as private and offers Instagram instead. The refusal is direct and local, not an analyst's invented boundary on her behalf. Agreeing to talk does not mean agreeing to every channel, every audience or unlimited further use of her family testimony. Her chosen distinction also prevents treating her solely as a door that opens when the protagonist has a good motive. [HH-E04]

Fumiya then asks her to keep their conversation from Aoi (`item/xhtml/p-0019.xhtml` P303–315). His embarrassed explanation and her apparent realization are shown; her full understanding of his motive is not. Aoi later reports repeated unannounced outings and objects to his unauthorized family contact (`item/xhtml/p-0030.xhtml` P549–560). That gives the covert route a concrete recipient cost without granting Aoi knowledge of every prior exchange.

Ordinary preference evidence is modest but concrete. At Cocoon City's food court she and Kikuchi choose cheese dakgalbi; she reports often eating cheesecake with her sister when Mizusawa asks about cheese. The first hurried self-introduction's incomplete “チー…” is not the sole basis for a favorite-food claim. The later meal and report establish these food experiences and a plausible liking, without an exclusive favorite, a household rule, a diet or a lifelong preference hierarchy. [HH-E05]

## 4. Becoming a council president through somebody else's example

Haruka calls herself the least clever sister whose strength was energy. This is self-description, not a measured ranking. She says she had understood making people smile as her role; later, going to school and talking to people became frightening. Her phrase about several things occurring is not an exhaustive causal history. Fumiya connects it to the death he has heard about, but the exact scope of her wording should remain distinct from his inference. Her mother offered acceptance and some relief. Haruka reports that the deeper unease continued. [HH-E04]

Her account of Aoi's response supplies a different resource: watch me and I will show a perfect example. Haruka describes a brighter everyday world and the possibility that she too can become admirable. The gain is actual in her report and later achievement. It is not proof that all maternal consolation was worthless or that a flawless model is the only possible route to agency.

The council result is hers. She reports becoming president at her middle school and later tells Aoi how much election work, asking others and persistence it took. Her felt lack of Aoi's ease does not invalidate the work; it makes visible the difference between receiving a model and personally carrying out action. Nor does a selected school role establish an adult political vocation, universal leadership skill or the exact quality of her eventual administration. [HH-E04, HH-E06]

The same relation is vulnerable. On learning of the stalled high-school handoff, Haruka has to accommodate a sister who neither appears ideal nor performs her former office. Her later plea carries love and pressure together. She reports her own achievement to the person whose praise she especially wants, touches her face and asks for renewed conversation and the admired sister's stories. Her statement that otherwise she does not know what to do exposes an external warrant for her own direction. It is not a right to require Aoi's permanent perfection. Haruka reassures Aoi that Fumiya and the others are doing all the council work (V11 `item/xhtml/p-0030.xhtml` P519). Fumiya's earlier DM says he is trying to help with the council (`item/xhtml/p-0028.xhtml` P199–203). These are situated assurances and reports, not proof that every task is complete or costless. [HH-E06]

## 5. Asking to know, and being injured by an answer

The R05 correction is essential: Haruka's inquiry is not simply continued against her explicit refusal. She asks Kikuchi what she thinks of Aoi, stops Fumiya's attempt to interrupt and asks how Kikuchi knows. These are selected acts of participation. She is seeking access to her sister's world, not merely being mined while wholly passive. The food-court setting also matters; this is an ordinary shared meal that becomes an exposing inquiry, not a private therapeutic appointment. [HH-E05]

Participation does not establish informed anticipation of its emotional force. The represented coughing, shock, trembling and eventual tears make the cost visible. Fumiya cannot determine every cause of every tear, and neither can this dossier. Her request to continue should not be converted into a blanket permission to publish her words, investigate Nagisa, define the family or claim that the encounter was harmless. Kikuchi later directly identifies her creative absorption and harm. Accuracy and injury coexist with Haruka's decision to ask. [HH-E05, HH-E07]

A week after Cocoon, Fumiya reports that Haruka has not replied to his follow-up (V11 `item/xhtml/p-0028.xhtml` P199–200). That establishes an interval without a response; it does not establish a final refusal, deliberate punishment or a diagnosis. Her later initiative should not erase a period in which his chosen channel does not yield access.

Haruka's testimony also complicates a ready-made deprivation model. She describes abundant praise and birth-thanks language and says mother and daughter were close. Fumiya initially struggles with evidence that does not fit the answer he expected. Her report is not simply cancelled when the later historical account reveals injurious meaning-making. It preserves how familial affection was received by this particular daughter, even while her later admission that the home is unusual leaves conflict unresolved.

## 6. Reading and acting in V12

Haruka initially receives a manuscript from the visitors and leaves it outside Aoi's room after Aoi says she does not want it. Later she has taken it and nearly finished reading it on the sofa. The text does not show an instruction from Fumiya commanding her to produce the ensuing emotional reaction. She reads, cries and names Nagisa as her sister. Her recognition gives her a stake in the fiction independent of a courier's assignment. The object addressed to Aoi has also affected another bereaved reader. [HH-E08]

She calls it a novel for her sister, approaches, and presses the bundle on Aoi. Her initiative is forceful and consequential. Aoi imagines rejecting it but takes it away. A later beneficial response does not turn this into an unpressured original request. Haruka's tears cannot be dismissed as manipulative theater without evidence, but sincerity cannot erase pressure either. This is a different instance of the same broader problem: love, personal need and an effort to reach another person do not automatically supply authority over that person's uptake.

After Aoi leaves, Haruka's DM reports her unsteady departure. It provides Fumiya enough information to search; he later replies that he has found her. The message is practical action within a selected channel. It does not transmit the full private p0022 reading experience or establish a continuing supervision arrangement. Nor does later public play prove that the sisters have settled their family history or that Haruka no longer needs the model's apparent coherence. [HH-E09]

## 7. Recipient and knowledge differences

| Recipient / field | Haruka's evidenced conduct | Access or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Nagisa in childhood play | cheers, celebrates and receives thanks | local playful alliance, not all later beliefs or Nagisa's final mind |
| Aoi in shared play | invites, wins, explains a move, teases, remembers and continues | skill advantage reverses; encouragement intention partly uncertain |
| Aoi as model | reports effort, seeks praise/conversation and voices dependence | achievement is hers; flawless performance cannot be demanded as a debt |
| Mother | reports receiving frequent affirmation and some relief | her account neither proves all maternal meaning harmless nor supplies Yoko's private doctrine |
| Fumiya | tests his presence, asks about school, permits conversation, refuses LINE and offers Instagram | partial trust and a selected channel, not all-purpose access or heard access to his private qualifications |
| Kikuchi | asks for interpretation, interrupts an attempted stop, asks how she understands, cries | participation and harm coexist; no informed publication authorization or completed repair |
| V12 manuscript / delivery peers | receives, reads independently, interprets and urges uptake | her own response matters; reader pressure is not eliminated by later benefit |

Fumiya's spoken affirmation that he likes Aoi is heard at p0019 P239. His “as one person” qualification at P240 remains thought. Haruka must not be modeled as hearing the qualification or as possessing a final romantic classification. Likewise her account of the family is not proof that she knows every private disagreement between Aoi and Yoko or each adult/institutional response to Nagisa's persecution.

## 8. Rival accounts and scope of reconstruction

| Rival | Counterevidence and warranted revision |
|---|---|
| Passive copy of a perfect sister | Game initiative, local superior skill, guarded first meeting, campaign effort, selected contact channel and direct questions establish her own action. Model dependence still matters. |
| Fully autonomous after becoming president | Her desire for praise and fear about losing the model remain direct. Achievement does not settle a durable basis of value. |
| Merely a source of information or delivery route | She wants to learn, chooses what to share, reads as another bereaved recipient and reacts personally. Her subjectivity is consequential beyond informational utility. |
| Freely consenting to every part of the inquiry | She invites particular answers but is visibly hurt; no blanket use/representation permission follows. |
| Always naive or always socially expert | The alarm and channel refusal coexist with enthusiasm, incomplete understanding and dependence. Context changes her response. |
| A complete cure or an intentionally coercive rescue | V12 independent feeling and pressure are both evidenced; comprehensive intent and durable effect are not. |

Era-conditioned scenario constraints are possible, but exact new behavior is not established. In the retrospective game state, an invitation to shared play can be ordinary company and carry local teasing; do not script a conscious therapeutic plan. In the V11 first-contact state, unfamiliarity and place matter: she already tests the stranger and protects one communication channel, so unconditional disclosure is a poor default. In the V11 inquiry state, asking to continue and suffering from the answer are compatible. In the V12 relay state, her own recognition can motivate forceful action, but a future equivalent encounter might include refusal or a different response. None of these is a blinded prediction test.

**Do not infer:** a new vocation; every school/household routine; an exclusive favorite; a fixed personality score; a diagnosis from distress; informed consent to all testimony reuse; Aoi's permanent duty to perform; Nagisa's authentic final wish; access to Fumiya's unspoken romantic qualifier; or complete model independence and sibling repair.
