## 1. Responsibility and evidence boundary

This monograph reconstructs Natsubayashi Hanabi, ordinarily called Tama, through the admitted V12 boundary with V06.5/V08.5 kept story-local. It asks how an apparently stable moral core is learned, claimed and made effective; how directness varies by recipient; and when preserving an owned ideal creates obligations or costs for other people. Her class resistance, craft, ordinary friendship, family position, reciprocal teaching and self-criticism are separate evidentiary fields, not illustrations of one infallible trait.

The new primary work is targeted: V05/V06/V07/V08/V08.5 audit returns and V12's shop/interview scene. V01–V04 and V09–V11 are retained through the verified provisional file, current cumulative owners and accepted R04/R05 context, not newly reread in their entirety. §16 and the separate review notes make that limit recoverable. No whole-corpus promotion or final audit is claimed.

The prospective volume readings retain historical authority. Supplemental evidence is used only under its routed chronology. Tama's directness is not treated as a diagnosis, natural moral infallibility, or a fixed essence that makes all social adaptation corrupt.

## 2. Reconstructive thesis

Tama is the series' strongest test of whether deliberately learned social form can preserve rather than betray a stable evaluative core. Her difficulty in V05 is not lack of feeling, courage, or expressive ability. It is a mismatch among direct delivery, limited interest in most classmates, settled reputation, hostile recipients, and the exceptional cost of one failed approach under harassment.

Copied drills fail because they assume Tomozaki's deficits and goals. A distributed team succeeds only after Tama retains authority over the reason for changing and the method is rebuilt around her actual strengths. At the climax she uses the expanded interface to communicate the same principle she held before training: collective attack remains wrong even when directed at someone who has done wrong.

Her development is therefore neither `becoming normal` nor remaining untouched. She becomes more variable, legible, and strategically capable while preserving the right to refuse. That combination exposes Hinami's unresolved contradiction: Hinami loves Tama's unchanged correctness and violates that correctness through revenge on her behalf.

V12 changes what a stable core can mean. Tama explains that her hero ideal began in imitation of fiction and became hers through repeated acts. She also says she cannot always take the smoother course without later disliking herself. A borrowed value can become owned, while ownership can become a self-demand. Her cake remake protects a child's singular celebration and transfers delay and work to staff, queued customers and a visiting friend; she recognizes that her position as the shop manager's daughter gives the decision unusual latitude. This is neither a revelation that all her care was selfish nor confirmation that every costly refusal is right. It establishes an ethical remainder that the V05 triumph alone could conceal. [TH-P08](#th-p08)

The proposed central account is consequently **a learned commitment made answerable through practice, with uneven freedom over its application**. Tama can change delivery, choose privacy and accept help, yet find particular compromises intolerable. Her strength is continuity she can endorse; its corresponding risk is treating continued self-respect as a reason others must absorb the cost. This hypothesis would weaken if she proved uniformly incapable of compromise across low-stakes domains or, conversely, if later evidence showed she could always revise the governing end without strain. The admitted scenes support neither extreme.

## 5. Stable tendency versus developmental state

Tama's stable tendency is not `never change`. It is to require a reason she can own and to preserve a relatively consistent evaluative core. V05 demonstrates development because the following variables change:

- curiosity about particular classmates;
- willingness to vary delivery;
- capacity to use a publicly recognized character;
- understanding of recipient and timing;
- ability to coordinate with a support team.

What remains stable is the reason for action and the ability to refuse an unacceptable collective end. Continuity and development are therefore mutually supporting rather than opposites.

## 6. The V05 curriculum as a model of consent and fit

The first training cycle fails because it treats Tama as a smaller Tomozaki. Her baseline expressive fundamentals are already stronger, her desired relationship breadth is narrower, and public failure is more dangerous because the class has already stabilized a hostile reading.

The revised curriculum distributes responsibility:

- Tama retains final authority over whether and why to change;
- Tomozaki decomposes problems and revises assumptions;
- Mizusawa contributes social form and recipient modeling;
- Mimimi supplies relationship knowledge and emotional support;
- Kikuchi contributes interpretation;
- other peers help create safer practice and public legibility.

This is not perfect consent governance, but it is materially different from imposing one coach's universal ideal. The learner can reject methods, preserve the end, and use support without surrendering authorship.

That improvement does not erase how the project begins. Fumiya records without advance permission and imports exercises that fit his old limitations. Tama's immediate objection and later participation are separate facts. Nor is choosing adaptation under harassment evidence of a fair choice environment. Her later wish to flee after the keepsake is destroyed sets a limit to the claim that principled refusal makes her invulnerable. The successful stopping intervention depends on a support network and changed reception as well as her judgment. [DR05 §§4–10; CS §10; TH-P01](#th-p01)

## 7. Directness, form, and authenticity

Tama disproves both of these equations:

`direct = automatically effective`

and

`learned form = automatically false`.

Direct speech can fail when recipients lack context, when reputation preloads a hostile interpretation, or when a system punishes the speaker. Learned form can improve transmission while leaving the message and reason intact.

Her V05 stopping intervention is decisive because the new interface serves an old principle. If the training had replaced the principle with popularity or retaliation, success would be ethically different. Instead, technique expands her ability to make owned judgment socially consequential.

## 8. Relationship-conditioned reconstruction

### 8.1 Mimimi

Mimimi is Tama's closest relational field. Tama sees the person beyond rank and provides direct noncomparative recognition. Mimimi, in return, supplies energy, access, protection, and a wider interface. Their bond is reciprocal, although each could become overburdened if required to regulate the other's whole self-worth or safety.

The earlier appended V05 bonus makes reciprocity visible through unequal knowledge. Mimimi quietly declines a large karaoke outing for Tama's fit; Tama notices shrimp in a menu photograph and asks the café to omit it from Mimimi's crab pasta. Neither tells the other what she has done. Tama can be irritated by not being listened to, decline a grasping gesture, leave for the bathroom for an ordinary reason, speak politely to staff, and then privately enjoy Mimimi's appetite. This is neither a pure caregiver/dependent relation nor perfect mutual transparency. It is familiar affection with mundane inattention, bodily limits and concealed work. Shrimp dislike belongs to Mimimi; mushroom risotto is Tama's observed order, not a certified favorite. [TH-P06](#th-p06)

In V08.5, Tama does not interrupt Mimimi and Kikuchi merely because she notices a complicated atmosphere. She waits, later checks whether they are all right, and asks Mimimi to explain her own account before responding. Her statement about warmth in being leaned on also admits weight. The reassurance does not promise unlimited availability or make reliance automatically healthy. [TH-P07](#th-p07)

V12 adds Mimimi's fear that physical separation from Tama may make it difficult to keep liking herself; she does not doubt their tie. This is Mimimi's current self-theory, not an unshown Tama reply or proof that Tama's earlier care was wrong. A relationship specialist must preserve the value of particular recognition while asking whether the two can sustain self-regard and limits outside constant proximity. Their observed later presence beside Hinami supplies ordinary affiliation, not a newly negotiated care contract. [REL §17.1; DR12 §4.4/§7; V12 p0013:P26–79, p0026:P212–234]

### 8.2 Hinami

Hinami admires and loves Tama's unchanged correctness. That direct admission is strong evidence that Hinami's actual values exceed her adaptation doctrine. It is also a danger: V05 protection becomes revenge and makes Tama the justification for conduct Tama opposes. Love does not transfer Tama's moral authority to Hinami.

### 8.3 Tomozaki

Tomozaki's relation to Tama shifts from observer to teacher and collaborator. His most important learning is that effective instruction starts with learner state, recipient, goal, and risk rather than copying the teacher's path. Tama then supplies him with a stopping rule his strategy lacks.

V06 reverses the teacher relation in a low-pressure field. Tama says she no longer has to force herself to join every energetic act merely to remain with friends. When Fumiya directly requests a comic face, she can be puzzled, agree to capture and separately agree to upload, then ask him to try and teach him how to improve. This is not only his successful training made visible; her existing expressive competence becomes help he receives. His reflexive self-effacement also meets her direct request that he accept gratitude. [TH-P02](#th-p02)

In V12 he arrives seeking a model for Kikuchi's story and first encounters a worker too busy to stop for his inquiry. He is drawn into practical assistance, observes both the cake decision and its externalities, and afterward asks how she maintains self-belief. She reverses the question because she finds her own account difficult to formulate. Her explanation is therefore addressed, co-produced speech, not a preexisting philosophical manifesto read out for him. On leaving, he deletes a summary that would fit her into his borrowed-identity theory and sends remembered words instead. That act limits his use of her without proving a complete consent or representation policy. [TH-P08](#th-p08)

### 8.4 Erika, the class, and the support network

Tama distinguishes act from person more consistently than the retaliating class. Stopping the attack on Erika does not mean approving harassment, erasing accountability, or providing forgiveness. It asserts that collective cruelty remains wrong under a changed target.

The group support is real, but an adequate institutional resolution, aggressor-provided restitution and durable system reform are unestablished; the earlier V04 teacher's local questioning is not erased. The burden should not be narrated as safely solved by individual courage.

Actor precision matters. After Konno leaves in V05, Akiyama and other group members explicitly apologize to Tama. Tama gives a weighty acknowledgment, neither lightly dismissing the harm nor retaliating. This is a real difference from an undifferentiated claim that nobody apologized. It does not supply Konno's apology, a full account of each participant's acts, restitution or restored trust. Akiyama's later cold war is with Konno; it does not nullify this earlier Tama-facing act. [TH-P01](#th-p01)

### 8.5 Mizusawa: an unfamiliar recipient who can change

Tama initially associates Mizusawa with Nakamura's teasing and avoids that experience. When he asks whether she dislikes him, she says she still needs to talk with him to know what he is like. This does not erase her prior judgment about how he behaved; it suspends a global judgment about the person. He is unsettled by her direct answers and visibly enjoys them. Fumiya's account that he is calibrating his questions to her is an observer inference, supported by the change of interaction rather than access to a private technique. [TH-P03](#th-p03)

This dyad demonstrates a narrow but valuable form of revisability. A group association need not define every later encounter. Tama can cross it without becoming indiscriminately interested in everyone, and Mizusawa's fluency need not mean he is the only one who teaches. Their quick compatibility is evidence against a portable deficit carried unchanged by Tama, not proof that all hostile recipients are easily repaired.

### 8.6 Family and craft: praise, embarrassment and material limits

Tama's parents remember a child who refused unwanted acts and opposed bullying. They also tease her about insisting that taste should be enough and report that she has begun accepting the importance of making quality perceptible. These are affectionate parental recollections. Fumiya's sense of continuity with the class arc is an interpretation, not verification of every childhood event. Tama protests their public retelling and is embarrassed by friends tasting her work, while her response to her mother's service is direct gratitude. The combination is richer than a person always answering everyone with one blunt register. [TH-P04–05](#th-p04)

The grandfather is an active quality judge, not an invisible inheritance symbol. Some of Tama's products enter the shop, while a particular batch is refused for shape even though its taste is praised. The record permits a contrast between family possibility and a fully owned future: she likes both product and work but still intends to learn through experience before deciding succession. Her being an only child does not make succession inevitable, because alternative relatives or trusted employees are explicitly possible. [TH-P04–05](#th-p04)

### 8.7 Customers and staff: the authority of one person's standard

The V12 customer is a mother collecting a birthday cake for a child. Tama notices small damage, rejects a colleague's quick repair and insists that this celebration happens only once. The mother first says such extensive action is unnecessary, then thanks her. That sequence neither proves the customer secretly wanted a whole remake from the outset nor makes the eventual thanks false. [TH-P08](#th-p08)

Tama has substantial practical capacity: service calls, packaging and customer timing appear precise to Fumiya; the repair returns with a finished cake after about ten minutes. But the same scene explicitly gives staff, the queue and Fumiya more work. The colleague's concern is not simple indifference to the child, and the text supplies no full business accounting with which to prove a universally correct policy. Tama later acknowledges that being the shop manager's daughter made her insistence possible. Care, competence and unequal authority occupy the same act. A literary account must keep the recipients who absorb its cost visible.

## 9. Ordinary-life and preference evidence

Tama's ordinary evidence includes compact direct conversation, close time with Mimimi, selective peer participation, willingness to rehearse when purpose is accepted, public performance inside a structured production, and experimental cake-making connected to family work.

These details prevent the crisis from defining the whole person. They show selective interest rather than no interest, usable performance rather than compulsory performance, and practical exploration rather than a predetermined vocation.

### 9.1 Work pleasure exists before the later moral test

In V08 Tama explicitly likes the shop's sweets and its work. She already helps on holidays and sometimes learns baking from her grandfather on free weekends. A product being tasted by friends, a product cleared for sale and an original design are distinct achievements. V10's first original cake must not become her first baking or first useful shop contribution. V12 therefore returns to an already enjoyed practice; it does not introduce a labor identity invented solely to explain one heroic remake. That earlier liking does not certify that every later shift is happy or that the job is her chosen lifelong vocation. [TH-P04–05](#th-p04)

Quality standards also join the social arc without becoming a universal allegory. A truthful taste still needs an effective shape, just as a principle may need usable delivery. The father's analogy is attributed and the practical standards are specific. They do not prove that professional presentation can never deceive or that all private values should be optimized for an audience.

### 9.2 Objects do not automatically disclose taste

Fumiya initially notices pastel possessions and stuffed toys in Tama's room. The text immediately corrects the easy inference: they are largely childhood gifts retained, rather than her current taste. Do not turn their presence into a favorite palette or a hidden girlish essence. Likewise, the bracelet worn in the appended bonus is an observed purchase/use, not a complete jewelry preference; risotto is one order; remembered karaoke discomfort concerns a noisy setting with some less-close people. [TH-P04; TH-P06](#th-p04)

Her willingness to try a smaller karaoke occasion with Mimimi can coexist with that discomfort. The source does not show its complete outcome. The parallel karaoke story in V08.5 cannot fill the missing result, establish canonical music preferences or prove she became comfortable with every format.

The mainline V06.5 peer outing also shows active selection: she finds a repeated smallness gag boring, refuses a proposed karaoke trip and considers another activity before joining ordinary restaurant company. Her learned comic opening can be used and revised. Neither one refusal nor one acceptance defines permanent music taste or an immutable public persona. Exact route: `OEBPS/Text/part0033.xhtml` P10–65, story-local after V05; the early reviewer and integrating author independently read the complete window. This is ordinary selection, not a successful karaoke outcome.

### 9.3 Humor and bodily ease vary by recipient

With Mimimi, Tama can reject a grasp, correct a crude joke, arrange food quietly and privately laugh at an appetite. With Fumiya she can offer direct thanks, demand that it be received and teach exaggerated expression. With parents she protests affectionate exposure; with customers she uses service calls, polite explanation and apology. None of these samples is a complete idiolect, but together they refute a script in which every line must be a curt moral correction. [TH-P02–08](#th-p02)

### 9.4 What remains unknown

No complete favorite-food hierarchy, fashion/music profile, work schedule, household labor census, wage or business plan is established here. The V12 shared damaged cake is an offered meal and aftermath of labor, not a new durable cake preference. Her skill does not establish equal authority with every employee or a general entitlement to recruit visiting friends. Narrow ordinary observations make the model more useful precisely when they retain their limits.

## 10. Self-presentation and focalization

Tama is often described from outside because she does not supply long explanatory monologues. Other characters may convert brevity into a global theory of incapacity, purity, or stubbornness. The V05 training process corrects those projections by testing actual skills and preferences.

Her public character is best treated as a tool she can deploy, vary, and refuse. It is neither a prison that automatically falsifies her nor proof that every setting has become easy.

## 12. Rival readings and counterevidence

### `Tama succeeds by becoming less authentic`

The climax uses new delivery to carry her prior principle. The content and reason remain hers.

### `Tama was already perfect and did not need to change`

Recipient fit, social legibility, and strategic range materially improve. Refusing a universal-deficit model does not erase real learning.

### `Tama forgives Erika`

She stops collective retaliation. Konno's apology, Tama's forgiveness of Konno, restitution and completed justice are not established; Akiyama's separate apology is retained in §8.4.

### `Hinami protects Tama because Hinami secretly shares the same morality`

Hinami values Tama's morality but violates it through excessive retaliation. Admiration and internalization are different.

### `The cake proves Tama will inherit the family business`

It proves a concrete trial and output only.

## 15. Proposed current V12 account: preserving a self without erasing other people

### 15.1 A learned ideal can be real without being naturally innocent

The strong version of the old account treated Tama as a durable evaluative core whose interface changes. That remains a useful description of the V05 test: she opposes the same collective cruelty under a reversed target. V12 requires a formation and cost qualification. She does not describe an original pure self that precedes learning. She remembers imitating a hero from manga or animation, without certifying which medium, and says that repeated acts made this borrowed image hers. Her own use of defeating Konno makes Fumiya laugh; it is evidence of the image through which she understands her history, not an instruction for the analyst to call Konno an ontological villain. [TH-P08](#th-p08)

Nor is accepting a borrowed ideal simply the opposite of Hinami's formation. Both people have learned forms, relational history and self-directed effort. Fumiya sees Tama's willingness to continue despite distortion as an answer to Hinami/Alcia's demand for perfect external correctness. That is his comparison. Tama does not independently give him an authorized theory of Hinami or a prescription that another person should copy. The candidate's account is narrower: she can acknowledge an imperfect origin and imperfect conduct while retaining a practice she recognizes as hers.

This strengthens the rejection of `learned = fake`, while revising any implication that stable moral judgment must be unborrowed. It also removes a sentimental hierarchy in which Tama is genuine because she never imitates and other people are false because they do.

### 15.2 The cake tests authorship and cost together

Before the incident, Fumiya sees fluent work. Tama's pace and customer timing differ from the familiar school image because the field is practiced and demands concrete tasks. His request for an interview is displaced by legitimate shop work. He himself becomes useful through holding a door, packing cooling agents and carrying boxes. This matters to his attention: he receives a social subject with work and recipients already underway, rather than a free moral oracle waiting to answer a plot question. [TH-P08](#th-p08)

The damaged birthday cake then offers competing defensible interests. The colleague proposes a rapid repair. Tama attends to a small flaw and to the child's singular day; the mother initially releases the shop from doing more. The finished remake and thanks are real benefits. Equally real are the queue, the colleague's awkwardness, the visitor's diverted labor and the narrator's statement that the whole shop bears a consequence of Tama's choice. The customer's eventual gratitude cannot supply every other person's assent.

There is insufficient evidence for a business-policy verdict. We do not know prices, staff contracts, a standing remake protocol or what each waiting customer experiences. It would be excessive to turn this into a diagnosis of selfishness. It would be equally excessive to narrate the absorbed cost as proof that her integrity automatically improves every recipient's world. The **value judgment** offered here is conditional and limited: care becomes ethically incomplete when one person's endorsed standard silently assigns others the burden. The source's observed cost and Tama's own privilege acknowledgment support retaining that question; they do not resolve all versions of it.

### 15.3 Ownership can coexist with a constrained answer space

Tama agrees there may have been a cleverer way. She describes herself as distorted and explains that letting such a case go lightly might make her dislike herself afterward. This is direct self-report of an experienced limit. It is neither a clinical diagnosis nor proof that she cannot ever compromise. The V06 choice not to join every energetic act, V08 conditional succession and V08.5 decision to wait all prevent that global inference. [TH-P02/04/07/08](#th-p08)

The tension is more precise: she has freedom in methods, participation and disclosure, while particular self-defining acts can feel compulsory from within. An owned reason need not produce an unlimited menu of psychologically available responses. Her continuity becomes strongest when it can accept support and modify expression; it becomes costly when retaining the self depends on doing the demanding act despite other legitimate claims. This is a substantive reason for independent monograph responsibility rather than a dossier about correct bluntness.
