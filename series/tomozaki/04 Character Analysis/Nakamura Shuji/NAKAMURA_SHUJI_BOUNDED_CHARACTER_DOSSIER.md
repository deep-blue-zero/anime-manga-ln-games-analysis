## 1. The person the account must explain

Nakamura can be aggressive, status-conscious and difficult to approach. He can also work seriously at something he values, accept another person's superior skill, comfort Yuzu, make strangers less nervous, value a relationship's particular history and refuse a group demand when he thinks it is hurting his partner. An account that gives the first set of behaviors to an early false self and the second to a redeemed later self loses the chronology: care for Yuzu already appears in V01, alongside the competitive antagonism. An account that calls everything pride is equally uninformative. Pride changes what he will expose publicly, but it does not make the content of every attachment interchangeable. [NS-E01–E06]

The most defensible central account is **seriousness defended through a rough social interface**. Nakamura cares about being a real competitor and about actual people whose history with him matters. His means of protecting those commitments can be dismissive, intimidating or resistant to explanation. Those means sometimes protect a legitimate limit and sometimes impose costs on others. Neither a good end nor an abrasive form decides the whole act in advance. This is an interpretive account tested against the cases below, not a quotation of his complete self-theory.

The evidence is richest in peer competition, Yuzu's relationship, ordinary male-group belonging, educational route talk and selected collective care. It does not provide a complete childhood, family psychology, private couple protocol or settled vocation. His limited focalization does not imply a simple interior life; it limits what this dossier can certify.

## 3. Competition: threatened status and genuine learning

Nakamura's early excuses matter because they are behavior under defeat, not evidence that he is constitutionally unable to learn. The later game contains techniques Fumiya recognizes as newly acquired. In V01 `text/part0034.html` P86, the expert observer identifies a combination Nakamura previously could not perform; P244–246 identifies other difficult execution as the product of sustained practice. That evaluation is Fumiya's situated expertise, while the performed play is the immediate evidence. Nakamura can improve substantially and still lose to a much stronger opponent. A result-only portrait would erase the improvement just as an effort-only portrait would erase the remaining gap. [NS-E01]

Fumiya's defense gives Nakamura an unusual form of recognition: a person who defeated him refuses the crowd's dismissal of his work. The renewed challenge at P307 preserves rivalry rather than replacing it with grateful submission. It is reasonable to infer that being taken seriously matters to him. It is not reasonable to infer a complete self-worth system, that every aggressive act conceals insecurity, or that acknowledgment automatically repairs all earlier treatment.

This distinction also protects Yuzu's side. His intense practice has relational costs; the V01 reading preserves his brusque rejection of her ability to help him practice. Her chosen effort to become useful has its own agency and vulnerability. His seriousness is real without making his dismissal necessary or harmless. The later comfort is another act, not a retroactive permission slip.

## 4. Yuzu: an actual history, incomplete communication

When Yuzu breaks down after confronting the public atmosphere in V01, Nakamura approaches, recognizes that she has pushed herself and tells her she has worked hard. He offers a hand; she ultimately rises on her own. This is particular recipient knowledge and care in the same early volume that contains his antagonism. It should prevent an invented chronology in which empathy appears only after Fumiya reforms him. It does not prove that he always understands her or that Fumiya's possessive inner joke is an ethical account of the contact. [NS-E01]

The camp sequence provides another limit on external optimization. Friends can arrange information and opportunities around Nakamura's pride, but the actual outing with Yuzu is theirs to choose. Nakamura's demand that the information remain private indicates an immediate boundary in that local status field. The friends' affection is compatible with deception; his eventual choice does not certify his informed consent to the group's manipulation of Takei or to every hidden assumption in the plan. [NS-E03]

By V07 he supplies Fumiya an account of why Yuzu cannot be replaced by a hypothetical better specification: their lived time is part of the relation itself. This is a theory offered from his own relationship, not a universal proof that historical closeness is romantic. Its force is particularity. The actual person is not interchangeable with an imagined superior candidate. [NS-E06]

The supplemental birthday story tests communication rather than simply confirming good boyfriend status. The reader knows Yuzu's fear, her effort to conceal distress and her direct refusal of a breakup she anticipates. Nakamura appears awkward, chooses a familiar bench and eventually produces a birthday present. His apparent avoidance has a different explanation from the one she fears. A caring surprise and a costly gap in reassurance coexist. The scene does not establish that he knowingly cultivated her anguish; equally, the gift does not make the anguish imaginary or settle a future communication policy. [NS-E05]

## 5. Ordinary sociability and friendship

Nakamura's intimidating appearance and abrasive speech do not exhaust his social skill. At the V07 festival, he approaches Gumi and her friends, notices two visitors' nervousness and contrasts it with Gumi's conspicuous lack of nervousness. The joke relaxes the table. He then participates in first-name introductions. Fumiya's surprise is evidence of a changed experience of him, not proof that Nakamura has just acquired the capacity. [NS-E06]

This scene deserves an ordinary-life place in the dossier. It has no need to redeem a bully, instruct Fumiya or prove romance. Nakamura is entertaining guests and enjoying a mixed social setting. Fumiya's fleeting thought about his having a girlfriend is not evidence of infidelity. Familiarity and hosting are not automatic sexual intent. Conversely, the scene does not license a general prediction that everyone will feel safe around him.

The later acknowledgment of Mizusawa's stronger preferences supplies a related friendship test. He can find a friend's increasing independence inconvenient while regarding it positively. That is more specific than generic loyalty: the friend is allowed to be less convenient. Birthday-message gathering with Yuzu also belongs to his own care contribution, rather than being credited wholesale to the protagonist's plan. [NS-E08–E09]

## 6. Family, education and what remains unchosen

In V08 `text/part0014.html` P223–226, Nakamura reports that his father's acquaintance holds a senior company position and that some accommodation is available, coupled with a requirement that he first reach at least the stated university level. The immediate task, in his account, is that educational threshold. This is evidence for a materially mediated route and a family condition. It is not proof that his family owns the company, that the job is guaranteed, that he has chosen the work for its own sake or that a comprehensive parental psychology is available. [NS-E07]

This account changes how one reads his apparent ease. A socially forceful classmate can have a route constrained and enabled by family arrangements. He is not merely selecting from limitless possibilities. Nor should his situation be equated with Takei's tuition pressure simply because both discuss university: the specific conditions differ. The dossier preserves the contrast without ranking either household's moral worth or inventing actual income.

## 7. Refusal in V11: protection, protest and the limits of knowledge

The cafeteria discussion begins with practical fallout: Yuzu reports the missing council handoff and proposes helping. Nakamura immediately declines. He cites the way Hinami spoke to them and the ignored contact. When friends appeal to enduring friendship, he agrees that it has not simply broken. His sharper objection comes when Yuzu begins blaming herself: her being made to feel that way is precisely why he will not cooperate. He leaves with his tray; Takei follows; Yuzu decides to follow him after apologizing to the remaining group. [NS-E10]

Several judgments must remain separate. His protest against the treatment is explicit; self-respect is a supported interpretation of it. A partner-protective reason is explicit. His manner exerts pressure on the discussion and leaves the remaining friends feeling abandoned. He does not have the reader's full retrospective access to Hinami's family or her later private chapter. The scene can therefore support a legitimate limit on further demanded labor without making his interpretation of Hinami complete. It can support care for Yuzu without proving that she has negotiated her own capacity threshold with him.

Calling the refusal either healthy boundaries or cruel abandonment would skip the literary work. The question is what he refuses, on what available information, with what claim about friendship and with what effects on the people present. Aoi's need for withdrawal does not create a blanket duty to perform for friends; their hurt does not give them total access to her reasons. Neither side's incomplete knowledge makes the other side's costs disappear.

## 8. V12 participation and the current boundary

The later manuscript delivery shows that V11 refusal was not an immutable personality rule. Nakamura arrives with Yuzu and Takei; Haruka receives the paper; later he sits beside Yuzu at the school event. Those are actual changes in local participation. The source does not show the private conversation that produced them. Hinami's hypothesis that the visitors may be there to apologize is her hypothesis, not a visitor motive report. [NS-E11]

He can participate again without the analyst inventing a complete reconciliation protocol, an apology to Hinami, newly shared family knowledge, or a standing agreement to absorb future group costs. The final ordinary arrangement preserves affiliation. It does not certify that every concern he voiced in V11 has been answered.

## 10. Rivals and do-not-infer constraints

- **He is only a status bully.** Rejected as complete account by genuine learning, early comfort, ordinary hosting, history-based attachment and practical care. Earlier harm remains part of the record.
- **Defeat reforms him into a good friend.** Too linear. Some care is early; recognition and friendship change at different clocks; no total redemption event is shown.
- **His protectiveness proves a fully equal relationship.** Unsupported beyond the local acts. Yuzu's fear, labor and unshown private negotiations remain relevant.
- **He stops caring about Hinami in V11.** Contradicted by his explicit distinction between friendship and cooperation, then locally changed V12 participation.
- **He owes no further consideration because he refused.** A refusal establishes his choice, not the universal rightness of every supporting interpretation or effect.
- **University connections disclose a settled adult identity.** Unsupported. Preserve the reported condition, not a guaranteed career or class diagnosis.

Do not invent private childhood trauma, psychiatric explanations, a comprehensive sexual history, hidden apology, complete family finances, all-purpose dominance motive or privileged access to Hinami's interior. Do not put later private supplemental knowledge into an earlier Nakamura's mind.

## 11. Supported literary transfer domains

The dossier can inform cautious interpretation of familiar peer competition, recognition after defeat, ordinary hosting, rough advice, particular couple care and refusal under perceived unfair blame. A new case must establish era, audience, relationship standing, known history and the exact demand before inferring a likely response. Severe unfamiliar crisis, professional management, adult marriage/parenting, legal decisions, precise private desire rankings and unseen household disputes fall outside the demonstrated domain. No dialogue generator or derived model is created here.
