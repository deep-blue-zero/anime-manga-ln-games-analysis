# Kikuchi Fuka — character monograph through Volume 11

> **Authority notice:** This is a valuable pre-remediation character-study candidate, not yet mature literary authority. It requires deeper chronology, relationship governance, Japanese voice/register, rival readings, and claim/locator routing after cumulative backfill.

## 1. Responsibility and evidence boundary

This provisional monograph reconstructs Kikuchi Fuka through V11, with an intended responsibility for distinguishing selective sociability from deficit, observation from authority, fiction from testimony, chosen relationship from idealized compatibility, and artistic vocation from publication outcome.

The historical volume readings remain authoritative for their prospective boundaries. V06.5 and V08.5 are used only under their story-local placement. Kikuchi's fictional figures are evidence about her modeling and desires, not direct access to the people they resemble. No post-V11 manuscript, contract, publication, or relationship outcome is admitted.

## 2. Reconstructive thesis

Kikuchi's arc is not a shy person's linear conversion into universal social ease. It is a movement from being observed as a quiet exception toward choosing **which worlds deserve participation**, **which forms can carry her interior knowledge**, and **what obligations follow when interpretation affects real people**.

Her greatest competence is perceptual and formal: she notices relational structures that socially fluent characters miss and renders them through fiction. That competence is neither passive purity nor omniscience. It can make her vulnerable to self-erasure when she treats another person's apparent nature as a law she must accommodate. It can also wound when an accurate model is spoken or written without sufficient consent and impact governance.

Her relationship with Tomozaki develops by repeated choice rather than predestined compatibility. V07 establishes mutual selection; V08 proves that selection does not solve maintenance; V09 repairs through fuller disclosure and a jointly authored history; V10-V11 make her a collaborator in the Hinami project while exposing jealousy, priority, fictionalization, and privacy costs. At the current boundary she is both a girlfriend protecting her own stake and a writer confronting why she writes.

## 5. Selective habitat is not a universal social deficit

Kikuchi often prefers quiet, books, writing, and lower-intensity interaction. The source does not establish that those preferences are symptoms to eliminate. Her V07 attempt to participate everywhere is important because it looks like growth while making her own affinities and energy disappear.

The `炎人` counterexample and `作家志望` choice reframe development. A person can learn public forms and still select a narrower habitat. Agency is shown not by maximum sociability but by the ability to enter, refuse, and sustain environments for owned reasons.

Her later participation is therefore not evidence that the earlier Kikuchi was false. It is evidence of range:

- she can work privately and present publicly;
- she can prefer one-to-one talk and join a coordinated group;
- she can support Tomozaki and still identify jealousy;
- she can end a call rather than continue after her words are dismissed;
- she can collaborate on Hinami without surrendering the question of artistic harm.

## 6. Observation, interpretation, and epistemic authority

Kikuchi's models are often strong because she attends to patterns others normalize. She identifies missing motives, incongruent values, and relational structures. Tomozaki repeatedly uses her descriptions to revise his own more tactical reading.

Three layers must remain separate:

1. **observed conduct:** what Hinami, Tomozaki, Mimimi, or another person directly does;
2. **Kikuchi's model:** an inference that may organize the conduct accurately;
3. **fictional interiority:** a designed consciousness serving a literary structure.

V11 deliberately tests these boundaries. Her account of a person who lost a god or warrant is substantially corroborated by Hinami's family history. Yet applying that account to Haruka causes pain. Accuracy does not erase timing, exposure, recipient, or consent. Alcia's inner states still cannot be cited as Hinami testimony.

## 7. Fiction as form, relationship, and risk

Kikuchi's fiction performs at least five functions:

- it externalizes models that ordinary dialogue cannot hold;
- it lets her examine incompatible values without resolving them into one winner;
- it carries private desire, as in the difference between public and private endings;
- it gives other characters a perspective from which to understand themselves;
- it creates exposure risk when recognizable people and pain become material.

`純混血とアイスクリーム` reaches Tomozaki after direct reassurance fails because narrative permits identification without immediate argumentative defense. That is a communicative affordance, not proof that fiction is truer than testimony.

The conditional publication approach raises a professional problem. Wider circulation increases consequence. The missing question is no longer whether Kikuchi has skill; it is what motive can justify continuing when insight may wound, and what consent or transformation practices should constrain use of living models.

## 8. Relationship-conditioned reconstruction

### 8.1 Tomozaki Fumiya

Tomozaki initially treats Kikuchi as a route inside an assignment. The relationship becomes real when he admits limits and invests in what they actually share. She, in turn, helps him separate practiced form from false content.

Their romantic relationship is strongly authored at initiation: Tomozaki distinguishes real alternatives, returns for clarification, asks, and receives consent. Its difficulty is maintenance rather than legitimacy. V08-V11 repeatedly separate:

- being chosen from receiving time;
- knowing a partner loves someone from having complete information;
- voluntary support from absence of jealousy;
- apology from standing governance;
- coauthorship from equal exposure to risk.

Kikuchi's development is visible when she protects her stake directly. She questions, objects, names hurt, asserts the relationship against Rena, ends a failed conversation, and later selects collaboration for an explicit purpose. Tomozaki's V11 repair is meaningful because he thanks her as a source of action rather than asking her to disappear for Hinami.

### 8.2 Hinami Aoi

Kikuchi knows Hinami primarily through observation, testimony, and modeled fiction, not reciprocal intimacy of equal depth. Hinami becomes a major artistic and ethical problem because her visible structure invites explanation while her informational boundaries remain strong.

Kikuchi's insight should not be confused with ownership. The final collective project improves on a single-author model by distributing partial perspectives, but Hinami has neither participated nor consented at the endpoint.

### 8.3 Mimimi

Mimimi's continuing love creates a real relational condition. Kikuchi knows of the confession and does not reduce Mimimi to a defeated rival. Her own insecurity is still valid. The current source supports mutual awareness and restraint, not an absence of asymmetry or cost.

### 8.4 The peer and professional worlds

The festival lets Kikuchi move from private writer to public collaborator. Later birthdays, tournament attendance, group planning, and the Hinami project broaden that membership. The editor's interest adds a professional audience whose values, deadlines, and consequences differ from friendship. None of these domains wholly replaces the quiet affinity world in which her authorship formed.

## 9. Self-presentation and focalization

Kikuchi is often presented through Tomozaki's admiration. That focalization can make quietness appear morally pure and interpretive skill nearly magical. Later volumes correct the idealization:

- she can misjudge timing or impact;
- she can want incompatible things;
- she experiences jealousy and anger;
- she can self-exclude through idealized accommodation;
- her art can expose people;
- she requires information, touch, time, and reassurance rather than existing as effortless understanding.

Her politeness is neither simple concealment nor transparent essence. It is a form that can carry attention and can also delay conflict. Boundary speech becomes a meaningful developmental addition, not replacement of a false earlier self.

## 10. Ordinary-life and preference evidence

Kikuchi's preferences are clearest in repeated low-stakes choices:

- sustained reading and discussion of Michael Andy;
- writing, revising, and privately testing fictional structures;
- quieter conversation and chosen one-to-one time;
- scheduling dates and asking for ordinary couple maintenance;
- participating in festivals, birthdays, travel, games, and peer projects when the purpose matters;
- observing speech and behavior before offering an interpretation.

These ordinary patterns establish both stability and change. She does not cease preferring books and selected company when she gains public authorship. Nor does literary compatibility eliminate ordinary relationship needs.

## 12. Rival readings and counterevidence

### `Kikuchi is naturally complete and needs no social development`

She gains real range in collaboration, public authorship, boundary speech, conflict, and repair. The error lies in treating universal assimilation as the only form of development.

### `Her fiction predicts the correct romance`

Public and private endings diverge, and the text marks them as authored possibilities. Real choices still require direct clarification and consent.

### `Because her Hinami interpretation is accurate, using it is justified`

Haruka's reaction and Kikuchi's own motive crisis directly reject that inference. Accuracy and permission are independent.

### `Her support for Tomozaki proves she is no longer jealous`

She explicitly retains jealousy and hurt. Chosen cooperation is stronger evidence of agency when its cost is preserved.

### `Professional publication is secured`

Only a conditional route and possible schedule exist. Completion, editorial acceptance, contract, release, reception, and sustainable vocation remain open.

## 13. Current endpoint and open hypotheses

### Established at high confidence

1. Kikuchi's social development is best modeled as increased selectable range, not compulsory universal ease.
2. She is a high-competence writer and observer whose models remain fallible and ethically bounded.
3. Her relationship with Tomozaki is mutually selected and repeatedly repaired, not maintenance-complete.
4. She increasingly protects her own interest through direct boundary speech and refusal.
5. Fiction can communicate across defenses without becoming testimony or automatic permission.
6. V11 turns artistic motive and impact governance into her principal unresolved vocational problem.

### High-medium hypotheses

- Kikuchi's most durable route joins selected habitat with public authorship rather than choosing one against the other.
- Her willingness to join the group project can become coauthorship rather than self-erasure only if her jealousy, privacy, and exposure judgments remain speakable.
- Professional attention may sharpen rather than solve the gap between insight and authorization.

### Open

- why she ultimately chooses to write;
- manuscript completion and publication outcome;
- consent and fictionalization practices for living models;
- durable couple rules for time, privacy, Hinami priority, and third-party contact;
- whether the collective project is executed and how Hinami responds; and
- the long-term compatibility of romantic, artistic, and professional commitments.
