# Narita Tsugumi / Gumi — the work of keeping life easy

This account is accepted at its stated bounded literary scope through the admitted V12 boundary. See the [implementation review](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_CHARACTER_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md) and [source and review manifest](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_LITERARY_EVIDENCE_AND_REVIEW_MANIFEST.json) for independent acceptance, source returns, corrections and remaining limits. A bounded literary role is separate from a derived reconstruction model or aggregate release.

**Bounded individual dossier.** Narita Tsugumi / 成田つぐみ is called Gumi at her karaoke workplace. This is a positive source identity bridge, not a guessed nickname or an alias for Hinami Haruka. The given name remains the attested hiragana; no alternative kanji is invented. B is the accepted bounded individual literary responsibility; it is separate from a mature-monograph role, an exhaustive occurrence index or a derived behavioral model. `NT-E` is local navigation and does not replace existing source C/Q or cumulative identities.

**Evidence control.** DIRECT establishes represented speech, action or focalized thought, not the truth of every proposition spoken. CORROBORATED INFERENCE identifies a reading supported across routes; the governing person-level account remains an INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS tested against counterevidence. Fairness and authority judgments are VALUE JUDGMENTS grounded in the stated acts and limits. Unshown motive, knowledge, outcome and policy remain OPEN.

## 1. A chosen orientation with actual content

Narita is often useful to Fumiya as a countervoice to his effort-centered life. That function should not swallow the person doing the speaking. She chooses a job for its combination of acceptable pay, low burden and flexible shifts; shops with named friends; tires and wants somewhere to rest; resists an inconvenient detour, then takes the initiative when returning or arguing would be more troublesome; works alongside a colleague; jokes across ages and roles; and imagines an easier future while wanting enough self-support not to become helpless if a partner leaves. These are particular preferences and decisions, not simply a philosophical refusal to be Fumiya. [NT-E01–E07]

The governing account is **ease as an end that sometimes requires competent action**. She does not need to love effort in order to act purposefully. Nor does practical success prove she secretly shares the protagonist's joy in challenge. Her own account makes some exertion acceptable because it secures an environment in which less exertion will be needed. The relationship between action and desired life differs from his, even when both can describe an activity as worthwhile.

Her comic claims of having no desire are self-presentation with an internal qualification: she wants not to have to strive. Money, flexible time, comfortable rest and reduced dependence give that preference positive content. A literal empty-person interpretation contradicts the choices the scene supplies. The opposite overcorrection—making her a fully worked-out philosopher of liberation—also exceeds the evidence. The orientation is tested locally; its whole biography and universal application are unavailable.

## 3. Work is selected, not merely endured

The V04 consultation makes her job choice explicit. She works because life cannot be sustained by doing literally nothing, and she wants the money needed for ease. Her stated criteria are a rate she finds acceptable, relatively easy work and considerable shift flexibility. This is her practical comparison of the job, not an independently audited labor-market survey. It does not establish her total earnings or that every coworker experiences the job as easy. [NT-E02]

The workplace also supplies conduct. She calls out orders and room extensions, and in V05 washes glasses in a coordinated sequence while Mizusawa rinses. The scene is unhurried because the shift is quiet, but work continues. Her low-effort orientation is therefore not evidence that she refuses to perform basic tasks or makes others do everything. The available demonstration is routine competent participation, not a certificate of exceptional service or future professional mastery. [NT-E01; NT-E03]

The account needs both scale and motive. She can be effective without universally enjoying the task, and she can find some social pleasure in the setting without selecting it as a permanent vocation. A model that equates ability with liking would make her activity incoherent; a model that equates avoiding challenge with inability would erase the work.

## 4. Advice: a different planet, not an all-knowing oracle

Fumiya's wish to motivate classmates strikes Narita as alien: not only does he work hard himself, he wants to make unmotivated people work. She nevertheless agrees to consult, framing it as harmless enough to her and as communication between different orientations. Her teasing welcome gives the exchange an interpersonal tone beyond clue delivery. She is amused and engaged, not merely a passive information device. [NT-E02]

Her explanation identifies effort cost and return. A person who values ease can move when the return becomes attractive enough. She also infers that Konno's willingness to dominate others already consumes energy and therefore indicates desires stronger than Narita's professed wish to do little. The inference may be useful; it is not direct access to a person she knows only through Fumiya's account in this exchange.

Importantly, when Fumiya asks what Konno actually wants, Narita refuses the pretense of knowing. Her comic no-desire logic cannot literally prove ignorance of every other person's desire, but the practical boundary is sound: her general cost model does not supply the specific target's full motive. Fumiya's amused sense that she has become an authority on laziness is his framing. It does not transform her into an omniscient strategist or make her estimates a general psychological law.

The ethical problem of motivating someone else also remains. Narita provides a model; Fumiya chooses how to use it. Her consultation does not give him Konno's permission, and it does not make Narita co-author of every later class consequence. The V05 return shows that she asks what happened; it does not establish that she anticipated the escalating conflict.

## 5. Named friends, fatigue and changing the least costly action

The supplement begins this part of her life away from Fumiya. Narita shops with Mamiya Yoko and Fujii Hitomi. The narrated group preference for certain fashion shops belongs to this story-local first-high-school-year context, rather than a permanent exclusive brand identity. She handles a friend's garment playfully and later wants rest after walking in the hot city. A lemon-tea bottle is an actual object; its presence is not enough to establish a favorite drink. [NT-E04]

The detour to Mizusawa's workplace tests her cost reasoning in motion. Initially she would prefer the nearby café; she has no energy to call out to him. The friends become curious. She eventually agrees because arguing about whether to go seems more troublesome than going. At the doorway the others become hesitant; she presses forward because walking back now feels worse and because she knows a high-school acquaintance has entered. The action changes from resisting the excursion to leading it, without a conversion into an adventure-seeker.

This is more than comic laziness. Costs are comparative and sequential. Her own actions can change when the locally easier option changes. The source gives a particular choice process rather than a mathematical utility function: it does not establish that she always calculates optimally, that her friends never influence her or that bodily fatigue is a medical condition.

She also corrects a mistaken identity at the bar. The master calls for Yuji, and a different Mizusawa appears; Narita directly points out that they mean another Mizusawa. Her clarity cuts through the attempted familiar greeting. The brothers' names and distinct roles should remain separate; there is no need to make every social misunderstanding evidence of her incompetence.

## 6. Familiarity, teasing and a small actual refusal

Narita's ease at the bar contrasts with her friends' nervousness. She can address the bartender, identify her acquaintance, tease the brothers and decline the invitation to call Takahiro by his given name. The brief refusal is actual recipient-specific agency. It should not become an invariant honorific policy: the surrounding exchange turns the shared surname into a joke, and her communicative familiarity is not reducible to which address she initially accepts. [NT-E05]

The group enjoys the brothers' timed banter. The scene explicitly distinguishes nonalcoholic drinks; it should not be turned into a story about underage drinking, adult expertise or hidden romance. Narita's presence in a bar setting and confident speech do not establish comprehensive adult experience. A local accepted space and familiar worker make this encounter different from every unknown venue.

At the V07 festival she is again noticeably relaxed while the other visitors straighten up and become nervous. Nakamura jokes about the contrast; she introduces herself by given name at a loose point in the conversation and teases Mizusawa about his presentation and relationship status. These are actual ordinary exchanges, not an inferred romantic interest in every man she jokes with. Fumiya's surprise at how naturally the others banter locates his own developing competence. [NT-E06]

The pattern supports informal, playful and sometimes deliberately slack social performance with known recipients. It does not establish a complete idiolect, habitual voice pitch, global flirtatiousness or a guaranteed response to severe unfamiliar stress. No independently heard audio is available in these returns.

## 7. A warm interface can carry an information cost

The V05 workplace scene is a useful counterweight to an entirely charming portrait. Narita casually mentions Fumiya's earlier effort to motivate Konno to Mizusawa. Mizusawa already knows in that particular case. Fumiya nonetheless wonders inwardly about revealing such material before asking him. The text gives a potential boundary problem alongside routine competent work and easy affiliation. [NT-E03]

Do not exaggerate the event. Narita is not shown knowing a secrecy agreement and deliberately breaking it; Fumiya does not speak the concern as a reprimand; no later negotiated confidentiality rule is supplied. The supported point is that easy conversational circulation can outpace permission. Her ability to make an exchange comfortable is not evidence that every information owner has approved it.

Nor is the privacy concern an excuse to erase genuine ordinary rapport. It is precisely because the interaction is relaxed and the consultation has become a casual story that disclosure can seem natural. A bounded account should preserve how a low-burden style helps people talk while sometimes avoiding a clarification another person might have needed.

## 8. Future ease with a practical anti-dependence limit

Asked about the future in V08, Narita gives the comic answer that she wants to marry a wealthy person. She immediately complicates it: leaning entirely on someone else could leave her unable to stand if that person went away. Rebuilding ordinary independence from that position would itself be hard and a poor return on effort. Her minimum self-support is therefore not an analyst-imposed virtue that disproves her wish for ease. It is part of the same practical orientation. [NT-E07]

Her phrasing supports a future preference and an anticipated vulnerability. It does not provide an actual wealthy partner, a degree or occupation selected to secure independence, savings, a complete financial plan or a future marital outcome. The prevention logic is clear; the institutional route remains sparse. The dossier must not supply a career in order to make the preference respectable.

Fumiya then describes wanting to keep setting goals and taking challenges. Narita calls that way of life intolerable to her. He proposes that they share a wish to enjoy life; she says she does not understand his synthesis. The analyst can compare orientations, but cannot report mutual philosophical agreement. Her refusal to endorse his common premise protects a difference the protagonist would like to reconcile. It is not proof that she is incapable of reflection.

This also limits a universal cost model. She has reasons, but the evidence does not show an explicit numerical ordering or a policy that predicts every case. The practical future limit, the work choice and the friends' detour resemble each other; resemblance is not a license to erase their different stakes, audiences and information.

## 10. Rivals, supported domains and endpoint

The **lazy therefore incapable** reading fails on purposeful work and initiative. The **secretly industrious** reading substitutes the protagonist's value for her expressed reason. The **desireless** reading ignores the positive conditions she wants. The **pure dependent spouse fantasy** misses the explicit minimum-independence rule. The **wise cost oracle** exceeds her own refusal to name Konno's exact motive and her occasional loose handling of another person's information. The **universally friendly flirt** reading overextends selected banter and ignores recipient/context differences.

Supported literary transfer domains include a familiar part-time workplace, low-stakes peer leisure, comparative inconvenience, casual advice and discussion of a future preference under the stated orientation. New interpretation should establish actual fatigue, request, standing, alternatives and information rather than generating a default refusal to work. Confidence falls sharply for severe unfamiliar crisis, sustained physically demanding labor, independent household management, financial planning, adult partnership, private family values or a detailed educational history. No clinical explanation of stamina or personality is supplied.

At the V12 boundary, the cast sheet preserves an identity and retrieval responsibility, not new plot conduct. No actual marriage, wealth, profession, wider private biography or final reformed work ethic is established. The absence of a fresh late psychological episode does not invalidate the earlier individual evidence and does not authorize fabricating one.

## 12. Downstream handoff and remaining debts

The effort/future specialist should preserve an end that values ease without interpreting all competent action as love of exertion. The ensemble should retain Narita's named friends, ordinary workplace, selected initiative and privacy cost, rather than retrieving her only for a clue about Konno or Mizusawa. The voice specialist can use the actual metaphors and recipient exchanges with the stated limits; it cannot infer an exhaustive speaking model from a comic cadence.
