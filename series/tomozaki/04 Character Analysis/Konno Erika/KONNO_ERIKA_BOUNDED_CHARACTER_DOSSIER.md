# Konno Erika — control of the occasion, particular care and unfinished accountability

This account is accepted at its stated bounded literary scope through the admitted V12 boundary. See the [implementation review](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_CHARACTER_IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md) and [source and review manifest](../../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_SUPPORTING_LITERARY_EVIDENCE_AND_REVIEW_MANIFEST.json) for independent acceptance, source returns, corrections and remaining limits. **Bounded B dossier.** Konno Erika is one person; Erika and Konno are not separate supporting subjects. Independent review has accepted its bounded literary scope. It gives harmful agency, ordinary enjoyment and particular help one individual home without declaring mature-monograph breadth, a redemption, a diagnosis or a derived reconstruction model. `KE-E` labels are local retrieval references; `CRI-KON-001`, `CRI-SOC-002`, `CRI-OQ-SOC-001` and existing SOC/CS identities remain unchanged.

**Evidence control.** DIRECT establishes represented speech, action or focalized thought, not the truth of every proposition spoken. CORROBORATED INFERENCE identifies a reading supported across routes; the governing person-level account remains an INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS tested against counterevidence. Fairness and authority judgments are VALUE JUDGMENTS grounded in the stated acts and limits. Unshown motive, knowledge, outcome and policy remain OPEN.

## 1. Governing account and its rival

Konno's conduct is intelligible through the importance she gives to controlling how an occasion defines her and the people around her. She can use group hierarchy to impose burdens while avoiding a direct command, calibrate harassment to what bystanders will tolerate, and react to humiliation in ways that destroy her own advantageous position. She can also ask a friend's opinion, accept a specific correction, enjoy clothes and food, act efficiently to relieve that friend's distress, and make a useful contribution to a shared performance. The capacities are not distributed between a real kind girl and an artificial bully. They belong to the same source-limited account. [KE-E01–E08]

The resulting interpretation is **recipient-specific flexibility within a status-sensitive and sometimes coercive social practice**. She is neither unable to listen nor safe to depend on without qualification. A trusted friend's competence can receive a response very different from a lower-status classmate's reluctance or a public challenge. Her agency includes the ability to care and the choice to harm. The latter is not excused by the former; the former should not be erased to make condemnation easier.

This is an inference across represented behavior and other characters' interpretations. The source does not provide a comprehensive Konno interior, childhood explanation, complete desire ordering or family history. Claims about why she selects a target must retain their observer and evidentiary strength. The dossier explains a bounded range of demonstrated practices; it does not pretend to possess the origin of her personality.

## 3. Public framing: why the manner matters

The V01 confrontation is not merely an argument about whether a game is worthwhile. Konno's reply after Fumiya's defense makes earnest speech itself available for ridicule; followers take their cue from her. Fumiya experiences this as a change in what counts as acceptable behavior in the room. That is his analytical interpretation of a directly represented sequence, not a universal definition imposed by an omniscient narrator. Yuzu then attempts a costly intervention that disrupts the frame. Konno leaves rather than continuing that exact exchange. [KE-E01]

This supplies a bounded example of tactical stopping, but not a general ethical restraint. Her withdrawal is compatible with a tactical response to an occasion she can no longer control; the exact motive is not directly narrated. The fact that she stops there does not establish that she stops whenever harm becomes apparent. Later harassment and her collapse under a different public threat prevent the extrapolation.

In V04, the captaincy scene shows a more deniable method. A classmate is offered a formally available choice under a group frame that makes refusal costly. Konno hurries Hirabayashi toward an answer; followers reinforce the pressure. Kawamura questions whether the choice is being forced and offers time. That adult action must remain in the evidence. Its presence does not prove an adequate institutional remedy, and Hirabayashi's eventual assent does not make the surrounding pressure uncoerced. [KE-E02]

Calling Konno a social genius would hide the limits; calling her merely loud would hide the mechanism. The act uses an existing norm and a coalition. Its power depends on recipient position, bystander expectations and the cost of resisting, not on verbal force alone.

## 4. Motive mixture, rejected certainty and chosen harm

The V04 source reading allows several motives to be reconstructed: status preservation, irritation, care for Yuzu and sensitivity around Nakamura. These motives need not be mutually exclusive. A strategy may work by activating more than one of them. Yet its success does not prove that the strategist has exhaustively understood Konno, and it does not mean she authorized the motivational frame constructed around her. [KE-E02]

The V05 effort ledger describes a reactive status-preservation regime: no visible submission, control of interpretation and calibrated use of the sympathy threshold. These are useful descriptions of the observed crisis. They are not a medical diagnosis or a complete account of what gives her pleasure in life. Romantic vulnerability helps explain why public cues can wound her; it does not make the classmates she harms responsible for managing that vulnerability.

Konno's own agency must survive a system explanation. Atmosphere makes some attacks easier, but she is not only a passive channel for it. Sustained harassment and the treatment of Akiyama remain attributable choices. The observer's knowledge of every preparatory conversation, the degree of subordinate pressure and the actor who damaged the lead case remain incomplete. Where the source leaves an actor unidentified, this dossier does not fill the blank with either Konno or Hinami simply because a causal story would become tidier. [KE-E03]

## 5. Reverse targeting and the right not to be persecuted

Hinami engineers cues around accumulated resentment, fashion/status vulnerability and relational hurt. Konno overreaches; former allies and other classmates become active in the return attack. The crowd's conduct is not simply Konno experiencing the precise fair equivalent of what she did. Nor does Hinami's causal influence mean she personally performed or completely controlled every later action. Accepted Hinami analysis is particularly important here: the retaliatory final cue goes beyond securing cessation, and owning a protective motive does not supply a general right to vengeance. [KE-E03; KE-E10]

Tama stops the reverse persecution. The person with the strongest injury claim refuses to turn that injury into unlimited permission for others to attack. This protects Konno without erasing responsibility. It does not demonstrate Tama's private forgiveness, although a qualified forgiveness is not ruled out merely because she avoids a light dismissal. The source's bounded point is that stopping collective cruelty and completing repair are different acts.

Konno's exit also has a social meaning. She leaves without meeting others' eyes; Yuzu follows while the remaining group stays behind. That departure is neither a full confession nor evidence that she has no attachment left. Yuzu follows her and later helps ordinary re-entry; that is meaningful care without demonstrating a complete or permanent social settlement. The source does not let that care count as aggressor restitution. [KE-E04]

## 6. The apology belongs to the people who make it

V05 `text/part0028.html` gives an exact sequence. Konno departs at P2–6. Akiyama subsequently approaches Tama and directly apologizes at P17; the narrator names it an apology at P18; other group members follow at P19. Tama's P21–24 response has weight and restraint, rather than an easy assurance that nothing mattered. Class relaxation follows. [KE-E04]

The actor distinction is essential to Konno's dossier. Akiyama's apology is not hers. The brief apologies cannot be erased to preserve a general argument about incomplete justice, and they cannot be expanded into complete participation accounting, restitution, durable safeguards or restored trust. Yuzu's strap work and re-entry labor belong to Yuzu; Tama's intervention belongs to Tama. None silently supplies the act Konno has not been shown to perform.

The current CRI §19, CS §20 and SOC §18 own this demonstrated correction. Historical readings retain their earlier wording. The dossier follows the qualified present baseline while leaving the frozen analyst's prior horizon discoverable. No stable claim or source question is renumbered. Akiyama is not silently merged with the Konno-group given name Yumi; the exact source identity bridge remains unverified.

## 7. Clothes, food and accepting a friend's judgment

The concurrent V06.5 outing is valuable because it shows a person in ordinary company rather than only in a sanction mechanism. Konno complains about the cold, tries on a jacket, explicitly calls Yuzu over and asks what she thinks. Yuzu gives a positive judgment with a specific correction about length. Konno accepts it, thanks her, puts the garment back and considers another suggestion. Later she admires Yuzu's pancake photograph. [KE-E05]

The interpretive point is not that one accepted correction proves humility. It is that Konno can recognize a friend's competence and allow it to change an immediate choice. Her standing with Yuzu differs from her standing with Hirabayashi. A person who imposes in one field can be receptive in another. Yuzu experiences pleasure in being consulted and likes the mixture of frankness and reserve; that is Yuzu's situated response, not an omniscient ethical verdict on Konno.

The actual outfit, weather complaint and meal belong to this occasion. They do not establish a permanent black-jacket preference, a favorite food, habitual photographic skill or a fashion vocation. Her body and style are described through the scene's focalization; they are not a basis for an invented body-image diagnosis.

## 8. Practical help and unequal permissions

Konno's response to Yuzu's anxiety is efficient and particular. She considers why direct messaging may feel too heavy, recruits a different communication channel and uses a photograph to invite reciprocal disclosure without an explicit request. Yuzu experiences the help as reassuring even while continuing to judge the prior harassment wrong. The text permits affection and criticism in the same friend. It does not require the analyst to choose between blind loyalty and total moral separation. [KE-E06]

The phone sequence makes hierarchy concrete. At P256 Konno takes Kawaguchi Mutsumi's phone before waiting for a reply. P257–259 records assent after the taking. At P260 she obtains assent before sending the first message. Those are different permission states. It is inaccurate either to call the initial taking already assented or to claim the first message has no assent. The later actions do not establish a universal permission for every use of another person's device.

The returned photo and its partial clues also have limited force. The girls are investigating under anxiety. Neither an apparent contextual sign nor the reader's knowledge that a surprise is coming proves an affair or a complete deception policy. Konno even acknowledges that Nakamura may not respond in the expected format. Her method is a situated prediction, not omniscient social control.

This ordinary care does not settle her relationship with Tama or Akiyama. One can do real work for a friend while remaining answerable for other harm. The dossier must preserve both positive agency and the recipient-specific exclusions of that agency.

## 9. A useful performer whose wish for ease can produce quality

At the V07 rehearsal, Konno asks whether a written-sounding line can become easier to say. Kikuchi accepts the suggestion. Fumiya infers a wish to make the task easier, then notices that the changed line also sounds better. The directly represented proposal and acceptance support practical communicative competence; the exact motive remains his interpretation. [KE-E07]

This is an important complication of the earlier effort conflict. Avoiding unnecessary difficulty can improve a collective product. It need not be pure altruism to be useful. Conversely, a useful line does not establish a wholesale change in values. The scene supplies ordinary cooperation between named people, not apology or a claim that Kikuchi's growing social fluency has cured Konno.

The role here is neither only bully nor only audience. She is a performer affected by the script's spoken form and a participant who can offer an actionable revision. A separate authorship specialist should preserve Kikuchi's acceptance as well as Konno's contribution; neither person owns the whole collaborative product.

## 11. Rival readings and supported inference limits

**Flat cruelty** fails because the ordinary care, receptivity and useful work are real. **Misunderstood innocence** fails because those acts do not erase sustained harmful agency. **Every act serves status** is too strong: some motive mixture is supported, but enjoyment and friendship need not be reduced to one hidden aim. **Public defeat teaches the necessary lesson** mistakes injury and functional coexistence for tested reform. **No apology ever occurred** is false for Akiyama/group; **Konno apologized through them** is an actor error. **Tama's protection equals forgiveness** exceeds what the intervention establishes.

Supported literary transfer domains include familiar peer status encounters, trusted-friend consultation, ordinary group leisure, practical collaborative tasks and behavior under visible public threat. Interpretation becomes weaker for private remorse, unfamiliar severe crisis, family conflict, long-term romantic fidelity, adult work, parenting or a wholly new low-status recipient. Those fields require source evidence, not a vivid imagined backstory. Do not infer a clinical condition, childhood wound, complete sexuality, named future occupation, hidden restitution or a stable universal kindness/cruelty score.
