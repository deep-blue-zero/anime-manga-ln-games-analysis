# Game models, learned form and the work of making a life one's own

This canonical administrative derivative has passed independent review at the R09_1 local literary scope. The [ensemble and thematic integration review](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_ENSEMBLE_AND_THEMATIC_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md) binds the accepted argument, exact corrections, actual read/source scopes and canonical render. Preserved authoring-history references describe their earlier stage. Cross-specialist convergence, final synthesis, literary release, distinct models and repository publication retain separate gates.

Fumiya's useful discovery is that an acquired way of acting can become his. The series repeatedly complicates what that discovery permits him to conclude. Learning to speak does not tell him whom to love; choosing whom to love does not allocate the time a relationship needs; understanding another person's difficulty does not authorize selecting their answer. Meanwhile, the apparent alternatives to his practiced life are also made through models, habits, other people and repeated action. NO NAME is a constructed identity, Tama's hero ideal has an imitated origin, and Kikuchi's author perspective is neither an unmediated inner essence nor merely another version of Fumiya's player.

The governing interpretation here is that **the novels make learned form valuable by letting it become answerable practice: a person can use, vary, refuse and teach it, discover wants through it, and own consequences that a detached operation would leave outside the self. That ownership remains incomplete wherever the actor's model absorbs another person's ends, treats a local result as a total warrant, or leaves others carrying unexamined costs.** This is an INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS tested across distinct cases, not a definition the novels announce in one speech. The gains are real without becoming permanent, uniformly distributed or ethically self-validating. [GF-E01–03]

## 1. Scope, terms and the questions this theme owns

This draft fulfills architecture §9.1 domain 1: what technique can teach; when practiced form becomes owned competence; what optimization sees and misses; how desire and refusal enter a model. It does not replace domain 2's account of effort and livelihood, domain 3's group systems, domain 4's causal/meaning regimes, domain 5's relationship governance, domain 6's authorship and personhood, or domain 7's Japanese language responsibility. Their distinctions are necessary here; their full arguments remain separate work. In particular, the accompanying authorship draft asks who may represent whom, not just how a performer comes to inhabit a form.

The admitted corpus is the fourteen Japanese witnesses: main V01–V12, V06.5 and V08.5. The supplements retain unit-local clocks. The V05 appended earlier bonus is not a later event merely because of its binding; V07's Special Edition art material is not additional mainline conduct; V08.5's parallel karaoke and adapted VR do not complete unshown mainline activities. Late family histories revise interpretation without making early characters know what the reader later learns. The frozen readings and entering snapshot retain their original horizons. No future book, adaptation or remembered translation supplies an outcome.

“Form” covers a practiced expression, conversational move, social role, tactical method, genre, model or material arrangement; these are compared rather than treated as identical. “Ownership” asks who can endorse and alter an end, use a method as equipment, recognize a consequence as personally theirs, and respond to the other people involved. It does not mean self-creation without antecedents. “Authenticity” names this problem of inhabited and answerable action, not a verified frequency claim about one Japanese authenticity word. DIRECT action or self-report, another person's interpretation, an analyst hypothesis and a VALUE JUDGMENT retain different force. A confession can directly establish what someone says without proving an exhaustive theory of their life.

Four separations organize the argument:

| Question | Useful evidence | What that evidence cannot settle alone |
|---|---|---|
| Has a repertoire been acquired? | independent execution, transfer, correction, explanation, teaching | whether the actor wants the task or endorses the end |
| Is the end personally inhabited? | a named wish, refusal of a feasible task, valued loss, chosen continued practice | whether the choice is wise, stable or fair to others |
| Can the arrangement be revised? | changed method, usable refusal, renegotiation, actual stopping | unlimited freedom, equal bargaining power or a permanent rule |
| What happens to recipients? | actual uptake, dissent, labor, delay, harm and benefit | retroactive consent, complete justice or a total verdict on the person |

These are retrieval questions, not an achievement ladder everyone must climb. Narita's wish for ease and Tama's particular commitments should not be forced to end in Fumiya's pleasure in continual challenge. Nor does an actor have to give a polished philosophical account before an ordinary preference can count. [GF-R01–03, GF-R12]

## 2. The early game analogy opens a field before it threatens to own it

Fumiya begins as an expert in an activity whose structure he can study and a novice who has largely refused to test social life. That asymmetry matters. Hinami does not create his capacity for disciplined attention or his enjoyment of mastery. She shows him how capacities he already values can approach a field he has classified as opaque and unworthy of effort. Decomposable tasks replace the conclusion that an unsuccessful public appearance expresses a defective person. A posture, expression or conversational contribution can be practiced. The early movement is therefore a revision in what can be attempted, not proof that starting conditions are equal. [GF-R01; CRI-TOM-001/002]

The V01 conversation about conversation is concrete. Fumiya identifies introducing and expanding a topic; Hinami tells him that identifying a function lets him observe how a skilled person performs it. He then objects that understanding a good move does not mean the body can execute it. The analogy exposes a training problem instead of treating social fluency as a gift possessed by a higher kind of human. Hinami also accepts level-appropriate incompleteness: he need not understand the whole atmosphere theory before continuing. This is a pedagogy of tractable entry, not total knowledge delivered all at once. [GF-R02]

That same scene refuses to make gaming only a quantitative doctrine. The embarrassing `おにただ` habit opens a shared discussion of Buin's technical construction, distinctive world and appealing character. Hinami becomes animated before restoring the lesson. Fumiya's response is not successful because he has won a social point; they like an actual work and can talk about it. Their common language also contains retrospective family warmth, craft appreciation and humor. A reading in which games merely teach them to optimize would miss the independent good from which their analogy draws its persuasive energy. [GF-E04; GF-R02]

The limit is present in the local atmosphere definition. Hinami calls atmosphere the good/bad standard of a particular group. That is an account of what receives approval inside that field, not a warrant that the group's standard is morally right. The V05 class can accordingly become legible without becoming just. Being able to predict the audience's response is a capacity; deciding whether to recruit that response is another question. The series' critique develops from this difference rather than requiring the initial teaching to have been false.

## 3. V03 discovers an end problem and corrects an anti-form overreaction

The fireworks assignment is consequential because Fumiya can perform it. He refuses a feasible confession because he does not know whether he loves Kikuchi and objects to judging connections through task completion. The objection is not that he needs a better line or a more suitable target. His question asks what should precede the search for a route. Hinami answers that a purported true want may be temporary error; efficient productive growth is more reliable. That criticism has force. Wants can change, and a feeling's intensity does not certify its wisdom. The dispute remains serious only if her objection is preserved rather than caricatured as an inability to recognize feelings. [GF-E05; GF-R03]

Fumiya's attempted escape initially confuses the source of an acquired form with its present meaning. He abandons presentation and training, withdraws from contact and retreats into Atafami, which also loses some pleasure. This does not uncover an untouched self who becomes happy when performance stops. He dislikes the neglected appearance as the person who has to live it; the earlier planned actions had produced real experience. Ignoring Kikuchi also exposes a relational cost of the withdrawal. The person claiming to protect sincere connection can fail to answer a real initiative while defending an abstract authentic self. [GF-R04; Fumiya §3.3; EFFORT §9]

Kikuchi's distinction between recited material and a communicated lived image helps correct the opposition. Learned delivery can carry something the speaker actually means. Fumiya then uses practiced serious form for thanks and planned presentation to repair the coaching relation. The hybrid is not a compromise between half a lie and half a truth. It changes the relation between technique and purpose: retain useful skills, formulate goals from what he wants, remove conflicting objectives. Hinami can accurately restate that proposal. Her understanding does not mean she adopts its deepest premise. [GF-R04–05]

The fresh Japanese return prevents a retrospective victory speech. Hinami says Fumiya has not proved the existence of the want, while she cannot prove its absence. She yields part of the structure and still demands later demonstration. Their shared Found gesture closes the encounter as contact between differently held ideals. His narration distinguishes it from proving correctness or rejecting weakness; that is his experienced meaning of the gesture, not a completed universal settlement. The ongoing hierarchy and proof demand remain. The scene gives a usable practice under disagreement, not a final theory of an infallible inner desire. [GF-E06; GF-R05]

## 4. A teacher's successful route cannot become every learner's route

Tama makes the inadequacy of a universal curriculum observable. The copied drills address Fumiya's earlier deficits, although she already has strong expressive fundamentals. Her problem includes a limited wish to know most classmates, a settled hostile reputation, particular recipients and a high cost for one failed approach. More repetition of the wrong exercise would make the teacher's route visible without making her situation more tractable. The correction requires observing the learner before claiming to improve her. [GF-E07; GF-R06]

The revised project distributes expertise. Tama supplies an end related to Mimimi and her own judgment; Fumiya supplies decomposition and revision; Mizusawa contributes recipient design; Mimimi supplies person-specific knowledge; Takei provides a different practice relation; Kikuchi's model makes other classmates imaginatively interesting. This is generative authorship beyond receiving a late veto over Hinami's assigned task. It matters that Tama can invent, accept, alter and refuse parts of the work. It also matters that the project begins with an unannounced recording and requires a harmed person to absorb tactical costs. Better fit does not make the circumstances fair or every method authorized. [EFFORT §§22–24; Tama §6]

At the reversal, the new delivery carries a judgment continuous with her earlier one: collective cruelty does not become right when its target has done wrong. A pun alone does not produce that effect. New ties, an altered public reading and support carry the words; her own judgment supplies the refusal. The class's immediate response establishes a changed possibility of action rather than permanent justice. After Konno leaves, Akiyama and other members actually apologize. That real act must be distinguished both from Konno's unestablished apology and from complete accounting, restitution, restored trust or institutional reform. [GF-R06; CRI §19]

Tama later varies and refuses the smallness routine, separates permission to capture an image from permission to upload it, and teaches Fumiya expression. These ordinary reversals matter more than a general claim that he successfully redesigned her. She already has capacities he can receive; a useful public character remains equipment where she can decline its use. No later hostile classroom test proves that the equipment can never become constraining. [GF-R07]

The thematic result is double. Copying can begin learning, but the demonstrated success of one person does not supply an adequate model of another's deficits. Conversely, the failure of copied drills does not imply that imitation is intrinsically corrupt. It identifies the need for discriminating uptake: preserve what fits, recognize what does not, and keep the learner's end in view. The principle will recur in Kikuchi's failed universal sociability and successful color practice.

Hinami's V06 acknowledgment supplies a harder countercase to any assumption that owning an end makes it benign. The retaliation is technically disciplined; she recognizes that the last blow exceeded cessation and accepts its revenge character. Her attachment to Tama's correctness helps explain the selected end without proving it defensible. Fumiya reaches a qualified concession through the NO NAME standard rather than completing a universal ethical argument. A named motive and an admired style can constrain one account while leaving apology, restitution and a general stopping rule unestablished. The problem is not too little technique or merely an uncontrolled interruption by emotion: skill can make an affectively selected end more effective. Tama's actual refusal of returned collective cruelty also prevents the protected person's interests from being silently identified with the protector's satisfaction. [GF-R22; GF-E02–03]

## 5. Ordinary clothing makes learned intuition visible without making it infallible

The V06.5 shopping unit belongs early in the second term. Its location after V06 in the package cannot turn it into a late-stage cure for the conflicts of that volume. The outing begins with comic competition: Fumiya beats Hinami at a less-practiced arcade game and frames her fashion test as retaliation. The framing remains his. The actual lesson asks whether repeated coordinated purchases, wearing and reflection have become independent discrimination. [GF-R08]

Fumiya chooses gray partly by a feeling he cannot fully verbalize. Hinami calls that a good sign, then articulates plausible learned patterns. This is important counterevidence to an all-period Hinami who permits only explicit calculation. She can credit an intuition as acquired perception while disputing the authority of an unproved life end. Means that become intuitive and ends that demand justification are different objects. The scene does not establish a universal theory of unconscious learning; it depicts one recipient's developing judgment.

The practical correction is material and situated. An outfit is seen from sides and back, not only in one's mirror. A simple technique can be useful to a high-school beginner while seeming obvious or dated elsewhere. Fumiya's later wrong sizes expose how much he still cannot see. Independent selection is consequently a real transfer with continuing dependence on fitting rooms, staff, feedback and more examples. There is no need to choose between pure imitation and instant expert freedom.

Money also gives the lesson an end outside appearance rank. Acquiring a better eye permits cheaper purchases; Fumiya imagines wages available for arcade play and downloadable content. His clothes are not merely a coach's costume, and the money does not become abstract optimization for its own sake. Hinami's environmental account of motivation and his enjoyment condition for continued game practice remain attributed, state-specific accounts. Their final equipment-shop/battle banter lets both participate. A shared deliberate form can be companionable before it answers any ultimate question about the self. [GF-E08; GF-R08]

## 6. Voluntary performance can still exclude its performer

Kikuchi's V07 attempt at universal sociability prevents ownership from being reduced to voluntary initiation. She wants to manage rehearsal and imitate people who seem to inhabit the world correctly. The norm requires a universal Popol; the existing person and her preferred habitat appear as defects to remove. Her effort is chosen at the action level while the end leaves her interests outside the ideal world. This is a different failure from Tama's initially irrelevant drills. There the teacher misestimates a learner; here the learner can internalize a supposedly desirable model that discounts herself. [GF-E09; GF-R09]

Kikuchi's author account must retain her resistance to Fumiya's vocabulary. When he calls her perspective that of a player, she says those are his words. The novel's character/author relation supplies a different way of thinking about how a world should fit together. The distinction is substantive. Her concern for composition and other people cannot be assumed to be merely a detached gamer waiting to enter play. The correction is to count her among the people in the world she cares for, while retaining that care. [GF-R10]

Selected affinity, the writer-aspirant profile, revised Chris's craft and a returnable garden supply positive alternatives. They are more than refusing the class. Yet profile creation is not already reciprocal community, a declaration is not a publishing career, and a private ending is not a complete integration of public ethics and personal desire. The V07 public play, private novel and direct romantic choice must remain separate evidence objects. Fiction can help make a choice expressible without choosing for the living recipient. [GF-R09–10; accompanying authorship theme]

Mizusawa offers another countercase to treating visible form as emptiness. His V03 confession is received and refused. Pain and relief make the chosen act personally consequential; pain is not what makes it ethically good, and Hinami owes no reversal because the loss helped him feel present. His bar-story waiting self-policy and V03 persistence do not form a clean invented sequence from never acting to acting. Their exact seam remains OPEN; the current accepted study preserves audience and phase rather than smoothing them into one permanent policy. [GF-E10; GF-R11]

V12 retests the positive possibility. Fumiya borrows approachable, light form to speak more honestly and offers an awkward friendship acknowledgment through fictional names. Mizusawa is embarrassed, jokes and gives direct thanks. Constructed presentation, mixed origins and actual reciprocity coexist. His admission that access to Aoi was also an initiating motive does not prove every later act was merely a means. Neither the friend's pleasure nor permission for one instance of sharing licenses an absent Hinami's information. A relation can become more than its origin while remaining answerable for the origin's costs. [GF-R13]

## 7. Desire changes the model, but it cannot make all interests fit

V07's chosen confession and V08's professional aim show learned means serving ends Hinami did not supply. Their force should not be minimized because external structures made them possible. Nor should a chosen partner or vocation be treated as a finished result. The relationship needs allocation, disclosure and usable disagreement; the professional route needs constraints beyond online skill. Self-authorship becomes more demanding as more genuinely wanted ends compete for finite time. [GF-R14]

Fumiya's V09 attempted solution exposes the danger. He can abandon a successful character in a game and test another despite short-run losses. Ashigaru interprets this as confidence in change, an account with explanatory value rather than an omniscient certificate. Transferred to friendship, the same readiness can become deleting groups and reducing contact as though relationships were replaceable assets. Mimimi's correction is recipient-specific: the proposed sacrifice removes something Kikuchi actually values. An optimization can be internally consistent because it has omitted the beneficiary's account. [GF-E11; EFFORT §§56–58]

Kikuchi's subsequent reselection matters precisely because cost and alternatives have become visible. She chooses an existing person whose Hinami priority does not simply disappear, and rejects narrowing his world as the gift she supposedly needs. This does not invalidate her earlier agency or give every later demand her advance assent. Their future specialness is to be made through practice. No present choice completes a standing rule for privacy, third parties or time. The self-authoring subject thus meets a limit a solo skill model cannot erase: a shared end must remain choosable by another person. [GF-R14; accepted R07 FK-E08]

Narita supplies a more ordinary limit to universalization. She values ease and enough independence not to become helpless if a partner leaves. She works and can take initiative for that end; competence does not prove a concealed passion for exertion. Fumiya describes continual goals and challenge, she calls that life intolerable to her, and she does not endorse his proposed shared premise of enjoying life. The fresh source supports this actual nonagreement, not a fully developed rival philosophy. Her comic refusal nevertheless prevents the analyst from awarding everyone's self-authorship to Fumiya's preferred game. [GF-E12; GF-R12]

The work, rest, food and company in these cases are owned goods, not merely therapeutic instruments or evidence of improved rank. Their significance does not depend on completing a future profession. Nor is ordinary enjoyment an automatic ethical verdict: Narita's relaxed information relay can outpace permission, just as Fumiya's sincere priorities can leave a partner waiting. The point is to keep the good and its conditions visible together.

## 8. A game can test a credential without judging an entire life

The V08 encounter with Ashigaru distinguishes repeatable technical strength from one consequential opportunity. Fumiya's deciding-game response under pressure does not invalidate his accumulated skill; an estimated long series is not a played experiment, and one result is not an all-context ranking. He gains information about how practiced action changes when a residual chance of failure becomes intolerable. This is evidence about the game model's conditions of application, not an instruction to reject competitive measurement. [GF-R15; accepted Ashigaru AS-E01–03]

V11 makes the extra-domain extension explicit. Fumiya already says there are no reasons, grounds or plausible logic for his reassurance, while invoking the person Hinami has uniquely failed to defeat and commanding belief. V12 therefore does not introduce his first recognition that support can lack proof. The earlier scene combines confessed groundlessness with an asserted competitive credential. Hinami chooses the test and wins the first-to-three set 3–2 after being down 0–2. Her real adaptive victory defeats the credential; it is not illegitimate because it frustrates his rescue story. [GF-E13; GF-R16]

The result cannot establish that care never mattered, that Hinami has no preferences, or that Fumiya's learning was counterfeit. Both retain capacity while losing a reason or warrant that had carried too much. The asymmetry is revealing: he can value experimental defeat elsewhere yet collapse when this loss bears the story of unique usefulness. Revisability is a real capacity with failure conditions, not a completed immunity to protected identity. Hinami's concluding bad-game judgment is likewise her crisis statement, not an omniscient law about the world's fairness.

Nor should the crisis absorb the earlier V08 gatherings into a wholly instrumental history. Hinami protects the NO NAME information boundary, participates through secondary-character preparation and a bounded contest, loses, returns and reports fun. The 3–1 mirror-match loss is not her V06 individual win or the V11 Boxman set. Actual volunteered pleasure can coexist with retained secrecy and an unsettled general life reason. It is evidence of a life already containing valued experience, not a successful treatment retrospectively discovered by the final volume. That chronology gives the later revaluation something real to receive. [GF-R23; GF-E04/GF-E13]

The final admitted volume gives a different use to shared form. Hinami and Fumiya select Found for a public exhibition whose meaning includes their history, although Jack is his current main and she initially hovers Boxman. The match and winner are not narrated. The choice does not undo V11, explain the thirty-one input-empty frames or reestablish unbeatable status. It changes what this coming encounter is asked to carry: recognizable shared play and an audience-directed expression of enjoyment. His final god-tier judgment remains an owned assertion rather than proof that every person should agree. [GF-R19]

## 9. V12 makes borrowed ideals owned and keeps their externalities

Tama's explanation of hero imitation revises an apparently convenient contrast. She is not genuine because she alone never copied. A manga-or-animation hero image, with the medium left uncertain in her account, became hers through repeated acts. The V05 continuity was genuine without requiring an unlearned moral essence. The V12 cake simultaneously shows the burden of that continuity. Skilled work protects a child's singular celebration; a colleague, queue and visiting Fumiya absorb extra work and delay. The mother initially says the extensive response is unnecessary before giving thanks. Different recipients do not supply one shared authorization. [GF-E14; GF-R17]

Tama says letting it pass could make her dislike herself and acknowledges the latitude of being the shop manager's daughter. `店長の娘` supplies that role, not proof of legal ownership of the business. Her self-description of distortion is not a diagnosis or an admission that every kindness is selfish. The act can serve the child and continued self-respect together. What it cannot supply is a universally correct remake policy, unanimous assent or an effortless capacity to revise every self-defining end. Ownership can be experienced as an inward demand as well as freedom. [CRI §19; Tama §15]

Kikuchi's color learning provides a companion case with material correction. Attention changes through comparison, light and practice rather than by adopting an inspiring proposition alone. The costume mistake is a correct written blue name paired with a mismatched scarlet sample. Fumiya imagines possible mechanisms; no culprit is established. He proposes and opens the package after Kikuchi's assent, without that being Mitsu's prior permission; Kikuchi has touched the cord without pulling. His later acknowledgment of initiation and Mitsu's expert revision remain distinct from the pair's useful proposal. Pins are at P779–780; the following P781–784 concerns movement/carrying toward the tsunami marker and must not be relabeled as pin discovery. [GF-R18; CRI §21]

The material sequence refuses both pure obedience and amateur self-sufficiency. Mitsu can refuse, delegate within a task, notice what the novices cannot and accept part of a proposal while correcting its execution. A good result neither proves prior access was authorized nor erases a novice's real contribution. The more general thematic inference is modest: the world can correct a learned form, and expertise can be distributed, while responsibility still belongs to particular actors at particular steps.

The first independent-star answer shows the same requirement at the level of inquiry. Peer interviews and role-play initially make the remedy attractive. Kikuchi's authored Alcia later accuses Chris of hearing an echo of the desired answer; this is not actual Hinami's testimony or an immediate rejection spoken by the interviewed models. The writer and Fumiya accept the criticism of their method. More diligent collection would not fix an inquiry that permits the answer to select its evidence. The question has to become revisable. [GF-E15; GF-R20]

## 10. The changed frame does not require the earlier life to have been unreal

Hinami's V12 inward response supplies a further distinction between experience and the category used to judge it. Mathematics, track, game screens and applause return with different sensory textures, after being counted as interchangeable first places. The novel's ellipse preserves recalcitrant observations while revising a demanded shape. This is a literary metaphor for revaluation, not mathematical proof of human worth, factual relativism or a rule that everyone needs precisely two psychological supports. Her achievements were already lived; the change is in their present uptake. [GF-E16; GF-R21]

The response is genuine and bounded. She reports a little self-liking; Fumiya expressly permits temporariness. She later attends the school event and accepts shared public play. Neither the new frame nor the metaphor decides Nagisa's death, repairs every family relation, resumes the presidency or tutorial arrangement, or demonstrates enduring recovery. Her stale estimate of Mimimi is not authoritative current truth: actual Mimimi has already voiced partial self-regard, and V12 adds liking the self who refused opportunism while continuing to love Fumiya. Her conditional worry about a “next Tama” concerns her own future self-regard under separation, while she trusts the existing tie. It is not a predicted replacement trajectory. [GF-R21; current Mimimi §17]

The support medium also changes. Fumiya retracts the proposed replacement Nagisa and offers the jointly made story beside doubt. The distinction from V11 lies in the carrier and jurisdiction of the assurance, not in the discovery that assurance is unproved. A story can remain available as a reminder without a living person acquiring an exclusive office of maintaining the recipient. Its future efficacy remains his confidence. Neither a shared form nor an admitted wish supplies Nagisa's missing standpoint. [GF-R19, GF-R21]

Finally, the creative project has an ordinary labor bill. Fumiya and Mimimi neglect actual event preparation; Ashigaru delivers his guest contribution in the underprepared setting. The account cannot make the returned Hinami retroactively organize that work or infer that the talk was improvised. The local literary success does not pay every practical cost. This is not a minor disclaimer attached after transcendence: it tests whether the newly valued life includes other people's already ongoing work. [GF-E17; GF-R19]

## 11. Rival readings and what would actually discriminate them

| Rival | What it sees | Disposition and counterevidence |
|---|---|---|
| The series proves life is a fair, optimizable game | many skills become tractable; deliberate practice has real effects | REJECT the universal conclusion. Unequal starting fields, hostile recipients, finite time, singular loss and other people's ends remain. Fumiya's final value judgment is his. |
| Natural spontaneity is genuine; rehearsal falsifies it | rote performance can fail to communicate lived content | REJECT the partition. V03's withdrawal fails; deliberate thanks, acquired intuition and V12 friendship carry real investment. Spontaneity can also be evasive or harmful. |
| Since wants can change, only results are trustworthy | desire is fallible and a method needs feedback | PRESERVE the criticism, reject its exclusive jurisdiction. A result cannot select its own end; first place and retained skill do not certify a livable self. |
| Voluntary action proves ownership | people initiate rehearsal, travel, work and care | REVISE. Kikuchi's assimilation excludes her; borrowed reasons can remain insufficient; a self-selected commitment can become a strain. |
| Owned action is therefore right | sincerity matters and recipients can benefit | REJECT. Revenge, acknowledged disclosure violations, the cake's labor distribution and knowingly persuasive care rhetoric retain costs. |
| Fumiya's failures invalidate the skills | the same repertoire supports overcontrol | REJECT. The failure concerns use, scope or governing warrant; useful technique and its abusive application can coexist. |
| Plural contribution solves unilateral control | different peers correct a singular model | REVISE. Actual contribution improves evidence; collective authorship still lacks an absent subject's permission and does not equalize knowledge. |
| Ordinary pleasure is the final cure | games, company, food and craft have independent value | REJECT cure; preserve value. Enjoyment can precede crisis, coexist with grief or be received without permanent self-belief. |
| The ending settles authenticity once and for all | ownership and received value become more explicit | REJECT permanence. No new defeat, sustained routine, complete consent protocol or future vocation tests every gain. |

A discriminating later test would ask whether an actor can revise a self-defining end as well as its means, preserve a recipient's refusal when confident of benefit, or acknowledge cost before favorable reception. The corpus does not supply all such tests. These are open analytical questions, not simulated future scenes, predictions scored after access or demands for another book to complete the theme.
