# Familiar words, different voices — language that can reach someone without becoming their truth

This canonical administrative derivative has passed independent review at the R09_7 local literary scope. The [ensemble and thematic integration review](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_ENSEMBLE_AND_THEMATIC_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md) binds the accepted argument, exact corrections, actual read/source scopes and canonical render. Preserved authoring-history references describe their earlier stage. Cross-specialist convergence, final synthesis, literary release, distinct models and repository publication retain separate gates.

## 1. What changes when the words travel

The novels do not give each person one true voice that eventually replaces an artificial one. Fumiya can use learned lightness to make an honest conversation possible and use forceful sincerity to claim an answer for someone else. Kikuchi can initiate a consequential choice through polite grammar and speak more directly while performing an invented character. Mimimi can joke while disclosing love, defending a future freedom or describing a serious lack of self-confidence. Tama can speak conventionally to a customer without surrendering her own judgment. A change in delivery does not by itself identify development, truth or moral authority. [LV-C01; LV-L02–10/13–15/20]

The stronger longitudinal argument concerns **the movement of familiar language into different acts of ownership**. Game terms become assessments of life; a borrowed model becomes a negotiated way to act; a statement of affection can be heard without its private qualification; maternal gratitude passes through fiction into an imagined sister’s voice; a written character name can carry the reading “I.” The words remain recognizable, and their force changes with who speaks, to whom, under which conditions and in which narrative layer. Portability does not make the voices interchangeable. [LV-C02; LV-L01–04/11/17–22]

This is a positive capacity as well as an epistemic danger. Shared game names, stories, jokes and ordinary forms let people reach one another without inventing a wholly private language. A person can take up something borrowed and make an owned choice with it. The difficulty is that a successful utterance can invite too large an inference: that the speaker knows the recipient completely, that a desired meaning has become a fact, or that accurate words spoken through a fictional model belong to the real person modeled. The final novel preserves language’s power while repeatedly exposing those jumps.

The ending does not solve the problem by discovering that reassurance may lack grounds. V11 already states that lack. Nor does it replace every difficult word with a final correct definition. Its more specific achievements include a retracted offer of substitution, an admitted limited self-liking, an owned writing purpose, an available story and chosen play. Several utterances remain assurances, interpretations or wishes. The language specialist’s task is to preserve what they do without silently converting them into proof of what they say. [LV-C03/04; LV-L03/04/10/18/21/22]

## 2. The useful unit is an utterance in an encounter

A lexeme alone cannot tell us who has acted. `好き` may be a romantic clarification, a particular person’s admiration, private self-regard or an answer whose qualification remains unspoken. `理由` can concern a writing purpose, an offered warrant or the missing explanation around a death. A familiar nickname can persist through rejection. The relevant unit combines **form, proposition, speaker or focalizer, recipient, era, medium, qualification and response**. Where any element is missing, the claim must stay correspondingly limited. [LV-C02/05; JVL §§2/10–11]

Fumiya’s early `共感能力` supplies a small positive case. Abstract analysis becomes something said in ordinary company, and he subsequently differentiates Mimimi and Tama rather than retaining the first category as a universal account. The source supports this encounter’s use of an unusual formulation. It does not establish that analysis always works socially, or that speaking without preparation is always more sincere than learned phrasing. The early point is that language can become contact with an actual recipient. [JVL-01; LOC-V01; LV-L24]

Attribution is not a clerical step after interpretation. If an analyst gives Haruka Fumiya’s unspoken qualification, the resulting relationship has information the scene withholds. If the analyst assigns Fumiya’s punishment reformulation to Mizusawa, the dialogue becomes one person’s lecture. If an English paraphrase turns a self-description into an instruction, Hinami’s final statement about herself becomes unsolicited advice to Fumiya. Each error changes agency and standing. Exact wording matters because it can prevent an argument about language from inventing the person who supposedly spoke it. [LV-C06; LV-L04/11/24]

This discipline does not imply that only direct quotation is interpretable. Narration, reported speech, a reader’s inference and authored fiction all supply real evidence of different things. The task is to maintain the relation between the evidence and its subject. A fictional line can disclose an author’s hypothesis; a focalized self-condemnation can directly show a present view; a described pause can change an encounter. None needs to become a transparent report of every mind present.

## 3. Game verdicts change speaker, recipient and scope

V01’s positive verdict is more carefully limited than a simple conversion to Hinami’s position. Fumiya tells Yuzu that he cannot yet call life `神ゲー` but can call it `良ゲー`. The pairing of minimum scope with emphatic confidence matters: he is firm about what his experience supports while declining the larger conclusion. He says he will not claim what he does not think. His narration directs the feeling toward a familiar gamer, but Yuzu is the actual interlocutor and answers. An inwardly imagined audience does not replace the person who hears the words. [LV-C07; LV-L01]

The game vocabulary remains available when its speaker changes. Hinami’s V11 bad-game conclusion is addressed crisis speech after a particular competitive warrant fails. It is serious evidence about her state. It is not an omniscient discovery that the world is objectively worthless or that all earlier capacities were false. Fumiya’s loss does not invalidate every use of learned technique, and her set victory does not establish a permanent total ranking of persons. The comparison has force precisely because the same evaluative domain is being used by different people under different conditions. [JVL-05/11; LOC-04B]

At V12’s close, Fumiya uses the strongest positive evaluation, now qualifying it by comparison with Atafami. These final lines are his narration. Hinami has spoken just before, but the concluding verdict is not a declaration jointly pronounced by the cast or overheard by an audience. It expresses the life-story he wants his public play to offer. The match result is not shown. Turning the verdict into an objective theorem or every character’s final creed would strip the ending of its particular owner. [LV-C07; LV-L04]

Thus there is development without lexical replacement. The early qualification, another person’s crisis verdict and the final narrated commitment are not stages on a single scale measuring how close the work has come to one correct adjective. Their relation concerns what a person can presently affirm and through what experience. The forms retain competition, pleasure, contingency and the possibility of another failure.

## 4. Plain speech can negotiate an end or demand a warrant

In V03, Fumiya repeatedly says what he wants to continue and what he wants to exclude. He retains the skills and proposes dropping goals that conflict with what he genuinely wants. Hinami restates the proposed structure: use skills while making goals from his desired ends. Her response matters. The exchange is not only his private manifesto; there is an actual interlocutor who reformulates what she has heard. Their game names and a familiar gesture then make the working accommodation available through a shared history. [LV-C08; LV-L02]

The language is plain and can be assertive, but it leaves a proposition to be negotiated. The resulting accommodation is not comprehensive equal governance of every later task. It does preserve the distinction between accepting a method and accepting every end previously attached to it. The word `ハイブリッド` names that proposal in use; it is not a theoretical guarantee that Fumiya will never again confuse ability with authority.

V11’s `俺を信じろ` does something different. Fumiya puts his confidence before Hinami as an answer she should accept and invokes the opponent she had not defeated. Crucially, he also explicitly says he has no reason, grounds or suitable logic. The narration calls the confidence groundless, comparing it with a god. His repeated competitive claim remains the practical credential even while he disclaims logical proof. These are simultaneous features of the appeal, not evidence that he secretly mistakes a deductive argument for certainty. [LV-C09; LV-L03]

The distinction from V03 is not a discovery of forceful speech. It is what the speech asks of the recipient. A wished-for arrangement can be answered; an imperative backed by a unique personal credential asks the other person to treat this speaker as sufficient support. The later set defeats that stated competitive basis. It does not show that all care is impossible or that plain imperatives are always abusive. The relationship specialist owns the full overreach; this specialist preserves the different work done by broadly similar direct grammar.

V12 must not be made the first discovery of ungrounded affirmation. Its later imagined voice again uses confidence explicitly compared with divine groundlessness. The important changes concern speaker, channel and offered support. The final real conversation first offers Fumiya as replacement Nagisa and immediately retracts the offer. He points to a story rather than installing himself as the new exclusive person. The retraction belongs to the exchange, and the recipient’s momentary response does not constitute a completed agreement to the withdrawn claim. The eventual assurance remains assurance. His narration even describes adding reason as an accessory to groundless confidence. The source explicitly marks the status of the explanation instead of claiming that the metaphor has supplied demonstrated lifelong stability. [LV-C09/10; LV-L04/21]

## 5. Kikuchi’s politeness does not wait to become agency

V09’s choosing scene is explicit without a change to a uniformly blunt register. Kikuchi says she wants Fumiya, takes his hand and asks to participate in choosing. Her polite `選ばせてください` belongs to an act she initiates. She also interrupts his attempt to deny having the right to be selected. The request is not an absence of desire waiting for a stronger person to translate it. It makes her own position consequential within the encounter. [LV-C11; LV-L05]

The force comes from context as well as grammar. She has reflected on the asymmetry of being chosen without choosing, receives information from Mimimi and refuses the smaller replacement person he has tried to construct. Fumiya narrates his response and his reading of her feelings; those interpretations are not all words she declares. The choice restarts a relation without settling every future rule about time, disclosure or contact. Polite permission language cannot be used as a certificate of permanent equality any more than as evidence of permanent passivity.

This is not the first point at which she can disagree. Earlier reviewed source samples include her rejection of Fumiya’s vocabulary and her claim to her own evaluation of his importance. The source-facing history therefore blocks a neat before/after tale in which the quiet girl first acquires a self when she selects a partner in V09. The event strengthens an existing agency under a new problem. [JVL-12; KF-P09/21; LV-L07]

The ordinary supplemental recipients make the counterevidence positive. Before Fumiya’s influence, Kikuchi eagerly retells a book to Goda Sayaka, who asks questions and responds. She still uses polite forms; the exchange is expansive because she has a wanted subject and an engaged listener. In the story-local New Year’s household, she answers Riku briefly, invites her father to read the script and jokes about his busyness. The shorter familiar forms do not represent a later global stage replacing polite speech. They belong to another relation and a particular home occasion. [LV-C11/12; LV-L06]

The V11 boundaries preserve the same point under cost. A stammer and a polite ending can accompany a real demand to stop; a statement that words are not reaching Fumiya can make injury audible. The call’s ending does not identify who disconnects. The source supports the utterance without licensing the analyst to add a purposeful hangup as its dramatic completion. A richer account of her voice can preserve both precision and hesitation without inventing a wholesale new personality. [JVL-14; LOC-11D/E]

## 6. Mimimi’s joke can carry the proposition it makes difficult to say

Mimimi’s love disclosure uses playful reversal without becoming mere play. The earlier declaration momentarily makes Fumiya question the romantic interpretation and then confirms it. The next request for ordinary conduct is qualified: she is not asking him to forget. Removing that qualification produces a different and easier relation, one in which normality means nothing happened. The actual speech asks for continued interaction with an acknowledged event still present. [LV-C13; JVL-16; LV-L08]

After non-selection, she says she still loves him and rejects blame as the only available frame. Her final spirited warning refuses his assuming that the love will remain forever. The role nickname and insult keep a familiar channel open while making a consequential future limit. They specify neither a deadline nor a completed cessation of feeling. Fumiya’s positive interpretation of her strength is his reception of the moment, not a transparent report that she has privately resolved every conflict. [LV-L08]

The Kita-Yono restraint supplies a different use of the same recognizable brightness. A softened proposal about not going home together leads to an actual departure. The hedge does not make the action indecisive; the action does not prove a universal no-contact rule. Later reviewed evidence includes a resumed walk, so freezing the first local restraint into her permanent final voice would contradict the maintained state. Familiar joking and particular limits remain available together. [LV-C13; LV-L09]

Orthographic elongations and exuberant delivery are attested here. Their frequency and uniqueness are not. An analyst cannot generate an authentic Mimimi by adding long vowels and a nickname to every line, or erase distress because a line is funny. The language has force because another person knows the history to which the joke speaks. Its recurrence preserves relationship continuity through changed options rather than guaranteeing that those options never change.

## 7. The person is not the specification, and the concept need not originate with its owner

Mimimi’s V11 group speech explicitly distinguishes confidence in abilities and presentation from confidence in herself. She can list competence, joke about invincibility and then state the lack concerning `『私』そのもの`. The source gives this contrast rather than requiring the analyst to infer incompetence from distress or complete self-regard from visible success. Her self-account is addressed with Fumiya, Kikuchi and Mizusawa present; it is not a private internal scene. [LV-C14; LV-L10]

Kikuchi repeats the phrase and supplies the term `自己肯定感`; Mizusawa agrees. Fumiya says he recognizes the familiar internet expression. The technical-sounding label therefore enters after a particular person has described her problem. It does not make the label’s user the owner of the experience or certify every ensuing application to Hinami. The group can share a concept while retaining different grounds for using it.

V12 gives Mimimi a positive current statement about liking the self who chose a difficult restraint. She distinguishes obtaining someone from owning herself. Kikuchi relates the claim to the star model; Mimimi openly asks about the term and jokes that she does not understand the difficult vocabulary. That is not evidence she lacks the agency she has just articulated. Nor is her assent a complete theoretical endorsement of every later use of the model. Her actual account has a local reason, a recipient and a temporal limit independent of mastering the author’s metaphor. [LV-C14; LV-L10]

This is one of the series’ strongest checks on reading dialogue as a procession of philosophical mouthpieces. The abstract term can help connect accounts, but life need not wait for the most sophisticated available vocabulary. A person can recognize a priority, incur a cost and express it in her own idiom. The specialist must preserve the difference between that act and a total theory later written around it.

## 8. Liking is not one kind of knowledge

Fumiya tells Haruka that he likes Hinami. The as-a-person qualification is narration he decides not to add. Haruka hears the unqualified answer and continues her questions. If the analyst inserts the qualification into the spoken line, Haruka acquires a classification the scene does not give her. If the analyst ignores the narration, the reader loses part of how Fumiya understands the answer. The correct reconstruction preserves both unequal access and the unresolved wider category. [LV-C15; LV-L11]

The same lexeme does different work in Mimimi’s explicitly romantic clarification, Hinami’s stated admiration of Tama, ordinary preferences and a person’s present self-liking. Shared wording cannot decide that those acts are secretly the same. Their objects and contexts matter: enjoying a game, valuing a principled friend, choosing a partner and being able to like one’s own conduct do not imply identical desire or entitlement.

Tama’s `私のヒーロー` is similarly particular. The possessive names what Mimimi has been to this speaker. It challenges reducing her to public comparative rank without declaring that ambition no longer matters or that everyone must share Tama’s evaluation. The actual relation makes a value available which the shared scoreboard cannot exhaust. A translation that keeps only an abstract compliment loses the person-specific claim; a translation that makes it universal praise overextends it. [LV-C15; LV-L12]

Qualification can also be private or conjectural. Hinami’s loneliness-with-tolerance formulation does not say she never feels lonely or requests rescue. Fumiya’s “special” can state an owned attachment without proving reciprocal romance. A later observer can have more evidence than one earlier interlocutor and still lack a right to choose what the other person wants. The language specialist supplies these distinctions to the relationship and care specialists; it cannot settle romantic classification by treating a high-salience word as a category test.

## 9. Learned lightness can make a difficult truth sayable

Fumiya’s V12 conversation with Mizusawa gives language learning a positive outcome that is more specific than becoming fluent. He says he once treated anything other than directly stating his thought as insincere. His narration now identifies Mizusawa as the model for lightness intended to make the other person comfortable enough to speak honestly. Deliberate presentation and wanted disclosure are therefore not opposites. The chosen manner can serve the relation he wants, even when he knows its source. [LV-C16; LV-L13]

He cannot quite state the friendship directly. He borrows Libra and Sharif to say that an instrumental beginning need not exhaust what a relation becomes, and hesitantly introduces best friendship. The narration identifies the evasion of too direct a formulation. Mizusawa then answers the actual Fumiya, using his given name, laughs and gives thanks. Fiction is an intermediary through which something real becomes sayable. It does not have to be an accurate total model of either person for this exchange to occur.

The hedges here cannot automatically be read as weak belief in the relation. Embarrassment, familiarity and the difficulty of naming closeness contribute to their local force. Conversely, the narration’s felt success does not mean every withheld proposition has now been communicated. Mizusawa’s offer of further conversation is particular participation, not permission from Hinami for the continued use of her history. One strategic motive can coexist with actual friendship, and an accepted personal disclosure cannot waive an absent person’s rights. [MZ-P03–05; CRI-REL-004/005]

The same portable game language still calls the work a formidable means to defeat the demon king. That does not annul the warmth. It also prevents the analyst from assuming the new conversational openness has removed every strategic frame. Real gratitude and an instrumental project can be present in one exchange. A mature language account preserves their coexistence rather than deciding that the person’s most casual or most serious line reveals the one real voice beneath everything else.

## 10. Tama’s words belong to different recipients before they become a theory

The family-shop scene makes Tama’s range directly visible. A customer-service greeting changes into a plain recognition of Fumiya. She calls for reservations, asks him for practical help and addresses the customer differently. When the cake is damaged, the first reassuring apology belongs to the neighboring staff member. Tama’s own later apology concerns the wait after the remake. These are different speakers and stages; a role description such as “shop staff apologizes” cannot supply exact Tama dialogue. [LV-C17; LV-L15]

Her form is neither a permanently blunt essence nor an entirely borrowed public mask. She can use conventional politeness to carry a demanding judgment about what the customer should receive. That judgment also delays other work and relies on her position as the manager’s daughter. The ability to apologize or serve competently does not establish that the decision was costless or that every recipient wanted it in the same way. Register indicates a local relation and task; it cannot settle the whole ethics of the act.

After closing, her self-account uses `歪んでる` to question the very strength others admire. She connects a borrowed hero ideal with repeated action and identifies both pride and a self-binding limit: lightly passing over the situation could make her dislike herself later. The term is her description of a difficult relation to an ideal, not a diagnosis supplied by the analyst. It does not mean her earlier judgment was false, nor that every supposedly distorted action should be celebrated as authentic. Her account includes uncertainty and the possibility that someone else might act more skillfully. [LV-L15]

Fumiya then deletes his ready-made interpretive connection before sending remembered words. He worries that once he summarizes, the words will cease to be Tama’s. That is an actual attempt to resist annexing a person into the desired model. It is not an unmediated transcript: memory, selection and circulation remain his acts. The scene makes the literary duty concrete. A specialist may need to interpret, but should preserve the account and its inconvenient qualifiers before treating it as the answer to someone else’s problem.

The earlier `一貫した隙` discussion supplies another limit. Mizusawa proposes an account of recognizable openness, using different examples. Tama’s own response and joking exchange remain part of the conversation. Her later public pun does not prove that her speech has become a mechanically reproduced social product. The crowd intervention’s ethical objection comes first and is helped by changed relationships and actual ratification. Language can be learned and still be hers; its success cannot be reduced to copying a phrase. [JVL-20/21; LOC-V04/LOC-12E; LV-L24]

## 11. Mizusawa’s self-qualification is evidence of a revisable account

Mizusawa’s V11 explanation of why Hinami matters to him is not a confession from Hinami. He conditionally imagines someone afraid to reveal herself but lonely, connects his own capacity to understand with the meaning it gives him, and then asks whether this is a childish hero wish. The conjectural ending qualifies his self-account. Fumiya responds as though he has encountered a truth he could not find alone. That reception is evidence of Fumiya’s changed view, not independent confirmation of every proposition about Hinami. [LV-C18; LV-L16]

The hedge should therefore be neither discarded nor idealized. Removing it gives the speaker a settled motive diagnosis he does not claim. Treating it as proof of universal honesty would use one form as a lie detector. The source supports a particular person testing his own explanation in a difficult conversation. Later direct thanks and awkward naming of friendship widen the repertoire without making every past performance false.

Casual fluency also does not mean costless expression. He can explain performance and remain embarrassed when made to perform; he can model lightness and unusually speak without his familiar joke. Those differences are interpretively useful because the reader has local contrasts. They are not a frequency table of particles or a complete public/private baseline. The monograph owns the full developmental account; this specialist preserves the modality and recipient conditions under which useful propositions were actually uttered.

## 12. Yuzu can refuse softly; a loud group cannot supply her missing words

Yuzu’s captaincy refusal is syntactically softened and behaviorally consequential. She says she does not want the role this year; Fumiya had expected her to yield and is surprised. Konno withdraws the demand before redirecting it at Hirabayashi. The source gives a weak-looking resistance that the focalizer understands as difficult progress. A stronger agency claim does not require rewriting her into someone who speaks like Tama. [LV-C19; LV-L14]

Fumiya’s interpretation of the earlier year remains conjectural. The fact that he now sees her resist does not certify that every prior acceptance was coerced or that every later accommodation lacks ownership. The exact negative proposition must survive the soft delivery, while the broader psychological model retains its evidence limits. Her chosen public work, ordinary taste and educational preference belong to her individual study, not to a theory that hesitant syntax excludes independent ends.

Her familiar departure formula in the V09 setup likewise performs a particular social act. It can facilitate a wanted repair while leaving the meeting’s prior consent and the speakers’ knowledge uneven. Brightness is neither empty decoration nor automatic harmlessness. In V12, participation in delivery and a visible final wave are actual; added words are obscured by Takei. No amount of knowledge of her probable tone can reconstruct their content.

Takei’s loudness consequently has more than one effect. It reaches a withdrawn recipient and later fills a public room; it also masks another person’s speech. Those results do not turn him into either a wise messenger or meaningless noise. The language field has material conditions—distance, volume, timing and simultaneous voices—that can make access unequal even when people are together. Fumiya’s later lip-reading of Hinami is another bounded channel, not independently heard dialogue. [JVL §13.3–13.4; LV-L14/19]

## 13. Hinami’s familiar expression does not identify her whole real self

Hinami explicitly calls `おにただ` a habit in the early coaching conversation. She traces it to a childhood game, describes attempts to conceal it and reacts with pleasure when Fumiya recognizes the reference. Technical and aesthetic enthusiasm interrupts the lesson before she restores its subject. This is direct evidence of shared interest and a self-reported recurring form. It is not a count of how often she uses it or a license to put it into every future sentence. [LV-C20; LV-L17]

The scene also resists a simple distinction between a calculated lesson and an authentic interruption. Both are things she can do with this recipient. Her enthusiasm has an independent object; restoring the coaching role does not erase it. Conversely, a familiar childish expression cannot become a password that automatically reveals the complete person behind her public presentation.

NO NAME’s naming history and Fumiya’s later mask interpretation require the same restraint. The source represents middle-school self-negation and the adoption of a competitive name; literal nonexistence does not follow from that self-theory. The later second-mask formulation belongs to an observer reconsidering his access. Neither permits the analyst to label every public act false and every sharp private remark true. The modes involve audiences, purposes and performed control, and later disclosure does not make all earlier private details universally known. [JVL-07/10; LOC-01A/B]

The final familiar plain speech is not proof that all modes have fused into one unguarded register. Hinami can say stop to jostling, react with irritation, attend as an audience member and speak competitively. Her direct statement is that she has stopped clinging to correctness; it describes herself. Fumiya’s invitation to enjoy follows. A translation that turns her line into “you should stop being correct” changes the subject and the relationship’s action. No narrated match result or permanent recovery can be supplied by that change. [LV-C20; LV-L04/19]

## 14. Narrative person determines access without certifying a worldview

V11 frames a disclosure in present conversation, breaks into third-person Aoi-centered childhood narration and eventually moves into first-person self-accusation. A source summary that calls the whole history a spoken first-person account gives Fumiya an unsupported transcript. A summary that calls it wholly third-person loses the later interior shift. The Japanese person-marking is materially relevant to what the reader receives and what another actor can be credited with hearing. [LV-C21; LV-L18]

The represented childhood warmth remains positive evidence. Birth gratitude is narrated as something Yoko repeatedly says; the later hospital speech assigns meaning to the death. Her tears and the account’s conjectures about her coping remain present alongside Aoi’s terrible reception of the words. A formation explanation cannot convert the mother into a named religious affiliation, an emotionless doctrine or the single cause of every later response. The same sequence both gives access and shows the cost of interpreting another person through an inherited explanatory form.

V12’s explicit Hinami `私` gives the reader another private account that revisits several eras. It does not make every proposition contemporaneous with the act of reading, nor turn the focalizer’s self-condemnation into the novel’s objective ontology. Her judgment of her room’s visual reminder is hers. Her whispered self-hate is described as something she believes Fumiya did not hear. Reader access to those lines is not a completed disclosure to the network. [LV-L19]

The issue is wider than recognizing quotation marks. A first-person narrator can remember speech, hypothesize another motive, imagine a scene or describe a sensation as if it were a truth about the world. The analyst needs the framing as well as the pronoun. The text’s ability to draw the reader close to one perspective is a literary achievement, not a reason to erase the evidence boundary precisely where the experience is most convincing.

## 15. Reason, cause, meaning and prayer do not form a one-word-per-concept dictionary

Hinami’s V10 disclosure places `理由` beside `原因` in a statement of causal helplessness. She cannot recover the decisive explanation or know which counterfactual advice would have mattered. Elsewhere a reason can be a warrant for belief or a purpose for writing. English paraphrase must retain those functions rather than automatically translating one written form into one fixed philosophical variable. The concepts are analytically distinguishable; their Japanese words do not supply a permanent one-to-one code. [LV-C22; LV-L18/22]

The distinction between an antecedent cause and a retrospectively usable meaning remains essential. A story may help someone live with an unknown event without determining how it happened. An owned reason can make a choice intelligible without making every means permissible. Hinami’s inability to know Nagisa’s final intention survives the force of the later imagined encounter. Neither the act of praying nor the language of a god establishes supernatural testimony.

The ellipse supplies a particularly exact language case. Hinami says she already knew the mathematical word but had not heard it as something about herself in this way. The fictional demonstration and its two centers change the significance of a known term. **Development means becoming more direct.** V03 negotiation, V11 imperative, Kikuchi’s polite selection, Yuzu’s softened refusal and Tama’s customer speech show why force cannot be ranked on one scale. **REJECT the global arc.** Preserve particular changes in what a person can say and whose answer matters.
