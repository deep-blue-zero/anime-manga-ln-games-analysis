# Effort after the question of winning

This canonical administrative derivative has passed independent review at the R09_2 local literary scope. The [ensemble and thematic integration review](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_ENSEMBLE_AND_THEMATIC_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md) binds the accepted argument, exact corrections, actual read/source scopes and canonical render. Preserved authoring-history references describe their earlier stage. Cross-specialist convergence, final synthesis, literary release, distinct models and repository publication retain separate gates.

## 1. Effort changes what can be chosen; winning does not choose for us

Tomozaki gives sustained practice real efficacy. Fumiya has learned something difficult before his school-life instruction begins. Hinami can teach him usable means. Tama can acquire a conversational repertoire without becoming Hinami. A professional's judgment, a craftswoman's correction and a writer's labor make differences that goodwill alone cannot supply. The literary argument cannot begin by treating competence as a fraud or end by making spontaneity the sole sign of a life that belongs to its actor. Yet the same series repeatedly refuses a larger inference: that effectiveness, victory, effort's intensity or even sincere desire can settle what all that ability ought to serve. [TE-E01–03]

The development is an expanding ability to **choose, inhabit, revise, continue and sometimes stop a practice**, while recognizing that other people have ends of their own. Effort becomes more answerable to an actor's wanted life, the particular recipient, available capacity and the costs of the chosen method. None of these fields is permanently solved. A wanted rescue can neglect a partner or a public obligation; an owned ethic can delay customers; a voluntary adaptation can exclude the adapter's own pleasures. A successful performance may remain worthwhile while its governing justification fails. Conversely, a loss can expose a bad claim without erasing skill, affection or the value of having acted. [TE-E04–07,12–16]

This is a thematic inference from different acts, not a doctrine spoken intact by one character. The protagonists do not reach a final formula that guarantees self-authorship. The last performance has not even been played on the page. What becomes available is a richer set of questions: whose end is this, how was it chosen, what does the method let us learn, whose participation does it require, and what would make continuation or revision intelligible? Those questions should illuminate ordinary pleasure as well as crisis. A thrift purchase that leaves wages for an arcade or DLC, a run enjoyed in its movement, food eaten on a work break and a guest's ability to address an audience all belong to the inquiry. They are ways of spending a life, not filler between its important ranked contests. [TE-E08,10–11,17]

## 2. Six judgments that the narrative keeps apart

The distinction below is analytical vocabulary. It does not purport to replace the characters' Japanese terms or classify every act with a numerical score. One action can answer several questions well and another badly. [TE-E01]

| Judgment | What the source can establish | Invalid transfer to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Exertion | Someone practices, works, studies, attends, spends time or persists. | Large expenditure therefore has a good end, deserves another person, or must continue. |
| Mastery | A repertoire becomes reliable within a task and conditions. | The same standing governs romance, morality, grief or all future conditions. |
| Victory/result | A particular contest, response or output happens. | The winner's life theory is true, the method ethical, or the loser without value. |
| Ownership | An actor can avow or inhabit this desire, adaptation or refusal. | The end is harmless, wholly independent in origin, or entitled to other people's labor. |
| Justified means and allocation | Particular permissions, recipient needs and costs support a method here. | A beneficial outcome supplies prior permission or indefinite capacity. |
| Future viability | A route, resources and obligations support some prospect under stated conditions. | A first opportunity, follower promise, expert compliment or selected goal proves a sustainable career. |

These separations allow a positive account of discipline without requiring that everyone possess a complete theory before acting. Fumiya can begin an experiment before knowing the full life he will want. Ashigaru can report that a reason for his work changed as he did it. Mimimi can discover what running feels like during a contest. The point is to preserve opportunities for feedback and revision, rather than to make perfect preliminary self-knowledge another admission test for living. [TE-E02,08,11]

## 3. Prior mastery is both an enabling resource and an unreliable jurisdiction

Fumiya's initial gamer ethic matters because it joins labor to a wanted practice. He does not merely admire a badge of success from outside it. He notices choices, makes corrections and understands the time hidden inside a result. This makes Hinami's challenge to his account of real-life possibilities consequential: she reaches someone who already takes agency seriously in one domain while treating another as exempt from useful effort. The ensuing instruction works partly because it can recruit an existing competence rather than manufacture a person from nothing. The move from game to life is nonetheless an analogy with limits. Social recipients are not a fixed ruleset, and learning an effective move does not specify the desired relationship. [TE-E02; TE-P01]

Hinami's early training has actual beneficial effects: more reliable posture, expression and conversation make formerly inaccessible encounters possible. Other people answer those encounters and become co-producers of experience. The acquired form therefore changes the field in which a desire can emerge. It is too simple to say that external technique merely masks an already complete true self, or that every scripted act is false. When practiced form helps Fumiya notice something he wants to ask, another person's response can become a new reason to continue. The expansion of technique is valuable precisely because it can enable ends beyond its teacher's plan. [TE-E03; EFF §§4.2,13,22–23]

The reversal is equally important. Existing mastery can lend excessive authority to a cross-domain conclusion. Fumiya's seriousness does not entitle him to prescribe a person for Hinami, and Hinami's social success does not entitle her to prescribe a romance for him. Shared competitive standards can make a disagreement mutually intelligible, but a game only produces the result of its agreed contest. It does not prove that the winning position has acquired jurisdiction over the loser's life. The provisional competitive accommodation of V03, the ethical confrontation in V06 and the failed rescue credential in V11 are distinct uses of that shared language. They are not successive measurements of one universal hierarchy. [TE-E04,14; TE-P02,05,11]

## 4. A goal can be permitted without becoming co-authored

The V03 rupture is not adequately described as Fumiya refusing effort. He contests an assigned relationship end. His first attempt to separate genuine desire from rationally produced desire overshoots: the existence of a causal or instrumental account does not by itself make liking unreal. The subsequent hybrid retains practice and strategy while demanding a role for what he wants. V04 shows why this is an operational negotiation rather than a completed emancipation. A right to refuse an assignment differs from participation in designing the curriculum, and participation in a method differs from ownership of its larger destination. [TE-E04; TE-P02; EFF §§9–10,13–16]

Yuzu offers a useful nonprotagonist comparison. Her wish to understand Nakamura gives the learning an end she can name; asking for mathematical help is purposeful action within that relation. It is neither pure submission nor proof that every later burden of repair should fall to her. Her interests also exceed that one romance: shopping and clothes have particular pleasures, and her reported attraction to a university through an admired model is a real educational preference without being a demonstrated admissions plan or a modeling career. These acts do not have to become independent of all social influence to count as hers. Their ownership lies in her particular uptake and action, not in having invented every ingredient alone. [TE-E05,09; TE-P03; Yuzu §§16.1–2,17]

The same care is needed with permission. In the V06.5 phone episode the relevant phone owner and person whose assent is at issue is Kawaguchi Mutsumi; Yuzu is a beneficiary and observer. Treating all helpful action as Yuzu's general acquiescence would falsify the relation that makes the act possible. This small distinction exposes a larger danger: an account of effort that names only the beneficiary can lose the person providing the resource, the scope of the agreement and the possibility of refusal. [TE-E05; early coverage receipt, V06.5 part0029 P245–310; current Yuzu account]

Owned ends remain revisable and can remain ethically incomplete. Fumiya's choices in a romance curriculum affect Kikuchi and Mimimi, who are not rewards for his improved competence. Kikuchi's later voluntary assimilation can leave her own interests outside the arrangement. His own wanted rescue in V12 is real even after his unique usefulness fails, but he explicitly knows that one formulation will secure his partner's acceptance and recognizes its unfairness. The desire's sincerity intensifies the problem of allocation; it does not settle it. [TE-E05,12; TE-P08,12]

## 5. Fitted learning and the positive agency of a learner

Tama's V05 learning is a major test of whether adaptation means obedience. A tutor-centered replication of Hinami cannot simply be imposed on a different learner. Fumiya has to estimate Tama's existing state, translate methods into forms she can use and accept contributions from Yuzu, Mimimi, Mizusawa and Tama herself. Their roles are not interchangeable encouragement: they supply feedback, recipient knowledge and different usable examples. Tama's own action determines whether a technique is incorporated into a life she can recognize. [TE-E06; EFF §§22–23]

The process also gives form a generative function. A conversational exercise can make genuine curiosity easier; deliberate attention may precede the felt ease subsequently called natural. This is a causal claim supported by particular practice, not an instruction that every person should train away resistance or make every relation efficient. A useful technique becomes problematic when the teacher treats it as sufficient evidence for the end it should serve. The ordinary possibility of enjoying a conversation is distinct from an assigned requirement to acquire a partner or to maximize social standing. [TE-E03,06]

Tama's later cake work preserves both sides. She is a competent worker addressing customers before Fumiya requests an explanation. Her childhood hero model is an adopted form she now owns, not evidence of an untouched natural purity. She cannot dismiss the damaged birthday cake lightly, and the remake matters to the recipient. It also delays others and depends partly on her privilege as the shop manager's daughter. Sharing the unsold cake and discussing the cost do not make the earlier delay imaginary. Her account is valuable because it holds purpose, embodiment, social position and cost together. It is not a universal victory of rigidity or a settled succession to the shop. [TE-E06,13; TE-P13; Tama §§6–7,15.1–3]

## 6. Victory can terminate an oppression without completing justice

The V05 punitive response makes an owned end insufficient as an ethical defense of effort. Hinami's intervention is skilled, effective and emotionally invested. Tama's pain matters to her, and V06 later makes revenge and love legible in the confrontation. Removing the immediate class pressure does not establish a measured, proportionate or authorized method. Tama's stopping intervention matters as a distinct act by the person supposedly being helped. Neither Hinami's calculation nor Fumiya's partial moral objection can absorb that standing. [TE-E07; TE-P04–05; EFF §§24,29]

The evidence also requires actor-specific repair. Akiyama apologizes and other group members apologize in V05 after Konno has left. A generic assertion that nobody acknowledges harm would be false. Those acts do not establish Konno's own apology, full restitution, blanket forgiveness or an institution capable of preventing recurrence. Yuzu's repair work and later useful Konno behavior likewise have their own value without becoming substitute aggressor accountability. This combination is central to the thematic argument: the source neither withholds all positive action until a perfect system exists nor lets a successful immediate outcome certify the whole remedy. [TE-E07; TE-P04; CS §20/SOC §18/CRI §19]

A stopping rule need not be a complete policy announced in advance. Tama can stop this escalation without having solved every future conflict. What must remain visible is that someone's effort has become excessive for its recipient, and that the recipient's intervention is evidence rather than an obstacle the efficient helper should overcome. This prepares the later question of a care project that spends other people's privacy, time and authority in pursuit of a sincerely wanted good. [TE-E07,12]

## 7. Calculability, competitive pleasure and the difference between decision and result

V06 gives a precise local instance of choosing an exposure. Fumiya observes Hinami give ground and take damage; he interprets this as preferring a calculable loss to an incalculable risk. The interpretation is his, not an avowal recovered from her mind, and cannot become a universal rule that she never risks or feels. Their actual play also includes Fumiya's excitement at this opponent and an individual Hinami win inside the 5–1 set. Skill, uncertainty tolerance and the pleasure of contest coexist without forming one monotonic scale. [TE-E10; TE-P05]

V08 then distinguishes a decision's quality from both its immediate outcome and its long-run expectation. Fumiya's early risky combo actually succeeds. Later he defends against a small failure and loses the relevant opportunity. Ashigaru interprets this through avoidance of error and willingness to trust a practiced action; Fumiya recognizes something in it. This is an attributed professional model with narrated uptake, not a clinical diagnosis or an exhaustive explanation of Fumiya. Crucially, the earlier combo's success does not retroactively make its choice sound. Nor does Ashigaru's forecast that Fumiya would come out ahead across a hundred games mean that a hundred-game experiment occurred. The actual series result and the estimate answer different questions. [TE-E10; TE-P07]

This is not a mandate to risk more everywhere. A sensible decision depends on stakes, conditions, practiced reliability and the end of this particular contest. Fumiya's failure under this format does not prove all his acquired mastery false. The professional's more useful contribution is to reveal another part of the task that rank alone conceals: a player's ability includes acting under a specified opportunity and making a performance intelligible to its audience. [TE-E10–11]

## 8. Process pleasure, comparison and a locally chosen stop

Mimimi's separately routed race narrative supplies strong counterevidence to an effort theory restricted to ranking. Her running is not free of competition: she wants to win, compares herself to Aoi and carries their earlier asymmetry. The change happens within that live contest, when attention to movement and her own stride becomes pleasurable. She wins the narrated hundred metres. Her personal-best intention is not a measured achieved personal best: no recorded time is supplied. Refusing Aoi's requested rematch is then a real local stop. The account need not invent permanent liberation from comparison in order to value it. [TE-E08; TE-P06]

Person-specific recognition from Tama remains another real good. Later concern about depending on Tama's physical presence does not make that earlier support false or embarrassing. V09 already gives Mimimi self-regarding restraint and continuing love; V12 deepens rather than originates that capacity. She can like having refused to exploit Fumiya's vulnerability while still wanting him. The distinction between not choosing opportunism and no longer loving is essential. Her novice performance work and investment in comedy similarly open activity without proving that a professional comedy career has been selected or made viable. [TE-E08,16; inherited Mimimi R07 FM-C and source receipt]

Ordinary enjoyment does not require moral perfection. Race exhilaration, joking, clothes, food and game fandom are positive parts of particular lives, but a pleasurable act can still disregard a protest or impose a burden. Neither the presence of comparison nor the presence of joy supplies the entire verdict. A plausible account of agency must allow a person to enjoy something, act clumsily, learn and choose another occasion without turning each instance into a final personality definition. [TE-E08–09]

## 9. Motives change without producing a single awakening

Mizusawa makes detached success an explicit problem: an outcome can be easy to obtain while the actor remains absent from its risk. Inhabiting rejection can matter more to him than preserving an effortless image. That does not make pain intrinsically improving or oblige Hinami to reward sincerity. Current accepted chronology preserves an actual V03 confession and refusal alongside the later-summer V06.5 waiting statement. Their exact temporal seam remains open. A simple waiting-to-first-pursuit ladder would erase the earlier act and manufacture an awakening the evidence does not support. [TE-E09; TE-P02; Mizusawa §3.1]

V12's friendship account is likewise plural. Aoi was one reason for approaching Fumiya; it does not follow that every joke, outing, act of care or later attachment was an instrument. On a work break, with food and familiar teasing, deliberate lightness enables a difficult exchange rather than concealing it. Learned form, strategic origin and later genuine liking can coexist. The source does not require a pure first motive before a friendship can acquire value. Nor does that value grant a general waiver to share all his or Hinami's private life. [TE-E09,12; TE-P12; REL §17.1/JVL §13.3]

The pattern also resists a simple authentic-versus-influenced opposition. Tama's hero ideal, Yuzu's admired model, Fumiya's borrowed conversational form, Hinami's mathematically rewarded hope and Mimimi's shared performance all involve received material. The relevant question is how a person takes part in the form, what it lets them feel or do, and whether they can revise it. Influence is not yet dispossession; ownership is not yet an ethical blank cheque. [TE-E03,09,13]

## 10. A demonstrated route is not yet a viable future

Ashigaru's advice matters because it gives professional aspiration conditions. University, work, family support and the possibility of trying can be discussed instead of leaving a dream as a total identity verdict. The advice is a professional's situated recommendation. His own later report of having worked elsewhere first cannot be moved backward into what Fumiya heard in V08. A route can be reasonable to investigate without being a guaranteed livelihood or a universal life plan. [TE-E11; TE-P07,09; Ashigaru §§3–5,8–9]

V10 makes reciprocity unusually concrete. Endo is a working creator with a price and a limited amount of time; reducing quality to fit an inadequate budget does not become a viable answer just because two teenagers care greatly. The negotiated promotional arrangement creates actual obligations and assent, not imaginary sponsorship. It includes a six-month promotional period and an explicit later discussion of continuation. The follower figures and three-month doubling commitment are stated targets, not achieved results. Fumiya's acceptance under Mizusawa's partly bluffing presentation also contains an asymmetry: Mizusawa's quiet physical objection precedes Fumiya echoing the claim. The form enables a real undertaking without making every component consensually predesigned or commercially proved. [TE-E11; TE-P09]

The ordinary compromise over the game's form belongs here. What Hinami would value cannot be inferred from maximal production expenditure. Fumiya argues for rule quality as a recipient-specific priority, and a feasible shooting-game form meets time constraints. This is an attempt to translate care into an available product, not evidence that enough devotion can pay any price. Endo's independent work and Ashigaru's audience contribution remain part of the cost structure. [TE-E11; TE-P09]

Other futures need similarly differentiated language. Tama's university/trial arrangement does not settle shop succession. Yuzu's preference does not establish admission or a model career. Kikuchi has writing opportunities but no shown publication contract or indefinitely sustainable output schedule. Mimimi's participation is not a selected professional route. Fumiya has skill, a professional aspiration and obligations, but no demonstrated continuing income or final career success. Retaining these limits protects the specificity of each wanted future; it should not become a condescending claim that only a conventional secured path may be chosen. [TE-E11,17]

## 11. The reason can fail while ability and relationships remain

The V11 crisis separates retained capacity from a governing reason. Aoi's mathematical achievement once gave hope that her own effort could improve a life. That positive agency must survive any later account of coercive perfection. Her subsequent achievements and real affections are not all empty acts whose hidden sole meaning the reader can now decode. Yet her attempt to secure value through a controllable result becomes vulnerable to what another person's action or an irreversible loss cannot settle. Explaining that vulnerability is different from asserting that she lacked all knowledge of chance or all earlier enjoyment. Her private middle-school account already recognizes luck and the need to include emotion in reasoning. [TE-E14; TE-P06,10–11]

Fumiya's V11 appeal has a different failure. He already offers groundless confidence while also invoking the special standing of a player she has never beaten. The actual loss defeats that named credential. It does not prove his kindness false, all earlier progress a lie, every skill gone or Hinami's doctrine universally true. V12 must therefore be read as a change in the carrier and standing of an offer, rather than his first invention of faith without evidence. [TE-E14; TE-P11]

This matters for effort because loss of a reason does not mechanically destroy a repertoire, and retained skill does not manufacture a replacement end. Neither further training nor another victory can alone answer whether to want this performance or who has the right to demand it. The series gives relationships, received stories, ordinary care and chosen play a role in that answer without turning them into guaranteed substitutes. [TE-E14–15]

## 12. A wanted rescue still has to meet another person's work

V12 begins the next phase by letting Fumiya retain a wish after unique usefulness has failed. Kikuchi can care for this particular exhausted partner, question his sole-rescuer posture and offer help. Their relation is not exhausted by producing a novel. He comes to articulate things he likes about her, including her playful, competitive elaboration, that do not derive from writing utility. Accepting such care is a positive change in how effort can be shared. It does not erase his narrated awareness that one appeal will secure her acceptance and is unfair. [TE-E12; TE-P12]

The writing party then becomes an actual plural method. Mimimi contributes embodied and self-regarding experience; Mizusawa offers disclosures and reluctantly agrees to one attempt at unscripted acting. The actual interviews and role-play initially make the independent-star remedy attractive. Only later does Kikuchi's authored Alcia accuse Chris of selecting the desired answer in advance; that manuscript objection is neither the actual role-play's initial outcome nor Hinami's actual testimony. Contributor agreement is specific and can include pressure. Kikuchi then stops and changes course. Her initial refusal of a solo-trip companion is real; the later telephone invitation and date are a changed agreement, not evidence the first no meant yes. [TE-E12,15; TE-P12]

This is more than a lesson in consulting enough helpers. A contributor may agree to share one story without waiving another person's privacy. An author may permit circulation of a draft without having the modeled subject's permission. A partner may join a journey without promising every future allocation. Haruka's reading, pressure and message have her own sibling stake, while the eventual recipient experiences intrusion as well as response. These are fields in which effort must remain answerable after the project produces a benefit. [TE-E12,15]

## 13. Craft makes limits productive without making every intervention rightful

The island sequence gives observation and practice a material test. Mitsu's neighboring shades are not interchangeable because an amateur has learned a name. Light, angle, wearer and relation change an appearance. The teens must handle objects and test combinations; her correction preserves expert work rather than rewarding a pure attitude. Mitsu herself refuses some help, accepts a limited task through a trusted local and continues work with bodily difficulty. Her choices cannot become either total independence or a clinical lesson in accepting assistance. [TE-E13; TE-P14]

The costume episode is particularly instructive because usefulness and unauthorized access coexist. Fumiya proposes opening the package, opens it with Kikuchi's assent, and later owns that proposal. The written blue-color name is correct; the attached scarlet sample mismatches it. The exact mechanism and culprit remain guessed. Kikuchi's later trial, the pin and color/movement changes, and Mitsu's skilled sewing produce a usable result. Her acceptance of that result cannot grant prior permission to open the package. An effort study that records only ingenious repair would lose a central fact about the method. [TE-E13; TE-P14; CRI §21]

Likewise, the storm-damaged birds remain damaged. Combining them with new material and allowing children to describe what they see helps create enough usable work. This neither undoes the loss nor establishes that the storm was necessary for a better festival. The children make meanings of their own, while adults, young people and elders can take part for different reasons. Skilled and improvised labor can recover an occasion without supplying one correct interpretation for everyone. [TE-E13,15; TE-P14–15]

Kikuchi cannot outsource her final authorship to exhaustive observation. Andi's surviving revisions and fallible resident reports make a resource, not an infallible manual. The represented progression from believing through telling, recording and wishing to praying is directly marked; the couple's explanation of each change remains their interpretation. Her eventual writing reason is owned through choosing what to offer under uncertainty. It does not certify the imagined Nagisa as a real final witness. The work is completed, but no publishing outcome follows. [TE-E15; TE-P15]

## 14. Re-entry and expressive purpose without a completed future

Aoi's slight present self-liking and public return are actual changes. They are not too small to count simply because permanence is untested. She joins a school audience, answers peers, accepts an exhibition and says she has stopped clinging to correctness. The p0026 P267 remark is her self-description, not an instruction that Fumiya should stop. His goal begins to include what professional play might show an audience rather than only whether he can certify himself by winning. The character choice Found is narrated. The match and result are not. [TE-E16–17; TE-P16]

The ordinary group scene also refuses to make recovery equivalent to immediately resuming perfect labor. Sitting beside Mimimi and Tama, exchanging familiar annoyance, receiving a look or visible wave, and joining play matter in their own right. They do not prove restored presidency, resumed coaching or complete family repair. Nor does ordinary return erase costs. Fumiya and Mimimi have actually neglected council preparation while absorbed in the novel. The guest has been selected, but what the guest will do remains unsettled; a monitor/projector fallback exists and a printed program is positively present. Ashigaru's actual professional work carries the event. The text does not establish whether that work was composed extemporaneously. [TE-E16; TE-P16; CRI §23]

The ending consequently supports a qualified future orientation. More than one source of pleasure, worth and continuation becomes imaginable, and an audience-facing purpose is being selected. No durable protocol, livelihood or final competitive validation is supplied. A useful thematic ending is therefore not “effort pays off” or “results do not matter.” Results matter enough to change opportunities and expose claims; they remain insufficient to choose the whole life that follows. [TE-E17]
