# A peer world no one owns — Mimimi and Tama, distributed capacities and unequal standing

This canonical administrative derivative has passed independent review at the R08 local literary scope. The [ensemble and thematic integration review](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_ENSEMBLE_AND_THEMATIC_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md) binds the accepted argument, exact corrections, actual read/source scopes and canonical render. Preserved authoring-history references describe their earlier stage. Cross-specialist convergence, final synthesis, literary release, distinct models and repository publication retain separate gates.

## 1. The independent problem this ensemble owns

The friends can do things together that no one member can do alone. That achievement is not adequately described as Fumiya acquiring enough allies, Hinami's social training succeeding, or a collection of complementary personalities finding the right roles. It depends on people responding in ways the organizer cannot supply: laughing, refusing, asking, revising a proposal, keeping company, accepting a task, bringing an independent skill, or declining to do more. The network is made through those responses. It has no single owner whose good purpose makes the others' time, information and capacities freely available. [EP-C01–03; EP-L01–04]

The central argument is that **distributed capacity grows faster than a shared account of authority and cost**. Learner-specific teaching, particular recognition, public production and collective writing become possible through differences among the participants. The same differences leave knowledge uneven, labor easy to overlook and standing difficult to transfer. A group can accurately understand more than one person and still arrange the answer in advance. It can care sincerely and still put pressure on an unwilling recipient. It can keep a friendship available while leaving an injury unanswered. The important development is not a steady movement from manipulation to pure cooperation. It is a widening repertoire whose beneficial and harmful uses repeatedly require fresh judgment.

Mimimi–Tama is a coequal relationship argument in §§3–8, not a small example of this general thesis. Its history cannot be reconstructed from a list of contributions to Fumiya. Particular recognition helps Mimimi return to a world she still wants; Mimimi helps make more of that world accessible to Tama. Their meals, refusals, gestures, concealed accommodations and pleasure in other friends matter independently of rescue. The relation also exposes the risk of locating personal value in being needed. Tama's expanded agency is something Mimimi genuinely welcomes, even where it unsettles her place. V12 gives Mimimi's fear of future physical separation without supplying Tama's answer. That is a changing relationship, not a stable caregiver/dependent classification. [EP-C04–10]

The other four mandatory cases—Hinami–Tama, Hinami–Mizusawa, Fumiya–Mizusawa and Fumiya–Rena—receive substantive ownership in §§9–12 and the directional register. Their person-level histories remain with the seven accepted monographs and bounded dossiers. The three central Fumiya dyads retain their independent R07 responsibilities. Class punishment mechanisms retain the mandatory social-system specialist; this ensemble owns what happens to particular relationships within those mechanisms. The three R07 responsibilities have separate independent local acceptance; their decisions did not substitute for the independent review of this ensemble. Its source claims continue to route through the current person studies and cumulative owners.

## 2. Relations precede the plan and exceed the protagonist's progress

Fumiya enters people who already have histories, not a room waiting for his development. Yuzu wants a particular relation with Nakamura before he can teach her the game. Nakamura's dismissive treatment of her as a practice partner and his later comfort in the same early band are different acts by the same person. His real improvement after losing is recognized by the opponent who defeats him. Respect can therefore grow across rivalry without making either the initial aggression or the skill gap disappear. Later shared-history advice comes from his own relationship, not from a completed conversion into Fumiya's assistant. [EP-L01/12]

The direction of expertise is already plural. Fumiya can teach Yuzu a game and need her understanding of ordinary presentation. Mizusawa can make social form intelligible and receive from Fumiya an example of personally inhabited effort. Tama can need a safer classroom interface while already possessing stronger expression fundamentals than the coach's former baseline. Kikuchi can be uncomfortable leading a rehearsal and have a work worth performing. These are differences in task and standing, not positions on one total ranking of people.

Other people also offer entries before or outside the protagonist. Goda Sayaka notices Kikuchi's response, recommends a book without pretending to know its contents, then reads it so that enthusiasm can be shared. Her later apology and library companionship are acts by an adult with a particular recipient. They prevent Fumiya from becoming the first person who ever makes Kikuchi communicable. Kana's bonus-story invitation explicitly includes Tama. Sakura later proposes outings and jokes about effort; Yuki shares a menu with Tama; both join the meal. They are neither a uniformly rejecting outside world nor interchangeable products of successful training. [EP-L05/17]

This matters to the V08–V09 centrality problem. The value of Fumiya's friendships does not depend on his occupying the assigned center of all of them. His gaming field, school group, self-search alliance, work and romance do not have one end. Rejecting a centrality metric is not rejection of the people encountered through it. His later attempt to cut away groups therefore loses something he wants rather than merely simplifying a schedule. The friends' objections help reveal that loss; they do not acquire ownership of his entire future. Chosen affiliation has to coexist with a partner's claims on time and information. [SOC-C15–17; REL-C13; EP-C02]

An ensemble account should consequently ask what each person actually chooses, what a particular relation enables, and who can decline. It should not make every meal concealed repair work or every enjoyable outing a stage of Fumiya's improvement. Ordinary company is one of the things the participants are trying to preserve. Its return can be a good in itself even when there is no completed theory of why everyone belongs.

## 3. Mimimi and Tama: ordinary companionship has its own reasons

### 3.1 Two participants, not two care functions

Mimimi enjoys Tama's directness, energy and replies. Tama is a particular receiver for exaggeration and a particular person who can fail to give the desired response. That friction belongs to their pleasure. Tama can be irritated when Mimimi is not listening, evade a grasp and leave for the bathroom. Mimimi can want the other girl to stay while making a comic demand she does not obtain. Neither the familiar style nor Fumiya's appreciative gaze proves romance, universal permission for touch or an absence of bodily limits. [EP-C04; EP-L05]

The earlier appended V05 bonus is unusually strong evidence because the reader receives both focalizers. Mimimi privately admires the newly worn bracelet while failing to hear Tama's account. Tama anticipates an ordinary comic evasion and is surprised by a cheek poke instead. She says she is leaving for the bathroom because she actually needs to go, not because she is secretly staging abandonment. Mimimi's protest and speculation cannot replace that explicit account. This small mismatch is analytically useful: intimacy has not made either girl transparent to the other.

The meal is also a wanted activity. Mimimi enjoys the crab cream pasta, wants another bite of risotto and gets corrected for taking before the answer. Tama privately laughs at her appetite. The second focalization supplies a detail a one-way caretaker account would miss: roughly a quarter of Tama's risotto is eaten, but she receives pasta in exchange. This does not retrospectively provide permission for the first reach. It does keep the shared pleasure and material reciprocity visible, rather than converting every intrusion into proof that one girl only serves the other.

### 3.2 Concealed kindness makes care and labor unevenly visible

Kana invites both girls to a larger karaoke outing. Mimimi likes that noisy form but expects it to fit Tama poorly. She declines by claiming they already have a next destination and does not tell Tama that she has refused for her. The source gives her reason and her preferred aesthetic of kindness. It does not show Tama choosing the refusal, all classmates excluding Tama, or Mimimi secretly wanting repayment. [EP-C05; EP-L05]

Meanwhile Tama notices shrimp in the menu photograph and asks staff to remove it from Mimimi's crab pasta. The staff check rather than instantly obey an unlimited demand; the kitchen can make the adjustment, and Tama thanks them. Her later concealment closely resembles Mimimi's: she chose to do it herself. The acts are reciprocal without being identical. One gives up a preferred social format, one checks food and recruits a small piece of service work, and neither fully sees the other's act.

The concealment has two possible forces that the study must retain. It can spare a friend the burden of gratitude or the feeling that her presence is costly. It can also prevent recognition and future revision. When Mimimi says she might order the same pasta again, Tama privately wonders whether she will have to arrange the omission again. The source does not show resentment or a fixed unpaid duty. It does show how unannounced care can become a prospective recurring task before anyone has discussed it. A gift is not made false by that risk, and warmth is not proof that the risk is absent.

### 3.3 A smaller invitation changes the option, rather than proving the larger one harmless

Tama remembers disliking karaoke in a noisy group containing less-close people. That is narrower than a permanent hatred of music or every singing format. Mimimi later proposes a brief trial with just the two of them. Tama is surprised, considers the particular company and agrees to a little. Mimimi is pleased; Tama is moved by that pleasure and wants to enjoy it too. They leave toward the trial. Its completed outcome is not narrated. [EP-C04–05]

This is a genuine change in available action. The alternative protects something Mimimi wants without requiring Tama to accept the original group form. It still contains Mimimi's hope that Tama will become more comfortable joining others. The analyst need not choose between pure selflessness and covert normalization. Mimimi wants shared life to widen; Tama can try under revised conditions. The decisive questions are the actual format, recipient, scope and answer. The parallel karaoke story in V08.5 cannot supply the missing result.

The episode already makes the later ensemble problem concrete. Good company does not require identical preferences, and fit need not mean permanent separation. A relation can produce a new option rather than forcing one person's preferred option or eliminating it altogether. That achievement is ordinary and local. It does not require a crisis, an exclusive bond or a final judgment about which girl is socially stronger.

## 4. Particular recognition changes Mimimi's options without abolishing rank

### 4.1 Tama answers from a history Fumiya cannot manufacture

Mimimi's V02 difficulty is not that no one notices her skill. She knows Hinami has worked, loves her and resents what repeated comparison is doing to her own feeling. Wanting the stronger friend out of the competitive field becomes a source of shame. Fumiya's earnest encouragement takes her effort seriously but initially assumes continuing has the same function it has in his own practice. More assurance about ability cannot by itself answer the use being made of the result. [CS-MIM-02; EP-L06]

Tama's answer has a different standing. She calls Mimimi **her** hero and names acts received: smiling, trying, hiding strain and helping. Hinami's excellence cannot make that history have happened with someone else. The familiar comic formulation of being first lets the statement enter their existing exchange. It is not a new public ranking in which Tama can guarantee Mimimi will always win, and it does not require Hinami to become less capable. [EP-C06; EP-L06]

Fumiya helps create the encounter and Mimimi agrees to leave with them. Tama speaks, and Mimimi responds. The response is not a passive repair applied to a patient. Tears become joking, gesture and renewed participation. The text also keeps Tama's physical protests when the joking becomes heavy. Restored familiarity can be affectionate without rendering every protested act an ideal method of care. Fumiya's narration of a beautiful intimate world is his view, not independent evidence for a romantic category.

The immediate change should be preserved. Mimimi can return to track and ordinary relation; the affirmation is not exposed as worthless because a later fear survives. What it changes is the set of ways she can understand herself at that moment. A person already affected by her has a claim the abstract leaderboard cannot cancel. That claim is powerful precisely because it is particular. It is not a guarantee that she can carry it into every setting unchanged.

### 4.2 Gratitude can be received as a service condition the speaker did not impose

Tama's speech includes gratitude for labor and hidden strain. It is not an instruction that Mimimi must always conceal suffering. Yet the later running story makes visible a possible narrowing: Mimimi worries about her place when the support she once supplied is less needed. The relationship's strength and its vulnerability therefore arise partly from the same history. Being recognized for what one has actually done can be sustaining; making that service the condition of continued significance would be more precarious. [EP-C06–07]

The distinction prevents blaming Tama for a contract she never states. She does not promise to be constantly present, require endless help or say that Mimimi matters only while useful. Nor does it permit treating the later concern as merely an erroneous private thought with no relation to their actual arrangement. Mimimi really has supplied access and translation; that role really changes. Her interpretation has something to work on, without being the whole truth of the friendship.

The relation consequently differs from both a ranking substitute and a self-sufficiency lesson. Tama does not cure comparison by teaching that relationships should never matter. She offers an existing relationship as evidence. The open question is how that evidence remains usable when the old role, the social setting or physical availability changes. The answer has to preserve the relation's pleasure and reciprocity, rather than demanding that Mimimi stop receiving value from it in order to count as autonomous.

## 5. Tama's chosen change is also an act toward Mimimi

### 5.1 A friend can become a reason without becoming the author of the end

When Tama is targeted, Mimimi stays near and tries to keep familiar interaction possible. Her brightness has an audience and a cost; it is not proof that she can absorb the situation indefinitely. Tama notices that suffering and chooses to seek a different way of reaching classmates. The fact that the reason includes Mimimi does not make the choice belong to Mimimi. She has not issued a demand that Tama become normal. The choice is also constrained by harassment, so owning it does not make the surrounding options fair. [EP-C08; EP-L07]

Tama has something to teach the would-be teacher at the outset. Fumiya's copied drills do not fit her actual baseline. He initially records without advance permission; later acceptance of instruction does not rewrite that start. The corrected project requires him to stop treating his own former deficits as portable facts about another person. Tama's expression, recipient history, limited interest in many classmates and exposure to a hostile interpretation differ from his. Those differences determine what useful support would be.

The curriculum is distributed in a concrete sense. Mizusawa contributes social reading and recipient design; Takei offers a safer relation in which to practice; Mimimi supplies expressive examples and knowledge of the person; Kikuchi makes classmates available as lives worth knowing; Fumiya organizes and revises. Tama selects, learns and contributes. These are neither interchangeable acts of kindness nor proof that all participants know every purpose equally. The learner remains the person whose judgment the new means must serve. [EFFORT §§22–23; EP-C03]

### 5.2 The keepsake locates injury in a shared material history

The damaged keepsake hurts because the relation is real before the object's destruction. Tama can feel that she has injured Mimimi even though other people caused the damage. That excessive self-attribution should not become the analyst's assignment of fault. Mimimi's concern and Tama's distress alter each other's reasons, but the aggressors' responsibility remains with them. There is no ethical rule under which hurting a loved person's object obliges the victim to improve enough to prevent further attacks.

Yuzu's repair labor is also her own. It restores something material and particular; it is not the destroyer's restitution. Tama's later moral stopping is hers, and neither the repair nor the stopping supplies Konno's missing apology. Akiyama and other group members do apologize after Konno leaves. Preserving that exact act makes the unfinished state more precise: brief apology, full participation accounting, accountable repair, safeguards and renewed trust have different evidence. [CRI §19; SOC §18; EP-L08]

The result is not simply that friendship makes Tama adapt. The relation gives change a reason, while other relations and skills make the chosen change effective. Its success then changes the first relation again. If the analyst ends with the training victory, Mimimi remains permanently the useful bridge and Tama permanently the rescued friend. The later evidence refuses both assignments.

## 6. After expansion: gladness is stronger than loss, without making loss unreal

The post-V05 running story begins with Mimimi noticing Tama's new reach. Sakura and Yuki can enjoy her directness without Mimimi supplying every retort. Tama can say the repeated smallness joke is boring, decline karaoke, consider bowling and choose the restaurant. This is not submission to a successful persona. The routine is usable and revisable. The other girls respond; Tama's more comfortable participation is a relation with actual recipients, not a permanent property acquired in isolation. [EP-C07; EP-L09]

Mimimi privately recognizes both the improvement and her reduced role. She had understood herself as supplying the group competence Tama lacked. Now she sees Tama use something of it herself. The sadness is a real feeling about changed dependence, and Fumiya's growing ease widens the comparison. Her figurative black and white self-debate does not diagnose separate selves. Her suspicion that everyone else is changing while she is merely decorated is her harsh interpretation, not a certified result of a developmental contest.

The strongest counterevidence to possessive care is unusually explicit. Mimimi says Tama's friends, place and reception make her happy. When Tama freely joins the meal, Mimimi's smile follows, and the pleasure of having her there with everyone matters more than the loneliness of not being needed. A theory that she secretly wants Tama unable to function would have to erase this positive choice and direct private valuation. It cannot be rescued by treating every acknowledged insecurity as the real motive and every joy as a mask.

The new meal also shows a network beyond the dyad. Sakura has preferences and fatigue; Yuki shares a menu with Tama; Takei and Mimimi exchange loud greetings across the restaurant; Fumiya's familiar reply is received from her uncertain position. The classmates are doing ordinary things that do not all have Mimimi's private question as their end. Their contribution is partly that they can enjoy Tama without knowing or solving that question. Enlarged belonging does not have to make every participant a therapist.

Mimimi's later process-valued running supplies a wanted activity outside the old bridge role. Maternal advice, Hinami's suggestions, Fumiya's remembered terms and her own experiment have different standing in that change. It is not Tama silently repairing her once more, nor proof that one run permanently severs comparison. The relation can remain valuable while more than one source of pleasure and action becomes available. Its growth is better described as less exclusive dependence in some practices than as completed emotional independence. [EFFORT §31.2; NM-P07–12]

This is the coequal ensemble consequence: widening a person's other relations may unsettle a valued bond without devaluing it. The participants need not prefer either monopoly or emotional erasure. They can want the growth and still have to discover what their continuing importance means when the former service is no longer indispensable.

## 7. Received reliance, privacy and an unanswered future

### 7.1 Tama can wait as well as confront

The V08.5 year-end café scene changes the meaning of direct care. Tama notices a complicated atmosphere when returning to Mimimi and Kikuchi and waits. Afterward she asks whether they were all right, receives Mimimi's account and then asks why Mimimi thinks relying on strength might make love false. She does not begin by declaring an exhaustive diagnosis. Her claim that she reads the air when she wants to is a particular correction to the global incapacity model, not proof of universal social insight. [EP-C09; EP-L10]

Mimimi's question is her own. She has just received respect from the girl whose boyfriend she loves and is comparing their reasons. She fears that wanting somewhere to place a heavy burden might make her feeling less real. Tama answers from the recipient side: being leaned on can feel heavy and difficult, and it can feel warm. The two descriptions occupy the same answer. Removing the weight to make the reassurance kind would erase the care's cost; removing the warmth to expose a dependency would erase its received value.

The source does not turn this into a universal consent rule. Tama has not promised to carry every future burden or certified that every reliance is healthy. She has answered a friend whose particular history she knows. Her opening question and her decision to wait are as important as the eventual reassurance. They make room for an account without forcing the earlier private conversation to become hers. Fumiya is not present to receive this exchange; Kikuchi has already left. The reader's fuller knowledge does not spread backward through the group.

### 7.2 V12 names Mimimi's self-regard problem more exactly

Kikuchi later asks the living model about Nana's rescue through being a best friend's first. Mimimi does not disown Tama's affection. She trusts Tama not to leave abruptly. Her concern is that education and work may put them physically apart and that she may then struggle to keep liking herself without the nearby person who calls her first. Her conditional question about seeking a next Tama belongs to that fear. Fumiya's ensuing image of her meeting another strong support is his imagined future. Neither is an observed replacement, a selected arrangement or a prophecy that this friendship ends. [EP-C10; EP-L11]

This is stronger direct access than an observer simply labeling her dependent. It is also narrower than a declaration that dependence has made all earlier recognition inadequate. Mimimi can value what Tama has given and question whether her own use of it travels. V09 already gave an affirmative relation to the self who continues to love; V12 adds an ethically owned refusal and more explicit self-liking. No simple first discovery of self-worth or completed transition to isolation follows.

Tama is absent from the interview. She cannot be made to assent, promise availability or answer a fear she is not shown hearing. This asymmetry is a current relationship fact. It matters even though both girls later sit beside Hinami at the public event. Shared presence can preserve affiliation without certifying a new separation plan. A mature account must keep the new question available without scripting the absent participant's reassuring line.

### 7.3 Tama's independent world changes the ethical demand on the friendship

Tama also has craft, family relationships and prior bonds that cannot be reduced to being Mimimi's anchor. V08 shows liked shop work, products judged by her grandfather and an undecided succession. V12 first presents a busy worker with customers before Fumiya can obtain an interview. She remakes a damaged birthday cake, recruits labor and later acknowledges the unusual latitude of being the shop manager's daughter. Her hero ideal is learned through fiction and owned through repeated action; it can also make certain compromises feel like self-betrayal. [TH-P04–08; EP-L11]

These observations qualify a tempting division in which Tama possesses effortless inner certainty and Mimimi needs external support. Tama receives family instruction, has a practice she enjoys, accepts particular help and experiences self-binding. Her costs can move outward to staff, customers and Fumiya, just as Mimimi can absorb cost or displace it in the neglected event. Neither girl is the universal independent one or the universal person who needs care.

The defensible future question is therefore reciprocal. Can Mimimi remain significant without the same old service, and can Tama remain a valued friend without being required to regulate Mimimi wherever she goes? Can each say what help is wanted, what cannot be supplied and what ordinary pleasure they still choose? The source offers resources for that relation—particular recognition, revised invitations, waiting, actual refusals, shared humor and other wanted practices. It does not show a negotiated enduring answer. That open condition belongs inside the completed source boundary rather than outside the literary account.

Several rivals have real evidence but fail as total accounts. **Tama is only dependent** notices Mimimi's early bridge work and misses the hero statement, food adjustment, waiting and recipient-side insight. **Mimimi wants her dependent** notices role-loss sadness and misses explicit greater joy in Tama's expanded company. **Relational firstness cures rank once and for all** preserves the V02 success but cannot explain the later running story or direct V12 question. **The relation is unhealthy because value is received from another person** notices vulnerability but cannot explain the warmth, particularity and freely wanted practices without making all attachment suspect. **The girls are so close that permission is unnecessary** erases actual refusals and uneven knowledge. **V12 requires a replacement or complete independence** supplies a future the speakers do not select. The stronger account preserves the initial rescue, the reciprocity, the joy of widening and the incompletion. Their importance is not disproved when neither can be the other's entire answer. It is made more specific. The relation has earned coequal treatment because it changes both persons' possible actions, carries its own ordinary pleasures and supports a longitudinal problem no single trait or crisis can contain.

## 9. Hinami–Tama: protection can appropriate the person it values

Hinami's wish that Tama should not have to change has an intelligible object. She admires direct judgment maintained under cost, rather than merely wanting to optimize a classmate's popularity. The wish is evidence against treating every attachment as instrumental. It is also distinct from Tama's choice. In V05 Hinami can understand the tactical reason for training and acknowledge that she lacks the right to stop Tama and Fumiya, while still wanting the world to accommodate the person she values. That concession is an actual recognition of standing; later overreach does not erase it. [EP-C11; EP-L07/13]

The reverse direction prevents the dyad from becoming a portrait of Hinami's secret values. Tama selects help because Mimimi's pain matters and because her own judgment needs a receivable form. She does not exist to remain a perfect exception to Hinami's doctrine. She learns, becomes less dependent on one bridge and can use the acquired form against an action done in her name. Her relation to Hinami includes trust and concern without complete access to Hinami's formation history.

The retaliation then makes the authority problem sharper than a dispute about whether adaptation is genuine. Hinami models vulnerabilities, activates grievance and continues after Konno's defeat is already visible. In V06 she acknowledges the excess as revenge and names her attachment to Tama's correctness. The reason is hers, but the injured person's moral claim is not transferable. Tama rejects collective persecution even when its new target harmed her; she also tells Hinami she did not want that action and refuses the polished denial. The protected person supplies a counterjudgment rather than confirming the protector's self-account. [REL §8.1; HA-E15–16; TH §8.2]

This does not make Tama the sole moral author of the scene. Her new legibility and network support matter to reception. ## 20. Counterevidence the ensemble account must survive

**A group is valuable only when everyone owns the same end. REJECT.** Mimimi and Tama's different preferences, Yokoyama's unequal stake, the birthday's varied motives and the island's plural practices show real cooperation without identical ownership. Retain the need for a particular task to be intelligible and acceptable; difference alone does not make every coalition fair.

**Every useful relation is secretly instrumental. REJECT as a total account.** Instrumental origins and tactical actions are direct in several cases. They do not erase later curiosity, ordinary enjoyment, received friendship or particular concern. The countererror is automatic absolution: a valued present relation cannot retroactively authorize every means by which access arose.

**More participants always make care safer. REVISE.** Distributed learner support corrects a copied curriculum; the V12 party broadens available testimony. The same party can arrange those words toward one answer, obtain a reluctant performance and reach a refusing recipient. Plurality changes capacity and creates opportunities for correction; it is not subject consent.

**The network has learned a reliable ethical procedure by the ending. REJECT the completed claim.** There are real local permissions, refusals, stops and corrections. There is also ongoing pressure, absent representation rules and actual neglected public work. Calling all intervention equivalent would erase development; calling the last output a procedure would erase counterevidence.

**Warmth proves forgiveness, or unresolved harm makes warmth false. REJECT both.** Konno's re-entry, class production, Hinami–Tama concern and the final peer gathering can all be real while accountability remains incomplete. Akiyama's apology must be preserved at its actual strength. No universal account of forgiveness can be inferred from the absence of a light dismissal.

**The most socially skilled person should decide for everyone. REJECT.** Tama's prior fundamentals correct Fumiya, her judgment stops Hinami, Narita declines pretending to know a specific motive, Kikuchi questions her own conclusion, and residents can answer a writer differently. Expertise has domains and recipients. The question of an end and the right to give information do not follow from skill.

**Independence means having no need of the network. REJECT as a literary endpoint.** Received value, practical help and chosen company remain positive. Unbounded availability and exclusive self-worth regulation remain risks. The source supports distinctions among kinds of dependence, not an obligation to prove worth alone.

**An ending with people together closes their future. REJECT.** Present re-entry and shared play matter; the future match, occupation, support durability, school-role arrangements, pursuit/contact stops and family negotiation are unshown. Unknown review cells and later no-new-test states are not claims of source absence. An explicit open condition is part of an adequate literary account.
