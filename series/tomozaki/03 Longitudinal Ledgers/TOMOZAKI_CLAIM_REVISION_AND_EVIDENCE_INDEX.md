# Tomozaki — claim revision and evidence index through Volume 11

## 1. Responsibility and authority

This index is the canonical cumulative crosswalk for **material claim lineage, present disposition, and evidence retrieval** in the audited Japanese-light-novel corpus of *Bottom-Tier Character Tomozaki* (`弱キャラ友崎くん`). It answers four questions that the frozen volume readings should not be forced to answer alone:

1. Which local claim or question belongs to a reusable corpus-wide problem?
2. What changed between its first supportable formulation and the V11 boundary?
3. Which exact source-facing reading section preserves the evidence and historical wording?
4. Which cumulative ledger, monograph, relationship study, or specialist owns the next interpretation?

The authority order is:

1. the admitted Japanese witnesses bounded by the [source lock](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/TOMOZAKI_SOURCE_LOCK_AND_INVENTORY.md);
2. the frozen source-facing readings for the relevant volume or story-local supplement unit;
3. this cross-volume index for current claim-family disposition and retrieval;
4. the [character-state ledger](TOMOZAKI_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), [relationship-state ledger](TOMOZAKI_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), and [effort/competition/goal-ownership ledger](TOMOZAKI_EFFORT_COMPETITION_AND_GOAL_OWNERSHIP_LEDGER.md) within their bounded responsibilities;
5. later validated character, relationship, social-system, voice/register, and thematic syntheses.

This ordering does not turn an inference into direct evidence. A current family formulation may reconcile several volume claims, but the local claim remains frozen in its reading and the primary text remains evidentiary authority.

The index is governed by the [synthesis architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/TOMOZAKI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [analytical method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/TOMOZAKI_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). It is mutable current authority, not a retroactive rewrite of the V01–V11 prospective sequence.

### 1.1 What this index does not own

This file is not:

- a plot summary, scene catalogue, or replacement for any deep reading;
- a dump of every local observation merely because it has an ID;
- a character or relationship monograph;
- a systematic Japanese voice/register inventory;
- an EPUB locator manifest;
- permission to harmonize contradictions, private focalization, or supplement chronology;
- a claim that the published sequence or series ends at V11; or
- authority to make a provisional monograph override a frozen reading.

Local claims remain local when their retrieval burden is already satisfied by one source unit, their later relevance is only corroborative, or promoting them would erase the context that gives them force. Their exclusion from a corpus family is **not rejection**.

## 2. Source, chronology, and knowledge-state controls

### 2.1 Admitted evidence plane

The controlling numbered sequence is:

`V01 -> V02 -> V03 -> V04 -> V05 -> V06 -> V07 -> V08 -> V09 -> V10 -> V11`.

V06.5 and V08.5 are publication-order supplements with mixed internal chronology. They do not become simple steps in that developmental chain. Each supplemental claim retains:

- its story-local diegetic position;
- its later disclosure position for the reader/analyst;
- its narrator or focalizer;
- the distinction between reader knowledge and actor knowledge; and
- its continuity classification, including parallel or adaptation-derived exclusions.

Retrospective evidence may revise the current explanation of an earlier state without making the earlier analyst or another actor know it. The V08.5 childhood-sister evidence, for example, can establish an earlier diegetic fact while leaving the V07 sister-count conflict correctly unresolved at the V07 freeze.

### 2.2 Minimum claim-state tuple

Every corpus family below should be read as:

`<source position, diegetic position, continuity layer, focalizer, actor knowledge, reader knowledge, evidence class, revision disposition>`.

When a compact entry omits one field, the linked reading supplies source-facing record controls. In particular:

- direct self-report is not omniscient truth about the speaker;
- another character's interpretation is not interior access;
- demonstrated capacity is not identical to confidence, status, self-concept, value, or authority;
- explanation of motive or formation is not justification;
- beneficial effect does not purify method;
- accurate interpretation does not transfer disclosure, recovery, or decision authority; and
- V11 crisis state is not timeless essence.

### 2.3 Conflict classes

Before two local claims are called inconsistent, classify the apparent conflict as `DEVELOPMENTAL_CHANGE`, `FOCALIZATION_LIMIT`, `SELF_THEORY_GAP`, `KNOWLEDGE_STATE_DIFFERENCE`, `STRATEGIC_OR_AFFECTIVE_SPEECH`, `RETROSPECTIVE_REFRAMING`, `SUPPLEMENT_PLACEMENT`, `SOURCE_LAYER_DIVERGENCE`, `GENUINE_TEXTUAL_TENSION`, or `UNRESOLVED`. The family entries below name the controlling distinction where it matters.

### 3.2 Coverage classes

Every ID in that universe has one, and only one, retrieval treatment at this layer:

- **INDEXED LOAD-BEARING** — explicitly named in the claim-family or open-question crosswalk because it establishes, changes, bounds, disputes, or closes a claim reused across volumes or downstream artifacts.
- **INTENTIONALLY LOCAL** — the set difference between the complete universe and the explicit indexed manifests in §3.3. The frozen reading remains its authoritative destination. This includes local package observations, scene-bounded state, already-discharged predictions, corroborative variants, and details whose force depends on their full local context.
- **SOURCE ESCALATION PENDING** — an indexed family whose L1 route is sufficient for current synthesis but whose final wording-, attribution-, causality-, register-, or high-propagation use needs targeted L2/L3 recovery. This is an overlay, not a third disposition.

The complement rule makes selective indexing auditable: a future validator can extract the 459-ID universe from the readings, extract the explicit manifests below, and compute the intentionally local set without guessing what was forgotten. No local ID is renumbered or silently converted into a corpus ID.

## 5. Corpus-family semantics

Corpus IDs use `CRI-<DOMAIN>-NNN`. They are stable retrieval handles, not replacements for local IDs. Their dispositions have the architecture-defined meanings:

- `PRESERVE`: later evidence leaves the material formulation intact;
- `STRENGTHEN`: later evidence increases support within the stated boundary;
- `REVISE`: a bounded portion survives in changed form, with the abandoned portion named;
- `DOWNGRADE`: the claim becomes less supported, narrower, or more viewpoint-bound;
- `REJECT`: the formulation is contradicted or no longer supportable;
- `OPEN`: evidence does not yet choose among live accounts.

A chain such as `PRESERVE -> REVISE -> STRENGTHEN` describes the family through source positions; it does not overwrite the earlier row. `REJECT` applies to a formulation, not necessarily to every observation that once supported it. `OPEN` is a substantive disposition, not missing work.

## 6. Source-routing and epistemic claim families

### CRI-SRC-001 — supplements are story-local evidence, not extra main-chain steps

**Current V11 formulation.** V06.5 and V08.5 may establish earlier diegetic facts and private states, but package order cannot be substituted for story chronology and private supplemental focalization cannot be transferred to another actor.

Frozen anchors:

- `V06.5-C01` — “V06.5 is a mixed-chronology anthology and must be routed unit by unit.” (`PRESERVE`; SR06.5 §14)
- `V06.5-C19` — “Private supplemental viewpoints change analyst knowledge only where the information is not communicated in the numbered narrative.” (`PRESERVE actor/reader boundary`; SR06.5 §14)
- `V08.5-C02` — “Units 1, 3, and 6 establish an interstitial V07-V08 layer but do not continue the V08 ending.” (`STRENGTHEN specificity`; SR08.5 §14)
- `V08.5-C21` — “No V08.5 unit repairs the V08 relationship crisis or retroactively changes a numbered prospective freeze.” (`PRESERVE`; SR08.5 §14)

**Revision path.** The first supplement establishes the routing rule; the second demonstrates why it matters for family history, romance, and an unresolved numbered-volume endpoint. No later main volume discharges that method constraint.

**Counterevidence and limit.** A retrospective scene can genuinely strengthen a current causal or character model. That is not the same as inserting later reader knowledge into an earlier freeze.

**Owners.** Source lock; architecture; all cumulative ledgers. Any specialist using a supplemental state must name both diegetic and disclosure positions.

## 7. Character, agency, and form claim families

### CRI-TOM-001 — life becomes testably learnable without becoming objectively fair

**Current V11 formulation.** Tomozaki's V01 revision is an experimentally grounded commitment to causal learning, not assent that life is universally fair or a `神ゲー`. Frozen anchors:

**Owners.** Tomozaki and Hinami monographs; game/form/self-authorship specialist; effort ledger.
