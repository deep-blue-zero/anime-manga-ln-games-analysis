# Tomozaki longitudinal ledgers

## Responsibility

This directory is the canonical home for recurring cross-volume analytical questions once they become too persistent to retrieve and revise reliably inside individual volume deep readings.

The bootstrap began with no substantive ledger. After V02, one responsibility met the then-current promotion rule and is instantiated and current through V11:

- `TOMOZAKI_EFFORT_COMPETITION_AND_GOAL_OWNERSHIP_LEDGER.md` — tracks effort regimes, competition, goal origin/ownership, comparison targets, stopping conditions, self-worth coupling, collateral costs, and revision state.

The V11 architecture/role-gap review established that the original rule was applied too narrowly. The completed readings now demonstrate additional independent retrieval burdens that cannot be delegated to a terminal synthesis without flattening chronology.

## Governing architecture

The canonical promotion, evidence, revision, and completion rules now live in [`../00 Frameworks and Methods/TOMOZAKI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/TOMOZAKI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). This README is a directory guide, not an alternate architecture.

## Candidate responsibilities — not predetermined theses

Potential ledgers include:

- character state, self-concept, and development;
- relationships, intimacy, mentorship, rivalry, and boundary negotiation;
- social skill, tactics, habit formation, and internalization;
- peer-network position, status, and group norms;
- authenticity, self-presentation, masking, and performance;
- agency, refusal, rule-following, and self-authored goals;
- game, rank, optimization, objective, and rule metaphors;
- aspiration, adulthood, study, work, and future planning;
- speech/register or recurring formal/imagistic patterns when they become longitudinally meaningful.

These are candidate retrieval domains, not claims about what the series ultimately means.

## Promotion rule

Create a ledger when all of the following are true:

1. the same analytical question has recurred across multiple frozen source states;
2. later evidence can reasonably strengthen, revise, downgrade, reject, or reopen earlier claims;
3. retrieving the full state from volume readings alone has become error-prone or cumbersome;
4. the new ledger has one clear analytical responsibility not already owned by another artifact.

Avoid creating overlapping ledgers merely because a topic sounds important.

## Current-boundary decision

The former decision not to promote a second ledger is superseded by the V11 architecture-remediation audit. It confused integration inside a short final-synthesis candidate with cumulative responsibility. Character state, relationship state, claim/evidence lineage, and social-atmosphere systems recur across enough frozen source states to require independent homes. Japanese voice/register also requires an independent layer, but its mature backfill must use targeted source checks rather than extrapolation from English summaries or remembered phrasing.

This decision does not invalidate the original effort/competition ledger and does not authorize rewriting any frozen volume reading.
