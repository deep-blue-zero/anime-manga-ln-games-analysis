# Tomozaki — longitudinal character-state ledger through Volume 11

## 1. Responsibility, authority, and non-responsibilities

This ledger is the canonical cumulative layer for retrieving **character state across time** in the audited Japanese light-novel corpus of *Bottom-Tier Character Tomozaki* (`弱キャラ友崎くん`). It exists because a terminal profile cannot safely carry the historical distinctions the sequential readings established: acquired competence can precede confidence; public status can survive while a governing reason fails; the same outward behavior can be submission in one state and owned care in another; and a crisis self-description cannot be projected backward as an eternal trait.

The ledger has five responsibilities:

1. preserve the sequence in which each material capacity, self-theory, value, relationship-conditioned mode, and failure pattern became supportable;
2. distinguish stable tendency from developmental state, role performance, recipient effect, stress response, and retrospective reinterpretation;
3. keep character self-description separate from demonstrated behavior and from another character's theory;
4. maintain explicit revision dispositions and counterevidence rather than silently replacing an earlier model with the V11 endpoint; and
5. hand bounded state to character monographs, relationship analysis, specialist synthesis, and future prospective reading.

It is not a plot synopsis, a substitute for the immutable prospective freezes, a relationship-state ledger, a diagnosis, a moral ranking, or a simulation guide. It does not establish post-V11 outcomes. The preserved volume readings remain authoritative for what the analyst could support at each historical boundary. This ledger is authoritative only for cumulative **state routing and revision** through the current source lock.

The current character monographs are useful retrieval aids, but they are downstream, retrospective, and provisionally subject to architecture remediation. They did not supply evidence to this ledger and do not outrank the sequential readings. Where a monograph and a frozen reading appear to differ, return first to the relevant frozen reading and then amend this ledger transparently if needed.

## 2. Chronology and evidence-plane control

### 2.1 Main-chain boundary

The controlling prospective sequence is:

`V01 -> V02 -> V03 -> V04 -> V05 -> V06 -> V07 -> V08 -> V09 -> V10 -> V11`.

Each numbered reading freezes its own horizon before the next main volume. Later evidence may revise the **current** model but may not make an earlier actor, reader, or analyst know what had not yet been disclosed.

### 2.2 Supplemental placement

V06.5 and V08.5 are mixed-chronology anthologies. Their units enter a character history only at the supported story-local position.

| Supplemental layer | Legitimate use in this ledger | Prohibited use |
|---|---|---|
| V06.5 | middle-school Hinami; early and V01-era Kikuchi formation; V03-camp private states; V05-V06 Mimimi development; V06-concurrent Yuzu; end-of-summer Mizusawa; the single post-V06 Mimimi continuation | treating package order as chronology; giving private viewpoint knowledge to other actors; rewriting V01-V06 freezes |
| V08.5 | childhood/middle-school Hinami and the formation of `NO NAME`; V07-V08 couple and Mimimi baselines; Rena's pre-V08 formation; V08-threshold Kikuchi state | treating the alternate karaoke or adapted VR bonus as main continuity; using interstitial stories to repair the V08 ending; asserting Nagisa's fate before V10 |

Retrospective evidence can establish an earlier **diegetic fact** while remaining later **analyst knowledge**. For example, V08.5 establishes that Aoi had two younger sisters in childhood, but it does not alter what the V07 analyst could responsibly conclude from the then-unresolved sister-count discrepancy.

### 2.3 Three clocks

Every state claim should be readable on three clocks:

| Clock | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| diegetic clock | When did the behavior or state occur? | Hinami's basketball and first nanashi encounter occur before V01. |
| disclosure clock | When did the text make the evidence available? | The basketball/`NO NAME` chain enters the corpus through V08.5. |
| actor-knowledge clock | Who knew it at the time? | Hinami's private retrospective does not become Tomozaki's knowledge merely because the reader has it. |

This separation is mandatory for family history, private romantic states, diaries, fiction, and any third-party reconstruction.

## 3. Character-state and epistemic schema

### 3.1 Epistemic classes

This ledger uses the method's classes:

- **DIRECT** — explicit statement or unambiguous action, bounded to what the statement or action actually establishes;
- **CORROBORATED INFERENCE** — multiple observations or consequences support the model with weak counterevidence;
- **INTERPRETIVE HYPOTHESIS** — useful but meaningfully contestable reconstruction;
- **VALUE JUDGMENT** — explicit normative judgment resting on separated descriptive evidence;
- **OPEN / UNRESOLVED** — evidence is insufficient, conflicting, focalized, or beyond the source boundary.

A self-report is DIRECT evidence that a character presently uses that description. It is not automatically DIRECT proof that the description exhausts the person. A fictional character's interiority is DIRECT evidence of the writer's constructed model, not testimony by the modeled person.

### 3.2 State dimensions

For each major character, the ledger tracks only dimensions supported by evidence:

| Dimension | Operational question |
|---|---|
| stable tendency | What pattern recurs across multiple contexts and survives meaningful counterpressure? |
| developmental state | What is newly available, newly chosen, newly fragile, or currently suspended at this boundary? |
| competence | What can the character demonstrably do? |
| confidence | What action can the character expect or tolerate under uncertainty? |
| status | What access, legitimacy, centrality, or vulnerability does the social field assign? |
| self-concept | How does the character presently classify or value the self? |
| agency | Can the character select, refuse, revise, disclose, and bear an action rather than merely execute it? |
| value / governing reason | What currently makes an action worth doing or a result count? |
| relationship-conditioned mode | Which capacities, defenses, registers, or values change with recipient? |
| stress state | What appears under shame, singular stakes, conflict, loss, or overload? |
| continuity status | Is this a main-chain state, a retrospectively placed fact, a parallel exclusion, or an open boundary? |

### 3.3 Revision dispositions

- **PRESERVE** — wording and scope remain adequate.
- **STRENGTHEN** — later evidence supports the same bounded claim more strongly.
- **REVISE** — the core observation remains, but its cause, scope, or formulation changes.
- **DOWNGRADE** — possible but less secure or more local than previously believed.
- **REJECT** — materially contradicted or shown to rest on a false binary.
- **OPEN** — not yet adjudicable.

### 3.4 Anti-flattening rules

The following inferences are prohibited unless a later admitted source independently earns them:

1. V11 crisis behavior is not a timeless essence.
2. Retained competence is not equivalent to an available reason to use it.
3. A behavior learned from another person is not therefore false or unowned.
4. A voluntary behavior is not therefore self-authored at every goal level.
5. A correct interpretation is not therefore authority to disclose, expose, cure, or choose for its subject.
6. Public warmth is not proof of an unguarded interior; controlled behavior is not proof of absent feeling.
7. Absence of a retest does not strengthen a claim.
8. Relationship-specific behavior does not automatically generalize to other recipients.
9. A successful intervention does not erase collateral cost or establish consent.
10. An unresolved endpoint remains unresolved even if a likely continuation is easy to imagine.

### 6.5 Current V11 boundary

Tomozaki's available competencies remain broad: elite play, planning, public communication, negotiation by proxy, friendship, romance repair, and reflective revision. His current **deployment state** is unstable after Hinami invalidates the unique credential on which he rested his command. This is not a return to V01: the peer network, girlfriend, professional evidence, learned repertoire, and history of owned choice remain real. It is a new crisis in which a plural social self is temporarily subordinated to a failed story of singular necessity.

Current open variables are recovery of an owned reason to play; school return; a non-savior support stance toward Hinami; professional route and exit governance; privacy and allocation rules with Kikuchi; and tolerance for being valuable without being uniquely necessary. (`DR11 §§5.1, 8-9`)
