## 1. Bounded cumulative responsibility

This ledger preserves **attested Japanese forms and their changing analytical uses** across the completed light-novel corpus. Its responsibility is to distinguish who says or thinks a term, to whom, at which developmental boundary, through which narrative layer, and with what locally supported force. ## 2. Evidence and temporal controls

Every row carries a recoverable tuple:

`〈volume/story-local time, narrative layer, speaker/focalizer, recipient/access, local form, supported force, revision/limit〉`.

**Attested local form** is stronger than an invented reconstruction but narrower than a stable speech habit. A speaker can use a blunt imperative in one emergency, polite request grammar in an intimate choice, and a nickname during a painful separation. None alone establishes how that person always talks, whether they are truthful, or whether their conduct is justified. This ledger therefore makes no frequency claims about particles, honorifics, self-pronouns, hesitation, or dialect.

Three narrative boundaries are especially important:

- **Narration versus spoken speech.** Tomozaki's internal commentary frequently interprets the same encounter that his dialogue only partly expresses. The distinction affects what another character knows. JVL-04's as-a-person qualification is not spoken to Haruka.
- **Retrospective interiority versus complete reported transcript.** V11's disclosure-framed family history changes from third-person Aoi-centered narration into first-person interiority. Reader access exceeds what can automatically be certified as verbatim speech. JVL-08 retains both modes.
- **Authored fiction versus its model.** Kikuchi's Alcia dialogue and inner scenes remain an authored hypothesis even where later evidence supports parts of it. JVL-13 cannot be quoted as Hinami's testimony.

The main developmental chain now reaches V01→V12; §13 owns the new V12 attestation and current adjudication, while the earlier rows retain their stated historical scene boundaries. V06.5/V08.5 retain unit-local chronology; their private voices cannot be transplanted into an earlier numbered actor's knowledge. V08.5's NO NAME sequence precedes the initial meeting by its own narration. V06.5's Kikuchi diary is image-only and its existing reading remains the evidence owner; this operation did not re-OCR or re-view those images and adds no purported exact diary quotation. Parallel/adaptation-derived supplemental material supplies no mainline speech evidence here.
