# Tomozaki — Japanese voice, register, and key terms through V11

## 1. Bounded cumulative responsibility

This ledger preserves **attested Japanese forms and their changing analytical uses** across the completed light-novel corpus. Its responsibility is to distinguish who says or thinks a term, to whom, at which developmental boundary, through which narrative layer, and with what locally supported force. It gives later character and specialist work a source-grounded language spine without pretending that a few decisive utterances constitute a complete idiolect.

The governing [method](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/TOMOZAKI_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) and [architecture](../00%20Frameworks%20and%20Methods/TOMOZAKI_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) require this home because wording-sensitive claims recur independently of plot chronology. The [claim index](TOMOZAKI_CLAIM_REVISION_AND_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md) owns claim-family revision; the [character-state](TOMOZAKI_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md), [relationship-state](TOMOZAKI_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_LEDGER.md), [social-system](TOMOZAKI_SOCIAL_ATMOSPHERE_AND_GROUP_SYSTEMS_LEDGER.md), and [effort/goal](TOMOZAKI_EFFORT_COMPETITION_AND_GOAL_OWNERSHIP_LEDGER.md) ledgers own their respective cumulative states. This file complements those owners rather than absorbing them.

The [targeted source-locator audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_TARGETED_SOURCE_LOCATOR_AUDIT.md) supplies the witness checks, exact package-member/P convention, and **LOC** identifiers cited here. All thirteen hashes/byte sizes and CRC checks passed. Independent parent review examined source context across all twelve escalation queues and ten supplemental records, including the V08 refusal call; it also inspected and reran the 92-marker verification. Two recipient corrections from that review are incorporated in JVL-02 and JVL-18. **Independent source/semantic review: PASS.** This does not certify repository publication or close the literary questions retained below.

**What “voice” means here:** literary prose voice, represented dialogue, written messages, and explicitly narrated delivery. No audio performance was admitted. Descriptions such as trembling, teasing, or matter-of-fact concern what the prose represents, not an independently heard actor's performance. Quoted Japanese is minimal diagnostic wording, not a transcript; English glosses are analytical paraphrases, not an official translation.

## 2. Evidence and temporal controls

Every row carries a recoverable tuple:

`〈volume/story-local time, narrative layer, speaker/focalizer, recipient/access, local form, supported force, revision/limit〉`.

**Attested local form** is stronger than an invented reconstruction but narrower than a stable speech habit. A speaker can use a blunt imperative in one emergency, polite request grammar in an intimate choice, and a nickname during a painful separation. None alone establishes how that person always talks, whether they are truthful, or whether their conduct is justified. This ledger therefore makes no frequency claims about particles, honorifics, self-pronouns, hesitation, or dialect.

Three narrative boundaries are especially important:

- **Narration versus spoken speech.** Tomozaki's internal commentary frequently interprets the same encounter that his dialogue only partly expresses. The distinction affects what another character knows. JVL-04's as-a-person qualification is not spoken to Haruka.
- **Retrospective interiority versus complete reported transcript.** V11's disclosure-framed family history changes from third-person Aoi-centered narration into first-person interiority. Reader access exceeds what can automatically be certified as verbatim speech. JVL-08 retains both modes.
- **Authored fiction versus its model.** Kikuchi's Alcia dialogue and inner scenes remain an authored hypothesis even where later evidence supports parts of it. JVL-13 cannot be quoted as Hinami's testimony.

The main developmental chain remains V01→V11. V06.5/V08.5 retain unit-local chronology; their private voices cannot be transplanted into an earlier numbered actor's knowledge. V08.5's NO NAME sequence precedes the initial meeting by its own narration. V06.5's Kikuchi diary is image-only and its existing reading remains the evidence owner; this operation did not re-OCR or re-view those images and adds no purported exact diary quotation. Parallel/adaptation-derived supplemental material supplies no mainline speech evidence here.

## 12. Reconciliation, review, and maintenance

The ledger contains twenty-four stable **JVL** rows covering all seven principals, with supplementary conceptual and cross-speaker retrieval tables. Its source dependency discharges the twelve defined escalation queues at the level of located, attributed evidence. It does not substitute for the independent longitudinal convergence audit, and it does not open specialist or full-series readiness on its own.

Required current propagation follows the locator audit's **COR-01–COR-06**: preserve the school-year tension; narrow mixed-person family-history attribution; separate Haruka-facing speech from the unspoken liking qualification; avoid assigning the phone disconnect; identify Tomozaki's punishment reformulation; and avoid categorical post-V11 ignorance claims unsupported by particular communication evidence. The [longitudinal reconciliation audit](../08%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/TOMOZAKI_LONGITUDINAL_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT.md) owns the propagation receipt. The earlier prospective readings remain unchanged.

For a later admitted source or new language-dependent claim:

1. verify the witness and preserve the prior boundary;
2. identify whether the needed observation is speech, thought, narration, writing, or represented delivery;
3. recover the shortest sufficient exact passage with local context;
4. add a state-indexed row or revise a current formulation while retaining its earlier supported scope;
5. propagate any changed attribution, chronology, or modality to affected cumulative owners;
6. retain genuinely OPEN intent, consent, motive, and source tensions; and
7. review any proposed habitual-register claim against multiple recipients, states, and counterexamples before promotion.

No reconstruction/simulation model is created by this ledger. No speech style is a lie detector, no exact quotation is evidence beyond its scene, and no current boundary becomes a completed character arc merely because the ledger has reached V11.
