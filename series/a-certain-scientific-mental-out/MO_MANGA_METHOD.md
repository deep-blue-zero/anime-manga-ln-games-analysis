# A Certain Scientific Mental Out — analytical method

## 1. Adaptation and source-unit contract

This method adapts the [generic manga method](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) to bounded acquired sequence with Japanese V01–05 metadata available. Pair it with [MO_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md](MO_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). The imported witnesses and publisher identity establish project scope; no actual plot, character, arc, chronology or visual design has been inspected. Source admission and run authorization remain separate future gates.

Five observed Japanese volumes establish an available sequence, not a complete publication endpoint. The English project title is the established inventory alias; no licensed English edition has been inspected. Publisher metadata identifies a distinct spin-off with its own creator and volume sequence. Keep its knowledge and relationship states local even if a name is shared with Astral Buddy or Railgun. Similar authorship or characters do not prove sequel order, direct continuity or equivalent access to information.

After admission, map actual EPUB spine/image order, printed pages, chapter boundaries, spreads, covers and extras. Read every page in source order, including silent panels and transitions. Chapter or coherent causal sequences are working units, with complete volume reconciliation as an acquisition boundary. A file is a container, not a reason to compress all its narrative into a synopsis. If a long chapter crosses sessions, preserve exact coverage and the complete argument in progress; don't declare an unfinished unit complete.

## 2. Own-work first and franchise knowledge

Maintain separate disclosure, depicted-time and reader/character-knowledge frontiers for this manga. No source in another franchise root is a default prerequisite or canonical override. Record prior familiarity/exposure as an analyst limitation; it cannot supply motives, hidden identities, future events or corrected chronology. Preserve the source's deliberate anonymity until its own disclosure, even when the analyst recognizes a figure.

Shared names, creators, setting or event labels justify a counterpart question only. A cross-title bridge requires independently admitted, directly inspected evidence on both sides, exact edition/chronology selectors and an explicit comparison scope. Freeze each work's prospective findings first; later comparison is a retrospective layer, not a rewrite of earlier knowledge. If the source explicitly depends on an unread event, record the local reference and bounded incompleteness rather than invent the event. Different manga or adaptations govern their own form and disclosure even where they retell an event.

## 3. Page, panel and Japanese evidence

Inspect full-page composition, panel sequence, gutters, size and density, page turns, juxtaposition, repeated composition, focal distance, gaze, gesture, lettering and speech/thought attribution as actually visible. Distinguish a visual observation from inferred emotion. A close-up, background effect or speed line has no fixed universal meaning; explain how sequence and context make it consequential. Silent pages cannot be excluded by OCR. OCR is a retrieval aid and must be checked against the page for consequential wording or attribution.

Analyze source-language address, person-reference, register, particles, ellipsis, hesitation, narration and sound effects where they change interpretation. Identify speaker, thinker, listener and the social/informational occasion; preserve alternative readings when Japanese subject omission or balloon placement leaves attribution uncertain. Use brief original anchors and labeled working translations rather than dumping dialogue. No official translation or adaptation is admitted by default. Typography/colour claims belong only to the actual witness inspected, not presumed magazine, monochrome or screen versions.

Follow the [medium/lane policy](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md): manga's still-image lane must actually see the pages. Extracted text alone cannot establish spatial composition, expressions or visual timing. The current bootstrap has inspected no narrative images or AV; any future adaptation needs a separately authorized source track and relevant medium method.

## 4. Causal, psychological and ethical argument

Write a coherent factual account of initiating conditions, acts/decisions, alternatives, resistance, consequences and what connects them. Depicted succession is not automatically causation, and a character explanation is not a verified world rule. Distinguish `OBSERVATION`, `ATTRIBUTED_REPORT`, `INFERENCE`, `HYPOTHESIS` and `VALUE_JUDGMENT`. Separate narrator/character/analyst standards and avoid inferring author endorsement from depiction.

For any claim about intention, testimony, influence, private knowledge or institutional position, distinguish who asserts it, what the panels independently show, who can know it and what remains an inference. This is a prospective evidence discipline, not a claim about the work's powers or plot. Preserve visual and verbal contradictions and uncertainty about attribution instead of explaining them with remembered franchise lore.

Every major interpretation needs exact source anchors, an explicit reasoning chain, a credible counterreading or falsifying condition, state scope and uncertainty. Keep the argument as connected prose with enough evidence to assess it; a list of themes or trait adjectives is not analysis. Negative cases and alternative motives remain visible. Do not fit the whole work to a predetermined moral or franchise-level thesis.

Character accounts name subjects and distinguish action, self-report, motivation inference, stress, performance/disguise, habit and development. Relationships are directed A→B and B→A, with asymmetry and knowledge routes; do not substitute “closer” for an explanation of whose trust, obligation, expectation or behavior changed. Capture ordinary initiative, humor, practical habits, annoyance, preferences, repair and downtime during reading. Crisis scenes cannot stand for daily personality, and gaps in inspection cannot establish “never.”

## 5. State, evidence and revision

Use source alias `MO-JP-Vnn::<native-image-member>@<spine-ordinal>::page/<printed-or-image-coordinate>/panel/<index>` after binding the witness and convention. Spine/image/panel indexes are one-based; define panel order for ambiguous layouts in the first admitted locator map. Unnumbered pages use native member and image ordinal; never invent printed pages. Chapter labels and two-page spread sides are additional coordinates. Panel quotations/wording include balloon or text-region anchors when needed. Preserve immutable aliases and private source receipts outside public Git.

Each claim selects work, edition/language, source disclosure horizon, depicted time (or unresolved), continuity/frame, focalizer, actor knowledge and relationship direction. Depicted recollection, dream or hypothetical is typed only if source-supported. Maintain append-only `MO-Snnn` states and `MO-Cnnn` claims; the retrieval path is mature claim → exact claim version → full reading section → page/panel/native source. Distributed patterns need the necessary independent instances and counterinstances, not one convenient citation.

Use `PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN`. Keep prior formulation, evidence, reasoning and horizon visible. Classify apparent contradiction as attribution error, knowledge difference, unreliable report, temporal change, edition/translation variance, cross-title divergence or unresolved inconsistency before reconciling it. Later knowledge revises a claim, not the bytes of a frozen prospective reading. Predictions record their pre-exposure boundary and retain failures at adjudication.

## 6. Acceptance and handoff

Each future reading delivers exact coverage/witness receipt; entering state/exposure; full factual causal reconstruction; sustained interpretation with visual/Japanese evidence; named character, directional relationship and knowledge changes; ordinary-life cases; counterreadings; claims and exit state; and a transaction listing every required cumulative/source update or explicit reviewed/no-supported-change outcome. No fixed length cap governs the argument. Locator tables, checkpoint synopses and output receipts do not replace the full reading.

Reinspect load-bearing pages and verify page completeness, semantic attribution and any OCR ambiguity before acceptance. Missing or unread pages block affected completion claims. A chapter can be provisionally interpreted while a specific gap is repaired, but its unresolved influence remains visible to downstream work. Full-volume reconciliation checks that all chapters, transitions, extras and silent pages received explicit coverage decisions; a highest-number frontier cannot conceal holes.

Apply handoff §15.6 before semantic acceptance: sample opening, substantial middle and concluding/exit passages, extending to major relations, for stable known names, clear pronouns, purposeful role labels and correct speaker/thinker/recipient attribution while preserving concealment and knowledge boundaries. Record scope and classify supported findings as justified role reference, source-required anonymity, knowledge-boundary-required, unnecessary genericization, ambiguous referent or incorrect identity/attribution. Never certify semantics through counts or automatic substitution.

The receiver verifies full delivered files, source/knowledge boundary, target identity, hashes and drift. No partial preview or summary may replace maintained substantial work. The eventual retrospective and franchise comparison layers return to decisive sources and keep earlier prospective results retrievable. Reconstruction follows its separate evidence/model/evaluation contract, not familiarity or a monograph alone.
