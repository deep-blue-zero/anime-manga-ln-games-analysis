# A resource, a doctrine and a choice are not the same rule

The castle actually needs some form of sustaining light: E11 stages pre-Lara dimming, Rigmor and Rowan's productive example, Rowan's donation to Lara, and sleeping relatives; E12 Lisa reports gradual recovery after Lara's changed light (`SYL-W0041`, `SYL-W0044`, `SYL-W0050`). These observations justify taking family risk seriously. They do not establish that every princess must obtain light from the same object, that blood sacrifice is necessary, or that the king owns a daughter's future. The difference between material constraint and official explanation is the series' causal and ethical core.

| Speaker/rule | What is observed | What remains an assertion |
|---|---|---|
| Rowan: royal duty, ban, cleansing blood | Court's need, his light gift, Rigmor's loss, his explicit enactment of anti-human law and E12 order | Humans alone caused all catastrophe; only piercing a loved person produces proper light (`SYL-W0037`-`SYL-W0043`, `SYL-W0048`). |
| Grace: transformation, revival, human true-love route | Medicine and altered body, collected foam, donor light, court sleep, depleted fish form | Her route is uniquely sufficient or morally neutral; exact dosage, expiry and light formula (`SYL-W0042`, `SYL-W0044`-`SYL-W0045`). |
| Lisa: sea-world love, human blindness | Historical research, imitation medicine, her sight/interpretation of blade, later different light | No human can understand; all royal light must have a sea-world object or prior-princess pattern (`SYL-C0030`-`SYL-C0031`, `SYL-W0049`). |
| Lara/Mari: a future with care | Refusal of victim selection, Mari's approach without sight, reported death/revival, unusual light and partial recovery | That one relation alone is a reproducible magical cure or that an undetermined romance has been certified (`SYL-C0059`-`SYL-C0061`). |

Law is historically authored. Young Grace and Rigmor dream of the land before Rowan's prohibition; a royal elder pressures princesses while the castle is already dim. A human rejects Grace and orders soldiers; Rigmor arrives and dies amid a blade event; Grace later implicates herself. Rowan makes the ban and exiles her (`SYL-C0053`-`SYL-C0054`). Trauma explains why he seeks protection; a mixed causal chain and Rigmor's final counsel prevent the law from presenting itself as nature. Lisa's forced custodianship and hated human legs show how protection of the family can become domination of particular family members.

E12 is a strong falsifier of universal victim necessity, not a full magical manual. Lara does not execute Rowan's chosen stabbing; altered light appears, Mari reportedly revives, and the family gradually recovers. The weapon, fish-like maw, falling Mari and light are adjacent in sampled imagery, but exact strike order, transfer and sufficient conditions remain `SYL-D0002` debts. The same discipline applies to ecological effects: E10 public authorities restrict boating and E12 news measures improvement after six months, not a scientifically isolated supernatural cause or zero residue (`SYL-W0040`, `SYL-W0051`). Grace's spell weakening does not adjudicate her present body (`SYL-W0052`).

The political result is a future conversation, not abolition by miracle. Lara holds a returned ring and proposes repeated dialogue about human-mermaid life when the court awakens; Lisa expects the king's hatred to persist. A liberal reading may overstate Lara's individual autonomy if the material family need remains; a dynastic reading may overstate necessity if it reintroduces coerced blood despite counterevidence. The defensible synthesis holds survival and consent simultaneously: the family deserves aid and an account of its danger, while no person is merely its fuel.
