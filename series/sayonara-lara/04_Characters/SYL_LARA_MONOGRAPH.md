# Lara: from assigned sacrifice to scarred agency

## Governing account

Lara's development is neither an escape from attachment nor discovery of the one correctly classified lover. She begins with an expansive curiosity about humans before the original prince becomes its romantic focus (E01 S03-S05; `SYL-C0001`). She is then made answerable for a family catastrophe and revived through others' designs. Her culminating choice is to want the world and the people in it *without* accepting a sovereign or a witch's test for what that desire must produce. The E12 formulation joins a very particular address to Mari with a general commitment to tomorrow; it refuses disappearance and coerced victimhood, not affection (`SYL-C0058`-`SYL-C0060`). Her departure after six months tests whether this agency survives beyond a rescue household, and exposes its cost to Mari (`SYL-C0062`).

## Developmental states and contrary pressure

| Boundary | What Lara can newly do or say | What she still cannot command |

| E01-E02 | Names human curiosity, rescues a prince, survives foam through an externally arranged second life; enters Mari's home | Her voice, body, safe route home, family information and survival terms are governed by others. The ring signifies both home and authorization (`SYL-C0001`-`SYL-C0011`). |
| E03-E05 | Receives practical care, names Mari a friend, tries school and work, acquires shopping/food/custard competence | She misreads social permission, depends on Mari's household, and treats a prince-like image as a possible solution. | E06-E08 | Confronts Lisa's medicine and incompatible light doctrines, resists exclusion, admits fear of death, returns from the sea/land choice and promises cake | Temporary medicine relief does not resolve mortality. Her growing intimacy with Luca and friendship with Mari are different relationships; E07 blade memory interrupts easy romantic salvation (`SYL-C0031`-`SYL-C0039`). |
| E09-E11 | Seeks mutual lover status with Luca, struggles with his pace, receives the E10 royal blade command and E11 generational history | She cannot set the blade's motion or make the father/Grace's restoration programs harmless. Her `愛する者` is not an exclusive name supplied by the elders (`SYL-C0044`, `SYL-C0050`-`SYL-C0056`). |
| E12 | Refuses to stab, revises a wish to disappear into a wish to live, helps bring Mari back, accepts an altered light and future dialogue, leaves on still-painful legs | She cannot guarantee court conversion, permanent survival or Mari's comfort with departure. The train is a choice under pain, not a certificate of self-sufficiency (`SYL-C0058`-`SYL-C0062`). |

The first attraction matters. The original prince is not merely a mistake the story later corrects with a “real” object: Lara's interest follows older curiosity, and the ship tragedy teaches the danger of making another person's recognition the condition of existence. Luca resembles the earlier princely image but is a distinct modern person who attends to Lara as Lara. He praises her in E07, yet asks for pace in E09 and later talks about their actual dating and breakup in E12. These experiences contribute to Lara's understanding without establishing exclusive heterosexuality or rendering Mari a substitute romance (`SYL-R0039`, `SYL-R0042`, `SYL-R0057`).

## Guilt, competence and self-erasure

Grace's E02 blame and rescue script give Lara a seemingly moral way to carry the family's suspended lives. Rowan makes the duty explicit: her unique light should rebuild the dynasty, even if a loved human must be pierced (E06/E10, `SYL-C0029`, `SYL-W0039`). Lara's readiness to sacrifice herself can look noble, but in E12 she calls her own disappearance a way nobody will be hurt—repeating the older catastrophe's premise that her life is negotiable. Mari objects using neither royal doctrine nor a demand for Lara to stay forever: they promised boxing and cake, and Lara's interruptions made solitude less absolute (E12 S07). Lara's subsequent correction is exact: she is glad she revived, however foolish and troublesome, and wants tomorrow. It is an acceptance of a fallible person rather than proof she no longer imposes on anyone.

Her learned competence matters because the wish to leave is not wholly abstract. E04's purchase, E05's bakery skill, E07's encounter with art and a modern date, and E08's promised custard cake give small means of participating. None alone proves she can afford independent housing or navigate every institution. The six-month jump with bags and a train supplies the later decision but elides much preparation. A reading that calls her departure pure emancipation needs that omission as counterevidence, as well as Mari's explicit loneliness.

## Body and chosen identity

Lara's body moves from mermaid to painful human legs, to foam, revival, temporary reversion and threatened blade. The ring follows these transitions as a portable royal claim, not a device that settles who she is. In E10 Rowan describes human legs as contaminating beauty; in E12 he claims the spear removes medicine's curse and presents mermaid form as proper (`SYL-L0074`, `SYL-L0084`). Lara rejects the aesthetic judgment by recalling irreplaceable human days. When Lisa returns the ring in the coda, Lara can carry family identity without promising obedience to its law. The last line about the still-painful scar—`このキズが私`—refuses a spotless makeover. Walking is a repeated practice in a changed body, not a magical proof that pain and lifespan have vanished (`SYL-F0065`).

## Speech, relationships and the strongest contrary reading

Lara repeatedly gives declarations sooner than the other person can assimilate them. Luca describes abrupt dating and breakup; Mari calls out sudden arrivals and departures. Her direct sincerity is a strength, but it can move the relational burden to those who must accommodate it. The finale's `私と生きたい` is strong person-directed language, yet the concrete arrangement Lara chooses is departure. This creates a genuine discrepancy, not a secret marriage or a betrayal proved by one line. The dyad study tracks each direction separately.

The strongest counteraccount says that the end merely replaces a family demand with the privilege of a heroine who can leave after others pay the costs: Grace expends herself, Rowan sacrifices light, Lisa waits a century, Mari dies and revives, and Mari is lonely. That charge is not answered by invoking autonomy as a trump card. Lara does acknowledge the family future and promises repeated dialogue rather than erasing them; she affirms Mari's shared-life wish while choosing to test independent residence. But the story leaves the material mechanism of her survival and the practical terms of return unsettled. Her agency is therefore achieved as an ongoing responsibility, not absolution (`SYL-C0061`-`SYL-C0062`).
