# Lisa: duty performed in the despised body

Lisa is Lara's sister and Rowan's delegated keeper of an unfinished restoration. Her human identity, Shiomi Lisa (`潮見リサ`), is not a momentary disguise: she and Minase Kōta spend decades navigating documents, research, captivity and human institutions after displacement. Kōta finds Lisa in a human exhibition, while she recalls first finding and valuing him; their reciprocal “finding” coexists with hierarchy and his professed allegiance (E06 S04/S07-S10; `SYL-C0028`, `SYL-R0027`). Lisa has genuine knowledge of medicine's limits and real trauma from species-targeted capture. Neither experience certifies her universal pronouncement that a human-mermaid relation is impossible (`SYL-C0030`).

The contradiction is embodied. Rowan assigns her long custodial waiting, then condemns the human legs through which she has survived. Lisa herself uses human `仮定と検証`, the work of Horikoshi Seizō and a partially effective imitation medicine to keep Lara alive (`SYL-C0029`-`SYL-C0031`). She insists in E08 that princess light necessarily arises from deep love for the sea world, while Grace prescribes human true love; both speak with vested interests. Lisa's claim has a historical basis in the family and her long vigilance, but she cannot turn that sample into an exceptionless law. Her E09-E10 pursuit retrieves Lara and also ends in an attempt to sever human matters, leaving the distinction between rescue and control active (`SYL-C0048`-`SYL-C0052`).

She is not simply Rowan's mouthpiece. In E08 she gives Mari the temporary medicine and allows Lara a limited land choice under threat of death; this is protective and still constraining. In E12 she tells Mari the blade and true light are invisible to humans and condemns their supposed failure to understand, yet points her toward Lara in the mist. She does not command Mari to remain safely away. Wounded Kōta voices objection to making Lara pierce a beloved because he too loves someone, pressuring Lisa's abstraction from beside her (`SYL-R0056`). Human incapacity to see supernatural light remains untested as universal, but Mari's decision proves the ethical mistake of equating such vision with understanding.

Six months later Lisa calls Lara's light unlike earlier princesses and reports gradual family recovery. She expects Rowan's hatred not to vanish, and Lara promises repeated conversation with the court. Lisa then admits she had feared Lara's change and returns the royal ring (`SYL-C0061`). The act recognizes a sister who may carry an inheritance without obeying its old rule. It is not a categorical renunciation of every anti-human conviction, a medicine cure, or proof the sleeping court has awakened. Lisa remains accountable for earlier coercion and fear, and Rowan's future is not hers to settle. Her monograph thus describes a partial change in how she handles Lara's autonomy, not a conversion cutscene. Performance and Kōta's ultimate health remain bounded by missing channels and insufficient ending evidence (`SYL-D0001`-`SYL-D0002`).

## Survival work and a doctrine she cannot live by

E06 stretches Lisa across the centuries that Lara sleeps. As a child she values an anglerfish-like companion whose light finds useful worlds in darkness; when humans imprison and exhibit Lisa as a seductive monster, Kōta uses a human form to find her, and each names the other as the one who first “found” them. She refuses his claim that his service can end after the rescue. This is not a disposable tool relationship, although his continuing `リサ様` and pledge to follow her everywhere preserve an asymmetry. At the old castle Lisa drinks a painful or scarce medicine to continue and refuses Kōta's suggestion to turn back. Her drive predates the latest direct conflict with Lara and survives many failures (E06 S04–S10; `SYL-C0028`, `SYL-L0040`–`L0045`).

The later museum role is especially revealing. Humans once controlled a public account of Lisa's body in a cage; she now explains aquatic migration to children under her own human name, Shiomi Lisa. The trout's return to its natal river makes homecoming a visible public subject, even as she lives on land because the old home cannot sustain her without another's light. She can teach inside a human institution, use identity documents for Kōta, wager on Horikoshi Seizō's scientific `仮定と検証`, and preserve an imperfect medicine through generations. She then speaks at his grave as though his short life produced only a fake, although that product delays their foaming. Knowledge of a human's utility has not become recognition of his full personhood. Her contradiction is not “she is secretly open-minded” but that her inherited ethical category fails to describe the practices sustaining her (E06 S03/S10–S13; `SYL-C0030`–`C0031`, `SYL-L0046`–`L0048`).

Her mission is both chosen and delegated. Rowan orders her to wait for Lara, and she does; he calls the legs that made waiting possible ugly. Yet Lisa independently steals or secures medicine, recruits a scientist, carries a human cover identity, searches the lake, reads Grace's mirror-location clue, judges when to approach Mari, and at E08 offers the last available temporary treatment rather than simply seize Lara. These are costly decisions, not the reflexes of an obedient daughter. They also keep Lara under a singular interpretation: her light belongs to the family. E05's “you need neither that house nor that girl” and E06's “why won't you illuminate us?” show how fear can recast a younger sister's independent attachments as delay or betrayal (E05 S09–S10; E06 S09–S14; E08 S03–S08; `SYL-C0025`, `C0031`, `C0038`–`C0040`).

## How she responds when the evidence resists her

Her treatment of Kōta is the strongest internal countercase to treating her as nothing but a family functionary. She remembers him as a distinctive creature who could locate worlds in darkness before a human body is useful; after he rescues her she refuses the notion that his task is finished and names him as the light that continues to illuminate her. Yet the later public documents and his `どこまでも` pledge also show Lisa authoring his new name and accepting a life organized around her search. In E07 she refuses his offer to act against Grace; in E12 his injury and objection to forced sacrifice press on her doctrine from within their bond. A reconstruction should preserve the possibility of reciprocal care *and* the danger that she lets loyalty become service (E06 S04/S07–S10; E07 S10; E12 S05; `SYL-L0043`–`L0045`, `L0057`, `L0085`).

Toward Lara she alternates modes rather than moving once from enemy to ally. E04 she reaches by phone with “beloved sister” language while ending on a withheld `でも`. E05 at reunion she promises protection and then says Lara does not need the Otsu house or Mari. E08 she gives a lifesaving vial and more time near family but presents a sea-love rule as exceptionless. E10 she retrieves Lara from the chaos yet blocks further Mari contact while Rowan demands a victim. E12 she points Mari toward Lara and later returns the ring, admitting that Lara's change frightened her. The trajectory is a sequence of controlled concessions under altered evidence, not a hidden plan that was benevolent all along (`SYL-L0032`, `L0037`–`L0039`, `L0062`–`L0064`, `L0073`, `L0077`, `L0085`, `L0088`).

This makes Lisa's fear more precise than generic prejudice. She has known human violence first-hand, has lived through bodily transformations she calls ugly or is taught to call ugly, and has been left responsible for relatives who cannot argue back. Lara's modern attachments may signal to Lisa that the only available rescue resource is slipping away, while also revealing that the hated land can be a genuine home. She is able to use that land's science before she is able to welcome it as a moral community. Her late admission that she feared *the changing Lara* names the personal dimension hidden inside institutional language. The monograph should not make fear an excuse: she still attempts to decide which people Lara needs, and the source does not show her apologizing to Mari or to Seizō's memory (E06 S06–S13; E12 S11; `SYL-C0030`–`C0031`, `C0061`).

Lisa can notice anomalies without promptly changing her doctrine. She knows Grace's potion and Seizō's imitation differ, yet uses the latter; she sees Lara's human legs and growing social world, yet repeats that mermaid-human love cannot be; she proposes a sea-world-love tradition despite Grace's human-prince account and Rowan's blood doctrine. In E10 she calls Grace gone and the spell loosened, but that is an interpretation of a crisis, not a verified present fate. In E12 she tells Mari humans cannot see the heart-blade, then permits Mari to move toward Lara through the mist. This action does not show Lisa suddenly believes humans have magical sight. It shows that her protective decision can depart from the strongest version of her exclusionary premise when Lara's danger is immediate and Kōta, himself transformed and wounded, objects to forcing a beloved sacrifice (`SYL-C0040`, `C0048`, `C0059`; `SYL-R0054`–`R0056`).

The ring return six months later is the clearest admission she owes Lara an account. Lisa says she had actually feared the changing Lara; the fear was not only a neutral prognosis of death. She describes new light unlike earlier princesses and yields the symbol she had withheld. This is a specific correction in her sister relationship: she can let Lara hold royal identity without immediately commanding a return to Rowan's path. It is not enough evidence to write a generalized conversion to trust of human science, the Otsu home or a human-mermaid polity. Her E06 trauma remains part of her evidence, even though it never licensed a universal verdict (`SYL-C0061`, E12 S11).

If a new human institution materially helped the mermaid family, E06 suggests Lisa would evaluate whether its technique works, possibly collaborate under strict control, and initially speak dismissively about the humans involved. Seizō's partial medicine is the positive precedent and his grave the negative one. Whether she would later acknowledge those people, rather than only their output, would depend on repeated interaction, family security and who in the family contests her account; E12 provides a narrow precedent for changing a decision after Lara/Kōta/Mari make the cost visible. Do not predict either instant reconciliation or immutable fanaticism. Also abstain on her unshown fate after the coda, the exact duration of her medicine, and the nature of Kōta's remaining injury (`SYL-W0020`, `W0050`–`W0052`).
