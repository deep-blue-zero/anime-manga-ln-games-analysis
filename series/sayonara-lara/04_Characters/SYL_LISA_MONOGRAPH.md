# Lisa: duty performed in the despised body

Lisa is Lara's sister and Rowan's delegated keeper of an unfinished restoration. Her human identity, Shiomi Lisa (`潮見リサ`), is not a momentary disguise: she and Minase Kōta spend decades navigating documents, research, captivity and human institutions after displacement. Kōta finds Lisa in a human exhibition, while she recalls first finding and valuing him; their reciprocal “finding” coexists with hierarchy and his professed allegiance (E06 S04/S07-S10; `SYL-C0028`, `SYL-R0027`). Lisa has genuine knowledge of medicine's limits and real trauma from species-targeted capture. Neither experience certifies her universal pronouncement that a human-mermaid relation is impossible (`SYL-C0030`).

The contradiction is embodied. Rowan assigns her long custodial waiting, then condemns the human legs through which she has survived. Lisa herself uses human `仮定と検証`, the work of Horikoshi Seizō and a partially effective imitation medicine to keep Lara alive (`SYL-C0029`-`SYL-C0031`). She insists in E08 that princess light necessarily arises from deep love for the sea world, while Grace prescribes human true love; both speak with vested interests. Lisa's claim has a historical basis in the family and her long vigilance, but she cannot turn that sample into an exceptionless law. Her E09-E10 pursuit retrieves Lara and also ends in an attempt to sever human matters, leaving the distinction between rescue and control active (`SYL-C0048`-`SYL-C0052`).

She is not simply Rowan's mouthpiece. In E08 she gives Mari the temporary medicine and allows Lara a limited land choice under threat of death; this is protective and still constraining. In E12 she tells Mari the blade and true light are invisible to humans and condemns their supposed failure to understand, yet points her toward Lara in the mist. She does not command Mari to remain safely away. Wounded Kōta voices objection to making Lara pierce a beloved because he too loves someone, pressuring Lisa's abstraction from beside her (`SYL-R0056`). Human incapacity to see supernatural light remains untested as universal, but Mari's decision proves the ethical mistake of equating such vision with understanding.

Six months later Lisa calls Lara's light unlike earlier princesses and reports gradual family recovery. She expects Rowan's hatred not to vanish, and Lara promises repeated conversation with the court. Lisa then admits she had feared Lara's change and returns the royal ring (`SYL-C0061`). The act recognizes a sister who may carry an inheritance without obeying its old rule. It is not a categorical renunciation of every anti-human conviction, a medicine cure, or proof the sleeping court has awakened. Lisa remains accountable for earlier coercion and fear, and Rowan's future is not hers to settle. Her monograph thus describes a partial change in how she handles Lara's autonomy, not a conversion cutscene. Performance and Kōta's ultimate health remain bounded by missing channels and insufficient ending evidence (`SYL-D0001`-`SYL-D0002`).
