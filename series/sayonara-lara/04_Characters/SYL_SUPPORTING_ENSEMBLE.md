# Supporting ensemble: alternatives to the single saving couple

The original prince belongs to Lara's first-life tragedy, not the modern lover role by default. Her rescue and attraction follow an earlier wish to understand humans; his human attachment and the ship crisis make her recognition-dependent sacrifice catastrophic (E01 S03-S11; `SYL-C0001`). Luca's resemblance reopens this script but does not make him a reincarnation or identical person. He values Lara as an individual in E07, asks for a slower mutual pace in E09, helps seek her in E10-E12, and speaks of their actual dating and breakup after the six-month jump. His finished portrait promises a future encounter without restoring lover status or reducing him to a false answer (`SYL-R0042`, `SYL-R0049`, `SYL-R0057`). His ordinary safety objections and apology are part of care, not a magical deficiency.

Kōta's literal finding capacity and long loyalty to Lisa are tested by her earlier recognition of him. Their search, human names and scientific/historical labor make survival a collaboration, though naming and fealty preserve asymmetry (E06 S04/S07-S12; `SYL-C0028`). When wounded at the E12 shore, he opposes a coerced killing because he loves someone. The episode does not certify a reciprocal romantic category or final wound outcome. Rigmor likewise cannot be reduced to Grace's lost twin or Rowan's perfect princess: she shares the land dream, chooses Rowan, follows Grace into danger and counsels a life for love beyond law. Her death's exact wound chain remains uncertain (`SYL-R0050`).

Mari's parents and older brother anchor the cost of hiding a mermaid within a household. Mari's E10 disclosure revises the invented-sister cover; the brother's boat and life-jacket arguments add material risk and concern rather than a simple rescue vehicle (`SYL-R0048`). Keiko's maternal presence, family warmth and the image of someone beside Mari shape the boxer's ambitions; neither makes her a deceased absence inferred from silence nor a mere explanatory tool. Coach, boxing peers and workplace figures demand training, schedules, competence and repair from both girls. E05 bakery work transforms a total “work is misery” judgment into a day-specific satisfaction, and E08 competition does not convert Mari's athletic future into Lara's proof of love (`SYL-F0026`, `SYL-F0043`).

Himeka and Ouji's E04 school interaction provides a human comparator: liking someone does not automatically secure reciprocal dating, and an answer to a confession need not deny the confessor's worth. Yoshiya's bakery support and Lara's growing contacts multiply the people who see her beyond a prince/witch/king taxonomy (`SYL-C0021`). The ensemble is deliberately bounded: it preserves each verified function and limit without manufacturing equal-length biographies for minor figures. Exact identities and relationship histories should be resolved via `SYL_CHARACTER_STATE_AND_READINESS`, then the episode locators, not by assuming role labels in a secondary English caption are definitive.
