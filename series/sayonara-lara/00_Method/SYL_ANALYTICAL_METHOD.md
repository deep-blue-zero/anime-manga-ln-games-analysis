# Sayonara Lara: full-series analytical method

## 1. Mandate and authority

Build a source-grounded literary and audiovisual account of the television series, with independently useful character and relationship analyses and a reliable basis for comparison within the project. The planned narrative boundary is Episodes 01-12, to be confirmed against the supplied source inventory. Specials, promotional voice dramas, later releases, adaptations, and other continuities are not silently admitted.

This is a proposed series-specific method. Upon receipt, Codex must reconcile it with live governance, adopt it through the repository's ordinary acceptance process, and record the adopted version in `CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md`. The packet's `draft_noncurrent` status is intentional; shipping this file does not open the sequential-analysis gate. The relevant requirements are [G01-G09, G12-G14] in the package source notes.

The central questions concern alienation, desire, love, companionship, self-authorship, comedy, embodiment, and the ending. They are research questions, not predetermined conclusions. A final interpretation may preserve, substantially revise, or reject the earlier conversational thesis.

**Governing distinction:** primary event; character account of that event; audiovisual presentation; analyst inference; creator explanation; reception. Do not collapse any of these into another.

## 2. Source model and admissibility

The primary object is the identified Japanese-language audiovisual episode, not an English plot summary and not a subtitle file by itself. Work bundle-first under the global episode-bundle specification [G06]. Preserve the source-to-derivative mapping and the ability to return to the retained episode.

| Witness | Permitted evidentiary use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Identified original Japanese AV track/encode | Performed utterance, visible action, sound-image relation | Must actually be inspected through an adequate route |
| Provenanced Japanese subtitles | Linguistic reading and temporal navigation | Captions can abbreviate, normalize, omit, mistime, or misattribute speech |
| Corrected/aligned Japanese derivative | Search, scene navigation, tested timing | Preserve untouched witness and a correction history |
| English subtitles | Translation comparison and comprehension aid | Not the source for exact Japanese wording or register |
| Timestamped frames and contact sheets | Static composition and sampled progression | Cannot establish unseen movement between samples |
| Audio derivative | Sound and performance within inspected intervals | Compression and downmix may remove relevant features |
| ASR/forced alignment | Candidate wording and timing | Model output, not certified dialogue or performance judgment |
| Interviews/official promotion | Attributed intent, production choices, marketing framing | Not a substitute for dramatized evidence |
| Audience commentary | What sampled viewers argued or expected | Not proof of a scene or representative national opinion |
| Earlier chat analyses | Recovery leads, hypotheses, intellectual history | Not independently verified episode evidence |

Record whether Japanese text is official captioning, a script, fan transcription, or ASR. Do not call any Japanese-language text the original screenplay without provenance. Where the audible line and captions differ, retain both and adjudicate the particular discrepancy.

At intake, recover episode/version, hashes, audio track, duration, subtitle provenance, time origin, offsets/drift, visual sampling, cue/frame crosswalks, and missing layers. Do not infer these from an archive filename. A Windows acquisition path is historical metadata, not a current portable retrieval route. Source registers in Git contain sanitized pointers and analytical coverage; full dialogue/frame joins and raw measurements stay in the evidence plane.

## 3. Knowledge-boundary discipline

This project has already discussed the ending. A new sequential pass is therefore a **chronological, source-bounded reread with disclosed prior exposure**, not an honestly blind first viewing.

For each episode, distinguish:

1. facts and interpretations available through that episode;
2. earlier conversational assumptions being tested;
3. later knowledge known to the analyst but excluded as support;
4. retrospective corrections made after the sequential freeze.

Do not write a prediction after learning its outcome and label it prospective. A fresh session can reduce anchoring but cannot certify ignorance. Where the receiving agent has no later material in context, record that limited condition rather than claiming a psychological clean slate.

Freeze each episode's entering and exiting knowledge states. Later discoveries update the claims/revisions ledger and current syntheses; they do not silently rewrite the old reading as though the revelation was already known. Correct factual transcription errors transparently, preserving the original state and a correction trail.

Creator and reception materials are normally consulted after a source-first episode/checkpoint argument has been recorded. Existing exposure is disclosed. Documentary evidence can challenge interpretation without being used to predetermine it.

## 4. Episode transaction

Each authorized episode is one atomic transaction, governed by the continuation policy [G08]. Its output is not complete merely because a prose reading exists.

### A. Enter

Read current entrypoint, method, architecture, prior freeze, relevant character/relationship states, open claims, and material evidence debts. Confirm authorized episode, source identity, and actual capability route. Record both the committed transaction boundary and the separate audiovisual-completion boundary.

### B. Reconstruct before interpreting

Read the whole supplied Japanese subtitle witness and inspect the episode's visual progression across its full duration, including cold open, title, OP/ED variations, eyecatches, and post-credit content. Use contact sheets for reconnaissance, then open individual frames where expression, composition, text, or action matters. Describe coverage truthfully: full contact-sheet review is not full-motion viewing.

Build an analyst-validated scene map. Mechanical shot/scene indexes are navigation inputs, not automatic dramatic divisions. A scene entry records stable scene ID, actual interval, location/time layer, participants, event, information change, and source locators.

Produce a plain narrative reconstruction before advancing a governing thesis. Preserve inconvenient events, comedy, minor relationships, and contradictory details instead of selecting only the material that fits alienation or romance debates.

### C. Read through six interacting lenses

**Dramatic construction and knowledge.** What changes, what is withheld, and whose perception governs the scene? Distinguish story chronology from presentation order; objective event from memory, dream, staged story, character theory, or joke.

**Character, agency, and institutions.** Identify desire, fear, competence, self-report, available alternatives, decision, cost, and response. Separate chosen dependence from coerced duty, capability from permission, and causal responsibility from accepted blame. Do not infer a clinical diagnosis from withdrawal or grief.

**Relationships and the language of love.** Track each participant's direction of feeling, named relationship, behavior, expectations, boundaries, and knowledge. Friendship, romance, family, admiration, rescue, and dependence may overlap without being interchangeable.

**Visual and temporal form.** Examine composition, gaze, distance, blocking, thresholds, bodies, water, props, color, motif recurrence, and editing where inspected. Do not turn palette into an automatic emotional dictionary. Test a motif through recurrence and variation, not a single attractive frame.

**Performed language, sound, and music.** Separate Japanese lexical/register evidence from sound actually heard or measured. Track forms of address, particles, hesitation, rhythm, laughter, exertion, overlap, silence, cueing, and vocal contrast only within the supported inspection route. The audio protocol governs the details.

**Comedy and genre.** Recover setup, Lara's or another character's interpretive premise, Mari's response, timing, audience knowledge, escalation, and aftermath. Ask whether the joke changes authority, intimacy, vulnerability, or plot rather than treating it as disposable tonal relief.

These are analytical responsibilities, not six mandatory equal-length essays. The episode's distinctive problem determines emphasis.

### D. Test the argument

For each load-bearing claim, record positive evidence, best alternative account, counterevidence, scope, and what observation would change the conclusion. Prefer a small number of well-supported major claims to dozens of trivial ledger entries.

### E. Close

Write the episode reading; synchronize applicable cumulative homes; register locators and unresolved obligations; update character readiness; freeze the boundary; perform required repository checks; commit the complete transaction. Only then continue when continuous execution is authorized.

## 5. What an episode reading must contain

Each `SYL_EPnn_DEEP_READING.md` should make the following recoverable without rigidly repeating a paragraph template:

- source edition/language, knowledge boundary, and actual inspection coverage;
- concise, accurate summary and scene map;
- a sustained episode-level argument with close readings;
- character and relationship changes, including continuity and contradiction;
- distinctive comedy, formal, Japanese-language, and performance findings;
- strongest counterreading and unresolved causal/linguistic questions;
- claim/ledger deltas, freeze, next operation, and evidence debts.

Use explicit stable anchors for major scenes/claims. A serious episode normally warrants several developed arguments, not a plot synopsis decorated with thematic nouns. There is no automatic word-count certificate of completion.

## 6. Claim and locator contract

Use local stable IDs without manufacturing global character-registry identities:

- `SYL-C0001`: interpretive claim;
- `SYL-E01-S01`: analyst scene ID after verified segmentation;
- `SYL-L0001`: Japanese language note;
- `SYL-P0001`: performance observation;
- `SYL-D0001`: evidence debt;
- `SYL-H0001`: prior hypothesis.

The numbers identify records, not confidence or chronology guarantees. Preserve source-native cue/frame IDs rather than replacing them with convenient new numbering.

A consequential citation should resolve through:

`claim -> episode/scene -> exact witness + interval/cue/frame -> retained source`

Recommended locator content is `episode; source ID; source hash or immutable revision; start/end; JP cue ID; frame IDs; inspection route`. A claim about an entire scene needs context, not just a single line. If a time is unknown, record `UNLOCATED`; never infer a precise timestamp from recollection.

Classify findings as `OBSERVED`, `CHARACTER_ASSERTION`, `INTERPRETATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `PARATEXT_ASSERTION`, or `RECEPTION_ASSERTION`. These local evidence tags supplement, not replace, repository authority metadata. Use the existing transitions `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`.

Confidence is reasoned and scoped. Avoid numerical confidence that suggests measurement. Record competing explanations rather than forcing every dispute into a binary result.

## 7. Series-specific tests

### Alienation and reintegration

Test distinct mechanisms: loss of home; inability to read social conventions; bodily displacement; fear of rejection; guilt about wanting; inherited role; narrowed participation; chosen solitude; secure attachment; and capacity to explore. Do not equate Mari's discipline with pathology or Lara's dependence with failure.

At each checkpoint ask: what can each girl now understand, choose, refuse, tolerate, name, or do without mediation? What remains unchanged? Does the bond expand either life, simply stabilize it, or generate new obligations? Is the apparent symmetry false in important respects?

### Love, yuri, romance, and classification

Keep at least five questions separate: (a) the emotional centrality of the female dyad; (b) yuri as a declared critical/reception category; (c) same-sex romantic attraction in either direction; (d) mutual romantic recognition; (e) explicit couple formation. None logically guarantees the next.

Test friendship/companionship, romance, asymmetric attraction, and deliberately uncategorized love as live alternatives. Do not require a kiss or confession to recognize well-supported romantic construction. Conversely, rescue, cohabitation, tenderness, or the word 'love' alone does not establish romance.

Compare the show's treatment of Lara/Mari with its independently established male-directed romantic scenes. Observe gaze, self-consciousness, fantasy, proximity, dialogue, sound, framing, and continuity. Neither heterosexual nor same-sex romance should be presumed from gender alone. Male-directed attraction does not establish exclusive heterosexuality; lack of attraction in sampled scenes does not establish that it never occurs.

The Japanese terms for love, romantic feeling, liking, beloved persons, friends, companions, princes, departure, and return require full utterance context. Dictionary glosses do not decide relationship ontology. Preserve subject ambiguity, address, quotation, ellipsis, and who knows what.

### Comedy

Test the user's proposed engine: Lara's expansive sincerity versus Mari's economical, often unenthusiastic response. Seek reversals: Mari's enthusiasm, Lara's deadpan or despair, Grace's interventions, and occasions where the supposed straight person becomes the source of absurdity. Identify whether the joke depends on wording, dialect, image, delivery, cut timing, or cross-modal contrast.

Do not replace a specific rhythm analysis with generic boke/tsukkomi labels. Do not infer performance from punctuation. Ask whether comedy grants Lara survivable mistakes, masks injury, redistributes status, or occasionally trivializes material the drama asks us to take seriously.

### Causality, law, and family

Separate shown magical consequences from Grace's or Lisa's account of them. Track changing rules for light, bodily transformation, voice, sacrifice, resurrection, royal obligations, and who has jurisdiction. A later explanation can revise an earlier claim, but do not harmonize contradictions without evidence. Motivation can explain coercion without excusing it.

### Ending

Assess causal intelligibility, Lara's agency, Mari's agency, relational communication, thematic consistency, and dramatic preparation independently. A departure may express liberation, abandonment, both, or neither. Loving someone need not entail cohabitation; independence does not inherently require travel. Ask what the film actually prepares and communicates, not whether the viewer can invent a satisfying missing conversation.

## 8. Character analysis and reconstruction

Build person-specific models, not type descriptions. Record state over time, competing desires, self-description versus conduct, relationship-conditioned behavior, refusal thresholds, humor, speech, competence, and limits. Preserve Lara's curiosity apart from romantic pursuit and Mari's life apart from being Lara's helper.

Lara and Mari warrant dedicated longitudinal monographs once the verified corpus supports them. Grace and Rowan have planned dedicated homes because their choices and institutions are expected to carry independent explanatory weight; Rowan in particular must be tested as a major ideological axis rather than treated only as family context. Lisa and other consequential figures earn dedicated homes through sustained independent explanatory value. Shorter figures can remain in a substantial supporting-ensemble study; word counts or name frequency do not determine eligibility.

Reconstruction is a separate responsibility. A monograph does not automatically certify simulation. Later behavior models must specify source boundary, state, interlocutor, situation, plausible response range, contradictions, and abstention conditions. No model may infer unsourced sexual behavior or convert the protagonists into stock 'ditzy mermaid' and 'cold boxer' caricatures.

Qualitative ethics can address personhood, autonomy, obligation, ends, reflexivity, instrumentalization, and harm where evidence warrants. Any numeric PACTRIH work must first recover its live governing method and canonical dataset, preserve the axes' meanings, and avoid a total morality score. No cross-project numerical edits are authorized by this method.

## 9. Coverage, debts, and advancement

Maintain separate states for source availability, processing, inspection, narrative transaction completion, audiovisual completion, semantic acceptance, branch publication, and main integration.

An episode transaction may close with explicitly scheduled later performance work **only when** its present claims are bounded to inspected evidence, the architecture permits that phase split, and every deferred requirement has a claim-linked owner and acceptance condition. This does not certify an audiovisual deep reading as complete. Missing words, identity, or action needed for a central narrative claim block that episode's closure.

Final voice/comedy/ending claims cannot bypass material pending evidence. An unavailable channel is not permission to invent an observation or silently redefine a requirement as optional.

## 10. Acceptance and revision

The method succeeds when another analyst can recover why a conclusion was reached, what could overturn it, and which evidence was actually examined. It fails when fluent prose masks absent source inspection.

Amend the method only for a demonstrated analytical need. Preserve existing IDs and adequate work; backfill only material gaps. Adoption, amendments, and exceptions belong in the current entrypoint and acceptance record, not in a second competing method.
