# Legacy hypotheses and research agenda

## 1. What this document preserves

This is a reconstruction of the analytical questions raised in the preceding conversation, not a new reading of the episodes. It preserves enough of that discussion to prevent loss of useful questions while preventing unverified statements from acquiring authority through repetition.

The conversation included extensive English-subtitle-based readings of Episodes 01-04, a proposed early-series synthesis, discussion of an Episode 12 Reddit thread, and creator-interview interpretation. This package does not contain the complete original conversation, original episode bundles, or a verified audit of those readings. Numerical counts, timestamps, quotations, identities, and performance descriptions must be checked against the new source inventory before reuse.

On adoption, this document's intended home is `90_Legacy_and_Superseded/SYL_LEGACY_AND_HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md`. Preserve its role as non-evidentiary provenance. The active claims ledger records which hypotheses receive actual support, contradiction, or revision during the new pass. A hypothesis becoming persuasive does not retrospectively turn the old conversation into primary evidence.

## 2. Analytical disposition of the conversation

Preserve the strongest ideas as questions. Do not preserve the previous assistant's confidence as evidence.

The early analysis repeatedly treated ordinary care, shopping, meals, household friction, and reciprocal rescue as alternatives to the heroine's inherited fairy-tale roles. It also proposed that Lara and Mari occupy opposite relations to the world: displacement versus emotional narrowing. Those are useful organizing hypotheses, but they risk over-smoothing the characters into complementary therapeutic functions.

The conversation initially leaned toward a romantic interpretation, then became more skeptical, then revised again after reading finale reception. These changes are part of the interpretive history. None is the mandatory destination of the new source pass.

The owner's final proposal distinguishes yuri themes or reception from a queer-romance plot and favors a deeply important female friendship reading of Lara/Mari. Preserve that distinction without treating the conclusion as commissioned proof. A strong result may agree with the owner, disagree, or distinguish different levels of the work more precisely.

## 4. Character research briefs, not completed profiles

### Lara

The legacy model joins curiosity, courage, emotionally direct care, romantic idealization, and a reflex toward guilt or self-removal. Test whether these really belong to one stable psychological organization or were overgeneralized from a few early scenes.

Discriminate curiosity from romantic appetite; responsibility from self-blame; dependence from chosen attachment; accepting help from exploiting a caregiver. Track learned competence, what survives historical displacement, and whether return to family or departure from Mari changes the structure of agency. Do not confuse elapsed calendar years with lived experience.

### Mari

The legacy model emphasizes practical care, reserve, irritation, boxing, and a maternal relationship shaping ambition. Test against warmth, enjoyment, friendships, conflict, and independent goals outside scenes with Lara. Do not diagnose grief, emotional avoidance, or exclusive boxing motivation from a small sample.

A major question is whether the story grants Mari an arc with its own closure or largely makes her the means of Lara's liberation. That question remains important under friendship, romance, or deliberately unlabeled companionship readings.

### Grace

Separate what Grace does, what she says she did, what she knows, and what the episode confirms. The 'in-universe author' reading may illuminate her theatrical control, but it must not become an all-purpose explanation for unexplained mechanics. Comic degradation does not automatically remove her power; threatening language is not automatically proof of omniscience.

### Lisa and the mermaid family

Test how personal affection, inherited hostility, political duty, jealousy, and actual survival needs interact. 'Loves Lara but needs a savior' is a candidate conflict, not an established verdict. Preserve differences among family members; do not collapse them into one oppressive institution.

### Other characters

Rowan, Keiko, Luca, the original prince, Himeka, Ouji, Yoshiya, and the household/boxing ensemble are candidate analytical subjects from prior discussion. Verify spellings, Japanese forms, relationships, appearances, and roles. Distinguish the original prince from later figures rather than treating visual resemblance or archetypal role as identity.

Himeka/Ouji may provide a useful comparison between nonreciprocity and rejection of personhood. It remains necessary to inspect the actual confession, response, and aftermath rather than importing that interpretation from an English summary.

## 5. Mandatory legacy-claim audit

### Audio and temporal claims

The conversation asserted a hierarchy of character vocal registers, broad prosodic tendencies, timing, and audio-informed scene structure without supplying recoverable measurement or inspection records. **Do not treat those statements as measured results.** This package does not establish that those measurements occurred.

Route each useful claim to the audio protocol. A verified creator statement about contrasting acting styles does not validate an unperformed pitch study. A script with overlapping subtitle cues does not establish overlapping voiced delivery. A long interval without translated dialogue is not proof of silence.

### Words and subtitles

Earlier English quotation marks do not certify exact Japanese wording. Recover the actual cue, speaker, scene context, language witness, and any audible difference before using it for relationship classification or moral reasoning. Verify distinctions around love, liking, companionship, family, duty, and goodbye in context rather than assigning fixed meanings to individual Japanese terms.

### Causality and world rules

Earlier discussion sometimes moved quickly from an authority's accusation to a moral interpretation. Audit whether a kingdom's decline, bodily transformation, survival condition, or family suspension is demonstrated, asserted, inferred, or contradicted. Do not repair rule gaps with a metaphor about self-authorship.

### Biography and identity

A missing family member is not automatically dead; a resemblance is not reincarnation; a public synopsis is not an inspected scene. Dates, episode titles, subtitle counts, names, and source paths from earlier answers require source recovery where analytically relevant.

### Reception and classification

'Western fans over-index romance' is not established by one Reddit thread. The study can analyze the sampled comments' reasoning and expectations without claiming national prevalence. Equally, 'yuri goggles' should not substitute for evaluating actual visual, linguistic, and narrative evidence.

## 6. Verified paratext leads and their limits

Three creator interviews were inspected while preparing this architecture. Detailed source identifiers and URLs are in the source notes [P01-P03]. They can seed `SYL_EXTERNAL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`, but they are not episode witnesses.

Koide's Febri discussion of toughness supplies a useful challenge to a neat therapeutic-growth reading [P01]. The second interview distinguishes intended acting styles and discusses a deliberate reduction in explanatory dialogue [P02]. Kawahara frames Mari's origin through companionship against isolation and describes Lara's outward movement as a central concern [P03]. None settles whether the dramatized bond is romantic, how effective the ending is, or what every audible line sounds like.

The earlier Reddit finale URL is a reception lead, not newly inspected evidence in this package. Other interview, promotional, or voice-drama links mentioned previously remain unverified leads until retrieved. Preserve speaker attribution: interviewer formulations, director statements, scriptwriter statements, and songwriters' interpretations are not interchangeable.

## 7. Research order and safeguards

The chronological reader should record scene observations and local conclusions before comparing them with this seed register. Access only the episode range permitted by the source boundary. A later full-series reviewer may deliberately examine all hypotheses together, using the complete evidence state.

For the central relationship, write the strongest supported friendship reading and strongest supported romantic reading, then identify which evidence genuinely discriminates between them. Do not manufacture symmetrical evidentiary strength. A third reading may be more explanatory, but 'ambiguous' must name what remains indeterminate and why.

For the finale, first reconstruct what happens. Then evaluate causal adequacy, character motivation, relational acknowledgment, formal emphasis, and thematic resolution separately. Do not invent a return promise, a permanent separation, a romantic confession, or a reason for travel that the source never supplies.
