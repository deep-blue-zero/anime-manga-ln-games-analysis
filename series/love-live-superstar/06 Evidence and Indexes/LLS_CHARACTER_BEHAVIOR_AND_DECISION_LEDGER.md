# Love Live! Superstar!! — Character Behavior and Decision Ledger

## Purpose

Model-facing conditional behavior evidence. The unit is not a personality adjective but an auditable rule:

> **trigger + perceived stakes + current state → likely response, with named exceptions.**

Rules remain provisional until recurrence/contrast strengthens them. A single scene may establish a local mechanism without proving a lifelong trait.

## Fields

- Rule ID
- Character / temporal boundary
- Trigger/context
- Perceived stakes
- Active goal / avoidance goal
- Observed response
- Escalation/de-escalation condition
- Counterexample / limitation
- Evidence locator
- Confidence
- Revision state

## S1E01 rules

### LLS-BHV-KANON-001 — High-stakes self-evaluation can suppress performance

- **Character/state:** Kanon @ S1E01.
- **Trigger:** singing becomes an important formal evaluation tied to a desired future.
- **Perceived stakes:** institutional admission plus judgment of her own adequacy.
- **Goal/avoidance:** wants to sing/succeed; simultaneously wants to avoid another self-confirming failure.
- **Observed response:** fragmented speech followed by inability to begin the required solo.
- **Contrast:** she can sing in ordinary public space when the moment is not coded as consequential.
- **Limitation:** one episode does not establish the full causal mechanism or its durability.
- **Evidence:** entrance audition contrasted with street singing and 「何でもない時はいくらでも声が出るのに」.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN/STRENGTHEN candidate.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-002 — Defending another person can unlock assertiveness unavailable for self-advocacy

- **Trigger:** Kanon perceives Keke as being treated unfairly or dismissed without adequate explanation.
- **Perceived stakes:** another person's agency/fairness, not Kanon's own eligibility.
- **Observed response:** sustained direct confrontation with Ren; demands explanation rather than blanket refusal.
- **Break condition:** Ren asks whether Kanon herself wants to become a school idol.
- **Observed shift:** fluent advocacy collapses into 「私は…」.
- **Evidence:** Keke/Ren confrontation.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN/STRENGTHEN candidate.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-003 — Shame can be managed by minimization and substitute goals

- **Trigger:** social attention returns to the failed music-course route or her musical ambition.
- **Perceived stakes:** pity, exposure of continued desire, renewed confrontation with failure.
- **Observed response:** 「もう気にしてないし」 / 「普通科の方が気楽」; joking class-introduction dream of owning a cat.
- **Alternative explanation:** ordinary humor/social awkwardness contributes; not every joke is defensive behavior.
- **Evidence:** walk to school; class introduction after noticing Keke.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-004 — Supporting another's dream can function as a safer substitute for pursuing her own

- **Trigger:** Keke's explicit school-idol desire combined with Kanon's belief that her own singing route is over.
- **Observed response:** Kanon can endorse/help Keke while excluding herself from equivalent risk.
- **Pressure:** Keke refuses the one-way helper arrangement and returns recognition/support to Kanon.
- **Outcome at boundary:** Kanon explicitly owns 「歌が好きだ！」 under different conditions from the audition.
- **Limitation:** does not yet prove a stable rescuer/leader identity.
- **Confidence:** high for episode mechanism; low for longitudinal trait.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-KEKE-001 — Recognized affinity/ability prompts direct recruitment rather than cautious observation

- **Character/state:** Keke @ S1E01.
- **Trigger:** hears Kanon singing and identifies the voice as exceptional/valuable to her project.
- **Goal:** become a school idol and find collaborators.
- **Observed response:** immediate pursuit, overt praise, repeated direct recruitment.
- **Counterexample:** none yet; boundary too early for stability claim.
- **Confidence:** high locally / medium longitudinally.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-CHISATO-001 — Support is expressed as personal desire rather than command

- **Character/state:** Chisato @ S1E01.
- **Trigger:** Kanon says she promised herself she would quit singing after failure.
- **Observed response:** 「私はかのんちゃんの歌 聴いていたいけどな」: states her own wish instead of telling Kanon what she must do.
- **Model implication:** current evidence favors non-coercive supportive framing with Kanon.
- **Limitation:** one interaction only.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-REN-001 — Perceived unauthorized action prompts procedural/formal opposition

- **Character/state:** Ren @ S1E01.
- **Trigger:** Keke recruits for an unapproved school-idol activity.
- **Perceived stakes:** seriousness/order of a music-centered school institution.
- **Observed response:** formal challenge and demand that unauthorized activity stop.
- **Diagnostic move:** redirects Kanon's advocacy toward Kanon's own desire by asking if she also wants to be a school idol.
- **Limitation:** motive remains underdetermined.
- **Confidence:** high for behavior; medium/low for internal stakes.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

## S1E02 rules and revisions

### LLS-BHV-KANON-001 — STRENGTHEN / NARROW

The public-performance problem persists despite major gains in commitment. Kanon ends with 「歌えるかな」 when directly asked to sing in public space. S1E02 therefore strengthens the durability of the context-sensitive block while narrowing any claim that she is generally unable to act under musical pressure: she can train and compose intensely.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-002 — STRENGTHEN

- **New trigger evidence:** Ren's refusal is no longer merely about Keke personally; Kanon interprets it as unfair institutional overreach.
- **Observed response:** rejects a quiet workaround, argues that accepting Ren's position would let her control the school, and continues to seek another route.
- **Model update:** other-protective assertiveness can generalize into explicit fairness/jurisdiction principles when Kanon's own interest is also growing.
- **Evidence:** `00:07:47–00:08:30`.
- **Confidence:** high.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-005 — Qualified language can coexist with committed action

- **Character/state:** Kanon @ S1E02.
- **Trigger:** trusted friend asks what Kanon herself now wants after a practical workaround is proposed.
- **Perceived stakes:** public exposure of a still-new school-idol interest without certainty about competence/outcome.
- **Observed response:** completes 「本気で ちょっとスクールアイドルに興味があるの」 and continues acting on it.
- **Contrast:** S1E01 minimization often supported withdrawal or denial.
- **Model implication:** after S1E02, mitigation markers (`ちょっと`, epistemic qualifiers) cannot automatically be classified as avoidance; state and enacted behavior must be checked.
- **Evidence:** `00:08:24.300–00:08:30.100`.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN/STRENGTHEN candidate.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-006 — Desire is checked against third-party obligations and practical feasibility

- **Trigger/context:** a desired route would require quitting school, using another club instrumentally, or absorbing Chisato into the project.
- **Perceived stakes:** family consent, fairness to other students/clubs, and another person's existing path.
- **Observed response:** rejects Keke's transfer proposal; rejects using another club as a temporary shell; tells Keke they cannot ask Chisato to become a school idol because Chisato is in the music course.
- **Alternative reading:** the Chisato case may become overprotective/presumptive because Kanon answers before Chisato states her own desire.
- **Evidence:** `00:05:09–05:32`; `00:07:47–08:24`; `00:11:48–12:01`.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-007 — Received emotional material can trigger intensive translational creativity

- **Trigger:** Keke supplies lyric material and a clear emotional purpose.
- **Perceived stakes:** make a song capable of carrying Keke's feeling into their shared performance goal.
- **Observed response:** values the words explicitly, seeks Chinese reference material, becomes absorbed in composition, produces a song Chisato identifies as carrying both Keke's feeling and Kanon's own style.
- **Model implication:** Kanon may respond to another person's articulated desire by building form around it rather than merely offering verbal encouragement.
- **Evidence:** `00:14:35–17:38`.
- **Confidence:** high locally; longitudinal stability open.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-KEKE-002 — STRENGTHEN / RELATION-SPECIFY

Keke's persistence is now person-specific as well as project-specific: she explicitly wants to begin school idols **with Kanon**, not merely recruit any adequate singer.

### LLS-BHV-KEKE-003 — Institutional obstruction prompts overt escalation before accommodation

- **Character/state:** Keke @ S1E02.
- **Trigger:** club application and direct appeal are rejected.
- **Perceived stakes:** ability to pursue school-idol aspiration at all.
- **Observed response:** quickly proposes school transfer, then organizes a conspicuous freedom/equality petition.
- **De-escalation/redirection:** Kanon's family/practical objection stops the transfer plan; formal headmistress intervention redirects the conflict into a performance challenge.
- **Limitation:** theatrical/comedic framing means escalation should not automatically be generalized to every conflict domain.
- **Evidence:** `00:04:46–05:32`; `00:08:38–09:24`.
- **Confidence:** high locally.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-KEKE-004 — Sincerity justifies effort rather than exemption from effort

- **Belief:** 「スクールアイドルに一番大切なものは気持ち」.
- **Observed response:** accepts Chisato's running/dance regimen and continues despite repeated collapse.

- **Evidence:** `00:12:25–14:23`, later training montage.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN/STRENGTHEN candidate.

### LLS-BHV-CHISATO-002 — Concrete skill request activates diagnostic coaching rather than generic reassurance

- **Trigger:** Kanon/Keke ask for dance help under a first-place constraint.
- **Perceived stakes:** their ability to execute a credible performance.
- **Observed response:** tests current ability, identifies stamina deficit, finds a positive rhythmic foothold, prescribes progression, and explicitly refuses to lower choreography to current competence.

- **Evidence:** `00:11:25–14:23`; `00:17:39–17:45`.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.

### LLS-BHV-REN-001 — REVISE / STRENGTHEN

S1E02 shows that Ren's opposition is not merely procedural. Her explicit stake is institutional prestige: music-related activity that fails to excel can lower Yuigaoka's value. Preserve the formal/procedural style, but revise the inferred governing stake from generic order to **quality-controlled institutional representation**.

## S1E03 rules and revisions

### LLS-BHV-KANON-001 — STRENGTHEN / REVISE TO MEANING-SENSITIVE GENERALIZATION

- **New evidence:** a sparsely populated morning attempt that Kanon expects to be safe still fails once she connects singing to the festival's first-place requirement.
- **Revision:** the trigger is not simply physical stage size or current audience count. Anticipated consequential evaluation can spread backward into preparation contexts.
- **Current rule:** **self-relevant singing + anticipated consequential evaluation / downstream stakes → elevated risk of vocal-performance block**, even before the formal stage itself.
- **Evidence:** `00:41–00:59`; `02:31–03:09`.
- **Confidence:** high.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-005 — PRESERVE / QUALIFY

S1E03 continues to show that verbal uncertainty does not equal inactivity. Kanon never reaches certainty that she will sing, but continues training and ultimately chooses 「私　歌ってみる」. Her model should distinguish **uncertain commitment** from avoidance.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-006 — STRENGTHEN / BURDEN RISK

The obligation-checking tendency can become self-erasing when Kanon concludes that her weakness threatens Keke's dream. The same concern for third-party cost that made her ethically careful in S1E02 can escalate into unilateral self-removal.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-008 — Perceived burden can trigger moralized self-removal

- **Observed response:** repeated 「私のせいで」 → global self-description as 「足手まとい」 → refusal logic that she should not stand onstage.
- **Countercondition:** direct evidence that her presence is itself part of Keke's valued end, not merely an instrument for winning.
- **Model implication:** reassurance about competence may be weaker than correcting Kanon's mistaken model of the other person's preferences.
- **Evidence:** `12:38–14:48`.
- **Confidence:** high locally.
- **Revision state:** OPEN/STRENGTHEN candidate.

### LLS-BHV-KANON-009 — Relational scaffolding can restore action without eliminating uncertainty

- **Trigger:** high-stakes public performance remains unavoidable, but failure has an explicit contingency and trusted people/audience support become salient.
- **Observed response:** moves from repeated self-reassurance to 「歌える / 一人じゃないから」 and enters the duet.
- **Key condition:** the successful configuration includes Keke's fallback plan, Keke's physical co-presence, visible supporters, crowd light, and extensive prior practice.
- **Limitation:** do not reduce this to “friendship cures stage fright”; durability and necessary/sufficient conditions remain open.
- **Evidence:** `16:11–16:21`; `18:06–18:53`.
- **Confidence:** high episode-locally / medium longitudinally.
- **Revision state:** OPEN.
