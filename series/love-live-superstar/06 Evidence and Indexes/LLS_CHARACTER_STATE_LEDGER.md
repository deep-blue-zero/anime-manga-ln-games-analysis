# Love Live! Superstar!! — Character State Ledger

## Purpose

Time-indexed longitudinal state record for character reconstruction. This ledger separates current developmental state from stable disposition and must never silently replace an earlier state with a later one.

Canonical local authority for detailed interpretation remains the episode deep reading. This ledger is a compact model-facing distillation.

## State schema

For each materially affected character record:

- temporal boundary;
- stated desire;
- enacted desire;
- fear / avoidance goal;
- self-conception;
- public/private role;
- defensive strategy;
- demonstrated capacity;
- newly gained/lost capacity;
- unresolved conflict;
- evidence locator;
- confidence.

## S1E01 boundary

### Shibuya Kanon @ S1E01

- **Stated desire:** initially minimizes singing desire; eventually states 「歌が好きだ！」.
- **Enacted desire:** repeatedly sings outside evaluative settings; moves toward Keke while singing at the episode climax.
- **Primary fear / avoidance:** another personally meaningful evaluative failure and especially renewed disappointment in herself.
- **Self-conception under stress:** interprets music-course failure as evidence that she may lack talent and should stop singing.
- **Public role:** ordinary-course first-year; not a school idol at this boundary.
- **Private role:** someone who still sings naturally when the act is not coded as a consequential test.
- **Defensive strategies:** minimization (「もう気にしてないし」), substitute aspiration/joking, withdrawal from self-relevant discussion, converting participation into support for someone else's dream.
- **Demonstrated capacities:** fluent public self-presentation when stakes are not immediate; sustained confrontation when defending another person; acute responsiveness to another person's aspiration; conditional ability to sing in public space.
- **New capacity demonstrated:** can convert private preference into explicit self-authorization under relationally supportive, self-chosen conditions.
- **Not established:** durable cure of evaluative-stage singing block; school-idol commitment; stable leadership role.
- **High-value evidence:** entrance examination; Ren's direct school-idol question; 「歌が好きなのに」 silence; final 「歌が好きだ！」 / 「もしかして私…歌えた？」 sequence.
- **Confidence:** high.

### Tang Keke @ S1E01

- **Stated desire:** become a school idol in Japan and recruit others.
- **Enacted desire:** actively searches for/recruits Kanon after hearing her sing; openly states school-idol ambition; persists after resistance.
- **Primary fear / avoidance:** insufficient evidence at this boundary for a stable fear model.
- **Self-conception:** treats school-idol participation as open to people who genuinely want it rather than as an elite category reserved for prequalified talent.
- **Public role:** new international student; explicit initiator of the school-idol project.
- **Defensive strategy:** not yet securely established; enthusiasm presently functions more as forward action than concealment.
- **Demonstrated capacities:** rapid recognition/praise of another's ability; direct recruitment; willingness to expose her own desire publicly; persistence under institutional resistance.
- **Unresolved conflict:** whether desire alone is enough to overcome school/institutional barriers; what happens when enthusiasm meets sustained rejection or failure.
- **High-value evidence:** first pursuit of Kanon; class introduction; argument with Ren; response to Kanon's claim that singing is over.
- **Confidence:** high for current desire/behavior; low for deeper defensive structure.

### Arashi Chisato @ S1E01

- **Stated desire:** wants to keep hearing Kanon's singing.
- **Enacted desire:** checks on Kanon's future relationship to singing without issuing a command.
- **Self-conception / role:** pre-existing friend who knows the entrance-exam context; current school-idol relation not established at this boundary.
- **Demonstrated relational capacity:** can express personal preference without converting it into an obligation for Kanon.
- **Unresolved:** own ambitions, conflicts, and relation to the school-idol project remain underdetermined here.
- **High-value evidence:** conversation about Kanon's promise to quit singing.
- **Confidence:** high for the relational observation; low for broader model.

### Hazuki Ren @ S1E01

## S1E06 boundary

## S2E04 boundary - leadership transfer, type-fit exclusion, and two new members

##### 6. Shibuya Kanon — decentering formal office without withdrawing responsibility

#### 6.1 The group already perceives her as leader

Kinako's `かのん先輩が部長だと思ってたっす` is revealing because it comes from a newcomer. Kanon's de facto group position is legible from outside.

>
> `だからこそ 新しくなろうとしている`
>

The logic is generative rather than self-denigrating. There is no `足手まとい`, no claim that she lacks worth, and no attempt to disappear from the group. She is still choosing how authority should be distributed.

This matters because “Kanon refuses leadership” would be too broad. S1E10-S1E11 already showed that she can accept visible responsibility when the task fits. Here the task is **institutional office in a group entering a new cohort phase**. Kanon thinks another person should carry it.

S2E04 contains no new competitive failure or talent-comparison event. Kanon does not continue the S2E03 `たまたま` / `何も結果は残してない` line of thought in current story time. She participates normally in group governance and recruitment.

The correct transition is therefore not “resolved.” It is:

> **S2E03 result-discounting is not current foreground state in S2E04; recurrence under an equivalent trigger remains OPEN.**

This preserves trigger specificity.

#### 6.5 Kanon remains part of Chisato's leadership even when not president

Kanon's earlier childhood words become a causal resource in Chisato's present decision. That is an important form of influence which does **not** require Kanon to hold office in the present scene.

S2E04 therefore adds a distinction useful for later character simulation:

> **formal decentering can coexist with durable relational influence.**

##### 7. Yoneme Mei — hidden fandom, type-based self-exclusion, and co-presence

#### 7.1 Mei's interest is not uncertain

At this boundary, “Mei is not interested” is not a viable descriptive model. It is a **defensive public claim**.

#### 7.2 `たまたま` is a denial device, but not Kanon's exact mechanism

Mei says the live encounter was `たまたま`, then repeats that she merely happened to be there.

This creates an interesting formal recurrence with Kanon's S1E03/S2E03 `たまたま`. But the mechanisms must not be collapsed.

- **Kanon:** uses `たまたま` to discount positive evidence because accepting it would increase the legitimacy/stakes of her own aspiration.

Common function: **self-protective downgrading of evidence**.

Her later argument is not “I need more practice” or “Liella! is too advanced.” It is:

She treats social presentation and temperament as prior proof of non-eligibility.

This is precisely why the S2E01 prestige solution is insufficient for her. She does not need the seniors to explain that they were once beginners. She needs permission to stop treating her own current self-image as a final classification.

Mei's speech includes:

- `ね～よ`

- `～ちまう`

Yet the same person repeatedly protects Shiki from isolation, joined the science club without interest in science, worries about Shiki repeating middle-school social isolation, and later admits that Shiki's proximity would help her try.

The rough register must therefore not be modeled as a simple hostility marker.

#### 7.5 The decisive line is relational rather than self-esteem based

Mei does not end by saying “I am cute after all” or “my personality is actually idol-like.” She says:

> `四季が近くにいてくれたら…`
>
> `頑張れそうな気がするんだ。`

This is a much stronger endpoint because it does not require false self-certainty. She can try while still embarrassed, rough-spoken, and unconvinced that she matches an imagined idol type.

The result **STRENGTHENS** the series' recurring relational-agency model: support can change what action is possible without erasing the actor's uncertainty.
