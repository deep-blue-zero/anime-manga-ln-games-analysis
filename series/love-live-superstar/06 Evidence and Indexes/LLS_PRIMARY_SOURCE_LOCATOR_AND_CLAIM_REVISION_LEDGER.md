| Transition | Meaning |

## 2. Evidence and adjudication contract

A material record states:

1. The exact claim and target artifact, its original episode/disclosure horizon, and whether the challenge is factual, inferential, interpretive or a value judgment.
2. Source object/edition/track, calibrated time range and retrievable cue/frame/shot or existing event reference; the relevant AV inspection/calibration record when performance or motion bears load.
3. The observation separated from its proposed explanation; actual lane, measurement or reported perception, resolution/confidence, focused re-view and any required attribution corroboration.
4. Material contrary evidence and at least the serious rival explanation, including differences in knowledge, chronology, audience, diegesis, source wording or production convention where relevant.
5. The integrator's reasoned transition, current formulation, exact canonical home and boundary, rather than reliance on model confidence or shared agreement.
6. Effects on the five cumulative ledgers, checkpoint deltas, qualification record, monographs and specialists; each affected dependency is updated or explicitly pending before the corresponding completion claim.

An integrator may contradict an inaccessible-channel report only with inspectable evidence of error, or request verification on concrete reliability grounds. A missing anchor, uncertain singer identity or inadequate sampling does not become true because an AV model repeated it. Follow the single return plus single independent verification route; unresolved disputes stay OPEN with dependent claims provisional.

Exact Japanese wording and lyrics remain text-witness claims. A listening divergence is recorded and verified, not silently substituted. Perceived delivery never directly establishes motive, inner state or an actor's intention. Mark retrospective full-series evidence as retrospective; it cannot be written back into an earlier prospective observation.
