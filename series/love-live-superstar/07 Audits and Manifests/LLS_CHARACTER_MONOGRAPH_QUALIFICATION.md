# Love Live! Superstar!! — Character Monograph Qualification

## Responsibility and assessment boundary

This is the mutable qualification and production-obligation record required by the [architecture](../00%20Frameworks/LoveLiveSuperstar_Multi_Document_Architecture_V2.md) and the [self-contained character monograph standard](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_PROJECT_INITIATION_AND_ARCHITECTURE_POLICY.md#self-contained-character-monograph-standard). It assesses whether existing admitted evidence supports a differentiated individual portrait. It does not supply those portraits, certify the earlier interpretations by a new source inspection, or assign reconstruction grades.

Every decision below has the same assessment boundary: **the accepted TV corpus through S3E08**. The evidence may concentrate in earlier episodes; no S3E09–S3E12 knowledge is used. The music backfill remains complete only through S3E02. This architecture review neither advances that boundary nor establishes new native-AV coverage.

The stable IDs here are project-local assessment identities for the television incarnation. They do not alter the global character registry. Name variation in earlier records, such as `Yonome` / `Yoneme Mei`, does not create another subject. Supporting characters are eligible on the same evidence test as Liella! members.

## Outcome and production rules

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `QUALIFIED_PENDING` | The reviewed evidence supports a differentiated person-level portrait; a monograph is required and outstanding. |
| `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY` | A real monograph exists and has passed separately recorded self-containment and fidelity review at its declared boundary. |
| `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` | The reviewed individual evidence does not yet support a differentiated portrait; preserve that evidence and reassess at the named trigger. |
| `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION` | A material accepted finding challenges qualification or the portrait; retain the prior decision and explain the issue until resolved. |

An unassessed character has no outcome. Do not substitute `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE` for an incomplete assessment. A qualified character need not have an exhaustive biography, tested crisis behavior, or equally rich evidence in every monograph dimension. Unknowns remain explicit. Conversely, membership, prominence, a recurring role, or a shared group argument does not alone qualify a person.

**Current production state:** 14 assessed subjects are `QUALIFIED_PENDING`; three are `DEFERRED_EVIDENCE`. No monograph is created or accepted by this assessment. For every record below, `monograph_path: NONE_NOT_CREATED`, `self_containment_review: NOT_PERFORMED`, and `fidelity_review: NOT_PERFORMED`. These shared fields apply individually to every listed ID; replace them with an actual path and two distinct review results only when that work exists. The character layer remains incomplete while any qualified obligation is outstanding, and the supporting-cast review below is also unfinished.

## Monograph acceptance and maintenance

Each qualified portrait follows all fourteen evidence-bounded responsibilities in the governing standard: identity/circumstances; history and disclosure timing; psychology; cognition/decisions; behavior; communication; relationships; ordinary preferences; source-supported intimate/romantic psychology where applicable; capabilities/limits; development; conditional operational profile; uncertainty; and traceability. Unknown or untested dimensions remain visible. A linked ledger cannot carry missing personality, relationship or speech explanation for the monograph.

Before changing any outcome to `COMPLETE_AT_BOUNDARY`, record a real monograph path and two separately assessed results: **self-containment** (the document alone supports a faithful bounded understanding) and **fidelity** (its claims survive source and current-claim checks). A fresh reader's question-based check is proportionate; central portraits merit an independent monograph-only retrieval probe when useful. Neither check creates a reconstruction capability grade. Preserve the prior assessment when later findings require `REASSESS_AFTER_REVISION` or a successor portrait.
