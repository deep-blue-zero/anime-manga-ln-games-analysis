## 3. Standing analytical responsibilities

Use the following seven responsibilities, assigning actual workers by evidence need and the available verified runtime. They are not seven redundant general summaries or a requirement that every specialist watch every second. The principal alone has mandatory whole-episode coverage; each specialist/reviewer inspects the diagnostic scope needed for its own claims.

| Responsibility | Source work and contribution | Ownership limit |
|---|---|---|
| Independent principal and run integrator | Whole episode in order; narrative/character/ethical argument; reconcile all significant run-local findings and counterexamples | Sole writer of candidate reading and run-local cumulative deltas; no old-corpus access or shared-ledger write-through before cross-run release |
| Music and dramaturgy | Songs/fragments, composition/rehearsal/performance, M0–M3, segment diegesis, allocation, creative agency, causal consequences | Assigned observations and proposed event/delta records |
| Voice and character acting | Contextual spoken/sung delivery, interaction, pauses/overlap, voice/body alignment and mismatch | Observations and conditional character consequences; no actor-intention claims |
| Visual direction and choreography | Gesture/gaze paths, camera/editing, spatial relations, formation and listener reactions | Distinguish observed paths, sampled endpoints and measured timing |
| Score, ambience and sound | Underscore, offscreen sound, bridges, silence, acoustic space and recurrences | Source-grounded sound observations; no unsupported track identity |
| Character, relationships and Japanese language | Exact wording/register, practical reasoning, ordinary preferences, directed behavior and developmental state | Text-grounded verification and person-level evidence retained for monographs |
| Independent critical reviewer | Inspect decisive source intervals; challenge attribution, causality, missing evidence, lost detail, future leakage and rival readings | Independent review and dispositions; never ceremonial approval |

The default for a substantive episode has a principal, at least one genuinely independent specialist, and a normally distinct independent critic. Combine specialist responsibilities only when adequate coverage and depth remain recoverable; name who actually covered each one. There is no standing reduced configuration for an ordinary television episode. A separately scoped short relay follows its own proportional review contract and cannot be misrepresented as a whole-episode reading.

The repository-aware coordinator is an additional setup/publication responsibility, not a source-reading contribution. During the independent phase, that owner alone maintains current routing and coverage metadata from verified receipts; the old interpretive ledgers remain untouched. It supplies no feedback based on old interpretations to independent workers and does not choose their initial questions to steer them toward old theses. The independent principal's semantic review uses the run's own source-aware specialists and critic. After the scoped cross-run freeze, designate one current-corpus reconciler; this can be the principal only after the exposure change is recorded. Each artifact still has exactly one mutable owner.

Use stable reasoning classes from architecture Section IV.5, resolving actual models and effort at runtime. `/swarm`, when available and verified in Antigravity, may orchestrate this configuration under [AV protocol Section 2.1](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md#21-antigravity-swarm-execution-route). Distinguish observed source coverage, accepted run-local units, committed high-water mark and integrated current authority. Pending or failed validation is not publication completion.
