# Love Live! Superstar!! — Audiovisual Reconciliation Profile V2.4

This profile implements the [corpus AV reconciliation protocol](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) for the [governing V2 method](LoveLiveSuperstar_Analytical_Method_V2.md) and [continuous-AV companion](LoveLiveSuperstar_Continuous_AV_Companion_Method_V2.md). The [architecture](LoveLiveSuperstar_Multi_Document_Architecture_V2.md) assigns canonical homes. The existing [current-state entrypoint](../01%20Source%20Lock%20and%20Inventory/LLS_CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md) controls the present frontier and gates; the [execution plan](../07%20Audits%20and%20Manifests/LLS_V2_4_AV_EXECUTION_PLAN.md) prepares the independent run.

V2.4 adopts these obligations for newly undertaken source readings, declared AV re-audits and the proposed independent reading. It does not retrospectively certify native AV, multi-agent participation or independence for existing readings. The already completed portions of the V2.3 musical backfill and existing frozen season checkpoints retain their historical status. Earlier units are reopened only for a material evidence gap or an authorized reread, not to populate an empty coverage column.

The initial V2.4 scaffold has no new AV observations, accepted transitions or inspected episodes. The candidate independent run is `NOT_STARTED`. The preparation gate does not authorize source execution. The existing S3E09 continuation hold remains in force for the legacy sequence; naming an independent folder, another provider or a noncurrent draft does not remove it. Any separately bounded full-36 run must be activated truthfully under the entrypoint, initiation and continuation rules without silently extending the legacy run.

## 2. Two reconciliation routes

### 2.1 Bounded relay against an existing reading

The bundle/text reading author identifies material audiovisual questions and prepares separate neutral-observation and withheld-adjudication packets. The AV reviewer inspects its assigned source intervals before seeing the claims, freezes its log, then adjudicates. The integrator verifies the return, resolves or preserves disputes, and alone writes accepted deltas to cumulative homes. A bounded evidence-completion relay is not automatically a complete replacement episode reading.

### 2.2 Independent AV-primary reading, then comparison

The independent run uses its own authorized chronological frontier, fresh whole-unit principal/run integrator and source-aware specialists/critic. Its principal context is separate from the repository-aware setup coordinator; only the latter inspects old ledgers and interpretive frameworks during setup. The independent principal owns candidate readings and run-local deltas, while the coordinator maintains current routing from receipts without feeding prior interpretations back into the run. It develops a literary reading as well as AV observations. Text and measurement routes verify Japanese wording, lyrics, attribution and precision. Within each episode, independent observations freeze before participants compare interpretations; the principal then produces the accepted run-local reading and exit state.

There is a second, **cross-run comparison barrier**. Keep existing V2 episode interpretations, music backfill, cumulative theses and season conclusions away from the independent workers until the independent scope's episode readings, season checkpoints and retrospective synthesis are frozen. If the authorized scope is all three seasons, that means the independent 36-episode run and its full-series synthesis, not merely each episode's first log. Earlier scope-limited release is a declared change in experimental design; do not continue calling later work independent of material already exposed.

This arrangement tests what the AV run notices without prompting from V2. It creates no second current authority. Candidate artifacts retain noncurrent status until their declared responsibilities are accepted through the project architecture. Reconciliation maps useful findings into the existing episode/erratum, ledger and synthesis homes, preserving independent originals and historical boundaries. Do not overwrite a frozen V2 reading with the independent reading. Do not expose an unfinished legacy prospective account to later independent-run findings and then report it as unexposed.

## 3. Claim classes and required coverage

Queue by **episode**, with stable event/scene/claim IDs and internal segments where the question changes. A season may batch delivery or integration, but cannot erase missing episode coverage. An independent principal requires full-episode synchronized AV; specialist and bounded relay inspection is proportional to its responsibility. For a targeted interval include its setup, the event and its immediate consequence when causality is at issue.

| Claim or responsibility | Required inspection and verification | Granularity and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Every newly analyzed or re-audited **M2/M3 musical event** | Continuous AV of the material event and context; Japanese text for wording/lyrics; claim-level review of performance form and consequence | Event plus consequential internal segments. State-changing classification is tested, not presumed true |
| Load-bearing spoken or sung delivery: breath, voice quality, prosody, interaction or register realization | Verified auditory observation; synchronized AV when the claim relates delivery to face, body, cut or action | Line/interaction and within-character comparison; linguistic register remains text-grounded |
| Bodily performance, gaze/gesture trajectory, choreography or changing formation | Continuous AV at adequate effective resolution, with targeted re-view | Scene or performance segment; endpoints alone do not establish a path |
| Sound–image or music–gesture synchronization | Calibrated AV, plus adequate frame-level inspection or measurement for precise claims | Each asserted relation; ±0.5 s navigation does not certify subsecond synchronization |
| Diegetic status, playback versus live performance, subjective/presentation-space transitions | AV plus visible source/listener evidence and relevant dialogue | Segment-level classification with explicit uncertainty; track credit and camera focus do not resolve diegesis |
| Offscreen/unmarked speaker or singer attribution | Corroborating text, visible lip movement, established source-grounded voice contrast or another adequate route | Individual claim; uncertain ensemble allocation stays open; real-actor recognition is excluded |
| Load-bearing score, ambience, silence, sound bridge or reprise | Verified audio/AV; measurements where needed; inspect each compared occurrence | Cue/scene and recurrence pair; similar mood is not proof of musical identity |
| Exact Japanese wording, lyrics, address, credit or onscreen text | Primary-language text or legible source frame; AV may flag divergence | Cue/line/frame. Model listening transcription never silently replaces the text witness |
| Exact pause, onset, shot duration, energy or acoustic similarity | Deterministic measurement with source/stream/interval and method recorded | `AM`; perceived timing remains approximate and measurement does not establish emotion |
| Static composition or costume detail | Adequate retained frame or anchored AV observation | No blanket AV requirement if the claim is genuinely static |
| M0/M1 music and non-load-bearing AV description | Proportional screen; compact record for useful recurrence or context | Do not inflate incidental presence into a compulsory full event essay; escalate if it becomes load-bearing |
| Narrative, motive, relationship, institution or thematic inference | Source-grounded argument using the relevant channels, independent critical review and counterevidence | AV does not confer special authority on an interpretation of inner state or theme |

For older episodes, distinguish **historical coverage** from **newly requested coverage**. A row requesting new AV verification is not a claim that the historical artifact was invalid when accepted. Conversely, an old `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED` decision does not satisfy a newly adopted required AV question. Record which claim now needs which evidence and why.

## 4. Dispatch packets and independent observation

The coordinator keeps expected interpretations out of neutral briefs, shared boards and filenames. Even the governing method's hypothesis section and a cumulative ledger can reveal the answer under review. The execution plan and companion specify how to send all applicable controls without those interpretive contents.

### 4.1 Neutral packet

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `queue_id` | Stable new AV queue ID, linked later to the existing claim/event ID |
| `episode` and `source_identity` | Authorized episode, edition, continuous object and text/bundle witnesses |
| `frontier` and `exposure_boundary` | Permitted earlier evidence; later evidence and prior-run interpretations withheld |
| `question` | An observable question without an expected answer, thesis or character-state verdict |
| `channel` | Delivery, movement, editing, music, diegesis, sound–image, attribution or another defined responsibility |
| `interval` and `context` | Declared-clock bounds and verified cues/frames, or explicitly unknown until located |
| `requirement` | `REQUIRED`, `OPTIONAL` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, with the assigned responsibility |
| `escalation_state` | Targeted or full-episode AV requirement; not an inspection result |
| `output_home` | Assigned contribution/transaction record; no shared-ledger write permission |

Neutral questions suited to this series include: “Which voices, bodies and listeners are identifiable in each segment, and what evidence identifies them?”; “Does the apparent acoustic source or listening situation change across this sequence?”; “What changes in delivery and bodily response across the exchange?”; and “What does the soundtrack do before, during and after this decision?” These are templates, not findings about any episode. Avoid an expected state label such as “How does the song heal the group?”

### 4.2 Withheld adjudication packet

After the required logs are frozen, release the matching `queue_id`, existing `claim_ref`, exact or faithfully bounded claim, material disconfirmation condition, relevant reading sections and affected cumulative homes. A new independent reading has no prior-run claim comparison during its blind phase; its candidate interpretations are released internally only after the unit's observation barrier, and V2 claims only after the separate cross-run barrier.

Record each participant's actual exposure, context separation, inspected intervals, capability probe, clock calibration, sampling limit and freeze reference. Observations added after release are explicitly post-exposure. A shared board instruction or a different model name is not evidence that the required independence occurred. If it did not, retain the observations with `blind_order_achieved: false` and satisfy the affected independent role through a fresh source-aware context before claiming completion.

## 5. Adjudication and disputed observations

Every queued existing claim receives a disposition, including unchanged ones:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Queue ID, existing claim/event locator and contributor |
| Transition | `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT` or `OPEN` |
| Basis | Observation IDs, calibrated inspected interval and anchor, actual inspection route, source/knowledge boundary |
| Reliability | `REPORTED` or `ANCHORED`, relevant sampling/precision, re-view and corroboration |
| Argument | Reason for disposition, contrary evidence, plausible alternative including production convention |
| Result | Bounded formulation; affected episode/ledger/synthesis homes and remaining obligation |

`REPORTED` is an observer's evidentiary report. `ANCHORED` requires supporting observations tied to calibrated intervals and cues/frames, successful focused re-view, adequate resolution, and any required corroboration. High confidence, repeated wording, majority agreement or an agent's self-declared expertise does not supply those conditions. Load-bearing claims require adequately anchored observations. New findings go into a separate proposed-findings record until there is an existing claim to revise.

An integrator must take an observation from a channel it cannot inspect seriously without treating it as infallible. Distinguish:

- **Contradiction:** inspectable evidence of a clock/locator failure, internal inconsistency, conflict with the source text or available frame, or a claim outside the lane's permissions.
- **Verification request:** a material reliability concern, such as missing anchors, inadequate sampling, uncertain singer attribution, conflated scenes or independently conflicting AV reports, which the integrator cannot settle directly.

Return a material dispute to the original reviewer once with a specific question and grounds. If unresolved, permit one independent verification route: a fresh blinded AV session, higher-resolution targeted inspection, deterministic measurement or identified human viewing/listening. Evaluate that result by the same source and precision requirements. Continued disagreement stays `OPEN`; do not loop until a preferred interpretation wins. Record every route and the consequence for dependent claims. A narrower supported claim may replace an overbroad one, but the gap cannot be silently renamed optional.

When audio appears to differ from the Japanese text, use `SUBTITLE_DIVERGENCE_SUSPECTED` with the interval and route the wording question to text verification. Avoid a private, unrecorded correction to the source witness. The affected exact-wording claim waits for resolution or is narrowed explicitly.

## 6. Single-owner integration and canonical homes

The designated integrator performs receiver acceptance under the [handoff contract](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_ANALYTICAL_HANDOFF_AND_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md), checking complete outputs, source and exposure bounds, hashes/revisions and drift from the handed-off base. Only that owner updates shared cumulative documents and the entrypoint. This shared-ledger and entrypoint write rule applies to a bounded relay or other already-admitted targeted reconciliation (§2.1), and to the independent run only after its cross-run comparison barrier has frozen (§2.2). Before that freeze, the independent principal owns the candidate readings and run-local deltas; the repository-aware coordinator alone updates current routing in the entrypoint from neutral receipts; neither writes independent interpretations into the canonical ledgers. Specialists retain distinct assigned contributions, not independent copies of the canonical ledgers.

| Responsibility | Canonical destination after acceptance |
|---|---|
| Episode-local reading | Established episode home; frozen readings receive attributable addenda or errata under the architecture |
| Cross-run claim transition, earlier formulation and revision chain | [Primary-source locator and claim-revision ledger](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_PRIMARY_SOURCE_LOCATOR_AND_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md) |
| AV coverage, source/clock references, independent freezes, queues and links to adjudications | [AV evidence and reconciliation index](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_AV_EVIDENCE_AND_RECONCILIATION_INDEX.md) |
| Current developmental state | [Character-state ledger](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_CHARACTER_STATE_LEDGER.md) |
| Conditional decisions, ordinary behavior, exceptions and counterexamples | [Behavior and decision ledger](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_CHARACTER_BEHAVIOR_AND_DECISION_LEDGER.md) |
| Japanese speech behavior and verified performed delivery | [Character voice-model ledger](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_CHARACTER_VOICE_MODEL_LEDGER.md) |
| Directional interpersonal conditioning and repair | [Relationship-conditioning matrix](../06%20Evidence%20and%20Indexes/LLS_RELATIONSHIP_CONDITIONING_MATRIX.md) |

| Cross-episode score/sound or visual recurrence | Architecture-owned evidence/index home, linked to observations and argument rather than a duplicate event history |
| Mature person-level portrait and specialist argument | Architecture-owned individual monographs and existing specialist synthesis package after their dependencies and reviews |

Preserve earlier formulations, event IDs, evidence locators and historical source frontiers. A targeted current-ledger change records its reason and transition; a new AV result does not retrospectively become evidence that an earlier analyst heard or saw it. Correct frozen episode/checkpoint claims through attributable revisions without changing frozen bytes. The claim-revision ledger owns the transition history; the AV index points to it rather than maintaining a competing history.

Do not write accepted full-series findings into a still-prospective state without a declared retrospective boundary. If the independent scope extends beyond the legacy frontier, distinguish its accepted run-local coverage from the historical V2 high-water mark and the project's eventual reconciled coverage. Technical inventory, independent inspection, accepted sequential reading and integrated synthesis are different facts.

## 7. Completion, recovery and publication

For each episode or bounded relay, preserve a transaction receipt with participant assignments, actual sources and intervals, independent-observation freezes, exposure exceptions, significant specialist findings, critic challenges, dispositions, required gaps, ledger/claim write-through and the next permitted operation. Keep actual observed model/effort settings there; leave unknowns unknown. Use existing handoff stage states independently from analytical authority status.

Complete the declared transaction only when its required source coverage, genuine independent roles where applicable, critical examination, material disagreement handling and cumulative updates are satisfied. The source-facing reading must retain useful detail and counterexamples supplied by specialists. Perform the bounded name-and-reference check from handoff Section 15 before semantic acceptance.

Completion has separate levels:

1. A participant has frozen its assigned observation log.
2. The unit's required analysis and review have been accepted, with an accurate coverage statement.
3. The authorized independent run has reached its terminal boundary and frozen its scoped retrospective outputs.
4. Cross-run comparison and required claim-level verification have been integrated into the designated current homes.
5. The architecture's corpus-release requirements, including character monographs and specialist synthesis, are satisfied.

No level silently confers the next. An unresolved mandatory AV finding blocks the corresponding AV-complete claim even if the narrative prose is polished. Optional questions may remain open when the artifact's declared responsibility is met. Completion of the independent run neither removes the legacy continuation hold nor declares the whole V2 corpus finished.

On interruption, recover the last durable accepted unit, source/exposure frontier, actual written outputs and unresolved requirements before resuming. Do not infer completion from an agent's last message or repeat an ambiguous write blindly. Full working contributions stay recoverable until their substance is accepted and durably preserved; public Git receives sanitized analytical records, not raw source media or private execution payloads.

Publish through the live repository checklist on the continuing series branch. Semantic acceptance, an author commit, a successful exact-head repository audit and merge to `main` are distinct events. This profile changes no repository-global controls and creates no automation, credentials or paid-service authorization.
