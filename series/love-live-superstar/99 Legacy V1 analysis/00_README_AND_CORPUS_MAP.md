## Authority and purpose

This directory preserves the recoverable first-pass analytical corpus from the archived conversation and later manual recovery of document-block responses copied directly from the original chat. It is **historical/legacy material**, not current analytical authority. The active V2 project is routed by `LLS_CURRENT_STATE_AND_CORPUS_MAP.md` in the parent Love Live! Superstar!! analytical root.
