## 10. Initial falsification register for R2–R5

These are **candidate hypotheses to test**, not current conclusions.

### H1 — service/competence and attachment are coupled

Candidate: Taihou converts attachment into anticipatory practical service, not only declarations of desire.

Falsify with: repeated evidence that service collapses outside erotic/possessive framing or that she is operationally careless when performing the secretary role.

### H2 — attachment-linked hyper-attunement can override consent/boundary checks

Candidate: monitoring and intrusion arise because she treats knowing/serving the Commander as overriding ordinary privacy constraints.

Falsify with: evidence of generalized boundary ignorance or unrelated intrusive behavior across peers.

### H3 — uncertainty about being wanted is a major destabilizer

Candidate: explicit doubt about whether her devotion is welcomed can produce more severe dysregulation than ordinary rivalry.

Falsify with: stronger collapse in unrelated competence/failure contexts, or repeated easy tolerance of attachment uncertainty.

### H4 — jealousy/exclusivity is relationship-specific rather than universal hostility

Candidate: intense rivalry activates around perceived competition for the Commander while Taihou remains capable of ordinary peer affiliation/cooperation elsewhere.

Falsify with: broad hostility, sabotage, or inability to sustain peer relations independent of romantic competition.

### H5 — Taihou has real peer-social competence despite a Commander-centric preference

Candidate: she may prefer the Commander/solitude yet can converse, ally, joke, compete, and be drawn into activities by peers.

Falsify with: peer scenes showing systematic social incapacity rather than preference/intensity.

### H6 — romantic rivalry does not erase competence recognition

Candidate: Taihou can acknowledge a rival's superior experience or other strengths while still opposing her relationally.

Falsify with: repeated prestige-protective denial whenever a rival is salient.

### H7 — care-giving exceeds self-care

Candidate: Taihou readily optimizes the Commander's comfort while underweighting her own rest/body needs.

Falsify with: robust self-protective stopping rules or evidence that the memory's overextension is premise-specific.

### H8 — intrusive/deceptive behavior is functionally attachment-linked

Candidate: duplicate keys, observation, concealment, and related maneuvers should be decomposed by attachment/service function rather than treated as generalized manipulativeness.

Falsify with: sophisticated instrumental deception across unrelated professional/peer domains.

### H9 — attachment threat can produce withdrawal and self-condemnation, not only aggression

Candidate: Taihou's high-intensity attachment system has an inward-collapse pathway when she believes she herself is the unwanted problem.

Falsify with: repeated externalization/blame/aggression under analogous rejection cues.

### H10 — explicit reassurance from the attachment figure has unusually high regulatory power

Candidate: direct confirmation that the Commander wants/needs her rapidly restores approach behavior after relational doubt.

Falsify with: later established contexts where reassurance fails, intensifies distrust, or has little effect.
