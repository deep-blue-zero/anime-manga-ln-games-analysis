## Current analytical questions

- practical service is genuinely competent and attachment-linked rather than merely theatrical;
- Taihou understands privacy, work, and command constraints but can selectively underweight them when Commander access/service is highly valued and assumed welcome;
- peer social competence is real despite Commander-centric preference;
- rivalry does not erase comparative competence judgment;

- external competitor/harasser cues can produce outward territorial escalation, while self-as-unwanted appraisal can produce withdrawal and self-condemnation;
- self-authored performance and erotic initiative are organized competence domains rather than universal loss of control.

R3 now resolves several of the former longitudinal questions:

- H8 Commander-specific tracking/access/collection behavior persists even in secure Dorm3D intimacy, while generalized cross-domain manipulation remains unsupported;
- H10 Commander reassurance/authorization is now strongly supported as a recurrent short-term regulator of acceptance uncertainty.

R5 resolves the central adversarial questions as follows: historical N5 mutual indispensability is **REVISE + BOUND** into reciprocal consequentiality with selective dependency engineering; selective boundary weighting is **STRENGTHEN + SPECIFY**; care-receiving is **PRESERVE + DOWNGRADE GENERALIZATION**; long-duration rivalry is **PRESERVE + BOUND**; peer criticism, serious professional failure, acute hard refusal, and other high-stakes edges remain OPEN; generalized manipulation remains unsupported outside Commander attachment.
