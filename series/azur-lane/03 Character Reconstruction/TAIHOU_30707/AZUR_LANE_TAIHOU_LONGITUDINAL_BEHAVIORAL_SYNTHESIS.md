# Azur Lane - Taihou Longitudinal Behavioral Synthesis

## 0. Purpose

This artifact is Phase R3 of Taihou's V1 character reconstruction. R2 established a narrative-bounded conditional model from the seven-part character memory and the full 28-scene CN narrative surface. R3 asks a stricter question:

> Which R2 mechanisms remain explanatory when Taihou is observed across short-form character text, peer/social interaction, public and group settings, combat lines, affinity/oath material, and the separately tagged Dorm3D ordinary-life/intimacy layer?

R3 is therefore a falsification and stabilization pass, not a volume-of-quotes pass. Evidence is weighted by context, independence, causal clarity, and source responsibility. Repeated Commander-facing skin lines do not outvote sustained narrative counterexamples merely because they are numerous. Dorm3D is not treated as 125 independent votes for a global trait. Skin contexts are not forced into one literal chronology. Regional JP/EN/TW/KR wording and JP performed voice remain downstream responsibilities.

Final R3 verdict:

`TAIHOU_R3_LONGITUDINAL_SYNTHESIS_PASS_WITH_MUTUAL_INDISPENSABILITY_STATE_DEPENDENT_CARE_RECIPROCITY_AND_PERSISTENT_ATTACHMENT_INTRUSION`

The R2 architecture survives, but R3 materially revises three areas:

1. Taihou's service system is better modeled as one half of a broader drive toward **mutual indispensability**, not merely one-way usefulness.
2. Boundary overreach persists even in high-security intimacy and even when Taihou can reflect on being excessive. The missing variable is therefore not simple perspective-taking capacity; it is how much behavioral weight she gives autonomy constraints relative to attachment goals.
3. Care-receiving is strongly state-dependent. Early secretary-role rest can feel like loss of usefulness/proximity, while secure established intimacy can support easy acceptance of care and visible relaxation.

# 2. Executive longitudinal model

R3 supports the following compact model.

Taihou has a highly salient Commander attachment, but she is not globally organized by one undifferentiated state called obsession. She repeatedly classifies situations by:

1. whether Commander attachment is currently relevant;
2. whether the relationship feels secure or uncertain;
3. whether a legitimate task, authority, or welfare constraint is salient;
4. whether another person is merely present, a familiar peer, or a meaningful attachment competitor;
5. whether Taihou controls the interaction/presentation frame;
6. whether the setting is public, private, professional, combat, leisure, or established domestic intimacy.

Her most stable attachment objective is not best expressed as simple possession. Across service, gifts, performance, domesticity, tracking, reassurance, and contrived dependency, Taihou repeatedly seeks a relationship in which each side is difficult to replace.

She wants to be:

- the person who notices the Commander's needs first;
- the person who can solve burdens before they are voiced;
- the person whose performance captures his attention;
- the person with privileged access to his spaces, schedule, possessions, and memories;
- the person who is chosen over alternatives;
- the person whose absence or service would be felt.

But R3 adds the reciprocal side. She also creates situations in which **the Commander is needed by Taihou**:

- deliberately presenting herself as unable to open a bottle so that Commander help becomes necessary (`juustagram post:580`);
- asking for reassurance that she will not be forgotten (`DORM3DDAILYCONVERSATION12017`);
- accepting massage and physical care in established intimacy (`DORM3DDAILYCONVERSATION12045`);
- framing gifts, photographs, shared home space, role-play, and domestic routines as mutually constitutive relationship acts;
- repeatedly seeking explicit confirmation that Commander presence and choice are reciprocal.

The best current umbrella formulation is therefore:

> **Taihou seeks mutual indispensability through usefulness, attention capture, privileged access, reciprocal care, and repeated confirmation of exclusive or special choice.**

This does not mean she requires literal social isolation. R3 repeatedly shows functioning peer relations and negotiated shared attention. It means that Taihou is strongly motivated to make the Commander-Taihou bond uniquely consequential to both participants.

# 3. Commander-absent and ordinary peer contexts

The strongest anti-caricature evidence in R3 comes from moments where Commander attachment is absent or only background context.

## 3.1 Independent leisure exists

`juustagram post:48` is a small but high-value counterexample to totalization. Taihou independently posts that an outdoor hot spring in winter is excellent. Eldridge and San Diego reply in ordinary leisure terms. There is no Commander bid, no rivalry, and no service performance.

One short post cannot establish a large hobby profile, but it is direct evidence against:

`Commander absent -> Taihou has no ordinary reward state`

## 3.2 Ordinary peer attention is not always threat classification

Several social replies are mundane and socially competent:

- `post:135`: Taihou asks Roon whether she used break time to get pudding;
- `post:139`: Taihou notices Roon had disappeared backstage to care for a small animal;
- `post:584`: Taihou gives a blunt practical reaction to an overpowering scent and suggests ventilation.

These lines are important precisely because they are unremarkable. A robust simulator needs ordinary peer behavior, not only signature-character moments.

## 3.3 Albacore: resistant permeability, not stable hostility

Across the memory, later narrative, and social threads, Albacore repeatedly disrupts Taihou's preferred frame. Taihou can be startled, irritated, suspicious, or threatening when Commander access is implicated. Yet the relationship repeatedly resumes after the threat cue is removed.

`post:137-138` adds another low-stakes instance: Taihou distrusts Albacore's unusually special gift box, then expresses relief when no further surprise appears. Illustrious explicitly reads the relationship as close/good.

The longitudinal relationship rule is:

> Albacore can trigger resistance and attachment-protective escalation, but the relationship is durable enough to survive those activations. Threat removal often restores ordinary peer function quickly.

This is a better model than either "best friends, therefore threats are fake" or "threats occur, therefore the relationship is hostile."

## 3.4 Port integration is real

The R2 Yamashiro scene already showed Taihou acknowledging that she once thought only the Commander mattered but now finds the port comfortable and may try more activities. The social corpus independently supports that broader world through routine peer replies, group participation, shared performance, public festival discussion, and non-Commander leisure.

R3 therefore promotes the following from a narrative-specific observation to a longitudinal rule:

> **Commander centrality is a preference and attachment hierarchy, not evidence that Taihou lacks a broader social world. Familiarity can expand that world without displacing Commander centrality.**

# 4. Professional, task, and public contexts

## 4.1 Legitimate task salience remains a real brake

R2's strongest boundary correction came from `shanchenglifu2` and the `junheboss` exercise sequence: Taihou can choose not to interrupt the Commander's work and can comply with operational deployment even while verbally preferring Commander access.

R3 does not find evidence that overturns this. Instead, short-form material shows that Taihou often tries to **integrate** task and attachment rather than simply abandon task structure.

Examples include:

- mission/commission lines that route work through service;
- `DORM3D chat 30707106`, where the Commander says staying under the kotatsu would delay work and Taihou proposes moving the work materials home so both task completion and proximity can continue;
- domestic preparation lines where she handles weather, food, clothing, paperwork, and recovery in advance.

This suggests a more mature rule than simple obedience:

> When legitimate work constraints are salient, Taihou often searches for a solution that preserves both task completion and closeness. If the task cannot be reframed, she is capable of restraint/compliance.

## 4.2 Public attention does not automatically cause severe jealousy

Public/ensemble settings repeatedly expose Taihou to shared Commander attention without catastrophic escalation. R2's masquerade and shared-date examples survive the broader social evidence. Fleet Chat places the Commander inside a large Sakura social group; Taihou competes rhetorically for message volume and attention but remains inside the shared channel structure.

This means:

`many women present` is not a sufficient trigger.

More specific cues matter:

- a rival claiming privileged service;
- a rival implying superior intimacy;
- another person intruding into a Commander-only frame;
- evidence Commander may prefer another option;
- uncertainty about Taihou's own acceptance.

## 4.3 Fleet Chat as a boundary/reassurance micro-test

In `activity_chat:27`, Taihou boasts that she could easily generate 999+ message notifications for the Commander. Akagi labels this harassment; Amagi warns the group not to trouble him. The Commander then explicitly says it is not a burden and authorizes messages, promising to reply during rest.

This is analytically useful because it separates three components:

1. **peer correction** makes the possible burden/consent issue explicit;
2. **Commander authorization** resolves whether the behavior is welcome in that context;
3. Taihou does not enter `dafeng7`-style collapse because there is no sustained evidence that rejection is likely; the attachment figure immediately clarifies acceptance.

This strengthens H10 without pretending that all correction produces the same emotional severity.

# 5. Character-text longitudinal pass

## 5.1 Base affinity/oath preserves the intense early model

The base affinity progression contains some of Taihou's most severe short-form material:

- threat framing toward perceived "pests" near the Commander;
- claims to know everything about him because she watches continuously;
- disposal of another girl's gift;
- possession of the Commander's room key;
- the workaround that if casual entry is forbidden she can simply live with him;
- an oath formulation in which a ring not intended for Taihou would have been discarded.

These records confirm that R2 did not invent the access, surveillance, and territoriality problem. The same motifs are native to core character text.

At the same time, R3 must resist treating this one relationship channel as a complete global personality sample. The base layer is intentionally Commander-facing and is not peer-neutral evidence.

## 5.2 Self-authored presentation recurs across skins

Multiple independent skin contexts strengthen R2 N2:

- `307072` presents a deliberate strategy of ignoring earlier holidays so that Lunar New Year becomes a stronger attention-capture moment; Taihou learned appearance and performance skills from Yat Sen and evaluates the effort by whether the Commander's gaze lingers;
- `307073` explicitly emphasizes carefully prepared racing presentation and competition with other girls for visual attention;
- `307075-307078` repeatedly show Taihou constructing private-date, domestic, bridal, or leisure environments rather than passively waiting for romantic attention.

This makes "seductive" an inadequate trait description. Taihou's presentation is often **planned work**:

`desired impression -> preparation/learning -> staged encounter -> evaluate Commander attention -> adapt`

## 5.3 Secure intimacy is not uniformly confident

R3 also finds a useful counterexample to a one-note confident-seductress model. In `307075:feeling5`, when the Commander unexpectedly gives Taihou control over the rest of their date, she becomes flustered and starts planning how to use the time "effectively."

The important distinction is not simply romance versus no romance. Taihou is often most confident when she has authored the frame. Unexpectedly receiving open-ended relational agency can briefly disrupt that prepared confidence.

Provisional modifier:

> **Self-authored romantic frame -> high confidence; unexpected reciprocal opening -> possible planning-fluster even at high intimacy.**

## 5.4 Established intimacy does not erase exclusivity language

Later/high-security skin material continues to contain:

- desire for exclusive time;
- private "love nest" framing;
- wishes that others cannot intrude;
- fear reactions to Albacore appearing;
- combat defeat language centered on not being left by the Commander.

Therefore, relationship security should not be modeled as a simple cure for possessiveness. It changes how possession is expressed and regulated, but exclusivity fantasy remains available.

# 6. Combat and failure

The 14 combat records require special caution because many game voice lines are short, heightened, and skin-conditioned.

## 6.1 Combat violence is not always Commander-specific

Some battle lines explicitly fuse violence with Commander territory: obstacles between Taihou and Commander should be destroyed; enemies have interrupted a date or banquet; victory is offered to Commander.

But other battle lines are generic destruction language without a Commander target frame, including `307075:battle:0` and `307078:battle:0`.

Thus R3 rejects both extremes:

- "all Taihou violence is merely romantic jealousy";
- "violent words prove a global propensity for serious interpersonal harm."

Better rule:

> Combat gives Taihou a legitimate high-intensity register in which destructive language is easy to access. Attachment cues can personalize that register, but lexical violence alone does not determine ordinary-world intent.

## 6.2 Defeat responses are relationship-sensitive and heterogeneous

Defeat lines vary substantially:

- base `lose` uses a broad violent curse;
- `307075:lose:0` breaks into incomplete Commander-directed distress;
- `307078:lose:0` asks the Commander not to leave her.

This variation argues against one universal failure response derived from a single line. Failure can recruit aggression, attachment fear, or dysfluent distress depending on context.

## 6.3 Ordinary non-attachment failure remains under-observed

Dorm3D `12025` gives one minor performance-failure example: Taihou apologizes for poor battlefield performance, but the interaction quickly turns the proposed punishment into a Commander-pleasing intimate frame. This is useful for showing that failure need not automatically produce self-condemnation, but it is not a clean high-stakes professional failure test.

R3 therefore keeps severe non-attachment failure as OPEN.

# 7. Dorm3D as established-intimacy / ordinary-life stratum

Dorm3D is the largest new evidence layer in R3 and produces the most important revisions. It must nonetheless be interpreted as a local relationship state, not as universal baseline.

## 7.1 Domestic competence expands the service system

Dorm3D repeatedly extends secretary-style attentional care into ordinary domestic life:

- `12002`: records preferences to improve service;
- `12009`: monitors food/health and prepares preferred meals;
- `12010`: repairs a damaged shirt obtained through questionable access;
- `12018`: records pipa music so care can continue even when physically absent;
- `12029`: prepares bathing and recovery routines;
- `12031`: makes tableware herself for a special dinner;
- `12032`, `12048`, `12053`: notices fatigue/physical strain and offers targeted comfort;
- Dorm3D chat `30707109`: explicitly learned a massage technique in advance because she expected it to become useful.

This strengthens H1 while also showing why "service is only an excuse" remains false. The care is often practically competent.

## 7.2 Security does not extinguish access intrusion

Dorm3D decisively prevents an overly optimistic development model.

Even in a domestic/intimate stratum, Taihou still:

- states she will reach any place related to Commander by any method (`12001`);
- acquires and stores Commander clothing/objects (`12010`, `12012`, other collection references);
- takes the Commander's room key and converts retrieval into an intimate game (`12015`);
- saves every photograph and remembered utterance (`12027`);
- counts visit frequency (`12041`);
- proposes a shared home partly because access to his private belongings would become easier (`12049`).

This is strong longitudinal support for H8. Intrusive access behavior is not only an early-affinity artifact caused by insecurity. It remains available when attachment feels secure.

The function changes, however. In later intimacy the behavior is often folded into domesticity, erotic play, memorabilia, and shared-home construction rather than presented as covert courtship alone.

## 7.3 Meta-awareness exists without automatic self-correction

`DORM3DVIDEO1201` is especially diagnostic. After describing a nightmare and a situation in which she waited for the Commander in the rain, Taihou explicitly recognizes that he repeatedly tolerates her willfulness and asks whether being indulged might turn her into an excessive person.

This revises the earlier H2 formulation in an important way.

R2 had already rejected the idea that Taihou cannot represent the Commander's autonomy. R3 goes further: **she can sometimes recognize her own excess even while secure and accepted**, without a `dafeng7`-style rejection threat.

Yet the scene does not show durable self-limitation. She shifts responsibility playfully back toward the Commander and resumes intimacy.

Therefore:

`awareness of possible excess != sufficient behavioral brake`

The stronger mechanism is weighting:

`recognize boundary/autonomy concern`
`+ evaluate attachment reward / assumed permission / established pattern`
`-> concern may still lose the decision competition`

## 7.4 Reassurance has repeated regulatory power

Dorm3D gives H10 the longitudinal recurrence R2 lacked.

### `DORM3DDAILYCONVERSATION12008`
Taihou detects another girl's scent and immediately fears losing preferred status. The Commander states that he chooses only Taihou. Her uncertainty collapses rapidly; she expresses relief and redirects the state into renewed effort to remain his sole choice.

### `DORM3DDAILYCONVERSATION12017`
A nightmare that the Commander forgot her produces clingy proximity-seeking. The Commander explicitly says he will not forget her. Taihou immediately returns to confident acceptance.

### Fleet Chat
Possible burden is raised by peers; Commander explicitly authorizes contact. No inward collapse follows.

These are not identical experiments, but together they support a stable rule:

> **When distress is generated by uncertainty about Commander acceptance, memory, or choice, explicit credible Commander reassurance has unusually high short-term regulatory leverage.**

This does not establish durability under betrayal, hard rejection, or severe repeated conflict.

## 7.5 Care-receiving is state-dependent, not globally weak

`DORM3DDAILYCONVERSATION12045` is a direct counterexample to the strongest version of H7. Taihou says battle has made her tired. When the Commander offers massage, she accepts, guides the pressure, and reports relaxing deeply.

This matters because the memory's rest request produced loneliness and role disruption. The two scenes are not contradictory once relationship state and function are separated:

### Early secretary/rest frame
`care for Taihou = temporarily stop serving / lose role / lose proximity`
`-> emotionally costly`

### Secure established-intimacy frame
`care for Taihou = reciprocal closeness / Commander investment / no loss of belonging`
`-> easy acceptance and relaxation`

The revised rule is:

> **Taihou is not generally unable to receive care. Receiving care becomes difficult when it implies loss of usefulness, access, or relational role; it becomes easy when care itself confirms secure reciprocity.**

## 7.6 Domesticity is a strategy of relationship continuity

Dorm3D repeatedly converts space and routine into attachment infrastructure:

- shared home/family photograph fantasies;
- preparing weather gear and recovery space;
- moving work into the home so task and proximity can coexist;
- arranging food, clothing, tea, gifts, photos, and furniture around repeated togetherness;
- treating "our home" as a way to reduce separation and normalize access.

This is more than generic domestic affection. Taihou tries to make the relationship **structurally continuous**: a life in which the Commander's work, rest, possessions, memory, and Taihou's presence are increasingly integrated.

## 7.7 Established intimacy preserves separation sensitivity

Dorm3D hello/video material repeatedly shows discomfort when Commander visits are delayed or separation is anticipated. Examples include concern that she did something wrong, fear that he might not come, difficulty tolerating him eventually leaving, and anxiety during absence.

This provides recurrence for mild inward attachment insecurity, but it does not replicate the severity of `dafeng7`.

R3 therefore distinguishes:

- **ordinary separation sensitivity** - common, approach-seeking, reassurance-responsive;
- **self-as-unwanted collapse** - rarer, more severe, withdrawal/self-condemnation;
- **external competitor threat** - more outward and territorial.

# 8. Mutual indispensability: the major R3 addition

R2 correctly emphasized usefulness and privileged service. R3 shows that a one-directional caregiver model is incomplete.

Taihou appears to pursue **mutual indispensability** through two complementary strategies.

## 8.1 Make Taihou indispensable to Commander

Repeated means:

- anticipate needs;
- remove work burdens;
- prepare food/clothes/tea/recovery;
- learn performance or massage skills;
- remember preferences and schedules;
- create domestic infrastructure;
- become the person who already knows what is needed.

## 8.2 Make Commander indispensable to Taihou

Repeated or plausible means include:

- explicit inability-to-be-without-you language;
- direct reassurance requests;
- deliberate helplessness in `post:580`, where Taihou claims she cannot open a bottle and therefore "must" have Commander help;
- accepting care, massage, gifts, and reciprocal attention as proof of relationship;
- turning photographs, shared homes, gifts, and routines into evidence that the relationship is mutually constitutive.

The second channel explains why pure "competence" cannot define Taihou. She is capable of competence, but sometimes **chooses dependency as an attachment action**.

This suggests a new provisional rule, N5:

> **Taihou alternates between instrumental competence and selectively performed/accepted dependency when either route can increase reciprocal necessity and closeness.**

The evidence does not support a claim that every display of weakness is fake. It supports the narrower point that Taihou can strategically use need, just as she can strategically use usefulness.

# 9. Longitudinal state architecture after R3

The R2 architecture can now be expanded into a more simulation-useful state system.

## S0 - COMMANDER_SALIENCE_LOW / ORDINARY_PEER

Typical conditions:

- Commander absent or irrelevant to immediate exchange;
- no romantic competition;
- low stakes.

Expected behavior:

- ordinary conversation;
- blunt practical comment when appropriate;
- leisure interest;
- teasing/complaint with familiar peers;
- ability to notice and respond to others' activities;
- no requirement to inject Commander into every sentence.

Evidence: Juustagram `post:48`, `post:135`, `post:139`, `post:584`; R2 peer scenes.

Confidence: high that state exists; breadth of hobbies still medium.

## S1 - PROFESSIONAL / TASK-CONSTRAINED

Typical conditions:

- legitimate mission structure;
- Commander work or team objective clearly salient;
- authority/procedure legible.

Expected behavior:

- attachment preference may remain verbally visible;
- competent role execution remains possible;
- restraint/compliance can beat immediate access impulse;
- likely attempt to solve for both task completion and closeness if possible.

Evidence: `shanchenglifu2`, `junheboss1-5`, character-text mission/service lines, Dorm3D chat `30707106`.

Confidence: high for ordinary professional constraint; high-stakes value conflict open.

## S2 - SECURE_COMMANDER_SERVICE

Typical conditions:

- relationship assumed welcome;
- Taihou has a useful role or clear care opportunity.

Expected behavior:

- high attention to Commander state;
- anticipatory service;
- confident initiative;
- low hesitation about ordinary proximity;
- reward from usefulness;
- risk that "helpful" access becomes self-authorizing.

Evidence: memory, base secretary text, Dorm3D domestic care.

Confidence: high.

## S3 - SELF_AUTHORED_PRESENTATION / ATTENTION_CAPTURE

Typical conditions:

- Taihou controls or has prepared the romantic/public frame;
- Commander attention is a salient reward.

Expected behavior:

- deliberate costume/performance/environment design;
- high erotic or theatrical confidence;
- competitive comparison may rise;
- willingness to learn skills from others;
- use of humor, double meaning, staged circumstance, or spectacle.

Evidence: `307072`, `307073`, pipa and festival scenes, private-date scenes, Dorm3D gifts/role-play.

Confidence: high.

## S4 - TERRITORIAL_COMPETITION

Typical conditions:

- a person is classified as a meaningful Commander competitor or harasser.

Expected behavior:

- possession/claim rhetoric;
- comparative competition;
- increased surveillance/attention to rival behavior;
- potentially aggressive display;
- continued ability to recognize domain competence;
- negotiation/shared access remains possible when security and social framing permit.

Evidence: Akagi scenes, `huihua3`, `post:474`, `post:481`, base affinity lines.

Confidence: high for state; severe harm ceiling open.

## S5 - ACCEPTANCE_UNCERTAINTY / SELF_AS_PROBLEM

Typical conditions:

- credible evidence suggests Taihou herself may be unwanted, forgotten, or relationally devalued.

Expected behavior by severity:

- mild: clinginess, reassurance seeking, increased visit/proximity bids;
- stronger: negative reinterpretation of relationship evidence;
- severe observed case: withdrawal and self-condemnation;
- explicit credible Commander reassurance can rapidly restore approach.

Evidence: `dafeng7`; Dorm3D `12008`, `12017`, hello/video separation material.

Confidence: high for direction, medium for severity thresholds.

## S6 - ESTABLISHED_DOMESTIC_INTIMACY

Typical conditions:

- high relationship security/permission presumed;
- ordinary shared-life or home frame.

Expected behavior:

- domestic service and environment design;
- reciprocal physical intimacy;
- continuity-building through home, possessions, photos, food, routines;
- willingness to receive care;
- persistent tracking/collection/access behaviors rather than disappearance of those traits;
- strong exclusivity fantasy remains available.

Evidence: Dorm3D daily/chat/video/gift corpus; high-security skin contexts.

Confidence: high within this stratum; do not project to baseline stranger relationship.

## S7 - RECIPROCAL_CARE / MUTUAL_DEPENDENCY MODIFIER

This is best treated as a modifier that can occur inside S2 or S6 rather than a fully separate chronological stage.

Expected behavior:

- Taihou either provides care or accepts care when doing so confirms closeness;
- may deliberately create a small dependency opportunity;
- care is easier to receive when it does not remove her from relationship participation.

Evidence: `post:580`, Dorm3D `12045`, gifts, massage, reciprocal home routines.

Confidence: medium-high.

## Overlays independent of state

### PUBLIC / PRIVATE
Publicity affects performance style but does not map directly onto security. Taihou can be bold in public self-presentation and extremely bold in private frames.

### COMBAT
Combat permits a high-intensity destructive register. Attachment may personalize targets, but combat rhetoric is not automatically ordinary-world intent.

### THEATRICAL / GIMMICK PREMISE
Extreme wording can function as stage competition, erotic possession metaphor, or joke. Realized action must be inspected.

### FATIGUE / WELFARE
Fatigue can activate caregiving in either direction. Relationship security changes whether receiving care feels like loss of role or proof of reciprocity.

# 10. H1-H10 longitudinal transitions

## H1 - Attachment is enacted through useful service

**R3 transition: STRENGTHEN + DIFFERENTIATE.**

Preserved evidence:

- base secretary competence;
- repeated food, clothing, work, recovery, preference-recording, and domestic preparation;
- learned skills deployed for Commander welfare/attention;
- task competence outside romance remains visible from R2.

Revision:

Useful service is central but not exclusive. `post:580` and reciprocal-care material show that Taihou can also create or accept dependence in the opposite direction.

Current formulation:

> Taihou commonly turns attachment into practical usefulness and anticipatory care, but the larger relational objective is mutual indispensability rather than one-way service alone.

Confidence: high.

## H2 - Attachment certainty can suppress explicit boundary checking
