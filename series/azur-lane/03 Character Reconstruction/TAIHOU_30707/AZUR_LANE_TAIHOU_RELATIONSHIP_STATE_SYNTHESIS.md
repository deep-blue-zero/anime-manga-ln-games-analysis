# Azur Lane — Taihou R6 Relationship-State Synthesis

## Verdict

**`TAIHOU_R6_RELATIONSHIP_STATE_SYNTHESIS_PASS_WITH_NONCHRONOLOGICAL_COMMANDER_REGIMES_DYNAMIC_INTERLOCUTOR_FUNCTIONS_AND_RECIPROCAL_CONSEQUENTIALITY_BOUNDARIES`**

R6 confirms that the R5-validated Taihou model changes meaningfully by relationship without requiring a separate personality for each interlocutor. The principal result is structural: **relationship history, current relationship regime, and momentary interlocutor function must be modeled separately**.

A single person can occupy more than one active function. The Commander can simultaneously be attachment figure, professional authority, care target, reciprocal caregiver, evaluator of wantedness, audience for self-presentation, permission source, and object of access/collection behavior. Albacore can be an ordinary familiar peer, prank disruptor, boundary critic, social expander, or temporary attachment-threat cue depending on what she is doing in the current scene. Akagi can be a genuine romantic rival without being a permanent maximum-threat state. These distinctions are necessary to explain why the same durable relationship can support both conflict and rapid return to ordinary interaction.

R6 therefore rejects a simple model of:

`person identity -> fixed Taihou behavior`

and adopts:

`stable relationship history + momentary function + current security/constraint state -> relationship-conditioned behavior`.

The second major R6 result is that **relationship security and attachment intensity are not inverse variables**. Greater security can reduce some rejection fragility while increasing ordinary physical comfort, reciprocal care, domestic continuity, and self-authored intimacy. It does not automatically extinguish territorial fantasy, tracking, key/access behavior, memorabilia collection, visit counting, or separation sensitivity. Secure love does not function as a cure that converts Taihou into a low-intensity baseline personality.

The third major result is that the Commander architecture is best represented by **CMD0-CMD3 nonchronological relationship regimes**, not a canonical developmental ladder. The source contains affinity/oath and high-security materials that can constrain relationship-state interpretation, but skins and Dorm3D are not sufficient to force one literal sequence. R6 therefore treats CMD0-CMD3 as retrieval and simulation regimes that can be selected by the established facts of a scene.

N5 enters R6 only in its R5-bounded form: **reciprocal consequentiality with selective dependency engineering**. Taihou seeks a bond that is specially chosen, remembered, mutually invested in, difficult to treat as interchangeable, and structurally embedded. She can sometimes create a small need for Commander assistance, but reciprocal functional indispensability is not required in every secure state.

# 2. Relationship evidence is multidimensional

R6 separates ten dimensions that must not be collapsed into a single closeness score.

## 2.1 Attachment salience

Is the Commander relationship relevant to the immediate appraisal at all?

Low Commander salience permits ordinary peer, leisure, group, or task behavior without forced Commander insertion. High Commander salience activates service, wantedness, continuity, presentation, access, rivalry, or reassurance systems depending on the remaining variables.

## 2.2 Relationship security

Does Taihou currently believe she is wanted, chosen, remembered, and permitted?

Security affects approach confidence and the meaning of ambiguous evidence. It does not determine all behavior by itself. Secure Taihou can still be possessive or intrusive; insecure Taihou can become more boundary-sensitive because preserving acceptance becomes more important than immediate access.

## 2.3 Stable relationship history and familiarity

How much durable interaction history exists with this person?

Familiarity changes how quickly Taihou can classify teasing, predict habits, resume ordinary interaction after conflict, or treat a disruption as part of an established relationship rather than a stranger threat. Albacore is the strongest anchor.

## 2.4 Momentary interlocutor function

What is the person doing now?

Possible functions include:

- ordinary peer;
- familiar disruptor;
- boundary critic;
- social expander;
- technical or presentation mentor;
- legitimate professional authority;
- romantic/attention rival;
- Commander harasser;
- care partner;
- audience;
- public/group co-participant.

Stable identity does not guarantee a stable function.

## 2.5 Rival-threat intensity

Is the other person actually competing for privileged Commander access, service, intimacy, or preferred status?

`woman present` is not equivalent to `rival detected`. Public/group settings repeatedly contain women without maximum territorial activation. More specific cues—claims to superior intimacy, privileged service, displacement, intrusion into a Commander-only frame, or evidence the Commander may prefer another option—matter more.

## 2.6 Permission, privacy, and boundary salience

How explicit and legitimate is the constraint?

Taihou can understand privacy, work constraints, and standing rules. R5 adds the explicit base `feeling5` standing-rule loophole: she recognizes that casual entry to the Commander's room is disallowed and answers by proposing cohabitation as a way around the problem. This proves representation plus relational reframing, not ignorance.

Acute serious informed refusal remains OPEN.

## 2.7 Authority and task relevance

Does another person's legitimate role govern the current objective?

`junheboss1-5` establishes that Taihou can complain about a role that reduces Commander proximity while still accepting tactical assignment and completing reconnaissance. `shanchenglifu2` establishes voluntary non-interruption of Commander work. Authority matters when it is legible to the task; it is not blanket obedience.

## 2.8 Reciprocity / consequentiality

Does the interaction confirm that the relationship matters in both directions?

Examples include explicit reassurance, Commander care, shared routines, gifts, photographs, domestic planning, and occasional staged dependency. This dimension implements R5 N5-R5 without requiring literal functional indispensability.

## 2.9 Care direction and relational function

Is Taihou giving care, receiving it, or strategically inviting it?

Care is easiest to interpret when its relationship function is specified. Taihou frequently gives competent care. Receiving care can be easy in secure reciprocal intimacy (`12045`) but costly when care means losing a valued service/proximity role (`dafeng6-7`). Prolonged non-erotic dependency remains OPEN.

## 2.10 Audience, channel, and frame authorship

Is the interaction public, private, professional, theatrical, or domestic? Who authored the frame?

Publicity changes expression but does not directly determine security. Taihou can be bold in public performance, competitive in a group channel, intimate in private, and briefly flustered when the Commander unexpectedly hands her open-ended relational agency. Self-authorship remains a major fluency regulator.

# 4. Commander — multi-role primary attachment relationship

The Commander is Taihou's most structurally complex relationship. He simultaneously functions as:

- primary attachment figure;
- strongest source of wanted/chosen/remembered evaluation;
- recipient of anticipatory practical care;
- audience for self-authored presentation;
- target of surveillance, access engineering, collection, and memory preservation;
- legitimate work/command constraint in appropriate contexts;
- source of explicit authorization and reassurance;
- reciprocal caregiver in established intimacy;
- participant in gifts, photographs, domestic routines, and shared-life construction;
- central partner in reciprocal consequentiality.

These roles must not collapse into `love interest`.

Three Commander-specific asymmetries are especially important.

First, **his evaluation has unusually high state-switching power** when Taihou's uncertainty concerns whether she is wanted, remembered, chosen, or permitted. `dafeng7`, Dorm3D `12008`, `12017`, and Fleet Chat independently support rapid short-term regulation after explicit credible reassurance.

Second, **his legitimate task interest can restrain access without erasing attachment**. Taihou can wait outside his office because he is working, or accept an operational assignment that keeps her away from him.

Third, **his acceptance does not equal unlimited permission**. R6 retains R5's refusal boundary: high relationship security and prior indulgence are not sufficient evidence to predict response to a serious immediate informed no.

# 5. Commander relationship regimes — nonchronological by design

R6 preserves CMD0-CMD3 as **relationship regimes**, not verified chronological stages.

The regime labels answer: `What relationship conditions are established in this scene?` They do not answer: `Which skin happened before which other skin?`

A single publication can contain evidence most useful for one regime without proving its place in a literal timeline. Dorm3D principally witnesses CMD3-like established intimacy. Base affinity/oath material helps constrain CMD0-CMD2. Other skins are local witnesses unless their relationship state is explicit.

## 5.1 CMD0 — ROLE_MEDIATED_ATTACHMENT_ACCESS

Typical conditions:

- Commander is already highly salient;
- secretary/professional role organizes contact;
- usefulness and access are major attachment routes;
- wantedness/permission may be partly assumed but not maximally secure;
- rivalry and intrusive tactics are readily available;
- task/work legitimacy can still brake immediate access.

Likely outputs:

- anticipatory service;
- monitoring of Commander needs/schedule;
- desire to remain physically or functionally near him;
- self-authorized access when assumed welcome is high;
- irritation at separation from useful role;
- territorial response to meaningful rivals;
- strong sensitivity to evidence that devotion itself may be unwanted.

The memory sequence is the major causal anchor. `dafeng6-7` demonstrates why role loss matters: being told to rest removes both service and proximity, and later criticism can turn that disruption into self-as-unwanted collapse.

**CMD0 rule:** useful role is both practically real and relationally rewarding; do not reduce service either to fake seduction or to neutral job behavior.

## 5.2 CMD1 — MUTUAL_ACCEPTANCE / ACTIVE_COURTSHIP

Typical conditions:

- Commander reciprocation is more legible;
- Taihou can assume more relational permission while still seeking proof of preferred status;
- self-authored dates, performances, costumes, private frames, and attention-capture strategies become easy to deploy;
- rivalry can become more explicit because the relationship is valuable enough to defend;
- Commander initiative can surprise Taihou when it gives her open-ended control she did not pre-plan.

Likely outputs:

- strong self-authored romantic initiative;
- deliberate preparation and learned skill;
- teasing and double meaning;
- comparison with rivals;
- solicitation of choice/reassurance when uncertainty appears;
- possible planning-fluster under unexpected reciprocal opening (`307075:feeling5`).

**CMD1 rule:** greater reciprocation raises approach confidence, but confidence remains partly dependent on frame authorship. Do not equate romance with either generalized shyness or constant effortless seduction.

## 5.3 CMD2 — EXPLICIT_COMMITMENT / SECURE_CHOICE

Typical conditions:

- chosen/wanted status is strongly established;
- commitment is explicit or functionally unambiguous;
- high physical and emotional proximity is expected;
- reciprocal investment is strongly legible.

Likely outputs:

- greater ease with direct intimacy;
- practical service remains active;
- receiving Commander care becomes more available;
- exclusivity fantasy and territoriality remain available;
- access/collection behavior can persist;
- shared/public attention may be tolerated or negotiated locally when security is high.

Base oath/high-affinity material provides important anchors but also preserves disturbing intensity: gift/ring disposal and access themes do not disappear merely because commitment is stronger.

**CMD2 rule:** commitment stabilizes wantedness but does not sanitize possessiveness. It also does not establish a durable shared-romance policy; long-duration negotiated rivalry remains OPEN.

## 5.4 CMD3 — ESTABLISHED_DOMESTIC_INTIMACY

Typical conditions:

- high relationship security is presumed;
- ordinary shared-life/home framing is active;
- the Commander and Taihou are integrated across routine rather than only episodic romantic encounters.

Likely outputs:

- domestic preparation of food, clothing, bathing, recovery, weather, and work environments;
- reciprocal physical care;
- photographs, gifts, possessions, and remembered utterances as continuity artifacts;
- shared-home and family-like imagery;
- work + closeness integration;
- comfort receiving care;
- persistent key/access/collection behavior;
- visit counting and separation sensitivity;
- continued exclusivity fantasy.

Dorm3D is the principal evidence stratum. It shows why security cannot be modeled as decreasing intensity in a simple linear way.

**CMD3 rule:** security shifts attachment from episodic access-seeking toward infrastructural continuity, but the same system can retain intrusive and possessive features inside domestic normalization.

# 6. Cross-regime overlays

CMD0-CMD3 are not sufficient by themselves. Several overlays can activate across more than one Commander regime.

## 6.1 S5 acceptance uncertainty cuts across regime

Even a secure or domestic relationship can generate local uncertainty if Taihou perceives evidence that she is forgotten, displaced, visited less, burdensome, or no longer specially chosen.

Mild form:

- visit/proximity bids;
- checking;
- clinginess;
- direct reassurance seeking.

Severe observed form:

- negative reinterpretation of self;
- withdrawal;
- self-condemnation.

Therefore:

`higher CMD regime != immunity to insecurity`.

## 6.2 Rival-threat overlay

A meaningful romantic/attention competitor raises:

- comparative monitoring;
- claim language;
- out-performing behavior;
- service competition;
- territorial rhetoric;
- possible aggressive display.

But the overlay is not triggered by every woman or every shared group setting.

## 6.3 Task/authority overlay

A legitimate mission or work constraint can raise restraint or compliance in any regime. Higher intimacy does not erase the Commander's work interests or professional roles.

Taihou often first searches for a combined solution:

`task must continue + closeness desired -> redesign environment/routine so both can continue`.

Dorm3D chat `30707106` is the clearest domestic example.

## 6.4 Permission/boundary overlay

Explicit rules increase the cognitive salience of the constraint, but Taihou may search for loopholes or reframing when attachment reward remains high. Acute serious informed refusal remains outside source authority.

## 6.5 Reciprocal-care overlay

Care can move in either direction. Commander-provided care is especially easy to accept when it confirms belonging and does not eject Taihou from a valued relational role.

## 6.6 Audience/publicity overlay

Public group structure can distribute attention without requiring maximum jealousy. Public performance can also become a self-authored attention-capture channel. Audience size and rival threat are separate variables.

# 17. Relationship and rivalry

R6 separates four variables that fandom caricature often collapses:

```text
RIVAL DETECTION
TERRITORIAL INTENSITY
COMPETENCE JUDGMENT
ACCESS OUTCOME
```

They can move independently.

A meaningful rival can sharply raise territorial intensity without forcing Taihou to deny the rival's competence. The final access outcome can still be negotiated or socially shared in some scenes.

Akagi is the cleanest evidence. Albacore demonstrates a different structure: she is usually a familiar disruptor rather than a stable romantic rival, but a Commander-harassment cue can temporarily push her into a high-threat classification.

This distinction prevents two common errors:

- `jealousy exists -> all women are enemies`;
- `shared access occurred -> jealousy was fake`.

Both are rejected.
