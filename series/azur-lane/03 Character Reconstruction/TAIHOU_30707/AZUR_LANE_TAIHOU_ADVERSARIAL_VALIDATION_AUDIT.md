## R5 falsification pass over the active-provisional character monograph

# 0. Authority and verdict

This artifact is the canonical **R5 adversarial validation audit** for **Taihou / TAIHOU_30707**. It does not replace the R4 monograph. Its responsibility is narrower and stricter: attack the integrated R4 model against the pinned source surface, route each major claim through the formal revision vocabulary, identify where R4 over-compressed the evidence, and define the post-R5 authority boundary.

The audit re-screened the high-value CN source surfaces behind R4 rather than merely comparing R4 prose against R3 prose. The most consequential checks included the seven-part `dafeng1–dafeng7` memory, full narrative scenes such as `shanchenglifu2`, `junheboss1–5`, `gongmingdepassion17`, `jiarixintiaodayoulun21`, `huihua3–5`, social threads including `post:48`, `post:135`, `post:137–139`, `post:474`, `post:481`, `post:580`, Fleet Chat `activity_chat:27`, the base/skin character-dialogue ledger, and Dorm3D groups including `12001`, `12008`, `12010`, `12012`, `12015`, `12017`, `12025`, `12027`, `12041`, `12045`, `12049`, and `DORM3DVIDEO1201`.

**Final R5 verdict:**

`TAIHOU_R5_PASS_WITH_N5_REVISED_TO_RECIPROCAL_CONSEQUENTIALITY_HARD_REFUSAL_AND_HIGH_STAKES_EDGES_OPEN`

The central R4 architecture survives. Taihou is still better modeled through conditional weighting, attachment security, threat attribution, task legitimacy, frame authorship, relationship state, and context than through a static trope label. R5 finds no contradiction requiring rejection of the state architecture, the selective-boundary model, the peer/professional counterweight, the dual attachment-threat model, the reassurance regulator, or the persistent-intrusion result.

R5 does, however, make one major conceptual revision:

> **The evidence supports reciprocal consequentiality much more strongly than literal mutual indispensability.**

Taihou repeatedly seeks a bond that is specially chosen, remembered, mutually invested in, difficult to treat as interchangeable, and embedded in daily life. She can make herself useful, solicit explicit choice, receive care, and even deliberately create a small need for Commander help. But the stronger statement that she generally seeks to make both parties *indispensable* to one another overstates the causal reach of the evidence. Deliberate dependency engineering is demonstrated as a capability, not yet as a universal attachment mechanism.

Accordingly, historical N5 `mutual indispensability` is **REVISE + BOUND** into:

> **N5-R5 — reciprocal consequentiality with selective dependency engineering:** Taihou seeks repeated evidence that the Commander–Taihou relationship is uniquely consequential to both participants. She commonly builds her own consequentiality through usefulness, attention capture, privileged access, memory, and domestic continuity; she also values Commander investment through reassurance, care, shared routines, gifts, and explicit choice, and can sometimes stage a small dependency opportunity. The source does not establish that reciprocal functional necessity is required in every secure attachment state.

The R4 monograph remains **active_provisional** after R5. R5 is a necessary but not sufficient promotion gate. R6 relationship-state synthesis, R7 multilingual textual speech reconstruction, R8 novel-situation simulation validation, and the performed-voice/source-boundary work remain independent downstream responsibilities.

# 1. Adversarial method

R5 applies four controls.

## 1.1 Falsification over confirmation

For each R4 rule, the audit asks what source pattern would make the rule false, too broad, or causally unnecessary. Repetition is not treated as validation if every repetition belongs to the same Commander-heavy publication function.

## 1.2 Competing-model comparison

R4 mechanisms are compared against narrower alternatives. In particular, N5 is tested against:

- possession/exclusivity;
- proximity seeking;
- reassurance dependence;
- service identity;
- attention capture;
- ordinary reciprocal affection without a necessity motive.

A larger theory is retained only where it explains evidence that the narrower models cannot explain without becoming an ad hoc list.

## 1.3 Negative evidence and missing experiments

Absence is not converted into proof of incapacity. If the corpus lacks a clean serious hard refusal, grave professional failure, prolonged unreassured separation, or long-duration rival-sharing test, R5 returns **OPEN** rather than forcing a prediction.

## 1.4 State and source separation

Dorm3D remains a high-security established-intimacy stratum rather than a raw vote for baseline. Character text remains Commander-heavy. Public, peer, professional, combat, and narrative counterexamples retain disproportionate importance for global claims. CN remains semantic authority. No JP/EN/TW/KR stylistic or acoustic conclusion is imported into R5.

# 3. Target A — N5 mutual indispensability versus narrower models

## 3.1 Evidence that survives attack

The source does show two-way relational investment.

Taihou repeatedly tries to become highly consequential to the Commander through:

- anticipatory work and care;
- preference tracking;
- food, clothing, recovery, and domestic preparation;
- learned performance and presentation;
- privileged spatial and object access;
- photos, diaries, possessions, and remembered utterances;
- making absence noticeable through continuity-building.

The source also shows that Commander action toward Taihou matters as more than passive permission:

- `dafeng7` turns explicit Commander acceptance into rapid restoration of approach;
- Dorm3D `12008` resolves rival-scent uncertainty through explicit exclusive choice;
- `12017` resolves fear of being forgotten through direct reassurance;
- `12045` shows Taihou accepting and relaxing into Commander-provided massage;
- high-security domestic material frames gifts, photographs, home space, and routines as reciprocal relationship investment.

Most importantly, Juustagram `post:580` demonstrates **deliberate dependency engineering as a real capability**. Taihou echoes the bottle-opening pretext and says she also cannot open the cap, therefore Commander help is necessary. This cannot be reduced to one-way service identity.

## 3.2 Why literal mutual indispensability is too strong

The adversarial problem is causal scope.

One staged small dependency proves:

`Taihou can strategically create a need for Commander help.`

It does not prove:

`Taihou generally needs every secure relationship state to make Commander functionally indispensable to her.`

Likewise, receiving massage proves that reciprocal care is acceptable and rewarding; it does not by itself prove that the reward specifically comes from constructing necessity rather than intimacy, trust, erotic closeness, or ordinary reciprocity. Reassurance proves high regulatory leverage of Commander evaluation; it does not automatically become functional dependence engineering. Shared domestic infrastructure proves continuity-building; it can be explained by proximity, memory, access, mutual investment, and exclusivity without requiring literal indispensability.

The original umbrella therefore risks absorbing almost any affectionate behavior after the fact.

## 3.3 Competing-model adjudication

### Possession-only

**REJECT as complete model.** It cannot explain ordinary peer competence, task compliance, enjoyment of receiving care, or cases where shared access is negotiated.

### Service-identity-only

**REJECT as complete model.** It cannot explain reassurance needs, erotic/self-authored presentation, collection/access behavior, receiving care, or staged need.

### Reassurance-dependence-only

**REJECT as complete model.** Under secure conditions Taihou is often highly agentic, prepared, playful, and initiating rather than passively reassurance-seeking.

### Reciprocal consequentiality

**STRENGTHEN.** This narrower model explains why Taihou wants to matter to the Commander and wants Commander investment to matter to her without requiring every scene to instantiate literal necessity.

### Selective dependency engineering

**PRESERVE + BOUND.** Directly demonstrated as an available attachment tactic, but recurrence is too limited to promote it into a universal strategy.

## 3.4 N5 transition

**N5: `ACTIVE_PROVISIONAL mutual indispensability` → `REVISE + BOUND`.**

Current formulation:

> **Taihou seeks reciprocal consequentiality: a relationship repeatedly affirmed as specially chosen, memorable, mutually invested in, and structurally embedded. She can use both competence and selective dependency to intensify that consequentiality, but reciprocal functional indispensability is not established as a required master motive.**

Confidence: high for reciprocal consequentiality; medium for deliberate dependency engineering as a recurring cross-context strategy.

# 4. Target B — explicit autonomy and boundary pressure

## 4.1 The weighting model survives

R5 finds stronger evidence for selective weighting than a caricature of conceptual boundary blindness.

Taihou demonstrates that she can represent legitimate constraints:

- `shanchenglifu2`: she says she considered entering the Commander’s office but refrains because he is working;
- `junheboss1–5`: she accepts another commander’s operational authority, role assignment, and task-specific limits even while her preference to engage the Commander remains explicit;
- Fleet Chat `activity_chat:27`: peers explicitly raise harassment/burden, and Commander authorization changes the social meaning of continued messaging;
- `DORM3DVIDEO1201`: she explicitly wonders whether repeated indulgence is making her excessive;
- her own private collection-space material demonstrates that privacy is a legible concept, not an unknown category.

R5 also identifies an important explicit-rule test in base `307070:feeling5`. Taihou directly refers to the Commander having said **not to casually enter and leave his room**, then constructs a loophole: if casual entry is disallowed, living together from the outset would solve the problem.

This is not ignorance of the rule. It is recognition followed by relational reframing that preserves the desired access.

## 4.2 What this does and does not establish

The explicit standing-rule case strengthens:

> `boundary represented → attachment reward remains high → search for workaround/reframing is possible`

It does not establish the response to:

> `immediate, serious, informed, unambiguous refusal of physical intimacy or access in a high-stakes moment.`

No clean source experiment supplies that state. Flirtatious persistence, playful search/contact premises, standing access rules, and Commander-indulgent scenes cannot safely substitute for it.

## 4.3 H2 transition

**H2: `STRENGTHEN + REVISE` → `STRENGTHEN + SPECIFY`.**

Current formulation:

> **Taihou can understand privacy, autonomy, explicit standing rules, work constraints, and her own potential excess. Under attachment-salient conditions she may nevertheless underweight those constraints, reinterpret them, or search for a loophole when closeness, service, assumed welcome, or an established relational pattern offers a strong competing reward. Clear legitimate task structure and uncertainty about acceptance can increase restraint. Response to an acute serious informed refusal remains OPEN.**

The R4 “weighting model” is retained as an analytical representation, not a literal numeric utility function.

Confidence: high for selective weighting and workaround behavior; OPEN for hard-refusal ceiling.

# 5. Target C — care-receiving without role loss or erotic conversion

`Taihou cannot receive care.`

It is not sufficient to establish a broad law that all non-role-threatening care will be easy. The source is much richer in Taihou caring for Commander than in prolonged Commander care for Taihou, and much of the latter is physically intimate or erotically compatible.

Confidence: high that a state distinction exists; medium-high that relational function is the dominant general mechanism outside the directly observed contrast.

> **Severe self-condemnation is directly established in an attachment-devaluation context, not as a generic response to criticism.**

This keeps grave professional guilt and peer-competence criticism outside high-confidence simulation.

# 9. Target G — can meta-awareness itself brake behavior?

`DORM3DVIDEO1201` is the strongest direct test.

Taihou recognizes that the Commander repeatedly accommodates her willfulness and explicitly asks whether his indulgence could turn her into an excessive person. This establishes real meta-awareness without a rejection crisis.

What follows matters more than the admission. Taihou does not demonstrate durable self-limitation. She partially reframes responsibility toward the Commander for making her unable to restrain herself, confirms that he is coming to see her, and returns to intimacy.

The source therefore directly rejects:

`insight → automatic correction.`

It does **not** establish:

`insight never contributes to restraint.`

Other evidence shows that task legitimacy and uncertainty about welcome can restrain her. Meta-awareness may contribute when coupled to stronger constraints, but the corpus does not isolate that effect.

**Target G transition: `STRENGTHEN negative claim + BOUND`.**

Current rule:

> **Awareness of possible excess is neither absent nor sufficient. Durable restraint requires additional decision weight—such as task legitimacy, explicit constraint, uncertainty about welcome, or another sufficiently salient brake. Whether self-reflection alone can ever produce sustained change remains OPEN.**
