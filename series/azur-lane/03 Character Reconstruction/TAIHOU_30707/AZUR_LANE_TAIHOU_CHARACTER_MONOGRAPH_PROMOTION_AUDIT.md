A mature monograph may retain OPEN domains if those domains are clearly bounded rather than silently filled.

For Taihou, the promotion decision therefore asks:

1. Is the corpus/source authority stable enough for the claimed scope?
2. Are the central cognitive and motivational claims adversarially validated?
3. Are relationship states modeled independently from generic personality labels?
4. Are CN semantics and JP/EN/TW/KR textual realizations separated correctly?
5. Does novel-situation prediction work before prose generation?
6. Does the simulator survive removal of signature speech markers?
7. Are C4/C5 unknowns preserved as abstention boundaries?
8. Is JP performed voice clearly separated rather than fabricated?

10. Is revision provenance sufficient to freeze V1 without losing claim history?

# 3. Why promotion is justified

## 3.1 The core model survived adversarial testing by becoming narrower, not vaguer

R5 did not simply affirm R4. It materially revised the most ambitious higher-order claim.

Historical:

> mutual indispensability

Current:

> **reciprocal consequentiality with selective dependency engineering**

The current formulation explains the coexistence of competent service, reciprocal care, reassurance value, domestic continuity, attention capture, and occasional staged need without claiming that secure attachment always requires reciprocal functional necessity.

This is a promotion-positive result because the model survived falsification through **precision gain**, not rhetorical insulation.

## 3.2 The boundary model is cognitively stronger than “boundary-blind”

The integrated V1 distinguishes conceptual recognition from behavioral weighting:

```text
represent privacy/autonomy/task concern
+ attachment reward
+ assumed welcome / permission pattern
+ closeness/service value
+ competing-constraint legitimacy
→ weighted action
```

This accounts simultaneously for voluntary work restraint, professional compliance, recognition of privacy, meta-awareness of excess, and persistent key/access/tracking behavior.

The unresolved acute serious hard-refusal experiment remains OPEN. Promotion does not require inventing that answer.

## 3.3 Relationship architecture predicts conditional switching

R6 resolves a major simulation problem by separating:

- stable relationship history;
- momentary interlocutor function;
- Commander relationship regime;
- attachment security;
- rival/threat intensity;
- task/authority;
- permission/privacy;
- care direction;
- audience/channel;
- frame authorship.

This allows Albacore to remain a durable peer while functioning as either harmless disruptor or credible Commander-threat cue. It allows Akagi to be both genuine rival and legible competent peer. It allows Yamashiro to support ordinary sociality without implying reduced Commander preference.

R8 confirms that these distinctions generate different actions under controlled novel probes.

## 3.4 Textual multilingualism is modeled as independent realization, not decoration

R7 proves that Taihou cannot be represented by one English-like semantic sentence decorated with regional catchphrases.

Canonical order is:

```text
CN semantic/appraisal state
→ R6 relationship condition
→ action prediction
→ choose one locale witness
→ locale-specific textual realization
```

Key regional identities are preserved:

- CN: name self-reference, `指挥官大人` / `您`, particles/elongation, semantic authority;
- JP: `大鳳`, `指揮官様`, elegant/feminine polite morphology, documented JP-local dependency framing;
- EN: first-person naturalization, contractions, recurrent `my Commander`, bounded local expansions/rewrites;
- TW: conservative CN-like realization and triangulation value;
- KR: `다이호`, `지휘관님`, polite/intimate endings, documented KR-local dependency framing.

Regional additions cannot silently revise CN-global semantics.

## 3.5 R8 validates cognition before voice

R8's strongest contribution is methodological. It predicts appraisal/action first and only then permits dialogue.

Fourteen supported probes pass without semantic failure. The most diagnostic controlled pair keeps Albacore's stable relationship history constant while changing momentary function:

```text
harmless prank
→ startle / irritation / verification / de-escalation

credible Commander-threat cue
→ territorial intervention
→ de-escalation after threat removal
```

That difference remains after locale-style ablation. The simulator is therefore not relying on `ですわ`, `my Commander`, self-name, or `지휘관님` to make the character seem correct.

- typo/format repair that does not alter analytical meaning may be handled conservatively;
