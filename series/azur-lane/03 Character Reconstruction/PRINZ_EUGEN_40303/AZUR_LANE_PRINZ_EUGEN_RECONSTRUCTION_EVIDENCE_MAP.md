## Initial hypothesis register

All substantive character hypotheses are intentionally **OPEN / UNFORMED** at R1.

This is not analytical timidity; it is the correct consequence of the current publication state. The R1 map records questions, not answers:

- **Q1:** What functions does teasing/provocation serve across different interlocutors and stakes?
- **Q2:** How much of her apparent detachment is stable affect, strategic presentation, boredom management, defensive distance, or playful control?
- **Q3:** What does she value when loyalty, survival, factional obligation, curiosity, and personal attachment compete?
- **Q4:** What happens when she cannot control the conversational or strategic frame?
- **Q5:** How does she respond to genuine vulnerability—hers or someone else's?
- **Q6:** Which behaviors are specific to Commander intimacy and which recur with peers?
- **Q7:** Does increased closeness produce greater disclosure, greater testing, softer control, stronger possessiveness, or some other change?
- **Q8:** How does operational competence interact with her social persona?
- **Q9:** Are there contexts in which the teasing persona disappears entirely, and what replaces it?
- **Q10:** Which regional speech differences materially alter perceived intensity, distance, humor, or intimacy?

No answer to Q1–Q10 is canonical until direct evidence is read.
