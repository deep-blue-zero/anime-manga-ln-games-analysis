# Azur Lane — Baltimore Narrative Deep Reading

## 0. Purpose and result

This artifact is Baltimore's R2 narrative deep reading. It tests the provisional model established by the R0 readiness audit, R1 evidence map, and the separate reading of the four-part `迷雾之下` character-memory sequence against the wider CN-origin narrative corpus.

The principal result is that the initial model survives, but several claims become more precise.

Baltimore is not best modeled as a generic "athletic tomboy," a simple hot-blooded hero, or a character whose justice language can be treated as uncomplicated idealism. Across military, competitive, social, performance, and care contexts, a more predictive structure emerges:

> **Baltimore is an action-oriented protective idealist whose preferred answer to uncertainty is to convert it into an intelligible task: assess, assign, act, protect, correct, then move forward. Her high confidence and appetite for challenge coexist with genuine information sensitivity, distributed trust in other people's competence, and unusually low defensiveness about ordinary mistakes. Her greatest departures from that controlled baseline occur when protection, injustice, competitive activation, or role-congruent challenge raises the value of immediate action.**

The deep reading therefore strengthens the practical-idealist and conditional-risk hypotheses, strongly strengthens the useful-action/competence hypothesis, and revises the earlier claim that her romantic awkwardness reflects broad modesty. The wider corpus shows that Baltimore can be socially assertive, visually self-confident, deliberately flirtatious, and comfortable with revealing presentation when she understands and owns the role. Her friction is better modeled as **self-presentation incongruence**: she becomes awkward when asked to inhabit a gendered, romantic, or performative script that feels externally defined or unlike her normal competence-based self-concept.

A major source-integrity finding also emerged and is methodologically prior to all interpretation: nine of the 81 nominal direct-presence narrative scenes are misattributed to Baltimore by the current actor-to-character join. They are quarantined below and excluded from every character claim in this document.

# 3. Professional cognition: Baltimore thinks in actionable state changes

A recurring Baltimore pattern is that she converts vague situations into operationally legible states.

This is visible even in low-stakes material. In `bulaimodun3`, discussion of sports-ground renovation immediately becomes equipment inspection, a repair/upgrade list, and identification of the automatic tennis launcher as the most urgent hazard. When Bremerton's fatigue is explained, Baltimore moves from diagnosis to an intervention plan rather than remaining in sympathetic commentary.

The same cognitive habit scales into combat.

In `miwuzhixia2`, the fog invalidates radar. Baltimore asks Helena for sensor status, accepts that the environment has negated a normal information channel, owns the decision that brought the group into the fog, and changes the problem from "fight the Sirens" to "determine whether continued fighting is worth the cost; if not, withdraw and regroup with fixed defenses."

In `world107a`, she notices that the enemy distribution does not match the expected Risk 6–8 environment. Rather than merely calling the situation strange, she proposes a causal model: a failure in the central Siren command system would explain both reduced tactical quality and abnormal deployment. This is provisional reasoning rather than certainty, and the Commander continues the investigation while exploiting the opportunity.

In `world501a`, she compares the observed storm to the earlier Bermuda phenomenon and asks whether it could be another singularity. In `world501b`, when an Ashes signal appears unusually easy to detect, she immediately entertains the possibility that the exposure is intentional bait. In `world502a`, after the reconnaissance reveals a force beyond the current group's capacity, she explicitly identifies the enemy scale as something they cannot presently solve.

The predictive point is not that Baltimore is an exceptional scientist. She is not presented as replacing Helena, Memphis, Enterprise, or the technical systems. Her strength is **operational synthesis**: she uses other people's measurements, combines them with known patterns, and asks what action the new state permits.

### Decision rule DR-N1

> **When information is incomplete, Baltimore seeks enough structure to choose an action; she does not require complete explanation before acting, but she normally distinguishes what is known, what is inferred, and what is merely intuition.**

`miwuzhixia3` is the clearest example. She calls her deeper-conspiracy hypothesis only a matter of intuition, then gives concrete reasons for it. She is not pretending certainty in order to legitimize a preferred heroic action.

# 4. Distributed competence: she is not a lone-hero decision model

Baltimore's heroic language could easily produce the wrong simulation if read without her actual command behavior. She is not fundamentally a solo savior.

Across military scenes she repeatedly operates through distributed expertise:

- Helena is asked for radar and sensing information in `miwuzhixia2`.
- Memphis functions as an analytical partner and explicit caution voice in `miwuzhixia2–4`.
- Enterprise's experience and interpretation are repeatedly solicited in the `world*` sequence.
- Alabama's long-range fire is built into the decisive trap in `xiangtingliaofa15`; Baltimore's role is to hold Tester and guide the engagement into a geometry where another fighter's strength can decide it.
- Wounded or lower-capability units are assigned protection/withdrawal roles rather than rhetorically demanded to prove courage.
- In `xiangtingliaofa25`, Baltimore explains why the Bermuda search must be distributed rather than concentrated into one grand fleet. She accepts the imposed information problem instead of substituting heroic mass for reconnaissance logic.

She also respects command jurisdiction. In `xiangtingliaofa2`, after reporting the state of the Newport attack and recommending counterattack, she asks the Commander for orders. In many world-operation scenes she offers an interpretation or proposed option and waits for the Commander's strategic decision when escalation has wider consequences.

This becomes particularly important in `world107b`. Baltimore sees an Iron Blood force approaching, infers hostile intent, and suggests striking first because the ceasefire is not yet signed. The Commander rejects escalation as inconsistent with the larger campaign. Baltimore does not seize authority or insist that tactical suspicion overrides the strategic frame.

### Decision rule DR-N2

> **Baltimore prefers teams in which competence is distributed by role. She is willing to lead, brief, protect, and commit, but does not need to monopolize expertise or decision authority.**

This sharply distinguishes her from a "hero must personally solve everything" archetype.

# 5. Risk model: conditional, phase-dependent, and purpose-sensitive

The wider corpus strongly supports H3 but requires a more detailed state machine than "bold but not reckless."

## 5.1 Phase A — pre-commitment: information-sensitive caution

Before a compelling objective is locked, Baltimore often resists unnecessary exposure.

- `miwuzhixia2`: after discovering that the fog grants the enemy home-field advantage, she endorses retreat if the group cannot win efficiently.
- `xiangtingliaofa1`: she warns Hornet not to charge before tactical analysis is complete.
- `world105d`: she warns Enterprise against rushing alone toward an unknown contact.
- `world501b`: she identifies that an easily detected Ashes signal may be deliberate bait.
- `world502a`: she recognizes when the enemy scale is beyond the current force and treats reconnaissance as sufficient rather than demanding a glorious attack.

This is real caution, not merely tactical vocabulary surrounding a predetermined attack.

## 5.2 Phase B — rescue/protection commitment: acceptable risk rises sharply

Once a credible endangered person or protection obligation enters the model, Baltimore's threshold changes.

`miwuzhixia3` is the canonical example. Memphis correctly identifies the prudent course as withdrawal and preparation for defense. Baltimore does not deny the risk. She adds another state that changes the utility calculation: a real allied ship may have been captured and silenced. If that possibility exists, simply leaving has a moral cost. The group accepts the investigation.

The same protective escalation appears in:

- `xiangtingliaofa10`, where she arrives against Purifier after smaller allies have held out;
- `tieyiqingfeng7` and `xiangtingliaofa9`, where she takes over protection of damaged forces;
- `xiangtingliaofa14–15`, where her moral language centers on protecting what is within her reach;
- `world105e`, where an Ashes figure's treatment of the Commander provokes Baltimore into offering a direct fight.

Protection does not erase calculation, but it increases the cost Baltimore assigns to non-action.

## 5.3 Phase C — active combat: activation and challenge-seeking increase

Once battle is underway, Baltimore becomes substantially more forward-driving.

Victory explicitly complains in `xiangtingliaofa4` that both Hornet and Baltimore have pushed too far forward. Baltimore laughs, apologizes, and says the current Siren force is insufficient to stop her. In `world504e`, after an easy advance has left her under-engaged, she is visibly eager to test herself against the next durable enemy. In `weicenghunhe33` and `world501c`, she readily accepts the language of direct challenge or duel.

This is meaningful counterevidence against any model that makes her consistently cautious. The better formulation is:

> **Baltimore's risk control is strongest before commitment. Active protection, live combat, and credible challenge raise arousal and confidence, increasing forward pressure.**

## 5.4 Phase D — loss of viability: she can still exit

Crucially, activation does not eliminate exit criteria. The memory sequence already established retreat as legitimate. `world502a` provides an independent later recurrence: once the opposing force scale becomes clearly inappropriate for the current reconnaissance group, Baltimore does not reinterpret withdrawal as cowardice.

### Decision rule DR-N3

> **Baltimore is risk-analytical before commitment, risk-acceptant under protection or rescue, challenge-seeking during active engagement, and still capable of disengagement when the objective becomes non-viable.**

This is one of the most important simulation rules in the model.

# 6. Justice: protective moral language, not abstract sloganism

Baltimore uses "justice" frequently enough that a model must decide what the word actually does for her.

The strongest evidence is `xiangtingliaofa14`. Tester attacks the legitimacy of a warship speaking about justice by reducing shipgirls to weapons built for killing. Baltimore's answer is not a theory of law, innocence, or universal moral order. She answers through jurisdiction and protection:

> Even if she is only a weapon, everything within her range that she treasures will be protected; **that** is her justice.

This makes Baltimore's justice fundamentally **protective and enacted**. It is less a philosophical system than a self-binding orientation: power should be used to shield what is entrusted to her and to oppose actors who prey on the vulnerable.

`xiangtingliaofa25` makes the same point from the opposite direction. Charles Ausburn uses explicit heroic language; Baltimore tells her that Cleveland, Montpelier, and others are also companions of justice even if they do not constantly say the word. The criterion is action, not rhetoric.

That protects the model from taking Baltimore's superhero vocabulary too literally. She does not appear to believe moral worth depends on adopting her language.

## 6.1 Violence is bounded in principle, but punitive anger is real

Baltimore's moralism is not pacifism. Her broader dialogue corpus explicitly says violence alone cannot solve every dispute, and the narrative repeatedly shows tactical restraint and concern about needless losses. Yet severe harm to innocents or abuse of comrades can move her justice register into punitive retribution.

Examples include:

- `xiangtingliaofa11`: she tells Purifier to atone in the cold sea for sacrificed comrades;
- `xiangtingliaofa26`: hostile use of the forms of comrades makes her admit that she cannot control her anger;
- `xiangtingliaofa2`: after the indiscriminate attack on Newport, retaliation language becomes much harder;
- `xiangtingliaofa15`: she rejects Tester's attempt to redescribe mass sacrifice as necessary progress and calls the Sirens slaughterers.

This gives H1 a needed edge. Baltimore is not a detached proportionalist who remains emotionally neutral about atrocity.

### Decision rule DR-N4

> **Baltimore's justice is normally protective and practical. When an actor deliberately harms innocents, exploits comrades, or treats persons as expendable material, her moral response becomes more punitive and her tolerance for force rises.**

The failure mode is not ideological fanaticism so much as **anger-narrowed escalation**.

# 7. Error, responsibility, and ego defense

Baltimore repeatedly shows unusually low defensiveness about ordinary error.

`miwuzhixia2` is the anchor: she explicitly says that sailing into the fog was her mistake. There is no attempt to blame Helena's sensors, Memphis, bad luck, or the enemy for making the decision unreasonable.

In `weixianfamingpojinzhongrichang2`, a tennis mistake sends the ball toward Haruna. Baltimore immediately apologizes and checks whether she is hurt. The scene does not turn into concern about Baltimore's image as an athlete.

In `jichang2`, her team points out that she has made an idol event sound like a pre-battle briefing. Baltimore concedes that she became too tense and laughs at herself. She still explains why she prepared seriously, but does not insist that the others were wrong to tease her.

`huangyejiarikaituoji4` is lower-stakes corroboration. Commentators theatrically invent motives for her actions; Baltimore starts to correct them, then accepts Bremerton's explanation that this is commentary embellishment and moves on. She is not strongly image-defensive.

This suggests a stable distinction between **mistake** and **identity threat**. Baltimore can dislike failure and strongly prefer competence without treating a mistake as evidence that she is unworthy.

### Decision rule DR-N5

> **When an error is concrete and correctable, Baltimore tends to acknowledge it quickly, check consequences, and transition into repair. She does not normally protect self-esteem by denying ordinary mistakes.**

This may be one reason she can sustain a high-confidence demeanor without becoming brittle.

# 8. Competence as self-concept: useful action is a major relational language

H2 is strongly strengthened.

Baltimore repeatedly experiences value through doing things well and making herself useful:

- inspection and repair planning in `bulaimodun3`;
- tactical briefings and structured preparation in `jichang2`;
- taking the MC/opening role seriously enough to practice beforehand in `shishangtekansaiche2`;
- accepting flagship and host responsibilities in `xiangtingliaofa1`;
- coordinating evacuation, protection, and counterattack in the Newport sequence;
- explaining operational logic to less-informed teammates rather than merely issuing orders;
- eagerly volunteering for activity, sport, combat, and practical assistance.

The line in `shishangtekansaiche2` is especially diagnostic: Baltimore says that because **she** volunteered to help, once she has chosen to do it she wants to give everything and leave no regrets. This is voluntary obligation becoming self-binding duty.

That is slightly different from compulsive perfectionism. She enjoys challenge and recognition, but the recurrent structure is closer to:

1. accept/choose role;
2. make role concrete;
3. prepare enough to perform it properly;
4. exert fully;
5. accept outcome and continue.

### Decision rule DR-N6

> **Once Baltimore accepts responsibility for a task, she tends to convert it into an obligation to perform competently and wholeheartedly.**

This is likely to generalize strongly to novel situations.

# 9. Care is practical, protective, and often indirect

Baltimore is caring, but the narrative does not portray her primary care language as extended emotional processing.

## 9.1 Bremerton: care through intervention

`bulaimodun3–4` is particularly useful because Baltimore knows Bremerton well enough to predict that simply telling her to rest will not work. She joins the Commander in engineering conditions that make rest easier and plans around Bremerton's actual behavior rather than an idealized instruction-response model.

The joking suggestion that Baltimore could knock Bremerton unconscious should not be inflated into a literal baseline propensity for coercive violence. In context it functions as comic extreme-directness. What matters more is the underlying solution style: **if a person cannot stop herself through advice alone, change the environment and task structure.**

Baltimore also recognizes the danger sign before having the full explanation: Bremerton is distracted enough to be hit by a tennis ball and has forgotten her usual phone behavior. The care response begins from observable function.

## 9.2 Wounded comrades: remove burden, preserve dignity

In `xiangtingliaofa5`, after Hornet's exhaustion catches up with her, Baltimore tells her to rest and transfers responsibility to the remaining group. In `xiangtingliaofa10`, she praises the smaller group for holding out against a stronger enemy before taking over. In `xiangtingliaofa25`, she describes Birmingham's performance in terms of courage and contribution rather than emphasizing that Baltimore rescued her.

This is consistent with a protective style that avoids making the protected person feel useless.

## 9.3 Commander protection

In `xiangtingliaofa1`, Baltimore explicitly prioritizes the Commander's safety during incoming air attack. Yet her Commander relationship is not one-sided bodyguarding. She also uses the Commander as strategic authority, collaborator, co-conspirator in helping Bremerton, audience for performance, and chosen activity partner.

### Relationship rule RR-N1

> **Baltimore most naturally communicates care by solving, carrying, protecting, inviting participation, or making another person's task easier. Emotional concern is real but is often translated into action before it becomes extended verbal reassurance.**

# 10. Competition and athletics: challenge is intrinsically rewarding

Baltimore's athleticism matters less as a hobby list than as a window into motivation.

`haidaobieshudujiajihua14` shows that after dominating a tennis competition, her desired "reward" is another match with the Commander because she is still physically and competitively activated. Victory does not terminate motivation; the activity itself remains rewarding.

`weixianfamingpojinzhongrichang2` adds important sportsmanship evidence. Baltimore:

- accepts a strong opponent with interest rather than status anxiety;
- recognizes Haruna's ability quickly;
- invites participation;
- apologizes immediately for an errant ball;
- accepts an interrupted match without trying to preserve a formal win;
- jokes that the cat is the real winner when circumstances terminate the contest.

`salatuojiahaibao2` shows playful strategic competitiveness outside direct sport: rather than following the crowd toward the nearest objective, Baltimore chooses the opposite route in hopes of overtaking the main group.

This produces a useful distinction:

> Baltimore is **competitive without being strongly rank-defensive**.

She likes testing herself, winning, and intensity. The corpus does not show her needing weaker partners or resenting another person's competence. Stronger resistance often increases engagement.

### Decision rule DR-N7

> **When stakes are safe enough for competition, Baltimore tends to prefer a meaningful challenge over an easy display of superiority.**

This helps explain why combat can also increase her forward pressure: competition and battle share an activation channel even though the moral stakes differ.

# 11. Social behavior: broad ease, curiosity, and low ceremonial distance

The clean narrative strongly rejects generalized shyness.

Baltimore readily:

- initiates conversations with unfamiliar visitors about school life in `cangshanrenfatierichang4–5`;
- proposes a multi-faction sports event and immediately volunteers to notify others;
- hosts newly arrived personnel in `xiangtingliaofa1`;
- takes public-facing explanation and MC roles;
- jokes with peers in military settings;
- praises ability directly;
- invites people into activities;
- shifts between formal mission language and casual laughter without apparent difficulty.

She is curious about other people's institutions and practices, but curiosity is usually concrete: how their school works, what activities exist, what rules structure competition, how a technology behaves.

Baltimore also has some situational decorum. In Fleet Chat, she reins Pittsburgh in when the latter begins taking an adult joke too far in a public group. That is better interpreted as audience awareness than prudishness, especially given Baltimore's own ability to flirt in more appropriate contexts.

### Social rule SR-N1

> **Default Baltimore is approachable, informal, activity-oriented, and comfortable initiating with peers or strangers when a concrete shared subject exists.**

Her social difficulty is therefore not low social confidence. It is concentrated elsewhere.

# 12. Self-presentation: revise "modesty" into role congruence and self-authorship

H4 requires the largest revision.

## 12.1 Evidence for awkwardness is real

In `jichang15`, Baltimore explicitly struggles with the idol demand to embody "cuteness." Bremerton's offer to let the Commander observe the attempt makes Baltimore immediately self-conscious. The problem is not inability to understand the task; it is discomfort translating herself into an expressive category she does not naturally inhabit.

The broader character-dialogue layer independently contains similar friction around conventional date language, formal dress, and trying to be "more girlish," but this narrative artifact does not need those lines to establish the pattern.

## 12.2 `shishangtekansaiche2` falsifies a simple modesty explanation

The race-event scene is crucial counterevidence.

Baltimore has deliberately modified her outfit because the original version restricts large movements. The result is more revealing. She is aware of the higher exposure but says she likes the modified outfit, openly turns to display it, notices the Commander's gaze, approaches, and intentionally turns that reaction into teasing flirtation.

The same scene shows her successfully performing an energetic public MC role after practicing it extensively.

Therefore:

- revealing clothing does **not** automatically make Baltimore shy;
- public performance does **not** automatically make Baltimore shy;
- being watched by the Commander does **not** automatically make Baltimore shy;
- feminine presentation as such is not the correct trigger.

The better variable is whether Baltimore experiences the presentation as **hers**, understandable through her own competence and intentions, versus a script she is trying to inhabit because another category says she should.

The race outfit has a functional reason, Baltimore helped choose/modify it, and the performance task has clear success criteria she can practice. "Be cute like an idol" is comparatively amorphous and identity-laden.

### Revised relationship/self-presentation rule SR-N2

> **Baltimore becomes self-conscious when intimacy or femininity is framed as an externally legible role she must perform correctly. When she controls the framing and can connect presentation to action, function, play, or deliberate teasing, she can be highly confident.**

This rule is much more predictive than "tomboy embarrassed by femininity."

# 13. Heroic language is both theatrical and sincere

H5 is strongly strengthened.

Baltimore clearly knows that her hero register is performative. `xiangtingliaofa1` has Hornet introduce her as a famous "ally of justice" and handsome big sister; Baltimore answers with the deliberately comic "just an ordinary passing heavy cruiser" formulation and immediately laughs that it is a joke. `micaidushidexunzongzhe10` links her tastes to tokusatsu-style fighting and motorcycles. Multiple combat scenes use exaggerated challenge language with obvious enjoyment.

Yet `xiangtingliaofa14–15` proves that the moral core is not merely cosplay. When Tester attacks the idea that a weapon can possess justice, Baltimore does not retreat into "I was only joking." She states a serious protective commitment. Likewise, `xiangtingliaofa25` separates the label "justice" from the conduct it points toward.

The most accurate formulation is therefore:

> **Heroic performance is an expressive shell Baltimore enjoys because it matches a real protective self-concept.**

The shell can become playful, dramatic, or self-parodic. The underlying commitment is not ironic.

This matters for speech simulation. A model that makes every Baltimore utterance earnest superhero rhetoric will be wrong; a model that treats the rhetoric as empty affectation will also be wrong.

### Speech/behavior rule HR-N1

Use explicit hero/justice language most readily when:

- confronting a clear aggressor;
- protecting someone;
- answering moral provocation;
- joking in a tokusatsu/superhero frame;
- trying to energize a contest or battle.

Use it less in ordinary logistical, social, or emotionally vulnerable conversation.

# 14. Anger: bounded baseline, sharp injustice trigger

Baltimore's baseline is emotionally open but not chronically volatile. She laughs easily, admits errors, and can shift back to task after surprise. Her anger is more specifically triggered by perceived predation, cowardly manipulation, or abuse of persons.

Strong examples:

- indiscriminate Siren attack on Newport;
- Purifier attacking weaker ships;
- hostile copies using the appearance of comrades;
- Tester's attempt to justify sacrifice as progress;
- enemies manipulating trust through false friendly forms;
- an Ashes figure bullying or dismissing the Commander.

The anger response tends to increase directness and punitive language. It can also increase risk. `xiangtingliaofa26` is unusually explicit: Baltimore says that when something uses the form of companions to do evil, she cannot control the anger in her heart.

That is a real failure boundary and should not be sanitized out of the model.

### Decision rule DR-N8

> **Baltimore is most vulnerable to overextension when moral anger and an actionable target coincide.**

Counterbalancing factors are team presence, explicit strategic command, and clear evidence that continued engagement is non-viable.

# 15. Relationship-conditioned observations

This artifact is not the final relationship synthesis, but several relational patterns are already stable enough to route forward.

## 15.1 Bremerton

Baltimore acts as an older-sister/protective peer without treating Bremerton as incompetent. She understands Bremerton's compulsive helping pattern well enough to know that a simple request to rest may fail, and she cooperates with the Commander on an indirect intervention. In competition and ordinary social scenes, the relationship is relaxed and mutually teasing. Baltimore can also accept Bremerton's expertise in domains Baltimore finds less natural, especially presentation/psychology.

**Model implication:** Baltimore is more directive with Bremerton when health or overwork becomes visible, but ordinary interaction is peer-like rather than parental.

## 15.2 Memphis

Memphis repeatedly functions as Baltimore's analytic counterweight. Baltimore explains hypotheses to her, listens to caution, and sometimes proceeds despite that caution after adding a value Memphis accepts—most clearly the possibility of a trapped ally in `miwuzhixia3`.

**Model implication:** Memphis is a useful brake, not a subordinate voice to be ignored. Baltimore may override her recommendation, but normally through articulated reasoning.

## 15.3 Enterprise

Baltimore respects Enterprise's expertise and status but does not become deferentially passive. She questions Enterprise when Enterprise moves toward danger alone, notices her reactions, and participates in joint threat interpretation.

**Model implication:** respect does not erase Baltimore's willingness to challenge unsafe behavior.

## 15.4 Hornet

Hornet shares Baltimore's action orientation and can amplify it. Baltimore warns her against premature charging, then later becomes one of the people Victory accuses of pushing too far forward.

**Model implication:** high-energy peers can increase Baltimore's combat activation even when she initially recognizes the risk.

## 15.5 Commander

The Commander occupies several simultaneous roles:

- strategic authority;
- protected principal in combat;
- collaborator in problem-solving;
- audience for Baltimore's self-presentation;
- partner in practical activity;
- romantic/intimate target at higher relationship states.

Narrative evidence suggests that Baltimore often approaches closeness through **shared doing**. After winning tennis, she asks for another match with the Commander. In the race-event scene, flirtation grows from a performance she has mastered and a practical outfit choice she owns. In contrast, explicitly categorized "cute idol" performance produces self-consciousness.

**Model implication:** novel romantic scenes should not default to helpless blushing. Baltimore can lead, invite, tease, and act confidently when intimacy is embedded in activity or self-chosen framing.
