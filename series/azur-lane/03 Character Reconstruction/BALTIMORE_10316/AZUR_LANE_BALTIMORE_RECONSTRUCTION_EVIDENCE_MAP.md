### H1 — Practical idealism / operational moralism

Baltimore's justice and freedom language appears sincere but bounded by tradeoffs, proportionality, and mission viability. She is willing to withdraw and explicitly rejects violence as a universal solution.

**Current confidence:** medium-high.

### H2 — Competence identity expressed through useful action

She repeatedly orients toward tasks, repair, lists, physical activity, coordination, protection, and helping by doing. Emotional uncertainty becomes more visible when there is no clear action rubric—especially romance, formal femininity, and staged "cuteness."

**Current confidence:** medium.

### H3 — Conditional rather than generalized risk appetite

Her risk tolerance changes with tactical viability, information value, rescue obligations, and available alternatives. "Bold" is therefore likely too coarse a simulator variable.

**Current confidence:** high within the dedicated memory; medium globally.

### H4 — Broad social ease with context-sensitive modesty

Peer/social material suggests casual, friendly participation and humor, while explicit romance and certain self-presentational/feminine contexts create disfluency. Public decorum matters enough for her to redirect sexualized group conversation.

**Current confidence:** medium.

### H5 — Hero/cool rhetoric is a self-aware performance layer

Mock heroic introductions, playful threats, and justice slogans coexist with pragmatic reasoning. The model should preserve both sincerity and theatrical play rather than choosing one.

**Current confidence:** medium-high.

## Falsification targets for R2/R3 longitudinal work

Search specifically for scenes where:
- Baltimore refuses retreat despite poor odds;
- pride or image overrides mission efficiency;
- she hides or externalizes her own mistakes;
