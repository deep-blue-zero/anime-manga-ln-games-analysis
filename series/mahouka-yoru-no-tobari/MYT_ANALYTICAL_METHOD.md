## Reconstruct assignment, action and attribution

Identify the actor, issuing and receiving parties, evidence available at the decision, option the actor believed possible, constraints, actual result and who learned it. Preserve separate lanes for an order, an operative's interpretation, an apparent result and a result the text verifies. Consider rival accounts such as faulty intelligence, a deliberate cover story, recognition after the event or an unanticipated intervention. Test the difference against what changes in evidence, access, timing or ability; leave the initiator or the mechanism open when the prose establishes only an effect.
