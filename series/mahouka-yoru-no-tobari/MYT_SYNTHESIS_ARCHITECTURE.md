## Standing source-reading topology

The proposed configuration requires an accountable principal analyst who inspects the whole admitted unit and is also the analytical integrator; separate independent specialist contexts for intelligence provenance, operation causality and institutional discretion, and for original Japanese focalization, speech/register, twins' independent agency and directed relationship evidence; and a distinct independent critical reviewer. The specialist contexts form their own observations from assigned original-language evidence before receiving the principal's interpretations. The critical reviewer directly inspects decisive and contradictory source evidence, checking who acts or speaks, what a quoted report can establish, who knew it and when, whether command caused the result, contrary evidence about the twins, and prior franchise knowledge smuggled into the argument, before acceptance. The reviewer receives the interpretation after inspecting decisive evidence where practical. No present unit type qualifies for the reduced configuration; record any later authorized change under [topology §4.1](../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EXECUTION_TOPOLOGY_AND_CAPABILITY_ROUTING_POLICY.md#multi-agent-source-reading). Verify these roles, evidence access and separation before first start; a runtime without adequate independent contexts is a required-capability stop. An owner-authorized deviation must state its exact scope and is recorded as a deviation, without ordinary acceptance or high-water advance unless explicitly authorized.

The unit checkpoint is the contribution and reconciliation record. Keep participant identity/role and separation, assignment, actual exposure and inspected coverage, material locator-backed observations, rivals and corrections, unresolved evidence obligations, and explicit adoption, qualification, rejection or open disposition for each material finding. Keep the finding and its reasoning recoverable; no generic participation summary, duplicated canonical artifact, or permanent per-agent ledger. Freeze prospective captures before resolution, separately by context where needed. Only the integrator updates shared readings, cumulative state and entrypoint after reconciliation. Record observed execution facts without inferring model settings. Keep source material and private transcripts in the evidence plane.
