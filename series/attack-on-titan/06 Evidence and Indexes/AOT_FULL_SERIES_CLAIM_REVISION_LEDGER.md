# 『進撃の巨人』 / *Attack on Titan*
## Full-Series Claim Revision Ledger — V01–V34
### V19 → V27 → V34 claim histories, post-checkpoint deltas, residual open states, and specialist routing

## 0. Function and authority

This ledger is the first retrospective evidence-stabilization artifact after completion of the thirty-four-volume Japanese-manga sequential reading. It does not replace the V19 or V27 checkpoints. Those documents remain immutable historical authorities for what the project could responsibly claim at their own publication boundaries.

This ledger answers a different question:

> **Given the complete V01–V34 manga, which earlier claims should be preserved, strengthened, revised, downgraded, rejected, or left open—and where must each mature claim be argued next?**

The ledger is therefore a firewall against two symmetrical errors:

1. treating the V27 synthesis as though V28–V34 never occurred; and
2. treating the ending as though every earlier uncertainty, source conflict, motive problem, and state transition had always been obvious.

The Japanese manga page remains primary evidentiary authority. Volume deep readings and cumulative ledgers are routing and interpretive infrastructure. Generated scenarios are never evidence.

# I. Method and controlled vocabulary

## 1. Claim-history chain

The default chain is:

> **V19 boundary formulation → V27 boundary formulation → V01–V34 current formulation**

An older formulation is not labeled defective merely because later evidence changed it. The transition describes the effect of later evidence on current authority.

## 2. Permitted transitions

- `PRESERVE` — later evidence leaves the mature formulation materially unchanged.
- `STRENGTHEN` — later evidence materially increases support or extends the tested range without changing the governing formulation.
- `REVISE` — later evidence requires a materially different formulation, scope, causal structure, or qualification.
- `DOWNGRADE` — later evidence weakens confidence or narrows the claim without fully rejecting it.
- `REJECT` — the current corpus no longer supports the claim as formulated.
- `OPEN` — the evidence remains insufficient for responsible closure.

No other transition labels are used.

## 3. Epistemic distinctions

This ledger distinguishes:

- **TEXTUAL FACT** — directly established by the manga;
- **CHARACTER BELIEF / TESTIMONY** — established as something a character says, remembers, or believes;
- **STRONG INFERENCE** — best explanation of multiple source observations but not directly narrated as omniscient fact;
- **SYNTHETIC INTERPRETATION** — cross-volume formulation that organizes evidence and counterevidence;
- **OPEN MECHANISM / OPEN CAUSAL WEIGHT** — explicitly unresolved even at V34.

A strong character statement remains speaker-positioned unless the source independently establishes its truth.

## 4. Primary-source verification codes

| Code | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `PSV-0` | No immediate page reinspection required for ledger use; reopen for exact quotation or disputed elevation. |
| `PSV-JA` | Reinspect exact Japanese wording/register. |
| `PSV-VIS` | Reinspect page/panel sequence, composition, body language, or visual juxtaposition. |
| `PSV-MECH` | Reinspect mechanism, chronology, and source-position evidence. |
| `PSV-JA+VIS` | Reinspect both wording and visual sequence. |
| `PSV-MIX` | Reinspect wording, visual sequence, and mechanism/source-position boundaries. |

## 5. Confidence rule

Confidence attaches to the formulation actually written. A `HIGH` claim may contain an explicitly `OPEN` sub-question; this means the supported boundary is high-confidence, not that every adjacent mechanism has been solved.

# II. Executive disposition summary

The mature V27 corpus was already conservative. Consequently, the thirty-two core claims produce no full-series `REJECT`, `DOWNGRADE`, or terminal `OPEN` transition at the row level; their unresolved submechanisms remain explicit inside the revised formulations. The most consequential revisions concern Eren’s causality, freedom/responsibility, Levi’s direct-choice ethic, Annie’s later state, Mikasa’s love/agency threshold, post-coup legitimacy, and Titan/Paths ontology.

The single hypothesis-level rejection is the attempted universalization of Eren’s anti-instrumentalization from Historia and friends to persons generally. The local care principle survives; the universal ethic does not.

## 2.1 Mature full-series governing formulation

> **The complete manga moves from freedom as escape, through freedom as authorship, to freedom as non-transferable responsibility among causally entangled persons. Recognition, love, memory, power, victimization, and inherited purpose may explain action and sometimes constrain it; none automatically grants jurisdiction over other lives or transfers authorship of the resulting harm. Ordinary life supplies the positive value that extermination, reproductive salvation, heroic abstraction, and inherited office repeatedly fail to exhaust.**

This is a synthesis routing formulation, not a substitute for the twenty specialist documents. Its strongest counterpressure remains the series’ refusal to offer a complete metaphysical account of future-memory determinism, Ymir’s interiority, retained dead-shifter agency, or the final tree.

## AOT-FSCR-C001 — Eren’s freedom drive predates bereavement but cannot be reduced to one origin

**Claim class:** character causality / motive architecture.  
**V19 boundary:** Freedom desire predates Carla; her death gives the motive punitive/exterminatory form.  
**V27 boundary:** Eren's confinement-resistance and positive outside horizon predate bereavement. Carla's death gives the drive an exterminatory object, but later action cannot be explained by revenge alone. The relation among disposition, chosen commitment, inherited influence, and self-coercion remains open.  
**V01–V34 current formulation:** Eren’s resistance to confinement and his positive outside-world horizon predate Carla’s death. Bereavement gives that drive an exterminatory object, but the complete manga establishes a wider, irreducibly multi-causal architecture: attachment, Paradis protection, rejection of inherited-body sacrifice, future-memory/Founder constraint, alliance heroization, death fear, and an intrinsic desire for an unobstructed world all matter. The deepest causal origin of the flattening desire remains OPEN.  
**Transition:** `STRENGTHEN`.

### Post-V27 adjudication

V33 converts the populated outside world from merely an enemy problem into a disappointed personal horizon, and V34 explicitly states that Eren likely would have flattened the world even without knowing that his friends would stop him. This does not erase the protection, temporal, or relational channels; it blocks every account that makes any one of them exhaustive.

**Earlier evidence route:** V21 distinguishes Armin's continuing sea dream from Eren's revenge displacement (`AOT_V21_E006`); V24–V25 show Eren interpreting his drive as older than the immediate enemy and continuing after human recognition (`AOT_V24_E020-E023`, `AOT_V25_E008-E014`).  
**Post-checkpoint evidence:** `AOT_V33_E005`, `AOT_V33_E007`, `AOT_V33_E009`, `AOT_V33_E010`, `AOT_V34_E035`, `AOT_V34_E038`, `AOT_V34_E039`, `AOT_V34_E040`.

### Counterpressure and inference ceiling

Future-memory and Founder temporal collapse materially constrain the decision environment. Eren’s own inability to explain why he wanted the unobstructed world means the text does not supply a complete first-cause psychology. ‘Born this way,’ ‘forced by the future,’ ‘only protecting friends,’ and ‘only seeking defeat’ are all too simple.

**Primary specialist home:** `AOT_02_EREN_JAEGER_FREEDOM_DESIRE_CAUSALITY_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md`.  
**Secondary routes:** `AOT_14_FREEDOM_AUTONOMY_PERSONHOOD_INHERITANCE_AND_RESPONSIBILITY.md`, `AOT_19_ENDING_CAUSALITY_MEMORY_HISTORICAL_RECURRENCE_AND_POSTWAR_TIME.md`.

## AOT-FSCR-C002 — Freedom culminates as non-transferable responsibility under causes

**Claim class:** central philosophical synthesis.  
**V19 boundary:** Freedom develops into authorship under inherited constraints plus access to positive horizons.  
**V27 boundary:** Freedom is authored, revisable agency under inheritance **and among other agents**. Self-authorship is incomplete without reciprocal limits on the right to spend other people's bodies, relationships, and futures.  
**V01–V34 current formulation:** Freedom is not the absence of inheritance, attachment, coercion, memory, or causation. It is contested authorship under those conditions, judged by the jurisdiction a person claims over other agents and by whether responsibility is transferred to victims, children, followers, or fate. Causes may explain and constrain action; they do not automatically justify, absolve, or erase authorship.  
**Transition:** `REVISE`.

### Post-V27 adjudication

V28 places Eren’s self-certifying ‘I am free’ beside his denial of Armin and Mikasa’s authority over their own minds. V30 makes causality recursive rather than absent, V33 exposes ‘freedom’ as an empty horizon produced by occluding victims, and V34 separates temporal constraint from absolution. Mikasa supplies the relational counterexample: love remains, obedience does not.

**Earlier evidence route:** Kruger names freedom and imposes mission (`AOT_V22_E010-E012`); Reiner constructs a role after manipulated selection; Eren distinguishes self-entered hell (`AOT_V24_E003-E023`); the V26 mirror and V27 sovereignty crisis show authorship becoming self-coercive and interpersonally invasive (`AOT_V26_E029-E030`, `AOT_V27_E001-E005`, `E035-E043`).  
**Post-checkpoint evidence:** `AOT_V28_E016`, `AOT_V28_E024`, `AOT_V30_E029`, `AOT_V30_E030`, `AOT_V33_E010`, `AOT_V33_E035`, `AOT_V33_E036`, `AOT_V33_E050`, `AOT_V34_E029`, `AOT_V34_E034`, `AOT_V34_E038`, `AOT_V34_E040`, `AOT_V34_E041`.

## AOT-FSCR-C003 — Home and safety are plural goods, not a license to erase other homes

**Claim class:** home / ordinary-life / political ethics.  
**V19 boundary:** Ordinary safety/home has intrinsic value; the problem is safety naturalized as permanent unchosen confinement.  
**V27 boundary:** Safety and home remain intrinsic goods, but no group's home is morally exhaustive. Protection becomes illegitimate when it preserves territory or population by denying protected persons political standing or pre-authoring other people's lives.  
**V01–V34 current formulation:** Home is place, relation, memory, ordinary routine, future expectation, and political obligation. It is intrinsically valuable and irreducibly plural. A project that preserves one home by rendering every other home expendable corrupts the value it claims to defend; the ending creates a political opportunity to negotiate among homes but does not guarantee permanent peace.  
**Transition:** `STRENGTHEN`.

### Post-V27 adjudication
