# Attack on Titan — V01–V34 Synthesis Evidence Matrix

## 0. Function and authority

This matrix is the **retrieval and coverage bridge** between the canonical volume-local deep readings, `AOT_FULL_SERIES_CLAIM_REVISION_LEDGER.md`, and the twenty specialist synthesis homes defined by the post-V34 architecture. It answers a narrower question than a synthesis essay: **where should a later writer retrieve the evidence needed to support or challenge a stabilized full-series claim?**

It does **not** replace the deep readings, manufacture new interpretations to fill empty cells, or make every listed volume equally probative. `P` means primary/high-diagnostic support for that domain in the volume; `S` means significant supporting evidence; `—` means the matrix does not currently route that volume as materially necessary for the domain. Character numbers refer to specialist documents 02–09.

Historical checkpoints and the frozen prospective register remain immutable. This artifact routes them forward; it does not rewrite their earlier knowledge boundaries. Exact Japanese wording, disputed mechanism, or load-bearing visual form still escalates to the original Japanese manga page.

### Reinspection priority

- **P0 — mandatory before specialist publication:** V30–V34. These volumes carry the densest recursive-causality, Ymir, Rumbling, ordinary-life, Mikasa/Eren, and ending evidence, and the claim ledger explicitly calls for primary-page reinspection.
- **P1 — selective locator backfill before load-bearing specialist use:** V01–V18. Their canonical migrated deep readings contain stable evidence IDs and chapter anchors, but image/page locators remain selectively incomplete; this is a provenance gap, not an interpretive failure.
- **P2 — normal verification:** V19–V29. Their canonical native V2.1 readings already contain image/page-level locators sufficient to seed the locator index; specialists should still re-open the primary page when wording or visual form is load-bearing.
