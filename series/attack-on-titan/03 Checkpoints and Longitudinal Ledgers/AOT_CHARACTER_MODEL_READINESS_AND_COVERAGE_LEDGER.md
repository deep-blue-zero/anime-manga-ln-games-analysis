## Meaning of statuses

**STRONG PROVISIONAL @V19** — enough evidence for bounded V19-state hypothetical reasoning and prospective prediction, provided scenario distance and low-stakes limitations are stated.

**PROVISIONAL-HIGH** — enough breadth for useful prediction in several domains but not yet enough to imply unrestricted transfer.

**PROVISIONAL** — useful reconstruction is possible in well-evidenced domains, but one or more major dimensions remain thin.

**CONTEXT-STRONG** — highly reconstructible inside a specific relation/arc, less reliable elsewhere.

**PARTIAL** — character summary is possible; free novel-situation simulation should remain cautious.

Sequential source coverage is now complete through V34. No character is automatically a `CANONICAL FULL-SERIES MODEL` merely because source coverage is complete; generation and validation are now permitted downstream.

## 50% checkpoint re-adjudication

The checkpoint strengthens several model-level conclusions that were previously distributed across volume readings.

### 1. State/role conditioning is mandatory

Reiner and Bertolt make trait averaging especially dangerous. Reiner's soldier and Warrior roles are both psychologically real; Bertolt's V19 decisiveness is a marked transition rather than proof of timeless boldness. Any model that collapses these states into one adjective set is invalid.

### 2. Recognition cannot be used as a simple violence inhibitor

Annie, Reiner and especially Bertolt show that affection, guilt, pity and explicit human recognition may coexist with continued lethal mission. Predictions must therefore model competing commitments rather than assuming empathy automatically produces restraint.

### 3. Eren's freedom model has materially changed

V18-V19 require any current Eren model to include pluralized/universalized birthright and collective capability. Earlier anger/extermination evidence remains active, so the model must preserve contradiction rather than selecting one as the "real" Eren.

### 4. Armin's model must include stale-model failure

Armin is not merely good at predicting others. His strength is building and revising models. V19 provides a canonical case where an earlier interpersonal lever no longer works because Bertolt has changed.

### 5. Senior-command models remain domain-skewed

Levi, Erwin and Hange are extremely well evidenced under crisis, command, institutional conflict and technical decision-making. Their apparent high readiness must not be converted into high-confidence leisure, romance, domestic preference or broad everyday voice simulation.

### 6. Ordinary-life evidence is a first-class validation control

Jean currently provides unusually useful peer-group/ordinary behavior relative to several more narratively central adults. Sasha and Connie also preserve low-stakes and culturally specific behavior that can later expose overdramatic simulation bias.

## Required readiness gates for future canonical models

A major character normally needs:

- multiple developmental states or evidence that the baseline remains stable;
- at least two materially different pressure contexts;
- more than one relationship context;
- Japanese voice evidence beyond a single famous scene;
- ordinary-life control evidence or an explicit domain limitation;
- negative constraints/counterexamples;
- primary-source routing for load-bearing model claims;
- prospective or clearly labeled retrospective validation.
