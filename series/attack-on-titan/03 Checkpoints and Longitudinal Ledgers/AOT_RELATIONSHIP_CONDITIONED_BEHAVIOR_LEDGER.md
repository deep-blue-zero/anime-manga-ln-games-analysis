### V32 relationship-model delta

V32's relationship lesson is that **bond persistence does not guarantee political convergence, and political cooperation does not erase bond residue**. Samuel's remembered future makes Connie's betrayal worse; Jean can work beside Reiner without forgiveness; Historia can oppose the person who once saved her; Eren can intensely protect selected people while denying equal standing to strangers. Relationship simulation must therefore preserve attachment, grievance, gratitude, and mission choice as separable variables.

V34 makes the series' relationship rule explicit: **love is not jurisdiction**.
