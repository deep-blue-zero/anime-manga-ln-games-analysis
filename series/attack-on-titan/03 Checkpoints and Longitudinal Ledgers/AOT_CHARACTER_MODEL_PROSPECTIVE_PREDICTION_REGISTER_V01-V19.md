# Attack on Titan — Character Model Prospective Prediction Register V01-V19

## 0. Freeze statement

This artifact is the prospective holdout register required by `AOT_CHARACTER_MODELING_AND_SIMULATION_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md` and `AOT_CHARACTER_RECONSTRUCTION_AND_VALIDATION_METHOD_V1.md` before any analytical exposure to Volume 20.

The prediction text below was frozen using only the canonical V01-V19 corpus. Later evidence may be appended as adjudication, but **must not rewrite, silently soften, or delete the frozen prediction wording**. Later results use only:

`PASS | PARTIAL | FAIL | NOT_TESTED | CONFOUNDED`

A failed prediction is model-revision evidence. It is not grounds to alter the historical prediction.

### Scoring rule

A later scene is a fair test only when the relevant state vector is sufficiently comparable: character boundary, knowledge state, role, relationship, stakes, time pressure, physical/emotional condition, and available alternatives. A superficially similar action under materially different conditions should be `CONFOUNDED`, not forced into PASS/FAIL.

### Scenario distance

- `D0` — canon-near: substantially the same pressure/relationship class.
- `D1` — analogous transfer: new event with close canonical analogues.
- `D2` — moderate transfer: important unfamiliar features remain.
- `D3` — far transfer: outside current cultural/institutional/life-experience coverage.

This register concentrates on D0-D1 predictions because the immediate purpose is longitudinal validation against V20-V34.

# V. Frozen-register audit

## Included characters

**Broad Class A:** Eren, Mikasa, Armin, Jean, Historia, Reiner, Levi, Erwin, Hange.

**Domain-limited A-D:** Bertolt, Ymir.

**Narrow Class B probes:** Annie, Sasha, Connie, Zeke/Beast operator.

## Deliberately excluded from broad prediction

No unrestricted prospective model is asserted for characters whose V01-V19 evidence is too sparse, too high-stakes-only, or too relation-locked. Exclusion is a coverage judgment, not a claim of character simplicity.

## Known blind spots at freeze

- ordinary leisure, food, domestic habit, rest, romance, and boredom are under-sampled for most characters;
- manga evidence supports written-Japanese voice, not anime performance;
- V19 is unusually crisis-heavy and may overrepresent battle-state repertoires;
- later political/history revelations may change knowledge states dramatically, confounding apparent personality tests;
- injury, Titan mechanics, command succession, or radical role changes may make some later scenes incomparable to the frozen V19 state.

## Immutability rule

After this freeze, later volumes may append adjudication tables or a separate adjudication ledger. The prose under each `Frozen prediction` and `Would count against the model` field is historical test material and must not be rewritten after outcome knowledge.
