# AOT Everyday Life, Preferences, and Low-Stakes Behavior Ledger

## Purpose

This ledger exists to correct crisis-domain bias. It records ordinary social behavior, routine, humor, training, food, rest, embarrassment, irritation, and material preferences when the manga actually supplies them.

**Unknown is a valid result.** A character's ideology does not authorize invented hobbies, food preferences, domestic competence, flirting style, or leisure behavior.

## Coverage rule

Use `ATTESTED`, `LIMITED`, `NO_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`, or `CONTEXT_LOCKED`.

## Anti-invention rules

Do not infer an everyday preference from:

- a military ration eaten once;
- a survival necessity;
- anime-only comic material unless a separate adaptation layer is activated;
- fandom reputation;
- merchandise or parody;
- one expressive reaction without recurrence;
- ideological symbolism.

A model may say "the manga currently gives insufficient evidence". That is preferable to a vivid but unsupported answer.
