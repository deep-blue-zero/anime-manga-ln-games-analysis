### V34 behavior-model delta

V34 closes the sequential behavior corpus by making **responsibility non-transferable**. Future knowledge, inherited roles, grief, love, biological power and institutional history remain causally explanatory, but none automatically erase authorship. Eren is the strongest negative control: genuine love, temporal constraint, political design, remorse and an irreducible desire to flatten the world coexist. Mikasa supplies the reciprocal positive constraint: attachment can survive while behavioral obedience ends. Armin and Zeke make ordinary non-instrumental life a direct decision variable rather than decorative background.
