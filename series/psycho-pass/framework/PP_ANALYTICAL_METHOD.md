# Psycho-Pass — Analytical method

This method turns authorized episode evidence into revisable interpretations of persons, institutions, moral arguments and audiovisual form. It establishes questions and tests, not findings about an uninspected story. The [source register](../source-inventory/PP_SOURCE_REGISTER.md) owns exact editions and the [architecture](PP_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns where accepted work lives.

## Governing method and adopted AV companion

The governing method is the [generic anime episode method](../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_ANIME_EPISODE_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). The [continuous audiovisual method](../../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_CONTINUOUS_AUDIOVISUAL_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md) is its companion for performance, motion, temporal construction and sound-image relations. Apply the [lane-routing policy](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_MEDIUM_AND_INSPECTION_LANE_ROUTING_POLICY.md), [bundle specification](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_EPISODE_BUNDLE_SPECIFICATION.md) and [AV reconciliation protocol](../../../governance/source-policies/MANGA_ANIME_AUDIOVISUAL_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) together. All anime duties remain operative: source-language inspection, scene construction, causality, character and relationship change, visual grammar, music and sound, uncertainty, counterreadings and cumulative updates.

The adopted local profile is `PP_AV_PRIMARY_CHARACTER_AND_FORM`. It assigns the principal an AV-first character and episode-form study as part of each full reading. Its load-bearing responsibilities are behavioral timing, performed speech and silence, distributed reaction changes, police/social spatial organization, whole-episode editing and pacing, and interactions between sound and image. These cannot always be reconstructed from selected stills and dialogue. The recognized AV-primary mirror route governs the character/AV interpretation, while verified bundle locators and source-language evidence independently support quotations, exact terminology and reproducibility. A continuous viewing does not certify text accuracy, a complete bundle or complete model perception.

For each authorized unit the admission packet must state the actual AV question and its evidence need. Whole-episode continuity may be selected under bundle §§8.2, 8.5 and 8.9 when the assigned responsibility concerns distributed performance or whole-episode construction. That is the intended profile for these extended episodes, subject to a recorded unit-specific rationale. A bounded disputed gesture or synchronization claim may use targeted clips with context. Static inventory, a legible sign or exact wording alone receives `VIDEO_NOT_REQUIRED` where the governing evidence permits it. Do not impose video where it adds no relevant evidence, and do not use a targeted clip to certify a whole episode. If runtime cannot inspect a required channel, record the missing coverage, prepare an adequate faithful route or leave that claim and unit acceptance pending. Provider preference grants no fallback waiver.

## Episode identity, order and disclosure

Use `PP-S01NE-E01` through `PP-S01NE-E11` for New Edit units. A media witness includes edition, immutable hash and clock convention; a later re-encode gets its own witness identity. Original broadcast identifiers `PP-S01OB-E01` through `E22` are reserved for independently verified comparison material. An original pair is not presumed to equal a New Edit episode and no timestamp mapping follows from numbering. Reordered or added material requires a segment-level evidence crosswalk only after both witnesses are admitted for that comparison.

Every episode reading declares entering and terminal knowledge boundaries for the **viewer in the selected edition**, each focal character where material, and the analyst. Freeze entering predictions, uncertainties and candidate explanations before revealing the next unit. Write or finalize its opening synopsis only afterward. Record prior familiarity and later-source exposure, including pretrained knowledge that cannot be excluded. A contaminated analyst may contribute retrospective interpretation with disclosure; that contribution cannot certify a blind prospective inference.

Maintain release availability, in-universe event order and knowledge/disclosure order separately. A flashback's depiction locator, inferred event date and audience disclosure are different fields. Unknown dates remain unknown; an event may have only relative constraints. A later revelation can revise the current franchise conclusion while the historical reading remains valid evidence of what was defensible at its own horizon. New Edit additions may disclose information at a different point from the original edition even when continuity is shared. Never import the later release's knowledge into an earlier prospective freeze.

## Opening story synopsis and episode reconstruction

After source/boundary identification and a compact entering-context statement, each plot-bearing reading begins with a substantial **current-episode story synopsis**, before extended interpretation. It must let a reader reconstruct what happens: situation, important actions in causal order, named participants, decisions, reversals, consequences and the ending state. Preserve significant parallel action and shifts of perspective. Include ordinary and connective scenes when they change relationships, routines, information or the force of a later choice. Identify unresolved causal gaps as gaps.

The synopsis is narrative prose, not a list of themes, claims, timestamps or analyst conclusions. Keep it bounded to this edition and unit; no later explanations. Describe an assertion as something its speaker says, not automatically a world fact. Use known proper names and role/alias distinctions at the legitimate knowledge boundary. A brief synopsis is inadequate if it erases a turning point, a secondary person or the mechanism of a decision. The following scene map supplies precise locators, but does not carry story explanation omitted from the synopsis. Finalize the synopsis after prospective captures freeze so it cannot backfill supposedly prior knowledge.

Reconstruct each consequential sequence as initiating condition → actor's information and options → action/inaction → immediate outcome → changed state. Distinguish shown causality, explicitly reported causality and the analyst's causal hypothesis. Test omitted alternatives, time constraints, coercion and mistaken information before calling a decision freely chosen or inevitable. Keep the episode's actual ordering and transitions alongside any event-order reconstruction.

## Persons, agency and ordinary life

For every differentiated individual, identify what they want, fear, attend to, know and can do at the current boundary. Separate stable disposition, present state, public role, strategy, self-report and another person's attribution. Describe the stimulus, response and consequence behind a psychological inference. Avoid diagnosing a person from a single expressive image, institutional label or numerical display. The work's categories are objects of analysis, not the analyst's validated psychometrics.

Track agency through the alternatives perceived, institutional constraints, available resources, cost of dissent and later consequences. Competence is domain-specific and may change under fatigue, threat, social pressure or familiarity. Record restraint and non-action when the source establishes an available alternative. Preserve contradictory behavior and mundane variation rather than converting a person into an ideology, occupational function or trauma summary.

Attend to routine work, humor, rest, food, material surroundings, preferences, awkwardness, affection and practical care when present. Minor interactions may disclose ordinary baseline behavior that makes a crisis legible. Do not invent such domains to fill a portrait. Their absence is an evidence limit. Keep sexual, romantic or intimate inference specifically sourced, directional, horizon-bounded and open to less intimate explanations.

Track relationships directionally: A's trust, dependence, obligation, knowledge and available influence toward B do not establish B's reciprocity. Record exchanges, repairs, breaches and asymmetries with context. Identify the difference between a rank relation, performed deference, emotional attachment and practical coordination. Group descriptions retain consequential named members and dissenting conduct.

## Institutions, policing and measurement

For Sibyl and each observed institution, distinguish official claim, announced rule, implemented procedure, measured output, actual enforcement, exception and consequence. Ask who observes, who classifies, who acts, who bears error, who can appeal and who benefits. A displayed measurement establishes that an output is displayed at that moment; its validity, interpretation and causal role require evidence. A character's belief in an institution is separately tracked from its demonstrated reliability.

Follow incentives, hierarchy, delegated authority, discretion, resource constraints and accountability through concrete cases. Record whether a claimed constraint is formal, technical, cultural, individual or merely asserted. Consider selective enforcement, incomplete information, dependency and unintended effects. One exception may challenge a universal claim without establishing the opposite universal claim. Do not import external legal, scientific or political categories as settled explanations.

Keep institutional capacity, legitimacy, morality and effectiveness distinct. A procedure can achieve a local aim while imposing an unacknowledged cost; a character can object convincingly yet act inconsistently. Locate the affected people and mechanisms behind abstractions such as order, freedom, safety or responsibility. Institution/world-state records link to source observations, not only to a thematic essay.

## Philosophical claims and narrative support

For a substantial philosophical or political proposition, preserve the speaker, immediate stakes, audience, rhetorical purpose and relevant Japanese wording. State the proposition precisely enough to test. Separate descriptive, causal and normative premises, and identify hidden quantifiers or shifts in what a term means. Track how action, consequence, framing and rival voices support or resist it.

An eloquent speech, a quoted thinker or a visually commanding character is not automatically the work's position. Nor does a speaker's bad conduct logically refute every proposition they state. Test coherence, application and consequences separately. A governing interpretation normally needs at least two materially plausible rival explanations and the strongest source counterexample. Explain why the preferred reading fits better, what it leaves unexplained and what later evidence would change it. Where no confident winner exists, preserve the competition.

Use outside philosophy only when authorized and clearly separated as secondary interpretive context with provenance. Do not retroactively attribute unsaid arguments or unavailable reading to a character. Quotation recognition without verified wording and source is a hypothesis. Avoid turning a case-specific causal conflict into a franchise-wide thesis before the corpus supports that scale.

## Japanese, performance and audiovisual form

Source-language dialogue, visible text, translation and the analyst's gloss are separate witnesses. Preserve names, pronouns, address forms, register changes, ellipsis, negation, modal force and who is addressed when they affect an interpretation. Check decisive wording against the admitted Japanese source and aligned audio/text. Supplier tags and Japanese-script detection do not establish officialness or fidelity. Machine transcription/translation remains an attributed derivative until reviewed. Cite only bounded necessary excerpts, keeping restricted transcripts outside Git.

Observe delivery and listening: pace, hesitation, overlap, volume, breath, emphasis, turn-taking, silence, reaction delay and whether expression is visible or obscured. A subtitle's timing is not proof of the precise spoken interval. An auditory observation requires actual listening; waveform measurement or decoded audio metadata alone does not provide it.

For consequential shots and sequences, describe framing, scale, angle, depth, blocking, gaze, motion, composition, light, color, cut placement and the relation to adjacent shots. Explain the effect in this context and test another explanation such as continuity, genre convention or staging necessity. Record recurring motifs as candidates with variations and counterinstances before asserting a stable symbolism. Do not infer a character's thought directly from color or editing.

Separate dialogue, environmental sound, sound effects, music and silence. Observe entrance/exit, repetition, sound perspective and synchronization with image/action. Distinguish source music from score where supported; uncertainty remains explicit. Record opening/ending/credits/post-credit material within the admitted unit and their evidence roles. Do not drop them merely because they recur, or force fresh interpretation where inspection finds no material change.

Every load-bearing continuous-AV observation follows the [reconciliation protocol](../integration/PP_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md): actual stream and sampling disclosure, calibration, `REPORTED` versus `ANCHORED`, source reinspection and bounded dispute handling. A sampling limitation can defeat a fine-grained motion claim even when whole-episode semantic coverage is adequate.

Retain the companion's distinct evidence classes: `CAV` for model continuous audiovisual observation, `AO` for model auditory observation, `FRM` for sampled-frame evidence, `TXT` for primary-language text, `AM` for deterministic measurement, `HL` for human listening/viewing, `INF` for supported inference and `INT` for interpretation. An emotion or intention inferred from observed behavior remains INF/INT. Exact timing finer than perceived resolution requires AM; exact wording requires verified TXT. A listening transcription stays an attributed AO/HL observation with uncertainty and is never itself TXT.

## Reading output and acceptance

A complete reading contains the opening story synopsis, source/edition and coverage receipt, scene/causal reconstruction, significant character and directed-relationship changes, institutional/world evidence, Japanese/performance and formal analysis, governing claims with alternatives and counterevidence, and an explicit ending state. Preserve the independent specialists' material contributions and the critical review, including rejected objections and unresolved differences. Give a current corpus status, not a generic checklist claiming every topic is complete.

Each observation gets a stable source-facing ID and each material proposition a stable claim ID. Mark observed event, textual evidence, character assertion, supported inference, hypothetical extrapolation and unknown separately. Coverage and confidence are different axes. A required evidence channel that is partial blocks the affected acceptance; optional gaps have explicit bounded consequences.

Before advancing an authorized high-water mark, accept the complete unit transaction: reading, local evidence/state, local claims/disclosure, necessary master dependencies, qualification reassessment, source locators and independent review dispositions. The principal cannot quietly accept only a synopsis or a ledger while calling the episode complete. The [architecture](PP_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [integration contract](../integration/PP_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) govern that transaction and subsequent synthesis.
