# S1 New Edit — Claims and disclosure

**Accepted records through `PP-S01NE-E02`: 24 local claims (`CLM-0001`–`0024`), 2 frozen prospective records (`PP-S01NE-PRE-0001`–`0002`), 15 disclosure events (`DISC-0001`–`0015`), 17 adjudicated multi-agent disputes (`DSP-01`–`17`), 9 retained `OPEN` horizon questions.** This ledger preserves the installment's changing explanations and legitimate knowledge, complementing [source observations](PP_S01NE_EVIDENCE_AND_STATE_LEDGER.md). It is not a prewritten philosophical reading.

## Prospective and disclosure records

Before the next authorized unit, freeze the entering boundary, current explanation, plausible alternatives, unresolved questions and conditional predictions. Give the freeze a stable `PP-S01NE-PRE-####` identity, exact artifact/version hash, source cutoff, writer and actual prior exposure. Preserve this record before source exposure and before finalizing the next reading's opening synopsis. A prediction distinguishes observation from extrapolation and states what would count for or against it. Silence about a future event is not a successful prediction.

A disclosure record `PP-S01NE-DISC-####` identifies the proposition or event, who knows/believes/suspects it, how it is acquired, exact edition/unit/locator, reliability and the earliest evidenced boundary. Audience access, focal-character access and analyst awareness are separate fields. Knowledge is not automatically shared across a scene, organization or relationship. Distinguish hearing a report, believing it and the underlying fact.

In-universe event dates or relative ordering are linked evidence constraints, not computed from the episode number. Release dates are metadata with independent provenance. For New Edit material, record its position in this version without inferring original-broadcast audience knowledge. If an authorized original-edition comparison later identifies inserted or reordered disclosure, create a paired crosswalk and separate edition horizons. Do not silently retitle an earlier freeze as if it had always contained later knowledge.

## Claims, alternatives and corrections

A material local claim uses `PP-S01NE-CLM-####`. Preserve its proposition, scope and continuity, applicable source/knowledge boundary, claim kind, supporting observation IDs/locators, contrary evidence, strongest alternatives, confidence, falsification/review trigger, dependencies and acceptance history. A character's claim about Sibyl, morality or another person is tagged as that character's assertion before any analytical endorsement.

Claim kinds distinguish observed/descriptive proposition, causal inference, psychological inference, normative argument attribution, formal interpretation and explicit speculation. A complex paragraph can require multiple claims when different evidence bears on its parts. Do not let an abstract thesis conceal an unverified fact or missing causal step. Two plausible rivals are expected for a governing interpretation; record why the evidence discriminates among them or why it does not.

Use the AV protocol's transitions where applicable: `PRESERVE`, `STRENGTHEN`, `REVISE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `REJECT`, `OPEN`. Each disposition states the prior claim/version, new evidence or argument, source boundary, reviewer and resulting assertion. Preserve rejected interpretations and the reason for rejection. Distinguish a factual error, a new disclosure, a witness difference and a legitimate changed interpretation. Revised claim IDs retain lineage rather than being silently reused for a different proposition.

## Independent work and compression checks

The durable unit checkpoint retains each assignment, source scope actually inspected, independence/exposure state, substantive observations, rival explanation, counterexample and final disposition. A reviewer reacting only to the principal's prose has not verified the source. Fresh neutral-source inspection is preferred before claims exposure. A contaminated reviewer records the limitation and cannot certify blindness.

Review whether synopsis and analysis preserve consequential secondary persons, ordinary scenes, Japanese rhetorical distinctions, causal options, directed relationships, institutional exceptions and audiovisual evidence. Check for unjustified trait generalization, ideological reduction, erased counterexamples and a source event converted into an overbroad thesis. Objection count and agreement rate are not quality measures. A rejected objection must retain the evidence-based reason; disagreement alone is not a revision mandate.

A new independent finding is separately identified before assessing whether it changes an existing claim. If local claims affect the current franchise understanding, send a bounded delta with evidence, uncertainty and dependency paths to the [master continuity/claim ledger](../../franchise/ledgers/PP_CONTINUITY_AND_CLAIM_LEDGER.md). Keep frozen local conclusions visible at their historical horizon even after master revision.
