## 2. Story Synopsis (`PP-S01NE-E01`)

*PSYCHO-PASS Season 1 New Edit* Episode 1 opens in media res atop a towering, neon-lit building at night. Walking along red bolted steel girders high above the city, a white-haired man (**Shōgo Makishima**, named later in the cold open and credits) delivers a calm internal monologue: anyone who calls humanity foolish includes themselves; understanding people requires observing what they look at rather than looking at them alone; and because the opposite of love is indifference rather than hatred, killing or inflicting pain requires genuine interest. Briefly closing his eyes, Makishima undercuts his own solemnity with a thin smile and a whispered *"Just kidding"* (*"nante ne"*). The opening theme *"abnormalize"* drives directly into a kinetic combat sequence inside the red-framed tower as a bruised, dark-suited man (**Shinya Kōgami**) fights armed opponents upward while Makishima watches amused from a spiral staircase. As the music cuts abruptly to cold room tone and echoing footsteps, Makishima descends toward the bleeding Kōgami above an unidentified motionless figure lying on the dark tiled floor. Over their silent standoff, a quiet, retrospective female voiceover—identifiable by voice and official captions as **Akane Tsunemori**—speculates that the two men understood each other from the first glance, fated to converge and looking solely at one another. Speaking across close-ups of each other's eyes, Makishima names *"Kōgami Shinya"* and Kōgami names *"Makishima Shōgo"* before a horizontal signal glitch snaps into the title card and shifts back to Akane's very first night on duty.

In pouring rain at a cordoned-off derelict zone (`District 13, Sector 6` in later report text), twenty-year-old rookie Public Safety Bureau (**PSB**, under the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare) Inspector **Akane Tsunemori** arrives breathless for her first assignment with Criminal Investigation Department (**CID**) Division 1. Senior Inspector **Nobuchika Ginoza** cuts short her stiff wet salute without returning it, warning that severe staffing shortages leave no time for rookie concessions, though he tosses her a dry field jacket. Ginoza briefs her on their target, thirty-nine-year-old **Nobuo Okura**: after maintaining a clear `29.3` weekly average, Okura's Psycho-Pass Hue crashed over three days to a clouded **Forest Green** (`126.3`), tripping a street scanner. He refused mandatory therapy from a security drone, fled into an off-registry abandoned ward lacking drone relays, and abducted a young female passerby as a hostage. As an armored transport van arrives, Ginoza delivers a stark indoctrination: Akane must never view **Enforcers** as fellow human beings like herself. Exceeding permissible Crime Coefficient thresholds, they are "ruined personalities" granted one permitted social role outside isolation as "hunting hounds"—beasts set loose to hunt beasts—while Akane becomes their "second owner." Four Enforcers disembark: flippant, playfully teasing **Shūsei Kagari**, laconic **Yayoi Kunizuka**, grizzled veteran **Tomomi Masaoka** (sporting an articulated metallic prosthetic left hand), and taciturn **Shinya Kōgami**. Ginoza keeps Yayoi and volatile Kagari under his own command and assigns steady Masaoka and Kōgami to accompany Akane.

When Akane asks how to proceed, Masaoka offers an easygoing introduction to the **Dominator**, a biometric enforcement firearm linked to the **Sibyl System** that authenticates Akane via cyan HUD (`2092.04.01`, age 20), communicates through directional audio audible only to its wielder, and unlocks exclusively on authorized targets. Asking for a tactical briefing, Akane runs headlong into Kōgami's blunt pragmatism: Enforcers hunt, Inspectors observe and bear formal responsibility, and if Akane objects to Kōgami's methods she is free to shoot him—since Enforcers are Latent Criminals too. Without even introducing himself, Kōgami walks off alone into the rain-soaked district, leaving Masaoka to introduce them both. Elsewhere in a shabby tenement room littered with pill blister packs, vials, and a syringe, an agitated Okura rants over his bound, bleeding hostage (**Chika Shimazu**): having lived timidly and obediently his whole life only to have a single scanner hit destroy his prospects for work and marriage, he resolves to seize whatever he wants—threatening to rape "a real-flesh woman, not virtual."

Patrolling the surprisingly bustling, neon-lit back alleys of the supposedly "uninhabited" sector behind Masaoka, Akane is startled by a vagrant whose Dominator scan reads a safe `52.1` (trigger locked). Moments later, testing Kōgami's unsettling remark, Akane hesitantly raises her Dominator at Masaoka's back. The HUD registers **`127.4`**—higher than Okura's initial street alert—and unlocks as a **"discretionary enforcement target"** (*nin'i shikkō taishō*). Masaoka pauses, turns over his shoulder with a calm, unbothered half-smile, and walks on, leaving Akane shaken that a colleague who talks so warmly is officially classified as a Latent Criminal. Leading her into the wet neon arcade, Masaoka advises the academy valedictorian to forget deductive classroom theories: police work is "a lump of absurdity" (*rifujin no katamari*) in an era where machines supposedly see through every human heart yet hatred, deceit, and cruelty persist. Before Akane can voice her dissent, Kagari radios from the fourth floor of the KT Building, reporting that Chika is near her limit and requesting permission to move in. With Ginoza's approval, Kagari bursts in and shoots Okura (`193.6`) with Non-Lethal Paralyzer—but illicit stimulants let Okura shrug off the stun, smash through a window, and drag Chika into the street. Instantly every officer's Dominator updates: Okura's threat level has crossed **`300`** into **Lethal Eliminator**—what Masaoka bitterly calls "Sibyl's oracle," declaring Okura irredeemable and no longer needed in the world.

Hiding briefly in a dark stairwell, Okura thrusts a handheld Psycho-Pass scanner into Chika's eyes (`110`, **Steelblue**—whose screen politely recommends nearby drone therapy) while maliciously convincing her that her Hue is ruined like his and that capture means lifelong torture as a Latent Criminal. Cornered moments later inside a coastal warehouse by Masaoka and Akane, Okura shrieks at them to drop their weapons. Demonstrating veteran craft, Masaoka deliberately slides his biometric-locked Dominator—glowing with red lock LEDs—across the concrete and raises his hands beside Akane. Laughing maniacally, Okura grabs Masaoka's gun, aims it, and screams *"Die!"*—only to stare in bewildered rage when the unauthorized weapon refuses to fire. Distracted by the trap, Okura never sees Kōgami, who has circled behind them using Masaoka and Akane as a decoy. Offering a dry *"My condolences,"* Kōgami fires Lethal Eliminator; Okura's arm and upper torso violently swell and rupture, drenching Chika in gore.

Screaming and blood-soaked, Chika recoils from Akane's desperate attempts to reassure her. Masaoka tells Akane to check Chika with her gun: trauma-induced stress ("Psycho-Hazard") has spiked Chika's Crime Coefficient to **`162.9`**, marking the rescued victim herself as an enforcement target. Saying it cannot be helped, Masaoka raises his Dominator to paralyze her—and Akane lunges forward, wrapping her arms around Masaoka's waist to stop him. Masaoka shouts furiously that subduing panicked targets is exactly what Paralyzer is for and roars that **"the Dominator is Sibyl's eye"**—the city's system itself has judged Chika a threat. While Akane and Masaoka clash, Kōgami says nothing; he simply turns and walks silently down the steps after Chika, who has bolted into a dark storage aisle. Surrounded by spilled fuel jerrycans she knocked over in her flight, a terrified Chika ignites a disposable lighter to keep pursuers back. Kōgami corners her and aims; as Akane begs him to stop because Chika is a victim, Kōgami's Dominator transforms into **Lethal Eliminator**, commanding Chika's immediate elimination. Turning his face toward Akane, Kōgami moves his lips silently—completely unvoiced beneath a throbbing electronic pulse—and draws back his trigger finger. Screaming *"Stop!"*, Akane fires her Paralyzer into Kōgami's back. As Kōgami collapses unconscious into the puddle, Akane's knees buckle onto the wet floor; remaining seated and non-threatening, she pleads with Chika to put down the lighter so the gun will not kill her, offering a fragile, trembling human smile. Chika clicks off the flame and drops the lighter into the puddle. Immediately, Akane's Dominator downgrades Chika back to **Non-Lethal Paralyzer**. Just as Chika begins to answer Akane with a faint relieved smile, a Paralyzer blast from the overhead catwalk drops Chika unconscious: **Ginoza**, standing above flanked by Yayoi and Kagari, has fired down to enforce custody, icily ordering Akane to justify her situational judgment in a formal report. Watching from behind, Masaoka scratches his head and mutters that they have received quite an extraordinary rookie.

Rather than breaking for credits, the New Edit bridges seamlessly into an anesthetized memory/reflection voiced by Kōgami (`Isoflurane 2.3%` on his hospital monitor). Over slow piano, bare hands turning a Dominator, Kōgami's reflection in a cracked windowpane, a brief alley shot of Ginoza alongside an **unnamed cigarette-smoking colleague**, and daylight shots of Kōgami lighting a `Spinel` cigarette on a high-rise balcony, Kōgami outlines the cold utilitarian calculus of the Sibyl System: it intentionally builds human non-adapters into its own design (`"the system incorporates them into the system"`), pursuing "the greatest happiness of the greatest number" rather than everyone's happiness and tolerating pockets of disorder because "a perfect society is established by giving up on a perfect society." Pivoting sharply (*"However..."*), he recalls *"that incident"* and *"his death"*—a crime "clearly abnormal" and impossible under Sibyl administration. Crushing his cigarette pack triggers violent strobe flashes of spinning exhaust fans and a grotesque, pale, blood-spattered screaming face, hard-cutting to Kōgami's agitated electrocardiogram (`HR 112→111`, `BP 158/106`, `SpO2 98%`) and an inverted close-up of his eyes opening as he slowly flexes his stiff right fingers back into motion.

Twenty-seven seconds later, an inverted eye-opening match cut wakes Akane beneath her polka-dot alarm clock (`11.05.Wed, 08:15`). Her floating holographic jellyfish concierge **Candy** cheerily announces `November 5` and a spotless **Powder Blue** Psycho-Pass Hue—even as Akane sits up with dark eye circles, flashes back to shooting Kōgami, grips her blanket sweating, and buries her face groaning *"Ah, I blew it."* Requesting **"Victor Horta, Hôtel Tassel,"** she overlays her stark gray concrete apartment with warm Art Nouveau woodwork and stained glass, negotiates Candy's `240 kcal` suggestion down to an extruded `200 kcal` Chinese-flavored breakfast block, watches morning news and weather (`Shinjuku Area Stress Level 3`), and chooses a casual outfit via holographic mirror compact. The instant her door shuts behind her, the warm interior dissolves back into pitch-dark raw concrete illuminated by one blue standby LED. At a bright rooftop café served by teardrop drones, Akane meets her friends **Yuki Funahara** and **Kaori Minase**. Forbidden to explain classified PSB details, Akane confides that she made a terrible first-day mistake and could not sleep. Kaori teases her as a **"mental beauty"** (*mentaru bijin*) whose Hue stays Clear Color without emotional care, and both friends scold her self-doubt as a "luxury problem"—noting Akane scored `700` points and rejected elite ministries like Economy and Science & Technology, whereas Yuki received a `C` rating for physical work and Kaori endures stiff shoulders as a systems engineer. Sensing Akane's real unease, Kaori apologizes and affirms her not from Sibyl's aptitude score but from **"her intuition as a friend,"** while Yuki lightheartedly recites official Sibyl liturgy—*"Those who are able do what they ought to do; this is the grace Sibyl bestowed upon humanity"*—immediately undercutting it with a playful *"Just kidding"* (*"nanchite ne"*) that makes Akane pause.

Approaching the monolithic PSB tower for her `14:30` shift, Akane composes herself in her compact mirror, pixel-shifts from casual trench coat into her dark Inspector suit, and visits **Comprehensive Analysis Laboratory No. 2** to check on Kōgami. Outside the door, Yayoi steps out fastening her tie and sweeps past Akane's hesitant smile without a word. Inside, languid blonde analyst **Shion Karanomori**—who openly identifies herself as a Latent Criminal overworked because of her medical license—teases Akane mischievously about shooting "Shinya-kun" and lobbies the "fast-track" Inspector to build a CID pool and bar lounge. Pulling up live infirmary video, Shion explains that Akane's direct spinal Paralyzer hit temporarily left Kōgami unable to stand or talk, yet instantly drops her provocative banter upon seeing Akane's quiet guilt, gently reassuring her that a full day of rest will clear the aftereffects.

In the cluttered concrete CID Division 1 office—where Kagari plays a handheld console amid desk toys and Yayoi reads guitar sheet music through a red earpiece—bare-handed Akane struggles to draft **Form 71** (her formal apology/incident report) until her input tablet jams. Asking for a spare, she is mocked by Kagari (*"Enforcer Kōgami is using it—shot by a Paralyzer and under treatment!"*), earning an instant off-frame smack from Yayoi, who quietly hands Akane her own spare tablet (*"Here. Use this"*). Masaoka arrives late, only for an **Area Stress Elevation Alert** at the **Ikō Greishill** shopping mall in Adachi Ward to interrupt (briefly waking Kōgami in the infirmary). Coming off shift, Kagari cheerily wishes them luck; Yayoi calmly volunteers to cover Kōgami's vacancy, but Masaoka declines and ushers a startled Akane out as sole Inspector on duty. Sealing mall exits with patrol drones, Masaoka wraps himself, Akane, and the Dominator carrier drone in full-body holographic **Commissa-chan** cartoon police-mascot costumes to avoid alarming crowds. Without using a scanner, Masaoka spots a pale, sweating youth (**Youth Latent Criminal** in credits) glaring bitterly at a flirting couple from behind a faux-classical column, telling an astonished Akane that *"a beast smells the scent of a beast."* Speaking through the holo-suit's squeaky Commissa-chan child-voice modulator, Masaoka politely requests a scan; when the youth shoves Akane down and bolts, Masaoka smoothly blocks, throws, and pins him inside the unblinking cartoon smile, scans his clouded Hue reflected in the mascot's giant pupils, and marches him toward a loading-dock van (`POLICE 103`) past indifferent shoppers as the youth pleads *"Wait—I haven't done anything yet!"* At dusk beside the van, Masaoka reflects that committing crime and catching criminals draw on the same crime-linked talent—driving up his own Crime Coefficient until he is treated as a hunting hound—and bluntly tells a dejected Akane that bringing Enforcers to scenes and watching so they do not trouble the public is sufficient for her job. Hurt, Akane asks if he is telling her not to interfere at all just as the heavy black `POLICE` door slides shut across Masaoka's profile.

That night in the high-rise PSB cafeteria, Kagari joins Akane over her curry udon, laughing bitterly that Enforcers are **"prisoners"** (*toraware no mi*) confined to the CID floor and dorms even off duty. Apologizing for his earlier teasing and asking to call her "Akane-chan," he asks why someone who passed PSB standards did not pick a better career. Clenching her fists on her lap, Akane confesses she earned **`A` ratings across all 13 Ministries and 6 public corporations**, yet out of more than `500` graduating students she alone earned an `A` for the Public Safety Bureau—leading her to hope PSB held work only she could do, where she might discover her "true life" and the meaning of being born. Kagari's easy humor collapses into bitter alienation. Comparing her dilemma to pre-Sibyl relics, he sits back—camera lingering on his chunky metallic wrist cuff—and recounts that he was rejected at his **age-five Psycho-Pass screening** with zero prospect of rehabilitation, leaving only lifelong isolation versus becoming a PSB "hunting hound" doing hitman work. As the camera cranes high overhead, Kagari rises, circles the table, slams his palm down with a loud crack, drops "Akane-chan" for a cold *"you"* (*anta*), and demands: *"Why the hell did you become an Inspector?!"*

Shaken, Akane visits Kōgami in the infirmary and bows deeply in apology. Kōgami notes dryly that an Inspector apologizing to an Enforcer is rare and declines to blame her for acting on her own judgment. When Akane asks whether she merely dragged the team down, Kōgami gazes at his upturned palm, slowly curls it into a fist, and confesses that years of obeying the Dominator soaked a hunting hound's reflexes into his hands until he swallowed neat rationales and forgot that a detective (*deka*) is supposed to protect people rather than hunt them down. He tells Akane she judged for herself and placed **justice (`seigi`) ahead of assigned role (`yakume`)**, adding that under such a superior he might be able to work as a detective rather than just a dog. Moved to tears, Akane bows and thanks him—prompting Kōgami's wry note that thanking an Enforcer is rare too. Yet when Akane wipes her eyes and hopefully asks whether Kōgami would have spared Chika had there been time to think calmly, his tenderness vanishes (*"I wonder"*). He admits he did not hesitate because he believed hesitation meant death and could not afford to die before settling his own score: violent red-amber flashes surge as he forces himself upright coughing, gasping that he has unfinished business—a personal **`yakume` (duty/score)** he must settle no matter what—leaving Akane staring in startled unease.

In the closing scene back at CID Division 1, Kōgami has returned to his desk. Standing composed before Ginoza, Akane defends her warehouse decision steadily: Chika's Crime Coefficient spike was clinically **transient** (*ikkasei*), and her medical chart (`56.3`, one-week average `58.7`, discharge feasible upon stabilization) confirms therapy is succeeding. Across the office, Kagari pauses mid-typing to gaze at Akane in surprised reassessment. Asked by Ginoza if he has anything to say, Kōgami answers cleanly without turning from his consoles: **"No. Inspector Tsunemori fulfilled her duty (`gimu`). That's all."** As the ending theme *"Namae no Nai Kaibutsu"* swells over the silent office, Akane bows formally to Ginoza, meets the watchful eyes of Kagari and Kōgami with steady composure, and returns to her post before the full ending credits and CID booking-chart height lineup roll without any post-credit scene.
