# Franchise chronology and claim reconciliation

This is the current canonical home for cross-installment chronology/disclosure reconciliation, philosophical/thematic claims, recurring audiovisual patterns and unresolved continuity disagreements. **Current reconciled records through `PP-S01NE-E01`: 4 chronology/edition records (`PP-CHR-0001`–`0004`), 4 franchise claims (`PP-FCLM-0001`–`0004`), 4 accepted reconciliation deltas (`PP-DELTA-0001`–`0004`).** Official release/edition metadata remains in the [source register](../../source-inventory/PP_SOURCE_REGISTER.md); inventory entries are not narrative records.

## Three separate temporal dimensions

A chronology record `PP-CHR-####` explicitly distinguishes these fields:

| Dimension | Required content and safeguards |
| --- | --- |
| Release | Exact edition/installment, verified first availability and medium/region, precision and metadata provenance. An unknown date stays unknown; a rebroadcast date cannot silently replace initial publication. |
| Event | Depicted or inferred in-universe event date/relative constraint, supporting observation IDs and locators, precision/confidence and conflicts. Episode order does not prove event order. |
| Disclosure | Proposition, audience edition or named character, earliest supported acquisition/belief boundary, source unit/locator and reliability. Analyst awareness and prior exposure remain separate. |

Use a partial order where the source gives only before/after constraints. Do not invent a total timeline, a calendar date or an interval duration to make a chart look complete. Store presentation order separately when it differs from event order. One occurrence can have multiple disclosure records and one release can reveal several different event times.

The current source model reserves New Edit and original broadcast as edition witnesses of the same continuity. Original broadcast coverage is zero. Cross-edition comparison requires independently admitted bytes and segment mapping; numbering alone creates no correspondence. Later revelations may change a current event hypothesis or institutional explanation without altering earlier prospective freezes. A genuine conflicting source claim remains a conflict until adjudicated; it is not automatically a different continuity.

## Reconciled proposition and motif records

A franchise proposition uses `PP-FCLM-####`. Record its exact assertion, kind and scope, current admitted/reconciled boundary, local claim/evidence lineage by installment/edition/unit, decisive locators, counterexamples, rival explanation, confidence, dependencies and accepted transition. Explain how contributions from different installments agree or differ. Do not erase scale, focalizer, exception or uncertainty to produce a short universal statement.

For a philosophical proposition, identify its attributed speaker and argumentative premises separately from the analyst's claim about narrative support. For a formal motif, record occurrences and absences/variants where inspected, actual audiovisual features, possible function and competing explanations. A shared color, composition or musical phrase does not establish shared meaning without contextual evidence. A formal recurrence can change function across time.

New findings and old-claim adjudications have separate identities even when later integrated together. The [state ledger](PP_FRANCHISE_STATE_LEDGER.md) owns current people/relationship/institution states; this ledger owns the cross-source proposition and revision reasoning. Link rather than independently rewrite the other owner's conclusion.

## Deltas, disagreements and authority

Each local proposal `PP-DELTA-####` identifies its base master version, proposed affected records, before/after assertion, source lineage, evidence gaps, required/optional status, prospective/retrospective effect and dependent artifacts. The [protocol](../../integration/PP_RECONCILIATION_PROTOCOL.md) owns adjudication. Record accepted, qualified, rejected or open integration outcomes and the corresponding claim transition. Preserve material dissent and the evidence that could resolve it.

Classify a disagreement as factual/locator, language, edition, chronology/disclosure, causal or interpretive before deciding its remedy. Correct factual coordinates and propagate their consequences. Compare interpretive alternatives against shared evidence. An unresolved optional interpretation can remain OPEN; an unresolved required fact or channel blocks the affected accepted conclusion or synthesis. Neither majority vote nor provider reputation determines truth.

Historical local readings and prediction freezes remain source-horizon artifacts. Current master claims may be revised through attributed lineage. Public summaries must route to the current accepted version and identify which parts remain open. Reconciliation updates targeted records, not the entire master corpus after every episode.
