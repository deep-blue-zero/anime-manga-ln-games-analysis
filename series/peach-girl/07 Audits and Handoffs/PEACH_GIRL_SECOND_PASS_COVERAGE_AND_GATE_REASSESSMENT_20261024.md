## What changed in responsibility, rather than just length

The original nine studies already had substantial literary arguments: four monographs, directional relationships, ensemble, and three specialist studies. Old Momo retained self-agency, real Tōji choice and choice before rescue; old Kairi retained mixed care, changing priority, actual withdrawal and failed forecasts; old Tōji distinguished sincere protection from exclusion and later restraint from patient care; old Sae retained genuine vulnerability without redemption and helpful truth without innocence. Directional and specialist studies already connected knowledge, agency and form. Bounded later errors do not justify portraying this prior corpus as uniformly inadequate.
