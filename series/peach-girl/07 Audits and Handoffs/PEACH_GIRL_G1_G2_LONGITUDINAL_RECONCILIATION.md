## Four histories and current retrieval

The four ledgers retain the full event histories and every historical full claim version. Allocation order is not story chronology. Current claim projection is the28-row index; terminal PG-FR-V18 lists the same28 versions. For other domains, the latest applicable subject/direction/proposition row is the current projection, qualified by explicit dated corrections. Historical compact “current” views earlier in each ledger are historical boundary views, not competing present authorities. This retrieval convention matters because a broad text search can return both an old mistaken proposition and its correction.

Character development is not a smooth improvement curve. Momo's growing initiative includes escaping Ryo, asking for disclosure, setting deadlines, rescuing others and choosing a partner; it also includes pressured pursuit after Kairi's refusal. Kairi's concrete care and sincere love coexist with coercive contact, avoidance and incomplete disclosure. Tōji's acknowledged faults and patient support coexist with the V18 physical restraint. Sae's gratitude, loss, costly shielding and useful reports coexist with violence and renewed impersonation. Misao's care does not automatically undo an explicit romantic refusal; Ryo's selective vulnerability does not settle accountability for his coercion.

Directional relationships cannot be read from couple labels alone. Momo–Kairi begins with unequal categories, becomes public romance in V09, ends by Kairi's explicit refusal in V12, reconnects under “second for now” terms, and changes again through missed disclosure and the real Tōji reunion. V18 begins with an actual Momo→Tōji yes and Momo→Kairi no. The final Momo→Kairi declaration143 occurs before the rescue and is reciprocated173–175. Earlier phases remain actual. At final exit Momo/Kairi form the romantic pair; Sae remains nearby as a social tie. Tōji's grief and Momo's gratitude remain; no marriage or future stability is depicted.

Commitment rows distinguish proposal, reply, imposed action and outcome. A hotel reservation is not consent. An offered casual relation may be refused. Accepting comfort is not accepting romance; accepting romance is not permission for every encounter. A stop can be heeded in one episode and violated in another. The V18 first stop115, pin125, intimacy130–132, second stop133 and release144 require separate retrieval. “He stopped” and “he restrained her” are both true, at different points.

Information/reputation history tracks who actually receives content. Kairi's V15 first-choice declaration reaches Misao53–54, not Momo. Kairi's true V16 doorstep motive reaches Misao in V17_54–57, while Momo receives his false Misao explanation32/77. Present love82 and comparison92 then actually reach Momo. V18 Sae transmits the Shiranami effort47–50; Misao transmits injury/hospital61 and first-choice63. The police cause still does not reach Momo. A reader's knowledge that an explanation exists cannot be installed in her state.

Reputation and biography remain distinct. Sexualized slurs, magazine imagery, a commercial contract claim and rival assertions are not exhaustive sexual histories. Sae's public pregnancy claim and later acknowledgment of nonpregnancy are recorded separately; the latter does not establish that she knowingly invented the original belief. The V17 audience Momo/Kairi/Tōji receives the correction; Misao's receipt and a schoolwide correction are unshown.
