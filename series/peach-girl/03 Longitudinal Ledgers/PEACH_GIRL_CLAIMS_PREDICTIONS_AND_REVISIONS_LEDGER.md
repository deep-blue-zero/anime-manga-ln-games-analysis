# Claims, Predictions and Revisions

This instrument owns material interpretive propositions and their versioned evidence, alternatives, forecast terms and revision/dependency history. Observations live in readings; character, relationship and information states live in their domain instruments. A claim points to these homes rather than copying their whole evidence.

It is the canonical route for why a consequential interpretation changed. An unresolved question can remain open without an invented answer. No predictions, character conclusions or narrative claims are created at bootstrap.

## Records and allocation

| Type / stable identifier | Required fields |
| --- | --- |
| Claim `PG-CL-####@version` | Scoped formulation; evidence/interpretation class; source/temporal/disclosure boundary; supporting observations; strongest available counterevidence or bounded none-found statement; competing account; support strength and uncertainty basis; discriminating evidence; affected domain records/artifacts; first freeze; current disposition |
| Prospective forecast `PG-PR-####` | Exact statement before outcome access; originating freeze; admissible future test and resolution condition; alternatives; rationale/source observations; target boundary if meaningful; status; adjudication evidence/date/frontier |
| Revision `PG-RV-####` | Claim + prior/new versions; transition; old/new formulations; reason; new source frontier; supporting/counterevidence; prior revision; downstream obligations and disposition |
| Current claim index | Claim ID, active version, scope/cutoff, latest revision and unresolved debt |

Claim labels are the method's `ESTABLISHED_SOURCE_FACT / STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION / PLAUSIBLE_COMPETING_INTERPRETATION / SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS / UNRESOLVED_QUESTION`. These are distinct from support strength `HIGH / MODERATE / LOW / UNRESOLVED`, and from revision transitions `PRESERVE / STRENGTHEN / REVISE / DOWNGRADE / REJECT / OPEN`. A clearly established utterance can support only a weak motive inference.

Forecast statuses: `OPEN / SUPPORTED_FOR_TESTED_SCOPE / DISCONFIRMED / MIXED / NOT_TESTED / UNRESOLVABLE`. Specify an actual test; absence of contradiction is not success. There is no forecast quota. Keep alternative outcomes and unresolved forecasts rather than reconstructing impressive predictions after seeing the ending.

## Evidence, update and history

Allocate IDs monotonically only when warranted by admitted source observations or an actual interpretation/forecast. Every claim/forecast has stable `PG-EV-VNN-####` source routes, and domain refs where material. A local observation with no consequential interpretation does not need a claims row merely to fill the ledger. Source inventory/provenance facts remain in the source map, not this narrative-claims instrument.

At every future VNN closeout, assess inherited material claims and due forecasts, append actual new claims or revisions, and identify all dependent homes requiring amendment. A changed central interpretation is not closed until dependent current-state projections and affected specialist/model responsibilities have dispositions. Record no-material-change cases in the reading's transaction receipt without duplicating unchanged claims.

Versions and original forecast terms are immutable. A later revelation produces a new claim version/revision with its actual learned-after frontier. Errata distinguish a mistaken earlier observation from legitimate later recontextualization and preserve a recoverable before-image. Supersession of an entire artifact uses the repository authority graph; rejection of a claim does not erase its ID.

Each unit freeze retains active claim versions and open forecast terms. A fresh execution reconstructs the entering state from that snapshot and the pinned historical rows, not from the latest current index. Uncertainty records the unresolved premise, missing evidence or competing explanation rather than a confidence adjective alone.

Bootstrap source-derived claims, forecasts, revisions and current-index rows: **0**.
