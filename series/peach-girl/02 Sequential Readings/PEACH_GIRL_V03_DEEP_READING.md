# Volume 3 — the lion and the deer

Volume 3 completes the first great cycle of the series. It is the volume in which the machinery of Sae's deception is exposed: first to Tōji, by overhearing; then to the class, by public testimony. The exposure arrives in Sae's own words. Asked how she makes people believe her, she explains that if you see a lion beside a wounded deer you assume the lion did it, so 「要は最初に泣いたもん勝ち」 ("the point is, whoever cries first wins", i0109–i0110). The volume tests that theory against Momo's appearance and finds it accurate until it is turned back on its author. Two further truths emerge. The report that had shaped Momo's body for two years, that Tōji disliked dark-skinned girls, was a middle-school friend's lie (i0045–i0046). And Momo's feelings for Kairi have begun, by her own admission, to include something she cannot name (i0071, i0100, i0105). The volume ends with Tōji publicly restored to Momo and asking to start over, and with Momo refusing: 「とーじとはつきあえない」 ("I can't go out with you", i0174). She wants to, by her own narration (「やり直したい」), and she does not say why she refuses.

**The breakup is sealed.** Momo throws fruit at Tōji and turns his proposal around: 「こっちから別れてやるわよっっ!!!」 ("I'll be the one to dump you!", i0008–i0009). He topples (i0011–i0012). Outside, Kairi confesses that he let the kiss slip in front of Sae. Momo says she wants Tōji to be jerked around and discarded by Sae (i0013–i0016). Meanwhile Sae tells the class that Tōji's wound reopened because of Momo (i0022), a claim the hospital scene at i0035 undercuts.

**Harassment and the swim meet.** Momo finds thumbtacks in her shoes (i0019). She is tripped (i0024), drenched with a bucket, has her uniform soaked (i0029–i0031), and is nominated by Sae, as a former swimmer, for nearly every event in the class swim meet (i0025–i0027). Alone, she wants 「さえのいない世界」 ("a world without Sae", i0031). Kairi keeps his distance in public so as not to start new rumors, and Momo sees through it (i0042–i0043). She agrees, tentatively, to a summer sea trip with him (i0043).

**The tan was based on a lie.** Kyō-chin, the middle-school friend, meets Momo by chance and confesses that she invented Tōji's preference, because she liked Tōji herself. His real type was 「活発で明るいコ」 ("an active, bright girl", i0044–i0047). Momo laughs, then breaks down sobbing on Kairi, regretting her own 「別れてやる」 (i0048–i0055). She leaves flowers at Tōji's hospital door without entering: 「とーじが好き いまでも気持ちは変わらない／でももうやり直せない」 ("I love Tōji, my feelings haven't changed, but we can't start over", i0056–i0058).

**The swim meet.** Provoked by Sae's sunscreen taunt, Momo decides to swim all out (i0060). Sae whispers to three girls (i0061). She refuses, he embraces her anyway, tells her he loves the smile she has only for Tōji, embraces her again and leaves. Momo is left with her heart pounding: 「なにあんなヤツにときめいてんだあたしは」 ("why is my heart fluttering for a guy like him?", i0086–i0100). She wins three races in a row (i0116–i0119), and Kako publicly cheers for her (i0118).

**Exposure.** Sae threatens to accuse Kairi of assault if he exposes her (i0108). Kairi then flatters her into explaining her method, with Tōji listening unseen (i0109–i0114). When Sae calls Kairi a playboy, Momo slaps Sae in front of the class and defends him: 「岡安にもいいとこいっぱいあるんだから!!」 ("Okayasu has lots of good points!", i0124–i0130). ## 5. Close reading

### 5.1 The lion and the deer: the volume's theory of belief

Sae's lion-and-deer explanation (i0109–i0110) is the most explicit theoretical statement the series has made so far. It is placed in Sae's mouth, illustrated with Momo as the lion and Sae as the fawn, and overheard by the person most deceived by it. The theory is precise. Observers assign blame by visible vulnerability, not by evidence, and so whoever presents as the victim first wins. Its premise is the volume-one thesis seen from the other side. Momo's appearance (「キツそう」, V01 i0008) makes her the lion to any onlooker, and Sae's pale, small, 華奢 build (V01 i0012) makes her the deer. 「安達ならやりそうだって口をそろえていってた」 ("they all said Adachi seemed the type") ties the method directly to PG-CL-0001.

The volume dramatizes the theory and then its failure:

- **Confirmation.** The class's 土下座 demand (i0131–i0141) is the theory in action. Momo slapped Sae, which is true, and the class punishes her as the aggressor. That she was provoked by Sae's attack on Kairi is invisible to them.
- **Inversion.** Tōji turns the metaphor on its author in public (i0154). After that the class revisits its evidence: 「とーじから安達の悪口って聞いたことないよな／いつもさえちゃんから聞くばっかで」 (i0159). For the first time the source of an account is examined rather than its content.
- **Counter-testimony.** The witnesses Kairi gathers (i0162–i0164) supply what Momo alone could not: independent people with the same complaint. The admirer's testimony works because Sae, provoked, speaks in her real voice in front of everyone (i0164).

The resolution is therefore not a vindication of Momo by authority. It comes from an accumulation of independent accounts and from Sae's own self-exposure. The volume's implicit epistemology is that testimony from a single interested party, whether Momo or Sae, is weak, and that convergence and source scrutiny are what work.

### 5.2 Momo: the end of the tan, and the start of something else

Two long-running elements of Momo's self-construction end in V03.

**The sunscreen regime** rested on a lie (i0045–i0046). The source makes the revelation deliberately anticlimactic. Kyō-chin delivers it in passing, happily attached elsewhere, and Momo's first reaction is laughter (i0048–i0049) before grief arrives (i0052). The flashback at i0039–i0040 reveals the crush's origin: a swimmer returning a baseball. It frames her body, the tanned, broad-shouldered swimmer, as the very body Tōji first smiled at. Her decision to swim at the meet (i0060) is presented as defiance of Sae, but it also lets her recover a pleasure she had suppressed (「泳ぐことが楽しくてしかたなかった」, i0062). The book does not say she gives up her complexion concerns for good; the decision is local to the meet.

**Her account of Kairi changes.** V02 i0141 recorded her claim that she had never felt her heart race for Kairi. V03 complicates it three times:
1. Irritation she cannot explain when he flirts with other girls (i0068–i0071).
2. 「ときめいてんだ」 after his changing-room embrace (i0100).
3. A reflective admission of reliance and of wanting his attention (i0105–i0106), which she frames as being 「流されやすい」 (easily swayed).

Her public defense of Kairi (i0130), at the cost of a slap that turns the class against her, is the most consequential choice of the volume in this respect. She defends Kairi against Sae's description of him as 「軽そー」 and 「顔目当て」. She does not defend Tōji or herself. Her mature reading of him (「スケベでバカばっかやってるけど…いいとこいっぱいある」) is the volume's most balanced character judgment.

Momo's narration names this feeling attraction-like and then rejects it as a lapse. The analysis does not resolve whether it is romantic. Comfort, gratitude and arousal are all supported. What is established is that her V02 self-report is no longer complete (PG-RV for PG-CL-0011 is not needed; see the new PG-CL-0013).

**The final refusal (i0174).** Her stated feelings are unambiguous: she wants to go back (「やり直したい」). Her refusal is unexplained at this frontier. The evidence offers three candidate explanations, none decisive:
- **Trust injury.** At i0057 she says Tōji 「一度もあたしのこと信じようとしてくれなかった」 and that 「もうやり直せない」. This is her own most recent stated reason, and it predates his apology.
- **Unsettled feelings about Kairi** (i0100–i0106). She has just named a feeling she does not understand.
- **Unwillingness to resume a relationship that collapsed under rumor.** This is weakly supported.

The trust-injury reading has the strongest direct textual support. The Kairi reading is plausible and recorded as a competitor.

### 5.3 Tōji: from belief to responsibility

V03 settles the PG-CL-0005 question about the source of his credulity. Kairi's explanation, 「免疫なさすぎ…家族や友だちに恵まれてた」 (i0127), and Tōji's own words, 「親友だと思ってた子が心配してしてくれた忠告を なんでわざわざ疑ったりする」 (i0154), both frame it as a reasonable inference from a trusting background, not as stupidity or callousness. His shame (「合わす顔ない」, i0128) and his public apology (i0155–i0156) are framed as taking responsibility. Notably he says he should have stood by her 「何があっても」 ("no matter what"), which accepts Momo's i0057 complaint directly, although he did not hear it.

His public claim 「安達はオレの彼女だぞ」 (i0150) is an action to protect her, and he immediately retracts it in private as presumptuous (i0170). That is a sign of respect for the breakup she declared. It is also the volume's clearest demonstration of his decency, and it answers the V01 secrecy (CM-0002) by reversing it publicly.

### 5.4 Sae: method, coalition and collapse

The volume converts Sae from a successful deceiver into a socially isolated one. Several facts are newly established:

- **Instruction of physical sabotage** (i0067): 「肩ヒモを切れっていったのよ」. The underwater holding goes beyond the instruction as she stated it. Whether she foresaw it is not shown.
- **Threat of a false sexual-assault accusation** against Kairi (i0108). This is the most serious deception tool she has shown, and it is a threat only.
- **Method self-description** (i0109–i0110) and a stated plan to 「モノにしちゃう」 Tōji over the summer (i0112).
- **Past pattern**: multiple girls' testimony (i0163). This is independent of Momo and is the first evidence about Sae's life before the series.
- **Coalition mechanics**: the girls joined her because she presented as Momo's victim (i0068), and they abandoned her once that frame broke (i0165).

Sae's last line, 「あたしはわるくないのよ———っ!!!」 (i0166), sustains a victim persona even in isolation. The marginal labels 保身・自己愛・責任転嫁 (self-preservation, self-love, shifting blame; i0160) are the narrative layer's explicit verdict. The analysis records them as framing, distinct from the observed conduct that supports them.

Forecast PG-PR-0005 asked whether Sae would pursue Tōji more openly after the breakup. It is supported. She stated a plan to win him over the summer (i0112) and publicly claimed that he had led her on and said he loved her (i0160).

### 5.5 Kairi: strategist, rival, friend

Kairi's role in V03 is decisive and contradictory:

- **Engineer of the exposure.** He lets Tōji see Momo's humiliation (i0080–i0081), extracts Sae's method while Tōji listens (i0109–i0114), keeps the letter of his promise not to 喋る, provokes Tōji into action by tying him up and taunting him (i0135–i0137), and gathers witnesses (i0162). His own words state the aim: 「とーじの本気ってヤツ見せてもらいましょ」.
- **Rival who promotes the other man.** He tells Momo he loves the smile she has only for Tōji (i0095–i0096), pushes her to talk to Tōji (i0020), and sends the couple off (i0169).
- **Still physically intrusive.** He enters the girls' changing room, makes sexualized remarks, and embraces Momo after an explicit refusal (i0086–i0099). In this scene the comic framing and the sincere framing coexist. Momo's later reflection that she did not resist while held does not erase her prior refusal.
- **Public womanizer persona.** He arranges summer dates with several girls (i0068–i0069). It is unclear whether this is genuine, a performance, or a way to keep distance from Momo.

PG-CL-0006 is strengthened again. He takes concrete actions that lower his own chances (reuniting Momo and Tōji) and gives reasons for them in terms of her happiness. His interior is still shown only through what he says.

### 5.6 Form

- **Sae's chibi cat** marks her scheming and triumph throughout (i0011, i0121, i0152, i0165), and her crumbling to dust at i0167 ends the arc in farce. The register lets the volume conclude a harrowing harassment plot with comic justice.
- **Visualized metaphor.** The lion and deer are drawn literally (i0109), so the reader holds Sae's theory as an image that Tōji will later quote.
- **Color page continuity** (i0054) presents Kairi holding the crying Momo as a full color image. It gives that comfort scene the visual weight usually reserved for romantic climaxes, a formal signal that is noted but not interpreted as a forecast.
- **The final page** places Tōji's 「やり直さないか?」 and Momo's refusal against a recalled image of their café date (i0173), so the reader sees the past she says she wants beside the refusal she gives.

## 6. Counter-readings

- **Momo refuses Tōji because she has fallen for Kairi.** Possible but under-supported. Her strongest explicit reason (i0057) concerns trust, and she frames her Kairi feeling as a lapse. Retained at low-to-moderate support.
- **Kairi's changing-room embrace was pure manipulation.** His speech supports Tōji and her smile with Tōji, against his own interest. A sincere reading is better supported. The intrusion and the disregard of her refusal remain recorded either way.
- **The class's turn is too quick to be credible.** It is mediated by several independent testimonies and by Sae's self-exposure, not by Tōji's word alone.
- **Sae did not intend the drowning risk.** Supported by her own words (she asked only for the strap to be cut). Holding Momo under was the girls' conduct.
