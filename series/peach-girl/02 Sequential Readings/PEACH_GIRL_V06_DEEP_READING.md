# Volume 6 — rescue, mishearing and the revenge mirror

Tōji reaches Momo and rejects the belief that her worth or his love depends on what may have happened while she could not choose. The rescue nevertheless produces a second crisis: she hears his shame as rejection, before he explains that he blames himself. Restored communication and care strengthen their relationship. They then learn directly that Sae drugged Momo. Anger leads the couple and Kairi to a revenge scheme that imitates the original means: staged relationship evidence, a tampered drink, incapacitation and delivery to a supposed actor. The final call reveals their control was illusory. Rescue and restored intimacy coexist with newly chosen harmful conduct; neither strand cancels the other.

## 4. Close reading

### 4.1 Claims become proof before they become knowledge

The opening keeps Momo in an epistemic trap (EV0002,0006–0008). Jigoro supplies a confident account of her desire, while the missing interval deprives her of an immediate answer. She first tries to disprove him through bodily indications drawn from peers' anecdotes, then remembers variation and loses that certainty. The analysis records her reasoning as part of distress; it cannot turn virginity lore into reliable forensic evidence. The opened wrapper raises her fear because it is offered as proof, but a displayed object does not prove its asserted history. Jigoro's initial certainty and later hesitation likewise establish neither completion nor nonoccurrence of the unshown act.

Sae adds another means of making a scene appear evidentiary. Her acrobatic window photography (EV0010) captures a situation she helped manufacture. The exact frames remain unshown; the important established act is acquiring a selective visual record while the occupant is distressed. This extends V05's impersonated body and false illustrated account: an apparently direct image can conceal the decisions that produced what it shows. Actual later use of these photographs remains open. They cannot be treated as an already circulated school rumor.

Jigoro's first doubt comes from Momo's crying not matching Sae's account (EV0011), before the later admission. He is capable of noticing dissonance, rather than possessing either complete knowledge or no agency throughout. At EV0034 he explains conditional recruitment; at EV0035 he learns the fan story and drink were deliberately false. His retrospective memory of fragile Sae at EV0037 explains an attachment and its disillusionment, not the analyst's proof that she was formerly innocent or has undergone a clinically legible personality change. Her contempt for his bruised face collides with his wish to protect. Even this disclosure does not provide an independent account of the missing hotel interval (CL0018@3).

### 4.2 Reaching someone is not the end of rescuing them

Tōji's hotel recheck is a concrete continuation of verification (EV0003): he finds the location in the part Sae claimed to search. She responds with physical obstruction, using the copied perfume as a weapon against sight (EV0004). The same sensory object has successively functioned as gift cue, identity cue and obstacle to recognition. Chance brings Misao and Kairi into the search. Their adult access and driving limitations make rescue collaborative and contingent; Tōji cannot do it through passion alone.

Arrival supports PR0010, but his repeated blows and Momo's wish not to be seen complicate a simple savior tableau (EV0016–0019). He is not shown newly accepting Sae's betrayal story. His rage at Jigoro arises beside her shame, and Misao's physical intervention limits an escalating retaliation. Kairi's initial permission to hit is itself a choice; his attention to Momo does not cleanse that choice. Sae's switch from cheering violence to protecting the model's face reveals practical values and performed allegiance. Misao treats the injured man without thereby exonerating him. Care here includes stopping a rescuer and treating someone who has caused harm.

Misao offers Momo options rather than an established result (EV0020,0029). Her police/evidence comments and explanation of assault distress remain attributed source positions. Momo's refusal to disclose is a choice under fear and missing knowledge, not evidence that the situation was consensual. The earlier invented parent call gains hotel access; the later actual notification gains family reassurance. Keeping those events distinct prevents treating effective institutional help as uniformly truthful. Kairi also gains private knowledge by overhearing Momo (EV0031): useful advocacy includes a privacy cost.

### 4.3 An incomplete sentence recreates an earlier relationship fear

EV0023 divides one speech event into what Momo hears and what she misses. Tōji says he cannot face her; she leaves before he explains shame at failing to accompany/find her. The reader has both fragments. Her conclusion is false about his present feeling but intelligible through the previous breakup over Kairi's kiss and the sexual stigma she applies to herself. This is not renewed irrationality without history, and it is not evidence that he actually rejects her. It is a failure of shared access to a statement whose eventual meaning differs from its first alarming fragment (CL0020@1).

The railway scene gives that misunderstanding immediate bodily stakes (EV0025–0026). Momo discovers herself in danger, then voices a wish to avoid hearing loss; the source does not establish a prior premeditated attempt. Tōji denies impurity-based rejection, reaches toward her and acts to bring both clear. His declaration that he would die too combines desperate solidarity with pressure. The analysis does not convert it into sound advice or a universal romantic ideal. The passing train withholds the outcome from watching Kairi/Misao and the reader before the safe aftermath. A small affectionate gesture near her tears then replaces the scale of physical danger. The untailed love caption is not assigned a fabricated speaker.

The next morning makes the causal correction explicit (EV0028): he does not accept Momo merely to compensate for his own deceived encounter, and she has done nothing wrong. She recognizes his mistaken identity rather than calling it informed choice of Sae. These are asymmetrical violations, not an exchange of equal voluntary betrayals. He later distinguishes Jigoro from Kairi's emotional proximity (EV0030), preserving a specific jealousy boundary without making Momo guilty. The rival's joking provocation continues. Local trust has changed; jealousy and sexualized boundary play have not disappeared (CL0005@6,0015@4,0017@3).

### 4.4 The admission changes blame and action at a particular time

Kairi's reconstruction at i0102 makes a plan intelligible, but plausibility is not proof of every proposed aim or outcome. Momo's reluctance to think Sae capable of crime persists despite prior betrayal; that residual charity explains why the later direct hearing matters. The self-incriminating admission at EV0035 confirms Sae's intentional drink interference as her own account, supported by the already depicted collapse. The exact drug is unspecified; no dose, diagnosis or chemical certainty is inferred. Jigoro hears the truth and the trio overhears it now. They did not possess this evidence during V05, the hotel rescue or the railway misunderstanding.

Sae's envy statement supplies unusually explicit motive evidence (EV0036). It strengthens the comparison account without erasing her specific excitement at winning Tōji later. Wanting him and wanting Momo deprived can work together. Her assertion that a handsome model compensates for harm reveals contempt for Momo's choice, not an authoritative assessment of what happened. Momo's response moves from grief into expressed anger and comic physical force. The wall strike, demand for release and attempt at direct confrontation precede Kairi's revenge proposal (EV0038–0039). A reading that only restores a helpless victim would miss this initiative; a reading that only celebrates regained power would miss what she subsequently authorizes.

### 4.5 Revenge borrows the original crime's grammar

The school breakup scene initially withholds its script (EV0041). Earlier revenge discussion creates suspicion, but explicit confirmation arrives at EV0044. Reading chronologically preserves the interim tension rather than presenting every hurtful word as immediately transparent acting. Sae supplies a second audience: she watches, privately celebrates, then approaches Tōji. His café confession targets a real desire. Her excitement and triumphant bathroom soliloquy remain genuine depicted responses even though his words are false. It would compress her characterization to make the whole response mere impersonal status accounting.

Tōji's addition to the cup, Sae drinking and later collapse (EV0043) invert the preceding trap. Her willingness to meet privately does not authorize being drugged or moved while unconscious. At EV0044 Momo explicitly considers retaliation normally the same level as Sae, then chooses it because she cannot forgive. Tōji carries out the incapacitation; Kairi designs and conducts the delivery. Their responsibilities are distinct, not absorbed into a single clever strategist. Kairi's claim that equivalent suffering will teach Sae is an intention/theory, not a demonstrated result. The criterion used in evaluating these means is informed voluntary agency and bodily safety: neither the earlier wrong nor an anticipated lesson supplies the missing permission (CL0021@1).

The couple assumes the intended actor makes the threat safely fictitious (EV0045). Kairi's claim that being gay guarantees non-assault belongs to his reassurance, not the analysis's view of sexuality. His objectifying delivery language and the unseen receiver's terms should not be softened into harmless theater, even before the final call. The opening restraint/danger at EV0049 is a real depicted loss of control for Sae; the act threatened and the eventual result remain unshown. At EV0050 the supposed actor is still waiting. At minimum, delivery and recipient diverged from their plan. No safe prank, completed sexual act, moral lesson, rescue or repentance can be asserted at the V06 boundary.
