# Volume 4 — the victim complex

Volume 4 opens on the morning after Momo's refusal and closes the first arc's emotional account. The reconciliation does not come from Tōji persuading Momo or from new evidence about Sae. It comes from Momo recognizing her own habit of mind. Kairi tells her that the one who does not trust is her (「信じてないのはももちゃんのほうじゃん」, i0031), and that she will keep blaming others (i0032). In a full-page monologue that repeats the opening captions of volume 1 word for word, she names the pattern 被害者意識 (victim complex): her appearance really is misjudged, but that misjudgment had also become her reason for never risking rejection (i0034–i0035). She then asks Tōji to go out with her again (i0048). The rest of the volume follows the couple's first ordinary months: summer, swim club, an awkward first-kiss problem and a private hand sign. Momo's late discovery of how much Kairi did for her is the volume's emotional peak (i0068–i0083). In the last chapter Sae, now ostracized and outwardly servile to Momo, finds a new audience in a fashion magazine's studio. She tells the No. 1 model, 大路吾郎 (Ōji Gorō), called ジゴロー (Jigoro), the same kind of story she told Tōji, and the volume ends with Jigoro asking Sae out in front of the whole school (i0170).

## 2. Prospective entering state (PG-FR-V03)

Sae had been exposed to Class 3 and abandoned by her coalition. Tōji had publicly claimed Momo as his girlfriend, apologized, retracted the claim in private as presumptuous, and asked to start over. Momo refused while wanting to (「やり直したい…だけど」) and gave no reason. She had self-reported fluttering and reliance toward Kairi, which she disavowed as a lapse, and had tentatively agreed to a summer sea trip with him.

Open forecasts: PG-PR-0001 (pool-note authorship), PG-PR-0006 (Momo will give Tōji an explicit reason, concerning trust or uncertainty, not love for Kairi), PG-PR-0007 (the sea trip with Kairi will occur or be discussed in V04).

## 3. Orientation

**The reason, and the confrontation.** At the class party in the cafeteria, the classmates apologize to Momo, she forgives them, and they tease her and Tōji as a couple, not knowing that she has refused him. She walks out (i0008–i0017). In the infirmary, Kairi tells the nurse Misao 「失恋しちゃった」 ("I got my heart broken"), and Misao answers that this time he seemed fairly serious (i0018–i0019). He finds Momo alone at the bus stop, and she tells him she refused Tōji: 「やり直す自信がないの」 ("I don't have the confidence to start over"), because Tōji believed Sae easily, doubted her at every turn, and was the one who proposed the breakup (i0022–i0023). Then: if something happens again and he does not believe her, 「あたし今度こそ立ち直れない」 ("this time I won't recover", i0029). Kairi first reveals that the line that won Momo's respect in volume 1 was originally Tōji's (i0026–i0027), and explains Tōji's credulity as being lost in Sae's maze (i0028). Then he loses patience: 「信じてないのはももちゃんのほうじゃん」 ("you're the one who doesn't trust"), she can go on blaming other people, 「つきあっとれんわ」 ("I can't be bothered with you"), and he leaves (i0030–i0032).

**Self-recognition and reconciliation.** Next morning Momo concedes in narration that he was right (i0033). A full-page monologue names her 被害者意識 (victim complex), listing her black skin, sharp eyes and chlorine-damaged red hair and the labels 「キツそう」「ナマイキ」「遊んでる」 ("looks harsh", "cheeky", "fast"), in the same words as the V01 opening. She then names the excuses that followed from them: "confessing won't work because my skin is dark"; "we can't get back together because Tōji is stubborn" (i0034). 「ホントは拒絶されるのが怖くて…傷つくのがイヤで逃げてたくせに」 ("really I was scared of rejection… I ran away because I didn't want to get hurt", i0035). At the end-of-term ceremony Tōji appears with a shaved head as 「けじめと戒め」 ("a reckoning and a reminder to myself"), insisting it is not meant to win her back, and apologizes again: he should not have swallowed Sae's words without seeing or hearing for himself (i0036–i0041). Momo runs after him, hugs him from behind, apologizes for having thought only of herself as the victim, lists his faults, says she still loves him, and asks him to go out with her again (i0043–i0048).

**Summer and the new term.** The summer is summarized: the reunion was 「1か月と10日前」 (one month and ten days ago, i0052). Tōji spent most of the holiday in remedial classes. They met in the library, at movies and the arcade, and once at the fireworks. There was no trip and, by Momo's account, no physical intimacy (i0051–i0055). There is no mention of Kairi or of a sea trip. Second-year girls press Momo into joining the swim club, which needs members, and Tōji encourages her: 「オレ安達がどんなに焼けても全然オッケーだから」 ("I'm fine with it however tanned you get", i0058–i0063, i0094).

**Kairi.** Kairi is avoiding her (i0064–i0066). From Sumire (すみれ), a girl from class 1-1 whom Sae hurt in middle school, Momo learns that Kairi phoned her the night before the swim meet and recruited her for 「リベンジ」 ("revenge"): 「彼が助けたかったのはこのコなんだって」 ("I realized it was this girl he wanted to save", i0067–i0068). Momo reinterprets his changing-room embrace as 「最後の抱擁」 ("the last embrace", i0071), runs to him and apologizes. He turns it into a joke about wanting to take advantage while Tōji was away (i0076). She thanks him, starts to say 「もしとーじより先に出会ってたら あたし岡安のこと——」 ("if I had met you before Tōji, I would have…"), and he cuts her off with comic panic and a standing offer to take her if she gets bored of Tōji (i0077–i0081). Alone, she thanks him for loving her and cries (i0082–i0083).

**The kiss problem and the sign.** Tōji leans in to kiss her in an empty classroom, and she pushes him away (i0087–i0089). The swim-club girls call it the second time (「一度ならず二度までも」, i0092). Momo admits in narration that she has wanted to kiss him all along, and that it is odd for each of them to have kissed someone else (Momo with Kairi, Tōji with Sae) but not each other (i0097–i0098). Kairi, posing as the nurse, guesses at Tōji's trouble and provokes his jealousy (i0101–i0103). Momo proposes a private sign, an L made with the fingers to mean 「好き」 (love), and fights with Tōji when he will not do it: 「つきあってから一度も好きって言われたことないもん!」 ("you haven't said you love me once since we started going out", i0104–i0108). Called to the blackboard, he turns to the class and makes the sign with both hands; the class laughs, and only Momo understands (i0121–i0124). He kisses her forehead behind Sae's back (i0128).

**Sae after exposure.** Sae is ignored and bullied: trampled in a fire drill after Momo leaves her behind (i0056–i0057), her baking knocked to the floor by girls she used to mock (i0099), a ball thrown at her head in gym (i0109–i0111). Momo remembers that Sae was the first to approach her at high school (i0112) and partners with her (i0113–i0114). Sae becomes ostentatiously servile, calling her 「ももさま」 (i0116). In private, after giving Momo and Tōji her movie ticket, Sae fantasizes about being popular with Momo groveling, and stamps furiously on Momo's indoor shoes (i0142–i0143).

**The studio and Jigoro.** A magazine editor, Honda (本田), photographs Momo's loafers, with Sae clinging to her (i0130–i0134). Sae is chosen as a candidate for the magazine's reader-voted "girlfriend" feature and brings Momo to the studio (i0146–i0148). There, Sae collides with Jigoro, who is struck by how light she is (i0151–i0153). She gives the staff cookies that Momo bought as homemade (i0155). When Momo, bored and needing to cook dinner, tries to leave, Sae begs her to stay, and Jigoro sees Momo as a bully (i0156). He asks Sae why she bows to 「あんな女」 ("a woman like that"). Sae tells him that Momo dominates her at school, insisted on coming and is moody, and Jigoro concludes that Momo is jealous of Sae's looks (i0157–i0159). At school Sae inflates the encounter into love at first sight and is mocked (i0160–i0163). Jigoro then drives to the school, stops girls who are forcing Sae to spray-paint 「あたしは大嘘つきの大バカ者です」 ("I am a huge liar and a big idiot") on a wall, carries her off, and asks her to go out with him in front of the whole school (i0164–i0171).

## 5. Close reading

### 5.1 The victim complex: the series turns its thesis inward

Volumes 1 to 3 built PG-CL-0001: people read Momo by her tan, eyes and hair, and those readings are wrong and costly. Volume 4 does not withdraw that claim. A drunk salaryman propositions her for compensated dating (援助) within pages (i0024), and in the last chapter Jigoro reads her posture as bullying (i0156). What the volume adds is a second layer, which Momo states herself. The full-page monologue at i0034 repeats the V01 opening captions (「キツそう」「ナマイキ」「遊んでる」／いつも見た目でソンをする) and then continues where V01 stopped: 「だから」 ("so"). Because she was misjudged, she concluded that confessing could not work and that talking could not repair the relationship. The misjudgment was real, but it had also become a reason not to risk rejection: 「ホントは拒絶されるのが怖くて…傷つくのがイヤで逃げてた」 (i0035).

This is the first time the series has turned its thesis on its narrator. It recasts the V03 refusal. The reason Momo gave Kairi, fear of not being believed again (i0029), is real, but she now judges it as avoidance. The sequence is precise:
1. Kairi's argument is an analogy (staying indoors for fear of an accident) and a reversal (「信じてないのはももちゃんのほうじゃん」, i0031).
2. Momo's narration concedes, angrily (「悔しいけどあんたの言うとおりよ」, i0033).
3. Her apology to Tōji uses the term exactly: 「自分ばっか被害者のつもりで」 ("thinking only of myself as the victim", i0045).

The source does not say that the victim complex was unjustified. It says it had become self-defeating. PG-CL-0001 is revised to include this internalization (PG-RV-0015) and a new claim records the self-recognition (PG-CL-0015).

### 5.2 Reconciliation as mutual accountability

The reconciliation is arranged as two parallel admissions:
- **Tōji's** is physical and verbal. The shaved head, 「けじめと戒め」, is explicitly not a bid to win her back (i0039–i0040). His account of his error is the one Kairi gave him: he took words at face value, he should not have believed Sae's account of things he had not seen or heard himself (i0041). This is the most precise statement of his epistemic fault so far, and it is his own.
- **Momo's** is the victim-complex admission and a list of his faults, followed by love that includes them: 「そーゆー所も引っくるめて」 ("including all of that", i0046). She loves him specifically for the remorse (「後悔してあたしのために悩んでくれるとーじが…大好き」, i0047).

Momo, not Tōji, makes the final request (i0048). In V01 Tōji confessed first, after Momo had delayed for two years. Here she asks first. The summer summary (i0051–i0055) then shows a slow, chaste relationship. Her narration frames this as deliberate: 「あせる必要ないんだから／とーじとは遠回りした分 今度こそ大切にしていきたい」 ("there's no need to rush; because we took the long way round, this time I want to take care of it", i0056).

### 5.3 Kairi: the hidden work, and the farewell that is not called one

V03 showed Kairi bringing in witnesses. V04 shows what lay behind it: phone calls the night before the meet to girls he had never spoken to, offering them 「リベンジ」 (i0068). Sumire's account is single-witness testimony, but Kairi confirms the act (「バレちゃった——?」, i0076). His stated motive is opportunism: gain Momo's gratitude and move in while Tōji was in hospital. Momo reads the joke as 「気を遣わせないための思いやり」 ("kindness, so I wouldn't feel I owed him", i0076). The source supports her reading more than his. He had already pushed her toward Tōji (V03 i0020), defended Tōji's credulity at his own expense here (「何オレとーじの弁解してんだろ」, i0028), and told Misao he had lost (i0018). Misao's independent view, 「今度の恋は結構マジみたいだったのに」 (i0019), is the first assessment of his seriousness from an adult who knows his habits. PG-CL-0006 is strengthened (PG-RV-0014), with his stated motive kept as the competing account.

The farewell scene (i0074–i0083) is built around a sentence Momo does not finish: 「もしとーじより先に出会ってたら あたし岡安のこと——」 (i0079). Kairi interrupts it with exaggerated comic panic (i0080). The interruption itself is meaningful: he stops her before she can say something that would oblige either of them. Her private 「好きになってくれて…ありがとう」 (i0082) attributes love to him, which he does not state in this scene, and her tears, 「一生の別れでもないのに」 ("even though it isn't goodbye forever", i0083), mark it as a loss. Her later wish to keep Kairi 「以前どおり」 ("as before") while dating Tōji is judged by her own narration as selfish (i0085). PG-CL-0013 (her feelings toward Kairi) is strengthened: the counterfactual makes the feeling's romantic category likelier than V03 allowed, while her present choice of Tōji is unambiguous (PG-RV-0013).

### 5.4 The kiss and the sign: two kinds of reticence

The middle chapters are comic but structurally exact. Momo refuses a kiss she wants (i0088, i0097). Tōji will not say or sign 「好き」, which she wants to hear (i0107–i0108). Each partner withholds what the other needs, and each withholding is explained by embarrassment, not by doubt (「心の準備」, i0092; 「恥ずかしすぎ」, i0107). Her narration names the deeper motive for the kiss: both have kissed someone else, and a kiss would 「今までのこと払拭する」 ("wipe out everything that happened before", i0097).

The resolution reverses V01's secrecy. In V01 Momo demanded that the relationship be kept secret (CM-0002). Here Tōji makes their private sign in front of the class (i0122). The audience structure matters: the class sees a joke (「王様ゲーム?」), and only Momo receives the message. Sae's messages so far split their audiences to deceive; this code splits its audience to protect a private meaning, and the couple controls it. Tōji's comment, 「やっちまえば案外……どってことなかった」 ("once I did it, it was no big deal", i0125), shows that his reticence can be overcome when the cost is understood. PG-CL-0017 records this.

### 5.5 Sae after exposure: the performance resumes

V04 shows Sae from inside her isolation for the first time, and the evidence on her sincerity is mixed:

- **Pressures.** She is trampled (i0057), her baking is destroyed (i0099), she is hit with a ball (i0111), and later she is forced to write that she is a liar (i0165). Some of this comes from girls she previously mocked (i0099), which matches the V03 pattern testimony.
- **Response to kindness.** When Momo partners with her, Sae wipes her eyes (i0114). Momo's partnering repeats Sae's own first approach at the entrance exam (i0112), so it is the volume's clearest act of reciprocity.
- **Public servility** (「ももさま」, i0116), which classmates read as a 豹変 (sudden transformation) and Momo finds embarrassing.
- **Private hostility.** The fantasy of Momo groveling and the stamping on her shoes (i0142–i0143) are shown to the reader alone. Neither Momo nor Tōji sees them, and both credit Sae's withdrawal from the movie as consideration (i0143). She then scrubs the shoes clean, apparently from fear of discovery (i0144).
- **Exploitation and deception outside school.** She switches from 「ももさま」 to 「ももちゃん」 when calling in her favor (i0146). She passes off Momo's cookies as homemade (i0155) and gives Jigoro a false account of Momo (i0158). At school she inflates his words (i0160–i0161).

The servility is at least partly performative (PG-CL-0016). Momo's prediction that Sae will learn 「人の痛み」 (others' pain, i0115) is contradicted in the same volume. The forecast is Momo's, not the analysis's, but her model of Sae has drifted from her accurate V02 model toward charity. PG-CL-0003 is strengthened (PG-RV-0018): deception continues after exposure, now aimed at outsiders who lack the class's corrective testimony.

### 5.6 Jigoro: the structure repeats with a new audience

The final chapter reruns the V01–V03 mechanism on a stranger. Jigoro sees a scene: Momo standing with arms crossed and snapping 「早くしてよ!!」, Sae small and pleading. The author's aside 「こう見える」 ("this is how it looks", i0156) draws Momo as he sees her, a tyrant. Sae then supplies the account (i0158). It is the lion-and-deer method of V03 i0109 in miniature: the visible hierarchy is taken as evidence, and the first to cry wins. Jigoro even offers the motive Sae would want: Momo is jealous because Sae is cute (i0159).

What draws him to Sae is shown before any story: her lightness when he lifts her (「なんて軽いんだ」, i0153). The staff rumor that he has no girlfriend because ordinary women do not catch his eye (i0150) sets up the confession as exceptional. His rescue of Sae is real and admirable on its face (i0166). It also vindicates Sae's exaggerated claim in public, after classmates had called it a lie (i0162), so Sae's credibility at school is restored by an outsider's act, not by her honesty. PG-CL-0018 records this repetition. Forecasts PG-PR-0008 and PG-PR-0009 test where it leads.

### 5.7 Form

- **The verbatim callback** (i0034) re-sets the V01 opening captions on a dark lace ground, so the reader sees the series' first page reread by its narrator.
- **Comic registers carry ethical information.** Sae's chibi faces and cat ears mark scheming (i0142–i0144). Kairi's comic panic interrupts Momo's unfinished sentence (i0080) and acts as a form of tact.
- **Marginal asides** do analytical work: 「こう見える」 (i0156) marks a point of view; 「話ふくらんでる」 (i0160) labels exaggeration; 「当てずっぽ」 (i0102) tells the reader Kairi was guessing.
- **The two-audience sign** (i0122–i0124) is staged with the class laughing in the foreground and Momo's face in close-up, the visual form of a private message in public.

## 6. Counter-readings

- **Momo reunites with Tōji because Kairi rejected her.** Kairi's withdrawal comes after his confrontation, and she does feel abandoned (i0066). But her monologue and apology concern her own avoidance and name Tōji as the person she loves (i0034–i0047), and her counterfactual about Kairi comes after the reunion. Low support.
- **Kairi's opportunism explanation is true.** He did act when Tōji was absent, but he then defended Tōji, pushed Momo toward him, and withdrew. Retained as a competitor at low-to-moderate support.
- **Sae's servility is sincere and the shoe-stamping is a momentary lapse.** She does clean the shoes, and she does cry when partnered. Against this: the fantasy content, the cookie lie and the false account to Jigoro all occur within days. A mixed account (genuine relief, persistent rivalry) is better supported than pure sincerity or pure performance.
- **Jigoro's interest is in Sae's appearance only.** His fixation on her lightness supports a physical, even protective attraction. His confession says he could not forget her, which is not yet evidence of knowing her. Recorded as open.
