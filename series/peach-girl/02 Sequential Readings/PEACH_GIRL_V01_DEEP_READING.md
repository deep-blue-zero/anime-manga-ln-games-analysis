# Volume 1 — the body that speaks before its owner

The first volume of Peach Girl is organized around one premise, which it states and tests repeatedly. Adachi Momo's tanned skin, chlorine-reddened hair and sharp eyes make other people decide who she is before she speaks. The volume's opening caption is her own: 「あたしはいつも外見でソンをする」 ("I always lose out because of how I look"). It then follows the costs of that misreading through four connected arenas: strangers who proposition her, classmates who gossip about her, a rival who exploits her, and the two boys whose interest in her becomes the volume's central relationship problem. By the end, Momo has the boy she wanted since middle school, Tōjigamori Kazuya, and is on the point of losing him. The cause is not a change in either of their feelings. It is a set of false and half-true transmissions, most of them traceable to her friend Kashiwagi Sae. The final page leaves Momo refusing a kiss from Tōji in the place where she saw him kiss Sae, with each of them now misreading the other.

## 3. Orientation: what happens

Opening day, from Momo's first-person narration. A middle-aged man on the street offers Momo money for sex, and she drives him off with a kick (i0007–i0008). At school, her friend Sae has bought the 風呂敷 bag Momo had picked out the day before, after mocking Momo's taste in it (i0009–i0011). A full-page "fashion check" (i0012) catalogs how Sae copies Momo's hairpins, makeup, nails, socks and shoes. Momo hides from Sae her long love for Tōji, a middle-school classmate (i0013–i0017). Tōji defends her against classmates' rumor that her tan comes from tanning salons and "playing around" (i0016). Momo's private plan is to lose her tan until she is paler than Tōji and then confess (i0019). When Sae guesses that Momo likes someone, Momo panics and points at a passing boy as a decoy: Okayasu Kairi, the most popular boy in their year (i0020–i0024).

About a week later, Sae approaches Kairi with a gift. Kairi, who had watched the bag episode in the shop, rebuffs her for undercutting her friend (i0025–i0028). Next, graffiti pairing Kairi and Momo appears on another class's blackboard (i0031). Kairi then tells classmates he has kissed Momo (i0035). A bloc of his female fans confronts Momo, and one of them stamps on the purikura charm of Tōji that Momo carries; Momo attacks them (i0036–i0043). When Kairi appears and treats the fight as entertainment, Momo punches him (i0044–i0047). He explains that two years earlier she rescued him from drowning in the Japan Sea, and he believes she gave him mouth-to-mouth. She says a lifeguard did (i0048–i0050). Asked how to stop the rumor, he kisses her: 「ウワサを本当にすればいい」 (i0052–i0053).

Chapter 2 follows the fallout. Momo rinses her mouth and insists it was not a kiss (i0058–i0059). Tōji ignores her at the pool (i0061). Tōji confronts Kairi and punches the wall beside his head when Kairi talks provocatively about Momo's body (i0069–i0072). Tōji and Sae, hidden in a hedge, overhear Momo telling Kairi that she has loved Tōji since middle school. They also hear the reason for her tan-removal plan: a friend told her Tōji dislikes dark-skinned girls (i0073–i0080). Tōji does not remember saying so. Kairi rescues Momo from a man trying to drag her to a hotel (i0081–i0086). Two notes, each signed with the other's name, summon Momo and Kairi to the pool. Kairi falls in retrieving his note, and Momo saves him with mouth-to-mouth just as Sae leads Tōji to the pool gate (i0087–i0096). Walking home, Tōji tells Momo he overheard her and that he likes her too (i0099–i0101).

Chapter 3 is the brief happy interval and its sabotage. Tōji tells Kairi 「オレのだから」 ("she's mine"), and Momo is now his girlfriend (i0112–i0114). Momo asks to keep the relationship secret because of the Kairi rumor, and especially from Sae (i0115–i0117). Sae then "consults" Momo about a boyfriend who kissed her and tried to push her down at his empty house. She names him as Tōji (i0122–i0127) and smiles with her tongue out after Momo leaves (i0128). That afternoon, Momo pays at a café with Tōji's wallet, which Sae had "found" and returned that day (i0121–i0122), and finds a condom packet in it (i0129–i0131). Momo begins avoiding Tōji (i0132). Sae falsely assures Tōji that Momo has told her about them. She gives him a false explanation of Momo's coldness, proposes to be his "practice" kiss, and overrides his refusals (i0133–i0138). Momo and Kairi see the kiss under the pergola (i0139–i0140).

In Chapter 4, Kairi tries to win Momo for himself (i0145–i0149). Momo argues that Sae must have forced the kiss (i0150). At lunch she confronts Sae in the restroom and tells her that she and Tōji are dating. Sae insists Tōji kissed her and insinuates that Tōji wants Momo only for sex (i0155–i0159). Tōji asks Momo to wait for him at the pool after school. Sae, eavesdropping, tells him Momo has moved the meeting to the cafeteria (i0161–i0162). Momo waits in the sun with Kairi, who gives her a string bikini and then asks her seriously to go out with him. She turns his reason (「一途な所」, her single-mindedness) into an affirmation that she will go on loving Tōji (i0163–i0170). Sae brings Tōji to the pool fence. Seeing Momo laughing with Kairi, he hears Sae's fabricated report that Momo finds him boring and Kairi more exciting (i0171–i0173). He drags Momo to the pergola and moves to kiss her. She pushes him away: 「いやっ」 (i0174–i0178).

## 5. Close reading

### 5.1 The opening as thesis: labels in the air

The first two story pages (i0007–i0008) build an argument through layout. On i0007, a sequence of reaction panels shows schoolboys and girls watching Momo shout at the man. Their speech identifies her (「安達じゃん? ウチのクラスの…」) before she identifies herself. i0008 devotes a full page to her glaring face. Three quoted labels float around her like captions she did not write: 「キツそう」 (looks harsh), 「ナマイキ」 (cheeky), 「遊んでる」 (plays around). Her first-person caption sits below them. The page stages the volume's information problem: other people's descriptions of Momo circulate as free-floating propositions with no attributed speaker. Her own account must compete with them.

The incident that occasions this is not ambiguous. A stranger propositioned a uniformed schoolgirl for money (i0009, 「2万でどお?」), and she refused him violently. Sae's joke (「ももちゃんていかにもしてそーだもんねー」) repeats the stranger's inference in friendly form. The book places these inferences side by side and lets the reader see that they share a logic. A morally defensible refusal reads, to onlookers, as further evidence of the "hard" persona.

The motif returns in more dangerous form at i0081–i0082. A man offers 30,000 and then 50,000 yen, grips Momo's wrist and pulls her toward a hotel. Momo notes his strength: 「チビのくせに馬鹿力」. This second incident is not comic. Her protest dissolves into 「やだやだやだやだ やぁ———っ」 (i0083), and she is freed only by Kairi. The repetition establishes that the opening was a pattern, not an anecdote, and it raises the stakes from social insult to physical danger.

### 5.2 Momo: an anxious romantic inside a combative surface

At the V01 boundary, the most strongly supported account of Momo is that her outward combativeness and her inward romantic anxiety are both real, and that they operate in different registers. Her speech toward antagonists is rough: casual imperatives (どけろっつってんだよ, i0042), insults (ドテカボチャ, 色魔, バカ安), and repeated physical retaliation against the fan girls and against Kairi (i0043, i0046, i0057, i0086, i0149, i0165). Her narration about Tōji is the opposite: long-held, planful and fearful. She chose the school for him (i0063). She has a two-year sun-avoidance regime with hourly sunscreen and long sleeves (i0078). She will not confess until she meets a self-imposed condition (i0019). When Tōji confesses first, her reaction is disbelief (「夢だったんじゃないか」, i0109).

Three features matter for later modeling.

1. **She acts on second-hand reports about Tōji rather than asking him.** The tan plan rests entirely on an unnamed friend's report (i0077). The crisis of Chapters 3–4 likewise rests on Sae's report and the packet, never on a question to Tōji. Momo's intention to confront him (「確かめなきゃ とーじに さえのこと」, i0139) is preempted by what she then sees. This is a recurring decision pattern in V01. It is not yet a stable trait: the relevant situations are few and all involve Tōji.
2. **Her self-model takes in other people's appearance-based theories.** When Sae suggests that Tōji wants her body, Momo's thought turns first to the men who have treated her that way (i0160: 「そりゃ確かに いままでこの外見のせいで そーゆー手合いに絡まれることはあったけど…」). Then she rejects the inference: 「とーじはそんな人じゃないいっ!!」. The insinuation works because it reuses a real pattern in her life.
3. **She can deceive, and in V01 she does so once, defensively.** The decoy statement to Sae (i0021) is a deliberate lie, established by her narration. It is also the root of later trouble: it supplies the premise for her accusation over the graffiti (i0032), and Sae turns it against her later (i0127). The source does not present this as a moral failing. It frames it as an understandable panic that compounds.

Momo's handling of Kairi's proposal is a deft verbal move (i0170). Kairi names her single-mindedness as what he likes. She asks whether that means she may go on loving Tōji, which turns his compliment into a reason to decline. His panicked 「タンマ!! いまのなし」 and her laugh (「ぷっ…」) show the refusal landing without cruelty. A counter-reading is that she is softening toward Kairi. The laugh, her earlier 「ありがと……」 after the street rescue (i0085), and his ability to make her laugh while she waits all support some warmth. But her explicit choice and her refusal to leave the meeting place (i0167) point the other way at this boundary. Both are recorded. Romantic interest in Kairi is not evidenced.

### 5.3 Sae: from copying to engineered misinformation

Sae is introduced entirely through Momo's focalization: the fashion diagram, the bag and the "she copies everything" caption. A cautious reading must ask whether Momo is an unreliable narrator of her friend. Within V01 the answer is that her account of Sae's imitation is corroborated by independent sources and then exceeded by what the reader sees directly:

- Kairi independently saw the bag episode and calls it out (i0027–i0028).
- Other girls describe Sae's approach to Kairi as fawning (i0026). Note that this is a hostile audience's label, not a neutral observation.
- Sae's request to call Tōji 「とーじ」 (i0073) repeats the imitation pattern in a new domain, Momo's private name for him.
- Sae's monologue at i0119 is shown with no witness: 「なんであんな色黒女がモテんのよっっ!! 学園ナンバー1はこのあたしよっっ」. The margin note 「本音」 marks it as her real voice. This is the first evidence that bypasses Momo's perspective, and it establishes hostility and a competitive status motive.

From Chapter 3 on, the reader receives direct evidence of deliberate deception. By the method's standard this requires both knowledge and intentional misrepresentation:

- **Cafeteria relay (i0161–i0162).** Sae is shown under the bench hearing 「プールサイド」 and writing it down. She then tells Tōji that Momo wants the cafeteria. Knowledge and false transmission are both depicted. `ESTABLISHED_SOURCE_FACT` that the relay was false. `STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION` that it was intended to keep them apart, given her use of the gap at i0171–i0173.
- **"Momo told me about you two" (i0135).** Momo had refused precisely this (i0116). Sae learned of the relationship by spying (i0108). Her 「そ!」 is a false affirmation.
- **Fabricated quotations (i0173).** The words Sae attributes to Momo are nowhere spoken by Momo. The reader has just watched Momo say the opposite (i0170).
- **The pergola kiss (i0137–i0140, i0157).** Tōji refused twice. Sae said 「いーから黙って!!」 and took hold of him. To Momo she later says 「キスされちゃった」, presenting herself as the passive party.

Her claim that Tōji kissed her a week ago and tried to force sex on her (i0123–i0126) is not refuted by any directly depicted counter-event. The source's evidence against it is circumstantial but strong. Tōji tells Sae that he has never kissed anyone (i0137–i0138), in a scene the reader sees and Sae cannot be lying in. Sae's smile after Momo leaves (i0128) is depicted. And her account would place the alleged events before Tōji had even learned of Momo's feelings, which fits the theory that she built the story after witnessing the confession. The claim is recorded as `DISPUTED`, leaning toward fabrication; no admission has been shown.

Several plausible acts of Sae's are **not** shown and remain hypotheses: the blackboard graffiti (i0031), the two forged pool notes (i0087), and the condom packet in the wallet (i0121–i0122, i0130). Each fits the pattern, and Sae's timed arrival with Tōji at the pool (i0094) makes the notes especially suggestive. But at this frontier their authorship is `UNRESOLVED`. Recording them as Sae's acts would convert pattern into evidence.

Sae's motive is a separate question. V01 supports at least three components without resolving their weight:

1. **Rivalry and status with respect to Momo.** The imitation pattern, i0119's 「ナンバー1」 and her irritation at being forgotten (i0108).
2. **Attraction to Tōji specifically.** Her fantasy of having both popular boys at her sides (i0118); her flushed 「か…かわいいっ」 when Tōji admits inexperience (i0138), an aside unlikely to be strategic because no one hears it.
3. **Pleasure in control or disruption.** Her repeated satisfied smiles (i0128, i0154, i0173).

These are recorded as competing and possibly combined accounts (`PG-CL-0002`).

### 5.4 Tōji: protective, private and inexperienced

Tōji speaks little, and the volume uses that to make his inner state less accessible than Momo's or Sae's. What is evidenced:

- **Public defense of Momo against appearance-based rumor**, twice: to classmates (i0016) and to Kairi (i0070). Both use the same frame: people misjudge her by appearance; he knows her.
- **Violent anger at sexualized talk about her** (i0072). The punch into the wall is the volume's most forceful male gesture before the ending. It is a threat (「ブッ殺す」), aimed at Kairi.
- **Embarrassment and sincerity in romance.** He blushes and stammers on overhearing Momo (i0080). He confesses awkwardly (i0101). He has never kissed anyone because middle school was all baseball (i0138).
- **Trust in Sae as a neutral friend.** He wants Momo's permission to tell Sae (i0116), accepts her as an adviser (i0135–i0137), and believes her report at the fence (i0172–i0173).

The ending is the critical test. Tōji grips Momo hard enough to hurt (i0175: 「手が…」), takes her to the pergola, and moves to kiss her without asking. On Sae's advice ("kiss her without saying anything"), this was the remedy for Momo's supposed distress. By Sae's fresh report, it is also a response to Momo supposedly finding him boring. The reader knows both premises are false. Momo knows neither premise. She sees only force, at the site of his kiss with Sae, colored by Sae's line about 「やりたい盛り」. Her 「いやっ」 is therefore both a boundary and a misreading. Tōji's conduct is both an unasked-for advance and a misinformed attempt at repair. The volume ends before either can explain, and this analysis does not resolve his motive beyond what the page shows. His hard stare on i0178 could register hurt, anger or confusion.

### 5.5 Kairi: provocation, rescue and a memory of being saved

Kairi is introduced through a lie Momo tells about him. He turns out to have a prior, private connection to her: she saved his life, and he remembers the feel of long hair. The color verse on i0003, which opens the volume before any character is named, becomes legible at i0049 as his memory. The book has thus given Kairi's point of view the first image of the volume, while the narration belongs to Momo. That formal priority is a fact about the book. What it forecasts is not established.

His conduct in V01 combines:

- **Public provocation and manipulation of rumor.** He claims the kiss (i0035), lets the story spread (i0051) and turns the fight into a spectacle (i0044).
- **Unilateral physical intimacy.** The kiss at i0053, the cheek-rubbing 「マーキング」 (i0111), the hug (i0079) and the attempted kiss at i0148. Momo's responses are consistently negative and often violent. The source frames Kairi's persistence comically (「こりない」, i0064) without making his advances welcome within the story.
- **Real protective action**, freeing her from the man at i0083.
- **Detailed attention.** He noticed her trembling (i0085) and inferred a first kiss. He recalls her swimming ability. He is the first to articulate 「一途な所」 as what he values in her (i0170).
- **A shift toward seriousness.** His proposal at i0168 is drawn with an intense close-up (i0169) and a direct 「マジ」. Its sincerity is supported by the framing but not established.

Classmates' reputation of Kairi (「だれとも本気でつきあわない」, i0065) is an audience's account, not the source's verdict. His first-name familiarity with the school nurse (「操ちゃん」) and his unverified older sister (i0027, doubted at i0031) are background facts left open.

Kairi's knowledge is noteworthy: by i0075 he knows Momo's real love is 「とーじ」, which he picked up from the purikura. His use of the rescue memory to claim a "kiss" is partly a misunderstanding (he believes she gave him mouth-to-mouth) and partly opportunistic: he chose to say it in public, to girls mocking her.

### 5.7 Manga form and reader positioning

- **First-person captions** belong to Momo throughout. Other characters' interiority appears only in dialogue, thought balloons or brief asides. The two key exceptions are Sae's unguarded i0119 and her aside at i0138. Neither is captioned narration, but both give the reader access Momo lacks.
- **Reader-only scenes** create the volume's dramatic irony: the coercive prelude to the pergola kiss (i0137–i0138), Sae under the lab bench (i0161), and the cafeteria waiting (i0166, i0171). The reader is placed to judge Sae's later statements as false and to see both Momo and Tōji as victims of misinformation, which is a judgment neither character can make.
- **Visualized falsehoods.** Sae's claims are twice illustrated with images that look like events: the silhouetted kiss on i0123, and Momo with fabricated speech on i0173. On i0126, Momo imagines Tōji with Sae. These images are claims or imaginings, not depicted story events, and the analysis must not cite them as such.
- **Scene doubling.** The pergola is used twice: Sae kisses Tōji there (i0140), and Tōji tries to kiss Momo there (i0176–i0177). The volume makes the second scene readable only through the first. The second street harassment repeats the first. Kairi's rescue line repeats Tōji's defense word for word. The swimming rescue happens twice (two years ago, and i0091–i0094). These are at least formal parallels. Whether they become motifs depends on later recurrence.
- **Comic register.** Slapstick violence (Momo's kicks and punches, 「ドテカボチャ」, sweat drops, chibi faces) cushions events that are not trivial: unwanted kisses, sexual harassment, deception. The analysis records these events at their face content while noting that the comic framing is part of the narrative's tone. It does not dictate how the events should be judged within the story.

## 6. Counter-readings considered

- **Momo is unreliable about Sae.** Possible for the opening chapter. By the end of the volume, Momo's view is corroborated and exceeded by scenes outside her access (i0119, i0138, i0161–i0162, i0172–i0173). Retained only for the graffiti, notes and packet, whose authorship is not shown.
- **Tōji kissed Sae willingly.** At i0138, Tōji refuses twice. The kiss panel shows him as passive, and his face is hidden. His willingness is not depicted, and nothing shows him pushing her away either. `UNRESOLVED_QUESTION` as to his participation. Not `ESTABLISHED` either way.
- **Sae's boyfriend story is true and Tōji is two-timing.** Contradicted by Tōji's inexperience disclosure, the confession chronology and Sae's subsequent smile. Retained as a low-support alternative because no admission exists.
- **Kairi's kiss claim was malicious.** Partly supported: he made it to girls mocking her, and he let it spread. Partly countered: he sincerely believes the CPR memory, and he later says his feeling for her began then. Motive `UNRESOLVED`.
- **Momo is drawn to Kairi.** Supported by small signs of warmth (laughter, thanks). Contradicted by her explicit statements and choices. `PLAUSIBLE_COMPETING_INTERPRETATION` at low support.
- **Tōji's final approach is predatory.** It is forceful and unasked. But the reader knows it is driven by Sae's misinformation and Sae's advice to kiss Momo without words. The source withholds Tōji's interior on i0175–i0178. Recorded as an unrequested advance by a misinformed actor; no further motive inferred.
