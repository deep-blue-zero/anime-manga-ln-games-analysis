# Volume 2 — testimony without credit

Volume 2 is about credibility. Its central question is not what happened but whose account of what happened will be believed, and at what cost. Momo now understands Sae accurately. The reader has watched Sae fake a bruise, feign a sprain, organize a class-wide silence and stage a kiss to be seen. Yet Momo's every attempt to say so makes her look jealous and violent to Tōji, who reasons that Sae 「なんのメリットもない」 in lying to him (i0111). The volume's turning point comes when Momo finally gets a promised hearing, by threatening to jump from a fourth-floor hospital window. Two truths about kissing then surface at the worst moment: Kairi did kiss Momo, and Tōji's kiss with Sae was presented to him as "practice" for Momo. Neither has the context that would make it innocent, and the volume ends on Tōji's 「別れよう……」 ("let's break up").

## 5. Close reading

### 5.1 The asymmetry of credibility

V02's structure is a credibility trap. Momo's accusations against Sae are, by V02, true, and the reader has seen independent proof of each (i0040, i0063, i0130–i0133). But every accusation is delivered in a form that confirms the image Sae projects of Momo: jealous (i0030), aggressive (i0036–i0041), excessive (i0112). Momo diagnoses this herself (i0049, 「ワガママで独占欲むき出しの暴力女」), but cannot escape it. The manga's form reinforces the trap: Sae's private tells (the glance at i0039, the tongue at i0040, the tissue at i0063, the 「くすっ」 at i0044) are drawn in panels visible to the reader and invisible to Tōji.

Tōji's reasoning is not stupid. It applies a reasonable heuristic, "people do not lie without a motive", to a case where he cannot see the motive (i0111). Momo's diagnosis that he is 「自分のことに関してはてんでニブイ」 (i0149) correctly identifies the missing premise: he does not perceive Sae's interest in him. This marks a meaningful shift from V01. There, Momo's crisis came from believing Sae. In V02 the crisis is Tōji believing Sae, while Momo, now accurate, cannot be heard.

### 5.2 Symmetry and its breakdown

The hospital argument (i0105–i0111) is built as a mirror. Momo suspects Sae's fall was staged; Tōji suspects Kairi's stumble was staged. Each defends their own friend with 「誤解してる」. Momo even notices the reflection: 「それはあたしのさっきのセリフでしょ」. The mirror holds a structural insight. Both partners have an intimate of the opposite sex who wants them, and both read the other's intimate as the threat and their own as benign. The volume does not make the two cases equivalent. Sae is shown scheming (i0129–i0133). Kairi is shown refusing to scheme and urging fair means (i0135, i0145). But it lets each character's suspicion be equally sincere.

The ending breaks the mirror through two parallel omissions:

- Momo did not tell Tōji that Kairi kissed her. Her reason: it happened before they were dating and she could not resist (i0156). Both statements are supported by V01 (i0053; she kneed him, i0057). Her omission was not a lie by the method's standard, since she was never asked. But Sae frames it as one.
- Tōji did not tell Momo that he refused Sae's "practice" twice (V01 i0138). Instead he says 「キスだけどキスじゃない」, which concedes the practice framing. Sae then supplies it in the most humiliating form.

Each omission, revealed by Sae, hands the other a betrayal story. Neither speaks the context that would defuse it. The source makes this communication failure the immediate cause of the breakup proposal.

### 5.3 Tōji's interior opens

V02 gives Tōji's motives more access than V01 did, through his own statements:

- **Repair after a mistake.** He comes to apologize the same evening, still in uniform, waiting at her gate (i0019–i0022). He also asks Sae to carry an apology (i0062).
- **Jealousy toward Kairi as a long-standing insecurity.** 「安達とつきあうまえからオレ不安だった」 (i0137). The confession itself was hurried by fear after the pool rescue (i0138). This partly recontextualizes V01's confession (PG-EV-V01-0032). It was sincere, and it was also pre-emptive. This is a `REVEALED_NOT_NEW` element, not a new development.
- **A perceptive observation that is not Sae's.** 「あいつといるときの安達のほうが自然体…すごく生き生きしてて」 (i0139). It is a direct observation of Momo's behavior with Kairi, and it matches what the reader saw (her laughter at V01 i0170). It is the strongest piece of evidence in either volume that something in Momo's interaction with Kairi is distinctive. Momo answers with a different category, physiological arousal, and her heart races only for Tōji (i0141). The two statements are compatible: liveliness with Kairi and romantic arousal for Tōji can coexist. This analysis does not resolve which matters more.
- **A trust heuristic** that Sae has no motive (i0111). It begins to waver at i0149 when the cafeteria lie is exposed.

His breakup proposal is framed as exhaustion and loss of confidence (「やってく自信ない」), not punishment.

### 5.4 Momo's escalation pattern

Momo's methods in V02 escalate as each fails: a demand (i0030), a direct question (i0032), a physical push in anger (i0036), a phone disclosure (i0050), then a threat of self-harm from a fourth-floor window (i0112–i0116). The last is the volume's most consequential act by Momo. In comic register it is framed as typical Momo bravado (「足の1本でも折って入院すれば」), but it is real danger: she falls, and only Tōji's grip saves her (i0118–i0120). Afterward she shudders at it herself (i0124). Tōji names it accurately as 「ほとんど脅迫」 (i0126), affectionately. A provisional decision tendency is recorded: when Momo is not believed by someone she loves, she escalates to a dramatic act that forces attention. The support is three escalation steps across two days and should not be generalized further.

She also shows **fairness under her own scrutiny**. She tells Tōji about her decoy lie (i0051), concedes that she has been angry about the kiss (i0052), and in her inner voice catches herself, horrified, at possibly retaliating with the broom (i0037).

The plan at i0130–i0133 is the volume's darkest material. Sae proposes to exploit post-surgical immobility (「体の自由がきかないから あたしが迫っても逃げられない」) to create a 「既成事実」 (fait accompli), and to use Tōji's sense of responsibility to force him away from Momo. Kairi translates it bluntly, 「抵抗できないとーじを無理やり」 ("force a Tōji who can't resist"), and Sae corrects only the vocabulary. The manga's comic framing (chibi faces, 「謀略家!!」) does not change what the plan is, and Kairi's refusal (「ぜってーやだ」) is the volume's explicit judgment on it. This analysis describes the plan at the level its narrative function requires and does not reproduce more.

Motive evidence moves. Sae states her goal outright: 「あたしはとーじが欲しい」 (i0122). She also claims to have liked Tōji before Momo (i0145), which is unverified. Kairi offers the counter-theory that she wants Tōji because he is Momo's (「マネッコ」). The imitation pattern (V01 i0012; V02 i0041) supports Kairi's theory. Sae's unguarded 「かわいいっ」 (V01 i0138) and her persistence support real attraction. PG-CL-0002 is revised to give the explicit goal statement weight while keeping the imitation account as a strong competitor.

### 5.6 Kairi: ally by choice

Kairi does more practical good for Momo in V02 than anyone else. He gets her home safely (i0012–i0013) and gives advice that centers her choice (i0016). He learns of and transmits the hospitalization (i0080, i0083), takes her there (i0086–i0097), testifies for her (i0107–i0108), extracts Sae's plan by pretending to agree, then refuses it (i0129–i0135). Momo herself says: 「いま あたしの味方はあんただけだわ」 (i0098).

This is not disinterested. He keeps pursuing her physically (the stairs embrace i0072, the pucker at i0098). He states that he will not give up (「人を好きになるのは自由じゃん」, i0144). And he admits he exploited the kiss rumor (「便乗」, i0146). It was his careless admission to Sae that handed her the 「決定打」 (i0147–i0148), which he then rushes to prevent (i0153). His testimony at i0108 also reverses the fall, taking blame he did not strictly earn. That is a kind, misleading act in Momo's favor. PG-CL-0006 strengthens: there is now evidence of costly choices in Momo's interest against his romantic interest (refusing an alliance that would have delivered Momo to him).

### 5.7 Form notes

- Comic deformation (Sae as a cat with ears, chibi Kairi as a skeleton at i0103, fireworks) marks Sae's malice in a register that keeps it readable as farce. The plan at i0130–i0131 is drawn in a darkened, framed imagined sequence, which visually separates hypothetical from actual.
- The window sequence (i0112–i0120) uses wind lines and a shattered flowerpot, a stand-in for Momo's own fall, to register real danger inside an otherwise comic argument.
- V02's final page splits the faces of Momo and Tōji on a diagonal (i0166), with a small panel of the gleeful Sae and the stricken Kairi. The composition keeps the two schemers' reactions on the same page as the break.

## 6. Counter-readings

- **Sae's bruise or fall could be real.** Refuted by i0040 and i0063.
- **Tōji's breakup is Sae's victory alone.** Partly. His insecurity about Kairi predates Sae (i0137). His observation of Momo's liveliness with Kairi is his own (i0139). Sae accelerates and supplies evidence. She does not create his fear.
- **Momo lied about Kairi.** By the method's standard (knowledge plus intentional misrepresentation), an omission without a question is not a lie. Her earlier 「岡安とはホントになんでもない」 (i0152) was true of her feelings and silent on the kiss. Classification: omission.
- **Kairi joined Sae.** Refuted by i0135. His enthusiasm was a pretense to learn the plan; motive inferred from the reversal.
- **The hospital kiss was an accident.** The slip on Tōji's spilled tea is depicted (i0099). The kiss after it is Sae's act, and her later aside indicates intent to be seen (i0132). Accident at most covers the fall.
