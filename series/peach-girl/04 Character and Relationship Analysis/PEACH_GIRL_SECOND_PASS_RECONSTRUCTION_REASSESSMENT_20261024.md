## Four different achievements

A character monograph explains development and meaning. A time-indexed dossier makes an evidenced state, its available knowledge, competing goals and unsupported domains retrievable at a selected scene cutoff. An executable prototype maps declared inputs to categories. An operational model requires validation beyond this exposed source corpus. Progress in any one of these does not establish the others.

The four old prototypes are narrow categorical hypotheses. The reviewed dossiers supply a distinct temporal and contextual representation, including explicit contrary cases and reasons to abstain. They are literary reconstruction artifacts, not conversational agents or new executable policies. Misao and Ryo support bounded representations with thinner ordinary-life and stranger evidence; equal completeness is not asserted, and executable policies for them remain NOT_PROPOSED.

## What the original probes establish

The original package contains eight narrative probes drawn from seven narrative context groups, plus four knowledge-guard cases. Five narrative labels match the rules and three contradict them: K2, T2 and S1. The four guard cases are implementation checks. Neither twelve nominal cases nor five matching narrative labels is a predictive accuracy estimate. Related contexts, known outcomes, designer source exposure and a tiny categorical space prevent that interpretation.

K2 limits an automatic-withdrawal account of Kairi: attachment and hurt can coexist with imposed continuation. T2 limits a generalized Tōji-respects-exit rule: actual earlier patience does not exclude later restraint. S1 limits an invariant-control account of Sae: she can supply useful truth or comfort while control returns elsewhere. These are bounded counterexamples to the tested rules, not evidence that each character always behaves in the opposite way. The failures and every original rule/result remain preserved.

The runner rejects known_facts labels outside the model whitelist, checks the exact domain, then selects the first matching rule from supplied features and compares categorical labels. The features themselves are not whitelisted. It does not require every allowed known-fact label, validate feature provenance or contradictions, independently determine whether a feature was observable at the selected time, prove a claimed motive, or enforce the recorded narrative cutoff. Permitted known-fact labels can accompany outcome-informed features that the runner never checks. All four saved guards specifically exercise forbidden-known-fact rejection. Unsupported-domain and no-rule branches are present but untested by those saved guards; runtime hash/freeze metadata is not independently enforced. The guards show limited implementation behavior, not temporal leakage control.

Before any future prediction claim, an input such as an exit being sought or a latent romantic priority would need a source/time route showing that it was available before the target action and independently supplied. The retrospective cases do not establish that condition merely by recording the input. This is an unresolved validation responsibility, not a claim that a particular old probe has been proved fraudulent.

## Requirements for dossier retrieval

Each dossier must distinguish a phase interval from a usable snapshot. A row spanning multiple events cannot supply every later fact at its opening. Selected exact scene endpoints must name what has been received, what remains merely suspected or reported, what the reader knows separately, and which future facts are unavailable. A full V18 evidence inventory is the analyst's boundary, not every character's simultaneous knowledge.

A worked retrieval case must identify the selected cutoff, supplied facts, rival priorities, supported alternatives, contrary evidence and an abstention. It may recover a source-demonstrated action or test whether an interpretation preserves a refusal, a change of mind or a named recipient. It cannot award held-out success for an already known outcome. Replaying such cards is semantic consistency review, not a replacement predictive experiment.

Written address, questions, ellipses, jokes and self-correction are source-bounded characterization. They do not establish acoustic voice, accent, unrestricted dialogue generation, clinical diagnosis or general decision reliability. Unknown domains remain explicit, including unshown private history, the full commercial network, most stranger contexts and future relationship repair.
