## R-01 — after V10 i0093: useful credibility with a knowingly false proxy account

Witness: PG-JP-NE-V10-W01; original OPF-spine locators below.

**Scene and knowledge.** Ryō participates in the phone deception and gives Momo the account that Kairi called while he was nearby, cannot come, and cannot notify her because he lacks her number. The earlier impersonation is his conduct, not a rumor he innocently repeats. At i0093 Momo responds as though he has come specially to convey the message; the source gives him her response. [V10 i0087–0093; original i0093 repeated.]

**Competing goals and mechanism.** The false proxy explanation allows considerate older-brother conduct to continue the credibility earned through useful advice and apparent understanding. Access to Momo is an evidenced result. A complete advance plan for every earlier kindness is not established; comfort and accurate perception can be real effects even when he later exploits trust.

**Unavailable and limits.** Future party consent, gift refusal, room restraint, Momo's defensive preparations and later Sae transactions are unavailable at this cutoff. Momo does not share his knowledge of his own deception. Neither her trust nor attraction grants later contact permission. His favorable Aki account remains attributed rather than fully verified or globally disproved. A representation should retrieve this asymmetry, not flatten both participants into identical knowledge.

## R-02 — after V10 i0169: explicit exit override

Witness: PG-JP-NE-V10-W01; original OPF-spine locators below.

**Scene and knowledge.** Momo's earlier acceptance of a requested, limited garden hug has not authorized every later contact. After unwanted closeness and an imposed gift, she says she is leaving at i0168; he physically pulls her back, and at i0169 says “帰さない.” Both hear the refusal-to-release exchange. He knows the immediate boundary because she has stated it. [V10 i0126, i0154, i0164–0169; originals i0168–0169 repeated.]

**Mechanism and alternatives.** The scene moves from recruited credibility to control of departure. It supports a direct local violation without needing a theory that Momo secretly wanted restraint or that prior consent is permanently transferable. His choice to stop her is depicted; his unspoken complete purpose or intended final extent is not. Earlier help and her prior attraction remain true counterevidence to a caricature in which no trust existed, but do not negate this act.

**Unavailable and abstention.** V11 escape, injury, recipient-specific explanations and later negotiations have not occurred. Do not treat subsequent resistance as already known to him, invent exact next speech, or call every aspect of the party's business legally established.
