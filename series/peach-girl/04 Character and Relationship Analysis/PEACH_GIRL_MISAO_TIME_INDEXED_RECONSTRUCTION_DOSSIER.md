## M-01 — after V06 i0053: help without the later home account

Witness: PG-JP-NE-V06-W01; original OPF-spine locators below.

**Scene and accessible evidence.** The useful Misao action is directly at i0051: she treats injured Jigorō despite Kairi's objection, says an injured person cannot be left, and advises medical examination for the back injury. At i0053 the scene ends with Kairi and Sae leaving and Jigorō alone. Misao is not pictured there; this cutoff must not invent her location, line of sight or receipt of everything Sae says while dressing at i0050–0052. Her earlier emergency participation and the visible aftermath are available; the unobserved hotel interval remains incomplete. [V06 i0037–0053; selected repeats i0049–0053.]

**Goals and mechanisms.** Aid to Momo and basic care for an injured apparent offender can coexist. Professional obligation is strongly supported by treatment/referral; personal warmth and contextual judgment are compatible additional explanations. Her action resists Kairi's preference for withholding treatment. It establishes neither equal culpability nor complete knowledge of the alleged assault.

**Unavailable and competing evidence.** The later Tōji-to-Misao home report and her relay to Momo have not happened by this cutoff. Do not give her later drug knowledge, a clinical reconstruction, or Momo's later reporting refusal here. Her parental-contact assertion at i0037 remains unverified under F0005; the internal role qualification does not prove a separate parental-contact lie. This is practical care amid incomplete information, not a guarantee of flawless safeguarding or privacy.

Her private address to the absent Tōji — “わたしだけはきみの味方よ” — is presented as her thought/commitment, not a new message he receives on this page. Kairi hears her warning and opposition. **Unavailable and counterevidence.** Kairi's later restraint of Momo, Misao's physical interruption, his coerced hold on Misao, and the waiting episode all lie ahead. Her later intervention is counterevidence to a single fixed alliance, but cannot yet be this snapshot's knowledge. A later favorable Ryō account does not verify his integrity. A bounded representation should retrieve this partiality and incomplete disclosure rather than produce an allegedly optimal counseling script.

## M-03 — after V14 i0063: a real refusal, before later confidence and choices

Witness: PG-JP-NE-V14-W01; original OPF-spine locators below.

**Scene and access.** Kairi has confessed his romantic feelings. Misao answers at i0062 that she thinks of him only as a younger brother and expects that to continue. At i0063 he thanks her and says he feels clearer. She has received his confession and witnessed his immediate verbal response. She does not thereby know his eventual adjustment or every later relationship decision. [V14 i0057–0063; original i0062–0063 repeated.]

**State and competing meanings.** Her care and recognition were real, but they did not constitute reciprocal romance. Her explicit refusal is stronger than an inference from gratitude or facial softness. His relief can coexist with hurt; the page does not certify a completed emotional cure. Her surprise also limits any retrospective claim that she had always known his feelings and deliberately cultivated them.

**Unavailable and limits.** The later telephone communication of her chocolates/non-confession history, her later expressed increased confidence and V15 pursuit of Ryō, and Kairi’s later explicit “Momo first” statement have not occurred at this endpoint. Misao’s own earlier history is not newly learned by her when she later tells it; Kairi has not yet received that later account here. Do not characterize this refusal as already based on hearing that later first-choice declaration. No contact or future romantic reward is owed for his confession. Later encouragement and gratitude must remain separate from this no.
