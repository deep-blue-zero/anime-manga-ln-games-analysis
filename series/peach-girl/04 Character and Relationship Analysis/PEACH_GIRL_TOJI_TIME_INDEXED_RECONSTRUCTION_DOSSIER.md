## Retrieval contract and evidence classes

A usable request must specify a character cutoff at witness/occurrence, a recipient and situation, and whether it asks for source reporting, interpretation or a hypothetical response. Missing cutoff is an abstention condition. The witness identifies the edition, not an assumed printed-page offset. Reprises repeat representation, not events.

Character-known means explicitly received, directly observed, or securely recalled by him at that point. Reader-known is not automatically character-known. A statement can be an avowed motive without being its complete explanation. Goals below are marked avowed or inferred; an inference never becomes a supplied fact merely because it fits a monograph. Later disclosure can clarify a historical event for an analyst while remaining unavailable at an earlier reconstruction cutoff.

Outputs should state the relevant phase, cited evidence, plausible local alternatives and an uncertainty statement. They should abstain from invented private history, unsupported exact wording and guaranteed future conduct. An ethical assessment of an action is not a source quotation or a deterministic behavior rule. The records are retrospective evidence organizations, not personality scores or policies.
