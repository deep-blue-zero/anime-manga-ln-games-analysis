# AoButa Character State Ledger — Final

## 1. Purpose

This ledger distinguishes:

- **genuine development**;
- **revelation of prior hidden state**;
- **world-state substitution**;
- **recognition-state change**;
- **relationship-specific expression**;
- **retrospective recontextualization**.

It is a reference layer, not a replacement for the specialist character documents.

## 2. Central characters

## 2.1 Azusagawa Sakuta

### Initial state

At the beginning of Volume 1, Sakuta is a socially injured, anti-smartphone high-school student whose sarcasm performs several functions at once:

- refusal of ambient social authority;
- protection against embarrassment;
- testing whether another person can tolerate directness;
- concealment of how strongly unsupported suffering affects him.

His first governing impulse is:

> if another person is suffering and nobody will act, he will act.

That is his greatest virtue and the source of many early ethical failures.

### Major discontinuities

- V1: becomes exceptional recognizer of Mai.
- V3: refuses to select a “real” Rio but violates information boundaries elsewhere.
- V5: experiences Hanakaede's return as Kaede's death.
- V6: treats his own life as morally expendable repayment to Shoko.
- V7: exists as two Sakutas across possible states and survives with moral residue.
- V9: encounters another Sakuta's happier family biography.
- V11: meets a counterpart who appears more competent at helping.
- V14–15: becomes the likely observer implicated in reality rewriting.

### Genuine development

1. **Recognition of his own life as morally valuable.**
   
   `生きていたい` is the first decisive break from the idea that disappearing for another person is the purest form of gratitude.

2. **Shared rather than unilateral moral jurisdiction.**
   
   V7's disclosure to Mai before changing the past is a major procedural advance.

3. **Distributed care.**
   
   By the university arc he increasingly calls Rio, relies on Miori, Ikumi, Sara, Takumi, Shoko, institutions, and the ensemble.

4. **Informational restraint.**
   
   His refusal to read the Toko/Miori diary because he wants Miori to tell him herself is one of the clearest reversals of earlier intrusion.

5. **Ordinary competence.**
   
   Tutoring, teacher-license coursework, driving, research, and smartphone use replace crisis as the main proof of adulthood.

6. **Belief without sight.**
   
   The final Ebina scene completes his recognition arc.

### Final state

- education trainee at Minegahara;
- mathematics teacher candidate;
- still dating Mai;
- socially embedded rather than proudly isolated;
- studies how apparent “general opinion” is constructed;
- cannot see Ebina's ghost but believes her testimony enough to listen.

### Final classification

> `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT` + `RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION`

Final thesis:

> **Sakuta matures from exceptional rescuer to adult listener: his distinction is no longer that he sees the impossible, but that he knows what disbelief can do.**

Evidence route: V1 thesis/backfill; V6–7 backfill; V8–15 native ledgers, especially V15 final section.

## 2.2 Sakurajima Mai

### Initial state

Mai begins as a nationally visible actress who lacks sufficient authority over:

- her body;
- her work;
- her public image;
- her mother's decisions;
- her place at school.

She is simultaneously hypervisible and socially erased.

### Genuine development

- resumes acting on terms she experiences as her own;
- accepts romance only after testing ordinary durability rather than crisis intensity;
- develops independent sisterhood with Nodoka;
- becomes an active caregiver to Kaede/Hanakaede;
- rejects Sakuta's unilateral martyrdom;
- articulates `ふたりで幸せになる`;
- develops adult professional and educational logistics;
- trusts Sakuta against her own rewritten Toko self-perception;
- gives the ceiling-pattern metaphor and `同じ景色` thesis.

### Relationship-specific expression

Mai's teasing, jealousy, tenderness, and domesticity do not replace professional control. They appear because Sakuta becomes one of the few people before whom she need not maintain one uninterrupted public performance.

### Final state

- continuing major actor, including taiga-drama work;
- university progress remains affected by career schedules;
- durable partner to Sakuta;
- co-author of the series' final adulthood ethic;
- not reduced to wife/romantic reward.

### Final classification

> `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT` + `RELATIONSHIP_SPECIFIC_EXPRESSION`

Final thesis:

> **Mai moves from the girl whose reality depends upon Sakuta's sight to the adult partner who helps Sakuta live inside a shared world without demanding that either person lose independent vocation or perception.**

Evidence route: V1 backfill; V4/V6–7 backfill; V9, V12–15 native ledgers.

## 2.3 `かえで` / Kaede

### Initial state

Kaede is the post-amnesia autobiographical continuity who develops within the Azusagawa household after severe bullying and dissociative memory loss.

She is not best described as:

- merely a symptom;
- the “fake sister”;
- a temporary costume for Hanakaede.

Her distinctiveness is established through:

- self-reference;
- speech;
- habits;
- handedness;
- goals;
- relationships;
- fear of being looked through toward Hanakaede.

### Development

- creates ordinary-life goals;
- expands public range;
- reaches school;
- understands she may disappear;
- authors how she wishes to be remembered.

### Discontinuity

Hanakaede's autobiographical return ends Kaede's active continuity in V5.

### Continuing relation

- notebook;
- photographs;
- annual zoo pass;
- Ueno Zoo;
- stories distributed across Sakuta, Mai, Nodoka, and others.

### R1 state

Volume 14–15 realizes an older separately embodied Kaede with:

- four years of life;
- Minegahara attendance;
- animal club;
- friends;
- domestic competence.

She discovers Hanakaede's later diary, fears disappearance, and chooses:

> `帰りたい`

### Final classification

> `AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_CONTINUITY_CHANGE` + `WORLD_STATE_CHANGE` + `CURRENT_PERSON_AGENCY`

Final thesis:

> **Kaede remains a person whether embodied only in the past, remembered through objects, or temporarily realized as an R1 current subject; love does not justify either reducing her to illness or keeping her alive as Sakuta's reward against her own stated direction.**

Evidence route: `AOBUTA_V05_BF001–BF012`; Animal Land; V8; V14–15.

## 2.4 `花楓` / Hanakaede

### Initial/revealed state

Hanakaede returns with the pre-amnesia autobiographical continuity and no ordinary first-person memory of Kaede's two years.

She inherits:

- a body shaped by trauma;
- learned academic content;
- relationships she did not remember forming;
- Kaede's notebook;
- Sakuta's grief;
- a family reorganized around both continuities.

### Central developmental problem

She initially experiences Kaede's love and achievements as debt:

> if Kaede gave her these relationships, she must repay Kaede by completing Kaede's life.

### Genuine development

- rejects Minegahara after being admitted;
- chooses correspondence education;
- learns digital communication;
- asks to know Kaede without becoming Kaede;
- works a bounded part-time job;
- says “not yet” to indefinite employment;
- moves between two homes;
- maintains friendship with Kotomi;
- later chooses university.

### Final state

- university student;
- ordinary family participant;
- not required to reproduce Kaede;
- carries Kaede historically rather than as command.

### Final classification

> `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT` + `INHERITED_HISTORY_WITHOUT_SUBSTITUTION`

Final thesis:

> **Hanakaede's autonomy is proved not by rejecting Kaede, but by choosing what to carry from Kaede and what future to author herself.**

Evidence route: V8 evidence ledger; both bonuses; V9; V13–15.

## 2.5 Makinohara Shoko

### Initial structure

Shoko first exists for Sakuta as an apparently older first love associated with kindness and his chest scars.

The series later reveals:

- current younger Shoko;
- serious heart disease;
- future Shoko states generated from denied futurity;
- donor histories involving Sakuta, Mai, and ultimately Toko.

### Major continuity problem

The “older Shoko” is not simply one fixed adult traveling backward through one timeline. The completed series treats her as a genuinely experienced possible future person whose memories and actions have causal force.

### Genuine development/current life

- learns to say she wants to live;
- relinquishes entitlement to Sakuta's romantic future;
- accepts a rewritten history that may erase the future self who loved him;
- survives through Toko's donated heart;
- develops ordinary school life;
- becomes animal-club president;
- considers medical study and rare-disease support without treating donor debt as compulsory vocation.

### Final classification

> `POSSIBLE_WORLD_PERSON` + `GENUINE_CURRENT_DEVELOPMENT`

Final thesis:

> **Shoko teaches Sakuta that another person's kindness can remain causally alive inside him without requiring that the future person who gave it remain the current world.**

Evidence route: V6–7 backfill; V8–9; V14–15.

## 2.6 Mito Miori

### Initial apparent state

Miori appears socially relaxed, difficult to classify, unusually distant from smartphone life, and resistant to Sakuta's repeated attempt to call them friends.

### Retrospective revelation

Volume 14 reveals:

- lifelong friendship with Toko;
- years of unresolved grief;
- Miori as singer/uploader behind public Toko songs;
- serial reality variants;
- roughly fifty Sakutas;
- inability to establish durable friendship in unstable worlds.

This is primarily:

> `REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_HIDDEN_STATE`

rather than personality suddenly changing.

### Genuine development

- discovers the complete/reversible Toko song;
- cries and directly grieves;
- narrates Toko as biography rather than mystery;
- accepts the exchange diary;
- recognizes Toko has become memory;
- accepts Sakuta as friend;
- buys a smartphone for chosen reachability;
- leaves for other possibility states with a mission rather than fleeing blindly.

### Final state

- physically absent from RF;
- relation with Sakuta preserved through number exchange, departure language, and complete song;
- exact cross-world mechanics unresolved.

### Final classification

> `REVELATION` + `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT` + `CROSS_WORLD_SINGULARITY_HYPOTHESIS`

Final thesis:

> **Miori stops using serial possibility to keep Toko's death provisional and becomes able to carry Toko as memory while permitting a new friendship to become real.**

Evidence route: V13–15 native ledgers.

## 2.7 Kirishima Toko

### Final evidence-separated state

The name refers to three overlapping but distinguishable objects.

#### Human Toko

- real girl from Shizuoka;
- Miori's lifelong friend;
- composer/lyricist;
- Mai fan;
- dies at sixteen on Christmas Eve;
- donor whose heart saves current Shoko.

#### Public Toko

- Toko's name and compositions;
- Miori's voice/performance/uploading;
- audience recognition;
- anonymous Christmas aesthetic.

#### Toko role/attractor

- prestige persona entered by Nene and many others;
- later attached to Mai through mass recognition/R1.

### Final classification

> `REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_STATE` + `DISTRIBUTED_PUBLIC_IDENTITY`

Final thesis:

> **The series refuses to compress author, performer, public persona, donor, and remembered friend into one simple identity bearer.**

Evidence route: V13–15.

## 2.8 Iwamizawa Nene

### Initial state

A once-visible model/Miss Campus winner whose expected Tokyo success fails to confirm the special adult identity she anticipated.

### Crisis

- viral Toko comparison;
- Toko aesthetics and voice become replacement self;
- Nene name/history become inaccessible;
- Takumi forgets;
- social visibility collapses.

### Genuine development

- reclaims failure, embarrassment, ambition, and relationship as Nene biography;
- accepts that “be yourself” is not a self-explanatory command;
- later applies for announcer work;
- becomes a Hokkaido television announcer.

### Final classification

> `RECOGNITION_STATE_CHANGE` + `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT`

Final thesis:

> **Nene answers `何者か` not by occupying borrowed prestige, but by building an adult vocation through ordinary work.**

Evidence route: V13 and V15.

## 4. Final character-ledger conclusion

The completed ensemble rejects the apparent structure:

> one extraordinary boy repeatedly fixes emotionally unstable girls.

The longitudinal state is more complicated.

Earlier title heroines:

- retain independent futures;
- later help Sakuta;
- enter professions;
- disagree with him;
- leave romantic possibilities unresolved or behind;
- form a durable network.

The final character architecture is therefore:

> **a community of particular people whose identities become more stable not by ceasing to need others, but by remaining capable of choice inside relationships that no longer require substitution.**
