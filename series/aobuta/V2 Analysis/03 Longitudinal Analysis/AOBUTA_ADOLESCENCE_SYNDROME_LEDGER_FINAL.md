# AoButa Adolescence Syndrome Ledger — Final

## 1. Scope and governing rules

This ledger records the **literal impossible events** across the completed light-novel series before the specialist synthesis attempts thematic unification.

It preserves five distinctions:

1. **observable event** — what happens in the narrated world;
2. **character explanation** — what Rio, Shoko, Sakuta, Miori, or counterpart Sakuta believes;
3. **strong final model** — what the completed series makes most plausible;
4. **literary/social function** — what human conflict the event externalizes;
5. **residual ambiguity** — what Volume 15 still does not settle.

The ledger does not diagnose characters. It does not treat quantum vocabulary as scientific proof. It does not collapse all cases into one hard-magic law.

## 3. Phenomenological families

### A. Recognition-state phenomena

- Mai invisibility;
- Sakuta nonrecognition;
- returned Ikumi invisibility;
- Nene/Toko invisibility;
- Mai/Toko identity rewrite.

Common feature:

> what society can perceive or identify changes without the body simply ceasing to exist.

### B. Recursive and prospective time phenomena

- Tomoe's loops;
- Shoko future states;
- `#夢見る`;
- Tomoe's long university-era simulation.

Common feature:

> future information is experienced before current history settles, but experience does not make the outcome inevitable.

### C. Multiplication, exchange, and continuity phenomena

- Rio duplication;
- Mai/Nodoka crossed embodiment;
- Kaede/Hanakaede continuity replacement;
- Ikumi world displacement;
- simultaneous R1 Kaede/Hanakaede.

Common feature:

> body, memory, role, and personhood cease aligning one-to-one.

### D. World-state phenomena

- V7 rewritten future;
- V9 adjacent world;
- W1 Ikumi exchange;
- R1 composite rewrite;
- RF stabilization.

Common feature:

> possibilities become causally and morally significant worlds rather than private fantasy.

## 4. Strongest final model and its limits

The completed series' strongest internal model is not one simple equation. It is a layered account:

1. adolescent conflict produces a literal impossible state;
2. recognition and observation often determine who can perceive or remember that state;
3. possibility worlds can exist with locally coherent autobiographies;
4. dreams and Miori make possibilities cross-observable;
5. Sakuta's persistent recognition may help actualize observed possibilities;
6. resolving the ethical conflict changes whether the impossible state must remain current.

The model explains much, but does not settle:

- why each case takes its exact form;
- whether observer language is literal quantum physics;
- why Sakuta is special beyond biography and repeated exposure;
- the smartphone clue;
- every world-exchange rule.

## 5. Final ledger conclusion

Adolescence Syndrome is neither merely metaphor nor a fully systematized magic technology.

Its completed-series function is:

> **to make socially denied, internally contradictory, counterfactual, or relationally unstable realities impossible for the surrounding world to ignore.**

The finale does not defeat that function. It transfers its ethical lesson into adulthood:

> the adult need not perceive the same impossible object in order to recognize the adolescent who reports it.
