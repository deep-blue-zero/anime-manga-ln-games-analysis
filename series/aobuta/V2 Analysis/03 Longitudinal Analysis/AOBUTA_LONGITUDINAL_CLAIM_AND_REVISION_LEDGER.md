# AoButa Longitudinal Claim and Revision Ledger

## 1. Function

This ledger records how major interpretations changed across the sequential reading. It is the principal safeguard against hindsight flattening.

It preserves the difference among:

- what an early volume established;
- what it reasonably invited the reader to suspect;
- what later volumes actually corrected;
- what the completed series makes most plausible;
- and what remains unresolved.

The ledger does **not** silently rewrite the historical `AOBUTA_VXX_DEEP_READING.md` artifacts. It routes later full-series claims back through those local readings and their primary-source evidence.

## 2. Status vocabulary

- `FINAL_CONFIRMED` — the completed series strongly sustains the initial claim.
- `FINAL_REVISED` — the initial claim captured part of the truth but required material reformulation.
- `FINAL_CORRECTED` — later evidence overturned a concrete earlier identification or mechanism.
- `FINAL_REJECTED` — the completed series rejects the claim as a governing interpretation.
- `FINAL_PARTIAL_UNRESOLVED` — part of the claim survives but the mechanism remains open.
- `FINAL_UA` — the series deliberately leaves the issue unresolved.

## 4. Synthesis use rule

A specialist document may rely on the **final status** in this ledger only if it also preserves the route back to:

> local volume artifact → evidence ID or backfill ID → primary-source locator.

When the route ends in a migrated V1–V7 scene still marked `pending_targeted_backfill`, the specialist document should trigger a targeted EPUB reopening before quoting or making a load-bearing exact-language claim.

## 5. Claims that remain especially sensitive

The following should never be flattened into settled fact merely because the main series is complete:

- exact observer physics;
- exact smartphone mechanics;
- the red-randoseru girl's literal identity;
- the subjective fate of every R1-specific person/continuity;
- the exact distribution of R1 memory in RF;
- whether every Syndrome case belongs to one universal causal family;
- Toko's complete private intention beyond what the song and diary support.

These belong in Document 18's residual-metaphysics register and should be repeated there rather than “resolved” here.
