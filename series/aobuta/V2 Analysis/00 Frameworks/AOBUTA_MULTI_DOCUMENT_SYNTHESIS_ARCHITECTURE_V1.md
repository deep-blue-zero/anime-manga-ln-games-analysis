# 『青春ブタ野郎シリーズ』
## Multi-Document Full-Series Synthesis Architecture v1.0
### Longitudinal thematic corpus, primary-home rules, retrospective correction, phenomenon/world ledgers, and delivery standard

# 0. Purpose

This document defines the recommended **multi-document architecture** for a definitive source-grounded synthesis of 鴨志田一's 『青春ブタ野郎シリーズ』 / *Rascal Does Not Dream*.

It is an architectural companion to:

> `AOBUTA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD_V2.md`

The distinction is fundamental:

> **The analytical method determines how the Japanese primary sources are read, classified, verified, and interpreted.**
>
> **The architecture determines where each full-series question receives its deepest treatment, how the completed volume readings are reused without duplication, how later revelations revise earlier readings, and how every synthesis claim remains traceable to primary evidence.**

The project already possesses a substantial sequential evidence base:

- canonical deep readings for Volumes 1–15;
- canonical deep readings for the two late-authored bonus novels *Animal Land* and *Spring Days*;
- original-Japanese source metadata and checksums for much of the corpus;
- evidence classification and locator ledgers for the native-v2 volumes;
- retrospective corrections accumulated through the final volume;
- and a final-volume synthesis that resolves the series' central ethical arc while deliberately preserving residual metaphysical ambiguity.

The final synthesis should therefore **not** be written as one enormous chronological recap.

A multi-document structure is necessary because AoButa operates simultaneously at several analytical scales:

- Sakuta's development from socially injured adolescent to adult listener;
- Mai's movement from invisible title heroine to enduring co-protagonist and equal partner;
- the Sakuta/Mai romance as an evolving ethic of mutuality, privacy, shared reality, and future life;
- Kaede/Hanakaede as the series' most sustained problem of autobiographical continuity, embodiment, inheritance, and family grief;
- Shoko as the hinge connecting time, sacrifice, survival, possible worlds, organ donation, and moral residue;
- the recurring title-heroines as both local protagonists and a cumulative social ensemble;
- Adolescence Syndrome as literal event, embodied experience, interpersonal crisis, social externalization, and repeatedly revised mechanical system;
- recognition, attention, gaze, `空気`, celebrity, rumor, social media, and the collective construction of social reality;
- body, memory, name, relationship, role, and autobiographical history as partially separable components of personhood;
- dreams, time loops, future states, possible worlds, observation, counterfactuals, and reality rewriting;
- grief, continuing bonds, death, donor ethics, irreversibility, and the fear that healing betrays the dead;
- intervention ethics, privacy, paternalism, self-sacrifice, autonomy, and distributed care;
- school, work, family, medicine, public institutions, and ordinary adulthood;
- Japanese first-person narration, forms of address, humor, title language, translation-sensitive terms, and dialogue register;
- place, season, transit, objects, photographs, diaries, rings, phones, scars, and visual paratext;
- late-authored bonus fiction whose **story position** and **publication position** are not the same;
- and a finale that provides strong ethical closure while intentionally leaving parts of the hard metaphysics unresolved.

The architecture should preserve **four different forms of truth**.

## 0.1 Local first-pass truth

What a particular volume establishes at its own endpoint under the original sequential spoiler boundary.

This remains preserved in:

> `AOBUTA_VXX_DEEP_READING.md`

and the two canonical bonus artifacts.

## 0.2 Prospective series truth

What an earlier volume reasonably invites the reader to suspect before later confirmation.

Examples include:

- unresolved Shoko identity;
- recurrence of recognition mechanics;
- the Kirishima Toko problem;
- smartphone non-participation;
- possible-world architecture.

This belongs in prospective-signal and claim-revision ledgers.

## 0.3 Retrospective full-series truth

What Volumes 1–15, taken together, finally allow the project to say.

This is the governing mode of the definitive synthesis.

It may revise earlier interpretations, but it must never silently rewrite what an earlier reader could know.

## 0.4 Residual ambiguity

What remains legitimately unresolved even after Volume 15.

Examples include:

- the exact physical ontology of possible worlds;
- the exact observer mechanism;
- the unfinished smartphone clue;
- Miori's cross-world mechanics;
- the red-randoseru girl's literal ontology;
- the precise distribution of R1 memory after stabilization.

The final corpus must preserve these as open questions rather than treating completion of the series as permission to invent closure.

# I. Governing architectural principles

## 1. Separate documents by governing question

The strongest transferable principle from other synthesis architectures is:

> **Do not divide the corpus merely by character name or plot arc. Divide it by the question the document is responsible for answering.**

A character may therefore appear in several documents for different analytical reasons.

Example:

The Volume-7 Mai/Sakuta material may appear in:

- Sakuta's character document — because it changes his self-sacrifice ethic;
- Mai's document — because it demonstrates her agency and refusal of unilateral martyrdom;
- the romance document — because it establishes reciprocal future-oriented love;
- the Shoko/time document — because it sits inside the fatal-future architecture;
- the ethics document — because sacrifice, consent, and substituted suffering require normative analysis.

What must not happen is five copies of the same scene summary with the same conclusion.

## 2. Every major subject receives one primary analytical home

Other documents may summarize evidence when answering another question.

They should then cross-reference the primary home.

This prevents the full corpus from becoming a collection of overlapping 20,000-word essays that repeat the same arcs.

## 3. Per-volume artifacts remain immutable historical records

The existing sequential deep readings are not rough drafts to be rewritten into final-series knowledge.

They preserve:

- what the volume itself established;
- what remained uncertain;
- what the project inferred at that time;
- what prospective signals existed;
- and how later volumes altered the picture.

The definitive synthesis may supersede them for **current interpretive state**, but never for **historical reading state**.

## 4. Distinguish development from retrospective revelation

AoButa repeatedly creates apparent changes that may instead be:

- newly revealed information;
- altered observer knowledge;
- actual autobiographical discontinuity;
- world-state substitution;
- social misrecognition;
- or genuine psychological development.

Every longitudinal claim should therefore be classifiable as:

- `GENUINE_DEVELOPMENT`
- `REVELATION_OF_PRIOR_STATE`
- `RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION`
- `WORLD_STATE_CHANGE`
- `RECOGNITION_STATE_CHANGE`
- `AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_CONTINUITY_CHANGE`
- `PERFORMANCE_OR_ROLE_CHANGE`
- `RELATIONSHIP_SPECIFIC_EXPRESSION`
- `UNRESOLVED_MIXTURE`

## 5. Preserve literal mechanics and thematic meaning simultaneously

The final synthesis must retain the central AoButa methodological discipline:

> **A phenomenon can have literal diegetic rules and literary/psychological meaning at the same time.**

Never reduce:

> “Mai literally became socially unrecognizable”

to:

> “Mai merely felt ignored.”

But never let the literal impossibility obscure the social argument about:

- attention;
- celebrity;
- visibility;
- recognition;
- public role.

## 6. Character theories are not narrator-certified physics

Rio, Shoko, Sakuta, Miori, and counterpart Sakuta repeatedly construct models of impossible events.

Those models may be strong.

They remain character epistemology unless the narrative establishes more.

The final corpus should preserve the V2 evidence distinction:

- TF textual fact;
- CB character belief;
- SI strong inference;
- TI interpretation;
- UA unresolved ambiguity.

## 7. Bonus publication position and story position must remain distinct

*Animal Land* and *Spring Days* are especially important because they are late-authored canonical interpolations into an earlier chronological period.

The architecture must never silently imply:

> because a bonus later fits an earlier scene, the original earlier volume necessarily anticipated every detail of that later publication.

For each bonus track:

```yaml
story_position:
publication_position:
analytical_reading_position:
retrospective_authority:
```

## 8. Ethical closure can exceed metaphysical closure

Volume 15 gives the series a strong final answer to:

- recognition;
- memory;
- adulthood;
- grief;
- self-authorship;
- listening.

It does not solve every physical detail of Adolescence Syndrome.

The final corpus should not treat that asymmetry as an analytical defect.

One of the series' final achievements is precisely:

> **the ethical problem becomes clearer than the cosmological one.**

# II. Existing evidence layers and required normalization

The final project should be understood as four layers.

# Layer A — Immutable sequential artifacts

Current canonical analytical corpus:

```text
AOBUTA_V01_DEEP_READING.md
AOBUTA_V02_DEEP_READING.md
...
AOBUTA_V15_DEEP_READING.md
AOBUTA_BONUS01_ANIMAL_LAND_DEEP_READING.md
AOBUTA_BONUS02_SPRING_DAYS_DEEP_READING.md
```

These remain the canonical local readings.

## Important asymmetry

Volumes 1–7 were migrated from earlier analyses and do **not** yet possess the same locator completeness as the native-v2 later volumes.

Their historical interpretation should not be rewritten.

But before the definitive synthesis is evidence-locked, load-bearing V1–V7 claims should receive **selective locator backfill** from the Japanese EPUBs.

The correct procedure is:

> **backfill provenance, not interpretation.**

Do not use later knowledge to rewrite the old essay merely because the source has been reopened.

# Layer B — Full-series longitudinal ledgers

These are structured reference artifacts that track:

- character state;
- relationship state;
- phenomenon mechanics;
- world-state topology;
- retrospective correction;
- object/place continuity;
- and claim development.

They bridge the volume analyses and specialist corpus.

# Layer C — Full-series specialist documents

These answer the major literary, thematic, philosophical, linguistic, and mechanical questions.

They are the main reference corpus.

# Layer D — Reader-facing synthesis and administrative package

This contains:

- `AOBUTA_FULL_SERIES_SYNTHESIS.md`;
- README;
- evidence indexes;
- source inventory;
- checksums;
- corpus JSON;
- delivery audit.

# III. Recommended developmental phase model

Unlike *Classroom of the Elite*, AoButa does not need year-boundary specialist corpora.

Its major internal transitions are better understood as **developmental phases**.

These should be used inside chronology and claim ledgers, not necessarily as separate mini-corpora.

## Phase I — Recognition and unstable selfhood
### Volumes 1–5

Dominant problems:

- visibility versus recognition;
- `空気`;
- social construction;
- contradictory selves;
- comparison;
- body versus autobiographical identity;
- Kaede/Hanakaede discontinuity;
- Sakuta's early intervention style.

This phase establishes the basic grammar.

## Phase II — Futurity, sacrifice, world revision, and family
### Volumes 6–9

Dominant problems:

- Shoko;
- survival;
- fatal futures;
- donor possibility;
- reciprocal sacrifice;
- adjacent worlds;
- family rupture;
- mother/Sakuta recognition;
- reconciliation without restoration;
- self-authored inheritance.

This phase expands Syndrome from local social crises into world-history and family continuity.

## Interpolated ordinary-life bridge
### *Animal Land* and *Spring Days*

Story-position function:

- remembrance after differentiation;
- Hanakaede's public-gaze adaptation;
- work;
- school participation;
- adult competencies;
- ordinary future-building.

Publication-status function:

> late-authored canonical reflection upon an earlier chronological period.

## Phase III — University, social permeability, and alternate selfhood
### Volumes 10–12

Dominant problems:

- social attunement;
- public versus private self;
- ideal alternate biography;
- rescue identity;
- love and opacity;
- university adulthood;
- larger ensemble agency.

## Phase IV — Collective recognition, Kirishima Toko, and reality selection
### Volumes 13–15

Dominant problems:

- prestige identity;
- mass social recognition;
- Miori/Toko authorship;
- grief;
- observer status;
- reality rewriting;
- counterfactual goods;
- adulthood;
- memory;
- institutionalized listening.

The final synthesis should use these phases to explain progression without forcing every volume into one linear escalation model.

# XIII. Specific quality-control rules for AoButa

## 1. Do not turn Adolescence Syndrome into diagnosis

Even when a case has obvious psychological resonance, the literal phenomenon remains textually real unless the novel says otherwise.

## 2. Do not call every world state a timeline

Use the final world-state ledger.

Distinguish:

- simulated future;
- possibility world;
- rewritten reality;
- counterpart state;
- current stabilized continuity.

## 3. Do not collapse Kaede into Hanakaede

Use stable naming throughout.

Their distinction is foundational to the series' personhood argument.

## 4. Do not let final knowledge colonize V1–V14

When saying:

> “Volume 1 ultimately anticipates...”

distinguish:

- retrospective thematic rhyme;
- demonstrable foreshadowing;
- later reuse;
- coincidence/structural recurrence.

## 5. Do not reduce Mai to Sakuta's girlfriend

Her career, celebrity, sisterhood, self-authorship, and role as source of the final adulthood metaphor require independent treatment.

## 6. Do not reduce other title heroines to harem members

Ask:

- what independent future does the character acquire?
- what does she later contribute to others?
- can the relationship survive without romantic consummation?

## 7. Preserve Sakuta's ethically mixed interventions

Do not rewrite all successful interventions as justified.

The series itself gives enough evidence for:

- courage;
- intrusiveness;
- paternalism;
- self-sacrifice;
- growth toward restraint.

## 8. Keep organ donation literal

Toko's heart in Shoko does not make Shoko Toko.

Bodily continuity does not equal personal continuity.

## 9. Preserve Miori/Toko authorship distinction

Final attribution:

- Toko writes/composes;
- Miori sings/performs/uploads;
- the public identity is composite.

## 10. Treat the final observer model as strongest model, not omniscient law

Counterpart Sakuta's explanation is highly important.

It retains character-theory status.

## 11. Do not complete the smartphone clue from imagination

Volume 15 does not supply a fully formalized technological law.

The final synthesis may analyze the clue.

It must not invent the missing sentence.

## 12. Preserve the red-randoseru girl as unresolved ontology

Strong symbolic function is not literal identification.

## 13. Do not call R1 morally meaningless

Its persons and preferences matter.

The final ethical case depends upon taking them seriously.

## 14. Do not treat adulthood as disenchantment

The ending is:

> memory reclassification + continued trust,

not:

> “the supernatural was childish nonsense.”

# XIV. Recommended planning scale

The existing 17 sequential analytical artifacts already exceed roughly two hundred thousand words.

A final specialist corpus should therefore avoid reproducing them.

Reasonable planning ranges:

- **18 specialist prose/reference documents** (`01`–`18`);
- **3 major evidence/index documents** (`19`–`21`);
- **7 or more structured ledgers**;
- **one continuous full-series synthesis**;
- **one README/corpus map**.

Suggested prose scale:

- core character/theme documents: 8,000–15,000 words each;
- mechanical/identity/world documents: 10,000–18,000 words;
- narrower bonus/visual documents: 5,000–10,000 words;
- matrices/ledgers: as long as evidence requires;
- continuous synthesis: approximately 35,000–60,000 words.

A plausible complete specialist corpus would therefore fall around:

> **140,000–210,000 words**

in addition to the existing volume analyses.

Depth should come from:

- longitudinal comparison;
- cross-volume argument;
- Japanese verification;
- counterreadings;
- synthesis.

Not repeated plot recap.

2. Which final-series claims are genuinely retrospective rather than explicit in Volume 1?

5. How did Sakuta's intervention ethics change?
6. Why is Mai a co-protagonist rather than only the principal love interest?
7. What distinguishes Kaede from Hanakaede?

9. What does Shoko's donor history establish about body and identity?
10. What does R1 establish about the moral standing of possible-world persons?
11. Why is R1 not preserved even though it contains genuine goods?

# XVIII. Final architectural rationale

AoButa should not be synthesized as fifteen isolated “girl-of-the-volume” stories.

Nor should it be reduced to one Sakuta/Mai romance with supernatural side cases.

The architecture should make visible the way the series gradually binds its local crises into one longitudinal argument.

Mai establishes that:

> being visible is not the same as being recognized.

Tomoe demonstrates that social reality can become recursive when fear of the group governs behavior.

Rio demonstrates that contradiction can belong to one person without requiring one side to be declared false.

Mai and Nodoka demonstrate that body, role, envy, and selfhood can be exchanged without becoming interchangeable.

Kaede and Hanakaede force the series to confront the hardest form of autobiographical discontinuity.

Shoko expands those problems into time, death, sacrifice, and possible lives.

Volumes 8 and 9 ask whether inherited wishes and repaired families require restoration of the past.

The bonuses show that ordinary work, places, photographs, and repeated outings can carry identity forward without supernatural escalation.

The university arc asks what happens when social awareness, ideal selves, romance, and rescue identities become more complicated than the schoolyard `空気` of Volume 1.

Nene and Kirishima Toko scale recognition into public identity.

Miori scales possibility into reality.

Volume 15 then refuses both of the simplistic endpoints available to Sakuta.

He will not:

> keep every impossible possibility actual forever.

He will not:

> declare the experiences fake so he can become an adult.

He turns them into memory.

That movement only makes sense if the architecture keeps:

- mechanics;
- identity;
- recognition;
- grief;
- ethics;
- ordinary life;
- language;
- and relationship

analytically separable long enough to show how they finally converge.

The governing full-series question should therefore be:

> **What does it mean to recognize another person when body, memory, name, role, social perception, possible worlds, and even one's own senses may disagree—and can adulthood preserve the ethical truth of adolescent experience without requiring the impossible to remain permanently present?**

The final answer should not be predetermined.

But the architecture should make it possible to test the answer rigorously from Volume 1's invisible Bunny Girl to the education trainee who says:

> **I cannot see what you see. I believe you. Tell me about it.**
