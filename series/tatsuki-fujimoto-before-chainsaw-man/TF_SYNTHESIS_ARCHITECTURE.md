## 5. Substantive study acceptance

Each story reading must explain observed actions, knowledge, relationships, language and form through full causal prose. A collection synthesis compares those arguments, not merely their topic labels. It should say when apparently similar features serve different functions or fail to support a proposed unifying claim.

An independent character/relationship/form specialist is earned by a distinct question that needs more sustained evidence than the full reading or integrated subset synthesis can contain. If no extra specialists are needed, the role-gap review names the substantial accepted sections carrying their responsibilities. A short story can support nuanced literary interpretation without supporting a general character simulator.

A monograph must preserve source-local development, self-report/behavior distinctions, ordinary/crisis contrast, relationship conditioning, counterevidence and uncertainty. A comparison must preserve each story's identity, narrative assumptions and noncomparable elements. Shared authorship is a selection principle, not proof of a common fictional world or the creator's personal beliefs.

No universal word ceiling applies. Check inferential steps, explanatory mechanisms, representative and disconfirming evidence, named subjects, temporal distinctions and scope limits. A tidy matrix or shortened summary is not a replacement for full reading/synthesis prose. Transferred versions must be compared for lost reasoning before acceptance.

## 6. Conflict handling and separate gates

First identify collection, story and witness before calling evidence contradictory. Different stories may have different premises. Within a work inspect source layer, attribution, event/disclosure time and frame before testing changed context, mistaken belief, retrospective explanation, edition variance or genuine inconsistency. Keep supported alternatives OPEN.

Dependency order: source/story binding and authorized run → independent readings with synchronized ledgers → declared-subset completion → longitudinal/claim reconciliation → role-gap/specialist readiness → substantive studies or justified integrated sections → comparative convergence → 22–26 synthesis readiness → validation → declared-scope freeze.

## 7. Source-informed reconstruction pathway

Reconstruction is derived use, separate from literary monographs and collection comparison. Each future subject/state requires its own evidence map, operational model and validation record, created only if evidence supports them. A figure in another story or later work is not the same subject without a source-grounded identity decision. Shared motif cannot pool behavioral evidence.

The evidence map records story/person/state, exact witness and horizon, situation/stakes, interlocutor/audience, DS speech/DB shown behavior/NI interior presentation/RB report/OS observer-role/INF inference, locator, full reading/claim route, Japanese status, counterevidence and limitation. Ordinary-life sample, negative-evidence opportunity and relationship-specific contrast are required coverage checks; absence remains a gap.

The model binds evidence-base commit, version, continuity/frame, temporal boundary and excluded material. Distinguish disposition, developmental state and temporary condition; use change causes DISPOSITION, KNOWLEDGE, RELATIONSHIP, CONTEXT, ROLE, RESOURCE, REVEALED_NOT_NEW and UNRESOLVED. Rules specify trigger + perceived stakes + state → response family, with inhibitors, exceptions, counterexamples, alternatives and falsifiers. OBSERVED is not CANDIDATE_POLICY, and promotion needs actual validation.

Appraisal precedes decision objective, then speech act, relationship filter, Japanese register and wording. Preserve thought/speech distinctions and source-attested address, self-reference, endings and pragmatic contrasts. Evaluate decision fidelity separately from fluent surface wording. No acoustic voice is inferred.

Confidence is HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/OPEN. D0 is attested, D1 a close variant, D2 novel within the story/state, D3 cross-scenario. D3 declares chronology, relationship and setting assumptions and may require abstention. A different story's circumstances are not automatically a valid same-person test.

Genuine held-out validation requires evidence withheld from the model author before exposure/model fitting, a frozen prediction record and independent review. One-shots may leave no clean test after a full reading. Then label RETROSPECTIVE_CONSISTENCY_TEST and retain prospective uncertainty. The missing17–21 volume is not a default held-out character test, and its acquisition/admission would need separate authorization.

Frozen predictions record model/version, rule IDs, state, assumptions, response family, confidence/distance, test scope and falsifier. Independent adjudication records actual opportunity, exact evidence and SUPPORTED/PARTLY_SUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/NO_DIAGNOSTIC_OPPORTUNITY/CONFOUNDED/UNRESOLVED with reasoning. Use plausible decoys, context/interlocutor perturbations, abstention controls and generic-personality baselines where useful. Fidelity outcomes are PASS/PASS_WITH_BOUND/NEEDS_TARGETED_SOURCE_REVIEW/INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, with qualitative bounds and counts/exclusions, not unsupported accuracy percentages.

Generated dialogue/scenes/predictions remain synthetic test output, never source observations, quotations or literary evidence. No simulation is authorized now. The source can test a model; the model cannot fill missing story evidence. Coverage makes all twelve evaluator dimensions and six scenario types assessable without self-assigned grades. Designated discovery/evaluation agents own their global records.
