# Partial-collection analytical method

## The available volume is not the whole retrospective collection

The project identity is Tatsuki Fujimoto Before Chainsaw Man / 藤本タツキ短編集. The available Japanese source is **22–26 only**; 17–21 is absent. The collection's English title, age labels and author reputation must not predetermine the interpretation of its unread stories or turn a partial acquisition into an author-career narrative.

Each mapped story in 22–26 is the primary reading unit. Interpret it independently before comparing stories. Collection arrangement, publication chronology and later retrospective framing are different questions. Story order in the EPUB does not establish the order in which the author created or published the works, and observed differences do not automatically prove artistic maturation.

[The source map](TF_SOURCE_AND_COLLECTION_MAP.md) owns identity/gaps; [the architecture](TF_SYNTHESIS_ARCHITECTURE.md) owns synthesis responsibilities. This method adapts the [generic manga reference](../../governance/analytical-methods/GENERIC_MANGA_ANALYTICAL_METHOD.md). No narrative source has been inspected or admitted.

## Binding sources and resetting boundaries

Before future reading require an owner-defined story/run scope, satisfied initiation gate with OPEN state, immutable witness binding and a demonstrated STILL_IMAGE/Japanese lettering route. One file may contain several separately authorized works; file access is not blanket admission.

Map actual titles, order, image occurrences and story spans. Preserve covers/spreads and classify notes, title pages, jokes, creator comments, extras and previews by role and spoiler horizon. Historical package checks are not legibility or completeness assessment. Do not create synthetic individual EPUBs and call them primary witnesses.

Assign a work-qualified cast and timeline for each story. Similar names, designs or motifs do not prove a shared world or continuity with Chainsaw Man, Look Back or another work. Keep outside familiarity and marketing descriptions quarantined through exposure disclosure. Within each story freeze scene-level entering knowledge; the ending may inform labeled retrospective interpretation without becoming earlier character knowledge.

Segment scenes by actual changes in goal, configuration, viewpoint, time, disclosure or form, not by extraction batch. Record a hypothetical or framed narrative only if source signals support it; no such structure is assumed now.

## Full causal interpretation

Each reading must explain the story in connected prose: entering conditions, named actors' perceived stakes/options, decisions or constraints, resulting changes and the evidence for causal links. Distinguish purposeful action, misunderstanding, coincidence, social rules and a person's retrospective account. A short work still needs full reasoning; a compressed synopsis does not preserve how the story creates its effect.

Use personal names once established within the source horizon, preserving anonymity and unresolved aliases where required. Never replace identified people routinely with archetypes, gender/role labels or parallels to later characters. Keep actor, observer, thinker, speaker, recipient and affected person clear.

Label evidence OBSERVATION, SELF_REPORT, NARRATOR_PRESENTATION, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, EVALUATION or UNRESOLVED with HIGH/MODERATE/LOW/UNRESOLVED confidence. Speech, thought, narration and reports of another person's motive are distinct evidence routes. Separate disposition candidates from developmental state and situational response; a dramatic crisis does not certify a general personality.

Track relationships directionally and locally. A's orientation toward B does not prove B's reciprocity. Preserve knowledge and power asymmetries, expressed wishes, conduct, response and rival explanations. Observe ordinary life, play and routine when actually present; absent contexts are gaps, and negative evidence requires a real opportunity.

For contentious acts when encountered, separate depiction, framing, intention, consent, consequence and evaluation. Give only the detail needed with locators. Narrative sympathy is not endorsement; later affection does not establish prior consent. Do not force a reading to match an author's public reputation.

## Japanese expression and graphic form

Inspect source-language speech/thought, asides, captions, diegetic text, effects and ruby. Attribute speaker/thinker with confidence. Register claims need brief attested Japanese forms, labeled glosses, context/interlocutor and comparison with baseline/exception. Private thought is not public speech and translation is not Japanese voice authority.

Read silent and OCR-resistant pages fully. For panel/page/spread composition, gaze, grouping, proximity, withheld reaction, gutters, rendering register, recurrence and colour if present, explain observed feature → proposed effect → competing explanation. Do not infer symbolism from prominence or continuity from similarity. A comparative formal claim later needs independent evidence in each story, including differences that weaken it.

Verify actual facing-page layout for page-turn claims. An image index is not a printed page label; panel endpoints do not show continuous motion; drawn effects do not establish acoustic performance. Other media remain excluded.

## Locators, evidence routes and revision

Use `TF-JP-C2226-W01 / <verified story key> / <mapped scene> / i0001 / p<visible label or NONE> / panel 1 / balloon 1`. Whole-witness image occurrences are one-based in actual reading order; keep any story-local numbering as an explicit crosswalk. Assign panel/balloon order from the inspected page and preserve ambiguity. Crops cite parent image and region.

L1 exact panel/balloon anchors support disputed wording, visual inference and tests; L2 is scene-level; L3 story-only navigation; L4 pattern requires representative L1 support for each story and declared search scope. No invented locator may fill a blank.

The evidence plane retains image-review states REVIEWED_MATERIAL/NO_MATERIAL_ADDITION/DUPLICATE/EXCLUDED/UNRESOLVED_READABILITY, raw pages and derivatives. Git stores safe source aliases, actual coverage, exclusions and concise quotations. OCR/search assists retrieval, not inspection. Private IDs, paths, acquisition receipts and bulk text stay out of Git.

Material claims retain full source-grounded warrant, strongest counterreading and possible falsifier. Changes use PRESERVE/STRENGTHEN/REVISE/DOWNGRADE/REJECT/OPEN, with prior formulations and prospective freezes kept. New acquisition cannot silently broaden an earlier claim's scope.

## Complete reading and comparative acceptance

Create a future `readings/TF_C2226_<STORYKEY>_DEEP_READING.md` with `artifact_type: deep_reading` only in an authorized run. It includes source/admission/coverage, exposure and entering state, governing/rival theses, movement-level causal argument, person/directed states, ordinary/Japanese evidence, form, counterreadings, claim/forecast changes, ledger-update manifest and exit uncertainty.

Update source/story coverage, local evidence and claims in the same transaction. A prose-only result or a table-only result is incomplete. No-change is a reasoned reading-manifest statement, not a filler data row. No universal word ceiling limits warranted reasoning.

Before acceptance perform handoff §15.6's bounded name/reference review over opening, substantive middle, conclusion and material relationships. Preserve purposeful roles/concealment but correct unjustified genericization, ambiguous pronouns or misattribution. Inspect consequential evidence and full argumentative depth; syntax/length alone cannot certify quality.

Only after accepted local readings may 22–26 synthesis compare formal or thematic patterns. It must name the admitted stories, preserve noncomparability/counterexamples and avoid claims about unseen 17–21, all Fujimoto work or the creator's private psychology. A later broader project needs new evidence, authorization and an explicit revised scope.

### Current-unit story synopsis

*Prospective amendment, 2026-11-13, under initiation policy v1.11 §5.3.*

Each completed reading of a mapped story in the supplied 22–26 volume includes a clearly labeled **Current-unit story synopsis** near its front, after brief witness/coverage and entering context, before governing theses and extended analysis. The unit is that authorized story, with its own cast, chronology and ending. Collection arrangement or the 22–26 label does not turn separate stories into one plot, establish creation order or supply a biographical explanation.

Write connected, accessible prose explaining the opening situation, named participants and stakes, consequential actions and decisions, causal turns, disclosures and ending state. Preserve concrete physical and scene mechanics established by the inspected pages. Distinguish inherited story context from newly presented events, shown or narrated events from testimony and belief, and established outcomes from conjecture or unresolved possibilities. Preserve source-required anonymity and framed/temporal uncertainty where the actual pages warrant it. Material event claims carry retrievable source locators or stable observation references; the synopsis remains prose rather than a replacement evidence table. Scale detail to the story without a fixed word count.

This unit-end, spoiler-bearing account may disclose the current story's ending only after all applicable entering and internal prospective captures are frozen. Shield it and the completed reading from earlier-horizon analysts. Another story, absent 17–21, Chainsaw Man, Look Back or outside familiarity cannot fill a missing event or settle ambiguity. The synopsis supplements the complete local causal, linguistic, visual, character, relationship and counterreading argument and synchronized source/state/claim updates; collection comparison retains its separate acceptance prerequisites. Require both the synopsis and all existing duties. No frozen reading is rewritten, and the CLOSED analysis lock remains governing.
