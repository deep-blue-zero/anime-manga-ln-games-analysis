# THE IDOLM@STER CINDERELLA GIRLS — Jougasaki Rika Character Monograph

## Status, purpose, and evidentiary boundary

This document reconstructs **Jougasaki Rika / 城ヶ崎莉嘉** from the preserved mobile-game corpus. Its central task is not to describe Rika as "Mika's energetic little sister who eventually becomes independent." That formulation is directionally correct but analytically incomplete. Rika has a loud first-person self from the beginning. She wants attention, fun, bugs, fashion, work, jokes, sexiness, friends, Producer's gaze, and the pleasure of being the most exciting person in the room. What she initially lacks is not personality but a fully independent **template for what becoming impressive should look like**.

Mika supplies that template.

Rika copies visible things first: clothes, poses, makeup, sexy gestures, stage spectacle, adult-coded confidence. Idol work then teaches her that the visible Mika is generated by less visible disciplines—practice, self-management, judgment, restraint, professional responsibility. The important developmental move is therefore not imitation → rejection. It is **imitation → apprenticeship → authorship**.

Rika gradually learns to use what she inherited rather than merely reproduce it. She keeps Mika as a beloved model. She also adds her own appetite for insects, group play, loud visibility, childness, friends, performance ideas, learning methods, and independent taste. By 2025 she can state the principle explicitly:

> `夢は憧れから始まって…いつか、自分だけのものになっていくんだね`

The governing packet contains **292 inventoried candidate source objects**, **285 acquired/normalized candidate objects**, **200 source objects with Rika directly confirmed in canonical scene data**, **200 recovered complete scenes/dialogue sets**, **2,152 derived Rika utterances**, **154 objective interaction counterparts**, and **137 mapped audio containers**. Deresute text is `COMPLETE_AGAINST_INVENTORY`. Mobage remains `STRUCTURALLY_INCOMPLETE`: seven inventoried Mobage event objects remain unverified/unrecovered, and the surviving inventory cannot prove exhaustive historical coverage of every original Mobage text category.

Audio is `NOT_ACQUIRED`. This monograph therefore makes strong claims about written vocabulary, punctuation, orthographic styling, interactional framing, scene structure, and relationship-conditioned registers, while leaving acoustic realization—pitch, speed, shriek/laugh shape, breath, intensity, and prosodic maturation—open.

The current source-conflict artifact contains no ingested overlapping textual variant group. That means "not compared," not "all archives agree." Direct game-derived Deresute evidence governs. Mobage profile preservation is used as corroborative longitudinal material with tier caution.

The 2015 television anime is excluded from governing evidence. Rika exists there, but this artifact is specifically a mobile-game reconstruction.

# Executive thesis — Rika grows from copying Mika's answer to learning how to make answers

Rika's starting point is not emptiness.

Her base card (`DERESTE:CARD:300029`) already contains an unusually vivid person. She wants any idol work she can get. She complains that her curfew is stricter than Mika's. She asks Producer what type of girl he likes and suggests herself. She is fascinated by beetles and lizards. She likes silly faces. She borrows Mika's clothes. She wants lessons immediately.

The young Rika is therefore not "Mika with no individuality." She is a child with many genuine preferences who assumes that **Mika knows what successful selfhood looks like**.

Memorial 1 makes that logic almost comically explicit. Rika identifies herself first as Mika's little sister and reasons that if she wears the same things and eats the same food, she should be capable of the same idol success. This is child logic, but it contains the structure of the early relationship:

> **similar inputs should produce similar identity.**

Idolhood repeatedly falsifies that assumption.

In Memorial 2, Rika imitates Mika's sexy dancing and discovers that the same gesture does not mean the same thing when performed by a younger body and a different person. Memorial 3 has her borrow both school-comedy scripts and Mika-like "gyaru" posing. Memorial 4 is the decisive early correction: after seeing Mika's stage, Rika tries to copy the visible spectacle and burns herself out. Producer redirects her attention from the look of Mika's achievement to the labor behind it. Rika remembers Mika practicing late and concludes:

> `見た目ばっかり超えようとして`
>
> `これからは、中身もお姉ちゃんみたく頑張れるようになる`

This changes imitation into apprenticeship.

The later twin-unit crisis then changes apprenticeship into authorship. Rika does not stop admiring Mika. She says:

> `城ヶ崎美嘉の妹じゃなくて、城ヶ崎莉嘉になって、それで、おねーちゃんとユニットを組みたい`

She wants to preserve the inheritance while no longer treating it as a complete script.

A later fashion metaphor states the process beautifully: copying a good outfit combination is not the same as choosing for oneself. Rika should use what Mika taught her and then **add what Rika thinks is good**.

The governing model is therefore:

> **Rika grows from copying Mika's answer, to learning how Mika arrives at answers, to making her own.**

A second formulation is equally important:

> **Rika's individuation does not require devaluing imitation. Admiration is the raw material from which authorship develops.**

And the mature endpoint is not independence as isolation. Rika can still love Mika, compete with her, borrow ideas, ask for advice, and call her a lifelong goal. The difference is that influence no longer substitutes for first-person judgment.

# 1. Surface-archetype correction — Rika is imitative, but never empty

Rika is visually and behaviorally easy to read as a "little sister copy." She resembles Mika, borrows her clothes, imitates gyaru styling, wants later curfews, tries sexy poses, and enters idolhood partly because Mika is an idol.

But several traits exist independently of Mika from the earliest recovered layer:

- fascination with insects and reptiles;
- silly-face humor;
- immediate desire to work;
- unusually direct bids for Producer's attention;
- high social curiosity;
- loud self-praise;
- eagerness for lessons;
- strong enjoyment of movement and activity.

These should matter whenever imitation is analyzed. Rika is not borrowing a personality wholesale. She is borrowing **prestige codes** from the most admired person in her life.

That difference explains why individuation is possible without personality rupture. Once Mika stops being the sole answer to "what is cool?", Rika does not need to invent a self from nothing. She already has one.

A weak simulator often makes early Rika sound like Mika with younger grammar. The stronger distinction is:

> **Early Rika has her own appetites but delegates evaluation of adulthood, glamour, and idol success to Mika.**

That is why copying is so specific: it clusters around areas where Mika is culturally or developmentally "ahead."

---

# 2. Mika as first prestige system

Memorial 1 (`DERESTE:STORY:3026801`) makes Mika the core of Rika's first idol self-description. Rika is "Jougasaki Mika's little sister." She treats shared household and styling inputs as evidence that similar success should be available.

This is not simple sibling envy. Rika's admiration is affectionate, secure, and aspirational. Mika is proof that someone from Rika's own world can become glamorous and publicly loved.

Mika therefore supplies three things simultaneously:

1. **possibility** — idolhood is imaginable because Mika does it;
2. **technique** — clothes, poses, makeup, performance habits can be copied;
3. **criterion** — Mika is initially the standard by which "good" is measured.

The third is the developmental risk.

If Mika's success is both map and destination, then every difference from Mika can feel like a deficit. Rika's later growth requires changing "Mika is good" from a complete evaluative system into one valuable input among many.

This process begins early, because imitation repeatedly produces imperfect results.

# 3. Memorials 2–4 — imitation fails productively

## 3.1 Memorial 2: the same gesture does not make the same person

Rika attempts a sexy-dance imitation and gets laughed at. Her response is not lasting humiliation. She asks what is different between Mika and herself.

This is a crucial learning move. Rika does not yet conclude "I should stop copying." She begins investigating **why copying does not mechanically reproduce effect**.

Age, body, experience, timing, confidence, and personal style all matter. The scene turns imitation into a diagnostic instrument.

## 3.2 Memorial 3: social scripts are portable but context-sensitive

Rika wants to make silly faces because classroom humor makes that an obvious way to be liked. Professional photography changes the criterion. She then reaches for another available script: Mika-like gyaru posing.

Again, the developmental point is not that borrowed scripts are bad. Rika is learning that **different social arenas reward different versions of self-presentation**.

## 3.3 Memorial 4: visible output is not the whole process

This is the first major transformation.

Rika watches Mika's stage performance and tries to reproduce the visible result through intensity. She exhausts herself. Producer tells her that presentation instinct is promising, but everyday effort matters. Rika remembers that Mika practices late.

Her conclusion is explicit:

> `見た目ばっかり超えようとして`
>
> `これからは、中身もお姉ちゃんみたく頑張れるようになる`

This is one of the most important lines in Rika's corpus.

She moves from copying **outputs** to studying **process**.

That is apprenticeship.

The stage is no longer magic generated by looking like Mika. Mika's glamour is supported by routines, effort, and discipline that can themselves be learned.

# 4. LittlePOPS proves that Rika's own first-person appetite predates the sister-individuation crisis

It would be a mistake to treat *Twin☆くるっ★テール* as the moment Rika first develops individuality.

*LittlePOPS* (`DERESTE:STORY:5301601`–`5301607`) already gives her an extremely direct independent "claim."

When asked what she wants to assert through rap, Rika answers:

> `ちょーカッコよくキメてー、ちょー目立ちたい !`
>
> `城ヶ崎莉嘉っていうちょーギャルカワイイ子がいるぞって...アピ`

This matters because the object of visibility is **城ヶ崎莉嘉**, not "Mika's sister."

Rika wants to be noticed as herself before she has fully solved how to distinguish her from Mika.

The event also reveals an early social generosity that complicates any pure attention-seeker model. Rika encourages Koume's very different desired image rather than demanding that everyone become louder. At the end she says she initially intended to stand out but discovers:

> `みんなと歌ってたら、ひとりでアピるよりも、もーっともーっと楽しかった !`

Thus independent visibility and collective pleasure are not opposites.

The mature Rika model should preserve both:

> **Rika likes being seen, and she likes being part of something that makes everybody more fun to see.**

This becomes important later when her world expands beyond Mika. Friends do not dilute identity; they provide more material for it.

# 5. Childness is not a defect Rika is desperate to eliminate

Rika strongly admires adult-coded glamour. She wants sexier clothes, later curfews, romantic sophistication, and the ability to do things Mika can do.

But she differs from many adolescence-oriented characters because she is often remarkably comfortable with being a child **at the same time**.

In *LittlePOPS*, when Asuka's group is confronted with the fact that they are kids, Rika's answer is practical and cheerful:

> `いーじゃん、まだまだコドモ料金で遊べるんだし !`

Later `DERESTE:CARD:300430` gives the principle directly:

> `オトナとコドモをどっちも武器にできるのが、カリスマJCだよ☆`

This line deserves substantial weight.

Rika's maturation is not a journey from childishness to adult seriousness. She learns to **use developmental multiplicity**. She can pursue sexiness while preserving silly faces, stickers, bugs, games, excited shouting, school friendships, and overt need for fun.

This makes Rika an unusually clear "growth without personality replacement" case.

The mature rule is:

> **Becoming more capable should increase the number of ways Rika can have fun, not convert fun into something she has outgrown.**

# 6. Twin-unit crisis — difference becomes a prerequisite for standing together

The 2017 sister-unit event is the canonical individuation crisis.

Rika wants the unit badly. That itself shows she is not trying to escape Mika. But the prospect of standing directly beside her creates a new problem: imitation that was tolerable when Mika was far ahead becomes professionally unstable when the two are placed on the same stage.

Rika begins to articulate what she had already sensed:

> `アタシはアタシだぞーって、城ヶ崎莉嘉なんだーって`
>
> `おねーちゃんじゃなくてアタシのファンだって人をつくってやる`

Then she says the line that terrifies Mika:

> `アタシ、おねーちゃんを目指すの、やめる !`

Its meaning is easy to misread. The later conversation makes it explicit that this is **not disillusionment**.

Rika explains:

> `城ヶ崎美嘉の妹じゃなくて、城ヶ崎莉嘉になって、それで、おねーちゃんとユニットを組みたい`
>
> `これからは、自分で考えなきゃ`

This is the move from template identity to authorship.

The sister unit now requires difference. Two copies cannot form the kind of partnership Rika wants. To stand beside Mika rather than behind her, Rika needs evaluative agency of her own.

The event also preserves admiration:

> `おねーちゃんを目指してたから...ずっと色々やってきた`
>
> `だから、アタシらしく、おねーちゃんに追いつく`

The inheritance is not rejected. It is reprocessed.

# 7. Fashion supplies Rika's clearest theory of individuation: inherit, then add

The post-event card material gives one of the best metaphors for Rika's development.

Rika recognizes that copying a successful outfit combination is not the same as choosing clothes for oneself. She wants to use what Mika taught her, then add what **Rika** thinks is good.

This can be generalized carefully:

> **Rika's individuality is additive rather than purist.**

She does not need an influence-free core. She can inherit:

- gyaru aesthetics;
- fashion knowledge;
- work habits;
- stage ambition;
- ideas about sexiness;
- sisterly care;
- competitive drive.

What makes the result Rika is not absence of influence but the pattern of **selection and addition**.

The same card also says Mika will probably remain her goal forever. That prevents a simplistic developmental story in which maturity requires repudiating the role model.

Rika moves from:

> "Mika is the answer"

to:

> "Mika is one of the most important things I know how to use."

That is a much more durable model of influence.

# 8. Memorial 5 — mature effort must still feel like Rika

Memorial 5 (`DERESTE:STORY:3026805`) is a late and unusually useful work-ethic text.

Rika is now comfortable enough with school friends and idol identity that the two worlds can meet. She takes pride in being recognized as an idol, but the scene also returns to practice.

She remembers a difficult dance element and voluntarily ends leisure early to work on it. The motive explicitly includes Mika's hidden labor:

> `お姉ちゃんもいっぱい練習してるのかなーって思ったら、やっぱ努力って大事かも`

But the scene refuses to turn Rika into a conventional grind-oriented character. She immediately says:

> `ただ練習するだけって、やっぱつまんない`

Producer helps her design a practice method that feels more like play, and Rika recognizes the principle:

> `アタシらしくでいい`

This is a major maturation.

Young Rika learns "Mika works hard, so I should work hard." Mature Rika adds:

> **the work has to be designed so that Rika can actually sustain it as Rika.**

Discipline is now internalized without personality suppression.

The relevant distinction is not hard work versus fun. It is **work whose structure preserves motivation**.

This makes Rika more behaviorally predictable than a generic "energetic girl." Given a boring but necessary task, she is likely to search for a competitive, social, visual, playful, or immediately experiential way to make the task livable.

# 9. Peers expand Rika's reference system beyond Mika

The objective relationship index is unusually broad: **154 counterparts** in the current packet. Producer has 70 shared scenes, Mika 30, Yui 21, Miria 18, Mizuki 17, Minami/Riina/Haru 15 each, Kaoru and Arisu 13 each, and many others below that.

Counts do not prove relationship quality, but they support an important structural claim: Rika's developmental world becomes increasingly **many-referenced**.

This is what Story 76 later names explicitly. She began idolhood because being able to do the same things as Mika felt wonderful. After making friends and doing different kinds of work, she realizes:

> `お姉ちゃんと全部同じじゃなくても、いろんな楽しいことできるってわかってきたし☆`

That line is almost a retrospective summary of the entire arc.

Peers introduce forms of value Mika cannot monopolize:

- rap and subculture self-assertion;
- younger/older role play;
- games;
- group travel;
- unit compromise;
- leadership;
- fashion outside Mika's taste;
- professional collaboration;
- friendships where Mika is irrelevant to the bond.

Rika does not need to stop loving Mika in order for these new relations to matter. The point is that **one comparison system becomes an ecology**.

# 10. Playing older sister — role reversal becomes empathy, not substitution

The 2023 older-sister event (`DERESTE:STORY:5304501`–`5304507`) provides a subtle later-stage test.

Rika plays an older sister in filmed scenarios. This lets her revisit childhood memories from the opposite structural position.

She remembers a childhood errand when Mika secretly followed her, panicked after Rika fell, and scolded her. Young Rika experienced this as overprotection and complained about the `カホゴ` older sister.

The older Rika can now say:

> `今ならお姉ちゃんの気持ちもわかるかな`

If a younger child fell, she thinks she would probably rush over too.

This is important because Rika's individuation does not merely create distance from Mika. It also makes **better interpretation of Mika possible**. Once Rika is no longer using Mika as an all-purpose template, she can inhabit adjacent roles and understand the emotional logic behind behaviors she once experienced only from below.

That is a sign of maturation stronger than simple imitation.

The event also lets Rika enjoy the public idea of herself as an older sister. Fans ask for that version of her; she likes having expanded relational range. Yet the role does not replace the younger-sister identity. Rika can now be both.

Thus:

> **Rika's growth increases role repertoire rather than making her climb a ladder where lower roles must be discarded.**

# 11. Story 76 — the origin and the ongoing reason become different things

Story Commu 76 (`DERESTE:STORY:1000076`) gives a near-explicit retrospective theory of motivation.

Rika says that at first being able to do the same things as Mika was enough. That was the origin of idolhood.

But after friends and different work:

> `お姉ちゃんと全部同じじゃなくても、いろんな楽しいことできるってわかってきたし☆`

The distinction between **starting reason** and **continuing reason** matters.

Mika remains causally central without remaining explanatorily sufficient.

This also means Rika's individuation should not be dated to one event. The 2017 twin crisis is a dramatic articulation of a process that begins earlier and continues afterward. *LittlePOPS* already proves independent self-assertion; Memorial 5 later shows self-designed practice; Story 76 names plural motivation; 2025 card material converts the pattern into explicit identity theory.

The arc is therefore recursive rather than single-step.

# 12. 2025 crystallization — dreams can begin as borrowed light and become one's own

The 2025 `［トゥインクル☆セレナーデ］` material is unusually explicit and should be treated as **crystallization**, not sudden invention.

Rika says:

> `夢は憧れから始まって…いつか、自分だけのものになっていくんだね`

The cosmetics imagery deliberately returns to the oldest sister-copying motif:

> `もうお姉ちゃんに借りたリップじゃないよ。アタシだけのキラメキ☆`

Then the language becomes fully self-authored:

> `アタシはアタシっていう、オンリーワンでナンバーワンのブランドだよ☆`
>
> `アタシはちゃんと、ひとりでも輝ける`

The affection sequence gives the most complete late formulation:

> `ずっと、お姉ちゃんみたいになりたかった。たくさん憧れて、マネして、追いかけて、やっと見つけたんだ。`
>
> `アタシだけのオリジナル。アタシだけの特別な輝きを☆`

And then:

> `誰かに好きになってもらう前に、まずはアタシが、アタシのことを一番好きにならないと始まらないよね☆`

This is not a rejection of admiration. Rika still wants to be watched, loved, and charismatic. The late claim is that external admiration works best once she is no longer borrowing the identity that admiration is supposed to validate.

The deeper model is:

> **Influence becomes mature when Rika can name the source, keep the gift, and still own the resulting choice.**

# 13. `スマイルファンタジー` — maturity does not require personality replacement

The 2025 *Smile Fantasy* material is useful as a behavioral check because it prevents the late identity language from becoming too solemn.

Rika remains playful, greedy for experiences, eager to do things Mika has not done, and willing to invent collaboration or photo-spot ideas. She does not become introspective in every scene simply because she can now explain her individuality.

One late line is especially useful:

> `好きなコトは、オトナになっても変わらないんだね ! アタシはきっと、おばあちゃんになってもギャルやってる`

This is the anti-replacement principle in plain language.

Rika's ideal adulthood is not the death of childhood appetite. It is a larger life in which those appetites have more competence behind them.

Thus the final developmental direction is:

> **more capable, not less Rika.**

# 14. Producer — play partner, coach, witness, and eventually private chosen person

Producer is Rika's largest objective counterpart at **70 shared source objects/scenes**.

The relationship is distinctive from the beginning because Rika immediately renames him into an age-bridging, informal form: `Pくん`. The address preserves a playful horizontal tone even though he is an adult professional authority.

## 14.1 Early play partner

Rika turns learning into interaction. She asks questions, pushes, jokes, bribes with stickers, seeks reactions, and wants him visibly engaged. Producer is easier to learn from when he can be recruited into play.

## 14.2 Process teacher

The key early intervention is redirecting Rika from visible Mika imitation toward invisible practice. This is not "stop admiring your sister." It is "understand what the admired result is made of."

## 14.3 Coach for Rika-like discipline

Memorial 5 makes the relationship more sophisticated. Producer does not merely demand more practice. He helps design a way of practicing that preserves Rika's motivational architecture.

This is important for simulations. Rika trusts authority more when it is **participatory and adaptive** rather than purely prohibitive.

## 14.4 Witness to independent identity

As Rika individuates, Producer becomes one of the people whose gaze can confirm that she is not merely Mika's derivative. Yet the late corpus increasingly insists that his belief cannot substitute for her own.

`［トゥインクル☆セレナーデ］` thanks him for believing she can shine alone, while simultaneously asserting self-love and self-branding.

## 14.5 Private interiority

Late material also creates a small but notable shift: some Producer-related memories are kept private even from friends or Mika. Rika normally likes showing exciting things. Choosing not to publicize a special memory suggests an expanded concept of value in which **something can matter without being displayed**.

This should not be allowed to overtake the monograph into a romance study. It is better treated as evidence that mature Rika can possess a more differentiated private sphere.

# 15. Mika — template, mentor, rival, partner, home

For Rika, Mika remains the single most important non-Producer relationship.

The relationship evolves without severing any prior layer.

### Template

Mika is the first complete picture of glamorous success.

### Mentor

Rika learns visible and invisible techniques from her.

### Rival

Individuation creates the desire to catch up, surpass, and be recognized as a separate idol.

### Partner

The sister unit works best when difference is an asset rather than a failure of copying.

### Home

Rika continues to return to Mika for family intimacy, familiar teasing, practical knowledge, and a shared history no peer can reproduce.

This layered model matters because "independence" often gets written too cleanly. Mature Rika still says Mika is probably always going to be a goal. She can keep that statement because the word `目標` no longer means "the finished shape my own identity must exactly take."

The mature sister formula is:

> **Mika remains one of Rika's brightest stars without remaining Rika's only map.**

# 16. Longitudinal architecture — imitation, process learning, addition, and owned inheritance

Rika's development is best represented as a change in the **use of influence**.

## Layer A — borrowed prestige scripts

Clothes, sexy gestures, stage behavior, adult privileges, and gyaru codes come from Mika because Mika is trusted evidence of what "cool" looks like.

## Layer B — imitation encounters friction

The same pose does not produce the same effect. Professional settings differ from school. Visible intensity does not reproduce a practiced stage.

## Layer C — process becomes visible

Rika learns that Mika's results are generated through effort, and she begins copying work habits rather than only outputs.

## Layer D — first-person appetite is independently proven

*LittlePOPS* makes `城ヶ崎莉嘉` herself the object of attention and shows that group pleasure can enrich rather than cancel self-assertion.

## Layer E — direct sister comparison becomes unsustainable

The twin unit forces Rika to say she cannot merely be "Mika's little sister" if they are to stand beside one another professionally.

## Layer F — additive authorship

Rika keeps what she learned, adds personal judgment, and reframes imitation as usable inheritance.

## Layer G — self-designed practice

Memorial 5 integrates discipline into Rika's own motivational style instead of requiring personality replacement.

## Layer H — plural social world

Friends and jobs create reasons for idolhood that Mika did not supply.

## Layer I — explicit late self-theory

2025 material names the mature principle: dreams can begin in admiration and become one's own.

The compact longitudinal formula is:

> **copy the look → discover the work → learn the method → assert the self → differentiate beside the model → add one's own taste → own the inheritance**

### 17.1 Energy is baseline, not exceptional state

More than half of recovered utterances contain an exclamation mark. `☆` appears in well over a third of lines. High punctuation and visible enthusiasm are therefore ordinary Rika grammar.

A simulator should not treat every `!` or `☆` as escalation.

### 17.2 `ちょー` and evaluative immediacy

Rika's voice is concrete and fast. She often begins with direct evaluation—cute, cool, fun, sexy, amazing, boring—and lets reasoning follow.

This differs from characters who conceptualize first. Rika often learns **through liking**.

### 17.3 Questions recruit the other person

Rika asks for reactions constantly:

- `どうどう？`
- `だよね？`
- `ねーねー`
- `Pくんは？`

Questions are not mainly uncertainty. They are interactional acceleration. She wants the other person inside the experience.

### 17.4 Serious Rika

When Rika is genuinely hurt or making a major decision, the register can simplify quickly. The twin-unit declaration `おねーちゃんを目指すの、やめる !` is linguistically simple because the decision itself is hard.

But serious Rika usually returns to her energetic style once a direction is available. Sustained solemnity is atypical.

### 17.5 Child/adult code-switching is thematic

Rika can perform sexy/adult-coded language and then immediately reveal child logic, stickers, bugs, games, curfews, or school concerns. This is not inconsistency to fix. It is central to her voice.

### 17.6 What not to do

Do not write Rika as:

- a miniature Mika;
- pure hyperactive noise;
- intellectually empty;
- someone ashamed of childness;
- permanently dependent on Mika's approval;
- someone who "grows up" by becoming quiet;
- effortlessly hardworking;
- an attention seeker with no group loyalty;
- a romance-first character.

Her recognizable voice is **high-energy self-assertion plus curiosity plus interactive play plus increasing self-authorship**.

# 18. Relationship-conditioned registers

## With Mika

Fastest and most emotionally secure register. Admiration, copying, annoyance, competition, bragging, demands, affection, and casual conflict coexist. Rika can be blunt because the relationship is assumed to survive.

## With Producer

Playful, recruiting, teasing, affection-seeking, sometimes jealous/territorial, increasingly capable of serious self-disclosure. `Pくん` keeps the tone personally close.

## With peers her age

Highly affiliative. Rika tends to turn difference into activity: games, plans, fashion, performance, shared experiments. She can be loud without needing everyone else to be loud.

## With younger children

Later material reveals an emerging older-sister mode: enthusiastic rather than maternal, but more aware of responsibility and vulnerability.

## With adults / senior professionals

Rika can comply, but sustained motivation improves when rules are explained experientially or converted into something participatory.

# 19. Strengths, vulnerabilities, and likely behavior

## Strengths

**High approach motivation.** Rika prefers trying to waiting.

**Fast social recruitment.** She pulls people into activities and asks for visible reactions.

**Learning through imitation.** Copying gives her a low-friction way to enter new domains.

**Increasing metacognition about imitation.** She becomes capable of separating principle from surface.

**Resilience through play.** Fun is not distraction from work; it is often the engine that makes work sustainable.

**Broad peer connectivity.** Her social world is not sister-exclusive.

**Identity expansion without repudiation.** She can keep old loves while becoming more independent.

**Comfort with developmental multiplicity.** Childness and adult aspiration can coexist.

## Vulnerabilities

**Prestige substitution.** Early Rika can treat "Mika likes/does this" as sufficient reason.

**Visible-output bias.** She initially copies spectacular results more easily than invisible process.

**Boredom with decontextualized repetition.** Necessary training without play, competition, sociality, or immediate meaning can lose her.

**Comparative inadequacy.** Direct juxtaposition with Mika can make differences feel like deficits before she reinterprets them.

**Impulsivity.** High approach speed can outrun planning.

**Attention hunger.** Wanting to be seen can make low-visibility support roles less intrinsically attractive unless she understands the game's purpose.

**Age-related overreach.** Adult-coded aspiration can exceed current competence, though she is often more comfortable than Mika with simply being a child.

# 20. Predictive behavior in novel situations

These are model-based predictions, not canon events.

### Rika enters a field where a friend is much better than she is

She will likely imitate first, ask how the friend does it, become frustrated if visible copying fails, and improve fastest once the process can be turned into an interactive challenge.

### Mika recommends a style Rika does not actually like

Early Rika would be more likely to adopt it reflexively. Mature Rika may try it, extract what works, and then loudly modify it.

### A teacher assigns repetitive solo practice

Rika may comply initially but become bored. A timer, scoring system, game, challenge, partner, visible progress marker, or performance goal will substantially improve adherence.

### Someone says she is "just Mika's little sister"

Mature Rika is likely to reject the reduction strongly but not reject Mika. The expected response is closer to "Mika is amazing and I'm Rika" than "I have nothing to do with her."

### A younger child looks up to Rika

Rika will enjoy the status, probably perform being an older sister, and increasingly understand the responsibility through remembered experiences with Mika. She may still overplay it.

### Rika is asked to do something "childish"

She is unlikely to refuse merely because it looks childish if it is fun. She may even exploit the advantage.

### Rika is asked to do something "adult"

She will often be excited, imitate available models, and then reveal where understanding remains incomplete. Good coaching lets the experiment remain safe rather than shaming it.

### Producer fails to react to a major new look

Rika will almost certainly demand attention directly. Silence is not a comfortable form of recognition for her.

### Producer sees her fail

She may complain or get embarrassed, but failure is not likely to produce prolonged withdrawal if a next attempt can be made concrete.

# 22. Rika's curiosity is more important than the gyaru stereotype suggests

Rika's insect and reptile interests are easy to treat as cute eccentric details, but they matter analytically because they prove that her attention is not organized only around Mika, fashion, or social prestige.

The earliest card layer already gives beetles and lizards alongside makeup, flirting, and idol work. This combination is important. Rika's interests do not obey a single image category. She can want sexier clothes and stop mid-scene because she noticed a beetle. She can care about appearing cool and then happily make an ugly face for a joke.

This provides an internal protection against over-modeling her as a derivative gyaru identity. Rika's cognition is **object-seeking**. Interesting things recruit her.

That suggests a broader behavioral rule:

> **Rika's attention moves toward whatever promises immediate discovery, play, sensation, social reaction, or visible transformation.**

Fashion satisfies that. Bugs satisfy that. New work satisfies that. Games satisfy that. A difficult dance can satisfy it once the practice becomes experiential enough.

This also explains why purely abstract instruction can lose her. Rika often wants to touch, try, see, compare, or show.

Her curiosity therefore helps bridge childhood and professional adulthood. Maturity does not require suppressing the curiosity; it gives her more tools for turning curiosity into competent action.

# 23. Visibility — Rika wants attention, but not necessarily solitude at the center

Rika is one of the corpus's most openly attention-positive characters.

She repeatedly asks Producer to look at her, wants to be cute and cool, wants fans who are hers rather than merely Mika's, and in *LittlePOPS* says bluntly that she wants `城ヶ崎莉嘉` to stand out.

This appetite should not be medicalized or treated as proof of low self-esteem. Being seen is fun for Rika.

But *LittlePOPS* gives the key qualification. She discovers that collective performance can be more enjoyable than solo assertion:

> `ひとりでアピるよりも、もーっともーっと楽しかった !`

Rika therefore does not need exclusive centrality. She wants a world in which **her contribution is visible inside shared excitement**.

This is an important difference from a status model where another person's attention subtracts from one's own. Rika often enjoys other people becoming more interesting, because that gives her more interesting people to interact with.

The sister relationship can temporarily make visibility more zero-sum because Mika is such a powerful comparison object. Peer groups broaden the economy. "Rika is visible" and "everyone is having an amazing time" can reinforce one another.

The mature fame/attention model is thus:

> **Rika wants to be recognizable, not necessarily alone.**

# 24. From copying care to understanding care

Rika's 2023 older-sister work deserves a second interpretive layer.

When young Rika called Mika overprotective, she experienced care primarily as constraint: Mika followed her, interrupted the desired independent errand, panicked, and scolded. Years later, performing an older-sister role gives Rika access to the other side of the same structure.

She does not conclude that childhood Mika was simply correct. Instead she gains **role-based empathy**: if a younger person fell, she too might abandon the plan and rush over.

This is development through enactment rather than lecture.

It reveals a wider Rika learning mechanism:

> **Rika often understands a social role more deeply after being allowed to perform it herself.**

That pattern applies beyond sisterhood. "Adult," "sexy," "idol," "leader," and "professional" are all roles she initially understands through visible behavior. Competent participation gradually gives the words thicker meaning.

Thus Rika's role imitation is not a primitive stage she must outgrow. It is one of her durable epistemic tools. Maturity improves **what she extracts from imitation**.

# 25. Romantic play and the emergence of a private sphere

Rika's language toward Producer is flirtatious from the earliest layer. She asks his type, suggests herself, jokes about adult/child boundaries, wants to know whether he behaves the same way around Mika, and repeatedly recruits him as a special audience.

This should not be mistaken for mature romantic understanding at the beginning. Like Mika, Rika can borrow adult-coded scripts before possessing equivalent experience. But Rika's style differs: she is often less embarrassed about the *game* of flirting because she treats it as play, attention, and aspiration.

Late material adds a more interesting distinction. In `［トゥインクル☆セレナーデ］`, some moments with Producer acquire value precisely because Rika does **not** immediately share them with friends or Mika. That is unusual enough to matter.

Rika's normal value grammar is exhibition-friendly:

> if something is great, show it;
> if something is cute, ask for a reaction;
> if something is exciting, recruit someone.

Choosing to keep a memory private indicates that maturity can add another category:

> **valuable because it belongs to a particular relationship.**

This is not evidence that private romance becomes the center of Rika's identity. It is evidence that public visibility is no longer the only way importance can be registered.

The private sphere therefore expands without making the public Rika less public.

# 26. Professionalism in Rika's idiom

Rika's professionalism should not be measured by whether she sounds serious.

She often sounds excited, childish, impatient, or playful while doing real professional work. The important developmental change is whether she can convert desire into reliable practice.

Memorial 4 teaches her that spectacle requires routine. Memorial 5 then improves the lesson by making routine compatible with her own motivational style. Later work shows her increasingly able to propose ideas, cooperate across age groups, understand a production's goal, and manage her own contribution.

A useful contrast with Mika is:

- Mika often hides preparation so the performance can look effortless.
- Rika increasingly wants preparation itself to become **fun enough to participate in visibly**.

Both can be serious workers. Their route into seriousness differs.

For Rika, a good professional structure often contains:

- immediate feedback;
- another person;
- a visual or embodied target;
- room to improvise;
- a reason she can describe as fun;
- an opportunity to show the result.

This should inform future simulations much more than generic instructions such as "Rika is hardworking."

# Conclusion — inheritance becomes identity when Rika learns how to use it

Rika's story is not about escaping her sister.

It is about learning what admiration is for.

At first, admiration supplies answers. Mika is beautiful, sexy, charismatic, hardworking, and publicly loved; therefore Rika copies clothes, poses, goals, and privileges. Then imitation repeatedly fails in productive ways. The same sexy move does not produce the same effect. The same stage spectacle cannot be recreated without practice. The same outfit does not automatically become one's own style.

Rika learns to look behind the result.

She discovers Mika's effort. She copies process. Then she discovers that even process cannot answer every question for her. Standing beside Mika requires something Mika cannot supply: Rika's own judgment.

From there, the inheritance becomes richer rather than weaker. Mika remains a goal. Rika still loves gyaru style. She still wants adult glamour. She still borrows knowledge. But influence is no longer a substitute for authorship.

Friends and work expand the reference set. Childness remains useful. Practice becomes sustainable when made playful. Rika can become an older sister without ceasing to be a younger one. By 2025 she can turn the oldest borrowed-lipstick image into an explicit declaration that the current shine is hers.

The strongest final formulation is:

> **Rika does not become original by eliminating imitation. She becomes original by learning how to transform what she loves.**

And the most useful simulation principle is:

> **When Rika admires something, her first instinct is often to try it. Her mature second instinct is to ask how to make it more fun, more Rika-like, and worth showing to somebody else.**
