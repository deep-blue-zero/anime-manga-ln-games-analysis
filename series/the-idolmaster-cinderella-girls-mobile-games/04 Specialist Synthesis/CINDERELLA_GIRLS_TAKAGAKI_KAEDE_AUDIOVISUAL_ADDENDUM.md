# THE IDOLM@STER CINDERELLA GIRLS — Takagaki Kaede Audiovisual Addendum
## Voice, expression, visual persona, and longitudinal identity validation

## 0. Purpose and authority

This document is a **supplemental audiovisual validation artifact** for `CINDERELLA_GIRLS_TAKAGAKI_KAEDE_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md` and its canonical adversarial audit, `CINDERELLA_GIRLS_TAKAGAKI_KAEDE_MONOGRAPH_AUDIT.md`.

It does **not** supersede either document. The V1 monograph remains the current integrated character reconstruction; the canonical audit remains the governing critique of that V1. This addendum asks a different question:

> **What becomes clearer when Kaede is no longer treated only as written dialogue, but as a voiced and visually directed character whose sprite expressions, card illustrations, scene transitions, laughter, role presentation, and audiovisual continuity can be compared against the existing textual model?**

The answer is that the monograph's governing interpretation survives. No core thesis requires rejection. The audiovisual material does, however, justify several meaningful additions and targeted revisions before any V1.1/V2 rewrite.

The most important additions are:

1. **`［等身大の距離で］` should become a major pre-*Pretty Liar* identity node.** It gives Kaede an explicit intermediate theory of authenticity: idol work taught her to expose more of her interior life, but she still believed that performing a self larger than her ordinary scale was not truly hers. *Pretty Liar* later revises that doctrine rather than simply introducing self-authorship from nowhere.
2. **Kaede values perceptual proximity.** Small venues, visible faces, Producer's reactions, and human-scale interaction matter because they let performer and audience remain mutually legible. This helps explain why she can value large ambition without allowing scale to become the sole measure of professional worth.
3. **Sensory metaphor is not only decorative diction; it can be a cognitive process.** `［夜風の誘い］` shows Kaede using an immediately felt night wind to organize autobiographical transitions in her life. The environment becomes the structure through which she discovers the thought.
4. **The visual and voiced presentation strengthens the plural-self model.** Glamorous Kaede, ordinary Kaede, comic Kaede, drunken Kaede, professional Kaede, lyrical Kaede, and aspirational Kaede remain recognizably continuous in face, posture, vocal softness, and interactional tempo. The franchise rarely asks the viewer to identify one register as the uniquely authentic woman beneath the others.
5. **Serious Kaede becomes clearer before she becomes harsher.** In the supplied *Pretty Liar* material, the major change is semantic ownership and willingness to state a difficult proposition. Her presentation does not require an abrupt conversion into an aggressive register.
6. **`ふふっ` and dajare need a broader functional model.** The short pun clips and wider scenes support self-amusement, affiliative warmth, teasing, social softening, and sometimes flirtation. Not every laugh or pun should be reverse-engineered into strategic relationship management.

The current result is therefore **PRESERVE WITH AUDIOVISUAL STRENGTHENING AND TARGETED REVISION**.

# II. Governing result: the core monograph survives

The V1 monograph's central proposition remains strong:

> **Kaede's problem is not deciding whether the mysterious songstress or the soft-spoken pun-loving woman is the real Kaede. Her development is increasingly becoming the author of the relationship among these forms.**

The audiovisual material makes this thesis more convincing because the distinction among her registers is not merely lexical.

The franchise repeatedly places the **same recognizably mature visual and vocal subject** into different modes:

- elegant public idol;
- hesitant ordinary woman;
- woman who enjoys a terrible sound association;
- intoxicated adult whose composure genuinely becomes messy;
- mentor speaking to a younger idol;
- professional discussing image and criticism;
- performer inhabiting goddess-like visual rhetoric;
- woman who wants to be seen at ordinary human scale;
- woman who finally chooses to create an aspirational Kaede rather than merely expose an ordinary one.

The supplied visual material is especially important because Deresute's finite sprite system tends to **preserve facial identity while varying expression state**, rather than redesigning Kaede when she changes register. That limitation becomes analytically useful. It demonstrates how little formal discontinuity is required for the game to communicate very different Kaede states.

The joke does not reveal a different face beneath the songstress. The serious line does not summon a separate hard-faced authority persona. The intoxicated card does not cease to be visually glamorous simply because Kaede becomes silly and physically unreliable. The goddess role does not require the ordinary woman to disappear.

The audiovisual evidence therefore strengthens the following formulation:

> **Kaede's plurality is additive and selectable, not a closet of mutually exclusive masks.**

# III. `［等身大の距離で］`: the missing intermediate authenticity doctrine

This is the largest substantive addition produced by the audiovisual review.

V1 correctly identifies an early opposition between the externally projected mysterious songstress and the ordinary woman Kaede wants people to recognize. It also correctly identifies *Pretty Liar* as the major crystallization of mature self-authorship. What V1 underweights is that `［等身大の距離で］` gives Kaede a **coherent intermediate doctrine** between those positions.

## 1. Modeling as professionally carrying somebody else's foreground

Kaede explicitly says that while she worked as a model, the clothes and accessories were the protagonists. She was the professional who wore what was asked of her and completed the assignment.

That does not mean modeling was fake or artistically worthless. It means Kaede retrospectively distinguishes a form of professionalism based heavily on **correct embodiment of an assigned object/image** from idol work, where interior presence itself becomes part of the commodity and relation.

The line matters because it gives unusually direct support to one of the monograph's strongest early distinctions:

> **Kaede did not begin as an incompetent person. She began as a person who could competently inhabit externally specified forms before she had much practice making interior desire publicly legible.**

## 2. Idolhood teaches permission to expose interiority

She then states that becoming an idol taught her she could show more of her own interior because doing so lets more people enjoy and respond to her.

This is a major developmental correction to any simplistic model in which public performance necessarily moves Kaede farther from herself. Idolhood is initially liberating **because the public role can contain more Kaede than modeling did**.

The crucial point is that Kaede does not yet conclude that any freely chosen aspirational construction is authentic. Instead, she moves toward a more conservative solution:

> **the ordinary, unoverstated, life-sized Kaede should be sufficient.**

## 3. `等身大` as a necessary but incomplete solution

The card's language is explicit. Kaede worries that performing a presence larger than herself for the sake of pleasing people is not truly like her. She chooses not to overdress herself symbolically or stretch toward an image that feels bigger than her current scale.

This should be read historically rather than treated as the franchise's final definition of Kaede.

For someone whose earlier work repeatedly involved being shaped by somebody else's requested image, `等身大` is a form of **reclamation**. Kaede needs to establish that ordinary scale is legitimate before she can later choose extraordinary scale without experiencing it as confiscation.

This creates a much better longitudinal sequence:

> **externally carried form → permission to show interiority → life-sized authenticity → realization that life-sized authenticity can itself become restrictive → self-authored aspiration.**

That makes *Pretty Liar* more demanding, not less.

When Kaede later says:

> `本当の私、誰の幻想でもない、ありのままの私を見てほしい。`

and then revises herself toward:

> `私は、自分が望む高垣楓を、つくりたい。`

she is not merely rejecting other people's expectations. She is correcting **her own earlier theory that ordinary unadorned scale has privileged access to truth**.

The V1.1/V2 monograph should therefore treat `等身大` as one of Kaede's most important intermediate self-theories.

## 4. Ordinary scale and aspiration remain available simultaneously

The same material also prevents a clean phase replacement. Kaede's later embrace of self-authored aspiration does not invalidate her love of human-scale presentation.

Even after she can choose elevated forms, the desire to be ordinary, relaxed, visible, and mutually legible remains genuine. The mature solution is not:

> ordinary Kaede was immature; goddess Kaede is mature.

It is:

> **ordinary scale ceases to be the only scale allowed to count as authentic.**

# IV. Perceptual proximity: a new relationship and audience concept

`［等身大の距離で］` also reveals a useful concept that V1 contains implicitly but does not name strongly enough: **perceptual proximity**.

Kaede says that a small venue is valuable because she can see people's faces clearly. She connects stage experience with being watched by people who matter, and she treats Producer's visible reaction as emotionally important because the happiness generated by her singing returns to her through the faces of the people receiving it.

This suggests a deeper principle than “Kaede likes intimate venues.”

> **Kaede values situations where the other person remains perceptible as a responding subject rather than dissolving into abstract audience scale.**

That principle helps unify several domains without over-totalizing them.

## 1. Fans

For Kaede, a fan is not only a quantity in a venue. The meaningful audience is repeatedly capable of becoming:

- a face;
- a remembered encounter;
- somebody whose reaction can be read;
- somebody whose support persists across time;
- a partner in the construction of idol Kaede.

This does not mean large audiences are bad. Kaede is not anti-scale. The point is that scale is most meaningful when it does not eliminate the sense that somebody is actually receiving the song.

## 2. Producer

Producer occupies the privileged extreme of this structure. Kaede repeatedly asks to see his reaction, involves him in ordinary off-time, and treats his gaze as unusually safe because he can recognize both aspiration and ordinary scale without demanding one as the exclusive truth.

The `等身大の距離` formulation is therefore not merely romantic phrasing. It describes a **preferred epistemic relationship**: close enough that the other person's response remains readable, but not so controlling that their interpretation replaces Kaede's own authorship.

## 3. Professional value

This concept also explains why a small stage can retain professional value even for a large star. Human scale is not an apology for insufficient ambition. It can supply forms of feedback and historical continuity unavailable in a purely scalar metric.

The 2015 anime independently dramatizes something very similar, but because the anime remains outside this artifact's governing source boundary, that convergence should be treated separately rather than folded into the game claim as proof.

# V. *Pretty Liar* 3–4: seriousness, revision, and permitted correction

The *Pretty Liar* videos do not overturn the existing interpretation. They make its **behavioral texture** clearer.

## 1. Serious Kaede does not require a hard visual conversion

The supplied scenes move Kaede through professional discussion, domestic exposure, embarrassment, self-description, conflict, reflection, and eventual conceptual revision. Yet the VN direction generally keeps her inside a restrained expression vocabulary.

Her appearance can move among:

- neutral attention;
- slight concern;
- closed-eye warmth or embarrassment;
- composed seriousness;
- moments of more exposed uncertainty;

without creating an entirely new “angry Kaede” or “true Kaede” visual identity.

This strengthens the audit's correction to V1's original serious-register language.

The right rule is not:

> when Kaede becomes serious, all ornament disappears and her whole presentation hardens.

It is closer to:

> **when misunderstanding would defeat her purpose, Kaede increasingly reduces semantic ambiguity; vocal softness, politeness, aesthetic phrasing, and visual composure can remain.**

For simulation, the distinction matters. A generated Kaede should not need to become forceful in volume or socially aggressive merely because she has stopped yielding on the substance.

## 2. Kanade's role is epistemically intimate

The audiovisual framing reinforces Kanade's unusual position in Kaede's relationship architecture.

Kanade does not merely reassure Kaede or decode her. She is allowed to challenge the adequacy of Kaede's own self-explanation.

That is a very high level of relational permission.

Kaede is older, more established, and professionally formidable. Yet the relationship is allowed to reverse the usual authority direction. The younger Kanade can effectively say:

> **the way you are describing yourself is incomplete.**

Kaede does not punish the challenge, reduce it to youthful insolence, or retreat permanently into seniority. She is wounded, thinks, returns, and allows the critique to transform her proposition.

This suggests a more precise relationship rule:

> **For Kaede, exceptional trust can include granting another person temporary epistemic authority without granting them ownership of her identity.**

Kanade can expose a contradiction. Kaede still has to choose what to do with it.

## 3. *Pretty Liar* remains crystallization, not origin

The audiovisual material also supports the canonical audit's chronology correction.

Kaede is already capable of:

- choosing work;
- enjoying transformation;
- making spontaneous jokes;
- forming strong preferences;
- accepting and rejecting professional proposals;
- wanting to show more of herself;
- experimenting with image.

*Pretty Liar* does not inject agency into a previously passive woman. Its special importance is **conceptual**: Kaede acquires a more adequate theory for capacities she has already been exercising unevenly.

The line `私は、自分が望む高垣楓を、つくりたい` is therefore best understood as an act of **self-interpretation catching up to behavior**.

# VI. Story Commu 4: associative play and social perception can coexist

Story Commu 4 is one of the highest-yield early audiovisual samples because it puts several Kaede functions next to one another.

She joins Kawashima Mizuki in adult-peer banter, immediately catches the `アナ` sound association, makes the pun, participates in teasing, notices Rin's troubled state, offers to listen, discusses performance motivation, and then returns to light wordplay.

The sequence sharpens several claims.

## 1. Punning does not imply failure to read the room

One poor generative model of Kaede would treat “pun mode” as a state in which she becomes obliviously goofy until the joke ends.

Story Commu 4 argues against that.

Kaede's lexical-associative play and her social perception appear **concurrently**. She can enjoy `アナ、だけに` and, shortly afterward, notice that Rin is carrying something difficult.

The correct rule is:

> **A pun does not mean Kaede has stopped attending to the other person.**

Her mind can be acoustically playful and interpersonally observant at the same time.

## 2. The pun can begin in self-amusement

The short pun clips supplied earlier in the conversation, including brief `ふふっ` realizations, further support the canonical audit's warning not to make every pun strategic.

Kaede often appears to discover a satisfying sound relation, experience a small pulse of amusement, release it into the conversation, and continue. The listener's reaction can become part of the social function, but it does not need to be the cause of the joke.

This should revise V1's somewhat elegant claim that the “badness” of the pun is centrally useful because it makes Kaede safe to answer.

A better hierarchy is:

> **phonological pleasure first; interpersonal effect second when the scene makes it relevant.**

The effects can include:

- approachability;
- teasing;
- tension release;
- warmth;
- self-amusement;
- mild flirtation;
- deliberate puncturing of grandeur;
- occasionally awkwardness.

## 3. Mentorship by permission receives stronger behavioral support

Kaede does not solve Rin's problem for her. She admits that she herself has never fully reasoned out why she stands onstage. She offers experiential evidence: stage performance makes her nervous, then happy, then makes her want to improve; if it were not enjoyable, she probably would not continue.

This is a strong example of Kaede's mature-social style:

> **she can use her own incompleteness to give someone else permission not to possess a complete answer yet.**

This is different from classic senior wisdom. Kaede's authority can consist of making uncertainty survivable rather than eliminating it.

# VII. `［夜風の誘い］`: sensory metaphor as cognition, not only style

The monograph already identifies Kaede's lyrical/aesthetic register. The audiovisual source makes a stronger cognitive claim warranted.

The scene gives her an actual night wind. Kaede notices it, then begins narrating changes in her life through successive winds:

- the first wind associated with becoming a model and encountering an unfamiliar sophisticated urban world;
- the next wind associated with becoming an idol and feeling a tailwind;
- the present wind as another moment of movement and possibility.

The key is the order.

Kaede does not appear to begin with a fully abstract proposition—“my career has had several transitions”—and then decorate it with wind imagery. The **sensory event itself prompts and structures the autobiographical thought**.

That supports a stronger generative rule:

> **Kaede often thinks by allowing an immediately perceived sensory feature to become a conceptual bridge.**

Her metaphor is therefore frequently a method of cognition.

Useful source domains include:

- wind → movement, transition, direction;
- light → visibility, hope, stage presence;
- flowers → temporality, beauty, maturation;
- night → intimacy, loosened social expectation;
- drink/taste → shared adult time, bodily pleasure, lowered inhibition;
- distance/space → relational legibility;
- music/voice → expression beyond ordinary explanation.

The model should not mechanically insert these motifs. The important principle is **scene-grounding**: Kaede usually works from something materially present or affectively immediate.

## 1. Alcohol remains both intimacy and cost

The same material reinforces the canonical audit's alcohol correction.

Kaede explicitly notes that she has not drunk this heavily in a long time. She becomes highly talkative, physically unsteady, silly, forgetful, and dependent enough that the scene cannot be sanitized into “elegant adult drinking.”

At the same time, alcohol remains sincerely pleasurable and relational. It lets her celebrate, talk, walk, dream, joke, and spend time with trusted people.

The mature model should preserve both sides:

> **alcohol is a culturally and personally meaningful pleasure environment that can lower inhibition and deepen intimacy, but Kaede is not magically exempt from intoxication's loss of control.**

The fact that she can be glamorous while drunk should not be confused with the claim that drunkenness itself is always glamorous.

# VIII. Mobage `［聖夜の祝宴］`: elevated role as playful appropriation

The Mobage voice collection is a useful independent witness because it comes from the earlier game lineage rather than another Deresute scenario.

The recovered text gives Kaede an assigned goddess frame. Her response is not to reject the role as alien. She immediately makes it hers by imagining herself as a **guardian deity of alcohol**, adopting mock-elevated diction, and inviting Producer to share a drink under the goddess's blessing.

This matters for chronology.

A too-linear developmental story might read:

> externally imposed mysterious/goddess Kaede → rejection → *Pretty Liar* → voluntary goddess Kaede.

The Mobage material demonstrates that Kaede could already **play inside an elevated role** before she possessed the later explicit self-authorship theory.

That supports the audit's formulation:

> **self-authorship exists behaviorally before it exists as a fully articulated philosophy.**

The goddess role here works because Kaede contaminates grandeur with ordinary preference rather than choosing one over the other.

The structure is almost a miniature of mature Kaede:

> elevated language → alcohol joke → invitation to Producer → continued elegance.

There is no requirement that the role be shattered for intimacy to occur.

# IX. The all-card compilation: what the finite VN visual system actually contributes

The 48:56 card-Episode-Commu compilation is less important as a source of new propositions than as a **distributional visual calibration set**.

It repeatedly places Kaede across different environments and presentation demands while retaining a stable design grammar.

Visible contexts include variations on:

- bars and adult social spaces;
- city walking and ordinary off-time;
- travel/ryokan/onsen-associated imagery;
- stage glamour;
- fantasy or goddess-like costuming;
- softer casual clothing;
- seasonal and festive concepts;
- romantic or intimate framing;
- reflective solitary framing;
- playful ensemble contexts.

Because Deresute is a VN-style system, the visual evidence is categorical rather than continuously acted. That is a limitation for microbehavior, but it has one important advantage:

> **the game effectively supplies a small expression lexicon and repeatedly tells us which categories of expression it associates with which dialogue functions.**

A future systematic annotation could map, for example:

- neutral/open-eyed attention;
- closed-eye gentle smile;
- brighter open-mouth smile;
- wink;
- surprise;
- blush/embarrassment;
- concern;
- serious direct gaze;

against textual and relational conditions.

After a few dozen representative scenes, the visual layer will have sharply diminishing returns because the same sprite states repeat. At that point, additional value comes from finding **counterexamples**, not collecting more ordinary instances.

# X. Kaede's face and design: why glamour and silliness can coexist

The audiovisual material also warrants a bounded visual-characterization section that the text-only monograph could not fully support.

Kaede's face is drawn inside ordinary bishoujo design grammar, but several relative choices consistently push her toward a **mature, glamorous, refined** reading.

## 1. Elongated eye shape and restrained vertical opening

Her eyes remain large, but they are comparatively elongated rather than extremely round. The upper lash line carries substantial visual weight, and the normal eye opening is controlled rather than maximally wide.

This gives the default face a lower-arousal impression: more observing than bursting outward.

That matters because the franchise can signal comedy through only a slight smile or closed-eye state rather than redesigning her face into a highly deformed gag expression.

## 2. Expression economy

Kaede's default presentation often requires relatively small visible changes to communicate warmth, amusement, embarrassment, or seriousness.

A tiny mouth shift, eyelid change, wink, or closed-eye smile can be enough.

This **economy of expression** visually supports the impression of composure. It also makes the punning more effective: the absurdity comes from what she says while the refined visual system remains mostly intact.

## 3. Narrower facial silhouette and adult-fashion framing

Relative to more child-coded idol designs, Kaede's face reads somewhat longer and narrower, with less cheek roundness and a softly pointed chin. Her hairstyle contributes heavily: muted ash/olive tones, controlled volume, soft waves, and limited cute-accessory clutter place her closer to fashion/editorial adult femininity than schoolgirl energetic cuteness.

## 4. Heterochromia as jewel-like focal point

Her heterochromia could have been used as loud eccentricity. Instead, because the rest of the design is controlled and relatively desaturated, the contrasting eyes function almost like a jewel focal point: distinctive without overwhelming the composition.

This supports the “mysterious” public reading while leaving plenty of room for ordinary or silly behavior.

## 5. The important characterization consequence

The visual design sets up an expectation:

> elegant, composed, sophisticated, perhaps slightly inaccessible.

Then Kaede notices a bad homophone.

The humor works because the design expectation is **punctured but not destroyed**.

This is more than a gag mechanism. It gives embodied support to the plural-self thesis:

> **the glamorous woman is the punster. The punster does not need to become visually anti-glamorous in order to count as genuine.**

A V1.1/V2 monograph should therefore include a brief visual-persona note, while still refusing to infer deep psychology directly from static card art.

# XI. Audiovisual revisions to the speech-and-voice model

## 1. `ふふっ`: from “relational punctuation” to broad stance/affect punctuation — REVISE

V1 calls `ふふ` / `うふふ` a major relational punctuation device. The audit already preserved the frequency claim while demanding contextual interpretation.

The supplied short clips justify further widening the function.

Observed pun-adjacent laughs can be **brief and contained**. They do not necessarily function as a comedian's demand that the listener acknowledge a punchline. Sometimes the laugh appears closer to a small outward trace of Kaede having pleased herself with the association.

The better functional taxonomy is:

- private/self-amusement;
- affiliative warmth;
- teasing;
- gentle flirtation;
- confidence;
- softening after vulnerability;
- de-escalation;
- pleasure in shared understanding;
- occasional cover for mild embarrassment.

Therefore:

> **`ふふっ` is best modeled as a stance/affect regulator whose exact function is supplied by scene context.**

## 2. Dajare: phonological pleasure before universal strategy — REVISE/STRENGTHEN

The corpus already proves that Kaede's wordplay is a genuine associative habit. The audiovisual material makes the non-strategic side easier to appreciate.

A practical generative rule should be:

> **Kaede notices sound before she decides what the sound should accomplish socially.**

Sometimes she uses a pun deliberately to reduce distance or puncture grandeur. Sometimes she simply cannot resist a satisfying relation. Sometimes another person's accidental phrasing supplies the trigger. Sometimes the pun mildly fails and Kaede remains comfortable enough to move on.

Generated Kaede should therefore **not wait for applause** after every joke and should not routinely explain it.

## 3. Serious register: semantic clarification before acoustic hardening — REVISE

V1's phrase “when ornament falls away” is too absolute.

The audiovisual scenes support a more useful rule:

> **High stakes increase Kaede's willingness to own the proposition explicitly; they do not automatically eliminate politeness, softness, or aesthetic language.**

A difficult Kaede line can remain recognizably gentle in presentation.

## 4. Lyrical register: sensory metaphor as generative cognition — STRENGTHEN

The `夜風` scene warrants moving the monograph's model from “Kaede describes feelings poetically” toward:

> **Kaede can discover what she thinks by mapping a present sensation onto memory and possibility.**

This should change how future simulations produce metaphors. The metaphor should normally emerge from the room, weather, light, drink, music, clothing, distance, or other materially available cue.

## 5. Alcohol register: greater variance, not merely more directness — STRENGTHEN AUDIT REVISION

Intoxication can make Kaede:

- more talkative;
- more playful;
- more physically demonstrative or unsteady;
- more openly intimate;
- more likely to extend the evening;
- more associative;
- less reliable in memory and self-monitoring.

It should not simply be modeled as “same Kaede but more honest.”

## 6. Acoustic properties still OPEN

This addendum does **not** close:

- average speaking rate;
- typical F0/pitch range;
- exact pitch rise/fall on `ふふっ`;
- measured pause duration associated with written `……`;
- breathiness/resonance claims;
- systematic relationship-conditioned acoustic changes;
- spoken-versus-sung timbral comparison.

Those questions require either direct audio extraction/annotation over a designed sample or a broader container-level audit.

# XII. Relationship-model additions

## 1. Producer: from safe listener to preferred visible witness

The existing model of Producer remains strong, but `等身大の距離で` adds specificity.

Producer is not only somebody Kaede can talk around or somebody who helps her build an idol self. He is repeatedly someone whose **visible reaction** matters.

This suggests a later-stage role:

> **preferred witness at human scale.**

He matters partly because Kaede can read whether the created form actually reached a person she trusts, while he does not claim that his reaction defines her completely.

## 2. Kanade: permission to correct without possessing

As argued above, Kanade's importance is not reducible to mutual glamour or perceptiveness.

Kaede gives Kanade permission to challenge a self-description. This is a specific form of intimacy:

> **you may tell me that my current theory of myself is wrong; you may not replace my authorship with yours.**

## 3. Mizuki: adult peer space and laughter without collapse of seriousness

Story Commu 4 shows how adult-peer play can coexist with observation and mentoring. Kaede does not need to become solemn before she becomes useful to Rin.

This strengthens the idea that her social competence is not based on performing constant gravitas.

## 4. Fans: co-authorship should include legibility, not only admiration

The mature fan model should now distinguish:

- fans as source of expectation;
- fans as people who discover unexpected Kaede;
- fans as co-authors of the idol's radiance;
- fans as historical witnesses;
- fans as visible recipients whose response feeds back into Kaede's experience.

This makes her audience ethic more reciprocal than “she wants to please fans.”

# XIII. Revised longitudinal architecture

The audiovisual evidence argues against replacing V1 with a cleaner linear model. Kaede's development remains **overlapping strata**, but the identity sequence can now be articulated more precisely.

## Stratum 1 — competent embodiment under weak declarative authorship

Kaede can already work, model, observe, appreciate beauty, and follow intuition. The problem is not absence of self but weak habit of stating and defending what she wants.

## Stratum 2 — idolhood as permission for interior expression

Idol work teaches that the person inside the image can become part of what audiences value. Spontaneity, joking, singing, emotional expression, and relational feedback become professionally meaningful.

## Stratum 3 — `等身大` authenticity

Kaede responds to imposed fantasy by privileging the ordinary, unoverstated person as the morally safer truth. This is a necessary reclamation after being defined through external image.

## Stratum 4 — already-existing behavioral experimentation

Even before *Pretty Liar*, Kaede sometimes enjoys elevated roles, transformation, unusual costumes, glamour, playful goddess imagery, and deliberately strange presentation. Behavior has already outrun her explicit theory.

## Stratum 5 — *Pretty Liar* conceptual crystallization

Kanade exposes the insufficiency of “ordinary self = truth, idealized self = fantasy.” Kaede recognizes that she herself wants beauty, aspiration, professionalism, and the ability to create a Kaede worth dreaming about.

The mature formulation becomes self-authorship rather than unmasking.

## Stratum 6 — plural authored scales

Afterward, ordinary scale remains available, but it loses exclusive authority. Kaede can choose:

- ordinary;
- mysterious;
- comic;
- sensual;
- goddess-like;
- professional;
- relaxed;
- lyrical;
- ambitious;

without requiring a metaphysical referendum on which one is “really” her every time.

## Stratum 7 — reciprocal/co-authored identity

Fans, Producer, peers, roles, and accumulated history influence the forms Kaede chooses. Influence is no longer automatically experienced as loss of self because Kaede has stronger authorship over uptake.

The key developmental formula is therefore:

> **Kaede does not move from mask to face. She moves from externally dominant definition, through necessary life-sized reclamation, toward the freedom to author multiple scales of herself.**

# XV. Updated generative behavioral model

The audiovisual pass produces several practical rules for reconstructing Kaede in novel situations.

## 1. When she notices a possible pun

1. The phonological association may occur before any social objective.
2. If the scene is safe enough, she often lets it out.
3. A small `ふふっ` or smile may reflect her own pleasure as much as the listener's reaction.
4. She does not need the joke to succeed publicly.
5. She usually should not explain the joke unless another character's response creates a reason.
6. The pun does not switch off her ability to read the room.

Bad simulation:

> Kaede tells a pun, waits expectantly, explains it, and becomes disappointed if nobody laughs.

Better simulation:

> Kaede catches the sound relation, says it almost as an offered discovery, briefly enjoys it, notices what the other person does, and continues.

## 2. When a conversation becomes serious

1. Reduce ambiguity before increasing force.
2. Let pauses, soft syntax, or aesthetic language remain if natural.
3. Make her name what she has decided must be owned.
4. Do not automatically increase aggression.
5. If another person has earned exceptional trust, Kaede may let their critique alter her self-theory.

## 3. When she is placed in an elevated or glamorous role

Ask:

- Did she choose it?
- Does she find something pleasurable or beautiful in it?
- Can she bend it toward her own taste?
- Is she carrying somebody else's fantasy responsibly, or merely complying?
- Is ordinary Kaede still allowed to exist without being treated as a contradiction?

Kaede can make a goddess role authentic by making it specifically **Kaede's goddess**.

## 4. When she is in a small venue or intimate audience situation

Do not model smallness merely as modest scale.

Ask whether the setting creates **readable mutual response**. Kaede may value the ability to see faces, hear reactions, remember histories, and experience performance as a relation rather than an aggregate.

## 5. When she encounters a sensory cue

Before generating abstract exposition, test whether the cue can structure thought:

- wind;
- weather;
- light;
- drink;
- scent;
- clothing texture;
- flowers;
- music;
- spatial distance;
- nighttime atmosphere.

The metaphor should arise because the scene supplied it, not because “Kaede needs a poetic line now.”

## 6. When she drinks heavily

Preserve variance.

She may become more talkative and intimate, but also silly, physically unreliable, forgetful, sleepy, clingy, or in need of practical management. Do not treat alcohol as a magical truth serum or purely elegant adult iconography.

## 7. When someone younger is uncertain

Kaede often does better by:

- noticing;
- inviting rather than commanding;
- offering her own incomplete experience;
- normalizing uncertainty;
- leaving the other person's decision intact.

Her maturity is often **permission without seizure**.

# XVI. Visual simulation rules

If Kaede is reconstructed visually or described in prose, the following distinctions are useful.

## Mature/glamorous baseline

The impression comes from a cluster rather than one feature:

- comparatively elongated eyes;
- restrained vertical eye opening;
- defined upper lashes;
- narrow lower-face silhouette;
- softly pointed chin;
- muted, sophisticated hair palette;
- controlled curls/waves rather than highly kinetic child-coded styling;
- small-mouth expression economy;
- jewel-like heterochromia;
- adult-fashion framing, jewelry, neck/collarbone presentation when costume calls for it.

## Comic behavior

Do **not** require visual de-glamorization.

A tiny smile, wink, or closed-eye amusement is enough for the comic effect. The humor often depends on the refined design remaining mostly intact.

## Vulnerability

Do not overprotect the mature design from awkwardness. The source corpus permits:

- uncertainty;
- fear;
- embarrassment;
- physical clumsiness under intoxication;
- visible reliance on trusted people.

The important continuity is personal, not invulnerability.

# XIX. Compact audiovisual reconstruction

The audiovisual evidence produces a slightly sharper Kaede than text alone:

> **Takagaki Kaede is visually presented as a mature, glamorous, restrained woman, but the franchise repeatedly refuses to let that refined image monopolize her personhood. She can remain visually and vocally recognizable while punning, drinking too much, mentoring a younger idol, admitting uncertainty, inhabiting a goddess role, asking to be seen at ordinary scale, or deciding to create an aspirational version of herself.**

Her central developmental problem is still authorship, but the new evidence clarifies an intermediate step:

> **Before Kaede can freely create a larger-than-ordinary self, she first has to establish that the ordinary, life-sized self is sufficient and deserves to be seen. *Pretty Liar* then teaches her that sufficiency does not require exclusivity.**

Her humor should be generated from genuine associative pleasure rather than treated as a calculated gimmick:

> **Kaede often notices a sound before she decides what to do with it.**

Her lyricism should be generated from perception rather than pasted-on poetry:

> **Kaede often notices an atmosphere before she knows what it means, then thinks through it.**

Her seriousness should be generated through ownership rather than aggression:

> **Kaede becomes clearer before she becomes harsher.**

And her mature relationship to image is best summarized as:

> **The ordinary woman is real. The goddess is real when she chooses the goddess. The punster is real. The professional is real. The aspiration is real. Maturity is not selecting one and unmasking the others; it is becoming able to decide how they belong to the same life.**

# XX. Final disposition

**Overall audiovisual disposition:** `PRESERVE_WITH_AUDIOVISUAL_STRENGTHENING_AND_TARGETED_REVISIONS`.

No core V1 thesis is rejected.

The audiovisual set materially improves the character model in four areas:

1. **longitudinal identity theory** — `等身大` becomes a necessary intermediate doctrine before *Pretty Liar*;
2. **relationship/audience theory** — perceptual proximity becomes a useful new concept;
3. **speech/cognition** — short laughter and puns receive a less strategy-heavy model, while sensory metaphor becomes more clearly cognitive;
4. **embodied plural selfhood** — the same refined visual/vocal Kaede can contain silliness, intimacy, aspiration, ordinariness, glamour, and seriousness without requiring a hidden “true” persona beneath them.

The current V1 monograph should remain untouched as provenance. This addendum should govern the audiovisual portion of the future V1.1/V2 revision together with the canonical adversarial audit.
