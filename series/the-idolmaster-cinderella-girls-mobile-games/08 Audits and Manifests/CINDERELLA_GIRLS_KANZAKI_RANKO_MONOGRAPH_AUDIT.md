# THE IDOLM@STER CINDERELLA GIRLS — Kanzaki Ranko Monograph Audit

## 0. Audit purpose and result

This document audits `CINDERELLA_GIRLS_KANZAKI_RANKO_CHARACTER_MONOGRAPH.md` against the integrated mobile-game evidence packet. It does **not** use the 2015 television anime as governing evidence. The question is whether the V1 monograph reconstructs the game-corpus Kanzaki Ranko accurately enough to function as a derived character model.

The audit tests five things:

1. **Claim validity** — whether the monograph's major propositions are actually supported by the recovered Japanese text.
2. **Adversarial robustness** — whether contrary scenes force revisions rather than being smoothed into the thesis.
3. **Longitudinal calibration** — whether a persuasive late formulation has been projected backward as a clean developmental sequence.
4. **Linguistic-model validity** — whether the proposed Ranko-go/register rules distinguish semantic function, intimacy, work, embarrassment, and theatrical play with sufficient precision.
5. **Predictive usefulness** — whether the model explains less-foregrounded scenes rather than only the scenes from which it was built.

The overall disposition is:

> **PRESERVE WITH TARGETED REVISIONS.**

The monograph's central reconstruction survives. Ranko's private fantasy does have a protective origin; she does become more capable of transmitting herself without abandoning it; idol work does turn private world-making into public art; Producer is a privileged early interpreter and materializer; Asuka exposes the danger of equating aesthetic similarity with mutual understanding; and mature Ranko increasingly treats fantasy, ordinary speech, multiple appearances, and shared worlds as compatible parts of one self.

The most important correction is that V1 makes this development slightly **too sequential**. The evidence does not show a clean replacement of:

> defense -> art -> communication -> self-authorship.

All four functions overlap much earlier than that formulation implies. Early Ranko is already drawing, designing, choosing visual concepts, enjoying performance, and using fantasy relationally. Late Ranko can still use the same language defensively or to manage embarrassment. What changes most securely is not the disappearance of old functions but **control, permeability, and functional range**.

A better longitudinal formulation is therefore:

> **Ranko's fantasy begins with a strong defensive burden, but artistic, cognitive, and relational functions are present early. Development reduces the degree to which the fantasy must operate as a closed shell and increases her ability to choose among registers, invite others into the world, and create new forms without treating any one form as mandatory.**

The second major correction concerns ordinary speech. V1 is right that plain Japanese often accompanies vulnerability and relational repair, but the corpus shows at least four distinct triggers:

- **vulnerability/exposure**;
- **accommodation to another person's comprehension**;
- **professional clarity or task constraints**;
- **comic or involuntary register collapse**.

Conversely, full Ranko-go can carry sincere affection, trust, gratitude, and vulnerability. Ordinary speech is therefore **not a sincerity meter**.

The third correction concerns Producer. He is not best described as a person who simply “does not demand translation.” He sometimes explicitly requests ordinary speech when the task requires it. The stronger model is that he **does not make normalization a condition of acceptance**. He can ask for clarity without treating Ranko-go itself as something to cure.

No governing thesis merits `REJECT`. The audit primarily converts several elegant binaries into more probabilistic, layered models.

# I. Audit method

## 1. Governing evidence

The current Ranko packet contains:

- 297 inventoried candidate source objects;
- 195 source objects with Ranko directly confirmed in canonical scene data;
- 195 recovered complete scenes/dialogue sets containing her;
- 2,219 derived Ranko utterances;
- 109 objective interaction counterparts;
- 141 mapped audio containers;
- Deresute text `COMPLETE_AGAINST_INVENTORY`;
- Mobage text `STRUCTURALLY_INCOMPLETE`;
- audio `REPRESENTATIVE_ONLY`.

The Deresute layer is strong enough for corpus-wide textual claims. Mobage remains a revision vector, particularly for earliest characterization, system dialogue, event bodies, Idol Produce, Memory Episodes, Cinderella History, birthday/seasonal material, and other categories for which no complete surviving denominator has been established.

The current source-conflict document contains no ingested overlapping variant group for Ranko. That means “not compared,” not “all archives agree.” Direct Deresute game data remains Tier 1 and is sufficient for the present textual model, but a later independent transcript sample would be useful specifically for stylized-language verification.

## 2. Procedure

The monograph was decomposed into claim families:

- childhood and defensive origin;
- fantasy as agency;
- Producer relationship;
- Ranko-go semantics;
- source-authored glosses;
- ordinary-speech/register switching;
- embarrassment and affect regulation;
- Asuka;
- Chitose and other peers;
- professionalism;
- artistic authorship;
- self-creation;
- darkness/light worldview;
- longitudinal development;
- behavioral prediction.

Each family was tested against direct source anchors and against less-foregrounded scenes. The standard project dispositions are used:

- **PRESERVE** — formulation is well calibrated.
- **STRENGTHEN** — evidence is broader or stronger than V1 indicates.
- **REVISE** — core idea survives but wording/scope should change.
- **DOWNGRADE** — plausible but currently too general or too confident.
- **REJECT** — contradicted strongly enough to remove.
- **OPEN** — current evidence cannot settle it.

## 3. Important limitation

The “held-out” tests below are not machine-learning blind tests. The V1 monograph and this audit were both produced from the same complete packet. The tests instead ask whether the abstractions transfer to scenes **not used as the monograph's main argumentative anchors**.

# III. Strongest preserved and strengthened theses

## 1. The shell is real, but it is not the whole ontology — STRENGTHEN + REVISE

Story Commu 12 remains the indispensable origin source. Ranko describes the gap between a timid child and imagined protagonists who could move, fight, travel, use magic, and become whatever she wanted. She later explicitly names the dark self as a `殻` and the dark language as something used to protect weakness.

This is unusually strong evidence because the defensive model is not merely imposed by the analyst. Ranko says it herself.

But the V1 timeline can be improved. The earliest card material already contains:

- drawing and sketchbook practice;
- deliberate fashion preference;
- pleasure in creating visual concepts;
- requests to show Producer a design she wants to wear;
- curiosity about idol work;
- deliberate performance choices;
- affectionate use of mythic language.

`DERESTE:CARD:200073` is especially important. Ranko spends a day drawing in a garden, worries about revealing the sketch, and finally explains in ordinary speech that she has designed an idol outfit she would like to wear. The fantasy world is already **creative production**, not only defensive enclosure.

The best revision is therefore functional layering:

> **Defense is historically foundational, but not chronologically exclusive. Ranko's fantasy is simultaneously refuge, aesthetic pleasure, cognition, authorship, humor, and relationship technology from very early in the recovered corpus. Development changes the balance among these functions rather than replacing one with another.**

## 2. “A defense can be sincere” — PRESERVE

V1's distinction between defensive function and authentic content should remain untouched.

Ranko does not expect others to believe literally that her sketchbook is an occult grimoire or that ordinary jobs are supernatural battles. Yet the transformation is not empty parody. The symbolic rendering tracks genuine preferences and emotions. Her dark language frequently preserves more of the **felt shape** of an experience than a literal paraphrase would.

This is exactly why the source-authored glosses are analytically useful but insufficient as a total model. The ordinary gloss may tell us what practical proposition is being conveyed, while the theatrical line tells us how Ranko wants the proposition to exist in the interaction.

## 3. Ranko-go is a productive semantic system — STRENGTHEN

The target-line derivative contains:

- 465 utterances with source-authored full-width parenthetical gloss structure;
- 166 lines containing `闇`;
- 102 lines containing `魔力`;
- 100 lines containing `魂`;
- 72 lines containing `翼`;
- 135 uses of `我が友` across 135 lines;
- recurrent `瞳`, `世界`, `盟約`, `契約`, `言霊`, gods, demons, angels, fate, feast, ritual, and stage imagery.

The important finding is not frequency by itself. These symbols are **polysemous**.

`魔力` can denote stamina, confidence, affect, talent, creative capacity, support, or the force behind a performance. `翼` can denote freedom, agency, relationship, capability, or literal stage/fantasy imagery. `闇` can be rest, mystery, selfhood, beauty, private refuge, performance style, or simply a comic framing device.

A dictionary that translates each token one-to-one will therefore generate bad Ranko. The correct model is:

> **select a symbolic domain appropriate to the scene, then compose the current situation inside that domain.**

This finding should be strengthened in any V1.1/V2 revision.

## 4. Producer is safe because clarity is negotiable, not forbidden — REVISE

The V1 phrase “the person who does not demand translation” is too absolute.

Memorial 2 itself complicates it: Producer asks Ranko to use ordinary words for vocal training. Later, professional work requires the same thing. What differentiates him is not a promise never to ask for plain speech. It is that plain speech is a **functional request**, not a demand that Ranko stop being Ranko.

Story Commu 12 and Dark Illuminate show why this matters. Producer's acceptance establishes a secure base, but that base eventually has to support communication outward rather than permanent dependence on one decoder.

The revised model should be:

> **Producer is the early relationship in which Ranko learns that somebody can seek comprehension without making normalization the price of belonging.**

That is stronger and more precise than “never asks for translation.”

## 5. Asuka remains the crucial communication crisis — STRENGTHEN

`DERESTE:STORY:5102406` survives adversarial review as one of the strongest character texts in Ranko's packet.

The key points are unusually explicit:

- Asuka admits that he put Ranko inside a category produced by his own imagination.
- Ranko admits that she could not communicate the simple word “different.”
- Ranko states that imagination can make her strong while exposing that world to another person remains frightening.
- She says that, until then, only Producer had received her self-created world in the way she needed.
- Reconciliation occurs through differentiation: Ranko's freedom and Asuka's self-observing narrative power are different strengths.

The scene directly refutes the idea that stylistic similarity guarantees intimacy.

It also refutes a weaker model of growth in which Ranko simply becomes more “normal.” The repaired pair immediately resumes heightened symbolic language. The result is **more communication without aesthetic surrender**.

# IV. Required revisions

## 1. Replace linear transformation with functional accretion — REVISE

The V1 sequence is elegant:

> private fantasy as refuge -> protective identity -> shared artistic language -> chosen self-authorship.

It should be kept as a high-level retrieval heuristic, but not as a literal stage theory.

Three pieces of counterevidence matter:

1. **Early artistic authorship.** Costume design and visual world-making are present before the major communication crises.
2. **Early relational use.** Even base cards use Ranko-go to invite Producer into shared play and trust.
3. **Late defensive use.** Embarrassment, surveillance, and unwanted exposure can still reactivate buffering after Ranko has become highly socially capable.

A better model is a stack of persistent functions:

- **protective function** — distance from judgment/exposure;
- **cognitive function** — translating reality into symbolic narrative;
- **artistic function** — designing/staging imagined worlds;
- **relational function** — inviting trusted people into those worlds;
- **performative function** — mobilizing courage and stage presence;
- **self-authoring function** — selecting among multiple possible forms.

Development changes **which function dominates**, how freely she can switch, and how permeable the boundary becomes.

## 2. Ordinary speech has multiple causes — REVISE

The strongest counterexample to a vulnerability-only model is Story Commu 16.

Ranko speaks ordinary Japanese to Anastasia because she knows that full Ranko-go would be harder for her to understand. Anastasia explicitly notices and thanks her for speaking in “heart words” that are easier to follow. Ranko's motivation is accommodation and care.

This scene is analytically important because it occurs well before the later self-theorizing material. It shows that Ranko is already capable of **audience design** in ordinary conversation.

Other triggers include:

- work requirements (`5103405`);
- exhaustion and camera pressure (`5303004`–`5303005`);
- sudden fear (`1000034`);
- ordinary domestic ease;
- apology and relational repair (`5102406`);
- embarrassment and tactile intimacy.

The revised generative rule should be:

> **Plain speech becomes more likely when semantic precision, accommodation, professional function, or naked emotional exposure outweigh the value of symbolic framing. But it is neither necessary nor sufficient for sincerity.**

## 3. Full Ranko-go can be intimate — REVISE

V1 knows this, but several sections still create a latent hierarchy in which theatrical speech is “buffered” and ordinary speech is “direct.”

That distinction is useful probabilistically but becomes misleading if treated as depth ordering.

The base and high-affection cards contain fully theatrical language used for:

- asking Producer to remain with her;
- describing him as guide or key;
- thanking him for opening paths;
- imagining shared futures;
- placing him inside her most private symbolic world.

Later `2201481` is nearly all theatrical and is simultaneously one of her most intimate Producer texts. Dream/reality blur because **conversation with him expands the fantasy world**. The symbolic mode is not hiding intimacy there; it is the chosen form of intimacy.

The relevant axis is therefore not:

> Ranko-go = less sincere / ordinary = more sincere.

It is:

> **What kind of exposure is Ranko managing, and does symbolic form help or obstruct the communicative goal?**

## 4. “Sole translator” needs historical and phenomenological qualification — REVISE

Ranko says in the Dark Illuminate repair that the only person to whom she had been able to communicate her world was Producer. That direct statement deserves full weight.

Yet `5103405` also gives her a school teacher who does not understand the code but **tries**, and that attempt matters deeply to Ranko. Story Commu 16 gives Anastasia partial accommodation. Koume and other peers can maintain relationships without total decoding.

The strongest reading is that Producer is uniquely secure as the **whole-world recipient** in Ranko's self-understanding at that stage, not literally the only human being who can understand any Ranko-go proposition.

V1.1 should use “privileged interpreter,” “secure base,” or “first whole-world recipient” instead of “sole translator” when speaking analytically.

## 5. “Ethics of imaginative recognition” is one abstraction too high — DOWNGRADE

Ranko repeatedly responds well to people whose tastes or social positions are unusual. She understands Nao's embarrassment around “girly” taste, works comfortably with Koume/Shouko, and often treats unusual imaginative commitments as things to enter rather than correct.

This supports a stable **sympathy toward unconventional self-expression**.

Calling it an “ethic,” however, risks converting a recurring relational disposition into a systematic moral theory. The current packet does not show Ranko applying a formulated general principle across difficult moral conflicts.

Recommended revision:

> **Ranko has a strong relational preference for imaginative recognition: when another person's unusual taste or self-authored world is sincere and non-harmful, she tends to meet it with curiosity, participation, and protection from ridicule.**

That is strong enough without over-philosophizing it.

## 6. Dream/reality integration is mature evidence, not necessarily a final stage — DOWNGRADE

`DERESTE:STORY:2201481` is an excellent text. Ranko says that conversation with Producer blurs the boundary between dream and reality and expands her fantasy world; she values a world containing both dream/reality and light/dark.

But one late episode should not be made to carry more longitudinal certainty than it can bear.

The scene strongly supports:

- mature permeability;
- symbolic integration;
- Producer as relational expansion;
- reduced need for a hard fantasy/reality binary.

It does **not** prove that Ranko has permanently resolved every tension between withdrawal and communication. The `functional layering` model is safer: integration is now an available and explicit way she can understand herself.

# V. Speech and linguistic-model audit

## 1. Parenthetical glosses — STRENGTHEN

The current target-line derivative contains **465 Ranko utterances with source-authored full-width parenthetical glosses**.

This makes Ranko unusually valuable for linguistic modeling because the script repeatedly gives us two layers:

- the performed/symbolic utterance;
- an ordinary-language semantic bridge.

V1 correctly refuses to assume that the parenthetical text is a verbatim hidden thought. That caution should be elevated into a permanent methodological rule.

A generated model should use the glosses to learn **semantic equivalence classes**, not to infer a two-step cognitive algorithm of “think normally -> encrypt into Ranko-go.”

## 2. Core vocabulary — STRENGTHEN

The derivative contains, among other recurring tokens:

- `闇` in 166 lines;
- `魔力` in 102 lines;
- `魂` in 100 lines;
- `翼` in 72 lines;
- `我が友` in 135 lines;
- `瞳` in 54 lines;
- `世界` in 121 lines.

These counts confirm a stable symbolic lexicon. They do **not** support one-to-one substitution.

For generation, use semantic domains:

- **power/energy** — `魔力`, force, awakening, resonance;
- **relationship/recognition** — `我が友`, `瞳`, contract, wings;
- **self/feeling** — `魂`, heart, resonance, seals;
- **agency/freedom** — wings, flight, awakening, incarnation;
- **performance/social world** — feast, ritual, stage, summoning, kingdom;
- **aesthetic polarity** — light/dark, heaven/underworld, angel/demon.

Then let the immediate scene determine which meaning is activated.

## 3. Laughter is a stance system, not a single tic — PRESERVE

The target derivative includes roughly:

- `クク` in 19 lines;
- `フフ` in 43 lines;
- `えへへ` in 33 lines;
- `ハーッハッハ` in 17 lines.

V1's distinction is useful:

- `ククク` often initiates theatrical posture;
- `ハーッハッハ` is maximally performative proclamation;
- softer `フフ` can remain poised or intimate;
- `えへへ` more readily exposes ordinary delight or embarrassment.

This should remain probabilistic. Context matters more than token assignment.

## 4. The most important correction: serious Ranko does not always simplify — REVISE

The monograph's generative rules correctly say to clarify the person when conflict requires it. That rule is strong for Asuka and `Unlock Starbeat`.

It should not become a universal claim that seriousness reduces symbolic density.

`2201481` is serious, intimate, and heavily theatrical. High-affection card lines often remain fully mythic. Ranko may prefer symbolic language precisely because the emotion matters.

The revised rule:

> **When the problem is misunderstanding, professional clarity, or explicit repair, Ranko becomes more likely to simplify. When the problem is intimacy itself, she may instead preserve or intensify symbolic framing because it is the form in which she can safely and beautifully carry the feeling.**

## 5. Fear is an excellent mixed-register stress test — STRENGTHEN

Story Commu 34 is almost a laboratory demonstration.

Ranko enters the haunted-house scene already frightened. Across the sequence she alternates among:

- plain screams;
- childish complaint (`こ、小梅ちゃん、ひどいっ…… !`);
- direct desire to go home;
- full symbolic lines (`我が耳は閉ざされし扉よ ! !`);
- deliberate “I can't hear you” ordinary avoidance;
- horror narration (`聞こえないはずの、足音……`);
- physical clinging and trembling.

This proves that register is not binary state detection. Multiple modes can coexist within seconds because they do different jobs.

# VI. Held-out / less-foregrounded scene tests

## Test 1 — Story Commu 34: haunted house

**Prediction from V1:** fear should expose ordinary Ranko while allowing fantasy to aestheticize danger.

**Actual result:** strongly confirmed, but with more rapid code-mixing than the binary model predicts.

Ranko screams and pleads plainly, then describes danger through dark language, then returns to plain avoidance. The scene supports the core person-model and revises the register model toward **simultaneous multi-function switching**.

**Disposition:** `PRESERVE + REVISE linguistic mechanics`.

## Test 2 — Story Commu 16: Anastasia

**Prediction from V1:** with someone she wants to protect or accommodate, Ranko should reduce opacity.

**Actual result:** stronger than predicted. Ranko deliberately speaks ordinary Japanese so Anastasia can understand her, asks whether Anastasia is lonely, and later apologizes when Anastasia's loneliness becomes explicit.

This is excellent evidence that plain speech can be **audience-centered accommodation**, not only exposed selfhood.

**Disposition:** `STRENGTHEN social accommodation; REVISE vulnerability-only speech rule`.

## Test 3 — `Unlock Starbeat` pre-crisis (`5303004`)

**Prediction from V1:** surveillance can make sincere theatricality compulsory and therefore exhausting.

**Actual result:** strongly confirmed. During ordinary rehearsal Ranko is frequently plain-speaking. Once she notices the camera, she visibly tries to re-enter the persona despite fatigue and mistakes. The next chapter then makes the cost explicit.

**Disposition:** `STRENGTHEN`.

## Test 4 — Mobage Refresh Room 46 with Asuka

**Prediction from V1:** intimacy with Asuka should allow very high symbolic density without requiring constant translation.

**Actual result:** confirmed. Ranko sustains elaborate metaphor across a normal-weather/umbrella conversation, with Asuka answering inside the same symbolic field. The scene ends with an ordinary `うん♪`, demonstrating permeability rather than register replacement.

**Disposition:** `STRENGTHEN relationship-conditioned permeability`.

## Test 5 — early rose-design cards

**Prediction from a strict shell-to-art stage model:** early fantasy should still be predominantly defensive.

**Actual result:** contradicted in degree. Early Ranko is already sketching, designing costumes, choosing performance forms, and using the fantasy to communicate desired aesthetics to Producer.

**Disposition:** `REVISE longitudinal sequence`.

# VII. Relationship-model audit

## 1. Producer — STRENGTHEN with one wording revision

Producer has 67 shared scenes in the objective co-occurrence index, by far Ranko's largest indexed counterpart. The number alone does not prove intimacy, but the qualitative material is abundant.

The governing relationship model survives:

- early recognition;
- safe interpretation;
- materialization of fantasy;
- stage/costume/song support;
- emotional secure base;
- later co-creation and shared-world language.

The one required wording change is:

> not “Producer never asks Ranko to translate,” but **“Producer can ask for clarity without making clarity a condition for accepting the person.”**

## 2. Asuka — STRENGTHEN

Asuka has 27 shared scenes, the largest non-Producer count and the strongest explicit peer communication arc.

The V1 treatment is correctly central. No downgrade is needed.

## 3. Chitose — PRESERVE

Chitose's value is not that she “decodes” Ranko better than everyone else. It is that she is willing to **answer within the imaginative frame** while also accepting ordinary Ranko without using ordinary behavior as a debunking mechanism.

That is a distinctive relational fit and should be preserved.

## 4. Koume and other unconventional peers — PRESERVE with lower abstraction

The corpus supports low-pressure compatibility with Koume, Shouko, Nao, Riina, and others. What should be reduced is the philosophical generalization, not the observed relational ease.

# VIII. Revised generative Ranko model

A V1.1/V2 character generator should use the following model.

### 1. First classify the function of the utterance

Is Ranko:

- performing;
- explaining;
- protecting herself;
- comforting someone;
- flirting/expressing affection;
- repairing misunderstanding;
- fulfilling a work requirement;
- playing inside a shared fantasy;
- thinking aloud?

Do **not** choose register from emotional intensity alone.

### 2. Choose the symbolic domain from the scene

Map what is actually present into a relevant mythic field. Avoid generic stacks of `闇`, `魔力`, `魂`, and `翼` when the scene does not support them.

### 3. Preserve multiple authentic modes

Ranko-go, mixed speech, and ordinary Japanese are all Ranko. Plain speech may be more exposed in some contexts, but it is not the only authentic self.

### 4. Use plain speech more often when precision matters

Especially likely under:

- relational repair;
- professional instruction;
- accommodation to a listener;
- explicit apology;
- direct self-disclosure.

But not mandatory.

### 5. Under embarrassment, allow either collapse or stronger buffering

She may blurt ordinary speech, stammer, or hide inside heightened fantasy. Both are canonical responses.

### 6. With trusted interpreters, increase permeability rather than automatically lowering fantasy density

Producer and Asuka can receive extremely elaborate Ranko-go. Trust means Ranko can also exit it without identity threat.

### 7. Keep the shy, conscientious teenager present without calling her the “real” one

The ordinary girl and the demon king are not rival ontologies. They are modes available to the same self.

# X. Audit conclusion

The V1 Ranko monograph is fundamentally successful. It avoids the most damaging simplification—“chuunibyou mask outside, normal girl inside”—and reconstructs a person for whom fantasy is simultaneously a protection, a language, an aesthetic, an imaginative cognitive habit, a professional resource, and an authored way of becoming visible.

The audit's main intervention is to make that model **less teleological and less binary**.

Ranko does not pass through a sequence in which defense disappears and art takes its place. She accumulates options. The shell becomes more permeable; the fantasy becomes more shareable; ordinary speech becomes safer; professional adaptation becomes easier; alternate appearances become less threatening; other people become collaborators rather than merely observers.

But the old functions remain available. She can still hide. She can still become embarrassed. She can still use theatricality to buffer intimacy. She can still fear being misunderstood. Maturity is therefore best measured by **choice** rather than by reduction in chuunibyou behavior.

The audited compact model is:

> **Kanzaki Ranko is a shy, conscientious, highly imaginative girl whose self-created fantasy language began partly as protection from exposure but rapidly became much more: a way to think, act, design, perform, love, joke, and build worlds with other people. Growth does not reveal an ordinary “true Ranko” by stripping away the fantasy. It gives Ranko enough communicative security and professional control that she can choose when to speak plainly, when to speak mythically, when to mix the two, and what new form of herself to create next.**

The best next external test remains a later cross-media audit against the 2015 anime, but only after the mobile-game model is revised from this canonical critique. A dedicated audio/voice audit would also be particularly valuable because the current text model predicts distinct performance states—proclamation, ordinary embarrassment, gentle intimacy, register switching, theatrical laughter, and singing—that representative audio has not yet tested systematically.
