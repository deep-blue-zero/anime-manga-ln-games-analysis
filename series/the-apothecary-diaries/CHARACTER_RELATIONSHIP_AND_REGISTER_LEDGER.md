# Character, relationship and register ledger

## Responsibility and source restraint

This is a cumulative routing surface for subject states, directed relations, ordinary-life evidence and written Japanese expression. No character roster, personality, relationship or voice profile is inferred from the title or external familiarity. Reconstruction readiness is **NOT_ASSESSED** for all subjects because none has been admitted or discovered in this project.

The registers below share subject/evidence keys but cannot substitute for one another. A relationship claim requires its own directional/contextual evidence; a linguistic observation is not a personality diagnosis. Each substantive row points to full prose analysis and exact evidence routes.

## Operational schemas

| Register / key | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Subject identity `AP-SUB-nnn` | Source-established name/form, alias/hidden identity boundary, first admitted occurrence, witness/continuity, allowed name at the frontier, identity uncertainty and evidence; later identity links preserve concealment history |
| Subject state `AP-CS-nnnn` | Subject; witness/version and frontier; time/disclosure/knowledge links; situation; ordinary/crisis/ambiguous context; evidence channel; goals and alternatives; behavior/speech/interiority as observed; affect/resource/role condition; state versus disposition hypothesis; counterevidence; confidence; full argument |
| Directed relationship `AP-REL-nnnn` | A→B; frontier and context; A's knowledge/belief about B; action/speech/withholding; trust/obligation/access or other source-earned dimensions; audience and institutional conditions; evidence; rival reading; B→A link if separately supported; change mechanism |
| Written register `AP-L-nnnn` | Speaker/narrator and recipient/audience; spoken/interior/reported mode; witness and exact Japanese locator; verified short wording and ruby/base distinction; speech act/objective; register/address/modality/ellipsis; gloss and alternatives; contextual comparison; source language; interpretive claim and uncertainty |
| Opportunity/control `AP-O-nnnn` | Subject/relationship; ordinary-life or negative-evidence question; actual opportunity; stakes/resources/audience; observed action/non-action; absent-information limit; comparable contexts; evidence and counterexample; generalization ceiling |
| Reconstruction coverage obligation `AP-RC-nnnn` | Source-established subject and model boundary; evidence channel/context/relationship/time coverage; represented and absent scenarios; contradictions; written-voice evidence; excluded witnesses; held-out plan/exposure; no readiness grade unless assigned by designated independent evaluator |

Register dimensions are descriptive, not mandatory positive findings. Use `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_ESTABLISHED` with reason when the source does not supply them. Do not turn an unavailable internal state into a table entry labeled “motivation.”

## Change causes and context

A proposed character change must classify its explanation as `DISPOSITION / KNOWLEDGE / RELATIONSHIP / CONTEXT / ROLE / RESOURCE / REVEALED_NOT_NEW / UNRESOLVED`, allowing multiple live alternatives. The row records the before-state, new evidence, after-state and residual counterevidence. An apparent contradiction may arise from different audiences, stakes or information; it does not automatically prove inconsistency or duplicity.

Ordinary routine is required evidence where available, not optional texture. Preserve low-stakes choices, habitual practice and mundane conversation in full readings. Crisis scenes have special conditions. Negative evidence requires an actual opportunity and records its denominator/limitations. “No evidence found” remains different from “observed refusal.”

Directed edges stay asymmetric. Do not normalize several relationships into a single affection/trust score. Keep exact address terms and context to support changes in social distance; a shift in register may indicate role, strategy, mood or development, and the strongest competing explanation remains visible.

## Reconstruction firewall

Future model candidates route to these records but live in separate model artifacts. A policy includes trigger/stakes/state, response family, inhibitors, overrides, exceptions, support, rivals and falsifier. Observations do not become promoted policies without testing. No synthetic output enters a primary evidence cell.

The architecture specifies twelve coverage dimensions and six diagnostic scenario families, with prospective held-out prediction freezes and an independent evaluator. No monograph, register table or self-assigned confidence can stand in for that evaluation. The global character discovery/capability registries remain outside this author's ownership.

## Update and review protocol

Create subject keys only after source inspection establishes names/identities. Each accepted unit appends new states, directed-edge changes, consequential register examples and opportunities; record explicit no-change coverage where relevant. The updater links evidence IDs, full reading sections and affected claim versions. A later naming discovery adds an alias/identity event without rewriting a frozen earlier boundary.

At checkpoint, inspect whether evidence overrepresents crises, one interlocutor, asserted motives or memorable catchphrases. Preserve substantial counterexamples, ambiguity and unremarkable situations. If a source channel is missing, state the gap and restrict the dependent conclusion. A compact current-view table may aid navigation, but historical states and underlying full reasoning remain intact.
